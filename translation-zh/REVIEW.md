# Points for a consistency pass

Found while drafting; not yet settled across books.

- Divination: *nachash* is 占卜 in Leviticus 19:26 and Deuteronomy 18:10, but 法術 in Numbers 23:23.
- עַם: 百姓 and 人民 are mixed in Exodus, Numbers and Deuteronomy.
- *yabbem*: 盡夫弟的本分 (Genesis 38) and 盡夫兄弟的本分 (Deuteronomy 25).
- Debir: 底璧 (Joshua) and 底壁 (Judges 1:11).
- Deuteronomy 14:13's birds are aligned with Leviticus 11:14 by a note; worth a specialist's look.
- Names table (i18n/zh-Hant.names.js): names of several words were often wrong and about 30 were fixed after
  Deuteronomy; single-word names sample at about 95%. Keys shared by several people (Hezron, Naaman, Talmai,
  Shemer, Abihail…) keep one form; the text uses each passage's Union Version form.
- Where the Leningrad Codex names a person or place differently from the Union Version, the text follows the
  Hebrew with the Union Version's form in a note (e.g. 1 Samuel 30:30, 2 Kings 20:12).
- 毗 / 毘: both forms appear in names (毗 in Genesis, Judges, Samuel, 1 Chronicles; 毘 in 2 Kings, 1 Chronicles 5, Ezra).
  Settle on one (the classic Union Version has 毘) and apply it everywhere, names table included.
- Governors: Ezra uses 省長 for *pechah* and *tirshata* (和合本 總督 for Tattenai); check Haggai and Daniel follow (Nehemiah does: 5:14–18; 7:65; 8:9; 10:2; 12:26).
- Siaha (Ezra 2:44): 西亞哈 against 和合本 西亞; doubtful.
- Delaiah (Nehemiah 7:62): 第來雅 as in Ezra 2:60, against 和合本 Nehemiah's 第萊雅; the passage-by-passage rule would give 第萊雅.
- Names-table keys with several bearers whose Nehemiah form differs (Mattaniah 瑪探雅/瑪他尼, Miniamin, Micaiah, Azarel, Azrikam,
  Eliakim, Berechiah, Mehetabel) were left alone; the text uses Nehemiah's Union Version forms.
- Ginnethoi (Nehemiah 12:4): 近頓 as 和合本, the Hebrew form only in a note. Nehemiah 12:23 renders *sefer divrei ha-yamim* 《歷代志》, noted as not the biblical book.
- Barachel (Job 32:2): 巴拉迦別 in the text from memory of the classic Union Version; the 1989 file has 巴拉迦. Check a classic edition.
- Behemoth 比希摩 (Job 40:15) is a coined transliteration; *go'el* is 救贖者 in Job 19:25 but 至近的親屬 in Ruth.
- Psalm title terms of uncertain meaning are transliterated with coined forms (示迦庸, 希迦庸, 瑪斯基勒, 米克坦, 舍米尼, 尼希錄, 基提, 亞拉莫);
  慕拉便 follows the Union Version. A specialist should confirm the forms before Psalms goes further.
- *ruach qodsheka* (Psalm 51:13): 你聖潔的靈 in the text, traditional 你的聖靈 in a note; Isaiah 63:10–11 should follow the same choice.
