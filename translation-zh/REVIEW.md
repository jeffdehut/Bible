# Points for a consistency pass

Found while drafting; not yet settled across books.

- Divination: *nachash* is 占卜 in Leviticus 19:26 and Deuteronomy 18:10, but 法術 in Numbers 23:23.
- עַם: 百姓 and 人民 are mixed in Exodus, Numbers and Deuteronomy.
- *yabbem*: 盡夫弟的本分 (Genesis 38) and 盡夫兄弟的本分 (Deuteronomy 25).
- Debir: 底璧 (Joshua) and 底壁 (Judges 1:11).
- Deuteronomy 14:13's birds are aligned with Leviticus 11:14 by a note; worth a specialist's look.
- Names table (i18n/zh-Hant.names.js): names of several words were often wrong and about 30 were fixed after
  Deuteronomy; single-word names sample at about 95%. Keys shared by several people (Hezron, Naaman, Talmai,
  Shemer, Abihail…) keep one form; the text uses each passage's Union Version form.
- Where the Leningrad Codex names a person or place differently from the Union Version, the text follows the
  Hebrew with the Union Version's form in a note (e.g. 1 Samuel 30:30, 2 Kings 20:12).
- 毗 / 毘: both forms appear in names (毗 in Genesis, Judges, Samuel, 1 Chronicles; 毘 in 2 Kings, 1 Chronicles 5, Ezra).
  Settle on one (the classic Union Version has 毘) and apply it everywhere, names table included.
- Governors: Ezra uses 省長 for *pechah* and *tirshata* (和合本 總督 for Tattenai); check Haggai and Daniel follow (Nehemiah does: 5:14–18; 7:65; 8:9; 10:2; 12:26).
- Siaha (Ezra 2:44): 西亞哈 against 和合本 西亞; doubtful.
- Delaiah (Nehemiah 7:62): 第來雅 as in Ezra 2:60, against 和合本 Nehemiah's 第萊雅; the passage-by-passage rule would give 第萊雅.
- Names-table keys with several bearers whose Nehemiah form differs (Mattaniah 瑪探雅/瑪他尼, Miniamin, Micaiah, Azarel, Azrikam,
  Eliakim, Berechiah, Mehetabel) were left alone; the text uses Nehemiah's Union Version forms.
- Ginnethoi (Nehemiah 12:4): 近頓 as 和合本, the Hebrew form only in a note. Nehemiah 12:23 renders *sefer divrei ha-yamim* 《歷代志》, noted as not the biblical book.
- Barachel (Job 32:2): 巴拉迦別 in the text from memory of the classic Union Version; the 1989 file has 巴拉迦. Check a classic edition.
- Behemoth 比希摩 (Job 40:15) is a coined transliteration; *go'el* is 救贖者 in Job 19:25 but 至近的親屬 in Ruth.
- Psalm title terms of uncertain meaning are transliterated with coined forms (示迦庸, 希迦庸, 瑪斯基勒, 米克坦, 舍米尼, 尼希錄, 基提, 亞拉莫);
  慕拉便 follows the Union Version. A specialist should confirm the forms before Psalms goes further.
- *ruach qodsheka* (Psalm 51:13): 你聖潔的靈 in the text, traditional 你的聖靈 in a note; Isaiah 63:10–11 should follow the same choice.
- בָּרוּךְ הַבָּא (Psalm 118:26) is 是蒙福的 by the Psalms rule, but Mark 11:9 has 是應當稱頌的; settle one rendering for the quotation in both places.
- *ne'um YHWH*: settled as 耶和華說 (opening) / 這是耶和華說的 (closing) everywhere; Kings and Chronicles brought into line (Isaiah 1–12).
- Isaiah 7:14 *almah*: 少女 in the text, 童女 (和合本, Matthew 1:23) in a note; Matthew 1:23 itself will read 童女 from the Greek *parthenos*.
- Isaiah 13–27: animals of the ruins (13:21–22; 14:23 *qippod* 刺蝟, 和合本「箭豬」) are uncertain and rendered loosely; Kir-hareseth and Kir-heres (16:7, 11) both 吉珥‧哈列設 as the Union Version; 23:10 and 24:16 (*razi li*) rest on uncertain Hebrew.
- Isaiah 28–39: 28:10, 13 *tsav la-tsav* transliterated as 擦夫拉擦夫 (coined); 28:25, 27 spices as modern 蒔蘿、孜然 against 和合本「小茴香、大茴香」; 38:14 *sus agur* 雨燕或鶇鳥 uncertain. 39:6 *amar YHWH* rendered 這是耶和華說的 to match 2 Kings 20:17, which blurs the *ne'um*/*amar* line kept at Isaiah 22:14. Perazim 毗拉心 (as 2 Samuel 5:20) against the classic 毘.
- Isaiah 40–52: Sinim (49:12) 希尼地 in the text, against 和合本「秦國」 (an identification with China; its margin has 希尼國): a departure from the names rule to check. 40:6 follows the Masoretic vowels 有人說 (English "I said", with 1QIsa-a and the Septuagint). Trees in 41:19 and 44:14 (杉樹 *tidhar*, 黃楊樹 *te'ashur*, 柏樹 *tirzah*, 月桂樹 *oren*) are uncertain. *Shevi* (52:2) 坐在寶座上 rather than 被擄的.
- Isaiah 53–66: 53:8 and 53:11 rest on difficult Hebrew (53:11 「必看見」 without object, Qumran 「光」 in a note). 57:9 *melekh* 王 in the text, 摩洛 in a note. 62:4 and 65:11 translate the names (「我喜悅她」「有夫之婦」; 時運之神、命運之神) as the Union Version does, with the Hebrew in notes. 65:15 「主耶和華必殺死你」 left without quotation marks (curse formula or narrative). 66:4 *ta'alulim* 災禍 uncertain. Javan in the names table is 希臘 (Daniel); Isaiah 66:19 has the Union Version's 雅完.
- Jeremiah 1–12: 2:2 *chesed* (the bride's devotion) is 忠貞 in the text, against the glossary's 慈愛 (note gives *chesed*). 3:7–10 *achot* 姊妹 against 和合本「妹妹」. 8:8 *soferim* 經學家 (Ezra's rule) for the scribes who copy the law. Birds in 8:7 (斑鳩、雨燕、鶇鳥) and 10:5 *tomer miqshah* 稻草人 are uncertain. *Tannim* (9:10; 10:22) 豺狼, as Isaiah 34:13, against 和合本「野狗」. Shihor (2:18) is 西曷 as 和合本 Jeremiah; the names table keeps 西曷河 for the key.
- Jeremiah 13–25: the potter (18:2–6) is 陶匠 as Isaiah 45:9 and 64:7, but Isaiah 29:16 and 30:14 have 窯匠; settle one. Samaria (23:13) 撒瑪利亞 as Isaiah, against the names table's 撒馬利亞. 17:19 *sha'ar benei ha-am* 平民門 and 19:2 哈珥西門 (*Charsit*) need a check against a classic Union Version. 15:11, 17:16, 23:26 rest on difficult Hebrew. Narratives with dialogue (18:1–12; 21:1–7) now use 「」 as English, unlike 11:1–17.
- Settled after Jeremiah 13–25: the potter is 陶匠 everywhere (Isaiah 29:16, 30:14 changed from 窯匠); Samaria is 撒馬利亞 in the text, as the classic Union Version (Jeremiah changed from 撒瑪利亞).
- Jeremiah 33–42: Rab-saris and Rab-mag (39:3, 13) follow 和合本 Jeremiah's 拉撒力, 拉墨 joined to the names (撒西金‧拉撒力), against Kings' 拉伯撒利; the CUV's hyphenation is uncertain. 35:3 Jeremiah the Rechabite is 雅利米雅 as 和合本. *ben ha-melekh* (36:26; 38:6) is 王子, where 和合本 reads a name 哈米勒. Tanhumeth 單戶篾 (Jeremiah 40:8) against 2 Kings 25:23 單戶蔑. 33:4–5, 37:16 (*chanuyot*) and 40:5 rest on difficult Hebrew. 38:23 follows the passive reading (城必被火焚燒) with the Masoretic 「你必用火焚燒」 in a note.
- Jeremiah 43–52: 46:25 Amon of No is 挪的亞們 (the 1989 CUV text has 亞捫, the Ammonite form; the classic text should be checked). 48:5 keeps Isaiah 15:5's 魯希坡的山坡, though 和合本 Jeremiah's name is 魯希 and 坡 already means the ascent; both should change together. 49:1, 3 *malkam* follows 和合本 瑪勒堪 in the text, not 米勒公 (1 Kings 11). 49:3 Ai is 愛 as 和合本 Jeremiah, against Joshua's 艾. 48:21 Jahzah 雅雜 as 和合本 Jeremiah (the names table has 雅哈撒). *hoy* in 47:6 is 唉 (a lament), not 禍哉. 43:12 (*atah*), 46:17, 48:30, 51:3, 51:13 and 52:23 (*ruchah*) rest on difficult Hebrew.
- Lamentations: *eikhah* rendered 怎麼……竟……！ (1:1; 2:1; 4:1–2); the acrostics are only described in notes. 1:8 *nidah*, 1:14 *nisqad*, 2:1 *ya'iv*, 3:65 *meginnat lev* and 4:7 (*peninim*) rest on rare or difficult words. 3:22 follows the Masoretic 「我們沒有滅絕」 against the Syriac/Targum reading many translations use.
- Ezekiel 1–7: *chashmal* (1:4, 27) is 琥珀金, a new rendering against 和合本「光耀的精金」; check before chapter 8. 1:18, 7:10–13 and 7:23 (*rattoq*) rest on difficult Hebrew. *shiqqutsim* is 可厭之物, kept apart from *gillulim* 可憎的偶像 and *to'evot* 可憎的事 (the three are close in Chinese). Diblah (6:14) is 第伯拉他 as 和合本, which includes the Hebrew directional ending; Buzi and Diblah added to the names table.
- Settled after Ezekiel 1–7: *chashmal* is 琥珀金 in 1:4, 1:27 and 8:2 (the Septuagint's *elektron*; 和合本「光耀的精金」 in the note at 1:4).
- Ezekiel 8–19: Pelatiah (11:1, 13) is 毘拉提, the classic form, against the 1989 text's and the names table's 毗拉提 (see 毗 / 毘 above). 10:2, 6 *galgal* is coined as 旋輪 to keep it apart from *ofan* 輪. 13:18–20 *kesatot* 符帶 and 16:30 *libbatekh*, 16:36 *nechoshet*, 19:7, 19:10 (*bedamkha*) rest on uncertain Hebrew. Chapter 16 renders the sexual imagery plainly (16:25–26); a native reviewer should judge the register.
- Ezekiel 20–32: 20:39, 21:15–21 (the sword song), 23:42–43, 27:19 (Uzal or 「紡成的」), 28:13 (*tuppim*, *neqavim*), 30:16 and 32:27 rest on difficult Hebrew.
  28:2–9 renders *el*/*elohim* in the king's claim as 神明 (或譯「神」). Migdol is 密奪 as Jeremiah, against 和合本 Ezekiel's 「色弗尼塔」; Kilmad (27:23) is 基抹 as 和合本,
  the same form as Chemosh; Gammadim (27:11) is transliterated 迦瑪 (和合本「勇士」). 29:3 and 32:2 *tannim* 大海怪 (和合本「大魚」). 30:18 keeps the Masoretic *chasakh* (白晝必退去).
  Chapter 23 renders the sexual imagery as plainly as chapter 16 (23:3, 8, 20–21); a native reviewer should judge the register.
- Ezekiel 33–48: 38:2–3, 39:1 follow the Union Version and the Septuagint in reading Rosh as a name (羅施), with 「米設、土巴的大領袖」 in a note; *nasi* is 領袖 (和合本「王」).
  37:1–14 renders *ruach* as 靈／氣息／風 by context, so the wordplay survives only in the note. The temple terms of 40–42 are coined in part (衞房 *ta*, 牆柱 *ayil*, 門廊 for the gate *ulam*,
  隔開的地方 *gizrah*, 樓廊 *attiq*, 臺層 *azarah*, 虎口 *zeret*) and rest on uncertain Hebrew (40:14, 30; 41:6–11, 15–17, 21; 42:2–12; 46:22); an architectural specialist should check them.
  The land *terumah* is 獻地／聖獻地 (和合本「供地」「聖供地」). 48:6, 31 Reuben 呂便 as Genesis; 1 Samuel 10 has 流便. 48:35 『耶和華在那裏』 (和合本「耶和華的所在」).
- Daniel: Darius is 大流士 as in Ezra (the classic Union Version has 大利烏); Ashpenaz 亞施毘拿 and *peres* 毘勒斯 (5:28) take the classic 毘 (names table keeps 毗, see 毗 / 毘 above).
  *ashshafim* 巫師, *gazerin* 占卜的 and the officials of 3:2 (謀士、司庫、法官、執法官) are approximate; *sar* for angels is 君 throughout (10:13 和合本「魔君」).
  7:13 「像人子的」 and 7:18 「至高者的聖民」 keep the traditional renderings, with the other readings only in notes. 9:24–27 follows the Masoretic accents in 9:25 against 和合本;
  9:24 *qodesh qodashim* is 至聖所 (或譯「至聖者」). *shiqquts* is 可憎之物 here (Mark 13:14) but 可厭之物 in Ezekiel; settle one. 2:1, 8:12, 9:26–27, 11:18 and 11:39 rest on difficult Hebrew/Aramaic.
- Darius: settled as the classic Union Version's 大利烏 (Ezra, Nehemiah, Daniel), as 古列 and 推羅; the 1989 text has 大流士. The names table follows.
- Hosea and Joel: Lo-ruhamah and Lo-ammi are 羅‧路哈瑪, 羅‧阿米 with the 1989 text's ‧ (the classic text may print them unbroken). Baal-peor 巴力‧毗珥 (9:10) and Pethuel 毗土珥 (Joel 1:1) keep 毗, as earlier books and the names table (see 毗 / 毘 above).
  Hosea 5:13 and 10:6 read *malki rav* 大王 against 和合本「耶雷布王」. Hosea 4:4, 4:18, 5:2, 6:9, 7:4–7, 8:10, 8:13, 10:5, 11:7, 12:1, 13:2, 13:9 and Joel 1:17, 2:6, 4:21 rest on difficult Hebrew.
  Moph (Hosea 9:6) is 摩弗 as 和合本 Hosea, though the same city is 挪弗 elsewhere. *Yevanim* (Joel 4:6) 希臘人 as 和合本 Joel, against Isaiah 66:19's 雅完.
- 毗 / 毘: settled as 毗 everywhere (text, notes and names table), the standard modern form; 毘 is its variant.
- Amos, Obadiah, Jonah, Micah: Sikkuth and Kiyyun (Amos 5:26) are coined as 西固特、基雲 (和合本 translates 「摩洛的帳幕」「偶像的星」 after the Septuagint); Karnaim (6:13) 加寧 after Genesis 14:5's 亞特律‧加寧.
  *anakh* (Amos 7:7–8) is 鉛垂線, against 和合本「準繩」 (kept for *qav*). Micah 2:12 *botsrah* is 羊圈 in the text, against 和合本's name 波斯拉. Micah 4:8 *Migdal-eder* is translated 羊群的守望樓 (Genesis 35:21 以得臺).
  Obadiah keeps Jeremiah 49's wording except where the Hebrew differs (Obadiah 5 stays a question; 6 uses 搜遍). Amos 2:7, 3:12, 6:10, 7:4, Obadiah 7, 20, Micah 1:10–15, 2:4–11, 6:9–10, 6:14, 7:3 rest on difficult Hebrew.
  Moresheth is 摩利沙 (1:1, as Jeremiah 26:18) but Moresheth-gath 摩利設‧迦特 (1:14), as 和合本. The names-table keys Obadiah (俄巴底) and Micah (米迦) are shared with other bearers and were left alone; the text has 俄巴底亞, 彌迦.
- Nahum to Malachi: Nahum 2:8 *hutsav* 已經定了 (和合本「王后」); 2:2 *mefits* 那驅散人的; 3:17 *minnezarayikh*, *tafsarayikh* 衞兵、官員 approximate. Habakkuk 3:1 *shigyonot* reuses the coined 示迦庸 (Psalm 7:1) rather than a new plural form.
  Zephaniah 1:11 keeps 和合本's name 瑪革提施 for *makhtesh*; 3:8 follows the Septuagint *le'ed* 作見證 (as the English) against the Masoretic *la'ad* and 和合本「擄掠」.
  Haggai and Zechariah use 和合本's 約撒答 for Jehozadak, against Ezra's 約薩達 and the names table's 約薩答 (left alone). Zechariah 1:8 *seruqqim* 栗色 (和合本「黃馬」); 11:7 the staffs translated 恩惠、聯合 (和合本「榮美」「聯索」).
  Difficult Hebrew: Nahum 1:10, 12; 2:9; Habakkuk 1:9, 11; 2:5; 3:9; Zephaniah 2:1–2; 3:18; Haggai 2:16, 19; Zechariah 2:12; 6:6; 9:15; 14:6; Malachi 2:15–16.
- Matthew 1–10: *magoi* is 占星家 (2:1), a departure from 和合本「博士」 (noted); a reviewer should confirm. 4:15 keeps Isaiah 8:23's 外族人的加利利 (and our Isaiah note's quotation of Matthew), against the New Testament's 外邦人 for *ethne* elsewhere.
  *Artos* is 食物 in 4:3–4 and 6:11 (as Deuteronomy 8:3) but 餅 in 7:9. 5:5 承受這地 as Psalm 37:11. The genealogy's *Asaph* and *Amos* (critical text) are 亞薩, 阿摩司, the psalmist's and prophet's forms; 和合本 follows *Asa*, *Amon*.
  The Lord's Prayer (6:9–13) should be read aloud by native readers for prayability. 10:28 *psyche* is 靈魂 (body and soul), 10:39 生命.
- Matthew 11–20: the commandments in 19:18 follow our Exodus 20 and Matthew 5:21, 27 (不可謀殺，不可通姦，不可偷竊), but Mark 10:19 has 不可殺人，不可姦淫，不可偷盜; align Mark. Simon Bar-jona (16:17) is 西門巴約拿 (classic, unbroken; 1989 西門‧巴‧約拿). (Settled: Mark 10:19 now reads as Exodus 20.)
  Caesarea Philippi is 該撒利亞腓立比 as Mark 8:27 (names table changed from 該撒利亞‧腓立比; Caesarea from the 1989 凱撒利亞 to 該撒利亞). The stater (17:27) is 一塊錢 as 和合本, to keep 銀幣 for the denarius; stadia (14:24) 斯他丟.
  11:12 (*biazetai*) is uncertain; 12:18–21 and 13:35 differ from both the Hebrew and our Old Testament (noted). 13:30 leaves the master's order to the reapers without inner quotation marks.
- Matthew 21–28: our Zechariah 9:9 note quotes Matthew 21:5 as 「又騎着驢，就是騎着驢駒」; Matthew's Greek (καὶ ἐπὶ πῶλον) has two animals, and 21:5 now reads 「騎着驢，又騎着驢駒，就是負軛之驢的崽子」: align the Zechariah note.
  22:39 follows Matthew 19:19 and Leviticus 19:18 (要愛你的鄰舍，如同愛自己), but Mark 12:31, 33 have 要愛鄰舍，像愛自己一樣; align Mark. 21:9 and 23:39 keep Mark 11:9's 是應當稱頌的 (the 蒙福/應當稱頌 point above is still open).
  *Nomikos* (22:35) is 律法專家, new; Luke uses the word often. 26:17 keeps Mark's 除酵節 against the Old Testament's 無酵節; settle one. 21:30 κύριε to a father is 父親; 26:50 (*eph' ho parei*) is uncertain.
- Settled after Matthew: "love your neighbour" as 要愛你的鄰舍，如同愛自己 (Mark 12:31, 33 aligned with Matthew); the feast 無酵節 everywhere (Mark 14, Matthew 26 aligned with the Old Testament).
