# Points for a consistency pass

Found while drafting; not yet settled across books.

- Divination: *nachash* is 占卜 in Leviticus 19:26 and Deuteronomy 18:10, but 法術 in Numbers 23:23.
- עַם: 百姓 and 人民 are mixed in Exodus, Numbers and Deuteronomy.
- *yabbem*: 盡夫弟的本分 (Genesis 38) and 盡夫兄弟的本分 (Deuteronomy 25).
- Debir: 底璧 (Joshua) and 底壁 (Judges 1:11).
- Deuteronomy 14:13's birds are aligned with Leviticus 11:14 by a note; worth a specialist's look.
- Names table (i18n/zh-Hant.names.js): names of several words were often wrong and about 30 were fixed after
  Deuteronomy; single-word names sample at about 95%. Keys shared by several people (Hezron, Naaman, Talmai,
  Shemer, Abihail…) keep one form; the text uses each passage's Union Version form.
- Where the Leningrad Codex names a person or place differently from the Union Version, the text follows the
  Hebrew with the Union Version's form in a note (e.g. 1 Samuel 30:30, 2 Kings 20:12).
- 毗 / 毘: both forms appear in names (毗 in Genesis, Judges, Samuel, 1 Chronicles; 毘 in 2 Kings, 1 Chronicles 5, Ezra).
  Settle on one (the classic Union Version has 毘) and apply it everywhere, names table included.
- Governors: Ezra uses 省長 for *pechah* and *tirshata* (和合本 總督 for Tattenai); check Haggai and Daniel follow (Nehemiah does: 5:14–18; 7:65; 8:9; 10:2; 12:26).
- Siaha (Ezra 2:44): 西亞哈 against 和合本 西亞; doubtful.
- Delaiah (Nehemiah 7:62): 第來雅 as in Ezra 2:60, against 和合本 Nehemiah's 第萊雅; the passage-by-passage rule would give 第萊雅.
- Names-table keys with several bearers whose Nehemiah form differs (Mattaniah 瑪探雅/瑪他尼, Miniamin, Micaiah, Azarel, Azrikam,
  Eliakim, Berechiah, Mehetabel) were left alone; the text uses Nehemiah's Union Version forms.
- Ginnethoi (Nehemiah 12:4): 近頓 as 和合本, the Hebrew form only in a note. Nehemiah 12:23 renders *sefer divrei ha-yamim* 《歷代志》, noted as not the biblical book.
- Barachel (Job 32:2): 巴拉迦別 in the text from memory of the classic Union Version; the 1989 file has 巴拉迦. Check a classic edition.
- Behemoth 比希摩 (Job 40:15) is a coined transliteration; *go'el* is 救贖者 in Job 19:25 but 至近的親屬 in Ruth.
- Psalm title terms of uncertain meaning are transliterated with coined forms (示迦庸, 希迦庸, 瑪斯基勒, 米克坦, 舍米尼, 尼希錄, 基提, 亞拉莫);
  慕拉便 follows the Union Version. A specialist should confirm the forms before Psalms goes further.
- *ruach qodsheka* (Psalm 51:13): 你聖潔的靈 in the text, traditional 你的聖靈 in a note; Isaiah 63:10–11 should follow the same choice.
- בָּרוּךְ הַבָּא (Psalm 118:26) is 是蒙福的 by the Psalms rule, but Mark 11:9 has 是應當稱頌的; settle one rendering for the quotation in both places.
- *ne'um YHWH*: settled as 耶和華說 (opening) / 這是耶和華說的 (closing) everywhere; Kings and Chronicles brought into line (Isaiah 1–12).
- Isaiah 7:14 *almah*: 少女 in the text, 童女 (和合本, Matthew 1:23) in a note; Matthew 1:23 itself will read 童女 from the Greek *parthenos*.
- Isaiah 13–27: animals of the ruins (13:21–22; 14:23 *qippod* 刺蝟, 和合本「箭豬」) are uncertain and rendered loosely; Kir-hareseth and Kir-heres (16:7, 11) both 吉珥‧哈列設 as the Union Version; 23:10 and 24:16 (*razi li*) rest on uncertain Hebrew.
- Isaiah 28–39: 28:10, 13 *tsav la-tsav* transliterated as 擦夫拉擦夫 (coined); 28:25, 27 spices as modern 蒔蘿、孜然 against 和合本「小茴香、大茴香」; 38:14 *sus agur* 雨燕或鶇鳥 uncertain. 39:6 *amar YHWH* rendered 這是耶和華說的 to match 2 Kings 20:17, which blurs the *ne'um*/*amar* line kept at Isaiah 22:14. Perazim 毗拉心 (as 2 Samuel 5:20) against the classic 毘.
- Isaiah 40–52: Sinim (49:12) 希尼地 in the text, against 和合本「秦國」 (an identification with China; its margin has 希尼國): a departure from the names rule to check. 40:6 follows the Masoretic vowels 有人說 (English "I said", with 1QIsa-a and the Septuagint). Trees in 41:19 and 44:14 (杉樹 *tidhar*, 黃楊樹 *te'ashur*, 柏樹 *tirzah*, 月桂樹 *oren*) are uncertain. *Shevi* (52:2) 坐在寶座上 rather than 被擄的.
- Isaiah 53–66: 53:8 and 53:11 rest on difficult Hebrew (53:11 「必看見」 without object, Qumran 「光」 in a note). 57:9 *melekh* 王 in the text, 摩洛 in a note. 62:4 and 65:11 translate the names (「我喜悅她」「有夫之婦」; 時運之神、命運之神) as the Union Version does, with the Hebrew in notes. 65:15 「主耶和華必殺死你」 left without quotation marks (curse formula or narrative). 66:4 *ta'alulim* 災禍 uncertain. Javan in the names table is 希臘 (Daniel); Isaiah 66:19 has the Union Version's 雅完.
- Jeremiah 1–12: 2:2 *chesed* (the bride's devotion) is 忠貞 in the text, against the glossary's 慈愛 (note gives *chesed*). 3:7–10 *achot* 姊妹 against 和合本「妹妹」. 8:8 *soferim* 經學家 (Ezra's rule) for the scribes who copy the law. Birds in 8:7 (斑鳩、雨燕、鶇鳥) and 10:5 *tomer miqshah* 稻草人 are uncertain. *Tannim* (9:10; 10:22) 豺狼, as Isaiah 34:13, against 和合本「野狗」. Shihor (2:18) is 西曷 as 和合本 Jeremiah; the names table keeps 西曷河 for the key.
- Jeremiah 13–25: the potter (18:2–6) is 陶匠 as Isaiah 45:9 and 64:7, but Isaiah 29:16 and 30:14 have 窯匠; settle one. Samaria (23:13) 撒瑪利亞 as Isaiah, against the names table's 撒馬利亞. 17:19 *sha'ar benei ha-am* 平民門 and 19:2 哈珥西門 (*Charsit*) need a check against a classic Union Version. 15:11, 17:16, 23:26 rest on difficult Hebrew. Narratives with dialogue (18:1–12; 21:1–7) now use 「」 as English, unlike 11:1–17.
- Settled after Jeremiah 13–25: the potter is 陶匠 everywhere (Isaiah 29:16, 30:14 changed from 窯匠); Samaria is 撒馬利亞 in the text, as the classic Union Version (Jeremiah changed from 撒瑪利亞).
- Jeremiah 33–42: Rab-saris and Rab-mag (39:3, 13) follow 和合本 Jeremiah's 拉撒力, 拉墨 joined to the names (撒西金‧拉撒力), against Kings' 拉伯撒利; the CUV's hyphenation is uncertain. 35:3 Jeremiah the Rechabite is 雅利米雅 as 和合本. *ben ha-melekh* (36:26; 38:6) is 王子, where 和合本 reads a name 哈米勒. Tanhumeth 單戶篾 (Jeremiah 40:8) against 2 Kings 25:23 單戶蔑. 33:4–5, 37:16 (*chanuyot*) and 40:5 rest on difficult Hebrew. 38:23 follows the passive reading (城必被火焚燒) with the Masoretic 「你必用火焚燒」 in a note.
