Proverbs by Topic

After its opening chapters, Proverbs is hundreds of short sayings, and a subject comes back again and again, far apart. Here every verse is a square, chapter by chapter. Choose a topic to light up everything the book says about it, and read it together.

The collections the book marks

Topics

    A verse is put under a topic when its Hebrew uses one of the topic's words (by Strong's number, from STEPBible's tagged text) or its English does, so a saying about the tongue is found whether it says tongue, lips, mouth or words. It is a way in, not a commentary: a few verses will be found by a word used in passing.
