The Psalms

A hundred and fifty songs and prayers in five books. Each tile is a psalm, as wide as it is long and colored by its type. Tap one to read its heading, its story and its links to the New Testament; choose a type, an author or a group to see where they fall.

Color by

A ring in the corner marks a lament or song of the whole people, not one person.

Groups

From lament to praise

Who wrote them

As the headings name them. Fifty psalms have no name over them; Psalm 88 names both the sons of Korah and Heman.

At a glance

The types follow one common way of sorting the psalms, compiled for this site (data/psalms.csv). Scholars sort many psalms differently, and many mix types: a lament may end in praise, a royal psalm may give thanks. The headings, authors and settings are the Bible's own, as this translation gives them; the New Testament links are the strongest cross-references from each psalm (OpenBible.info).
