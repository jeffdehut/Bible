    About this map

    A style study for the project's Bible maps, after the Eilers sheet Ancient Jerusalem (A. H. Eilers & Co., St. Louis), redrawn from open data.

    What we kept from Eilers

    • Hachured hills and the valleys between them
    • A "?" after places whose location is uncertain
    • A grid with an index of places keyed to it

    What we changed

    • Labels never overlap. More appear as you zoom in.
    • Type is never smaller than 11 pixels on screen
    • The walls are left out: no open survey of the ancient walls exists, and drawing them from guesswork would look more certain than it is

    How it was made

    • The ground comes from fine elevation data (about 5 m); the hachures and watercourses are drawn from it, so the Kidron, the Central Valley and the Valley of Hinnom show as they are today, partly filled with the debris of centuries.
    • Places are those OpenBible.info locates within the city: springs, pools, hills, gardens and buildings. Many places it can only put "in Jerusalem", such as most of the gates Nehemiah names, are left off.
    • The journeys follow the Gospels, Acts and 2 Samuel. Every place is named in the verse given, and the build checks each name against this project's translation.
    • Names are spelled as in this project's Open Plain English Translation.

    Sources

    • Places: OpenBible.info Bible Geocoding Data, CC BY 4.0
    • Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM, GMTED2010, ETOPO1)
    • Style: the Eilers Sunday School wall maps, published before 1931
