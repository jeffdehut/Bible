Privacy

Last changed 30 October 2026

In short: reading here needs no account and sends nothing about you anywhere. Your highlights, notes, bookmarks and settings stay in your browser. Only if you choose to sign in, to keep them on all your devices, does this site keep anything: your email address, your highlights, notes and bookmarks, and your reading plan. You can delete them whenever you like.

Without an account

Everything you do is kept in your own browser, on your own device:

  • your display settings (text size, light or dark, verse numbering);
  • your highlights, notes, markings and bookmarks, the markings you made, your reading plan and the days you've marked, and the chapter you were reading;
  • copies of the books you have opened, so they work offline.

None of it is sent to us. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it; make a backup first (under My highlights and notes) if you want to keep your notes.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It keeps your highlights, notes, markings, bookmarks and reading plan the same on every device where you sign in. The account holds:

  • your email address, to send you sign-in links;
  • your highlights, notes, markings and bookmarks, each with the verse and the time it was changed, and the markings you made (their names, symbols and colors);
  • your reading plan, if you follow one: which plan, the day you started and the days you've marked read.

It does not hold what else you read, what you search for, where you are, or anything else. We send you email only to sign you in: no newsletters, and we never sell or share your address or your notes.

Accounts are stored with Supabase, which runs the database and sends the sign-in emails for us. The database lets each person read and change only their own notes. The people who run this site can see the database, as the operators of any such service can; they look at it only to keep the service working.

Deleting your account

In the reader, open the menu, choose Sync across devices, then Delete my account. Your account and every highlight, note and plan in it are deleted at once and for good. What is saved on your device stays there. Signing out, instead, leaves your notes both on the device and in your account.

What other services see

There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Like any website, the pages are delivered by a hosting service that keeps ordinary server logs, and they load fonts from Google Fonts and, on the maps, genealogy and timeline, a charting library from cdnjs. These services see your device's internet address when it fetches from them, as with any web request.

Reading aloud

Listening uses the voices built into your device and browser, so nothing is sent to us. Most of those voices work on the device itself. A few are provided online by the maker of your browser (Google's voices in Chrome, the "Online (Natural)" voices in Microsoft Edge); with those, the verses being read are sent to that company to be spoken. Choose a voice not marked online if you prefer.
