Open Data

Everything this site is built from is open: download the translation and the data, use it in your own study or software, and share it, with credit as each license asks. The complete source, with the scripts that build every file, is on GitHub.

Words and lexicons

  • Word-by-word data (Genesis; one file per book)

    Each verse's Hebrew or Greek words with a transliteration, gloss, Strong's number and grammar, from STEPBible's tagged texts. The other books are beside it in data/bible/words/.

    STEPBible's abridged Brown–Driver–Briggs (Hebrew) and Abbott-Smith (Greek), by Strong's number, in data/bible/lexicon/.
