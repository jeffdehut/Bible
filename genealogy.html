        The Generations

        זֶה סֵפֶר תּוֹלְדֹת אָדָם“This is the book of the generations of Adam.” Genesis 5:1

        Every family tie the Bible records, from Adam to the apostles.

        • Whole shows everyone descended from Adam whom the Bible names, with the families who married into them. Time runs from left to right, a generation to a column, colored by era, and the line from Adam to Jesus runs straight through in gold. Choosing anyone draws their line back to Adam.
        • Close shows the person chosen with their ancestors above and their descendants below, for anyone, inside Adam’s family or not.

        Sources. People, names and family links: STEPBible TIPNR (Tyndale House, Cambridge), CC BY 4.0. Lifespans follow Floyd Nolen Jones’s refinement of Ussher’s chronology, from Theographic (CC BY-SA 4.0); they are traditional dates, not modern estimates.

        Where a genealogy skips generations, the link is marked as an ancestor link. People the text mentions without naming are shown in italics. TIPNR also fills two gaps with unnamed generations of its own, nine between Matthan and Jacob to match the count of Luke 3 and four between Ithamar and Eli; no verse names or counts them, so they are left out: Matthew 1:15 calls Matthan the father of Jacob, and Eli is shown as a descendant of Ithamar. The chart of the whole family is laid out when the site is built, by scripts/connections.py.
