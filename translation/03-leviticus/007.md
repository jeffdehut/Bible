**1** "These are the instructions for the guilt offering. It is most holy.

**2** The guilt offering is to be slaughtered in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered, and its blood is to be splashed against all sides of the altar.

**3** All its fat is to be offered from it: the fat tail, the fat that covers the internal organs,

**4** the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, by the loins, and the long lobe of the liver, which is to be removed with the kidneys.

**5** The priest is to burn them on the altar as an offering by fire to Yahweh.[^1] It is a guilt offering.

**6** Any male among the priests may eat it. It is to be eaten in a holy place. It is most holy.

**7** The guilt offering is like the sin offering: there is one rule for both. It belongs to the priest who makes atonement with it.

**8** The priest who offers anyone's burnt offering is to have for himself the skin of the burnt offering he has offered.

**9** Every grain offering that is baked in an oven, and every one prepared in a pan or on a griddle, belongs to the priest who offers it.

**10** Every grain offering, whether mixed with oil or dry, belongs to all of Aaron's sons, to each one equally.

**11** "These are the instructions for the fellowship offering that is offered to Yahweh.

**12** If someone offers it as an expression of thanks,[^2] then along with the thanksgiving sacrifice he is to offer cakes made without yeast and mixed with oil, wafers made without yeast and spread with oil, and cakes of fine flour well soaked and mixed with oil.

**13** He is to present his offering with cakes of bread made with yeast, along with his thanksgiving fellowship offering.

**14** From each kind of offering he is to present one as a contribution[^3] to Yahweh. It belongs to the priest who splashes the blood of the fellowship offering.

**15** The meat of his thanksgiving fellowship offering must be eaten on the day it is offered. He must not leave any of it until morning.

**16** But if the sacrifice he offers is a vow offering[^4] or a freewill offering, it is to be eaten on the day he offers his sacrifice, and what is left of it may be eaten the next day.

**17** Any meat of the sacrifice that is left on the third day must be burned up in the fire.

**18** If any of the meat of his fellowship offering is eaten on the third day, the one who offered it will not be accepted. It will not be credited to him. It is spoiled meat,[^5] and the person who eats any of it will bear the consequences of his wrongdoing.

**19** Meat that touches anything unclean must not be eaten. It must be burned up in the fire. As for other meat, anyone who is clean may eat it.

**20** But if anyone eats meat from the fellowship offering that belongs to Yahweh while he is unclean,[^6] that person must be cut off from his people.[^7]

**21** If anyone touches anything unclean, whether human uncleanness or an unclean animal or any unclean detestable creature,[^8] and then eats any of the meat of the fellowship offering that belongs to Yahweh, that person must be cut off from his people."

**22** Yahweh said to Moses,

**23** "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: You must not eat any fat of an ox, a sheep or a goat.

**24** The fat of an animal that has died[^9] and the fat of an animal torn by wild beasts may be used for any other purpose, but you must certainly not eat it.

**25** Anyone who eats the fat of an animal from which an offering by fire may be presented to Yahweh, the person who eats it must be cut off from his people.

**26** You must not eat any blood, whether of a bird or of an animal, wherever you live.

**27** Anyone who eats any blood, that person must be cut off from his people."

**28** Yahweh said to Moses,

**29** "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: Whoever offers his fellowship offering to Yahweh must bring part of his fellowship offering as his offering to Yahweh.

**30** His own hands are to bring the offerings by fire to Yahweh. He is to bring the fat together with the breast, so that the breast may be waved as a wave offering[^10] before Yahweh.

**31** The priest is to burn the fat on the altar, but the breast belongs to Aaron and his sons.

**32** You are to give the right thigh to the priest as a contribution from your fellowship offerings.

**33** The one among Aaron's sons who offers the blood and the fat of the fellowship offering is to have the right thigh as his portion.

**34** For I have taken the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution from the Israelites, from their fellowship offerings, and I have given them to Aaron the priest and his sons as their permanent share from the Israelites.

**35** This is the portion[^11] of Aaron and the portion of his sons from the offerings by fire to Yahweh, from the day they were presented to serve Yahweh as priests.

**36** Yahweh commanded that the Israelites should give it to them from the day he anointed them. It is a permanent rule for all their generations."

**37** These are the instructions for the burnt offering, the grain offering, the sin offering, the guilt offering, the ordination offering[^12] and the fellowship offering,

**38** which Yahweh commanded Moses on Mount Sinai, on the day he commanded the Israelites to bring their offerings to Yahweh in the wilderness of Sinai.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *todah*, "thanksgiving, praise". Also in verses 13 and 15.
[^3]: Hebrew *terumah*: something set apart and given for a sacred purpose, traditionally "heave offering" (Exodus 25:2). Also in verses 32 and 34.
[^4]: Literally "a vow": an offering promised to Yahweh in a vow.
[^5]: Hebrew *piggul*, "foul, rotten": meat that has become unfit to eat and offensive to God.
[^6]: Literally "and his uncleanness is upon him".
[^7]: Hebrew *karet*, "be cut off": removed from the community of Israel, whether by death at God's hand or by being shut out. The exact meaning is debated. Also in verses 21, 25 and 27.
[^8]: Hebrew *sheqets*, "detestable thing", the word used for unclean creatures in chapter 11.
[^9]: Hebrew *nevelah*: an animal that died of itself rather than being slaughtered.
[^10]: Hebrew *tenufah*, traditionally "wave offering": a rite of presenting something to Yahweh, perhaps by lifting it up or by moving it toward the altar and back. Also in verse 34.
[^11]: Literally "the anointing". The word may mean the share that belongs to them because they are anointed, or it may be a related word meaning "portion".
[^12]: Hebrew *millu'im*, "filling", from the phrase "fill the hand", meaning to install someone as a priest (chapter 8; Exodus 29).
