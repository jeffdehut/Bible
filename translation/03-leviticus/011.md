**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses and Aaron,

**2** "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: These are the creatures you may eat, of all the animals that live on land.

**3** You may eat any animal that has divided hooves, completely split in two, and that chews the cud.[^2]

**4** But among those that chew the cud or have divided hooves, you must not eat these: the camel, because although it chews the cud, it does not have divided hooves. It is unclean for you.

**5** The rock badger,[^3] because although it chews the cud, it does not have divided hooves. It is unclean for you.

**6** The hare, because although it chews the cud, it does not have divided hooves. It is unclean for you.

**7** And the pig, because although it has divided hooves, completely split in two, it does not chew the cud. It is unclean for you.

**8** You must not eat their meat, and you must not touch their carcasses. They are unclean for you.

**9** "Of all the creatures that live in the water, you may eat these: you may eat anything in the water that has fins and scales, whether in the seas or in the rivers.

**10** But anything in the seas or in the rivers that does not have fins and scales, among all the swarming creatures in the water and all the living creatures that are in the water, is detestable[^4] to you.

**11** They are to remain detestable to you. You must not eat their meat, and you must detest their carcasses.

**12** Anything in the water that does not have fins and scales is detestable to you.

**13** "These are the birds you are to detest. They must not be eaten, because they are detestable: the eagle,[^5] the bearded vulture, the black vulture,

**14** the kite, every kind of falcon,

**15** every kind of raven,

**16** the ostrich,[^6] the nighthawk, the gull, every kind of hawk,

**17** the little owl, the cormorant, the great owl,

**18** the white owl, the pelican, the carrion vulture,

**19** the stork, every kind of heron, the hoopoe and the bat.

**20** "All winged swarming creatures that walk on all fours[^7] are detestable to you.

**21** But among all the winged swarming creatures that walk on all fours, you may eat those that have jointed legs above their feet,[^8] with which to leap on the ground.

**22** Of these you may eat: every kind of locust, every kind of bald locust, every kind of cricket and every kind of grasshopper.[^9]

**23** But all other winged swarming creatures that have four legs are detestable to you.

**24** "You will become unclean by these. Whoever touches their carcasses will be unclean until evening.

**25** Whoever carries any part of their carcasses must wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening.

**26** Every animal that has hooves but whose hooves are not completely split, and that does not chew the cud, is unclean for you. Whoever touches them will become unclean.

**27** All the four-footed animals that walk on their paws are unclean for you. Whoever touches their carcasses will be unclean until evening.

**28** Whoever carries their carcasses must wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening. They are unclean for you.

**29** "These are unclean for you among the swarming creatures that swarm on the ground: the mole rat,[^10] the mouse, every kind of great lizard,

**30** the gecko, the monitor lizard, the lizard, the skink and the chameleon.

**31** These are the ones that are unclean for you among all the swarming creatures. Whoever touches them when they are dead will be unclean until evening.

**32** Anything on which one of them falls when they are dead will become unclean, whether it is any article of wood, or clothing, or leather, or sackcloth, any article that is used for any work. It must be put into water, and it will be unclean until evening. Then it will be clean.

**33** If any of them falls into a clay pot, everything in it will become unclean, and you must break the pot.

**34** Any food that may be eaten will become unclean if water from such a pot has come on it, and any liquid that may be drunk will become unclean in any such pot.[^11]

**35** Anything on which any part of their carcass falls will become unclean. An oven or a stove[^12] must be broken down. They are unclean, and they must remain unclean for you.

**36** However, a spring or a cistern that collects water will remain clean, but whoever touches their carcass in it will become unclean.[^13]

**37** If any part of their carcass falls on any seed that is to be sown, the seed remains clean.

**38** But if water has been put on the seed and any part of their carcass falls on it, it is unclean for you.

**39** "If any animal that you may eat dies, whoever touches its carcass will be unclean until evening.

**40** Whoever eats any of its carcass must wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening, and whoever carries its carcass must wash his clothes and will be unclean until evening.

**41** "Every swarming creature that swarms on the ground is detestable. It must not be eaten.

**42** Whatever moves on its belly,[^14] whatever walks on all fours, or whatever has many feet, among all the swarming creatures that swarm on the ground, you must not eat them, because they are detestable.

**43** Do not make yourselves detestable[^15] by any swarming creature that swarms. Do not make yourselves unclean by them, so that you become unclean through them.

**44** For I am the LORD your God. Consecrate yourselves and be holy, because I am holy. Do not make yourselves unclean by any swarming creature that moves on the ground.

**45** For I am the LORD, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt to be your God. So be holy, because I am holy.

**46** "These are the instructions about land animals, birds, every living creature that moves in the water and every creature that swarms on the ground,

**47** to distinguish between the unclean and the clean, and between the living creatures that may be eaten and the living creatures that may not be eaten."

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "brings up the cud": brings up food from the stomach to chew it again.
[^3]: Hebrew *shafan*, the rock hyrax, a small animal that lives among rocks; traditionally "coney". The hyrax and the hare are not true ruminants, but they move their jaws as though chewing the cud.
[^4]: Hebrew *sheqets*, "detestable, loathsome": a creature to be avoided because it makes a person unclean. Also in verses 11–13, 20, 23, 41–43.
[^5]: The identification of many of the birds in verses 13–19 is uncertain. *Eagle* is Hebrew *nesher*, which may also be the griffon vulture; *bearded vulture* is *peres*, "breaker"; *black vulture* is *ozniyyah*, perhaps the osprey; *kite* is *da'ah*; *falcon* is *ayyah*; *nighthawk* is *tachmas*; *gull* is *shachaf*; *little owl* is *kos*; *cormorant* is *shalakh*; *great owl* is *yanshuf*, perhaps the ibis; *white owl* is *tinshemet* (compare verse 30); *pelican* is *qa'at*; *carrion vulture* is *racham*; *heron* is *anafah*; *hoopoe* is *dukhifat*.
[^6]: Hebrew *bat ha-ya'anah*, perhaps "the eagle-owl".
[^7]: Literally "that go on four", an expression for creatures that walk or crawl rather than fly. Also in verses 21, 27 and 42.
[^8]: The Hebrew text is written "that do not have jointed legs" but read "that have jointed legs".
[^9]: The identification of these four kinds of locusts and grasshoppers (Hebrew *arbeh*, *sol'am*, *chargol* and *chagav*) is uncertain.
[^10]: The identification of the animals in verses 29–30 is uncertain. *Mole rat* is Hebrew *choled*, perhaps the weasel; *great lizard* is *tsav*; *gecko* is *anaqah*; *monitor lizard* is *koach*; *lizard* is *leta'ah*; *skink* is *chomet*; *chameleon* is *tinshemet*, the word used for a bird in verse 18.
[^11]: Literally "Of all the food that may be eaten, that on which water comes shall be unclean; and all drink that may be drunk in any vessel shall be unclean." The verse probably refers to the clay pot of verse 33.
[^12]: Hebrew *kirayim*, perhaps a stand for cooking pots.
[^13]: Literally "but whoever touches their carcass will become unclean".
[^14]: In the Hebrew text the letter *vav* in the word for "belly" (*gachon*) is written large. According to Jewish tradition it marks the middle letter of the Torah.
[^15]: Literally "Do not make your selves (*nephesh*) detestable". Also in verse 44.
