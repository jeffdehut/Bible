**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Tell the Israelites: Any Israelite, or any foreigner[^2] who lives in Israel, who gives any of his children to Molech[^3] must be put to death. The people of the land[^4] are to stone him.

**3** I myself will set my face against that man and cut him off from among his people, because he has given some of his children to Molech, and so made my sanctuary unclean and profaned my holy name.

**4** If the people of the land close their eyes to that man when he gives any of his children to Molech, and do not put him to death,

**5** I myself will set my face against that man and against his family. I will cut off from among their people both him and all who follow him in prostituting themselves to Molech.[^5]

**6** If anyone turns to mediums and spiritists,[^6] prostituting himself by following them, I will set my face against that person and cut him off from among his people.

**7** Consecrate yourselves and be holy, because I am Yahweh your God.

**8** Keep my decrees and do them. I am Yahweh, who makes you holy.

**9** "Anyone who curses his father or his mother must be put to death. He has cursed his father or his mother; his blood is on his own head.[^7]

**10** If a man commits adultery with another man's wife, if he commits adultery with his neighbor's wife, both the man and the woman who commit adultery must be put to death.

**11** If a man lies with his father's wife, he has dishonored his father.[^8] Both of them must be put to death; their blood is on their own heads.

**12** If a man lies with his daughter-in-law, both of them must be put to death. They have committed a perversion;[^9] their blood is on their own heads.

**13** If a man lies with a male as one lies with a woman, both of them have done a detestable thing. They must be put to death; their blood is on their own heads.

**14** If a man marries a woman and her mother, it is depravity. He and they must be burned in the fire, so that there will be no depravity among you.

**15** If a man has sexual intercourse with an animal, he must be put to death, and you must kill the animal.

**16** If a woman approaches any animal to mate with it, you must kill the woman and the animal. They must be put to death; their blood is on their own heads.

**17** If a man marries his sister, his father's daughter or his mother's daughter, and they have sexual relations,[^10] it is a disgrace.[^11] They must be cut off in the sight of their people. He has had sexual relations with his sister; he will bear the consequences of his wrongdoing.

**18** If a man lies with a woman during her monthly period and has sexual relations with her, he has laid bare the source of her flow, and she has exposed the source of her blood. Both of them must be cut off from among their people.

**19** Do not have sexual relations with your mother's sister or your father's sister, for that is to lay bare one's own close relative. They will bear the consequences of their wrongdoing.

**20** If a man lies with his aunt,[^12] he has dishonored his uncle. They will bear the consequences of their sin; they will die childless.

**21** If a man marries his brother's wife, it is an impurity.[^13] He has dishonored his brother. They will be childless.

**22** "Keep all my decrees and all my rulings, and do them, so that the land where I am bringing you to live will not vomit you out.

**23** Do not follow the customs of the nation that I am driving out before you. Because they did all these things, I loathed them.

**24** But I have said to you, 'You will take possession of their land. I will give it to you to possess, a land flowing with milk and honey.' I am Yahweh your God, who has set you apart from the peoples.

**25** So you must distinguish between the clean and the unclean animals, and between the unclean and the clean birds. Do not make yourselves detestable by any animal or bird or anything that moves on the ground, which I have set apart for you as unclean.

**26** You are to be holy to me, because I, Yahweh, am holy, and I have set you apart from the peoples to be mine.

**27** "A man or a woman who is a medium or a spiritist[^14] must be put to death. They are to be stoned; their blood is on their own heads."

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ger*, a resident foreigner (see 16:29).
[^3]: See 18:21. Also in verses 3–5.
[^4]: Here the members of the local community. Also in verse 4.
[^5]: Literally "to whore after Molech", a common image for unfaithfulness to Yahweh (17:7). So also in verse 6.
[^6]: Hebrew *ovot* and *yidde'onim*. See 19:31.
[^7]: Literally "his blood is on him": he himself is responsible for his death. So also in verses 11–13, 16 and 27.
[^8]: Literally "he has uncovered his father's nakedness" (see 18:8). So also in verses 17–21 with the other relatives.
[^9]: Hebrew *tevel*. See 18:23.
[^10]: Literally "and he sees her nakedness and she sees his nakedness".
[^11]: Hebrew *chesed*, here a rare word meaning "disgrace", spelled the same as the word for "faithful love".
[^12]: Hebrew *dodah*, the wife of his father's brother (18:14).
[^13]: Hebrew *niddah*, the word used for a woman's menstrual impurity (15:19).
[^14]: Literally "in whom there is a spirit of the dead or a spirit that knows" (19:31).
