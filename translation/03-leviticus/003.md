**1** "If his offering is a fellowship offering,[^1] and he offers an animal from the herd, whether male or female, he is to offer one without defect before the LORD.[^2]

**2** He is to lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the tent of meeting. Then Aaron's sons, the priests, are to splash the blood against all sides of the altar.

**3** From the fellowship offering he is to present an offering by fire to the LORD: the fat[^3] that covers the internal organs and all the fat that is on the internal organs,

**4** the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, by the loins,[^4] and the long lobe of the liver,[^5] which he is to remove with the kidneys.[^6]

**5** Aaron's sons are to burn it on the altar on top of the burnt offering, which is on the wood on the fire, an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**6** "If his offering for a fellowship offering to the LORD is from the flock, whether male or female, he is to offer one without defect.

**7** If he offers a lamb as his offering, he is to present it before the LORD.

**8** He is to lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it in front of the tent of meeting. Then Aaron's sons are to splash its blood against all sides of the altar.

**9** From the fellowship offering he is to present its fat as an offering by fire to the LORD: the whole fat tail,[^7] which he is to remove close to the backbone,[^8] the fat that covers the internal organs and all the fat that is on the internal organs,

**10** the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, by the loins, and the long lobe of the liver, which he is to remove with the kidneys.

**11** The priest is to burn it on the altar as food,[^9] an offering by fire to the LORD.

**12** "If his offering is a goat, he is to present it before the LORD.

**13** He is to lay his hand on its head and slaughter it in front of the tent of meeting. Then Aaron's sons are to splash its blood against all sides of the altar.

**14** From it he is to present his offering as an offering by fire to the LORD: the fat that covers the internal organs and all the fat that is on the internal organs,

**15** the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, by the loins, and the long lobe of the liver, which he is to remove with the kidneys.

**16** The priest is to burn them on the altar as food, an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma. All the fat belongs to the LORD.

**17** This is a permanent decree for all your generations, wherever you live: you must not eat any fat or any blood."

[^1]: Hebrew *zevach shelamim*, "a sacrifice of *shelamim*", traditionally "peace offering". *Shelamim* is related to *shalom*, "peace, well-being, wholeness", but its exact sense is uncertain. Most of the meat of this sacrifice was eaten by the worshiper and his household (7:11–21).
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *chelev*: the layers of fat around the internal organs, not the fat mixed into the meat.
[^4]: Or "on the sinews". Hebrew *kesalim*, the muscles of the lower back.
[^5]: Literally "the appendage on the liver", probably the lobe that projects from it.
[^6]: Or "which he is to remove along with the kidneys". Literally "on the kidneys he shall remove it".
[^7]: Hebrew *alyah*: the broad tail of the fat-tailed sheep, a breed common in the Middle East.
[^8]: Hebrew *atseh*, found only here: probably the base of the spine.
[^9]: Literally "bread", a common word for food in general. Also in verse 16.
