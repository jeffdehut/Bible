**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: When a woman conceives and gives birth to a boy, she will be unclean for seven days. She will be unclean just as during the days of her monthly period.[^2]

**3** On the eighth day the flesh of the boy's foreskin is to be circumcised.

**4** Then she is to remain for thirty-three days in the blood of her purification.[^3] She must not touch anything holy or come into the sanctuary until the days of her purification are completed.

**5** If she gives birth to a girl, she will be unclean for two weeks, as during her monthly period. Then she is to remain for sixty-six days in the blood of her purification.

**6** "When the days of her purification are completed, whether for a son or for a daughter, she is to bring to the priest at the entrance of the tent of meeting a lamb a year old for a burnt offering, and a young pigeon or a turtledove for a sin offering.[^4]

**7** He is to offer it before Yahweh and make atonement for her, and she will be clean from her flow of blood.[^5] These are the instructions for a woman who gives birth to a boy or a girl.

**8** If she cannot afford a lamb,[^6] she is to take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one for a burnt offering and the other for a sin offering. So the priest will make atonement for her, and she will be clean."

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "as in the days of the impurity of her sickness" (see 15:19–24).
[^3]: That is, the time during which her bleeding after childbirth continues while she becomes clean. Also in verse 5.
[^4]: Hebrew *chatta't*. Here no wrongdoing is involved: the offering removes the impurity of childbirth. See the note on 4:3.
[^5]: Literally "from the source of her blood".
[^6]: Literally "If her hand does not find enough for a sheep or goat".
