**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses,[^2]

**2** "Command Aaron and his sons: These are the instructions[^3] for the burnt offering. The burnt offering is to stay on its hearth on the altar all night until morning, and the fire of the altar is to be kept burning on it.

**3** The priest is to put on his linen garment,[^4] with linen undergarments[^5] next to his body. He is to remove the ashes of the burnt offering that the fire has consumed on the altar, and put them beside the altar.

**4** Then he is to take off his garments and put on other garments, and carry the ashes outside the camp to a ritually clean place.

**5** The fire on the altar is to be kept burning on it. It must not go out. Every morning the priest is to burn wood on it, arrange the burnt offering on it, and burn the fat of the fellowship offerings on it.

**6** A fire is to be kept burning on the altar continually. It must not go out.

**7** "These are the instructions for the grain offering. Aaron's sons are to present it before the LORD, in front of the altar.

**8** One of them is to take from it a handful of the fine flour of the grain offering and of its oil, with all the frankincense that is on the grain offering, and burn it on the altar as a pleasing aroma, its memorial portion for the LORD.

**9** Aaron and his sons are to eat what is left of it. It is to be eaten without yeast in a holy place. They are to eat it in the courtyard of the tent of meeting.

**10** It must not be baked with yeast. I have given it to them as their share of my offerings by fire. It is most holy, like the sin offering and the guilt offering.

**11** Any male among Aaron's descendants may eat it. It is their permanent share,[^6] for all your generations, from the offerings by fire to the LORD. Whatever touches them will become holy."[^7]

**12** The LORD said to Moses,

**13** "This is the offering that Aaron and his sons are to present to the LORD on the day he is anointed:[^8] a tenth of an ephah[^9] of fine flour as a regular grain offering, half of it in the morning and half of it in the evening.

**14** It is to be prepared with oil on a griddle. Bring it well soaked,[^10] and present the grain offering in broken pieces[^11] as a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**15** The priest from among his sons who is anointed to succeed him is to prepare it. It is a permanent decree: it is to be burned completely for the LORD.

**16** Every grain offering of a priest is to be burned completely.[^12] It must not be eaten."

**17** The LORD said to Moses,

**18** "Speak to Aaron and his sons: These are the instructions for the sin offering. The sin offering is to be slaughtered before the LORD in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered. It is most holy.

**19** The priest who offers it as a sin offering is to eat it. It is to be eaten in a holy place, in the courtyard of the tent of meeting.

**20** Whatever touches its meat will become holy. If any of its blood is spattered on a garment, you must wash the spattered part in a holy place.

**21** The clay pot in which it is boiled must be broken. But if it is boiled in a bronze pot, the pot is to be scoured and rinsed with water.

**22** Any male among the priests may eat it. It is most holy.

**23** But no sin offering whose blood is brought into the tent of meeting to make atonement in the holy place may be eaten. It must be burned up in the fire.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Verses 1–23 are 6:8–30 in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law": here the rules for each kind of offering. Also in verses 7 and 18.
[^4]: Hebrew *mad*, "garment", perhaps a long tunic.
[^5]: Shorts reaching from the waist to the thighs (Exodus 28:42).
[^6]: Hebrew *choq*, "a fixed portion, a rule". Also in verse 15.
[^7]: Or "Anyone who touches them must be holy." Also in verse 20.
[^8]: Or "from the day he is anointed". *He* is Aaron, or each high priest after him.
[^9]: An ephah was probably about 22 liters (two-thirds of a bushel), so a tenth of an ephah was about 2 liters (2 quarts).
[^10]: Hebrew *murbekhet*, a rare word: perhaps "well mixed" or "fried".
[^11]: Hebrew *tufinei*, a word found only here, of uncertain meaning: perhaps "baked" or "crumbled".
[^12]: Hebrew *kalil*, "whole, complete": a whole offering, nothing of which is eaten. Also in verse 15.
