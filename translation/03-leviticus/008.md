**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Take Aaron and his sons with him, the garments, the anointing oil, the bull for the sin offering, the two rams and the basket of bread made without yeast,

**3** and assemble the whole community at the entrance of the tent of meeting."

**4** Moses did as Yahweh commanded him, and the community assembled at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**5** Moses said to the community, "This is what Yahweh has commanded to be done."

**6** Then Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them with water.

**7** He put the tunic on Aaron, wrapped the sash around him, clothed him with the robe and put the ephod[^2] on him. He tied the woven waistband of the ephod around him, fastening the ephod to him with it.

**8** He put the breastpiece[^3] on him, and put the Urim and the Thummim[^4] into the breastpiece.

**9** He put the turban on his head, and on the turban, at the front, he put the gold plate,[^5] the holy crown,[^6] just as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**10** Then Moses took the anointing oil and anointed the tabernacle[^7] and everything in it, and so consecrated them.

**11** He sprinkled some of the oil on the altar seven times, and anointed the altar and all its utensils, and the basin with its stand, to consecrate them.

**12** He poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron's head and anointed him to consecrate him.

**13** Then Moses brought Aaron's sons forward, clothed them with tunics, wrapped sashes around them and tied caps on them, just as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**14** Then he brought the bull for the sin offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the bull for the sin offering.

**15** He slaughtered it,[^8] and Moses took the blood and put it with his finger on the horns of the altar all around, and so purified the altar.[^9] He poured out the blood at the base of the altar. So he consecrated it, to make atonement for it.

**16** Moses took all the fat that was on the internal organs, the long lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys with their fat, and burned them on the altar.

**17** But the bull, with its skin, its flesh and its dung, he burned up in the fire outside the camp, just as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**18** Then he presented the ram for the burnt offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the ram's head.

**19** He slaughtered it, and Moses splashed the blood against all sides of the altar.

**20** He cut the ram into its pieces, and Moses burned the head, the pieces and the fat.

**21** He washed the internal organs and the legs with water, and Moses burned the whole ram on the altar. It was a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma, an offering by fire to Yahweh, just as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**22** Then he presented the other ram, the ram of ordination,[^10] and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the ram's head.

**23** He slaughtered it, and Moses took some of its blood and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear, on the thumb of his right hand and on the big toe of his right foot.

**24** Then he brought Aaron's sons forward, and Moses put some of the blood on the lobes of their right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands and on the big toes of their right feet. Then Moses splashed the blood against all sides of the altar.

**25** He took the fat: the fat tail, all the fat that was on the internal organs, the long lobe of the liver, the two kidneys with their fat, and the right thigh.

**26** From the basket of bread made without yeast that was before Yahweh, he took one cake made without yeast, one cake of bread made with oil and one wafer, and he put them on the fat portions and on the right thigh.

**27** He put all these in the hands of Aaron and in the hands of his sons, and waved them as a wave offering before Yahweh.

**28** Then Moses took them from their hands and burned them on the altar on top of the burnt offering. They were an ordination offering, a pleasing aroma. It was an offering by fire to Yahweh.

**29** Moses took the breast and waved it as a wave offering before Yahweh. It was Moses' portion from the ram of ordination, just as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**30** Then Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood that was on the altar, and sprinkled it on Aaron and his garments, and on his sons and his sons' garments with him. So he consecrated Aaron and his garments, and his sons and his sons' garments with him.

**31** Moses said to Aaron and his sons, "Boil the meat at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and eat it there with the bread that is in the basket of the ordination offerings, just as I commanded:[^11] 'Aaron and his sons are to eat it.'

**32** Whatever is left over of the meat and the bread you must burn up in the fire.

**33** Do not leave the entrance of the tent of meeting for seven days, until the time of your ordination is completed, for your ordination will take seven days.[^12]

**34** What has been done today, Yahweh has commanded to be done, to make atonement for you.

**35** You must stay at the entrance of the tent of meeting day and night for seven days and keep Yahweh's charge,[^13] so that you do not die, for that is what I have been commanded."

**36** So Aaron and his sons did everything that Yahweh had commanded through Moses.[^14]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ephod*, a priestly garment worn over the robe (Exodus 28:6–14).
[^3]: Hebrew *choshen*, a square of woven cloth worn on the high priest's chest (Exodus 28:15–30).
[^4]: Hebrew *urim* and *tummim*, perhaps "lights" and "perfections". They were objects used to learn God's will (Exodus 28:30; Numbers 27:21), but how they worked is not known.
[^5]: Hebrew *tsits*, literally "flower" or "shining thing" (Exodus 28:36–38).
[^6]: Hebrew *nezer ha-qodesh*, "the holy diadem", the sign of Aaron's consecration (Exodus 29:6).
[^7]: Hebrew *mishkan*, "dwelling place": the portable sanctuary, also called the tent of meeting.
[^8]: Literally "He slaughtered". The Hebrew does not say whether Moses or Aaron did it. So also in verses 19, 20, 21 and 23.
[^9]: Hebrew *chitte*, literally "un-sin", the verb related to *chatta't*, "sin offering".
[^10]: Hebrew *millu'im*, "filling": see 7:37. Also in verses 28, 29, 31 and 33.
[^11]: The same Hebrew consonants can be read "as I was commanded", as in verse 35.
[^12]: Literally "for seven days he will fill your hand".
[^13]: Or "do what Yahweh requires". Hebrew *mishmeret*, "guard duty, obligation".
[^14]: Literally "by the hand of Moses".
