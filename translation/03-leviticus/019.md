**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Speak to the whole community of the Israelites and tell them: Be holy, because I, Yahweh your God, am holy.

**3** Each of you must respect[^2] his mother and his father, and you must keep my Sabbaths. I am Yahweh your God.

**4** Do not turn to idols,[^3] and do not make gods of cast metal for yourselves. I am Yahweh your God.

**5** "When you sacrifice a fellowship offering to Yahweh, sacrifice it in such a way that you will be accepted.

**6** It must be eaten on the day you sacrifice it or on the next day. Whatever is left over until the third day must be burned up in the fire.

**7** If any of it is eaten on the third day, it is spoiled meat;[^4] it will not be accepted.

**8** Whoever eats it will bear the consequences of his wrongdoing, because he has profaned what is holy to Yahweh. That person must be cut off from his people.

**9** "When you reap the harvest of your land, do not reap all the way to the edges of your field, and do not gather the gleanings[^5] of your harvest.

**10** Do not go back over your vineyard,[^6] and do not gather the grapes that have fallen in your vineyard. Leave them for the poor and for the foreigner.[^7] I am Yahweh your God.

**11** "Do not steal. Do not deceive or lie to one another.

**12** Do not swear falsely by my name, and so profane the name of your God. I am Yahweh.

**13** Do not oppress your neighbor or rob him. Do not keep back the wages of a hired worker overnight until morning.

**14** Do not curse the deaf or put a stumbling block in front of the blind. Fear your God. I am Yahweh.

**15** "Do not do wrong in judging. Do not show partiality to the poor or favor to the powerful.[^8] Judge your neighbor fairly.

**16** Do not go around spreading slander among your people. Do not stand by when your neighbor's life is at stake.[^9] I am Yahweh.

**17** Do not hate your brother in your heart. Rebuke your neighbor frankly, so that you do not incur sin because of him.[^10]

**18** Do not take revenge or bear a grudge against any of your people, but love your neighbor as yourself.[^11] I am Yahweh.

**19** "Keep my decrees. Do not let two different kinds of your livestock mate. Do not sow your field with two kinds of seed. Do not wear a garment made of two kinds of material.[^12]

**20** "If a man lies with a woman and has sexual intercourse with her, and she is a slave woman promised to another man, but she has not been ransomed or given her freedom, there must be an inquiry.[^13] They are not to be put to death, because she had not been freed.

**21** He is to bring his guilt offering to Yahweh at the entrance of the tent of meeting, a ram as a guilt offering.

**22** With the ram of the guilt offering the priest is to make atonement for him before Yahweh for the sin he has committed, and he will be forgiven for the sin he has committed.

**23** "When you come into the land and plant any kind of fruit tree, regard its fruit as forbidden.[^14] For three years it is to be forbidden to you. It must not be eaten.

**24** In the fourth year all its fruit is to be holy, an offering of praise to Yahweh.

**25** But in the fifth year you may eat its fruit, so that its yield will increase for you. I am Yahweh your God.

**26** "Do not eat anything with the blood.[^15] Do not practice divination or sorcery.[^16]

**27** Do not round off the hair at the sides of your head, or ruin the edges of your beard.

**28** Do not make cuts in your bodies for the dead,[^17] or put tattoo marks[^18] on yourselves. I am Yahweh.

**29** Do not profane your daughter by making her a prostitute, or the land will turn to prostitution and the land will be full of depravity.

**30** Keep my Sabbaths, and revere my sanctuary. I am Yahweh.

**31** Do not turn to mediums or to spiritists.[^19] Do not seek them out, and so become unclean through them. I am Yahweh your God.

**32** Stand up in the presence of the gray-haired, and show respect to the elderly. Fear your God. I am Yahweh.

**33** "When a foreigner lives with you in your land, do not mistreat him.

**34** The foreigner who lives with you is to be treated like the native-born among you. Love him as yourself, for you were foreigners in the land of Egypt. I am Yahweh your God.

**35** Do not do wrong in judging, or in measures of length, weight or quantity.

**36** You are to have honest scales, honest weights, an honest ephah[^20] and an honest hin.[^21] I am Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt.

**37** Keep all my decrees and all my rulings, and do them. I am Yahweh."

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "fear". Also in verse 30, "revere".
[^3]: Hebrew *elilim*, "worthless things, nothings", a mocking word for idols.
[^4]: Hebrew *piggul*. See 7:18.
[^5]: The ears of grain that are dropped or missed by the reapers.
[^6]: Or "Do not strip your vineyard bare". The verb means to go over a vineyard a second time to pick the grapes that are left.
[^7]: Hebrew *ger*, a resident foreigner (see 16:29). Also in verses 33 and 34.
[^8]: Literally "Do not lift up the face of the poor, and do not honor the face of the great."
[^9]: Literally "Do not stand on the blood of your neighbor". It may also mean "Do not seek your neighbor's life", for example by false testimony.
[^10]: Or "so that you do not bear sin because of him".
[^11]: Quoted in Matthew 19:19; 22:39; Mark 12:31; Romans 13:9; Galatians 5:14; James 2:8.
[^12]: Hebrew *sha'atnez*, a word of uncertain origin. Deuteronomy 22:11 explains it as wool and linen woven together.
[^13]: Hebrew *biqqoret*, a word found only here: perhaps "investigation", "punishment" or "compensation".
[^14]: Literally "you shall treat its fruit as its foreskin". Also in the next sentence: "it shall be uncircumcised to you".
[^15]: Literally "on the blood": perhaps eating meat with the blood still in it, or eating beside the blood as part of a ritual.
[^16]: Hebrew *nichesh*, reading omens, and *onen*, perhaps reading signs in the clouds.
[^17]: Literally "for a *nephesh*", here a dead person.
[^18]: Hebrew *ketovet qa'aqa*, a phrase found only here: probably marks cut or pricked into the skin.
[^19]: Hebrew *ovot* and *yidde'onim*: spirits of the dead, or people who claimed to call them up and consult them (1 Samuel 28:3–25).
[^20]: An ephah was a dry measure of probably about 22 liters (two-thirds of a bushel).
[^21]: A hin was a liquid measure of about a gallon (4 liters).
