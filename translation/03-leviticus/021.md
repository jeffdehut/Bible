**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses, "Speak to the priests, the sons of Aaron, and tell them: A priest must not make himself unclean for a dead person[^2] among his people,

**2** except for a close relative: his mother or his father, his son or his daughter, his brother,

**3** or his unmarried sister, who is close to him because she has no husband. For her he may make himself unclean.

**4** He must not make himself unclean as a husband[^3] among his people, and so profane himself.

**5** Priests must not shave bald patches on their heads or shave off the edges of their beards or make cuts in their bodies.

**6** They are to be holy to their God and must not profane the name of their God, because they present the offerings by fire to Yahweh, the food[^4] of their God. So they must be holy.

**7** They must not marry a woman who is a prostitute or who has been defiled,[^5] and they must not marry a woman divorced from her husband, for the priest is holy to his God.

**8** You are to treat him as holy, because he offers the food of your God. He is to be holy to you, because I, Yahweh, who make you holy, am holy.

**9** If a priest's daughter profanes herself by prostitution, she profanes her father. She must be burned in the fire.

**10** "The priest who is highest among his brothers,[^6] on whose head the anointing oil has been poured and who has been ordained[^7] to wear the garments, must not let the hair of his head hang loose or tear his clothes.

**11** He must not go near any dead body.[^8] He must not make himself unclean even for his father or his mother.

**12** He must not leave the sanctuary, or profane the sanctuary of his God, because the consecration[^9] of the anointing oil of his God is on him. I am Yahweh.

**13** He is to marry a woman who is a virgin.

**14** He must not marry a widow, a divorced woman, or a woman defiled by prostitution. He is to marry only a virgin from his own people,

**15** so that he does not profane his descendants among his people, for I am Yahweh, who makes him holy."

**16** Yahweh said to Moses,

**17** "Speak to Aaron and tell him: None of your descendants in any generation who has a defect is to come near to offer the food of his God.

**18** For no man who has a defect may come near: no man who is blind or lame, or who has a disfigured face or a deformed limb,[^10]

**19** or a man who has a broken foot or a broken hand,

**20** or who is hunchbacked or a dwarf, or who has a spot in his eye, or who has a festering rash or scabs, or crushed testicles.[^11]

**21** No man among the descendants of Aaron the priest who has a defect is to come near to present the offerings by fire to Yahweh. He has a defect; he must not come near to offer the food of his God.

**22** He may eat the food of his God, both the most holy and the holy things.

**23** But he must not go near the veil or come near the altar, because he has a defect. He must not profane my holy places,[^12] for I am Yahweh, who makes them holy."

**24** So Moses spoke to Aaron and his sons and to all the Israelites.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "for a *nephesh*", here a dead person.
[^3]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "He shall not make himself unclean, a husband (or master) among his people, to profane himself." It may mean that he must not make himself unclean for his wife's relatives, or that as a leader among his people he must not make himself unclean.
[^4]: Literally "bread", a common word for food in general (3:11). Also in verses 8, 17 and 21–22.
[^5]: Or "a woman defiled by prostitution" (as in verse 14).
[^6]: Literally "the priest who is great among his brothers", that is, the high priest.
[^7]: Literally "who has filled his hand" (8:33).
[^8]: Literally "any *nephesh* of the dead".
[^9]: Hebrew *nezer*, "consecration" or "crown" (8:9).
[^10]: The exact meaning of the Hebrew words *charum* and *sarua* is uncertain.
[^11]: The meaning of several of the Hebrew words in this verse is uncertain.
[^12]: Or "my holy things". Literally "my sanctuaries".
