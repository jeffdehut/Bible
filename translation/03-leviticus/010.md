**1** Aaron's sons Nadab and Abihu each took his firepan,[^1] put fire in it and laid incense on it. They offered before the LORD[^2] unauthorized fire,[^3] which he had not commanded them to offer.

**2** Fire came out from before the LORD and consumed them, and they died before the LORD.

**3** Then Moses said to Aaron, "This is what the LORD meant when he said, 'Among those who are near me I will show myself holy, and before all the people I will be glorified.'" And Aaron was silent.

**4** Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan, the sons of Aaron's uncle Uzziel, and said to them, "Come here and carry your relatives[^4] away from the front of the sanctuary, outside the camp."

**5** So they came and carried them, still in their tunics, outside the camp, as Moses had said.

**6** Then Moses said to Aaron and to his sons Eleazar and Ithamar, "Do not let your hair hang loose, and do not tear your clothes,[^5] or you will die, and the LORD will be angry[^6] with the whole community. But your relatives, the whole house of Israel, may mourn over the burning that the LORD has sent.[^7]

**7** Do not leave the entrance of the tent of meeting, or you will die, for the LORD's anointing oil is on you." So they did as Moses said.

**8** The LORD said to Aaron,

**9** "You and your sons with you must not drink wine or beer[^8] when you go into the tent of meeting, or you will die. This is a permanent decree for all your generations.

**10** You are to distinguish between the holy and the common,[^9] and between the unclean and the clean,

**11** and you are to teach the Israelites all the rules that the LORD has spoken to them through Moses."

**12** Moses said to Aaron and to his surviving sons, Eleazar and Ithamar, "Take the grain offering that is left over from the offerings by fire to the LORD, and eat it without yeast beside the altar, for it is most holy.

**13** Eat it in a holy place, because it is your share and your sons' share of the offerings by fire to the LORD. That is what I have been commanded.

**14** But the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution you may eat in a ritually clean place, you and your sons and your daughters with you, for they have been given as your share and your sons' share from the fellowship offerings of the Israelites.

**15** The thigh of the contribution and the breast of the wave offering are to be brought together with the fat portions for the offerings by fire, to be waved as a wave offering before the LORD. They will belong to you and to your sons with you as a permanent share, just as the LORD has commanded."

**16** Then Moses searched carefully[^10] for the goat of the sin offering, and found that it had been burned up. He was angry with Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron's surviving sons, and said,

**17** "Why did you not eat the sin offering in the holy area? It is most holy, and it was given to you to take away the guilt[^11] of the community, to make atonement for them before the LORD.

**18** Look, its blood was not brought inside the holy place. You should certainly have eaten it in the holy area, as I commanded."

**19** Aaron replied to Moses, "Look, today they offered their sin offering and their burnt offering before the LORD, and yet things like these have happened to me. If I had eaten the sin offering today, would that have been pleasing in the LORD's sight?"

**20** When Moses heard this, he was satisfied.[^12]

[^1]: Hebrew *machtah*, a pan for carrying burning coals, traditionally "censer".
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "strange fire": fire or incense that the LORD had not authorized (compare Exodus 30:9).
[^4]: Literally "your brothers". Nadab and Abihu were their cousins.
[^5]: Letting the hair hang loose and tearing one's clothes were signs of mourning (21:10). Or "Do not uncover your heads".
[^6]: Literally "he will be angry".
[^7]: Literally "may weep the burning that the LORD has burned".
[^8]: Hebrew *shekhar*, an alcoholic drink made from grain or fruit, perhaps a kind of beer.
[^9]: Hebrew *chol*: what is ordinary, not set apart as holy.
[^10]: Literally "seeking, he sought".
[^11]: Literally "to bear the iniquity".
[^12]: Literally "it was good in his eyes".
