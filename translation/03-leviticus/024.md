**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Command the Israelites to bring you pure oil of pressed olives[^2] for the light, so that a lamp can be kept burning regularly.

**3** Outside the veil of the testimony[^3] in the tent of meeting, Aaron is to keep it in order before Yahweh regularly, from evening until morning. This is a permanent decree for all your generations.

**4** He is to keep the lamps on the pure gold lampstand[^4] in order before Yahweh regularly.

**5** "Take fine flour and bake twelve loaves[^5] with it, using two-tenths of an ephah[^6] for each loaf.

**6** Set them in two rows,[^7] six to a row, on the pure gold table before Yahweh.

**7** Put pure frankincense on each row, to be a memorial portion for the bread, an offering by fire to Yahweh.

**8** Every Sabbath day he is to set it out before Yahweh regularly. It is an everlasting covenant on the part of the Israelites.[^8]

**9** It is to belong to Aaron and his sons, and they are to eat it in a holy place, because it is most holy for him, a portion of the offerings by fire to Yahweh. It is a permanent share."

**10** Now the son of an Israelite woman, whose father was an Egyptian, went out among the Israelites. A fight broke out in the camp between the son of the Israelite woman and an Israelite man.

**11** The son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the Name[^9] and cursed. So they brought him to Moses. His mother's name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.

**12** They put him in custody until the will of Yahweh should be made clear to them.[^10]

**13** Then Yahweh said to Moses,

**14** "Take the man who cursed outside the camp. All who heard him are to lay their hands on his head, and the whole community is to stone him.

**15** And tell the Israelites: Anyone who curses his God will bear the consequences of his sin.[^11]

**16** Whoever blasphemes the name of Yahweh must be put to death. The whole community must stone him. Whether he is a foreigner[^12] or native-born, when he blasphemes the Name, he must be put to death.

**17** "Anyone who takes the life of any human being[^13] must be put to death.

**18** Anyone who takes the life of an animal must make it good, life for life.

**19** If anyone injures his neighbor, whatever he has done must be done to him:

**20** fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth. Whatever injury he has caused to someone must be caused to him.[^14]

**21** Whoever kills an animal must make it good, but whoever kills a human being must be put to death.

**22** You are to have one law[^15] for the foreigner and for the native-born alike, for I am Yahweh your God."

**23** Then Moses spoke to the Israelites, and they took the man who had cursed outside the camp and stoned him. The Israelites did as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "pure olive oil, beaten": oil from olives crushed by hand, the finest grade (Exodus 27:20).
[^3]: The veil in front of the ark, which held the testimony, the stone tablets of the covenant (Exodus 25:16; 26:33).
[^4]: Literally "the pure lampstand": the lampstand of pure gold (Exodus 25:31). So also "the pure table" in verse 6 (Exodus 25:23–24).
[^5]: Hebrew *challot*, round loaves or cakes (2:4). These were the bread of the Presence (Exodus 25:30).
[^6]: Literally "two tenths". An ephah was probably about 22 liters (two-thirds of a bushel), so two-tenths of an ephah was about 4 liters (4 quarts).
[^7]: Or "two piles". Also in verse 7.
[^8]: Or "It is from the Israelites, an everlasting covenant."
[^9]: That is, the name of Yahweh (verse 16). The Hebrew verb *naqav* can mean "pronounce, designate" or "pierce", here perhaps "speak the name in a curse". Also in verse 16.
[^10]: Literally "to make a decision for them at the mouth of Yahweh".
[^11]: Literally "he will bear his sin".
[^12]: Hebrew *ger*, a resident foreigner (see 16:29). Also in verse 22.
[^13]: Literally "strikes any *nephesh* of a human being". So also in verse 18, "strikes the *nephesh* of an animal".
[^14]: Compare Exodus 21:23–25; Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38.
[^15]: Hebrew *mishpat*, "judgment, ruling".
