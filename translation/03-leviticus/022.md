**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Tell Aaron and his sons to treat with care[^2] the holy offerings of the Israelites, which they consecrate to me, so that they do not profane my holy name. I am Yahweh.

**3** Say to them: In all your generations, if any of your descendants comes near the holy offerings that the Israelites consecrate to Yahweh while he is unclean,[^3] that person must be cut off from my presence. I am Yahweh.

**4** None of Aaron's descendants who has a skin disease[^4] or a discharge may eat any of the holy offerings until he is clean. If anyone touches anything made unclean by a dead body,[^5] or a man has an emission of semen,

**5** or a man touches any swarming creature that makes him unclean, or any person who makes him unclean, whatever his uncleanness may be,

**6** the person who touches any such thing will be unclean until evening. He must not eat any of the holy offerings unless he has bathed his body in water.

**7** When the sun has set, he will be clean, and after that he may eat the holy offerings, for they are his food.

**8** He must not eat an animal that has died or that was torn by wild beasts, and so make himself unclean by it. I am Yahweh.

**9** The priests are to keep my charge, so that they do not incur sin because of it and die for it when they profane it. I am Yahweh, who makes them holy.

**10** "No outsider[^6] may eat any holy offering. Neither a priest's resident guest[^7] nor a hired worker may eat a holy offering.

**11** But if a priest buys someone with his money,[^8] that person may eat it, and those born in his household may eat his food.

**12** If a priest's daughter marries an outsider, she must not eat any of the holy contributions.[^9]

**13** But if a priest's daughter becomes a widow or is divorced, and she has no children, and she returns to her father's household as in her youth, she may eat her father's food. But no outsider may eat it.

**14** "If anyone eats a holy offering unintentionally, he must add a fifth of its value to it and give the holy offering to the priest.

**15** The priests must not profane the holy offerings of the Israelites, which they set apart for Yahweh,

**16** by letting them eat their holy offerings and so bringing on them guilt that requires a penalty.[^10] For I am Yahweh, who makes them holy."

**17** Yahweh said to Moses,

**18** "Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the Israelites and tell them: Any Israelite, or any foreigner[^11] in Israel, who presents his offering as a burnt offering to Yahweh, whether for any of their vows or as any of their freewill offerings,

**19** must offer a male without defect from the cattle, the sheep or the goats, so that you may be accepted.

**20** Do not offer anything that has a defect, because it will not be accepted on your behalf.

**21** When anyone presents a fellowship offering to Yahweh to fulfill a vow[^12] or as a freewill offering, from the herd or from the flock, it must be without defect to be accepted. There must be no defect in it.

**22** You must not offer to Yahweh anything blind, injured or maimed, or anything with a running sore, a festering rash or scabs.[^13] You must not put any of these on the altar as an offering by fire to Yahweh.

**23** You may present as a freewill offering an ox or a sheep that has a limb too long or too short,[^14] but it will not be accepted for a vow.

**24** You must not offer to Yahweh an animal whose testicles are bruised, crushed, torn or cut. You must not do this in your land.[^15]

**25** You must not accept any such animals from a foreigner[^16] and offer them as the food of your God. They are damaged; they have a defect. They will not be accepted on your behalf."

**26** Yahweh said to Moses,

**27** "When an ox, a sheep or a goat is born, it is to stay with its mother for seven days. From the eighth day on, it will be accepted as an offering by fire to Yahweh.

**28** But do not slaughter a cow or a ewe and its young on the same day.

**29** When you sacrifice a thanksgiving offering to Yahweh, sacrifice it in such a way that you will be accepted.

**30** It must be eaten the same day. Do not leave any of it until morning. I am Yahweh.

**31** "Keep my commandments and do them. I am Yahweh.

**32** Do not profane my holy name. I must be treated as holy among the Israelites. I am Yahweh, who makes you holy,

**33** who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God. I am Yahweh."

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "that they keep themselves separate from the holy things of the Israelites".
[^3]: Literally "and his uncleanness is upon him".
[^4]: Hebrew *tsara'at*, traditionally "leprosy": a range of skin conditions that made a person ritually unclean (chapter 13).
[^5]: Literally "anything unclean by a *nephesh*", here a dead person.
[^6]: Hebrew *zar*, "stranger": here anyone who does not belong to a priest's family. Also in verses 12 and 13.
[^7]: Hebrew *toshav*, someone living in the priest's household who is not a member of the family.
[^8]: That is, a slave.
[^9]: Hebrew *terumat ha-qodashim*: the holy things set apart as a contribution for the priests (7:14).
[^10]: Literally "and they will cause them to bear the iniquity of guilt when they eat their holy things". It is not clear whether "them" means the priests or the people.
[^11]: Hebrew *ger*, a resident foreigner (see 16:29).
[^12]: Or "to make a special vow".
[^13]: The exact meaning of several of the Hebrew words in this verse is uncertain.
[^14]: Literally "overgrown or stunted".
[^15]: That is, you must not offer such animals. Some understand it as "you must not castrate animals".
[^16]: Hebrew *ben nekhar*, a foreigner who is not a resident among the Israelites.
