**1** "When anyone[^1] brings a grain offering[^2] to the LORD,[^3] his offering is to be of fine flour.[^4] He is to pour oil on it and put frankincense[^5] on it.

**2** He is to bring it to Aaron's sons, the priests, and the priest is to take from it a full handful of its fine flour and its oil, together with all its frankincense. Then the priest is to burn it on the altar as the memorial portion[^6] of the offering, an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**3** The rest of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is most holy,[^7] a portion of the offerings by fire to the LORD.

**4** "When you bring a grain offering baked in an oven, it is to be of fine flour: cakes[^8] made without yeast and mixed with oil, or wafers made without yeast and spread with oil.

**5** "If your offering is a grain offering made on a griddle, it is to be of fine flour mixed with oil, made without yeast.

**6** Break it into pieces and pour oil on it. It is a grain offering.

**7** "If your offering is a grain offering made in a pan,[^9] it is to be made of fine flour with oil.

**8** You are to bring the grain offering made from these things to the LORD. It is to be presented to the priest, and he is to bring it to the altar.[^10]

**9** The priest is to take the memorial portion from the grain offering and burn it on the altar, an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**10** The rest of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is most holy, a portion of the offerings by fire to the LORD.

**11** No grain offering that you bring to the LORD is to be made with yeast, for you must not burn any yeast[^11] or any honey[^12] as an offering by fire to the LORD.

**12** You may bring them to the LORD as an offering of the first produce,[^13] but they must not be offered up on the altar as a pleasing aroma.[^14]

**13** You must season every grain offering of yours with salt. Do not leave the salt of the covenant of your God out of your grain offering. You are to offer salt with all your offerings.

**14** "If you bring a grain offering of firstfruits to the LORD, you are to bring fresh ears of grain roasted in the fire, crushed kernels of new grain,[^15] as the grain offering of your firstfruits.

**15** Put oil on it and lay frankincense on it. It is a grain offering.

**16** The priest is to burn its memorial portion, some of its crushed grain and some of its oil, together with all its frankincense, as an offering by fire to the LORD.

[^1]: Hebrew *nephesh*, "a person, a living being".
[^2]: Hebrew *minchah*, "gift, tribute". In Leviticus it is an offering of flour or grain. Traditionally "meat offering", in the old sense of "food".
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Hebrew *solet*, finely ground flour, perhaps semolina.
[^5]: An aromatic resin from trees of southern Arabia and East Africa, burned as incense.
[^6]: Hebrew *azkarah*, "reminder, memorial": the part burned on the altar, standing for the whole offering. Or "token portion". Also in verses 9 and 16.
[^7]: Literally "a holy of holies": the highest degree of holiness. Only the priests could eat it, and only in a holy place (6:9–11 (6:16–18 in most English Bibles)).
[^8]: Hebrew *challot*, probably round cakes or loaves, perhaps pierced.
[^9]: Hebrew *marcheshet*, probably a deep pan for frying or boiling (compare 7:9).
[^10]: Literally "You shall bring the grain offering that is made from these to the LORD, and he shall present it to the priest, and he shall bring it near to the altar."
[^11]: Hebrew *se'or*, sourdough: a piece of fermented dough used to make bread rise.
[^12]: Hebrew *devash*: bees' honey, or a sweet syrup made from fruit such as dates or grapes.
[^13]: Hebrew *reshit*, "the first, the best": the first of the harvest given to the LORD (23:17).
[^14]: Literally "but they must not go up to the altar as a pleasing aroma".
[^15]: Hebrew *geres karmel*: freshly harvested grain, crushed or coarsely ground. The exact meaning of *karmel* is uncertain.
