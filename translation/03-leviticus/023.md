**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: These are the LORD's appointed festivals,[^2] which you are to proclaim as holy assemblies.[^3] These are my appointed festivals.

**3** For six days work may be done, but the seventh day is a Sabbath of complete rest,[^4] a holy assembly. You must not do any work. It is a Sabbath to the LORD wherever you live.

**4** "These are the LORD's appointed festivals, the holy assemblies that you are to proclaim at their appointed times.

**5** In the first month,[^5] on the fourteenth day of the month, at twilight,[^6] is the LORD's Passover.

**6** On the fifteenth day of the same month is the LORD's Festival of Unleavened Bread. For seven days you are to eat bread made without yeast.

**7** On the first day you are to hold a holy assembly. You must not do any regular work.[^7]

**8** For seven days you are to present an offering by fire to the LORD. On the seventh day there is to be a holy assembly. You must not do any regular work."

**9** The LORD said to Moses,

**10** "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: When you come into the land that I am giving you and reap its harvest, you are to bring to the priest a sheaf[^8] of the first of your harvest.

**11** He is to wave the sheaf before the LORD so that you may be accepted. The priest is to wave it on the day after the Sabbath.[^9]

**12** On the day you wave the sheaf, you are to offer a male lamb a year old without defect as a burnt offering to the LORD,

**13** with its grain offering of two-tenths of an ephah[^10] of fine flour mixed with oil, an offering by fire to the LORD, a pleasing aroma, and its drink offering of a quarter of a hin[^11] of wine.

**14** You must not eat any bread or roasted grain or fresh grain until this very day, until you have brought the offering of your God. This is a permanent decree for all your generations, wherever you live.

**15** "From the day after the Sabbath, the day you bring the sheaf of the wave offering, you are to count off seven full weeks.[^12]

**16** Count fifty days, to the day after the seventh Sabbath. Then present a new grain offering to the LORD.[^13]

**17** From wherever you live, bring two loaves of bread as a wave offering, made of two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour and baked with yeast, as firstfruits to the LORD.

**18** Along with the bread, present seven male lambs a year old without defect, one young bull and two rams. They are to be a burnt offering to the LORD, with their grain offering and their drink offerings, an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**19** You are also to offer one male goat as a sin offering and two male lambs a year old as a fellowship offering.

**20** The priest is to wave them, together with the bread of the firstfruits, as a wave offering before the LORD, along with the two lambs. They are to be holy to the LORD, for the priest.

**21** On that same day you are to make a proclamation: there is to be a holy assembly for you. You must not do any regular work. This is a permanent decree for all your generations, wherever you live.

**22** "When you reap the harvest of your land, do not reap all the way to the edges of your field as you reap, and do not gather the gleanings of your harvest. Leave them for the poor and for the foreigner.[^14] I am the LORD your God."

**23** The LORD said to Moses,

**24** "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you are to have a day of complete rest, a day of remembrance announced with blasts of the horn,[^15] a holy assembly.

**25** You must not do any regular work, and you are to present an offering by fire to the LORD."

**26** The LORD said to Moses,

**27** "But the tenth day of this seventh month is the Day of Atonement.[^16] It is to be a holy assembly for you. You must deny yourselves,[^17] and you are to present an offering by fire to the LORD.

**28** Do not do any work on that same day, because it is a Day of Atonement, to make atonement for you before the LORD your God.

**29** Anyone who does not deny himself on that same day must be cut off from his people.

**30** Anyone who does any work on that same day, I will destroy that person from among his people.

**31** You must not do any work. This is a permanent decree for all your generations, wherever you live.

**32** It is a Sabbath of complete rest for you, and you must deny yourselves. From the evening of the ninth day of the month until the following evening you are to keep your Sabbath."

**33** The LORD said to Moses,

**34** "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: On the fifteenth day of this seventh month is the LORD's Festival of Shelters,[^18] for seven days.

**35** On the first day there is to be a holy assembly. You must not do any regular work.

**36** For seven days you are to present an offering by fire to the LORD. On the eighth day you are to hold a holy assembly and present an offering by fire to the LORD. It is a closing assembly.[^19] You must not do any regular work.

**37** "These are the LORD's appointed festivals, which you are to proclaim as holy assemblies for presenting offerings by fire to the LORD: burnt offerings and grain offerings, sacrifices and drink offerings, each on its own day.

**38** They are in addition to the LORD's Sabbaths,[^20] and in addition to your gifts, all your vow offerings and all your freewill offerings that you give to the LORD.

**39** "So on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you have gathered in the produce of the land, you are to celebrate the LORD's festival for seven days. The first day is to be a day of complete rest, and the eighth day is to be a day of complete rest.

**40** On the first day you are to take for yourselves the fruit of majestic trees,[^21] palm fronds, branches of leafy trees and willows from the stream,[^22] and you are to rejoice before the LORD your God for seven days.

**41** You are to celebrate it as a festival to the LORD for seven days each year. This is a permanent decree for all your generations. You are to celebrate it in the seventh month.

**42** You are to live in shelters for seven days. All the native-born in Israel are to live in shelters,

**43** so that your descendants may know that I made the Israelites live in shelters when I brought them out of the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God."

**44** So Moses announced the LORD's appointed festivals to the Israelites.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *mo'adim*, "appointed times": the fixed times for meeting with the LORD. Also in verses 4, 37 and 44.
[^3]: Hebrew *miqra'ei qodesh*, "holy callings-together": days when the people gathered for worship. Traditionally "holy convocations".
[^4]: Hebrew *shabbat shabbaton*: a solemn day of complete rest (16:31). Also in verse 32; "a day of complete rest" in verses 24 and 39 is *shabbaton*.
[^5]: The month of Abib (Exodus 13:4), later called Nisan, around March–April.
[^6]: Literally "between the two evenings".
[^7]: Literally "work of labor": ordinary daily work. Also in verses 8, 21, 25, 35 and 36.
[^8]: Hebrew *omer*, here a sheaf of grain. Elsewhere the word is a measure (Exodus 16:36).
[^9]: Later Jewish tradition debated whether this means the weekly Sabbath or the first day of the festival (verse 7). Also in verses 15 and 16.
[^10]: Literally "two tenths". An ephah was probably about 22 liters (two-thirds of a bushel), so two-tenths of an ephah was about 4 liters (4 quarts). Also in verse 17.
[^11]: A hin was about a gallon (4 liters), so a quarter of a hin was about a quart (1 liter).
[^12]: Literally "seven complete Sabbaths".
[^13]: This festival is called the Festival of Weeks in Exodus 34:22 and Deuteronomy 16:10. It was later called Pentecost, from the Greek word for "fiftieth".
[^14]: Hebrew *ger*, a resident foreigner (see 16:29).
[^15]: Literally "a remembrance of a blast", probably on the ram's horn (25:9).
[^16]: Hebrew *yom ha-kippurim*, "the day of atonements". See chapter 16.
[^17]: Literally "afflict your souls (*nephesh*)", traditionally understood as fasting (16:29). Also in verses 29 and 32.
[^18]: Hebrew *sukkot*, "booths, shelters": huts made of branches (verses 42–43). Traditionally "Feast of Tabernacles".
[^19]: Hebrew *atseret*, a solemn gathering, perhaps the one that closes the festival.
[^20]: Or "besides the offerings of the LORD's Sabbaths".
[^21]: Literally "the fruit of a majestic tree". Jewish tradition understands it as the citron.
[^22]: Or "poplars from the stream".
