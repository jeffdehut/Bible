**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the Israelites and tell them: This is what Yahweh has commanded.

**3** Any Israelite[^2] who slaughters an ox, a lamb or a goat in the camp, or who slaughters it outside the camp,

**4** and does not bring it to the entrance of the tent of meeting to present it as an offering to Yahweh in front of Yahweh's tabernacle, that man will be held guilty of bloodshed.[^3] He has shed blood, and that man must be cut off from among his people.

**5** This is so that the Israelites will bring to Yahweh the sacrifices that they have been sacrificing in the open fields. They are to bring them to the priest at the entrance of the tent of meeting and sacrifice them as fellowship offerings to Yahweh.

**6** The priest is to splash the blood against Yahweh's altar at the entrance of the tent of meeting and burn the fat as a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

**7** They must no longer offer their sacrifices to the goat-demons,[^4] after whom they have prostituted themselves.[^5] This is to be a permanent decree for them, for all their generations.

**8** "Tell them also: Any Israelite, or any foreigner[^6] who lives among them, who offers a burnt offering or a sacrifice

**9** and does not bring it to the entrance of the tent of meeting to offer it to Yahweh, that man must be cut off from his people.

**10** "Any Israelite, or any foreigner who lives among them, who eats any blood, I will set my face against that person who eats the blood, and I will cut him off from among his people.

**11** For the life[^7] of a creature is in the blood, and I have given it to you on the altar to make atonement for your lives, because it is the blood that makes atonement by means of the life.[^8]

**12** That is why I have said to the Israelites, 'None of you may eat blood, and no foreigner who lives among you may eat blood.'

**13** "Any Israelite, or any foreigner who lives among them, who hunts and catches a wild animal or a bird that may be eaten must pour out its blood and cover it with earth.

**14** For the life of every creature is its blood; its blood is its life.[^9] That is why I have said to the Israelites, 'You must not eat the blood of any creature, because the life of every creature is its blood. Anyone who eats it must be cut off.'

**15** "Anyone, whether native-born or foreigner, who eats an animal that has died or that was torn by wild beasts must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he will be unclean until evening. Then he will be clean.

**16** But if he does not wash his clothes or bathe his body, he will bear the consequences of his wrongdoing."[^10]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "Any man, any man of the house of Israel". So also in verses 8, 10 and 13.
[^3]: Literally "blood will be counted to that man".
[^4]: Hebrew *se'irim*, "hairy ones, goats": demons or idols thought to live in the wilderness (compare Isaiah 13:21; 34:14).
[^5]: Literally "after whom they go whoring", a common image for unfaithfulness to Yahweh.
[^6]: Hebrew *ger*, a resident foreigner (see 16:29). Also in verses 10, 12, 13 and 15.
[^7]: Hebrew *nephesh*, "life, soul, living being". Also in verse 14.
[^8]: Or "makes atonement for the life".
[^9]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "For the life of all flesh, its blood is in its life."
[^10]: Literally "he will bear his iniquity".
