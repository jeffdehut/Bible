**1** "Do not make idols[^1] for yourselves, and do not set up a carved image or a standing stone[^2] for yourselves. Do not place a carved stone[^3] in your land to bow down to it, for I am Yahweh[^4] your God.

**2** Keep my Sabbaths, and revere my sanctuary. I am Yahweh.

**3** "If you follow my decrees and keep my commandments and do them,

**4** I will give you your rains at the right time, the land will yield its crops, and the trees of the field will yield their fruit.

**5** Your threshing will last until the grape harvest, and the grape harvest will last until sowing time. You will eat your fill of food and live securely in your land.

**6** I will give peace in the land, and you will lie down with no one to make you afraid. I will rid the land of dangerous animals,[^5] and the sword will not pass through your land.

**7** You will chase your enemies, and they will fall before you by the sword.

**8** Five of you will chase a hundred, and a hundred of you will chase ten thousand, and your enemies will fall before you by the sword.

**9** I will turn toward you and make you fruitful and multiply you, and I will uphold my covenant with you.

**10** You will still be eating last year's harvest[^6] when you have to clear out the old to make room for the new.

**11** I will put my dwelling place[^7] among you, and I will not reject you.[^8]

**12** I will walk among you and be your God, and you will be my people.

**13** I am Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt so that you would no longer be their slaves. I broke the bars of your yoke and made you walk upright.

**14** "But if you do not listen to me and do not carry out all these commandments,

**15** if you reject my decrees and loathe my rulings, so that you do not carry out all my commandments and you break my covenant,

**16** then I in turn will do this to you: I will bring sudden terror on you, wasting disease and fever that make the eyes fail and the life ebb away.[^9] You will sow your seed in vain, because your enemies will eat it.

**17** I will set my face against you, and you will be defeated by your enemies. Those who hate you will rule over you, and you will flee when no one is pursuing you.

**18** "And if after all this you still do not listen to me, I will go on to discipline you seven times over for your sins.

**19** I will break your proud strength. I will make your sky like iron and your land like bronze.

**20** Your strength will be spent in vain, because your land will not yield its crops, and the trees of the land will not yield their fruit.

**21** "If you continue to walk in opposition to me[^10] and refuse to listen to me, I will go on to strike you seven times over, as your sins deserve.

**22** I will send wild animals against you, and they will rob you of your children, destroy your livestock and make you few in number, so that your roads will be deserted.

**23** "If in spite of these things you do not accept my discipline, but continue to walk in opposition to me,

**24** then I too will walk in opposition to you, and I myself will strike you seven times over for your sins.

**25** I will bring a sword on you to take vengeance for the covenant.[^11] When you gather in your cities, I will send a plague among you, and you will be handed over to the enemy.

**26** When I cut off your supply of bread,[^12] ten women will bake your bread in a single oven, and they will dole out your bread by weight. You will eat, but you will not be satisfied.

**27** "If in spite of this you still do not listen to me, but continue to walk in opposition to me,

**28** then I will walk in opposition to you in fury, and I myself will discipline you seven times over for your sins.

**29** You will eat the flesh of your sons, and you will eat the flesh of your daughters.

**30** I will destroy your high places,[^13] cut down your incense altars[^14] and pile your corpses on the corpses of your idols,[^15] and I will reject you.

**31** I will turn your cities into ruins and make your sanctuaries desolate, and I will not take pleasure in your pleasing aromas.[^16]

**32** I myself will make the land desolate, so that your enemies who come to live in it will be appalled at it.

**33** I will scatter you among the nations, and I will draw the sword and pursue you. Your land will become desolate, and your cities will become ruins.

**34** Then the land will enjoy its Sabbaths[^17] all the time that it lies desolate and you are in the land of your enemies. Then the land will rest and enjoy its Sabbaths.

**35** All the time that it lies desolate it will have rest, the rest it did not have during your Sabbaths when you lived in it.

**36** "As for those of you who are left, I will bring fear into their hearts in the lands of their enemies. The sound of a windblown leaf will put them to flight. They will flee as one flees from the sword, and they will fall when no one is pursuing them.

**37** They will stumble over one another as if fleeing from the sword, though no one is pursuing. You will not be able to stand before your enemies.

**38** You will perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies will devour you.

**39** Those of you who are left will waste away in the lands of your enemies because of their wrongdoing. And because of the wrongdoing of their ancestors also, they will waste away like them.[^18]

**40** "But if they confess[^19] their wrongdoing and the wrongdoing of their ancestors, the unfaithfulness they committed against me, and also how they walked in opposition to me,

**41** so that I in turn walked in opposition to them and brought them into the land of their enemies, if then their uncircumcised hearts[^20] are humbled and they pay for their wrongdoing,

**42** then I will remember my covenant with Jacob. I will remember also my covenant with Isaac and also my covenant with Abraham, and I will remember the land.

**43** For the land will be left behind by them, and it will enjoy its Sabbaths while it lies desolate without them, and they will pay for their wrongdoing, because they rejected my rulings and loathed my decrees.

**44** Yet in spite of this, when they are in the land of their enemies, I will not reject them or loathe them so as to destroy them completely and break my covenant with them, for I am Yahweh their God.

**45** For their sake I will remember the covenant with their ancestors,[^21] whom I brought out of the land of Egypt in the sight of the nations, to be their God. I am Yahweh."

**46** These are the decrees, the rulings and the instructions that Yahweh established between himself and the Israelites on Mount Sinai through Moses.[^22]

[^1]: Hebrew *elilim*, "worthless things". See 19:4.
[^2]: Hebrew *matsevah*: a stone pillar set up as an object of worship (Exodus 23:24).
[^3]: Hebrew *even maskit*, a stone with carved images.
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^5]: Literally "evil beasts".
[^6]: Literally "You will eat old grain, long stored".
[^7]: Hebrew *mishkan*, "dwelling place", the word for the tabernacle (8:10).
[^8]: Literally "My soul will not loathe you." So also in verse 30, "My soul will loathe you."
[^9]: Literally "consuming the eyes and making the soul (*nephesh*) pine away".
[^10]: Hebrew *qeri*, a word found only in this chapter: "hostility, opposition", or perhaps "indifference". Also in verses 23–24, 27–28, 40–41.
[^11]: Literally "a sword avenging the vengeance of the covenant".
[^12]: Literally "When I break for you the staff of bread".
[^13]: Hebrew *bamot*: hilltop shrines and places of worship.
[^14]: Hebrew *chammanim*, a word of uncertain meaning: perhaps incense altars, or sun pillars.
[^15]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a contemptuous word for idols, perhaps related to the word for dung.
[^16]: Literally "I will not smell your pleasing aroma." See 1:9.
[^17]: Or "make up for its Sabbaths", "pay off its Sabbaths". The same verb (*ratsah*) is translated "pay for" in verses 41 and 43.
[^18]: Or "together with them".
[^19]: Or "Then they will confess". The Hebrew can be read as a condition or as a prediction.
[^20]: That is, hearts closed to God and unwilling to obey.
[^21]: Literally "the covenant of the first ones".
[^22]: Literally "by the hand of Moses".
