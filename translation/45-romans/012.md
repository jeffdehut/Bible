**1** So I urge you, brothers and sisters,[^1] by the mercies of God, to offer your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing to God, which is your reasonable worship.[^2]

**2** Do not be conformed to this age,[^3] but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you may discern what the will of God is, what is good and pleasing and perfect.[^4]

**3** For by the grace given to me I tell everyone among you not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think, but to think with sober judgment,[^5] each according to the measure of faith that God has assigned to him.

**4** For just as in one body we have many parts, and the parts do not all have the same function,

**5** so we, who are many, are one body in Christ, and individually we are parts of one another.

**6** We have gifts that differ according to the grace given to us; so let us use them:[^6] if prophecy, in proportion to our faith;[^7]

**7** if service, in serving; the one who teaches, in teaching;

**8** the one who encourages, in encouragement; the one who gives, with generosity;[^8] the one who leads,[^9] with diligence; the one who shows mercy, with cheerfulness.

**9** Let love be genuine. Hate what is evil; hold fast to what is good.

**10** Love one another warmly as brothers and sisters; outdo one another in showing honor.

**11** Do not be lacking in zeal; be fervent in spirit;[^10] serve the Lord.

**12** Rejoice in hope; endure in trouble; persist in prayer.

**13** Share in the needs of God's holy people;[^11] pursue hospitality.

**14** Bless those who persecute;[^12] bless, and do not curse.

**15** Rejoice with those who rejoice; weep with those who weep.

**16** Live in harmony with one another. Do not set your mind on high things, but associate with the lowly.[^13] Do not be wise in your own sight.[^14]

**17** Repay no one evil for evil. Take thought for what is honorable in the sight of all people.[^15]

**18** If possible, so far as it depends on you, live at peace with all people.

**19** Do not take revenge yourselves, dear friends, but leave room for the wrath,[^16] for it is written, "Vengeance is mine; I will repay, says the Lord."[^17]

**20** But[^18] if your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him something to drink; for by doing this you will heap burning coals on his head.[^19]

**21** Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.

[^1]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike.
[^2]: Or "your spiritual worship". Greek *logikos*, "rational, reasonable", and *latreia*, "worship, service", used of the worship of the temple (9:4).
[^3]: Literally "this age".
[^4]: Or "the good and pleasing and perfect will of God".
[^5]: The Greek plays on words built on *phroneo*, "think".
[^6]: The Greek has no verb in verses 6–8; "let us use them" is supplied.
[^7]: Or "in agreement with the faith". Literally "according to the proportion of faith".
[^8]: Or "sincerity".
[^9]: Or "the one who cares for others".
[^10]: Or "aglow with the Spirit".
[^11]: Literally "the holy ones"; traditionally "saints".
[^12]: The SBLGNT here follows Westcott and Hort. Other editions read "those who persecute you". Compare Matthew 5:44; Luke 6:28.
[^13]: Or "but give yourselves to humble tasks".
[^14]: Compare Proverbs 3:7; Isaiah 5:21.
[^15]: Compare Proverbs 3:4 in the Septuagint.
[^16]: That is, God's wrath. Literally "give place to the wrath".
[^17]: Deuteronomy 32:35, in a form close to the Septuagint ("On the day of vengeance I will repay"). The Hebrew has "Vengeance is mine, and repayment". *Says the Lord* is Paul's addition; in Deuteronomy the speaker is the LORD. Also quoted in Hebrews 10:30.
[^18]: Many manuscripts read "So".
[^19]: Proverbs 25:21–22, as in the Septuagint. The Hebrew has "give him bread to eat … give him water to drink".
