**1** We who are strong ought to bear the weaknesses of those who are not strong, and not to please ourselves.

**2** Let each of us please his neighbor for his good, to build him up.

**3** For even Christ did not please himself, but, as it is written, "The insults of those who insult you have fallen on me."[^1]

**4** For whatever was written in former days was written for our instruction, so that through endurance and through the encouragement of the scriptures we might have hope.

**5** May the God of endurance and encouragement grant you to live in harmony with one another, in keeping with Christ Jesus,

**6** so that together, with one voice, you may glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**7** Therefore welcome one another, just as Christ also welcomed you,[^2] for the glory of God.

**8** For I tell you[^3] that Christ became a servant of the circumcised on behalf of the truthfulness of God, to confirm the promises made to the patriarchs,

**9** and so that the Gentiles might glorify God for his mercy. As it is written: "So I will praise you among the Gentiles,[^4] and sing praises to your name."[^5]

**10** And again it says, "Rejoice, you Gentiles, with his people."[^6]

**11** And again, "Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles, and let all the peoples extol him."[^7]

**12** And again Isaiah says, "The root of Jesse will come, the one who rises to rule the Gentiles; in him the Gentiles will hope."[^8]

**13** May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that you may overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.

**14** I myself am convinced about you, my brothers and sisters,[^9] that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, and able to instruct one another.

**15** But on some points I have written to you rather boldly, by way of reminding you, because of the grace given to me by God

**16** to be a minister[^10] of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles, serving the good news of God as a priest, so that the offering of the Gentiles[^11] may be acceptable, made holy by the Holy Spirit.

**17** So in Christ Jesus I have reason to boast in what I do for God.

**18** For I will not dare to speak of anything except what Christ has accomplished through me to bring the Gentiles to obedience, by word and deed,

**19** by the power of signs and wonders, by the power of the Spirit,[^12] so that from Jerusalem and all around as far as Illyricum[^13] I have fully proclaimed[^14] the good news of Christ.

**20** And so I have made it my ambition to proclaim the good news, not where Christ has already been named, so that I would not build on someone else's foundation,

**21** but as it is written: "Those who were not told about him will see, and those who have not heard will understand."[^15]

**22** This is why I have so often been prevented from coming to you.

**23** But now, since I no longer have any room for work in these regions, and since I have longed for several years[^16] to come to you,

**24** whenever I go to Spain[^17] — for I hope to see you as I pass through and to be sent on my way there by you, once I have first enjoyed your company for a while —

**25** but now I am going to Jerusalem to serve God's holy people.[^18]

**26** For Macedonia and Achaia have been pleased to make a contribution for the poor among God's holy people in Jerusalem.

**27** They were pleased to do it, and indeed they are in debt to them. For if the Gentiles have shared in their spiritual blessings, they also ought to serve them with material blessings.[^19]

**28** So when I have completed this, and have delivered to them what has been collected,[^20] I will set out by way of you to Spain.

**29** And I know that when I come to you, I will come in the fullness of the blessing of Christ.[^21]

**30** I urge you, brothers and sisters, by our Lord Jesus Christ and by the love of the Spirit, to join me in my struggle by praying to God for me,

**31** that I may be rescued from the unbelievers in Judea, and that my service for Jerusalem may be acceptable to God's holy people,

**32** so that by the will of God I may come to you with joy and be refreshed in your company.

**33** May the God of peace be with you all. Amen.

[^1]: Psalm 69:10 (69:9 in most English Bibles).
[^2]: Some manuscripts read "us".
[^3]: Many manuscripts read "Now I tell you that Jesus Christ".
[^4]: Greek *ethne*, "nations" or "Gentiles", the word translated "nations" in the Old Testament passages quoted in verses 9–12; also in verses 10–12.
[^5]: Psalm 18:50 (18:49 in most English Bibles); 2 Samuel 22:50. Both have "Yahweh" after "praise you".
[^6]: Deuteronomy 32:43, as in the Septuagint. The Hebrew has "Praise his people, you nations".
[^7]: Psalm 117:1. In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, Yahweh.
[^8]: Isaiah 11:10, as in the Septuagint. The Hebrew reads "On that day the root of Jesse will stand as a signal for the peoples. The nations will seek him".
[^9]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike; also in verse 30.
[^10]: Greek *leitourgos*, as in 13:6, a word used of those who serve in worship. *Serving … as a priest* translates *hierourgeo*, "perform holy service".
[^11]: That is, the Gentiles themselves offered to God. Or "the offering made by the Gentiles".
[^12]: Many manuscripts read "the Spirit of God", and some "the Holy Spirit". The SBLGNT reads "the Spirit", following the Greek text behind the New International Version.
[^13]: A Roman province on the eastern coast of the Adriatic Sea.
[^14]: Literally "fulfilled".
[^15]: Isaiah 52:15, as in the Septuagint, which has "those who were not told about him" where the Hebrew has "what they were not told".
[^16]: Many manuscripts read "many years".
[^17]: Many manuscripts add "I will come to you".
[^18]: Literally "the holy ones"; traditionally "saints"; also in verses 26 and 31.
[^19]: Literally "fleshly things". *Serve* is Greek *leitourgeo*, related to "minister" in verse 16.
[^20]: Literally "and have sealed to them this fruit".
[^21]: Many manuscripts read "the blessing of the good news of Christ".
