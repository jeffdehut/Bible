**1** So I ask, has God rejected his people?[^1] By no means! For I myself am an Israelite, a descendant of Abraham,[^2] of the tribe of Benjamin.

**2** God has not rejected his people, whom he foreknew. Or do you not know what the scripture says in the passage about Elijah,[^3] how he pleads with God against Israel?

**3** "Lord, they have killed your prophets, they have torn down your altars; I alone am left, and they are seeking my life."[^4]

**4** But what was God's answer to him? "I have kept for myself seven thousand men who have not bowed the knee to Baal."[^5]

**5** So too at the present time there is a remnant, chosen by grace.

**6** And if it is by grace, it is no longer by works; otherwise grace would no longer be grace.[^6]

**7** What then? What Israel is seeking, it has not obtained. The chosen have obtained it, but the rest were hardened,

**8** as it is written: "God gave them a spirit of stupor, eyes that would not see and ears that would not hear, to this very day."[^7]

**9** And David says: "Let their table become a trap and a net, a snare and a retribution for them.

**10** Let their eyes be darkened so that they cannot see, and bend their backs continually."[^8]

**11** So I ask, did they stumble so as to fall? By no means! Rather, through their trespass salvation has come to the Gentiles, to make them jealous.

**12** Now if their trespass means riches for the world, and their failure means riches for the Gentiles, how much more will their full inclusion[^9] mean!

**13** Now I am speaking to you Gentiles.[^10] Inasmuch then as I am an apostle to the Gentiles, I make much of my ministry,

**14** in the hope that somehow I may make my own people[^11] jealous and save some of them.

**15** For if their rejection means the reconciliation of the world, what will their acceptance mean but life from the dead?

**16** If the firstfruits are holy, so is the whole batch of dough;[^12] and if the root is holy, so are the branches.

**17** But if some of the branches were broken off, and you, though a wild olive shoot, were grafted in among them and came to share in the rich root[^13] of the olive tree,

**18** do not boast over the branches. If you do boast, remember that you do not support the root, but the root supports you.

**19** You will say then, "Branches were broken off so that I could be grafted in."

**20** That is true. They were broken off because of unbelief, but you stand by faith. Do not be arrogant, but be afraid.

**21** For if God did not spare the natural branches, neither will he spare you.[^14]

**22** Note then the kindness and the severity of God: severity toward those who fell, but God's kindness toward you, if you continue in his kindness. Otherwise you too will be cut off.

**23** And they too, if they do not continue in unbelief, will be grafted in, for God is able to graft them in again.

**24** For if you were cut from what is by nature a wild olive tree and grafted, contrary to nature, into a cultivated olive tree, how much more will these natural branches be grafted into their own olive tree!

**25** For I do not want you to be unaware of this mystery, brothers and sisters,[^15] so that you may not be wise in your own sight: a hardening has come on part of Israel[^16] until the full number[^17] of the Gentiles has come in,

**26** and in this way[^18] all Israel will be saved. As it is written: "The Deliverer will come from Zion; he will turn away ungodliness from Jacob.

**27** And this will be my covenant with them, when I take away their sins."[^19]

**28** As regards the good news, they are enemies for your sake; but as regards election, they are loved for the sake of the patriarchs.

**29** For the gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable.

**30** For just as you were once disobedient to God but have now received mercy because of their disobedience,

**31** so they too have now been disobedient, so that by the mercy shown to you they too may now receive mercy.[^20]

**32** For God has shut up all in disobedience so that he may have mercy on all.

**33** Oh, the depth of the riches and wisdom and knowledge of God![^21] How unsearchable are his judgments, and how untraceable his ways!

**34** For who has known the mind of the Lord? Or who has been his counselor?[^22]

**35** Or who has given to him first, so that he should be repaid?[^23]

**36** For from him and through him and to him are all things. To him be the glory forever. Amen.

[^1]: Compare 1 Samuel 12:22; Psalm 94:14.
[^2]: Literally "of the seed of Abraham".
[^3]: Literally "in Elijah".
[^4]: 1 Kings 19:10, 14, shortened and with the first two lines in the reverse order.
[^5]: 1 Kings 19:18. The Hebrew has "I will leave seven thousand in Israel".
[^6]: Many manuscripts add "But if it is by works, it is no longer grace; otherwise work is no longer work."
[^7]: Deuteronomy 29:3 (29:4 in most English Bibles), combined with Isaiah 29:10, which has "a spirit of deep sleep". Both verses name the LORD where Paul has "God".
[^8]: Psalm 69:23–24 (69:22–23 in most English Bibles), as in the Septuagint, with "a net" added. The Hebrew has "and a snare for those at ease" and "make their loins tremble continually".
[^9]: Literally "their fullness".
[^10]: Many manuscripts read "For I am speaking to you Gentiles".
[^11]: Literally "my flesh".
[^12]: Literally "the lump". The first portion of the dough was offered to God (Numbers 15:17–21).
[^13]: Literally "the root of the richness". Many manuscripts read "the root and the richness".
[^14]: Many manuscripts read "perhaps he will not spare you either".
[^15]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike.
[^16]: Or "a partial hardening has come on Israel".
[^17]: Literally "the fullness".
[^18]: Or "and so".
[^19]: Isaiah 59:20–21, as in the Septuagint, with the last line from Isaiah 27:9 (compare Jeremiah 31:33–34). The Hebrew of 59:20 reads "A Redeemer will come to Zion, to those in Jacob who turn from rebellion".
[^20]: Or "so they too have now been disobedient because of the mercy shown to you, so that they too may receive mercy". Many manuscripts leave out the second "now".
[^21]: Or "Oh, the depth of the riches of the wisdom and knowledge of God!"
[^22]: Isaiah 40:13, as in the Septuagint. The Hebrew reads "Who has measured the Spirit of the LORD, or instructed him as his counselor?" In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, YHWH ("the LORD").
[^23]: Compare Job 41:3 (41:11 in most English Bibles).
