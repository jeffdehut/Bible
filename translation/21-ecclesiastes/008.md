**1** Who is like the wise? And who knows the interpretation of a thing? A person's wisdom makes his face shine, and the hardness of his face is changed.[^1]

**2** I say:[^2] Keep the king's command,[^3] because of the oath before God.[^4]

**3** Do not be in a hurry to leave his presence. Do not persist in an evil matter, for he does whatever he pleases.

**4** For the king's word is power, and who can say to him, "What are you doing?"

**5** Whoever keeps a command will know no harm,[^5] and a wise heart knows the time and the way.[^6]

**6** For there is a time and a way for every matter, though a person's trouble lies heavy on him.

**7** For he does not know what will happen, and who can tell him how it will happen?

**8** No one has power over the wind[^7] to hold back the wind, and no one has power over the day of death. There is no discharge in war, and wickedness will not save those who practice it.[^8]

**9** All this I have seen, as I set my heart on every deed that is done under the sun, at a time when one person has power over another to his harm.[^9]

**10** And then I saw the wicked buried. They used to come and go from the holy place, and they were forgotten in the city where they had done such things.[^10] This too is vapor.[^11]

**11** Because the sentence against an evil deed is not carried out quickly, the hearts of human beings are fully set on doing evil.

**12** Though a sinner does evil a hundred times and lives long,[^12] yet I also know that it will be well with those who fear God, who fear before him.

**13** But it will not be well with the wicked, and he will not lengthen his days like a shadow,[^13] because he does not fear before God.

**14** There is a vapor that occurs on earth:[^14] there are righteous people who are treated as the deeds of the wicked deserve, and there are wicked people who are treated as the deeds of the righteous deserve. I said that this too is vapor.

**15** So I commended joy, because there is nothing better for a person under the sun than to eat and drink and be glad. That will go with him in his toil through the days of his life that God has given him under the sun.

**16** When I set my heart to know wisdom and to see the task that is done on earth (for neither by day nor by night do one's eyes see sleep),

**17** then I saw all the work of God: that no one can find out the work that is done under the sun. However much a person toils to seek it, he will not find it; and even if a wise person claims to know it, he cannot find it out.

[^1]: That is, his stern look is softened.
[^2]: Literally "I". The word stands alone in the Hebrew, perhaps meaning "I say", or "I advise you".
[^3]: Literally "the king's mouth".
[^4]: Or "because of your oath to God", or "because of God's oath".
[^5]: Literally "will not know an evil thing".
[^6]: Literally "time and judgment" (Hebrew *mishpat*): the right time and the right way. Also in verse 6.
[^7]: Or "the spirit", the breath of life. Hebrew *ruach*. Also later in the verse.
[^8]: Literally "its owners".
[^9]: Or "to his own harm", or "to the other's harm".
[^10]: The Hebrew of this verse is difficult. Literally "And so I saw the wicked buried, and they came, and from the holy place they go, and they are forgotten in the city where they did so". Some take the second half to mean that those who had acted rightly ("so") and had come from the holy place were forgotten. Some Hebrew manuscripts and the Septuagint read "they were praised" instead of "they were forgotten".
[^11]: Hebrew *hevel*, "breath, vapor"; see 1:2.
[^12]: Literally "and prolongs for himself".
[^13]: Or "and his days will not be long; they are like a shadow".
[^14]: Literally "There is a vapor that is done on the earth".
