**1** Send your bread out on the surface of the waters, for after many days you will find it.[^1]

**2** Give a portion to seven, and even to eight, for you do not know what disaster may come upon the earth.

**3** If the clouds are full, they pour out rain on the earth; and whether a tree falls to the south or to the north, in the place where the tree falls, there it will lie.

**4** Whoever watches the wind will not sow, and whoever looks at the clouds will not reap.

**5** Just as you do not know the way of the wind,[^2] or how the bones are formed in the womb of a pregnant woman,[^3] so you do not know the work of God, who makes everything.

**6** In the morning sow your seed, and in the evening do not let your hand rest, for you do not know which will succeed, this or that, or whether both alike will be good.

**7** Light is sweet, and it is good for the eyes to see the sun.

**8** For if a person lives many years, let him rejoice in them all; but let him remember the days of darkness, for they will be many. All that comes is vapor.[^4]

**9** Rejoice, young man, in your youth, and let your heart cheer you in the days of your youth. Walk in the ways of your heart and in the sight of your eyes,[^5] but know that for all these things God will bring you into judgment.

**10** Remove grief from your heart, and put away trouble from your body,[^6] for youth and the prime of life[^7] are vapor.

[^1]: Perhaps a picture of trading by sea, or of generosity that is repaid.
[^2]: Or "the way of the breath (or spirit) into the bones in the womb". Hebrew *ruach*.
[^3]: Literally "like the bones in the womb of the full one".
[^4]: Hebrew *hevel*, "breath, vapor"; see 1:2.
[^5]: Compare Numbers 15:39.
[^6]: Literally "flesh".
[^7]: Hebrew *shacharut*, a word found only here: perhaps "the dawn of life" (from *shachar*, "dawn"), or "the time of black hair" (from *shachor*, "black").
