**1** The word of the LORD[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] set your face against Mount Seir[^3] and prophesy against it.

**3** Say to it: This is what the Lord GOD[^4] says: Look, I am against you, Mount Seir. I will stretch out my hand against you and make you a desolate waste.

**4** I will lay your cities in ruins, and you will become a desolation. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

**5** Because you have kept up an ancient hostility and handed the Israelites over to the power of the sword at the time of their disaster, at the time of their final punishment,[^5]

**6** therefore, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will hand you over to bloodshed,[^6] and bloodshed will pursue you. Since you did not hate bloodshed,[^7] bloodshed will pursue you.

**7** I will make Mount Seir a desolate waste, and I will cut off from it all who come and go.

**8** I will fill its mountains with its slain. On your hills and in your valleys and in all your ravines, those killed by the sword will fall.

**9** I will make you a desolation forever, and your cities will never be restored.[^8] Then you will know that I am the LORD.

**10** Because you said, "These two nations and these two lands will be mine, and we will take possession of them," though the LORD was there,

**11** therefore, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will deal with you according to the anger and the jealousy you showed in your hatred against them. I will make myself known among them when I judge you.

**12** Then you will know that I, the LORD, have heard all the contemptuous things you said against the mountains of Israel: "They are laid waste; they have been given to us to devour."

**13** You boasted against me with your mouth and heaped up your words against me. I heard it.

**14** This is what the Lord GOD says: While the whole earth rejoices,[^9] I will make you a desolation.

**15** As you rejoiced over the inheritance of the house of Israel because it was laid waste, so I will do to you. You will be a desolation, Mount Seir, and all Edom, all of it. Then they will know that I am the LORD.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: The mountain country of Edom, south and east of the Dead Sea (Genesis 36:8).
[^4]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^5]: Literally "at the time of the guilt of the end", the same phrase as in 21:30, 34 (21:25, 29 in most English Bibles).
[^6]: Literally "I will make you into blood". The Hebrew *dam*, "blood", sounds like *Edom*. Also later in the verse.
[^7]: Literally "if you did not hate blood". The meaning is uncertain.
[^8]: The Hebrew text is written "will not be inhabited" but read "will not return", that is, will not be restored.
[^9]: Or "To the joy of the whole earth".
