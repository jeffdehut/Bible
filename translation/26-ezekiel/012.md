**1** The word of Yahweh[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] you are living in the middle of a rebellious house. They have eyes to see but do not see, and ears to hear but do not hear, for they are a rebellious house.

**3** As for you, son of man, pack your belongings for exile,[^3] and go into exile by day in their sight. Go into exile from your place to another place in their sight. Perhaps they will see,[^4] though they are a rebellious house.

**4** Bring out your belongings by day in their sight, like belongings for exile, and in the evening go out in their sight like people going out into exile.

**5** In their sight dig through the wall, and carry your belongings out through it.

**6** In their sight carry them on your shoulder, and take them out in the dark. Cover your face, so that you cannot see the land, for I have made you a sign[^5] for the house of Israel.

**7** So I did as I was commanded. I brought out my belongings by day, like belongings for exile, and in the evening I dug through the wall with my hand. I took them out in the dark, carrying them on my shoulder in their sight.

**8** In the morning the word of Yahweh came to me:

**9** Son of man, has not the house of Israel, that rebellious house, said to you, "What are you doing?"

**10** Say to them: This is what the Lord Yahweh[^6] says: This oracle concerns the prince in Jerusalem[^7] and the whole house of Israel who are there.[^8]

**11** Say: I am a sign for you. As I have done, so it will be done to them. They will go into exile, into captivity.

**12** The prince who is among them will carry his things on his shoulder[^9] in the dark and go out. They will dig through the wall to bring him out through it. He will cover his face, so that he will not see the land with his eyes.

**13** I will spread my net over him, and he will be caught in my snare. I will bring him to Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans, yet he will not see it, and there he will die.[^10]

**14** I will scatter to every wind all those around him, his helpers and all his troops,[^11] and I will draw a sword after them.

**15** Then they will know that I am Yahweh, when I disperse them among the nations and scatter them through the countries.

**16** But I will let a few of them[^12] escape the sword, famine and plague, so that they may tell about all their detestable practices[^13] among the nations where they go. Then they will know that I am Yahweh.

**17** The word of Yahweh came to me:

**18** Son of man, eat your bread with trembling, and drink your water with shuddering and anxiety.

**19** Say to the people of the land: This is what the Lord Yahweh says about those who live in Jerusalem, in the land of Israel: They will eat their bread with anxiety and drink their water in dismay, so that their land will be stripped of everything in it,[^14] because of the violence of all who live in it.

**20** The cities that are now inhabited will be laid waste, and the land will become desolate. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.

**21** The word of Yahweh came to me:

**22** Son of man, what is this proverb[^15] you have about the land of Israel: "The days drag on, and every vision comes to nothing"?

**23** Therefore say to them: This is what the Lord Yahweh says: I will put an end to this proverb, and they will no longer use it as a proverb in Israel. Instead say to them: "The days are near, and so is the fulfillment[^16] of every vision."

**24** For there will no longer be any false vision or flattering divination[^17] within the house of Israel.

**25** For I am Yahweh. I will speak, and the word that I speak will be done. It will no longer be delayed. In your own days, rebellious house, I will speak the word and do it, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**26** The word of Yahweh came to me:

**27** Son of man, look, the house of Israel is saying, "The vision that he sees is for many days from now, and he prophesies about times far off."

**28** Therefore say to them: This is what the Lord Yahweh says: None of my words will be delayed any longer. Whatever word I speak will be done, declares the Lord Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Literally "make for yourself things for exile", the few things a person could carry into exile. Also in verses 4 and 7.
[^4]: Or "understand".
[^5]: Hebrew *mofet*, "sign, portent". Also in verse 11.
[^6]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^7]: Literally "The prince is this oracle in Jerusalem". A play on words: *nasi*, "prince", and *massa*, "oracle", also "burden", both come from the verb *nasa*, "lift, carry", which is used for carrying the baggage in verses 6–7 and 12. For *nasi*, see 7:27.
[^8]: Literally "among them".
[^9]: Literally "on the shoulder he will lift".
[^10]: Zedekiah, the last king of Judah, tried to escape from Jerusalem by night when the city fell. He was captured, blinded and taken to Babylon (2 Kings 25:4–7; Jeremiah 52:7–11).
[^11]: Hebrew *agappim*, a word used only in Ezekiel, probably "troops"; literally "wings" of an army.
[^12]: Literally "men of number", men few enough to be counted.
[^13]: Hebrew *to'evot*, things that are abhorrent, especially in worship.
[^14]: Literally "will be desolate of its fullness".
[^15]: Hebrew *mashal*, "saying, proverb". Also in verse 23.
[^16]: Literally "the word". The saying answers the proverb of verse 22.
[^17]: Literally "smooth divination".
