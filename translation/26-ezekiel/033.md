**1** The word of Yahweh[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] speak to your people and say to them: When I bring a sword against a land, and the people of the land take one man from among them and make him their watchman,[^3]

**3** and he sees the sword coming against the land and blows the ram's horn[^4] and warns the people,

**4** then if anyone hears the sound of the horn but does not take the warning, and the sword comes and takes him away, his blood will be on his own head.

**5** He heard the sound of the horn but did not take the warning; his blood will be on himself. If he had taken the warning, he would have saved his life.

**6** But if the watchman sees the sword coming and does not blow the horn, so that the people are not warned, and the sword comes and takes the life of any one of them, that person is taken away for his guilt, but I will hold the watchman responsible for his blood.[^5]

**7** As for you, son of man, I have made you a watchman for the house of Israel.[^6] Whenever you hear a word from my mouth, you are to warn them from me.

**8** When I say to the wicked, "Wicked one, you will surely die," and you do not speak to warn the wicked from his way, that wicked person will die for his guilt, but I will hold you responsible for his blood.[^7]

**9** But if you warn the wicked to turn from his way, and he does not turn from his way, he will die for his guilt, but you will have saved your life.

**10** As for you, son of man, say to the house of Israel: This is what you have been saying: "Our transgressions and our sins weigh on us, and we are wasting away because of them. How then can we live?"

**11** Say to them: As I live, declares the Lord Yahweh,[^8] I take no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but rather in the wicked turning from his way and living. Turn back, turn back from your evil ways! Why should you die, house of Israel?

**12** As for you, son of man, say to your people: The righteousness of the righteous will not save him on the day he transgresses. As for the wickedness of the wicked, he will not fall because of it on the day he turns from his wickedness. And the righteous will not be able to live by his righteousness on the day he sins.

**13** When I tell the righteous that he will surely live, but he trusts in his righteousness and does wrong, none of his righteous deeds[^9] will be remembered; he will die for the wrong he has done.

**14** And when I say to the wicked, "You will surely die," but he turns from his sin and does what is just and right,

**15** if the wicked gives back what he took in pledge, repays what he has stolen, and walks in the decrees that give life, doing no wrong, he will surely live; he will not die.

**16** None of the sins[^10] he has committed will be remembered against him. He has done what is just and right; he will surely live.

**17** Yet your people say, "The way of the Lord is not fair."[^11] But it is their own way that is not fair.

**18** When the righteous turns from his righteousness and does wrong, he will die for it.

**19** And when the wicked turns from his wickedness and does what is just and right, he will live because of it.

**20** Yet you say, "The way of the Lord is not fair." I will judge each of you according to his ways, house of Israel.

**21** In the twelfth year of our exile, in the tenth month, on the fifth day of the month,[^12] a fugitive from Jerusalem came to me and said, "The city has fallen!"[^13]

**22** Now the hand of Yahweh had been on me in the evening, before the fugitive came, and he had opened my mouth by the time the man came to me in the morning. So my mouth was opened, and I was no longer silent.[^14]

**23** The word of Yahweh came to me:

**24** Son of man, the people living in these ruins on the soil of Israel are saying, "Abraham was only one man, yet he took possession of the land. But we are many; the land has surely been given to us as our possession."

**25** Therefore say to them: This is what the Lord Yahweh says: You eat meat with the blood,[^15] you lift up your eyes to your idols,[^16] and you shed blood. Should you then possess the land?

**26** You rely on your swords,[^17] you do detestable things,[^18] and each of you defiles his neighbor's wife. Should you then possess the land?

**27** Say this to them: This is what the Lord Yahweh says: As I live, those who are in the ruins will fall by the sword; those in the open field I will give to the wild animals to be devoured; and those in the strongholds and the caves will die of plague.

**28** I will make the land a desolate waste, and her proud strength will come to an end. The mountains of Israel will be desolate, with no one passing through.

**29** Then they will know that I am Yahweh, when I make the land a desolate waste because of all the detestable practices they have done.

**30** As for you, son of man, your people are talking about you beside the walls and in the doorways of the houses. They say to one another, each to his brother, "Come and hear what the word is that comes from Yahweh."

**31** They come to you as people do, and they sit before you as my people and hear your words, but they do not act on them. With their mouths they speak of love,[^19] but their hearts go after their own gain.

**32** Look, to them you are like a love song, sung with a beautiful voice and played well on the strings. They hear your words, but they do not act on them.

**33** But when it comes, and look, it is coming, they will know that a prophet has been among them.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Hebrew *tsofeh*, a lookout posted on a city wall to warn of danger (2 Samuel 18:24–27). Also in verses 6 and 7.
[^4]: Hebrew *shofar*, a horn blown as a signal or alarm. Also in verses 4–6.
[^5]: Literally "his blood I will require from the hand of the watchman".
[^6]: Verses 7–9 repeat 3:17–19, with small changes.
[^7]: Literally "his blood I will seek from your hand".
[^8]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^9]: The Hebrew text is written "all his righteousness" but read "all his righteous deeds".
[^10]: The Hebrew text is written "all his sin" but read "all his sins".
[^11]: Hebrew *lo yittakhen*, "is not measured right", "is not fair". Also in verse 20. The same words are used in 18:25, 29.
[^12]: In January 585 BC, counting from the exile of King Jehoiachin (1:2). Jerusalem had fallen to the Babylonians some months before (2 Kings 25:1–10).
[^13]: Literally "The city has been struck down!"
[^14]: See 3:26–27 and 24:25–27.
[^15]: Literally "on the blood". Eating meat with its blood in it was forbidden (Genesis 9:4; Leviticus 17:10–14; 19:26).
[^16]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a contemptuous word for idols, perhaps related to the word for dung.
[^17]: Literally "You stand on your swords".
[^18]: Hebrew *to'evah*, what is abhorrent, especially in worship. Also in verse 29.
[^19]: Hebrew *agavim*, "passion, love", the word used for "love song" in verse 32. The meaning of the clause is uncertain.
