**1** The word of Yahweh[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] make known to Jerusalem her detestable practices.[^3]

**3** Say: This is what the Lord Yahweh[^4] says to Jerusalem: Your origin and your birth were in the land of the Canaanites. Your father was an Amorite, and your mother a Hittite.[^5]

**4** As for your birth, on the day you were born your cord was not cut, you were not washed with water to clean you,[^6] you were not rubbed with salt, and you were not wrapped in cloths.

**5** No eye pitied you enough to do any one of these things for you out of compassion for you. Instead you were thrown out into the open field, because you were despised,[^7] on the day you were born.

**6** Then I passed by you and saw you kicking about in your blood, and I said to you in your blood, "Live!" I said to you in your blood, "Live!"[^8]

**7** I made you grow like a plant of the field.[^9] You grew up and became tall, and you came to the age of full beauty:[^10] your breasts were formed and your hair had grown, but you were naked and bare.

**8** Then I passed by you and saw you, and look, your time was the time for love. I spread the corner of my garment over you[^11] and covered your nakedness. I swore an oath to you and entered into a covenant with you, declares the Lord Yahweh, and you became mine.

**9** Then I bathed you with water, washed your blood off you, and anointed you with oil.

**10** I clothed you with embroidered cloth and put sandals of fine leather[^12] on your feet. I wrapped you in fine linen and covered you with silk.[^13]

**11** I adorned you with jewelry: I put bracelets on your arms and a necklace around your neck.

**12** I put a ring in your nose, earrings in your ears, and a beautiful crown on your head.

**13** So you were adorned with gold and silver, and your clothing was fine linen, silk and embroidered cloth. You ate fine flour, honey and oil.[^14] You became very, very beautiful, and you rose to be a queen.[^15]

**14** Your fame spread among the nations because of your beauty, for it was perfect through the splendor that I had put on you, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**15** But you trusted in your beauty and played the prostitute[^16] because of your fame. You poured out your acts of prostitution on everyone who passed by; it was his.[^17]

**16** You took some of your clothes and made colorful high places for yourself, and on them you played the prostitute. Such things should not come about, and they should never be.[^18]

**17** You also took your beautiful jewels, made of my gold and my silver that I had given you, and made for yourself male images,[^19] and you played the prostitute with them.

**18** You took your embroidered clothes to cover them, and you set my oil and my incense before them.

**19** Also my food that I gave you, the fine flour, the oil and the honey I fed you, you set before them as a pleasing aroma.[^20] That is what happened, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**20** You took your sons and your daughters, whom you had borne to me, and sacrificed them to the images[^21] as food. Was your prostitution not enough?[^22]

**21** You slaughtered my children and gave them up, offering them to the images.[^23]

**22** In all your detestable practices and your acts of prostitution you did not remember the days of your youth, when you were naked and bare, kicking about in your blood.

**23** After all your wickedness (woe, woe to you, declares the Lord Yahweh!)

**24** you built yourself a mound[^24] and made yourself a lofty place in every square.

**25** At the head of every street you built your lofty place and made your beauty detestable. You spread your legs for everyone who passed by and multiplied your acts of prostitution.

**26** You played the prostitute with the Egyptians, your neighbors with their large genitals,[^25] and multiplied your prostitution to provoke me to anger.

**27** So I stretched out my hand against you and cut down your allotted portion.[^26] I handed you over to the will[^27] of those who hate you, the daughters of the Philistines, who were ashamed of your lewd conduct.[^28]

**28** You played the prostitute with the Assyrians too, because you were never satisfied. You played the prostitute with them, and still you were not satisfied.

**29** You multiplied your prostitution with the land of traders, Chaldea,[^29] and even with this you were not satisfied.

**30** How sick your heart is,[^30] declares the Lord Yahweh, that you did all these things, the deeds of a brazen prostitute!

**31** When you built your mound at the head of every street and made your lofty place in every square, you were not like a prostitute, for you scorned payment.

**32** You adulterous wife, who takes strangers instead of her husband!

**33** All prostitutes are given gifts, but you gave your gifts to all your lovers, and bribed them to come to you from all around for your acts of prostitution.

**34** So in your prostitution you were the opposite of other women. No one chased after you for prostitution,[^31] and you gave payment, while no payment was given to you. So you were the opposite.

**35** Therefore, you prostitute, hear the word of Yahweh.

**36** This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Because your lust[^32] was poured out and your nakedness was uncovered in your prostitution with your lovers, and because of all your detestable idols,[^33] and because of the blood of your children whom you gave to them,

**37** therefore, look, I am going to gather all your lovers whom you pleased, all those you loved along with all those you hated. I will gather them against you from all around and uncover your nakedness to them, and they will see all your nakedness.

**38** I will judge you as women who commit adultery and shed blood are judged,[^34] and I will bring on you the bloodshed of fury and jealousy.

**39** I will hand you over to them, and they will tear down your mound and demolish your lofty places. They will strip you of your clothes, take your beautiful jewels, and leave you naked and bare.

**40** They will bring up a mob against you, and they will stone you with stones and hack you to pieces with their swords.

**41** They will burn your houses with fire and carry out judgments on you in the sight of many women. I will put an end to your prostitution, and you will no longer give payment.

**42** Then I will satisfy my fury on you,[^35] and my jealousy will turn away from you. I will be calm and no longer angry.

**43** Because you did not remember the days of your youth, but provoked me[^36] with all these things, I in turn will bring your ways down on your head, declares the Lord Yahweh. Did you not commit this lewdness on top of all your detestable practices?

**44** Look, everyone who uses proverbs will use this proverb about you: "Like mother, like daughter."

**45** You are your mother's daughter, who loathed her husband and her children, and you are the sister of your sisters, who loathed their husbands and their children. Your mother was a Hittite and your father an Amorite.

**46** Your older sister is Samaria, who lives with her daughters to your north,[^37] and your younger sister, who lives to your south, is Sodom with her daughters.

**47** You not only walked in their ways and acted according to their detestable practices; in a very short time[^38] you became more corrupt than they in all your ways.

**48** As I live, declares the Lord Yahweh, your sister Sodom and her daughters never did what you and your daughters have done.

**49** Look, this was the guilt of your sister Sodom: she and her daughters had pride, plenty of food[^39] and carefree ease, but she did not help[^40] the poor and needy.

**50** They were haughty and did detestable things before me, so I removed them when I saw it.[^41]

**51** Samaria did not commit half your sins. You have done more detestable things than they did, and you have made your sisters[^42] seem righteous by all the detestable things you have done.

**52** You too must bear your disgrace, for you have pleaded your sisters' case.[^43] Because of your sins, in which you acted more detestably than they, they are more in the right than you. So you too be ashamed and bear your disgrace, for you have made your sisters seem righteous.

**53** I will restore their fortunes,[^44] the fortunes of Sodom and her daughters and the fortunes of Samaria and her daughters, and I will restore your fortunes along with theirs,[^45]

**54** so that you may bear your disgrace and be ashamed of all that you have done, when you bring them comfort.

**55** Your sisters, Sodom and her daughters, will return to their former state; Samaria and her daughters will return to their former state; and you and your daughters will return to your former state.

**56** You would not even mention your sister Sodom[^46] in the day of your pride,

**57** before your wickedness was exposed, as it is now that you have become the scorn of the daughters of Aram[^47] and all her neighbors, and of the daughters of the Philistines, those all around who despise you.

**58** You must bear the penalty of your lewdness and your detestable practices, declares Yahweh.

**59** For this is what the Lord Yahweh says: I will deal with you[^48] as you have done, you who despised the oath by breaking the covenant.

**60** Yet I will remember my covenant with you in the days of your youth, and I will establish with you an everlasting covenant.

**61** Then you will remember your ways and be ashamed when you receive your sisters, those older than you and those younger, and I give them to you as daughters, but not on the basis of your covenant.[^49]

**62** I will establish my covenant with you, and you will know that I am Yahweh,

**63** so that you will remember and be ashamed, and never again open your mouth because of your disgrace, when I make atonement for you[^50] for all that you have done, declares the Lord Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Hebrew *to'evot*, things that are abhorrent, especially in worship. In this chapter Jerusalem is pictured as an abandoned baby girl whom Yahweh rescues and later marries, and who becomes an unfaithful wife (compare chapter 23).
[^4]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^5]: Amorites and Hittites were among the peoples who lived in Canaan before Israel (Genesis 15:19–21). Jerusalem was a Canaanite city until David captured it (2 Samuel 5:6–9). Also in verse 45.
[^6]: Hebrew *lemish'i*, a word found only here, probably "for cleansing".
[^7]: Literally "in the loathing of your *nephesh*".
[^8]: The Septuagint does not repeat this sentence.
[^9]: Literally "Ten thousand, like the plant of the field, I made you".
[^10]: Literally "you came into the ornament of ornaments". The meaning is uncertain.
[^11]: Literally "I spread my wing over you", a sign of taking a woman as wife (Ruth 3:9).
[^12]: Hebrew *tachash*, of uncertain meaning (Exodus 25:5).
[^13]: Hebrew *meshi*, found only here and in verse 13, perhaps silk or a fine fabric.
[^14]: In this chapter the Hebrew text several times writes "you" (feminine) in the verb with an old ending, *-ti*, which looks like "I", and reads it with the usual ending (here and in verses 18, 22, 31, 43, 47 and 51).
[^15]: Literally "you prospered to royalty".
[^16]: Hebrew *zanah*, "act as a prostitute", a common image for unfaithfulness to Yahweh (6:9). Its noun, *taznut*, "prostitution", recurs through the chapter.
[^17]: Literally "to him let it be". The meaning is uncertain.
[^18]: Literally "not coming, and it will not be". The meaning is uncertain.
[^19]: Literally "images of a male".
[^20]: Literally "a soothing smell", the phrase used for offerings to Yahweh (6:13).
[^21]: Literally "to them", the images of verse 17. Also in verse 21.
[^22]: Literally "Was it a small thing, from your prostitution?"
[^23]: Literally "making them pass over to them", the phrase used for child sacrifice (20:26, 31; 2 Kings 16:3; 21:6).
[^24]: Hebrew *gav*, "back, mound", and *ramah*, "high place", probably raised platforms for worship and prostitution. Also in verses 25, 31 and 39.
[^25]: Literally "great of flesh". *Flesh* is used for the sexual organs, as in 23:20 and Leviticus 15:2.
[^26]: Hebrew *choq*, "allotted portion", here probably the food and clothing a husband provides for his wife (Exodus 21:10).
[^27]: Literally "the *nephesh*", the appetite or desire.
[^28]: Hebrew *zimmah*, shameful, lewd behavior. Also in verses 43 and 58.
[^29]: Literally "the land of Canaan". *Canaan* also means "traders" (17:4).
[^30]: The meaning is uncertain. The word translated *heart* is an unusual form, and some take it from a related Akkadian word for "rage": "How I am filled with rage against you".
[^31]: Literally "after you there was no prostituting".
[^32]: Hebrew *nechoshet*, literally "bronze" or "copper", perhaps meaning money, or lust. The meaning is uncertain.
[^33]: Literally "the idols of your detestable practices". Hebrew *gillulim*, a contemptuous word for idols, perhaps related to the word for dung.
[^34]: Literally "with the judgments of adulteresses and of women who shed blood".
[^35]: Literally "make my fury rest on you". See 5:13.
[^36]: Or "raged against me".
[^37]: Literally "on your left". Facing east, the left hand is north and the right hand south. *Her daughters* are the towns around her.
[^38]: Or "as if that were too little".
[^39]: Literally "fullness of bread".
[^40]: Literally "strengthen the hand of".
[^41]: Or "as I saw fit".
[^42]: The Hebrew text is written "your sister" but read "your sisters".
[^43]: Literally "for you have mediated for your sisters": your sins make theirs look small by comparison.
[^44]: Hebrew *shav shevut*, literally "turn the turning", traditionally "turn the captivity": to restore good fortune.
[^45]: Literally "the fortunes of your fortunes in their midst".
[^46]: Or "Was not your sister Sodom a byword in your mouth".
[^47]: Some Hebrew manuscripts read "Edom", which differs by one similar letter.
[^48]: The Hebrew text is written "you will do" but read "I will do".
[^49]: Or "though they are not part of your covenant".
[^50]: Or "when I forgive you". Hebrew *kipper*, "make atonement".
