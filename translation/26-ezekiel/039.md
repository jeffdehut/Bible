**1** As for you, son of man,[^1] prophesy against Gog and say: This is what the Lord Yahweh[^2] says: Look, I am against you, Gog, chief prince[^3] of Meshech and Tubal.

**2** I will turn you around and drive you on,[^4] and bring you up from the far reaches of the north, and bring you against the mountains of Israel.

**3** I will strike your bow from your left hand and make your arrows drop from your right hand.

**4** On the mountains of Israel you will fall, you and all your troops and the peoples who are with you. I will give you as food to birds of prey of every kind and to the wild animals.

**5** You will fall on the open field, for I have spoken, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**6** I will send fire on Magog and on those who live securely in the coastlands. Then they will know that I am Yahweh.

**7** I will make my holy name known among my people Israel, and I will no longer let my holy name be profaned. Then the nations will know that I am Yahweh, the Holy One in Israel.

**8** Look, it is coming, and it will be done, declares the Lord Yahweh. This is the day I have spoken of.

**9** Then those who live in the cities of Israel will go out and use the weapons for fuel and burn them: small shields and large shields, bows and arrows, war clubs[^5] and spears. They will make fires with them for seven years.

**10** They will not need to gather wood from the field or cut it from the forests, because they will make their fires with the weapons. They will plunder those who plundered them and loot those who looted them, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**11** On that day I will give Gog a burial place there in Israel, the Valley of the Travelers,[^6] east of the sea. It will block the way of travelers.[^7] There they will bury Gog and all his hordes,[^8] and they will call it the Valley of Hamon-gog.[^9]

**12** For seven months the house of Israel will be burying them, in order to cleanse the land.

**13** All the people of the land will bury them, and it will bring them renown on the day I display my glory, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**14** They will set apart men to go through the land continually and bury, with those who pass through, the bodies that remain on the face of the land, so as to cleanse it. At the end of the seven months they will make their search.[^10]

**15** As they pass through the land, whenever anyone sees a human bone, he will set up a marker beside it, until the gravediggers have buried it in the Valley of Hamon-gog.

**16** A city will also be named Hamonah.[^11] So they will cleanse the land.

**17** As for you, son of man, this is what the Lord Yahweh says: Say to the birds of every kind and to all the wild animals, "Assemble and come! Gather from all around to my sacrifice, which I am sacrificing for you, a great sacrifice on the mountains of Israel, and you will eat flesh and drink blood.

**18** You will eat the flesh of the mighty and drink the blood of the princes of the earth, as if they were rams, lambs and goats, and bulls, all of them fattened animals of Bashan.[^12]

**19** You will eat fat until you are full and drink blood until you are drunk, at my sacrifice, which I have sacrificed for you.

**20** At my table you will eat your fill of horses and charioteers,[^13] of mighty men and warriors of every kind, declares the Lord Yahweh."

**21** I will display my glory among the nations, and all the nations will see the judgment I have carried out and my hand that I have laid on them.

**22** From that day on, the house of Israel will know that I am Yahweh their God.

**23** And the nations will know that the house of Israel went into exile for their guilt, because they were unfaithful to me. So I hid my face from them and handed them over to their enemies, and they all fell by the sword.

**24** I dealt with them according to their uncleanness and their transgressions, and I hid my face from them.

**25** Therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: Now I will restore the fortunes[^14] of Jacob and have compassion on the whole house of Israel, and I will be jealous for my holy name.

**26** They will bear[^15] their disgrace and all the unfaithfulness they showed toward me, when they live securely on their own land with no one to make them afraid,

**27** when I bring them back from the peoples and gather them from the lands of their enemies, and show myself holy through them in the sight of many nations.

**28** Then they will know that I am Yahweh their God, because I sent them into exile among the nations and then gathered them back to their own land. I will not leave any of them there any longer.

**29** I will no longer hide my face from them, for I will have poured out my Spirit on the house of Israel, declares the Lord Yahweh.

[^1]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. *The Lord Yahweh* is Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^3]: Or "prince of Rosh, Meshech and Tubal" (38:2).
[^4]: Hebrew *shishe'tikha*, a word found only here, of uncertain meaning.
[^5]: Literally "hand sticks".
[^6]: Hebrew *ovrim*, "those who pass through". It may also be a place name, Abarim, the mountains east of the Dead Sea (Numbers 27:12). *The sea* is probably the Dead Sea.
[^7]: Literally "and it blocks the travelers". The meaning is uncertain.
[^8]: Or "his multitude". Hebrew *hamon* (29:19).
[^9]: *Hamon-gog* means "the horde of Gog". Also in verse 15.
[^10]: The meaning of this verse is uncertain.
[^11]: *Hamonah* means "horde".
[^12]: A fertile region east of the Jordan, famous for its cattle (Amos 4:1; Psalm 22:13, 22:12 in most English Bibles).
[^13]: Literally "horse and chariot".
[^14]: Literally "turn the turning", an idiom for restoring what was lost. Or "bring back the captives".
[^15]: Or "They will forget", reading the same consonants as *nashu*.
