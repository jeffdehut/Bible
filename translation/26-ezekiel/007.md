**1** The word of Yahweh[^1] came to me:

**2** And you, son of man,[^2] this is what the Lord Yahweh[^3] says to the land of Israel: An end! The end has come on the four corners of the land.

**3** Now the end is on you.[^4] I will let loose my anger against you; I will judge you according to your ways, and I will bring on you the penalty for all your detestable practices.[^5]

**4** My eye will not pity you, and I will not spare. I will bring your ways down on you, and your detestable practices will be in your midst. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.

**5** This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Disaster! A disaster like no other![^6] Look, it is coming!

**6** An end has come! The end has come! It has awakened against you.[^7] Look, it is coming!

**7** Doom[^8] has come to you, you who live in the land. The time has come; the day is near: tumult, and not joyful shouting on the mountains.[^9]

**8** Now, very soon, I will pour out my fury on you and spend my anger against you. I will judge you according to your ways, and I will bring on you the penalty for all your detestable practices.

**9** My eye will not pity, and I will not spare. I will repay you according to your ways, and your detestable practices will be in your midst. Then you will know that I, Yahweh, am the one who strikes.

**10** Look, the day! Look, it is coming! Doom has gone out. The rod has blossomed;[^10] arrogance has budded.

**11** Violence has grown into a rod of wickedness. Nothing will be left of them, nothing of their crowds, nothing of their wealth, and there will be no splendor among them.[^11]

**12** The time has come; the day has arrived. Let the buyer not rejoice, and let the seller not mourn, for wrath is on all her crowds.[^12]

**13** For the seller will not go back to what he sold, even while they are still alive.[^13] For the vision concerning all her crowds will not be turned back, and because of his guilt, no one will keep hold of his life.[^14]

**14** They have blown the trumpet and made everything ready, but no one goes out to battle, for my wrath is on all her crowds.

**15** The sword is outside, and plague and famine are inside. Whoever is in the field will die by the sword, and whoever is in the city, famine and plague will devour him.

**16** Those of them who escape and survive will be on the mountains like doves of the valleys, all of them moaning, each for his guilt.

**17** All hands will hang limp, and all knees will turn to water.[^15]

**18** They will put on sackcloth, and horror will cover them. Shame will be on every face, and every head will be shaved bald.

**19** They will throw their silver into the streets, and their gold will be like something unclean.[^16] Their silver and their gold will not be able to save them on the day of Yahweh's wrath. They will not satisfy their hunger[^17] or fill their stomachs with it, for it made them stumble into guilt.[^18]

**20** Their beautiful jewelry they turned into a source of pride,[^19] and with it they made their detestable images, their vile idols.[^20] Therefore I will make it something unclean for them.

**21** I will hand it over to foreigners as plunder and to the wicked of the earth as spoil, and they will profane it.

**22** I will turn my face away from them, and they will profane my treasured place.[^21] Robbers will enter it and profane it.

**23** Make the chain,[^22] for the land is full of bloody crimes,[^23] and the city is full of violence.

**24** I will bring the worst of the nations, and they will take possession of their houses. I will put an end to the pride of the strong, and their holy places will be profaned.

**25** Anguish is coming! They will seek peace, but there will be none.

**26** Disaster will come on disaster, and rumor will follow rumor. They will seek a vision from a prophet, but instruction will perish from the priest, and counsel from the elders.

**27** The king will mourn, the prince[^24] will be clothed with despair, and the hands of the people of the land[^25] will tremble. I will deal with them according to their ways, and by their own standards[^26] I will judge them. Then they will know that I am Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^4]: *You* is feminine singular, the land of Israel, in verses 3–9. The Septuagint has a shorter form of verses 3–11, in a different order.
[^5]: Literally "I will put on you all your detestable practices". Also in verse 8. Hebrew *to'evot*, things that are abhorrent, especially in worship.
[^6]: Literally "one disaster", that is, a unique disaster. Some Hebrew manuscripts read "Disaster after disaster!"
[^7]: A play on words: *qets*, "end", and *heqits*, "it has awakened".
[^8]: Hebrew *tsefirah*, a rare word of uncertain meaning; also in verse 10. Others translate "Your turn", or "The cycle". In Isaiah 28:5 the same word means "crown, garland".
[^9]: Hebrew *hed*, probably the shout of those treading grapes at harvest (Isaiah 16:10; Jeremiah 25:30). The meaning is uncertain.
[^10]: The same consonants can be read "Injustice has blossomed" (Hebrew *mutteh*, as in 9:9).
[^11]: The meaning of this verse is uncertain. *Their wealth* (Hebrew *hemehem*) and *splendor* (*noah*) are words found only here.
[^12]: *Her* refers to the land. Also in verses 13–14.
[^13]: Under the law of Leviticus 25:13–16, land that had been sold went back to its owner in the year of Jubilee. Literally "while their life is still among the living".
[^14]: The meaning of the last part of the verse is uncertain. Literally "and each in his guilt, his life, they will not strengthen themselves".
[^15]: Literally "all knees will go water". This may mean that they will become as weak as water, or that they will wet themselves in fear (compare 21:12; 21:7 in most English Bibles).
[^16]: Hebrew *niddah*, the impurity of menstruation (Leviticus 15:19–24), used for anything abhorrent. Also in verse 20.
[^17]: Literally "satisfy their *nephesh*".
[^18]: Literally "it was the stumbling block of their guilt". See 3:20.
[^19]: Literally "the beauty of his ornament he set for pride". Some take "his ornament" to be the temple, Yahweh's beautiful ornament.
[^20]: Hebrew *shiqqutsim*, "detestable things", a contemptuous word for idols.
[^21]: Literally "my hidden one", probably the temple.
[^22]: Hebrew *rattoq*, found only here, probably a chain for leading captives away. The meaning is uncertain.
[^23]: Literally "judgment of blood".
[^24]: Hebrew *nasi*, "leader, chief". Ezekiel often uses this title for the king of Israel (12:10; 34:24; 37:25; chapters 44–48).
[^25]: Hebrew *am ha-arets*, the landowning citizens of Judah.
[^26]: Literally "by their own judgments".
