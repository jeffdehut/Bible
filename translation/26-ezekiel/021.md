**1** The word of the LORD[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] set your face toward the south; preach[^3] against the south, and prophesy against the forest of the countryside in the Negev.[^4]

**3** Say to the forest of the Negev: Hear the word of the LORD. This is what the Lord GOD[^5] says: Look, I am about to set a fire in you, and it will consume every green tree in you and every dry tree. The blazing flame will not be put out, and every face from south to north will be scorched by it.

**4** All people[^6] will see that I, the LORD, have kindled it; it will not be put out.

**5** Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! They are saying of me, 'Is he not just telling parables?'"

**6** The word of the LORD came to me:

**7** Son of man, set your face toward Jerusalem; preach against the sanctuaries,[^7] and prophesy against the land of Israel.

**8** Say to the land of Israel: This is what the LORD says: Look, I am against you. I will draw my sword from its sheath and cut off from you both righteous and wicked.

**9** Because I will cut off from you righteous and wicked, my sword will go out from its sheath against all people from south to north.

**10** All people will know that I, the LORD, have drawn my sword from its sheath; it will not go back again.

**11** As for you, son of man, groan! Groan with breaking heart[^8] and bitter grief before their eyes.

**12** When they ask you, "Why are you groaning?" you are to say: Because of the news that is coming. Every heart will melt and every hand go limp; every spirit will faint and every knee turn to water.[^9] Look, it is coming, and it will happen, declares the Lord GOD.

**13** The word of the LORD came to me:

**14** Son of man, prophesy and say: This is what the Lord[^10] says: Say: A sword, a sword is sharpened, and polished too.

**15** It is sharpened for slaughter, polished to flash like lightning! Or shall we rejoice? The scepter of my son despises every tree.[^11]

**16** It has been given to be polished, to be grasped in the hand. The sword is sharpened and it is polished, to be put into the hand of the killer.

**17** Cry out and wail, son of man, for it is against my people; it is against all the princes of Israel. They are thrown to the sword along with my people. Therefore strike your thigh.[^12]

**18** For it is a test. And what if even the scepter that despises does not continue? declares the Lord GOD.[^13]

**19** As for you, son of man, prophesy, and strike your hands together. Let the sword strike twice, even three times.[^14] It is a sword for the slain, the sword for the great slaughter, which surrounds them,[^15]

**20** so that hearts may melt and many may stumble. At all their gates I have set the slaughter of the sword.[^16] Ah! It is made to flash like lightning; it is drawn for slaughter.

**21** Cut sharply to the right! Turn to the left! Go wherever your edge is directed.[^17]

**22** I too will strike my hands together, and I will satisfy my fury.[^18] I, the LORD, have spoken.

**23** The word of the LORD came to me:

**24** As for you, son of man, mark out two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to come by. Both of them are to start from the same land. Make a signpost;[^19] make it at the head of the road to a city.

**25** Mark out a road for the sword to come to Rabbah of the Ammonites,[^20] and another to Judah, to Jerusalem the fortified.

**26** For the king of Babylon stands at the fork in the road,[^21] at the head of the two roads, to practice divination. He shakes the arrows, he consults the household gods,[^22] he examines the liver.[^23]

**27** Into his right hand comes the lot for Jerusalem: to set up battering rams, to open the mouth for slaughter, to raise the battle cry, to set battering rams against the gates, to pile up a ramp, to build a siege wall.

**28** But to them it will seem a false divination, since they have sworn solemn oaths.[^24] But he will bring their guilt to remembrance, so that they will be captured.

**29** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have brought your guilt to remembrance, by your open transgressions, so that your sins are seen in all your deeds, because you have been brought to remembrance, you will be taken by force.[^25]

**30** And you, profane[^26] and wicked prince of Israel, whose day has come, the time of final punishment,[^27]

**31** this is what the Lord GOD says: Remove the turban,[^28] take off the crown! Things will not stay as they are.[^29] Raise up what is low, and bring down what is high.

**32** A ruin, a ruin, a ruin I will make it! This too will not be,[^30] until he comes to whom judgment belongs, and I will give it to him.[^31]

**33** As for you, son of man, prophesy and say: This is what the Lord GOD says about the Ammonites and their taunts: Say: A sword, a sword is drawn for slaughter, polished to consume,[^32] to flash like lightning,

**34** while they see false visions for you and divine lies for you,[^33] to lay you on the necks of the slain, the wicked whose day has come, the time of final punishment.

**35** Return it to its sheath! In the place where you were created, in the land of your origin, I will judge you.

**36** I will pour out my indignation on you; I will blow on you with the fire of my wrath, and I will hand you over to brutal men,[^34] skilled in destruction.

**37** You will be fuel for the fire; your blood will be in the middle of the land. You will no longer be remembered, for I, the LORD, have spoken.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. Verses 1–5 are 20:45–49 in most English Bibles, and verses 6–37 are 21:1–32.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Literally "drip", that is, let your words flow (Amos 7:16; Micah 2:6). Also in verse 7.
[^4]: The verse uses three words for the south: *teman*, *darom* and *negev*. The Negev is the dry land in the south of Judah. Also in verse 3.
[^5]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^6]: Literally "all flesh". Also in verses 9–10.
[^7]: Or "the holy places". The Hebrew word is plural.
[^8]: Literally "with breaking of loins".
[^9]: Literally "go water". See 7:17.
[^10]: Hebrew *Adonai*, "the Lord", without the name YHWH.
[^11]: The meaning of these two sentences is very uncertain. Literally "Or shall we rejoice? The rod of my son despises every tree." *Rod* (Hebrew *shevet*) also means "scepter" and "tribe"; *my son* may be the king of Judah (2 Samuel 7:14; Psalm 2:7), and some hear an echo of Genesis 49:9–10. Also in verse 18.
[^12]: A gesture of grief and dismay (Jeremiah 31:19).
[^13]: The meaning of the verse is very uncertain. Literally "For a testing. And what if also a despising rod will not be?"
[^14]: Literally "let the sword be doubled, the third time".
[^15]: Literally "the sword of the great slain one, which surrounds them". The meaning of *surrounds* (Hebrew *choderet*, found only here) is uncertain.
[^16]: Hebrew *ivchat*, a word found only here, perhaps "slaughter" or "the glittering point". *Drawn* (Hebrew *me'uttah*) is also uncertain.
[^17]: The Hebrew of this verse is difficult. Literally "Make yourself one, go right; set yourself, go left, wherever your face is appointed." The sword is addressed.
[^18]: Literally "make my fury rest". See 5:13.
[^19]: Literally "a hand", that is, a marker pointing the way.
[^20]: The capital of Ammon, east of the Jordan; modern Amman.
[^21]: Literally "the mother of the road".
[^22]: Hebrew *teraphim*, household idols (Genesis 31:19), here consulted for guidance.
[^23]: Babylonian diviners read omens from the livers of sacrificed animals. *Shakes the arrows* is another form of divination, drawing lots marked on arrows.
[^24]: The meaning is uncertain. Literally "sworn ones of oaths are theirs". The people of Jerusalem may be trusting in their oath of loyalty to the king of Babylon (17:13–19).
[^25]: Literally "by the hand".
[^26]: Or "slain". Hebrew *chalal* can mean either.
[^27]: Literally "the time of the guilt of the end". Also in verse 34.
[^28]: Hebrew *mitsnefet*, elsewhere the high priest's turban (Exodus 28:4, 37–39).
[^29]: Literally "This, not this".
[^30]: Literally "Also this was not". The meaning is uncertain.
[^31]: The words may echo Genesis 49:10, often understood as "until he comes to whom it belongs". *Judgment*, Hebrew *mishpat*, may here mean "the right" to rule.
[^32]: The meaning of the Hebrew word, *lehakhil*, is uncertain.
[^33]: *You* is feminine singular in verses 34–37, perhaps the sword, or the land of Ammon.
[^34]: Literally "burning men".
