**1** In the thirtieth year,[^1] in the fourth month, on the fifth day of the month, while I was among the exiles by the river Chebar,[^2] the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God.

**2** On the fifth day of the month, in the fifth year of the exile of King Jehoiachin,[^3]

**3** the word of the LORD[^4] came to Ezekiel son of Buzi, the priest,[^5] in the land of the Chaldeans,[^6] by the river Chebar, and the hand of the LORD came on him there.[^7]

**4** I looked, and I saw a storm wind coming from the north: a great cloud, with fire flashing,[^8] and brightness all around it. In the middle of it, in the middle of the fire, was something like the gleam of amber.[^9]

**5** In the middle of it was the likeness of four living creatures.[^10] This was their appearance: they had a human form.

**6** Each had four faces, and each of them had four wings.

**7** Their legs were straight,[^11] and the soles of their feet were like the sole of a calf's foot. They gleamed like the gleam of polished bronze.[^12]

**8** Under their wings on their four sides they had human hands.[^13] As for the faces and the wings of the four of them,

**9** their wings touched one another. They did not turn as they went; each of them went straight ahead.

**10** The likeness of their faces was this: each had the face of a human being; the four of them had the face of a lion on the right side; the four of them had the face of an ox on the left side; and the four of them had the face of an eagle.[^14]

**11** Such were their faces. Their wings were spread out above. Each had two wings touching those of another, and two covering their bodies.

**12** Each of them went straight ahead. Wherever the spirit[^15] would go, they went. They did not turn as they went.

**13** As for the likeness of the living creatures, their appearance was like burning coals of fire, like the appearance of torches. Fire[^16] moved back and forth among the living creatures. The fire was bright, and lightning came out of the fire.

**14** The living creatures darted back and forth like the appearance of a flash of lightning.[^17]

**15** As I looked at the living creatures, I saw a wheel[^18] on the ground beside the living creatures, one for each of their four faces.[^19]

**16** The appearance of the wheels and the way they were made was like the gleam of beryl,[^20] and the four of them had the same likeness. Their appearance and the way they were made were like a wheel within a wheel.[^21]

**17** When they went, they went in any of their four directions;[^22] they did not turn as they went.

**18** As for their rims, they were high and awesome, and the rims of all four were full of eyes all around.[^23]

**19** When the living creatures went, the wheels went beside them, and when the living creatures rose from the ground, the wheels rose.

**20** Wherever the spirit would go, they went, wherever the spirit would go, and the wheels rose along with them, for the spirit of the living creatures[^24] was in the wheels.

**21** When they went, the wheels went; when they stood still, the wheels stood still; and when they rose from the ground, the wheels rose along with them, for the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.

**22** Over the heads of the living creatures there was the likeness of a dome,[^25] gleaming like awesome crystal,[^26] spread out over their heads above.

**23** Under the dome their wings were stretched out straight, one toward another. Each of them had two wings that covered it; each of them had two that covered their bodies.[^27]

**24** When they went, I heard the sound of their wings, like the sound of many waters, like the voice of the Almighty,[^28] a sound of tumult like the noise of an army camp. When they stood still, they let their wings drop.

**25** And there came a voice from above the dome that was over their heads. When they stood still, they let their wings drop.

**26** Above the dome that was over their heads was something that looked like lapis lazuli,[^29] in the likeness of a throne, and on the likeness of the throne, high above it, was a likeness with the appearance of a human being.

**27** I saw that from what looked like his waist upward he was like the gleam of amber, like the appearance of fire enclosed all around;[^30] and from what looked like his waist downward I saw something like the appearance of fire, and there was brightness all around him.

**28** Like the appearance of the rainbow[^31] in the clouds on a day of rain, so was the appearance of the brightness all around. This was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the LORD.[^32] When I saw it, I fell on my face, and I heard the voice of someone speaking.

[^1]: The meaning of "the thirtieth year" is uncertain. It may be Ezekiel's age; priests began their service at thirty (Numbers 4:3).
[^2]: Probably a large canal near the city of Nippur in Babylonia, southeast of Babylon.
[^3]: In the summer of 593 BC (the fourth month, verse 1). King Jehoiachin of Judah had been taken to Babylon in 597 BC (2 Kings 24:8–15).
[^4]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^5]: The Hebrew could also be read as calling Buzi the priest. Verses 2–3 speak of Ezekiel in the third person; the rest of the book is told in the first person.
[^6]: That is, Babylonia. The Chaldeans were the people of southern Mesopotamia who ruled the Babylonian empire.
[^7]: A phrase Ezekiel uses for the LORD's overpowering grip on him in his visions (3:14, 22; 8:1; 37:1; 40:1).
[^8]: Literally "fire taking hold of itself", perhaps fire flashing back and forth (the same phrase is used in Exodus 9:24).
[^9]: Hebrew *chashmal*, a word found only in Ezekiel (here, verse 27 and 8:2), of uncertain meaning. The Septuagint translates it *elektron*, which can mean amber or electrum, a shining alloy of gold and silver. Many translations have "gleaming metal". In modern Hebrew the word means "electricity".
[^10]: Hebrew *chayyot*, "living beings". In chapter 10 Ezekiel sees them again and recognizes them as cherubim (10:15, 20).
[^11]: Hebrew *regel* means both "foot" and "leg". Literally "their legs were a straight leg".
[^12]: Or "burnished bronze". The word *qalal* is found only here and in Daniel 10:6.
[^13]: The Hebrew text is written "and his hands were human" but read "and human hands".
[^14]: Or "vulture". Hebrew *nesher* can mean either.
[^15]: Or "the wind". Hebrew *ruach*, "spirit, wind, breath". Also in verses 20–21.
[^16]: Literally "It", probably the fire.
[^17]: Hebrew *bazaq*, a word found only here, probably "flash of lightning". The Septuagint does not have this verse.
[^18]: Hebrew *ofan*, the ordinary word for a wheel (1 Kings 7:30–33). The wheels are the *ofannim*.
[^19]: The meaning is uncertain. Literally "one wheel on the ground beside the living creatures, to his four faces".
[^20]: Hebrew *tarshish*, a precious stone whose identity is uncertain (Exodus 28:20).
[^21]: Perhaps two wheels set crosswise, so that they could go in any of four directions without turning (verse 17).
[^22]: Literally "on their four sides".
[^23]: Or "full of gleaming points". The Hebrew word for "eye" also means "gleam", as in "like the gleam of" in verses 4, 7, 16 and 22. The meaning of the first part of the verse is uncertain; literally "And their rims, and height was theirs, and fear was theirs, and their rims were full of eyes all around, the four of them."
[^24]: Or "the spirit of life". Hebrew *ruach ha-chayyah*, with "living creature" in the singular, used of the whole group; also in verses 21–22.
[^25]: Hebrew *raqia*, the word used for the dome of the sky in Genesis 1:6–8: something hammered or spread out. Traditionally "firmament".
[^26]: Or "ice". Hebrew *qerach* means both.
[^27]: The Hebrew repeats the clause: literally "to each, two covering them, and to each, two covering them, their bodies". Probably each creature covered its body with two wings, as in verse 11.
[^28]: Hebrew *Shaddai*, traditionally "the Almighty"; the meaning is uncertain.
[^29]: Hebrew *sappir*, traditionally "sapphire". It was probably lapis lazuli, a deep blue stone (Exodus 24:10).
[^30]: Literally "like the appearance of fire, a house for it all around". The meaning is uncertain.
[^31]: Literally "the bow" (Genesis 9:13–16).
[^32]: Hebrew *kavod*, "weight, honor, glory": here the visible splendor of God's presence (Exodus 24:16–17; 40:34–35).
