**1** In the sixth year, in the sixth month, on the fifth day of the month,[^1] as I was sitting in my house, with the elders of Judah sitting in front of me, the hand of the Lord GOD[^2] fell on me there.

**2** I looked, and I saw a likeness with the appearance of fire.[^3] From what looked like his waist downward was fire, and from his waist upward was something like the appearance of brightness, like the gleam of amber.[^4]

**3** He stretched out the form of a hand and took me by a lock of my hair, and the Spirit[^5] lifted me up between earth and heaven and brought me in visions of God to Jerusalem, to the entrance of the inner gate that faces north, where the image of jealousy stood, the image that provokes jealousy.[^6]

**4** And there was the glory of the God of Israel, like the vision that I had seen in the plain.

**5** He said to me, "Son of man,[^7] lift up your eyes toward the north." So I lifted up my eyes toward the north, and there, north of the altar gate, in the entrance, was this image of jealousy.

**6** He said to me, "Son of man, do you see what they are doing, the great detestable practices[^8] that the house of Israel is committing here, to drive me far from my sanctuary?[^9] But you will see yet more great detestable practices."

**7** He brought me to the entrance of the court, and I looked, and there was a hole in the wall.

**8** He said to me, "Son of man, dig through the wall." So I dug through the wall, and there was an entrance.

**9** He said to me, "Go in and see the evil, detestable practices that they are committing here."

**10** So I went in and looked, and there, carved on the wall all around, were all kinds of creeping things and unclean animals,[^10] and all the idols[^11] of the house of Israel.

**11** Seventy of the elders of the house of Israel were standing in front of them, with Jaazaniah son of Shaphan[^12] standing among them. Each had his censer in his hand, and a fragrant cloud of incense was rising.

**12** He said to me, "Son of man, have you seen what the elders of the house of Israel are doing in the dark, each in the room of his carved images?[^13] For they say, 'The LORD does not see us. The LORD has abandoned the land.'"

**13** He said to me, "You will see yet more great detestable practices that they are committing."

**14** Then he brought me to the entrance of the north gate of the house of the LORD, and there were the women, sitting and weeping for Tammuz.[^14]

**15** He said to me, "Have you seen this, son of man? You will see still greater detestable practices than these."

**16** Then he brought me into the inner court of the house of the LORD, and there at the entrance of the temple of the LORD,[^15] between the porch and the altar, were about twenty-five men, with their backs to the temple of the LORD and their faces toward the east, bowing down to the sun in the east.[^16]

**17** He said to me, "Have you seen this, son of man? Is it a trivial thing for the house of Judah to commit the detestable practices they have committed here, that they must also fill the land with violence and provoke me to anger again and again? Look, they are putting the branch to their nose![^17]

**18** Therefore I too will act in fury. My eye will not pity, and I will not spare. Though they cry in my ears with a loud voice, I will not listen to them."

[^1]: In 592 BC, fourteen months after the vision of chapter 1 (1:1–2).
[^2]: *The Lord GOD*: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". God's personal name, YHWH, is written GOD in capitals after "Lord", and "the LORD" elsewhere, as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: The Septuagint reads "the likeness of a man" (Hebrew *ish*, "man", for *esh*, "fire"), as in 1:26–27.
[^4]: Hebrew *chashmal*. See 1:4.
[^5]: Or "a spirit" or "a wind". Hebrew *ruach*.
[^6]: Literally "where the seat of the image of jealousy that makes jealous was". Hebrew *semel*, "image, statue", as in Deuteronomy 4:16 and 2 Chronicles 33:7, 15, where King Manasseh sets up an image in the temple. *Jealousy*, Hebrew *qin'ah*, is the zeal with which the LORD guards his people's loyalty (Exodus 20:5).
[^7]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^8]: Hebrew *to'evot*, things that are abhorrent, especially in worship. Also in verses 9, 13, 15 and 17.
[^9]: Or "so that they are far from my sanctuary".
[^10]: Hebrew *sheqets*, the word for the unclean creatures that Israel was not to eat (Leviticus 11:10–42).
[^11]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a contemptuous word for idols, perhaps related to the word for dung.
[^12]: Probably of the family of Shaphan, King Josiah's secretary, whose sons supported Jeremiah (2 Kings 22:3–14; Jeremiah 26:24; 36:10–12). This Jaazaniah is not the one in 11:1.
[^13]: Hebrew *maskit*, "carved image, picture" (Leviticus 26:1; Numbers 33:52). Literally "in the rooms of his images".
[^14]: A Mesopotamian god (Sumerian *Dumuzi*) of shepherds and of growing things, whose death was mourned each year in the summer. The fourth month of the Babylonian and Jewish calendars is named after him.
[^15]: Hebrew *heikhal*, the main hall of the temple. The *porch* (Hebrew *ulam*) was the entrance hall in front of it (1 Kings 6:3).
[^16]: The form of the Hebrew verb for "bowing down" is unusual.
[^17]: The meaning is uncertain; perhaps a gesture used in worship. Jewish tradition counts this verse among the "corrections of the scribes", saying that the original text read "my nose" and that it was changed out of reverence.
