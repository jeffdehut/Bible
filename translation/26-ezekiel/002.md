**1** He said to me, "Son of man,[^1] stand on your feet, and I will speak with you."

**2** As he spoke to me, the Spirit[^2] came into me and set me on my feet, and I heard him speaking to me.

**3** He said to me, "Son of man, I am sending you to the Israelites, to rebellious nations[^3] that have rebelled against me. They and their ancestors have revolted against me to this very day.

**4** The children are hard-faced and stubborn-hearted.[^4] I am sending you to them, and you are to say to them, 'This is what the Lord GOD[^5] says.'

**5** And whether they listen or refuse to listen,[^6] for they are a rebellious house, they will know that a prophet has been among them.

**6** And you, son of man, do not be afraid of them or of their words, though briers and thorns[^7] are all around you and you live among scorpions. Do not be afraid of their words, and do not be terrified by their looks,[^8] for they are a rebellious house.

**7** You are to speak my words to them, whether they listen or refuse to listen, for they are rebellious.

**8** But you, son of man, listen to what I am saying to you. Do not be rebellious like that rebellious house. Open your mouth and eat what I am giving you."

**9** Then I looked, and I saw a hand stretched out to me, and in it was a scroll.[^9]

**10** He spread it out in front of me. It had writing on the front and on the back, and written on it were laments, mourning and woe.

[^1]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal. The LORD addresses Ezekiel this way more than ninety times in the book.
[^2]: Or "a spirit". Hebrew *ruach*, "spirit, wind, breath", here without the article.
[^3]: Hebrew *goyim*, the word usually used for the nations other than Israel.
[^4]: Literally "hard of face and strong of heart".
[^5]: *The Lord GOD*: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". God's personal name, YHWH, is written GOD in capitals after "Lord", and "the LORD" elsewhere, as in most English Bibles. Ezekiel names God this way more than two hundred times.
[^6]: Literally "or hold back". Also in verse 7.
[^7]: Hebrew *sarabim* and *sallonim*, rare words, probably "briers and thorns". Literally "are with you".
[^8]: Literally "by their faces".
[^9]: Literally "a scroll of a book".
