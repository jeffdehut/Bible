**1** The word of the LORD[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] prophesy against the shepherds[^3] of Israel. Prophesy and say to them, to the shepherds: This is what the Lord GOD[^4] says: Woe to the shepherds of Israel, who have been feeding themselves! Should not shepherds feed the flock?

**3** You eat the fat,[^5] you clothe yourselves with the wool, and you slaughter the fattened animals, but you do not feed the flock.

**4** You have not strengthened the weak, you have not healed the sick, you have not bound up the injured, you have not brought back the strays, and you have not searched for the lost. With force and harshness you have ruled them.

**5** So they were scattered for lack of a shepherd, and they became food for all the wild animals, and they were scattered.

**6** My sheep wandered over all the mountains and on every high hill. My sheep were scattered over the whole face of the earth, with no one to look for them or search for them.

**7** Therefore, you shepherds, hear the word of the LORD:

**8** As I live, declares the Lord GOD, because my flock has become plunder, and my flock has become food for all the wild animals for lack of a shepherd, and my shepherds have not looked for my flock, but the shepherds have fed themselves and have not fed my flock,

**9** therefore, you shepherds, hear the word of the LORD.

**10** This is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am against the shepherds, and I will demand my flock from their hand.[^6] I will stop them from feeding the flock, and the shepherds will no longer feed themselves. I will rescue my flock from their mouths, so that they will no longer be food for them.

**11** For this is what the Lord GOD says: I myself will search for my flock and look after them.

**12** As a shepherd looks after his flock on the day he is among his scattered sheep, so I will look after my flock. I will rescue them from all the places where they were scattered on a day of cloud and thick darkness.

**13** I will bring them out from the peoples and gather them from the countries, and I will bring them into their own land. I will feed them on the mountains of Israel, by the streams and in all the inhabited places of the land.

**14** I will feed them in good pasture, and their grazing land will be on the high mountains of Israel. There they will lie down in good grazing land, and they will feed on rich pasture on the mountains of Israel.

**15** I myself will shepherd my flock, and I myself will make them lie down, declares the Lord GOD.

**16** I will search for the lost, bring back the strays, bind up the injured and strengthen the sick, but the fat and the strong I will destroy.[^7] I will shepherd the flock with justice.

**17** As for you, my flock, this is what the Lord GOD says: I am going to judge between one sheep and another, between the rams and the male goats.

**18** Is it not enough for you to feed on the good pasture, that you must trample the rest of your pasture with your feet? Is it not enough for you to drink the clear water, that you must muddy the rest with your feet?

**19** And my flock must feed on what your feet have trampled and drink what your feet have muddied.

**20** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says to them: I myself will judge between the fat sheep and the lean sheep.

**21** Because you push with flank and shoulder, and butt all the weak with your horns until you have scattered them far away,

**22** I will save my flock, and they will no longer be plunder. I will judge between one sheep and another.

**23** I will set over them one shepherd, my servant David,[^8] and he will feed them. He will feed them and be their shepherd.

**24** I, the LORD, will be their God, and my servant David will be prince[^9] among them. I, the LORD, have spoken.

**25** I will make a covenant of peace with them and rid the land of savage beasts, so that they may live securely in the wilderness and sleep in the forests.

**26** I will make them and the places around my hill a blessing. I will send down the rain in its season; there will be showers of blessing.

**27** The trees of the field will yield their fruit, and the land will yield its produce, and they will be secure in their land. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I break the bars of their yoke and rescue them from the hand of those who enslaved them.

**28** They will no longer be plunder for the nations, and the wild animals of the land will not devour them. They will live securely, and no one will make them afraid.

**29** I will raise up for them a planting of renown,[^10] and they will no longer be swept away by famine in the land, or bear the scorn of the nations any more.

**30** Then they will know that I, the LORD their God, am with them, and that they, the house of Israel, are my people, declares the Lord GOD.

**31** You are my flock, the flock of my pasture. You are human beings,[^11] and I am your God, declares the Lord GOD.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: That is, the rulers of Israel, its kings and leaders. Kings in the ancient Near East were often called shepherds of their people (Jeremiah 23:1–4).
[^4]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^5]: The same consonants can be read *chalav*, "the milk".
[^6]: That is, I will hold them responsible for my flock.
[^7]: The Septuagint reads "I will watch over", which differs by one similar letter in Hebrew.
[^8]: That is, a king from the line of David (37:24–25; Jeremiah 30:9; Hosea 3:5).
[^9]: Hebrew *nasi*, "leader, chief". Ezekiel often uses this title for the king of Israel (7:27; 37:25; chapters 44–48).
[^10]: Literally "a planting for a name", a land famous for its crops.
[^11]: Hebrew *adam*, "humankind": the flock stands for the people.
