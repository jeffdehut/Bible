**1** He said to me, "Son of man,[^1] eat what you find. Eat this scroll, and go, speak to the house of Israel."

**2** So I opened my mouth, and he fed me this scroll.

**3** He said to me, "Son of man, feed your stomach and fill your belly with this scroll that I am giving you." So I ate it, and in my mouth it was as sweet as honey.

**4** He said to me, "Son of man, go to the house of Israel and speak my words to them.

**5** For you are not being sent to a people of strange speech and difficult language,[^2] but to the house of Israel,

**6** not to many peoples of strange speech and difficult language, whose words you cannot understand. Surely, if I sent you to them, they would listen to you.

**7** But the house of Israel will not be willing to listen to you, for they are not willing to listen to me. For the whole house of Israel is hard-headed and stubborn-hearted.[^3]

**8** Look, I have made your face as hard as their faces, and your forehead as hard as their foreheads.[^4]

**9** I have made your forehead like diamond,[^5] harder than flint. Do not be afraid of them, and do not be terrified by their looks,[^6] for they are a rebellious house."

**10** He said to me, "Son of man, take to heart all my words that I speak to you, and listen with your ears.

**11** Go to the exiles, to your own people, and speak to them. Say to them, 'This is what the Lord Yahweh[^7] says,' whether they listen or refuse to listen."[^8]

**12** Then the Spirit[^9] lifted me up, and I heard behind me the sound of a great rumbling:[^10] "Blessed be the glory of Yahweh from his place!"[^11]

**13** It was the sound of the wings of the living creatures brushing against one another, and the sound of the wheels beside them, the sound of a great rumbling.

**14** The Spirit lifted me up and took me away, and I went in bitterness, my spirit burning,[^12] and the hand of Yahweh was strong on me.

**15** I came to the exiles at Tel-abib,[^13] who lived by the river Chebar, and I sat where they were living.[^14] I sat there among them for seven days, overwhelmed.[^15]

**16** At the end of seven days the word of Yahweh came to me:

**17** "Son of man, I have made you a watchman[^16] for the house of Israel. Whenever you hear a word from my mouth, you are to warn them from me.

**18** When I say to the wicked, 'You will surely die,' and you do not warn him, and you do not speak to warn the wicked from his wicked way so as to save his life, that wicked person will die for his guilt, but I will hold you responsible for his blood.[^17]

**19** But if you warn the wicked, and he does not turn from his wickedness or from his wicked way, he will die for his guilt, but you will have saved your life.[^18]

**20** And when a righteous person turns from his righteousness and does wrong, and I put a stumbling block[^19] in front of him, he will die. Because you did not warn him, he will die for his sin, and the righteous deeds he has done will not be remembered, but I will hold you responsible for his blood.

**21** But if you warn the righteous person not to sin, and he does not sin, he will surely live, because he took the warning, and you will have saved your life."

**22** The hand of Yahweh was on me there, and he said to me, "Get up, go out to the plain,[^20] and there I will speak with you."

**23** So I got up and went out to the plain, and there was the glory of Yahweh standing there, like the glory I had seen by the river Chebar, and I fell on my face.

**24** Then the Spirit came into me and set me on my feet. He spoke with me and said to me, "Go, shut yourself inside your house.

**25** And you, son of man, look, they will put ropes on you and tie you up with them, so that you cannot go out among them.

**26** I will make your tongue stick to the roof of your mouth, so that you will be unable to speak and unable to rebuke them,[^21] for they are a rebellious house.

**27** But when I speak with you, I will open your mouth, and you are to say to them, 'This is what the Lord Yahweh says.' Whoever will listen, let him listen, and whoever refuses, let him refuse, for they are a rebellious house.

[^1]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^2]: Literally "deep of lip and heavy of tongue". Also in verse 6.
[^3]: Literally "strong of forehead and hard of heart".
[^4]: The name Ezekiel, Hebrew *Yechezqel*, means "God makes strong" or "God makes hard". *Hard* in verses 7–9 (*chazaq*) comes from the same root.
[^5]: Hebrew *shamir*, a very hard stone, perhaps emery or diamond (Jeremiah 17:1; Zechariah 7:12).
[^6]: Literally "by their faces".
[^7]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. *The Lord Yahweh* is Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^8]: Literally "or hold back". Also in verse 27.
[^9]: Or "a spirit" or "a wind". Hebrew *ruach*. Also in verses 14 and 24.
[^10]: Or "a great earthquake". Also in verse 13.
[^11]: Some scholars read *berum*, "as the glory of Yahweh rose from its place", instead of *barukh*, "blessed", with a small change to the Hebrew (compare 10:4, 18–19).
[^12]: Literally "bitter, in the heat of my spirit".
[^13]: A settlement of the exiles in Babylonia. The name probably comes from Akkadian *til abubi*, "mound of the flood", that is, an ancient ruin mound. The modern city of Tel Aviv took its name from this verse.
[^14]: The Hebrew text is written "and where they were living" but read "and I sat where they were living".
[^15]: Or "appalled". Hebrew *mashmim*, "stunned, desolate".
[^16]: Hebrew *tsofeh*, a lookout posted on a city wall to warn of danger (2 Samuel 18:24–27). Verses 17–19 are repeated, with changes, in 33:7–9.
[^17]: Literally "his blood I will seek from your hand". Also in verse 20.
[^18]: Hebrew *nafshekha*, "your life, yourself". Also in verse 21.
[^19]: Hebrew *mikhshol*, "something to trip over", Ezekiel's word for what brings a person down into sin or into ruin (7:19; 14:3–4; 18:30; 44:12).
[^20]: Or "the valley". Hebrew *biq'ah*, a broad valley.
[^21]: Literally "you will not be a man who reproves them".
