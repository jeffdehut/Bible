**1** The word of the LORD[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] there were two women, daughters of one mother.

**3** They played the prostitute[^3] in Egypt; in their youth they played the prostitute. There their breasts were squeezed, and there their virgin nipples were fondled.

**4** Their names were Oholah, the older, and Oholibah her sister.[^4] They became mine, and they bore sons and daughters. As for their names, Oholah is Samaria, and Oholibah is Jerusalem.

**5** Oholah played the prostitute while she was mine.[^5] She lusted after her lovers, the Assyrians, warriors[^6]

**6** clothed in blue, governors and commanders, all of them desirable young men, horsemen riding on horses.

**7** She gave herself as a prostitute to them, the choicest men of Assyria, all of them, and she defiled herself with everyone she lusted after, with all their idols.[^7]

**8** She did not give up the prostitution she had practiced since Egypt, for in her youth men had lain with her, fondled her virgin nipples, and poured out their lust[^8] on her.

**9** Therefore I handed her over to her lovers, to the Assyrians, after whom she lusted.

**10** They uncovered her nakedness, took her sons and her daughters, and killed her with the sword. She became a byword[^9] among women, because judgments were carried out on her.[^10]

**11** Her sister Oholibah saw this, yet she was more corrupt than she in her lust, and in her prostitution she was worse than her sister.

**12** She lusted after the Assyrians, governors and commanders, warriors clothed in splendor,[^11] horsemen riding on horses, all of them desirable young men.

**13** I saw that she had defiled herself; both of them went the same way.

**14** But she carried her prostitution further. She saw men carved on the wall, images of Chaldeans drawn in red,[^12]

**15** with belts around their waists and flowing turbans on their heads, all of them looking like officers,[^13] in the likeness of Babylonians whose native land is Chaldea.

**16** As soon as she saw them, she lusted after them, and she sent messengers to them in Chaldea.

**17** The Babylonians came to her, to the bed of love, and defiled her with their lust. After she had been defiled by them, she turned away from them in disgust.

**18** When she carried on her prostitution openly and exposed her nakedness, I turned away from her in disgust, just as I had turned away from her sister.

**19** Yet she multiplied her prostitution, remembering the days of her youth, when she played the prostitute in the land of Egypt.

**20** She lusted after their paramours,[^14] whose genitals were like those of donkeys and whose emission was like that of horses.[^15]

**21** So you longed for the lewdness of your youth, when men from Egypt fondled your nipples for the sake of your young breasts.[^16]

**22** Therefore, Oholibah, this is what the Lord GOD[^17] says: Look, I am going to stir up your lovers against you, those from whom you turned away in disgust, and I will bring them against you from every side:

**23** the Babylonians and all the Chaldeans, Pekod and Shoa and Koa,[^18] and all the Assyrians with them, desirable young men, governors and commanders all of them, officers and men of renown,[^19] all of them riding on horses.

**24** They will come against you with weapons,[^20] chariots and wagons, and with a horde of peoples. They will set themselves against you on every side with large shields, small shields and helmets. I will hand you over to them for judgment,[^21] and they will judge you by their own standards.[^22]

**25** I will direct my jealousy against you, and they will deal with you in fury. They will cut off your nose and your ears, and those of you who are left will fall by the sword. They will take your sons and your daughters, and those of you who are left[^23] will be consumed by fire.

**26** They will strip you of your clothes and take your beautiful jewels.

**27** I will put an end to your lewdness and to the prostitution you brought from the land of Egypt. You will not lift up your eyes to them, nor remember Egypt anymore.

**28** For this is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am going to hand you over to those you hate, to those from whom you turned away in disgust.

**29** They will deal with you in hatred and take away everything you have worked for, and leave you naked and bare. The nakedness of your prostitution will be exposed, your lewdness and your prostitution.

**30** These things will be done to you[^24] because you played the prostitute with the nations and defiled yourself with their idols.

**31** You have walked in the way of your sister, so I will put her cup into your hand.

**32** This is what the Lord GOD says: You will drink your sister's cup, deep and wide. You will be laughed at and mocked, for it holds so much.[^25]

**33** You will be filled with drunkenness and sorrow. It is a cup of horror and desolation, the cup of your sister Samaria.

**34** You will drink it and drain it; you will gnaw its broken pieces and tear your breasts. For I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.

**35** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have forgotten me and thrown me behind your back, you too must bear the penalty of your lewdness and your prostitution.

**36** The LORD said to me: Son of man, will you judge Oholah and Oholibah? Then declare to them their detestable practices.[^26]

**37** For they have committed adultery, and blood is on their hands. They have committed adultery with their idols, and they have even offered up to them as food the sons they bore to me.[^27]

**38** Moreover, they have done this to me: on that same day they defiled my sanctuary and profaned my Sabbaths.

**39** When they had slaughtered their children for their idols, on that same day they came into my sanctuary to profane it. Look, that is what they did in the middle of my house.

**40** They even sent for men who came from far away, to whom a messenger was sent; and look, they came. For them you bathed yourself,[^28] painted your eyes, and adorned yourself with jewelry.

**41** You sat on a splendid couch, with a table spread before it, on which you had set my incense and my oil.

**42** The sound of a carefree crowd was there. Along with men from the common crowd, Sabeans[^29] were brought from the wilderness. They put bracelets on the women's arms and beautiful crowns on their heads.

**43** Then I said of her who was worn out by adulteries, "Now they will use her as a prostitute,[^30] her too."[^31]

**44** They went in to her as men go in to a prostitute. So they went in to Oholah and to Oholibah, the lewd women.

**45** But righteous men will judge them with the sentence for adulteresses and the sentence for women who shed blood, because they are adulteresses and blood is on their hands.

**46** For this is what the Lord GOD says: Bring up a mob against them, and hand them over to terror and plunder.

**47** The mob will stone them and cut them down with their swords. They will kill their sons and their daughters and burn their houses with fire.

**48** So I will put an end to lewdness in the land, so that all women may take warning and not imitate your lewdness.

**49** They will bring your lewdness down on you, and you will bear the penalty for your sins with your idols. Then you will know that I am the Lord GOD.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Hebrew *zanah*, "act as a prostitute", a common image for unfaithfulness to the LORD (6:9). Its nouns, *taznut* and *zenut*, "prostitution", recur through the chapter. Compare chapter 16.
[^4]: *Oholah* means "her tent" and *Oholibah* "my tent is in her", perhaps referring to places of worship: the northern kingdom's own shrines, and the LORD's temple in Jerusalem.
[^5]: Literally "under me".
[^6]: Hebrew *qerovim*, "near ones", perhaps "neighbors", or here "warriors". Also in verse 12.
[^7]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a contemptuous word for idols, perhaps related to the word for dung. Also in verses 30, 37, 39 and 49.
[^8]: Literally "their prostitution". Also in verse 17.
[^9]: Literally "a name".
[^10]: Assyria captured Samaria, the capital of the northern kingdom, in 722 BC and took its people into exile (2 Kings 17:5–6).
[^11]: Hebrew *mikhlol*, "perfection", probably splendid clothing.
[^12]: Hebrew *shashar*, a red pigment, vermilion (Jeremiah 22:14).
[^13]: Hebrew *shalishim*, a title of high-ranking officers, perhaps the third man in a chariot. Also in verse 23.
[^14]: Hebrew *pilagshim*, usually "concubines", here male lovers; *their* probably refers to the Egyptians.
[^15]: Literally "whose flesh was the flesh of donkeys and whose issue was the issue of horses". *Flesh* here means the sexual organs (16:26).
[^16]: Literally "when they handled, from Egypt, your nipples, for the sake of the breasts of your youth". Some scholars read "to squeeze the breasts of your youth", with a small change to the Hebrew (compare verse 3).
[^17]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^18]: Peoples of eastern Mesopotamia, subject to Babylon. Pekod is also named in Jeremiah 50:21.
[^19]: Literally "called ones", perhaps "counselors" or "men summoned".
[^20]: Hebrew *hotsen*, a word found only here, of uncertain meaning.
[^21]: Literally "I will set judgment before them".
[^22]: Literally "by their judgments".
[^23]: Hebrew *acharit*, "what is left, the end". Twice in this verse.
[^24]: Literally "Doing these things to you".
[^25]: The Hebrew of this line is difficult. Literally "it will be for laughter and for mockery, great to contain".
[^26]: Hebrew *to'evot*, things that are abhorrent, especially in worship.
[^27]: Literally "they have made them pass over to them for food" (16:20–21).
[^28]: The Hebrew changes here from "they" to "you" (feminine singular). Also in verse 41.
[^29]: The Hebrew text is written "drunkards" but read "Sabeans", people of Sheba in southern Arabia. The meaning of the verse is uncertain.
[^30]: The Hebrew text is written "a time, he will use her as a prostitute" but read "now they will use her as a prostitute".
[^31]: The meaning of the verse is uncertain. Literally "Now they will commit her prostitution, and she".
