**1** "And you, son of man,[^1] take a sharp sword and use it as a barber's razor: pass it over your head and over your beard. Then take scales for weighing, and divide the hair.

**2** A third of it you are to burn in the fire in the middle of the city, when the days of the siege are completed. A third you are to take and strike with the sword all around the city. And a third you are to scatter to the wind, and I will draw a sword after them.

**3** Take a few hairs from there and tie them up in the hem of your robe.[^2]

**4** Take some of these again, throw them into the fire, and burn them in the fire. From there a fire will spread to the whole house of Israel.

**5** This is what the Lord Yahweh[^3] says: This is Jerusalem. I set her in the middle of the nations, with countries all around her.

**6** But she has rebelled against my rulings more wickedly than the nations, and against my decrees more than the countries around her. For they have rejected my rulings and have not walked in my decrees.

**7** Therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: Because you have been more unruly[^4] than the nations around you, and have not walked in my decrees or kept my rulings, and have not even acted according to the rulings of the nations around you,[^5]

**8** therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: Look, I, even I, am against you.[^6] I will carry out judgments in your midst in the sight of the nations.

**9** Because of all your detestable practices,[^7] I will do to you what I have never done before, and the like of which I will never do again.

**10** Therefore fathers will eat their children in your midst, and children will eat their fathers. I will carry out judgments on you, and I will scatter all who are left of you to every wind.

**11** Therefore, as I live, declares the Lord Yahweh,[^8] because you have defiled my sanctuary with all your vile idols[^9] and with all your detestable practices, I too will cut you down.[^10] My eye will not pity, and I will not spare.

**12** A third of you will die of plague and perish by famine in your midst; a third will fall by the sword all around you; and a third I will scatter to every wind, and I will draw a sword after them.

**13** Then my anger will be spent, and I will satisfy my fury on them,[^11] and I will be appeased.[^12] Then they will know that I, Yahweh, have spoken in my jealousy,[^13] when I have spent my fury on them.

**14** I will make you a ruin and a disgrace among the nations around you, in the sight of everyone who passes by.

**15** You will be[^14] a disgrace and a taunt, a warning and a horror to the nations around you, when I carry out judgments on you in anger and in fury and with furious punishments.[^15] I, Yahweh, have spoken.

**16** When I send against them the deadly arrows of famine, arrows for destruction, which I will send to destroy you, I will bring more and more famine on you, and I will cut off your supply of bread.[^16]

**17** I will send famine and wild animals against you, and they will leave you childless. Plague and bloodshed will sweep through you, and I will bring the sword against you. I, Yahweh, have spoken."

[^1]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^2]: Literally "in your wings", the corners or edges of a garment.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. *The Lord Yahweh* is Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^4]: The meaning of the Hebrew word, *hamonkhem*, is uncertain. It may come from *hamah*, "be in turmoil".
[^5]: Compare 11:12, where they are said to have acted according to the rulings of the nations around them.
[^6]: *You* is feminine singular: Jerusalem is addressed. Also in verses 9–17.
[^7]: Hebrew *to'evot*, things that are abhorrent, especially in worship. Ezekiel uses the word more than forty times.
[^8]: Hebrew *ne'um Adonai YHWH*, "the declaration of the Lord Yahweh", a formula that marks a prophet's words as Yahweh's own solemn statement.
[^9]: Hebrew *shiqqutsim*, "detestable things", a contemptuous word for idols.
[^10]: Literally "I will diminish" or "I will clip"; the object is not stated, and the meaning is uncertain. The verb is used for a shaved beard in Jeremiah 48:37, which fits the sign of verses 1–4. Others translate "I will withdraw".
[^11]: Literally "make my fury rest on them". Also in 16:42; 21:22 (21:17 in most English Bibles); 24:13.
[^12]: Or "I will take comfort".
[^13]: Or "zeal". Hebrew *qin'ah*.
[^14]: Literally "She will be", speaking of Jerusalem in the third person.
[^15]: Literally "rebukes of fury".
[^16]: Literally "break the staff of bread for you" (4:16; Leviticus 26:26).
