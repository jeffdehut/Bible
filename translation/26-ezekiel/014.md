**1** Some of the elders of Israel came to me and sat down in front of me.

**2** Then the word of the LORD[^1] came to me:

**3** Son of man,[^2] these men have set up their idols[^3] in their hearts[^4] and put the stumbling block of their guilt[^5] in front of their faces. Should I let myself be consulted by them at all?

**4** Therefore speak to them and say to them: This is what the Lord GOD[^6] says: Anyone of the house of Israel who sets up his idols in his heart and puts the stumbling block of his guilt in front of his face, and then comes to the prophet, I, the LORD, will answer him myself as he comes[^7] with his many idols,

**5** so that I may take hold of the house of Israel by their hearts,[^8] for they have all turned away from me through their idols.

**6** Therefore say to the house of Israel: This is what the Lord GOD says: Turn back![^9] Turn away from your idols, and turn your faces away from all your detestable practices.[^10]

**7** For anyone of the house of Israel, or of the foreigners who live in Israel, who separates himself from me, sets up his idols in his heart, and puts the stumbling block of his guilt in front of his face, and then comes to the prophet to consult me through him, I, the LORD, will answer him myself.

**8** I will set my face against that man and make him a sign and a byword,[^11] and I will cut him off from among my people. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

**9** And if the prophet is deceived and speaks a word, it is I, the LORD, who have deceived that prophet,[^12] and I will stretch out my hand against him and destroy him from among my people Israel.

**10** They will bear their guilt; the guilt of the one who consults will be the same as the guilt of the prophet,

**11** so that the house of Israel will no longer stray from me or defile themselves anymore with all their transgressions. Then they will be my people, and I will be their God, declares the Lord GOD.

**12** The word of the LORD came to me:

**13** Son of man, if a land sins against me by acting unfaithfully,[^13] and I stretch out my hand against it, cut off its supply of bread,[^14] send famine on it, and cut off from it both people and animals,

**14** even if these three men, Noah, Daniel[^15] and Job, were in it, they would save only themselves[^16] by their righteousness, declares the Lord GOD.

**15** If I send wild animals through the land and they leave it childless, and it becomes so desolate that no one passes through it because of the animals,

**16** even if these three men were in it, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, they could not save their sons or their daughters. They alone would be saved, and the land would be desolate.

**17** Or if I bring a sword against that land and say, "Let a sword pass through the land," and I cut off from it both people and animals,

**18** even if these three men were in it, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, they could not save their sons or their daughters; they alone would be saved.

**19** Or if I send a plague into that land and pour out my fury on it in bloodshed, to cut off from it both people and animals,

**20** even if Noah, Daniel and Job were in it, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, they could not save son or daughter. They would save only themselves by their righteousness.

**21** For this is what the Lord GOD says: How much worse will it be when I send my four terrible judgments against Jerusalem, sword and famine and wild animals and plague, to cut off from it both people and animals!

**22** Yet look, some survivors will be left in it, sons and daughters who will be brought out. Look, they will come out to you, and when you see their ways and their deeds, you will be consoled for the disaster I have brought on Jerusalem, for everything I have brought on it.

**23** They will console you when you see their ways and their deeds, and you will know that it was not without cause that I did all that I did in it, declares the Lord GOD.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a contemptuous word for idols, perhaps related to the word for dung. Also in verses 4–7.
[^4]: Literally "have made their idols go up on their heart". Also in verses 4 and 7.
[^5]: That is, the idols that make them fall into guilt. See 7:19.
[^6]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^7]: The Hebrew text is written "by it" but read "he comes".
[^8]: Or "seize the house of Israel in their hearts".
[^9]: Or "Repent!" Hebrew *shuvu*, "turn back, return".
[^10]: Hebrew *to'evot*, things that are abhorrent, especially in worship.
[^11]: Hebrew *meshalim*, "proverbs, sayings": his name will be used as an example of disaster.
[^12]: Hebrew *pittah*, "entice, deceive", the verb used in 1 Kings 22:20–22 of the spirit that deceived Ahab's prophets.
[^13]: Hebrew *ma'al*, a breach of faith, especially against God.
[^14]: Literally "break its staff of bread" (4:16; Leviticus 26:26).
[^15]: The Hebrew text is written *Dan'el* but read *Daniyyel*, "Daniel"; also in verse 20 and 28:3. Dan'el is also the name of a righteous king in an ancient Canaanite story found at Ugarit, and some think he is meant here rather than the Daniel of the book of Daniel.
[^16]: Literally "their *nephesh*". Also in verse 20.
