**1** As for you, son of man,[^1] prophesy to the mountains of Israel and say: Mountains of Israel, hear the word of Yahweh.[^2]

**2** This is what the Lord Yahweh[^3] says: Because the enemy has said of you, "Aha! The ancient heights[^4] have become our possession,"

**3** therefore prophesy and say: This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Because, yes, because they made you desolate and crushed you from every side, so that you became the possession of the rest of the nations, and you have become the talk of gossips and the slander of the people,[^5]

**4** therefore, mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord Yahweh. This is what the Lord Yahweh says to the mountains and the hills, to the ravines and the valleys, to the desolate ruins and the abandoned cities that have become plunder and a mockery to the rest of the nations all around,

**5** therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: Surely in the fire of my jealousy I have spoken against the rest of the nations and against all Edom, who with wholehearted joy and utter contempt took my land as their own possession, so that its pastureland would be plunder.[^6]

**6** Therefore prophesy concerning the land of Israel, and say to the mountains and the hills, to the ravines and the valleys: This is what the Lord Yahweh says: I am speaking in my jealousy and my wrath, because you have borne the scorn of the nations.

**7** Therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: I swear[^7] that the nations around you will themselves bear their scorn.

**8** But you, mountains of Israel, will put out your branches and bear your fruit for my people Israel, for they will soon come home.

**9** For look, I am for you; I will turn to you, and you will be plowed and sown.

**10** I will multiply people on you, the whole house of Israel, all of it. The cities will be inhabited and the ruins rebuilt.

**11** I will multiply human beings and animals on you, and they will increase and be fruitful. I will settle you as you were in former times and do you more good than at the beginning. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.

**12** I will make people walk on you, my people Israel. They will possess you, and you will be their inheritance, and you will never again rob them of their children.

**13** This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Because people say to you, "You devour people, and you rob your nations[^8] of their children,"

**14** therefore you will no longer devour people or rob[^9] your nations of their children, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**15** I will no longer let you hear the scorn of the nations, and you will no longer bear the reproach of the peoples or make your nations stumble, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**16** The word of Yahweh came to me:

**17** Son of man, when the house of Israel lived on their own soil, they defiled it by their ways and their deeds. Their way before me was like the impurity of a woman in her period.[^10]

**18** So I poured out my wrath on them for the blood they had shed on the land, and because they had defiled it with their idols.[^11]

**19** I scattered them among the nations, and they were dispersed among the countries. I judged them according to their ways and their deeds.

**20** But when they came to the nations, wherever they went, they profaned my holy name, because it was said of them, "These are the people of Yahweh, and yet they had to leave his land."

**21** I was concerned for my holy name, which the house of Israel had profaned among the nations where they went.

**22** Therefore say to the house of Israel: This is what the Lord Yahweh says: It is not for your sake that I am about to act, house of Israel, but for the sake of my holy name, which you have profaned among the nations where you went.

**23** I will show the holiness of my great name, which has been profaned among the nations, which you have profaned among them. Then the nations will know that I am Yahweh, declares the Lord Yahweh, when I show myself holy through you before their eyes.[^12]

**24** I will take you from the nations and gather you from all the countries, and I will bring you into your own land.

**25** I will sprinkle clean water on you, and you will be clean. From all your impurities and from all your idols I will cleanse you.

**26** I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit within you. I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh.

**27** I will put my Spirit[^13] within you and make you walk in my decrees, and you will keep my rulings and carry them out.

**28** You will live in the land I gave your ancestors. You will be my people, and I will be your God.

**29** I will save you from all your impurities. I will summon the grain and make it plentiful, and I will not bring famine on you.

**30** I will multiply the fruit of the trees and the produce of the field, so that you will never again suffer the disgrace of famine among the nations.

**31** Then you will remember your evil ways and your deeds that were not good, and you will loathe yourselves[^14] for your guilt and your detestable practices.

**32** It is not for your sake that I am acting, declares the Lord Yahweh; let that be known to you. Be ashamed and humiliated because of your ways, house of Israel.

**33** This is what the Lord Yahweh says: On the day I cleanse you from all your guilt, I will resettle the cities, and the ruins will be rebuilt.

**34** The desolate land will be farmed, instead of lying desolate in the sight of all who pass by.

**35** They will say, "This land that was desolate has become like the garden of Eden, and the cities that were ruined, desolate and torn down are fortified and inhabited."

**36** Then the nations that are left around you will know that I, Yahweh, have rebuilt what was torn down and replanted what was desolate. I, Yahweh, have spoken, and I will do it.

**37** This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Once more I will let the house of Israel ask me[^15] to do this for them: I will multiply their people like a flock.

**38** Like the flock for sacrifices,[^16] like the flock of Jerusalem at her appointed festivals, so the ruined cities will be filled with flocks of people. Then they will know that I am Yahweh.

[^1]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^4]: Hebrew *bamot*, "heights", the word used elsewhere for the high places of worship (6:3).
[^5]: Literally "you have been taken up on the lip of the tongue and the whispering of the people".
[^6]: The meaning of the last words is uncertain. Literally "for the sake of its pastureland for plunder", or "to drive it out as plunder".
[^7]: Literally "I have lifted my hand", a gesture of swearing an oath (20:5–6).
[^8]: The Hebrew text is written "your nation" but read "your nations". Also in verses 14 and 15.
[^9]: The Hebrew text is written "make … stumble" but read "rob … of their children".
[^10]: Hebrew *niddah*, the impurity of menstruation (Leviticus 15:19–24).
[^11]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a contemptuous word for idols, perhaps related to the word for dung. Also in verse 25.
[^12]: The oldest surviving manuscript of the Septuagint for Ezekiel, Papyrus 967 (about AD 200), does not have the rest of this verse after "I am Yahweh", or verses 24–38. It also places chapter 37 after chapters 38–39.
[^13]: Or "my spirit". Hebrew *ruach*, "spirit, wind, breath", as in verse 26.
[^14]: Literally "loathe your faces".
[^15]: Literally "I will let myself be sought by the house of Israel".
[^16]: Literally "the flock of holy things": the animals brought to Jerusalem for sacrifice at the festivals.
