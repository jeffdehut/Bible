**1** In the seventh year, in the fifth month, on the tenth day of the month,[^1] some of the elders of Israel came to consult the LORD,[^2] and they sat down in front of me.

**2** Then the word of the LORD came to me:

**3** Son of man,[^3] speak to the elders of Israel and say to them: This is what the Lord GOD[^4] says: Have you come to consult me? As I live, I will not let myself be consulted by you, declares the Lord GOD.

**4** Will you judge them? Will you judge them, son of man? Make known to them the detestable practices[^5] of their ancestors,

**5** and say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: On the day I chose Israel, I raised my hand[^6] to the descendants of the house of Jacob and made myself known to them in the land of Egypt. I raised my hand to them and said, "I am the LORD your God."

**6** On that day I raised my hand to them, swearing that I would bring them out of the land of Egypt into a land I had searched out for them, a land flowing with milk and honey, the most beautiful of all lands.[^7]

**7** And I said to them, "Each of you, throw away the vile idols[^8] you set your eyes on, and do not defile yourselves with the idols[^9] of Egypt. I am the LORD your God."

**8** But they rebelled against me and were not willing to listen to me. None of them threw away the vile idols they set their eyes on, nor did they abandon the idols of Egypt. Then I said I would pour out my fury on them and spend my anger against them in the middle of the land of Egypt.

**9** But I acted for the sake of my name, so that it would not be profaned in the sight of the nations among whom they lived, in whose sight I had made myself known to them by bringing them out of the land of Egypt.

**10** So I brought them out of the land of Egypt and led them into the wilderness.

**11** I gave them my decrees and made known to them my rulings, by which a person who does them will live.[^10]

**12** I also gave them my Sabbaths, to be a sign between me and them, so that they might know that I am the LORD who makes them holy.

**13** But the house of Israel rebelled against me in the wilderness. They did not walk in my decrees, and they rejected my rulings, by which a person who does them will live, and they greatly profaned my Sabbaths. Then I said I would pour out my fury on them in the wilderness to make an end of them.

**14** But I acted for the sake of my name, so that it would not be profaned in the sight of the nations in whose sight I had brought them out.

**15** I also raised my hand to them in the wilderness, swearing that I would not bring them into the land I had given them, a land flowing with milk and honey, the most beautiful of all lands,

**16** because they rejected my rulings and did not walk in my decrees, and they profaned my Sabbaths, for their hearts went after their idols.

**17** Yet my eye spared them, and I did not destroy them; I did not make an end of them in the wilderness.

**18** I said to their children in the wilderness, "Do not walk in the decrees of your fathers or keep their rulings, and do not defile yourselves with their idols.

**19** I am the LORD your God. Walk in my decrees, and keep my rulings and do them.

**20** Keep my Sabbaths holy, and they will be a sign between me and you, so that you may know that I am the LORD your God."

**21** But the children rebelled against me. They did not walk in my decrees or keep my rulings by doing them, by which a person who does them will live, and they profaned my Sabbaths. Then I said I would pour out my fury on them and spend my anger against them in the wilderness.

**22** But I held back my hand and acted for the sake of my name, so that it would not be profaned in the sight of the nations in whose sight I had brought them out.

**23** Moreover, I raised my hand to them in the wilderness, swearing that I would scatter them among the nations and disperse them through the countries,

**24** because they had not carried out my rulings, but had rejected my decrees and profaned my Sabbaths, and their eyes were set on the idols of their fathers.

**25** So I also gave them decrees that were not good and rulings by which they could not live.

**26** I made them unclean through their gifts, when they offered up every firstborn,[^11] so that I might devastate them, so that they would know that I am the LORD.

**27** Therefore, son of man, speak to the house of Israel and say to them: This is what the Lord GOD says: In this also your ancestors blasphemed me, by acting unfaithfully against me.

**28** When I brought them into the land that I had raised my hand to give them, wherever they saw any high hill or any leafy tree, there they offered their sacrifices, there they presented the offerings that provoked me,[^12] there they offered their pleasing aromas,[^13] and there they poured out their drink offerings.

**29** So I said to them, "What is this high place that you go to?" And it is called Bamah to this day.[^14]

**30** Therefore say to the house of Israel: This is what the Lord GOD says: Will you defile yourselves in the way your ancestors did, and play the prostitute after their vile idols?

**31** When you offer your gifts, making your sons pass through the fire, you defile yourselves with all your idols to this day. So should I let myself be consulted by you, house of Israel? As I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will not let myself be consulted by you.

**32** What you have in mind[^15] will never happen, when you say, "We will be like the nations, like the clans of the lands, serving wood and stone."

**33** As I live, declares the Lord GOD, with a strong hand and an outstretched arm and with fury poured out, I will be king over you.

**34** I will bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the countries where you have been scattered, with a strong hand and an outstretched arm and with fury poured out.

**35** I will bring you into the wilderness of the peoples, and there I will enter into judgment with you face to face.

**36** As I entered into judgment with your ancestors in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so I will enter into judgment with you, declares the Lord GOD.

**37** I will make you pass under the rod,[^16] and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant.[^17]

**38** I will purge from among you those who rebel and transgress against me. I will bring them out of the land where they live as foreigners, but they will not enter the land of Israel. Then you will know that I am the LORD.

**39** As for you, house of Israel, this is what the Lord GOD says: Go, each of you, serve your idols, now and hereafter, if you will not listen to me; but you will no longer profane my holy name with your gifts and your idols.[^18]

**40** For on my holy mountain, on the high mountain of Israel, declares the Lord GOD, there the whole house of Israel, all of them, will serve me in the land. There I will accept them, and there I will require your contributions[^19] and the choicest of your gifts, with all your holy offerings.

**41** I will accept you as a pleasing aroma, when I bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the countries where you have been scattered, and I will show myself holy among you in the sight of the nations.

**42** Then you will know that I am the LORD, when I bring you into the land of Israel, the land I raised my hand to give to your ancestors.

**43** There you will remember your ways and all your deeds by which you defiled yourselves, and you will loathe yourselves[^20] for all the evil you have done.

**44** Then you will know that I am the LORD, when I deal with you for the sake of my name, not according to your evil ways or your corrupt deeds, house of Israel, declares the Lord GOD.[^21]

[^1]: In 591 BC, eleven months after the date in 8:1.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^4]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^5]: Hebrew *to'evot*, things that are abhorrent, especially in worship.
[^6]: Literally "I lifted my hand", the gesture of one who swears an oath (Exodus 6:8). Also in verses 6, 15, 23, 28 and 42.
[^7]: Literally "it is the beauty of all lands". Also in verse 15.
[^8]: Literally "the detestable things of his eyes". Hebrew *shiqqutsim*, "detestable things", a contemptuous word for idols. Also in verses 8 and 30.
[^9]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a contemptuous word for idols, perhaps related to the word for dung. Also in verses 8, 16, 18, 24, 31 and 39.
[^10]: Leviticus 18:5. Also in verses 13 and 21.
[^11]: Literally "in making every first opening of the womb pass over", the phrase used for child sacrifice (16:21; verse 31). Exodus 13:12 uses the same words for giving every firstborn to the LORD.
[^12]: Literally "the provocation of their offering".
[^13]: Literally "a soothing smell", the phrase used for offerings to the LORD (6:13). Also in verse 41.
[^14]: A play on words: *mah*, "what", *bamah*, "high place", and *ba'im*, "go". Hebrew *bamah*: a place of worship, often on a hilltop.
[^15]: Literally "what comes up in your spirit".
[^16]: As a shepherd counts his sheep, letting them pass one by one under his staff (Leviticus 27:32).
[^17]: Hebrew *masoret*, a word found only here, probably "bond" or "obligation".
[^18]: The Hebrew of this sentence is difficult. Literally "Go, each serve his idols, and afterward, if you are not listening to me, and my holy name you will not profane anymore with your gifts and with your idols".
[^19]: Hebrew *terumot*, "contributions": gifts set apart for a sacred purpose (Exodus 25:2).
[^20]: Literally "loathe your faces".
[^21]: In most English Bibles chapter 20 has five more verses, 20:45–49, which are 21:1–5 in the Hebrew.
