**1** The word of the LORD[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] prophesy and say: This is what the Lord GOD[^3] says: Wail, "Alas for the day!"

**3** For the day is near, the day of the LORD is near. It will be a day of clouds, a time of doom for the nations.[^4]

**4** A sword will come against Egypt, and anguish will be in Cush,[^5] when the slain fall in Egypt, and they carry off her wealth,[^6] and her foundations are torn down.

**5** Cush, Put and Lud,[^7] all the mixed peoples,[^8] Kub,[^9] and the people of the land of the covenant[^10] will fall with them by the sword.

**6** This is what the LORD says: Those who support Egypt will fall, and her proud strength will come down. From Migdol to Syene[^11] they will fall in her by the sword, declares the Lord GOD.

**7** They will be desolate among desolate lands, and her cities will lie among ruined cities.

**8** Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I set fire to Egypt and all her helpers are crushed.

**9** On that day messengers will go out from me in ships to terrify Cush in its complacency. Anguish will come on them on the day of Egypt's doom,[^12] for look, it is coming!

**10** This is what the Lord GOD says: I will put an end to the hordes[^13] of Egypt by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar[^14] king of Babylon.

**11** He and his people with him, the most ruthless of the nations, will be brought in to destroy the land. They will draw their swords against Egypt and fill the land with the slain.

**12** I will make the streams[^15] dry land and sell the land into the hands of evil men. By the hands of foreigners I will lay waste the land and everything in it. I, the LORD, have spoken.

**13** This is what the Lord GOD says: I will destroy the idols[^16] and put an end to the worthless gods[^17] of Memphis.[^18] There will no longer be a prince from the land of Egypt, and I will put fear in the land of Egypt.

**14** I will make Pathros[^19] desolate, set fire to Zoan[^20] and carry out judgments on Thebes.[^21]

**15** I will pour out my wrath on Sin,[^22] the stronghold of Egypt, and cut off the hordes of Thebes.[^23]

**16** I will set fire to Egypt. Sin will writhe in anguish, Thebes will be breached, and Memphis will face enemies by day.[^24]

**17** The young men of Aven and Pi-beseth[^25] will fall by the sword, and the cities themselves will go into captivity.[^26]

**18** In Tehaphnehes[^27] the day will grow dark[^28] when I break the yokes of Egypt there, and her proud strength comes to an end in her. A cloud will cover her, and her daughters[^29] will go into captivity.

**19** So I will carry out judgments on Egypt. Then they will know that I am the LORD.

**20** In the eleventh year, in the first month, on the seventh day of the month,[^30] the word of the LORD came to me:

**21** Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt. Look, it has not been bound up for healing; no bandage has been put on it to bind it and make it strong enough to grasp the sword.

**22** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am against Pharaoh king of Egypt. I will break his arms, both the strong one and the broken one, and make the sword fall from his hand.

**23** I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them among the countries.

**24** I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon and put my sword in his hand, but I will break the arms of Pharaoh, and he will groan before him with the groans of a man mortally wounded.

**25** I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, but the arms of Pharaoh will drop. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I put my sword into the hand of the king of Babylon and he stretches it out against the land of Egypt.

**26** I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them among the countries. Then they will know that I am the LORD.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Literally "a time of nations".
[^5]: The region south of Egypt (ancient Nubia). Also in verses 5 and 9.
[^6]: Or "her multitude". Hebrew *hamon*, as in 29:19.
[^7]: *Put* was probably Libya, and *Lud* probably Lydia, in western Asia Minor (27:10).
[^8]: Or "all Arabia", reading the same consonants as *arav*.
[^9]: Hebrew *Kuv*, a place not otherwise known.
[^10]: Literally "the sons of the land of the covenant": perhaps Egypt's allies, or Judeans living in Egypt.
[^11]: From the north of Egypt to its southern frontier (29:10).
[^12]: Literally "on the day of Egypt".
[^13]: Or "the wealth". Hebrew *hamon*, as in verse 4.
[^14]: Hebrew *Nevukhadre'tsar*, a spelling of Nebuchadnezzar closer to the Babylonian name (29:18).
[^15]: Hebrew *ye'orim*, the branches and canals of the Nile (29:3).
[^16]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a contemptuous word for idols, perhaps related to the word for dung. Ezekiel uses it often.
[^17]: Hebrew *elilim*, "worthless things, nothings", a word that sounds like *elohim*, "gods".
[^18]: Hebrew *Nof*, Memphis, the old capital of Lower Egypt, south of the Nile Delta. Also in verse 16.
[^19]: Southern (Upper) Egypt.
[^20]: Tanis, a city in the northeastern Nile Delta.
[^21]: Hebrew *No*, Thebes, the great city of Upper Egypt. Also in verses 15 and 16.
[^22]: Probably Pelusium, a fortress on the eastern edge of the Nile Delta, guarding the road from Canaan. Also in verse 16.
[^23]: The Hebrew word *hamon*, "hordes", sounds like *Amon*, the chief god of Thebes (Jeremiah 46:25).
[^24]: Literally "and Nof, enemies of daytime". The meaning is uncertain.
[^25]: *Aven*, "wickedness", is a play on *On*, the city of Heliopolis (Genesis 41:45). *Pi-beseth* was Bubastis, in the Nile Delta.
[^26]: Literally "and they will go into captivity". The Hebrew pronoun is feminine: the cities, or the women.
[^27]: Hebrew *Techafneches*, the city called Tahpanhes in Jeremiah 43:7–9, in the eastern Nile Delta.
[^28]: The Hebrew is pointed *chasakh*, "hold back": "the day will be withheld". The same consonants can be read *chashakh*, "grow dark".
[^29]: That is, the towns around her. Or "her women".
[^30]: In April 587 BC. The broken arm (verse 21) probably refers to the defeat of Pharaoh Hophra's army, which had marched out to relieve Jerusalem during the siege (Jeremiah 37:5–11).
