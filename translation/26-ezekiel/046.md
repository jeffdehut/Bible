**1** "'This is what the Lord Yahweh[^1] says: The gate of the inner court that faces east is to be shut on the six working days, but on the Sabbath day it is to be opened, and on the day of the new moon it is to be opened.

**2** The prince[^2] is to enter by way of the porch of the gate from outside and stand by the doorpost of the gate. The priests are to offer his burnt offering[^3] and his fellowship offerings,[^4] and he is to bow down at the threshold of the gate and then go out. But the gate is not to be shut until evening.

**3** The people of the land are to bow down at the entrance of that gate before Yahweh on the Sabbaths and the new moons.

**4** The burnt offering that the prince presents to Yahweh on the Sabbath day is to be six lambs without defect and a ram without defect.

**5** The grain offering[^5] is to be an ephah[^6] with the ram, and with the lambs as much as he wishes to give, and a hin of oil for each ephah.

**6** On the day of the new moon he is to offer a young bull[^7] without defect, and six lambs and a ram, which are to be without defect.

**7** As a grain offering he is to provide an ephah with the bull and an ephah with the ram, and with the lambs as much as he can afford, and a hin of oil for each ephah.

**8** When the prince enters, he is to come in by way of the porch of the gate and go out the same way.

**9** When the people of the land come before Yahweh at the appointed festivals, whoever enters by the north gate to worship is to go out by the south gate, and whoever enters by the south gate is to go out by the north gate. No one is to go back through the gate by which he came in; each is to go out straight ahead.

**10** The prince is to be among them: when they come in, he is to come in, and when they go out, they are to go out.

**11** At the festivals and the appointed times, the grain offering is to be an ephah with a bull and an ephah with a ram, and with the lambs as much as he wishes to give, and a hin of oil for each ephah.

**12** When the prince provides a freewill offering, a burnt offering or fellowship offerings as a freewill offering to Yahweh, the gate that faces east is to be opened for him, and he is to offer his burnt offering and his fellowship offerings as he does on the Sabbath day. Then he is to go out, and after he has gone out the gate is to be shut.

**13** You are to provide a year-old lamb without defect as a burnt offering to Yahweh every day. Morning by morning you are to provide it.

**14** With it, morning by morning, you are to provide a grain offering of a sixth of an ephah, with a third of a hin of oil to moisten the fine flour,[^8] a grain offering to Yahweh. These are permanent decrees, to be kept continually.

**15** So they are to provide the lamb, the grain offering and the oil morning by morning, as a regular burnt offering.

**16** This is what the Lord Yahweh says: If the prince gives a gift to one of his sons, it is that son's inheritance and will belong to his sons. It is their property by inheritance.

**17** But if he gives a gift from his inheritance to one of his servants, it will belong to the servant until the year of liberty,[^9] and then it will return to the prince. His inheritance belongs to his sons alone; it is theirs.

**18** The prince must not take any of the people's inheritance, driving them off their property. He is to give his sons their inheritance from his own property, so that none of my people will be driven from his property.'"

**19** Then he brought me through the entrance at the side of the gate to the holy rooms for the priests, which faced north. There I saw a place at the far end,[^10] on the west side.

**20** He said to me, "This is the place where the priests are to boil the guilt offering[^11] and the sin offering,[^12] and where they are to bake the grain offering, so that they do not bring them out into the outer court and make the people holy."[^13]

**21** Then he brought me out into the outer court and led me around to the four corners of the court, and in each corner of the court there was a court.

**22** In the four corners of the court were enclosed courts,[^14] forty long and thirty wide.[^15] The four corner courts had the same measurements.[^16]

**23** Around each of the four of them was a row of stonework, and hearths for cooking were built under the rows all around.

**24** He said to me, "These are the kitchens where those who serve the house[^17] will boil the sacrifices of the people."

[^1]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh". *Yahweh* is God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. The message begun in 44:6 continues to verse 18.
[^2]: Hebrew *nasi*, "leader, chief", the ruler of Israel (44:3).
[^3]: Hebrew *olah*: an animal burned whole on the altar (40:38; Leviticus 1).
[^4]: Hebrew *shelamim*, traditionally "peace offerings" (Leviticus 3). Also in verse 12.
[^5]: Hebrew *minchah*, "gift, tribute": an offering of flour or grain (Leviticus 2).
[^6]: An ephah was probably about 22 liters (two-thirds of a bushel), and a hin about a gallon (4 liters).
[^7]: Literally "a bull, a son of the herd".
[^8]: Hebrew *solet*, finely ground flour (Leviticus 2:1).
[^9]: The year of Jubilee, every fiftieth year, when land returned to the families that owned it (Leviticus 25:10–13).
[^10]: The Hebrew text is written "at their far end" but read "at the far end".
[^11]: Hebrew *asham*, perhaps "reparation offering" (40:39).
[^12]: Hebrew *chatta't*. Or "purification offering" (40:39).
[^13]: That is, pass on holiness to the people by contact (44:19).
[^14]: Hebrew *qeturot*, a word of uncertain meaning: perhaps "enclosed" or "small".
[^15]: The unit is not given; probably the long cubit of 40:5, so that each court was about 70 by 52 feet (21 by 16 m).
[^16]: In the Hebrew text, dots are written over the word for "corner", a scribal mark of doubt.
[^17]: That is, the temple, as throughout chapters 40–48.
