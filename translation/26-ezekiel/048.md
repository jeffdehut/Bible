**1** "These are the names of the tribes: At the northern end, beside the way of Hethlon to Lebo-hamath, as far as Hazar-enan, at the border of Damascus, with Hamath to the north beside it, and extending from the east side to the west side,[^1] Dan will have one portion.

**2** Next to the territory of Dan, from the east side to the west side, Asher will have one portion.

**3** Next to the territory of Asher, from the east side to the west side, Naphtali will have one portion.

**4** Next to the territory of Naphtali, from the east side to the west side, Manasseh will have one portion.

**5** Next to the territory of Manasseh, from the east side to the west side, Ephraim will have one portion.

**6** Next to the territory of Ephraim, from the east side to the west side, Reuben will have one portion.

**7** Next to the territory of Reuben, from the east side to the west side, Judah will have one portion.

**8** Next to the territory of Judah, from the east side to the west side, will be the contribution[^2] you are to set apart, 25,000 wide,[^3] and as long as one of the tribal portions, from the east side to the west side, with the sanctuary in the middle of it.

**9** The contribution you are to set apart for Yahweh[^4] will be 25,000 long and 10,000 wide.[^5]

**10** This will be the holy contribution for the priests: on the north side 25,000 long, on the west side 10,000 wide, on the east side 10,000 wide, and on the south side 25,000 long. The sanctuary of Yahweh will be in the middle of it.

**11** It will be for the consecrated priests, the sons of Zadok,[^6] who kept my charge and did not go astray when the Israelites went astray, as the Levites did.

**12** It will be a special portion for them from the holy contribution of the land, a most holy place, next to the territory of the Levites.

**13** Alongside the territory of the priests, the Levites will have an area 25,000 long and 10,000 wide. The whole length will be 25,000 and the width 10,000.

**14** They must not sell any of it or exchange it, and this choicest part of the land must not pass to others, for it is holy to Yahweh.

**15** The remaining area, 5,000 wide and 25,000 long, will be for the common use of the city, for houses and for open land. The city will be in the middle of it.

**16** These will be its measurements: the north side 4,500, the south side 4,500,[^7] the east side 4,500 and the west side 4,500.

**17** The city will have open land: 250 on the north, 250 on the south, 250 on the east and 250 on the west.

**18** The rest of the area, along the length beside the holy contribution, will be 10,000 on the east and 10,000 on the west. It will lie beside the holy contribution, and its produce will be food for the workers of the city.

**19** The workers of the city, from all the tribes of Israel, will farm it.

**20** The whole contribution will be 25,000 by 25,000. You are to set apart the holy contribution as a square, together with the city's property.

**21** What remains on both sides of the holy contribution and of the city's property will belong to the prince.[^8] It will extend eastward from the 25,000 of the contribution to the eastern border, and westward from the 25,000 to the western border, alongside the tribal portions. It will belong to the prince. The holy contribution and the sanctuary of the house[^9] will be in the middle of it.

**22** The property of the Levites and the property of the city will lie in the middle of what belongs to the prince; the land between the territory of Judah and the territory of Benjamin will belong to the prince.[^10]

**23** As for the rest of the tribes: from the east side to the west side, Benjamin will have one portion.

**24** Next to the territory of Benjamin, from the east side to the west side, Simeon will have one portion.

**25** Next to the territory of Simeon, from the east side to the west side, Issachar will have one portion.

**26** Next to the territory of Issachar, from the east side to the west side, Zebulun will have one portion.

**27** Next to the territory of Zebulun, from the east side to the west side, Gad will have one portion.

**28** Next to the territory of Gad, on the south side, toward the Negev, the boundary will run from Tamar to the waters of Meribath-kadesh, and along the Wadi to the Great Sea.[^11]

**29** This is the land you are to allot as an inheritance to the tribes of Israel, and these will be their portions, declares the Lord Yahweh.[^12]

**30** These are the exits of the city: On the north side, which measures 4,500,

**31** the gates of the city will be named after the tribes of Israel. There will be three gates on the north: one gate for Reuben, one gate for Judah and one gate for Levi.

**32** On the east side, which measures 4,500, there will be three gates: one gate for Joseph, one gate for Benjamin and one gate for Dan.

**33** On the south side, which measures 4,500, there will be three gates: one gate for Simeon, one gate for Issachar and one gate for Zebulun.

**34** On the west side, which measures 4,500, there will be three gates: one gate for Gad, one gate for Asher and one gate for Naphtali.

**35** The distance all around will be 18,000.[^13] And from that day on the name of the city will be 'Yahweh Is There.'"[^14]

[^1]: Literally "and they will be his, the east side, the sea". Each tribe's portion is a strip running across the land from east to west, from the Jordan to the Mediterranean (47:15–20). Compare Joshua 13–19, where the tribes receive very different territories.
[^2]: Hebrew *terumah*: something set apart and given for a sacred purpose (45:1). Also in verses 9–10, 12, 18 and 20–21.
[^3]: The unit is not given; probably the long cubit of 40:5 (about 21 inches or 53 cm), as in 45:1, so that 25,000 was about 8 miles (13 km). The same is true of the other measurements in this chapter.
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^5]: This is perhaps the priests' portion (verses 10–12), which lies beside the Levites' portion of the same size (verse 13). Compare 45:1–5.
[^6]: See 40:46; 44:15.
[^7]: The Hebrew text writes the word "five" twice here, but it is read only once.
[^8]: Hebrew *nasi*, "leader, chief", the ruler of Israel (44:3; 45:7–8).
[^9]: That is, the temple, as throughout chapters 40–48.
[^10]: Literally "And from the property of the Levites and from the property of the city, in the middle of what is for the prince it will be; between the border of Judah and the border of Benjamin, it will be for the prince." The meaning is uncertain.
[^11]: See 47:19.
[^12]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^13]: About 6 miles (9.5 km), if the unit is the long cubit.
[^14]: Hebrew *YHWH shammah*.
