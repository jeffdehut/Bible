**1** The word of the LORD[^1] came to me:

**2** As for you, son of man,[^2] will you judge? Will you judge the city of bloodshed?[^3] Then make known to her all her detestable practices.[^4]

**3** Say: This is what the Lord GOD[^5] says: A city that sheds blood in her midst, so that her time comes, and that makes idols[^6] for herself, to defile herself!

**4** You have become guilty by the blood you have shed, and you have defiled yourself with the idols you have made. You have brought your days near, and you have come to the end of your years.[^7] Therefore I have made you a disgrace to the nations and an object of mockery to all the countries.

**5** Those who are near and those who are far from you will mock you, you with a defiled name, full of turmoil.

**6** Look, the princes of Israel in you, each using his power,[^8] have been intent on shedding blood.

**7** In you father and mother are treated with contempt; in your midst the foreigner is oppressed; in you the fatherless and the widow are wronged.

**8** You have despised my holy things and profaned my Sabbaths.

**9** In you are slanderers intent on shedding blood; in you are those who eat on the mountains;[^9] in your midst they commit lewdness.[^10]

**10** In you they uncover their fathers' nakedness;[^11] in you they violate women who are unclean in their menstruation.[^12]

**11** One man commits a detestable act with his neighbor's wife; another lewdly defiles his daughter-in-law; and another in you violates his sister, his father's daughter.

**12** In you they take bribes to shed blood. You take interest and profit,[^13] and you make gain from your neighbors by extortion. And you have forgotten me, declares the Lord GOD.

**13** Look, I strike my hands together[^14] at the dishonest gain you have made and at the blood that is in your midst.

**14** Can your heart endure, or can your hands be strong, in the days when I deal with you? I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it.[^15]

**15** I will disperse you among the nations and scatter you through the countries, and I will put an end to your uncleanness.

**16** You will be profaned through your own doing[^16] in the sight of the nations, and you will know that I am the LORD.

**17** The word of the LORD came to me:

**18** Son of man, the house of Israel has become dross to me. All of them are copper,[^17] tin, iron and lead in the furnace; they have become the dross of silver.

**19** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because all of you have become dross, look, I am going to gather you into the middle of Jerusalem.

**20** As silver, copper, iron, lead and tin are gathered into the middle of a furnace to blow fire on them and melt them, so I will gather you in my anger and in my fury, and I will put you there and melt you.

**21** I will gather you and blow on you with the fire of my wrath, and you will be melted in it.

**22** As silver is melted in a furnace, so you will be melted in it. Then you will know that I, the LORD, have poured out my fury on you.

**23** The word of the LORD came to me:

**24** Son of man, say to her: You are a land that has not been cleansed, that has had no rain[^18] on the day of indignation.

**25** There is a conspiracy of her prophets[^19] in her midst, like a roaring lion tearing its prey. They devour people,[^20] they take treasure and precious things, they make many widows in her midst.

**26** Her priests do violence to my instruction[^21] and profane my holy things. They make no distinction between the holy and the common, and they do not teach the difference between the unclean and the clean. They shut their eyes to my Sabbaths, and I am profaned among them.

**27** Her officials within her are like wolves tearing their prey, shedding blood and destroying lives to get dishonest gain.

**28** Her prophets cover over these deeds with whitewash for them,[^22] seeing false visions and divining lies for them, saying, "This is what the Lord GOD says," when the LORD has not spoken.

**29** The people of the land[^23] practice extortion and commit robbery. They oppress the poor and needy and extort from the foreigner without justice.

**30** I looked for a man among them who would build up the wall and stand in the breach before me for the land, so that I would not destroy it, but I found no one.

**31** So I have poured out my indignation on them; I have consumed them with the fire of my wrath. I have brought their ways down on their heads, declares the Lord GOD.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Literally "the city of bloods" (24:6, 9).
[^4]: Hebrew *to'evot*, things that are abhorrent, especially in worship. Also in verse 11.
[^5]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^6]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a contemptuous word for idols, perhaps related to the word for dung. Also in verse 4.
[^7]: Literally "you have come to your years".
[^8]: Literally "each according to his arm".
[^9]: That is, those who eat sacrificial meals at the shrines on the hills (18:6).
[^10]: Hebrew *zimmah*, shameful, lewd behavior (16:27). Also in verse 11.
[^11]: That is, they have sexual relations with their father's wife (Leviticus 18:8; 20:11).
[^12]: Literally "the one unclean in *niddah*" (18:6; Leviticus 18:19).
[^13]: Hebrew *neshekh* and *tarbit* (18:8).
[^14]: Literally "I have struck my palm", a gesture of anger or scorn (21:19, 22).
[^15]: Literally "and I have done it".
[^16]: Literally "you will be profaned in yourself". The meaning is uncertain.
[^17]: Or "bronze". Hebrew *nechoshet*. Also in verse 20.
[^18]: Hebrew *gushmah*, an unusual form, probably "rained on".
[^19]: The Septuagint reads "her princes", which differs by one letter in Hebrew (*nesi'eha* for *nevi'eha*).
[^20]: Literally "They devour *nephesh*".
[^21]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law".
[^22]: Literally "plaster whitewash for them". See 13:10–16.
[^23]: Hebrew *am ha-arets*, the landowning citizens of Judah (7:27).
