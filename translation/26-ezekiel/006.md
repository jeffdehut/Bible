**1** The word of Yahweh[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] set your face toward the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them.

**3** Say: Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord Yahweh.[^3] This is what the Lord Yahweh says to the mountains and the hills, to the ravines and the valleys: Look, I myself am bringing a sword against you, and I will destroy your high places.[^4]

**4** Your altars will be made desolate, and your incense altars[^5] will be smashed. I will make your slain fall in front of your idols.[^6]

**5** I will lay the corpses of the Israelites in front of their idols, and I will scatter your bones around your altars.

**6** Wherever you live, the cities will be laid waste and the high places made desolate, so that your altars will be laid waste and held guilty,[^7] your idols smashed and brought to an end, your incense altars cut down, and your works wiped out.

**7** The slain will fall among you, and you will know that I am Yahweh.[^8]

**8** But I will leave some of you alive: some of you will escape the sword among the nations, when you are scattered through the countries.

**9** Then those of you who escape will remember me among the nations where they have been taken captive, how I was broken[^9] by their heart, which played the prostitute[^10] and turned away from me, and by their eyes, which played the prostitute after their idols. They will loathe themselves[^11] for the evil they have done, for all their detestable practices.[^12]

**10** And they will know that I am Yahweh. It was not for nothing that I said I would bring this disaster on them.

**11** This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Clap your hands and stamp your feet, and say "Alas!" over all the evil, detestable practices of the house of Israel, for they will fall by the sword, by famine and by plague.

**12** Whoever is far away will die of plague, whoever is near will fall by the sword, and whoever is left and spared[^13] will die of famine. So I will spend my fury on them.

**13** And you will know that I am Yahweh, when their slain lie among their idols around their altars, on every high hill, on all the mountaintops, under every green tree and under every leafy oak, the places where they offered a pleasing aroma[^14] to all their idols.

**14** I will stretch out my hand against them and make the land a desolate waste, from the wilderness to Diblah,[^15] wherever they live. Then they will know that I am Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^4]: Hebrew *bamot*: places of worship, often on hilltops.
[^5]: Hebrew *chammanim*, probably small altars for burning incense. Also in verse 6.
[^6]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a contemptuous word for idols, perhaps related to the word for dung. Also in verses 5–6, 9 and 13.
[^7]: Or "made desolate". The Hebrew form usually means "be held guilty".
[^8]: A formula that recurs throughout Ezekiel: what Yahweh does, in judgment and in rescue, will make people recognize who he is.
[^9]: Literally "I was broken". Some scholars read *shavarti*, "I broke (their heart)", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^10]: Hebrew *zanah*, "act as a prostitute", a common image for unfaithfulness to Yahweh (chapters 16 and 23).
[^11]: Literally "loathe their faces".
[^12]: Hebrew *to'evot*, things that are abhorrent, especially in worship. Also in verse 11.
[^13]: Or "besieged". Hebrew *natsur*, "kept, guarded".
[^14]: Literally "a soothing smell", the phrase used for offerings to Yahweh (Leviticus 1:9).
[^15]: Or "more desolate than the wilderness toward Diblah". Diblah is otherwise unknown. Some Hebrew manuscripts read "Riblah", which differs by one similar letter: a town in Syria, north of the land of Israel (2 Kings 25:6, 20–21).
