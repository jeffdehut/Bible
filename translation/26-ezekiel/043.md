**1** Then he led me to the gate, the gate that faces east.

**2** And there was the glory[^1] of the God of Israel, coming from the east. Its sound was like the sound of many waters, and the earth shone with his glory.

**3** The vision I saw was like the vision I had seen when I came to destroy the city,[^2] and the visions were like the vision I had seen by the river Chebar.[^3] And I fell on my face.

**4** The glory of Yahweh[^4] came into the house[^5] by the gate that faces east.

**5** Then the Spirit[^6] lifted me up and brought me into the inner court, and there the glory of Yahweh filled the house.

**6** I heard someone speaking to me from the house, while a man was standing beside me.

**7** He said to me, "Son of man,[^7] this is the place of my throne and the place of the soles of my feet, where I will live among the Israelites forever. The house of Israel will no longer defile my holy name, neither they nor their kings, by their prostitution[^8] and by the corpses[^9] of their kings at their death.[^10]

**8** When they set their threshold by my threshold and their doorpost beside my doorpost, with only a wall between me and them,[^11] they defiled my holy name by the detestable practices they committed. So I consumed them in my anger.

**9** Now let them put their prostitution and the corpses of their kings far away from me, and I will live among them forever.

**10** As for you, son of man, describe the house to the house of Israel, so that they will be ashamed of their guilt. Let them measure its plan.[^12]

**11** If they are ashamed of all they have done, make known to them the design of the house, its arrangement, its exits and its entrances, all its forms and all its decrees, all its forms and all its laws.[^13] Write it down before their eyes, so that they may keep its whole design and all its decrees and carry them out.

**12** This is the law of the house: on the top of the mountain, the whole area all around will be most holy. Yes, this is the law of the house."

**13** These are the measurements of the altar in cubits, the cubit being a cubit and a handbreadth:[^14] its base is a cubit deep and a cubit wide, with a rim of one span[^15] around its edge. And this is the height[^16] of the altar:

**14** From the base on the ground to the lower ledge it is two cubits, and the width is one cubit; and from the smaller ledge to the larger ledge it is four cubits, and the width is one cubit.

**15** The altar hearth[^17] is four cubits high, and from the hearth four horns[^18] project upward.

**16** The altar hearth is square, twelve cubits long and twelve wide, square on its four sides.

**17** The ledge is square, fourteen cubits long and fourteen wide on its four sides, with a rim around it of half a cubit, and its base is a cubit all around. Its steps face east.

**18** Then he said to me, "Son of man, this is what the Lord Yahweh[^19] says: These are the decrees for the altar on the day it is made, for offering burnt offerings[^20] on it and for splashing blood against it.

**19** You are to give a young bull[^21] as a sin offering[^22] to the Levitical priests who are descendants of Zadok,[^23] who come near to me to serve me, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**20** You are to take some of its blood and put it on the four horns of the altar, on the four corners of the ledge and on the rim all around. So you will purify it from sin[^24] and make atonement for it.[^25]

**21** You are to take the bull of the sin offering and burn it in the appointed place of the house, outside the sanctuary.

**22** On the second day you are to offer a male goat without defect as a sin offering, and they are to purify the altar as they purified it with the bull.

**23** When you have finished purifying it, you are to offer a young bull without defect and a ram from the flock without defect.

**24** You are to present them before Yahweh, and the priests are to throw salt[^26] on them and offer them up as a burnt offering to Yahweh.

**25** For seven days you are to provide a goat for a sin offering each day. A young bull and a ram from the flock, without defect, are also to be provided.

**26** For seven days they are to make atonement for the altar and cleanse it, and so consecrate it.[^27]

**27** When they have completed these days, from the eighth day on, the priests are to offer your burnt offerings and your fellowship offerings[^28] on the altar, and I will accept you, declares the Lord Yahweh."

[^1]: Hebrew *kavod*, the visible splendor of God's presence (1:28). Ezekiel had seen it leave the temple by the east gate and stop on the mountain east of the city (10:18–19; 11:22–23).
[^2]: That is, when Ezekiel was shown in his vision the destruction of the city (chapters 8–11).
[^3]: See 1:1–3.
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^5]: That is, the temple, as throughout chapters 40–48.
[^6]: Or "a spirit" or "a wind". Hebrew *ruach*.
[^7]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^8]: Hebrew *zenut*, "prostitution", an image for unfaithfulness to Yahweh (6:9). Also in verse 9.
[^9]: Or "the memorials". Hebrew *pigre*, usually "corpses". Also in verse 9.
[^10]: The Hebrew is pointed *bamotam*, "their high places". The same consonants can be read *bemotam*, "at their death".
[^11]: The royal palace was built beside the temple, so that only a wall separated them (1 Kings 7:1–12).
[^12]: Hebrew *tokhnit*, "measurement, pattern" (28:12).
[^13]: The Hebrew text is written "all its law" but read "all its laws".
[^14]: The long cubit of 40:5, about 21 inches (53 cm).
[^15]: Hebrew *zeret*, the span of the hand, half a cubit: here about 10½ inches (27 cm).
[^16]: Hebrew *gav*, "back", here perhaps the base or the height. The meaning is uncertain.
[^17]: Hebrew *har'el* here and *ari'el* later in this verse and in verse 16: probably the hearth on top of the altar, where the fire burned. *Har'el* sounds like "mountain of God", and *ari'el* like "lion of God" (compare Isaiah 29:1).
[^18]: The projections at the four corners of the altar (Exodus 27:2).
[^19]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^20]: Hebrew *olah*: an animal burned whole on the altar (40:38; Leviticus 1).
[^21]: Literally "a bull, a son of the herd". Also in verses 23 and 25.
[^22]: Hebrew *chatta't*. Or "purification offering" (40:39). Also in verses 21, 22 and 25.
[^23]: See 40:46.
[^24]: Hebrew *chitte'*, from the same root as *chatta't*, "sin offering". Also in verses 22 and 23.
[^25]: Hebrew *kipper*, traditionally "make atonement". The word probably means "wipe clean, purge" (Leviticus 16:16–19). Also in verse 26.
[^26]: Salt was added to offerings (Leviticus 2:13).
[^27]: Literally "fill its hands", an idiom for consecrating (Exodus 28:41). The Hebrew text is written "its hand" but read "its hands".
[^28]: Hebrew *shelamim*, traditionally "peace offerings" (Leviticus 3).
