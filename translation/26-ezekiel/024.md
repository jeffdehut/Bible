**1** In the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month,[^1] the word of Yahweh[^2] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^3] write down the name of the day, this very day. The king of Babylon has laid siege to[^4] Jerusalem this very day.

**3** Tell the rebellious house a parable and say to them: This is what the Lord Yahweh[^5] says: Put on the pot, put it on, and pour water into it.

**4** Put into it the pieces of meat, all the good pieces, the thigh and the shoulder. Fill it with the choicest bones.

**5** Take the choicest of the flock, and pile the bones under it.[^6] Bring it to a rolling boil, and let its bones cook in it.

**6** Therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: Woe to the city of bloodshed,[^7] the pot whose rust[^8] is in it, whose rust has not gone out of it! Empty it piece by piece; no lot has fallen on it.[^9]

**7** For the blood she shed is in her midst. She put it on a bare rock; she did not pour it out on the ground to cover it with dust.[^10]

**8** To stir up fury and take vengeance, I have put her blood on a bare rock, so that it will not be covered.

**9** Therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: Woe to the city of bloodshed! I too will make the pile of wood great.

**10** Heap on the logs, kindle the fire, cook the meat well, mix in the spices,[^11] and let the bones be charred.

**11** Then set it empty on its coals, so that it becomes hot and its copper glows, so that its uncleanness may melt in it and its rust be consumed.

**12** It has worn me out with toil,[^12] yet its thick rust does not go out of it. Into the fire with its rust!

**13** Your uncleanness is lewdness.[^13] Because I tried to cleanse you, but you would not be cleansed from your uncleanness, you will not be clean again until I have satisfied my fury on you.[^14]

**14** I, Yahweh, have spoken. It is coming, and I will do it. I will not hold back, I will not spare, and I will not relent. According to your ways and your deeds they will judge you, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**15** The word of Yahweh came to me:

**16** Son of man, look, I am about to take from you the delight of your eyes with a sudden blow,[^15] but you must not mourn or weep or shed any tears.

**17** Groan in silence; do not perform mourning rites for the dead. Bind on your turban, put your sandals on your feet, do not cover your upper lip, and do not eat the bread of others.[^16]

**18** So I spoke to the people in the morning, and in the evening my wife died. The next morning I did as I was commanded.

**19** The people said to me, "Will you not tell us what these things you are doing mean for us?"

**20** So I said to them, "The word of Yahweh came to me:

**21** Say to the house of Israel: This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Look, I am about to profane my sanctuary, the pride of your strength, the delight of your eyes and the longing of your heart.[^17] The sons and daughters you left behind will fall by the sword.

**22** And you will do as I have done. You will not cover your upper lip or eat the bread of others.

**23** Your turbans will be on your heads and your sandals on your feet. You will not mourn or weep, but you will waste away because of your guilt and groan to one another.

**24** So Ezekiel will be a sign[^18] for you; you will do just as he has done. When this comes, you will know that I am the Lord Yahweh."

**25** As for you, son of man, on the day I take from them their stronghold, their joy and glory, the delight of their eyes and their heart's desire,[^19] their sons and their daughters,

**26** on that day a survivor will come to you to bring the news.[^20]

**27** On that day your mouth will be opened to the survivor; you will speak and no longer be silent.[^21] So you will be a sign for them, and they will know that I am Yahweh.

[^1]: Probably in January 588 BC. 2 Kings 25:1 and Jeremiah 52:4 give the same date for the beginning of the Babylonian siege of Jerusalem.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^4]: Literally "has leaned on".
[^5]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^6]: Literally "and also a pile of the bones under it". Some scholars read "a pile of wood" (compare verse 10), with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^7]: Literally "the city of bloods" (22:2). Also in verse 9.
[^8]: Hebrew *chel'ah*, a word found only in this passage, probably the rust or crust of a copper pot. Also in verses 11–12.
[^9]: That is, none is chosen to be spared; all are taken out.
[^10]: The law required that the blood of an animal killed for food be poured out and covered with dust (Leviticus 17:13).
[^11]: The meaning is uncertain. Others translate "boil away the broth".
[^12]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain.
[^13]: Hebrew *zimmah*, shameful, lewd behavior (16:27). Or "Because of your lewd uncleanness".
[^14]: Literally "made my fury rest on you". See 5:13.
[^15]: Or "with a stroke". Hebrew *maggefah*, "blow, plague".
[^16]: Literally "the bread of men", probably the food brought by neighbors to those in mourning (compare Jeremiah 16:7). Some scholars read "the bread of mourners" (Hosea 9:4), with a small change to the Hebrew. Also in verse 22. The other signs in the verse are also the reverse of what mourners did: they went barefoot and bareheaded and covered their faces (2 Samuel 15:30; Micah 3:7).
[^17]: Literally "your *nephesh*".
[^18]: Hebrew *mofet*, "sign, portent" (12:6). Also in verse 27.
[^19]: Literally "the lifting up of their *nephesh*".
[^20]: Literally "to make ears hear".
[^21]: This is fulfilled in 33:21–22.
