**1** The word of Yahweh[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] how is the wood of the vine better than any other wood, the vine branch that is among the trees of the forest?

**3** Is wood taken from it to make anything useful?[^3] Or do people take a peg from it to hang any vessel on?

**4** Look, it is put into the fire for fuel. When the fire has consumed both its ends and its middle is charred, is it any use for anything?

**5** Look, even when it was whole, it could not be made into anything useful. How much less, when the fire has consumed it and it is charred, can it still be made into anything useful!

**6** Therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh[^4] says: Like the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so I will give up the people who live in Jerusalem.

**7** I will set my face against them. Though they have come out of the fire, the fire will still consume them. Then you will know that I am Yahweh, when I set my face against them.

**8** I will make the land desolate, because they have acted unfaithfully,[^5] declares the Lord Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Literally "to do work". Also in verses 4–5.
[^4]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^5]: Hebrew *ma'al*, a breach of faith, especially against God (14:13).
