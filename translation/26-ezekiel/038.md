**1** The word of Yahweh[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] set your face toward Gog,[^3] of the land of Magog, the chief prince[^4] of Meshech and Tubal,[^5] and prophesy against him.

**3** Say: This is what the Lord Yahweh[^6] says: I am against you, Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal.

**4** I will turn you around, put hooks in your jaws, and bring you out with all your army, horses and horsemen, all of them in full armor, a great company with large shield and small shield, all of them wielding swords.

**5** Persia, Cush and Put[^7] are with them, all of them with shield and helmet,

**6** Gomer[^8] and all its troops, Beth-togarmah[^9] from the far reaches of the north with all its troops: many peoples are with you.

**7** Be ready, and prepare yourself, you and all your company that has gathered around you, and take charge of them.[^10]

**8** After many days you will be summoned.[^11] In the latter years you will come against a land that has been restored from the sword, whose people have been gathered from many peoples onto the mountains of Israel, which had long lain in ruins. Its people were brought out from the peoples, and now all of them live securely.

**9** You will go up and come like a storm; you will be like a cloud covering the land, you and all your troops and many peoples with you.

**10** This is what the Lord Yahweh says: On that day thoughts will come into your mind, and you will devise an evil plan.

**11** You will say, "I will go up against a land of open villages. I will come against a quiet people who live securely, all of them living without walls, with no bars or gates,"

**12** to seize spoil and carry off plunder, to turn your hand against the ruins that are now inhabited, and against the people gathered from the nations, who are acquiring livestock and goods, who live at the center of the earth.[^12]

**13** Sheba and Dedan, and the merchants of Tarshish and all its young lions,[^13] will say to you, "Have you come to seize spoil? Have you gathered your company to carry off plunder, to carry away silver and gold, to take livestock and goods, to seize great spoil?"

**14** Therefore prophesy, son of man, and say to Gog: This is what the Lord Yahweh says: On that day, when my people Israel are living securely, will you not take notice?

**15** You will come from your place in the far reaches of the north, you and many peoples with you, all of them riding horses, a great company, a mighty army.

**16** You will come up against my people Israel like a cloud covering the land. In the latter days[^14] I will bring you against my land, so that the nations may know me, when I show myself holy through you, Gog, before their eyes.

**17** This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Are you the one I spoke of in former days through my servants the prophets of Israel, who prophesied in those days, for years, that I would bring you against them?

**18** On that day, the day when Gog comes against the land of Israel, declares the Lord Yahweh, my wrath will flare up in my anger.[^15]

**19** In my jealousy, in the fire of my fury, I declare that on that day there will be a great earthquake in the land of Israel.

**20** The fish of the sea, the birds of the sky, the animals of the field, every creature that creeps on the ground, and all the people on the face of the earth will quake at my presence. The mountains will be thrown down, the cliffs will collapse, and every wall will fall to the ground.

**21** I will call for a sword against him on all my mountains, declares the Lord Yahweh. Every man's sword will be against his brother.

**22** I will enter into judgment with him by plague and bloodshed. I will rain down on him and his troops, and on the many peoples with him, torrents of rain, hailstones, fire and sulfur.

**23** So I will show my greatness and my holiness, and make myself known in the sight of many nations. Then they will know that I am Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Gog is not otherwise known. The name may recall Gyges (Assyrian *Gugu*), a king of Lydia in western Asia Minor in the 600s BC. Magog is named as a son of Japheth in Genesis 10:2; "the land of Magog" may mean "the land of Gog".
[^4]: Or "the prince of Rosh, Meshech and Tubal". Hebrew *nesi rosh*: *rosh* means "head, chief", but the Septuagint takes it as a name, *Rōs*. No people or place called Rosh is otherwise known. Also in verse 3 and 39:1.
[^5]: Peoples of central and eastern Asia Minor (27:13; 32:26).
[^6]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^7]: *Cush* is the region south of Egypt (ancient Nubia), and *Put* probably Libya (27:10; 30:5).
[^8]: Probably the Cimmerians, a people from north of the Black Sea who moved into Asia Minor (Genesis 10:2–3).
[^9]: Probably in eastern Asia Minor (27:14).
[^10]: Literally "and be for them a guard". The meaning is uncertain.
[^11]: Or "you will be called to account". Hebrew *tippaqed*.
[^12]: Hebrew *tabbur*, perhaps "navel": the center of the world, or a high place (Judges 9:37).
[^13]: Perhaps its rulers or warriors.
[^14]: Or "In days to come". Hebrew *be'acharit hayyamim*; similarly "in the latter years" in verse 8.
[^15]: Literally "my fury will rise in my nose".
