**1** The word of the LORD[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] say to the ruler of Tyre: This is what the Lord GOD[^3] says: Because your heart is proud, and you have said, "I am a god; I sit on the seat of the gods[^4] in the heart of the seas," though you are a human being and not a god, and you count your mind as the mind of a god,[^5]

**3** look, you are wiser than Daniel![^6] No secret is hidden from you.

**4** By your wisdom and your understanding you have gained wealth for yourself, and you have gathered gold and silver into your treasuries.

**5** By your great wisdom in trade you have increased your wealth, and your heart has grown proud because of your wealth.

**6** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you count your mind as the mind of a god,

**7** I am going to bring foreigners against you, the most ruthless of the nations. They will draw their swords against the beauty of your wisdom and defile your splendor.

**8** They will bring you down to the Pit,[^7] and you will die the death of the slain in the heart of the seas.

**9** Will you still say, "I am a god," in the presence of the one who kills you? You are a human being and not a god, in the hands of those who strike you down.

**10** You will die the death of the uncircumcised[^8] by the hand of foreigners, for I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.

**11** The word of the LORD came to me:

**12** Son of man, raise a lament over the king of Tyre and say to him: This is what the Lord GOD says: You were the seal of perfection,[^9] full of wisdom and perfect in beauty.

**13** You were in Eden, the garden of God. Every precious stone was your covering: carnelian, chrysolite and moonstone, beryl, onyx and jasper, lapis lazuli, turquoise and emerald,[^10] and gold. The workmanship of your settings and your mountings[^11] was in you; on the day you were created they were prepared.

**14** You were an anointed guardian cherub.[^12] I placed you; you were on the holy mountain of God. You walked among the stones of fire.[^13]

**15** You were blameless in your ways from the day you were created, until wrongdoing was found in you.

**16** Through your great trade you were filled with violence within, and you sinned. So I banished you in disgrace[^14] from the mountain of God, and I destroyed you, guardian cherub, from among the stones of fire.[^15]

**17** Your heart grew proud because of your beauty; you corrupted your wisdom for the sake of your splendor. I threw you to the ground. I exposed you before kings, for them to gaze at you.

**18** By your great guilt, by the dishonesty of your trade, you defiled your sanctuaries. So I brought out fire from within you, and it consumed you. I turned you to ashes on the earth in the sight of all who saw you.

**19** All who knew you among the peoples are appalled at you. You have come to a dreadful end,[^16] and you will be no more, forever.

**20** The word of the LORD came to me:

**21** Son of man, set your face toward Sidon[^17] and prophesy against it.

**22** Say: This is what the Lord GOD says: Look, I am against you, Sidon, and I will display my glory within you. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I carry out judgments on her and show myself holy in her.

**23** I will send plague into her and bloodshed into her streets. The slain will fall within her, by the sword that is against her on every side. Then they will know that I am the LORD.

**24** The house of Israel will no longer have a prickling brier or a painful thorn among all their neighbors who treat them with contempt. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.

**25** This is what the Lord GOD says: When I gather the house of Israel from the peoples among whom they have been scattered, I will show myself holy among them in the sight of the nations. They will live in their own land, which I gave to my servant Jacob.

**26** They will live in it securely. They will build houses and plant vineyards and live securely, when I carry out judgments on all their neighbors who treat them with contempt. Then they will know that I am the LORD their God.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Or "the seat of God". The Hebrew *elohim* can mean God or gods; so also "a god" at the end of the verse and in verse 6.
[^5]: Literally "you have set your heart like the heart of a god". The heart was thought of as the seat of understanding. Also in verse 6.
[^6]: The Hebrew text is written "Danel" but read "Daniel". The written form may refer to Danel, a wise and righteous king of ancient legend, known from texts found at Ugarit in Syria, rather than to the Daniel of the book of Daniel. Compare 14:14, 20.
[^7]: Hebrew *shachat*, the grave or the underworld.
[^8]: That is, a death in disgrace. In Ezekiel the uncircumcised lie dishonored in the underworld, with those killed by the sword (31:18; 32:19–32).
[^9]: Literally "a sealer of measurement" or "a seal of the pattern". The meaning is uncertain. The word *tokhnit*, "measurement, pattern", is used of the plan of the temple in 43:10.
[^10]: These are nine of the twelve stones of the high priest's breastpiece (Exodus 28:17–20), and the identification of many of them is uncertain. *Lapis lazuli* is Hebrew *sappir*, traditionally "sapphire"; *moonstone* is *yahalom*, traditionally "diamond"; *beryl* is *tarshish*. The Septuagint lists all twelve stones, with silver and gold.
[^11]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. *Tuppekha* elsewhere means "your tambourines", and *neqavekha* perhaps "your sockets", "your engravings" or "your pipes".
[^12]: Hebrew *keruv*: one of the winged heavenly beings who guard holy places (Genesis 3:24; Exodus 25:18–22; Ezekiel 10). The word for "you" here, *at*, is an unusual form. The Septuagint reads "With the cherub I placed you", taking the same consonants as *et*, "with"; in that reading the king is placed beside the cherub rather than being the cherub himself. *Anointed* is Hebrew *mimshach*, a word found only here, perhaps from *mashach*, "anoint", or meaning "with wings outspread". *Guardian* is literally "who covers" or "who shelters", as the cherubim sheltered the atonement cover with their wings (Exodus 25:20).
[^13]: Perhaps the precious stones of verse 13, or the burning coals among the cherubim (1:13; 10:2). Also in verse 16.
[^14]: Literally "I profaned you from", that is, I treated you as unholy and drove you out.
[^15]: The Septuagint reads "and the cherub drove you out from among the stones of fire", again keeping the king and the cherub distinct (verse 14).
[^16]: Literally "You have become terrors", as in 27:36.
[^17]: The Phoenician port city about 25 miles (40 km) north of Tyre.
