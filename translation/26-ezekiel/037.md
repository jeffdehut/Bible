**1** The hand of Yahweh[^1] was on me, and he brought me out by the Spirit[^2] of Yahweh and set me down in the middle of the valley.[^3] It was full of bones.

**2** He led me all around among them, and there were very many of them on the floor of the valley, and they were very dry.

**3** He said to me, "Son of man,[^4] can these bones live?" I said, "Lord Yahweh,[^5] you know."

**4** Then he said to me, "Prophesy over these bones and say to them, 'Dry bones, hear the word of Yahweh.

**5** This is what the Lord Yahweh says to these bones: I am going to make breath enter you, and you will come to life.

**6** I will put sinews on you, make flesh grow on you, cover you with skin and put breath in you, and you will come to life. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.'"

**7** So I prophesied as I had been commanded. As I was prophesying, there was a noise, a rattling,[^6] and the bones came together, bone to its bone.

**8** I looked, and there were sinews on them, and flesh had grown, and skin had covered them over; but there was no breath in them.

**9** Then he said to me, "Prophesy to the breath; prophesy, son of man, and say to the breath, 'This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Come from the four winds, breath, and breathe into these slain, so that they may live.'"

**10** So I prophesied as he commanded me, and the breath came into them. They came to life and stood on their feet, a very great army.

**11** Then he said to me, "Son of man, these bones are the whole house of Israel. Look, they are saying, 'Our bones are dried up, and our hope is gone. We are cut off completely.'

**12** Therefore prophesy and say to them, 'This is what the Lord Yahweh says: I am going to open your graves and bring you up from your graves, my people, and I will bring you into the land of Israel.

**13** Then you will know that I am Yahweh, when I open your graves and bring you up from your graves, my people.

**14** I will put my Spirit in you, and you will come to life, and I will settle you in your own land. Then you will know that I, Yahweh, have spoken, and I will do it,[^7] declares Yahweh.'"

**15** The word of Yahweh came to me:

**16** "As for you, son of man, take a stick[^8] and write on it, 'For Judah, and for the Israelites, his companions.'[^9] Then take another stick and write on it, 'For Joseph, the stick of Ephraim, and all the house of Israel, his companions.'

**17** Join them together into one stick, so that they become one in your hand.

**18** When your people say to you, 'Won't you tell us what you mean by these?'

**19** say to them, 'This is what the Lord Yahweh says: I am going to take the stick of Joseph, which is in the hand of Ephraim, and the tribes of Israel, his companions, and I will put them together with the stick of Judah. I will make them into one stick, and they will be one in my hand.'

**20** The sticks you write on are to be in your hand before their eyes.

**21** Then say to them, 'This is what the Lord Yahweh says: I am going to take the Israelites from among the nations where they have gone. I will gather them from all around and bring them into their own land.

**22** I will make them one nation in the land, on the mountains of Israel, and one king will be king over all of them. They will no longer be[^10] two nations, and they will never again be divided into two kingdoms.

**23** They will no longer defile themselves with their idols[^11] and their vile idols[^12] and all their transgressions. I will save them from all their dwelling places[^13] where they sinned, and I will cleanse them. They will be my people, and I will be their God.

**24** My servant David[^14] will be king over them, and they will all have one shepherd. They will walk in my rulings and keep my decrees and carry them out.

**25** They will live in the land I gave to my servant Jacob, where your ancestors lived. They will live in it, they and their children and their children's children, forever, and David my servant will be their prince[^15] forever.

**26** I will make a covenant of peace with them; it will be an everlasting covenant with them. I will establish them[^16] and multiply them, and I will set my sanctuary among them forever.

**27** My dwelling place[^17] will be with them. I will be their God, and they will be my people.

**28** Then the nations will know that I am Yahweh, who makes Israel holy, when my sanctuary is among them forever.'"

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Or "by a spirit from Yahweh". Hebrew *ruach* means "spirit", "wind" and "breath", and this chapter uses it in all three senses: "breath" in verses 5–10, "winds" in verse 9 and "my Spirit" in verse 14.
[^3]: Or "the plain". Hebrew *biq'ah*, a broad valley (3:22–23). Also in verse 2.
[^4]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^5]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^6]: Or "a quaking". Hebrew *ra'ash*.
[^7]: Literally "and I have done it".
[^8]: Hebrew *ets*, "wood, tree": here a stick, or perhaps a wooden tablet for writing. Also in verses 17, 19 and 20.
[^9]: The Hebrew text is written "his companion" but read "his companions". Also later in the verse and in verse 19.
[^10]: The Hebrew text is written "It will no longer be" but read "They will no longer be".
[^11]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a contemptuous word for idols, perhaps related to the word for dung.
[^12]: Hebrew *shiqqutsim*, "detestable things", a contemptuous word for idols.
[^13]: Some scholars read "from all their backslidings", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^14]: That is, a king from the line of David (34:23–24).
[^15]: Hebrew *nasi*, "leader, chief", Ezekiel's title for the king of Israel (34:24).
[^16]: Literally "I will give them". The meaning is uncertain.
[^17]: Hebrew *mishkan*, the word used for the tabernacle (Exodus 25:9).
