**1** "And you, son of man,[^1] take a brick[^2] and put it in front of you, and draw a city on it, Jerusalem.

**2** Lay siege to it: build a siege wall against it, pile up a ramp against it, set up camps against it, and place battering rams against it all around.

**3** Then take an iron griddle[^3] and set it up as an iron wall between you and the city. Set your face toward it, so that it is under siege, and besiege it. This is a sign for the house of Israel.

**4** Then lie on your left side, and lay the guilt[^4] of the house of Israel on it. For the number of days that you lie on it, you will bear their guilt.

**5** I have assigned you the years of their guilt as a number of days, 390 days.[^5] So you will bear the guilt of the house of Israel.

**6** When you have completed these, lie down a second time, on your right side, and bear the guilt of the house of Judah for 40 days. I have assigned you a day for each year, a day for each year.

**7** Set your face toward the siege of Jerusalem, with your arm bared, and prophesy against it.

**8** Look, I will put ropes on you, so that you cannot turn from one side to the other until you have completed the days of your siege.

**9** Take wheat and barley, beans and lentils, millet and spelt.[^6] Put them in one container and make bread for yourself from them. You are to eat it for the number of days that you lie on your side, 390 days.

**10** The food you eat is to be weighed out, twenty shekels[^7] a day; you are to eat it at set times.[^8]

**11** You are to drink water by measure, a sixth of a hin;[^9] you are to drink it at set times.

**12** You are to eat it as a barley cake, and bake it in their sight over human dung."[^10]

**13** Yahweh[^11] said, "This is how the Israelites will eat their bread, unclean, among the nations where I will drive them."

**14** Then I said, "Ah, Lord Yahweh![^12] I have never made myself unclean.[^13] From my youth until now I have never eaten anything that died by itself or was torn by wild animals, and no foul meat[^14] has ever come into my mouth."

**15** He said to me, "See, I will let you have cow dung instead of human dung. You may make your bread over it."

**16** He said to me, "Son of man, I am going to cut off the supply of bread[^15] in Jerusalem. They will eat bread by weight and with anxiety, and drink water by measure and in dismay,

**17** so that they will lack bread and water, and they will look at one another in dismay,[^16] and waste away in their guilt.

[^1]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal. Yahweh's speech, which began in 3:24, continues to the end of chapter 5.
[^2]: Hebrew *levenah*, a brick of sun-dried clay. In Babylonia clay was also used as a surface for writing and drawing.
[^3]: Hebrew *machavat*, a flat plate for baking (Leviticus 2:5).
[^4]: Hebrew *avon*, which means both wrongdoing and the punishment it brings. Also in verses 5–6 and 17.
[^5]: The Septuagint reads "190 days", here and in verse 9.
[^6]: Hebrew *dochan*, probably millet, and *kussemim*, a kind of wheat, perhaps spelt or emmer.
[^7]: Twenty shekels was about 8 ounces (230 grams).
[^8]: Literally "from time to time". Also in verse 11.
[^9]: A hin was about a gallon (4 liters), so a sixth of a hin was about 1⅓ pints (0.6 liter).
[^10]: Literally "with dung of human excrement". Dried dung was commonly burned as fuel (verse 15).
[^11]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^12]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^13]: Literally "Look, my *nephesh* has not been made unclean".
[^14]: Hebrew *piggul*, "foul, rotten": meat that has become unfit to eat (Leviticus 7:18). The law forbade priests to eat an animal that had died by itself or been torn by wild animals (Leviticus 22:8).
[^15]: Literally "break the staff of bread", as in Leviticus 26:26.
[^16]: Literally "they will be appalled, each man and his brother".
