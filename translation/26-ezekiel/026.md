**1** In the eleventh year, on the first day of the month,[^1] the word of Yahweh[^2] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^3] because Tyre has said about Jerusalem, "Aha! The gateway of the peoples is broken; it has swung open to me.[^4] I will be filled, now that she is laid waste,"

**3** therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh[^5] says: Look, I am against you, Tyre, and I will bring up many nations against you, as the sea brings up its waves.

**4** They will destroy the walls of Tyre and tear down her towers. I will scrape her soil from her and make her a bare rock.[^6]

**5** She will become a place for spreading nets in the middle of the sea, for I have spoken, declares the Lord Yahweh. She will become plunder for the nations,

**6** and her daughters on the mainland[^7] will be killed by the sword. Then they will know that I am Yahweh.

**7** For this is what the Lord Yahweh says: Look, I am going to bring against Tyre from the north Nebuchadnezzar[^8] king of Babylon, king of kings, with horses and chariots, with horsemen and a great army.[^9]

**8** He will kill your daughters on the mainland with the sword. He will build a siege wall against you, pile up a ramp against you, and raise a roof of shields[^10] against you.

**9** He will direct the blows of his battering ram against your walls and break down your towers with his axes.[^11]

**10** His horses will be so many that their dust will cover you. Your walls will shake at the noise of horsemen, wagons and chariots, when he enters your gates as people enter a city that has been breached.

**11** With the hoofs of his horses he will trample all your streets. He will kill your people with the sword, and your mighty pillars[^12] will fall to the ground.

**12** They will plunder your wealth and loot your merchandise. They will tear down your walls and destroy your fine houses, and they will throw your stones, your timber and your soil into the water.

**13** I will put an end to the noise of your songs, and the sound of your lyres will be heard no more.

**14** I will make you a bare rock; you will be a place for spreading nets. You will never be rebuilt, for I, Yahweh, have spoken, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**15** This is what the Lord Yahweh says to Tyre: Will not the coastlands shake at the sound of your fall, when the wounded groan, when the slaughter is carried out in your midst?

**16** Then all the princes of the sea will come down from their thrones, take off their robes and strip off their embroidered clothes. They will clothe themselves with trembling; they will sit on the ground, tremble every moment and be appalled over you.

**17** They will take up a lament[^13] over you and say to you, "How you have perished, you who were peopled from the seas,[^14] the renowned city, which was mighty on the sea, she and her inhabitants, who spread their terror over all who lived there![^15]

**18** Now the coastlands tremble on the day of your fall; the coastlands by the sea are terrified at your passing."

**19** For this is what the Lord Yahweh says: When I make you a desolate city, like cities that are not inhabited, when I bring up the deep[^16] over you and the great waters cover you,

**20** then I will bring you down with those who go down to the Pit,[^17] to the people of long ago. I will make you live in the world below,[^18] among ruins from long ago, with those who go down to the Pit, so that you will not be inhabited; and I will give glory in the land of the living.[^19]

**21** I will bring you to a dreadful end,[^20] and you will be no more. You will be sought, but you will never be found again, declares the Lord Yahweh.

[^1]: The month is not given. The eleventh year is 587 or 586 BC, the year Jerusalem fell (2 Kings 25:2–8).
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^4]: Literally "the doors of the peoples is broken; it has turned to me".
[^5]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^6]: The name Tyre, Hebrew *Tsor*, means "rock". The city stood on a rocky island off the coast of Phoenicia. Also in verse 14.
[^7]: Literally "her daughters in the field", the towns on the mainland that depended on Tyre. Also in verse 8.
[^8]: Hebrew *Nevukhadre'tsar*, a spelling closer to the Babylonian name *Nabu-kudurri-usur*, used often in Jeremiah and Ezekiel. Elsewhere the name is usually spelled *Nevukhadnetsar*, Nebuchadnezzar. He was king of Babylon from 605 to 562 BC.
[^9]: Literally "a company and a great people".
[^10]: Hebrew *tsinnah*, a large shield. Here probably a covering of shields to protect the attackers.
[^11]: Literally "his swords".
[^12]: Hebrew *matsevot*, standing stones or pillars, perhaps those in the temples of Tyre.
[^13]: Hebrew *qinah*, a funeral song (19:1).
[^14]: Literally "inhabited from the seas", perhaps "peopled by seafarers".
[^15]: Literally "who put their terror on all her inhabitants". The meaning is uncertain.
[^16]: Hebrew *tehom*, the deep waters of the ocean (Genesis 1:2).
[^17]: Hebrew *bor*, "pit, cistern", the grave or the underworld (Isaiah 14:15, 19).
[^18]: Hebrew *erets tachtiyyot*, "the land of the lowest places", the underworld.
[^19]: The Septuagint reads "nor stand in the land of the living".
[^20]: Literally "I will make you terrors".
