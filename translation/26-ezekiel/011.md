**1** Then the Spirit[^1] lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the house of the LORD,[^2] the gate that faces east. There at the entrance of the gate were twenty-five men, and I saw among them Jaazaniah son of Azzur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, leaders of the people.

**2** He said to me, "Son of man,[^3] these are the men who are planning wickedness and giving evil advice in this city.

**3** They say, 'The time is not near to build houses.[^4] This city is the pot, and we are the meat.'[^5]

**4** Therefore prophesy against them; prophesy, son of man."

**5** Then the Spirit of the LORD fell on me, and he said to me, "Say: This is what the LORD says: That is what you have said, house of Israel, but I know the things that come into your mind.[^6]

**6** You have killed many people in this city and filled its streets with the slain.

**7** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD[^7] says: The slain whom you have laid in the middle of it, they are the meat, and this city is the pot. But you I will bring out of it.[^8]

**8** You have feared the sword, and the sword is what I will bring on you, declares the Lord GOD.

**9** I will bring you out of the middle of it and hand you over to foreigners, and I will carry out judgments on you.

**10** You will fall by the sword; I will judge you at the border of Israel.[^9] Then you will know that I am the LORD.

**11** This city will not be your pot, and you will not be the meat in it. I will judge you at the border of Israel.

**12** Then you will know that I am the LORD, whose decrees you have not walked in and whose rulings you have not kept, but you have acted according to the rulings of the nations around you."

**13** While I was prophesying, Pelatiah son of Benaiah died. Then I fell on my face and cried out with a loud voice, "Ah, Lord GOD! Will you make a complete end of the remnant of Israel?"

**14** The word of the LORD came to me:

**15** "Son of man, your brothers, your own brothers, your relatives who have the right to redeem you,[^10] and the whole house of Israel, all of them, are those of whom the people living in Jerusalem have said, 'Keep far away from the LORD.[^11] This land was given to us as a possession.'

**16** Therefore say: This is what the Lord GOD says: Though I have sent them far away among the nations, and though I have scattered them among the countries, yet I have been a sanctuary for them for a little while[^12] in the countries where they have gone.

**17** Therefore say: This is what the Lord GOD says: I will gather you from the peoples and assemble you from the countries where you have been scattered, and I will give you the land of Israel.

**18** They will come there and remove from it all its vile idols[^13] and all its detestable practices.[^14]

**19** I will give them one heart,[^15] and I will put a new spirit within you.[^16] I will remove the heart of stone from their bodies and give them a heart of flesh,

**20** so that they will walk in my decrees and keep my rulings and do them. Then they will be my people, and I will be their God.

**21** But as for those whose heart goes after their vile idols and their detestable practices,[^17] I will bring their ways down on their heads, declares the Lord GOD."

**22** Then the cherubim lifted their wings, with the wheels beside them, and the glory of the God of Israel was over them above.

**23** The glory of the LORD went up from the middle of the city and stood on the mountain east of the city.[^18]

**24** The Spirit lifted me up and brought me to the exiles in Chaldea,[^19] in the vision, by the Spirit of God. Then the vision that I had seen left me.[^20]

**25** And I told the exiles all the things that the LORD had shown me.

[^1]: Or "a spirit" or "a wind". Hebrew *ruach*. Also in verse 24.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^4]: Or "Is it not the right time to build houses?" The meaning is uncertain. Literally "Not near the building of houses".
[^5]: They mean that they are safe in the city, like meat in a pot that protects it from the fire, or that they are the choice portion.
[^6]: Literally "what goes up in your spirit".
[^7]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^8]: Hebrew *hotsi*, "bring out", in a form that does not state the subject. Verse 9 has "I will bring you out".
[^9]: When Jerusalem fell, the leading officials of Judah were taken to the king of Babylon at Riblah, in the far north, and put to death there (2 Kings 25:18–21).
[^10]: Literally "the men of your redemption": the relatives who had the duty to buy back a family member's land, or the family member himself, if he was forced to sell (Leviticus 25:25, 47–49; Jeremiah 32:6–8).
[^11]: The same consonants can be read "They are far away from the LORD".
[^12]: Or "a sanctuary in small measure". Hebrew *me'at*, "a little".
[^13]: Hebrew *shiqqutsim*, "detestable things", a contemptuous word for idols. Also in verse 21.
[^14]: Hebrew *to'evot*, things that are abhorrent, especially in worship. Also in verse 21.
[^15]: Or "an undivided heart". Compare 18:31 and 36:26, "a new heart".
[^16]: The Hebrew changes here from "them" to "you".
[^17]: Literally "and to the heart of their vile idols and their detestable practices their heart goes". The meaning is uncertain.
[^18]: The Mount of Olives.
[^19]: That is, Babylonia.
[^20]: Literally "went up from me".
