**1** The word of Yahweh[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] set your face toward the Ammonites and prophesy against them.

**3** Say to the Ammonites: Hear the word of the Lord Yahweh.[^3] This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Because you said, "Aha!" over my sanctuary when it was profaned, and over the land of Israel when it was made desolate, and over the house of Judah when they went into exile,[^4]

**4** therefore, look, I am going to hand you over to the people of the east[^5] as a possession. They will set up their camps among you and pitch their tents among you. They will eat your fruit and drink your milk.

**5** I will make Rabbah[^6] a pasture for camels, and the land of Ammon a resting place for flocks.[^7] Then you will know that I am Yahweh.

**6** For this is what the Lord Yahweh says: Because you clapped your hands and stamped your feet and rejoiced with all the contempt in your heart[^8] over the land of Israel,

**7** therefore, look, I have stretched out my hand against you, and I will hand you over as plunder[^9] to the nations. I will cut you off from the peoples and make you perish from the countries; I will destroy you. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.

**8** This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Because Moab and Seir[^10] said, "Look, the house of Judah is like all the other nations,"

**9** therefore, look, I am going to lay open the flank of Moab, stripping it of its cities, the cities on its frontier, the glory of the land: Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon and Kiriathaim.[^11]

**10** I will give it, along with the Ammonites, to the people of the east as a possession, so that the Ammonites will not be remembered among the nations.

**11** And I will carry out judgments on Moab. Then they will know that I am Yahweh.

**12** This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Because Edom acted vengefully against the house of Judah, and incurred great guilt by taking revenge on them,

**13** therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: I will stretch out my hand against Edom and cut off from it both people and animals. I will make it a ruin; from Teman to Dedan[^12] they will fall by the sword.

**14** I will lay my vengeance on Edom by the hand of my people Israel, and they will deal with Edom according to my anger and my fury. Then they will know my vengeance, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**15** This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Because the Philistines acted in revenge and took vengeance with contempt in their hearts, destroying with endless hostility,[^13]

**16** therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: Look, I am going to stretch out my hand against the Philistines. I will cut off the Cherethites[^14] and destroy the rest of those on the seacoast.

**17** I will carry out great vengeance on them with furious punishments. Then they will know that I am Yahweh, when I lay my vengeance on them.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^4]: *You* is feminine singular in verses 3–5, addressing Ammon as a woman, and masculine singular in verses 6–7.
[^5]: Hebrew *bene qedem*, the desert tribes east of the Jordan (Judges 6:3). Also in verse 10.
[^6]: The capital of Ammon; modern Amman (21:25).
[^7]: Literally "the Ammonites a resting place for flocks".
[^8]: Literally "with all your contempt in *nephesh*". Also in verse 15.
[^9]: The Hebrew text is written *lebag*, a word of uncertain meaning, but read *lebaz*, "as plunder".
[^10]: The mountain country of Edom (Genesis 36:8).
[^11]: Towns of Moab north of the Arnon River (Numbers 32:37–38; Joshua 13:17–20). The Hebrew text is written *Qiryatam* but read *Qiryatayim*, "Kiriathaim".
[^12]: Teman was a region of Edom; Dedan was an oasis in northern Arabia, to the southeast of Edom.
[^13]: Literally "with everlasting enmity".
[^14]: A play on words: *hikhratti*, "I will cut off", and *Keretim*, "Cherethites", a people closely linked with the Philistines (1 Samuel 30:14; Zephaniah 2:5), perhaps from Crete.
