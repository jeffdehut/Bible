**1** The word of Yahweh[^1] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^2] prophesy against the prophets of Israel who are prophesying. Say to those who prophesy out of their own imagination:[^3] Hear the word of Yahweh.

**3** This is what the Lord Yahweh[^4] says: Woe to[^5] the foolish prophets,[^6] who follow their own spirit and have seen nothing!

**4** Your prophets, Israel, have been like foxes among ruins.[^7]

**5** You have not gone up into the breaches or built up a wall around the house of Israel, so that it might stand in the battle on the day of Yahweh.

**6** They have seen false visions[^8] and lying divination. They say, "declares Yahweh," when Yahweh has not sent them, and yet they expect him to fulfill their word.

**7** Have you not seen a false vision and spoken a lying divination, when you say, "declares Yahweh," though I have not spoken?

**8** Therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: Because you have spoken falsehood and seen lies, therefore, look, I am against you, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**9** My hand will be against the prophets who see false visions and give lying divinations. They will not be in the council of my people, or be written in the register of the house of Israel, or enter the land of Israel. Then you will know that I am the Lord Yahweh.

**10** Because, yes, because they have led my people astray, saying, "Peace," when there is no peace, and when someone builds a flimsy wall,[^9] they cover it with whitewash,[^10]

**11** say to those who cover it with whitewash that it will fall. There will be a flooding rain, and you, great hailstones,[^11] will fall, and a stormy wind will break out.

**12** When the wall has fallen, will people not say to you, "Where is the whitewash you covered it with?"

**13** Therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: In my fury I will make a stormy wind break out; in my anger there will be a flooding rain, and in fury, hailstones to destroy it.

**14** I will tear down the wall that you covered with whitewash and bring it down to the ground, so that its foundation is laid bare. When it falls,[^12] you will perish in the middle of it. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.

**15** So I will spend my fury on the wall and on those who covered it with whitewash. And I will say to you, "The wall is gone, and so are those who whitewashed it":

**16** the prophets of Israel who prophesied to Jerusalem and saw visions of peace for her when there was no peace, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**17** And you, son of man, set your face against the daughters of your people[^13] who prophesy out of their own imagination. Prophesy against them,

**18** and say: This is what the Lord Yahweh says: Woe to the women who sew bands[^14] on every wrist[^15] and make veils for the heads of people of every height, to hunt down lives![^16] Will you hunt down the lives of my people and keep your own lives alive?

**19** You have profaned me among my people for handfuls of barley and scraps of bread, putting to death people who should not die and keeping alive people who should not live, by your lies to my people, who listen to lies.

**20** Therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: Look, I am against your bands with which you hunt down lives there like birds.[^17] I will tear them from your arms, and I will set free the lives that you hunt down like birds.

**21** I will tear off your veils and rescue my people from your hands, and they will no longer be prey in your hands. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.

**22** Because you have disheartened the righteous with lies, when I had not brought him grief, and you have encouraged the wicked[^18] not to turn from his evil way and save his life,

**23** therefore you will no longer see false visions or practice divination. I will rescue my people from your hands. Then you will know that I am Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^3]: Literally "the prophets from their own heart". Also in verse 17.
[^4]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^5]: Hebrew *hoy*, a cry of mourning for the dead ("Alas!", 1 Kings 13:30). The prophets use it to announce disaster, and in that sense it is translated "Woe to" throughout this book. Also in verse 18.
[^6]: A play on words: *nevi'im*, "prophets", and *nevalim*, "fools". The *naval*, "fool", is someone who acts without regard for God (Psalm 14:1).
[^7]: Or "jackals". Hebrew *shu'alim*.
[^8]: Or "empty visions". Hebrew *shav*, "emptiness, falsehood". Also in verses 7–9 and 23.
[^9]: Hebrew *chayits*, found only here, probably a wall of loose stones.
[^10]: Hebrew *tafel*, a coat of plaster or whitewash, which makes a wall look solid. Also in verses 11–15.
[^11]: Literally "hailstones", Hebrew *elgavish*, a word found only in Ezekiel (13:11, 13; 38:22). Also in verse 13.
[^12]: *It* is feminine in Hebrew, unlike "the wall", and may refer to the city.
[^13]: That is, the women of your people.
[^14]: Hebrew *kesatot*, a word found only here (also in verse 20), probably bands or amulets used in magic.
[^15]: Literally "on all the joints of my hands". The meaning is uncertain.
[^16]: Hebrew *nefashot*, "lives, persons". Also in verses 19–20.
[^17]: The meaning of the Hebrew word translated "like birds" (*leforchot*, also at the end of the verse) is uncertain.
[^18]: Literally "strengthened the hands of the wicked".
