**1** In the tenth year, in the tenth month, on the twelfth day of the month,[^1] the word of Yahweh[^2] came to me:

**2** Son of man,[^3] set your face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy against him and against all Egypt.

**3** Speak and say: This is what the Lord Yahweh[^4] says: Look, I am against you, Pharaoh king of Egypt, the great monster[^5] lying in the middle of his streams,[^6] who has said, "My Nile is my own; I made it for myself."[^7]

**4** I will put hooks in your jaws and make the fish of your streams stick to your scales. I will haul you up out of your streams, with all the fish of your streams sticking to your scales.

**5** I will leave you in the wilderness, you and all the fish of your streams. You will fall on the open field; you will not be gathered up or collected.[^8] I have given you as food to the animals of the earth and the birds of the sky.

**6** Then all the inhabitants of Egypt will know that I am Yahweh. Because they have been a staff of reed[^9] for the house of Israel,

**7** when they grasped you by the hand, you splintered and tore open all their shoulders; and when they leaned on you, you broke and made all their backs go rigid.[^10]

**8** Therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: I am going to bring a sword against you, and I will cut off from you both human beings and animals.

**9** The land of Egypt will become a desolate waste. Then they will know that I am Yahweh. Because he said, "The Nile is mine; I made it,"

**10** therefore, look, I am against you and against your streams. I will make the land of Egypt an utter ruin, a desolate waste, from Migdol to Syene, as far as the border of Cush.[^11]

**11** No human foot will pass through it, and no animal's foot will pass through it. It will be uninhabited for forty years.

**12** I will make the land of Egypt a desolation among desolate lands, and its cities will lie desolate among ruined cities for forty years. I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them among the countries.

**13** For this is what the Lord Yahweh says: At the end of forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the peoples among whom they were scattered.

**14** I will restore the fortunes[^12] of Egypt and bring them back to the land of Pathros,[^13] the land of their origin. There they will be a lowly kingdom.

**15** It will be the lowliest of the kingdoms, and it will never again exalt itself above the nations. I will make them so few that they will not rule over the nations.

**16** Egypt will never again be the confidence of the house of Israel; it will only bring their guilt to mind, when they turned to them for help.[^14] Then they will know that I am the Lord Yahweh.

**17** In the twenty-seventh year, in the first month, on the first day of the month,[^15] the word of Yahweh came to me:

**18** Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar[^16] king of Babylon made his army labor hard against Tyre.[^17] Every head was rubbed bald, and every shoulder was rubbed raw.[^18] Yet he and his army got no wages from Tyre for the labor he had done against it.

**19** Therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: I am going to give the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. He will carry off its wealth,[^19] plunder its spoil and seize its loot, and it will be the wages for his army.

**20** As his payment for the labor he performed against it, I have given him the land of Egypt, because they did it for me, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**21** On that day I will make a horn[^20] sprout for the house of Israel, and I will open your mouth among them.[^21] Then they will know that I am Yahweh.

[^1]: In January 587 BC, counting from the exile of King Jehoiachin (1:2). The siege of Jerusalem had begun a year earlier (24:1–2).
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^4]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^5]: Hebrew *tannim*, a form of *tannin*, a great sea creature or dragon; here probably the crocodile of the Nile (compare 32:2).
[^6]: Hebrew *ye'orim*, the branches and canals of the Nile. *Ye'or*, the word translated "Nile" later in the verse, comes from the Egyptian word for the river.
[^7]: Literally "My Nile is mine, and I made me": either "I made it for myself" or "I made myself".
[^8]: That is, for burial.
[^9]: Egypt is like a cane of river reed: whoever leans on it for support is wounded when it snaps (2 Kings 18:21; Isaiah 36:6).
[^10]: Literally "and made all their loins stand". The Syriac reads "made all their loins shake".
[^11]: Migdol was in the north of Egypt, near the eastern edge of the Nile Delta; Syene (modern Aswan) was at its southern frontier. *Cush* is the region south of Egypt (ancient Nubia). The same phrase is used in 30:6.
[^12]: Literally "turn the turning", an idiom for restoring what was lost. Or "bring back the captives".
[^13]: Southern (Upper) Egypt.
[^14]: Literally "it will no longer be a confidence for the house of Israel, a reminder of guilt, when they turn after them".
[^15]: In April 571 BC, the latest date given in the book.
[^16]: Hebrew *Nevukhadre'tsar*, a spelling closer to the Babylonian name *Nabu-kudurri-usur*, used often in Jeremiah and Ezekiel. Elsewhere the name is usually spelled *Nevukhadnetsar*, Nebuchadnezzar. He was king of Babylon from 605 to 562 BC. Also in verse 19.
[^17]: According to the historian Josephus, Nebuchadnezzar besieged Tyre for thirteen years. The city submitted in the end, but it was not plundered.
[^18]: From carrying loads for the siege works.
[^19]: Or "its multitude". Hebrew *hamon*, "multitude, abundance, wealth", a word Ezekiel uses often of Egypt (30:4, 10; 31:2, 18; 32).
[^20]: A symbol of strength (1 Samuel 2:10; Psalm 132:17).
[^21]: Literally "I will give you an opening of the mouth among them": Ezekiel will speak freely (compare 3:26–27; 24:27; 33:22).
