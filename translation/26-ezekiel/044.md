**1** Then he brought me back by way of the outer gate of the sanctuary, the one facing east, and it was shut.

**2** Yahweh[^1] said to me, "This gate is to remain shut. It must not be opened, and no one may enter by it, because Yahweh, the God of Israel, has entered by it.[^2] So it is to remain shut.

**3** Only the prince,[^3] because he is the prince, may sit in it to eat bread before Yahweh. He is to enter by way of the porch of the gate and go out the same way."

**4** Then he brought me by way of the north gate to the front of the house.[^4] I looked, and the glory of Yahweh filled the house of Yahweh, and I fell on my face.

**5** Yahweh said to me, "Son of man,[^5] pay attention, look with your eyes and listen with your ears to all that I tell you about all the decrees of the house of Yahweh and all its laws.[^6] Pay attention to the entrance of the house and all the exits of the sanctuary.[^7]

**6** Say to the rebellious,[^8] to the house of Israel, 'This is what the Lord Yahweh[^9] says: Enough of all your detestable practices,[^10] house of Israel!

**7** You brought foreigners, uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh, into my sanctuary, to be in it and to profane my house, when you offered my food, the fat and the blood. They have broken my covenant,[^11] on top of all your detestable practices.

**8** You have not kept charge of my holy things yourselves; instead you have set them to keep my charge in my sanctuary for you.

**9** This is what the Lord Yahweh says: No foreigner, uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh, of all the foreigners who are among the Israelites, may enter my sanctuary.

**10** But the Levites who went far from me when Israel went astray, who went astray from me after their idols,[^12] will bear their guilt.

**11** They will serve in my sanctuary, having charge of the gates of the house and serving the house. They will slaughter the burnt offering and the sacrifice for the people, and they will stand before the people to serve them.

**12** Because they served them before their idols and became a stumbling block of guilt[^13] for the house of Israel, therefore I have sworn[^14] concerning them, declares the Lord Yahweh, that they will bear their guilt.

**13** They must not come near me to serve me as priests, or come near any of my holy things, the most holy things. They will bear their shame for the detestable practices they have committed.

**14** Yet I will appoint them to keep charge of the house, for all its service and for all that is to be done in it.

**15** But the Levitical priests, the sons of Zadok,[^15] who kept charge of my sanctuary when the Israelites went astray from me, they will come near to me to serve me. They will stand before me to offer me fat and blood, declares the Lord Yahweh.

**16** They will enter my sanctuary, and they will come near to my table to serve me and keep my charge.

**17** When they enter the gates of the inner court, they are to wear linen garments. They must have nothing woolen on them while they serve in the gates of the inner court and inside the house.

**18** They are to have linen turbans[^16] on their heads and linen undergarments around their waists. They must not wear anything that makes them sweat.

**19** When they go out into the outer court, into the outer court to the people, they are to take off the garments in which they have been serving and leave them in the holy rooms, and put on other garments, so that they do not make the people holy by their garments.[^17]

**20** They must not shave their heads or let their hair grow long. They are to keep the hair of their heads trimmed.

**21** No priest may drink wine when he enters the inner court.

**22** They must not marry a widow or a divorced woman, but only virgins of the descendants of the house of Israel, or a widow who is the widow of a priest.

**23** They are to teach my people the difference between the holy and the common, and show them how to tell the unclean from the clean.

**24** In a dispute they are to stand as judges, and they are to judge it by my rulings. They are to keep my laws and my decrees at all my appointed festivals, and they are to keep my Sabbaths holy.

**25** A priest[^18] must not go near a dead person and so become unclean, except that for a father or mother, son or daughter, brother, or sister who has no husband, they may make themselves unclean.

**26** After he has been cleansed, seven days are to be counted for him.

**27** On the day he enters the holy place, into the inner court, to serve in the holy place, he is to offer his sin offering,[^19] declares the Lord Yahweh.

**28** This is to be their inheritance: I am their inheritance. You are to give them no property in Israel; I am their property.

**29** They are to eat the grain offering,[^20] the sin offering and the guilt offering,[^21] and everything devoted[^22] in Israel will be theirs.

**30** The best of all the firstfruits of every kind, and every contribution[^23] of every kind from all your contributions, will belong to the priests. You are to give the priest the first of your dough,[^24] so that a blessing may rest on your house.

**31** The priests must not eat anything, bird or animal, that has died by itself or been torn by wild animals.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: The glory of Yahweh had entered by this gate (43:1–4).
[^3]: Hebrew *nasi*, "leader, chief", Ezekiel's title for the ruler of Israel (34:24; 37:25). In chapters 44–48 he has a special place in the worship of the temple (45:7–17; 46:1–18).
[^4]: That is, the temple, as throughout chapters 40–48.
[^5]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man": a human being, a mortal.
[^6]: The Hebrew text is written "all its law" but read "all its laws".
[^7]: That is, perhaps, to the rules for who may come in and go out (verses 6–9). Literally "Set your heart to the entrance of the house with all the exits of the sanctuary."
[^8]: Literally "to rebellion".
[^9]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh". The message that begins here runs to 46:18.
[^10]: Hebrew *to'evot*, things that are abhorrent, especially in worship. Also in verses 7 and 13.
[^11]: The Septuagint, the Syriac and the Vulgate read "You have broken my covenant".
[^12]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a contemptuous word for idols, perhaps related to the word for dung. Also in verse 12.
[^13]: See 3:20; 14:3.
[^14]: Literally "I have lifted my hand", a gesture of swearing an oath (20:5–6).
[^15]: See 40:46. The other Levites had served at the local shrines, the high places, before Josiah's reform (2 Kings 23:8–9).
[^16]: Hebrew *pe'er*, a headdress, the word used for the priests' caps in Exodus 39:28.
[^17]: That is, pass on holiness to the people by contact (Exodus 29:37; 30:29; Leviticus 6:20, 6:27 in most English Bibles).
[^18]: Literally "He". The Hebrew moves between singular and plural in verses 25–27.
[^19]: Hebrew *chatta't*. Or "purification offering" (40:39). Also in verse 29.
[^20]: Hebrew *minchah*, "gift, tribute": an offering of flour or grain (Leviticus 2).
[^21]: Hebrew *asham*, perhaps "reparation offering" (40:39).
[^22]: Hebrew *cherem*: something irrevocably given over to Yahweh (Leviticus 27:21, 28; Numbers 18:14).
[^23]: Hebrew *terumah*: something set apart and given for a sacred purpose (Exodus 25:2; Numbers 18:8).
[^24]: Hebrew *arisot*, probably coarse meal or dough (Numbers 15:20).
