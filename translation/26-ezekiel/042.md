**1** Then he brought me out into the outer court, by the way toward the north, and he brought me to the rooms opposite the separate yard[^1] and opposite the building on the north.

**2** Their length along the front, where the doorway faced north, was a hundred cubits,[^2] and the width was fifty cubits.[^3]

**3** Opposite the twenty cubits that belonged to the inner court,[^4] and opposite the pavement that belonged to the outer court, was gallery[^5] facing gallery, in three stories.

**4** In front of the rooms was a passage ten cubits wide, on the inner side, a way of one cubit,[^6] and their doorways were on the north.

**5** The upper rooms were narrower, for the galleries took more space from them than from the lower and the middle rooms of the building.

**6** For they were in three stories and had no pillars like the pillars of the courts. So the upper rooms were set back from the ground more than the lower and the middle ones.

**7** There was an outer wall parallel to the rooms, toward the outer court, in front of the rooms; it was fifty cubits long.

**8** For the rooms on the side of the outer court were fifty cubits long, while those facing the main hall[^7] were a hundred cubits.

**9** Below these rooms was the entryway from the east, as one enters them from the outer court.

**10** Along the width of the wall of the court toward the east,[^8] facing the separate yard and facing the building, were rooms,

**11** with a passage in front of them. They looked like the rooms on the north side; they had the same length and width, with all the same exits, arrangements and doorways.

**12** Like the doorways of the rooms on the south side was a doorway at the head of the passage, the passage in front of the matching wall,[^9] toward the east, as one enters them.

**13** Then he said to me, "The north rooms and the south rooms facing the separate yard are the holy rooms, where the priests who come near to Yahweh[^10] are to eat the most holy offerings.[^11] There they are to put the most holy offerings, the grain offering,[^12] the sin offering[^13] and the guilt offering,[^14] for the place is holy.

**14** When the priests enter, they must not go out of the holy place into the outer court. They are to leave there the garments in which they minister, for these are holy, and they are to put on other garments before they go near the area for the people."

**15** When he had finished measuring the inner house,[^15] he brought me out by the gate that faced east and measured the area all around.

**16** He measured the east side[^16] with the measuring reed: five hundred reeds[^17] by the measuring reed, all around.

**17** He measured the north side: five hundred reeds by the measuring reed, all around.

**18** He measured the south side: five hundred reeds by the measuring reed.

**19** He turned to the west side and measured: five hundred reeds by the measuring reed.

**20** He measured it on the four sides. It had a wall all around, five hundred long and five hundred wide, to separate the holy from the common.

[^1]: Hebrew *gizrah*, "cut-off area": the enclosed yard behind the temple (41:12). Also in verses 10 and 13.
[^2]: The long cubit of 40:5, about 21 inches (53 cm). A reed (verse 16) was six such cubits, about 10½ feet (3.2 m).
[^3]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. Literally "To the face of the length of the hundred cubits, the doorway of the north, and the width fifty cubits."
[^4]: The open space of twenty cubits around the temple (41:10).
[^5]: Hebrew *attiq*, a word of uncertain meaning, perhaps "gallery", "terrace" or "passage" (41:15). Also in verse 5.
[^6]: The Septuagint and the Syriac read "a hundred cubits long".
[^7]: Hebrew *heikhal*, the main room of the temple (41:1).
[^8]: The Septuagint reads "toward the south", which fits verses 12–13.
[^9]: Hebrew *haginah*, a word found only here, perhaps "matching". The meaning of this verse is uncertain.
[^10]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^11]: Literally "the holy things of the holy things": the offerings of the highest degree of holiness, which only the priests could eat, and only in a holy place (Leviticus 6:9–11, 6:16–18 in most English Bibles).
[^12]: Hebrew *minchah*, "gift, tribute": an offering of flour or grain (Leviticus 2).
[^13]: Hebrew *chatta't*. Or "purification offering" (40:39).
[^14]: Hebrew *asham*, perhaps "reparation offering" (40:39).
[^15]: That is, the temple area inside the outer wall (40:5).
[^16]: Hebrew *ruach*, "wind", here a direction or side. Also in verses 17–20.
[^17]: The Hebrew text is written "five cubits" but read "five hundred". Five hundred reeds would be 3,000 cubits, nearly a mile (1.6 km). Many think five hundred cubits are meant here, as in 45:2: about 875 feet (265 m).
