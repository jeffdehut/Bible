**1** On the third day Esther put on her royal robes[^1] and stood in the inner court of the king's palace, opposite the king's hall. The king was sitting on his royal throne in the royal hall, facing the entrance of the hall.

**2** When the king saw Queen Esther standing in the court, she won his favor, and the king held out to Esther the gold scepter that was in his hand. So Esther came near and touched the tip of the scepter.

**3** The king said to her, "What is it, Queen Esther? What is your request? Even if it is half the kingdom, it will be given to you."

**4** Esther said, "If it pleases the king, let the king and Haman come today to the banquet that I have prepared for him."

**5** The king said, "Bring Haman quickly, so that we may do what Esther has asked." So the king and Haman came to the banquet that Esther had prepared.

**6** As they were drinking wine,[^2] the king said to Esther, "What is your petition? It will be granted to you. And what is your request? Even if it is half the kingdom, it will be done."

**7** Esther answered, "My petition and my request is this:

**8** If I have found favor in the eyes of the king, and if it pleases the king to grant my petition and to do what I request, let the king and Haman come to the banquet that I will prepare for them, and tomorrow I will do as the king has said."

**9** Haman went out that day joyful and in good spirits. But when Haman saw Mordecai in the king's gate, and Mordecai neither stood up nor trembled before him, Haman was filled with rage against Mordecai.

**10** Nevertheless Haman restrained himself and went home. He sent for his friends and his wife Zeresh and brought them in.

**11** Haman told them of the glory of his wealth, the number of his sons, and all the ways the king had honored him and promoted him above the officials and the king's servants.

**12** Haman said, "What is more, Queen Esther let no one but me come with the king to the banquet she prepared. And tomorrow too I am invited by her, together with the king.

**13** Yet all this is worth nothing to me, as long as I see Mordecai the Jew sitting in the king's gate."

**14** Then his wife Zeresh and all his friends said to him, "Have a pole[^3] made, fifty cubits high,[^4] and in the morning tell the king to have Mordecai hanged on it. Then go with the king to the banquet in good spirits." The idea pleased Haman, and he had the pole made.

[^1]: Literally "Esther put on royalty".
[^2]: Literally "At the banquet of wine". Also in 7:2.
[^3]: Hebrew *ets*, "tree, wood": a pole or stake; see 2:23.
[^4]: A cubit was about 18 inches (45 cm), so fifty cubits was about 75 feet (23 meters).
