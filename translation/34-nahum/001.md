**1** An oracle[^1] concerning Nineveh.[^2] The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.[^3]

**2** The LORD[^4] is a jealous[^5] and avenging God; the LORD is avenging and full of wrath.[^6] The LORD takes vengeance on his adversaries and keeps his anger for his enemies.

**3** The LORD is slow to anger and great in power, and he will by no means leave the guilty unpunished.[^7] The LORD's way is in the whirlwind and the storm, and the clouds are the dust of his feet.

**4** He rebukes the sea and dries it up; he makes all the rivers run dry. Bashan and Carmel wither, and the blossom of Lebanon fades.[^8]

**5** The mountains quake before him, and the hills melt away. The earth heaves[^9] at his presence, the world and all who live in it.

**6** Who can stand before his fury? Who can endure the heat of his anger? His wrath is poured out like fire, and the rocks are shattered before him.

**7** The LORD is good, a stronghold in the day of distress. He knows[^10] those who take refuge in him.

**8** But with an overwhelming flood he will make a complete end of her place,[^11] and he will pursue his enemies into darkness.

**9** What are you plotting against the LORD?[^12] He will make a complete end; distress will not rise up a second time.

**10** For like tangled thorns, and drunk as with their drink,[^13] they will be consumed like dry stubble, completely.

**11** From you has come one who plots evil against the LORD, a counselor of wickedness.[^14]

**12** This is what the LORD says: Though they are at full strength and many, even so they will be cut down and pass away.[^15] Though I have afflicted you, I will afflict you no more.[^16]

**13** And now I will break his yoke from off you and tear off your bonds.

**14** The LORD has given a command concerning you:[^17] No more descendants will bear your name.[^18] From the house of your gods I will cut off the carved image and the cast idol. I will prepare your grave, for you are worthless.[^19]

[^1]: Hebrew *massa*, from the verb "to lift up, carry": a message "lifted up" by the prophet, or a "burden" laid on a nation. Traditionally "the burden of Nineveh".
[^2]: The capital of Assyria (Jonah 1:2). Nineveh fell to the Babylonians and the Medes in 612 BC. Nahum also speaks of the fall of Thebes in Egypt, in 663 BC, as past (3:8), so he prophesied between those dates.
[^3]: *Nahum* means "comfort". The town of Elkosh is unknown.
[^4]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^5]: Hebrew *qanno*, "jealous, zealous", the God who demands the loyalty of his people and defends them (Exodus 20:5; 34:14). Verses 2–8 form a partial acrostic poem: their lines begin with the letters of the Hebrew alphabet in order, from *aleph* to *kaph*, with some gaps.
[^6]: Literally "a lord of wrath".
[^7]: The words echo the LORD's description of himself in Exodus 34:6–7 (compare Numbers 14:18), but where Exodus has "abounding in faithful love", Nahum has "great in power".
[^8]: Bashan, Carmel and Lebanon were famous for their pastures, their forests and their fruitfulness.
[^9]: Literally "lifts itself up".
[^10]: Or "cares for".
[^11]: That is, Nineveh's place. The Hebrew is feminine.
[^12]: Or "What do you think of the LORD?" The speaker may be addressing Nineveh or Judah.
[^13]: The Hebrew of this verse is very difficult. Literally "For until thorns entangled, and as their drink drunk, they are consumed like dry stubble, full".
[^14]: Hebrew *beliyya'al*, "worthlessness", used of what is wicked and destructive. *You* is Nineveh, and the plotter may be a king of Assyria.
[^15]: The Hebrew is difficult. *They* are the Assyrians.
[^16]: *You* (feminine) is Judah, also in verse 13.
[^17]: *You* (masculine) is the king of Assyria.
[^18]: Literally "No more of your name will be sown".
[^19]: Or "for you are contemptible", literally "for you are light", of no weight. In most English Bibles chapter 1 has a verse 15, which is 2:1 in the Hebrew.
