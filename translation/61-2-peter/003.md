**1** Dear friends,[^1] this is now the second letter I am writing to you.[^2] In both of them I am trying to stir up your sincere minds by way of reminder,

**2** so that you will remember the words spoken beforehand by the holy prophets, and the command of the Lord and Savior given through your apostles.[^3]

**3** Above all, you must understand this: in the last days scoffers will come with their scoffing,[^4] following their own desires,

**4** and saying, "Where is the promise of his coming? Ever since the ancestors fell asleep,[^5] everything has continued as it was from the beginning of creation."

**5** For they deliberately overlook this: long ago, by the word of God, the heavens existed and the earth was formed out of water and through water.[^6]

**6** Through these[^7] the world of that time was flooded with water and perished.

**7** But by the same word[^8] the present heavens and earth have been stored up for fire, being kept for the day of judgment and destruction of the ungodly.

**8** But do not overlook this one thing, dear friends: with the Lord one day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years like one day.[^9]

**9** The Lord is not slow about his promise, as some count slowness, but he is patient with you,[^10] not wanting anyone to perish, but everyone to come to repentance.

**10** But the day of the Lord will come like a thief.[^11] On that day the heavens will pass away with a roar, the elements[^12] will be dissolved in the heat, and the earth and the works done on it will be found out.[^13]

**11** Since all these things are to be dissolved in this way, what kind of people you ought to be, in holy conduct and godliness,

**12** as you wait for the coming of the day of God and hasten it![^14] Because of it the heavens will be set on fire and dissolved, and the elements will melt in the heat.[^15]

**13** But according to his promise we are waiting for new heavens and a new earth,[^16] where righteousness lives.

**14** So, dear friends, since you are waiting for these things, make every effort to be found by him without spot or blemish,[^17] at peace.

**15** And regard the patience of our Lord as salvation, just as our dear brother Paul also wrote to you, according to the wisdom given to him,[^18]

**16** as he does in all his letters when he speaks in them of these matters. There are some things in them[^19] that are hard to understand, which the ignorant and unstable twist, as they do the other scriptures, to their own destruction.[^20]

**17** So you, dear friends, since you know this in advance, be on your guard, so that you are not carried away by the error of lawless people and lose your own firm footing.

**18** But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To him be the glory both now and to the day of eternity.[^21] Amen.[^22]

[^1]: Literally "Beloved"; also in verses 8, 14, 15 ("dear brother") and 17.
[^2]: The first may be 1 Peter, or a letter now lost.
[^3]: Literally "and of the command of your apostles, of the Lord and Savior". Verses 2–3 parallel Jude 17–18.
[^4]: Many manuscripts leave out "with their scoffing".
[^5]: That is, died. *The ancestors*: literally "the fathers", the ancestors of Israel, or perhaps the first generation of believers.
[^6]: Compare Genesis 1:2, 6–10. *Through water*: or "by means of water".
[^7]: That is, the water and the word of God, or the waters.
[^8]: Many manuscripts read "by his word".
[^9]: Compare Psalm 90:4, "For a thousand years in your sight are like yesterday when it has passed".
[^10]: Many manuscripts read "with us".
[^11]: Many manuscripts add "in the night". Compare Matthew 24:43; 1 Thessalonians 5:2.
[^12]: Greek *stoicheia*, "elements": the basic substances of which the world is made, or the heavenly bodies. Also in verse 12.
[^13]: Or "will be laid bare", exposed to God's judgment. Many manuscripts read "will be burned up". Some editions read, by a conjectural change, "will not be found".
[^14]: Or "and eagerly desire it".
[^15]: Compare Isaiah 34:4, "All the army of the heavens will rot away".
[^16]: Isaiah 65:17; 66:22.
[^17]: The opposite of the false teachers, "blots and blemishes" (2:13).
[^18]: It is not known which of Paul's letters is meant.
[^19]: Greek *en hais*, "in which", referring to the letters. Many manuscripts read *en hois*, "in which things", referring to the matters Paul writes about.
[^20]: This is the only place in the New Testament that refers to a collection of Paul's letters. *The other scriptures* sets them alongside the scriptures, probably the writings of the Old Testament; interpreters differ on what this implies about how Paul's letters were regarded when this was written.
[^21]: Literally "to the day of the age", that is, forever.
[^22]: Some manuscripts leave out "Amen".
