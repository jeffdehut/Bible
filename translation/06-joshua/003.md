**1** Early in the morning Joshua got up, and they set out from Shittim and came to the Jordan, he and all the Israelites. They camped there for the night before they crossed over.

**2** At the end of three days the officials went through the camp

**3** and commanded the people, "When you see the ark of the covenant[^1] of Yahweh[^2] your God, with the Levitical priests[^3] carrying it, you are to set out from your place and follow it.

**4** But keep a distance between you and it of about two thousand cubits[^4] by measure. Do not come near it. Then you will know the way you are to go, for you have not passed this way before."[^5]

**5** Joshua said to the people, "Consecrate yourselves,[^6] for tomorrow Yahweh will do wonders among you."

**6** Joshua said to the priests, "Take up the ark of the covenant and cross over ahead of the people." So they took up the ark of the covenant and went ahead of the people.

**7** Yahweh said to Joshua, "Today I will begin to make you great in the eyes of all Israel, so that they may know that as I was with Moses, so I will be with you.

**8** You are to command the priests who carry the ark of the covenant: 'When you come to the edge of the water of the Jordan, stand still in the Jordan.'"

**9** Joshua said to the Israelites, "Come here and listen to the words of Yahweh your God."

**10** Joshua said, "By this you will know that the living God is among you, and that he will certainly drive out before you the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Hivites, the Perizzites, the Girgashites, the Amorites and the Jebusites.

**11** Look, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth[^7] is crossing over ahead of you into the Jordan.

**12** Now choose twelve men from the tribes of Israel, one man from each tribe.

**13** When the soles of the feet of the priests who carry the ark of Yahweh, the Lord of all the earth, come to rest in the water of the Jordan, the water of the Jordan will be cut off. The water flowing down from upstream will stand up in a single heap."[^8]

**14** So the people set out from their tents to cross the Jordan, with the priests carrying the ark of the covenant ahead of the people.

**15** Now the Jordan overflows all its banks throughout the time of harvest.[^9] But as soon as those carrying the ark came to the Jordan, and the feet of the priests carrying the ark dipped into the edge of the water,

**16** the water flowing down from upstream stood still. It rose up in a single heap a great distance away, from Adam,[^10] the town beside Zarethan, while the water flowing down to the Sea of the Arabah,[^11] the Salt Sea,[^12] was completely cut off. And the people crossed over opposite Jericho.

**17** The priests who carried the ark of the covenant of Yahweh stood firm on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan, while all Israel crossed over on dry ground, until the whole nation had finished crossing the Jordan.

[^1]: Hebrew *aron*, "chest, box": the gold-covered chest that held the stone tablets of the covenant (Exodus 25:10–22; Deuteronomy 10:1–5).
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "the priests, the Levites".
[^4]: A cubit was about 18 inches (45 cm), so the distance was about 1,000 yards (900 meters).
[^5]: Literally "yesterday and the day before".
[^6]: That is, make yourselves ready to meet God by washing and keeping away from anything unclean (compare Exodus 19:10–15).
[^7]: Literally "the ark of the covenant, the Lord of all the earth".
[^8]: Hebrew *ned*, "heap", the word used of the sea in Exodus 15:8. Also in verse 16.
[^9]: The barley harvest, in spring, when the river was swollen with rain and melted snow.
[^10]: The Hebrew text is written "at Adam" but read "from Adam".
[^11]: Hebrew *aravah*: the deep rift valley of the Jordan and the Dead Sea, and its continuation south to the Gulf of Aqaba.
[^12]: The Dead Sea.
