**1** Joshua son of Nun secretly sent two men from Shittim[^1] as spies. "Go and look over the land," he said, "and Jericho." So they went and came to the house of a prostitute[^2] named Rahab, and they stayed[^3] there.

**2** The king of Jericho was told, "Look, some men from the Israelites have come here tonight to search out the land."

**3** So the king of Jericho sent word to Rahab: "Bring out the men who came to you, who came into your house, for they have come to search out the whole land."

**4** But the woman had taken the two men and hidden them.[^4] She said, "Yes, the men came to me, but I did not know where they were from.

**5** When the gate was about to be shut at dark, the men went out. I do not know where the men went. Chase after them quickly, for you will catch up with them."

**6** But she had taken them up to the roof and hidden them under the stalks of flax[^5] that she had laid out on the roof.

**7** The men chased after them on the road to the Jordan, as far as the fords. And as soon as the pursuers had gone out after them, the gate was shut.

**8** Before the men lay down for the night, she went up to them on the roof

**9** and said to the men, "I know that the LORD[^6] has given you the land, that terror of you has fallen on us, and that all who live in the land are melting in fear before you.

**10** For we have heard how the LORD dried up the water of the Red Sea[^7] before you when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites east of the Jordan,[^8] Sihon and Og, whom you devoted to destruction.[^9]

**11** When we heard it, our hearts melted, and no one had any courage left because of you,[^10] for the LORD your God is God in the heavens above and on the earth below.

**12** So now, please swear to me by the LORD that, since I have shown you faithful love,[^11] you will also show faithful love to my father's household. Give me a sure sign

**13** that you will spare the lives of my father and my mother, my brothers and my sisters,[^12] and everyone who belongs to them, and that you will save our lives from death."

**14** The men said to her, "Our lives for yours, even to death![^13] If you do not tell anyone about this business of ours, then when the LORD gives us the land, we will treat you with faithful love and faithfulness."

**15** Then she let them down by a rope through the window, for her house was built into the town wall,[^14] and she lived in the wall.

**16** She said to them, "Go to the hill country, so that the pursuers do not come across you. Hide there for three days, until the pursuers return. After that you can go on your way."

**17** The men said to her, "We will be free of this oath of yours that you have made us swear.

**18** When we come into the land, you must tie this cord[^15] of scarlet thread in the window through which you let us down, and you must gather your father and your mother, your brothers and all your father's household into the house with you.

**19** If anyone goes out of the doors of your house into the street, his blood will be on his own head,[^16] and we will be free of guilt. But if a hand is laid on anyone who is with you in the house, his blood will be on our heads.

**20** And if you tell anyone about this business of ours, we will be free of the oath you have made us swear."

**21** She said, "Let it be as you say." She sent them away, and they left. And she tied the scarlet cord in the window.

**22** They went and came to the hill country, and they stayed there three days, until the pursuers had returned. The pursuers searched all along the road but did not find them.

**23** Then the two men turned back. They came down from the hill country, crossed the river and came to Joshua son of Nun, and they told him everything that had happened to them.

**24** They said to Joshua, "The LORD has given the whole land into our hands. All who live in the land are melting in fear before us."

[^1]: *Shittim* means "acacia trees". It was Israel's camp in the plains of Moab, east of the Jordan (Numbers 25:1).
[^2]: Hebrew *zonah*. The Aramaic Targum calls her an innkeeper.
[^3]: Literally "lay down".
[^4]: Literally "hidden him", perhaps meaning each of them.
[^5]: Literally "flax of the tree", probably the stalks of flax laid out to dry.
[^6]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^7]: Hebrew *Yam Suph*, literally "Sea of Reeds". The Greek Septuagint calls it "the Red Sea".
[^8]: Literally "beyond the Jordan".
[^9]: Hebrew *charam*: to give something over completely to God, which in war meant destroying it and keeping nothing for oneself. See Numbers 21:21–35.
[^10]: Literally "no spirit rose up any more in anyone before you".
[^11]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Also in verse 14.
[^12]: The Hebrew text is written "my sister" but read "my sisters".
[^13]: Literally "Our life in place of yours, to die".
[^14]: Literally "in the side of the wall".
[^15]: Hebrew *tiqvah*, "cord". The same word means "hope". Also in verse 21.
[^16]: That is, he will be responsible for his own death.
