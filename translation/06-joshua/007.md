**1** But the Israelites acted unfaithfully in the matter of what was devoted to destruction.[^1] Achan[^2] son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took some of what was devoted, and the anger of the LORD[^3] burned against the Israelites.

**2** Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai,[^4] which is near Beth-aven, east of Bethel, and said to them, "Go up and spy out the land." So the men went up and spied out Ai.

**3** They came back to Joshua and said to him, "Not all the people need to go up. Let about two thousand or three thousand men go up and attack Ai. Do not make all the people toil up there, for the people of Ai are few."

**4** So about three thousand men from the people went up there, but they fled before the men of Ai.

**5** The men of Ai struck down about thirty-six of them. They chased them from the gate as far as Shebarim,[^5] and struck them down on the slope. The hearts of the people melted and became like water.

**6** Joshua tore his clothes and fell face down on the ground before the ark of the LORD until evening, he and the elders of Israel, and they threw dust on their heads.

**7** Joshua said, "Ah, Lord GOD![^6] Why did you bring this people across the Jordan at all, only to hand us over to the Amorites to destroy us? If only we had been content to stay on the other side of the Jordan!

**8** Please, Lord, what can I say, now that Israel has turned its back[^7] before its enemies?

**9** The Canaanites and all who live in the land will hear of it, and they will surround us and wipe our name off the earth. Then what will you do for your great name?"

**10** The LORD said to Joshua, "Get up! Why are you lying face down like this?

**11** Israel has sinned. They have broken my covenant, which I commanded them to keep. They have taken some of what was devoted to destruction. They have stolen it, they have lied about it, and they have put it among their own belongings.

**12** That is why the Israelites cannot stand against their enemies. They turn their backs before their enemies, because they themselves have become devoted to destruction. I will not be with you any more unless you destroy what is devoted to destruction from among you.

**13** Get up and consecrate the people. Say, 'Consecrate yourselves for tomorrow, for this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: "Things devoted to destruction are among you, Israel. You cannot stand against your enemies until you remove what is devoted to destruction from among you."'

**14** In the morning you are to come forward tribe by tribe. The tribe that the LORD takes[^8] is to come forward clan by clan. The clan that the LORD takes is to come forward household by household. And the household that the LORD takes is to come forward man by man.

**15** The one who is taken with the devoted things is to be burned with fire, he and everything he has, because he has broken the covenant of the LORD and has done a disgraceful thing in Israel."

**16** Joshua got up early in the morning and had Israel come forward tribe by tribe, and the tribe of Judah was taken.

**17** He had the clans of Judah[^9] come forward, and the clan of the Zerahites was taken.[^10] He had the clan of the Zerahites come forward man by man, and Zabdi was taken.

**18** He had Zabdi's household come forward man by man, and Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was taken.

**19** Joshua said to Achan, "My son, give glory to the LORD, the God of Israel, and give him praise.[^11] Tell me what you have done. Do not hide it from me."

**20** Achan answered Joshua, "It is true. I have sinned against the LORD, the God of Israel. This is what I did.

**21** Among the spoil I saw a beautiful robe from Shinar,[^12] two hundred shekels of silver, and a bar[^13] of gold weighing fifty shekels.[^14] I coveted them and took them. They are hidden in the ground inside my tent, with the silver underneath."

**22** So Joshua sent messengers, and they ran to the tent. There it was, hidden in his tent, with the silver underneath.

**23** They took the things out of the tent, brought them to Joshua and all the Israelites, and laid them out before the LORD.

**24** Then Joshua, and all Israel with him, took Achan son of Zerah,[^15] the silver, the robe, the bar of gold, his sons and his daughters, his cattle, his donkeys and his flock, his tent and everything he had, and they brought them up to the Valley of Achor.

**25** Joshua said, "Why have you brought disaster on us? The LORD will bring disaster on you today."[^16] And all Israel stoned him. They burned them with fire and stoned them with stones.

**26** They raised over him a great heap of stones, which is there to this day. Then the LORD turned from his burning anger. That is why the place has been called the Valley of Achor[^17] to this day.

[^1]: Hebrew *cherem*: something given over completely to God, which in war meant destroying it and keeping nothing for oneself. Also in verses 11–13 and 15; in verse 12 the related verb *charam* is also used.
[^2]: Called Achar in 1 Chronicles 2:7.
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Hebrew *ha-Ai*, "the ruin", always with the article.
[^5]: Or "as far as the quarries". *Shebarim* means "breaks".
[^6]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^7]: Literally "the back of the neck". Also in verse 12.
[^8]: Literally "captures", that is, picks out, probably by casting lots. Also in verses 15–18.
[^9]: Literally "the clan of Judah", meaning the clans of the tribe.
[^10]: Literally "he took the clan of the Zerahites".
[^11]: Or "and make your confession to him". Hebrew *todah* means both praise and confession.
[^12]: Babylonia.
[^13]: Literally "tongue". Also in verse 24.
[^14]: A shekel was about 2/5 ounce (11.5 grams), so two hundred shekels was about 5 pounds (2.3 kilograms), and fifty shekels about 1¼ pounds (575 grams).
[^15]: That is, a descendant of Zerah (verse 18).
[^16]: Hebrew *akhar*, "bring disaster, trouble", sounds like *Achan* and gives the Valley of Achor its name.
[^17]: *Achor* means "disaster, trouble".
