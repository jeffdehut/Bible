**1** The whole community of the Israelites assembled at Shiloh, and they set up the tent of meeting[^1] there. The land lay subdued before them.

**2** Seven tribes remained among the Israelites that had not yet been given their inheritance.

**3** So Joshua said to the Israelites, "How long will you put off going in to take possession of the land that the LORD,[^2] the God of your ancestors, has given you?

**4** Appoint three men from each tribe, and I will send them out. They are to set out and go through the land and write a description of it, with a view to their inheritance. Then they are to come back to me.

**5** They are to divide it into seven portions. Judah will stay in its territory in the south, and the house of Joseph will stay in its territory in the north.

**6** You are to write a description of the land in seven portions and bring it here to me, and I will cast lots for you here before the LORD our God.

**7** For the Levites have no share among you, because the priesthood of the LORD is their inheritance. And Gad, Reuben and the half-tribe of Manasseh have received their inheritance east of the Jordan,[^3] which Moses the servant of the LORD gave them."

**8** So the men set out and went. Joshua commanded those who went to write a description of the land: "Go and walk through the land and write a description of it, then come back to me, and I will cast lots for you here before the LORD in Shiloh."

**9** So the men went and passed through the land, and they wrote a description of it in a scroll, town by town, in seven portions. Then they came back to Joshua at the camp at Shiloh.

**10** Joshua cast lots for them in Shiloh before the LORD, and there Joshua divided the land among the Israelites according to their divisions.

**11** The lot for the tribe of the Benjaminites came up, clan by clan. The territory of their allotment lay between the people of Judah and the descendants of Joseph.

**12** On the north side their border began at the Jordan. The border went up to the slope north of Jericho, went up westward through the hill country, and ended at the wilderness of Beth-aven.

**13** From there the border passed along to Luz, to the slope south of Luz, that is, Bethel. Then the border went down to Ataroth-addar, on the hill south of Lower Beth-horon.

**14** On the west side the border curved and turned south, from the hill that faces Beth-horon on the south, and it ended at Kiriath-baal, that is, Kiriath-jearim, a town of the people of Judah. This was the west side.

**15** The south side began at the edge of Kiriath-jearim. The border went out westward[^4] and went out to the spring of the waters of Nephtoah.

**16** The border went down to the edge of the hill that faces the Valley of the Son of Hinnom, at the north end of the Valley of Rephaim. It went down the Valley of Hinnom to the southern slope of the Jebusite town[^5] and went down to En-rogel.

**17** It curved northward, went out to En-shemesh, and went out to Geliloth, which is opposite the Ascent of Adummim. Then it went down to the Stone of Bohan son of Reuben.

**18** It passed along to the northern slope opposite the Arabah,[^6] and went down into the Arabah.

**19** The border passed along to the northern slope of Beth-hoglah, and the border ended at the northern bay of the Salt Sea,[^7] at the southern end of the Jordan. This was the southern border.

**20** The Jordan formed its border on the east side. This was the inheritance of the Benjaminites, clan by clan, with its borders all around.

**21** The towns of the tribe of the Benjaminites, clan by clan, were: Jericho, Beth-hoglah, Emek-keziz,

**22** Beth-arabah, Zemaraim, Bethel,

**23** Avvim, Parah, Ophrah,

**24** Chephar-ammonah,[^8] Ophni and Geba: twelve towns, with their villages.[^9]

**25** Gibeon, Ramah, Beeroth,

**26** Mizpeh, Chephirah, Mozah,

**27** Rekem, Irpeel, Taralah,

**28** Zela, Haeleph, the Jebusite town, that is, Jerusalem, Gibeath[^10] and Kiriath: fourteen towns, with their villages. This was the inheritance of the Benjaminites, clan by clan.

[^1]: The tent where the LORD met with his people, that is, the tabernacle (Exodus 33:7; 40:34–35).
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise".
[^4]: Literally "toward the sea". The direction is unexpected here; compare 15:9.
[^5]: Literally "to the shoulder of the Jebusite on the south". Also in verse 28.
[^6]: Hebrew *aravah*: the deep rift valley of the Jordan and the Dead Sea, and its continuation south to the Gulf of Aqaba.
[^7]: The Dead Sea.
[^8]: The Hebrew text is written "Chephar-ammoni" but read "Chephar-ammonah".
[^9]: Hebrew *chatserim*: settlements without walls around a town. Also in verse 28.
[^10]: That is, Gibeah. *Kiriath*, "town", may be short for Kiriath-jearim (verse 14).
