**1** When the whole nation had finished crossing the Jordan, Yahweh[^1] said to Joshua,

**2** "Choose twelve men from the people, one man from each tribe,

**3** and command them, 'Take up twelve stones from here, from the middle of the Jordan, from the place where the priests' feet stood firm. Carry them over with you and put them down at the place where you camp tonight.'"

**4** So Joshua called the twelve men he had appointed from the Israelites, one man from each tribe.

**5** Joshua said to them, "Cross over ahead of the ark of Yahweh your God into the middle of the Jordan. Each of you is to lift a stone onto his shoulder, one for each of the tribes of the Israelites,

**6** so that this may be a sign among you. In time to come,[^2] when your children ask, 'What do these stones mean to you?'

**7** you are to tell them that the water of the Jordan was cut off before the ark of the covenant of Yahweh. When it crossed the Jordan, the water of the Jordan was cut off. These stones are to be a memorial for the Israelites forever."

**8** The Israelites did as Joshua commanded. They took up twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, one for each of the tribes of the Israelites, as Yahweh had told Joshua. They carried them over with them to the camp and put them down there.

**9** Joshua also set up twelve stones[^3] in the middle of the Jordan, at the place where the feet of the priests who carried the ark of the covenant had stood. They are there to this day.

**10** The priests who carried the ark stood in the middle of the Jordan until everything was done that Yahweh had commanded Joshua to tell the people, just as Moses had commanded Joshua. And the people crossed over in haste.

**11** When all the people had finished crossing, the ark of Yahweh and the priests crossed over in front of the people.

**12** The Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh crossed over armed[^4] ahead of the Israelites, as Moses had told them.

**13** About forty thousand men equipped for war crossed over before Yahweh for battle, to the plains of Jericho.

**14** On that day Yahweh made Joshua great in the eyes of all Israel, and they held him in awe all the days of his life, as they had held Moses in awe.

**15** Yahweh said to Joshua,

**16** "Command the priests who carry the ark of the testimony[^5] to come up out of the Jordan."

**17** So Joshua commanded the priests, "Come up out of the Jordan."

**18** When the priests who carried the ark of the covenant of Yahweh came up from the middle of the Jordan, and the soles of the priests' feet stepped out onto dry ground, the water of the Jordan returned to its place and flowed over all its banks as before.

**19** The people came up out of the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month,[^6] and they camped at Gilgal, on the eastern edge of Jericho.

**20** Joshua set up at Gilgal the twelve stones they had taken from the Jordan.

**21** He said to the Israelites, "In time to come, when your children ask their fathers, 'What are these stones?'

**22** you are to tell your children, 'Israel crossed this Jordan on dry ground.'

**23** For Yahweh your God dried up the water of the Jordan before you until you had crossed over, just as Yahweh your God did to the Red Sea,[^7] which he dried up before us until we had crossed over,

**24** so that all the peoples of the earth may know that the hand of Yahweh is strong, and so that you may fear Yahweh your God forever."[^8]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "tomorrow". Also in verse 21.
[^3]: That is, a second set of twelve stones, set up in the riverbed itself. The Greek Septuagint reads "other twelve stones".
[^4]: Hebrew *chamushim*; see 1:14.
[^5]: The stone tablets of the covenant are called "the testimony" (Exodus 25:16), so the ark of the covenant is also called "the ark of the testimony".
[^6]: The month of Abib, around March–April. On the tenth day of this month the Passover lamb was chosen (Exodus 12:3).
[^7]: Hebrew *Yam Suph*, literally "Sea of Reeds". The Greek Septuagint calls it "the Red Sea".
[^8]: Literally "all the days".
