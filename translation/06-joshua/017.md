**1** This was the allotment[^1] for the tribe of Manasseh, for he was Joseph's firstborn. Machir, Manasseh's firstborn and the father of Gilead, had received Gilead and Bashan, because he was a warrior.

**2** The allotment was also for the rest of the Manassites, clan by clan: for the descendants of Abiezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Hepher and Shemida. These were the male descendants of Manasseh son of Joseph, clan by clan.

**3** Now Zelophehad son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, had no sons, only daughters. These are the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah.

**4** They came before Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun and the leaders, and said, "Yahweh[^2] commanded Moses to give us an inheritance among our brothers."[^3] So, as Yahweh had commanded, Joshua gave them an inheritance among their father's brothers.

**5** So ten portions[^4] fell to Manasseh, besides the land of Gilead and Bashan, which is east of the Jordan,[^5]

**6** because the daughters of Manasseh received an inheritance among his sons. The land of Gilead belonged to the rest of the Manassites.

**7** The territory of Manasseh went from Asher[^6] to Michmethath, which faces Shechem. Then the border went south[^7] to the inhabitants of En-tappuah.

**8** The land of Tappuah belonged to Manasseh, but Tappuah itself, on the border of Manasseh, belonged to the Ephraimites.

**9** The border went down to the Kanah Valley.[^8] South of the valley, these towns belonged to Ephraim, among the towns of Manasseh. The border of Manasseh ran along the north side of the valley and ended at the sea.

**10** The land to the south belonged to Ephraim and the land to the north to Manasseh, with the sea as its border. They reached Asher on the north and Issachar on the east.

**11** Within Issachar and Asher, Manasseh had Beth-shean and its surrounding towns,[^9] Ibleam and its surrounding towns, the inhabitants of Dor and its surrounding towns, the inhabitants of En-dor and its surrounding towns, the inhabitants of Taanach and its surrounding towns, and the inhabitants of Megiddo and its surrounding towns: the three heights.[^10]

**12** But the Manassites could not take possession of these towns, for the Canaanites were determined to live in that land.

**13** When the Israelites grew strong, they put the Canaanites to forced labor, but they did not drive them out completely.

**14** The descendants of Joseph said to Joshua, "Why have you given us[^11] only one allotment and one portion as an inheritance? We are a numerous people, for Yahweh has blessed us so much."

**15** Joshua said to them, "If you are a numerous people, go up to the forest and clear land for yourselves there, in the land of the Perizzites and the Rephaim,[^12] since the hill country of Ephraim is too small for you."

**16** The descendants of Joseph said, "The hill country is not enough for us, and all the Canaanites who live in the valley land have chariots of iron,[^13] both those in Beth-shean and its surrounding towns and those in the Valley of Jezreel."

**17** Joshua said to the house of Joseph, to Ephraim and Manasseh, "You are a numerous people and very strong. You will not have only one allotment.

**18** The hill country will be yours. Though it is forest, you will clear it, and it will be yours to its farthest limits. For you will drive out the Canaanites, though they have chariots of iron and though they are strong."

[^1]: Literally "the lot", the share of land assigned by casting lots.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Numbers 27:1–11; 36:1–12.
[^4]: Hebrew *chavalim*, "measuring lines", and so measured shares of land.
[^5]: Literally "beyond the Jordan".
[^6]: Probably a town, not the tribe of Asher.
[^7]: Literally "to the right", that is, south, for someone facing east.
[^8]: Hebrew *nachal*: a valley with a stream that flows in the rainy season. Also later in the verse.
[^9]: Literally "daughters": the smaller towns that depended on it. Also in verse 16.
[^10]: Hebrew *sheloshet ha-nafet*, of uncertain meaning. It may refer to three hill towns, or to Naphath-dor (11:2; 12:23).
[^11]: Literally "me". The descendants of Joseph speak of themselves as one person in verses 14 and 16.
[^12]: An ancient people remembered for their great height (Deuteronomy 2:11; 3:11).
[^13]: Probably chariots with iron fittings. Also in verse 18.
