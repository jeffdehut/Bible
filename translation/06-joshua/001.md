**1** After the death of Moses the servant of Yahweh,[^1] Yahweh said to Joshua son of Nun, Moses' assistant,[^2]

**2** "Moses my servant is dead. Now get up and cross this Jordan, you and all this people, into the land that I am giving to them, to the Israelites.

**3** I have given you every place where the sole of your foot treads, as I promised Moses.

**4** From the wilderness and this Lebanon as far as the great river, the river Euphrates, all the land of the Hittites,[^3] and as far as the Great Sea in the west,[^4] will be your territory.

**5** No one will be able to stand against you[^5] all the days of your life. As I was with Moses, so I will be with you. I will not fail you or abandon you.

**6** Be strong and courageous, for you are the one who will lead this people to take possession of[^6] the land that I swore to their ancestors[^7] to give them.

**7** Only be strong and very courageous, and be careful to do everything in the law[^8] that Moses my servant commanded you. Do not turn from it to the right or to the left, so that you may succeed[^9] wherever you go.

**8** This book of the law must not depart from your mouth. Meditate[^10] on it day and night, so that you may be careful to do everything that is written in it. For then you will make your way prosper, and then you will succeed.

**9** Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be terrified or dismayed, for Yahweh your God is with you wherever you go."

**10** So Joshua commanded the officials[^11] of the people,

**11** "Go through the camp and command the people: 'Get provisions ready for yourselves, for in three days[^12] you will cross this Jordan to go in and take possession of the land that Yahweh your God is giving you to possess.'"

**12** To the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh Joshua said,

**13** "Remember the command that Moses the servant of Yahweh gave you: 'Yahweh your God is giving you rest, and he will give you this land.'[^13]

**14** Your wives, your little ones and your livestock may stay in the land that Moses gave you east of the Jordan.[^14] But all your fighting men[^15] are to cross over armed[^16] ahead of your brothers and help them,

**15** until Yahweh gives your brothers rest as he has given you, and they too take possession of the land that Yahweh your God is giving them. Then you may return to the land that is your possession and take possession of it, the land that Moses the servant of Yahweh gave you east of the Jordan, toward the sunrise."

**16** They answered Joshua, "Everything you have commanded us we will do, and wherever you send us we will go.

**17** Just as we obeyed Moses in everything, so we will obey you. Only may Yahweh your God be with you as he was with Moses.

**18** Anyone who rebels against your command[^17] and does not obey your words, whatever you command him, is to be put to death. Only be strong and courageous."

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *mesharet*, "attendant, servant": one who served a leader personally (Exodus 24:13; 33:11).
[^3]: Here "the Hittites" probably stands for the peoples of Syria and the north in general, as in some ancient Near Eastern texts.
[^4]: Literally "the Great Sea, the going in of the sun", that is, the Mediterranean in the west.
[^5]: Literally "No man will stand before you".
[^6]: Or "who will give this people as their inheritance". Literally "you will cause this people to inherit".
[^7]: Literally "fathers".
[^8]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law". Also in verse 8.
[^9]: Or "act wisely". The Hebrew verb *haskil* means both to act with understanding and to have success. Also at the end of verse 8.
[^10]: Hebrew *hagah*, "murmur, read aloud in a low voice", and so "ponder".
[^11]: Hebrew *shoterim*: officers who kept records and helped the leaders carry out their decisions.
[^12]: Literally "in yet three days".
[^13]: Or "and has given you this land". Compare Deuteronomy 3:18–20.
[^14]: Literally "beyond the Jordan", here seen from the west. Also in verse 15.
[^15]: Literally "all the mighty men of strength".
[^16]: Hebrew *chamushim*, probably "in battle formation". Also in 4:12.
[^17]: Literally "rebels against your mouth".
