**1** The allotment[^1] for the descendants of Joseph went from the Jordan at Jericho, east of the waters of Jericho, through the wilderness that goes up from Jericho into the hill country to Bethel.

**2** From Bethel it went out to Luz[^2] and passed along to the territory of the Archites at Ataroth.

**3** It went down westward to the territory of the Japhletites, as far as the territory of Lower Beth-horon and as far as Gezer, and it ended at the sea.

**4** So the descendants of Joseph, Manasseh and Ephraim, received their inheritance.

**5** This was the territory of the Ephraimites, clan by clan. The border of their inheritance on the east was Ataroth-addar, as far as Upper Beth-horon.

**6** The border went out westward, with Michmethath on the north. The border turned eastward to Taanath-shiloh and passed by it on the east to Janoah.

**7** From Janoah it went down to Ataroth and to Naarah, reached Jericho and went out at the Jordan.

**8** From Tappuah the border went westward to the Kanah Valley[^3] and ended at the sea. This is the inheritance of the tribe of the Ephraimites, clan by clan,

**9** together with the towns that were set apart for the Ephraimites within the inheritance of the Manassites, all those towns with their villages.[^4]

**10** But they did not drive out the Canaanites who lived in Gezer. So the Canaanites have lived among Ephraim to this day, and they were made to do forced labor.[^5]

[^1]: Literally "the lot", the share of land assigned by casting lots.
[^2]: Elsewhere Luz is another name for Bethel (18:13; Genesis 28:19).
[^3]: Hebrew *nachal*: a valley with a stream that flows in the rainy season.
[^4]: Hebrew *chatserim*: settlements without walls around a town.
[^5]: The Greek Septuagint adds that Pharaoh king of Egypt later captured Gezer and burned it (compare 1 Kings 9:16).
