**1** The LORD[^1] said to Joshua,

**2** "Tell the Israelites: 'Designate the towns of refuge that I spoke to you about through Moses,[^2]

**3** so that anyone who kills a person by mistake, without meaning to, may flee there. They will be a refuge for you from the avenger of blood.[^3]

**4** He is to flee to one of these towns, stand at the entrance of the town gate and state his case to the elders of that town. Then they are to take him into the town with them and give him a place, and he will live among them.[^4]

**5** If the avenger of blood pursues him, they must not hand the killer over to him, because he struck down his neighbor without meaning to, and he had not been his enemy before.[^5]

**6** He is to stay in that town until he has stood trial before the community, until the death of the high priest who is serving at that time. Then the killer may go back to his own town and his own home, to the town he fled from.'"

**7** So they set apart Kedesh in Galilee, in the hill country of Naphtali, Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, in the hill country of Judah.

**8** East of the Jordan, across from Jericho,[^6] they designated Bezer in the wilderness on the plateau,[^7] from the tribe of Reuben, Ramoth in Gilead from the tribe of Gad, and Golan in Bashan from the tribe of Manasseh.

**9** These were the designated towns[^8] for all the Israelites and for the foreigners[^9] living among them, so that anyone who killed a person by mistake could flee there and not die at the hand of the avenger of blood before he had stood trial before the community.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Numbers 35:9–34; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; 19:1–13.
[^3]: Hebrew *go'el ha-dam*: the relative whose duty was to avenge the death of a family member (Numbers 35:19). Also in verses 5 and 9.
[^4]: The Greek Septuagint does not have most of verses 4–6.
[^5]: Literally "and he did not hate him yesterday and the day before".
[^6]: Literally "Beyond the Jordan of Jericho, toward the sunrise".
[^7]: Hebrew *mishor*, "level land": the high tableland north of the Arnon.
[^8]: Hebrew *mu'adah*, a word found only here, probably "appointed".
[^9]: Hebrew *ger*: someone from elsewhere who lives among the Israelites, a resident foreigner.
