**1** Joshua was old, advanced in years, and the LORD[^1] said to him, "You are old and advanced in years, and a great deal of the land remains to be taken over.

**2** This is the land that remains: all the regions of the Philistines, and all the land of the Geshurites,[^2]

**3** from the Shihor,[^3] which is east of Egypt, northward to the border of Ekron, which is counted as Canaanite: the five rulers[^4] of the Philistines, those of Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath and Ekron; and the Avvim

**4** in the south; all the land of the Canaanites, and Mearah,[^5] which belongs to the Sidonians, as far as Aphek, to the border of the Amorites;

**5** the land of the Gebalites,[^6] and all Lebanon toward the sunrise, from Baal-gad below Mount Hermon to Lebo-hamath.[^7]

**6** All the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim, all the Sidonians, I myself will drive them out before the Israelites. Only allot the land to Israel as an inheritance,[^8] as I have commanded you.

**7** So now divide this land as an inheritance among the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh."

**8** With the other half of Manasseh,[^9] the Reubenites and the Gadites had received the inheritance that Moses gave them east of the Jordan, as Moses the servant of the LORD had given it to them:

**9** from Aroer, which is on the edge of the Arnon Valley,[^10] and the town that is in the middle of the valley, and the whole plateau[^11] of Medeba as far as Dibon,

**10** and all the towns of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, as far as the border of the Ammonites;

**11** and Gilead, and the territory of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan as far as Salecah,

**12** the whole kingdom of Og in Bashan, who reigned in Ashtaroth and in Edrei. He was one of the last of the Rephaim.[^12] Moses had struck them down and driven them out.

**13** But the Israelites did not drive out the Geshurites or the Maacathites, so Geshur and Maacath live among Israel to this day.

**14** Only to the tribe of Levi he gave no inheritance. The offerings by fire[^13] to the LORD, the God of Israel, are their inheritance, as he promised them.

**15** Moses gave an inheritance to the tribe of the Reubenites, clan by clan.[^14]

**16** Their territory was from Aroer, which is on the edge of the Arnon Valley, and the town that is in the middle of the valley, and the whole plateau by Medeba,

**17** Heshbon and all its towns on the plateau: Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon,

**18** Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath,

**19** Kiriathaim, Sibmah, Zereth-shahar on the hill in the valley,

**20** Beth-peor, the slopes of Pisgah, and Beth-jeshimoth,

**21** all the towns of the plateau, and the whole kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon. Moses had struck him down, together with the leaders of Midian, Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur and Reba, the princes[^15] of Sihon, who lived in the land.

**22** Along with the others they killed, the Israelites also killed Balaam son of Beor, the diviner, with the sword.[^16]

**23** The border of the Reubenites was the Jordan and its bank.[^17] This was the inheritance of the Reubenites, clan by clan, the towns and their villages.[^18]

**24** Moses gave an inheritance to the tribe of Gad, to the Gadites, clan by clan.

**25** Their territory was Jazer and all the towns of Gilead, and half the land of the Ammonites as far as Aroer, which faces Rabbah,

**26** and from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim to the border of Debir,[^19]

**27** and in the valley, Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth and Zaphon, the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon, with the Jordan and its bank as the border, up to the end of the Sea of Kinnereth,[^20] east of the Jordan.

**28** This was the inheritance of the Gadites, clan by clan, the towns and their villages.

**29** Moses gave an inheritance to the half-tribe of Manasseh. It was for half the tribe of the Manassites, clan by clan.

**30** Their territory was from Mahanaim, all Bashan, the whole kingdom of Og king of Bashan, and all Havvoth-jair,[^21] which is in Bashan, sixty towns,

**31** and half of Gilead, with Ashtaroth and Edrei, the towns of Og's kingdom in Bashan. These were for the descendants of Machir son of Manasseh, for half of the descendants of Machir, clan by clan.

**32** These are the inheritances that Moses gave out in the plains of Moab, beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho.

**33** But to the tribe of Levi Moses gave no inheritance. The LORD, the God of Israel, is their inheritance, as he promised them.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: A people in the south, near the Philistines (1 Samuel 27:8), not the Geshurites of verses 11 and 13 in the north.
[^3]: A stream on the border of Egypt. *Shihor* means "the black (river)".
[^4]: Hebrew *seranim*, a Philistine word for their rulers.
[^5]: Or "and from the cave".
[^6]: The people of Gebal, the coastal city later called Byblos.
[^7]: Or "the entrance of Hamath".
[^8]: Literally "make it fall to Israel as an inheritance", that is, assign it by lot.
[^9]: Literally "With him", that is, with the half of the tribe of Manasseh that had already received land east of the Jordan (verses 29–31).
[^10]: Hebrew *nachal*: a valley with a stream that flows in the rainy season. Also in verse 16.
[^11]: Hebrew *mishor*, "level land": the high tableland north of the Arnon. Also in verses 16, 17 and 21.
[^12]: An ancient people remembered for their great height (Deuteronomy 2:11; 3:11).
[^13]: Hebrew *ishshe*, traditionally "offerings made by fire": the offerings on the altar, of which the priests received a share (Deuteronomy 18:1).
[^14]: Literally "for their clans". Also in verses 23, 24, 28, 29 and 31.
[^15]: Hebrew *nesikhim*, perhaps "vassals". Numbers 31:8 calls them "the five kings of Midian".
[^16]: Numbers 31:8.
[^17]: Literally "the Jordan and border", that is, the river with the land beside it. Also in verse 27.
[^18]: Hebrew *chatserim*: settlements without walls around a town. Also in verse 28.
[^19]: Hebrew *Lidvir*, perhaps the town of Lo-debar (2 Samuel 9:4).
[^20]: The Sea of Galilee.
[^21]: *Havvoth-jair* means "the tent villages of Jair" (Numbers 32:41; Deuteronomy 3:14).
