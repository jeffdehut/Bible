**1** A long time afterward, when Yahweh[^1] had given Israel rest from all their enemies around them, and Joshua was old and advanced in years,

**2** Joshua summoned all Israel, their elders, their leaders,[^2] their judges and their officials, and said to them, "I am old and advanced in years.

**3** You yourselves have seen everything that Yahweh your God has done to all these nations for your sake, for it is Yahweh your God who has fought for you.

**4** See, I have allotted to you[^3] as an inheritance for your tribes these nations that remain, as well as all the nations I have wiped out, from the Jordan to the Great Sea in the west.[^4]

**5** Yahweh your God himself will push them back before you and drive them out of your way, and you will take possession of their land, as Yahweh your God promised you.

**6** So be very strong, and be careful to do everything that is written in the book of the law of Moses, without turning aside from it to the right or to the left.

**7** Do not mix with these nations, these that remain among you. Do not call on the names of their gods, or make anyone swear by them.[^5] Do not serve them or bow down to them.

**8** But hold fast to Yahweh your God, as you have done to this day.

**9** Yahweh has driven out before you great and powerful nations, and as for you, no one has been able to stand against you to this day.

**10** One of you puts a thousand to flight,[^6] for it is Yahweh your God who fights for you, as he promised you.

**11** So for your own sake[^7] take great care to love Yahweh your God.

**12** For if you ever turn back and cling to the rest of these nations, these that remain among you, and intermarry with them, and you mix with them and they with you,

**13** know for certain that Yahweh your God will no longer drive these nations out before you. They will be a snare and a trap for you, a whip on your sides and thorns in your eyes, until you perish from this good land that Yahweh your God has given you.

**14** Now I am about to go the way of all the earth.[^8] You know with all your heart and all your soul that not one word has failed of all the good promises that Yahweh your God made concerning you. All of them have come true for you. Not one word of them has failed.

**15** But just as every good promise that Yahweh your God made to you has come true for you, so Yahweh will bring on you every evil he has threatened,[^9] until he has destroyed you from this good land that Yahweh your God has given you,

**16** if you break the covenant of Yahweh your God, which he commanded you to keep, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them. Then the anger of Yahweh will burn against you, and you will quickly perish from the good land that he has given you."

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "heads".
[^3]: Literally "made fall to you", that is, assigned by lot.
[^4]: Literally "and the Great Sea, the going in of the sun".
[^5]: Hebrew *tashbi'u*, "cause to swear", that is, take or administer oaths in their names.
[^6]: Literally "pursues a thousand". Compare Leviticus 26:8; Deuteronomy 32:30.
[^7]: Literally "for your lives".
[^8]: That is, to die.
[^9]: Literally "every evil word".
