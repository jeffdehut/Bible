**1** Then Joshua summoned the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh

**2** and said to them, "You have kept everything that Moses the servant of Yahweh[^1] commanded you, and you have obeyed me in everything I commanded you.

**3** All this long time, up to this day, you have not abandoned your brothers, and you have faithfully kept the command of Yahweh your God.

**4** Now Yahweh your God has given your brothers rest, as he promised them. So now turn and go to your homes,[^2] to the land that is your possession, which Moses the servant of Yahweh gave you east of the Jordan.[^3]

**5** Only be very careful to keep the commandment and the law[^4] that Moses the servant of Yahweh gave you: to love Yahweh your God, to walk in all his ways, to keep his commandments, to hold fast to him, and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul."

**6** Then Joshua blessed them and sent them away, and they went to their homes.

**7** To one half of the tribe of Manasseh Moses had given land in Bashan, and to the other half Joshua had given land with their brothers west of the Jordan. When Joshua sent them away to their homes, he blessed them

**8** and said to them, "Go back to your homes with great wealth: with a very large number of livestock, with silver, gold, bronze and iron, and with a great quantity of clothing. Share the spoil of your enemies with your brothers."

**9** So the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh turned back and left the Israelites at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, to go to the land of Gilead, the land of their own possession, which they had acquired at Yahweh's command through Moses.

**10** When they came to Geliloth[^5] by the Jordan in the land of Canaan, the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh built an altar there by the Jordan, a large altar, impressive to look at.

**11** The Israelites heard it said, "Look, the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh have built the altar on the frontier of the land of Canaan, at Geliloth by the Jordan, on the Israelites' side."[^6]

**12** When the Israelites heard it, the whole community of the Israelites gathered at Shiloh to go up and make war against them.

**13** The Israelites sent Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead,

**14** and with him ten leaders, one leader for each family of all the tribes of Israel. Each of them was the head of his family among the clans[^7] of Israel.

**15** They came to the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead and said to them,

**16** "This is what the whole community of Yahweh says: 'What is this unfaithfulness you have committed against the God of Israel, turning away today from following Yahweh by building yourselves an altar, and so rebelling today against Yahweh?

**17** Was the wrongdoing at Peor[^8] not enough for us? We have not cleansed ourselves of it to this day, even though a plague came on the community of Yahweh.

**18** And now you are turning away today from following Yahweh! If you rebel against Yahweh today, tomorrow he will be angry with the whole community of Israel.

**19** But if the land you possess is unclean, cross over into the land that Yahweh possesses, where Yahweh's tabernacle[^9] stands, and take possession of land among us. Only do not rebel against Yahweh, and do not rebel against us,[^10] by building yourselves an altar other than the altar of Yahweh our God.

**20** When Achan son of Zerah acted unfaithfully in the matter of what was devoted to destruction,[^11] did not wrath fall on the whole community of Israel? And he was not the only one to die for his wrongdoing.'"

**21** Then the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh answered the heads of the clans of Israel:

**22** "The Mighty One, God, Yahweh! The Mighty One, God, Yahweh![^12] He knows, and let Israel know! If this was in rebellion or in unfaithfulness against Yahweh, do not spare us[^13] today.

**23** If we have built ourselves an altar to turn away from following Yahweh, or to offer burnt offerings[^14] and grain offerings[^15] on it, or to make fellowship sacrifices[^16] on it, may Yahweh himself call us to account.

**24** No, we did it because we were anxious about something. In time to come[^17] your descendants might say to our descendants, 'What do you have to do with Yahweh, the God of Israel?

**25** Yahweh has made the Jordan a border between us and you, you Reubenites and Gadites. You have no share in Yahweh.' So your descendants might make our descendants stop fearing Yahweh.

**26** So we said, 'Let us now build an altar, not for burnt offerings or for sacrifices,

**27** but to be a witness between us and you, and between our generations after us, that we will worship Yahweh in his presence with our burnt offerings, our sacrifices and our fellowship offerings. Then your descendants will not be able to say to our descendants in time to come, "You have no share in Yahweh."'

**28** And we said, 'If they ever say this to us or to our descendants in time to come, we will say, "Look at this copy of the altar of Yahweh, which our ancestors made, not for burnt offerings or for sacrifices, but as a witness between us and you."'

**29** Far be it from us to rebel against Yahweh and turn away today from following Yahweh by building an altar for burnt offerings, grain offerings or sacrifices, other than the altar of Yahweh our God that stands in front of his tabernacle!"

**30** Phinehas the priest, the leaders of the community and the heads of the clans of Israel who were with him heard what the Reubenites, the Gadites and the Manassites said, and they were satisfied.[^18]

**31** Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest said to the Reubenites, the Gadites and the Manassites, "Today we know that Yahweh is among us, because you have not committed this unfaithfulness against Yahweh. Now you have rescued the Israelites from the hand of Yahweh."

**32** Then Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest and the leaders returned from the Reubenites and the Gadites in the land of Gilead to the land of Canaan, to the Israelites, and brought back word to them.

**33** The Israelites were satisfied with the report, and the Israelites blessed God. They spoke no more of going up to make war against them, to destroy the land where the Reubenites and the Gadites lived.

**34** The Reubenites and the Gadites gave the altar its name:[^19] "It is a witness between us that Yahweh is God."

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "to your tents". Also in verses 6–8.
[^3]: Literally "beyond the Jordan". In verse 7, "west of the Jordan" is literally "beyond the Jordan toward the sea".
[^4]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law".
[^5]: Or "the region". Hebrew *gelilot*, "circles", perhaps of stones. Also in verse 11.
[^6]: Literally "on the side of the Israelites", that is, on the west bank of the Jordan.
[^7]: Hebrew *alafim*, literally "thousands", here the clans or divisions of Israel. Also in verses 21 and 30.
[^8]: See Numbers 25:1–9.
[^9]: Hebrew *mishkan*, "dwelling": the tent where Yahweh was present among his people. Also in verse 29.
[^10]: Or "do not make us rebels".
[^11]: Hebrew *cherem*: something given over completely to God, which in war meant destroying it and keeping nothing for oneself. See chapter 7.
[^12]: Hebrew *El Elohim YHWH*, three names for God, repeated for emphasis. It can also be read "The God of gods is Yahweh" (compare Psalm 50:1).
[^13]: Literally "do not save us".
[^14]: Hebrew *olot*: animals burned whole on the altar.
[^15]: Hebrew *minchah*: an offering of flour or grain. Also in verse 29.
[^16]: Hebrew *zivchei shelamim*, traditionally "peace offerings". In these sacrifices most of the meat was eaten by the worshipers. Also in verse 27.
[^17]: Literally "tomorrow". Also in verses 27 and 28.
[^18]: Literally "it was good in their eyes". Also in verse 33.
[^19]: The Hebrew does not give the name itself. The words that follow suggest *Ed*, "Witness".
