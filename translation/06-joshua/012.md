**1** These are the kings of the land whom the Israelites struck down, and whose land they took possession of east of the Jordan, toward the sunrise, from the Arnon Valley[^1] to Mount Hermon, with all the Arabah on the east:

**2** Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon. He ruled from Aroer, which is on the edge of the Arnon Valley, and from the middle of the valley, over half of Gilead as far as the Jabbok River,[^2] the border of the Ammonites,

**3** and over the Arabah as far as the Sea of Kinnereth[^3] on the east, and as far as the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea,[^4] on the east, on the road to Beth-jeshimoth, and on the south below the slopes of Pisgah.

**4** Also the territory of Og king of Bashan, one of the last of the Rephaim,[^5] who lived in Ashtaroth and in Edrei.

**5** He ruled over Mount Hermon, Salecah and all Bashan, as far as the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and over half of Gilead, as far as the border of Sihon king of Heshbon.

**6** Moses the servant of the LORD[^6] and the Israelites struck them down, and Moses the servant of the LORD gave their land as a possession to the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

**7** These are the kings of the land whom Joshua and the Israelites struck down west of the Jordan,[^7] from Baal-gad in the Valley of Lebanon to Mount Halak, which rises toward Seir. Joshua gave their land to the tribes of Israel as a possession according to their divisions,

**8** in the hill country, the Shephelah,[^8] the Arabah, the slopes,[^9] the wilderness and the Negev,[^10] the land of the Hittites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites and the Jebusites:

**9** the king of Jericho, one; the king of Ai, which is beside Bethel, one;

**10** the king of Jerusalem, one; the king of Hebron, one;

**11** the king of Jarmuth, one; the king of Lachish, one;

**12** the king of Eglon, one; the king of Gezer, one;

**13** the king of Debir, one; the king of Geder, one;

**14** the king of Hormah, one; the king of Arad, one;

**15** the king of Libnah, one; the king of Adullam, one;

**16** the king of Makkedah, one; the king of Bethel, one;

**17** the king of Tappuah, one; the king of Hepher, one;

**18** the king of Aphek, one; the king of Lasharon,[^11] one;

**19** the king of Madon, one; the king of Hazor, one;

**20** the king of Shimron-meron, one; the king of Achshaph, one;

**21** the king of Taanach, one; the king of Megiddo, one;

**22** the king of Kedesh, one; the king of Jokneam in Carmel, one;

**23** the king of Dor in Naphath-dor,[^12] one; the king of Goiim in Gilgal,[^13] one;

**24** the king of Tirzah, one. In all, thirty-one kings.

[^1]: Hebrew *nachal*: a valley with a stream that flows in the rainy season. Also in verse 2.
[^2]: Literally "the Jabbok, the *nachal*".
[^3]: The Sea of Galilee. Hebrew *Yam Kinarot*.
[^4]: The Dead Sea.
[^5]: An ancient people remembered for their great height (Deuteronomy 2:11; 3:11).
[^6]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^7]: Literally "beyond the Jordan toward the sea".
[^8]: The low hills between the coastal plain and the hill country of Judah.
[^9]: Hebrew *ashedot*; see 10:40.
[^10]: The dry country in the south of the land.
[^11]: Or "the king of Sharon". Some take the words with the previous name: "the king of Aphek in Sharon".
[^12]: Or "in the heights of Dor" (11:2).
[^13]: *Goiim* means "nations". The Greek Septuagint reads "Galilee" for "Gilgal".
