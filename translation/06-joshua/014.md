**1** These are the inheritances that the Israelites received in the land of Canaan, which Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun and the heads of the families[^1] of the tribes of the Israelites gave out to them.

**2** Their inheritance was assigned by lot, as Yahweh[^2] had commanded through Moses, for the nine and a half tribes.[^3]

**3** For Moses had given the two and a half tribes their inheritance east of the Jordan,[^4] but he had given no inheritance among them to the Levites.

**4** For the descendants of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim. And the Levites were given no share in the land, only towns to live in, with their pasturelands[^5] for their livestock and their possessions.

**5** The Israelites did as Yahweh had commanded Moses, and they divided the land.

**6** The people of Judah came to Joshua at Gilgal, and Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite[^6] said to him, "You know what Yahweh said to Moses the man of God about you and me at Kadesh-barnea.[^7]

**7** I was forty years old when Moses the servant of Yahweh sent me from Kadesh-barnea to spy out the land, and I brought him back a report of what I truly believed.[^8]

**8** But my brothers who went up with me made the people's hearts melt, while I followed Yahweh my God wholeheartedly.[^9]

**9** On that day Moses swore, 'The land on which your foot has walked will surely be an inheritance for you and your children forever, because you have followed Yahweh my God wholeheartedly.'[^10]

**10** Now look, Yahweh has kept me alive, as he promised, these forty-five years since Yahweh spoke this word to Moses, while Israel journeyed in the wilderness. And now here I am today, eighty-five years old.

**11** I am still as strong today as I was on the day Moses sent me. As my strength was then, so my strength is now, for war and for going out and coming in.[^11]

**12** So now give me this hill country that Yahweh spoke of on that day. For you heard on that day that the Anakim[^12] were there, with great fortified towns. Perhaps Yahweh will be with me, and I will drive them out, as Yahweh said."

**13** Joshua blessed him and gave Hebron to Caleb son of Jephunneh as an inheritance.

**14** So Hebron has belonged to Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite as an inheritance to this day, because he followed Yahweh, the God of Israel, wholeheartedly.

**15** The name of Hebron was formerly Kiriath-arba.[^13] Arba was the greatest man among the Anakim. And the land had rest from war.

[^1]: Literally "the heads of the fathers", the leaders of the family groups within each tribe.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Numbers 26:55–56; 34:13.
[^4]: Literally "beyond the Jordan".
[^5]: Hebrew *migrash*: the open land around a town, used for grazing (Numbers 35:2–5).
[^6]: The Kenizzites are listed among the peoples of Canaan in Genesis 15:19, and Kenaz is an Edomite name (Genesis 36:11). Caleb's family belonged to Judah (Numbers 13:6).
[^7]: Numbers 14:24, 30; Deuteronomy 1:36.
[^8]: Literally "as it was with my heart".
[^9]: Literally "I filled after Yahweh my God". Also in verses 9 and 14.
[^10]: Deuteronomy 1:36.
[^11]: That is, for leading in battle and in all the business of life.
[^12]: A people remembered for their great height (Numbers 13:28, 33).
[^13]: *Kiriath-arba* means "town of Arba", or "town of four".
