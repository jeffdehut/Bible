**1** The second lot came out for Simeon, for the tribe of the Simeonites, clan by clan. Their inheritance lay within the inheritance of the people of Judah.

**2** For their inheritance they had Beersheba, Sheba,[^1] Moladah,

**3** Hazar-shual, Balah, Ezem,

**4** Eltolad, Bethul, Hormah,

**5** Ziklag, Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susah,

**6** Beth-lebaoth and Sharuhen: thirteen towns, with their villages;[^2]

**7** Ain, Rimmon, Ether and Ashan: four towns, with their villages;

**8** and all the villages around these towns as far as Baalath-beer, Ramah of the Negev.[^3] This was the inheritance of the tribe of the Simeonites, clan by clan.

**9** The inheritance of the Simeonites was taken from the share of the people of Judah, because the portion of the people of Judah was too large for them. So the Simeonites received their inheritance within Judah's inheritance.

**10** The third lot came up for the Zebulunites, clan by clan. The border of their inheritance went as far as Sarid.

**11** Their border went up westward to Maralah, touched Dabbesheth, and touched the valley[^4] that faces Jokneam.

**12** From Sarid it turned east, toward the sunrise, to the border of Chisloth-tabor, went out to Daberath and went up to Japhia.

**13** From there it passed along eastward, toward the sunrise, to Gath-hepher and Eth-kazin, went out to Rimmon and turned toward Neah.[^5]

**14** Then the border went around it on the north to Hannathon, and it ended at the Valley of Iphtah-el.

**15** It included Kattath, Nahalal, Shimron, Idalah and Bethlehem:[^6] twelve towns, with their villages.[^7]

**16** This was the inheritance of the Zebulunites, clan by clan, these towns with their villages.

**17** The fourth lot came out for Issachar, for the Issacharites, clan by clan.

**18** Their territory included Jezreel, Chesulloth, Shunem,

**19** Hapharaim, Shion, Anaharath,

**20** Rabbith, Kishion, Ebez,

**21** Remeth, En-gannim, En-haddah and Beth-pazzez.

**22** The border touched Tabor, Shahazimah[^8] and Beth-shemesh, and their border ended at the Jordan: sixteen towns, with their villages.

**23** This was the inheritance of the tribe of the Issacharites, clan by clan, the towns with their villages.

**24** The fifth lot came out for the tribe of the Asherites, clan by clan.

**25** Their territory included Helkath, Hali, Beten, Achshaph,

**26** Allammelech, Amad and Mishal. On the west it touched Carmel and Shihor-libnath.

**27** Then it turned toward the sunrise to Beth-dagon, touched Zebulun and the Valley of Iphtah-el on the north at Beth-emek and Neiel, and went out to Cabul on the north,[^9]

**28** Ebron,[^10] Rehob, Hammon and Kanah, as far as Greater Sidon.

**29** Then the border turned to Ramah and to the fortified town of Tyre. The border turned to Hosah, and it ended at the sea in the region of Achzib,[^11]

**30** Ummah, Aphek and Rehob: twenty-two towns, with their villages.

**31** This was the inheritance of the tribe of the Asherites, clan by clan, these towns with their villages.

**32** The sixth lot came out for the Naphtalites, for the Naphtalites clan by clan.

**33** Their border ran from Heleph, from the oak at Zaanannim,[^12] Adami-nekeb and Jabneel as far as Lakkum, and it ended at the Jordan.

**34** The border turned westward to Aznoth-tabor, went out from there to Hukkok, touched Zebulun on the south and Asher on the west, and Judah at the Jordan[^13] toward the sunrise.

**35** The fortified towns were Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath, Kinnereth,

**36** Adamah, Ramah, Hazor,

**37** Kedesh, Edrei, En-hazor,

**38** Iron, Migdal-el, Horem, Beth-anath and Beth-shemesh: nineteen towns, with their villages.[^14]

**39** This was the inheritance of the tribe of the Naphtalites, clan by clan, the towns with their villages.

**40** The seventh lot came out for the tribe of the Danites, clan by clan.

**41** The territory of their inheritance included Zorah, Eshtaol, Ir-shemesh,

**42** Shaalabbin, Aijalon, Ithlah,

**43** Elon, Timnah, Ekron,

**44** Eltekeh, Gibbethon, Baalath,

**45** Jehud, Bene-berak, Gath-rimmon,

**46** Me-jarkon[^15] and Rakkon, with the territory facing Joppa.

**47** But the territory of the Danites slipped out of their hands.[^16] So the Danites went up and fought against Leshem. They captured it, put it to the sword, took possession of it and settled in it, and they named Leshem Dan, after their ancestor Dan.[^17]

**48** This was the inheritance of the tribe of the Danites, clan by clan, these towns with their villages.

**49** When they had finished dividing up the land as inheritances by its borders, the Israelites gave Joshua son of Nun an inheritance among them.

**50** As Yahweh[^18] had commanded,[^19] they gave him the town he asked for, Timnath-serah[^20] in the hill country of Ephraim. He rebuilt the town and settled in it.

**51** These are the inheritances that Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun and the heads of the families[^21] of the tribes of the Israelites gave out by lot at Shiloh before Yahweh, at the entrance of the tent of meeting. So they finished dividing the land.

[^1]: Sheba is not in the parallel list in 1 Chronicles 4:28. Without it the names in verses 2–6 come to thirteen.
[^2]: Hebrew *chatserim*: settlements without walls around a town. Also in verses 7–8 and throughout the chapter.
[^3]: Or "Ramath-negeb". The Negev is the dry country in the south of the land.
[^4]: Hebrew *nachal*: a valley with a stream that flows in the rainy season.
[^5]: The Hebrew is unusual. Literally "went out to Rimmon, the one curving, the Neah".
[^6]: Not the Bethlehem in Judah.
[^7]: Only five towns are named in this verse.
[^8]: The Hebrew text is written "Shahazumah" but read "Shahazimah".
[^9]: Literally "on the left", that is, north, for someone facing east.
[^10]: Probably Abdon (21:30). In Hebrew the letters for *d* and *r* look very much alike.
[^11]: Hebrew *mechevel Akhzivah*, "from the district, to Achzib". Some take *Mechevel* as a place name, Mahalab.
[^12]: Or "from Elon-bezaanannim". Compare Judges 4:11.
[^13]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "and in Judah, the Jordan".
[^14]: The names in verses 35–38 come to sixteen.
[^15]: *Me-jarkon* means "waters of Jarkon", perhaps the Yarkon river north of Joppa.
[^16]: Literally "went out from them".
[^17]: Leshem is called Laish in Judges 18, where the story is told at length (Judges 18:27–29).
[^18]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^19]: Literally "At the mouth of Yahweh".
[^20]: Called Timnath-heres in Judges 2:9.
[^21]: Literally "the heads of the fathers", the leaders of the family groups within each tribe.
