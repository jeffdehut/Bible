**1** When Jabin king of Hazor heard of this, he sent word to Jobab king of Madon, to the king of Shimron, to the king of Achshaph,

**2** and to the kings in the north, in the hill country, in the Arabah[^1] south of Kinnereth,[^2] in the western foothills[^3] and in Naphoth-dor[^4] on the west,

**3** to the Canaanites in the east and in the west, to the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites and the Jebusites in the hill country, and to the Hivites below Hermon in the land of Mizpah.

**4** They came out, they and all their armies with them, a great horde, as many as the sand on the seashore, with a very great number of horses and chariots.

**5** All these kings joined forces.[^5] They came and camped together at the waters of Merom to fight against Israel.

**6** Yahweh[^6] said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid of them, for tomorrow at this time I will hand all of them over to Israel, slain. You are to hamstring their horses and burn their chariots with fire."

**7** So Joshua and all the fighting men with him came against them suddenly at the waters of Merom and fell on them.

**8** Yahweh gave them into the hand of Israel. They struck them down and chased them as far as Greater Sidon and Misrephoth-maim, and as far as the Valley of Mizpeh to the east. They struck them down until no survivor was left.

**9** Joshua did to them as Yahweh had told him. He hamstrung their horses and burned their chariots with fire.

**10** At that time Joshua turned back and captured Hazor, and he put its king to the sword, for Hazor had formerly been the head of all these kingdoms.

**11** They put every person[^7] in it to the sword, devoting them to destruction.[^8] Nothing that breathed was left, and he burned Hazor with fire.

**12** Joshua captured all the towns of these kings and all their kings, and he put them to the sword. He devoted them to destruction, as Moses the servant of Yahweh had commanded.

**13** But Israel did not burn any of the towns that stood on their mounds,[^9] except Hazor alone, which Joshua burned.

**14** The Israelites took all the spoil of these towns and the livestock as plunder for themselves. But they put every human being to the sword until they had destroyed them. They left nothing that breathed.

**15** As Yahweh had commanded his servant Moses, so Moses commanded Joshua, and so Joshua did. He left nothing undone of all that Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**16** So Joshua took this whole land: the hill country, the whole Negev,[^10] the whole land of Goshen, the western foothills, the Arabah, and the hill country of Israel with its foothills,

**17** from Mount Halak,[^11] which rises toward Seir, as far as Baal-gad in the Valley of Lebanon below Mount Hermon. He captured all their kings and struck them down and put them to death.

**18** Joshua waged war against all these kings for a long time.

**19** Not one town made peace with the Israelites except the Hivites who lived in Gibeon. They took them all in battle.

**20** For it was Yahweh's doing to harden their hearts[^12] to meet Israel in battle, so that they would be devoted to destruction and receive no mercy, but be destroyed, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**21** At that time Joshua came and wiped out the Anakim[^13] from the hill country, from Hebron, from Debir, from Anab, from all the hill country of Judah and from all the hill country of Israel. Joshua devoted them and their towns to destruction.

**22** No Anakim were left in the land of the Israelites. Only in Gaza, in Gath and in Ashdod did some remain.

**23** So Joshua took the whole land, just as Yahweh had told Moses, and Joshua gave it as an inheritance to Israel, tribe by tribe, according to their divisions. And the land had rest from war.

[^1]: Hebrew *aravah*: the deep rift valley of the Jordan and the Dead Sea, and its continuation south to the Gulf of Aqaba.
[^2]: Hebrew *Kinarot*, the town and district of Kinnereth on the Sea of Galilee.
[^3]: Hebrew *shephelah*: the low hills between the central hill country and the coastal plain. Also in verse 16.
[^4]: Or "the heights of Dor".
[^5]: Or "met by agreement".
[^6]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^7]: Hebrew *nephesh*, "living being".
[^8]: Hebrew *charam*: to give something over completely to God, which in war meant destroying it and keeping nothing for oneself. Also in verses 12, 20 and 21.
[^9]: Hebrew *tel*: the mound formed by the ruins of earlier towns built one on top of another.
[^10]: The dry country in the south of the land.
[^11]: Or "the Bare Mountain". Also in 12:7.
[^12]: Literally "to make their hearts strong", the expression used of Pharaoh (Exodus 4:21; 14:4).
[^13]: A people remembered for their great height (Numbers 13:28, 33; Deuteronomy 2:10–11). Also in verse 22.
