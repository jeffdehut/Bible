**1** Now Jericho was shut up tight because of the Israelites. No one went out and no one came in.

**2** The LORD[^1] said to Joshua, "See, I have given Jericho into your hand, with its king and its mighty warriors.

**3** You are to march around the town, all the men of war, going around the town once. Do this for six days.

**4** Seven priests are to carry seven rams' horns[^2] ahead of the ark. On the seventh day you are to march around the town seven times, and the priests are to blow the rams' horns.

**5** When they sound a long blast on the ram's horn and you hear the sound of the horn, all the people are to give a loud shout. Then the wall of the town will fall down flat,[^3] and the people are to go up into the town, each man straight ahead."

**6** So Joshua son of Nun called the priests and said to them, "Take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven priests carry seven rams' horns ahead of the ark of the LORD."

**7** And he said[^4] to the people, "Go forward and march around the town, and let the armed men go ahead of the ark of the LORD."

**8** When Joshua had spoken to the people, the seven priests carrying the seven rams' horns before the LORD went forward and blew the rams' horns, and the ark of the covenant of the LORD followed them.

**9** The armed men went ahead of the priests who blew the rams' horns,[^5] and the rear guard followed the ark, while the horns kept sounding.

**10** But Joshua had commanded the people, "Do not shout or let your voice be heard. Do not let a word come out of your mouth until the day I tell you to shout. Then you are to shout."

**11** So he had the ark of the LORD go around the town, circling it once. Then they came back to the camp and spent the night in the camp.

**12** Joshua got up early in the morning, and the priests took up the ark of the LORD.

**13** The seven priests carrying the seven rams' horns ahead of the ark of the LORD went on, blowing the horns as they went. The armed men went ahead of them, and the rear guard followed the ark of the LORD, while the horns kept sounding.

**14** On the second day they marched around the town once and returned to the camp. They did this for six days.

**15** On the seventh day they got up early, at the break of dawn, and marched around the town in the same way seven times. That was the only day they marched around the town seven times.

**16** The seventh time, when the priests blew the rams' horns, Joshua said to the people, "Shout! For the LORD has given you the town.

**17** The town and everything in it is to be devoted to the LORD for destruction.[^6] Only Rahab the prostitute is to live, she and all who are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers we sent.

**18** But you must keep away from what is devoted to destruction. Otherwise you will devote it and then take some of what is devoted,[^7] and so make the camp of Israel something devoted to destruction and bring disaster[^8] on it.

**19** All the silver and gold, and the articles of bronze and iron, are holy to the LORD. They must go into the LORD's treasury."

**20** So the people shouted, and the rams' horns were blown. As soon as the people heard the sound of the horn, the people gave a loud shout, and the wall fell down flat. The people went up into the town, each man straight ahead, and they captured the town.

**21** They devoted to destruction everything in the town, men and women, young and old, cattle, sheep and donkeys, putting them to the sword.[^9]

**22** Joshua said to the two men who had spied out the land, "Go into the prostitute's house, and bring the woman out of it with everyone who belongs to her, as you swore to her."

**23** So the young men who had been spies went in and brought out Rahab, her father and her mother, her brothers and everyone who belonged to her. They brought out all her relatives[^10] and settled them outside the camp of Israel.

**24** Then they burned the town with fire, and everything in it. Only the silver and gold and the articles of bronze and iron they put into the treasury of the house of the LORD.

**25** But Joshua spared Rahab the prostitute, her father's household and everyone who belonged to her. She has lived among the Israelites to this day, because she hid the messengers Joshua had sent to spy out Jericho.

**26** At that time Joshua made them take an oath: "Cursed before the LORD is the man who sets out to rebuild this town, Jericho. At the cost of his firstborn he will lay its foundations, and at the cost of his youngest son he will set up its gates."[^11]

**27** So the LORD was with Joshua, and his fame spread through the whole land.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *shofarot ha-yovelim*, horns made from rams' horns, blown as trumpets. Also in verses 5–9, 13, 16 and 20.
[^3]: Literally "will fall under itself". Also in verse 20.
[^4]: The Hebrew text is written "they said" but read "he said".
[^5]: The Hebrew text is written "ahead of the priests; they blew the rams' horns" but read "ahead of the priests who blew the rams' horns".
[^6]: Literally "The town is to be *cherem* to the LORD". Hebrew *cherem*: something given over completely to God, which in war meant destroying it and keeping nothing for oneself. Also in verse 18, where the related verb *charam*, "devote to destruction", is also used, as in verse 21.
[^7]: The Greek Septuagint reads "so that you do not covet and take some of what is devoted". Compare 7:21.
[^8]: Hebrew *akhar*, "bring trouble", the word that gives the Valley of Achor its name (7:25–26).
[^9]: Literally "to the mouth of the sword".
[^10]: Literally "all her clans".
[^11]: See 1 Kings 16:34.
