**1** Then I saw another sign in heaven, great and amazing: seven angels with seven plagues, which are the last, for with them the fury of God is completed.

**2** And I saw what looked like a sea of glass mixed with fire, and those who had conquered the beast and its image and the number of its name, standing on the sea of glass[^1] and holding harps of God.[^2]

**3** They sang the song of Moses, the servant of God,[^3] and the song of the Lamb: "Great and amazing are your works, Lord God Almighty![^4] Just and true are your ways, King of the ages![^5]

**4** Who will not fear you, Lord, and glorify your name? For you alone are holy. All the nations will come and worship before you,[^6] for your righteous acts[^7] have been revealed."

**5** After this I looked, and the temple of the tent of the testimony[^8] in heaven was opened,

**6** and out of the temple came the seven angels who had the seven plagues, dressed in clean, shining linen,[^9] with golden sashes around their chests.

**7** Then one of the four living creatures gave the seven angels seven golden bowls full of the fury of God, who lives forever and ever.

**8** The temple was filled with smoke from the glory of God and from his power,[^10] and no one could enter the temple until the seven plagues of the seven angels were completed.

[^1]: Or "beside the sea of glass".
[^2]: That is, harps for the worship of God, or given by God.
[^3]: Compare the song Moses and the Israelites sang after crossing the sea (Exodus 15:1–18), and the song of Moses in Deuteronomy 32. The words of this song are drawn from many passages of the Old Testament.
[^4]: Compare Psalm 111:2, "Great are the works of Yahweh"; 139:14, "Wonderful are your works". *Almighty*: see 1:8.
[^5]: Compare Deuteronomy 32:4, "all his ways are just"; Psalm 145:17. The SBLGNT here follows Westcott and Hort. Many manuscripts and most other editions read "King of the nations", as in Jeremiah 10:7.
[^6]: Compare Jeremiah 10:7, "Who would not fear you, King of the nations?"; Psalm 86:9, "All the nations you have made will come and bow down before you, Lord, and they will glorify your name." In the Hebrew of Psalm 86:9, "Lord" is *Adonai*.
[^7]: Or "your just decrees". Greek *dikaiomata*.
[^8]: The tabernacle, called "the tabernacle of the testimony" in Exodus 38:21 and Numbers 1:50. *The testimony* was the stone tablets of the covenant kept in the ark (Exodus 25:16).
[^9]: Some manuscripts read "dressed in clean, shining stone".
[^10]: Compare Exodus 40:34–35, "the glory of Yahweh filled the tabernacle"; Isaiah 6:4, "and the house was filled with smoke"; 1 Kings 8:10–11.
