**1** After this I looked, and there was a door standing open in heaven. And the first voice, which I had heard speaking with me like a trumpet, said, "Come up here, and I will show you what must take place after this."[^1]

**2** At once I was in the Spirit,[^2] and there was a throne standing in heaven, with someone seated on the throne.

**3** The One seated there looked like jasper and carnelian,[^3] and around the throne was a rainbow that looked like an emerald.[^4]

**4** Around the throne were twenty-four thrones, and on the thrones sat twenty-four elders, dressed in white clothes, with golden crowns on their heads.

**5** From the throne came flashes of lightning and rumblings and peals of thunder.[^5] Before the throne seven torches of fire were burning, which are the seven spirits of God.[^6]

**6** Before the throne there was something like a sea of glass, like crystal.[^7] In the middle of the throne and around the throne[^8] were four living creatures, full of eyes in front and behind.[^9]

**7** The first living creature was like a lion, the second living creature was like an ox,[^10] the third living creature had a face like a human face, and the fourth living creature was like an eagle in flight.[^11]

**8** The four living creatures each had six wings,[^12] and they were full of eyes all around and within. Day and night they never stopped saying,[^13] "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty,[^14] who was and who is and who is to come."

**9** Whenever the living creatures gave glory and honor and thanks to the One seated on the throne, who lives forever and ever,

**10** the twenty-four elders would fall down before the One seated on the throne and worship him who lives forever and ever, and they would lay their crowns before the throne, saying,[^15]

**11** "You are worthy, our Lord and God,[^16] to receive the glory and the honor and the power, for you created all things, and by your will they existed and were created."[^17]

[^1]: The last words, "after this", may instead begin verse 2: "After this, at once I was in the Spirit."
[^2]: Or "in the spirit". See 1:10.
[^3]: Greek *sardion*, a red stone.
[^4]: Compare Ezekiel 1:26–28, where above the dome over the living creatures is "the likeness of a throne", with brightness all around it "like the appearance of the rainbow in the clouds". *Rainbow*: or "halo".
[^5]: Literally "lightnings and voices and thunders". Greek *phonai*, "voices, sounds". Compare Exodus 19:16; Ezekiel 1:13.
[^6]: See the note on 1:4. Compare Zechariah 4:2, the lampstand with seven lamps.
[^7]: Compare Ezekiel 1:22, "a dome, gleaming like awesome crystal".
[^8]: Or "In the middle, around the throne", perhaps meaning one on each side of it.
[^9]: The four living creatures are drawn from Ezekiel's vision (Ezekiel 1:5–21; 10:12–14), where the rims of the wheels are "full of eyes all around" (1:18), and from the seraphim of Isaiah 6:2–3.
[^10]: Or "a calf", "a young bull". Greek *moschos*.
[^11]: Compare Ezekiel 1:10.
[^12]: As do the seraphim in Isaiah 6:2.
[^13]: Literally "They have no rest day and night, saying".
[^14]: Isaiah 6:3, "Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of Armies!" In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, YHWH ("the LORD"). *Almighty*: see 1:8.
[^15]: The Greek has future tenses in verses 9–10 ("Whenever the living creatures will give …, the twenty-four elders will fall down …"), describing what happens again and again.
[^16]: Many manuscripts read "our Lord and God, the Holy One". The Roman emperor Domitian (AD 81–96) is reported to have had himself called "our lord and god".
[^17]: *They existed*: literally "they were".
