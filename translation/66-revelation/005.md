**1** Then I saw in the right hand of the One seated on the throne a scroll with writing on the inside and on the back, sealed with seven seals.[^1]

**2** And I saw a mighty angel proclaiming in a loud voice, "Who is worthy to open the scroll and break its seals?"

**3** But no one in heaven or on earth or under the earth was able to open the scroll or to look into it.

**4** I wept and wept, because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or to look into it.

**5** Then one of the elders said to me, "Do not weep. Look, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David,[^2] has conquered, so that he can open the scroll and its seven seals."

**6** Then I saw, in the middle of the throne and the four living creatures, and among the elders,[^3] a Lamb[^4] standing, looking as though he had been slaughtered.[^5] He had seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven spirits of God sent out into all the earth.[^6]

**7** He went and took it[^7] from the right hand of the One seated on the throne.

**8** When he had taken the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb. Each of them held a harp and golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of God's holy people.[^8]

**9** And they sang a new song:[^9] "You are worthy to take the scroll and to open its seals, for you were slaughtered, and by your blood you bought for God people from every tribe and language and people and nation.[^10]

**10** You have made them a kingdom and priests to our God, and they reign on the earth."[^11]

**11** Then I looked, and I heard the voice of many angels around the throne and the living creatures and the elders. Their number was ten thousand times ten thousand, and thousands upon thousands,[^12]

**12** saying in a loud voice, "Worthy is the Lamb who was slaughtered to receive the power and wealth and wisdom and might and honor and glory and blessing!"

**13** And I heard every creature in heaven and on earth and under the earth and on the sea, and everything in them, saying, "To the One seated on the throne and to the Lamb be the blessing and the honor and the glory and the power forever and ever!"[^13]

**14** The four living creatures said, "Amen!" And the elders fell down and worshiped.

[^1]: Compare Ezekiel 2:9–10, a scroll with "writing on the front and on the back"; and Isaiah 29:11, "the words of a sealed scroll". *Seals*: a document was sealed with wax or clay, impressed with its owner's mark, so that it could not be opened without breaking them.
[^2]: Genesis 49:9–10, "Judah is a lion's cub"; Isaiah 11:1, 10, "the root of Jesse", David's father.
[^3]: Or "between the throne with the four living creatures and the elders". Compare 4:6.
[^4]: Greek *arnion*, "lamb", the word used for Christ throughout Revelation. John 1:29, 36 has a different word, *amnos*.
[^5]: Greek *sphazo*, "slaughter", the word used for killing a sacrificial animal; also in verses 9 and 12. Compare Isaiah 53:7, "like a lamb that is led to the slaughter".
[^6]: Zechariah 4:10, "These seven are the eyes of the LORD, which range over the whole earth". See the note on 1:4.
[^7]: The Greek has no object; the scroll is meant.
[^8]: Literally "of the holy ones", traditionally "of the saints". Compare Psalm 141:2, "May my prayer be set before you like incense".
[^9]: Compare Psalm 96:1, "Sing to the LORD a new song"; also Psalms 33:3; 98:1; 144:9; 149:1; Isaiah 42:10.
[^10]: Literally "you bought for God from every tribe …". Many manuscripts read "you bought us for God".
[^11]: Compare 1:6; Exodus 19:6. Many manuscripts read "You have made them kings and priests … and they will reign".
[^12]: Literally "myriads of myriads and thousands of thousands". Compare Daniel 7:10, "Thousands upon thousands served him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him".
[^13]: Many manuscripts add "Amen".
