**1** Then I saw a beast coming up out of the sea.[^1] It had ten horns and seven heads, with ten diadems on its horns, and blasphemous names[^2] on its heads.

**2** The beast that I saw was like a leopard. Its feet were like a bear's, and its mouth was like a lion's mouth.[^3] And the dragon gave it his power and his throne and great authority.

**3** One of its heads seemed to have been slaughtered to death,[^4] but its mortal wound had been healed. The whole earth was amazed and followed the beast.[^5]

**4** They worshiped the dragon, because he had given his authority to the beast, and they worshiped the beast, saying, "Who is like the beast,[^6] and who can make war against it?"

**5** The beast was given a mouth speaking arrogant words and blasphemies,[^7] and it was allowed to exercise authority[^8] for forty-two months.[^9]

**6** It opened its mouth to utter blasphemies against God, blaspheming his name and his dwelling,[^10] that is, those who dwell in heaven.

**7** It was allowed to make war on God's holy people[^11] and to conquer them.[^12] And it was given authority over every tribe and people and language and nation.

**8** All who live on the earth will worship it, everyone whose name has not been written, since the foundation of the world, in the book of life of the Lamb who was slaughtered.[^13]

**9** If anyone has ears, let them hear.

**10** If anyone is to go into captivity, into captivity they will go.[^14] If anyone is to be killed with the sword, with the sword they will be killed.[^15] This calls for the endurance and the faithfulness of God's holy people.[^16]

**11** Then I saw another beast coming up out of the earth. It had two horns like a lamb, and it spoke like a dragon.

**12** It exercised all the authority of the first beast on its behalf,[^17] and it made the earth and those who live on it worship the first beast, whose mortal wound had been healed.

**13** It performed great signs, even making fire come down from heaven to earth in front of people.[^18]

**14** And it deceived those who live on the earth[^19] by the signs that it was allowed to perform on behalf of the beast, telling those who live on the earth to make an image for the beast that had been wounded by the sword and yet lived.[^20]

**15** It was allowed to give breath[^21] to the image of the beast, so that the image of the beast could even speak, and could cause all who would not worship the image of the beast to be killed.[^22]

**16** It also made everyone, small and great, rich and poor, free and slave, receive a mark on their right hand or on their forehead,[^23]

**17** so that no one could buy or sell unless they had the mark, that is, the name of the beast or the number of its name.

**18** This calls for wisdom.[^24] Let the one who has understanding calculate the number of the beast, for it is the number of a human being.[^25] Its number is 666.[^26]

[^1]: Compare Daniel 7:3, "Four great beasts came up out of the sea". The beast of this chapter combines features of all four of Daniel's beasts (Daniel 7:4–7; compare verse 2).
[^2]: Some editions read "a blasphemous name".
[^3]: Compare Daniel 7:4–6: the lion, the bear and the leopard.
[^4]: Literally "as slaughtered to death", the same word as that used of the Lamb in 5:6; also in verse 8. *Mortal wound*: literally "the wound of its death"; also in verse 12.
[^5]: Literally "marveled after the beast".
[^6]: A parody of the praise of God in Exodus 15:11, "Who is like you among the gods, Yahweh?"; compare Psalm 35:10.
[^7]: Compare Daniel 7:8, 20, "a mouth speaking arrogant words", and 11:36.
[^8]: Literally "it was given authority to act". Many manuscripts read "authority to make war".
[^9]: See the note on 11:2.
[^10]: Greek *skene*, "tent, tabernacle", from the same root as "dwell" (*skenoo*) later in the verse.
[^11]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "the saints"; also in verse 10.
[^12]: Compare Daniel 7:21, "this horn was making war against the holy ones and was prevailing over them".
[^13]: Or "in the book of life of the Lamb who was slaughtered from the foundation of the world". The Greek may be read either way; compare 17:8.
[^14]: Compare Jeremiah 15:2, "those destined for the sword, to the sword; … and those destined for captivity, to captivity"; 43:11. Many manuscripts read "If anyone holds captives, they go away".
[^15]: The SBLGNT here follows NA28; other editions read "If anyone kills with the sword, with the sword they must be killed."
[^16]: Literally "Here is the endurance and the faith of the holy ones".
[^17]: Literally "before it", "in its presence"; also in verse 14.
[^18]: Compare 1 Kings 18:38, "Then the fire of Yahweh fell"; 2 Kings 1:10.
[^19]: Many manuscripts read "it deceived my own people who live on the earth".
[^20]: Compare Daniel 3:1–7, where all are commanded to worship Nebuchadnezzar's statue of gold, in the Greek Old Testament his *eikon*, "image".
[^21]: Or "spirit". Greek *pneuma*.
[^22]: Compare Daniel 3:5–6, "Whoever does not fall down and worship will immediately be thrown into a furnace of blazing fire."
[^23]: Greek *charagma*, "mark, stamp, brand", a word used of the image or seal stamped on coins and documents; also in verse 17.
[^24]: Literally "Here is wisdom".
[^25]: Or "a human number", "the number of a man".
[^26]: Greek *hexakosioi hexekonta hex*, "six hundred sixty-six", written out in words, as in the SBLGNT and most editions; Tregelles prints it with Greek letters used as numerals (χξϛ). The apparatus records no other number. In Greek and Hebrew, letters also served as numbers, so that every name had a numerical value, the practice later called gematria. Interpreters have proposed many names whose letters add up to 666; one often suggested is "Nero Caesar" written in Hebrew letters (*nrwn qsr*). The text does not say whose name is meant.
