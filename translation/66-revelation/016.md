**1** Then I heard a loud voice from the temple saying to the seven angels, "Go and pour out on the earth the seven bowls of God's fury."

**2** So the first angel went and poured out his bowl on the earth, and foul and painful sores[^1] broke out on the people who had the mark of the beast and who worshiped its image.

**3** The second angel poured out his bowl into the sea, and it became blood like that of a corpse, and every living thing in the sea died.[^2]

**4** The third angel poured out his bowl into the rivers and the springs of water, and they became blood.

**5** And I heard the angel of the waters say, "You are just, the Holy One, who is and who was,[^3] because you have judged these things.

**6** For they poured out the blood of God's holy people[^4] and of prophets, and you have given them blood to drink.[^5] They deserve it."[^6]

**7** And I heard the altar say, "Yes, Lord God Almighty, true and just are your judgments."[^7]

**8** The fourth angel poured out his bowl on the sun, and it was allowed to scorch people with fire.

**9** People were scorched by the fierce heat, and they blasphemed the name of God, who had authority over these plagues, and they did not repent and give him glory.

**10** The fifth angel poured out his bowl on the throne of the beast, and its kingdom was plunged into darkness.[^8] People gnawed their tongues in agony,

**11** and they blasphemed the God of heaven because of their pains and their sores, and they did not repent of their works.

**12** The sixth angel poured out his bowl on the great river Euphrates, and its water was dried up,[^9] to prepare the way for the kings from the east.[^10]

**13** Then I saw three unclean spirits like frogs[^11] coming out of the mouth of the dragon, out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet.[^12]

**14** For they are spirits of demons, performing signs, who go out to the kings of the whole world, to gather them for the battle on the great day of God Almighty.[^13]

**15** ("Look, I am coming like a thief! Blessed is the one who stays awake and keeps their clothes on, so that they will not go about naked and be seen in their shame.")[^14]

**16** And they gathered them at the place that is called in Hebrew Armageddon.[^15]

**17** The seventh angel poured out his bowl into the air, and a loud voice came out of the temple,[^16] from the throne, saying, "It is done!"

**18** There were flashes of lightning, rumblings and peals of thunder, and there was a great earthquake, such as had never been since people have been on the earth,[^17] so mighty and so great was that earthquake.

**19** The great city was split into three parts, and the cities of the nations fell. God remembered Babylon the great[^18] and gave her the cup of the wine of the fury of his anger.

**20** Every island fled away, and the mountains could no longer be found.

**21** Huge hailstones, each weighing about a talent,[^19] fell from heaven on people, and people blasphemed God because of the plague of hail, for that plague was extremely severe.[^20]

[^1]: Literally "a bad and evil sore". Compare the sixth plague of Egypt, Exodus 9:9–11, "boils that broke out into sores on people and animals".
[^2]: Literally "every living soul, the things in the sea, died". Compare the first plague of Egypt, Exodus 7:17–21.
[^3]: As in 11:17, the title lacks "who is to come".
[^4]: Literally "of the holy ones", traditionally "of the saints".
[^5]: Compare Isaiah 49:26, "they will be drunk on their own blood as on new wine".
[^6]: Literally "They are worthy."
[^7]: Compare Psalm 19:10 (19:9 in most English Bibles), "the rulings of Yahweh are true, and righteous altogether"; 119:137. *Almighty*: see 1:8.
[^8]: Literally "its kingdom became darkened". Compare the ninth plague of Egypt, Exodus 10:21–23.
[^9]: Compare Isaiah 11:15; 44:27; Jeremiah 50:38, "A drought on her waters, and they will dry up!"; 51:36.
[^10]: Literally "from the rising of the sun".
[^11]: Compare the second plague of Egypt, Exodus 7:26–8:11 (8:1–15 in most English Bibles).
[^12]: Probably the second beast of 13:11–17 (compare 19:20).
[^13]: *Almighty*: see 1:8.
[^14]: Compare 3:3, 18; Matthew 24:43; 1 Thessalonians 5:2. *Keeps their clothes on*: literally "keeps his garments".
[^15]: Greek *Harmagedon*, probably from the Hebrew *har megiddo*, "the mountain of Megiddo". Megiddo, at the edge of the plain of Jezreel, was the site of famous battles (Judges 5:19; 2 Kings 23:29; compare Zechariah 12:11).
[^16]: Many manuscripts read "the temple of heaven".
[^17]: Compare Daniel 12:1, "a time of distress such as has never been since there was a nation".
[^18]: Literally "Babylon the great was remembered before God". See 14:8.
[^19]: Greek *talantiaios*, "weighing a talent", about 75 pounds (34 kilograms).
[^20]: Compare the seventh plague of Egypt, Exodus 9:23–25.
