**1** Then I watched as the Lamb opened one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures say in a voice like thunder, "Come!"[^1]

**2** I looked, and there was a white horse.[^2] Its rider held a bow. He was given a crown,[^3] and he went out conquering and to conquer.

**3** When he opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature say, "Come!"

**4** And another horse came out, fiery red. Its rider was allowed[^4] to take peace from the earth, so that people would slaughter one another, and he was given a great sword.

**5** When he opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature say, "Come!" I looked, and there was a black horse. Its rider held a pair of scales in his hand.

**6** And I heard what seemed to be a voice among the four living creatures, saying, "A measure of wheat for a denarius, and three measures of barley for a denarius,[^5] but do not damage the oil and the wine!"

**7** When he opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature say, "Come!"

**8** I looked, and there was a pale horse.[^6] Its rider's name was Death, and Hades[^7] followed with him. They were given authority over a fourth of the earth, to kill with sword and with famine and with plague and by the wild animals of the earth.[^8]

**9** When he opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of those who had been slaughtered because of the word of God and because of the testimony they had held.[^9]

**10** They cried out with a loud voice, "How long, Sovereign Lord,[^10] holy and true, before you judge and avenge our blood on those who live on the earth?"[^11]

**11** Each of them was given a white robe, and they were told to rest a little longer, until the number of their fellow servants and their brothers and sisters[^12] who were going to be killed as they had been was complete.[^13]

**12** I watched as he opened the sixth seal, and there was a great earthquake. The sun became black as sackcloth made of hair, and the whole moon became like blood,[^14]

**13** and the stars of the sky fell to the earth, as a fig tree drops its unripe figs when it is shaken by a strong wind.

**14** The sky vanished like a scroll being rolled up,[^15] and every mountain and island was moved from its place.

**15** Then the kings of the earth, the nobles, the generals,[^16] the rich, the powerful, and everyone, slave and free, hid themselves in the caves and among the rocks of the mountains.[^17]

**16** They said to the mountains and the rocks, "Fall on us and hide us[^18] from the face of the One seated on the throne and from the anger of the Lamb,

**17** for the great day of their anger has come, and who is able to stand?"[^19]

[^1]: Many manuscripts read "Come and see!"; also in verses 5 and 7.
[^2]: The four horses and riders of verses 1–8 recall the horses of different colors in Zechariah 1:8 and 6:1–8.
[^3]: Greek *stephanos*, a victor's wreath. See 2:10.
[^4]: Literally "it was given to him". Revelation often uses "was given" for what God permits.
[^5]: *Measure*: Greek *choinix*, a dry measure of about a quart (1 liter), roughly a day's ration of grain for one person. *Denarius*: the usual day's wage for a laborer. These are famine prices.
[^6]: Greek *chloros*, "pale green, yellowish green", the color of a corpse.
[^7]: Greek *Hades*, the realm of the dead, like the Hebrew Sheol.
[^8]: Compare Ezekiel 14:21, "my four terrible judgments … sword and famine and wild animals and plague". *Plague*: Greek *thanatos*, "death", which the Greek Old Testament uses for the Hebrew word for "plague".
[^9]: Many manuscripts read "the testimony of the Lamb".
[^10]: Greek *despotes*, "master", a title of God.
[^11]: Compare Psalm 79:10, "you avenge the blood of your servants that has been poured out"; Deuteronomy 32:43; Zechariah 1:12.
[^12]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike.
[^13]: Literally "until their fellow servants and their brothers who were going to be killed as they had been also were completed".
[^14]: Joel 3:4 (2:31 in most English Bibles), "The sun will be turned to darkness and the moon to blood"; compare Isaiah 13:10.
[^15]: Isaiah 34:4, "and the heavens will be rolled up like a scroll … like a withered fig from the fig tree".
[^16]: Greek *chiliarchoi*, "commanders of a thousand", senior military officers.
[^17]: Compare Isaiah 2:19, "People will go into caves in the rocks and holes in the ground, from the terror of Yahweh".
[^18]: Hosea 10:8, "Then they will say to the mountains, 'Cover us!' and to the hills, 'Fall on us!'"; compare Luke 23:30.
[^19]: Many manuscripts read "his anger". Compare Joel 2:11, "For the day of Yahweh is great and very terrible. Who can endure it?"; Malachi 3:2.
