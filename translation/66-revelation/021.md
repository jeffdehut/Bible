**1** Then I saw a new heaven and a new earth,[^1] for the first heaven and the first earth had passed away, and the sea was no more.

**2** And I saw the holy city, the new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband.[^2]

**3** And I heard a loud voice from the throne[^3] saying, "Look, God's dwelling[^4] is with humankind. He will dwell with them, and they will be his peoples,[^5] and God himself will be with them.[^6]

**4** He will wipe away every tear from their eyes.[^7] Death will be no more, and there will be no more mourning or crying or pain. The former things have passed away."[^8]

**5** Then the One seated on the throne said, "Look, I am making all things new."[^9] And he said,[^10] "Write this, for these words are trustworthy and true."

**6** Then he said to me, "They are done![^11] I am the Alpha and the Omega,[^12] the beginning and the end. To the thirsty I will give water from the spring of the water of life, without cost.[^13]

**7** The one who conquers will inherit these things, and I will be his God, and he will be my son.[^14]

**8** But as for the cowardly, the faithless,[^15] the detestable, murderers, the sexually immoral, sorcerers, idolaters and all liars, their share will be in the lake that burns with fire and sulfur, which is the second death."

**9** Then one of the seven angels who had the seven bowls full of the seven last plagues came and said to me,[^16] "Come, I will show you the bride, the wife of the Lamb."

**10** And he carried me away in the Spirit[^17] to a great, high mountain,[^18] and showed me the holy city Jerusalem[^19] coming down out of heaven from God,

**11** having the glory of God.[^20] Its radiance was like a most precious stone, like jasper, clear as crystal.

**12** It had a great, high wall with twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels, and on the gates were written names, the names of the twelve tribes of the Israelites.[^21]

**13** There were three gates on the east, three gates on the north, three gates on the south and three gates on the west.

**14** The wall of the city had twelve foundations, and on them were the twelve names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.

**15** The one who was speaking with me had a golden measuring reed to measure the city and its gates and its wall.[^22]

**16** The city was laid out as a square; its length was the same as its width. He measured the city with the reed: 12,000 stadia.[^23] Its length and width and height are equal.

**17** He also measured its wall: 144 cubits[^24] by human measure, which is also an angel's measure.[^25]

**18** The wall was built of jasper, and the city was pure gold, like clear glass.

**19** The foundations of the city wall were adorned with every kind of precious stone. The first foundation was jasper, the second sapphire, the third chalcedony, the fourth emerald,

**20** the fifth sardonyx, the sixth carnelian, the seventh chrysolite, the eighth beryl, the ninth topaz, the tenth chrysoprase, the eleventh jacinth and the twelfth amethyst.[^26]

**21** The twelve gates were twelve pearls; each of the gates was made of a single pearl. And the street of the city was pure gold, like transparent glass.

**22** I saw no temple in the city, for its temple is the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb.

**23** The city has no need of the sun or the moon to shine on it, for the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb.[^27]

**24** The nations will walk by its light, and the kings of the earth bring their glory into it.[^28]

**25** Its gates will never be shut by day, for there will be no night there.[^29]

**26** They will bring the glory and the honor of the nations into it.

**27** But nothing unclean will ever enter it,[^30] nor anyone who does what is detestable or false, but only those who are written in the Lamb's book of life.

[^1]: Isaiah 65:17, "For look, I am creating new heavens and a new earth"; 66:22.
[^2]: Compare Isaiah 52:1; 61:10, "as a bride adorns herself with her jewels"; 62:1–5.
[^3]: Many manuscripts read "from heaven".
[^4]: Greek *skene*, "tent, tabernacle". *Dwell* is the related verb *skenoo* (see 7:15).
[^5]: Many manuscripts read "his people".
[^6]: Some manuscripts read "will be with them as their God". Compare Leviticus 26:11–12, "I will walk among you and be your God, and you will be my people"; Ezekiel 37:27; Zechariah 2:14 (2:10 in most English Bibles).
[^7]: Isaiah 25:8. See 7:17.
[^8]: Many manuscripts read "for the former things have passed away". Compare Isaiah 35:10; 65:17, 19, "the sound of weeping and the sound of crying will no longer be heard in it".
[^9]: Compare Isaiah 43:19, "Look, I am doing a new thing!"
[^10]: Many manuscripts read "And he said to me".
[^11]: Literally "They have come to be". Many manuscripts read *gegona*, a first-person form, "I have come to be".
[^12]: See 1:8.
[^13]: Compare Isaiah 55:1, "Come, all you who are thirsty, come to the waters! … without money and without cost."
[^14]: Compare 2 Samuel 7:14, God's promise to David's heir, "I will be his father, and he will be my son."
[^15]: Many manuscripts add "and sinners".
[^16]: Literally "spoke with me, saying". See 17:1.
[^17]: Or "in the spirit". See 1:10.
[^18]: Compare Ezekiel 40:2, "and set me down on a very high mountain, and on it … was something like the structure of a city".
[^19]: Many manuscripts read "the great city, the holy Jerusalem".
[^20]: Compare Isaiah 60:1–2, "and the glory of Yahweh has risen on you"; Ezekiel 43:2–5.
[^21]: Compare Ezekiel 48:30–35, "the gates of the city will be named after the tribes of Israel".
[^22]: Compare Ezekiel 40:3, 5.
[^23]: About 1,400 miles (2,200 kilometers). A *stadion* was about 600 feet (185 meters).
[^24]: About 216 feet (65 meters). A cubit was about 18 inches (45 cm).
[^25]: Or "by a human measure, which the angel was using".
[^26]: Compare the twelve stones of the high priest's breastpiece, Exodus 28:17–20, and Isaiah 54:11–12, "and lay your foundations with sapphires". The modern names of some of these stones are uncertain.
[^27]: Compare Isaiah 60:19–20, "The sun will no longer be your light by day … But Yahweh will be your everlasting light".
[^28]: Compare Isaiah 60:3, 5, "Nations will come to your light, and kings to the brightness of your dawn". Many manuscripts read "the kings of the earth bring to it the glory and honor of the nations".
[^29]: Compare Isaiah 60:11, "Your gates will always stand open; day and night they will not be shut"; Zechariah 14:7.
[^30]: Compare Isaiah 52:1, "For the uncircumcised and the unclean will never enter you again."
