**1** Then the fifth angel blew his trumpet, and I saw a star that had fallen from heaven to the earth. He was given the key to the shaft of the abyss.[^1]

**2** He opened the shaft of the abyss, and smoke rose from the shaft like the smoke of a great furnace,[^2] and the sun and the air were darkened by the smoke from the shaft.

**3** Out of the smoke came locusts onto the earth,[^3] and they were given power like the power of the scorpions of the earth.

**4** They were told not to harm the grass of the earth or any green plant or any tree, but only those people who do not have the seal of God on their foreheads.[^4]

**5** They were not allowed to kill them, but only to torment them for five months, and their torment was like the torment of a scorpion when it stings someone.

**6** In those days people will seek death and will not find it; they will long to die, but death will flee from them.

**7** The locusts looked like horses prepared for battle.[^5] On their heads were what looked like crowns of gold, and their faces were like human faces.

**8** They had hair like women's hair, and their teeth were like lions' teeth.[^6]

**9** They had breastplates like breastplates of iron, and the sound of their wings was like the sound of chariots with many horses rushing into battle.[^7]

**10** They had tails like scorpions, with stings, and in their tails was their power to harm people for five months.

**11** They had as king over them the angel of the abyss. His name in Hebrew is Abaddon, and in Greek his name is Apollyon.[^8]

**12** The first woe[^9] has passed. Look, two more woes are still coming after this.

**13** Then the sixth angel blew his trumpet, and I heard a voice[^10] from the horns[^11] of the golden altar that is before God,

**14** saying to the sixth angel, who had the trumpet, "Release the four angels who are bound at the great river Euphrates."

**15** So the four angels were released, who had been made ready for the hour and day and month and year, to kill a third of humankind.

**16** The number of the troops of cavalry was twice ten thousand times ten thousand.[^12] I heard their number.

**17** This is how I saw the horses in my vision, and those who rode them: they had breastplates that were fiery red, dark blue and sulfur yellow.[^13] The heads of the horses were like the heads of lions, and out of their mouths came fire and smoke and sulfur.

**18** By these three plagues a third of humankind was killed: by the fire and the smoke and the sulfur that came out of their mouths.

**19** For the power of the horses is in their mouths and in their tails. Their tails are like snakes, with heads, and with them they do harm.

**20** The rest of humankind, who were not killed by these plagues, did not repent of the works of their hands. They did not stop worshiping demons, and idols of gold and silver and bronze and stone and wood, which cannot see or hear or walk.[^14]

**21** Nor did they repent of their murders or their sorceries[^15] or their sexual immorality or their thefts.

[^1]: Greek *abyssos*, "the deep", here the underworld, where evil powers are imprisoned (compare 20:1–3; Luke 8:31). In the Greek Old Testament it translates "the deep" of Genesis 1:2. Also in verses 2 and 11.
[^2]: Compare Genesis 19:28; Exodus 19:18.
[^3]: Compare the eighth plague of Egypt, Exodus 10:12–15, and the locusts of Joel 1–2.
[^4]: See 7:3.
[^5]: Compare Joel 2:4, "Their appearance is like the appearance of horses".
[^6]: Compare Joel 1:6, "Its teeth are a lion's teeth".
[^7]: Compare Joel 2:5, "With a noise like chariots".
[^8]: Hebrew *abaddon*, "destruction", a name for the realm of the dead (Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11). Greek *Apollyon*, "Destroyer".
[^9]: Greek *ouai*. See 8:13.
[^10]: Literally "one voice".
[^11]: Many manuscripts read "the four horns".
[^12]: Literally "two myriads of myriads", that is, 200 million. Many manuscripts read "ten thousand times ten thousand".
[^13]: Literally "fiery, hyacinth-colored and sulfur-like". *Hyacinth-colored*: dark blue or dark red.
[^14]: Compare Psalm 115:4–7; Daniel 5:23, "the gods of silver and gold, bronze, iron, wood and stone, which do not see or hear or know".
[^15]: Greek *pharmaka*, "drugs, potions, spells".
