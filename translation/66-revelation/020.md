**1** Then I saw an angel coming down from heaven, holding in his hand the key to the abyss[^1] and a great chain.

**2** He seized the dragon, that ancient serpent, who is the devil and Satan,[^2] and bound him for a thousand years.

**3** He threw him into the abyss, and locked it and sealed it over him, so that he would no longer deceive the nations until the thousand years were ended. After that he must be released for a short time.

**4** Then I saw thrones, and those seated on them were given authority to judge.[^3] I also saw the souls of those who had been beheaded because of their testimony to Jesus[^4] and because of the word of God, and those who had not worshiped the beast or its image and had not received its mark on their foreheads or on their hands. They came to life and reigned with the Messiah[^5] for a thousand years.

**5** The rest of the dead did not come to life until the thousand years were ended. This is the first resurrection.

**6** Blessed and holy is the one who has a share in the first resurrection. Over these the second death has no authority, but they will be priests of God and of the Messiah, and they will reign with him for a thousand years.

**7** When the thousand years are ended, Satan will be released from his prison,

**8** and he will go out to deceive the nations at the four corners of the earth, Gog and Magog,[^6] to gather them for battle. Their number is like the sand of the sea.

**9** They marched up across the breadth of the earth and surrounded the camp of God's holy people[^7] and the beloved city. But fire came down from heaven[^8] and consumed them.

**10** And the devil, who had deceived them, was thrown into the lake of fire and sulfur, where the beast and the false prophet also were,[^9] and they will be tormented day and night forever and ever.

**11** Then I saw a great white throne and the One seated on it. Earth and heaven fled from his presence, and no place was found for them.[^10]

**12** And I saw the dead, great and small, standing before the throne, and books[^11] were opened. Another book was opened, which is the book of life.[^12] The dead were judged according to their works, by what was written in the books.[^13]

**13** The sea gave up the dead that were in it, and Death and Hades[^14] gave up the dead that were in them, and each person was judged according to their works.

**14** Then Death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire. This is the second death, the lake of fire.

**15** And anyone who was not found written in the book of life was thrown into the lake of fire.

[^1]: See 9:1.
[^2]: Many manuscripts add "who deceives the whole world", as in 12:9.
[^3]: Literally "and they sat on them, and judgment was given to them". Compare Daniel 7:9, "thrones were set in place", and 7:22, "judgment was given in favor of the holy ones of the Most High".
[^4]: Or "the testimony of Jesus". See 1:2.
[^5]: Greek *tou christou*, "the Christ"; also in verse 6. See 11:15.
[^6]: Compare Ezekiel 38–39, where Gog, "of the land of Magog", leads the nations against Israel. Here both names stand for the nations.
[^7]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "the saints".
[^8]: Many manuscripts read "fire came down from God out of heaven". Compare Ezekiel 38:22; 39:6, "I will send fire on Magog"; 2 Kings 1:10.
[^9]: The Greek has no verb here; "were" is supplied.
[^10]: Compare Daniel 2:35, "not a trace of them could be found".
[^11]: Greek *biblia*, the plural of the word translated "scroll" elsewhere in Revelation. Compare Daniel 7:10, "and the books were opened".
[^12]: Literally "which is of life". See 3:5.
[^13]: Compare Psalm 62:13 (62:12 in most English Bibles), "For you repay each person according to what he has done"; Jeremiah 17:10.
[^14]: Greek *Hades*, the realm of the dead, like the Hebrew Sheol; also in verse 14.
