**1** "To the angel[^1] of the church in Sardis write: This is what the one who has the seven spirits of God[^2] and the seven stars says: I know your works. You have a reputation for being alive, but you are dead.[^3]

**2** Wake up,[^4] and strengthen what remains, which was about to die,[^5] for I have not found your works complete in the sight of my God.

**3** So remember what you have received and heard. Keep it, and repent. If you do not wake up, I will come like a thief, and you will not know at what hour I will come upon you.

**4** Yet you have a few people[^6] in Sardis who have not soiled their clothes, and they will walk with me dressed in white, because they are worthy.

**5** The one who conquers will be dressed like them in white clothes, and I will never erase their name from the book of life.[^7] I will acknowledge their name before my Father and before his angels.

**6** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

**7** To the angel of the church in Philadelphia write: This is what the Holy One, the True One, says, who has the key of David, who opens and no one will shut, and shuts and no one opens:[^8]

**8** I know your works. Look, I have set before you an open door, which no one is able to shut. I know that you have little strength, and yet you have kept my word and have not denied my name.

**9** Look, I will give you some of the synagogue of Satan,[^9] who say that they are Jews and are not, but are lying: look, I will make them come and bow down[^10] at your feet, and they will know that I have loved you.[^11]

**10** Because you have kept my word about endurance,[^12] I will also keep you from the hour of testing that is about to come on the whole world, to test those who live on the earth.

**11** I am coming soon. Hold fast to what you have, so that no one takes your crown.

**12** The one who conquers I will make a pillar in the temple of my God, and they will never go out of it again. I will write on them the name of my God, and the name of the city of my God, the new Jerusalem, which comes down out of heaven from my God, and my own new name.

**13** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

**14** To the angel of the church in Laodicea write: This is what the Amen says,[^13] the faithful and true witness, the beginning of God's creation:[^14]

**15** I know your works: you are neither cold nor hot. I wish you were either cold or hot.

**16** So, because you are lukewarm, and neither hot nor cold, I am about to spit you out of my mouth.

**17** For you say, 'I am rich; I have become wealthy and need nothing.' And you do not realize that you are wretched, pitiful, poor, blind and naked.

**18** I advise you to buy from me gold refined by fire, so that you may be rich; and white clothes to wear, so that the shame of your nakedness may not be seen; and salve to put on your eyes, so that you may see.

**19** Those whom I love, I correct and discipline.[^15] So be earnest, and repent.

**20** Look, I am standing at the door and knocking. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in to them and eat with them, and they with me.

**21** To the one who conquers I will give the right to sit with me on my throne,[^16] just as I also conquered and sat down with my Father on his throne.

**22** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches."

[^1]: Or "messenger"; also in verses 7 and 14. See 1:20.
[^2]: See the note on 1:4.
[^3]: Literally "You have a name that you are alive, and you are dead."
[^4]: Literally "Become watchful"; also in verse 3.
[^5]: Many manuscripts read "which you were about to throw away".
[^6]: Literally "a few names".
[^7]: Compare Exodus 32:32–33; Psalm 69:29 (69:28 in most English Bibles); Daniel 12:1.
[^8]: Isaiah 22:22, "What he opens, no one will shut, and what he shuts, no one will open." Many manuscripts read "who opens and no one will shut it except the one who opens, and no one will open".
[^9]: Literally "I give from the synagogue of Satan". The sentence breaks off and begins again with "look, I will make them come".
[^10]: Greek *proskyneo*, the verb translated "worship" elsewhere in Revelation. Compare Isaiah 60:14, "all who despised you will bow down at the soles of your feet"; also 45:14; 49:23.
[^11]: Compare Isaiah 43:4, "and I love you".
[^12]: Literally "the word of my endurance". Or "my command to endure".
[^13]: Hebrew *amen*, "firm, sure, true". Compare Isaiah 65:16, "the God of truth" (literally "the God of Amen").
[^14]: Or "the origin of God's creation", or "the ruler of God's creation". Greek *arche*, "beginning, origin, first place".
[^15]: Compare Proverbs 3:12, "for Yahweh corrects the one he loves". *Love*: Greek *phileo*.
[^16]: Literally "I will give him to sit with me on my throne".
