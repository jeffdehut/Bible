**1** Then he showed me the river of the water of life,[^1] bright as crystal, flowing from the throne of God and of the Lamb

**2** down the middle of the city's street. On either side of the river was the tree of life,[^2] bearing twelve kinds of fruit, yielding its fruit each month, and the leaves of the tree are for the healing of the nations.[^3]

**3** There will no longer be any curse.[^4] The throne of God and of the Lamb will be in the city, and his servants will worship him.[^5]

**4** They will see his face,[^6] and his name will be on their foreheads.

**5** There will be no more night. They will need no light of a lamp or light of the sun, for the Lord God will give them light,[^7] and they will reign forever and ever.[^8]

**6** Then he said to me, "These words are trustworthy and true. The Lord, the God of the spirits of the prophets, has sent his angel to show his servants what must soon take place.[^9]

**7** And look, I am coming soon! Blessed is the one who keeps the words of the prophecy of this scroll."[^10]

**8** I, John, am the one who heard and saw these things. When I heard and saw them, I fell down to worship at the feet of the angel who showed them to me.

**9** But he said to me, "Don't do that! I am a fellow servant with you and with your brothers and sisters[^11] the prophets, and with those who keep the words of this scroll. Worship God!"

**10** Then he said to me, "Do not seal up the words of the prophecy of this scroll, for the time is near.[^12]

**11** Let the one who does wrong still do wrong, and the filthy still be filthy; let the righteous still do what is right, and the holy still be holy."[^13]

**12** "Look, I am coming soon, and my reward is with me,[^14] to repay each person according to what they have done.[^15]

**13** I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end.[^16]

**14** Blessed are those who wash their robes,[^17] so that they may have the right to the tree of life and may enter the city by its gates.

**15** Outside are the dogs,[^18] the sorcerers, the sexually immoral, the murderers, the idolaters, and everyone who loves and practices falsehood.

**16** I, Jesus, have sent my angel to testify to you[^19] about these things for the churches. I am the root and the descendant of David,[^20] the bright morning star."[^21]

**17** The Spirit and the bride say, "Come!" And let the one who hears say, "Come!" Let the one who is thirsty come; let the one who wishes take the water of life without cost.[^22]

**18** I testify[^23] to everyone who hears the words of the prophecy of this scroll: if anyone adds to them, God will add to that person the plagues that are written in this scroll.

**19** And if anyone takes away from the words of the scroll of this prophecy, God will take away that person's share in the tree of life[^24] and in the holy city, which are written about in this scroll.[^25]

**20** The one who testifies to these things says, "Yes, I am coming soon." Amen. Come, Lord Jesus![^26]

**21** The grace of the Lord Jesus be with all.[^27]

[^1]: That is, the angel of 21:9. Many manuscripts read "a pure river of the water of life". Compare Ezekiel 47:1–12, the river that flows out from the temple; Zechariah 14:8, "On that day living water will flow out from Jerusalem".
[^2]: Genesis 2:9; 3:22–24. See 2:7.
[^3]: Or "flowing from the throne of God and of the Lamb. In the middle of the city's street, and on either side of the river, was the tree of life …". Compare Ezekiel 47:12, "They will bear fresh fruit every month … Their fruit will be for food and their leaves for healing."
[^4]: Compare Zechariah 14:11, "there will never again be a curse of destruction"; Genesis 3:17.
[^5]: Greek *latreuo*. See 7:15.
[^6]: Compare Psalm 17:15, "in righteousness I will see your face"; contrast Exodus 33:20.
[^7]: Compare Isaiah 60:19, "But the LORD will be your everlasting light". See 21:23.
[^8]: Compare Daniel 7:18, 27.
[^9]: See 1:1. Compare Daniel 2:28–29, 45. *The God of the spirits of the prophets*: compare Numbers 27:16, "the LORD, the God of the spirits of all living beings".
[^10]: In verse 7, as in verses 12–16 and 20, the speaker is Jesus.
[^11]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike.
[^12]: Contrast Daniel 8:26; 12:4, 9, where Daniel is told to seal the book until the time of the end.
[^13]: Compare Daniel 12:10, "Many will be purified, made white and refined, but the wicked will act wickedly."
[^14]: Compare Isaiah 40:10; 62:11, "Look, his reward is with him, and his recompense goes before him."
[^15]: Literally "as his work is". Compare Psalm 62:13 (62:12 in most English Bibles); Proverbs 24:12, "He will repay each person according to what he has done."
[^16]: See 1:8, 17; 21:6; Isaiah 44:6; 48:12.
[^17]: Many manuscripts read "Blessed are those who do his commands". See 7:14.
[^18]: A term of contempt for people regarded as impure. Compare Deuteronomy 23:19 (23:18 in most English Bibles); Philippians 3:2.
[^19]: *You* is plural in Greek.
[^20]: Literally "the root and the offspring of David". Compare Isaiah 11:1, 10; see 5:5.
[^21]: Compare Numbers 24:17, "A star will march out of Jacob". See 2:28.
[^22]: Compare Isaiah 55:1, "Come, all you who are thirsty, come to the waters! … without money and without cost." See 21:6.
[^23]: The speaker may be Jesus, "the one who testifies to these things" (verse 20), or John. Compare Deuteronomy 4:2, "Do not add anything to what I command you, and do not take anything away from it"; 13:1 (12:32 in most English Bibles).
[^24]: The Textus Receptus (followed by the King James Version) reads "the book of life".
[^25]: Many manuscripts read "may God add to that person" in verse 18 and "may God take away" here.
[^26]: Many manuscripts read "Amen. Yes, come, Lord Jesus!" Compare the Aramaic prayer *marana tha*, "Our Lord, come!" (1 Corinthians 16:22).
[^27]: The SBLGNT here follows NA28. Other editions read "be with God's holy people" (literally "the holy ones"), and Westcott and Hort read "the Lord Jesus Christ". Many manuscripts read "The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with all God's holy people. Amen."
