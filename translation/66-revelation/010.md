**1** Then I saw another mighty angel coming down from heaven, wrapped in a cloud, with a rainbow over his head. His face was like the sun, and his legs[^1] were like pillars of fire.

**2** He held in his hand a little scroll[^2] that lay open. He set his right foot on the sea and his left foot on the land,

**3** and he called out with a loud voice, like a lion roaring. When he called out, the seven thunders spoke with their own voices.[^3]

**4** When the seven thunders had spoken, I was about to write, but I heard a voice from heaven say, "Seal up what the seven thunders have said, and do not write it down."[^4]

**5** Then the angel whom I saw standing on the sea and on the land raised his right hand to heaven

**6** and swore by him who lives forever and ever, who created heaven and what is in it, the earth and what is in it, and the sea and what is in it:[^5] "There will be no more delay.[^6]

**7** But in the days of the sound of the seventh angel, when he is about to blow his trumpet, the mystery of God will be accomplished,[^7] as he announced to his own servants the prophets."[^8]

**8** Then the voice that I had heard from heaven spoke to me again, saying, "Go, take the scroll that lies open in the hand of the angel who is standing on the sea and on the land."

**9** So I went to the angel and told him to give me the little scroll. He said to me, "Take it and eat it. It will make your stomach bitter, but in your mouth it will be as sweet as honey."

**10** I took the little scroll from the angel's hand and ate it. It was as sweet as honey in my mouth, but when I had eaten it, my stomach was made bitter.[^9]

**11** Then I was told,[^10] "You must prophesy again about many peoples and nations and languages and kings."

[^1]: Literally "feet".
[^2]: Greek *biblaridion*, "little scroll", a different word from the scroll (*biblion*) of chapter 5; also in verses 9–10. Many manuscripts read *biblion*, "scroll", here and in verse 10.
[^3]: Compare Psalm 29:3–9, where "the voice of Yahweh" thunders seven times.
[^4]: Compare Daniel 8:26; 12:4, "keep the words secret and seal the book until the time of the end".
[^5]: Compare Daniel 12:7, where the man dressed in linen "raised his right hand and his left hand toward heaven and swore by the one who lives forever".
[^6]: Or "There will be no more time." Greek *chronos*, "time".
[^7]: Literally "was accomplished", a past tense used of what is certain to happen.
[^8]: Literally "as he announced the good news to his own servants the prophets". Compare Amos 3:7, "Surely the Lord Yahweh does nothing without revealing his plan to his servants the prophets."
[^9]: Compare Ezekiel 2:8–3:3, "So I ate it, and in my mouth it was as sweet as honey"; 3:14.
[^10]: Literally "they said to me", an indefinite plural.
