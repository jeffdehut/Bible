**1** "To the angel[^1] of the church in Ephesus write: This is what the one who holds the seven stars in his right hand says, the one who walks among the seven golden lampstands:[^2]

**2** I know your works, your labor and your endurance, and that you cannot tolerate evil people. You have tested those who call themselves apostles but are not, and you have found them to be false.

**3** You have endurance, and you have borne up for the sake of my name, and you have not grown weary.

**4** But I have this against you: you have abandoned the love you had at first.

**5** So remember how far you have fallen. Repent, and do the works you did at first. If not, I will come to you[^3] and remove your lampstand from its place, unless you repent.

**6** But you have this in your favor: you hate the works of the Nicolaitans,[^4] which I also hate.

**7** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who conquers[^5] I will give the right to eat[^6] from the tree of life, which is in the paradise of God.[^7]

**8** To the angel of the church in Smyrna write: This is what the first and the last says, who died and came to life:

**9** I know your suffering[^8] and your poverty, yet you are rich. And I know the slander of those who say that they are Jews and are not, but are a synagogue of Satan.[^9]

**10** Do not fear what you are about to suffer. Look, the devil is about to throw some of you into prison so that you may be tested, and for ten days you will have suffering. Be faithful until death, and I will give you the crown of life.[^10]

**11** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches. The one who conquers will not be harmed at all by the second death.[^11]

**12** To the angel of the church in Pergamum write: This is what the one who has the sharp two-edged sword says:

**13** I know where you live:[^12] where Satan's throne is.[^13] Yet you hold fast to my name, and you did not deny your faith in me[^14] even in the days of Antipas, my witness, my faithful one, who was put to death among you, where Satan lives.

**14** But I have a few things against you: you have some there who hold to the teaching of Balaam, who taught Balak to put a stumbling block before the Israelites, so that they would eat food sacrificed to idols and commit sexual immorality.[^15]

**15** In the same way you also have some who hold to the teaching of the Nicolaitans.

**16** So repent. If not, I will come to you soon and make war against them with the sword of my mouth.

**17** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who conquers I will give some of the hidden manna,[^16] and I will give them a white stone, with a new name written on the stone that no one knows except the one who receives it.[^17]

**18** To the angel of the church in Thyatira write: This is what the Son of God says, whose eyes are like a flame of fire and whose feet are like fine bronze:[^18]

**19** I know your works: your love and faith and service and endurance, and that your last works are greater than the first.

**20** But I have this against you: you tolerate the woman Jezebel,[^19] who calls herself a prophet, and who teaches and misleads my servants to commit sexual immorality and eat food sacrificed to idols.

**21** I have given her time to repent, but she refuses to repent of her sexual immorality.

**22** Look, I am throwing her onto a bed,[^20] and those who commit adultery with her into great suffering, unless they repent of her works.

**23** And I will strike her children dead.[^21] Then all the churches will know that I am the one who searches minds and hearts,[^22] and I will give to each of you according to your works.

**24** But to the rest of you in Thyatira, who do not hold this teaching and have not learned what they call 'the deep things of Satan,' I say this: I am not putting any other burden on you.

**25** Only hold fast to what you have until I come.

**26** To the one who conquers and keeps my works until the end, I will give authority over the nations,

**27** and he will rule them with an iron rod, as clay pots are shattered,[^23]

**28** just as I have received authority from my Father.[^24] And I will give him the morning star.[^25]

**29** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

[^1]: Or "messenger"; also in verses 8, 12 and 18. See 1:20.
[^2]: *This is what … says*: Greek *tade legei*, the Greek Old Testament's rendering of the prophets' "This is what Yahweh says"; also in verses 8, 12 and 18. *Walks among the seven golden lampstands*: see 1:13, 16, 20.
[^3]: Many manuscripts add "soon".
[^4]: A group known only from this chapter (also verse 15). Later Christian writers connected them with Nicolaus of Antioch (Acts 6:5), but what they taught is not known.
[^5]: Greek *nikao*, "conquer, be victorious", the verb translated "overcome" in John 16:33 and 1 John 5:4–5; also in verses 11, 17 and 26.
[^6]: Literally "I will give him to eat".
[^7]: Genesis 2:9; 3:22–24. *Paradise*: Greek *paradeisos*, "garden, park", the word used in the Greek Old Testament for the garden of Eden. Many manuscripts read "the paradise of my God".
[^8]: Many manuscripts read "I know your works, and your suffering". *Suffering*: or "tribulation", as in 1:9; also in verses 10 and 22.
[^9]: *Satan*: from the Hebrew *satan*, "adversary, accuser" (compare "the Accuser" in Zechariah 3:1–2); also in verses 13 and 24.
[^10]: Greek *stephanos*, a wreath given to a victor, rather than a king's crown (*diadema*, 12:3; 19:12).
[^11]: See 20:6, 14; 21:8.
[^12]: Many manuscripts read "I know your works and where you live".
[^13]: Pergamum was a center of the worship of the Roman emperor and of other gods. What is meant by "Satan's throne" is not certain.
[^14]: Literally "my faith".
[^15]: Numbers 25:1–3; 31:16. The prophets also use "sexual immorality" (Greek *porneia*) for unfaithfulness to God (Hosea 1–3; Ezekiel 16); also in verses 20–21.
[^16]: The food God gave Israel in the wilderness (Exodus 16:31–35). A jar of manna was kept before God in the ark (Exodus 16:33–34; Hebrews 9:4).
[^17]: Compare Isaiah 62:2; 65:15. The meaning of the white stone is not certain; such stones were used as tokens of admission and as votes to acquit.
[^18]: See 1:14–15.
[^19]: The name of the queen of Israel who promoted the worship of Baal (1 Kings 16:31; 18:19; 2 Kings 9:22). Many manuscripts read "your wife Jezebel".
[^20]: That is, probably a sickbed.
[^21]: Literally "kill with death"; perhaps "kill with plague" (compare 6:8).
[^22]: Literally "kidneys and hearts", the seat of the emotions and of thought. Compare Jeremiah 17:10, "I, Yahweh, search the heart and test the mind, to give each one according to his ways"; Psalm 7:10 (7:9 in most English Bibles).
[^23]: Psalm 2:8–9, following the Septuagint, "You will shepherd them with a rod of iron". The Hebrew reads "You will break them with a rod of iron; you will smash them like a potter's jar." *Rule*: Greek *poimaino*, "shepherd", and so "rule". Also in 12:5; 19:15.
[^24]: Literally "as I also have received from my Father".
[^25]: Compare 22:16, where Jesus is "the bright morning star", and Numbers 24:17, "A star will march out of Jacob".
