**1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus[^1] by command of God our Savior and of Christ Jesus our hope,

**2** to Timothy, my true child in the faith: Grace, mercy and peace from God the Father[^2] and Christ Jesus our Lord.

**3** I urged you, as I was going to Macedonia, to stay on in Ephesus,[^3] so that you might instruct certain people not to teach any different doctrine

**4** or to devote themselves to myths and endless genealogies, which promote speculations rather than God's plan,[^4] which works by faith.

**5** But the aim of our instruction is love that comes from a pure heart and a good conscience and sincere faith.

**6** Some have strayed from these things and turned aside to empty talk,

**7** wanting to be teachers of the Law, though they understand neither what they are saying nor the things about which they make confident assertions.

**8** Now we know that the Law is good, if one uses it lawfully,[^5]

**9** understanding this, that the Law is not laid down for a righteous person but for the lawless and rebellious, for the ungodly and sinners, for the unholy and profane, for those who kill their fathers or their mothers,[^6] for murderers,

**10** the sexually immoral, men who lie with males,[^7] slave traders,[^8] liars, perjurers, and whatever else is contrary to sound teaching,

**11** in keeping with the good news of the glory of the blessed God, with which I have been entrusted.

**12** I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who has strengthened me, because he considered me faithful, appointing me to his service,

**13** though formerly I was a blasphemer and a persecutor and a violent man. But I was shown mercy because I acted in ignorance and unbelief,

**14** and the grace of our Lord overflowed for me, with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.

**15** The saying is trustworthy[^9] and deserves full acceptance, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the foremost.[^10]

**16** But I received mercy for this reason, that in me, the foremost, Christ Jesus might display his perfect patience, as an example to those who were going to believe in him for eternal life.

**17** To the King of the ages, immortal, invisible, the only God,[^11] be honor and glory forever and ever.[^12] Amen.

**18** This instruction I entrust to you, Timothy, my child, in keeping with the prophecies once made about you, so that by them you may fight the good fight,[^13]

**19** holding on to faith and a good conscience. By rejecting conscience, some have suffered shipwreck in regard to the faith.

**20** Among them are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have handed over to Satan, so that they may be taught not to blaspheme.

[^1]: *Christ*: Greek *Christos*, "anointed one", the Greek equivalent of "Messiah". In Paul's letters it usually works as a name, so this translation writes "Christ". Paul's order, "Christ Jesus" or "Jesus Christ", is kept as the Greek has it.
[^2]: Many manuscripts read "God our Father".
[^3]: Literally "Just as I urged you to remain in Ephesus …". The Greek sentence is left unfinished; the sense is "so I urge you now".
[^4]: Greek *oikonomia*, "management, stewardship, plan", here the ordering of God's household; or "God's training". *Which works by faith*: literally "which is in faith".
[^5]: There is a play on words: *nomos*, "law", and *nomimos*, "lawfully".
[^6]: Or "those who strike their fathers or their mothers". The Greek words can mean either.
[^7]: Greek *arsenokoitai*, a rare compound of *arsen*, "male", and *koite*, "bed", which stand side by side in the Septuagint of Leviticus 18:22 and 20:13. Its exact reference is debated; see the note on 1 Corinthians 6:9.
[^8]: Or "kidnappers". Greek *andrapodistai*, people who captured others to sell them as slaves.
[^9]: The formula "The saying is trustworthy" occurs five times in the letters to Timothy and Titus (also 3:1; 4:9; 2 Timothy 2:11; Titus 3:8).
[^10]: Or "the worst"; also in verse 16. Greek *protos*, "first".
[^11]: Many manuscripts read "the only wise God".
[^12]: Literally "to the ages of the ages".
[^13]: Literally "wage the good warfare".
