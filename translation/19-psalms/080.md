**1** For the director. According to "Lilies".[^1] A testimony[^2] of Asaph. A psalm.[^3]

**2** Shepherd of Israel, listen, you who lead Joseph like a flock! You who are enthroned on the cherubim,[^4] shine out!

**3** Before Ephraim, Benjamin and Manasseh, stir up your might, and come to save us.

**4** God, restore us;[^5] let your face shine, and we will be saved.[^6]

**5** LORD God of Armies,[^7] how long will you smolder with anger[^8] against the prayer of your people?

**6** You have fed them the bread of tears, and made them drink tears by the measure.[^9]

**7** You make us an object of strife for our neighbors, and our enemies laugh among themselves.[^10]

**8** God of Armies, restore us; let your face shine, and we will be saved.

**9** You brought a vine out of Egypt; you drove out nations and planted it.

**10** You cleared the ground before it; it took deep root and filled the land.

**11** The mountains were covered with its shade, and the mighty cedars[^11] with its branches.

**12** It sent out its boughs to the Sea, and its shoots to the River.[^12]

**13** Why have you broken down its walls, so that all who pass along the road pluck its fruit?

**14** The boar from the forest[^13] ravages it, and all that moves in the field feeds on it.

**15** God of Armies, turn back, please! Look down from heaven and see; take care of this vine,

**16** the stock[^14] that your right hand planted, and the son[^15] you made strong for yourself.

**17** It is burned with fire; it is cut down. At the rebuke of your face they perish.

**18** Let your hand be on the man of your right hand,[^16] on the son of man[^17] you made strong for yourself.

**19** Then we will not turn away from you; give us life, and we will call on your name.

**20** LORD God of Armies, restore us; let your face shine, and we will be saved.

[^1]: Hebrew *el-shoshannim*, "according to lilies", probably the name of a tune (compare the titles of Psalms 45 and 69).
[^2]: Hebrew *edut*, "testimony", perhaps part of the name of the tune, "Lilies of the Testimony" (compare the title of Psalm 60).
[^3]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of Asaph*: or "for Asaph", a Levite who led the temple singers (see the note on 50:1). In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–20 are 80:1–19 in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Or "above the cherubim". Hebrew *keruvim*: winged heavenly beings. Two gold cherubim stood on the cover of the ark (Exodus 25:18–22).
[^5]: Or "bring us back". The refrain comes again, growing longer each time, in verses 8 and 20.
[^6]: *Let your face shine*: compare the priestly blessing in Numbers 6:25.
[^7]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. *The LORD God of Armies*: Hebrew *YHWH Elohim tseva'ot*. Compare "the LORD of Armies", traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Verse 20 has the same title, and verses 8 and 15 have *Elohim tseva'ot*, "God of Armies".
[^8]: Literally "will you smoke".
[^9]: Hebrew *shalish*, "a third", a measure of uncertain size, probably large.
[^10]: Literally "mock for themselves". The Septuagint reads "mock us".
[^11]: Literally "the cedars of God", that is, the tallest cedars.
[^12]: The Mediterranean Sea and the river Euphrates, the ideal limits of the land (compare Exodus 23:31).
[^13]: In the Hebrew text the letter *ayin* of *miya'ar*, "from the forest", is written raised above the line, a scribal mark. According to Jewish tradition it marks the middle letter of the book of Psalms.
[^14]: Hebrew *kannah*, found only here, probably "stock" or "root". Some take it as a verb, "protect".
[^15]: Hebrew *ben*, "son", here also a branch of the vine (compare Genesis 49:22, where Joseph is a fruitful *ben*). The Septuagint reads "the son of man", as in verse 18.
[^16]: Probably the king, or Israel as a whole. *Man of your right hand* may play on the name Benjamin, "son of the right hand" (verse 3).
[^17]: Hebrew *ben adam*, "son of man", a human being.
