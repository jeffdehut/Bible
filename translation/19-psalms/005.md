**1** For the director. With the Nehiloth.[^1] A psalm of David.[^2]

**2** Listen to my words, Yahweh;[^3] consider my sighing.

**3** Pay attention to the sound of my cry for help, my King and my God, for to you I pray.

**4** Yahweh, in the morning you hear my voice; in the morning I lay out my case before you[^4] and keep watch.

**5** For you are not a God who delights in wickedness; evil cannot stay with you as a guest.

**6** The boastful cannot stand before your eyes; you hate all evildoers.

**7** You destroy those who speak lies; Yahweh detests the violent and the deceitful.[^5]

**8** But I, through the greatness of your faithful love,[^6] will come into your house; I will bow down toward your holy temple in reverence for you.

**9** Yahweh, lead me in your righteousness, because of those who lie in wait for me; make your way straight before me.

**10** For there is nothing reliable in their mouth; their inner self is destruction. Their throat is an open grave; they flatter with their tongue.[^7]

**11** Declare them guilty, God; let them fall by their own schemes. Drive them out for their many offenses, for they have rebelled against you.

**12** But let all who take refuge in you rejoice; let them shout for joy forever. Spread your protection over them, so that those who love your name may exult in you.

**13** For you bless the righteous, Yahweh; you surround him with favor as with a shield.

[^1]: Hebrew *el-hannechilot*, a term of uncertain meaning, perhaps "for flutes".
[^2]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–13 are 5:1–12 in most English Bibles.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Literally "I set in order for you", perhaps a plea, or a sacrifice.
[^5]: Literally "a man of blood and deceit".
[^6]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^7]: Romans 3:13 quotes the second half of this verse.
