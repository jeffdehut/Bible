**1** A song of ascents. Of David.[^1] Look, how good and how pleasant it is when brothers live together in unity![^2]

**2** It is like the fine oil on the head, running down on the beard, the beard of Aaron, running down over the collar of his robes.[^3]

**3** It is like the dew of Hermon,[^4] which falls on the mountains of Zion. For there Yahweh[^5] has commanded the blessing: life forevermore.

[^1]: *A song of ascents*: Hebrew *shir ha-ma'alot* (see the note on 120:1). *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "the dwelling of brothers also together".
[^3]: Literally "over the mouth of his garments", the opening at the neck. The oil is the oil of anointing poured on Aaron's head when he was consecrated as high priest (Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 8:12).
[^4]: Mount Hermon, the highest mountain in the region, in the far north, known for its heavy dew.
[^5]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
