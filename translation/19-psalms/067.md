**1** For the director. With stringed instruments. A psalm. A song.[^1]

**2** May God be gracious to us and bless us; may he make his face shine on us.[^2] Selah.[^3]

**3** so that your way may be known on earth, your salvation among all nations.

**4** Let the peoples praise you, God; let all the peoples praise you.

**5** Let the nations be glad and sing for joy, for you judge the peoples with fairness, and guide the nations on earth. Selah.

**6** Let the peoples praise you, God; let all the peoples praise you.

**7** The earth has yielded its harvest; may God, our God, bless us.

**8** May God bless us, and let all the ends of the earth fear him.

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–8 are 67:1–7 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "make his face shine with us". The verse echoes the priestly blessing in Numbers 6:24–26.
[^3]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning. Also in verse 5.
