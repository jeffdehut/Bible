**1** Praise the LORD![^1] Give thanks to the LORD,[^2] for he is good; his faithful love[^3] endures forever.

**2** Who can tell of the mighty deeds of the LORD, or proclaim all his praise?

**3** Happy[^4] are those who keep justice, who do what is right at all times.

**4** Remember me, LORD, when you show favor to your people; come to me[^5] with your salvation,

**5** so that I may see the prosperity of your chosen ones, rejoice in the joy of your nation, and glory together with your inheritance.

**6** We have sinned, as our ancestors did;[^6] we have done wrong; we have acted wickedly.

**7** Our ancestors in Egypt did not understand your wonders; they did not remember your many acts of faithful love, but they rebelled by the sea, at the Red Sea.[^7]

**8** Yet he saved them for the sake of his name, to make his might known.

**9** He rebuked the Red Sea, and it dried up; he led them through the depths as through a desert.

**10** He saved them from the hand of those who hated them, and redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.

**11** The waters covered their adversaries; not one of them was left.

**12** Then they believed his words; they sang his praise.[^8]

**13** But they soon forgot what he had done; they did not wait for his counsel.

**14** They gave in to their craving in the wilderness, and put God to the test in the desert.

**15** He gave them what they asked for, but sent a wasting disease among them.[^9]

**16** In the camp they grew jealous of Moses, and of Aaron, the holy one of the LORD.

**17** The earth opened and swallowed Dathan, and covered the company of Abiram.

**18** Fire blazed among their company; flames burned up the wicked.[^10]

**19** They made a calf at Horeb and bowed down to a cast image.

**20** They exchanged their Glory[^11] for the image of an ox that eats grass.

**21** They forgot God their Savior, who had done great things in Egypt,

**22** wonders in the land of Ham,[^12] awesome deeds by the Red Sea.

**23** So he said he would destroy them, had not Moses, his chosen one, stood in the breach before him, to turn his wrath away from destroying them.[^13]

**24** Then they despised the pleasant land; they did not believe his word.

**25** They grumbled in their tents; they did not listen to the voice of the LORD.[^14]

**26** So he raised his hand against them,[^15] to make them fall in the wilderness,

**27** to make their offspring fall among the nations, and to scatter them through the lands.

**28** They joined in the worship of the Baal of Peor, and ate sacrifices offered to the dead.[^16]

**29** They provoked him to anger with their deeds, and a plague broke out among them.

**30** Then Phinehas stood up and intervened,[^17] and the plague was stopped.

**31** That was credited to him as righteousness,[^18] from generation to generation forever.

**32** They angered him at the waters of Meribah, and it went badly for Moses because of them;

**33** for they rebelled against his spirit,[^19] and he spoke rashly with his lips.[^20]

**34** They did not destroy the peoples, as the LORD had told them to,

**35** but they mixed with the nations and learned their ways.

**36** They served their idols, which became a snare to them.

**37** They sacrificed their sons and their daughters to demons.[^21]

**38** They shed innocent blood, the blood of their sons and their daughters, whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan, and the land was defiled with blood.

**39** They made themselves unclean by what they did, and prostituted themselves by their deeds.[^22]

**40** So the anger of the LORD burned against his people, and he abhorred his inheritance.

**41** He handed them over to the nations, and those who hated them ruled over them.

**42** Their enemies oppressed them, and they were brought low under their power.

**43** Many times he rescued them, but they kept rebelling in their plans, and they sank down in their iniquity.

**44** Yet he looked on their distress when he heard their cry.

**45** For their sake he remembered his covenant, and relented according to his many acts of faithful love.[^23]

**46** He caused them to be pitied by all who held them captive.

**47** Save us, LORD our God, and gather us from among the nations, so that we may give thanks to your holy name and glory in your praise.[^24]

**48** Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Let all the people say, "Amen!" Praise the LORD![^25]

[^1]: Hebrew *hallelu-yah*, "praise Yah" (see the note on 104:35). Also in verse 48. Psalm 106 has no other title.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. The rest of this verse is the same as 1 Chronicles 16:34 (compare 100:5; 107:1; 118:1; 136:1).
[^3]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Also in verses 7 and 45, where it is plural, "acts of faithful love".
[^4]: Hebrew *ashrei*: happy, fortunate; traditionally "blessed".
[^5]: Literally "visit me".
[^6]: Literally "with our ancestors". The psalm goes on to retell Israel's history as a history of rebellion and of God's mercy, from the exodus to the exile.
[^7]: Hebrew *yam suf*, literally "Sea of Reeds", traditionally "the Red Sea". Also in verses 9 and 22. For the rebellion at the sea, see Exodus 14:10–12.
[^8]: Compare Exodus 14:31–15:21.
[^9]: Literally "and sent leanness into their soul (*nephesh*)". The events of verses 14–15 are told in Numbers 11:4–34.
[^10]: The rebellion of Dathan and Abiram and the others is told in Numbers 16 (compare Deuteronomy 11:6).
[^11]: That is, God, the glory of Israel (compare Jeremiah 2:11; Romans 1:23). Jewish scribal tradition lists this verse among the places where the scribes are said to have changed a word out of reverence, reading "their glory" for an original "my glory". The story of the calf is told in Exodus 32.
[^12]: That is, Egypt (see 105:23).
[^13]: Like a soldier standing in a gap in a broken wall. See Exodus 32:9–14.
[^14]: The events of verses 24–27 are told in Numbers 13–14.
[^15]: The gesture of swearing an oath (compare Numbers 14:28–30; Ezekiel 20:23).
[^16]: Literally "sacrifices of the dead": either offerings to lifeless gods (compare Numbers 25:2), or offerings made for the dead. The events of verses 28–31 are told in Numbers 25:1–13.
[^17]: Hebrew *vayefallel*, "and he intervened", or "and he carried out judgment" (compare Numbers 25:7–8).
[^18]: The same words are used of Abraham in Genesis 15:6.
[^19]: That is, God's Spirit (compare Isaiah 63:10), or Moses' own spirit.
[^20]: The events are told in Numbers 20:2–13. *Meribah* means "quarreling".
[^21]: Hebrew *shedim*, a word found elsewhere only in Deuteronomy 32:17.
[^22]: A picture of Israel's unfaithfulness to God, as of a wife to her husband (compare Hosea 2; Ezekiel 16).
[^23]: The Hebrew text is written "his faithful love" but read "his acts of faithful love".
[^24]: Verses 47–48 are close to 1 Chronicles 16:35–36, where verse 35 begins "Say, 'Save us, God of our salvation! Gather us and rescue us from the nations…'"
[^25]: 1 Chronicles 16:36 has "Then all the people said, 'Amen!' and praised the LORD." The blessing in this verse closes Book 4 of the Psalms (Psalms 90–106). Books 1, 2 and 3 end in a similar way (41:14, English 41:13; 72:18–19; 89:53, English 89:52), and Psalm 150 closes the whole collection.
