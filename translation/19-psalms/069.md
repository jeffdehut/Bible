**1** For the director. According to "Lilies".[^1] Of David.[^2]

**2** Save me, God, for the waters have come up to my neck.[^3]

**3** I sink in deep mire, where there is no foothold; I have come into deep waters, and the flood sweeps over me.

**4** I am worn out with my crying; my throat is parched. My eyes grow dim as I wait for my God.

**5** More than the hairs of my head are those who hate me without cause;[^4] mighty are those who would destroy me, my enemies without reason.[^5] What I did not steal, must I now give back?

**6** God, you know my folly; my guilty deeds are not hidden from you.

**7** Do not let those who hope in you be put to shame because of me, Lord GOD of Armies;[^6] do not let those who seek you be disgraced because of me, God of Israel.

**8** For it is for your sake that I have borne insults, that shame has covered my face.

**9** I have become a stranger to my brothers, a foreigner to my mother's sons.

**10** For zeal for your house has consumed me,[^7] and the insults of those who insult you have fallen on me.[^8]

**11** When I wept and humbled myself with fasting,[^9] it brought insults on me.

**12** When I made sackcloth my clothing, I became a byword to them.

**13** Those who sit at the gate talk about me, and drunkards make songs about me.[^10]

**14** But as for me, my prayer is to you, LORD, at a time of favor. God, in your great faithful love,[^11] answer me with your sure salvation.[^12]

**15** Rescue me from the mire, and do not let me sink; let me be rescued from those who hate me, and from the deep waters.

**16** Do not let the flood sweep over me, or the deep swallow me up, or the pit[^13] close its mouth over me.

**17** Answer me, LORD, for your faithful love is good; in your great compassion turn to me.

**18** Do not hide your face from your servant, for I am in distress; answer me quickly.

**19** Come near to me[^14] and redeem me; set me free because of my enemies.

**20** You know the insults I suffer, my shame and my disgrace; all my adversaries are before you.

**21** Insults have broken my heart, and I am sick.[^15] I looked for sympathy, but there was none, and for comforters, but I found none.

**22** They gave me poison[^16] for food, and for my thirst they gave me sour wine to drink.[^17]

**23** Let their table before them become a trap, and a snare for those at ease.[^18]

**24** Let their eyes be darkened so that they cannot see, and make their loins tremble continually.[^19]

**25** Pour out your fury on them, and let your burning anger overtake them.

**26** Let their camp be desolate; let no one live in their tents.[^20]

**27** For they persecute the one you have struck, and they talk about the pain of those you have wounded.[^21]

**28** Add guilt to their guilt; let them have no share in your righteousness.[^22]

**29** Let them be blotted out of the book of the living; let them not be enrolled[^23] with the righteous.

**30** But I am afflicted and in pain; let your salvation, God, set me safe on high.

**31** I will praise the name of God with a song, and magnify him with thanksgiving.

**32** This will please the LORD more than an ox, a bull with its horns and hoofs.

**33** The humble will see it and be glad; you who seek God, let your hearts revive.

**34** For the LORD hears the needy, and does not despise his own who are prisoners.

**35** Let heaven and earth praise him, the seas and everything that moves in them.

**36** For God will save Zion and rebuild the cities of Judah; they will live there and possess it.

**37** The descendants[^24] of his servants will inherit it, and those who love his name will live in it.

[^1]: Hebrew *al-shoshannim*, "according to lilies", probably the name of a tune (see the title of Psalm 45).
[^2]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–37 are 69:1–36 in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *nephesh*, here "throat, neck". Elsewhere it means soul, life, self, as in verses 11 and 19.
[^4]: Compare John 15:25.
[^5]: Literally "my enemies of falsehood".
[^6]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^7]: Quoted in John 2:17.
[^8]: Quoted in Romans 15:3.
[^9]: Literally "When I wept in the fasting of my soul (*nephesh*)".
[^10]: Literally "and the songs of those who drink strong drink".
[^11]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Also in verse 17.
[^12]: Literally "in the faithfulness of your salvation".
[^13]: Literally "the well".
[^14]: Literally "to my soul (*nephesh*)".
[^15]: Hebrew *va'anushah*, found only here, perhaps "I am in despair".
[^16]: Hebrew *rosh*, a bitter and poisonous plant. The Septuagint reads "gall"; compare Matthew 27:34.
[^17]: Compare Matthew 27:48; Mark 15:36; John 19:28–29.
[^18]: Or "and for their allies". The Septuagint reads "and a retribution", and Romans 11:9–10 quotes verses 23–24 in that form.
[^19]: The Septuagint reads "and bend their backs continually", the wording quoted in Romans 11:10.
[^20]: Acts 1:20 applies this verse to Judas.
[^21]: The Septuagint reads "and they have added to the pain of those you have wounded".
[^22]: Literally "do not let them come into your righteousness".
[^23]: Literally "written".
[^24]: Literally "the seed".
