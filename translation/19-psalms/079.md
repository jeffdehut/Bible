**1** A psalm of Asaph.[^1] God, the nations have come into your inheritance; they have defiled your holy temple; they have made Jerusalem a heap of ruins.[^2]

**2** They have given the corpses of your servants as food to the birds of the sky, the flesh of your faithful ones[^3] to the wild animals of the earth.

**3** They have poured out their blood like water all around Jerusalem, and there is no one to bury them.[^4]

**4** We have become a disgrace to our neighbors, the scorn and mockery of those around us.

**5** How long, Yahweh?[^5] Will you be angry forever? Will your jealousy[^6] burn like fire?

**6** Pour out your wrath on the nations that do not know you, and on the kingdoms that do not call on your name.

**7** For they have devoured Jacob,[^7] and laid waste his homeland.[^8]

**8** Do not hold against us the iniquities of former times;[^9] let your compassion come quickly to meet us, for we have been brought very low.

**9** Help us, God of our salvation, for the sake of the glory of your name; rescue us and atone for our sins,[^10] for the sake of your name.

**10** Why should the nations say, "Where is their God?" Before our eyes, let it be known among the nations that you avenge the blood of your servants that has been poured out.[^11]

**11** Let the groaning of the prisoner come before you; by the greatness of your arm, preserve[^12] those condemned to die.[^13]

**12** Pay back our neighbors sevenfold into their laps[^14] for the taunts with which they have taunted you, Lord.

**13** Then we, your people, the sheep of your pasture, will give thanks to you forever; from generation to generation we will tell of your praise.

[^1]: *Of Asaph*: or "for Asaph", a Levite who led the temple singers (see the note on 50:1). In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles. The psalm mourns the plundering of Jerusalem and its temple, probably by the Babylonians (2 Kings 25:8–10; compare Psalm 74).
[^2]: Compare Micah 3:12 and Jeremiah 26:18.
[^3]: Hebrew *chasidim*, "the faithful, the loyal", related to *chesed*, "faithful love".
[^4]: 1 Maccabees 7:17 quotes verses 2–3.
[^5]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^6]: Or "zeal".
[^7]: The verb is singular in the Hebrew, "he has devoured", that is, the enemy. Verses 6–7 are nearly the same as Jeremiah 10:25.
[^8]: Or "his pasture", "his dwelling place".
[^9]: Literally "the iniquities of the first ones": either our own former sins, or the sins of our ancestors.
[^10]: Or "cover our sins", "forgive our sins". Hebrew *kapper*, the verb used for making atonement.
[^11]: Literally "Let the avenging of the poured-out blood of your servants be known among the nations before our eyes."
[^12]: Literally "leave over", that is, keep alive.
[^13]: Literally "the sons of death".
[^14]: Literally "into their bosom", the fold of a garment above the belt, where things were carried.
