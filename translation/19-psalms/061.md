**1** For the director. With a stringed instrument.[^1] Of David.[^2]

**2** Hear my cry, God; listen to my prayer.

**3** From the end of the earth I call to you, when my heart grows faint. Lead me to the rock that is higher than I.[^3]

**4** For you have been my refuge, a strong tower against the enemy.

**5** Let me live in your tent forever; let me take refuge in the shelter of your wings. Selah.[^4]

**6** For you, God, have heard my vows; you have given the inheritance of those who fear your name.

**7** Add days to the days of the king; may his years be like generation after generation.

**8** May he sit enthroned forever before God; appoint[^5] faithful love[^6] and truth to guard him.

**9** So I will sing praise to your name forever, as I fulfill my vows day after day.

[^1]: Hebrew *al-neginat*. Compare *binginot*, "with stringed instruments", in the titles of Psalms 4, 6, 54 and 55.
[^2]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–9 are 61:1–8 in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "On a rock too high for me, lead me". The Septuagint reads "You set me high on a rock".
[^4]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning.
[^5]: Hebrew *man*, an uncertain word, perhaps a short form of *mannah*, "appoint".
[^6]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
