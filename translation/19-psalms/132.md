**1** A song of ascents.[^1] LORD,[^2] remember David and all his hardships,[^3]

**2** how he swore an oath to the LORD and made a vow to the Mighty One of Jacob:[^4]

**3** "I will not enter the tent of my house or get into my bed;[^5]

**4** I will not give sleep to my eyes or slumber to my eyelids,

**5** until I find a place for the LORD, a dwelling place for the Mighty One of Jacob."

**6** Look, we heard of it in Ephrathah; we found it in the fields of Jaar.[^6]

**7** Let us go to his dwelling place; let us bow down at his footstool.[^7]

**8** Rise up, LORD, and go to your resting place, you and the ark of your might.[^8]

**9** May your priests be clothed with righteousness, and may your faithful ones[^9] shout for joy.

**10** For the sake of David your servant, do not reject your anointed one.[^10]

**11** The LORD swore to David a sure oath, from which he will not turn back: "One of your own descendants[^11] I will set on your throne.

**12** If your sons keep my covenant and my statutes[^12] that I will teach them, their sons also will sit on your throne forever."

**13** For the LORD has chosen Zion; he has desired it for his dwelling:

**14** "This is my resting place forever; here I will dwell,[^13] for I have desired it.

**15** I will richly bless its provisions; I will satisfy its needy with bread.

**16** Its priests I will clothe with salvation, and its faithful ones will shout aloud for joy.

**17** There I will make a horn sprout for David; I have prepared a lamp for my anointed one.[^14]

**18** His enemies I will clothe with shame, but on him his crown will shine."[^15]

[^1]: *A song of ascents*: Hebrew *shir ha-ma'alot* (see the note on 120:1). In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles. The psalm recalls how David brought the ark to Jerusalem (2 Samuel 6) and the LORD's promise to him (2 Samuel 7).
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Or "all his self-denial", the oath he kept (verses 3–5). Literally "all his being afflicted".
[^4]: Hebrew *avir ya'aqov*, an ancient title of God (Genesis 49:24; Isaiah 49:26). Also in verse 5.
[^5]: Literally "If I enter the tent of my house, if I go up on the couch of my bed", the form of an oath. Also in verse 4.
[^6]: *It* is probably the ark (verse 8). *Ephrathah* is another name for Bethlehem (Ruth 4:11; Micah 5:1, 5:2 in most English Bibles), or for the clan around Kiriath-jearim (1 Chronicles 2:50). *Jaar*, "forest", is probably Kiriath-jearim, "town of forests", where the ark stayed for twenty years until David brought it to Jerusalem (1 Samuel 7:1–2).
[^7]: The ark was pictured as the footstool of the LORD, who is enthroned above the cherubim (1 Chronicles 28:2; compare 99:5).
[^8]: Verses 8–10 are nearly the same as Solomon's prayer at the dedication of the temple in 2 Chronicles 6:41–42. Compare also Numbers 10:35.
[^9]: Hebrew *chasidim*, "the faithful, the loyal", related to *chesed*, "faithful love". Also in verse 16.
[^10]: Literally "do not turn away the face of your anointed one", that is, do not refuse his request (compare 1 Kings 2:16). Hebrew *meshichekha*, "your anointed one", the king of David's line, anointed at the LORD's command.
[^11]: Literally "From the fruit of your body". Compare 2 Samuel 7:12–16. Acts 2:30 recalls this oath.
[^12]: Hebrew *edot*, traditionally "testimonies": the solemn terms of the covenant.
[^13]: Or "here I will sit enthroned".
[^14]: Hebrew *meshichi*, "my anointed one", the king of David's line. The horn is a symbol of strength, and the lamp of a line of kings that does not die out (1 Kings 11:36). Later Jewish and Christian readers heard the promised Messiah in this verse; Luke 1:69 echoes it ("a horn of salvation in the house of his servant David").
[^15]: Or "will flourish", like the horn that sprouts in verse 17. Hebrew *nezer*, a crown or diadem, the sign of consecration worn by the king and the high priest (2 Samuel 1:10; Exodus 29:6).
