**1** A song of ascents.[^1] When Yahweh[^2] restored the fortunes of Zion,[^3] we were like those who dream.[^4]

**2** Then our mouths were filled with laughter, and our tongues with shouts of joy. Then they said among the nations, "Yahweh has done great things for them."

**3** Yahweh has done great things for us, and we were glad.[^5]

**4** Restore our fortunes, Yahweh, like streams in the Negev.[^6]

**5** Those who sow in tears will reap with shouts of joy.

**6** The one who goes out weeping, carrying the seed to sow,[^7] will surely come back with shouts of joy, carrying his sheaves.

[^1]: *A song of ascents*: Hebrew *shir ha-ma'alot* (see the note on 120:1). In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Or "brought back the captives of Zion". Hebrew *shivat tsiyyon*. Also in verse 4. The return from exile in Babylon may be meant.
[^4]: The Septuagint reads "like those who are comforted".
[^5]: Or "and we are glad".
[^6]: The dry south of Judah, whose empty streambeds fill suddenly with water when the rains come.
[^7]: Literally "carrying the drawing of seed", perhaps a bag of seed, or the handfuls drawn out for sowing.
