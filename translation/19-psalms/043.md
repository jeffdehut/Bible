**1** Vindicate me, God,[^1] and plead my case against an unfaithful nation;[^2] rescue me from the deceitful and unjust.[^3]

**2** For you are God my stronghold. Why have you rejected me? Why must I go about in mourning,[^4] oppressed by the enemy?

**3** Send out your light and your truth; let them lead me. Let them bring me to your holy mountain, to the place where you dwell.[^5]

**4** Then I will go to the altar of God, to God, my joy and my delight,[^6] and I will praise you with the lyre, God, my God.

**5** Why are you downcast, my soul?[^7] Why are you in turmoil within me? Hope in God, for I will yet praise him, my saving help[^8] and my God.

[^1]: Psalm 43 has no title, and its refrain (verse 5) is the refrain of Psalm 42 (42:6, 12). The two were probably once a single psalm.
[^2]: Hebrew *lo-chasid*, "not faithful", that is, ungodly. *Chasid* is related to *chesed*, "faithful love".
[^3]: Literally "from a man of deceit and injustice".
[^4]: Literally "dark", wearing the dark clothes of mourning.
[^5]: Literally "to your dwellings".
[^6]: Literally "the joy of my rejoicing".
[^7]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self.
[^8]: Literally "the salvation of my face".
