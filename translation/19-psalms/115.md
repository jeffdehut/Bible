**1** Not to us, LORD,[^1] not to us, but to your name give glory, because of your faithful love,[^2] because of your faithfulness.

**2** Why should the nations say, "Where, then, is their God?"

**3** Our God is in the heavens; he does whatever he pleases.

**4** Their idols are silver and gold, the work of human hands.

**5** They have mouths, but cannot speak; they have eyes, but cannot see.

**6** They have ears, but cannot hear; they have noses, but cannot smell.

**7** They have hands, but cannot feel; feet, but cannot walk; they make no sound in their throats.

**8** Those who make them will become like them, and so will all who trust in them.[^3]

**9** Israel, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield.[^4]

**10** House of Aaron, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield.

**11** You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield.

**12** The LORD has remembered us; he will bless. He will bless the house of Israel; he will bless the house of Aaron.

**13** He will bless those who fear the LORD, the small together with the great.

**14** May the LORD give you increase, you and your children.[^5]

**15** May you be blessed by the LORD, the maker of heaven and earth.

**16** The heavens are the heavens of the LORD,[^6] but the earth he has given to human beings.[^7]

**17** It is not the dead who praise the LORD,[^8] nor any who go down into silence.[^9]

**18** But we will bless the LORD from now on and forever. Praise the LORD![^10]

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. The Septuagint makes Psalm 115 part of Psalm 114 (see the note on 114:1). Verses 4–11 appear again, with small differences, in 135:15–20.
[^2]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^3]: Or "May those who make them become like them, and all who trust in them."
[^4]: Verses 9–11 call on Israel, the priests (the house of Aaron) and all who fear the LORD to trust in him. Each verse ends with the same refrain, which turns to speak of them as "their". Compare 118:2–4.
[^5]: Literally "May the LORD add to you, to you and to your sons".
[^6]: Literally "The heavens are heavens for the LORD".
[^7]: Literally "to the sons of *adam* (humankind)".
[^8]: Hebrew *Yah*, a short form of the name YHWH. Also in verse 18.
[^9]: That is, into the silent world of the dead (compare 94:17).
[^10]: Hebrew *hallelu-yah*, "praise Yah", the source of the word "Hallelujah".
