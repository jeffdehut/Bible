**1** For the director. With stringed instruments. A maskil of David.[^1]

**2** Listen to my prayer, God, and do not hide yourself from my plea for mercy.

**3** Pay attention to me and answer me. I am restless in my complaint, and I groan,

**4** at the voice of the enemy, because of the pressure of the wicked. For they bring down trouble on me, and in anger they bear a grudge against me.

**5** My heart writhes within me; the terrors of death have fallen on me.

**6** Fear and trembling come upon me, and horror overwhelms me.

**7** And I say, "Oh, that I had wings like a dove! I would fly away and be at rest.

**8** See, I would flee far away; I would lodge in the wilderness. Selah.[^2]

**9** I would hurry to find a shelter from the raging wind and the storm."

**10** Confuse them, Lord, divide their tongues,[^3] for I see violence and strife in the city.

**11** Day and night they go around it on its walls; wickedness and trouble are within it.

**12** Destruction is within it; oppression and deceit never leave its square.

**13** For it is not an enemy who taunts me, or I could bear it; it is not one who hates me who exalts himself against me, or I could hide from him.

**14** But it is you, a man like myself,[^4] my companion and my close friend.

**15** Together we used to share sweet counsel; we walked with the throng in the house of God.

**16** Let death come on them by surprise;[^5] let them go down alive to Sheol,[^6] for evil is in their dwellings, in their midst.

**17** As for me, I call to God, and Yahweh[^7] will save me.

**18** Evening and morning and at noon I complain and groan, and he hears my voice.

**19** He redeems my life[^8] in safety from the battle against me, for many are against me.[^9]

**20** God will hear and answer them,[^10] he who is enthroned from of old. Selah. They never change their ways,[^11] and they do not fear God.

**21** He has stretched out his hands against his friends;[^12] he has violated his covenant.

**22** His speech is smoother than butter,[^13] but war is in his heart; his words are softer than oil, but they are drawn swords.

**23** Cast your burden[^14] on Yahweh, and he will sustain you; he will never let the righteous be shaken.

**24** But you, God, will bring them down to the pit of destruction;[^15] people of bloodshed and deceit will not live out half their days. But as for me, I will trust in you.

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Maskil*: a term of uncertain meaning, perhaps "a skillful psalm" or "a psalm of instruction". *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–24 are 55:1–23 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning. Also in verse 20.
[^3]: Literally "Swallow up, Lord, divide their tongue". Compare the confusing of languages at Babel (Genesis 11:7–9).
[^4]: Literally "a man according to my rank", my equal.
[^5]: The Hebrew text is written as one word, *yeshimot*, "Desolations be on them!", but read as two, *yashi mavet*, "Let death come deceitfully on them".
[^6]: Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead. Compare Numbers 16:30–33.
[^7]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^8]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self.
[^9]: Literally "for with many they are with me": either many are against me, or many are on my side.
[^10]: That is, answer them with judgment. The same consonants can be read "humble them".
[^11]: Literally "who have no changes". The Hebrew of verses 20–22 is difficult in places.
[^12]: Literally "against those at peace with him".
[^13]: The Hebrew is unusual: literally "the butter-words of his mouth are smooth".
[^14]: Hebrew *yehav*, found only here, perhaps "what is given to you", your lot. The Septuagint reads "your care", and 1 Peter 5:7 echoes this line.
[^15]: Literally "the well of the pit".
