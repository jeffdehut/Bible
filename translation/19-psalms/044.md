**1** For the director. Of the sons of Korah. A maskil.[^1]

**2** God, we have heard with our own ears; our ancestors have told us the work you did in their days, in the days of old.

**3** With your own hand you drove out nations, but them you planted; you brought disaster on peoples, but them you set free.[^2]

**4** For it was not by their sword that they took the land, and their own arm did not give them victory; it was your right hand and your arm and the light of your face, because you delighted in them.

**5** You are my King, God; command victories[^3] for Jacob.

**6** Through you we gore our adversaries; through your name we trample those who rise up against us.

**7** For I do not trust in my bow, and my sword does not save me.

**8** But you have saved us from our adversaries, and you have put those who hate us to shame.

**9** In God we have boasted all day long, and we will praise your name forever. Selah.[^4]

**10** Yet you have rejected us and humiliated us; you no longer go out with our armies.

**11** You make us turn back from the adversary, and those who hate us take plunder for themselves.

**12** You hand us over like sheep to be eaten, and you have scattered us among the nations.

**13** You sell your people for nothing; you make no profit from their price.

**14** You make us a disgrace to our neighbors, the scorn and mockery of those around us.

**15** You make us a byword among the nations; the peoples shake their heads at us.

**16** All day long my disgrace is before me, and the shame of my face has covered me,

**17** at the voice of the one who taunts and reviles, at the sight of the enemy and the avenger.

**18** All this has come upon us, though we have not forgotten you or been false to your covenant.

**19** Our hearts have not turned back, and our steps have not strayed from your path.

**20** Yet you have crushed us in the haunt of jackals, and covered us with deep darkness.[^5]

**21** If we had forgotten the name of our God, or spread out our hands to a foreign god,

**22** would not God have searched this out? For he knows the secrets of the heart.

**23** Yet for your sake we are killed all day long; we are counted as sheep for the slaughter.[^6]

**24** Wake up! Why do you sleep, Lord? Rouse yourself! Do not reject us forever.

**25** Why do you hide your face, and forget our affliction and our oppression?

**26** For we have sunk down to the dust; our bodies cling to the ground.[^7]

**27** Rise up and help us! Redeem us for the sake of your faithful love.[^8]

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of the sons of Korah*: or "for the sons of Korah", a family of Levites who served as temple singers. *Maskil*: a term of uncertain meaning, perhaps "a skillful psalm" or "a psalm of instruction". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–27 are 44:1–26 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: *Them*: our ancestors (verse 2). *Set free*: or "let spread out", like a vine sending out its branches (compare 80:12, English 80:11).
[^3]: Literally "salvations".
[^4]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning.
[^5]: Hebrew *tsalmavet*, "deep darkness", traditionally read as "the shadow of death".
[^6]: Romans 8:36 quotes this verse.
[^7]: Literally "For our soul (*nephesh*) is bowed down to the dust; our belly clings to the ground."
[^8]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
