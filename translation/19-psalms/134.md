**1** A song of ascents.[^1] Come,[^2] bless the LORD,[^3] all you servants of the LORD, who stand in the house of the LORD by night.

**2** Lift up your hands toward the sanctuary,[^4] and bless the LORD.

**3** May the LORD bless you from Zion, the maker of heaven and earth.[^5]

[^1]: *A song of ascents*: Hebrew *shir ha-ma'alot* (see the note on 120:1). In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles. This is the last of the Songs of Ascents (Psalms 120–134).
[^2]: Literally "Look".
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Or "in holiness". Hebrew *qodesh*, "holiness, the holy place".
[^5]: "You" is singular: the priests' blessing on each worshiper (compare Numbers 6:24).
