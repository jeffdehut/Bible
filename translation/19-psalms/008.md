**1** For the director. According to the Gittith.[^1] A psalm of David.[^2]

**2** LORD,[^3] our Lord, how majestic is your name in all the earth! You have set your splendor above the heavens.[^4]

**3** Out of the mouths of children and nursing infants you have established strength[^5] because of your adversaries, to silence the enemy and the avenger.

**4** When I look at your heavens, the work of your fingers, the moon and the stars, which you have set in place,

**5** what is a human being, that you remember him, a son of man,[^6] that you care for him?

**6** You made him a little lower than God,[^7] and crowned him with glory and honor.

**7** You made him ruler over the works of your hands; you put everything under his feet:[^8]

**8** all sheep and cattle, and the wild animals too,

**9** the birds of the sky and the fish of the sea, whatever passes along the paths of the seas.

**10** LORD, our Lord, how majestic is your name in all the earth!

[^1]: Hebrew *gittit*, a term of uncertain meaning, perhaps an instrument or tune from Gath, or a song for the winepress (*gat*). Also in the titles of Psalms 81 and 84.
[^2]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–10 are 8:1–9 in most English Bibles.
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: The Hebrew of this line is difficult. Literally "which set your splendor on the heavens". Or "whose splendor is praised above the heavens".
[^5]: The Septuagint reads "you have prepared praise", the wording quoted in Matthew 21:16.
[^6]: Literally "son of *adam* (humankind)", that is, a human being, a mortal. Hebrews 2:6–8 quotes verses 5–7 from the Septuagint. Compare Job 7:17.
[^7]: Hebrew *elohim*, which can mean "God", "gods" or "heavenly beings": so also "a little lower than the heavenly beings". The Septuagint reads "a little lower than the angels", the wording quoted in Hebrews 2:7.
[^8]: Quoted in 1 Corinthians 15:27 and Ephesians 1:22.
