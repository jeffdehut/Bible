**1** Of David.[^1] Bless the LORD,[^2] my soul,[^3] and all that is within me, bless his holy name.

**2** Bless the LORD, my soul, and do not forget all his benefits:

**3** he forgives all your iniquity; he heals all your diseases;

**4** he redeems your life from the pit; he crowns you with faithful love[^4] and compassion;

**5** he satisfies you with good things as long as you live,[^5] so that your youth is renewed like the eagle's.[^6]

**6** The LORD does what is right,[^7] and brings justice for all who are oppressed.

**7** He made known his ways to Moses, his deeds to the people of Israel.[^8]

**8** The LORD is compassionate and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in faithful love.[^9]

**9** He will not always accuse, nor will he keep his anger forever.

**10** He has not dealt with us according to our sins, or repaid us according to our iniquities.

**11** For as high as the heavens are above the earth, so great is his faithful love toward those who fear him.

**12** As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us.

**13** As a father has compassion on his children, so the LORD has compassion on those who fear him.

**14** For he knows how we are formed;[^10] he remembers that we are dust.

**15** As for human beings, their days are like grass; they flourish like a flower of the field;

**16** when the wind passes over it, it is gone, and its place knows it no more.

**17** But the faithful love of the LORD is from everlasting to everlasting on those who fear him, and his righteousness to their children's children,

**18** to those who keep his covenant and remember to do his precepts.

**19** The LORD has established his throne in the heavens, and his kingdom rules over all.

**20** Bless the LORD, you his angels, you mighty ones who carry out his word, who obey the voice of his word.

**21** Bless the LORD, all his armies,[^11] you his servants who do his will.

**22** Bless the LORD, all his works, in all places where he rules. Bless the LORD, my soul.

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Also in verses 2 and 22. The psalmist speaks to himself.
[^4]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Also in verses 8, 11 and 17.
[^5]: Hebrew *edyekh*, "your ornament", a word of uncertain meaning here, perhaps "your years" or "your life". The Septuagint reads "your desire".
[^6]: Hebrew *nesher*, the eagle or the vulture (compare Isaiah 40:31).
[^7]: Literally "The LORD does righteous acts".
[^8]: Compare Moses' prayer in Exodus 33:13, "show me your ways".
[^9]: These words echo God's description of himself in Exodus 34:6 (compare 86:15; 145:8).
[^10]: Literally "our form", from the verb used in Genesis 2:7, where God forms the man from the dust.
[^11]: Hebrew *tseva'av*, "his armies", the heavenly hosts (compare "the LORD of Armies", 24:10).
