**1** The LORD[^1] reigns![^2] Let the peoples tremble! He is enthroned on the cherubim;[^3] let the earth quake!

**2** The LORD is great in Zion; he is exalted above all the peoples.

**3** Let them praise your great and awesome name. Holy is he![^4]

**4** The King in his might loves justice.[^5] You have established fairness; you have done justice and righteousness in Jacob.

**5** Exalt the LORD our God, and bow down at his footstool.[^6] Holy is he!

**6** Moses and Aaron were among his priests, and Samuel among those who called on his name.[^7] They called to the LORD, and he answered them.

**7** He spoke to them in the pillar of cloud;[^8] they kept his statutes[^9] and the decree he gave them.

**8** LORD our God, you answered them; you were a God who forgave them, yet you punished their misdeeds.[^10]

**9** Exalt the LORD our God, and bow down at his holy mountain, for the LORD our God is holy.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. Psalm 99 has no title in the Hebrew.
[^2]: Or "The LORD has become king!" (see the note on 93:1). Psalm 99 is the last of the "The LORD is king" psalms.
[^3]: Or "above the cherubim". Hebrew *keruvim*: winged heavenly beings. Two gold cherubim stood on the cover of the ark (Exodus 25:18–22).
[^4]: This refrain comes again in verse 5, and in a longer form at the end of verse 9 (compare Isaiah 6:3).
[^5]: Literally "And the strength of a king loves justice." Or "Mighty King, who loves justice, you have established fairness".
[^6]: The ark, or the temple where it stood (compare 132:7; 1 Chronicles 28:2).
[^7]: Aaron was the first high priest. Moses also served as a priest when the priesthood was set up (Exodus 24:4–8; Leviticus 8), and Samuel offered sacrifices (1 Samuel 7:9). All three were known for interceding for the people (Exodus 32:11–14; Numbers 17:11–13, English 16:46–48; 1 Samuel 7:5–9; compare Jeremiah 15:1).
[^8]: Compare Exodus 33:9 and Numbers 12:5.
[^9]: Hebrew *edot*, traditionally "testimonies": the solemn terms of the covenant.
[^10]: Or "and you avenged the wrongs done to them". *A God who forgave*: literally "a God who lifts", the word used for forgiving in Exodus 34:7.
