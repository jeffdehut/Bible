**1** For the director. With stringed instruments. A maskil of David,[^1]

**2** when the Ziphites came and said to Saul, "Is not David hiding among us?"[^2]

**3** God, save me by your name, and vindicate me by your might.

**4** God, hear my prayer; listen to the words of my mouth.

**5** For strangers[^3] have risen against me, and ruthless men seek my life;[^4] they do not set God before them. Selah.[^5]

**6** See, God is my helper; the Lord is the one who sustains my life.[^6]

**7** He will repay with evil those who lie in wait for me.[^7] In your faithfulness, destroy them.

**8** With a freewill offering I will sacrifice to you; I will give thanks to your name, Yahweh,[^8] for it is good.

**9** For he has rescued me from every trouble, and my eye has looked in triumph on my enemies.[^9]

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Maskil*: a term of uncertain meaning, perhaps "a skillful psalm" or "a psalm of instruction". *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verses 1–2, so verses 3–9 are 54:1–7 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: The events are told in 1 Samuel 23:19–28 and 26:1.
[^3]: Psalm 86:14, which is almost the same as this line, has "the arrogant" (*zedim*), a word that differs by one similar letter from *zarim*, "strangers".
[^4]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self.
[^5]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning.
[^6]: Literally "the Lord is among those who uphold my soul (*nephesh*)".
[^7]: The Hebrew text is written "Evil will return to those who lie in wait for me" but read "He will repay with evil those who lie in wait for me".
[^8]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^9]: Literally "my eye has looked on my enemies".
