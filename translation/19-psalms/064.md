**1** For the director. A psalm of David.[^1]

**2** Hear my voice, God, in my complaint; protect my life from the terror of the enemy.

**3** Hide me from the secret plots of those who do evil, from the scheming throng of evildoers,

**4** who sharpen their tongues like swords, and aim bitter words like arrows,

**5** to shoot from hiding at the blameless; suddenly they shoot at him, and they have no fear.

**6** They encourage one another in an evil plan;[^2] they talk of hiding snares; they say, "Who will see them?"

**7** They devise injustices: "We have perfected a well-made plan!" For the inner thoughts and the heart of a man are deep.[^3]

**8** But God will shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly they will be wounded.

**9** They will be brought down, with their own tongues turned against them;[^4] all who see them will shake their heads.

**10** Then all people will fear; they will proclaim the work of God and ponder what he has done.

**11** Let the righteous rejoice in Yahweh[^5] and take refuge in him; let all the upright in heart give praise.[^6]

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–11 are 64:1–10 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "They make strong for themselves an evil word".
[^3]: The Hebrew of this verse is difficult. Literally "They search out injustices; we have finished a searched-out search; and the inward part of a man and the heart are deep".
[^4]: Literally "They make him stumble, their tongue against them".
[^5]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^6]: Or "let them glory", "boast".
