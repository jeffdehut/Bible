**1** Give thanks to Yahweh,[^1] for he is good; his faithful love[^2] endures forever.

**2** Let the redeemed of Yahweh say so, those he redeemed from the hand of the adversary,

**3** and gathered from the lands, from the east and from the west, from the north and from the sea.[^3]

**4** Some wandered in the wilderness, in the desert wastes;[^4] they found no way to a city to live in.

**5** They were hungry and thirsty; their soul[^5] fainted within them.

**6** Then they cried out to Yahweh in their trouble, and he rescued them from their distress.[^6]

**7** He led them by a straight road, to go to a city to live in.

**8** Let them give thanks to Yahweh for his faithful love, and for his wonders for humankind.[^7]

**9** For he satisfies the thirsty soul, and fills the hungry soul with good things.

**10** Some sat in darkness and deep darkness,[^8] prisoners in misery and in iron,

**11** because they had rebelled against the words of God, and spurned the counsel of the Most High.[^9]

**12** So he humbled their hearts with hard labor; they stumbled, and there was no one to help.

**13** Then they cried out to Yahweh in their trouble, and he saved them from their distress.

**14** He brought them out of darkness and deep darkness, and snapped their chains.

**15** Let them give thanks to Yahweh for his faithful love, and for his wonders for humankind.

**16** For he shatters the doors of bronze, and cuts through the bars of iron.[^10]

**17** Some were fools because of their rebellious ways,[^11] and suffered affliction because of their iniquities.

**18** They loathed all food, and they drew near to the gates of death.

**19** Then they cried out to Yahweh in their trouble, and he saved them from their distress.

**20** He sent out his word and healed them, and rescued them from their graves.[^12]

**21** Let them give thanks to Yahweh for his faithful love, and for his wonders for humankind.

**22** Let them offer sacrifices of thanksgiving,[^13] and tell of his deeds with songs of joy.

**23** Some went down to the sea in ships, doing business on the great waters.

**24** They saw the works of Yahweh, his wonders in the deep.

**25** For he spoke and raised a stormy wind, which lifted high the waves of the sea.

**26** They went up to the heavens; they went down to the depths; their courage[^14] melted away in their peril.

**27** They reeled and staggered like drunkards, and all their skill[^15] was swallowed up.

**28** Then they cried out to Yahweh in their trouble, and he brought them out of their distress.

**29** He made the storm a calm, and its waves were still.

**30** They were glad when it grew quiet, and he guided them to the harbor[^16] they longed for.

**31** Let them give thanks to Yahweh for his faithful love, and for his wonders for humankind.

**32** Let them exalt him in the assembly of the people, and praise him in the council of the elders.

**33** He turns rivers into a desert, and springs of water into thirsty ground,

**34** a fruitful land into a salt waste, because of the wickedness of those who live there.

**35** He turns a desert into pools of water, and dry land into springs of water.

**36** There he settles the hungry, and they establish a city to live in.

**37** They sow fields and plant vineyards, and gather a fruitful harvest.

**38** He blesses them, and they become very many, and he does not let their cattle decrease.

**39** When they are diminished and brought low through oppression, trouble and sorrow,[^17]

**40** he pours contempt on nobles, and makes them wander in a trackless waste;[^18]

**41** but he lifts the needy out of affliction, and makes their families like flocks.

**42** The upright see it and are glad, and all injustice shuts its mouth.[^19]

**43** Whoever is wise, let him heed these things, and consider the acts of faithful love of Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. Psalm 107 has no title. It begins Book 5 of the Psalms (Psalms 107–150). Its first verse is the same as 106:1b (compare 100:5; 118:1; 1 Chronicles 16:34).
[^2]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Also in verses 8, 15, 21, 31 and 43, where it is plural, "acts of faithful love".
[^3]: *The sea* usually means the west. Here it may mean the south (the Red Sea), to complete the four directions.
[^4]: Literally "in the wasteland of a road", or "in the wasteland, (without) a road".
[^5]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Also in verses 9, 18 and 26.
[^6]: This refrain comes again in verses 13, 19 and 28, and the refrain of verse 8 in verses 15, 21 and 31. They frame four pictures of people rescued by God: travelers lost in the desert (verses 4–9), prisoners (10–16), the sick (17–22) and sailors (23–32).
[^7]: Literally "the sons of *adam*". Also in verses 15, 21 and 31.
[^8]: Hebrew *tsalmavet*, "deep darkness", traditionally read as "the shadow of death". Also in verse 14.
[^9]: Hebrew *Elyon*, a title of God.
[^10]: Compare Isaiah 45:2.
[^11]: Hebrew *evilim*, "fools", those whose folly is moral, not merely a lack of sense. Their sickness (verse 18) is the result of their sin.
[^12]: Hebrew *shechitotam*, a rare word, perhaps "their pits" or "their destruction". In the Hebrew text, a mark shaped like the letter *nun* written upside down stands after each of verses 20–25 and 39, the scribal sign found also at Numbers 10:35–36. Its meaning here is uncertain.
[^13]: Hebrew *todah*: a sacrifice given in thanksgiving (Leviticus 7:12).
[^14]: Literally "their soul (*nephesh*)".
[^15]: Literally "wisdom".
[^16]: Hebrew *mechoz*, a word found only here, probably "harbor" or "city".
[^17]: The thought of verses 39–41 is compressed: God humbles the proud and lifts up the needy (compare 1 Samuel 2:7–8).
[^18]: Verse 40 repeats Job 12:21a and 12:24b. Hebrew *tohu*, a formless waste (Genesis 1:2).
[^19]: Compare Job 5:16.
