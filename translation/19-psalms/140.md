**1** For the director. A psalm of David.[^1]

**2** Rescue me, LORD,[^2] from evil people;[^3] guard me from the violent,

**3** who plan evil things in their hearts and stir up wars every day.

**4** They sharpen their tongues like a serpent's; the venom of vipers is under their lips.[^4] Selah.[^5]

**5** Keep me, LORD, from the hands of the wicked; guard me from the violent, who plan to trip up my feet.

**6** The arrogant have hidden a trap for me, and cords; they have spread a net beside the path; they have set snares for me. Selah.

**7** I say to the LORD, "You are my God." Listen, LORD, to my voice as I plead for mercy.

**8** GOD the Lord, my strong salvation,[^6] you shield my head on the day of battle.

**9** Do not grant, LORD, the desires of the wicked; do not let their schemes succeed, or they will be exalted. Selah.

**10** As for the heads of those who surround me,[^7] may the trouble their own lips cause cover them.

**11** May burning coals fall on them;[^8] may they be thrown into the fire, into deep pits,[^9] never to rise again.

**12** Do not let a slanderer[^10] be established in the land; may evil hunt down the violent with blow upon blow.[^11]

**13** I know[^12] that the LORD upholds the cause of the afflicted, and justice for the needy.

**14** Surely the righteous will give thanks to your name; the upright will live in your presence.

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–14 are 140:1–13 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "from an evil human being (*adam*); from a man of violence". Also in verse 5.
[^4]: Romans 3:13 quotes this line.
[^5]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning. Also in verses 6 and 9.
[^6]: Literally "the strength of my salvation". *GOD the Lord*: Hebrew *YHWH Adonai*. Next to "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^7]: Literally "The head of those who surround me". Or, joining the word to the end of verse 9, "they lift up their heads".
[^8]: The Hebrew text is written "May they bring down burning coals on them" but read "May burning coals fall on them". *May they be thrown*: literally "may he make them fall".
[^9]: Hebrew *mahamorot*, a word found only here, perhaps "pits of water" or "deep pits".
[^10]: Literally "a man of tongue".
[^11]: Hebrew *lemadchefot*, a word found only here, perhaps "with thrust upon thrust" or "to their overthrow".
[^12]: The Hebrew text is written "you know" but read "I know".
