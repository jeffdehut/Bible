**1** For the director. Of the servant of Yahweh,[^1] of David.[^2]

**2** Rebellion speaks to the wicked deep in my heart:[^3] there is no fear of God before his eyes.[^4]

**3** For it flatters him in his own eyes, that his guilt will not be found out and hated.[^5]

**4** The words of his mouth are wickedness and deceit; he has stopped acting wisely and doing good.

**5** He plans wickedness on his bed; he sets himself on a path that is not good; he does not reject evil.

**6** Yahweh, your faithful love[^6] reaches to the heavens, your faithfulness to the clouds.

**7** Your righteousness is like the mighty mountains,[^7] your judgments like the great deep. Yahweh, you save humans and animals alike.

**8** How precious is your faithful love, God! Human beings take refuge in the shadow of your wings.

**9** They feast on the abundance of your house, and you give them drink from your river of delights.[^8]

**10** For with you is the fountain of life; in your light we see light.

**11** Continue your faithful love to those who know you, and your righteousness to the upright in heart.

**12** Do not let the foot of the proud come against me, or the hand of the wicked drive me away.

**13** There the evildoers lie fallen; they are thrust down and cannot rise.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–13 are 36:1–12 in most English Bibles.
[^3]: The Hebrew of this line is difficult. Literally "An oracle of rebellion to the wicked in the middle of my heart". *Oracle* (*ne'um*) is the word used in "says Yahweh". Or "I know deep in my heart what rebellion says to the wicked". The Septuagint reads "The lawless one says in himself that he will sin".
[^4]: Romans 3:18 quotes the second line of this verse.
[^5]: The Hebrew of this verse is difficult. Literally "For he makes it smooth to him in his eyes, to find his guilt, to hate".
[^6]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^7]: Literally "the mountains of God".
[^8]: The word for "delights" (*adanim*) is related to the name *Eden*.
