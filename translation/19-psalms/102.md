**1** A prayer of one who is afflicted, when he grows faint and pours out his complaint before Yahweh.[^1]

**2** Yahweh, hear my prayer; let my cry for help come to you.

**3** Do not hide your face from me in the day of my distress; turn your ear to me; in the day I call, answer me quickly.

**4** For my days vanish like smoke, and my bones burn like a hearth.

**5** My heart is struck down like grass and has withered; I even forget to eat my bread.

**6** Because of my loud groaning, my bones cling to my flesh.[^2]

**7** I am like a pelican in the wilderness;[^3] I have become like a little owl among the ruins.

**8** I lie awake; I have become like a lonely bird on a roof.

**9** All day long my enemies taunt me; those who mock me use my name as a curse.[^4]

**10** For I eat ashes like bread, and mix my drink with tears,

**11** because of your indignation and your anger, for you have lifted me up and thrown me away.

**12** My days are like a lengthening shadow;[^5] I wither away like grass.

**13** But you, Yahweh, sit enthroned forever, and your name endures[^6] through all generations.

**14** You will rise up and have compassion on Zion, for it is time to be gracious to her; the appointed time has come.

**15** For your servants hold her stones dear, and have pity on her dust.

**16** The nations will fear the name of Yahweh, and all the kings of the earth your glory.

**17** For Yahweh will build up Zion; he will appear in his glory.[^7]

**18** He will turn to the prayer of the destitute,[^8] and will not despise their prayer.

**19** Let this be written down for a generation to come, so that a people yet to be created may praise Yah:[^9]

**20** that he looked down from his holy height; from heaven Yahweh gazed on the earth,

**21** to hear the groaning of the prisoner, to set free those condemned to die,[^10]

**22** so that the name of Yahweh may be declared in Zion, and his praise in Jerusalem,

**23** when the peoples gather together, and the kingdoms, to serve Yahweh.

**24** He has broken my strength[^11] on the way; he has cut short my days.

**25** I say, "My God, do not take me away in the middle of my days, you whose years go on through all generations.

**26** Long ago you laid the foundation of the earth, and the heavens are the work of your hands.[^12]

**27** They will perish, but you will remain; they will all wear out like a garment. You will change them like clothing, and they will pass away.

**28** But you are the same,[^13] and your years will have no end.

**29** The children of your servants will live secure, and their offspring will be established before you."

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. This is the only psalm title that describes the one who prays, rather than naming a person. *Complaint*: or "lament". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–29 are 102:1–28 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: That is, I am nothing but skin and bones.
[^3]: Hebrew *qa'at*, an unclean bird (Leviticus 11:18), perhaps a pelican or a kind of owl. *Little owl*: Hebrew *kos* (Leviticus 11:17).
[^4]: Literally "swear by me", using my fate in their curses (compare Jeremiah 29:22).
[^5]: Literally "a shadow stretched out", as shadows grow long when the day is ending.
[^6]: Literally "your remembrance", the name by which you are remembered and called on.
[^7]: The verbs in verses 17–18 are in the past tense in Hebrew, speaking of what God will do as though it were already done.
[^8]: Hebrew *ar'ar*, "stripped, bare", perhaps also a shrub of the desert (compare Jeremiah 17:6).
[^9]: Hebrew *Yah*, a short form of the name Yahweh.
[^10]: Literally "the sons of death". Compare 79:11.
[^11]: The Hebrew text is written "his strength" but read "my strength". The Septuagint reads the same consonants differently: "He answered him in the way of his strength: Tell me the shortness of my days."
[^12]: Hebrews 1:10–12 quotes verses 26–28 from the Septuagint, which begins "You, Lord, in the beginning laid the foundation of the earth", and applies them to the Son.
[^13]: Literally "you are he" (compare Isaiah 41:4; 43:10).
