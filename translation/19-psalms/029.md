**1** A psalm of David.[^1] Ascribe to Yahweh,[^2] you heavenly beings,[^3] ascribe to Yahweh glory and strength.

**2** Ascribe to Yahweh the glory due his name;[^4] worship Yahweh in the splendor of holiness.[^5]

**3** The voice of Yahweh is over the waters; the God of glory thunders, Yahweh, over the mighty waters.

**4** The voice of Yahweh is powerful; the voice of Yahweh is majestic.

**5** The voice of Yahweh breaks the cedars; Yahweh breaks the cedars of Lebanon.

**6** He makes Lebanon skip like a calf, and Sirion[^6] like a young wild ox.

**7** The voice of Yahweh strikes with flames of fire.[^7]

**8** The voice of Yahweh shakes the wilderness; Yahweh shakes the wilderness of Kadesh.

**9** The voice of Yahweh makes the deer give birth,[^8] and strips the forests bare, and in his temple everyone says, "Glory!"

**10** Yahweh sat enthroned over the flood;[^9] Yahweh sits enthroned as King forever.

**11** May Yahweh give strength to his people; may Yahweh bless his people with peace.[^10]

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "sons of gods" (Hebrew *bene elim*): heavenly beings who serve in God's court (compare Job 1:6; Psalm 89:7, 89:6 in English).
[^4]: Literally "the glory of his name". Verses 1–2 are close to 1 Chronicles 16:28–29 and Psalm 96:7–9.
[^5]: Or "in holy attire".
[^6]: Another name for Mount Hermon (Deuteronomy 3:9).
[^7]: Literally "hews out flames of fire", perhaps of lightning.
[^8]: Or, with different vowels, "makes the oaks whirl".
[^9]: Hebrew *mabbul*, the word used for the great flood in Genesis 6–11.
[^10]: Or "Yahweh gives strength to his people; Yahweh blesses his people with peace."
