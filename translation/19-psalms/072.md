**1** Of Solomon.[^1] God, give the king your justice,[^2] and the king's son your righteousness.

**2** May he judge your people with righteousness, and your afflicted ones with justice.

**3** May the mountains bring well-being[^3] to the people, and the hills, righteousness.

**4** May he defend the cause of the afflicted among the people, save the children of the needy, and crush the oppressor.

**5** May they fear you[^4] as long as the sun endures, and as long as the moon, through all generations.

**6** May he be like rain falling on mown grass, like showers that water the earth.

**7** In his days may the righteous flourish,[^5] and peace abound, until the moon is no more.

**8** May he rule from sea to sea, and from the River[^6] to the ends of the earth.

**9** May the desert tribes[^7] bow down before him, and his enemies lick the dust.

**10** May the kings of Tarshish and of the coastlands bring tribute; may the kings of Sheba and Seba[^8] offer gifts.

**11** May all kings bow down to him, and all nations serve him.

**12** For he rescues the needy who cry for help, and the afflicted who have no one to help them.

**13** He has pity on the poor and the needy, and saves the lives[^9] of the needy.

**14** From oppression and violence he redeems their life, and their blood is precious in his sight.

**15** May he live long! May gold of Sheba be given to him; may prayer be made for him continually, and may people bless him all day long.

**16** May there be abundant grain[^10] in the land, waving on the tops of the mountains; may its fruit be like Lebanon, and may people flourish in the cities[^11] like the grass of the field.

**17** May his name endure forever; may his name increase[^12] as long as the sun shines. May all nations be blessed in him; may they call him happy.[^13]

**18** Blessed be the LORD[^14] God, the God of Israel, who alone does wonderful things.

**19** Blessed be his glorious name forever; may the whole earth be filled with his glory. Amen and Amen.[^15]

**20** The prayers of David son of Jesse are ended.[^16]

[^1]: Or "for Solomon", "about Solomon". Psalm 127 is the only other psalm with this title. In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles. The psalm is a prayer for the king. Later Jewish and Christian readers also heard in it a picture of the reign of the promised Messiah.
[^2]: Literally "your judgments".
[^3]: Hebrew *shalom*: peace, well-being, prosperity. Also in verse 7.
[^4]: The Septuagint reads "May he endure".
[^5]: The Septuagint reads "may righteousness flourish".
[^6]: That is, the Euphrates.
[^7]: Hebrew *tsiyyim*, "desert dwellers" or "desert creatures".
[^8]: Sheba was in southern Arabia; Seba was probably in Africa (compare Genesis 10:7; Isaiah 43:3).
[^9]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Also in verse 14.
[^10]: Hebrew *pissat bar*, found only here; perhaps "an abundance of grain".
[^11]: Literally "may they flourish from the city".
[^12]: Hebrew *yinnon*, found only here, perhaps "sprout", "increase".
[^13]: Compare God's promise to Abraham in Genesis 12:3; 22:18.
[^14]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^15]: Verses 18–19 are a doxology, a closing word of praise, which ends Book 2 of the Psalms (Psalms 42–72). Each of the five books ends with one (41:14; 41:13 in most English Bibles; 72:18–19; 89:53; 89:52 in most English Bibles; 106:48), and Psalm 150 closes the whole collection.
[^16]: This line probably closed an earlier collection of David's prayers. Several later psalms are also titled "of David" (for example Psalms 86, 101, 103, 108–110 and 138–145).
