**1** A psalm of David.[^1] LORD,[^2] hear my prayer; listen to my pleas for mercy. In your faithfulness answer me, in your righteousness.

**2** Do not bring your servant into judgment, for no one living is righteous before you.[^3]

**3** For the enemy has pursued my soul;[^4] he has crushed my life to the ground; he has made me sit in darkness like those long dead.[^5]

**4** So my spirit grows faint within me; my heart within me is appalled.

**5** I remember the days of old; I meditate on all your deeds; I reflect on the work of your hands.

**6** I spread out my hands to you; my soul thirsts for you like a parched land.[^6] Selah.[^7]

**7** Answer me quickly, LORD; my spirit fails. Do not hide your face from me, or I will be like those who go down to the pit.

**8** Let me hear of your faithful love[^8] in the morning, for in you I trust. Make me know the way I should go, for to you I lift up my soul.

**9** Rescue me from my enemies, LORD; I hide myself in you.[^9]

**10** Teach me to do your will, for you are my God. May your good Spirit[^10] lead me on level ground.

**11** For the sake of your name, LORD, give me life; in your righteousness bring me[^11] out of trouble.

**12** In your faithful love, destroy my enemies, and wipe out all my adversaries,[^12] for I am your servant.

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Or "for no one living will be found in the right before you". Paul echoes this line in Romans 3:20 and Galatians 2:16.
[^4]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Also in verses 6 and 8.
[^5]: Literally "like the dead of long ago". Compare Lamentations 3:6.
[^6]: Literally "my soul (*nephesh*) (is) like a weary land for you".
[^7]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning.
[^8]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Also in verse 12.
[^9]: Literally "to you I have covered", perhaps "I have hidden myself with you". The Septuagint reads "to you I have fled for refuge".
[^10]: Or "your good spirit". Hebrew *ruach*. Compare Nehemiah 9:20.
[^11]: Literally "bring my soul (*nephesh*)".
[^12]: Literally "all the adversaries of my soul (*nephesh*)".
