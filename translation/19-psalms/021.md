**1** For the director. A psalm of David.[^1]

**2** Yahweh,[^2] the king rejoices in your strength; how greatly he exults in your salvation!

**3** You have given him his heart's desire, and have not withheld the request of his lips. Selah.[^3]

**4** For you meet him with rich blessings;[^4] you set a crown of fine gold on his head.

**5** He asked you for life, and you gave it to him, length of days forever and ever.

**6** His glory is great through your salvation; you lay splendor and majesty on him.

**7** For you make him most blessed forever;[^5] you make him glad with the joy of your presence.

**8** For the king trusts in Yahweh, and through the faithful love[^6] of the Most High[^7] he will not be shaken.

**9** Your hand will find all your enemies; your right hand will find those who hate you.[^8]

**10** You will make them like a blazing furnace when you appear.[^9] Yahweh will swallow them up in his anger, and fire will consume them.

**11** You will destroy their offspring[^10] from the earth, their descendants from among human beings.

**12** Though they plan evil against you and devise schemes, they will not succeed.

**13** For you will make them turn their backs[^11] when you aim your bows at their faces.

**14** Be exalted, Yahweh, in your strength! We will sing and praise your might.

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–14 are 21:1–13 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning.
[^4]: Literally "blessings of good".
[^5]: Literally "you set him as blessings forever". Or "you make him a blessing forever".
[^6]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^7]: Hebrew *Elyon*, a title of God.
[^8]: In verses 9–13 "you" is probably the king, though it may be Yahweh.
[^9]: Literally "at the time of your face".
[^10]: Literally "their fruit".
[^11]: Literally "you will make them a shoulder".
