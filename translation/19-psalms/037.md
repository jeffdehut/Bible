**1** Of David.[^1] Do not fret because of evildoers; do not envy those who do wrong.[^2]

**2** For like grass they will soon wither, and like green plants they will fade.

**3** Trust in the LORD[^3] and do good; live in the land and cultivate faithfulness.[^4]

**4** Delight yourself in the LORD, and he will give you the desires of your heart.

**5** Commit your way to the LORD;[^5] trust in him, and he will act.

**6** He will make your righteousness shine like the light, and your justice like the noonday sun.

**7** Be still before the LORD and wait patiently for him. Do not fret because of the one who prospers in his way, the one who carries out evil schemes.

**8** Stop being angry and abandon your rage; do not fret: it leads only to evil.

**9** For evildoers will be cut off, but those who wait for the LORD, they will inherit the land.

**10** Yet a little while and the wicked will be no more; you will look closely at his place, and he will not be there.

**11** But the humble will inherit the land[^6] and delight in abundant peace.

**12** The wicked plots against the righteous and gnashes his teeth at him.

**13** The Lord laughs at him, for he sees that his day is coming.

**14** The wicked draw the sword and bend their bow to bring down the afflicted and the needy, to slaughter those whose way is upright.

**15** Their sword will enter their own heart, and their bows will be broken.

**16** Better is the little that the righteous has than the wealth of many wicked.

**17** For the arms of the wicked will be broken, but the LORD upholds the righteous.

**18** The LORD knows the days of the blameless, and their inheritance will last forever.

**19** They will not be put to shame in evil times; in days of famine they will have plenty.

**20** But the wicked will perish; the enemies of the LORD are like the glory of the pastures:[^7] they vanish; like smoke they vanish.

**21** The wicked borrows and does not repay, but the righteous is generous and gives.

**22** For those whom he blesses will inherit the land, but those whom he curses will be cut off.

**23** A man's steps are made firm by the LORD, and he delights in his way.[^8]

**24** Though he falls, he will not be thrown down, for the LORD holds his hand.

**25** I have been young and now I am old, yet I have not seen the righteous abandoned, or his children begging for bread.

**26** He is always generous and lends, and his children become a blessing.

**27** Turn away from evil and do good, and you will live in the land forever.

**28** For the LORD loves justice and will not abandon his faithful ones.[^9] They will be kept safe forever, but the children of the wicked will be cut off.

**29** The righteous will inherit the land and live in it forever.

**30** The mouth of the righteous utters wisdom, and his tongue speaks justice.

**31** The law[^10] of his God is in his heart; his steps do not slip.

**32** The wicked watches for the righteous and seeks to kill him.

**33** The LORD will not abandon him to his power, or let him be condemned when he is brought to trial.

**34** Wait for the LORD and keep his way, and he will exalt you to inherit the land; you will see it when the wicked are cut off.

**35** I have seen a wicked and ruthless man, spreading himself like a luxuriant tree in its native soil.[^11]

**36** But he passed away,[^12] and look, he was gone; I searched for him, but he could not be found.

**37** Watch the blameless and observe the upright, for there is a future[^13] for the person of peace.

**38** But rebels will be destroyed altogether; the future of the wicked will be cut off.

**39** The salvation of the righteous comes from the LORD; he is their stronghold in time of trouble.

**40** The LORD helps them and rescues them; he rescues them from the wicked and saves them, because they take refuge in him.

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: Psalm 37 is an acrostic: in the Hebrew, each letter of the alphabet in turn begins a section, usually of two verses (*aleph* at verse 1, *bet* at verse 3, and so on, to *tav* at verse 39). The *ayin* section begins in the middle of verse 28, with *le'olam*, "forever".
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Or "and graze in safety", "and feed on faithfulness".
[^5]: Literally "Roll your way on the LORD".
[^6]: Or "the earth". Jesus echoes this line in Matthew 5:5.
[^7]: Or "like the fat of lambs", which is burned up in sacrifice.
[^8]: It is not clear whether the LORD delights in the man's way, or the man in the LORD's.
[^9]: Hebrew *chasidim*, "faithful, loyal ones", related to *chesed*, "faithful love".
[^10]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, teaching".
[^11]: Literally "like a green native". The Septuagint reads "like the cedars of Lebanon".
[^12]: The Septuagint reads "I passed by".
[^13]: Or "descendants". Also in verse 38.
