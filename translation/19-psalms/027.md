**1** Of David.[^1] The LORD[^2] is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? The LORD is the stronghold of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?

**2** When evildoers come near me to devour my flesh, my adversaries and my enemies, it is they who stumble and fall.

**3** Though an army camps against me, my heart will not fear; though war rises against me, even then I will be confident.

**4** One thing I have asked of the LORD; this I will seek: to live in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to gaze on the beauty of the LORD, and to seek him[^3] in his temple.

**5** For he will hide me in his shelter in the day of trouble; he will conceal me under the cover of his tent; he will set me high on a rock.

**6** And now my head will be lifted up above my enemies around me, and I will offer sacrifices in his tent with shouts of joy; I will sing and make music to the LORD.

**7** Hear, LORD, when I call aloud; be gracious to me and answer me.

**8** My heart says to you, "Seek my face!"[^4] Your face, LORD, I will seek.

**9** Do not hide your face from me; do not turn your servant away in anger. You have been my help; do not reject me or abandon me, God of my salvation.

**10** Though my father and my mother abandon me, the LORD will take me in.

**11** Teach me your way, LORD, and lead me on a level path, because of those who lie in wait for me.

**12** Do not hand me over to the will[^5] of my adversaries, for false witnesses have risen against me, breathing out violence.

**13** If I had not believed that I would see the goodness of the LORD in the land of the living![^6]

**14** Wait for the LORD; be strong, and let your heart take courage. Wait for the LORD!

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Or "to inquire", "to reflect". Hebrew *levaqqer*.
[^4]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "To you my heart says, 'Seek (you plural) my face'": the heart repeats the LORD's own invitation.
[^5]: Literally "the soul (*nephesh*)", that is, the desire.
[^6]: The sentence is left unfinished, as if to say "I would have been lost". In the Hebrew text, dots are written above and below the word for "if not" (*lule*), a scribal mark of doubt. Without it the verse reads "I believe that I will see the goodness of the LORD in the land of the living."
