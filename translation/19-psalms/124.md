**1** A song of ascents. Of David.[^1] If it had not been Yahweh[^2] who was on our side, let Israel now say,

**2** if it had not been Yahweh who was on our side when people[^3] rose up against us,

**3** then they would have swallowed us alive, when their anger blazed against us;

**4** then the waters would have swept us away; the torrent would have gone over us;[^4]

**5** then the raging[^5] waters would have gone over us.

**6** Blessed be Yahweh, who has not given us as prey to their teeth.

**7** We have escaped[^6] like a bird from the fowler's trap; the trap is broken, and we have escaped.

**8** Our help is in the name of Yahweh, the maker of heaven and earth.

[^1]: *A song of ascents*: Hebrew *shir ha-ma'alot* (see the note on 120:1). *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *adam*, "human beings".
[^4]: Literally "over our soul (*nephesh*)". Also in verse 5.
[^5]: Or "arrogant". Hebrew *zeidonim*, related to *zedim*, "the arrogant".
[^6]: Literally "Our soul (*nephesh*) has escaped".
