**1** Praise the LORD![^1] Praise the LORD[^2] from the heavens; praise him in the heights!

**2** Praise him, all his angels; praise him, all his armies![^3]

**3** Praise him, sun and moon; praise him, all you shining stars!

**4** Praise him, you highest heavens,[^4] and you waters above the heavens!

**5** Let them praise the name of the LORD, for he commanded, and they were created.

**6** He established them forever and ever; he gave a decree that will not pass away.[^5]

**7** Praise the LORD from the earth, you sea monsters[^6] and all the depths,

**8** fire and hail, snow and mist,[^7] stormy wind that carries out his word,

**9** mountains and all hills, fruit trees and all cedars,

**10** wild animals and all livestock, creatures that move along the ground and winged birds,

**11** kings of the earth and all peoples, princes and all who rule the earth,[^8]

**12** young men and young women alike, old and young together.

**13** Let them praise the name of the LORD, for his name alone is exalted; his splendor is above the earth and the heavens.

**14** He has raised up a horn[^9] for his people, praise for all his faithful ones,[^10] for the Israelites, the people close to him. Praise the LORD!

[^1]: Hebrew *hallelu-yah*, "praise Yah", the source of the word "Hallelujah". *Yah* is a short form of the name YHWH. Also at the end of verse 14.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: The heavenly hosts (compare "the LORD of Armies", 24:10). The Hebrew text is written "his army" but read "his armies".
[^4]: Literally "heavens of the heavens". *The waters above the heavens*: compare Genesis 1:7.
[^5]: Or "a decree that they will not transgress". Compare Jeremiah 31:35–36; 33:25.
[^6]: Hebrew *tanninim*, the great creatures of the sea (Genesis 1:21).
[^7]: Or "smoke". Hebrew *qitor*.
[^8]: Literally "all judges of the earth".
[^9]: A symbol of strength. Perhaps a king for his people, or their strength and honor restored (compare 89:18, 89:17 in most English Bibles; 132:17).
[^10]: Hebrew *chasidim*, "the faithful, the loyal", related to *chesed*, "faithful love". *Praise for all his faithful ones*: either a reason for them to praise, or their glory.
