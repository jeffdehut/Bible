**1** Happy[^1] are those whose way is blameless,[^2] who walk in the law[^3] of Yahweh.[^4]

**2** Happy are those who keep his statutes,[^5] who seek him with all their heart.

**3** They do no wrong; they walk in his ways.

**4** You have commanded your precepts, to be kept with all care.

**5** Oh, that my ways were steady in keeping your decrees!

**6** Then I would not be put to shame, when I look to all your commands.

**7** I will give thanks to you with an upright heart, as I learn your righteous rulings.[^6]

**8** I will keep your decrees; do not utterly forsake me.

**9** How can a young man keep his path pure?[^7] By guarding it according to your word.

**10** With all my heart I seek you; do not let me stray from your commands.

**11** I have treasured your promise[^8] in my heart, so that I may not sin against you.

**12** Blessed are you, Yahweh; teach me your decrees.

**13** With my lips I recount all the rulings of your mouth.

**14** I rejoice in the way of your statutes as much as in all riches.

**15** I will meditate on your precepts and fix my eyes on your paths.

**16** I will delight in your decrees; I will not forget your word.

**17** Deal generously with your servant,[^9] so that I may live and keep your word.

**18** Open my eyes, so that I may see wonderful things in your law.

**19** I am a stranger[^10] on the earth; do not hide your commands from me.

**20** My soul[^11] is crushed with longing for your rulings at all times.

**21** You rebuke the arrogant, the accursed, who stray from your commands.[^12]

**22** Take scorn and contempt away from me,[^13] for I keep your statutes.

**23** Even though rulers sit and talk together against me, your servant meditates on your decrees.

**24** Your statutes are my delight; they are my counselors.[^14]

**25** My soul clings to the dust;[^15] give me life[^16] according to your word.

**26** I told you of my ways, and you answered me; teach me your decrees.

**27** Make me understand the way of your precepts, and I will meditate on your wonders.

**28** My soul weeps[^17] from grief; raise me up according to your word.

**29** Keep me from the way of falsehood, and graciously give me your law.

**30** I have chosen the way of faithfulness; I have set your rulings before me.[^18]

**31** I cling to your statutes; Yahweh, do not let me be put to shame.

**32** I will run in the way of your commands, for you set my heart free.[^19]

**33** Teach me, Yahweh, the way of your decrees,[^20] and I will keep it to the end.[^21]

**34** Give me understanding, and I will keep your law and obey it with all my heart.

**35** Lead me in the path of your commands, for I delight in it.

**36** Turn my heart to your statutes, and not to selfish gain.

**37** Turn my eyes away from looking at what is worthless; give me life in your way.

**38** Confirm your promise to your servant, the promise that is for those who fear you.[^22]

**39** Turn away the disgrace that I dread, for your rulings are good.

**40** See, I long for your precepts; in your righteousness give me life.

**41** May your faithful love[^23] come to me, Yahweh,[^24] your salvation according to your promise.

**42** Then I will have an answer for the one who taunts me,[^25] for I trust in your word.

**43** Do not take the word of truth utterly out of my mouth, for I hope in your rulings.

**44** I will keep your law continually, forever and ever.

**45** I will walk about in freedom,[^26] for I have sought your precepts.

**46** I will speak of your statutes before kings, and I will not be put to shame.

**47** I will delight in your commands, which I love.

**48** I will lift up my hands to your commands, which I love,[^27] and I will meditate on your decrees.

**49** Remember your word to your servant,[^28] by which you have given me hope.

**50** This is my comfort in my affliction: that your promise gives me life.

**51** The arrogant mock me without restraint, but I do not turn away from your law.

**52** I remember your rulings of old, Yahweh, and I take comfort.

**53** Burning anger seizes me because of the wicked, who forsake your law.

**54** Your decrees have been my songs in the house where I live as a stranger.[^29]

**55** In the night I remember your name, Yahweh, and I keep your law.

**56** This has been my lot: I have kept your precepts.[^30]

**57** Yahweh is my portion;[^31] I have promised to keep your words.[^32]

**58** I have sought your favor[^33] with all my heart; be gracious to me according to your promise.

**59** I have thought about my ways, and turned my feet to your statutes.

**60** I hurry and do not delay to keep your commands.

**61** The cords of the wicked are wrapped around me,[^34] but I do not forget your law.

**62** At midnight I rise to give thanks to you for your righteous rulings.

**63** I am a companion of all who fear you, of those who keep your precepts.

**64** The earth is full of your faithful love, Yahweh; teach me your decrees.

**65** You have dealt well with your servant,[^35] Yahweh, according to your word.

**66** Teach me good judgment[^36] and knowledge, for I believe in your commands.

**67** Before I was afflicted I went astray, but now I keep your promise.

**68** You are good, and you do good; teach me your decrees.

**69** The arrogant smear me with lies, but I keep your precepts with all my heart.

**70** Their hearts are unfeeling, like fat,[^37] but I delight in your law.

**71** It was good for me that I was afflicted, so that I might learn your decrees.

**72** The law from your mouth is better to me than thousands of pieces of gold and silver.

**73** Your hands made me and formed me;[^38] give me understanding, so that I may learn your commands.

**74** Those who fear you will see me and rejoice, for I hope in your word.

**75** I know, Yahweh, that your rulings are righteous, and that in faithfulness you have afflicted me.

**76** May your faithful love comfort me, according to your promise to your servant.

**77** Let your compassion come to me, so that I may live, for your law is my delight.

**78** Let the arrogant be put to shame, for they have wronged me with lies; but I will meditate on your precepts.

**79** Let those who fear you turn to me, those who know your statutes.[^39]

**80** May my heart be blameless in your decrees, so that I may not be put to shame.

**81** My soul faints with longing for your salvation;[^40] I hope in your word.

**82** My eyes fail, looking for your promise; I say, "When will you comfort me?"

**83** For I have become like a wineskin in the smoke,[^41] yet I do not forget your decrees.

**84** How many are the days of your servant? When will you bring judgment[^42] on those who persecute me?

**85** The arrogant have dug pits for me, they who do not live by your law.[^43]

**86** All your commands are faithful;[^44] they persecute me with lies; help me!

**87** They have almost wiped me from the earth, but I have not forsaken your precepts.

**88** In your faithful love give me life, and I will keep the statutes[^45] of your mouth.

**89** Forever, Yahweh, your word stands firm in the heavens.[^46]

**90** Your faithfulness endures from generation to generation; you established the earth, and it stands.

**91** By your rulings they stand to this day,[^47] for all things are your servants.

**92** If your law had not been my delight, I would have perished in my affliction.

**93** I will never forget your precepts, for by them you have given me life.

**94** I am yours; save me, for I have sought your precepts.

**95** The wicked lie in wait to destroy me, but I consider your statutes.

**96** I have seen a limit to all perfection, but your command is exceedingly broad.[^48]

**97** Oh, how I love your law![^49] It is my meditation all day long.

**98** Your commands make me wiser than my enemies, for they are always with me.

**99** I have more insight than all my teachers, for your statutes are my meditation.

**100** I understand more than the elders, for I keep your precepts.

**101** I hold back my feet from every evil path, so that I may keep your word.

**102** I do not turn aside from your rulings, for you yourself have taught me.

**103** How sweet your promise is to my taste, sweeter than honey to my mouth!

**104** From your precepts I gain understanding; so I hate every false path.

**105** Your word is a lamp for my feet[^50] and a light for my path.

**106** I have sworn an oath and confirmed it, to keep your righteous rulings.

**107** I am severely afflicted; Yahweh, give me life according to your word.

**108** Accept, Yahweh, the freewill offerings of my mouth,[^51] and teach me your rulings.

**109** My life is always in my hand,[^52] yet I do not forget your law.

**110** The wicked have set a trap for me, but I have not strayed from your precepts.

**111** Your statutes are my inheritance forever;[^53] they are the joy of my heart.

**112** I have set my heart on doing your decrees, forever, to the end.

**113** I hate the double-minded,[^54] but I love your law.

**114** You are my hiding place and my shield; I hope in your word.

**115** Go away from me, you evildoers, so that I may keep the commands of my God.

**116** Sustain me according to your promise, so that I may live; do not let me be put to shame in my hope.

**117** Uphold me, so that I may be safe, and I will always have regard for your decrees.

**118** You reject all who stray from your decrees, for their deceit is in vain.[^55]

**119** You remove all the wicked of the earth like dross;[^56] so I love your statutes.

**120** My flesh shudders[^57] in fear of you, and I am afraid of your rulings.

**121** I have done what is just and right;[^58] do not abandon me to my oppressors.

**122** Guarantee your servant's well-being;[^59] do not let the arrogant oppress me.

**123** My eyes fail, looking for your salvation and for your righteous promise.[^60]

**124** Deal with your servant according to your faithful love, and teach me your decrees.

**125** I am your servant; give me understanding, so that I may know your statutes.

**126** It is time for Yahweh to act;[^61] they have broken your law.

**127** Therefore I love your commands more than gold, more than fine gold.

**128** Therefore I consider all your precepts right in everything;[^62] I hate every false path.

**129** Your statutes are wonderful;[^63] so my soul keeps them.

**130** The unfolding[^64] of your words gives light; it gives understanding to the simple.

**131** I open my mouth and pant, for I long for your commands.

**132** Turn to me and be gracious to me, as is your way[^65] with those who love your name.

**133** Make my steps steady by your promise, and let no wickedness rule over me.

**134** Redeem me from human oppression,[^66] so that I may keep your precepts.

**135** Make your face shine on your servant,[^67] and teach me your decrees.

**136** Streams of water flow down from my eyes, because they do not keep your law.

**137** You are righteous, Yahweh,[^68] and your rulings are upright.

**138** You have commanded your statutes in righteousness and in great faithfulness.[^69]

**139** My zeal consumes me, because my adversaries forget your words.

**140** Your promise is thoroughly refined,[^70] and your servant loves it.

**141** I am small and despised, yet I do not forget your precepts.

**142** Your righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and your law is truth.

**143** Trouble and distress have found me, but your commands are my delight.

**144** Your statutes are righteous forever; give me understanding, so that I may live.

**145** I call with all my heart;[^71] answer me, Yahweh, and I will keep your decrees.

**146** I call to you; save me, and I will keep your statutes.

**147** I rise before dawn and cry for help; I hope in your word.[^72]

**148** My eyes are awake before the watches of the night,[^73] so that I may meditate on your promise.

**149** Hear my voice according to your faithful love; Yahweh, give me life according to your justice.[^74]

**150** Those who pursue wicked schemes draw near; they are far from your law.

**151** You are near, Yahweh, and all your commands are truth.

**152** Long ago I learned from your statutes that you have established them forever.

**153** Look on my affliction and rescue me,[^75] for I do not forget your law.

**154** Plead my case and redeem me; give me life according to your promise.

**155** Salvation is far from the wicked, for they do not seek your decrees.

**156** Your compassion is great, Yahweh; give me life according to your rulings.

**157** Many are my persecutors and my adversaries, but I do not turn away from your statutes.

**158** I look on the treacherous with disgust, because they do not keep your promise.

**159** See how I love your precepts! Yahweh, give me life according to your faithful love.

**160** The sum of your word[^76] is truth, and every one of your righteous rulings endures forever.

**161** Rulers persecute me without cause,[^77] but my heart stands in awe of your word.[^78]

**162** I rejoice over your promise like one who finds great plunder.

**163** I hate and detest falsehood, but I love your law.

**164** Seven times a day I praise you for your righteous rulings.

**165** Great peace have those who love your law; nothing makes them stumble.[^79]

**166** I wait for your salvation, Yahweh, and I do your commands.

**167** My soul keeps your statutes; I love them greatly.

**168** I keep your precepts and your statutes, for all my ways are before you.

**169** Let my cry come near to you, Yahweh;[^80] give me understanding according to your word.

**170** Let my plea come before you; rescue me according to your promise.

**171** Let my lips pour out praise, for you teach me your decrees.

**172** Let my tongue sing of your promise, for all your commands are righteous.

**173** Let your hand be ready to help me, for I have chosen your precepts.

**174** I long for your salvation, Yahweh, and your law is my delight.

**175** Let my soul live, and it will praise you; and let your rulings help me.

**176** I have strayed like a lost sheep; seek your servant, for I do not forget your commands.[^81]

[^1]: Hebrew *ashrei*: happy, fortunate; traditionally "blessed". Also in verse 2.
[^2]: *Aleph*: each verse of this stanza (verses 1–8) begins in Hebrew with this letter, the first of the alphabet. Psalm 119, the longest psalm, is an acrostic: it has twenty-two stanzas of eight verses, one stanza for each letter of the Hebrew alphabet in order, and every verse of a stanza begins with its letter. Almost every verse names God's teaching with one of eight words, and this translation keeps one English word for each: *law* (*torah*), *statutes* (*edot*), *precepts* (*piqqudim*), *commands* (*mitsvot*), *rulings* (*mishpatim*), *decrees* (*chuqqim*), *promise* (*imrah*) and *word* (*davar*). Compare Psalm 19:8–10 (19:7–9 in English).
[^3]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, teaching": here God's teaching as a whole.

[^5]: Hebrew *edot*, traditionally "testimonies": the solemn terms of the covenant.
[^6]: Literally "the rulings of your righteousness". Also in verses 62, 106, 160 and 164.
[^7]: *Bet*: each verse of this stanza (verses 9–16) begins in Hebrew with this letter.
[^8]: Hebrew *imrah*, "saying, word": what God has said, often his promise. This translation keeps "promise" for *imrah* and "word" for *davar*.
