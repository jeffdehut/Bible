**1** A maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite.[^1]

**2** I will sing of the faithful love[^2] of the LORD[^3] forever; with my mouth I will make your faithfulness[^4] known to all generations.

**3** For I said, "Faithful love will be built up forever; in the heavens you will establish your faithfulness."[^5]

**4** "I have made a covenant with my chosen one;[^6] I have sworn to David my servant:

**5** 'I will establish your offspring forever, and build your throne for all generations.'" Selah.[^7]

**6** The heavens praise your wonders, LORD, and your faithfulness in the assembly of the holy ones.[^8]

**7** For who in the skies can compare with the LORD? Who among the sons of the gods[^9] is like the LORD,

**8** a God greatly feared in the council of the holy ones, and awesome above all who are around him?

**9** LORD God of Armies,[^10] who is like you? You are mighty, LORD,[^11] and your faithfulness surrounds you.

**10** You rule the raging of the sea; when its waves rise, you calm them.

**11** You crushed Rahab[^12] like one who is slain; with your strong arm you scattered your enemies.

**12** The heavens are yours, and the earth is yours as well; the world and all that fills it, you founded them.

**13** The north and the south,[^13] you created them; Tabor and Hermon[^14] sing for joy at your name.

**14** Yours is an arm with might; your hand is strong, your right hand is raised high.

**15** Righteousness and justice are the foundation of your throne; faithful love and faithfulness[^15] go before you.

**16** Happy[^16] are the people who know the festal shout,[^17] who walk, LORD, in the light of your face.

**17** In your name they rejoice all day long, and in your righteousness they are lifted up.

**18** For you are the glory of their strength, and by your favor our horn[^18] is lifted high.[^19]

**19** For our shield belongs to the LORD, and our king to the Holy One of Israel.[^20]

**20** Once you spoke in a vision to your faithful ones,[^21] and said, "I have given help to a mighty man; I have raised up one chosen from the people.[^22]

**21** I have found David my servant; with my holy oil I have anointed him.[^23]

**22** My hand will be with him to support him; my arm also will strengthen him.

**23** No enemy will get the better of him;[^24] no wicked man[^25] will humble him.

**24** I will crush his adversaries before him, and strike down those who hate him.

**25** My faithfulness and my faithful love will be with him, and through my name his horn will be lifted high.

**26** I will set his hand on the sea, and his right hand on the rivers.[^26]

**27** He will call to me, 'You are my Father, my God, and the rock of my salvation.'

**28** I will also make him my firstborn,[^27] the highest[^28] of the kings of the earth.

**29** I will keep my faithful love for him forever, and my covenant with him will stand firm.

**30** I will establish his offspring forever, and his throne as long as the heavens endure.[^29]

**31** If his sons forsake my law, and do not walk in my rulings,

**32** if they violate my decrees, and do not keep my commands,

**33** then I will punish their transgression with the rod, and their iniquity with blows.[^30]

**34** But I will not take my faithful love away from him, or be false to my faithfulness.

**35** I will not violate my covenant, or change what my lips have spoken.

**36** Once for all I have sworn by my holiness; I will not lie to David.

**37** His offspring will endure forever, and his throne will be like the sun before me.

**38** Like the moon it will be established forever, a faithful witness in the skies."[^31] Selah.

**39** But you have rejected and spurned him; you are furious with your anointed.[^32]

**40** You have renounced the covenant with your servant; you have defiled his crown in the dust.[^33]

**41** You have broken down all his walls; you have made his strongholds a ruin.

**42** All who pass along the road plunder him; he has become a disgrace to his neighbors.

**43** You have raised the right hand of his adversaries; you have made all his enemies rejoice.

**44** You have also turned back the edge[^34] of his sword, and you have not made him stand in battle.

**45** You have put an end to his splendor,[^35] and hurled his throne to the ground.

**46** You have cut short the days of his youth; you have covered him with shame. Selah.

**47** How long, LORD? Will you hide yourself forever? Will your wrath burn like fire?

**48** Remember how short my life is![^36] For what futility you have created all humankind!

**49** Who can live and never see death? Who can rescue his life[^37] from the power of Sheol?[^38] Selah.

**50** Lord, where are your former acts of faithful love, which you swore to David in your faithfulness?

**51** Remember, Lord, how your servants are taunted, how I carry in my heart the insults of all the many peoples,[^39]

**52** with which your enemies have taunted, LORD, with which they have taunted the footsteps of your anointed.[^40]

**53** Blessed be the LORD forever. Amen and Amen.[^41]

[^1]: *Maskil*: a term of uncertain meaning, perhaps "a skillful psalm" or "a psalm of instruction". *Of Ethan*: or "for Ethan". A wise man named Ethan the Ezrahite is mentioned in 1 Kings 5:11 (4:31 in most English Bibles), and Ethan was also one of David's chief musicians (1 Chronicles 15:17, 19). *Ezrahite* probably means a descendant of Zerah (1 Chronicles 2:6). In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–53 are 89:1–52 in most English Bibles. The psalm celebrates God's covenant with David (verses 4–5 and 20–38; compare 2 Samuel 7:8–16) and then laments that God seems to have abandoned it, for the king of David's line has been defeated and shamed (verses 39–52).
[^2]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Here and in verse 50 it is plural, "acts of faithful love". Also in verses 3, 15, 25, 29 and 34.
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Hebrew *emunah*: firmness, reliability, faithfulness. Also in verses 3, 6, 9, 25, 34 and 50. *Chesed* and *emunah* each appear seven times in the psalm.
[^5]: The Septuagint reads "For you said".
[^6]: God speaks in verses 4–5, summing up the promise that is given in full in verses 20–38.
[^7]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning. Also in verses 38, 46 and 49.
[^8]: The heavenly beings who serve in God's court, as in verse 8.
[^9]: Literally "sons of gods" (Hebrew *bene elim*): heavenly beings who serve in God's court (compare 29:1; Job 1:6).
[^10]: Hebrew *YHWH Elohei tseva'ot*. Compare "the LORD of Armies", traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^11]: Hebrew *Yah*, a short form of the name YHWH. *Mighty*: Hebrew *chasin*, found only here.
[^12]: Hebrew *Rahav*, a sea monster, a picture of the chaos that God subdued (Job 26:12; Isaiah 51:9), and also a poetic name for Egypt (87:4).
[^13]: Literally "the right", since the south lies on the right hand of one who faces east.
[^14]: Two mountains in the north of Israel.
[^15]: Hebrew *chesed* and *emet*, the pair in God's description of himself in Exodus 34:6. *Emet* means firmness, reliability, truth.
[^16]: Hebrew *ashrei*: happy, fortunate; traditionally "blessed".
[^17]: Hebrew *teru'ah*, the shout or trumpet blast of worship and festival (compare Leviticus 23:24; Numbers 10:5).
[^18]: A symbol of strength, like the horns of a wild ox. Also in verse 25.
[^19]: The Hebrew text is written "you lift high our horn" but read "our horn is lifted high".
[^20]: That is, the king, "our shield", belongs to God. Or "For the LORD is our shield, the Holy One of Israel is our king".
[^21]: Hebrew *chasidim*, "faithful ones", related to *chesed*, "faithful love". The vision is probably the one given to Nathan the prophet (2 Samuel 7:4–17).
[^22]: Or "I have exalted a young man from the people".
[^23]: Hebrew *meshachtiv*, from the same root as *mashiach*, "anointed one". Acts 13:22 echoes the first half of this verse, together with 1 Samuel 13:14.
[^24]: Hebrew *yashi*, "deceive", or "exact payment from".
[^25]: Literally "a son of injustice". Compare 2 Samuel 7:10.
[^26]: Perhaps the Mediterranean Sea and the river Euphrates with its streams (compare 72:8; 80:12, 80:11 in most English Bibles). The king's rule mirrors God's rule over the sea (verse 10).
[^27]: Compare 2 Samuel 7:14 and Exodus 4:22, where Israel is God's firstborn son.
[^28]: Hebrew *elyon*, the word used as a title of God, "the Most High". Revelation 1:5 echoes this verse and verse 38, calling Jesus "the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead, and the ruler of the kings of the earth".
[^29]: Literally "as the days of the heavens".
[^30]: Compare 2 Samuel 7:14–15.
[^31]: Literally "and a witness in the skies is faithful": the moon, or God himself.
[^32]: Hebrew *meshichekha*, "your anointed one", from *mashiach*: the king of David's line, anointed with oil. Also in verse 52. Later Jewish and Christian readers also heard in this psalm the hope of the promised Messiah, the son of David.
[^33]: Literally "you have profaned his crown to the ground".
[^34]: Hebrew *tsur*, "rock", here probably "flint" or "edge".
[^35]: Hebrew *mittehar*, an unusual form, probably "from his brightness". Or "his purity".
[^36]: Literally "Remember: I, what is a lifetime!" The Hebrew is difficult.
[^37]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self.
[^38]: Literally "from the hand of Sheol". Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead.
[^39]: Literally "my carrying in my bosom all of many peoples". The Hebrew is difficult, and "the insults of" is supplied from the context.
[^40]: *Footsteps*: that is, every step he takes, wherever he goes. Later Jewish tradition used the phrase "the footsteps of the Messiah" for the troubled time before his coming.
[^41]: This blessing closes Book 3 of the Psalms (Psalms 73–89). Books 1, 2 and 4 end in a similar way (41:14, 41:13 in most English Bibles; 72:18–19; 106:48), and Psalm 150 closes the whole collection.
