**1** For the director. A psalm of David,[^1]

**2** when the prophet Nathan came to him, after he had gone in to Bathsheba.[^2]

**3** Be gracious to me, God, according to your faithful love;[^3] according to your great compassion, blot out my transgressions.

**4** Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin.

**5** For I know my transgressions, and my sin is always before me.

**6** Against you, you alone, have I sinned, and done what is evil in your sight, so that you are right when you speak, and blameless when you judge.[^4]

**7** Indeed, I was born in iniquity, and in sin my mother conceived me.

**8** Indeed, you desire truth in the inner being;[^5] in the hidden place you teach me wisdom.

**9** Purify me from sin with hyssop,[^6] and I will be clean; wash me, and I will be whiter than snow.

**10** Let me hear joy and gladness; let the bones you have crushed rejoice.

**11** Hide your face from my sins, and blot out all my iniquities.

**12** Create in me a clean heart, God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me.

**13** Do not cast me away from your presence, and do not take your holy spirit[^7] from me.

**14** Restore to me the joy of your salvation, and sustain me with a willing spirit.

**15** Then I will teach transgressors your ways, and sinners will return to you.

**16** Rescue me from bloodguilt,[^8] God, God of my salvation, and my tongue will sing aloud of your righteousness.

**17** Lord, open my lips, and my mouth will declare your praise.

**18** For you do not delight in sacrifice, or I would give it; you take no pleasure in burnt offering.

**19** The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit; a broken and crushed heart, God, you will not despise.

**20** Do good to Zion in your favor; build up the walls of Jerusalem.

**21** Then you will delight in right sacrifices,[^9] in burnt offerings and whole burnt offerings;[^10] then bulls will be offered on your altar.

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verses 1–2, so verses 3–21 are 51:1–19 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: *Gone in to*: had sexual relations with. The events are told in 2 Samuel 11–12.
[^3]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^4]: Romans 3:4 quotes the second half of this verse from the Septuagint: "so that you may be justified in your words, and prevail when you are judged".
[^5]: Hebrew *tuchot*, a rare word, perhaps "the inward parts" (compare Job 38:36).
[^6]: Literally "Un-sin me", the verb used of rites of purification. Hyssop, a small bushy plant, was used to sprinkle blood or water in such rites (Leviticus 14:4–7; Numbers 19:18).
[^7]: Literally "the spirit of your holiness". Compare Isaiah 63:10–11.
[^8]: Literally "from bloods", that is, from the guilt of shedding blood, or perhaps from a violent death.
[^9]: Literally "sacrifices of righteousness".
[^10]: Hebrew *kalil*, "whole": an offering burned up completely.
