**1** For the director. Of David. A psalm.[^1] Yahweh,[^2] you have searched me and you know me.

**2** You know when I sit down and when I rise up; you understand my thoughts from far away.

**3** You sift[^3] my path and my lying down; you are familiar with all my ways.

**4** Even before a word is on my tongue, look, Yahweh, you know it completely.[^4]

**5** You hem me in behind and before; you have laid your hand on me.

**6** Such knowledge is too wonderful for me; it is too high, I cannot reach it.

**7** Where can I go from your Spirit?[^5] Where can I flee from your presence?

**8** If I go up to the heavens, you are there; if I make my bed in Sheol,[^6] you are there too.

**9** If I take the wings of the dawn and settle at the far side of the sea,[^7]

**10** even there your hand will lead me, and your right hand will hold me fast.

**11** If I say, "Surely the darkness will overwhelm me,[^8] and the light around me will become night,"

**12** even the darkness is not dark to you; the night shines like the day, for darkness is as light to you.[^9]

**13** For you created my inmost being;[^10] you wove me together in my mother's womb.

**14** I praise you, for I am awesomely and wonderfully made.[^11] Wonderful are your works; my soul[^12] knows it very well.

**15** My frame[^13] was not hidden from you when I was made in secret, woven together in the depths of the earth.[^14]

**16** Your eyes saw my unformed body;[^15] in your book they were all written, the days that were shaped for me, each one of them.[^16]

**17** How precious to me are your thoughts, God! How vast is the sum of them!

**18** If I counted them, they would outnumber the grains of sand. I awake, and I am still with you.[^17]

**19** If only you would kill the wicked, God![^18] You bloodthirsty people,[^19] go away from me!

**20** They speak of you with evil intent; your enemies take your name in vain.[^20]

**21** Do I not hate those who hate you, Yahweh? Do I not loathe those who rise up against you?

**22** I hate them with complete hatred; they have become my enemies.

**23** Search me, God, and know my heart; test me and know my anxious thoughts.

**24** See if there is any hurtful way in me,[^21] and lead me in the everlasting way.[^22]

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Or "measure", "examine". Hebrew *zerita*, perhaps from the verb for winnowing grain.
[^4]: Literally "For there is not a word on my tongue, (but) look, Yahweh, you know all of it."
[^5]: Or "your spirit", "your breath". Hebrew *ruach*.
[^6]: Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead.
[^7]: That is, the far west. The dawn is in the east, and the sea, the Mediterranean, in the west.
[^8]: Hebrew *yeshufeni*, a verb that elsewhere means "crush" or "strike" (Genesis 3:15). Many scholars read "will cover me", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^9]: Literally "as the darkness, so the light".
[^10]: Literally "my kidneys", thought of as the seat of the deepest feelings and thoughts.
[^11]: Literally "for awesomely I am set apart (or wonderfully made)". The Septuagint reads "for you are awesomely wonderful".
[^12]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self.
[^13]: Literally "my bone(s)".
[^14]: A poetic picture of the womb, as hidden as the depths of the earth.
[^15]: Hebrew *golmi*, a word found only here: "my embryo", "my shapeless form".
[^16]: The Hebrew of this verse is difficult. The Hebrew text is written "and not (one) among them" (traditionally "when as yet there was none of them") but read "and to it (was) one among them".
[^17]: Some scholars read "I come to the end (of them)", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^18]: Hebrew *Eloah*, a poetic word for God.
[^19]: Literally "men of blood", that is, murderers.
[^20]: Literally "they lift up to falsehood, your enemies", echoing the command not to misuse Yahweh's name (Exodus 20:7). *Your enemies*: Hebrew *arekha*, a rare word, probably from Aramaic; it usually means "your cities".
[^21]: Or "any way of pain", "any idolatrous way". Hebrew *otsev* can mean "pain" or "idol".
[^22]: Or "the ancient way", the way of old.
