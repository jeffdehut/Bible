**1** A maskil of Asaph.[^1] God, why have you rejected us forever? Why does your anger smolder against the sheep of your pasture?

**2** Remember your congregation, which you acquired long ago, the tribe of your inheritance, which you redeemed, Mount Zion, where you have dwelt.

**3** Turn your steps[^2] to the everlasting ruins; the enemy has damaged everything in the sanctuary.

**4** Your adversaries have roared in the midst of your meeting place;[^3] they have set up their own signs as signs.[^4]

**5** They looked like men raising axes in a thicket of trees.[^5]

**6** And now all its carved work they smash with hatchet and hammers.

**7** They set your sanctuary on fire; they have desecrated the dwelling place of your name, bringing it to the ground.

**8** They said in their hearts, "Let us crush them completely!"[^6] They burned all the meeting places of God in the land.

**9** We do not see our signs; there is no longer any prophet, and none of us knows how long.

**10** How long, God, will the adversary taunt? Will the enemy despise your name forever?

**11** Why do you hold back your hand, your right hand? Take it from the fold of your robe[^7] and destroy them!

**12** Yet God is my King from of old, working salvation in the midst of the earth.

**13** You split the sea by your strength; you broke the heads of the sea monsters[^8] on the waters.

**14** You crushed the heads of Leviathan;[^9] you gave him as food to the creatures of the desert.[^10]

**15** You split open springs and streams; you dried up ever-flowing rivers.

**16** Yours is the day, and yours also the night; you established the moon[^11] and the sun.

**17** You set all the boundaries of the earth; summer and winter, you made them.

**18** Remember this: the enemy taunts, LORD,[^12] and a foolish people despises your name.

**19** Do not give the life of your dove to the wild beasts;[^13] do not forget the life of your afflicted forever.

**20** Look to the covenant, for the dark places of the land are full of the haunts of violence.

**21** Do not let the oppressed turn back in shame; let the afflicted and the needy praise your name.

**22** Rise up, God, plead your own cause; remember how fools taunt you all day long.

**23** Do not forget the clamor of your adversaries, the uproar of those who rise against you, which goes up continually.

[^1]: *Maskil*: a term of uncertain meaning, perhaps "a skillful psalm" or "a psalm of instruction". *Of Asaph*: or "for Asaph", a Levite who led the temple singers (see the note on 50:1). In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles. The psalm mourns the destruction of the temple, probably by the Babylonians (2 Kings 25:8–10; compare Psalm 79).
[^2]: Literally "Lift up your steps".
[^3]: Hebrew *mo'ed*, "appointed place, meeting place", the word used in "tent of meeting".
[^4]: Perhaps military standards or the emblems of their gods.
[^5]: The Hebrew of this verse is difficult. Literally "It is known as one who brings up axes on high in a thicket of wood".
[^6]: Literally "Let us oppress them together".
[^7]: Literally "from the midst of your bosom". The Hebrew text is written "your decree" (*chuqqekha*) but read "your bosom" (*cheqekha*).
[^8]: Hebrew *tanninim*, great creatures of the sea (Genesis 1:21).
[^9]: A many-headed sea monster, a picture of the forces of chaos (compare Job 40:25; 41:1 in most English Bibles; Isaiah 27:1).
[^10]: Literally "to a people, to desert creatures".
[^11]: Literally "the light".
[^12]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. Or "the enemy taunts the LORD".
[^13]: Literally "to the beast the soul (*nephesh*) of your turtledove". The Hebrew word *chayyat*, "beast", also means "life" or "community" in the next line.
