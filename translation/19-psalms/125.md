**1** A song of ascents.[^1] Those who trust in Yahweh[^2] are like Mount Zion, which cannot be shaken but stands firm forever.[^3]

**2** As the mountains surround Jerusalem, so Yahweh surrounds his people, from now on and forever.[^4]

**3** For the scepter of wickedness will not rest on the land allotted to the righteous,[^5] so that the righteous do not stretch out their hands to do wrong.

**4** Do good, Yahweh, to those who are good, to those who are upright in their hearts.

**5** But those who turn aside to their crooked ways, Yahweh will lead away with the evildoers. Peace be on Israel!

[^1]: *A song of ascents*: Hebrew *shir ha-ma'alot* (see the note on 120:1). In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "sits forever", or "is inhabited forever".
[^4]: Literally "Jerusalem: mountains are around her, and Yahweh is around his people".
[^5]: Literally "on the lot of the righteous", the share of land assigned to them by lot (compare Joshua 14:2). The scepter of a wicked ruler stands for foreign rule over the land.
