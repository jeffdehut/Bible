**1** God of vengeance,[^1] Yahweh,[^2] God of vengeance, shine out!

**2** Rise up, Judge of the earth; pay back to the proud what they deserve.

**3** How long will the wicked, Yahweh, how long will the wicked exult?

**4** They pour out arrogant words; all the evildoers boast.

**5** They crush your people, Yahweh, and afflict your inheritance.

**6** They kill the widow and the foreigner, and murder the fatherless.

**7** They say, "Yah[^3] does not see; the God of Jacob pays no attention."

**8** Understand, you senseless among the people! You fools, when will you be wise?

**9** He who planted the ear, does he not hear? He who formed the eye, does he not see?

**10** He who disciplines the nations, does he not punish, he who teaches humankind knowledge?[^4]

**11** Yahweh knows the thoughts of humankind, that they are only a breath.[^5]

**12** Happy[^6] is the one you discipline, Yah, and whom you teach from your law,

**13** to give him rest from days of trouble, until a pit is dug for the wicked.

**14** For Yahweh will not abandon his people; he will not forsake his inheritance.

**15** For justice will again be righteous,[^7] and all the upright in heart will follow it.

**16** Who will rise up for me against evildoers? Who will take a stand for me against evildoers?

**17** If Yahweh had not been my help, my soul[^8] would soon have lived in the land of silence.[^9]

**18** When I said, "My foot is slipping," your faithful love,[^10] Yahweh, held me up.

**19** When my anxious thoughts multiply within me, your comforts delight my soul.

**20** Can a throne of destruction be allied with you, one that frames trouble by decree?[^11]

**21** They band together against the life of the righteous, and condemn the innocent to death.[^12]

**22** But Yahweh has become my stronghold, and my God the rock of my refuge.

**23** He will turn their own iniquity back on them, and destroy them for their wickedness; Yahweh our God will destroy them.

[^1]: Literally "God of vengeances", the God who punishes wrong and sets it right. Psalm 94 has no title in the Hebrew. The Septuagint gives it one: "A psalm of David, for the fourth day of the week."
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *Yah*, a short form of the name Yahweh. Also in verse 12.
[^4]: The Hebrew breaks off, leaving the question unfinished: literally "he who teaches humankind knowledge".
[^5]: Hebrew *hevel*, "breath, vapor", something without substance (compare Ecclesiastes 1:2). 1 Corinthians 3:20 quotes this verse, with "the wise" in place of "humankind".
[^6]: Hebrew *ashrei*: happy, fortunate; traditionally "blessed".
[^7]: Literally "For judgment will return to righteousness".
[^8]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Also in verses 19 and 21.
[^9]: Hebrew *dumah*, "silence", a name for the realm of the dead (compare 115:17).
[^10]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^11]: Or "by law". Hebrew *choq*, "decree". *A throne of destruction*: unjust rulers.
[^12]: Literally "and condemn innocent blood".
