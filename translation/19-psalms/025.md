**1** Of David.[^1] To you, LORD,[^2] I lift up my soul.[^3]

**2** My God, in you I trust; do not let me be put to shame; do not let my enemies triumph over me.

**3** No one who waits for you will be put to shame; those who are treacherous without cause will be put to shame.

**4** Make your ways known to me, LORD; teach me your paths.

**5** Lead me in your truth[^4] and teach me, for you are the God of my salvation; for you I wait all day long.

**6** Remember your compassion, LORD, and your faithful love,[^5] for they have been from of old.

**7** Do not remember the sins of my youth or my offenses; according to your faithful love remember me, for the sake of your goodness, LORD.

**8** The LORD is good and upright; so he teaches sinners the way.

**9** He leads the humble in what is right, and teaches the humble his way.

**10** All the paths of the LORD are faithful love and faithfulness[^6] for those who keep his covenant and his statutes.[^7]

**11** For the sake of your name, LORD, forgive my guilt, for it is great.

**12** Who is the one who fears the LORD? He will teach him the way he should choose.

**13** He himself[^8] will live in prosperity, and his offspring will inherit the land.

**14** The close counsel[^9] of the LORD is for those who fear him, and he makes his covenant known to them.

**15** My eyes are always on the LORD, for he will free my feet from the net.

**16** Turn to me and be gracious to me, for I am lonely and afflicted.

**17** The troubles of my heart have multiplied;[^10] bring me out of my distress.

**18** See my affliction and my trouble, and forgive all my sins.

**19** See how many my enemies are, and how they hate me with violent hatred.

**20** Guard my life[^11] and rescue me; do not let me be put to shame, for I take refuge in you.

**21** May integrity and uprightness protect me, for I wait for you.

**22** Redeem Israel, God, from all its troubles.

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles. Psalm 25 is an acrostic: in the Hebrew, each verse begins with the next letter of the alphabet. The pattern is not complete: *bet* comes in the second word of verse 2, there is no verse for *vav* or *qof*, *resh* begins both verses 18 and 19, and verse 22, beginning with *pe*, stands outside the alphabet as a closing prayer (compare Psalm 34).
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self.
[^4]: Or "faithfulness". Hebrew *emet*.
[^5]: Hebrew *chasadim*, the plural of *chesed*, "loyal, faithful love": "your acts of faithful love".
[^6]: Hebrew *chesed ve'emet*: loyal love and reliability (compare Exodus 34:6).
[^7]: Hebrew *edot*, traditionally "testimonies": the solemn terms of the covenant.
[^8]: Literally "His soul (*nephesh*)".
[^9]: Or "friendship". Hebrew *sod*, the confidential talk of close friends.
[^10]: Literally "have grown wide".
[^11]: Hebrew *nephesh*.
