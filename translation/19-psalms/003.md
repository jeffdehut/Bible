**1** A psalm of David, when he fled from his son Absalom.[^1]

**2** Yahweh,[^2] how many are my adversaries! Many are rising up against me.

**3** Many are saying of me,[^3] "There is no salvation for him in God." Selah.[^4]

**4** But you, Yahweh, are a shield around me, my glory,[^5] the one who lifts up my head.

**5** I cry aloud to Yahweh, and he answers me from his holy mountain. Selah.

**6** I lay down and slept; I woke again, for Yahweh sustains me.

**7** I will not be afraid of the tens of thousands of people who have taken their stand against me on every side.

**8** Rise up, Yahweh! Save me, my God! For you have struck all my enemies on the jaw; you have broken the teeth of the wicked.

**9** Salvation belongs to Yahweh. May your blessing be on your people. Selah.

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". The events are told in 2 Samuel 15–18. In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–9 are 3:1–8 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "of my soul (*nephesh*)".
[^4]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning. Also in verses 5 and 9.
[^5]: Or "my honor": the one who gives me honor.
