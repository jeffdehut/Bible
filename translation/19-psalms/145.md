**1** A song of praise. Of David.[^1] I will exalt you, my God the King, and bless your name forever and ever.[^2]

**2** Every day I will bless you and praise your name forever and ever.

**3** Great is Yahweh,[^3] and most worthy of praise; his greatness is beyond searching out.

**4** One generation will commend your works to another; they will tell of your mighty acts.

**5** On the glorious splendor of your majesty and on your wonderful works[^4] I will meditate.

**6** They will speak of the power of your awesome deeds, and I will tell of your greatness.[^5]

**7** They will pour out the memory of your abundant goodness[^6] and sing joyfully of your righteousness.

**8** Yahweh is gracious and compassionate, slow to anger and great in faithful love.[^7]

**9** Yahweh is good to all, and his compassion is over all that he has made.

**10** All your works will give thanks to you, Yahweh, and your faithful ones[^8] will bless you.

**11** They will speak of the glory of your kingdom and talk of your might,

**12** to make known to human beings[^9] his mighty acts, and the glorious splendor of his kingdom.

**13** Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and your dominion endures through all generations.[^10]

**14** Yahweh upholds all who fall, and raises up all who are bowed down.

**15** The eyes of all look to you, and you give them their food at the proper time.

**16** You open your hand and satisfy the desire of every living thing.

**17** Yahweh is righteous in all his ways and faithful[^11] in all his deeds.

**18** Yahweh is near to all who call on him, to all who call on him in truth.

**19** He fulfills the desire of those who fear him; he hears their cry and saves them.

**20** Yahweh watches over all who love him, but all the wicked he will destroy.

**21** My mouth will speak the praise of Yahweh, and let every creature[^12] bless his holy name forever and ever.

[^1]: *A song of praise*: Hebrew *tehillah*, "praise", the word that gives the book its Hebrew name, *Tehillim*, "Praises". This is the only psalm with this title. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: Psalm 145 is an acrostic: in the Hebrew, each verse begins with the next letter of the alphabet, from *aleph* (verse 1) to *tav* (verse 21). There is no verse for *nun* (see the note on verse 13).
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Literally "the words (or matters) of your wonders".
[^5]: The Hebrew text is written "your great deeds" but read "your greatness".
[^6]: That is, tell of it eagerly, like water bubbling from a spring. Literally "They will pour out the memory of the abundance of your goodness".
[^7]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. The line repeats God's description of himself in Exodus 34:6 (compare 86:15; 103:8).
[^8]: Hebrew *chasidim*, "the faithful, the loyal", related to *chesed*, "faithful love".
[^9]: Literally "to the sons of *adam* (humankind)". *His mighty acts*: the Hebrew turns here from speaking to God ("your") to speaking of him ("his").
[^10]: In the Hebrew text there is no verse for the letter *nun*, which should come between verses 13 and 14. A Dead Sea Scroll (11QPs-a) has one: "God is faithful in his words, and faithful (*chasid*) in all his deeds." The Septuagint has the same line, with "the Lord" for "God". Its second half is the same as verse 17b. Compare also Daniel 3:33 (4:3 in most English Bibles).
[^11]: Hebrew *chasid*, "faithful, loyal", related to *chesed*, "faithful love".
[^12]: Literally "all flesh".
