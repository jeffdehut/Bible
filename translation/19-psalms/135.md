**1** Praise the LORD![^1] Praise the name of the LORD;[^2] praise him, you servants of the LORD,

**2** who stand in the house of the LORD, in the courts of the house of our God.

**3** Praise the LORD, for the LORD is good; sing praises to his name, for it is pleasant.[^3]

**4** For the LORD has chosen Jacob for himself, Israel as his treasured possession.[^4]

**5** For I know that the LORD is great, and our Lord is above all gods.

**6** Whatever the LORD pleases, he does, in the heavens and on the earth, in the seas and all the depths.

**7** He makes the clouds[^5] rise from the ends of the earth; he makes lightning for the rain, and brings out the wind from his storehouses.[^6]

**8** He struck down the firstborn of Egypt, both human and animal.

**9** He sent signs and wonders into your midst, Egypt, against Pharaoh and all his servants.

**10** He struck down many nations and killed mighty kings:

**11** Sihon king of the Amorites, and Og king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan.[^7]

**12** He gave their land as an inheritance, an inheritance to his people Israel.

**13** LORD, your name endures forever, your renown,[^8] LORD, from generation to generation.

**14** For the LORD will give justice to his people[^9] and have compassion on his servants.

**15** The idols of the nations are silver and gold, the work of human hands.[^10]

**16** They have mouths, but cannot speak; they have eyes, but cannot see.

**17** They have ears, but cannot hear, and there is no breath in their mouths.

**18** Those who make them will become like them, and so will all who trust in them.

**19** House of Israel, bless the LORD! House of Aaron, bless the LORD!

**20** House of Levi, bless the LORD! You who fear the LORD, bless the LORD!

**21** Blessed be the LORD from Zion, he who dwells in Jerusalem. Praise the LORD!

[^1]: Hebrew *hallelu-yah*, "praise Yah", the source of the word "Hallelujah". *Yah* is a short form of the name YHWH. Also in verses 3 and 21.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Or "for he is gracious", "for it (his name) is lovely".
[^4]: Hebrew *segullah*, a king's or an owner's personal treasure (Exodus 19:5).
[^5]: Or "vapors".
[^6]: This verse is the same as Jeremiah 10:13b and 51:16b.
[^7]: Compare Numbers 21:21–35 and Joshua 12.
[^8]: Literally "your remembrance", the name by which you are remembered (compare Exodus 3:15).
[^9]: Literally "will judge his people", that is, vindicate them. This verse is the same as Deuteronomy 32:36a.
[^10]: Verses 15–18 are nearly the same as 115:4–8.
