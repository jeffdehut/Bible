**1** A song of ascents.[^1] To you I lift up my eyes, you who are enthroned in the heavens.

**2** As the eyes of servants[^2] look to the hand of their master, as the eyes of a female servant look to the hand of her mistress, so our eyes look to Yahweh[^3] our God, until he is gracious to us.

**3** Be gracious to us, Yahweh, be gracious to us, for we have had more than enough of contempt.

**4** We have had more than enough[^4] of the scorn of those who are at ease, of the contempt of the proud.

[^1]: *A song of ascents*: Hebrew *shir ha-ma'alot* (see the note on 120:1). In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: Or "slaves".
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Literally "Our soul (*nephesh*) has been filled for itself".
