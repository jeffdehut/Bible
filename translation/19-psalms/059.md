**1** For the director. According to Do Not Destroy.[^1] Of David. A Miktam, when Saul sent men, and they watched the house in order to kill him.[^2]

**2** Rescue me from my enemies, my God; protect me from those who rise up against me.

**3** Rescue me from evildoers, and save me from bloodthirsty men.[^3]

**4** For see, they lie in wait for my life;[^4] fierce men band together against me, not for any transgression or sin of mine, Yahweh.[^5]

**5** For no guilt of mine they run and take up their positions. Rouse yourself to meet me, and see!

**6** You, Yahweh God of Armies,[^6] God of Israel, wake up to punish all the nations; show no mercy to any treacherous evildoers. Selah.[^7]

**7** They return at evening, howling like dogs, and prowl around the city.

**8** See, they spew from their mouths; swords are on their lips, for they think, "Who will hear?"[^8]

**9** But you, Yahweh, laugh at them; you mock all the nations.

**10** Because of his strength, I will watch for you,[^9] for God is my fortress.

**11** My faithful God[^10] will come to meet me; God will let me look in triumph on those who lie in wait for me.

**12** Do not kill them, or my people will forget; make them wander by your power, and bring them down, Lord, our shield.

**13** For the sin of their mouths, the words of their lips, let them be caught in their pride, for the curses and the lies they speak.

**14** Consume them in wrath, consume them until they are no more, so that they may know that God rules over Jacob, to the ends of the earth. Selah.

**15** They return at evening, howling like dogs, and prowl around the city.

**16** They wander about for food, and if they are not satisfied, they stay all night.[^11]

**17** But I will sing of your strength; I will sing aloud of your faithful love in the morning. For you have been my fortress, a refuge in the day of my distress.

**18** My Strength, I will sing praises to you, for God is my fortress, my faithful God.[^12]

[^1]: Hebrew *al-tashchet*, "do not destroy", probably the name of a tune (see the title of Psalm 57).
[^2]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". *Miktam*: a term of uncertain meaning (see the note on the title of Psalm 16). The events are told in 1 Samuel 19:11–17. In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–18 are 59:1–17 in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "men of blood".
[^4]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self.
[^5]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^6]: Hebrew *YHWH Elohim tseva'ot*. Compare "Yahweh of Armies", traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^7]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning. Also in verse 14.
[^8]: Literally "for who hears?"
[^9]: Literally "His strength, I will watch for you", perhaps meaning the enemy's strength. Verse 18 has "my strength", and the Septuagint reads that here: "My strength, I will watch for you".
[^10]: Literally "The God of my faithful love (*chesed*)". The Hebrew text is written "The God of his faithful love" but read "The God of my faithful love".
[^11]: Or "they growl", "they grumble".
[^12]: Literally "the God of my faithful love (*chesed*)".
