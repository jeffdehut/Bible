**1** Of David.[^1] Contend, LORD,[^2] with those who contend with me; fight against those who fight against me.

**2** Take hold of shield and buckler,[^3] and rise up to help me.

**3** Draw the spear and the battle-axe[^4] against those who pursue me. Say to me,[^5] "I am your salvation."

**4** Let those who seek my life be put to shame and disgraced; let those who plan harm against me be turned back and confounded.

**5** Let them be like chaff before the wind, with the angel of the LORD driving them away.

**6** Let their way be dark and slippery, with the angel of the LORD pursuing them.

**7** For without cause they hid their net for me in a pit; without cause they dug it for my life.[^6]

**8** Let ruin come on him unawares; let the net he hid catch him; let him fall into it, to his ruin.

**9** Then my soul[^7] will rejoice in the LORD and delight in his salvation.

**10** All my bones will say, "LORD, who is like you? You rescue the afflicted from those too strong for them, the afflicted and the needy from those who rob them."

**11** Malicious witnesses[^8] rise up; they question me about things I do not know.

**12** They repay me evil for good, leaving my soul bereaved.

**13** But as for me, when they were sick, my clothing was sackcloth; I humbled myself[^9] with fasting, and I prayed with my head bowed on my chest.[^10]

**14** I went about as though for my friend or my brother; I bowed down in mourning,[^11] like one who mourns for his mother.

**15** But when I stumbled, they rejoiced and gathered; attackers[^12] gathered against me, people I did not know; they tore at me without ceasing.

**16** Like godless people mocking at a feast,[^13] they gnashed their teeth at me.

**17** Lord, how long will you look on? Rescue my life[^14] from their ravages, my only life[^15] from the lions.

**18** I will give thanks to you in the great assembly; among a mighty throng I will praise you.

**19** Do not let those who are wrongfully my enemies gloat over me; do not let those who hate me without cause wink their eyes in malice.[^16]

**20** For they do not speak peace, but devise deceitful words against those who are quiet in the land.

**21** They open their mouths wide against me; they say, "Aha! Aha! Our eyes have seen it!"

**22** You have seen it, LORD; do not be silent. Lord, do not be far from me.

**23** Wake up! Rouse yourself to defend me, my God and my Lord, to plead my cause!

**24** Judge me[^17] according to your righteousness, LORD my God, and do not let them gloat over me.

**25** Do not let them say in their hearts, "Aha, just what we wanted!"[^18] Do not let them say, "We have swallowed him up."

**26** Let those who rejoice at my misfortune be put to shame and confounded together; let those who exalt themselves over me be clothed with shame and disgrace.

**27** Let those who delight in my vindication[^19] shout for joy and be glad; let them say always, "Great is the LORD, who delights in the well-being[^20] of his servant!"

**28** Then my tongue will tell of your righteousness, of your praise all day long.

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *magen* and *tsinnah*, a small round shield and a large one that covered the whole body.
[^4]: Hebrew *segor*, perhaps a weapon, or a verb: "and close the way".
[^5]: Literally "to my soul (*nephesh*)".
[^6]: Literally "For without cause they hid for me the pit of their net; without cause they dug for my soul (*nephesh*)".
[^7]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self.
[^8]: Literally "witnesses of violence".
[^9]: Literally "afflicted my soul (*nephesh*)", a phrase used of fasting (compare Leviticus 16:29).
[^10]: Literally "my prayer returned to my bosom". Or "may my prayer return to me", that is, unanswered.
[^11]: Or "in dark clothes", the clothing of mourning.
[^12]: Hebrew *nekhim*, a word of uncertain meaning, perhaps "attackers", "slanderers" or "outcasts".
[^13]: The Hebrew of this line is difficult. Literally "with the godless of the mockers of a cake".
[^14]: Hebrew *nephesh*.
[^15]: Literally "my only one", that is, my precious life (compare 22:21, 22:20 in most English Bibles).
[^16]: John 15:25 quotes "They hated me without cause", from this verse or from 69:5 (69:4 in most English Bibles).
[^17]: Or "Vindicate me".
[^18]: Literally "Aha, our soul (*nephesh*)!", that is, our desire.
[^19]: Or "my righteousness". Hebrew *tsedeq*.
[^20]: Hebrew *shalom*: peace, well-being.
