**1** A psalm of Asaph.[^1] The Mighty One, God, Yahweh,[^2] has spoken and summoned the earth from the rising of the sun to its setting.

**2** Out of Zion, perfect in beauty, God shines out.

**3** Our God comes, and he will not keep silent;[^3] before him is a devouring fire, and around him a raging storm.

**4** He calls to the heavens above, and to the earth, to judge his people:

**5** "Gather to me my faithful ones,[^4] who made a covenant with me by sacrifice."

**6** The heavens proclaim his righteousness, for God himself is judge. Selah.[^5]

**7** "Listen, my people, and I will speak; Israel, I will testify against you: I am God, your God.

**8** It is not for your sacrifices that I rebuke you, or for your burnt offerings, which are always before me.

**9** I will not take a bull from your house, or goats from your pens.

**10** For every animal of the forest is mine, the cattle on a thousand hills.

**11** I know every bird of the mountains, and all that moves in the field is mine.[^6]

**12** If I were hungry, I would not tell you, for the world and all that fills it are mine.

**13** Do I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats?

**14** Offer God a sacrifice of thanksgiving,[^7] and fulfill your vows to the Most High.[^8]

**15** Call on me in the day of trouble; I will rescue you, and you will honor me."

**16** But to the wicked God says, "What right have you to recite my decrees, or to take my covenant on your lips?

**17** You hate discipline, and you throw my words behind you.

**18** When you see a thief, you are pleased with him,[^9] and you throw in your lot with adulterers.

**19** You let your mouth loose for evil, and your tongue harnesses deceit.

**20** You sit and speak against your brother; you slander your own mother's son.

**21** These things you have done, and I kept silent; you thought that I was just like you. But I will rebuke you, and lay out the case before your eyes.

**22** Understand this, you who forget God, or I will tear you to pieces, with no one to rescue you.

**23** Whoever offers a sacrifice of thanksgiving honors me; and to the one who keeps to the way,[^10] I will show the salvation of God."

[^1]: *Of Asaph*: or "for Asaph". Asaph was a Levite whom David appointed to lead the temple singers (1 Chronicles 16:5–7; 25:1–2). Psalms 73–83 are also "of Asaph". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. *The Mighty One, God, Yahweh*: Hebrew *El Elohim YHWH*, three names for God together, as in Joshua 22:22. It can also be read "The God of gods, Yahweh".
[^3]: Or "May our God come, and not keep silent".
[^4]: Hebrew *chasidim*, "the faithful, the loyal", related to *chesed*, "faithful love".
[^5]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning.
[^6]: Literally "is with me".
[^7]: Hebrew *todah*: a sacrifice given in thanksgiving (Leviticus 7:12). Also in verse 23.
[^8]: Hebrew *Elyon*, a title of God.
[^9]: Or "you run with him", as the Septuagint reads.
[^10]: Literally "and the one who sets a way". The meaning is uncertain.
