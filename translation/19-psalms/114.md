**1** When Israel went out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of foreign speech,[^1]

**2** Judah became his sanctuary,[^2] Israel his dominion.

**3** The sea looked and fled; the Jordan turned back.[^3]

**4** The mountains skipped like rams, the hills like lambs.[^4]

**5** What is wrong with you, sea, that you flee? Jordan, that you turn back?

**6** You mountains, that you skip like rams? You hills, like lambs?

**7** Tremble,[^5] earth, before the Lord, before the God of Jacob,

**8** who turned the rock into a pool of water, the flint into a spring of water.[^6]

[^1]: The Septuagint joins Psalms 114 and 115 into a single psalm, and it divides Psalm 116 into two (116:1–9 and 116:10–19).
[^2]: Or "his holy people". God is not named until verse 7.
[^3]: The crossing of the Red Sea (Exodus 14:21–22) and of the Jordan (Joshua 3:14–17).
[^4]: Literally "like the young of the flock". Also in verse 6.
[^5]: Or "Writhe", as in labor pains.
[^6]: Compare Exodus 17:6 and Numbers 20:8–11.
