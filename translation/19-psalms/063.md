**1** A psalm of David, when he was in the wilderness of Judah.[^1]

**2** God, you are my God; I seek you earnestly.[^2] My soul[^3] thirsts for you; my body[^4] longs for you, in a dry and weary land without water.

**3** So I have looked for you in the sanctuary, to see your power and your glory.

**4** Because your faithful love[^5] is better than life, my lips will praise you.

**5** So I will bless you as long as I live; in your name I will lift up my hands.

**6** My soul will be satisfied as with the richest food,[^6] and my mouth will praise you with joyful lips.

**7** When I remember you on my bed, I meditate on you through the watches of the night.

**8** For you have been my help, and in the shadow of your wings I sing for joy.

**9** My soul clings to you; your right hand holds me up.

**10** But those who seek my life to destroy it will go down into the depths of the earth.

**11** They will be handed over to the power of the sword;[^7] they will become food for jackals.[^8]

**12** But the king will rejoice in God; all who swear by him[^9] will boast, for the mouths of those who speak lies will be stopped.

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". Compare 1 Samuel 23:14 and 2 Samuel 15:23–28. In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–12 are 63:1–11 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ashachareka*, related to *shachar*, "dawn"; so perhaps "I seek you at dawn".
[^3]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Also in verses 6, 9 and 10.
[^4]: Literally "my flesh".
[^5]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^6]: Literally "as with fat and fatness".
[^7]: Literally "They will pour him out on the hands of the sword".
[^8]: Or "foxes".
[^9]: By God, or perhaps by the king.
