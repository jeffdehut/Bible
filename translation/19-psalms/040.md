**1** For the director. Of David. A psalm.[^1]

**2** I waited patiently for the LORD;[^2] he turned to me and heard my cry for help.

**3** He drew me up from the pit of destruction,[^3] out of the miry clay; he set my feet on a rock and made my steps secure.

**4** He put a new song in my mouth, a song of praise to our God. Many will see and fear,[^4] and will trust in the LORD.

**5** Happy[^5] is the one who makes the LORD his trust, who does not turn to the proud, or to those who go astray after lies.[^6]

**6** Many are the wonders you have done, LORD my God, and your plans for us; no one can compare with you.[^7] If I would proclaim them and speak of them, they are too many to count.

**7** Sacrifice and offering you did not desire, but you have opened my ears;[^8] burnt offering and sin offering[^9] you did not ask for.

**8** Then I said, "Here I am, I have come; in the scroll of the book it is written about me.[^10]

**9** I delight to do your will, my God; your law[^11] is within my heart."[^12]

**10** I have proclaimed the good news[^13] of righteousness in the great assembly. See, I do not hold back my lips, LORD, as you know.

**11** I have not hidden your righteousness within my heart; I have spoken of your faithfulness and your salvation. I have not concealed your faithful love and your truth[^14] from the great assembly.

**12** You, LORD, will not withhold your compassion from me; your faithful love and your truth will always protect me.

**13** For evils without number have surrounded me; my sins have overtaken me, and I cannot see. They are more than the hairs of my head, and my heart fails me.

**14** Be pleased, LORD, to rescue me; LORD, come quickly to help me.[^15]

**15** Let those who seek my life,[^16] to sweep it away, be put to shame and confounded together; let those who delight in my harm be turned back and disgraced.

**16** Let those who say to me, "Aha! Aha!" be appalled because of their shame.

**17** But let all who seek you rejoice and be glad in you; let those who love your salvation say always, "Great is the LORD!"

**18** As for me, I am afflicted and needy; may the Lord think of me. You are my help and my deliverer; my God, do not delay.

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–18 are 40:1–17 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. *I waited patiently*: literally "I waited and waited".
[^3]: Literally "the pit of roaring", perhaps of rushing water, or "of desolation".
[^4]: In Hebrew "see" (*yir'u*) and "fear" (*yira'u*) sound alike.
[^5]: Hebrew *ashrei*: happy, fortunate; traditionally "blessed".
[^6]: Or "to false gods". Hebrew *rehavim*, "the proud", and *satei khazav*, "those who turn aside to falsehood", may both refer to idols and those who worship them.
[^7]: Or "they cannot be set out in order before you".
[^8]: Literally "ears you have dug for me", that is, you have made me able to hear and obey. Hebrews 10:5–7 quotes verses 7–9 from the Septuagint, which reads "but a body you have prepared for me".
[^9]: Hebrew *chata'ah*, a form of *chatta't*. Or "purification offering".
[^10]: Or "it is written for me", that is, prescribed for me.
[^11]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, teaching".
[^12]: Literally "in the middle of my inner parts".
[^13]: Hebrew *bissarti*, "I brought good news". In the Septuagint this is *euangelizomai*, the verb behind "gospel".
[^14]: Hebrew *chesed ve'emet*: loyal, faithful love and reliability, truth (compare 25:10). Also in verse 12.
[^15]: Verses 14–18 appear again, with small differences, as Psalm 70:2–6 (70:1–5 in English).
[^16]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self.
