**1** Of David.[^1] To you, LORD,[^2] I call; my rock, do not be deaf to me, for if you are silent to me, I will become like those who go down to the pit.[^3]

**2** Hear the sound of my pleas for mercy when I cry to you for help, when I lift up my hands toward your holy inner sanctuary.[^4]

**3** Do not drag me away with the wicked, with the evildoers, who speak peace with their neighbors while evil is in their hearts.

**4** Repay them according to their deeds, according to the evil of their actions. Repay them according to the work of their hands; give back to them what they deserve.

**5** Because they do not understand the deeds of the LORD or the work of his hands, he will tear them down and not build them up.

**6** Blessed be the LORD, for he has heard the sound of my pleas for mercy.

**7** The LORD is my strength and my shield; my heart trusts in him, and I am helped. My heart exults, and with my song I will give thanks to him.

**8** The LORD is their strength;[^5] he is a saving stronghold for his anointed.[^6]

**9** Save your people and bless your inheritance; be their shepherd and carry them forever.

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *bor*, "pit": the grave and the realm of the dead.
[^4]: Hebrew *devir*, the innermost room of the temple, the most holy place (1 Kings 6:16).
[^5]: The Septuagint reads "the strength of his people", which differs from the Hebrew by one letter.
[^6]: Hebrew *meshicho*, "his anointed one", the king anointed at the LORD's command.
