**1** A song of ascents.[^1] Out of the depths I call to you, Yahweh.[^2]

**2** Lord, hear my voice! Let your ears be attentive to my voice as I plead for mercy.[^3]

**3** If you, Yah,[^4] kept a record of iniquities,[^5] Lord, who could stand?

**4** But with you there is forgiveness, so that you may be feared.

**5** I wait for Yahweh; my soul[^6] waits, and in his word I put my hope.

**6** My soul waits for the Lord[^7] more than watchmen wait for the morning, more than watchmen for the morning.

**7** Israel, put your hope in Yahweh! For with Yahweh there is faithful love,[^8] and with him is abundant redemption.

**8** He himself will redeem Israel from all its iniquities.

[^1]: *A song of ascents*: Hebrew *shir ha-ma'alot* (see the note on 120:1). In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "to the voice of my supplications".
[^4]: Hebrew *Yah*, a short form of the name Yahweh.
[^5]: Literally "If you watch iniquities".
[^6]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Also in verse 6.
[^7]: The Hebrew has no verb: literally "My soul (is) for the Lord".
[^8]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
