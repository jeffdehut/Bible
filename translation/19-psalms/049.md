**1** For the director. Of the sons of Korah. A psalm.[^1]

**2** Hear this, all you peoples; listen, all who live in the world,

**3** both low and high,[^2] rich and poor together.

**4** My mouth will speak wisdom, and the meditation of my heart will be understanding.

**5** I will turn my ear to a proverb;[^3] I will set out my riddle to the music of the lyre.

**6** Why should I be afraid in days of trouble, when the wickedness of those who dog my steps[^4] surrounds me,

**7** those who trust in their wealth and boast of their great riches?

**8** No one can ever ransom a brother,[^5] or pay God the price for his life.

**9** For the ransom of their life[^6] is costly, and it will always fall short,[^7]

**10** that one should live on forever and never see the pit.[^8]

**11** For one sees that the wise die; the fool and the senseless perish together, and leave their wealth to others.

**12** Their inner thought is that their houses will last forever,[^9] their dwellings for all generations; they have named lands after themselves.

**13** But a human being in his splendor does not endure;[^10] he is like the animals that perish.

**14** This is the way of those who are foolishly confident, and of those after them who delight in their words.[^11] Selah.[^12]

**15** Like sheep they are appointed for Sheol;[^13] death will be their shepherd. The upright will rule over them in the morning, and their form[^14] will waste away in Sheol, far from its lofty dwelling.[^15]

**16** But God will redeem my life from the power of Sheol,[^16] for he will take me.[^17] Selah.

**17** Do not be afraid when someone grows rich, when the glory of his house increases;

**18** for when he dies he will take nothing with him; his glory will not go down after him.

**19** Though in his lifetime he congratulates himself,[^18] and people praise you when you do well for yourself,

**20** he will go to the generation of his ancestors,[^19] who will never see the light.

**21** A human being in his splendor, without understanding, is like the animals that perish.[^20]

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of the sons of Korah*: or "for the sons of Korah", a family of Levites who served as temple singers. In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–21 are 49:1–20 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "both sons of *adam* and sons of *ish*", two words for "man", perhaps common people and people of rank.
[^3]: Hebrew *mashal*, a saying, parable or poem.
[^4]: Literally "the iniquity of my heels", probably those who follow at my heels. Or "my deceivers", a word from the same root (compare Genesis 27:36).
[^5]: Literally "A brother, a man surely cannot ransom".
[^6]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Also in verses 16 and 19.
[^7]: Literally "and it ceases forever". Or "and one must give it up forever".
[^8]: Hebrew *shachat*, "pit", a name for the grave and the realm of the dead.
[^9]: Literally "Their inward part: their houses forever". The Septuagint reads "Their graves are their homes forever", reading *qibram*, "their grave", for *qirbam*, "their inward part", with two letters reversed.
[^10]: Literally "does not spend the night". Verse 21, the refrain, reads "without understanding" (*velo yavin*) where this verse has "does not endure" (*bal yalin*).
[^11]: The Hebrew of this verse is difficult. Literally "This is their way, folly is theirs, and after them with their mouth they are pleased".
[^12]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning. Also in verse 16.
[^13]: Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead. Also in verse 16.
[^14]: The Hebrew text is written *tsiram*, perhaps "their image", but read *tsuram*, "their form".
[^15]: The Hebrew of the second half of this verse is difficult. Literally "and their form is for Sheol to wear out, from a dwelling for it".
[^16]: Literally "from the hand of Sheol".
[^17]: The same verb is used when God "took" Enoch (Genesis 5:24) and Elijah (2 Kings 2:3, 5). Compare 73:24.
[^18]: Literally "he blesses his soul (*nephesh*)".
[^19]: Or "you will go to the generation of your ancestors", or "it (his soul) will go".
[^20]: Compare verse 13.
