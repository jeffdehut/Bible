**1** For the director. A psalm of David.[^1]

**2** In you, Yahweh,[^2] I take refuge; never let me be put to shame; in your righteousness rescue me.[^3]

**3** Turn your ear to me; rescue me quickly. Be a rock of refuge for me, a strong fortress to save me.

**4** For you are my rock and my fortress; for the sake of your name lead me and guide me.

**5** Bring me out of the net that they have hidden for me, for you are my refuge.

**6** Into your hand I commit my spirit;[^4] you have redeemed me, Yahweh, faithful God.

**7** I hate[^5] those who cling to worthless idols, but I trust in Yahweh.

**8** I will rejoice and be glad in your faithful love,[^6] because you have seen my affliction; you have known the distress of my soul.[^7]

**9** You have not handed me over to the enemy; you have set my feet in a wide place.

**10** Be gracious to me, Yahweh, for I am in distress; my eye wastes away with grief, my soul and my body too.[^8]

**11** For my life is spent with sorrow, and my years with groaning. My strength fails because of my guilt,[^9] and my bones waste away.

**12** Because of all my adversaries I have become a disgrace, especially to my neighbors, and a dread to my acquaintances; those who see me in the street flee from me.

**13** I am forgotten like a dead man, out of mind; I have become like a broken pot.

**14** For I hear the whispering of many, terror on every side,[^10] as they plot together against me; they scheme to take my life.

**15** But I trust in you, Yahweh; I say, "You are my God."

**16** My times are in your hand; rescue me from the hand of my enemies and from those who pursue me.

**17** Let your face shine on your servant; save me in your faithful love.

**18** Yahweh, do not let me be put to shame, for I call on you. Let the wicked be put to shame; let them be silent in Sheol.[^11]

**19** Let lying lips be silenced, which speak arrogantly against the righteous with pride and contempt.

**20** How great is your goodness, which you have stored up for those who fear you, which you have shown to those who take refuge in you, in the sight of all people!

**21** You hide them in the shelter of your presence from human plots;[^12] you keep them safe in a shelter from accusing tongues.[^13]

**22** Blessed be Yahweh, for he has shown me the wonder of his faithful love in a city under siege.[^14]

**23** As for me, I said in my alarm, "I am cut off from your sight." But you heard the sound of my pleas for mercy when I cried to you for help.

**24** Love Yahweh, all you his faithful ones![^15] Yahweh protects the loyal, but repays in full the one who acts with pride.

**25** Be strong, and let your heart take courage, all you who hope in Yahweh.

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–25 are 31:1–24 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Verses 2–4 appear again, with small differences, in 71:1–3.
[^4]: Hebrew *ruach*: spirit, breath. Jesus speaks the first line of this verse on the cross in Luke 23:46.
[^5]: Some Hebrew manuscripts and the Septuagint read "You hate".
[^6]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^7]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self.
[^8]: Literally "my soul (*nephesh*) and my belly".
[^9]: The Septuagint reads "in poverty", from a Hebrew word that looks similar.
[^10]: The same phrase occurs in Jeremiah 6:25; 20:3, 10; 46:5; 49:29.
[^11]: Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead.
[^12]: Literally "from the plots of man". The Hebrew word for "plots" is found only here.
[^13]: Literally "from the strife of tongues".
[^14]: Or "a fortified city".
[^15]: Hebrew *chasidim*, "faithful, loyal ones", related to *chesed*, "faithful love".
