**1** Praise the LORD,[^1] all nations! Extol him, all peoples![^2]

**2** For great is his faithful love[^3] toward us,[^4] and the faithfulness of the LORD endures forever. Praise the LORD![^5]

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. Psalm 117 is the shortest psalm.
[^2]: Romans 15:11 quotes this verse.
[^3]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^4]: Literally "is mighty over us".
[^5]: Hebrew *hallelu-yah*, "praise Yah", the source of the word "Hallelujah". *Yah* is a short form of the name YHWH.
