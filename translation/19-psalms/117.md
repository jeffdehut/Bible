**1** Praise Yahweh,[^1] all nations! Extol him, all peoples![^2]

**2** For great is his faithful love[^3] toward us,[^4] and the faithfulness of Yahweh endures forever. Praise Yah![^5]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. Psalm 117 is the shortest psalm.
[^2]: Romans 15:11 quotes this verse.
[^3]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^4]: Literally "is mighty over us".
[^5]: Hebrew *hallelu-yah*, "praise Yah", traditionally "Hallelujah". *Yah* is a short form of the name Yahweh.
