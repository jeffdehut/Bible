**1** In you, LORD,[^1] I take refuge; let me never be put to shame.[^2]

**2** In your righteousness rescue me and deliver me; turn your ear to me and save me.

**3** Be a rock of refuge for me,[^3] where I can always come; you have given the command to save me, for you are my rock and my fortress.

**4** My God, rescue me from the hand of the wicked, from the grasp of the unjust and the ruthless.

**5** For you are my hope, Lord GOD, my trust from my youth.

**6** On you I have leaned from birth;[^4] you are the one who brought me out[^5] from my mother's womb. My praise is always of you.

**7** I have become like a portent to many, but you are my strong refuge.

**8** My mouth is filled with your praise, with your splendor all day long.

**9** Do not cast me off in the time of old age; do not abandon me when my strength fails.

**10** For my enemies speak against me; those who watch for my life[^6] plot together,

**11** saying, "God has abandoned him; pursue him and seize him, for there is no one to rescue him."

**12** God, do not be far from me; my God, hurry to help me!

**13** Let my accusers[^7] be put to shame and consumed; let those who seek my harm be covered with scorn and disgrace.

**14** But as for me, I will always hope, and I will add to all your praise.

**15** My mouth will tell of your righteousness, of your salvation all day long, though I do not know their number.[^8]

**16** I will come with the mighty deeds of the Lord GOD; I will make mention of your righteousness, yours alone.

**17** God, you have taught me from my youth, and to this day I declare your wonderful deeds.

**18** So even to old age and gray hairs, God, do not abandon me, until I declare your strength[^9] to the next generation, your might to all who are to come.

**19** Your righteousness, God, reaches to the heights, you who have done great things. God, who is like you?

**20** You who have made me see[^10] many troubles and calamities will give me life again; from the depths of the earth you will bring me up again.

**21** You will increase my greatness, and turn and comfort me.

**22** I too will praise you with the harp for your faithfulness, my God; I will sing praises to you with the lyre, Holy One of Israel.

**23** My lips will shout for joy when I sing praises to you, and so will my soul, which you have redeemed.

**24** My tongue too will speak of your righteousness all day long, for those who seek my harm have been put to shame and disgraced.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Psalm 71 has no title. Verses 1–3 are close to 31:2–4 (31:1–3 in most English Bibles).
[^3]: Literally "a rock of dwelling". Psalm 31:3 (31:2 in most English Bibles) has "a rock of refuge (*ma'oz*), a house of fortresses".
[^4]: Literally "from the belly".
[^5]: Hebrew *gozi*, found only here; the meaning is uncertain, perhaps "the one who drew me out" or "my strength".
[^6]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Also in verses 13 and 23.
[^7]: Literally "the accusers of my soul (*nephesh*)". The word for "accusers" comes from the same root as *satan*, "adversary, accuser".
[^8]: Or "though I do not know how to count them".
[^9]: Literally "your arm".
[^10]: The Hebrew text is written "us" but read "me", both here and in "will give me life again".
