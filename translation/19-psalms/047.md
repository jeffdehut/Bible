**1** For the director. Of the sons of Korah. A psalm.[^1]

**2** Clap your hands, all you peoples; shout to God with loud songs of joy.

**3** For the LORD,[^2] the Most High,[^3] is awesome, a great King over all the earth.

**4** He subdues peoples under us, and nations under our feet.

**5** He chose our inheritance for us, the pride of Jacob, whom he loves. Selah.[^4]

**6** God has gone up with a shout, the LORD with the sound of the ram's horn.[^5]

**7** Sing praises to God, sing praises; sing praises to our King, sing praises.

**8** For God is the King of all the earth; sing praises with a skillful psalm.[^6]

**9** God reigns over the nations; God sits on his holy throne.

**10** The nobles of the peoples have gathered as the people of the God of Abraham.[^7] For the shields of the earth[^8] belong to God; he is highly exalted.

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of the sons of Korah*: or "for the sons of Korah", a family of Levites who served as temple singers. In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–10 are 47:1–9 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *Elyon*, a title of God.
[^4]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning.
[^5]: Hebrew *shofar*.
[^6]: Hebrew *maskil*, the term found in the titles of some psalms (see 42:1).
[^7]: The Septuagint reads "with the God of Abraham".
[^8]: That is, the rulers who protect the earth's peoples.
