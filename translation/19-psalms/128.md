**1** A song of ascents.[^1] Happy[^2] is everyone who fears Yahweh,[^3] who walks in his ways.

**2** You will eat the fruit of your labor;[^4] you will be happy, and it will go well with you.

**3** Your wife will be like a fruitful vine within your house,[^5] your children[^6] like olive shoots around your table.

**4** Look, this is how the man who fears Yahweh will be blessed.

**5** May Yahweh bless you from Zion; may you see the prosperity of Jerusalem all the days of your life.

**6** May you see your children's children. Peace be on Israel!

[^1]: *A song of ascents*: Hebrew *shir ha-ma'alot* (see the note on 120:1). In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ashrei*: happy, fortunate; traditionally "blessed". Also in verse 2.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Literally "the labor of your hands".
[^5]: Literally "in the innermost parts of your house".
[^6]: Literally "your sons". Also in verse 6.
