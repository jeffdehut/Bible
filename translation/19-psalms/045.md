**1** For the director. According to "Lilies".[^1] Of the sons of Korah. A maskil. A love song.[^2]

**2** My heart overflows with a good theme;[^3] I speak my verses for the king;[^4] my tongue is the pen of a skillful scribe.

**3** You are the most handsome of all human beings; grace has been poured on your lips; so God has blessed you forever.

**4** Strap your sword on your thigh, mighty warrior, in your splendor and your majesty.

**5** In your majesty ride on to victory[^5] for the cause of truth and humility and righteousness;[^6] let your right hand teach you awesome deeds.

**6** Your arrows are sharp in the hearts of the king's enemies; peoples fall beneath you.[^7]

**7** Your throne, God, is forever and ever;[^8] the scepter of your kingdom is a scepter of justice.[^9]

**8** You love righteousness and hate wickedness; so God, your God, has anointed you with the oil of gladness above your companions.[^10]

**9** All your robes are fragrant with myrrh and aloes and cassia; from ivory palaces the music of strings[^11] makes you glad.

**10** Daughters of kings are among your honored women; at your right hand stands the queen[^12] in gold of Ophir.[^13]

**11** Listen, daughter, consider and turn your ear: forget your people and your father's house.

**12** Then the king will desire your beauty. He is your lord, so bow down to him.

**13** The daughter of Tyre[^14] comes with a gift; the richest of the people seek your favor.

**14** The king's daughter is all glorious within;[^15] her clothing is woven with gold.

**15** In embroidered robes she is led to the king; the young women, her companions, follow her; they are brought to you.

**16** They are led in with gladness and rejoicing; they enter the palace of the king.

**17** Your sons will take the place of your ancestors; you will make them princes throughout the land.[^16]

**18** I will make your name remembered in every generation; so the peoples will praise you forever and ever.

[^1]: Hebrew *al-shoshannim*, "according to lilies", probably the name of a tune.
[^2]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of the sons of Korah*: or "for the sons of Korah", a family of Levites who served as temple singers. *Maskil*: a term of uncertain meaning, perhaps "a skillful psalm" or "a psalm of instruction". *A love song*: literally "a song of loves", perhaps "a wedding song". The psalm celebrates a king's wedding. In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–18 are 45:1–17 in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "a good word".
[^4]: Literally "I say my works to the king".
[^5]: Literally "succeed, ride". The verse begins "And your majesty", repeating the last word of verse 4.
[^6]: Literally "for the word of truth, and humility of righteousness". The Hebrew is compressed.
[^7]: The Hebrew order is "Your arrows are sharp, peoples fall beneath you, in the heart of the king's enemies".
[^8]: The Hebrew *kis'akha elohim* most naturally means "Your throne, God", addressing the king as *elohim*, "God". No other Israelite king is called this, though compare Isaiah 9:5 (9:6 in most English Bibles), where the royal child is called "Mighty God" (*el gibbor*). Other possible readings are "Your throne is God's throne forever" (compare 1 Chronicles 29:23, "Solomon sat on the throne of Yahweh as king") and "Your throne is like God's, forever". Verse 8 distinguishes the king from "God, your God". The Septuagint reads "Your throne, O God, is forever and ever", and Hebrews 1:8–9 quotes verses 7–8 in that form, applying them to the Son.
[^9]: Or "of uprightness".
[^10]: Or "so, God, your God has anointed you", continuing to address the king. Hebrew *meshachakha*, "has anointed you", from the same root as *mashiach*, "anointed one".
[^11]: Hebrew *minni*, probably "strings" (compare 150:4).
[^12]: Hebrew *shegal*, the king's consort.
[^13]: A land famous for its fine gold (1 Kings 9:28).
[^14]: That is, the people of Tyre, or perhaps a princess of Tyre.
[^15]: That is, inside the palace, or in her chamber.
[^16]: Or "in all the earth".
