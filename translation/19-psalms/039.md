**1** For the director. For Jeduthun.[^1] A psalm of David.[^2]

**2** I said, "I will guard my ways, so that I do not sin with my tongue; I will keep a muzzle on my mouth as long as the wicked are in my presence."

**3** I was silent and still; I kept quiet, even from good,[^3] and my pain grew worse.

**4** My heart grew hot within me; as I mused, a fire burned. Then I spoke with my tongue:

**5** "Yahweh,[^4] let me know my end, and what is the measure of my days; let me know how fleeting I am.

**6** See, you have made my days a few handbreadths,[^5] and my lifetime is as nothing before you. Surely every human being, however firmly he stands, is only a breath.[^6] Selah.[^7]

**7** Surely everyone goes about like a shadow;[^8] surely they are in turmoil for nothing. He heaps up wealth and does not know who will gather it.

**8** And now, Lord, what do I wait for? My hope is in you.

**9** Rescue me from all my offenses; do not make me the scorn of fools.

**10** I am silent; I do not open my mouth, for it is you who have done this.

**11** Remove your blow from me; I am worn out by the hostility of your hand.

**12** When you discipline someone with rebukes for guilt, you consume like a moth what is dear to him. Surely every human being is only a breath. Selah.

**13** Hear my prayer, Yahweh, and listen to my cry for help; do not be silent at my tears. For I am a foreigner with you, a temporary resident,[^9] like all my ancestors.

**14** Look away from me, so that I may smile again, before I go away and am no more."

[^1]: One of David's chief musicians (1 Chronicles 16:41; 25:1). The Hebrew text is written "Jedithun" but read "Jeduthun".
[^2]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–14 are 39:1–13 in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Or "to no avail".
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^5]: A handbreadth was about 3 inches (7.5 cm), the width of four fingers.
[^6]: Hebrew *hevel*, "breath, vapor", the word used throughout Ecclesiastes ("Vapor of vapors", Ecclesiastes 1:2). Also in verses 7 and 12.
[^7]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning. Also in verse 12.
[^8]: Literally "as an image".
[^9]: Hebrew *ger* and *toshav*, a foreigner living in the land and a temporary resident, without the rights of a citizen (compare Leviticus 25:23; 1 Chronicles 29:15).
