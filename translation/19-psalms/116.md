**1** I love Yahweh,[^1] for he hears my voice, my pleas for mercy.[^2]

**2** Because he has turned his ear to me, I will call on him as long as I live.[^3]

**3** The cords of death surrounded me; the distress of Sheol[^4] found me; I found trouble and sorrow.

**4** Then I called on the name of Yahweh: "Please, Yahweh, rescue my life!"[^5]

**5** Yahweh is gracious and righteous; our God is compassionate.

**6** Yahweh protects the simple; I was brought low, and he saved me.

**7** Return to your rest, my soul,[^6] for Yahweh has dealt generously with you.

**8** For you have rescued my life from death, my eyes from tears, my feet from stumbling.

**9** I will walk before Yahweh in the land of the living.[^7]

**10** I kept faith, even when I said,[^8] "I am greatly afflicted."

**11** I said in my alarm, "Everyone is a liar."[^9]

**12** What can I give back to Yahweh for all his goodness to me?

**13** I will lift up the cup of salvation[^10] and call on the name of Yahweh.

**14** I will fulfill my vows to Yahweh in the presence of all his people.

**15** Precious in the eyes of Yahweh is the death of his faithful ones.[^11]

**16** Please, Yahweh, truly I am your servant; I am your servant, the son of your female servant.[^12] You have loosed my bonds.

**17** To you I will offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving,[^13] and call on the name of Yahweh.

**18** I will fulfill my vows to Yahweh in the presence of all his people,

**19** in the courts of the house of Yahweh, in your midst, Jerusalem. Praise Yah![^14]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. *I love Yahweh*: literally "I love, for Yahweh hears". The Septuagint divides this psalm into two, verses 1–9 and verses 10–19 (see the note on 114:1).
[^2]: Literally "my voice, my supplications".
[^3]: Literally "in my days".
[^4]: Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead. *The distress*: or "the straits", "the pangs". Compare 18:5–6 (18:4–5 in English).
[^5]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Also in verses 7 and 8.
[^6]: Literally "to your resting places". The psalmist speaks to his own soul (*nephesh*), as in 42:6 (42:5 in English).
[^7]: Literally "in the lands of the living". Compare 56:14 (56:13 in English).
[^8]: Literally "I believed, for I speak". Or "I believed, even when I spoke". The Septuagint reads "I believed, therefore I spoke", the wording quoted in 2 Corinthians 4:13.
[^9]: Literally "All humankind (*adam*) is a liar". Compare Romans 3:4.
[^10]: Literally "the cup of salvations", perhaps a drink offering poured out, or a cup raised in thanksgiving for being rescued.
[^11]: Hebrew *chasidim*, "the faithful, the loyal", related to *chesed*, "faithful love". *Precious*: that is, costly: their death is no small matter to him.
[^12]: That is, a servant born in the household, and so bound to it for life (compare 86:16).
[^13]: Hebrew *todah*: a sacrifice given in thanksgiving (Leviticus 7:12).
[^14]: Hebrew *hallelu-yah*, "praise Yah", the source of the word "Hallelujah". *Yah* is a short form of the name Yahweh.
