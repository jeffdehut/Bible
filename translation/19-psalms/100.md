**1** A psalm for thanksgiving.[^1] Shout aloud to Yahweh,[^2] all the earth!

**2** Serve Yahweh with gladness; come before him with songs of joy.

**3** Know that Yahweh is God. It is he who made us, and we are his;[^3] we are his people, the sheep of his pasture.

**4** Enter his gates with thanksgiving, and his courts with praise; give thanks to him, bless his name.

**5** For Yahweh is good; his faithful love[^4] endures forever, and his faithfulness to all generations.

[^1]: Or "for the thank offering". Hebrew *todah*, "thanksgiving", also a sacrifice given in thanksgiving (Leviticus 7:12; compare 50:14). Also in verse 4. In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: The Hebrew text is written "and not we ourselves" but read "and we are his". The two words sound the same.
[^4]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. *His faithful love endures forever* is a refrain of praise found often in the Psalms and elsewhere (106:1; 107:1; 118:1; 136; 1 Chronicles 16:34).
