**1** For the director. Of the servant of Yahweh,[^1] of David, who spoke to Yahweh the words of this song on the day when Yahweh rescued him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul.[^2]

**2** He said: "I love you,[^3] Yahweh, my strength.

**3** Yahweh is my rock, my fortress and my deliverer; my God, my rock, in whom I take refuge; my shield and the horn[^4] of my salvation, my stronghold.

**4** I call on Yahweh, who is worthy of praise, and I am saved from my enemies.

**5** The cords of death[^5] surrounded me; the torrents of destruction[^6] terrified me.

**6** The cords of Sheol[^7] coiled around me; the snares of death confronted me.

**7** In my distress I called on Yahweh; to my God I cried for help. From his temple he heard my voice, and my cry for help before him reached his ears.

**8** Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations of the mountains quaked; they shook because he was angry.

**9** Smoke went up from his nostrils, and devouring fire from his mouth; coals blazed out from him.

**10** He bent the heavens and came down, with thick darkness under his feet.

**11** He rode on a cherub[^8] and flew; he swooped[^9] on the wings of the wind.

**12** He made darkness his hiding place, his shelter around him, dark waters, thick clouds of the skies.[^10]

**13** Out of the brightness before him his clouds passed by, with hail and coals of fire.[^11]

**14** Yahweh thundered in the heavens; the Most High[^12] uttered his voice, with hail and coals of fire.[^13]

**15** He sent out his arrows and scattered them, many lightning bolts, and threw them into confusion.

**16** The channels of the waters were seen; the foundations of the world were laid bare at your rebuke, Yahweh, at the blast of the breath of your nostrils.

**17** He reached down from on high and took me; he drew me out of many waters.

**18** He rescued me from my strong enemy, from those who hated me, for they were too strong for me.

**19** They confronted me on the day of my disaster, but Yahweh was my support.

**20** He brought me out into a wide place; he rescued me because he delighted in me.

**21** Yahweh rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands he repaid me.

**22** For I have kept the ways of Yahweh, and I have not done evil by turning from my God.

**23** For all his rulings are before me, and his decrees I have not put away from me.

**24** I was blameless before him, and I kept myself from my guilt.[^14]

**25** So Yahweh has repaid me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in his sight.

**26** With the faithful you show yourself faithful;[^15] with the blameless man you show yourself blameless.

**27** With the pure you show yourself pure, but with the crooked you show yourself shrewd.

**28** For you save a humble people,[^16] but haughty eyes you bring low.

**29** For you light my lamp; Yahweh my God lights up my darkness.

**30** For with you I can charge against a troop;[^17] with my God I can leap over a wall.

**31** This God, his way is perfect; the word of Yahweh is refined.[^18] He is a shield to all who take refuge in him.

**32** For who is God besides Yahweh? And who is a rock except our God?

**33** This God is the one who arms me with strength, and makes my way blameless.

**34** He makes my feet like the feet of a deer, and sets me on my heights.

**35** He trains my hands for war, so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze.

**36** You have given me the shield of your salvation; your right hand supports me, and your gentleness[^19] has made me great.

**37** You make a wide place for my steps beneath me, and my ankles have not slipped.

**38** I pursued my enemies and overtook them; I did not turn back until they were finished off.

**39** I crushed them, so that they could not rise; they fell beneath my feet.

**40** You armed me with strength for battle; you made those who rose against me bow down beneath me.

**41** You made my enemies turn their backs to me;[^20] those who hated me, I destroyed them.

**42** They cried for help, but there was no one to save them; they cried to Yahweh, but he did not answer them.

**43** I beat them as fine as dust before the wind; I poured them out like the mud of the streets.[^21]

**44** You rescued me from the strife of the people; you made me the head of nations.[^22] A people I did not know now serve me.

**45** As soon as they hear of me, they obey me; foreigners come cringing to me.[^23]

**46** Foreigners lose heart, and they come trembling out of their strongholds.

**47** Yahweh lives! Blessed be my rock! Exalted be the God of my salvation,

**48** the God who grants me vengeance and subdues peoples under me,

**49** who rescues me from my enemies. You lift me high above those who rise against me; you rescue me from violent men.

**50** So I will praise you, Yahweh, among the nations, and sing praises to your name.[^24]

**51** He gives great victories to his king;[^25] he shows faithful love[^26] to his anointed,[^27] to David and his offspring forever."

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–51 are 18:1–50 in most English Bibles. This song appears again, with small differences, as 2 Samuel 22; from verse 4 on, the verse numbers of the two chapters match. The notes below give some of the differences.
[^3]: Hebrew *erchamkha*, from the root used of tender, compassionate love. 2 Samuel 22 does not have this line.
[^4]: A symbol of strength, like the horns of a wild ox. 2 Samuel 22:3 adds "and my refuge; my savior, you save me from violence".
[^5]: 2 Samuel 22:5 has "the breakers of death".
[^6]: Hebrew *beliyya'al*, "worthlessness", here perhaps destruction or the realm of death.
[^7]: Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead.
[^8]: Hebrew *keruv*, one of the winged heavenly beings.
[^9]: 2 Samuel 22:11 has "he was seen", a word that differs by one similar letter.
[^10]: 2 Samuel 22:12 is shorter and has a different word for "dark".
[^11]: 2 Samuel 22:13 has "Out of the brightness before him coals of fire blazed."
[^12]: Hebrew *Elyon*, a title of God.
[^13]: These words repeat the end of verse 13. 2 Samuel 22:14 does not have them.
[^14]: That is, from doing wrong.
[^15]: Hebrew *chasid*, "faithful, loyal", and the verb from the same root, related to *chesed*, "faithful love".
[^16]: Or "an afflicted people". Hebrew *ani*, "afflicted, lowly".
[^17]: Or "I can run through a barricade".
[^18]: That is, tested and pure, like metal refined in a furnace.
[^19]: Hebrew *anvatkha*, "your humility", that is, your stooping down to help. 2 Samuel 22:36 has *anotkha*, perhaps "your answering" or "your help", and does not have "your right hand supports me".
[^20]: Literally "My enemies, you gave me their neck."
[^21]: Literally "I emptied them out". 2 Samuel 22:43 has "like the dust of the earth" and "I crushed them and trampled them".
[^22]: 2 Samuel 22:44 has "from the strife of my people; you kept me as the head of nations".
[^23]: Or "pretend to obey me".
[^24]: Romans 15:9 quotes this verse.
[^25]: The Hebrew text is written "He is a tower of salvation for his king" but read "He gives great victories to his king". In 2 Samuel 22:51 it is the other way around.
[^26]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^27]: Hebrew *meshicho*, "his anointed one", the king anointed at Yahweh's command.
