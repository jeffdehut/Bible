**1** Shout for joy in the LORD,[^1] you righteous! Praise is fitting for the upright.

**2** Give thanks to the LORD with the lyre; make music to him with a ten-stringed harp.[^2]

**3** Sing to him a new song; play skillfully, with shouts of joy.

**4** For the word of the LORD is upright, and all his work is done in faithfulness.

**5** He loves righteousness and justice; the earth is full of the faithful love[^3] of the LORD.

**6** By the word of the LORD the heavens were made, and all their host[^4] by the breath of his mouth.

**7** He gathers the waters of the sea as in a heap;[^5] he puts the deep waters in storehouses.

**8** Let all the earth fear the LORD; let all who live in the world stand in awe of him.

**9** For he spoke, and it came to be; he commanded, and it stood firm.

**10** The LORD frustrates the plans of the nations; he thwarts the purposes of the peoples.

**11** The plans of the LORD stand forever, the purposes of his heart through all generations.

**12** Happy[^6] is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people he has chosen as his inheritance.

**13** From heaven the LORD looks down; he sees all human beings.

**14** From the place where he sits enthroned he watches all who live on the earth.

**15** He forms the hearts of them all; he understands all their deeds.

**16** A king is not saved by a great army; a warrior is not rescued by great strength.

**17** A horse is a false hope for victory;[^7] for all its great strength it cannot save.

**18** See, the eye of the LORD is on those who fear him, on those who hope in his faithful love,

**19** to rescue them[^8] from death and keep them alive in famine.

**20** We wait[^9] for the LORD; he is our help and our shield.

**21** For in him our heart rejoices, because we trust in his holy name.

**22** May your faithful love be on us, LORD, as we hope in you.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "with a harp of ten". The *kinnor* (lyre) and *nevel* (harp) were both stringed instruments.
[^3]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^4]: That is, the sun, moon and stars.
[^5]: The Septuagint reads "as in a wineskin", from the same consonants with different vowels.
[^6]: Hebrew *ashrei*: happy, fortunate; traditionally "blessed".
[^7]: Or "salvation". Literally "The horse is a lie for victory".
[^8]: Literally "their soul (*nephesh*)".
[^9]: Literally "Our soul (*nephesh*) waits".
