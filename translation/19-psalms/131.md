**1** A song of ascents. Of David.[^1] Yahweh,[^2] my heart is not proud, and my eyes are not haughty. I do not concern myself[^3] with great matters, or with things too wonderful for me.

**2** Instead,[^4] I have calmed and quieted my soul,[^5] like a weaned child with its mother; my soul within me is like a weaned child.

**3** Israel, put your hope in Yahweh, from now on and forever.

[^1]: *A song of ascents*: Hebrew *shir ha-ma'alot* (see the note on 120:1). *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "I do not walk".
[^4]: Literally "If not", a form used in oaths: "Surely".
[^5]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Also in the next line. A weaned child no longer frets for the breast, but rests content on its mother.
