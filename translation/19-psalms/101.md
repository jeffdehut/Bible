**1** Of David. A psalm.[^1] I will sing of faithful love[^2] and justice; to you, Yahweh,[^3] I will sing praise.

**2** I will pay attention to the blameless way.[^4] When will you come to me? I will walk with integrity of heart within my house.

**3** I will not set anything worthless[^5] before my eyes. I hate the deeds of those who turn aside;[^6] they will not cling to me.

**4** A crooked heart will be far from me; I will have nothing to do with evil.[^7]

**5** Whoever slanders his neighbor in secret, him I will silence;[^8] whoever has haughty eyes and a proud heart, him I will not tolerate.

**6** My eyes will be on the faithful in the land, so that they may live with me; whoever walks in the blameless way will serve me.

**7** No one who practices deceit will live in my house; no one who tells lies will stand firm in my sight.

**8** Morning by morning[^9] I will silence all the wicked in the land, cutting off all evildoers from the city of Yahweh.

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles. The speaker is a king, who promises to rule justly.
[^2]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Or "I will act wisely in the blameless way".
[^5]: Literally "a thing of *beliyya'al*", "worthlessness".
[^6]: Hebrew *setim*, a rare word, "those who swerve", or "faithlessness".
[^7]: Literally "evil I will not know".
[^8]: Or "destroy". Also in verse 8.
[^9]: The king heard cases and gave judgment in the morning (compare 2 Samuel 15:2; Jeremiah 21:12).
