**1** For the director. According to "The Silent Dove of Far-off Places".[^1] Of David. A miktam,[^2] when the Philistines seized him in Gath.[^3]

**2** Be gracious to me, God, for a mere mortal hounds me; all day long an attacker oppresses me.

**3** Those who lie in wait for me hound me all day long, for many fight against me proudly.[^4]

**4** When I am afraid, I put my trust in you.

**5** In God, whose word I praise, in God I trust; I will not be afraid. What can flesh do to me?

**6** All day long they twist my words;[^5] all their thoughts are against me for evil.

**7** They gather together, they lie in hiding; they watch my steps,[^6] as they wait to take my life.[^7]

**8** Because of their wickedness, will they escape?[^8] In anger bring down the peoples, God.

**9** You have counted my wanderings;[^9] put my tears in your bottle.[^10] Are they not in your book?

**10** Then my enemies will turn back on the day I call. This I know: God is for me.

**11** In God, whose word I praise, in Yahweh,[^11] whose word I praise,

**12** in God I trust; I will not be afraid. What can a human being do to me?

**13** I am bound by vows to you, God;[^12] I will offer thanksgiving offerings to you.

**14** For you have rescued my life from death, and my feet from stumbling,[^13] so that I may walk before God in the light of life.[^14]

[^1]: Hebrew *al-yonat elem rechoqim*, probably the name of a tune: "the dove of silence of far-off places". The same consonants can be read "the dove of the distant terebinths".
[^2]: Hebrew *miktam*, a term of uncertain meaning (see the note on the title of Psalm 16).
[^3]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". The events are told in 1 Samuel 21:11–16 (21:10–15 in most English Bibles). In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–14 are 56:1–13 in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Hebrew *marom*, "on high": proudly, or perhaps addressed to God, "Most High".
[^5]: Or "they hurt my cause".
[^6]: Literally "my heels".
[^7]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Also in verse 14.
[^8]: The Hebrew of this line is difficult. Literally "On account of wickedness, deliver for them". Or "Because of their wickedness, cast them out".
[^9]: Or "my lament". Hebrew *nodi*, which sounds like *nod*, "bottle", in the next line.
[^10]: A skin bottle, for water, milk or wine.
[^11]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^12]: Literally "Upon me, God, are your vows", the vows I made to you.
[^13]: Literally "have you not rescued my feet from stumbling?"
[^14]: Or "in the light of the living".
