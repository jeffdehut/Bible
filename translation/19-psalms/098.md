**1** A psalm.[^1] Sing to the LORD[^2] a new song, for he has done wonders; his right hand and his holy arm have won him victory.[^3]

**2** The LORD has made his salvation known; in the sight of the nations he has revealed his righteousness.[^4]

**3** He has remembered his faithful love[^5] and his faithfulness to the house of Israel; all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.

**4** Shout aloud to the LORD, all the earth; break out in songs of joy and sing praises.

**5** Sing praises to the LORD with the lyre, with the lyre and the sound of song.

**6** With trumpets and the sound of the ram's horn,[^6] shout aloud before the King, the LORD.[^7]

**7** Let the sea roar, and all that fills it, the world and those who live in it.

**8** Let the rivers clap their hands; let the mountains sing for joy together

**9** before the LORD, for he comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness, and the peoples with fairness.[^8]

[^1]: In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "have saved for him".
[^4]: Or "his vindication", his justice that sets things right.
[^5]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^6]: Hebrew *shofar*. The trumpets (*chatsotserot*) were made of hammered silver (Numbers 10:2).
[^7]: Psalm 98 is one of the "The LORD is king" psalms (see the note on 93:1).
[^8]: Verse 9 is almost the same as 96:13.
