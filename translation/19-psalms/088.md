**1** A song. A psalm of the sons of Korah. For the director. According to Mahalath Leannoth.[^1] A maskil of Heman the Ezrahite.[^2]

**2** Yahweh,[^3] God of my salvation, by day I cry out, and by night, before you.[^4]

**3** Let my prayer come before you; turn your ear to my cry.

**4** For my soul[^5] is full of troubles, and my life draws near to Sheol.[^6]

**5** I am counted among those who go down to the pit; I have become like a man without strength,[^7]

**6** left alone[^8] among the dead, like the slain who lie in the grave, whom you remember no more, for they are cut off from your hand.

**7** You have put me in the lowest pit, in dark places, in the depths.

**8** Your wrath lies heavy on me, and you have afflicted me with all your breakers. Selah.[^9]

**9** You have taken my friends far from me; you have made me a horror to them. I am shut in, and I cannot get out.

**10** My eye grows dim from affliction. I call on you, Yahweh, every day; I spread out my hands to you.

**11** Do you work wonders for the dead? Do the shades[^10] rise up to praise you? Selah.

**12** Is your faithful love[^11] told in the grave, your faithfulness in Abaddon?[^12]

**13** Are your wonders known in the darkness, or your righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?[^13]

**14** But I cry to you for help, Yahweh; in the morning my prayer comes before you.

**15** Why, Yahweh, do you reject my soul? Why do you hide your face from me?

**16** I have been afflicted and close to death from my youth; I bear your terrors; I am helpless.[^14]

**17** Your burning anger has swept over me; your terrors have destroyed me.

**18** They surround me like water all day long; they close in on me together.

**19** You have taken lover and friend far from me; darkness is my only companion.[^15]

[^1]: Hebrew *al-machalat le'annot*. *Machalat* is a term of uncertain meaning (see the note on the title of Psalm 53). *Le'annot* may mean "for singing", "for responsive singing", or "for affliction".
[^2]: *Of the sons of Korah*: or "for the sons of Korah", a family of Levites who served as temple singers. *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Maskil*: a term of uncertain meaning, perhaps "a skillful psalm" or "a psalm of instruction". *Of Heman*: or "for Heman". A wise man named Heman is mentioned in 1 Kings 5:11 (English 4:31), and Heman was also one of David's chief musicians (1 Chronicles 15:17, 19; 25:1). *Ezrahite* probably means a descendant of Zerah (1 Chronicles 2:6). In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–19 are 88:1–18 in most English Bibles.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Literally "the day I cried out in the night before you". The Hebrew is unusual.
[^5]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Also in verse 15.
[^6]: Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead.
[^7]: Hebrew *eyal*, a word found only here, probably "strength".
[^8]: Literally "free", perhaps meaning released from life, or abandoned.
[^9]: Hebrew *selah*, a musical or liturgical term of uncertain meaning. Also in verse 11.
[^10]: Hebrew *repha'im*, the spirits of the dead (compare Isaiah 14:9).
[^11]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^12]: Hebrew *Abaddon*, "Destruction", a name for the realm of the dead (Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11).
[^13]: That is, the realm of the dead, where the dead are forgotten and forget.
[^14]: Hebrew *afunah*, a word found only here; the meaning is uncertain, perhaps "I am in despair" or "I am numb".
[^15]: Literally "my friends are darkness", or "my friends are in darkness". The psalm ends without any word of hope.
