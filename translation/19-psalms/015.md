**1** A psalm of David.[^1] Yahweh,[^2] who may stay as a guest in your tent? Who may live on your holy mountain?

**2** The one who lives blamelessly[^3] and does what is right, and speaks the truth from his heart;

**3** who does not go around slandering, does no harm to his neighbor, and does not heap insults on those close to him;

**4** in whose eyes a worthless person is despised, but who honors those who fear Yahweh; who keeps his oath even when it hurts, and does not change it;

**5** who does not lend his money at interest, and does not take a bribe against the innocent. Whoever does these things will never be shaken.

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "walks blamelessly".
