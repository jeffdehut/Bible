**1** Happy[^1] is the one[^2] who does not walk by the advice of the wicked, or stand in the way of sinners, or sit in the seat of mockers.

**2** Instead, his delight is in the law[^3] of Yahweh,[^4] and on his law he meditates[^5] day and night.

**3** He is like a tree planted by streams of water, which yields its fruit in its season, and its leaves do not wither. In all that he does, he prospers.

**4** Not so the wicked! They are like chaff that the wind blows away.

**5** So the wicked will not stand[^6] in the judgment, nor sinners in the assembly of the righteous.

**6** For Yahweh knows[^7] the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked will perish.

[^1]: Hebrew *ashrei*: happy, fortunate; traditionally "blessed". It describes a life that goes well, and is a different word from *barukh*, "blessed", used when God blesses or is blessed.
[^2]: Literally "the man".
[^3]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, teaching": here God's teaching as a whole. Also in the next line.
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^5]: Or "murmurs", reading quietly aloud.
[^6]: Or "will not rise".
[^7]: That is, watches over and cares for.
