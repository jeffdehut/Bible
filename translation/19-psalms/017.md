**1** A prayer of David.[^1] Hear, Yahweh,[^2] a just cause; listen to my cry; give ear to my prayer, which comes from lips free of deceit.

**2** Let my vindication come from your presence; let your eyes see what is right.

**3** You have tested my heart; you have examined me by night; you have refined me and found nothing. I have resolved that my mouth will not do wrong.[^3]

**4** As for the deeds of human beings, by the word of your lips I have kept myself from the ways of the violent.

**5** My steps have held to your paths; my feet have not slipped.

**6** I call on you, for you will answer me, God. Turn your ear to me; hear my words.

**7** Show the wonder of your faithful love,[^4] you who save those who take refuge in you from those who rise against them, by your right hand.

**8** Keep me as the pupil of your eye;[^5] hide me in the shadow of your wings

**9** from the wicked who assault me, my deadly enemies[^6] who surround me.

**10** They have closed up their hearts;[^7] with their mouths they speak arrogantly.

**11** They have tracked us down; now they surround us.[^8] They set their eyes to throw us to the ground.

**12** He is like a lion eager to tear its prey, like a young lion lurking in hiding.

**13** Rise up, Yahweh! Confront him; bring him down. Rescue my life[^9] from the wicked with your sword,

**14** from men, by your hand, Yahweh, from men of this world, whose portion is in this life. You fill their belly with what you have stored up; their children have plenty, and they leave what is left to their little ones.[^10]

**15** As for me, in righteousness I will see your face; when I awake, I will be satisfied with your likeness.

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: The Hebrew of this line is uncertain. Or "If I have planned evil, it has not passed my mouth".
[^4]: Hebrew *chasadim*, the plural of *chesed*, "loyal, faithful love": "your acts of faithful love".
[^5]: Literally "the little man, the daughter of the eye", that is, the pupil, which is guarded with great care (compare Deuteronomy 32:10).
[^6]: Literally "my enemies against (my) soul (*nephesh*)".
[^7]: Literally "their fat", perhaps the fat around the heart: they have made themselves unfeeling.
[^8]: The Hebrew text is written "surround me" but read "surround us".
[^9]: Hebrew *nephesh*.
[^10]: The Hebrew of this verse is difficult. It may describe the wicked, whose only reward is in this life, or God's care for his own people: "Your treasured ones, you fill their belly".
