**1** Of David.[^1] Judge me,[^2] Yahweh,[^3] for I have walked in my integrity; I have trusted in Yahweh without wavering.

**2** Test me, Yahweh, and try me; refine my heart and my mind.[^4]

**3** For your faithful love[^5] is before my eyes, and I have walked in your faithfulness.

**4** I do not sit with deceitful people, nor do I go with hypocrites.

**5** I hate the assembly of evildoers, and I will not sit with the wicked.

**6** I wash my hands in innocence, and go around your altar, Yahweh,

**7** making thanksgiving heard aloud, and telling of all your wonderful deeds.

**8** Yahweh, I love the house where you live, the place where your glory dwells.

**9** Do not sweep me away[^6] with sinners, or my life with the bloodthirsty,

**10** in whose hands are evil schemes, and whose right hands are full of bribes.

**11** But I walk in my integrity; redeem me and be gracious to me.

**12** My foot stands on level ground; in the great assemblies I will bless Yahweh.

[^1]: *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^2]: Or "Vindicate me".
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Literally "my kidneys and my heart". The kidneys were thought of as the seat of the emotions and the inner life.
[^5]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^6]: Literally "Do not gather my soul (*nephesh*)".
