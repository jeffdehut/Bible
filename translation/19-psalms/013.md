**1** For the director. A psalm of David.[^1]

**2** How long, Yahweh?[^2] Will you forget me forever? How long will you hide your face from me?

**3** How long must I wrestle with my thoughts,[^3] with sorrow in my heart every day? How long will my enemy be exalted over me?

**4** Look on me and answer me, Yahweh my God. Give light to my eyes, or I will sleep the sleep of death,

**5** or my enemy will say, "I have overcome him," and my adversaries will rejoice when I am shaken.

**6** But I trust in your faithful love;[^4] my heart will rejoice in your salvation. I will sing to Yahweh, for he has been good to me.

[^1]: *For the director*: Hebrew *lamenatseach*, probably the leader of the temple music. *Of David*: or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is verse 1, so verses 2–5 are 13:1–4 in most English Bibles, and verse 6 is divided there into two verses, 13:5–6.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "set counsels in my soul (*nephesh*)".
[^4]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
