**1** Praise Yah![^1] Sing to Yahweh[^2] a new song, his praise in the assembly of the faithful.[^3]

**2** Let Israel rejoice in their Maker; let the people of Zion[^4] be glad in their King.

**3** Let them praise his name with dancing; let them make music to him with tambourine and lyre.

**4** For Yahweh takes pleasure in his people; he crowns the humble with salvation.[^5]

**5** Let the faithful exult in glory; let them sing for joy on their beds.[^6]

**6** Let the high praises of God be in their throats, and a two-edged sword in their hands,

**7** to carry out vengeance on the nations, punishment on the peoples,

**8** to bind their kings with chains, and their nobles with fetters of iron,

**9** to carry out on them the judgment written.[^7] This is the honor of all his faithful ones. Praise Yah!

[^1]: Hebrew *hallelu-yah*, "praise Yah", the source of the word "Hallelujah". *Yah* is a short form of the name Yahweh. Also at the end of verse 9.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *chasidim*, "the faithful, the loyal", related to *chesed*, "faithful love". Also in verses 5 and 9.
[^4]: Literally "the sons of Zion".
[^5]: Or "he adorns the humble with victory".
[^6]: Perhaps the mats or couches on which they lay down to rest, or reclined at a festival meal.
[^7]: Perhaps the judgment on the nations written down in the prophets' oracles, or in God's own decrees.
