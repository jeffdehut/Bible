**1** Then Solomon said, "The LORD[^1] has said that he would live in thick darkness.[^2]

**2** But I have built you a lofty house, a place for you to live in forever."

**3** Then the king turned around and blessed the whole assembly of Israel, while the whole assembly of Israel stood.

**4** He said, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who spoke with his own mouth to my father David, and with his own hands has fulfilled it. He said,

**5** 'Since the day I brought my people out of the land of Egypt, I have not chosen a city from any of the tribes of Israel in which to build a house, so that my name would be there, nor did I choose anyone to be ruler over my people Israel.

**6** But I have chosen Jerusalem for my name to be there, and I have chosen David to be over my people Israel.'

**7** "My father David had it in his heart to build a house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.

**8** But the LORD said to my father David, 'You had it in your heart to build a house for my name. You did well to have this in your heart.

**9** But you are not the one who will build the house. Your son, who will come from your own body,[^3] is the one who will build the house for my name.'

**10** Now the LORD has kept the promise he made. I have succeeded my father David and sit on the throne of Israel, as the LORD promised, and I have built the house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.

**11** There I have put the ark, in which is the covenant[^4] of the LORD that he made with the Israelites."

**12** Then Solomon[^5] stood before the altar of the LORD in front of the whole assembly of Israel and spread out his hands.

**13** For Solomon had made a bronze platform,[^6] five cubits[^7] long, five cubits wide and three cubits high, and had put it in the middle of the court. He stood on it, knelt down in front of the whole assembly of Israel, and spread out his hands toward heaven.

**14** He said, "LORD, God of Israel, there is no God like you in heaven or on earth. You keep your covenant and show faithful love[^8] to your servants who walk before you with all their heart.

**15** You have kept what you promised to your servant, my father David. With your mouth you promised it, and with your hand you have fulfilled it, as it is today.

**16** So now, LORD, God of Israel, keep for your servant, my father David, what you promised him when you said, 'You will never lack a man before me to sit on the throne of Israel,[^9] if only your sons take care how they live,[^10] walking in my law as you have walked before me.'[^11]

**17** So now, LORD, God of Israel, let your word that you spoke to your servant David be confirmed.

**18** "But will God really live with human beings on the earth? Look, the heavens, even the highest heavens,[^12] cannot contain you. How much less this house that I have built!

**19** Yet turn to your servant's prayer and his plea, LORD my God. Listen to the cry and the prayer that your servant is praying before you,

**20** that your eyes may be open toward this house day and night, toward the place where you said you would put your name, so that you will listen to the prayer that your servant prays toward this place.

**21** Listen to the pleas of your servant and of your people Israel when they pray toward this place. Listen from the place where you live, from heaven. Listen and forgive.

**22** "When someone sins against his neighbor and is made to take an oath,[^13] and he comes and swears the oath before your altar in this house,

**23** then listen from heaven and act. Judge your servants: repay the guilty by bringing what they have done on their own heads, and vindicate the innocent,[^14] rewarding them as their righteousness deserves.

**24** "When your people Israel are defeated by an enemy because they have sinned against you, and they turn back and praise your name,[^15] and pray and plead before you in this house,

**25** then listen from heaven and forgive the sin of your people Israel, and bring them back to the land you gave to them and to their ancestors.

**26** "When the heavens are shut up and there is no rain because they have sinned against you, and they pray toward this place and praise your name, and turn from their sin because you afflict them,[^16]

**27** then listen in heaven and forgive the sin of your servants, your people Israel. For you will teach them the good way in which they should walk. And send rain on your land, which you have given to your people as their inheritance.

**28** "When there is famine in the land, or plague, or blight or mildew, or locusts or grasshoppers,[^17] or when their enemies besiege them in the land of their cities,[^18] whatever disaster, whatever sickness there is,

**29** whatever prayer, whatever plea is made by anyone among all your people Israel, each knowing his own affliction and his own pain and spreading out his hands toward this house,

**30** then listen from heaven, the place where you live, and forgive, and give to each person according to all his ways, since you know his heart, for you alone know the hearts of all human beings,

**31** so that they may fear you and walk in your ways all the days they live in the land you gave to our ancestors.

**32** "Likewise the foreigner, who is not of your people Israel but comes from a distant land because of your great name, your strong hand and your outstretched arm, when they come and pray toward this house,

**33** then listen from heaven, the place where you live, and do all that the foreigner asks of you, so that all the peoples of the earth may know your name and fear you, as your people Israel do, and may know that this house I have built bears your name.[^19]

**34** "When your people go out to battle against their enemies, wherever you send them, and they pray to you toward this city you have chosen and the house I have built for your name,

**35** then listen from heaven to their prayer and their plea, and uphold their cause.[^20]

**36** "When they sin against you, for there is no one who does not sin, and you are angry with them and hand them over to an enemy, and their captors carry them off to a land far away or near,

**37** and they come to their senses[^21] in the land where they have been taken captive, and turn and plead with you in the land of their captivity, saying, 'We have sinned, we have done wrong, we have acted wickedly,'

**38** and they turn back to you with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their captivity where they were taken captive, and pray toward their land, which you gave to their ancestors, the city you have chosen and the house I have built for your name,

**39** then listen from heaven, the place where you live, to their prayer and their pleas, and uphold their cause, and forgive your people who have sinned against you.

**40** "Now, my God, let your eyes be open and your ears attentive to the prayer made in this place.[^22]

**41** "Now arise, LORD God, and come to your resting place, you and the ark of your might.[^23] Let your priests, LORD God, be clothed with salvation, and let your faithful ones[^24] rejoice in what is good.

**42** LORD God, do not reject your anointed ones.[^25] Remember the faithful love for David your servant."[^26]

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. Verses 1–39 parallel 1 Kings 8:12–50.
[^2]: Hebrew *arafel*, the thick dark cloud in which God appeared at Sinai (Exodus 20:21).
[^3]: Literally "who will come out from your loins".
[^4]: That is, the stone tablets of the covenant (5:10).
[^5]: Literally "he".
[^6]: Hebrew *kiyyor*, the word for "basin" in 4:6, here a platform, perhaps shaped like a basin. This verse is not in 1 Kings 8.
[^7]: A cubit was about 18 inches (45 cm), so the platform was about 7½ feet square and 4½ feet high (2.3 by 2.3 by 1.4 m).
[^8]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Also in verse 42.
[^9]: Literally "A man will not be cut off for you from before me, sitting on the throne of Israel."
[^10]: Literally "keep their way".
[^11]: 1 Kings 8:25 reads "walking before me".
[^12]: Literally "the heavens and the heaven of heavens".
[^13]: Literally "and he lifts against him a curse to curse him": the accused is made to swear an oath that calls down a curse on himself if he is guilty (Exodus 22:10–11, which is 22:11–12 in most English Bibles; Numbers 5:19–22).
[^14]: Literally "returning to the wicked … and declaring the righteous righteous".
[^15]: Or "acknowledge your name". Also in verse 26.
[^16]: Or "when you answer them". The Hebrew *ta'anem* can come from either verb.
[^17]: Hebrew *arbeh* and *chasil*, two kinds of locusts, or locusts at two stages of growth.
[^18]: Literally "in the land of his gates".
[^19]: Literally "that your name is called over this house", a phrase that marks ownership.
[^20]: Literally "do their justice". Also in verse 39.
[^21]: Literally "bring back to their heart".
[^22]: Literally "to the prayer of this place". 1 Kings 8:52 has a similar request, but verses 40–42 differ from the ending of the prayer in 1 Kings 8:51–53.
[^23]: Verses 41–42 are close to Psalm 132:8–10.
[^24]: Hebrew *chasidim*, a word related to *chesed*, "faithful love".
[^25]: Literally "do not turn back the face of your anointed ones". Psalm 132:10 has the singular, "your anointed one".
[^26]: Literally "the faithful loves of David your servant": either the LORD's faithful love shown to David (Isaiah 55:3), or David's own faithful deeds.
