**1** Solomon began to build the house of the LORD[^1] in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah,[^2] where the LORD had appeared to his father David,[^3] at the place that David had prepared,[^4] on the threshing floor of Ornan[^5] the Jebusite.

**2** He began to build on the second day of the second month,[^6] in the fourth year of his reign.

**3** These are the foundations Solomon laid for building the house of God:[^7] the length, in cubits of the old standard,[^8] was sixty cubits, and the width twenty cubits.

**4** The porch[^9] in front of the house was twenty cubits long, across the width of the house, and 120 high.[^10] He overlaid it on the inside with pure gold.

**5** He paneled the great house[^11] with cypress wood[^12] and overlaid it with fine gold, and he decorated it with palm trees and chains.

**6** He adorned the house with precious stones for beauty, and the gold was gold from Parvaim.[^13]

**7** He overlaid the house with gold: its beams, its thresholds, its walls and its doors. And he carved cherubim[^14] on the walls.

**8** He made the most holy place.[^15] Its length, across the width of the house, was twenty cubits, and its width was twenty cubits. He overlaid it with six hundred talents[^16] of fine gold.

**9** The weight of the nails was fifty shekels[^17] of gold. He also overlaid the upper rooms with gold.

**10** In the most holy place he made two cherubim of sculptured work,[^18] and they overlaid them with gold.

**11** The wings of the cherubim were twenty cubits long in all. One wing of the first cherub, five cubits long, touched the wall of the house, and its other wing, five cubits long, touched the wing of the other cherub.

**12** One wing of the other cherub, five cubits long, touched the wall of the house, and its other wing, five cubits long, was joined to the wing of the first cherub.[^19]

**13** The wings of these cherubim spread out twenty cubits. They stood on their feet, facing the house.[^20]

**14** He made the veil[^21] of blue, purple and crimson yarn and fine linen, and he worked cherubim into it.

**15** In front of the house he made two pillars, thirty-five cubits long,[^22] with a capital[^23] on top of each, five cubits high.

**16** He made chains in the inner sanctuary[^24] and put them on the tops of the pillars. He made a hundred pomegranates and put them on the chains.

**17** He set up the pillars in front of the main hall,[^25] one on the right and one on the left. He named the one on the right Jakin[^26] and the one on the left Boaz.[^27]

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. "The house of the LORD" is the temple. This chapter parallels 1 Kings 6 and 7:15–22, in shorter form.
[^2]: The only other place the name Moriah occurs is Genesis 22:2.
[^3]: Literally "which was seen by David his father". See 1 Chronicles 21:15–22:1.
[^4]: Literally "which he prepared in the place of David".
[^5]: Called Araunah in 2 Samuel 24:16–25.
[^6]: Literally "in the second month, in the second".
[^7]: Literally "These Solomon founded to build the house of God".
[^8]: A cubit was about 18 inches (45 cm), so the house was about 90 feet long and 30 feet wide (27 by 9 m). The "old standard" was probably the older, shorter cubit, as against a longer one of about 21 inches (52 cm) (Ezekiel 40:5).
[^9]: Hebrew *ulam*: an entrance hall in front of the main hall; traditionally "porch" or "vestibule".
[^10]: About 180 feet (54 m). 1 Kings 6:3 does not give the height of the porch.
[^11]: That is, the main hall, the large room in front of the most holy place (1 Kings 6:17).
[^12]: Hebrew *berosh*, a tall conifer: perhaps the cypress or the juniper.
[^13]: A place not otherwise known.
[^14]: Hebrew *keruvim* (singular *keruv*, "cherub"): winged heavenly beings who guard holy places (Genesis 3:24; Exodus 25:18–22).
[^15]: Literally "the house of the holy of holies". Also in verse 10.
[^16]: A talent was about 75 pounds (34 kg), so six hundred talents was about 45,000 pounds (20,400 kg).
[^17]: A shekel was about ⅖ ounce (11.5 grams), so fifty shekels was about 1¼ pounds (575 grams).
[^18]: Hebrew *tsa'tsu'im*, a word found only here, of uncertain meaning.
[^19]: Literally "And the wing of the one cherub … to the wing of the other cherub".
[^20]: Literally "and their faces toward the house", that is, toward the main hall.
[^21]: Hebrew *parokhet*: the hanging that screened the most holy place (Exodus 26:31–33). 1 Kings 6:31–32 speaks of doors of olive wood.
[^22]: 1 Kings 7:15 and Jeremiah 52:21 give the height of each pillar as eighteen cubits.
[^23]: Hebrew *tsefet*, found only here. Elsewhere the capitals are called *koterot* (4:12; 1 Kings 7:16).
[^24]: Hebrew *devir*: the innermost room, the most holy place (1 Kings 6:5, 16). The sense here is uncertain.
[^25]: Hebrew *heikhal*, "palace, temple": here the large main room of the house, in front of the inner sanctuary. Traditionally "the temple" or "the nave".
[^26]: *Jakin* probably means "he establishes".
[^27]: *Boaz* probably means "in him is strength".
