**1** In the thirty-sixth year of Asa's reign,[^1] Baasha king of Israel came up against Judah and fortified[^2] Ramah, to prevent anyone from going out or coming in to Asa king of Judah.

**2** Then Asa brought out silver and gold from the treasuries of the house of Yahweh[^3] and of the king's house and sent them to Ben-hadad king of Aram, who lived in Damascus, saying,

**3** "There is a treaty[^4] between me and you, as there was between my father and your father. Look, I have sent you silver and gold. Go, break your treaty with Baasha king of Israel, so that he will withdraw from me."

**4** Ben-hadad listened to King Asa and sent the commanders of his armies against the cities of Israel. They struck Ijon, Dan, Abel-maim[^5] and all the storehouses in the cities of Naphtali.

**5** When Baasha heard of it, he stopped fortifying Ramah and gave up his work.

**6** Then King Asa took all Judah, and they carried away the stones and timber of Ramah, with which Baasha had been building. With them he fortified Geba and Mizpah.

**7** At that time Hanani the seer came to Asa king of Judah and said to him, "Because you relied on the king of Aram and did not rely on Yahweh your God, the army of the king of Aram has escaped from your hand.

**8** Were not the Cushites and the Libyans a vast army, with very many chariots and horsemen?[^6] Yet because you relied on Yahweh, he handed them over to you.

**9** For the eyes of Yahweh range throughout the whole earth, to show his strength for those whose hearts are fully devoted to him.[^7] You have acted foolishly in this. From now on you will have wars."

**10** Asa was angry with the seer and put him in the stocks,[^8] for he was enraged with him over this. At that time Asa also oppressed some of the people.

**11** The acts of Asa, from first to last, are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.[^9]

**12** In the thirty-ninth year of his reign Asa was diseased in his feet, and his disease became very severe. Yet even in his disease he did not seek Yahweh, but only the physicians.

**13** Asa slept with his ancestors.[^10] He died in the forty-first year of his reign.

**14** They buried him in the tomb he had cut for himself in the City of David. They laid him on a bier filled with spices and various kinds of ointments blended by the perfumer's art, and they made a very great fire in his honor.[^11]

[^1]: According to 1 Kings 15:33 and 16:8, Baasha reigned from the third to the twenty-sixth year of Asa. Verses 1–6 parallel 1 Kings 15:17–22.
[^2]: Literally "built". Also in verses 5 and 6.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Or "Let there be a treaty". Hebrew *berit*, "covenant". Also later in the verse.
[^5]: 1 Kings 15:20 has Abel-beth-maacah, and "all Chinneroth" in place of "all the storehouses in the cities of Naphtali".
[^6]: Or "horses". Hebrew *parashim*.
[^7]: Literally "to show himself strong with those whose heart is whole toward him".
[^8]: Literally "the house of the stocks". Hebrew *mahpekhet*, probably a frame that held a prisoner in a cramped position (Jeremiah 20:2).
[^9]: A record of the kings that the writer of Chronicles cites often. It has not survived as such. Verses 11–14 are close to 1 Kings 15:23–24.
[^10]: Literally "lay down with his fathers", a way of speaking of death.
[^11]: A ceremonial fire, probably of spices, made in honor of a king who died (21:19; Jeremiah 34:5).
