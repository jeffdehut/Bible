**1** Ahaz was twenty years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. He did not do what was right in the eyes of the LORD,[^1] as his ancestor[^2] David had done.

**2** He walked in the ways of the kings of Israel, and he even made cast images for the Baals.[^3]

**3** He burned incense in the Valley of Ben-hinnom[^4] and burned his sons in the fire,[^5] following the detestable practices of the nations whom the LORD had driven out before the Israelites.

**4** He sacrificed and burned incense at the high places,[^6] on the hills and under every green tree.

**5** So the LORD his God handed him over to the king of Aram. They defeated him and took many of his people captive and brought them to Damascus. He was also handed over to the king of Israel, who struck him with a great slaughter.

**6** Pekah son of Remaliah killed 120,000 in Judah in one day, all of them valiant men, because they had abandoned the LORD, the God of their ancestors.

**7** Zichri, a mighty warrior of Ephraim, killed Maaseiah the king's son, Azrikam the official in charge of the palace, and Elkanah, who was second to the king.

**8** The Israelites took captive from their relatives[^7] two hundred thousand women, sons and daughters. They also took a great amount of plunder from them, and they brought the plunder to Samaria.

**9** But a prophet of the LORD was there, whose name was Oded. He went out to meet the army as it came to Samaria and said to them, "Look, because the LORD, the God of your ancestors, was angry with Judah, he handed them over to you. But you have killed them in a rage that has reached up to heaven.

**10** And now you intend to make the people of Judah and Jerusalem your male and female slaves. But are you yourselves not guilty of sins against the LORD your God?

**11** Now listen to me, and send back the captives you have taken from your relatives, for the burning anger of the LORD is on you."

**12** Then some of the leaders of the Ephraimites, Azariah son of Johanan, Berechiah son of Meshillemoth, Jehizkiah son of Shallum and Amasa son of Hadlai, stood up against those who were coming back from the campaign.

**13** They said to them, "You must not bring the captives here, for you intend to bring guilt on us before the LORD, adding to our sins and our guilt. Our guilt is already great, and there is burning anger against Israel."

**14** So the armed men left the captives and the plunder before the officials and the whole assembly.

**15** Then the men who had been named[^8] got up and took charge of the captives. With the plunder they clothed all who were naked among them. They gave them clothes and sandals, gave them food and drink, and anointed them with oil. They put all who were weak on donkeys and brought them to Jericho, the city of palm trees, to their relatives. Then they returned to Samaria.

**16** At that time King Ahaz sent to the kings[^9] of Assyria for help.

**17** Again the Edomites had come and attacked Judah and carried away captives.

**18** The Philistines had raided the cities of the Shephelah[^10] and the Negev[^11] of Judah. They had captured Beth-shemesh, Aijalon and Gederoth, Soco with its villages,[^12] Timnah with its villages and Gimzo with its villages, and they settled there.

**19** For the LORD humbled Judah because of Ahaz king of Israel,[^13] for he had let Judah run wild[^14] and had been very unfaithful[^15] to the LORD.

**20** Tilgath-pilneser[^16] king of Assyria came against him and oppressed him instead of strengthening him.

**21** For Ahaz took a share from the house of the LORD, from the king's house and from the officials, and gave it to the king of Assyria, but it did not help him.

**22** In the time of his distress he became even more unfaithful to the LORD, this same King Ahaz.

**23** He sacrificed to the gods of Damascus, who had defeated him, for he said, "Because the gods of the kings of Aram help them, I will sacrifice to them so that they will help me." But they were the ruin of him and of all Israel.

**24** Ahaz gathered together the utensils of the house of God and cut the utensils of the house of God in pieces. He shut the doors of the house of the LORD, and he made himself altars on every street corner in Jerusalem.

**25** In every city of Judah he made high places to burn incense to other gods, and he provoked the LORD, the God of his ancestors, to anger.

**26** The rest of his acts and all his ways, from first to last, are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.

**27** Ahaz slept with his ancestors,[^17] and they buried him in the city, in Jerusalem, but they did not bring him into the tombs of the kings of Israel.[^18] And his son Hezekiah became king in his place.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. Verses 1–4 parallel 2 Kings 16:2–4.
[^2]: Literally "his father".
[^3]: Plural of *Baal*, "lord, master": the Canaanite god Baal, worshiped in many local forms.
[^4]: Literally "the valley of the son of Hinnom", south and west of Jerusalem. Its name was later shortened to *Gehenna*.
[^5]: That is, he offered them as burnt sacrifices (Deuteronomy 18:10). 2 Kings 16:3 has "he made his son pass through the fire".
[^6]: Hebrew *bamot*: places of worship, often on hilltops. Also in verse 25.
[^7]: Literally "their brothers". Also in verses 11 and 15.
[^8]: That is, the leaders named in verse 12.
[^9]: Plural in the Hebrew. Compare verse 20 and 2 Kings 16:7.
[^10]: The low hills between the hill country of Judah and the coastal plain.
[^11]: The dry region in the south of Judah.
[^12]: Literally "its daughters". Also later in the verse.
[^13]: Chronicles sometimes uses "Israel" for Judah (21:2).
[^14]: Or "he had acted without restraint in Judah". The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain.
[^15]: Hebrew *ma'al*: to break faith, to betray a trust (12:2). Also in verse 22.
[^16]: Tiglath-pileser III, king of Assyria about 745–727 BC (2 Kings 15:29; 16:7–10).
[^17]: Literally "lay down with his fathers", a way of speaking of death.
[^18]: 2 Kings 16:20 says he was buried with his ancestors in the City of David.
