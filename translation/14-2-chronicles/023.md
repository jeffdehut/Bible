**1** In the seventh year Jehoiada took courage and made a covenant with the commanders of hundreds: Azariah son of Jeroham, Ishmael son of Jehohanan, Azariah son of Obed, Maaseiah son of Adaiah and Elishaphat son of Zichri.[^1]

**2** They went around through Judah and gathered the Levites from all the cities of Judah, and the heads of the ancestral families[^2] of Israel, and they came to Jerusalem.

**3** The whole assembly made a covenant with the king in the house of God. Jehoiada said to them, "Here is the king's son! He shall reign, as the LORD[^3] promised concerning the sons of David.

**4** This is what you are to do. A third of you, priests and Levites who come on duty on the Sabbath, are to be gatekeepers at the thresholds,

**5** a third are to be at the king's palace, and a third at the Foundation Gate,[^4] and all the people are to be in the courtyards of the house of the LORD.

**6** No one is to enter the house of the LORD except the priests and those of the Levites who minister. They may enter, for they are holy, but all the people are to keep the charge of the LORD.[^5]

**7** The Levites are to surround the king on every side, each man with his weapons in his hand. Anyone who enters the house is to be put to death. Stay with the king wherever he goes."[^6]

**8** The Levites and all Judah did everything Jehoiada the priest commanded. Each took his men, those coming on duty on the Sabbath together with those going off duty on the Sabbath, for Jehoiada the priest had not dismissed the divisions.

**9** Jehoiada the priest gave the commanders of hundreds the spears, the small shields and the quivers[^7] that had belonged to King David, which were in the house of God.

**10** He stationed all the people, each man with his weapon in his hand, from the south side of the house to the north side,[^8] by the altar and the house, all around the king.

**11** Then they brought out the king's son and put the crown on him and gave him the statutes,[^9] and they made him king. Jehoiada and his sons anointed him, and they said, "Long live the king!"

**12** When Athaliah heard the noise of the people running and praising the king, she came to the people at the house of the LORD.

**13** She looked, and there was the king, standing by his pillar at the entrance. The commanders and the trumpeters were beside the king, and all the people of the land[^10] were rejoicing and blowing trumpets, and the singers with their musical instruments were leading the praise. Athaliah tore her clothes and cried out, "Treason! Treason!"

**14** Jehoiada the priest brought out the commanders of hundreds, who were in charge of the army, and said to them, "Bring her out between the ranks,[^11] and put to death with the sword anyone who follows her." For the priest had said, "Do not put her to death in the house of the LORD."

**15** So they seized her,[^12] and she went into the entrance of the Horse Gate of the king's palace, and there they put her to death.

**16** Jehoiada made a covenant between himself and all the people and the king, that they would be the LORD's people.[^13]

**17** Then all the people went to the temple of Baal and tore it down. They smashed its altars and its images, and they killed Mattan the priest of Baal in front of the altars.

**18** Jehoiada put the oversight of the house of the LORD into the hands of the Levitical priests,[^14] whom David had organized in divisions over the house of the LORD, to offer the burnt offerings of the LORD as it is written in the Law of Moses, with rejoicing and singing, as David had directed.[^15]

**19** He stationed the gatekeepers at the gates of the house of the LORD, so that no one who was unclean in any way could enter.

**20** He took the commanders of hundreds, the nobles, the rulers of the people and all the people of the land, and he brought the king down from the house of the LORD. They went through the Upper Gate to the king's palace, and they seated the king on the royal throne.

**21** All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet, for they had put Athaliah to death with the sword.

[^1]: Verses 1–21 parallel 2 Kings 11:4–20.
[^2]: Literally "the heads of the fathers".
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: 2 Kings 11:6 has "the Sur Gate".
[^5]: That is, observe what the LORD requires and stay outside.
[^6]: Literally "in his coming in and in his going out".
[^7]: Hebrew *shelatim*, perhaps "quivers" or another kind of shield. 2 Kings 11:10 mentions only "the spears and the *shelatim*".
[^8]: Literally "from the right shoulder of the house to the left shoulder of the house". Facing east, the right hand is south.
[^9]: Hebrew *edut*, traditionally "the testimony": the solemn terms of the covenant, perhaps in a written copy.
[^10]: Hebrew *am ha-arets*, the landowning citizens of Judah, who often took part in choosing the king. Also in verses 20–21.
[^11]: Or "out of the temple area".
[^12]: Or "they made way for her". Literally "they put hands to her".
[^13]: 2 Kings 11:17 reads "between the LORD and the king and the people".
[^14]: Literally "the priests, the Levites".
[^15]: Literally "by the hands of David".
