**1** Josiah was eight years old when he became king, and he reigned thirty-one years in Jerusalem.[^1]

**2** He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD[^2] and walked in the ways of his ancestor[^3] David. He did not turn aside to the right or to the left.

**3** In the eighth year of his reign, while he was still young, he began to seek the God of his ancestor David, and in the twelfth year he began to purify Judah and Jerusalem of the high places,[^4] the Asherah poles,[^5] the carved images and the cast images.

**4** In his presence they tore down the altars of the Baals,[^6] and he cut down the incense altars[^7] that stood above them. He smashed the Asherah poles, the carved images and the cast images and crushed them to dust, and he scattered it over the graves of those who had sacrificed to them.

**5** He burned the bones of the priests on their altars,[^8] and so he purified Judah and Jerusalem.

**6** In the cities of Manasseh, Ephraim and Simeon, as far as Naphtali, in their ruins all around,[^9]

**7** he tore down the altars and the Asherah poles, beat the carved images into powder and cut down all the incense altars throughout the land of Israel. Then he returned to Jerusalem.

**8** In the eighteenth year of his reign, when he had purified the land and the house, he sent Shaphan son of Azaliah, Maaseiah the governor of the city and Joah son of Joahaz, the recorder,[^10] to repair the house of the LORD his God.

**9** They came to Hilkiah the high priest and gave him the money that had been brought into the house of God, which the Levites who kept the threshold had collected from Manasseh and Ephraim and from all the rest of Israel, and from all Judah and Benjamin. Then they returned to Jerusalem.[^11]

**10** They handed it over to the men doing the work, who had been put in charge of the house of the LORD, and they paid it to the workers in the house of the LORD to repair and restore the house.

**11** They gave it to the carpenters and the builders to buy dressed stone, and timber for joists and for beams for the buildings that the kings of Judah had let fall into ruin.

**12** The men did the work faithfully. Over them were appointed Jahath and Obadiah the Levites, of the descendants of Merari, and Zechariah and Meshullam, of the descendants of Kohath, to supervise. The Levites, all who were skilled with musical instruments,

**13** were over the carriers of loads and supervised all the workers in every kind of service. Some of the Levites were secretaries, officials[^12] and gatekeepers.

**14** While they were bringing out the money that had been brought into the house of the LORD, Hilkiah the priest found the book of the law of the LORD given through Moses.

**15** Hilkiah said to Shaphan the secretary, "I have found the book of the Law[^13] in the house of the LORD." And Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan.

**16** Shaphan brought the book to the king, and he also reported to the king: "Your servants are doing everything that was entrusted to them.

**17** They have emptied out[^14] the money that was found in the house of the LORD and have handed it over to the men in charge and to the men doing the work."

**18** Shaphan the secretary also told the king, "Hilkiah the priest has given me a book." And Shaphan read from it before the king.

**19** When the king heard the words of the Law, he tore his clothes.

**20** The king commanded Hilkiah, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Abdon son of Micah,[^15] Shaphan the secretary and Asaiah the king's servant,[^16]

**21** "Go and inquire of the LORD for me and for those who are left in Israel and in Judah, about the words of the book that has been found. For great is the wrath of the LORD that has been poured out on us, because our ancestors did not keep the word of the LORD, to do all that is written in this book."

**22** So Hilkiah and those the king had sent[^17] went to Huldah the prophet, the wife of Shallum son of Tokhath son of Hasrah,[^18] the keeper of the wardrobe. She lived in Jerusalem, in the Second Quarter.[^19] They spoke to her about this,

**23** and she said to them, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Tell the man who sent you to me,

**24** 'This is what the LORD says: I am going to bring disaster on this place and on its inhabitants, all the curses written in the book that was read before the king of Judah.

**25** Because they have abandoned me and burned incense to other gods, provoking me to anger with all the works of their hands, my wrath will be poured out on this place, and it will not be quenched.'

**26** But to the king of Judah, who sent you to inquire of the LORD, say this: 'This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: As for the words you have heard,

**27** because your heart was tender and you humbled yourself before God when you heard his words against this place and against its inhabitants, and because you humbled yourself before me and tore your clothes and wept before me, I too have heard you, declares the LORD.

**28** I am going to gather you to your ancestors, and you will be gathered to your grave in peace. Your eyes will not see all the disaster I am going to bring on this place and on its inhabitants.'" And they brought back word to the king.

**29** Then the king sent word and gathered all the elders of Judah and Jerusalem.

**30** The king went up to the house of the LORD, and with him went all the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the priests and the Levites,[^20] and all the people, from the greatest to the least. He read in their hearing all the words of the book of the covenant that had been found in the house of the LORD.

**31** The king stood in his place and made a covenant before the LORD to follow the LORD and to keep his commands, his statutes[^21] and his decrees with all his heart and all his soul, to carry out the words of the covenant that were written in this book.

**32** Then he made all who were present in Jerusalem and Benjamin commit themselves to it.[^22] And the inhabitants of Jerusalem acted according to the covenant of God, the God of their ancestors.

**33** Josiah removed all the detestable idols from all the territory that belonged to the Israelites, and he made all who were present in Israel serve the LORD their God. All his days they did not turn away from following the LORD, the God of their ancestors.

[^1]: Verses 1–2 and 8–32 parallel 2 Kings 22:1–23:3.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "his father". Also in verse 3.
[^4]: Hebrew *bamot*: places of worship, often on hilltops.
[^5]: Hebrew *asherim*: wooden poles or trees that were symbols of the goddess Asherah. Also in verses 4 and 7.
[^6]: Plural of *Baal*, "lord, master": the Canaanite god Baal, worshiped in many local forms.
[^7]: Hebrew *chammanim*, a word of uncertain meaning: perhaps incense altars, or sun pillars (14:4). Also in verse 7.
[^8]: Compare 1 Kings 13:2; 2 Kings 23:16, 20.
[^9]: The Hebrew text is written as two words of uncertain meaning, *bhr btyhm*, but read *becharvotehem*, "in their ruins" (or "with their swords").
[^10]: Hebrew *mazkir*: a senior royal official, perhaps in charge of records and announcements.
[^11]: The Hebrew text is written "and the inhabitants of Jerusalem" but read "and they returned to Jerusalem".
[^12]: Hebrew *shoterim*: officers who kept records (19:11).
[^13]: Hebrew *sefer ha-torah*, "the book of the instruction", here the law given through Moses. It is often thought to be a form of Deuteronomy (compare Deuteronomy 28:58–61; 31:24–26).
[^14]: Or "melted down".
[^15]: 2 Kings 22:12 has "Achbor son of Micaiah".
[^16]: The title of a senior royal official.
[^17]: Literally "and those of the king".
[^18]: 2 Kings 22:14 has "Tikvah son of Harhas".
[^19]: Hebrew *ha-mishneh*, "the second", a newer district of Jerusalem (Zephaniah 1:10).
[^20]: 2 Kings 23:2 has "the priests and the prophets".
[^21]: Hebrew *edot*, traditionally "testimonies": the solemn terms of the covenant.
[^22]: Literally "he made stand", that is, in the covenant (2 Kings 23:3).
