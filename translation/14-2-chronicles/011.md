**1** When Rehoboam arrived in Jerusalem, he assembled the house of Judah and Benjamin, 180,000 chosen warriors,[^1] to fight against Israel and restore the kingdom to Rehoboam.

**2** But the word of Yahweh[^2] came to Shemaiah the man of God:

**3** "Say to Rehoboam son of Solomon king of Judah, and to all Israel in Judah and Benjamin,

**4** 'This is what Yahweh says: Do not go up or fight against your relatives.[^3] Go home, every one of you, for this thing is from me.'" So they listened to the words of Yahweh and turned back from going against Jeroboam.

**5** Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem, and he built cities for defense in Judah.

**6** He built Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa,

**7** Beth-zur, Soco, Adullam,

**8** Gath, Mareshah, Ziph,

**9** Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah,

**10** Zorah, Aijalon and Hebron, which are in Judah and Benjamin, fortified cities.

**11** He strengthened the fortresses and put commanders in them, with stores of food, oil and wine.

**12** In every city he put large shields and spears, and he made them very strong. So Judah and Benjamin were his.

**13** The priests and the Levites who were in all Israel came over to him from all their territories.

**14** The Levites left their pasturelands[^4] and their property and came to Judah and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons had rejected them from serving as priests of Yahweh.

**15** He appointed his own priests for the high places,[^5] for the goat-demons[^6] and for the calves he had made.[^7]

**16** After them,[^8] those from all the tribes of Israel who had set their hearts on seeking Yahweh, the God of Israel, came to Jerusalem to sacrifice to Yahweh, the God of their ancestors.

**17** They strengthened the kingdom of Judah, and they supported Rehoboam son of Solomon for three years, for they walked in the way of David and Solomon for three years.

**18** Rehoboam married Mahalath daughter[^9] of Jerimoth son of David, and of Abihail[^10] daughter of Eliab son of Jesse.

**19** She bore him sons: Jeush, Shemariah and Zaham.

**20** After her he married Maacah daughter of Absalom,[^11] and she bore him Abijah, Attai, Ziza and Shelomith.

**21** Rehoboam loved Maacah daughter of Absalom more than all his other wives and concubines.[^12] He had married eighteen wives and taken sixty concubines, and he fathered twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters.

**22** Rehoboam appointed Abijah son of Maacah as chief, the leader among his brothers, for he intended to make him king.

**23** He acted wisely and distributed some of his sons through all the districts of Judah and Benjamin, in all the fortified cities. He gave them abundant provisions and sought many wives for them.[^13]

[^1]: Literally "chosen men doing war". Verses 1–4 parallel 1 Kings 12:21–24.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "your brothers".
[^4]: Hebrew *migrash*: the open land around the Levites' towns, used for grazing (Numbers 35:2–5).
[^5]: Hebrew *bamot*: places of worship, often on hilltops.
[^6]: Hebrew *se'irim*, "hairy ones, goats": demons or idols thought to live in the wilderness (Leviticus 17:7).
[^7]: The gold calves Jeroboam set up at Bethel and Dan (1 Kings 12:28–33).
[^8]: That is, following the priests and the Levites.
[^9]: The Hebrew text is written "son" but read "daughter".
[^10]: Probably Mahalath's mother. Some take Abihail as a second wife of Rehoboam.
[^11]: In Hebrew "daughter" can mean any female descendant. 13:2 calls Abijah's mother Micaiah daughter of Uriel of Gibeah.
[^12]: Hebrew *pilegesh*, a wife of lower status than a principal wife. Also later in the verse.
[^13]: Literally "and he asked for a multitude of wives".
