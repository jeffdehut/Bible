**1** So all the work that Solomon did for the house of the LORD[^1] was finished. Then Solomon brought in the things his father David had dedicated,[^2] and he put the silver, the gold and all the utensils in the treasuries of the house of God.

**2** Then Solomon assembled the elders of Israel and all the heads of the tribes, the leaders of the ancestral families[^3] of the Israelites, in Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the covenant of the LORD from the City of David, which is Zion.

**3** All the men of Israel assembled before the king at the festival in the seventh month.[^4]

**4** When all the elders of Israel had come, the Levites[^5] took up the ark.

**5** They brought up the ark, the tent of meeting[^6] and all the holy utensils that were in the tent. The Levitical priests[^7] brought them up.

**6** King Solomon and the whole community of Israel who had gathered to him were before the ark, sacrificing so many sheep and cattle that they could not be counted or numbered.

**7** Then the priests brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD to its place, in the inner sanctuary[^8] of the house, in the most holy place,[^9] beneath the wings of the cherubim.[^10]

**8** The cherubim spread out their wings over the place of the ark, and the cherubim formed a covering above the ark and its poles.

**9** The poles were so long that their ends could be seen from the ark,[^11] in front of the inner sanctuary, but they could not be seen from outside. It is there to this day.[^12]

**10** There was nothing in the ark except the two tablets that Moses put there at Horeb,[^13] where the LORD made a covenant[^14] with the Israelites when they came out of Egypt.

**11** Now the priests came out of the holy place.[^15] All the priests who were present had consecrated themselves, without keeping to their divisions.[^16]

**12** All the Levites who were singers, Asaph, Heman and Jeduthun, with their sons and their relatives, dressed in fine linen, stood east of the altar with cymbals, harps and lyres. With them were 120 priests blowing trumpets.

**13** The trumpeters and the singers joined together to make one sound, praising and thanking the LORD. As they raised the sound with trumpets, cymbals and other musical instruments, and praised the LORD, "For he is good, for his faithful love[^17] endures forever,"[^18] the house, the house of the LORD, was filled with a cloud.

**14** The priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud, for the glory of the LORD filled the house of God.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. "The house of the LORD" is the temple. Verses 1–14 parallel 1 Kings 7:51–8:11.
[^2]: Literally "the holy things of David his father".
[^3]: Literally "the chiefs of the fathers".
[^4]: Literally "at the festival; it is the seventh month". The festival is the Festival of Shelters, held in that month (Leviticus 23:34). 1 Kings 8:2 also gives the old name of the month, Ethanim.
[^5]: 1 Kings 8:3 has "the priests".
[^6]: Hebrew *ohel mo'ed*: the tent where the LORD met with Moses and the people, that is, the tabernacle kept at Gibeon (1:3).
[^7]: Literally "the priests, the Levites". 1 Kings 8:4 has "the priests and the Levites".
[^8]: Hebrew *devir*: the innermost room of the temple. Also in verse 9.
[^9]: Literally "the holy of holies".
[^10]: Hebrew *keruvim* (singular *keruv*, "cherub"): winged heavenly beings who guard holy places (3:10–13; Exodus 25:18–22).
[^11]: 1 Kings 8:8 reads "from the holy place".
[^12]: Literally "And it is there to this day", probably the ark.
[^13]: Another name for Mount Sinai.
[^14]: Literally "where the LORD cut", the usual idiom for making a covenant.
[^15]: The main hall in front of the inner sanctuary. 1 Kings 8:10 goes on at once: "the cloud filled the house of the LORD". The account of the priests and the singers in verses 11b–13a is not in Kings.
[^16]: The priests normally served in turns, by divisions (8:14; 1 Chronicles 24).
[^17]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^18]: A refrain of worship found also in 7:3; 1 Chronicles 16:34; Psalms 106:1; 118:1; 136:1.
