**1** When all this was finished, all Israel who were present went out to the cities of Judah. They smashed the standing stones,[^1] cut down the Asherah poles,[^2] and tore down the high places[^3] and the altars throughout all Judah and Benjamin, and in Ephraim and Manasseh, until they had destroyed them all. Then all the Israelites returned to their cities, each to his own property.

**2** Hezekiah appointed the divisions of the priests and the Levites, division by division, each according to his service, both the priests and the Levites, for the burnt offerings and the fellowship offerings,[^4] to minister, to give thanks and to praise in the gates of the camp of the LORD.[^5]

**3** The king's share from his own possessions was for the burnt offerings: the morning and evening burnt offerings, and the burnt offerings for the Sabbaths, the new moons and the appointed festivals, as it is written in the law of the LORD.

**4** He told the people, those who lived in Jerusalem, to give the share due to the priests and the Levites, so that they could devote themselves to the law of the LORD.

**5** As soon as the word spread, the Israelites gave in abundance the firstfruits of grain, new wine, olive oil, honey[^6] and all the produce of the field. They brought in a tithe of everything in abundance.

**6** The people of Israel and Judah who lived in the cities of Judah also brought a tithe of cattle and sheep, and a tithe of the holy things that had been consecrated to the LORD their God, and they laid them in heaps.

**7** In the third month they began to lay the foundations of the heaps, and they finished in the seventh month.[^7]

**8** When Hezekiah and the officials came and saw the heaps, they blessed the LORD and his people Israel.

**9** Hezekiah asked the priests and the Levites about the heaps.

**10** Azariah the chief priest, of the house of Zadok, answered him, "Since the people began to bring the contributions[^8] into the house of the LORD, we have eaten and had enough, with plenty left over, for the LORD has blessed his people. This great amount is what is left over."

**11** Then Hezekiah gave orders to prepare storerooms in the house of the LORD, and they prepared them.

**12** They faithfully brought in the contributions, the tithes and the consecrated things. Conaniah the Levite was in charge of them, and his brother Shimei was second.

**13** Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath and Benaiah were overseers under Conaniah and his brother Shimei, by the appointment of King Hezekiah and of Azariah the official in charge of the house of God.

**14** Kore son of Imnah the Levite, the gatekeeper on the east side, was in charge of the freewill offerings to God, to distribute the contribution for the LORD and the most holy things.[^9]

**15** Under him were Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah and Shecaniah in the cities of the priests, faithfully distributing to their brothers by divisions, to great and small alike,

**16** apart from those enrolled by genealogy, males three years old and upward,[^10] all who entered the house of the LORD for their daily duty, for their service in their offices according to their divisions.

**17** The enrollment of the priests was by their ancestral families,[^11] and that of the Levites twenty years old and upward was by their offices, by their divisions.

**18** They were enrolled with all their little ones, their wives, their sons and their daughters, the whole community, for they had faithfully consecrated themselves as holy.

**19** As for the descendants of Aaron, the priests, who lived in the pasturelands[^12] around their cities, in every city there were men designated by name to distribute portions to every male among the priests and to everyone enrolled among the Levites.

**20** Hezekiah did this throughout Judah. He did what was good, right and faithful before the LORD his God.

**21** In every work that he undertook in the service of the house of God, and in the law and the command, seeking his God, he acted with all his heart, and he prospered.

[^1]: Hebrew *matsevot*: standing stones set up as objects of worship.
[^2]: Hebrew *asherim*: wooden poles or trees that were symbols of the goddess Asherah.
[^3]: Hebrew *bamot*: places of worship, often on hilltops.
[^4]: Hebrew *shelamim*, traditionally "peace offerings": sacrifices shared in a meal by the worshipers.
[^5]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. Literally "the camps of the LORD", that is, the temple courts (compare 1 Chronicles 9:18–19).
[^6]: Hebrew *devash*: honey, or a syrup made from fruit such as dates.
[^7]: The third month was the time of the wheat harvest and the Festival of Weeks, and the seventh month the end of the fruit harvest and the Festival of Shelters.
[^8]: Hebrew *terumah*: something set apart and given for a sacred purpose (Exodus 25:2). Also in verses 12 and 14.
[^9]: The portions of the offerings that only the priests could eat (Leviticus 6:10, 22; English 6:17, 29).
[^10]: The sense of the verse is uncertain. Some read "thirty years old" (compare 1 Chronicles 23:3).
[^11]: Literally "by the house of their fathers".
[^12]: Hebrew *migrash*: the open land around the towns, used for grazing (11:14; Numbers 35:2–5).
