**1** Solomon assigned[^1] 70,000 men to carry loads and 80,000 men to cut stone in the hills, and 3,600 to supervise them.[^2]

**2** Solomon sent this message to Huram[^3] king of Tyre: "Do for me as you did for my father David, when you sent him cedars to build himself a house to live in.[^4]

**3** I am about to build a house for the name of Yahweh[^5] my God, to dedicate it to him, to burn fragrant incense before him, for the bread that is set out regularly,[^6] and for burnt offerings morning and evening, on the Sabbaths, the new moons and the appointed festivals of Yahweh our God. This is a duty for Israel forever.

**4** The house I am building will be great, for our God is greater than all gods.

**5** But who is able to build him a house, since the heavens, even the highest heavens,[^7] cannot contain him? Who am I to build him a house, except as a place to burn incense[^8] before him?

**6** So now send me a skilled man to work in gold, silver, bronze and iron, and in purple, crimson[^9] and blue yarn, and who knows how to engrave, to work with the skilled craftsmen who are with me in Judah and Jerusalem, whom my father David provided.

**7** Send me also cedar, cypress[^10] and algum[^11] logs from Lebanon, for I know that your servants know how to cut the timber of Lebanon. My servants will work with your servants

**8** to prepare timber for me in abundance, for the house I am building will be great and wonderful.

**9** I will give your servants, the woodcutters who cut the timber, twenty thousand kors[^12] of ground wheat,[^13] twenty thousand kors of barley, twenty thousand baths[^14] of wine and twenty thousand baths of oil."[^15]

**10** Huram king of Tyre answered in a letter that he sent to Solomon: "Because Yahweh loves his people, he has made you king over them."

**11** Huram also said, "Blessed be Yahweh, the God of Israel, who made the heavens and the earth! He has given King David a wise son, endowed with insight and understanding, who will build a house for Yahweh and a royal palace for himself.[^16]

**12** So now I am sending you a skilled man, endowed with understanding, Huram-abi.[^17]

**13** He is the son of a woman from the daughters of Dan,[^18] and his father was a man of Tyre. He knows how to work in gold and silver, bronze and iron, stone and wood, and in purple and blue yarn, fine linen[^19] and crimson. He knows how to do every kind of engraving and to carry out any design that may be given to him, with your skilled craftsmen and the skilled craftsmen of my lord David, your father.

**14** So now let my lord send his servants the wheat and the barley, the oil and the wine, that he has spoken of.

**15** We will cut all the timber you need from Lebanon and bring it to you as rafts by sea to Joppa, and you can take it up to Jerusalem."

**16** Solomon took a census of all the foreigners[^20] who were in the land of Israel, following the census that his father David had taken of them,[^21] and they were found to number 153,600.

**17** He made 70,000 of them carriers of loads and 80,000 stonecutters in the hills, and 3,600 overseers to make the people work.

[^1]: Literally "counted".
[^2]: Verses 1–17 are 2:2–18 in most English Bibles. They parallel 1 Kings 5:15–32 (English 5:1–18), which gives the number of overseers as 3,300 (1 Kings 5:30, English 5:16).
[^3]: Called Hiram in 1 Kings 5. Also in verses 10 and 11.
[^4]: Literally "As you did with David my father, and sent him cedars to build him a house to live in." The words "so do for me" are understood.
[^5]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^6]: Literally "and for the regular arrangement": the bread of the Presence, set out in rows on the table every Sabbath (Leviticus 24:5–8).
[^7]: Literally "the heavens and the heaven of heavens".
[^8]: Or "to burn offerings". The Hebrew verb *qatar* means to make something go up in smoke.
[^9]: Hebrew *karmil*, a word for crimson found only in Chronicles. Exodus uses *tola'at shani* (Exodus 25:4). Also in verse 13.
[^10]: Hebrew *berosh*, a tall conifer: perhaps the cypress or the juniper.
[^11]: Hebrew *algummim*, a costly wood that cannot be identified with certainty; perhaps red sandalwood. In 1 Kings 10:11–12 it is called *almuggim* and is brought from Ophir.
[^12]: A kor was probably about 6 bushels (220 liters).
[^13]: Hebrew *chittim makkot*, literally "wheat of blows", probably wheat that had been threshed or ground. 1 Kings 5:25 (English 5:11) has *makkolet*, "food".
[^14]: A bath was about 6 gallons (22 liters).
[^15]: 1 Kings 5:25 (English 5:11) says Solomon gave Hiram twenty thousand kors of wheat as food for his household, and twenty kors of pressed olive oil.
[^16]: Literally "a house for his kingdom".
[^17]: Hebrew *le-Huram avi*, "Huram my father": probably a name, or a title such as "my master craftsman". In 1 Kings 7:13 he is called Hiram.
[^18]: 1 Kings 7:14 says his mother was a widow from the tribe of Naphtali.
[^19]: Hebrew *buts*, a fine linen. Exodus uses *shesh* (Exodus 25:4).
[^20]: Hebrew *gerim*: foreigners living among the Israelites.
[^21]: See 1 Chronicles 22:2.
