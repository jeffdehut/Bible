**1** "At that time Michael, the great prince who stands guard over your people,[^1] will arise. There will be a time of distress such as has never been since there was a nation until that time. But at that time your people will be delivered, everyone who is found written in the book.[^2]

**2** Many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth[^3] will awake, some to everlasting life, and some to disgrace and everlasting contempt.[^4]

**3** Those who are wise[^5] will shine like the brightness of the sky,[^6] and those who lead many to righteousness will shine like the stars forever and ever.

**4** But you, Daniel, keep the words secret and seal the book until the time of the end. Many will run to and fro, and knowledge will increase."[^7]

**5** Then I, Daniel, looked, and there stood two others, one on this bank of the river and one on that bank of the river.[^8]

**6** One of them said to the man dressed in linen, who was above the waters of the river, "How long will it be until the end of these wonders?"

**7** I heard the man dressed in linen, who was above the waters of the river. He raised his right hand and his left hand toward heaven and swore by the one who lives forever that it would be for a time, times and half a time,[^9] and that when the shattering of the power of the holy people comes to an end, all these things will be completed.[^10]

**8** I heard, but I did not understand. So I said, "My lord, what will be the outcome of these things?"

**9** He said, "Go your way, Daniel, for the words are kept secret and sealed until the time of the end.

**10** Many will be purified, made white and refined, but the wicked will act wickedly. None of the wicked will understand, but those who are wise will understand.

**11** From the time that the regular offering[^11] is taken away and the abomination that causes desolation[^12] is set up, there will be 1,290 days.

**12** Blessed is the one who waits and reaches 1,335 days.[^13]

**13** But you, go your way to the end. You will rest, and you will rise to receive your allotted share at the end of the days."[^14]

[^1]: Literally "who stands over the sons of your people". See 10:13, 21.
[^2]: Compare Exodus 32:32–33; Psalm 69:29 (69:28 in most English Bibles); Malachi 3:16; Revelation 20:12.
[^3]: Literally "the ground of dust". Compare Genesis 3:19.
[^4]: This is the clearest statement in the Old Testament of a resurrection of the dead (compare Isaiah 26:19). "Many" may mean a great number, or it may mean that not all will rise. Hebrew *dir'on*, "contempt, abhorrence", is found elsewhere only in Isaiah 66:24. Compare John 5:28–29.
[^5]: Hebrew *maskilim*. See 11:33. Also in verse 10.
[^6]: Literally "the brightness of the dome", Hebrew *raqia*, the sky pictured as a solid vault (Genesis 1:6–8).
[^7]: The Septuagint reads "and the land will be filled with injustice".
[^8]: Hebrew *ye'or*, a word usually used of the Nile; here the Tigris (10:4). Also in verses 6–7.
[^9]: That is, probably three and a half years. See 7:25.
[^10]: Literally "the hand of the holy people". Compare Revelation 10:5–7.
[^11]: See 8:11.
[^12]: Hebrew *shiqquts shomem*. See 11:31; compare 9:27 and Mark 13:14.
[^13]: The numbers in verses 11 and 12 differ from each other and from 8:14. Each is a little more than three and a half years (compare verse 7).
[^14]: Literally "and you will stand for your lot at the end of the days". *Rest* may refer to death and *rise* to resurrection (compare verse 2).
