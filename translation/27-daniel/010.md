**1** In the third year of Cyrus king of Persia,[^1] a word was revealed to Daniel, who was called Belteshazzar. The word was true, and it concerned a great conflict.[^2] He understood the word and had understanding of the vision.

**2** At that time I, Daniel, had been mourning for three whole weeks.[^3]

**3** I ate no rich food,[^4] no meat or wine entered my mouth, and I did not anoint myself at all until the three whole weeks were over.

**4** On the twenty-fourth day of the first month,[^5] I was on the bank of the great river, the Tigris.[^6]

**5** I lifted my eyes and looked, and there was a man dressed in linen, with a belt of fine gold from Uphaz[^7] around his waist.

**6** His body was like beryl,[^8] his face like the appearance of lightning, his eyes like flaming torches, his arms and his legs like the gleam of polished bronze, and the sound of his words like the sound of a multitude.[^9]

**7** I, Daniel, was the only one who saw the vision. The men who were with me did not see the vision, but great terror fell on them, and they fled and hid themselves.

**8** So I was left alone, and I saw this great vision. No strength was left in me. My face turned deathly pale,[^10] and I retained no strength.

**9** Then I heard the sound of his words, and as I heard the sound of his words, I fell into a deep sleep on my face, with my face to the ground.

**10** Then a hand touched me and set me trembling on my knees and the palms of my hands.

**11** He said to me, "Daniel, greatly loved man,[^11] understand the words that I am speaking to you, and stand up where you are,[^12] for I have now been sent to you." When he had spoken this word to me, I stood up trembling.

**12** Then he said to me, "Do not be afraid, Daniel, for from the first day that you set your heart to understand and to humble yourself before your God, your words have been heard, and I have come because of your words.

**13** The prince of the kingdom of Persia[^13] stood against me for twenty-one days. Then Michael,[^14] one of the chief princes, came to help me, and I was left there with the kings of Persia.[^15]

**14** Now I have come to make you understand what will happen to your people in the last days,[^16] for the vision is for days yet to come."

**15** While he was speaking these words with me, I turned my face toward the ground and was speechless.

**16** Then one who looked like a human being[^17] touched my lips. I opened my mouth and spoke, and I said to the one standing before me, "My lord, because of the vision pains have come over me,[^18] and I retain no strength.

**17** How can my lord's servant speak with my lord? As for me, no strength remains in me now, and no breath is left in me."

**18** Then the one who looked like a human being touched me again and strengthened me.

**19** He said, "Do not be afraid, greatly loved man. Peace be with you. Be strong, be strong!" As he spoke with me, I was strengthened, and I said, "Let my lord speak, for you have strengthened me."

**20** Then he said, "Do you know why I have come to you? Now I will go back to fight against the prince of Persia, and when I go out, the prince of Greece will come.

**21** But I will tell you what is written in the book of truth.[^19] There is no one who stands firmly with me against these except Michael, your prince.[^20]

[^1]: About 536 BC.
[^2]: Or "a great hardship". Hebrew *tsava gadol*, literally "a great army" or "a great service".
[^3]: Literally "three weeks of days". Also in verse 3.
[^4]: Literally "bread of delights".
[^5]: The month of Nisan, March–April, the month of Passover (Exodus 12:2, 18).
[^6]: Hebrew *Chiddeqel*.
[^7]: The location of Uphaz is unknown (Jeremiah 10:9).
[^8]: Hebrew *tarshish*, a precious stone, perhaps yellow jasper or topaz (Exodus 28:20; Ezekiel 1:16).
[^9]: Compare Ezekiel 1:7, 13–14, 24; Revelation 1:13–15.
[^10]: Literally "my splendor was turned on me to ruin".
[^11]: Literally "man of precious things". See 9:23. Also in verse 19.
[^12]: Literally "stand on your standing".
[^13]: An angelic being, the heavenly ruler of Persia. Compare "the prince of Greece" in verse 20.
[^14]: Michael means "Who is like God?". He is the angel who protects Israel (verse 21; 12:1). Compare Jude 9 and Revelation 12:7.
[^15]: The Septuagint and Theodotion read "and I left him there with the prince of the kingdom of Persia".
[^16]: Literally "at the end of the days". See 2:28.
[^17]: Literally "like the likeness of the sons of man"; in verse 18, "like the appearance of a man". Compare 7:13.
[^18]: Literally "my pangs have turned upon me", the word used for the pains of childbirth (1 Samuel 4:19).
[^19]: Or "the true book". Literally "the writing of truth".
[^20]: The Hebrew "your" is plural: the prince of Daniel's people (12:1).
