**1** They came to the other side of the sea, to the region of the Gerasenes.[^1]

**2** As soon as Jesus got out of the boat, a man with an unclean spirit came from the tombs to meet him.

**3** He lived among the tombs, and no one could restrain him anymore, not even with a chain.

**4** He had often been bound with shackles and chains, but he tore the chains apart and broke the shackles in pieces. No one was strong enough to subdue him.

**5** Night and day, among the tombs and in the hills, he kept shrieking and cutting himself with stones.

**6** When he saw Jesus from a distance, he ran and knelt down before him.

**7** He shouted at the top of his voice, "What do you want with me,[^2] Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I beg you by God, do not torment me!"

**8** For Jesus had been saying to him, "Unclean spirit, come out of the man!"

**9** Jesus asked him, "What is your name?" He replied, "My name is Legion,[^3] for there are many of us."

**10** And he begged Jesus again and again not to send them out of the region.

**11** A large herd of pigs was feeding there on the hillside.

**12** The spirits begged him, "Send us into the pigs, so that we may go into them."

**13** He gave them permission. The unclean spirits came out and went into the pigs, and the herd, about two thousand of them, rushed down the steep bank into the sea and drowned in the sea.

**14** The men who tended the pigs ran off and reported it in the town and in the countryside, and people came to see what had happened.

**15** They came to Jesus and saw the man who had been possessed by demons, the one who had had the legion, sitting there, dressed and in his right mind. And they were afraid.

**16** Those who had seen it told them what had happened to the demon-possessed man, and about the pigs.

**17** Then they began to beg Jesus to leave their region.

**18** As Jesus was getting into the boat, the man who had been possessed by demons begged to go with him.

**19** But Jesus did not let him. Instead he told him, "Go home to your own people, and tell them how much the Lord has done for you, and how he has had mercy on you."

**20** So the man went away and began to proclaim in the Decapolis[^4] how much Jesus had done for him. And everyone was amazed.

**21** When Jesus had crossed again in the boat to the other side, a large crowd gathered around him, and he stayed beside the sea.

**22** One of the leaders of the synagogue, named Jairus, came, and when he saw Jesus, he fell at his feet.

**23** He pleaded earnestly with him, "My little daughter is dying. Please come and put your hands on her, so that she will be healed and live."

**24** So Jesus went with him. A large crowd followed him, pressing in on him from all sides.

**25** A woman was there who had suffered from bleeding for twelve years.

**26** She had endured a great deal under many doctors and had spent all she had. Yet instead of getting better, she had grown worse.

**27** She had heard about Jesus, so she came up behind him in the crowd and touched his cloak.

**28** For she said, "If I can just touch his clothes, I will be healed."

**29** At once her bleeding stopped,[^5] and she felt in her body that she was healed of her affliction.

**30** At once Jesus realized that power had gone out from him. He turned around in the crowd and asked, "Who touched my clothes?"

**31** His disciples said to him, "You see the crowd pressing in on you, and you ask, 'Who touched me?'"

**32** But he kept looking around to see who had done it.

**33** Then the woman, frightened and trembling because she knew what had happened to her, came and fell down before him and told him the whole truth.

**34** He said to her, "Daughter, your faith has healed you.[^6] Go in peace, and be free of your affliction."

**35** While he was still speaking, people came from the house of the synagogue leader and said, "Your daughter has died. Why trouble the teacher any further?"

**36** But Jesus overheard[^7] what they said and told the synagogue leader, "Don't be afraid; just believe."

**37** He did not let anyone follow him except Peter, James, and John the brother of James.

**38** They came to the synagogue leader's house, and Jesus saw a commotion, with people weeping and wailing loudly.

**39** He went in and said to them, "Why all this commotion and weeping? The child is not dead but asleep."

**40** They laughed at him. But he put them all outside, took the child's father and mother and those who were with him, and went in where the child was.

**41** He took the child by the hand and said to her, "Talitha koum," which means, "Little girl, I tell you, get up!"

**42** At once the girl got up and began to walk around. She was twelve years old. At this they were completely overwhelmed with amazement.

**43** He gave them strict orders not to let anyone know about this, and he told them to give her something to eat.

[^1]: Many later manuscripts read "Gadarenes".
[^2]: Literally "What to me and to you?"; see 1:24.
[^3]: A Roman legion was a unit of several thousand soldiers.
[^4]: "The Ten Towns", a group of Greek cities east of the Jordan.
[^5]: Literally "the source of her blood dried up".
[^6]: Or "has saved you". The Greek verb *sozo* means both.
[^7]: Or "ignored". The Greek verb can mean either.
