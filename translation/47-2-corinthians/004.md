**1** Therefore, since we have this ministry[^1] through God's mercy,[^2] we do not lose heart.

**2** We have renounced the shameful things that are done in secret. We do not live by cunning or distort the word of God, but by openly stating the truth we commend ourselves to everyone's conscience before God.

**3** And even if our good news is covered,[^3] it is covered to those who are perishing.

**4** In their case the god of this age[^4] has blinded the minds of unbelievers, so that they do not see the light[^5] of the good news of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God.

**5** For we do not proclaim ourselves, but Christ Jesus as Lord, and ourselves as your servants[^6] for Jesus' sake.

**6** For it is God who said, "Light will shine out of darkness,"[^7] who has shone in our hearts to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Christ.

**7** But we have this treasure in clay jars, to show that the extraordinary power belongs to God and does not come from us.

**8** We are afflicted in every way, but not crushed; perplexed, but not driven to despair;

**9** persecuted, but not abandoned; struck down, but not destroyed.

**10** We always carry around in the body the dying of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our body.

**11** For we who are alive are always being handed over to death for Jesus' sake, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our mortal flesh.

**12** So death is at work in us, but life in you.

**13** Since we have the same spirit of faith[^8] as is shown in what is written, "I believed, therefore I spoke,"[^9] we also believe, and therefore we also speak,

**14** knowing that the one who raised Jesus[^10] will also raise us with Jesus[^11] and bring us together with you into his presence.

**15** For all this is for your sake, so that grace, as it reaches more and more people, may make thanksgiving overflow, to the glory of God.

**16** So we do not lose heart. Even though our outer self[^12] is wasting away, our inner self is being renewed day by day.

**17** For our light and momentary affliction is producing for us an eternal weight of glory beyond all comparison,

**18** because we look not at what can be seen but at what cannot be seen. For what can be seen is temporary, but what cannot be seen is eternal.

[^1]: Greek *diakonia*, "service, ministry" (see 3:7).
[^2]: Literally "as we have received mercy".
[^3]: Greek *kekalymmenon*, related to *kalymma*, "covering" (3:13–16); also later in the verse.
[^4]: Most interpreters take this to mean Satan (compare 11:14).
[^5]: Or "so that the light … does not dawn on them". Greek *photismos*, "illumination, light"; also in verse 6.
[^6]: Or "slaves". Greek *douloi*.
[^7]: Compare Genesis 1:3 and Isaiah 9:1 (9:2 in most English Bibles). Many manuscripts read "Let light shine out of darkness".
[^8]: Or "the same Spirit of faith".
[^9]: Psalm 116:10, following the Septuagint (where it is Psalm 115:1). The Hebrew can be read "I believed, even when I spoke" (see the note there).
[^10]: The words "the Lord" are not in the SBLGNT, which follows a marginal reading of Westcott and Hort; the other editions read "the Lord Jesus".
[^11]: Many manuscripts read "through Jesus".
[^12]: Literally "our outer human being"; and "our inner one".
