**1** Are we beginning to commend ourselves again? Or do we need, as some do, letters of recommendation to you or from you?

**2** You yourselves are our letter, written on our hearts, known and read by everyone.

**3** You show that you are a letter from Christ, delivered by us,[^1] written not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on tablets of stone but on tablets that are hearts of flesh.[^2]

**4** Such is the confidence we have through Christ toward God.

**5** Not that we are adequate[^3] in ourselves to claim anything as coming from us; our adequacy comes from God,

**6** who has made us adequate to be servants[^4] of a new covenant,[^5] not of the letter but of the Spirit. For the letter[^6] kills, but the Spirit gives life.

**7** Now if the ministry[^7] of death, carved in letters on stone, came with such glory that the Israelites could not gaze at Moses' face because of the glory of his face,[^8] a glory that was being brought to an end,[^9]

**8** how much more will the ministry of the Spirit come with glory?

**9** For if there was glory in the ministry of condemnation, much more does the ministry of righteousness overflow with glory.

**10** Indeed, what had glory has in this respect come to have no glory, because of the glory that surpasses it.

**11** For if what was being brought to an end came with glory, much more does what remains have glory.

**12** Since, then, we have such a hope, we act with great boldness,[^10]

**13** and not like Moses, who would put a covering[^11] over his face so that the Israelites could not gaze at the end[^12] of what was being brought to an end.

**14** But their minds were hardened. For to this very day, at the reading of the old covenant,[^13] the same covering remains, not lifted, because it is brought to an end in Christ.[^14]

**15** Indeed, to this day whenever Moses is read, a covering lies over their hearts.

**16** But whenever someone turns to the Lord, the covering is removed.[^15]

**17** Now the Lord is the Spirit,[^16] and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom.

**18** And we all, with uncovered faces, beholding as in a mirror[^17] the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another,[^18] as from the Lord, the Spirit.[^19]

[^1]: Literally "served by us". Greek *diakoneo*, related to *diakonia*, "ministry", in verses 7–9.
[^2]: Compare Exodus 31:18; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 36:26 ("a heart of flesh").
[^3]: Greek *hikanos*, "adequate, sufficient, competent", the word used in 2:16; so also "adequacy" and "made us adequate" in verses 5–6.
[^4]: Or "ministers". Greek *diakonoi*.
[^5]: Compare Jeremiah 31:31 and 1 Corinthians 11:25.
[^6]: Greek *gramma*, "letter, what is written", here probably the law written on stone (verse 7).
[^7]: Greek *diakonia*, "service, ministry"; also in verses 8 and 9.
[^8]: Exodus 34:29–35. The Hebrew says that the skin of Moses' face "was shining"; the Septuagint says that it "had been glorified".
[^9]: Greek *katargeo*, "make ineffective, abolish, bring to an end" (see 1 Corinthians 1:28); also in verses 11, 13 and 14. In verses 7, 11 and 13 it is often translated "fading".
[^10]: Greek *parresia*, "boldness, frankness, openness".
[^11]: Greek *kalymma*, the word the Septuagint uses in Exodus 34:33–35 for the covering Moses put over his face (Hebrew *masveh*), traditionally "veil"; also in verses 14–16.
[^12]: Greek *telos*, "end, goal, outcome".
[^13]: Here the scriptures of Moses, read in the synagogue (verse 15).
[^14]: Or "the same covering remains, since it is not revealed that in Christ it is being brought to an end". What is "brought to an end" may be the covering or the old covenant.
[^15]: Compare Exodus 34:34, "whenever Moses went in before the LORD to speak with him, he would take the covering off". The subject of "turns" is not stated: it may be anyone, "their hearts" (verse 15), or Moses. In the Hebrew, "the Lord" in Exodus 34:34 is God's personal name, YHWH ("the LORD").
[^16]: "The Lord" here takes up the word from verse 16.
[^17]: Or "reflecting". Greek *katoptrizomai*, from *katoptron*, "mirror".
[^18]: Literally "from glory to glory".
[^19]: Or "from the Lord who is the Spirit", or "from the Spirit of the Lord".
