**1** For it is unnecessary for me to write to you about the service to God's holy people,[^1]

**2** for I know your eagerness, which I boast about to the Macedonians, saying that Achaia has been ready since last year; and your zeal has stirred up most of them.

**3** But I am sending the brothers so that our boasting about you may not prove empty in this matter, so that you may be ready, as I said you would be,

**4** in case some Macedonians come with me and find that you are not ready, and we, to say nothing of you, would be humiliated in this undertaking.[^2]

**5** So I thought it necessary to urge the brothers to go on ahead to you and arrange in advance the gift[^3] you have promised, so that it may be ready as a willing gift and not as something extorted.[^4]

**6** The point is this: whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and whoever sows generously will also reap generously.

**7** Each one should give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly[^5] or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.[^6]

**8** And God is able to make every grace overflow to you, so that, always having enough of everything, you may overflow in every good work.

**9** (As it is written, "He has scattered abroad; he has given to the needy. His righteousness endures forever."[^7]

**10** He who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your seed for sowing and increase the harvest of your righteousness.)[^8]

**11** You will be enriched in every way for all generosity, which through us produces thanksgiving to God.

**12** For the ministry of this service[^9] is not only supplying the needs of God's holy people but is also overflowing in many thanksgivings to God.

**13** Through the proof that this ministry provides, they will glorify God for the obedience of your confession to the good news of Christ, and for the generosity of your sharing with them and with everyone,

**14** while in their prayers for you they long for you because of the surpassing grace of God upon you.

**15** Thanks be to God for his indescribable gift!

[^1]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "saints"; also in verse 12. Because this chapter takes up the collection again as if it had not been discussed in chapter 8, some scholars think chapters 8 and 9 were once separate letters or notes; others read chapter 9 as continuing chapter 8.
[^2]: Or "in this confidence". Greek *hypostasis*. Many manuscripts read "in this confident boasting".
[^3]: Literally "blessing", Greek *eulogia*; also later in the verse ("willing gift") and in verse 6 ("generously", literally "with blessings").
[^4]: Or "as an expression of greed".
[^5]: Literally "not out of grief".
[^6]: Compare Proverbs 22:8 in the Septuagint, which adds the line "God blesses a man who is cheerful and generous" (see the note there).
[^7]: Psalm 112:9.
[^8]: The words echo Isaiah 55:10 ("giving seed to the sower and bread to the eater") and Hosea 10:12. Many manuscripts read "May he who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food supply and multiply your seed for sowing and increase the harvest of your righteousness."
[^9]: Greek *leitourgia*, a word used both of public service and of priestly service in worship.
