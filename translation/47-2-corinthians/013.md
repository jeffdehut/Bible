**1** This is the third time I am coming to you. "Every matter must be established on the testimony of two witnesses or three."[^1]

**2** I warned those who sinned earlier and all the others when I was with you the second time, and I warn them now while I am absent: if I come again, I will not spare them,

**3** since you are seeking proof that Christ is speaking in me. He is not weak in dealing with you, but is powerful among you.

**4** For he was crucified in weakness, but he lives by the power of God. For we too are weak in him, but we will live with him by the power of God toward you.

**5** Test yourselves to see whether you are in the faith; examine yourselves.[^2] Or do you not realize that Jesus Christ is in you?[^3] Unless, indeed, you fail the test!

**6** And I hope you will realize that we have not failed the test.

**7** But we pray to God that you may not do anything wrong, not so that we may appear to have passed the test, but so that you may do what is right, even though we may seem to have failed.

**8** For we cannot do anything against the truth, but only for the truth.

**9** For we rejoice when we are weak and you are strong. This is what we pray for: that you may be made complete.[^4]

**10** This is why I write these things while I am absent, so that when I am present I may not have to be severe in using the authority the Lord gave me for building up and not for tearing down.

**11** Finally, brothers and sisters,[^5] rejoice.[^6] Put things in order, encourage one another,[^7] be of one mind, live in peace, and the God of love and peace will be with you.

**12** Greet one another with a holy kiss. All God's holy people[^8] greet you.[^9]

**13** The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ and the love of God and the fellowship[^10] of the Holy Spirit be with all of you.[^11]

[^1]: Deuteronomy 19:15. Literally "at the mouth of two witnesses and three".
[^2]: Greek *peirazo*, "test, try", and *dokimazo*, "test, prove". *Dokimazo* is related to *adokimos*, "failing the test" (verses 5–7), and *dokimos*, "having passed the test" (verse 7).
[^3]: Or "among you".
[^4]: Greek *katartisis*, "restoration, being put in order"; the related verb is translated "Put things in order" in verse 11.
[^5]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike.
[^6]: Or "farewell". The word was also used as a greeting.
[^7]: Or "be comforted", or "listen to my appeal". Greek *parakaleo* (see 1:3).
[^8]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "saints".
[^9]: Many English Bibles divide this verse into two, so that verse 13 here is verse 14 there.
[^10]: Or "the sharing in". Greek *koinonia* (compare 1 Corinthians 1:9).
[^11]: Many manuscripts add "Amen".
