**1** And I said: Listen, you heads of Jacob and rulers of the house of Israel! Is it not for you to know justice?

**2** You hate good and love evil. You tear the skin off my people[^1] and the flesh off their bones.

**3** You eat the flesh of my people and strip their skin off them. You break their bones in pieces and chop them up like meat for the pot,[^2] like flesh in a cauldron.

**4** Then they will cry out to the LORD,[^3] but he will not answer them. He will hide his face from them at that time, because of the evil they have done.

**5** This is what the LORD says about the prophets who lead my people astray, who cry "Peace!" when they have something to bite with their teeth, but declare war[^4] on anyone who puts nothing in their mouths:

**6** Therefore it will be night for you, with no vision, and darkness for you, with no divination.[^5] The sun will go down on the prophets, and the day will turn black over them.

**7** The seers will be ashamed and the diviners disgraced. They will all cover their lips,[^6] because there is no answer from God.

**8** But as for me, I am filled with power, with the Spirit of the LORD,[^7] and with justice and might, to declare to Jacob his rebellion and to Israel his sin.

**9** Hear this, you heads of the house of Jacob and rulers of the house of Israel, who detest justice and twist everything that is straight,

**10** who build Zion with bloodshed and Jerusalem with injustice.

**11** Her leaders judge for a bribe, her priests teach[^8] for a price, and her prophets practice divination for money. Yet they lean on the LORD and say, "Is not the LORD among us? No disaster will come on us."

**12** Therefore, because of you, Zion will be plowed like a field, Jerusalem will become a heap of ruins, and the temple mount a wooded height.[^9]

[^1]: Literally "their skin from on them". *My people* is supplied from verse 3.
[^2]: Literally "spread them out as in a pot". Some scholars read "like flesh in a pot", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Literally "consecrate war", as for a holy war.
[^5]: Hebrew *qesem*, the reading of omens, forbidden in Deuteronomy 18:10. Micah uses it with scorn of the prophets' practice. Also in verses 7 and 11.
[^6]: Literally "cover the mustache", a sign of shame or mourning (Leviticus 13:45; Ezekiel 24:17, 22).
[^7]: Or "by the Spirit of the LORD". Hebrew *ruach* can mean spirit, wind or breath.
[^8]: Hebrew *yoru*, the verb from which *torah*, "instruction", comes. The priests were to teach the people the LORD's instruction (Deuteronomy 33:10; Malachi 2:7).
[^9]: *The temple mount* is literally "the mountain of the house". Jeremiah 26:18 quotes this verse about a century later, as spoken in the days of Hezekiah.
