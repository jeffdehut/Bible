**1** But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah,[^1] too small to be among the clans[^2] of Judah, from you one will come out for me to be ruler in Israel, whose origins are from of old, from ancient days.[^3]

**2** Therefore he will give them up until the time when she who is in labor has given birth.[^4] Then the rest of his brothers will return to the people of Israel.

**3** He will stand and shepherd his flock[^5] in the strength of the LORD,[^6] in the majesty of the name of the LORD his God. And they will live secure, for now he will be great to the ends of the earth.

**4** And he will be our peace.[^7] When Assyria comes into our land and treads on our fortresses, we will raise up against it seven shepherds, eight leaders of men.[^8]

**5** They will shepherd[^9] the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod[^10] at its gates.[^11] He will rescue us from Assyria when it comes into our land and treads within our borders.

**6** The remnant of Jacob will be among many peoples like dew from the LORD, like showers on the grass, which do not wait for anyone or depend on human beings.[^12]

**7** The remnant of Jacob will be among the nations, among many peoples, like a lion among the animals of the forest, like a young lion among flocks of sheep, which tramples and tears as it goes, and there is no one to rescue.

**8** Your hand will be raised over your adversaries, and all your enemies will be cut off.

**9** On that day, declares the LORD,[^13] I will cut off your horses from among you and destroy your chariots.

**10** I will cut off the cities of your land and tear down all your strongholds.

**11** I will cut off sorceries from your hand, and you will have no more fortune-tellers.[^14]

**12** I will cut off your carved images and your standing stones[^15] from among you, and you will no longer bow down to the work of your hands.

**13** I will uproot your Asherah poles[^16] from among you and destroy your cities.[^17]

**14** In anger and wrath I will take vengeance on the nations that have not obeyed.[^18]

[^1]: Verses 1–14 are 5:2–15 in most English Bibles. Bethlehem, also called Ephrathah, was the home town of David (Ruth 4:11; 1 Samuel 17:12). Matthew 2:6 quotes this verse freely, together with words from 2 Samuel 5:2: "And you, Bethlehem, in the land of Judah, are by no means least among the rulers of Judah, for out of you will come a ruler who will shepherd my people Israel." Compare John 7:42.
[^2]: Literally "the thousands", the clans that each provided a unit of soldiers. The same consonants can be read "the chiefs", as in Matthew's "among the rulers of Judah".
[^3]: Or "from days long ago". *Origins* is literally "goings out".
[^4]: Perhaps the mother of the coming ruler (compare Isaiah 7:14), or Zion in her labor pains (4:9–10).
[^5]: *His flock* is added. Literally "He will stand and shepherd".
[^6]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^7]: Or "This will be our peace". Literally "This one will be peace".
[^8]: A number saying, as in Amos 1:3: as many leaders as are needed, and more.
[^9]: The verb *ra'ah*, "shepherd", can also be read *ra'a'*, "shatter".
[^10]: The mighty hunter, founder of cities in Babylonia and Assyria (Genesis 10:8–12).
[^11]: Literally "in its entrances". Some scholars read "with the drawn sword", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^12]: Dew and rain come from the LORD alone, not from human effort.
[^13]: Hebrew *ne'um YHWH*, "the declaration of the LORD", a formula that marks a prophet's words as the LORD's own solemn statement.
[^14]: Practices forbidden in Deuteronomy 18:10–14. *Fortune-tellers* is Hebrew *me'onenim*, a word of uncertain meaning.
[^15]: Hebrew *matsevot*, stone pillars set up at places of worship, forbidden in Deuteronomy 16:22.
[^16]: Hebrew *asherim*, wooden poles or trees that were symbols of the goddess Asherah (Deuteronomy 16:21).
[^17]: Some scholars read "your idols", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^18]: Or "that have not listened".
