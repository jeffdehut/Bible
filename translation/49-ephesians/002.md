**1** And you were dead in your wrongdoings and sins,

**2** in which you once lived,[^1] following the ways of this world,[^2] following the ruler of the authority of the air, the spirit that is now at work in those who disobey.[^3]

**3** Among them we too all once lived in the desires of our flesh,[^4] doing what the flesh and the mind wanted, and we were by nature children of wrath,[^5] like the rest.

**4** But God, who is rich in mercy, because of the great love with which he loved us,

**5** even when we were dead in our wrongdoings, made us alive together with Christ (by grace you have been saved)

**6** and raised us up with him and seated us with him in the heavenly places[^6] in Christ Jesus,

**7** so that in the ages to come he might show the surpassing riches of his grace in his kindness toward us in Christ Jesus.

**8** For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this[^7] is not from yourselves; it is the gift of God.

**9** It is not from works, so that no one can boast.

**10** For we are what he has made,[^8] created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared in advance so that we would live in them.[^9]

**11** So remember that at one time you, the Gentiles in the flesh,[^10] called "the uncircumcision" by what is called "the circumcision", which is made in the flesh by human hands,

**12** remember that at that time you were without Christ,[^11] shut out from the citizenship of Israel and strangers to the covenants of the promise, with no hope and without God in the world.

**13** But now in Christ Jesus you who once were far away have been brought near by the blood of Christ.

**14** For he himself is our peace. He made the two one and broke down the dividing wall that kept them apart,[^12] the hostility, in his flesh,[^13]

**15** abolishing the law of commandments expressed in decrees, so that in himself he might create one new humanity[^14] out of the two, making peace,

**16** and might reconcile both of them to God in one body through the cross, by which he put the hostility to death.[^15]

**17** He came and proclaimed the good news of peace to you who were far away and peace to those who were near.[^16]

**18** For through him we both have access to the Father in one Spirit.

**19** So then you are no longer strangers and foreigners,[^17] but you are fellow citizens with God's holy people[^18] and members of God's household,

**20** built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with Christ Jesus himself as the cornerstone.[^19]

**21** In him the whole building,[^20] joined together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord.

**22** In him you too are being built together into a dwelling place for God in the Spirit.[^21]

[^1]: Literally "walked", a common biblical picture for a way of life; also in verse 10.
[^2]: Literally "according to the age of this world".
[^3]: Literally "in the sons of disobedience".
[^4]: Greek *sarx*, "flesh": here human nature in its weakness and opposition to God. In verses 11 and 14 the word has its physical sense.
[^5]: That is, people facing God's wrath. *Children of*, like "sons of" in verse 2, is a Hebrew way of saying what belongs to someone or marks them.
[^6]: Literally "in the heavenlies".
[^7]: "This" is neuter in Greek and may refer to the whole of being saved by grace through faith, or to faith itself.
[^8]: Greek *poiema*, "a thing made, a work".
[^9]: Literally "walk in them".
[^10]: That is, Gentiles by physical birth.
[^11]: Or "without the Messiah".
[^12]: Literally "the middle wall of the partition".
[^13]: Or "broke down the dividing wall that kept them apart. In his flesh he abolished the hostility, the law …", taking "the hostility" with verse 15.
[^14]: Literally "one new man".
[^15]: Or "having put the hostility to death in himself".
[^16]: Compare Isaiah 57:19, "Peace, peace, to the far and to the near", and 52:7.
[^17]: Greek *paroikoi*, foreigners who live in a place without the rights of citizens.
[^18]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "saints".
[^19]: Greek *akrogoniaios*, the chief stone at the corner of a foundation; some take it to mean the top stone of a building. Compare Isaiah 28:16.
[^20]: Or "every building".
[^21]: Or "by the Spirit".
