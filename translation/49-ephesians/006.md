**1** Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.

**2** "Honor your father and mother" (this is the first commandment with a promise),

**3** "so that it may go well with you and you may live long on the earth."[^1]

**4** Fathers,[^2] do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord.

**5** Slaves,[^3] obey your earthly masters[^4] with fear and trembling, in sincerity of heart, as you obey Christ,

**6** not only when they are watching, to please people,[^5] but as slaves of Christ, doing the will of God from the heart,[^6]

**7** serving with goodwill, as serving the Lord and not people,

**8** knowing that whatever good each one does, this they will receive back from the Lord, whether they are slave or free.

**9** And masters, treat them in the same way, and stop threatening them, knowing that the one who is Master[^7] of both them and you is in heaven, and with him there is no favoritism.

**10** Finally,[^8] be strong in the Lord and in the strength of his might.

**11** Put on the full armor of God, so that you can stand against the schemes of the devil.

**12** For our struggle[^9] is not against flesh and blood,[^10] but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the world rulers of this darkness,[^11] against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places.[^12]

**13** Therefore take up the full armor of God, so that you may be able to resist on the evil day, and having done everything,[^13] to stand.

**14** Stand, then, having fastened truth around your waist like a belt and put on the breastplate of righteousness,[^14]

**15** and having put on your feet the readiness that comes from the good news of peace.[^15]

**16** In all circumstances[^16] take up the shield of faith, with which you will be able to put out all the flaming arrows of the evil one.

**17** And take the helmet of salvation[^17] and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.

**18** With every kind of prayer and request, pray at all times in the Spirit. To this end stay alert, with all perseverance and with requests for all God's holy people,[^18]

**19** and for me, that a message may be given to me when I open my mouth, to make known with boldness the mystery of the good news,

**20** for which I am an ambassador in chains, so that I may speak of it boldly, as I ought to speak.

**21** So that you too may know how things are with me and what I am doing, Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful servant[^19] in the Lord, will tell you everything.

**22** I am sending[^20] him to you for this very purpose, so that you may know how we are and so that he may encourage your hearts.

**23** Peace to the brothers and sisters,[^21] and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**24** Grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ with an undying love.[^22]

[^1]: Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16, as worded in the Septuagint. *On the earth*: or "in the land". The commandment continues "that the LORD your God is giving you".
[^2]: Greek *pateres*, "fathers", a word that can sometimes mean "parents".
[^3]: Greek *douloi*, "slaves"; also in verses 6 and 8. Slavery was part of the social order of the Roman world, and many members of the early churches were slaves or owned slaves. This passage addresses both (verses 5–9).
[^4]: Literally "masters according to the flesh". Greek *kyrioi*, "lords, masters, owners", the same word as "Lord" in verses 7–9.
[^5]: Literally "not according to eye-service, as people-pleasers".
[^6]: Or "doing the will of God, serving from the heart with goodwill", taking "from the heart" with verse 7. Literally "from the soul".
[^7]: Or "Lord". Greek *kyrios*, the same word as "masters" earlier in the verse.
[^8]: Or "From now on". Many manuscripts read "Finally, my brothers and sisters".
[^9]: Literally "wrestling".
[^10]: Literally "blood and flesh", that is, human beings.
[^11]: Many manuscripts read "of the darkness of this age".
[^12]: Literally "in the heavenlies".
[^13]: Or "having overcome everything".
[^14]: Compare Isaiah 11:5; 59:17.
[^15]: Compare Isaiah 52:7.
[^16]: Many manuscripts read "Above all".
[^17]: Compare Isaiah 59:17.
[^18]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "saints".
[^19]: Greek *diakonos*, "servant", traditionally "minister".
[^20]: Literally "I sent", written from the point of view of the readers, who will receive the letter when Tychicus arrives.
[^21]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike.
[^22]: Literally "in incorruptibility". The phrase may describe their love, or it may go with "grace", meaning "in immortality". Many manuscripts add "Amen".
