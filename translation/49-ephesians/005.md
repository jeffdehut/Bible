**1** So be imitators of God, as beloved children,

**2** and live[^1] in love, just as Christ also loved us and gave himself up for us,[^2] an offering and a sacrifice to God, a pleasing aroma.[^3]

**3** But sexual immorality and any kind of impurity or greed must not even be mentioned among you, as is fitting for God's holy people,[^4]

**4** and there must be no obscenity or foolish talk or crude joking, which are out of place, but rather thanksgiving.

**5** For you can be sure of this: no sexually immoral or impure or greedy person (that is, an idolater) has any inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God.[^5]

**6** Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things the wrath of God comes on those who disobey.[^6]

**7** So do not be partners with them.

**8** For once you were darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Live as children of light

**9** (for the fruit of the light[^7] is found in all goodness and righteousness and truth),

**10** discerning what pleases the Lord.

**11** Take no part in the fruitless works of darkness, but rather expose them.

**12** For it is shameful even to speak of the things they do in secret.

**13** But everything that is exposed by the light becomes visible,

**14** for everything that becomes visible is light.[^8] So it says,[^9] "Wake up, sleeper, and rise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you."[^10]

**15** So watch carefully how you live, not as unwise people but as wise,

**16** making the most of the time,[^11] because the days are evil.

**17** For this reason do not be foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord is.

**18** And do not get drunk with wine, which leads to reckless living, but be filled with the Spirit,[^12]

**19** speaking to one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making music with your heart to the Lord,

**20** always giving thanks for everything to God the Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ,

**21** submitting[^13] to one another in the fear of Christ.[^14]

**22** Wives, submit to your own husbands as to the Lord,[^15]

**23** because the husband is the head[^16] of the wife as Christ also is the head of the church, he himself being the savior of the body.

**24** But as the church submits to Christ, so also wives should submit to their husbands in everything.

**25** Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ also loved the church and gave himself up for her,

**26** to make her holy, cleansing her by the washing of water with the word,[^17]

**27** so that he might present the church to himself in splendor, without spot or wrinkle or anything of the kind, but holy and blameless.

**28** In the same way, husbands also ought to love their wives as their own bodies. He who loves his wife loves himself.

**29** For no one ever hated his own flesh,[^18] but he nourishes it and cares for it, just as Christ[^19] does the church,

**30** because we are members of his body.[^20]

**31** "For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh."[^21]

**32** This mystery[^22] is great, but I am speaking about Christ and the church.

**33** However, each one of you also must love his wife as himself, and the wife must respect[^23] her husband.

[^1]: Literally "walk", a common biblical picture for a way of life; also in verses 8 and 15.
[^2]: Some manuscripts read "you" for "us", in one or both places.
[^3]: Literally "for a smell of fragrance", the language of the sacrifices in the Old Testament (Exodus 29:18; Leviticus 1:9).
[^4]: Literally "for holy ones", traditionally "saints".
[^5]: Literally "of the Christ and God".
[^6]: Literally "on the sons of disobedience".
[^7]: Many manuscripts read "the fruit of the Spirit".
[^8]: Or "for whatever makes things visible is light".
[^9]: Or "he says".
[^10]: These words are not found in the Old Testament. They may come from an early Christian hymn; compare Isaiah 26:19 and 60:1.
[^11]: Literally "buying up the time".
[^12]: Or "by the Spirit". Literally "in spirit".
[^13]: Greek *hypotasso*, "to place oneself under, to be subject to"; also in verse 24. Here it is used of all believers toward one another. The word continues the sentence that begins with "be filled with the Spirit" in verse 18, and it also introduces the instructions to households that follow (5:22–6:9).
[^14]: Or "out of reverence for Christ".
[^15]: The SBLGNT has no verb in this verse; "submit" is carried over from verse 21. Many manuscripts add "submit"; some read "let them submit".
[^16]: Greek *kephale*, "head". What the image implies in passages like this (authority, source, or both) is debated.
[^17]: Greek *rhema*, a spoken word.
[^18]: That is, his own body.
[^19]: Many manuscripts read "the Lord".
[^20]: Many manuscripts add "of his flesh and of his bones". Compare Genesis 2:23.
[^21]: Genesis 2:24.
[^22]: Greek *mysterion*, a secret plan that was hidden until God revealed it.
[^23]: Literally "fear".
