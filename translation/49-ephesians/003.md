**1** For this reason I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus for the sake of you Gentiles—[^1]

**2** Surely you have heard[^2] of the stewardship[^3] of God's grace that was given to me for you.

**3** By revelation the mystery[^4] was made known to me,[^5] as I have written briefly before.[^6]

**4** When you read this, you can understand my insight into the mystery of Christ,

**5** which in other generations was not made known to human beings[^7] as it has now been revealed by the Spirit to his holy apostles and prophets:

**6** that the Gentiles are heirs together with them, members together of one body, and sharers together in the promise in Christ Jesus through the good news.

**7** Of this good news I became a servant[^8] according to the gift of God's grace, which was given to me by the working of his power.

**8** To me, the very least[^9] of all God's holy people,[^10] this grace was given: to proclaim to the Gentiles the good news of the unfathomable riches of Christ,

**9** and to bring to light for everyone[^11] what is the plan[^12] of the mystery that for ages past was hidden in God, who created all things,[^13]

**10** so that through the church the wisdom of God in all its variety might now be made known to the rulers and the authorities in the heavenly places,[^14]

**11** according to the eternal purpose[^15] that he carried out in Christ Jesus our Lord.

**12** In him we have boldness and access with confidence through faith in him.[^16]

**13** So I ask you not to lose heart[^17] over my sufferings for you, which are your glory.

**14** For this reason I kneel before the Father,[^18]

**15** from whom every family[^19] in heaven and on earth gets its name,

**16** that, according to the riches of his glory, he may grant you to be strengthened with power through his Spirit in your inner being,[^20]

**17** and that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith, in love,[^21] so that you, rooted and firmly founded,

**18** may have strength to grasp, with all God's holy people, what is the breadth and length and height and depth,

**19** and to know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge, so that you may be filled to all the fullness of God.[^22]

**20** Now to him who is able to do far more than all we ask or imagine, according to the power that is at work in us,

**21** to him be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus[^23] for all generations, forever and ever.[^24] Amen.

[^1]: The sentence breaks off here and is taken up again in verse 14 ("For this reason …"). Verses 2–13 are a digression.
[^2]: Literally "if indeed you have heard".
[^3]: Greek *oikonomia*, as in 1:10; here the task of a steward who manages what belongs to another.
[^4]: Greek *mysterion*, a secret plan that was hidden until God revealed it; also in verses 4 and 9.
[^5]: The SBLGNT here follows Holmes and a marginal reading of Westcott and Hort. Other editions and many manuscripts begin the verse with "that", making it what the readers have heard: "… given to me for you, that by revelation the mystery was made known to me".
[^6]: Probably earlier in this letter (1:9–10; 2:11–22).
[^7]: Literally "to the sons of men".
[^8]: Greek *diakonos*, "servant", traditionally "minister".
[^9]: Literally "the leaster", a comparative formed from a superlative.
[^10]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "saints"; also in verse 18.
[^11]: Some manuscripts leave out "for everyone", reading "to bring to light what is the plan …".
[^12]: Greek *oikonomia*, as in verse 2 and 1:10.
[^13]: Many manuscripts add "through Jesus Christ".
[^14]: Literally "in the heavenlies".
[^15]: Literally "the purpose of the ages".
[^16]: Or "through his faithfulness", that is, the faithfulness of Christ. Literally "through the faith of him".
[^17]: Or "I pray that I may not lose heart".
[^18]: Many manuscripts add "of our Lord Jesus Christ".
[^19]: Greek *patria*, "family, clan, people", a word related to *pater*, "father". Or "the whole family".
[^20]: Literally "into the inner man".
[^21]: The SBLGNT's punctuation takes "in love" with Christ's dwelling in your hearts. Many translations read "… through faith, so that you, rooted and founded in love, may have strength …".
[^22]: Literally "filled into all the fullness of God". Or "filled with all the fullness of God".
[^23]: Many manuscripts leave out "and", reading "in the church in Christ Jesus".
[^24]: Literally "to all the generations of the age of the ages".
