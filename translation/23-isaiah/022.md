**1** An oracle[^1] concerning the Valley of Vision:[^2] What is the matter with you now, that you have all gone up to the rooftops,

**2** you city full of noise, you town of tumult and celebration? Your slain were not slain by the sword, nor did they die in battle.

**3** All your rulers fled together; they were captured without a bow being drawn.[^3] All of you who were found were captured together, though they had fled far away.

**4** Therefore I said, "Look away from me; let me weep bitterly. Do not try to comfort me over the destruction of my dear people."[^4]

**5** For the Lord Yahweh of Armies[^5] has a day of tumult, trampling and confusion in the Valley of Vision, of battering down walls and of crying out to the mountain.[^6]

**6** Elam took up the quiver, with chariots of men and horsemen, and Kir uncovered the shield.[^7]

**7** Your choicest valleys were full of chariots, and the horsemen took their positions at the gate.

**8** He removed the covering of Judah.[^8] On that day you looked to the weapons in the House of the Forest.[^9]

**9** You saw that the breaches in the walls of the City of David were many, and you collected the waters of the Lower Pool.

**10** You counted the houses of Jerusalem and tore houses down to strengthen the wall.

**11** You made a reservoir between the two walls for the water of the Old Pool.[^10] But you did not look to the one who made it, or consider the one who planned it long ago.

**12** On that day the Lord Yahweh of Armies called for weeping and mourning, for shaving the head and putting on sackcloth.

**13** But look, there was joy and celebration, killing cattle and slaughtering sheep, eating meat and drinking wine: "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die!"[^11]

**14** Yahweh of Armies revealed himself in my hearing: Surely this guilt will not be atoned for you until you die, says the Lord Yahweh of Armies.

**15** This is what the Lord Yahweh of Armies says: Go, go to this steward, to Shebna, who is in charge of the palace:[^12]

**16** What right do you have here, and whom do you have here, that you have cut out a tomb for yourself here, cutting out a tomb on the height, carving a resting place for himself in the rock?[^13]

**17** Look, Yahweh is about to hurl you away violently, you strong man, and grasp you firmly.[^14]

**18** He will roll you up tightly like a ball and throw you into a wide land. There you will die, and there your splendid chariots will be, you disgrace to your master's house!

**19** I will thrust you from your office, and he will pull you down from your position.

**20** On that day I will call my servant Eliakim son of Hilkiah.

**21** I will clothe him with your robe and fasten your sash around him, and I will hand your authority over to him. He will be a father to the people of Jerusalem and to the house of Judah.

**22** I will place on his shoulder the key of the house of David. What he opens, no one will shut, and what he shuts, no one will open.[^15]

**23** I will drive him like a peg into a firm place, and he will become a seat of honor for his father's house.[^16]

**24** They will hang on him all the weight[^17] of his father's house, offspring and offshoots, all the small vessels, from bowls to all the jars.

**25** On that day, declares Yahweh of Armies, the peg driven into a firm place will give way. It will be cut down and fall, and the load[^18] hanging on it will be cut off. For Yahweh has spoken.

[^1]: Hebrew *massa*. See 13:1.
[^2]: A name for Jerusalem, perhaps from a valley near the city where the prophet saw visions.
[^3]: Literally "without the bow".
[^4]: Literally "the daughter of my people", a tender name for the people pictured as a young woman.
[^5]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. *Yahweh of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^6]: Hebrew *meqarqar qir*, a phrase of uncertain meaning that echoes its own sounds. Perhaps "a shouting of 'Qir!'".
[^7]: Kir, a region in Mesopotamia from which the Arameans came (Amos 9:7). Elam and Kir supplied troops to the Assyrian army.
[^8]: Or "the defenses of Judah were stripped away".
[^9]: The House of the Forest of Lebanon, a hall built by Solomon, named for its cedar pillars, where weapons were kept (1 Kings 7:2; 10:17).
[^10]: Compare Hezekiah's work on Jerusalem's water supply before the Assyrian siege (2 Kings 20:20; 2 Chronicles 32:2–5, 30).
[^11]: Paul quotes these words in 1 Corinthians 15:32.
[^12]: Literally "who is over the house", the title of the king's chief minister. Shebna appears as secretary, with Eliakim in this office, in 36:3.
[^13]: Literally "for himself". The Hebrew moves from "you" to "he".
[^14]: Or "and wrap you up tightly". The meaning of verses 17–18 is uncertain in places.
[^15]: Revelation 3:7 echoes this verse.
[^16]: Literally "a throne of glory".
[^17]: Hebrew *kavod*, "glory, weight".
[^18]: Hebrew *massa*, the word translated "oracle" in verse 1.
