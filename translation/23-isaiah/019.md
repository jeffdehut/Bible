**1** An oracle[^1] concerning Egypt: Look, the LORD[^2] is riding on a swift cloud and is coming to Egypt. The idols of Egypt tremble before him, and the heart of Egypt melts within it.

**2** I will stir up Egyptian against Egyptian; they will fight, brother against brother and neighbor against neighbor, city against city, kingdom against kingdom.

**3** The spirit of Egypt will be emptied out within it, and I will confuse its plans.[^3] They will consult the idols and the spirits of the dead,[^4] the mediums and the spiritists.

**4** I will hand Egypt over to a cruel master,[^5] and a fierce king will rule over them, declares the Lord[^6] GOD of Armies.[^7]

**5** The waters of the sea[^8] will dry up, and the river will be parched and dry.

**6** The canals will stink; the streams of Egypt[^9] will dwindle and dry up; reeds and rushes will wither.

**7** The plants along the Nile, at the mouth of the Nile, and every sown field along the Nile will dry up, blow away and be no more.[^10]

**8** The fishermen will mourn and lament, all who cast hooks into the Nile; those who spread nets on the water will waste away.

**9** Those who work with combed flax will be dismayed, and the weavers of white cloth.[^11]

**10** Her foundations[^12] will be crushed, and all who work for wages will be sick at heart.

**11** The officials of Zoan[^13] are nothing but fools; the wise counselors of Pharaoh give senseless advice. How can you say to Pharaoh, "I am a son of the wise, a son of ancient kings"?

**12** Where are your wise men now? Let them tell you and make known what the LORD of Armies has planned against Egypt.

**13** The officials of Zoan have become fools; the officials of Memphis[^14] are deceived. The cornerstones of her tribes have led Egypt astray.

**14** The LORD has poured into her a spirit of confusion,[^15] and they make Egypt stagger in all she does, as a drunkard staggers in his vomit.

**15** There is nothing Egypt can do, head or tail, palm branch or reed.[^16]

**16** On that day the Egyptians will be like women. They will tremble and be afraid before the hand that the LORD of Armies raises against them.

**17** The land of Judah will become a terror to Egypt. Everyone to whom it is mentioned will be afraid because of the plan that the LORD of Armies has planned against them.

**18** On that day there will be five cities in the land of Egypt that speak the language of Canaan[^17] and swear allegiance to the LORD of Armies. One of them will be called the City of Destruction.[^18]

**19** On that day there will be an altar to the LORD in the middle of the land of Egypt, and a standing stone[^19] to the LORD at its border.

**20** It will be a sign and a witness to the LORD of Armies in the land of Egypt. When they cry out to the LORD because of their oppressors, he will send them a savior and defender,[^20] who will rescue them.

**21** The LORD will make himself known to Egypt, and the Egyptians will know the LORD on that day. They will worship with sacrifice and grain offering,[^21] and make vows to the LORD and keep them.

**22** The LORD will strike Egypt, striking and healing. They will turn back to the LORD, and he will listen to their pleas and heal them.

**23** On that day there will be a highway from Egypt to Assyria. The Assyrians will come to Egypt and the Egyptians to Assyria, and the Egyptians will worship with the Assyrians.[^22]

**24** On that day Israel will be the third, with Egypt and Assyria, a blessing in the middle of the earth.

**25** The LORD of Armies will bless them,[^23] saying, "Blessed be Egypt my people, Assyria the work of my hands, and Israel my inheritance."[^24]

[^1]: Hebrew *massa*. See 13:1.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "swallow up".
[^4]: Hebrew *ittim*, a word found only here, perhaps "mutterers" or "ghosts". The *mediums* and *spiritists* (*ovot* and *yidde'onim*) are those who claim to speak with the dead (8:19).
[^5]: Literally "masters", a plural of majesty.
[^6]: Hebrew *ha-adon*, "the Lord", a title, not the name YHWH.
[^7]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^8]: Here the Nile, whose broad waters could be called a sea.
[^9]: Hebrew *Matsor*, a poetic name for Egypt.
[^10]: The first word of the verse, Hebrew *arot*, is uncertain: perhaps "plants", "meadows" or "bare places".
[^11]: Hebrew *chori*, a word found only here, probably "white cloth", that is, fine linen.
[^12]: Hebrew *shatoteha*, perhaps "her pillars", the leaders of the land, or "her weavers".
[^13]: Also called Tanis, a royal city in the Nile Delta.
[^14]: Hebrew *Noph*, Memphis, the ancient capital of Egypt near modern Cairo.
[^15]: Or "dizziness". Literally "a spirit of distortions".
[^16]: The top and bottom of society (9:13–14, 9:14–15 in most English Bibles).
[^17]: That is, Hebrew or a related language.
[^18]: Hebrew *ir ha-heres*. Some Hebrew manuscripts, the Great Isaiah Scroll from Qumran (1QIsa-a) and the Vulgate read *ir ha-cheres*, "City of the Sun", that is, Heliopolis in Egypt. The Septuagint reads "City of Righteousness".
[^19]: Hebrew *matsevah*, a standing stone, a pillar set up as a memorial or as an object of worship. The law forbade them in Israel (Deuteronomy 16:22).
[^20]: Hebrew *rav*, "a great one" or "one who contends", that is, defends their cause.
[^21]: Hebrew *minchah*, "gift, tribute", in Leviticus a grain offering.
[^22]: Or "and the Egyptians will serve the Assyrians". Hebrew *avad* means both "serve" and "worship".
[^23]: Literally "him", the land or people of verse 24.
[^24]: The Septuagint reads "Blessed be my people who are in Egypt and among the Assyrians, and Israel my inheritance".
