**1** Woe to the rebellious children, declares Yahweh,[^1] who carry out plans that are not mine, and make an alliance,[^2] but not by my Spirit,[^3] heaping sin on sin,

**2** who set out to go down to Egypt without asking my advice,[^4] to take refuge in Pharaoh's protection and to shelter in Egypt's shade!

**3** But Pharaoh's protection will be your shame, and shelter in Egypt's shade your disgrace.

**4** For though his officials are in Zoan and his envoys have reached Hanes,[^5]

**5** everyone will be put to shame[^6] because of a people who cannot help them, who bring neither help nor benefit, but only shame and disgrace.

**6** An oracle[^7] concerning the animals of the Negev:[^8] Through a land of trouble and distress, of lioness and lion, of viper and flying serpent,[^9] they carry their wealth on the backs of donkeys and their treasures on the humps of camels, to a people that cannot help them.

**7** Egypt's help is worthless and empty. Therefore I have called her "Rahab, who sits still."[^10]

**8** Now go, write it on a tablet in their presence, and inscribe it in a scroll, so that it may be there for the time to come, forever and ever.[^11]

**9** For they are a rebellious people, lying children, children unwilling to listen to the instruction[^12] of Yahweh,

**10** who say to the seers, "Do not see!" and to the prophets, "Do not prophesy to us what is right! Tell us smooth things; prophesy illusions!

**11** Get out of the way; turn aside from the path; stop confronting us with the Holy One of Israel!"[^13]

**12** Therefore this is what the Holy One of Israel says: Because you have rejected this word and trusted in oppression and deceit, and relied on them,

**13** this guilt will be for you like a breach in a high wall, bulging out and about to fall, whose collapse comes suddenly, in an instant.

**14** It will break like a potter's jar, smashed so ruthlessly that among its pieces not a fragment is found to take fire from a hearth or to scoop water from a cistern.

**15** For this is what the Lord Yahweh, the Holy One of Israel, says: In returning and rest you will be saved; in quietness and trust will be your strength. But you were not willing.

**16** You said, "No! We will flee on horses." So you will flee! "We will ride on swift horses." So your pursuers will be swift![^14]

**17** A thousand will flee at the threat of one; at the threat of five you will all flee, until you are left like a flagpole on a mountaintop, like a signal flag on a hill.

**18** Therefore Yahweh waits to be gracious to you; therefore he rises to show you compassion. For Yahweh is a God of justice. Happy are all who wait for him![^15]

**19** You people in Zion, who live in Jerusalem,[^16] you will weep no more. He will surely be gracious to you at the sound of your cry; as soon as he hears it, he will answer you.

**20** Though the Lord gives you the bread of adversity and the water of affliction, your teacher[^17] will no longer hide himself, but your eyes will see your teacher.

**21** Your ears will hear a word behind you: "This is the way; walk in it," whenever you turn to the right or to the left.

**22** Then you will defile your silver-plated idols and your gold-covered images. You will throw them away like a menstrual cloth and say to them, "Get out!"

**23** He will give rain for the seed you sow in the ground, and the bread that the ground produces will be rich and plentiful. On that day your cattle will graze in broad pastures.

**24** The oxen and donkeys that work the ground will eat seasoned fodder, winnowed with shovel and fork.

**25** On every high mountain and on every lofty hill there will be streams flowing with water, on the day of the great slaughter, when the towers fall.

**26** The light of the moon will be like the light of the sun, and the light of the sun will be seven times brighter, like the light of seven days, on the day Yahweh binds up the wounds of his people and heals the bruises from his blows.

**27** Look, the name of Yahweh comes from far away, burning with his anger, in thick, rising smoke.[^18] His lips are full of fury, and his tongue is like a devouring fire.

**28** His breath[^19] is like a flooding torrent that rises up to the neck, to sift the nations in the sieve of destruction, and to put in the jaws of the peoples a bridle that leads them astray.

**29** You will have a song as on the night when a festival is kept holy, and gladness of heart as when people go up with the flute to the mountain of Yahweh, to the Rock of Israel.

**30** Yahweh will make his majestic voice heard and show his arm coming down, in raging anger and the flame of a devouring fire, with cloudburst and storm and hailstones.

**31** For at the voice of Yahweh Assyria will be shattered, when he strikes with the rod.

**32** Every stroke of the appointed rod that Yahweh lays on him will be to the sound of tambourines and lyres, as he fights against them[^20] in battle with brandished arm.

**33** For a Topheth[^21] has long been prepared; it has been made ready for the king.[^22] Its fire pit has been made deep and wide, with plenty of fire and wood. The breath of Yahweh, like a stream of sulfur, sets it ablaze.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "pour out a drink offering", perhaps to seal a treaty, or "weave a web".
[^3]: Or "not by my spirit". Hebrew *ruach*.
[^4]: Literally "and have not asked my mouth".
[^5]: Probably Heracleopolis, a city in Egypt south of Memphis. *Zoan* is Tanis in the Nile Delta (19:11).
[^6]: The Hebrew text is written "Everyone has made a stench" but read "Everyone has been put to shame".
[^7]: Hebrew *massa*. See 13:1.
[^8]: The dry region south of Judah, through which envoys traveled to Egypt with gifts.
[^9]: Hebrew *saraf*, as in 14:29.
[^10]: *Rahab*, "arrogance, storming", is the name of a sea monster, a picture of chaos (51:9; Job 26:12), used also as a name for Egypt (Psalm 87:4). *Who sits still* may mean "the do-nothing". The Hebrew phrase is difficult.
[^11]: The vowel points read "forever" (*la'ad*). The same consonants can be read "as a witness" (*le'ed*).
[^12]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, teaching, law".
[^13]: Literally "make the Holy One of Israel cease from before us".
[^14]: A play on words: *nanus*, "we will flee" (on horses, *sus*), and *qal*, "swift", with *yiqqallu*, "will be swift".
[^15]: Hebrew *ashrei*: happy, fortunate; traditionally "blessed".
[^16]: Or "For a people will live in Zion, in Jerusalem".
[^17]: Or "your teachers". The Hebrew form can be singular or plural. Also later in the verse.
[^18]: Literally "and heavy is the lifting up". Hebrew *massa'ah*, related to *massa* (verse 6), here perhaps rising smoke.
[^19]: Or "His spirit". Hebrew *ruach*.
[^20]: The Hebrew text is written "against her" but read "against them".
[^21]: Hebrew *tofteh*, a form of *Topheth*, the burning place in the Valley of Hinnom outside Jerusalem where children were sacrificed (2 Kings 23:10; Jeremiah 7:31).
[^22]: Hebrew *melekh*, "king", perhaps also a play on *Molekh* (Molech), the god to whom children were burned there (Leviticus 18:21; 2 Kings 23:10).
