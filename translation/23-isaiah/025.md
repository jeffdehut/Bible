**1** LORD,[^1] you are my God; I will exalt you; I will praise your name. For you have done wonderful things, plans formed long ago, faithful and sure.[^2]

**2** For you have made the city a heap of rubble, the fortified town a ruin. The palace of foreigners is a city no more; it will never be rebuilt.

**3** Therefore a strong people will honor you; the city of ruthless nations will fear you.

**4** For you have been a refuge for the poor, a refuge for the needy in their distress, a shelter from the storm and a shade from the heat. For the breath of the ruthless is like a storm against a wall.

**5** Like heat in a dry land, you subdue the uproar of foreigners. As heat is cooled by the shade of a cloud, so the song of the ruthless is silenced.

**6** On this mountain the LORD of Armies[^3] will prepare for all peoples a feast of rich food, a feast of aged wine,[^4] of rich food full of marrow, of aged wine well refined.

**7** On this mountain he will swallow up the shroud that covers all peoples, the sheet that is spread over all nations.

**8** He will swallow up death forever.[^5] The Lord GOD will wipe away the tears from every face and remove the disgrace of his people from all the earth. For the LORD has spoken.

**9** On that day they will say, "Look, this is our God; we waited for him, and he has saved us. This is the LORD; we waited for him. Let us rejoice and be glad in his salvation."

**10** For the hand of the LORD will rest on this mountain. But Moab will be trampled down where he stands, as straw is trampled down in a dung pit.[^6]

**11** He will spread out his hands in it, as a swimmer spreads out his hands to swim, but he will bring down his pride, for all the skill of his hands.[^7]

**12** The high fortress of your walls he will bring down, lay low and cast to the ground, to the dust.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *emunah omen*, two words from the root *aman*, "be firm" (7:9).
[^3]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^4]: Literally "of lees", wine left to mature on its sediment. Also later in the verse.
[^5]: Or "in victory". Hebrew *lanetsach*. Paul quotes this line in 1 Corinthians 15:54, "Death has been swallowed up in victory". Revelation 7:17 and 21:4 echo the next line.
[^6]: The Hebrew text is written "in the water of a dung pit" but read "in a dung pit". *Dung pit* (*madmenah*) sounds like Madmen, a town of Moab (Jeremiah 48:2).
[^7]: *He* in the first line is probably Moab, and in the second the LORD. *Skill* (Hebrew *arbot*) is uncertain; perhaps "the tricks" or "the strokes" of his hands.
