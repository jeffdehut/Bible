**1** On that day seven women will take hold of one man and say, "We will eat our own bread and wear our own clothes; only let us be called by your name. Take away our disgrace!"[^1]

**2** On that day the branch[^2] of the LORD[^3] will be beautiful and glorious, and the fruit of the land will be the pride and splendor of the survivors of Israel.

**3** Whoever is left in Zion and remains in Jerusalem will be called holy, everyone in Jerusalem who is recorded for life.[^4]

**4** When the Lord has washed away the filth of the daughters of Zion and rinsed the bloodstains of Jerusalem from within her by a spirit[^5] of judgment and a spirit of burning,

**5** then the LORD will create over the whole site of Mount Zion and over her assemblies a cloud by day, and smoke and the glow of a flaming fire by night.[^6] For over all the glory there will be a canopy.[^7]

**6** It will be a shelter, a shade from the heat by day, and a refuge and hiding place from storm and rain.

[^1]: The shame of being unmarried and childless. So many men will have died in war (3:25) that women will plead for the protection of a husband's name.
[^2]: Hebrew *tsemach*, "sprout, growth". Here it may be the new growth of the land, matching "the fruit of the land". In Jeremiah 23:5; 33:15 and Zechariah 3:8; 6:12, "the Branch" is a title of a coming king of David's line, and many later readers took it so here: the Aramaic Targum reads "the anointed one (Messiah) of the LORD".
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Literally "everyone written for life", that is, entered in the register of the living.
[^5]: Or "wind" or "breath". Hebrew *ruach*.
[^6]: The cloud and the fire recall the pillar that led Israel through the wilderness (Exodus 13:21–22).
[^7]: Hebrew *chuppah*, a covering or canopy, the word later used for the canopy over a bride and groom (Psalm 19:6, 19:5 in most English Bibles; Joel 2:16).
