**1** Sing, barren woman, you who have not given birth! Break into song and shout, you who have not been in labor! For the children of the desolate woman will be more than the children of the married woman, says Yahweh.[^1]

**2** Enlarge the place of your tent; let the curtains of your dwellings be stretched out; do not hold back. Lengthen your ropes and strengthen your stakes.

**3** For you will spread out to the right and to the left. Your offspring will possess the nations and will settle the desolate cities.

**4** Do not be afraid, for you will not be put to shame; do not be disgraced, for you will not be humiliated. You will forget the shame of your youth, and you will no longer remember the disgrace of your widowhood.

**5** For your husband[^2] is your Maker; Yahweh of Armies[^3] is his name. Your Redeemer[^4] is the Holy One of Israel; he is called the God of all the earth.

**6** For Yahweh has called you back like a wife abandoned and grieved in spirit, like a wife married in her youth who has been rejected, says your God.

**7** For a brief moment I abandoned you, but with great compassion I will gather you.

**8** In a flood of anger[^5] I hid my face from you for a moment, but with everlasting faithful love[^6] I will have compassion on you, says Yahweh, your Redeemer.

**9** For this is like the waters of Noah to me:[^7] as I swore that the waters of Noah would never again pass over the earth, so I have sworn that I will not be angry with you or rebuke you.

**10** For the mountains may depart and the hills be shaken, but my faithful love will not depart from you, and my covenant of peace will not be shaken, says Yahweh, who has compassion on you.

**11** Afflicted one, storm-tossed and not comforted, look, I will set your stones in black mortar[^8] and lay your foundations with sapphires.[^9]

**12** I will make your battlements of rubies, your gates of sparkling jewels, and all your walls of precious stones.[^10]

**13** All your children will be taught by Yahweh,[^11] and great will be the peace of your children.

**14** You will be established in righteousness. You will be far from oppression, for you will have nothing to fear, and from terror, for it will not come near you.

**15** If anyone stirs up trouble,[^12] it will not be from me; whoever stirs up trouble against you will fall because of you.

**16** Look, I have created the smith who blows on the coals and produces a weapon fit for its work. I have also created the destroyer to wreak havoc.

**17** No weapon forged against you will succeed, and you will refute every tongue that rises against you in judgment. This is the heritage of the servants of Yahweh,[^13] and their vindication[^14] comes from me, declares Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. *You* is feminine singular in this chapter: Zion is addressed as a wife and mother. Galatians 4:27 quotes this verse.
[^2]: Hebrew *bo'alayikh*, "your husband, your master", from *ba'al*, "lord, owner, husband".
[^3]: *Yahweh of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^4]: Hebrew *go'el* (41:14). Also in verse 8.
[^5]: Hebrew *shetsef qetsef*, a sound play found only here. *Shetsef* probably means "flood" or "surge".
[^6]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Also in verse 10.
[^7]: Literally "For the waters of Noah this is to me". Yahweh's oath after the flood is in Genesis 8:21–22 and 9:11.
[^8]: Hebrew *pukh*, the black paint women used around their eyes (2 Kings 9:30), here perhaps a dark mortar that sets off the stones.
[^9]: Probably lapis lazuli, a deep blue stone (Exodus 24:10). The precious stones of verses 11–12 cannot all be identified with certainty. Revelation 21:18–21 echoes these verses.
[^10]: *Battlements* is literally "suns", perhaps shields or pinnacles that shine in the sun; *rubies* is Hebrew *kadkod*; *sparkling jewels* is *avne eqdach*, "stones of fire", perhaps garnets or beryl; *walls* is literally "border".
[^11]: Literally "will be disciples of Yahweh" (50:4). John 6:45 quotes this line.
[^12]: Hebrew *gur*, "stir up strife". The same word can mean "live as a foreigner".
[^13]: From here to the end of the book, Yahweh's servants are plural: those in Israel who are faithful to him (56:6; 63:17; 65:8–15; 66:14).
[^14]: Hebrew *tsedaqah*, "righteousness", here the right that Yahweh upholds for them.
