**1** Come near, you nations, and hear! Pay attention, you peoples! Let the earth hear, and all that fills it, the world and all that comes from it.[^1]

**2** For Yahweh[^2] is angry with all the nations and furious with all their armies. He has devoted them to destruction;[^3] he has handed them over to slaughter.

**3** Their slain will be thrown out, and the stench of their corpses will rise. The mountains will melt with their blood.

**4** All the army of the heavens[^4] will rot away, and the heavens will be rolled up like a scroll. All their army will wither, as a leaf withers and falls from the vine, like a withered fig from the fig tree.[^5]

**5** For my sword has drunk its fill in the heavens. Look, it comes down on Edom,[^6] on the people I have devoted to destruction, to judge them.

**6** Yahweh has a sword; it is filled with blood. It is gorged with fat, with the blood of lambs and goats, with the fat of the kidneys of rams. For Yahweh has a sacrifice in Bozrah[^7] and a great slaughter in the land of Edom.

**7** Wild oxen[^8] will go down with them, and young bulls with the mighty steers. Their land will drink its fill of blood, and their soil will be gorged with fat.

**8** For Yahweh has a day of vengeance, a year of repayment, to uphold the cause of Zion.[^9]

**9** Edom's streams will be turned into pitch, and its soil into sulfur; its land will become burning pitch.[^10]

**10** Night and day it will not be quenched; its smoke will go up forever.[^11] From generation to generation it will lie in ruins; forever and ever no one will pass through it.

**11** The pelican and the owl[^12] will possess it; the great owl and the raven will live there. He will stretch over it the measuring line of chaos and the plumb line of emptiness.[^13]

**12** Its nobles will have nothing there to call a kingdom, and all its princes will come to nothing.[^14]

**13** Thorns will grow up in its fortresses, nettles and thistles in its strongholds. It will be a haunt for jackals, a home[^15] for ostriches.

**14** Desert creatures will meet with hyenas, and the wild goat will call to its fellow.[^16] There too the night creature[^17] will settle and find for herself a place to rest.

**15** There the owl[^18] will nest and lay eggs; it will hatch them and gather its young in its shade. There too the kites will gather, each one with its mate.

**16** Search in the scroll of Yahweh, and read: Not one of these will be missing; none will be without its mate. For it is my mouth that has commanded it, and his Spirit that has gathered them.[^19]

**17** He has cast the lot for them, and his hand has divided the land among them with the measuring line. They will possess it forever; they will live in it from generation to generation.

[^1]: Or "all its offspring". Hebrew *tse'etsa'im*, what comes out of the earth: its plants, or the living things it brings forth.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *hecherim*, "devote to destruction": to give over to God by destroying completely (11:15). Also in verse 5, "the people I have devoted to destruction".
[^4]: Hebrew *tseva ha-shamayim*, the sun, moon and stars, pictured as an army. *Tseva* is the word for the nations' *armies* in verse 2.
[^5]: Revelation 6:13–14 echoes this verse; compare Mark 13:25.
[^6]: The land south and east of the Dead Sea, whose people were descended from Esau, Jacob's brother (Genesis 36). Edom was often Israel's enemy, and here it stands for all the nations hostile to Zion (compare 63:1–6).
[^7]: The chief city of Edom (63:1).
[^8]: Hebrew *re'emim*, traditionally "unicorns". The oxen and bulls of this verse may stand for Edom's leaders and warriors.
[^9]: Or "for Zion's cause", that is, to take up Zion's case against her enemies.
[^10]: The burning pitch and sulfur recall the destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah (Genesis 19:24).
[^11]: Revelation 14:11 and 19:3 echo this line.
[^12]: Hebrew *qa'at*, perhaps a pelican or a kind of owl (Leviticus 11:18; Psalm 102:7), and *qippod*, perhaps an owl or a hedgehog (14:23). The identification of many of the creatures in verses 11–15 is uncertain.
[^13]: Literally "the line of *tohu* and the stones of *bohu*", the words used in Genesis 1:2 for the earth before creation, "formless and empty". The builder's tools are used to undo the land. The *stones* are the weights of a plumb line.
[^14]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "Its nobles, and there is no kingdom there they will call, and all its princes will be nothing."
[^15]: Hebrew *chatsir*, usually "grass", but here probably a form of *chatser*, "courtyard, dwelling".
[^16]: *Desert creatures*, *hyenas* and *wild goats* are named also in 13:21–22. *Wild goat* is Hebrew *sa'ir*, "hairy one", perhaps a goat-demon thought to haunt ruins (13:21; Leviticus 17:7).
[^17]: Hebrew *lilit*, found only here. It may be a night bird, or the name of a female demon of the night known from Mesopotamian texts and later Jewish tradition as Lilith.
[^18]: Hebrew *qippoz*, found only here, perhaps an owl or a kind of snake.
[^19]: Literally "For my mouth, it has commanded, and his Spirit, he has gathered them." The Hebrew moves from "my" to "his". *Spirit* is Hebrew *ruach*, "spirit, wind, breath".
