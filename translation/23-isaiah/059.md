**1** Look, the hand of the LORD[^1] is not too short to save, nor his ear too dull to hear.

**2** But your wrongdoings have separated you from your God, and your sins have hidden his face from you, so that he does not hear.

**3** For your hands are defiled with blood and your fingers with guilt. Your lips have spoken lies; your tongue mutters wickedness.

**4** No one brings a case justly;[^2] no one goes to law honestly. They trust in empty words and speak lies; they conceive trouble and give birth to evil.

**5** They hatch the eggs of vipers and weave spiders' webs. Whoever eats their eggs dies, and when one is crushed, a snake hatches out.

**6** Their webs will not become clothing; they cannot cover themselves with what they make. Their works are works of evil, and acts of violence are in their hands.

**7** Their feet run to evil, and they hurry to shed innocent blood. Their thoughts are thoughts of evil; ruin and destruction are on their highways.

**8** They do not know the way of peace, and there is no justice in their tracks. They have made their paths crooked; no one who walks on them knows peace.[^3]

**9** That is why justice is far from us, and righteousness does not reach us. We hope for light, but look, there is darkness; for brightness, but we walk in gloom.

**10** We grope along the wall like the blind; we grope like those who have no eyes. We stumble at noon as in the twilight; among the strong we are like the dead.[^4]

**11** We all growl like bears; we moan and moan like doves. We hope for justice, but there is none; for salvation, but it is far from us.

**12** For our rebellious acts are many before you, and our sins testify against us. Our rebellious acts are with us, and we know our wrongdoings:

**13** rebelling and denying the LORD, turning away from following our God, speaking oppression and revolt, conceiving and uttering lies from the heart.

**14** So justice is driven back, and righteousness stands far away. Truth has stumbled in the public square, and honesty cannot enter.

**15** Truth is missing, and whoever turns away from evil makes himself a prey. The LORD saw it, and it was evil in his eyes that there was no justice.

**16** He saw that there was no one; he was appalled that there was no one to intervene.[^5] So his own arm brought him salvation, and his righteousness upheld him.

**17** He put on righteousness as a breastplate, and the helmet of salvation on his head. He put on garments of vengeance for clothing and wrapped himself in zeal as in a cloak.[^6]

**18** According to what they have done, so he will repay: fury to his adversaries, retribution to his enemies; to the coastlands[^7] he will repay retribution.

**19** So from the west they will fear the name of the LORD, and from the rising of the sun his glory. For he will come like a rushing river, driven by the wind of the LORD.[^8]

**20** A Redeemer[^9] will come to Zion, to those in Jacob who turn from rebellion, declares the LORD.[^10]

**21** As for me, this is my covenant with them, says the LORD: My Spirit, who is on you, and my words that I have put in your mouth will not depart from your mouth, or from the mouths of your children, or from the mouths of your children's children, says the LORD, from now on and forever.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "No one calls in righteousness".
[^3]: Romans 3:15–17 quotes verses 7–8 from the Septuagint.
[^4]: Hebrew *ashmannim*, a word found only here, perhaps "among the vigorous" or "in desolate places".
[^5]: Hebrew *mafgia*, the verb translated "made intercession" in 53:12.
[^6]: Paul takes up the images of this verse in Ephesians 6:14–17 and 1 Thessalonians 5:8.
[^7]: Hebrew *iyyim*, the islands and coasts of the Mediterranean (11:11), and in these chapters the most distant lands.
[^8]: Or "breath" or "Spirit". Hebrew *ruach*. Literally "like a narrow river, the wind of the LORD driving it". The words can also be read "When the enemy comes like a river, the Spirit of the LORD will raise a banner against him", taking *tsar* as "enemy" and *nosesah* from *nes*, "banner".
[^9]: Hebrew *go'el* (41:14).
[^10]: Romans 11:26–27 quotes verses 20–21 from the Septuagint: "The Deliverer will come from Zion; he will turn away ungodliness from Jacob. And this will be my covenant with them."
