**1** Woe to you, destroyer, you who have not been destroyed, you traitor, whom no one has betrayed! When you have finished destroying, you will be destroyed; when you have done with betraying,[^1] they will betray you.

**2** LORD,[^2] be gracious to us; we wait for you. Be their strength[^3] every morning, our salvation in time of distress.

**3** At the sound of the tumult, peoples flee; when you rise up, nations scatter.

**4** Your plunder is gathered as the caterpillar gathers; as locusts swarm, people swarm over it.

**5** The LORD is exalted, for he dwells on high; he has filled Zion with justice and righteousness.

**6** He will be the stability of your times, a wealth of salvation, of wisdom and knowledge. The fear of the LORD is Zion's treasure.[^4]

**7** Look, their heroes[^5] cry out in the streets; the envoys of peace weep bitterly.

**8** The highways are deserted; no one travels the roads. He has broken the treaty and despised the cities;[^6] he has no regard for anyone.

**9** The land mourns and languishes; Lebanon is ashamed and withers. Sharon is like the Arabah,[^7] and Bashan and Carmel shake off their leaves.

**10** Now I will arise, says the LORD; now I will lift myself up; now I will be exalted.

**11** You conceive chaff and give birth to straw; your breath[^8] is a fire that will consume you.

**12** The peoples will be burned to lime, like cut thorns set on fire.

**13** Hear, you who are far away, what I have done; and you who are near, acknowledge my might.

**14** The sinners in Zion are terrified; trembling has seized the godless: "Who of us can live with the devouring fire? Who of us can live with everlasting burning?"

**15** The one who walks in righteousness and speaks what is right, who rejects gain from extortion, who shakes his hands so that they hold no bribe, who stops his ears from hearing of bloodshed and shuts his eyes from looking at evil,[^9]

**16** he will dwell on the heights; his refuge will be the fortress of the rocks. His bread will be given him; his water will not fail.

**17** Your eyes will see the king in his beauty; they will see a land that stretches far away.

**18** Your mind will think back on the terror: "Where is the one who counted? Where is the one who weighed the tribute? Where is the one who counted the towers?"[^10]

**19** You will no longer see the insolent people,[^11] a people of speech too obscure to understand, with a stammering tongue that no one can make sense of.

**20** Look at Zion, the city of our festivals! Your eyes will see Jerusalem, a peaceful dwelling, a tent that will not be moved, whose stakes will never be pulled up and none of whose ropes will be broken.

**21** But there the LORD will be majestic for us, a place of rivers and broad streams, where no galley with oars will go, and no mighty ship will pass.

**22** For the LORD is our judge, the LORD is our lawgiver, the LORD is our king; he will save us.

**23** Your ropes hang loose; they cannot hold the mast firm in its place or spread the sail.[^12] Then plunder in abundance will be divided; even the lame will carry off the spoil.

**24** No one who lives there will say, "I am sick"; the people who live there will be forgiven their guilt.

[^1]: Hebrew *ka-nelotkha*, a word found only here, probably "when you have finished".
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "their arm". The prayer moves from "us" to "them" and back.
[^4]: Literally "his treasure".
[^5]: Hebrew *er'ellam*, a word of uncertain meaning, perhaps "their heroes" or "their messengers".
[^6]: The Great Isaiah Scroll from Qumran (1QIsa-a) reads "despised the witnesses", a difference of one similar letter.
[^7]: *Sharon*, *Bashan* and *Carmel* were fertile, wooded regions. The *Arabah* is the dry rift valley around the Jordan and the Dead Sea.
[^8]: Or "your spirit". Hebrew *ruach*.
[^9]: Compare the questions and answers of Psalms 15 and 24:3–6.
[^10]: Officials of the enemy who counted and weighed the tribute and surveyed the city's defenses. Paul echoes this verse in 1 Corinthians 1:20.
[^11]: Hebrew *no'az*, a word found only here, perhaps "fierce" or "insolent".
[^12]: *Your* is feminine singular, perhaps addressing the enemy pictured as a disabled ship.
