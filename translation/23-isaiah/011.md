**1** A shoot will come out from the stump of Jesse,[^1] and a branch from his roots will bear fruit.[^2]

**2** The Spirit of the LORD[^3] will rest on him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and might, the spirit of knowledge and of the fear of the LORD.[^4]

**3** His delight will be in the fear of the LORD.[^5] He will not judge by what his eyes see, or decide by what his ears hear.

**4** He will judge the poor with righteousness and decide with fairness for the humble of the earth. He will strike the earth with the rod of his mouth, and with the breath of his lips he will put the wicked to death.[^6]

**5** Righteousness will be the belt around his waist, and faithfulness the belt around his hips.

**6** The wolf will live with the lamb, and the leopard will lie down with the young goat. The calf and the young lion and the fattened calf will be together, and a little child will lead them.

**7** The cow and the bear will graze; their young will lie down together, and the lion will eat straw like the ox.

**8** The nursing child will play over the hole of the cobra, and the weaned child will put his hand into the viper's den.

**9** They will not harm or destroy on all my holy mountain, for the earth will be full of the knowledge of the LORD, as the waters cover the sea.[^7]

**10** On that day the root of Jesse will stand as a signal for the peoples. The nations will seek him, and his resting place will be glorious.[^8]

**11** On that day the Lord will reach out his hand a second time to reclaim the remnant of his people that is left, from Assyria, from Egypt, from Pathros, from Cush, from Elam, from Shinar, from Hamath and from the coastlands of the sea.[^9]

**12** He will raise a signal flag for the nations and gather the outcasts of Israel; he will bring together the scattered people of Judah from the four corners of the earth.

**13** The jealousy of Ephraim will depart, and the adversaries of Judah will be cut off. Ephraim will not be jealous of Judah, and Judah will not be hostile to Ephraim.[^10]

**14** They will swoop down on the slopes of the Philistines to the west;[^11] together they will plunder the people of the east. They will lay their hands on Edom and Moab, and the Ammonites will obey them.

**15** The LORD will utterly destroy[^12] the gulf of the Sea of Egypt.[^13] With his scorching wind[^14] he will wave his hand over the River[^15] and split it into seven streams, so that people can cross it in sandals.

**16** There will be a highway for the remnant of his people that is left from Assyria, as there was for Israel on the day they came up from the land of Egypt.

[^1]: Jesse was the father of King David (1 Samuel 16). The *stump* suggests a family tree that has been cut down (compare 6:13; 10:33–34).
[^2]: Hebrew *netser*, "branch, shoot". Matthew 2:23, "He will be called a Nazarene", is often thought to echo this word. Both Jewish and Christian interpreters have read verses 1–10 of the Messiah: the Aramaic Targum begins "A king will come out from the sons of Jesse, and the anointed one (Messiah) will grow up from his sons' sons". The Septuagint reads "and a flower will come up from the root".
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Or "the Spirit of wisdom … the Spirit of counsel … the Spirit of knowledge". Hebrew *ruach*, "spirit, wind, breath". The Septuagint translates "the fear of the LORD" here as "godliness", and "the fear of the LORD" in verse 3 as "the fear of God", so that it lists seven qualities. This became the basis of the traditional "seven gifts of the Holy Spirit".
[^5]: Literally "his smelling", that is, his enjoyment, as of a pleasing fragrance.
[^6]: Or "the spirit of his lips". Hebrew *ruach*. 2 Thessalonians 2:8 echoes this line.
[^7]: Habakkuk 2:14 repeats the second half of this verse, and Isaiah 65:25 repeats parts of verses 6–9.
[^8]: Literally "his resting place will be glory". Romans 15:12 quotes this verse from the Septuagint: "The root of Jesse will come, the one who rises to rule the nations; in him the nations will hope."
[^9]: *Pathros* is southern (Upper) Egypt; *Cush*, the region south of Egypt (ancient Nubia); *Elam*, a kingdom east of Babylonia, in what is now southwestern Iran; *Shinar*, Babylonia; *Hamath*, a city in Syria; *the coastlands* are the islands and coasts of the Mediterranean.
[^10]: *Ephraim*, the northern kingdom, and *Judah*, the southern kingdom, had been enemies since they divided (7:17).
[^11]: Literally "on the shoulder of the Philistines", the low hills toward the coast.
[^12]: Hebrew *hecherim*, "devote to destruction".
[^13]: Literally "the tongue of the Sea of Egypt", probably the Gulf of Suez on the Red Sea.
[^14]: Hebrew *ba'yam*, a word found only here. Perhaps "scorching" or "mighty". Or "With the might of his breath".
[^15]: The Euphrates.
