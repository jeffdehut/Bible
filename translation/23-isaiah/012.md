**1** On that day you will say,[^1] "I will praise you, Yahweh.[^2] Though you were angry with me, your anger has turned away, and you comfort me.[^3]

**2** Look, God is my salvation; I will trust and not be afraid. For Yah, Yahweh, is my strength and my song;[^4] he has become my salvation."

**3** With joy you will draw water from the springs of salvation.[^5]

**4** On that day you will say, "Give thanks to Yahweh; call on his name. Make known his deeds among the peoples; declare that his name is exalted.

**5** Sing praises to Yahweh, for he has done glorious things; let this be known in all the earth.

**6** Shout aloud and sing for joy, you who live in Zion,[^6] for great among you is the Holy One of Israel."

[^1]: *You* is singular in verse 1, and plural in verses 3–4: first one voice, then the whole people.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Or "may your anger turn away, and may you comfort me".
[^4]: Hebrew *Yah YHWH*. *Yah* is a short form of the name Yahweh. The line repeats Exodus 15:2 and Psalm 118:14. *My song* may also mean "my might".
[^5]: Or "wells".
[^6]: Literally "you inhabitant of Zion", feminine singular: Zion's people are addressed as a woman.
