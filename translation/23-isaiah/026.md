**1** On that day this song will be sung in the land of Judah: "We have a strong city; he sets up salvation as its walls and ramparts.[^1]

**2** Open the gates, so that the righteous nation may enter, the nation that keeps faith.

**3** You keep in perfect peace[^2] the one whose mind is steadfast,[^3] because he trusts in you.

**4** Trust in Yahweh[^4] forever, for in Yah, Yahweh,[^5] you have an everlasting Rock.

**5** For he has brought down those who live on high, the lofty city. He lays it low, lays it low to the ground, and casts it down to the dust.

**6** The foot tramples it, the feet of the poor, the steps of the needy."

**7** The path of the righteous is level; you, the Upright One, make smooth the way of the righteous.[^6]

**8** Yes, Yahweh, in the path of your judgments we wait for you. Your name and your renown are the desire of our soul.

**9** My soul longs for you in the night; yes, my spirit within me seeks you at dawn. For when your judgments come upon the earth, the inhabitants of the world learn righteousness.

**10** If the wicked are shown favor, they do not learn righteousness. In a land of honesty they act unjustly and do not see the majesty of Yahweh.

**11** Yahweh, your hand is lifted up, but they do not see it. Let them see your zeal for your people and be ashamed; let the fire meant for your enemies consume them.

**12** Yahweh, you will establish peace for us; indeed, all that we have done, you have done for us.

**13** Yahweh our God, other lords besides you have ruled over us, but your name alone do we honor.[^7]

**14** The dead do not live; the shades[^8] do not rise. That is why you punished them and destroyed them, and wiped out all memory of them.

**15** You have enlarged the nation, Yahweh; you have enlarged the nation; you are glorified. You have extended all the borders of the land.

**16** Yahweh, in distress they came looking for you; they poured out a whispered prayer when your discipline was on them.[^9]

**17** As a pregnant woman about to give birth writhes and cries out in her pains, so were we because of you, Yahweh.

**18** We were pregnant, we writhed, but we gave birth only to wind. We have not brought salvation to the earth, and no inhabitants of the world have been born.[^10]

**19** Your dead will live; my dead bodies will rise.[^11] Wake up and shout for joy, you who dwell in the dust! For your dew is a dew of light,[^12] and the earth will give birth to the shades.[^13]

**20** Go, my people, enter your rooms and shut your door behind you.[^14] Hide for a little while, until the wrath has passed by.

**21** For look, Yahweh is coming out from his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their guilt. The earth will uncover the blood shed on it and will no longer cover its slain.

[^1]: Literally "salvation he sets up, walls and rampart". The song may end at verse 6; the prayer that follows runs to verse 19.
[^2]: Literally "peace, peace". Hebrew *shalom shalom*, doubled for emphasis.
[^3]: Hebrew *yetser*, "what is formed": the mind, its purpose and inclination.
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^5]: Hebrew *Yah YHWH*. *Yah* is a short form of the name Yahweh (12:2).
[^6]: Or "the path of the righteous is level; you make level the way of the righteous". Hebrew *yashar* can be a title, "Upright One", or mean "level".
[^7]: Literally "only by you do we call your name to mind".
[^8]: Hebrew *repha'im*, the spirits of the dead (14:9). Also in verse 19.
[^9]: The Hebrew of this verse is difficult. *Came looking for you* is Hebrew *peqadukha*, perhaps "they missed you".
[^10]: Literally "have not fallen", that is, have not been born.
[^11]: Literally "my corpse", a collective: the dead of the speaker's own people.
[^12]: Or "a dew of herbs". Hebrew *orot* can mean "lights" or "plants" (2 Kings 4:39).
[^13]: This verse is often read, with Daniel 12:2, as a promise that the dead will rise. Others take it as an image of the nation's revival, as in Ezekiel 37.
[^14]: The Hebrew text is written "your doors" but read "your door".
