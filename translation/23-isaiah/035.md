**1** The wilderness and the dry land will be glad; the desert[^1] will rejoice and blossom like the crocus.[^2]

**2** It will blossom abundantly and rejoice, yes, rejoice with shouts of joy. The glory of Lebanon will be given to it, the splendor of Carmel and Sharon.[^3] They will see the glory of Yahweh,[^4] the splendor of our God.

**3** Strengthen the weak hands, and make firm the knees that give way.[^5]

**4** Say to those whose hearts are anxious, "Be strong, do not be afraid. Look, your God will come with vengeance; with the recompense of God he will come and save you."

**5** Then the eyes of the blind will be opened and the ears of the deaf unstopped.

**6** Then the lame will leap like a deer, and the tongue of the mute will shout for joy.[^6] For water will burst out in the wilderness and streams in the desert.

**7** The burning sand[^7] will become a pool, and the thirsty ground springs of water. In the haunts where jackals lay, grass will become reeds and papyrus.[^8]

**8** A highway will be there, a road, and it will be called the Way of Holiness. The unclean will not travel on it; it will be for those who walk that way, and fools will not wander onto it.[^9]

**9** No lion will be there, and no ravenous beast will go up on it; they will not be found there. But the redeemed[^10] will walk there.

**10** Those whom Yahweh has ransomed will return and come to Zion with singing. Everlasting joy will be on their heads. Gladness and joy will overtake them, and sorrow and sighing will flee away.[^11]

[^1]: Hebrew *aravah*, the dry steppe, and also the name of the rift valley of the Jordan and the Dead Sea (Deuteronomy 1:1). Also in verse 6.
[^2]: Hebrew *chavatselet*, a meadow flower, perhaps a crocus or an asphodel; traditionally "rose" (Song of Songs 2:1).
[^3]: Lebanon was famous for its cedars, and Carmel and Sharon, on the coast of Israel, for their woods and pastures (33:9).
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^5]: Hebrews 12:12 echoes this verse.
[^6]: Jesus' answer to John the Baptist in Matthew 11:5 and Luke 7:22 echoes verses 5–6.
[^7]: Hebrew *sharav*, "scorching heat", perhaps the shimmering heat of the desert or a mirage (49:10).
[^8]: Literally "In the haunt of jackals, its resting place, grass for reed and papyrus." Or "grass and reeds and papyrus". The meaning is uncertain.
[^9]: Or "it will be for them; whoever walks that way, even fools, will not go astray". The Hebrew is difficult.
[^10]: Hebrew *ge'ulim*, those whom Yahweh has rescued as next of kin (41:14).
[^11]: Verse 10 is repeated in 51:11.
