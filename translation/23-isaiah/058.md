**1** Shout aloud;[^1] do not hold back! Raise your voice like a ram's horn! Declare to my people their rebellion, and to the house of Jacob their sins.

**2** Yet day after day they seek me and delight to know my ways, as if they were a nation that did what is right and had not abandoned the justice of its God. They ask me for righteous judgments; they delight in drawing near to God.

**3** "Why have we fasted," they say, "and you do not see? Why have we denied ourselves,[^2] and you do not notice?" Look, on the day of your fast you pursue your own business and drive all your workers hard.[^3]

**4** Look, you fast only to quarrel and fight and to strike with a wicked fist. You cannot fast as you do today and make your voice heard on high.

**5** Is this the kind of fast I choose, a day for a person to deny himself? Is it to bow one's head like a reed and to lie on sackcloth and ashes? Is this what you call a fast, a day acceptable to Yahweh?[^4]

**6** Is not this the fast that I choose: to loosen the chains of wickedness, to untie the cords of the yoke, to set the oppressed free and to break every yoke?

**7** Is it not to share your bread with the hungry and to bring the homeless poor into your house, to cover the naked when you see them, and not to turn away from your own flesh and blood?[^5]

**8** Then your light will break out like the dawn, and your healing will spring up quickly. Your righteousness will go before you, and the glory of Yahweh will be your rear guard.[^6]

**9** Then you will call, and Yahweh will answer; you will cry for help, and he will say, "Here I am." If you remove the yoke from among you, the pointing finger and wicked speech,

**10** if you pour yourself out[^7] for the hungry and satisfy the needs of the afflicted, then your light will rise in the darkness, and your gloom will be like noon.

**11** Yahweh will guide you always and satisfy your needs[^8] in parched places and strengthen your bones. You will be like a watered garden, like a spring whose waters never fail.

**12** Your people[^9] will rebuild the ancient ruins; you will raise up the foundations of many generations. You will be called the Repairer of the Breach, the Restorer of Streets to Live In.

**13** If you keep your feet from trampling the Sabbath,[^10] from doing as you please on my holy day; if you call the Sabbath a delight and the holy day of Yahweh honored; if you honor it by not going your own ways, not pursuing your own business or speaking idle words,[^11]

**14** then you will find your delight in Yahweh, and I will make you ride on the heights of the earth[^12] and feed you with the inheritance of your ancestor Jacob.[^13] For the mouth of Yahweh has spoken.

[^1]: Literally "Call with the throat".
[^2]: Literally "humbled our *nephesh*", the phrase used for fasting on the Day of Atonement (Leviticus 16:29, 31). Also in verses 5 and 10.
[^3]: Or "and exact all your debts". Hebrew *atsevekhem*, perhaps "your laborers" or "your loans".
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^5]: Literally "from your own flesh", your relatives and fellow Israelites.
[^6]: Compare 52:12, and the pillar of cloud and fire that went before and behind Israel (Exodus 14:19–20).
[^7]: Literally "if you offer your *nephesh*". *The needs of the afflicted* is literally "the afflicted *nephesh*".
[^8]: Literally "satisfy your *nephesh*". Hebrew *tsachtsachot*, "parched places", is found only here.
[^9]: Literally "those from you".
[^10]: Literally "If you turn back your foot from the Sabbath".
[^11]: Literally "or speaking a word". *Your own business* is the phrase translated "your own business" in verse 3.
[^12]: Compare Deuteronomy 32:13.
[^13]: Literally "Jacob your father".
