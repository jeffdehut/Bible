**1** Come,[^1] all you who are thirsty, come to the waters! And you who have no money, come, buy grain and eat! Come, buy wine and milk without money and without cost.

**2** Why do you spend money on what is not bread, and your earnings on what does not satisfy? Listen carefully to me, and eat what is good, and you will delight in rich food.[^2]

**3** Turn your ear and come to me; listen, so that you may live. I will make an everlasting covenant with you, my faithful love promised to David.[^3]

**4** Look, I made him a witness to the peoples, a leader and commander for the peoples.

**5** Look, you will call a nation you do not know, and a nation that does not know you will run to you, because of Yahweh[^4] your God, the Holy One of Israel, for he has glorified you.

**6** Seek Yahweh while he may be found; call on him while he is near.

**7** Let the wicked abandon his way, and the evildoer his thoughts. Let him turn back to Yahweh, and he will have compassion on him, to our God, for he will freely forgive.

**8** For my thoughts are not your thoughts, and your ways are not my ways, declares Yahweh.

**9** For as the heavens are higher than the earth, so are my ways higher than your ways, and my thoughts than your thoughts.

**10** For as the rain and the snow come down from the heavens and do not return there without watering the earth and making it bear and sprout, giving seed to the sower and bread to the eater,

**11** so will my word be that goes out from my mouth: it will not return to me empty, without accomplishing what I desire and succeeding in the purpose for which I sent it.

**12** For you will go out with joy and be led out in peace. The mountains and the hills will break into singing before you, and all the trees of the field will clap their hands.

**13** Instead of the thornbush the juniper will grow, and instead of the brier the myrtle will grow. This will make a name for Yahweh, an everlasting sign that will not be cut off.

[^1]: Hebrew *hoy*, usually "Woe to" (1:4). Here it is a call for attention, like the cry of a seller in the market.
[^2]: Literally "and your *nephesh* will delight in fatness". Also in verse 3, "so that your *nephesh* may live".
[^3]: Literally "the sure faithful acts (*chesed*) of David", the lasting love Yahweh promised to David and his house (2 Samuel 7:15–16; Psalm 89:2–5, 29, 89:1–4, 28 in most English Bibles). Acts 13:34 quotes these words from the Septuagint: "I will give you the holy and sure things of David."
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
