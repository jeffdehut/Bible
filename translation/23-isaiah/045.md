**1** This is what the LORD[^1] says to his anointed,[^2] to Cyrus, whose right hand I have grasped to subdue nations before him and to strip kings of their armor,[^3] to open doors before him, so that gates will not be shut:

**2** I will go before you and level the mountains.[^4] I will break down doors of bronze and cut through bars of iron.

**3** I will give you treasures hidden in darkness, riches stored in secret places, so that you may know that I am the LORD, the God of Israel, who calls you by name.

**4** For the sake of Jacob my servant, and Israel my chosen, I call you by your name; I give you a title of honor, though you do not know me.

**5** I am the LORD, and there is no other; apart from me there is no God. I will arm you,[^5] though you do not know me,

**6** so that people may know, from the rising of the sun to its setting, that there is none besides me. I am the LORD, and there is no other.

**7** I form light and create darkness; I make well-being and create disaster.[^6] I, the LORD, do all these things.

**8** Rain down from above, you heavens, and let the clouds pour down righteousness.[^7] Let the earth open, so that salvation may bear fruit, and let righteousness spring up with it. I, the LORD, have created it.

**9** Woe to[^8] the one who quarrels with his Maker,[^9] a pot among the pots of earth! Does the clay say to the one who forms it, "What are you making?" or "Your work has no handles"?[^10]

**10** Woe to the one who says to a father, "What are you fathering?" or to a woman, "What are you giving birth to?"

**11** This is what the LORD says, the Holy One of Israel, and its Maker: Would you question me about things to come, about my children, or give me orders about the work of my hands?[^11]

**12** It was I who made the earth and created humankind on it. My own hands stretched out the heavens, and I commanded all their army.[^12]

**13** I myself have stirred him up in righteousness,[^13] and I will make all his ways straight. He will rebuild my city and set my exiles free, not for a price or a bribe, says the LORD of Armies.[^14]

**14** This is what the LORD says: The wealth of Egypt and the merchandise of Cush, and the Sabeans,[^15] men of great height, will come over to you and be yours.[^16] They will walk behind you, coming over in chains. They will bow down to you and plead with you:[^17] "Surely God is with you, and there is no other; there is no other god."

**15** Truly you are a God who hides himself, the God of Israel, the Savior.[^18]

**16** They will be put to shame and disgraced, all of them; the makers of idols go off together in disgrace.

**17** But Israel is saved by the LORD with an everlasting salvation. You will not be put to shame or disgraced, to ages everlasting.

**18** For this is what the LORD says, who created the heavens (he is God), who formed the earth and made it (he established it; he did not create it to be empty,[^19] but formed it to be inhabited): I am the LORD, and there is no other.

**19** I did not speak in secret, somewhere in a land of darkness. I did not say to the offspring of Jacob, "Seek me in vain." I, the LORD, speak what is right; I declare what is true.[^20]

**20** Gather together and come; draw near together, you survivors of the nations! They have no knowledge, those who carry around their wooden idols and keep praying to a god who cannot save.

**21** Declare it and present your case; let them take counsel together. Who announced this long ago, who declared it from of old? Was it not I, the LORD? There is no other god apart from me, a righteous God and a Savior; there is none besides me.

**22** Turn to me and be saved, all the ends of the earth! For I am God, and there is no other.

**23** By myself I have sworn; a word has gone out from my mouth in righteousness, and it will not turn back: To me every knee will bow, every tongue will swear allegiance.[^21]

**24** "Only in the LORD," it will be said of me,[^22] "are righteousness and strength."[^23] All who raged against him will come to him and be put to shame.

**25** In the LORD all the offspring of Israel will be found righteous[^24] and will glory.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *meshicho*, "his anointed one" (*mashiach*), the title of Israel's kings (1 Samuel 24:7; Psalm 2:2) and the source of the word "Messiah". Only here is it given to a foreign king. The text does not say that Cyrus was anointed with oil: The LORD has chosen and appointed him for a task, as a king was set apart by anointing.
[^3]: Literally "to loosen the loins of kings", that is, to ungird them, taking off the belts that held their weapons. The LORD will "gird" Cyrus in verse 5.
[^4]: Hebrew *hadurim*, a word of uncertain meaning, perhaps "swellings" or "rough places". The Great Isaiah Scroll from Qumran (1QIsa-a) and the Septuagint read "mountains".
[^5]: Literally "I will gird you" (verse 1).
[^6]: Hebrew *shalom*, "peace, well-being", and *ra'*, "evil, harm, disaster". The verse sets the LORD over all that happens, good and bad.
[^7]: Hebrew *tsedeq* and, later in the verse, *tsedaqah*, here God's saving order, which puts things right. Or "victory", "deliverance".
[^8]: Hebrew *hoy*, a cry of mourning, used by the prophets to announce disaster (see 1:4). Also in verse 10.
[^9]: Hebrew *yotser*, "former, potter", from the verb translated "form" in verses 7 and 18. Also later in this verse, "the one who forms it".
[^10]: Literally "and your work: He has no hands". The pot is made to criticize its potter. Compare 29:16; Jeremiah 18:6; Romans 9:20–21.
[^11]: Literally "The things to come, ask me! Concerning my children and concerning the work of my hands, command me!", perhaps said with irony.
[^12]: The stars, pictured as an army (40:26).
[^13]: Hebrew *tsedeq*, here God's right and saving purpose (41:2). *Him* is Cyrus.
[^14]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^15]: The people of Seba (43:3), far to the south.
[^16]: *You* is feminine singular in this verse: Zion is addressed as a woman.
[^17]: Literally "pray to you".
[^18]: The speaker may be the nations of verse 14, or the prophet.
[^19]: Hebrew *tohu*, "formless, empty" (Genesis 1:2). In verse 19, "in vain" is the same word.
[^20]: Literally "I speak righteousness; I declare what is upright".
[^21]: Romans 14:11 quotes this verse, and Philippians 2:10–11 echoes it, following the Septuagint: "every tongue will confess to God".
[^22]: Literally "Only in the LORD, he has said to me" or "of me". The Hebrew is difficult.
[^23]: Hebrew *tsedaqot*, plural, "righteous acts, victories".
[^24]: Or "will be vindicated". Hebrew *yitsdequ*.
