**1** This is what Yahweh[^1] says: Where is your mother's certificate of divorce, with which I sent her away?[^2] Or to which of my creditors did I sell you? Look, it was for your sins that you were sold, and for your rebellious acts that your mother was sent away.

**2** Why was there no one when I came? Why was there no one to answer when I called? Is my hand too short to redeem?[^3] Have I no power to rescue? Look, with a rebuke I dry up the sea; I turn rivers into a wilderness. Their fish rot for lack of water and die of thirst.

**3** I clothe the heavens with darkness and make sackcloth their covering.

**4** The Lord Yahweh has given me the tongue of one who is taught,[^4] so that I may know how to sustain the weary with a word.[^5] Morning by morning he wakens, he wakens my ear to listen like one who is taught.

**5** The Lord Yahweh has opened my ear, and I did not rebel; I did not turn back.

**6** I gave my back to those who struck me, and my cheeks to those who pulled out my beard. I did not hide my face from insults and spitting.[^6]

**7** But the Lord Yahweh helps me; therefore I have not been disgraced. Therefore I have set my face like flint, and I know that I will not be put to shame.

**8** He who proves me right is near.[^7] Who will bring charges against me? Let us stand up together. Who is my accuser?[^8] Let him come near me.

**9** Look, the Lord Yahweh helps me; who will declare me guilty? Look, they will all wear out like a garment; the moth will eat them up.

**10** Who among you fears Yahweh and obeys the voice of his servant? Whoever walks in darkness and has no light, let him trust in the name of Yahweh and rely on his God.

**11** But look, all of you who kindle a fire, who arm yourselves with burning arrows,[^9] walk in the light of your fire and among the burning arrows you have lit! This is what you will receive from my hand: you will lie down in torment.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: A husband who divorced his wife gave her a written certificate (Deuteronomy 24:1; Jeremiah 3:8). Yahweh has given Zion, the mother, no such certificate, and he has not sold her children to pay a debt (compare 2 Kings 4:1). Their exile is not a final break.
[^3]: Hebrew *padah*, "ransom, redeem".
[^4]: Hebrew *limmudim*, "those who are taught, disciples" (8:16). Also later in this verse. Verses 4–11 are the third of the servant passages (see 42:1). The speaker is not named, but verse 10 calls him "his servant".
[^5]: Hebrew *la'ut*, a word found only here, of uncertain meaning, perhaps "to help" or "to answer".
[^6]: Compare the treatment of Jesus in Matthew 26:67; Mark 14:65 and 15:19.
[^7]: Hebrew *matsdiq*, "one who declares in the right, vindicates", a term from the law court. Romans 8:33–34 echoes verses 8–9.
[^8]: Literally "Who is the lord of my judgment?", that is, the one who brings a case against me.
[^9]: Or "firebrands". Hebrew *ziqot*, perhaps flaming arrows or sparks. *Arm yourselves* is literally "gird yourselves".
