**1** Hear this, house of Jacob, you who are called by the name of Israel and have come from the waters of Judah,[^1] you who swear by the name of the LORD[^2] and invoke the God of Israel, but not in truth or righteousness.

**2** For they call themselves after the holy city and lean on the God of Israel; the LORD of Armies[^3] is his name.

**3** The former things I declared long ago; they went out from my mouth, and I announced them. Then suddenly I acted, and they came to pass.

**4** Because I knew that you are stubborn, your neck an iron sinew and your forehead bronze,

**5** I declared them to you long ago; before they came to pass I announced them to you, so that you could not say, "My idol did them; my carved image and my cast image commanded them."

**6** You have heard it; now see it all.[^4] Will you not declare it? From now on I announce to you new things, hidden things that you have not known.

**7** They are created now, not long ago; before today you have not heard of them, so that you could not say, "Look, I knew them."

**8** You have never heard, you have never known; from of old your ear has not been opened. For I knew that you would surely act treacherously, and that you were called a rebel from birth.[^5]

**9** For the sake of my name I hold back my anger; for the sake of my praise I restrain it for you, so as not to cut you off.

**10** Look, I have refined you, but not as silver; I have chosen you in the furnace of affliction.[^6]

**11** For my own sake, for my own sake, I do it; for how can my name be profaned?[^7] I will not give my glory to another.

**12** Listen to me, Jacob, Israel whom I called: I am he;[^8] I am the first, and I am also the last.

**13** My own hand laid the foundation of the earth, and my right hand spread out the heavens. When I call to them, they stand up together.

**14** Gather, all of you, and listen! Who among them declared these things? The one the LORD loves will carry out his purpose against Babylon, and his arm will be against the Chaldeans.[^9]

**15** I, I have spoken; I have called him. I have brought him, and he will succeed in his way.

**16** Come near to me and hear this: From the beginning I have not spoken in secret; from the time it came to be, I have been there. And now the Lord GOD has sent me, and his Spirit.[^10]

**17** This is what the LORD says, your Redeemer,[^11] the Holy One of Israel: I am the LORD your God, who teaches you what is for your good, who leads you in the way you should go.

**18** If only you had paid attention to my commands! Then your peace would have been like a river, and your righteousness like the waves of the sea.

**19** Your offspring would have been like the sand, and your descendants like its grains. Their name would never be cut off or destroyed from before me.

**20** Go out from Babylon! Flee from the Chaldeans! With a shout of joy declare this, proclaim it, send it out to the end of the earth. Say, "The LORD has redeemed his servant Jacob!"

**21** They did not thirst when he led them through the deserts. He made water flow for them from the rock; he split the rock, and the water gushed out.[^12]

**22** There is no peace, says the LORD, for the wicked.[^13]

[^1]: Literally "from the waters of Judah", perhaps "from the spring of Judah", the source of the family. Some scholars read *mimme'e*, "from the body of Judah", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^4]: Literally "You have heard; see all of it".
[^5]: Literally "from the womb".
[^6]: Or "I have tested you". The Hebrew verb *bachar*, "choose", has the sense "test" in later Hebrew and Aramaic. The Great Isaiah Scroll from Qumran (1QIsa-a) reads "I have tested you".
[^7]: Literally "how can it be profaned?"
[^8]: Hebrew *ani hu* (41:4).
[^9]: Or "and his arm will be on the Chaldeans". Literally "and his arm Chaldeans". *The one the LORD loves* is Cyrus (verse 15; 45:1).
[^10]: Or "the Lord GOD and his Spirit have sent me", or "the Lord GOD has sent me with his Spirit". Here a new speaker, the prophet or the servant, speaks of being sent (compare 49:1–6; 61:1). *Spirit* is Hebrew *ruach*.
[^11]: Hebrew *go'el* (41:14).
[^12]: As in the wilderness after the exodus (Exodus 17:6; Numbers 20:11).
[^13]: This line is repeated in 57:21. Each closes a section of chapters 40–66.
