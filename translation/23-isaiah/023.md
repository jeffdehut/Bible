**1** An oracle[^1] concerning Tyre: Wail, you ships of Tarshish![^2] For Tyre is destroyed, without house or harbor.[^3] From the land of Cyprus[^4] the news has reached them.[^5]

**2** Be silent, you who live on the coast, you merchants of Sidon, whom the seafarers have made rich.[^6]

**3** On the great waters came the grain of Shihor;[^7] the harvest of the Nile was her revenue, and she became the marketplace of the nations.

**4** Be ashamed, Sidon, for the sea has spoken, the stronghold of the sea: "I have not been in labor or given birth; I have not raised young men or brought up young women."

**5** When the report reaches Egypt, they will be in anguish at the report about Tyre.

**6** Cross over to Tarshish! Wail, you who live on the coast!

**7** Is this your joyful city,[^8] whose origin is from days of old, whose feet carried her to settle in distant places?

**8** Who has planned this against Tyre, the giver of crowns, whose merchants are princes, whose traders[^9] are the honored of the earth?

**9** Yahweh of Armies[^10] has planned it, to defile the pride of all glory and to humble all the honored of the earth.

**10** Go through your land as the Nile does, Daughter Tarshish; there is no longer any restraint.[^11]

**11** He has stretched out his hand over the sea and made kingdoms tremble. Yahweh has given orders concerning Canaan[^12] to destroy its strongholds.

**12** He said, "You will no longer celebrate, you oppressed virgin Daughter Sidon! Get up, cross over to Cyprus; even there you will find no rest."

**13** Look at the land of the Chaldeans! This is the people that is no more. Assyria assigned it to desert creatures. They set up their siege towers; they stripped its palaces bare and made it a ruin.[^13]

**14** Wail, you ships of Tarshish, for your fortress is destroyed!

**15** On that day Tyre will be forgotten for seventy years, the lifetime of one king.[^14] At the end of seventy years it will be with Tyre as in the song about the prostitute:

**16** "Take a lyre, go around the city, you forgotten prostitute! Play skillfully, sing many songs, so that you will be remembered."

**17** At the end of seventy years Yahweh will come to Tyre.[^15] She will return to her fees[^16] and will prostitute herself with all the kingdoms of the world on the face of the earth.

**18** But her profit and her fees will be holy to Yahweh. They will not be stored up or hoarded, but her profit will go to those who live in Yahweh's presence, so that they may eat their fill and wear fine clothes.[^17]

[^1]: Hebrew *massa*. See 13:1.
[^2]: Large seagoing ships, of the kind that sailed to Tarshish, a distant port in the west, perhaps in Spain (2:16). Also in verse 14.
[^3]: Literally "it is destroyed, from house, from entering". The meaning is uncertain.
[^4]: Hebrew *Kittim*, the people of Cyprus, later used of other peoples of the west. Also in verse 12.
[^5]: Literally "it has been revealed to them".
[^6]: Literally "who cross the sea have filled you".
[^7]: An arm of the Nile in the eastern Delta, here standing for the Nile.
[^8]: Literally "Is this for you the exultant one?"
[^9]: Literally "her Canaanites". The Canaanites of the coast, the Phoenicians, were known as traders, so *Canaanite* came to mean "merchant".
[^10]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. *Yahweh of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^11]: Or "there is no longer a harbor". Hebrew *mezach*, perhaps "belt" or "dockyard". The meaning of the verse is uncertain.
[^12]: Here Phoenicia, the coastal land of Tyre and Sidon.
[^13]: The Hebrew of this verse is difficult. It seems to point to the fate of Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans, as a warning to Tyre.
[^14]: Literally "like the days of one king", a normal human lifetime (Psalm 90:10).
[^15]: Or "will visit". Hebrew *paqad*, to come to someone to deal with them, in blessing or in punishment.
[^16]: Hebrew *etnan*, a prostitute's pay, here an image for Tyre's trade. Also in verse 18.
[^17]: Literally "for a stately covering".
