**1** Go down and sit in the dust, virgin daughter Babylon![^1] Sit on the ground without a throne, daughter of the Chaldeans![^2] For you will no longer be called tender and delicate.

**2** Take millstones and grind flour.[^3] Remove your veil; strip off your skirt; bare your legs and wade through the rivers.

**3** Your nakedness will be exposed; your shame will be seen. I will take vengeance, and I will spare no one.[^4]

**4** Our Redeemer, whose name is Yahweh of Armies,[^5] is the Holy One of Israel.[^6]

**5** Sit in silence and go into darkness, daughter of the Chaldeans, for you will no longer be called the queen of kingdoms.[^7]

**6** I was angry with my people; I profaned my inheritance and handed them over to you. You showed them no mercy; even on the old you made your yoke very heavy.

**7** You said, "I will be queen forever." You did not take these things to heart or think about how it would end.

**8** So now hear this, you lover of pleasure, who sit secure, who say in your heart, "I am, and there is no one besides me.[^8] I will never sit as a widow or know the loss of children."

**9** Both of these will come on you in a moment, in a single day: loss of children and widowhood. They will come on you in full measure, in spite of your many sorceries and the great power of your spells.

**10** You trusted in your wickedness; you said, "No one sees me." Your wisdom and your knowledge led you astray, and you said in your heart, "I am, and there is no one besides me."

**11** But disaster[^9] will come on you, and you will not know how to charm it away.[^10] Ruin will fall on you that you cannot buy off;[^11] catastrophe will come on you suddenly, which you do not foresee.

**12** Keep on, then, with your spells and your many sorceries, which you have labored at since your youth. Perhaps you will succeed; perhaps you will cause terror.

**13** You are worn out by all your counsel. Let them stand up and save you, those who divide up the heavens,[^12] who gaze at the stars, who make known at each new moon what is coming on you.

**14** Look, they are like straw; the fire burns them up. They cannot save themselves[^13] from the power of the flame. This is no coal to warm oneself by, no fire to sit in front of!

**15** That is what they are to you, those you have labored with, those who have traded with you since your youth. Each one wanders off his own way; there is no one to save you.

[^1]: A poetic name for the city of Babylon and its people, pictured as a young woman (compare "Daughter Zion", 1:8). *You* is feminine singular throughout this chapter.
[^2]: The people of southern Babylonia, whose kings ruled the Babylonian empire (13:19).
[^3]: Grinding grain with a hand mill was the work of slave women (Exodus 11:5). The once delicate lady must now work and cross rivers on the way into captivity.
[^4]: Literally "I will not meet a human being", perhaps "no one will stand in my way" or "I will not spare anyone".
[^5]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. *Yahweh of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^6]: This verse may be a cry of praise by Israel, breaking into Yahweh's words. *Redeemer* is Hebrew *go'el* (41:14).
[^7]: Hebrew *geveret*, "mistress, lady", the woman who rules a household or a queen mother. Also in verse 7.
[^8]: Babylon's boast takes up the words Yahweh uses of himself (45:5–6, 18, 21). Zephaniah 2:15 puts the same words in the mouth of Nineveh, and Revelation 18:7–8 echoes verses 7–9.
[^9]: Hebrew *ra'ah*, "evil, disaster", the same word as *wickedness* in verse 10.
[^10]: Hebrew *shachrah*, perhaps "to charm it away" or "its dawning": "you will not know when it is coming".
[^11]: Hebrew *kapper*, "atone, appease, ransom", the word used in 6:7 for atonement.
[^12]: Hebrew *hovre shamayim*, probably astrologers who divide the sky into regions to read the stars.
[^13]: Literally "their *nephesh*".
