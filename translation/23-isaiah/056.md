**1** This is what the LORD[^1] says: Keep justice and do what is right,[^2] for my salvation is about to come, and my righteousness to be revealed.

**2** Happy is the person who does this, the human being who holds fast to it, who keeps the Sabbath without profaning it and keeps his hand from doing any evil.

**3** Let no foreigner who has joined himself to the LORD say, "The LORD will surely separate me from his people." And let no eunuch say, "Look, I am only a dry tree."[^3]

**4** For this is what the LORD says: To the eunuchs who keep my Sabbaths, who choose what pleases me and hold fast to my covenant,

**5** I will give within my house and within my walls a memorial and a name[^4] better than sons and daughters. I will give them an everlasting name that will not be cut off.

**6** And the foreigners who join themselves to the LORD to serve him, to love the name of the LORD, and to be his servants, all who keep the Sabbath without profaning it and hold fast to my covenant,

**7** I will bring to my holy mountain and make them joyful in my house of prayer. Their burnt offerings and their sacrifices will be accepted on my altar, for my house will be called a house of prayer for all peoples.[^5]

**8** The Lord GOD declares, he who gathers the outcasts of Israel: I will gather still others to them besides those already gathered.

**9** All you wild animals of the field, come and devour, all you wild animals in the forest!

**10** Its watchmen[^6] are blind; none of them knows anything. They are all mute dogs that cannot bark. They lie around dreaming;[^7] they love to sleep.

**11** The dogs have a mighty appetite;[^8] they never know when they have had enough. And they are shepherds who have no understanding. They have all turned to their own way, each to his own gain, every one of them.

**12** "Come," they say, "let me get wine, and let us fill ourselves with beer! Tomorrow will be like today, only far better!"

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *tsedaqah*, the word translated "righteousness" later in the verse.
[^3]: The law shut men who had been castrated, and some foreigners, out of the assembly of the LORD (Deuteronomy 23:2–4; 23:1–3 in most English Bibles). A eunuch could have no children to carry on his name.
[^4]: Hebrew *yad va-shem*, literally "a hand and a name": a monument that keeps a name alive (2 Samuel 18:18). *Them* in the next sentence is literally "him", each one of them.
[^5]: Jesus quotes this line in Mark 11:17 when he drives the traders out of the temple courts.
[^6]: The leaders of Israel. *Its* is literally "his", Israel's.
[^7]: Hebrew *hozim*, a word found only here, perhaps "dreaming" or "panting".
[^8]: Literally "are strong of *nephesh*".
