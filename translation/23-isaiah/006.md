**1** In the year that King Uzziah died,[^year] I saw the Lord[^lord] sitting on a throne, high and lifted up, and the hem of his robe filled the temple.[^temple]

**2** Seraphim[^seraphim] stood above him. Each had six wings: with two he covered his face, with two he covered his feet,[^feet] and with two he flew.

**3** And they called to one another: "Holy, holy, holy is Yahweh of Armies![^armies] The whole earth is full of his glory."[^glory]

**4** The doorposts and thresholds[^thresholds] shook at the sound of the one who called, and the house was filled with smoke.

**5** Then I said, "Woe is me! I am ruined![^ruined] For I am a man of unclean lips, and I live among a people of unclean lips, and my eyes have seen the King, Yahweh of Armies!"

**6** Then one of the seraphim flew to me, holding in his hand a glowing coal[^coal] that he had taken with tongs from the altar.

**7** He touched my mouth with it and said, "See, this has touched your lips. Your guilt is taken away, and your sin is atoned for."[^atoned]

**8** Then I heard the voice of the Lord saying, "Whom shall I send? Who will go for us?"[^us] And I said, "Here I am. Send me!"

**9** He said, "Go, and say to this people: 'Keep on hearing, but do not understand; keep on seeing, but do not perceive.'

**10** Make the heart of this people dull,[^dull] make their ears heavy and shut their eyes, so that they may not see with their eyes, hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and turn back and be healed."[^quoted]

**11** Then I said, "How long, Lord?" And he answered, "Until cities lie in ruins with no one living in them, and houses with no people, and the land is left utterly desolate,

**12** until Yahweh sends the people far away, and the deserted places are many in the middle of the land.

**13** Even if a tenth remains in it, it will be burned again,[^burned] like a terebinth or an oak whose stump remains when it is cut down.[^felled] The holy seed is its stump."[^seed]

[^year]: About 740 BC.
[^lord]: Hebrew *Adonai*, "the Lord", not the name Yahweh. Also in verses 8 and 11.
[^temple]: Hebrew *heikhal*, the main hall of the temple, or a palace.
[^seraphim]: Hebrew *seraphim*, probably "burning ones". The same word is used of the venomous snakes in the wilderness (Numbers 21:6) and of a "flying serpent" in Isaiah 14:29 and 30:6. Here they are winged beings that attend the throne of God.
[^feet]: Perhaps their bodies. "Feet" is sometimes a polite word for the private parts (7:20).
[^armies]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. *Yahweh of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verse 5.
[^glory]: Or "His glory is the fullness of the whole earth". The threefold "holy" is echoed in Revelation 4:8, and this verse is sung in Jewish and Christian worship (the *Kedushah* and the *Sanctus*).
[^thresholds]: Literally "the foundations of the thresholds", perhaps the pivots on which the doors turned.
[^ruined]: Or "I am silenced". Hebrew *nidmeti*.
[^coal]: Or "a hot stone".
[^atoned]: Hebrew *kipper*, "atone, make atonement", the word used for the removal of sin by sacrifice.
[^us]: The plural may include the heavenly beings around the throne (compare 1 Kings 22:19–22).
[^dull]: Literally "fat".
[^quoted]: Verses 9–10 are quoted in Matthew 13:14–15; Mark 4:12; Luke 8:10; John 12:40 and Acts 28:26–27, mostly from the Greek Septuagint, which turns the commands into a description: "You will indeed hear but never understand … For the heart of this people has grown dull."
[^burned]: Or "it will be laid waste again".
[^felled]: Or "when it is felled". Hebrew *shalleket*, found also in 1 Chronicles 26:16 as the name of a temple gate.
[^seed]: The Septuagint does not have this sentence.
