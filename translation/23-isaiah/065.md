**1** I let myself be sought by those who did not ask for me; I let myself be found by those who did not seek me. I said, "Here I am, here I am," to a nation that was not called by my name.[^1]

**2** All day long I have held out my hands to a rebellious people, who walk in a way that is not good, following their own thoughts,

**3** a people who provoke me to my face continually, sacrificing in gardens and burning incense on bricks,[^2]

**4** who sit among the graves and spend the night in secret places,[^3] who eat the flesh of pigs, with broth of spoiled meat[^4] in their pots,

**5** who say, "Keep to yourself; do not come near me, for I am too holy for you!"[^5] These are smoke in my nostrils, a fire that burns all day long.

**6** Look, it is written before me: I will not keep silent, but I will repay; I will repay into their laps[^6]

**7** both your wrongdoings and the wrongdoings of your ancestors together,[^7] says Yahweh,[^8] because they burned incense on the mountains and insulted me on the hills. I will measure into their laps full payment for their former deeds.[^9]

**8** This is what Yahweh says: As when new wine is found in a cluster of grapes and someone says, "Do not destroy it, for there is a blessing in it,"[^10] so I will do for the sake of my servants, so as not to destroy them all.

**9** I will bring out offspring from Jacob, and from Judah heirs to my mountains. My chosen ones will possess them, and my servants will live there.

**10** Sharon will become a pasture for flocks, and the Valley of Achor[^11] a resting place for herds, for my people who have sought me.

**11** But you who abandon Yahweh, who forget my holy mountain, who set a table for Fortune and fill bowls of mixed wine for Destiny,[^12]

**12** I will destine you for the sword,[^13] and all of you will bow down to be slaughtered, because I called and you did not answer, I spoke and you did not listen. You did what was evil in my eyes and chose what does not please me.

**13** Therefore this is what the Lord Yahweh says: Look, my servants will eat, but you will go hungry; look, my servants will drink, but you will go thirsty; look, my servants will rejoice, but you will be put to shame.

**14** Look, my servants will sing for joy with gladness of heart, but you will cry out with pain of heart and wail with a broken spirit.

**15** You will leave your name to my chosen ones as a curse,[^14] and the Lord Yahweh will put you to death, but he will call his servants by another name.

**16** So whoever invokes a blessing in the land will be blessed by the God of truth,[^15] and whoever swears an oath in the land will swear by the God of truth. For the former troubles will be forgotten and hidden from my eyes.

**17** For look, I am creating new heavens and a new earth. The former things will not be remembered or come to mind.[^16]

**18** But be glad and rejoice forever in what I am creating, for look, I am creating Jerusalem to be a delight and its people a joy.

**19** I will rejoice over Jerusalem and delight in my people; the sound of weeping and the sound of crying will no longer be heard in it.[^17]

**20** There will no longer be in it an infant who lives only a few days, or an old man who does not live out his days. For one who dies at a hundred years will be thought a youth, and one who falls short of a hundred years will be thought accursed.[^18]

**21** They will build houses and live in them; they will plant vineyards and eat their fruit.

**22** They will not build for others to live in, or plant for others to eat. For like the days of a tree will be the days of my people, and my chosen ones will long enjoy the work of their hands.

**23** They will not labor in vain or bear children for sudden disaster, for they will be offspring blessed by Yahweh, and their descendants with them.

**24** Before they call, I will answer; while they are still speaking, I will hear.

**25** The wolf and the lamb will graze together, and the lion will eat straw like the ox, and dust will be the serpent's food.[^19] They will not harm or destroy on all my holy mountain, says Yahweh.

[^1]: Or "to a nation that did not call on my name", as the Septuagint reads. Romans 10:20–21 quotes verses 1–2 from the Septuagint, applying verse 1 to the nations and verse 2 to Israel.
[^2]: Perhaps altars or tiles of brick used in the worship of other gods.
[^3]: Hebrew *netsurim*, "guarded places", perhaps tombs or caves where people sought messages from the dead.
[^4]: Hebrew *piggulim*, "foul, rotten": meat unfit to eat and offensive to God (Leviticus 7:18). The Hebrew text is written "pieces" but read "broth".
[^5]: Literally "for I am holy to you", or perhaps "for I would make you holy", that is, by contact, as holy things passed on their holiness (Ezekiel 44:19).
[^6]: Literally "into their bosom", the fold of the garment over the chest. Also in verse 7.
[^7]: The Hebrew moves from "their" in verse 6 to "your" here and back to "their".
[^8]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^9]: Literally "I will measure their work first into their bosom". Or "I will measure out their payment in full".
[^10]: Perhaps the words of a song sung at the grape harvest. Psalms 57–59 and 75 are set to a tune called "Do Not Destroy".
[^11]: A valley near Jericho, named for the "trouble" (*akhar*) brought on Israel by Achan (Joshua 7:24–26). Hosea 2:17 (2:15 in most English Bibles) promises that it will become "a door of hope".
[^12]: Hebrew *Gad* and *Meni*, gods of good fortune and fate, honored with meals and drink offerings.
[^13]: A play on words: *u-maniti*, "I will destine", and *Meni*, "Destiny", in verse 11.
[^14]: Hebrew *shevu'ah*, "oath", here a formula for cursing: their name will be used in the words "May Yahweh make you like …" (compare Jeremiah 29:22). *You* in the next line is singular.
[^15]: Literally "the God of Amen". *Amen* means "firm, sure, true". Compare Revelation 3:14, where Jesus is called "the Amen".
[^16]: 2 Peter 3:13 and Revelation 21:1 take up the promise of new heavens and a new earth (also 66:22).
[^17]: Revelation 21:4 echoes this line.
[^18]: Or "and the sinner who reaches a hundred years will be accursed". Hebrew *ha-chote* means "the sinner" or "one who misses the mark".
[^19]: The serpent's curse in Genesis 3:14. Verse 25 repeats parts of 11:6–9.
