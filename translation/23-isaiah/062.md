**1** For the sake of Zion I will not keep silent, and for the sake of Jerusalem I will not rest, until her righteousness[^1] goes out like brightness, and her salvation like a burning torch.

**2** The nations will see your righteousness, and all kings your glory. You will be called by a new name that the mouth of Yahweh[^2] will give you.[^3]

**3** You will be a crown of beauty in the hand of Yahweh, a royal diadem in the palm of your God.

**4** You will no longer be called Abandoned, and your land will no longer be called Desolate. But you will be called My Delight Is in Her, and your land Married;[^4] for Yahweh delights in you, and your land will be married.

**5** For as a young man marries a young woman, so your sons will marry you;[^5] and as a bridegroom rejoices over his bride, so your God will rejoice over you.

**6** On your walls, Jerusalem, I have posted watchmen; all day and all night they will never be silent. You who call on Yahweh to remember,[^6] take no rest for yourselves,

**7** and give him no rest until he establishes Jerusalem and makes it the praise of the earth.

**8** Yahweh has sworn by his right hand and by his mighty arm: I will never again give your grain as food to your enemies, and foreigners will never again drink the new wine for which you have labored.[^7]

**9** But those who harvest it will eat it and praise Yahweh, and those who gather it will drink it in the courts of my sanctuary.

**10** Go through, go through the gates! Prepare the way for the people! Build up, build up the highway! Clear it of stones! Raise a signal flag over the peoples!

**11** Look, Yahweh has proclaimed to the end of the earth: Say to Daughter Zion, "Look, your salvation is coming! Look, his reward is with him, and his recompense goes before him."[^8]

**12** They will be called the Holy People, the Redeemed of Yahweh;[^9] and you will be called Sought After, the City Not Abandoned.

[^1]: Or "vindication". Hebrew *tsedeq*. Also in verse 2.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "will designate". Compare Revelation 2:17 and 3:12.
[^4]: Hebrew *Azuvah*, "Abandoned"; *Shemamah*, "Desolate"; *Cheftsi-vah*, "My Delight Is in Her", traditionally "Hephzibah", the name of King Hezekiah's wife (2 Kings 21:1); *Be'ulah*, "Married", traditionally "Beulah". The verb for "marry" (*ba'al*) also means "become master of" (54:5).
[^5]: The same consonants can be read *bonayikh*, "your Builder", a plural form like *bo'alayikh*, "your husband", in 54:5: "so your Builder will marry you".
[^6]: Literally "you who cause Yahweh to remember", by reminding him in prayer of his promises.
[^7]: Literally "If I give … and if foreigners drink …", the form of a solemn oath.
[^8]: The words repeat 40:10. Matthew 21:5 joins "Say to Daughter Zion" with Zechariah 9:9.
[^9]: Hebrew *ge'ulim*, those whom Yahweh has rescued as next of kin (41:14).
