**1** Arise, shine, for your light has come, and the glory of the LORD[^1] has risen on you.

**2** For look, darkness covers the earth, and thick darkness the peoples. But the LORD rises on you, and his glory appears over you.

**3** Nations will come to your light, and kings to the brightness of your dawn.

**4** Lift up your eyes and look around: they all gather and come to you. Your sons will come from far away, and your daughters will be carried on the hip.[^2]

**5** Then you will see and be radiant; your heart will throb and swell with joy,[^3] for the abundance of the sea will be turned to you, and the wealth of the nations will come to you.

**6** A multitude of camels will cover you, young camels of Midian and Ephah. All those from Sheba will come, bringing gold and frankincense and proclaiming the praises of the LORD.[^4]

**7** All the flocks of Kedar will be gathered to you; the rams of Nebaioth[^5] will serve you. They will go up as acceptable offerings on my altar, and I will make my glorious house more glorious still.

**8** Who are these that fly like a cloud, like doves to their windows?[^6]

**9** Surely the coastlands[^7] wait for me, with the ships of Tarshish[^8] in the lead, to bring your sons from far away, their silver and gold with them, for the name of the LORD your God, for the Holy One of Israel, because he has glorified you.

**10** Foreigners will rebuild your walls, and their kings will serve you. For in my anger I struck you, but in my favor I have had compassion on you.

**11** Your gates will always stand open; day and night they will not be shut, so that people may bring you the wealth of the nations, with their kings led in procession.[^9]

**12** For the nation or kingdom that will not serve you will perish; those nations will be utterly ruined.

**13** The glory of Lebanon will come to you, the juniper, the plane tree and the pine together,[^10] to beautify the place of my sanctuary, and I will make the place of my feet glorious.

**14** The sons of those who oppressed you will come bowing low to you, and all who despised you will bow down at the soles of your feet.[^11] They will call you the City of the LORD, the Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

**15** Instead of being abandoned and hated, with no one passing through, I will make you an everlasting pride, a joy from generation to generation.

**16** You will drink the milk of nations and nurse at the breasts of kings. Then you will know that I, the LORD, am your Savior, your Redeemer,[^12] the Mighty One of Jacob.

**17** Instead of bronze I will bring gold, and instead of iron I will bring silver; instead of wood, bronze, and instead of stones, iron. I will make peace your overseer and righteousness your ruler.[^13]

**18** Violence will no longer be heard in your land, nor ruin or destruction within your borders. You will call your walls Salvation and your gates Praise.

**19** The sun will no longer be your light by day, nor will the brightness of the moon shine on you. But the LORD will be your everlasting light, and your God will be your glory.[^14]

**20** Your sun will never set again, and your moon will not wane,[^15] for the LORD will be your everlasting light, and your days of mourning will be over.

**21** Your people will all be righteous; they will possess the land forever. They are the shoot I planted,[^16] the work of my hands, so that I may be glorified.

**22** The least of them will become a thousand, and the smallest a mighty nation. I am the LORD; when the time comes, I will do this quickly.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. *You* is feminine singular in this chapter: Zion is addressed as a woman (verse 14).
[^2]: Literally "will be nursed on the side", carried as small children are carried.
[^3]: Literally "your heart will tremble and grow wide".
[^4]: Midian and Ephah were Arabian tribes descended from Abraham (Genesis 25:2–4), and Sheba was a kingdom in southern Arabia famous for its trade in gold and spices (1 Kings 10:1–2). Compare the gifts of the wise men in Matthew 2:11.
[^5]: Kedar and Nebaioth were Arabian tribes, named as sons of Ishmael in Genesis 25:13.
[^6]: Hebrew *arubbot*, "openings, lattices", perhaps the holes of a dovecote.
[^7]: Hebrew *iyyim*, the islands and coasts of the Mediterranean (11:11), and in these chapters the most distant lands.
[^8]: Large seagoing ships (2:16), of the kind that sailed to Tarshish, a distant port in the west.
[^9]: Revelation 21:24–26 echoes verses 3 and 11.
[^10]: The trees named in 41:19. Their identification is uncertain.
[^11]: Revelation 3:9 echoes this line.
[^12]: Hebrew *go'el* (41:14). The line repeats 49:26.
[^13]: *Overseer* and *ruler* are the officials who once oppressed the people. Hebrew *noges* is the word for the slave drivers of Egypt (Exodus 3:7).
[^14]: Revelation 21:23 and 22:5 echo verses 19–20.
[^15]: Literally "will not be withdrawn".
[^16]: Literally "the shoot of my planting". Hebrew *netser*, the word for the "branch" in 11:1. The Hebrew text is written "his planting" but read "my planting".
