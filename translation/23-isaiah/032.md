**1** Look, a king will reign in righteousness, and rulers will rule with justice.

**2** Each of them will be like a shelter from the wind and a refuge from the storm, like streams of water in a dry land, like the shade of a great rock in a weary land.

**3** Then the eyes of those who see will no longer be closed,[^1] and the ears of those who hear will listen.

**4** The mind of the rash will understand and know, and the tongue of those who stammer will speak quickly and clearly.

**5** The fool will no longer be called noble, and the scoundrel will no longer be called honorable.[^2]

**6** For the fool speaks folly, and his mind plots evil: to practice godlessness, to spread error about Yahweh,[^3] to leave the hungry empty and to deprive the thirsty of drink.

**7** The scoundrel's ways are evil;[^4] he devises wicked schemes to ruin the poor[^5] with lies, even when the needy plead their just cause.

**8** But the noble person makes noble plans, and by noble deeds he stands.

**9** You women who are at ease, rise up and hear my voice! You daughters who feel secure, listen to what I say!

**10** In little more than a year[^6] you who feel secure will tremble, for the grape harvest will fail, and the harvest of fruit will not come.

**11** Tremble, you women who are at ease! Shudder, you who feel secure! Strip off your clothes, stand bare, and put sackcloth around your waists.

**12** Beat your breasts for the pleasant fields, for the fruitful vine,[^7]

**13** for the soil of my people, where thorns and briers grow up, yes, for all the houses of joy in the joyful city.

**14** For the palace is abandoned, the busy city deserted. The hill[^8] and the watchtower will become caves forever, the delight of wild donkeys, a pasture for flocks,

**15** until the Spirit[^9] is poured out on us from on high, and the wilderness becomes a fruitful field, and the fruitful field is thought of as a forest.[^10]

**16** Then justice will dwell in the wilderness, and righteousness will live in the fruitful field.

**17** The work of righteousness will be peace, and the effect of righteousness will be quietness and confidence forever.

**18** My people will live in a peaceful home, in secure dwellings and in undisturbed resting places.

**19** But hail will fall when the forest comes down, and the city will be utterly laid low.[^11]

**20** Happy are you[^12] who sow beside every stream, who let the ox and the donkey range free!

[^1]: Or "will no longer be dim". The Hebrew verb may come from *sha'ah*, "look", or from *sha'a*, "be smeared shut" (6:10).
[^2]: Hebrew *kilai*, a rare word, perhaps "cheat, swindler". Also in verse 7.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: A play on sounds: *kelai kelav*, "the scoundrel, his tools".
[^5]: The Hebrew text is written "the humble" but read "the poor".
[^6]: Literally "days upon a year".
[^7]: Literally "beating upon breasts". Hebrew *shadayim*, "breasts", sounds like *sede*, "fields".
[^8]: Hebrew *ophel*, "hill, mound", also the name of the fortified hill of Jerusalem south of the temple (2 Chronicles 27:3).
[^9]: Or "a spirit". Hebrew *ruach*.
[^10]: Hebrew *karmel*, "garden land, orchard" (29:17). The Hebrew text is written "and a fruitful field" but read "and the fruitful field". Also in verse 16.
[^11]: A play on sounds: *u-varad be-redet ha-ya'ar*, "and it will hail when the forest comes down". The meaning of the verse is uncertain.
[^12]: Hebrew *ashrei*: happy, fortunate; traditionally "blessed".
