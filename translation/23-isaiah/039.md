**1** At that time Merodach-baladan[^1] son of Baladan king of Babylon sent letters and a gift to Hezekiah, for he had heard that he had been ill and had recovered.[^2]

**2** Hezekiah was glad because of them[^3] and showed them his treasure house: the silver, the gold, the spices and the fine oil, his whole armory,[^4] and everything that was found in his treasuries. There was nothing in his palace or in all his realm that Hezekiah did not show them.

**3** Then the prophet Isaiah came to King Hezekiah and said to him, "What did these men say? And where did they come to you from?" Hezekiah said, "They came to me from a distant land, from Babylon."

**4** He asked, "What did they see in your palace?" Hezekiah said, "They saw everything in my palace. There is nothing in my treasuries that I did not show them."

**5** Then Isaiah said to Hezekiah, "Hear the word of Yahweh of Armies.[^5]

**6** The days are coming when everything in your palace, and all that your ancestors have stored up until this day, will be carried off to Babylon. Nothing will be left, says Yahweh.

**7** And some of your sons, your own descendants whom you will father, will be taken away, and they will be officials[^6] in the palace of the king of Babylon."

**8** Hezekiah said to Isaiah, "The word of Yahweh that you have spoken is good." For he thought,[^7] "There will be peace and security in my days."

[^1]: Marduk-apla-iddina II, who ruled Babylon in 722–710 and again in 703 BC. He was a long-time enemy of Assyria and was probably looking for allies. 2 Kings 20:12 calls him Berodach-baladan. This chapter is also found in 2 Kings 20:12–19.
[^2]: 2 Kings 20:12 does not have "and had recovered".
[^3]: 2 Kings 20:13 reads "Hezekiah listened to them".
[^4]: Literally "all the house of his equipment".
[^5]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. *Yahweh of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. 2 Kings 20:16 reads "the word of Yahweh".
[^6]: Hebrew *sarisim*, court officials. The word can also mean eunuchs.
[^7]: Literally "For he said". 2 Kings 20:19 reads "For he said, 'Is it not good, if there will be peace and security in my days?'" *Security* is literally "truth" or "faithfulness".
