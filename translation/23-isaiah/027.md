**1** On that day Yahweh[^1] will punish with his sword, hard and great and strong, Leviathan the fleeing serpent, Leviathan the twisting serpent, and he will kill the dragon[^2] that is in the sea.[^3]

**2** On that day sing of a delightful vineyard:

**3** I, Yahweh, am its keeper; I water it every moment. So that no one may harm it, I guard it night and day.

**4** I have no anger. If only there were briers and thorns to fight against! I would march against them; I would burn them all together.

**5** Or else let them take hold of my protection; let them make peace with me; yes, let them make peace with me.

**6** In days to come[^4] Jacob will take root; Israel will blossom and sprout, and fill the face of the world with fruit.

**7** Has he struck him as he struck those who struck him? Has he been killed as those who killed him were killed?[^5]

**8** By driving her away, by sending her off, you contended with her.[^6] He removed her with his fierce wind on the day of the east wind.

**9** Therefore by this the guilt of Jacob will be atoned for, and this will be the full fruit of the removal of his sin: when he makes all the altar stones like chalk stones crushed to pieces, no Asherah poles[^7] or incense altars will be left standing.

**10** For the fortified city is desolate, a settlement abandoned and deserted like the wilderness. There the calf grazes, there it lies down and strips its branches.

**11** When its twigs are dry, they are broken off; women come and make fires with them. For this is a people without understanding; therefore their Maker will have no compassion on them, and the one who formed them will show them no favor.

**12** On that day Yahweh will thresh from the flowing Euphrates[^8] to the Wadi of Egypt,[^9] and you will be gathered up one by one, people of Israel.

**13** On that day a great ram's horn[^10] will be blown, and those who were perishing in the land of Assyria and those who were scattered in the land of Egypt will come and worship Yahweh on the holy mountain in Jerusalem.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *tannin*, a sea monster or serpent (51:9; Genesis 1:21).
[^3]: Leviathan is a sea monster, a picture of the forces of chaos that God overcomes (Psalm 74:13–14; Job 3:8). Older Canaanite poems from Ugarit describe the monster Litan in almost the same words: "the fleeing serpent, the twisting serpent".
[^4]: Literally "Those who come". Or "In the coming days".
[^5]: Israel has been struck, but not destroyed as its enemies were.
[^6]: Hebrew *be-sa'se'ah*, a word found only here, perhaps "by measure" or "by driving away". *Her* is Israel, pictured as a woman sent away.
[^7]: Hebrew *asherim*: wooden poles or trees that were symbols of the goddess Asherah. The *incense altars* (*chammanim*) are as in 17:8.
[^8]: Literally "from the flowing stream of the River". Hebrew *shibbolet* can mean "flowing stream" or "ear of grain", a play on the image of threshing.
[^9]: The Wadi el-Arish, the southwestern border of the promised land.
[^10]: Hebrew *shofar*, a trumpet made from a ram's horn.
