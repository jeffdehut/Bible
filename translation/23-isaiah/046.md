**1** Bel bows down; Nebo stoops.[^1] Their idols are loaded on animals and cattle. The things you used to carry in procession are loaded as a burden[^2] on weary animals.

**2** They stoop and bow down together; they cannot rescue the burden. They themselves[^3] go into captivity.

**3** Listen to me, house of Jacob, all the remnant of the house of Israel, you whom I have carried since birth, whom I have borne since you were in the womb.[^4]

**4** Even to your old age I am he,[^5] and to your gray hairs I will carry you. I have made you, and I will bear you; I will carry you and rescue you.

**5** To whom will you compare me and make me equal? With whom will you liken me, that we may be alike?

**6** Those who pour out gold from the bag and weigh out silver on the scale hire a goldsmith, and he makes it into a god; they bow down and worship it.

**7** They lift it to their shoulders and carry it; they set it in its place, and it stands there; it cannot move from its place. Even when someone cries out to it, it does not answer; it cannot save anyone from his trouble.

**8** Remember this and stand firm;[^6] take it to heart, you rebels.

**9** Remember the former things of long ago, for I am God, and there is no other; I am God, and there is none like me.

**10** I declare the end from the beginning, and from ancient times what has not yet been done. I say, "My plan will stand, and I will do all that I please."

**11** I call a bird of prey from the east, the man of my plan[^7] from a far country. I have spoken, and I will bring it about; I have planned it, and I will do it.

**12** Listen to me, you stubborn of heart,[^8] who are far from righteousness.

**13** I am bringing my righteousness near; it is not far off, and my salvation will not be delayed. I will give salvation in Zion, and my glory to Israel.[^9]

[^1]: *Bel*, "lord", is a title of Marduk, the chief god of Babylon; *Nebo* (Nabu), his son, was the god of writing and wisdom. Their images were carried in procession at the New Year festival. Here they are carried off on pack animals as the city falls.
[^2]: Hebrew *massa*, the word translated "oracle" in 13:1. Also in verse 2.
[^3]: Literally "their *nephesh*".
[^4]: Babylon's gods must be carried by their worshipers (verses 1 and 7), but Yahweh has carried his people since their birth.
[^5]: Hebrew *ani hu* (41:4).
[^6]: Hebrew *hit'oshashu*, a word found only here, perhaps "take courage" or "show yourselves men". The meaning is uncertain.
[^7]: The Hebrew text is written "his plan" but read "my plan". The *bird of prey* and the man are Cyrus (44:28; 45:1).
[^8]: Literally "you mighty of heart".
[^9]: Or "I will give salvation in Zion to Israel, my glory".
