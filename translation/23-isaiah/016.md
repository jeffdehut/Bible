**1** Send lambs to the ruler of the land, from Sela, by way of the wilderness, to the mount of Daughter Zion.[^1]

**2** Like fluttering birds, like a scattered nest, so are the daughters of Moab at the fords of the Arnon.[^2]

**3** Give counsel; make a decision.[^3] Make your shade like night at high noon. Hide the outcasts; do not betray the fugitive.

**4** Let my outcasts from Moab stay with you;[^4] be a refuge for them from the destroyer. When the oppressor is no more and destruction has ceased, and the one who tramples has vanished from the land,

**5** then a throne will be established in faithful love,[^5] and on it will sit in faithfulness, in the tent of David, one who judges and seeks justice and is quick to do what is right.

**6** We have heard of the pride of Moab, how very proud he is, of his arrogance, his pride and his insolence; his boasting is empty.[^6]

**7** Therefore Moab wails; all of them wail for Moab. Moan for the raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth,[^7] utterly stricken.

**8** For the fields of Heshbon wither, and the vines of Sibmah, whose choice vines laid low the lords of the nations.[^8] They reached as far as Jazer and wandered into the wilderness; their shoots spread out and crossed the sea.

**9** So I weep with the weeping of Jazer for the vines of Sibmah. I drench you with my tears, Heshbon and Elealeh, for the battle shout has fallen on your summer fruit and your harvest.[^9]

**10** Joy and gladness are taken away from the fruitful field; in the vineyards no one sings or shouts. No one treads out wine in the presses; I have put an end to the shout.

**11** Therefore my heart moans for Moab like a lyre, and my inmost being for Kir-heres.[^10]

**12** When Moab appears and wears himself out on the high place,[^11] and comes to his sanctuary to pray, it will do him no good.[^12]

**13** This is the word that Yahweh[^13] spoke concerning Moab in the past.

**14** But now Yahweh has spoken: Within three years, counted as a hired worker counts them,[^14] the glory of Moab will be brought into contempt, with all its great crowd, and its survivors will be very few and feeble.

[^1]: Moab had once paid lambs as tribute to the king of Israel (2 Kings 3:4). *Sela*, "the rock", is a place in Edom, perhaps Petra.
[^2]: The river that runs into the Dead Sea from the east, the northern border of Moab.
[^3]: Verses 3–5 are probably the plea of Moab's refugees to Zion.
[^4]: Literally "my outcasts, Moab". The Septuagint reads "the outcasts of Moab".
[^5]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^6]: Literally "his idle talk is not so".
[^7]: Or "the men". Jeremiah 48:31 reads "the men of Kir-heres". Kir-hareseth and Kir-heres (verse 11) are probably Kir of Moab (15:1).
[^8]: Or "the lords of the nations have struck down its choice vines". The wine of Sibmah may have been strong enough to overcome rulers.
[^9]: Hebrew *hedad*, the shout of those treading grapes, here the shout of the enemy (Jeremiah 48:33). Also in verse 10.
[^10]: Literally "my bowels", thought of as the seat of feeling.
[^11]: A play on words: *nir'ah*, "appears", and *nil'ah*, "wears himself out".
[^12]: Literally "and he will not be able".
[^13]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^14]: Literally "like the years of a hired worker", who will not work a day beyond what he is paid for: exactly three years.
