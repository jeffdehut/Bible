**1** The righteous one perishes, and no one takes it to heart. The faithful[^1] are taken away, and no one understands that the righteous one is taken away from the evil to come.[^2]

**2** He enters into peace; they rest on their beds,[^3] all who walk uprightly.

**3** But you, come here, you children of a sorceress, offspring of an adulterer and a prostitute!

**4** Whom are you mocking? At whom do you open your mouth wide and stick out your tongue? Are you not children of rebellion, offspring of deceit?

**5** You inflame yourselves among the oaks,[^4] under every green tree; you slaughter children in the ravines, under the clefts of the rocks.[^5]

**6** Among the smooth stones of the ravine is your portion;[^6] they, they are your lot. To them you have poured out drink offerings and brought up grain offerings.[^7] Should I relent because of these things?

**7** On a high and lofty mountain you have made your bed; there too you went up to offer sacrifice.

**8** Behind the door and the doorpost you have set up your sign.[^8] For, deserting me, you uncovered your bed, climbed up into it and opened it wide. You made a bargain for yourself with them; you loved their bed; you gazed on their nakedness.[^9]

**9** You went to the king[^10] with oil and gave him many perfumes. You sent your envoys far away; you sent them down even to Sheol.[^11]

**10** You were wearied by your many journeys, but you did not say, "It is hopeless." You found new strength,[^12] so you did not grow faint.

**11** Whom did you dread and fear, that you lied, and did not remember me or take it to heart? Is it not because I have kept silent, and for a long time, that you do not fear me?

**12** I will expose your righteousness and your deeds,[^13] and they will not help you.

**13** When you cry out, let your collection of idols[^14] save you! The wind will carry them all off; a breath will take them away. But whoever takes refuge in me will inherit the land and possess my holy mountain.

**14** And it will be said, "Build it up, build it up, prepare the road! Remove every obstacle from the way of my people."

**15** For this is what the High and Lofty One says, who lives forever,[^15] whose name is Holy: I live in a high and holy place, and also with the one who is crushed and humble in spirit, to revive the spirit of the humble and to revive the heart of the crushed.

**16** For I will not accuse forever, nor will I always be angry. For then the spirit[^16] would grow faint before me, the breath of life that I have made.

**17** Because of the guilt of his greed I was angry, and I struck him; I hid my face and was angry. But he went on turning away, in the way of his own heart.

**18** I have seen his ways, but I will heal him. I will lead him and repay him with comfort, him and those who mourn for him,

**19** creating praise on their lips:[^17] Peace, peace, to the far and to the near, says Yahweh,[^18] and I will heal him.[^19]

**20** But the wicked are like the tossing sea, which cannot be still; its waters toss up mud and mire.

**21** There is no peace, says my God, for the wicked.[^20]

[^1]: Literally "men of *chesed*", those who show faithful love.
[^2]: Literally "from before the evil". Or "because of the evil": the righteous are removed by the wrongdoing of others, or spared the disaster that is coming.
[^3]: Their graves.
[^4]: Or "with the gods". Hebrew *elim*, "sacred trees" (1:29), can also mean "gods". *You* is plural in verses 3–5, then feminine singular in verses 6–13, as the people are addressed as an unfaithful wife.
[^5]: Children were sacrificed to other gods in the Valley of Ben-hinnom near Jerusalem (2 Kings 23:10; Jeremiah 7:31).
[^6]: A play on words: *chalqe nachal*, "the smooth stones of the ravine", and *chelqekh*, "your portion". The smooth stones may have been objects of worship.
[^7]: Hebrew *minchah*, "gift, tribute", in Leviticus a grain offering.
[^8]: Hebrew *zikkaron*, "memorial, reminder", perhaps a pagan symbol placed where the words of Yahweh's law were to be written (Deuteronomy 6:9).
[^9]: Literally "you saw a hand". *Hand* (Hebrew *yad*) may be a polite word for the male organ, or mean a memorial pillar (56:5).
[^10]: Or "to Molech", the god to whom children were sacrificed (Leviticus 18:21), whose name sounds like *melekh*, "king".
[^11]: Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead.
[^12]: Literally "You found the life of your hand".
[^13]: The words are said with irony: what they call righteousness will be shown for what it is.
[^14]: Literally "your collections", perhaps a gathering of idols or of foreign allies.
[^15]: Or "who inhabits eternity". The words *high and lofty* are the "high and lifted up" of 6:1 and 52:13.
[^16]: Hebrew *ruach*, "spirit, breath", in parallel with *neshamot*, "breaths".
[^17]: Literally "creating the fruit of the lips". Compare Hebrews 13:15.
[^18]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^19]: Ephesians 2:17 echoes this verse.
[^20]: This line repeats 48:22, with "my God" instead of "Yahweh".
