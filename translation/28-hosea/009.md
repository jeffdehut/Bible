**1** Do not rejoice, Israel; do not exult like the peoples. For you have prostituted yourself and left your God.[^1] You have loved a prostitute's wages on every threshing floor of grain.

**2** Threshing floor and winepress will not feed them, and the new wine will fail her.

**3** They will not remain in the land of the LORD.[^2] Ephraim will return to Egypt, and in Assyria they will eat unclean food.

**4** They will not pour out wine offerings to the LORD, and their sacrifices will not please him. They will be like the bread of mourners to them; all who eat it will be defiled.[^3] For their bread will be for themselves alone;[^4] it will not come into the house of the LORD.

**5** What will you do on the day of the appointed festival, on the day of the LORD's feast?

**6** For look, they have gone away from destruction, but Egypt will gather them, and Memphis[^5] will bury them. Nettles will take possession of their precious things of silver, and thorns will grow in their tents.

**7** The days of punishment have come; the days of repayment have come. Let Israel know it! "The prophet is a fool, the man of the spirit is mad!"[^6] So it is because of your great iniquity, and your hostility is great.

**8** The prophet is a watchman over Ephraim with my God,[^7] yet a fowler's snare is on all his ways, and hostility in the house of his God.

**9** They have sunk deep into corruption, as in the days of Gibeah.[^8] He will remember their iniquity; he will punish their sins.

**10** Like grapes in the wilderness I found Israel; like the first fruit on the fig tree in its first season I saw your ancestors. But they came to Baal-peor[^9] and dedicated themselves to the Shameful Thing,[^10] and they became detestable, like the thing they loved.

**11** Ephraim's glory will fly away like a bird: no birth, no pregnancy, no conception.

**12** Even if they raise children, I will bereave them until no one is left. Woe to them indeed when I turn away from them!

**13** Ephraim, as I saw, was like Tyre, planted in a pleasant meadow; but Ephraim will bring out his children to the slayer.[^11]

**14** Give them, the LORD—what will you give? Give them a womb that miscarries and breasts that are dry.

**15** All their wickedness is in Gilgal; there I came to hate them. Because of their wicked deeds I will drive them out of my house. I will love them no more; all their officials are rebels.[^12]

**16** Ephraim is struck down; their root is dried up; they will bear no fruit.[^13] Even if they give birth, I will kill the precious offspring of their womb.

**17** My God will reject them, because they have not listened to him, and they will be wanderers among the nations.

[^1]: Literally "from upon your God".
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Food in a house of mourning was unclean because of contact with the dead (Numbers 19:14–15; Deuteronomy 26:14).
[^4]: Literally "for their soul" or "for their appetite".
[^5]: Hebrew *Moph*, Memphis, the ancient capital of Lower Egypt, famous for its tombs.
[^6]: Probably the people's mockery of the prophet. The words could also be the prophet's own verdict on false prophets.
[^7]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain.
[^8]: See Judges 19–21.
[^9]: See Numbers 25:1–9. The god Baal of Peor was worshiped in Moab.
[^10]: Hebrew *boshet*, "shame", a word that later scribes and prophets put in place of the name Baal (compare Jeremiah 3:24; 11:13).
[^11]: The meaning of the Hebrew of the first half of the verse is uncertain. The Septuagint reads "Ephraim, as I saw, has given up his children as prey", which differs by one letter (*le-tsayid*, "for prey", for *le-tsor*, "like Tyre").
[^12]: A play on words: *sarehem*, "their officials", and *sorerim*, "rebels".
[^13]: A play on words: *peri*, "fruit", and *Ephraim*.
