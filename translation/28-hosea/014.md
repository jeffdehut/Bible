**1** Samaria will bear her guilt, because she has rebelled against her God. They will fall by the sword; their little ones will be dashed to pieces, and their pregnant women ripped open.[^1]

**2** Return, Israel, to Yahweh[^2] your God, for you have stumbled because of your iniquity.

**3** Take words with you and return to Yahweh. Say to him, "Take away all iniquity and accept what is good, and we will offer our lips as bulls.[^3]

**4** Assyria will not save us; we will not ride on horses. We will never again say 'Our God' to the work of our hands, for in you the orphan finds compassion."

**5** I will heal their unfaithfulness; I will love them freely, for my anger has turned away from him.

**6** I will be like the dew to Israel; he will blossom like the lily,[^4] and he will strike roots like Lebanon.[^5]

**7** His shoots will spread out; his splendor will be like the olive tree, and his fragrance like Lebanon.

**8** Those who live in his shade will return. They will flourish like grain;[^6] they will blossom like the vine. His fame will be like the wine of Lebanon.

**9** Ephraim, what more have I to do with idols?[^7] It is I who answer and watch over him.[^8] I am like a luxuriant cypress; your fruit comes from me.

**10** Who is wise? Let him understand these things. Who is discerning? Let him know them. For the ways of Yahweh are right, and the righteous walk in them, but rebels stumble in them.

[^1]: This verse is 13:16 in most English Bibles, and verses 2–10 are 14:1–9.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: That is, our words of prayer and praise in place of sacrifices. Literally "and we will pay bulls, our lips". The Septuagint reads "the fruit of our lips" (compare Hebrews 13:15), dividing the same consonants differently.
[^4]: Or "the lotus". Hebrew *shoshannah*.
[^5]: Probably the cedars of Lebanon, with their deep roots.
[^6]: Literally "They will make grain live".
[^7]: Or "Ephraim will say, 'What more have I to do with idols?'"
[^8]: Hebrew *ashurennu*, "I watch over him", sounds like *Ashur*, "Assyria" (compare 13:7).
