**1** Put the horn to your lips![^1] An eagle is over the house of the LORD,[^2] because they have broken my covenant and rebelled against my law.

**2** They cry out to me, "My God, we, Israel, know you!"

**3** Israel has rejected what is good; an enemy will pursue him.

**4** They set up kings, but not through me; they appointed officials, but without my knowledge. With their silver and their gold they made idols for themselves, so that they will be cut off.[^3]

**5** He has rejected your calf, Samaria![^4] My anger burns against them. How long will they be incapable of innocence?

**6** For it is from Israel! A craftsman made it; it is not God. The calf of Samaria will be broken to pieces.

**7** For they sow the wind, and they will reap the whirlwind. The standing grain has no head; it will produce no flour.[^5] Even if it should produce, foreigners would swallow it up.

**8** Israel is swallowed up. Now they are among the nations like a worthless vessel.[^6]

**9** For they have gone up to Assyria, a wild donkey wandering alone.[^7] Ephraim has hired lovers.[^8]

**10** Even though they hire them among the nations, I will now gather them together. Soon they will writhe[^9] under the burden of the king of princes.[^10]

**11** Because Ephraim has multiplied altars for sinning, they have become to him altars for sinning.[^11]

**12** Though I write for him the many things of my law,[^12] they are regarded as something strange.

**13** They sacrifice meat as gifts to me[^13] and eat it, but the LORD does not accept them. Now he will remember their iniquity and punish their sins. They will return to Egypt.

**14** Israel has forgotten his Maker and built palaces,[^14] and Judah has multiplied fortified cities. But I will send fire on his cities, and it will devour their strongholds.[^15]

[^1]: Literally "To your palate the horn!" Hebrew *shofar*, a ram's horn blown as an alarm.
[^2]: Literally "Like an eagle over the house of the LORD". Hebrew *nesher*, "eagle" or "vulture". *The house of the LORD* may here mean the land or people of Israel (compare 9:15). *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Or "so that it will be cut off", that is, the silver and gold, or Israel.
[^4]: Or "Your calf is rejected, Samaria!" The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. The golden calves set up by Jeroboam I at Bethel and Dan (1 Kings 12:28–30) were the chief images of the northern kingdom, whose capital was Samaria.
[^5]: A play on words: *tsemach*, "head of grain", and *qemach*, "flour".
[^6]: Literally "like a vessel in which there is no pleasure".
[^7]: Hebrew *pere*, "wild donkey", sounds like *Ephraim*.
[^8]: Literally "Ephraim has given love-gifts". The same verb is used of paying a prostitute.
[^9]: Or "Soon they will begin to dwindle". The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain.
[^10]: Probably the king of Assyria, who called himself "king of kings".
[^11]: The altars that should have dealt with sin have become a means of sinning.
[^12]: The Hebrew text is written "ten thousand things of my law" but read "the many things of my law".
[^13]: The meaning of the Hebrew *havhavay*, perhaps "my gifts", is uncertain.
[^14]: Or "temples". Hebrew *heikhalot*.
[^15]: Literally "her strongholds". The same words close the oracles of Amos 1:4–2:5.
