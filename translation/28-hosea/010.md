**1** Israel is a luxuriant vine[^1] that yields its fruit. The more his fruit increased, the more altars he built; the better his land became, the finer he made his standing stones.[^2]

**2** Their heart is false;[^3] now they must bear their guilt. He will break down their altars and destroy their standing stones.

**3** For now they will say, "We have no king, because we did not fear Yahweh.[^4] And the king, what could he do for us?"

**4** They speak mere words, swear empty oaths and make covenants. So lawsuits spring up like poisonous weeds in the furrows of a field.

**5** The inhabitants of Samaria fear for the calf[^5] of Beth-aven.[^6] Its people mourn over it, and its idolatrous priests[^7] wail[^8] over it, over its glory, because it has gone into exile from them.[^9]

**6** It too will be carried to Assyria as tribute to the great king.[^10] Ephraim will be put to shame, and Israel will be ashamed of his own plans.

**7** Samaria and her king will be cut off[^11] like a twig on the surface of the water.

**8** The high places of Aven,[^12] the sin of Israel, will be destroyed. Thorns and thistles will grow up over their altars. Then they will say to the mountains, "Cover us!" and to the hills, "Fall on us!"[^13]

**9** Since the days of Gibeah you have sinned, Israel.[^14] There they have remained. Will not war overtake them in Gibeah, war against the wicked?[^15]

**10** When I choose, I will discipline them. Nations will be gathered against them, when they are bound for their double iniquity.[^16]

**11** Ephraim was a trained heifer that loved to thresh, and I spared her fair neck.[^17] But now I will harness Ephraim; Judah must plow; Jacob must harrow for himself.

**12** Sow for yourselves righteousness; reap the fruit of faithful love.[^18] Break up your unplowed ground, for it is time to seek Yahweh, until he comes and rains righteousness on you.[^19]

**13** You have plowed wickedness and reaped injustice; you have eaten the fruit of lies. Because you have trusted in your own way and in the great number of your warriors,

**14** the roar of battle will rise among your people, and all your fortresses will be destroyed, as Shalman destroyed Beth-arbel on the day of battle,[^20] when mothers were dashed to pieces with their children.

**15** This is what Bethel will do to you,[^21] because of your great wickedness.[^22] At dawn the king of Israel will be utterly cut off.

[^1]: Hebrew *boqeq*, which usually means "laid waste" or "emptied"; here perhaps "spreading" or "luxuriant".
[^2]: Hebrew *matsevot*, standing stones set up as objects of worship. Also in verse 2.
[^3]: Or "divided". Literally "smooth".
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^5]: Hebrew plural, "heifers", perhaps said in mockery. See 8:5.
[^6]: Bethel. See 4:15.
[^7]: Hebrew *kemarim*, a word used only of priests of other gods (2 Kings 23:5; Zephaniah 1:4).
[^8]: Hebrew *yagilu*, which usually means "rejoice"; here perhaps the shouts of a ritual of lament.
[^9]: Compare 1 Samuel 4:21–22, "The glory has gone into exile from Israel."
[^10]: See 5:13.
[^11]: Or "silenced". Also in verse 15.
[^12]: That is, Beth-aven (verse 5). *Aven* means "wickedness".
[^13]: Compare Luke 23:30; Revelation 6:16.
[^14]: See 9:9.
[^15]: Literally "against the sons of wickedness". The Hebrew *alvah* is an unusual form of *avlah*, "wickedness".
[^16]: The Hebrew text is written "their two springs" or "their two eyes" but read "their double iniquity".
[^17]: Literally "I passed over the goodness of her neck". Or "I put a yoke on her fair neck". Threshing was light work, and an ox treading out grain was free to eat (Deuteronomy 25:4).
[^18]: Literally "reap according to faithful love". Hebrew *chesed*, loyal, covenant love.
[^19]: Or "teaches you righteousness". The Hebrew verb *yarah* can mean "to rain" or "to teach".
[^20]: Shalman and Beth-arbel are not otherwise known. Shalman may be Shalmaneser V of Assyria (2 Kings 17:3) or Salamanu, a king of Moab named in Assyrian records.
[^21]: Or "So it will be done to you, Bethel". The Septuagint reads "house of Israel".
[^22]: Literally "the evil of your evil".
