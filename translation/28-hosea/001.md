**1** The word of Yahweh[^1] that came to Hosea[^2] son of Beeri in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel.[^3]

**2** When Yahweh first spoke through Hosea, Yahweh said to Hosea, "Go, take for yourself a wife given to prostitution, and children of prostitution,[^4] for the land is committing flagrant prostitution by turning away from Yahweh."

**3** So he went and married Gomer daughter of Diblaim, and she conceived and bore him a son.

**4** Yahweh said to him, "Name him Jezreel,[^5] for in a little while I will punish the house of Jehu for the bloodshed of Jezreel, and I will put an end to the kingdom of the house of Israel.

**5** On that day I will break the bow of Israel in the Valley of Jezreel."

**6** She conceived again and bore a daughter. Yahweh said to him,[^6] "Name her Lo-ruhamah,[^7] for I will no longer have compassion on the house of Israel, that I should forgive them at all.[^8]

**7** But I will have compassion on the house of Judah, and I will save them by Yahweh their God. I will not save them by bow or sword or battle, by horses or horsemen."

**8** After she had weaned Lo-ruhamah, she conceived and bore a son.

**9** Yahweh said, "Name him Lo-ammi,[^9] for you are not my people, and I will not be yours."[^10]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *Hoshea*, "salvation" or "he has saved", the original name of Joshua (Numbers 13:16) and the name of the last king of Israel (2 Kings 17:1).
[^3]: Jeroboam II reigned from about 786 to 746 BC. Hosea's work as a prophet ran from the last years of Jeroboam's reign until shortly before the fall of Samaria in 722 BC.
[^4]: Literally "a wife of prostitutions and children of prostitutions". Hebrew *zenunim*, "prostitution, sexual unfaithfulness". It need not mean that she was a professional prostitute; it may mean that she was, or would become, unfaithful. Throughout the book the word pictures Israel's worship of other gods as a wife's unfaithfulness to her husband.
[^5]: Jezreel means "God sows". It was the royal city and valley where Jehu seized the throne by killing the family of Ahab (2 Kings 9–10). The name returns as a promise in 2:2, 24–25 (1:11; 2:22–23 in most English Bibles).
[^6]: Literally "He said to him". Also in verse 9.
[^7]: Lo-ruhamah means "not shown compassion" or "not loved". The Hebrew verb *racham* is used of the tender love of a parent.
[^8]: Or "but I will surely take them away". The Hebrew verb *nasa*, "lift up, carry", can mean "forgive" (bear away sin) or "take away".
[^9]: Lo-ammi means "not my people".
[^10]: Literally "and I, I will not be to you". Hebrew *lo-ehyeh*, "I will not be", probably echoes the name God gives himself in Exodus 3:14, *ehyeh*, "I am" or "I will be". Many translations, following the context and 2:25 (2:23 in most English Bibles), read "and I am not your God".
