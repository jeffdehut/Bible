**1** Yet the number of the Israelites will be like the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured or counted.[^1] And in the place where it was said to them, "You are not my people," it will be said to them, "Children of the living God."[^2]

**2** The people of Judah and the people of Israel will be gathered together. They will appoint for themselves one head, and they will go up from the land,[^3] for great will be the day of Jezreel.

**3** Say to your brothers, "Ammi," and to your sisters, "Ruhamah."[^4]

**4** Bring charges against your mother, bring charges, for she is not my wife, and I am not her husband. Let her remove her prostitution from her face and her adultery from between her breasts,

**5** or I will strip her naked and expose her as on the day she was born. I will make her like a wilderness, turn her into a parched land, and kill her with thirst.

**6** I will have no compassion on her children, because they are children of prostitution.

**7** For their mother has prostituted herself; she who conceived them has acted shamefully. For she said, "I will go after my lovers, who give me my bread and my water, my wool and my flax, my oil and my drink."

**8** Therefore I am going to hedge up your way with thorns,[^5] and I will build a wall against her, so that she cannot find her paths.

**9** She will pursue her lovers but not overtake them; she will look for them but not find them. Then she will say, "I will go back to my first husband, for it was better for me then than now."

**10** She did not know that it was I who gave her the grain, the new wine and the olive oil, and who lavished on her the silver and gold that they used for Baal.[^6]

**11** Therefore I will take back my grain in its time and my new wine in its season, and I will snatch away my wool and my flax, which were to cover her nakedness.

**12** Now I will uncover her shame[^7] before the eyes of her lovers, and no one will rescue her from my hand.

**13** I will put an end to all her celebrations: her festivals, her new moons, her Sabbaths and all her appointed feasts.

**14** I will lay waste her vines and her fig trees, of which she said, "These are my wages,[^8] which my lovers gave me." I will turn them into a thicket, and the wild animals will devour them.

**15** I will punish her for the feast days of the Baals,[^9] when she burned incense[^10] to them and decked herself with her nose ring and her jewelry, and went after her lovers, but me she forgot, declares Yahweh.[^11]

**16** Therefore I am going to win her back.[^12] I will lead her into the wilderness and speak tenderly to her.[^13]

**17** There I will give her back her vineyards, and make the Valley of Achor[^14] a door of hope. There she will respond[^15] as in the days of her youth, as on the day she came up out of the land of Egypt.

**18** On that day, declares Yahweh, you will call me "My husband," and you will no longer call me "My Baal."[^16]

**19** I will remove the names of the Baals from her mouth, and they will no longer be remembered by name.

**20** On that day I will make a covenant for them with the wild animals, the birds of the sky and the creatures that creep on the ground. I will break bow and sword and battle and banish them from the land, and I will let them lie down in safety.

**21** I will betroth you to myself forever. I will betroth you to myself in righteousness and justice, in faithful love[^17] and compassion.

**22** I will betroth you to myself in faithfulness, and you will know Yahweh.

**23** On that day I will answer, declares Yahweh. I will answer the heavens, and they will answer the earth.

**24** The earth will answer the grain, the new wine and the olive oil, and they will answer Jezreel.[^18]

**25** I will sow her for myself in the land. I will have compassion on Lo-ruhamah, and I will say to Lo-ammi, "You are my people," and he will say, "My God."[^19]

[^1]: Verses 1–2 are 1:10–11 in most English Bibles, and verses 3–25 are 2:1–23. Compare the promise to Abraham (Genesis 22:17; 32:13, 32:12 in most English Bibles).
[^2]: Quoted in Romans 9:26.
[^3]: Or "and they will spring up from the land", like a crop (see "Jezreel", "God sows", in 1:4), or "and they will take possession of the land". The meaning is uncertain.
[^4]: Ammi means "my people" and Ruhamah "shown compassion": the names of Hosea's children (1:6, 9) without "not".
[^5]: The Hebrew changes from "your" to "her".
[^6]: Or "which they made into Baal", that is, into images of Baal. Baal, "lord, master", was the Canaanite storm god, believed to give rain and fertility to the land.
[^7]: Hebrew *navlut*, a word found only here, perhaps "lewdness" or "genitals".
[^8]: Hebrew *etnah*, the payment given to a prostitute.
[^9]: The local forms of the god Baal worshiped at many shrines. Also in verse 19.
[^10]: Or "burned offerings". The Hebrew verb *qatar* means to make something go up in smoke.
[^11]: Hebrew *ne'um YHWH*, "the declaration of Yahweh", a formula that marks a prophet's words as Yahweh's own solemn statement. *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^12]: Or "to persuade her", "to entice her".
[^13]: Literally "speak to her heart".
[^14]: Achor means "trouble" (Joshua 7:24–26).
[^15]: Or "she will sing". Hebrew *anah*, "answer, respond", the same verb as in verses 23–24.
[^16]: Hebrew *ishi*, "my husband" (literally "my man"), and *ba'ali*, "my master", also used for "my husband", and the name of the god Baal.
[^17]: Hebrew *chesed*, loyal, covenant love.
[^18]: Jezreel, "God sows", here becomes a name of promise. Compare 1:4–5; 2:2.
[^19]: Quoted in Romans 9:25 and 1 Peter 2:10.
