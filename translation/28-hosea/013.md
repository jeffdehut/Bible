**1** When Ephraim spoke, there was trembling; he was exalted in Israel. But he incurred guilt through Baal and died.

**2** Now they sin more and more. They make for themselves cast images from their silver, idols skillfully made,[^1] all of them the work of craftsmen. They say of them, "Let those who sacrifice kiss calves!"[^2]

**3** Therefore they will be like the morning mist, like the dew that goes away early, like chaff that swirls from a threshing floor, like smoke from a window.[^3]

**4** But I am the LORD[^4] your God from the land of Egypt.[^5] You know no God but me, and there is no savior except me.

**5** It was I who knew you[^6] in the wilderness, in the land of drought.

**6** When they had pasture, they were filled; they were filled, and their hearts were lifted up. That is why they forgot me.

**7** So I will be like a lion to them; like a leopard I will lurk by the road.[^7]

**8** I will meet them like a bear robbed of her cubs, and I will tear open their chests.[^8] There I will devour them like a lioness; the wild animals will rip them apart.

**9** It is your destruction, Israel, that you are against me, against your helper.[^9]

**10** Where now is your king, that he may save you in all your cities? And where are your rulers,[^10] of whom you said, "Give me a king and officials"?

**11** I gave you a king in my anger, and I took him away in my wrath.

**12** Ephraim's iniquity is bound up; his sin is stored away.

**13** The pains of childbirth come on him, but he is an unwise child, for when the time comes he does not present himself at the opening of the womb.[^11]

**14** Shall I ransom them from the power of Sheol? Shall I redeem them from death? Where, O death, are your plagues? Where, O Sheol, is your destruction?[^12] Compassion is hidden from my eyes.

**15** Though he flourishes among his brothers,[^13] an east wind will come, the wind of the LORD rising from the wilderness. His spring will dry up, and his fountain will fail. He will plunder his treasury of every precious thing.[^14]

[^1]: Literally "according to their understanding".
[^2]: Or "People who offer human sacrifice kiss calves!" Literally "sacrificers of humankind kiss calves". The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. On kissing an idol, compare 1 Kings 19:18.
[^3]: An opening in the roof or wall through which smoke escaped.
[^4]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^5]: See 12:10 (12:9 in most English Bibles).
[^6]: The Septuagint reads "I shepherded you", a Hebrew reading that differs by one letter.
[^7]: Hebrew *ashur*, "I will watch", is spelled like *Ashur*, "Assyria".
[^8]: Literally "the enclosure of their heart".
[^9]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain.
[^10]: Literally "your judges".
[^11]: Literally "the breaking forth of children". The child who will not be born is a picture of Ephraim's refusal to change.
[^12]: Or "I will ransom them from the power of Sheol; I will redeem them from death." The Hebrew has no marker of a question. *Ehi*, "where?", can also be read "I will be": "I will be your plagues, O death; I will be your destruction, O Sheol." *Sheol* is the realm of the dead. Paul adapts the second half in 1 Corinthians 15:55, close to the Septuagint: "Where, O death, is your victory? Where, O death, is your sting?"
[^13]: A play on words: *yafri*, "flourishes", and *Ephraim*. Or "among the reeds".
[^14]: *He* is probably the conqueror whom the east wind represents.
