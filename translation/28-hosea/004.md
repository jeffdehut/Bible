**1** Hear the word of the LORD,[^1] you Israelites, for the LORD has a case[^2] against those who live in the land. There is no faithfulness, no faithful love,[^3] no knowledge of God in the land.

**2** There is cursing, lying, murder, stealing and adultery.[^4] They break all bounds, and bloodshed follows bloodshed.[^5]

**3** Therefore the land mourns,[^6] and all who live in it waste away, along with the wild animals and the birds of the sky, and even the fish of the sea are swept away.

**4** Yet let no one bring a charge, and let no one accuse, for your people are like those who bring charges against a priest.[^7]

**5** You will stumble by day, and the prophet will stumble with you by night, and I will destroy your mother.[^8]

**6** My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge. Because you have rejected knowledge, I reject you from serving as my priest. Since you have forgotten the law of your God, I also will forget your children.

**7** The more they increased, the more they sinned against me. I will change their glory into shame.[^9]

**8** They feed on the sin of my people,[^10] and they long for their iniquity.[^11]

**9** It will be like people, like priest. I will punish them for their ways and repay them for their deeds.[^12]

**10** They will eat but not be satisfied; they will prostitute themselves but not increase, for they have abandoned the LORD and stopped heeding him.[^13]

**11** Prostitution, wine and new wine take away understanding.[^14]

**12** My people consult their piece of wood, and their staff gives them answers.[^15] For a spirit of prostitution has led them astray, and they have prostituted themselves and left their God.[^16]

**13** They sacrifice on the tops of the mountains and burn incense on the hills, under oak, poplar and terebinth, because their shade is pleasant. That is why your daughters turn to prostitution and your daughters-in-law commit adultery.

**14** I will not punish your daughters when they turn to prostitution, or your daughters-in-law when they commit adultery, for the men themselves go off with prostitutes and sacrifice with shrine prostitutes.[^17] A people without understanding will come to ruin.

**15** Though you, Israel, play the prostitute, do not let Judah become guilty. Do not go to Gilgal, do not go up to Beth-aven,[^18] and do not swear, "As the LORD lives!"

**16** For Israel is stubborn, like a stubborn heifer. Can the LORD now pasture them like a lamb in a broad meadow?[^19]

**17** Ephraim[^20] is joined to idols; leave him alone!

**18** When their drinking is over, they give themselves to prostitution. Her rulers dearly love shame.[^21]

**19** A wind has wrapped her up in its wings,[^22] and they will be put to shame because of their sacrifices.[^23]

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *riv*, a lawsuit or legal dispute.
[^3]: Hebrew *chesed*, loyal, covenant love.
[^4]: The list recalls the Ten Commandments (Exodus 20:7, 13–16; Deuteronomy 5:11, 17–20).
[^5]: Literally "bloods touch bloods".
[^6]: Or "dries up". The Hebrew verb can have either meaning.
[^7]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. Some scholars read "for with you is my case, O priest", with small changes to the Hebrew.
[^8]: Probably the nation (compare 2:4, 2:2 in most English Bibles), or the priest's own family. *Destroy* (Hebrew *damah*) is the same verb as "are destroyed" in verse 6.
[^9]: According to an ancient Jewish scribal tradition (the "corrections of the scribes"), the original text read "They have exchanged my glory for shame" (compare Psalm 106:20; Jeremiah 2:11).
[^10]: Hebrew *chatta't* means both "sin" and "sin offering": the priests eat the people's sin offerings (Leviticus 6:19, 6:26 in most English Bibles), and so profit from their sin.
[^11]: Literally "they lift up his soul to their iniquity".
[^12]: Literally "I will punish him for his ways and repay him for his deeds".
[^13]: Literally "for they have abandoned the LORD to keep". Some join the first word of verse 11 to this verse: "they have abandoned the LORD to devote themselves to prostitution".
[^14]: Literally "take away the heart".
[^15]: A wooden idol and a diviner's rod, used to seek omens.
[^16]: Literally "they have prostituted themselves from under their God", as a wife leaves her husband's authority.
[^17]: Hebrew *qedeshot*, "consecrated women". See Genesis 38:21 and Deuteronomy 23:18 (23:17 in most English Bibles).
[^18]: Beth-aven, "house of wickedness", is a mocking name for Bethel, "house of God", where one of the royal shrines of the northern kingdom stood (1 Kings 12:28–33). Gilgal was another center of worship (9:15; 12:12, 12:11 in most English Bibles; Amos 4:4).
[^19]: Or "Now the LORD will pasture them like a lamb in a broad meadow."
[^20]: Ephraim, the largest tribe of the northern kingdom, is often used in Hosea as a name for the kingdom of Israel.
[^21]: The Hebrew of this verse is very uncertain. *Her rulers* is literally "her shields".
[^22]: Or "A spirit". Hebrew *ruach*. The wind will sweep them away (compare "a spirit of prostitution" in verse 12).
[^23]: The same consonants can be read "because of their altars", as the Septuagint does.
