**1** When I would heal Israel, the iniquity of Ephraim is exposed, and the wicked deeds of Samaria. For they practice deceit; a thief breaks in, and a band of raiders attacks in the street.

**2** But they do not consider[^1] that I remember all their wickedness. Now their deeds surround them; they are before my face.

**3** They make the king glad with their wickedness, and the officials with their lies.

**4** They are all adulterers. They are like an oven heated by a baker, who stops stirring the fire from the kneading of the dough until it is leavened.[^2]

**5** On the day of our king, the officials became sick with the heat of wine; he joined hands with mockers.[^3]

**6** For they draw near with their hearts like an oven in their plotting. All night their baker sleeps;[^4] in the morning it blazes like a flaming fire.

**7** All of them are hot as an oven, and they devour their rulers.[^5] All their kings have fallen,[^6] and none of them calls on me.

**8** Ephraim mixes himself with the peoples. Ephraim is a flat cake not turned over.[^7]

**9** Foreigners devour his strength, but he does not know it. Gray hair is sprinkled on him, but he does not know it.

**10** Israel's pride testifies against him,[^8] yet they do not return to Yahweh[^9] their God or seek him in spite of all this.

**11** Ephraim is like a dove, easily fooled and without sense.[^10] They call to Egypt; they go to Assyria.

**12** As they go, I will spread my net over them; I will bring them down like the birds of the sky. I will discipline them according to the report to their assembly.[^11]

**13** Woe to them, for they have strayed from me! Destruction to them, for they have rebelled against me! I would redeem them, but they speak lies about me.

**14** They do not cry out to me from their hearts when they wail on their beds. For grain and new wine they gather together;[^12] they turn away from me.

**15** Though I trained and strengthened their arms, they plot evil against me.

**16** They turn, but not to the Most High;[^13] they are like a faulty bow. Their officials will fall by the sword because of the insolence of their tongues. This will make them a laughingstock in the land of Egypt.

[^1]: Literally "they do not say to their hearts".
[^2]: The Hebrew of verses 4–7 is difficult. The general picture is of conspirators whose passions smolder like a baker's oven until they flare up against their kings.
[^3]: Literally "he drew his hand with mockers".
[^4]: The same consonants can be read "their anger smolders".
[^5]: Literally "their judges".
[^6]: Four of the last six kings of Israel were assassinated (2 Kings 15:10, 14, 25, 30).
[^7]: That is, burned on one side and raw on the other.
[^8]: See 5:5.
[^9]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^10]: Literally "without heart".
[^11]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain.
[^12]: Or "they drag themselves about". The Septuagint reads "they gash themselves" (compare 1 Kings 18:28), a Hebrew reading that differs by one letter.
[^13]: Hebrew *lo al*, "not upward". *Al* may be a title of God, "the Most High" (compare 11:7). The meaning is uncertain.
