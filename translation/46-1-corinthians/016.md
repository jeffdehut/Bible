**1** Now concerning the collection for God's holy people:[^1] you should do what I directed the churches of Galatia to do.

**2** On the first day of every week,[^2] each of you is to put something aside and save it up, as he may prosper, so that collections will not have to be made when I come.

**3** When I arrive, I will send those whom you approve with letters[^3] to take your gift[^4] to Jerusalem.

**4** If it is fitting for me to go too, they will go with me.

**5** I will come to you after I pass through Macedonia, for I am passing through Macedonia.

**6** Perhaps I will stay with you, or even spend the winter, so that you may send me on my way, wherever I go.

**7** For I do not want to see you now just in passing; I hope to spend some time with you, if the Lord permits.

**8** But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost,[^5]

**9** for a wide and effective door has opened to me, and there are many who oppose me.

**10** If Timothy comes, see that he has nothing to fear among you, for he is doing the work of the Lord, as I am.

**11** So let no one despise him. Send him on his way in peace, so that he may come to me, for I am expecting him with the brothers.[^6]

**12** Now concerning our brother Apollos: I strongly urged him to come to you with the brothers, but it was not at all his will[^7] to come now. He will come when he has the opportunity.

**13** Be watchful, stand firm in the faith, be courageous,[^8] be strong.

**14** Let all that you do be done in love.

**15** Now I urge you, brothers and sisters[^9] (you know that the household of Stephanas were the first converts in Achaia,[^10] and that they have devoted themselves to serving God's holy people),

**16** to submit to such people, and to everyone who works with them and labors.

**17** I am glad that Stephanas, Fortunatus and Achaicus have come, because they have made up for your absence,[^11]

**18** for they refreshed my spirit and yours. So give recognition to such people.

**19** The churches of Asia[^12] send you greetings. Aquila and Prisca,[^13] together with the church in their house, send you warm greetings in the Lord.

**20** All the brothers and sisters send you greetings. Greet one another with a holy kiss.

**21** I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand.[^14]

**22** If anyone does not love the Lord, let him be cursed![^15] Marana tha![^16]

**23** The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you.

**24** My love be with all of you in Christ Jesus.[^17]

[^1]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "saints"; also in verse 15. Here the believers in Jerusalem (verse 3; compare Romans 15:25–26).
[^2]: Literally "on one of the Sabbath", an expression for the first day of the week (compare Mark 16:2).
[^3]: Or "whomever you approve, I will send with letters". The Greek can be read either way.
[^4]: Literally "your grace". Greek *charis*, here a gift given freely.
[^5]: Greek *pentekoste*, "fiftieth": the Jewish Festival of Weeks, fifty days after Passover (Leviticus 23:15–21; Deuteronomy 16:9–12).
[^6]: Greek *adelphoi*, probably fellow workers traveling with Timothy; also in verse 12. Or "with the brothers and sisters".
[^7]: Or "it was not at all God's will". Literally "it was not at all will".
[^8]: Literally "act like men".
[^9]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike (see 1:10); also in verse 20.
[^10]: Literally "the firstfruits of Achaia" (see 15:20). Achaia was the Roman province of southern Greece, with Corinth as its capital.
[^11]: Or "they have supplied what you lacked". Literally "they have filled up your lack".
[^12]: The Roman province of Asia, with Ephesus as its chief city.
[^13]: Called Priscilla in Acts 18:2, 18 and 26. Many manuscripts read "Priscilla" here.
[^14]: Paul usually dictated his letters to a scribe (compare Romans 16:22) and added a closing greeting in his own handwriting (compare Galatians 6:11).
[^15]: Greek *anathema* (see 12:3).
[^16]: An Aramaic phrase. As divided in the SBLGNT, following NA28, it means "Our Lord, come!" (compare Revelation 22:20). The other editions divide the letters differently, *maran atha*, "Our Lord has come" or "Our Lord is coming".
[^17]: Many manuscripts add "Amen".
