**1** Brothers and sisters,[^1] I could not speak to you as spiritual people, but as people of the flesh,[^2] as infants in Christ.

**2** I gave you milk to drink, not solid food, for you were not yet able to take it. Indeed, even now you are not able,

**3** for you are still people of the flesh. For where there is jealousy and strife among you,[^3] are you not people of the flesh, and living in a merely human way?[^4]

**4** For when one says, "I belong to Paul," and another, "I belong to Apollos," are you not merely human?[^5]

**5** What then is Apollos? And what is Paul? Servants through whom you came to believe, as the Lord gave to each.[^6]

**6** I planted, Apollos watered, but God made it grow.

**7** So neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but only God, who makes it grow.

**8** The one who plants and the one who waters are one,[^7] and each will receive his own wages according to his own labor.

**9** For we are God's fellow workers;[^8] you are God's field, God's building.

**10** According to the grace of God given to me, I laid a foundation like a skilled master builder,[^9] and someone else is building on it. But let each one take care how he builds on it.

**11** For no one can lay any foundation other than the one already laid, which is Jesus Christ.

**12** If anyone builds on the foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay or straw,

**13** each one's work will become evident, for the Day[^10] will make it clear, because it will be revealed with fire,[^11] and the fire will test what sort of work each one has done.

**14** If the work that anyone has built on the foundation lasts, he will receive a reward.[^12]

**15** If anyone's work is burned up, he will suffer loss; he himself will be saved, but only as through fire.

**16** Don't you know that you are God's temple and that the Spirit of God lives in you?[^13]

**17** If anyone destroys[^14] God's temple, God will destroy that person. For God's temple is holy, and you are that temple.

**18** Let no one deceive himself. If anyone among you thinks he is wise in this age, let him become foolish so that he may become wise.

**19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness in God's sight. For it is written, "He catches the wise in their craftiness,"[^15]

**20** and again, "The Lord knows the thoughts of the wise, that they are futile."[^16]

**21** So let no one boast about human beings. For all things are yours,

**22** whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas or the world or life or death or the present or the future: all are yours,

**23** and you are Christ's, and Christ is God's.

[^1]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike (see 1:10).
[^2]: Greek *sarkinoi*, "made of flesh", and in verse 3 *sarkikoi*, "fleshly". *The flesh* in Paul's moral sense is human nature in its weakness and its opposition to God, contrasted here with the Spirit. Many manuscripts have *sarkikoi* in this verse too.
[^3]: Many manuscripts add "and divisions".
[^4]: Literally "walking according to a human being".
[^5]: Literally "are you not human beings?" Many manuscripts read "are you not people of the flesh?"
[^6]: Literally "and to each as the Lord gave". This may mean that the Lord gave each servant his task, or that he gave faith to each believer.
[^7]: Or "are equal", "have one purpose".
[^8]: Or "fellow workers with God". The Greek can mean working together with God or working together in God's service.
[^9]: Literally "a wise master builder". Greek *architekton*.
[^10]: The day of the Lord (1:8), the day of judgment.
[^11]: The subject may be the Day or each one's work.
[^12]: Or "wages", the word used in verse 8.
[^13]: "You" is plural in Greek, here and in verse 17: the believers together are God's temple. *In you*: or "among you".
[^14]: Or "ruins", "corrupts". The same Greek verb, *phtheiro*, is used twice in the sentence.
[^15]: Job 5:13. Paul's Greek is closer to the Hebrew than to the Septuagint.
[^16]: Psalm 94:11, with "the wise" in place of "humankind". The Hebrew says that their thoughts are "only a breath". In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, YHWH ("the LORD").
