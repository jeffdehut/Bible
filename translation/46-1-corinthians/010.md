**1** For I do not want you to be unaware,[^1] brothers and sisters,[^2] that our ancestors[^3] were all under the cloud, and all passed through the sea,[^4]

**2** and all were baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea,

**3** and all ate the same spiritual food,[^5]

**4** and all drank the same spiritual drink. For they drank from the spiritual rock that followed them, and the rock was Christ.[^6]

**5** Nevertheless, God was not pleased with most of them, for they were struck down in the wilderness.[^7]

**6** These things happened as examples[^8] for us, so that we would not crave evil things as they did.[^9]

**7** Do not become idolaters, as some of them did. As it is written, "The people sat down to eat and drink, and then got up to revel."[^10]

**8** We must not commit sexual immorality, as some of them did, and twenty-three thousand fell in a single day.[^11]

**9** We must not put Christ[^12] to the test, as some of them did, and were destroyed by the snakes.[^13]

**10** And do not grumble, as some of them did, and were destroyed by the destroyer.[^14]

**11** These things happened to them as examples, and they were written down to warn us, on whom the ends of the ages have come.

**12** So let anyone who thinks he is standing firm take care that he does not fall.

**13** No temptation[^15] has overtaken you except what is common to human beings. God is faithful, and he will not let you be tempted beyond what you can bear. With the temptation he will also provide the way out, so that you are able to endure it.

**14** Therefore, my dear friends,[^16] flee from idolatry.

**15** I am speaking to sensible people; judge for yourselves what I say.

**16** The cup of blessing[^17] that we bless, is it not a sharing[^18] in the blood of Christ? The bread that we break, is it not a sharing in the body of Christ?

**17** Because there is one bread,[^19] we who are many are one body, for we all share in the one bread.

**18** Consider the people of Israel:[^20] are not those who eat the sacrifices partners in the altar?

**19** What then am I saying? That food sacrificed to idols is anything, or that an idol is anything?

**20** No, but that what they sacrifice,[^21] they sacrifice to demons and not to God.[^22] I do not want you to be partners with demons.

**21** You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons. You cannot share in the table of the Lord[^23] and the table of demons.

**22** Or are we trying to provoke the Lord to jealousy?[^24] Are we stronger than he is?

**23** "Everything is permitted,"[^25] but not everything is beneficial. "Everything is permitted," but not everything builds up.

**24** No one should seek his own good, but the good of the other person.

**25** Eat whatever is sold in the meat market without raising questions[^26] because of conscience,

**26** for "the earth is the Lord's, and everything in it."[^27]

**27** If an unbeliever invites you and you want to go, eat whatever is set before you without raising questions because of conscience.

**28** But if someone says to you, "This has been offered in sacrifice,"[^28] do not eat it, for the sake of the one who told you, and for conscience' sake.[^29]

**29** I mean the other person's conscience, not your own. For why should my freedom be judged by someone else's conscience?[^30]

**30** If I take part with thanks,[^31] why am I denounced because of something for which I give thanks?

**31** So whether you eat or drink, or whatever you do, do everything for the glory of God.

**32** Do not cause anyone to stumble,[^32] whether Jews or Greeks or the church of God,

**33** just as I try to please everyone in everything, not seeking my own benefit but that of the many, so that they may be saved.

[^1]: Many manuscripts read "Now I do not want you to be unaware".
[^2]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike (see 1:10).
[^3]: Literally "our fathers".
[^4]: Exodus 13:21–22; 14:19–22.
[^5]: The manna (Exodus 16:4–35).
[^6]: Exodus 17:1–7; Numbers 20:2–13. Later Jewish tradition told of a rock or well that went with Israel through the wilderness.
[^7]: Compare Numbers 14:16, 29.
[^8]: Greek *typoi*, "patterns, examples, types"; in verse 11, *typikos*, "as examples".
[^9]: Compare Numbers 11:4, 34.
[^10]: Exodus 32:6. *Revel*: Greek *paizo*, "play, make merry", translating the Hebrew word used there.
[^11]: Numbers 25:1–9, which gives the number who died as 24,000.
[^12]: Some manuscripts read "the Lord".
[^13]: Numbers 21:4–9.
[^14]: Compare Numbers 14:2, 36–37 and 17:6–14 (16:41–49 in most English Bibles). For "the destroyer", see Exodus 12:23.
[^15]: Or "testing"; also later in the verse. Greek *peirasmos*.
[^16]: Literally "my beloved".
[^17]: The cup of wine over which a blessing, a prayer of thanks to God, was said at a Jewish meal.
[^18]: Greek *koinonia*, "sharing, participation, fellowship" (see 1:9); also later in the verse. In verses 18 and 20, "partners" is the related word *koinonoi*.
[^19]: Or "one loaf"; also later in the verse.
[^20]: Literally "Israel according to the flesh", that is, the people of Israel by descent.
[^21]: The SBLGNT here follows NA28; other editions read "what the Gentiles sacrifice".
[^22]: The words echo Deuteronomy 32:17, "They sacrificed to demons that were not God".
[^23]: Compare Malachi 1:7, 12, where the altar is called "the table of the LORD".
[^24]: Compare Deuteronomy 32:21.
[^25]: See 6:12, where the saying has "for me". Many manuscripts add "for me" here, in both places.
[^26]: Greek *anakrino*, "examine" (see 2:14); also in verse 27.
[^27]: Psalm 24:1. In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, YHWH ("the LORD").
[^28]: Greek *hierothyton*, "offered in a temple", a more neutral word than the one used in 8:1 and 10:19 (*eidolothyton*, "sacrificed to an idol"). Many manuscripts have *eidolothyton* here.
[^29]: Many manuscripts add "for the earth is the Lord's, and everything in it" (verse 26).
[^30]: The questions in verses 29–30 may be Paul's own, or an objection he imagines someone raising.
[^31]: Or "with grace". Greek *charis*.
[^32]: Literally "Be without cause of stumbling", a word related to the one used in 8:9.
