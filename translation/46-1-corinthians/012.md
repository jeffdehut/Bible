**1** Now concerning spiritual gifts,[^1] brothers and sisters,[^2] I do not want you to be uninformed.

**2** You know that when you were Gentiles,[^3] you were led astray to mute idols, however you were led.[^4]

**3** Therefore I want you to understand that no one speaking by the Spirit of God says, "Jesus is cursed!"[^5] and no one can say, "Jesus is Lord!" except by the Holy Spirit.

**4** There are different kinds of gifts,[^6] but the same Spirit;

**5** there are different kinds of service,[^7] and the same Lord;

**6** and there are different kinds of activities, but the same God, who works all of them in everyone.

**7** To each one the manifestation of the Spirit is given for the common good.

**8** To one is given through the Spirit a message of wisdom,[^8] to another a message of knowledge according to the same Spirit,

**9** to another faith by the same Spirit, to another gifts of healing by the one Spirit,

**10** to another the working of miracles,[^9] to another prophecy,[^10] to another the ability to distinguish between spirits,[^11] to another various kinds of tongues,[^12] to another the interpretation of tongues.

**11** All these are the work of one and the same Spirit, who distributes them to each one individually, just as he wills.

**12** For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the members of the body, though many, are one body, so it is with Christ.

**13** For in one Spirit[^13] we were all baptized into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, whether slaves or free, and we were all given one Spirit to drink.

**14** For the body does not consist of one member but of many.

**15** If the foot should say, "Because I am not a hand, I do not belong to the body," that would not make it any less a part of the body.

**16** And if the ear should say, "Because I am not an eye, I do not belong to the body," that would not make it any less a part of the body.

**17** If the whole body were an eye, where would the hearing be? If the whole body were hearing, where would the sense of smell be?

**18** But as it is, God has arranged the members in the body, each one of them, just as he wanted.

**19** If they were all one member, where would the body be?

**20** As it is, there are many members, but one body.

**21** The eye cannot say to the hand, "I have no need of you," nor again the head to the feet, "I have no need of you."

**22** On the contrary, the members of the body that seem to be weaker are all the more necessary,

**23** and the parts of the body that we think less honorable we clothe with greater honor, and our unpresentable parts[^14] are treated with greater modesty,

**24** which our presentable parts do not need. But God has put the body together, giving greater honor to the part that lacked it,

**25** so that there may be no division in the body, but that the members may have the same concern for one another.

**26** If one member suffers, all the members suffer with it; if a member is honored, all the members rejoice with it.

**27** Now you are the body of Christ, and individually members of it.

**28** And God has appointed in the church first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then miracles, then gifts of healing, forms of help, forms of guidance,[^15] and various kinds of tongues.

**29** Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Do all work miracles?[^16]

**30** Do all have gifts of healing? Do all speak in tongues? Do all interpret?

**31** But earnestly desire the greater gifts.[^17] And I will show you a still more excellent way.

[^1]: Or "spiritual people". Greek *pneumatika* or *pneumatikoi*; the form used can mean either.
[^2]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike (see 1:10).
[^3]: Or "pagans". Greek *ethne*, "nations".
[^4]: The Greek is difficult; literally "how you were led, being carried away". Some interpreters see a reference to frenzied experiences in pagan worship.
[^5]: Greek *anathema*, "cursed", devoted to destruction.
[^6]: Greek *charismata*, "gifts of grace" (see 1:7); also in verses 9, 28, 30 and 31.
[^7]: Greek *diakoniai*, "services, ministries".
[^8]: Literally "a word of wisdom"; and "a word of knowledge".
[^9]: Literally "workings of powers".
[^10]: Greek *propheteia*, speaking a message from God. In chapter 14 it is speech in the gathered church that others can understand, which builds up, encourages and comforts (14:3) and can lay bare the secrets of the heart (14:24–25), and which others are to weigh (14:29). How far it included prediction is discussed.
[^11]: Literally "discernments of spirits", probably the ability to judge whether an utterance comes from the Spirit of God or from another spirit. Compare 14:29.
[^12]: Greek *glossai*, "tongues, languages"; also in verses 28 and 30. Paul describes speech that others cannot understand unless it is interpreted (14:2–28). Whether he means human languages unknown to the speaker (compare Acts 2:4–11) or speech that is not any human language (compare 13:1, "the tongues of angels") is debated.
[^13]: Or "by one Spirit".
[^14]: That is, the private parts of the body.
[^15]: Greek *antilempseis*, "forms of help", and *kyberneseis*, "forms of guidance", literally "steering", as by a ship's pilot.
[^16]: Literally "Are all miracles?"
[^17]: Or "But you are eagerly seeking the greater gifts", a statement rather than a command. Many manuscripts read "the better gifts".
