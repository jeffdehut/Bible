**1** The Ziphites came to Saul at Gibeah and said, "Is not David hiding on the hill of Hachilah, which faces Jeshimon?"[^1]

**2** So Saul got up and went down to the wilderness of Ziph with three thousand chosen men of Israel, to search for David in the wilderness of Ziph.

**3** Saul camped on the hill of Hachilah, which faces Jeshimon, beside the road. David was staying in the wilderness. When he saw that Saul had come after him into the wilderness,

**4** David sent out scouts and learned that Saul had certainly come.[^2]

**5** Then David set out and came to the place where Saul had camped. David saw the place where Saul lay, with Abner son of Ner, the commander of his army. Saul was lying inside the camp,[^3] with the troops camped around him.

**6** David spoke to Ahimelech the Hittite and to Abishai son of Zeruiah,[^4] Joab's brother. He asked, "Who will go down with me into the camp to Saul?" Abishai said, "I will go down with you."

**7** So David and Abishai went to the troops by night, and there was Saul, lying asleep inside the camp, with his spear stuck in the ground near his head. Abner and the troops were lying around him.

**8** Abishai said to David, "Today God has handed your enemy over to you. Now please let me pin him to the ground with one thrust of the spear.[^5] I won't need to strike him twice."

**9** But David said to Abishai, "Do not destroy him. Who can raise his hand against the LORD's anointed[^6] and be guiltless?"

**10** David said, "As surely as the LORD lives, the LORD himself will strike him, or his day will come and he will die, or he will go down into battle and perish.

**11** The LORD forbid[^7] that I should raise my hand against the LORD's anointed. Now take the spear that is near his head and the water jug, and let us go."

**12** So David took the spear and the water jug from near Saul's head, and they left. No one saw it or knew about it, and no one woke up. They were all asleep, because a deep sleep[^8] from the LORD had fallen on them.

**13** Then David crossed over to the other side and stood on top of the hill some distance away, with a wide space between them.

**14** David called out to the troops and to Abner son of Ner, "Won't you answer, Abner?" Abner answered, "Who are you, calling to the king?"

**15** David said to Abner, "Aren't you a man? Who is like you in Israel? Then why didn't you guard your lord the king? For one of the people came to destroy your lord the king.

**16** What you have done is not good. As surely as the LORD lives, all of you deserve to die,[^9] because you did not guard your lord, the LORD's anointed. Now look, where are the king's spear and the water jug that were near his head?"

**17** Saul recognized David's voice and said, "Is that your voice, my son David?" David said, "It is my voice, my lord the king."

**18** He said, "Why is my lord pursuing his servant? What have I done? What evil am I guilty of?[^10]

**19** Now let my lord the king listen to the words of his servant. If it is the LORD who has stirred you up against me, may he accept an offering.[^11] But if it is human beings, may they be cursed before the LORD, for they have now driven me out from my share in the LORD's inheritance,[^12] saying, 'Go, serve other gods.'

**20** So now, do not let my blood fall to the ground far from the presence of the LORD. For the king of Israel has come out to look for a single flea,[^13] as one hunts a partridge[^14] in the mountains."

**21** Then Saul said, "I have sinned. Come back, my son David. I will never harm you again, because my life was precious in your eyes today. I have acted like a fool and have made a very great mistake."

**22** David answered, "Here is the king's spear.[^15] Let one of the young men come over and get it.

**23** The LORD repays everyone for his righteousness and his faithfulness. The LORD gave you into my hand[^16] today, but I would not raise my hand against the LORD's anointed.

**24** As your life was precious[^17] in my eyes today, so may my life be precious in the eyes of the LORD, and may he rescue me from all trouble."

**25** Saul said to David, "May you be blessed, my son David! You will do great things and you will surely succeed."[^18] So David went on his way, and Saul went back home.[^19]

[^1]: See 23:19. Also in verse 3.
[^2]: Literally "had come to certain"; some take the last word as a place name.
[^3]: Hebrew *ma'gal*; see 17:20. Also in verse 7.
[^4]: Zeruiah was David's sister (1 Chronicles 2:16).
[^5]: Literally "let me strike him with the spear and into the ground once".
[^6]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. "The LORD's anointed" (Hebrew *meshiach YHWH*, "the anointed one of the LORD") is the king; see 2:10. Also in verses 11, 16 and 23.
[^7]: Literally "Far be it from me, from the LORD".
[^8]: Hebrew *tardemah*, as in Genesis 2:21 and 15:12.
[^9]: Literally "you are sons of death".
[^10]: Literally "What evil is in my hand?"
[^11]: Literally "may he smell an offering", that is, be pleased with a sacrifice.
[^12]: Literally "from attaching myself to the inheritance of the LORD".
[^13]: The Septuagint reads "to seek my life".
[^14]: Hebrew *qore*, literally "the caller".
[^15]: The Hebrew text is written "Here is the spear, O king" but read "Here is the king's spear".
[^16]: Literally "into a hand".
[^17]: Literally "great". Also later in the verse.
[^18]: Literally "you will surely do, and you will surely prevail".
[^19]: Literally "to his place".
