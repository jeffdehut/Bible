**1** In those days the Philistines gathered their forces for war, to fight against Israel. Achish said to David, "You must understand that you and your men will go out with me in the army."

**2** David said to Achish, "Then you will see what your servant can do." Achish said to David, "Then I will make you my bodyguard[^1] for life."

**3** Now Samuel had died, and all Israel had mourned for him and buried him in Ramah, his own town. And Saul had removed the mediums and spiritists[^2] from the land.

**4** The Philistines gathered and came and camped at Shunem, and Saul gathered all Israel, and they camped at Gilboa.

**5** When Saul saw the Philistine army, he was afraid, and his heart trembled greatly.

**6** Saul inquired of the LORD,[^3] but the LORD did not answer him, not by dreams, nor by Urim,[^4] nor by prophets.

**7** Then Saul said to his servants, "Find me a woman who is a medium,[^5] so that I may go to her and consult her." His servants said to him, "There is a woman who is a medium at En-dor."

**8** So Saul disguised himself by putting on other clothes, and he went with two men. They came to the woman at night, and he said, "Please consult a spirit[^6] for me, and bring up for me the one I name to you."

**9** But the woman said to him, "Surely you know what Saul has done, how he has cut off the mediums and spiritists from the land. Why then are you setting a trap for my life to have me killed?"

**10** Saul swore to her by the LORD, "As surely as the LORD lives, you will not be punished[^7] for this."

**11** Then the woman asked, "Whom shall I bring up for you?" He said, "Bring up Samuel for me."

**12** When the woman saw Samuel, she cried out with a loud voice. The woman said to Saul, "Why have you deceived me? You are Saul!"

**13** The king said to her, "Do not be afraid. What do you see?" The woman said to Saul, "I see a divine being[^8] coming up out of the earth."

**14** He said to her, "What does he look like?" She said, "An old man is coming up, wrapped in a robe." Then Saul knew that it was Samuel, and he bowed with his face to the ground and lay down before him.

**15** Samuel said to Saul, "Why have you disturbed me by bringing me up?" Saul said, "I am in great distress. The Philistines are fighting against me, and God has turned away from me. He no longer answers me, either by prophets or by dreams. So I have called you to tell me what I should do."

**16** Samuel said, "Why do you ask me, when the LORD has turned away from you and become your enemy?[^9]

**17** The LORD has done to you[^10] just as he spoke through me. The LORD has torn the kingdom out of your hand and given it to your neighbor, to David.

**18** Because you did not obey the LORD[^11] and did not carry out his fierce anger against Amalek, the LORD has done this to you today.

**19** The LORD will also give Israel along with you into the hands of the Philistines. Tomorrow you and your sons will be with me. The LORD will also give the army of Israel into the hands of the Philistines."

**20** At once Saul fell full length on the ground, terrified by Samuel's words. There was no strength left in him, for he had eaten nothing[^12] all that day and all that night.

**21** The woman came to Saul and saw that he was badly shaken. She said to him, "Look, your servant[^13] has obeyed you. I took my life in my hands[^14] and did what you told me.

**22** Now please listen to your servant too. Let me set a piece of bread before you. Eat, so that you may have strength when you go on your way."

**23** But he refused and said, "I will not eat." But his servants, and the woman as well, urged him, and he listened to them. He got up from the ground and sat on the bed.

**24** The woman had a fattened calf in the house. She quickly slaughtered it, took flour, kneaded it and baked bread without yeast.

**25** She set it before Saul and his servants, and they ate. Then they got up and left that same night.

[^1]: Literally "the keeper of my head".
[^2]: Hebrew *ovot* and *yidde'onim*: spirits of the dead, or people who claimed to call them up and consult them (Leviticus 19:31; Deuteronomy 18:11). Also in verse 9.
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Hebrew *urim*, objects kept in the high priest's breastpiece and used to learn the LORD's decision (Exodus 28:30; Numbers 27:21).
[^5]: Literally "a woman who is mistress of an *ov*". Also later in the verse.
[^6]: Hebrew *ov*.
[^7]: Literally "guilt will not meet you".
[^8]: Hebrew *elohim*, "god(s), divine being". The verb "coming up" is plural.
[^9]: Hebrew *arekha*, a rare word, probably "your enemy". The Septuagint reads "and is with your neighbor".
[^10]: Literally "to him", or "for himself".
[^11]: Literally "did not listen to the voice of the LORD".
[^12]: Literally "he had not eaten bread".
[^13]: Hebrew *shiphchah*, "female servant"; see 1:18. Also in verse 22.
[^14]: Literally "I put my *nephesh* in my palm".
