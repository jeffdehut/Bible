**1** The boy Samuel was serving Yahweh[^1] under Eli. In those days the word of Yahweh was rare; visions were not common.[^2]

**2** One day Eli was lying down in his usual place. His eyes[^3] had begun to grow dim, and he could not see.

**3** The lamp of God[^4] had not yet gone out, and Samuel was lying down in the temple[^5] of Yahweh, where the ark of God was.

**4** Then Yahweh called Samuel, and he said, "Here I am."

**5** He ran to Eli and said, "Here I am. You called me." But Eli said, "I did not call you. Go back and lie down." So he went and lay down.

**6** Yahweh called again, "Samuel!" Samuel got up, went to Eli and said, "Here I am. You called me." But Eli said, "I did not call you, my son. Go back and lie down."

**7** Samuel did not yet know Yahweh, and the word of Yahweh had not yet been revealed to him.

**8** Yahweh called Samuel a third time. He got up, went to Eli and said, "Here I am. You called me." Then Eli understood that Yahweh was calling the boy.

**9** So Eli said to Samuel, "Go and lie down. If he calls you, say, 'Speak, Yahweh, for your servant is listening.'" So Samuel went and lay down in his place.

**10** Yahweh came and stood there, and called as he had before, "Samuel! Samuel!" Samuel said, "Speak, for your servant is listening."

**11** Yahweh said to Samuel, "I am about to do something in Israel that will make both ears of everyone who hears about it tingle.

**12** On that day I will carry out against Eli everything I have spoken against his family, from beginning to end.

**13** I have told him[^6] that I am going to judge his family forever because of the guilt he knew about: his sons were bringing a curse on themselves,[^7] and he did not restrain them.

**14** Therefore I have sworn to the family of Eli that the guilt of Eli's family will never be atoned for[^8] by sacrifice or offering."

**15** Samuel lay down until morning. Then he opened the doors of the house of Yahweh. Samuel was afraid to tell Eli about the vision.

**16** But Eli called Samuel and said, "Samuel, my son." He said, "Here I am."

**17** Eli said, "What was it that he said to you? Please do not hide it from me. May God deal with you, and more severely, if you hide from me a single word of all he said to you."[^9]

**18** So Samuel told him everything and hid nothing from him. And Eli said, "He is Yahweh. Let him do what seems good to him."

**19** Samuel grew up, and Yahweh was with him and let none of his words fall to the ground.[^10]

**20** All Israel from Dan to Beersheba knew that Samuel was established[^11] as a prophet of Yahweh.

**21** Yahweh continued to appear at Shiloh, for Yahweh revealed himself to Samuel at Shiloh through the word of Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "there was no vision breaking out".
[^3]: The Hebrew text is written "his eye" but read "his eyes".
[^4]: The lamp in the sanctuary, which was kept burning through the night (Exodus 27:20–21).
[^5]: Hebrew *heikhal*, "palace, temple"; see 1:9.
[^6]: Or "I will tell him".
[^7]: Or "were treating them with contempt". The Septuagint reads "were cursing God". In Jewish tradition this is one of the places where the scribes are said to have changed the wording out of reverence for God.
[^8]: Literally "if the guilt of Eli's house will be atoned for", a form of oath meaning that it certainly will not.
[^9]: Literally "Thus may God do to you, and thus may he add", a formula calling down punishment if the promise is broken.
[^10]: That is, made all his words come true.
[^11]: Hebrew *ne'eman*, "established, confirmed, trustworthy".
