**1** David left there and escaped to the cave of Adullam. When his brothers and all his father's household heard of it, they went down to him there.

**2** Everyone who was in distress, everyone who was in debt, and everyone who was bitter in spirit[^1] gathered to him, and he became their commander. About four hundred men were with him.

**3** From there David went to Mizpeh in Moab and said to the king of Moab, "Please let my father and mother come and stay with you[^2] until I know what God will do for me."

**4** So he left them with[^3] the king of Moab, and they stayed with him the whole time David was in the stronghold.

**5** But the prophet Gad said to David, "Do not stay in the stronghold. Go into the land of Judah." So David left and went to the forest of Hereth.

**6** Saul heard that David and the men with him had been discovered. Saul was sitting at Gibeah under the tamarisk tree on the height,[^4] with his spear in his hand, and all his servants were standing around him.

**7** Saul said to his servants who stood around him, "Listen, you Benjaminites! Will the son of Jesse give every one of you fields and vineyards? Will he make all of you commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds?

**8** Is that why all of you have conspired against me? No one tells me[^5] when my son makes a covenant with the son of Jesse. None of you is sorry for me[^6] or tells me that my son has stirred up my servant to lie in wait against me, as he does today."

**9** Then Doeg the Edomite, who was standing with Saul's servants,[^7] answered, "I saw the son of Jesse come to Nob, to Ahimelech son of Ahitub.

**10** Ahimelech inquired of Yahweh[^8] for him, gave him provisions, and gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine."

**11** Then the king sent for Ahimelech the priest, son of Ahitub, and all his father's family, the priests who were at Nob, and they all came to the king.

**12** Saul said, "Listen, son of Ahitub." He said, "Here I am, my lord."

**13** Saul said to him, "Why have you conspired against me, you and the son of Jesse? You gave him bread and a sword and inquired of God for him, so that he has risen against me to lie in wait, as he does today."

**14** Ahimelech answered the king, "Who among all your servants is as faithful as David? He is the king's son-in-law, captain of your bodyguard,[^9] and honored in your household.

**15** Was that day the first time I inquired of God for him? Far from it! Let not the king accuse[^10] his servant or any of my father's family, for your servant knew nothing at all about all this, small or great."

**16** The king said, "You will surely die, Ahimelech, you and all your father's family."

**17** Then the king said to the guards[^11] standing around him, "Turn and kill the priests of Yahweh, for they too are on David's side.[^12] They knew he was fleeing, yet they did not tell me."[^13] But the king's servants would not lift a hand to strike down the priests of Yahweh.

**18** The king said to Doeg, "You turn and strike down the priests." So Doeg the Edomite turned and struck down the priests himself. That day he killed eighty-five men who wore the linen ephod.[^14]

**19** He also put Nob, the town of the priests, to the sword: men and women, children and babies, oxen, donkeys and sheep, he put to the sword.

**20** But one son of Ahimelech son of Ahitub escaped. His name was Abiathar, and he fled to join David.[^15]

**21** Abiathar told David that Saul had killed the priests of Yahweh.

**22** David said to Abiathar, "I knew that day, when Doeg the Edomite was there, that he would be sure to tell Saul. I am to blame for[^16] the death of everyone in your father's family.

**23** Stay with me. Do not be afraid. The one who seeks my life seeks your life, but with me you will be safe."[^17]

[^1]: Literally "bitter of *nephesh*".
[^2]: Literally "go out with you".
[^3]: Literally "he led them before". With different vowels, "he left them with".
[^4]: Or "at Ramah".
[^5]: Literally "No one uncovers my ear". Also later in the verse.
[^6]: Literally "is sick for me".
[^7]: Or "who was in charge of Saul's servants".
[^8]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^9]: Hebrew *sar el mishma'tekha*, of uncertain meaning; perhaps "who turns aside to your obedience" or "who has access to your private council".
[^10]: Literally "put a thing against".
[^11]: Literally "runners".
[^12]: Literally "for their hand too is with David".
[^13]: The Hebrew text is written "did not uncover his ear" but read "did not uncover my ear".
[^14]: The Septuagint reads "305 men". For the linen ephod, see 2:18.
[^15]: Literally "fled after David".
[^16]: Literally "I have turned against every *nephesh* of your father's house". The Septuagint reads "I am guilty of".
[^17]: Literally "for you are a charge (in safekeeping) with me".
