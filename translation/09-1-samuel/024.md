**1** David went up from there and stayed in the strongholds of En-gedi.[^1]

**2** When Saul came back from pursuing the Philistines, he was told, "David is in the wilderness of En-gedi."

**3** So Saul took three thousand chosen men from all Israel and went to look for David and his men in front of the Rocks of the Wild Goats.

**4** He came to the sheepfolds by the road, where there was a cave, and Saul went in to relieve himself.[^2] David and his men were sitting in the back of the cave.

**5** David's men said to him, "This is the day Yahweh[^3] spoke of when he said to you, 'I am giving your enemy[^4] into your hands, and you can do to him whatever seems good to you.'" Then David got up and secretly cut off the corner of Saul's robe.

**6** Afterward David's conscience troubled him[^5] because he had cut off the corner of Saul's robe.

**7** He said to his men, "Yahweh forbid[^6] that I should do such a thing to my lord, Yahweh's anointed,[^7] and raise my hand against him, for he is Yahweh's anointed."

**8** With these words David restrained[^8] his men and did not let them rise up against Saul. Then Saul got up, left the cave and went on his way.

**9** Afterward David also got up, went out of the cave and called after Saul, "My lord the king!" When Saul looked behind him, David bowed with his face to the ground and lay down before him.

**10** David said to Saul, "Why do you listen to the words of people who say, 'David is seeking to harm you'?

**11** This very day your own eyes have seen how Yahweh gave you into my hands in the cave today. Some told me to kill you,[^9] but I spared you.[^10] I said, 'I will not raise my hand against my lord, for he is Yahweh's anointed.'

**12** Look, my father, see the corner of your robe in my hand! Since I cut off the corner of your robe and did not kill you, you can know and see that I am guilty of no evil or rebellion.[^11] I have not sinned against you, yet you are hunting me down to take my life.

**13** May Yahweh judge between me and you, and may Yahweh avenge me on you, but my hand will not be against you.

**14** As the ancient proverb says, 'Out of the wicked comes wickedness.' But my hand will not be against you.

**15** Against whom has the king of Israel come out? Whom are you pursuing? A dead dog! A single flea!

**16** May Yahweh be the judge and decide between me and you. May he see and plead my cause, and vindicate me by rescuing me from your hand."[^12]

**17** When David had finished saying these words to Saul, Saul said, "Is that your voice, my son David?" And Saul wept aloud.

**18** He said to David, "You are more righteous than I, for you have repaid me with good, while I have repaid you with evil.

**19** Today you have shown how well you have dealt with me, for Yahweh handed me over to you, but you did not kill me.

**20** When a man finds his enemy, does he let him go on his way unharmed? May Yahweh reward you with good for what you have done for me today.

**21** Now I know that you will surely be king, and that the kingdom of Israel will be established in your hand.

**22** So swear to me by Yahweh that you will not cut off my descendants after me, or wipe out my name from my father's family."

**23** So David swore this to Saul. Then Saul went home, but David and his men went up to the stronghold.

[^1]: Verse 1 is 23:29 in most English Bibles, and verses 2–23 are 24:1–22.
[^2]: Literally "to cover his feet", a polite expression.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: The Hebrew text is written "your enemies" but read "your enemy".
[^5]: Literally "David's heart struck him".
[^6]: Literally "Far be it from me, from Yahweh".
[^7]: Hebrew *meshiach YHWH*, "the anointed one of Yahweh", that is, the king; see 2:10. Also later in the verse and in verse 11.
[^8]: The meaning of the Hebrew verb is uncertain.
[^9]: Literally "And one said to kill you".
[^10]: Literally "and it had pity on you", that is, my eye. The Septuagint reads "and I spared you".
[^11]: Literally "that there is no evil or rebellion in my hand".
[^12]: Literally "and judge me from your hand".
