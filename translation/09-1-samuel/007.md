**1** So the men of Kiriath-jearim came and took up the ark of Yahweh.[^1] They brought it to the house of Abinadab on the hill,[^2] and they consecrated his son Eleazar to take care of the ark of Yahweh.

**2** From the day the ark came to stay at Kiriath-jearim, a long time passed, twenty years, and all the house of Israel lamented after Yahweh.[^3]

**3** Samuel said to the whole house of Israel, "If you are returning to Yahweh with all your heart, get rid of the foreign gods and the Ashtoreths[^4] among you. Set your hearts on Yahweh and serve him alone, and he will rescue you from the power of the Philistines."

**4** So the Israelites got rid of the Baals[^5] and the Ashtoreths, and served Yahweh alone.

**5** Then Samuel said, "Gather all Israel at Mizpah, and I will pray to Yahweh for you."

**6** So they gathered at Mizpah. They drew water and poured it out before Yahweh. That day they fasted, and they said there, "We have sinned against Yahweh." And Samuel judged[^6] the Israelites at Mizpah.

**7** When the Philistines heard that the Israelites had gathered at Mizpah, the rulers of the Philistines went up against Israel. When the Israelites heard of it, they were afraid of the Philistines.

**8** The Israelites said to Samuel, "Do not stop crying out to Yahweh our God for us, so that he will save us from the power of the Philistines."

**9** So Samuel took a lamb that was still nursing and offered it as a whole burnt offering to Yahweh. Samuel cried out to Yahweh for Israel, and Yahweh answered him.

**10** While Samuel was offering the burnt offering, the Philistines drew near to fight against Israel. But that day Yahweh thundered with a mighty thunder[^7] against the Philistines and threw them into confusion, and they were defeated by Israel.

**11** The men of Israel went out from Mizpah and pursued the Philistines, striking them down all the way to a point below Beth-car.

**12** Then Samuel took a stone and set it up between Mizpah and Shen.[^8] He named it Ebenezer,[^9] saying, "Up to now Yahweh has helped us."

**13** So the Philistines were subdued, and they did not come into Israel's territory again. The hand of Yahweh was against the Philistines all the days of Samuel.

**14** The towns the Philistines had taken from Israel were returned to Israel, from Ekron to Gath, and Israel rescued their territory from the power of the Philistines. There was also peace between Israel and the Amorites.

**15** Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life.

**16** Every year he went on a circuit to Bethel, Gilgal and Mizpah, and he judged Israel in all these places.

**17** Then he would return to Ramah, because his home was there, and there too he judged Israel. He built an altar there to Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Or "at Gibeah". Hebrew *ba-giv'ah*, "on the hill".
[^3]: The meaning of the Hebrew verb is uncertain: perhaps "longed for Yahweh" or "turned to follow Yahweh".
[^4]: Hebrew *ashtarot*, images of Astarte (Ashtoreth), a Canaanite goddess. Also in verse 4.
[^5]: Hebrew *ba'alim*, images or local forms of Baal, the Canaanite storm god.
[^6]: Or "led", "governed". Also in verses 15–17; see 4:18.
[^7]: Literally "with a great voice".
[^8]: Hebrew *ha-Shen*, "the Tooth", perhaps a rocky crag. The Septuagint reflects a Hebrew reading *ha-Yeshanah*, Jeshanah, a town named in 2 Chronicles 13:19.
[^9]: *Ebenezer* means "stone of help". See 4:1.
