**1** When Samuel grew old, he appointed his sons as judges for Israel.

**2** His firstborn son was named Joel, and his second son was named Abijah. They were judges in Beersheba.

**3** But his sons did not follow his ways.[^1] They turned aside after dishonest gain, took bribes and twisted justice.

**4** So all the elders of Israel gathered together and came to Samuel at Ramah.

**5** They said to him, "Look, you are old, and your sons do not follow your ways. Now appoint a king for us to judge us,[^2] like all the nations."

**6** But Samuel was displeased when they said, "Give us a king to judge us." So Samuel prayed to Yahweh.[^3]

**7** Yahweh said to Samuel, "Listen to the people in everything they say to you. For it is not you they have rejected; they have rejected me from being king over them.

**8** They are doing to you just what they have done from the day I brought them up out of Egypt until this day, forsaking me and serving other gods.

**9** Now listen to them. But you must solemnly warn them and tell them the rights[^4] of the king who will reign over them."

**10** Samuel told all the words of Yahweh to the people who were asking him for a king.

**11** He said, "These will be the rights of the king who will reign over you: he will take your sons and put them in his chariots and among his horsemen,[^5] and they will run in front of his chariots.

**12** He will appoint some to be commanders of thousands and commanders of fifties for him, and some to plow his ground and reap his harvest, and to make his weapons of war and the equipment for his chariots.

**13** He will take your daughters to be perfumers, cooks and bakers.

**14** He will take the best of your fields, your vineyards and your olive groves, and give them to his servants.[^6]

**15** He will take a tenth of your grain and of your vineyards, and give it to his officials[^7] and his servants.

**16** He will take your male and female slaves, the best of your young men[^8] and your donkeys, and put them to work for himself.

**17** He will take a tenth of your flocks, and you yourselves will become his slaves.

**18** On that day you will cry out because of the king you have chosen for yourselves, but Yahweh will not answer you on that day."

**19** But the people refused to listen to Samuel. "No!" they said. "We must have a king over us.

**20** Then we too will be like all the nations. Our king will judge us and go out before us and fight our battles."

**21** When Samuel had heard everything the people said, he repeated it to Yahweh.[^9]

**22** Yahweh said to Samuel, "Listen to them and give them a king." Then Samuel said to the men of Israel, "Go back, each of you, to your own town."

[^1]: The Hebrew text is written "his way" but read "his ways".
[^2]: Or "to govern us", "to lead us". Also in verses 6 and 20.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Hebrew *mishpat*, "justice, custom, right": here the king's claims on his people, or his way of ruling. Also in verse 11.
[^5]: Or "with his horses".
[^6]: Or "officials". Hebrew *avadim*, "servants", here the king's courtiers. Also in verse 15.
[^7]: Hebrew *sarisim*, court officials. The word can also mean "eunuchs".
[^8]: The Septuagint reads "your best cattle".
[^9]: Literally "spoke them in the ears of Yahweh".
