**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Samuel, "How long will you mourn for Saul, when I have rejected him from being king over Israel? Fill your horn with oil and go. I am sending you to Jesse of Bethlehem, for I have chosen[^2] a king for myself among his sons."

**2** Samuel said, "How can I go? If Saul hears of it, he will kill me." Yahweh said, "Take a heifer with you, and say, 'I have come to sacrifice to Yahweh.'

**3** Invite Jesse to the sacrifice, and I will let you know what to do. You will anoint for me the one I name to you."

**4** Samuel did what Yahweh said and came to Bethlehem. The elders of the town came trembling to meet him and asked, "Do you come in peace?"

**5** He said, "In peace. I have come to sacrifice to Yahweh. Consecrate yourselves and come with me to the sacrifice." Then he consecrated Jesse and his sons and invited them to the sacrifice.

**6** When they arrived, he saw Eliab and thought, "Surely Yahweh's anointed is standing here before him."[^3]

**7** But Yahweh said to Samuel, "Do not look at his appearance or at his height, for I have rejected him. It is not as a human being sees.[^4] A human being looks at the outward appearance,[^5] but Yahweh looks at the heart."

**8** Then Jesse called Abinadab and made him pass before Samuel. But Samuel said, "Yahweh has not chosen this one either."

**9** Then Jesse made Shammah pass by. But Samuel said, "Yahweh has not chosen this one either."

**10** Jesse made seven of his sons pass before Samuel, but Samuel said to Jesse, "Yahweh has not chosen these."

**11** Then Samuel asked Jesse, "Are these all the boys you have?" He said, "There is still the youngest, but he is looking after the sheep." Samuel said to Jesse, "Send for him and bring him. We will not sit down to eat[^6] until he comes here."

**12** So he sent for him and brought him in. He was ruddy,[^7] with beautiful eyes, and handsome. Yahweh said, "Get up and anoint him, for this is the one."

**13** So Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him in the presence of his brothers, and the Spirit of Yahweh rushed upon David[^8] from that day on. Then Samuel got up and went to Ramah.

**14** Now the Spirit of Yahweh had left Saul, and an evil spirit[^9] from Yahweh tormented him.

**15** Saul's servants[^10] said to him, "Look, an evil spirit from God is tormenting you.

**16** Let our lord command your servants here before you to look for a man who knows how to play the lyre.[^11] Whenever the evil spirit from God comes on you, he will play it,[^12] and you will feel better."

**17** So Saul said to his servants, "Find me a man who plays well and bring him to me."

**18** One of the young men[^13] answered, "I have seen a son of Jesse of Bethlehem who knows how to play. He is a brave man[^14] and a warrior. He speaks well[^15] and is handsome, and Yahweh is with him."

**19** So Saul sent messengers to Jesse and said, "Send me your son David, who is with the sheep."

**20** Jesse took a donkey loaded with bread,[^16] a skin of wine and a young goat, and sent them by his son David to Saul.

**21** David came to Saul and entered his service.[^17] Saul loved him very much, and David became his armor-bearer.

**22** Saul sent word to Jesse: "Let David stay in my service,[^18] for he has found favor in my eyes."

**23** Whenever the spirit from God came on Saul, David would take the lyre and play it. Then Saul would be relieved and feel better, and the evil spirit would leave him.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "I have seen".
[^3]: Literally "Surely before Yahweh is his anointed." Hebrew *meshicho*, "his anointed one"; see 2:10.
[^4]: Literally "For not what a human being sees". The Septuagint reads "For God does not see as a human being sees".
[^5]: Literally "looks to the eyes".
[^6]: Literally "We will not turn around", perhaps meaning "sit around the table".
[^7]: Or "red-haired". Hebrew *admoni*, "reddish", as of Esau in Genesis 25:25.
[^8]: Hebrew *tsalach*, "rush on, come powerfully on"; see 10:6.
[^9]: Or "a harmful spirit". Hebrew *ruach ra'ah*. Also in verses 15, 16 and 23.
[^10]: Or "officials". Hebrew *avadim*, "servants", here the king's courtiers. Also in verses 16 and 17.
[^11]: Hebrew *kinnor*, a stringed instrument. Also in verse 23.
[^12]: Literally "he will play with his hand". Also in verse 23.
[^13]: Hebrew *ne'arim*, "young men", here attendants.
[^14]: Hebrew *gibbor chayil*; see 9:1.
[^15]: Or "He is wise in what he does".
[^16]: Literally "a donkey of bread".
[^17]: Literally "stood before him".
[^18]: Literally "stand before me".
