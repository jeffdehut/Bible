**1** Soon afterward Jesus traveled through one town and village after another, proclaiming and bringing the good news of the kingdom of God. The Twelve were with him,

**2** and also some women who had been healed of evil spirits and illnesses: Mary, called Magdalene,[^1] from whom seven demons had gone out;

**3** Joanna the wife of Chuza, Herod's household manager;[^2] Susanna; and many others. These women were providing for them out of their own resources.

**4** When a large crowd was gathering and people from town after town were coming to him, he told them a parable:

**5** "A farmer went out to sow his seed. As he was sowing, some seed fell along the path. It was trampled on, and the birds of the sky ate it up.

**6** Other seed fell on rock, and when it came up, it withered because it had no moisture.

**7** Other seed fell among thorns, and the thorns grew up with it and choked it.

**8** Still other seed fell on good soil. It came up and produced a crop, a hundred times as much." As he said this, he called out, "Whoever has ears to hear, let them hear."

**9** His disciples asked him what this parable meant.

**10** He said, "To you it has been given to know the secrets[^3] of the kingdom of God, but to the rest it comes in parables, so that 'looking, they may not see, and hearing, they may not understand.'[^4]

**11** "This is what the parable means: the seed is the word of God.

**12** Those along the path are those who hear, and then the devil comes and takes away the word from their hearts, so that they may not believe and be saved.

**13** Those on the rock are the ones who receive the word with joy when they hear it. But they have no root. They believe for a while, and in a time of testing[^5] they fall away.

**14** The seed that fell among thorns stands for those who hear, but as they go on their way they are choked by the worries, riches, and pleasures of life, and their fruit does not ripen.

**15** But the seed on the good soil stands for those who hear the word with a good and noble heart, hold on to it, and patiently produce a crop.[^6]

**16** "No one lights a lamp and covers it with a jar or puts it under a bed. Instead, he puts it on a lampstand, so that those who come in can see the light.

**17** For nothing is hidden that will not be revealed, and nothing is kept secret that will not be known and come out into the open.

**18** So pay attention to how you listen. For whoever has will be given more, but whoever does not have, even what they think they have will be taken away from them."

**19** Then his mother and his brothers came to him, but they could not get near him because of the crowd.

**20** Someone told him, "Your mother and your brothers are standing outside, wanting to see you."

**21** But he answered them, "My mother and my brothers are those who hear the word of God and do it."

**22** One day Jesus got into a boat with his disciples, and he said to them, "Let us cross over to the other side of the lake." So they set out.

**23** As they sailed, he fell asleep. A windstorm came down on the lake, and the boat was filling with water, and they were in danger.

**24** They went and woke him, saying, "Master, Master, we are about to die!" He got up and rebuked the wind and the raging waves. They died down, and there was a calm.

**25** He said to them, "Where is your faith?" They were afraid and amazed, and they said to one another, "Who then is this, that he gives orders even to the winds and the water, and they obey him?"

**26** They sailed on to the region of the Gerasenes,[^7] which is opposite Galilee.

**27** When Jesus stepped out on land, a man from the town met him. He had demons, and for a long time he had worn no clothes. He did not live in a house but among the tombs.

**28** When he saw Jesus, he cried out, fell down before him, and shouted at the top of his voice, "What do you want with me,[^8] Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I beg you, do not torment me!"

**29** For Jesus had been ordering the unclean spirit to come out of the man. Many times it had seized him, and though he was kept under guard and bound with chains and shackles, he would break the bonds and be driven by the demon into deserted places.

**30** Jesus asked him, "What is your name?" "Legion,"[^9] he said, for many demons had gone into him.

**31** And they begged him not to order them to go into the abyss.[^10]

**32** A large herd of pigs was feeding there on the hillside. The demons begged Jesus to let them go into the pigs, and he gave them permission.

**33** The demons came out of the man and went into the pigs, and the herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake and drowned.

**34** When those who tended the pigs saw what had happened, they ran off and reported it in the town and in the countryside.

**35** People went out to see what had happened. They came to Jesus and found the man from whom the demons had gone out sitting at Jesus' feet, dressed and in his right mind. And they were afraid.

**36** Those who had seen it told them how the demon-possessed man had been healed.[^11]

**37** Then all the people of the region of the Gerasenes asked Jesus to leave them, for they were gripped with great fear. So he got into the boat and went back.

**38** The man from whom the demons had gone out begged to go with him, but Jesus sent him away, saying,

**39** "Go back home, and tell how much God has done for you." So the man went away and proclaimed throughout the whole town how much Jesus had done for him.

**40** When Jesus returned, the crowd welcomed him, for they were all waiting for him.

**41** Just then a man named Jairus came. He was a leader of the synagogue. He fell at Jesus' feet and pleaded with him to come to his house,

**42** because his only daughter, about twelve years old, was dying. As Jesus went, the crowds were pressing in on him.

**43** A woman was there who had suffered from bleeding for twelve years. She had spent all she had on doctors,[^12] but no one could heal her.

**44** She came up behind him and touched the edge[^13] of his cloak, and at once her bleeding stopped.

**45** Jesus asked, "Who touched me?" When they all denied it, Peter said,[^14] "Master, the crowds are surrounding you and pressing in on you."[^15]

**46** But Jesus said, "Someone touched me, for I know that power has gone out from me."

**47** When the woman saw that she could not go unnoticed, she came trembling and fell down before him. In the presence of all the people she told why she had touched him and how she had been healed at once.

**48** He said to her, "Daughter,[^16] your faith has healed you. Go in peace."

**49** While he was still speaking, someone came from the house of the synagogue leader and said, "Your daughter has died. Don't trouble the teacher any further."

**50** But when Jesus heard this, he answered him, "Don't be afraid; just believe, and she will be healed."

**51** When he came to the house, he did not let anyone go in with him except Peter, John, and James, and the child's father and mother.

**52** Everyone was weeping and mourning for her. But he said, "Stop weeping. She is not dead but asleep."

**53** They laughed at him, knowing that she was dead.

**54** But he took her by the hand[^17] and called out, "Child, get up!"

**55** Her spirit returned, and at once she got up. Then he directed that she be given something to eat.

**56** Her parents were overwhelmed with amazement, but he ordered them to tell no one what had happened.

[^1]: Probably meaning "from Magdala", a town on the western shore of the Sea of Galilee.
[^2]: Greek *epitropos*, a steward or administrator. This Herod is Herod Antipas (3:1).
[^3]: Greek *mysteria*, things once hidden and now revealed.
[^4]: Isaiah 6:9.
[^5]: Or "temptation".
[^6]: Literally "bear fruit with endurance".
[^7]: Many manuscripts read "Gadarenes"; also in verse 37.
[^8]: Literally "What to me and to you?"; see 4:34.
[^9]: A Roman legion was a unit of several thousand soldiers.
[^10]: Greek *abyssos*, the bottomless deep, thought of as a prison for evil spirits (Revelation 9:1–2; 20:1–3).
[^11]: Or "saved". Greek *sozo* means both; also in verses 48 and 50.
[^12]: Literally "had spent her whole living on doctors". Some manuscripts leave out these words.
[^13]: Or "tassel". Compare Numbers 15:38–39.
[^14]: Many manuscripts add "and those with him".
[^15]: Many manuscripts add "and you ask, 'Who touched me?'"
[^16]: Many manuscripts read "Take heart, daughter".
[^17]: Many manuscripts read "But he put them all outside, took her by the hand".
