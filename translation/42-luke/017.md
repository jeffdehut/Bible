**1** Jesus said to his disciples, "Things that cause people to stumble[^1] are bound to come, but how terrible it will be for the one through whom they come!

**2** It would be better for them to have a millstone hung around their neck and be thrown into the sea than to cause one of these little ones to stumble.

**3** So watch yourselves! If your brother[^2] sins,[^3] rebuke him, and if he repents, forgive him.

**4** Even if he sins against you seven times in a day, and seven times comes back to you and says, 'I repent,' you must forgive him."

**5** The apostles said to the Lord, "Increase our faith!"

**6** The Lord said, "If you had faith like a mustard seed, you could say to this mulberry tree,[^4] 'Be uprooted and planted in the sea,' and it would obey you.

**7** "Suppose one of you has a servant[^5] who is plowing or looking after sheep. When he comes in from the field, will you say to him, 'Come at once and sit down to eat'?

**8** Won't you rather say to him, 'Prepare my dinner, get ready,[^6] and serve me while I eat and drink. After that you can eat and drink'?

**9** Does he thank the servant because he did what he was commanded?[^7]

**10** So you too, when you have done everything you were commanded, should say, 'We are unworthy servants;[^8] we have only done what we ought to have done.'"

**11** On the way to Jerusalem, Jesus was passing along between Samaria and Galilee.[^9]

**12** As he was going into a village, ten men with skin diseases[^10] met him. They stood at a distance

**13** and called out loudly, "Jesus, Master,[^11] have mercy on us!"

**14** When Jesus saw them, he said to them, "Go and show yourselves to the priests."[^12] And as they went, they were made clean.

**15** One of them, when he saw that he was healed, turned back, praising God with a loud voice.

**16** He threw himself face down at Jesus' feet and thanked him. And he was a Samaritan.

**17** Jesus said, "Weren't all ten made clean? Where are the other nine?

**18** Was no one found to come back and give glory to God except this foreigner?"

**19** Then he said to him, "Get up and go; your faith has healed you."[^13]

**20** When Jesus was asked by the Pharisees when the kingdom of God would come, he answered them, "The kingdom of God is not coming in a way that can be watched for,

**21** nor will people say, 'Look, here it is!' or 'There!' For indeed, the kingdom of God is among you."[^14]

**22** Then he said to the disciples, "The time is coming when you will long to see one of the days of the Son of Man, and you will not see it.

**23** People will say to you, 'Look, there!' 'Look, here!' Do not go off or run after them.

**24** For as the lightning flashes and lights up the sky from one side to the other, so will the Son of Man be in his day.[^15]

**25** But first he must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation.

**26** Just as it was in the days of Noah, so too it will be in the days of the Son of Man.

**27** People were eating and drinking, marrying and being given in marriage, until the day Noah went into the ark. Then the flood came and destroyed them all.[^16]

**28** It was the same in the days of Lot. People were eating and drinking, buying and selling, planting and building.

**29** But on the day Lot left Sodom, it rained fire and sulfur from heaven and destroyed them all.[^17]

**30** It will be just like that on the day the Son of Man is revealed.

**31** On that day, whoever is on the housetop, with their goods in the house, must not go down to take them away. Likewise, whoever is in the field must not turn back.

**32** Remember Lot's wife![^18]

**33** Whoever tries to keep their life[^19] will lose it, but whoever loses it will keep it alive.

**34** I tell you, on that night there will be two people in one bed; one will be taken and the other left.

**35** There will be two women grinding grain together; one will be taken and the other left."[^20]

**37** They asked him, "Where, Lord?" He said to them, "Where the body is, there the vultures[^21] will gather."

[^1]: Or "to sin". Greek *skandala*, "stumbling blocks"; the verb in verse 2 is related.
[^2]: Or "brother or sister", that is, a fellow believer.
[^3]: Many manuscripts read "sins against you".
[^4]: Greek *sykaminos*, probably the black mulberry.
[^5]: Or "slave"; also in verses 9–10. Greek *doulos*.
[^6]: Literally "gird yourself", tucking up one's robe with a belt in order to work.
[^7]: Many manuscripts add "I think not."
[^8]: Greek *achreioi*, "useless, unprofitable"; here, servants who deserve no special credit.
[^9]: Or "through the region between Samaria and Galilee". Literally "through the middle of Samaria and Galilee".
[^10]: Greek *lepros*, traditionally "leper". The word covered various skin diseases, not only what is now called leprosy.
[^11]: Greek *epistata*, a title of respect for a leader or teacher.
[^12]: A priest had to confirm that someone was healed of a skin disease (Leviticus 13–14).
[^13]: Or "has saved you".
[^14]: Or "within you", "within your reach".
[^15]: Some manuscripts leave out "in his day".
[^16]: See Genesis 7.
[^17]: See Genesis 19:24.
[^18]: See Genesis 19:26.
[^19]: Greek *psyche*, life, soul, self.
[^20]: The Textus Receptus (followed by the King James Version) adds verse 36: "Two men will be in the field; one will be taken and the other left." The SBLGNT does not include it, so this translation has no verse 36.
[^21]: Or "eagles". Greek *aetoi*.
