**1** One day, as Jesus was teaching the people in the temple and proclaiming the good news, the chief priests and the scribes came up to him, together with the elders,

**2** and said to him, "Tell us, by what authority are you doing these things? Or who is it who gave you this authority?"

**3** He answered them, "I will also ask you a question. Tell me:

**4** John's baptism: was it from heaven or from human beings?"

**5** They discussed it among themselves, saying, "If we say, 'From heaven,' he will say, 'Why didn't you believe him?'

**6** But if we say, 'From human beings,' all the people will stone us, because they are convinced that John was a prophet."

**7** So they answered that they did not know where it was from.

**8** And Jesus said to them, "Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things."

**9** Then he began to tell the people this parable: "A man planted a vineyard, leased it to tenant farmers, and went away on a journey for a long time.

**10** At harvest time he sent a servant[^1] to the tenants, so that they would give him his share of the fruit of the vineyard. But the tenants beat him and sent him away empty-handed.

**11** He sent yet another servant. They beat that one too, treated him shamefully, and sent him away empty-handed.

**12** And he sent still a third. This one too they wounded and threw out.

**13** Then the owner of the vineyard said, 'What shall I do? I will send my beloved son. Perhaps they will respect him.'

**14** But when the tenants saw him, they discussed it with one another and said, 'This is the heir. Let us kill him, so that the inheritance will be ours.'

**15** So they threw him out of the vineyard and killed him. What then will the owner of the vineyard do to them?

**16** He will come and destroy these tenants and give the vineyard to others." When the people heard this, they said, "May that never happen!"

**17** But Jesus looked at them and said, "Then what does this scripture mean: 'The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone'?[^2]

**18** Everyone who falls on that stone will be broken to pieces, and anyone on whom it falls will be crushed."[^3]

**19** The scribes and the chief priests wanted to arrest him at that very hour, but they were afraid of the people, because they knew he had told this parable against them.

**20** So they watched him closely and sent spies who pretended to be sincere,[^4] to catch him in something he said, so that they could hand him over to the power and authority of the governor.

**21** They asked him, "Teacher, we know that you speak and teach what is right, and you show no favoritism,[^5] but you teach the way of God in truth.

**22** Is it lawful for us to pay tribute[^6] to Caesar or not?"

**23** But he saw through their trickery and said to them,[^7]

**24** "Show me a denarius.[^8] Whose image and inscription does it have?" They said, "Caesar's."

**25** He said to them, "Then give to Caesar what belongs to Caesar, and to God what belongs to God."

**26** They were not able to catch him in what he said in front of the people. Amazed at his answer, they fell silent.

**27** Some Sadducees, who deny that there is a resurrection, came to him with a question.

**28** "Teacher," they said, "Moses wrote for us that if a man's brother dies who has a wife but no children, the man must marry the widow and raise up children for his brother.[^9]

**29** There were seven brothers. The first married a wife and died childless.

**30** Then the second,[^10]

**31** and then the third married her, and in the same way all seven died, leaving no children.

**32** Finally the woman died too.

**33** So at the resurrection, whose wife will the woman be? For all seven had her as their wife."

**34** Jesus said to them, "The people of this age[^11] marry and are given in marriage.

**35** But those who are considered worthy to attain to that age and to the resurrection from the dead neither marry nor are given in marriage.

**36** Indeed, they can no longer die, because they are equal to the angels. They are children of God, since they are children of the resurrection.[^12]

**37** And that the dead are raised, even Moses showed in the passage about the bush, where he calls the Lord 'the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob'.[^13]

**38** He is not the God of the dead but of the living, for all are alive to him."

**39** Some of the scribes responded, "Teacher, you have spoken well."

**40** For they no longer dared to ask him anything.

**41** Then he said to them, "How can they say that the Messiah is the son of David?

**42** For David himself says in the book of Psalms, 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right hand

**43** until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet."'[^14]

**44** So David calls him 'Lord'. How then can he be his son?"

**45** While all the people were listening, he said to the disciples,

**46** "Beware of the scribes. They like to walk around in long robes, and they love to be greeted in the marketplaces and to have the best seats in the synagogues and the places of honor at banquets.

**47** They devour widows' houses[^15] and for show make long prayers. They will receive the greater condemnation."

[^1]: Or "slave"; also in verse 11. Greek *doulos*.
[^2]: Psalm 118:22. Literally "the head of the corner".
[^3]: Compare Isaiah 8:14–15; Daniel 2:34–35, 44–45.
[^4]: Or "righteous".
[^5]: Literally "you do not receive the face", that is, you do not judge by appearances.
[^6]: Greek *phoros*, the tribute paid to Rome.
[^7]: Many manuscripts add "Why are you testing me?"
[^8]: A Roman silver coin, worth about a day's wages for a laborer.
[^9]: Deuteronomy 25:5–6.
[^10]: Many manuscripts add "married the woman, and he died childless".
[^11]: Literally "the sons of this age".
[^12]: Literally "They are sons of God, being sons of the resurrection."
[^13]: Exodus 3:6, 15. In the Hebrew of Exodus 3:15, "the Lord" is God's personal name, YHWH ("the LORD").
[^14]: Psalm 110:1. In the Hebrew: "The LORD says to my lord", with *ne'um*, the word that introduces a prophetic oracle.
[^15]: That is, they exploit widows and take their property.
