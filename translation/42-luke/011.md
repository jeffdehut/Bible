**1** Once Jesus was praying in a certain place. When he had finished, one of his disciples said to him, "Lord, teach us to pray, just as John taught his disciples."

**2** He said to them, "When you pray, say: 'Father,[^1] may your name be honored as holy. May your kingdom come.[^2]

**3** Give us each day our daily bread.[^3]

**4** And forgive us our sins, for we ourselves also forgive everyone who is indebted to us.[^4] And do not bring us into testing.'"[^5]

**5** He said to them, "Suppose one of you has a friend, and you go to him at midnight and say, 'Friend, lend me three loaves of bread,

**6** because a friend of mine on a journey has come to me, and I have nothing to set before him.'

**7** And suppose he answers from inside, 'Don't bother me. The door is already locked, and my children are with me in bed. I can't get up and give you anything.'

**8** I tell you, even if he will not get up and give him anything because he is his friend, yet because of his shamelessness[^6] he will get up and give him as much as he needs.

**9** So I tell you: Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and the door will be opened to you.

**10** For everyone who asks receives, the one who seeks finds, and to the one who knocks, the door will be opened.

**11** Which father among you, if his son asks for a fish,[^7] will give him a snake instead of a fish?

**12** Or if he asks for an egg, will give him a scorpion?

**13** If you, then, though you are evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will the Father in heaven[^8] give the Holy Spirit to those who ask him!"

**14** Jesus was driving out a demon that was mute. When the demon had gone out, the man who had been mute spoke, and the crowds were amazed.

**15** But some of them said, "He drives out demons by Beelzebul,[^9] the ruler of the demons."

**16** Others, to test him, kept demanding from him a sign from heaven.

**17** But he knew their thoughts and said to them, "Every kingdom divided against itself is laid waste, and a divided household falls.[^10]

**18** If Satan too is divided against himself, how will his kingdom stand? I say this because you say that I drive out demons by Beelzebul.

**19** And if I drive out demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your own people[^11] drive them out? So they will be your judges.

**20** But if it is by the finger of God[^12] that I drive out demons, then the kingdom of God has come upon you.

**21** When a strong man, fully armed, guards his own house,[^13] his possessions are safe.

**22** But when someone stronger than he is attacks him and overpowers him, he takes away the armor the man trusted in and divides up his plunder.

**23** Whoever is not with me is against me, and whoever does not gather with me scatters.

**24** "When an unclean spirit goes out of a person, it goes through waterless places looking for rest, and not finding any, it says, 'I will go back to my house that I came from.'

**25** When it arrives, it finds the house swept and put in order.[^14]

**26** Then it goes and brings seven other spirits more evil than itself, and they go in and live there. And the final condition of that person is worse than the first."

**27** As he was saying these things, a woman in the crowd called out to him, "Blessed is the womb that carried you and the breasts that nursed you!"

**28** But he said, "Rather, blessed are those who hear the word of God and keep it!"

**29** As the crowds were growing, he began to say, "This generation is an evil generation. It asks for a sign, but no sign will be given to it except the sign of Jonah.

**30** For just as Jonah became a sign to the people of Nineveh, so the Son of Man will be a sign to this generation.

**31** The queen of the South will rise up at the judgment with the people of this generation and condemn them, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear Solomon's wisdom.[^15] And look, something greater than Solomon is here.

**32** The people of Nineveh will rise up at the judgment with this generation and condemn it, because they repented at Jonah's preaching.[^16] And look, something greater than Jonah is here.

**33** "No one lights a lamp and puts it in a hidden place[^17] or under a basket,[^18] but on a lampstand, so that those who come in may see the light.

**34** Your eye is the lamp of your body. When your eye is healthy,[^19] your whole body is full of light. But when it is bad,[^20] your body is full of darkness.

**35** So make sure that the light in you is not darkness.

**36** If then your whole body is full of light, with no part of it dark, it will be completely full of light, as when a lamp shines on you with its brightness."

**37** When he had finished speaking, a Pharisee invited him to eat with him. So he went in and reclined at table.

**38** The Pharisee was surprised to see that he did not first wash[^21] before the meal.

**39** But the Lord said to him, "Now you Pharisees clean the outside of the cup and the dish, but inside you are full of greed[^22] and wickedness.

**40** You fools! Didn't the one who made the outside make the inside too?

**41** But give as gifts to the poor what is inside,[^23] and then everything will be clean for you.

**42** "But woe to you[^24] Pharisees! You give a tenth[^25] of mint and rue and every garden herb, but you neglect justice and the love of God. These you should have done, without neglecting the others.

**43** Woe to you Pharisees! You love the best seats in the synagogues and greetings in the marketplaces.

**44** Woe to you![^26] You are like unmarked graves, and people walk over them without knowing it."[^27]

**45** One of the experts in the Law[^28] answered him, "Teacher, when you say these things, you insult us too."

**46** Jesus said, "Woe to you experts in the Law as well! You load people with burdens that are hard to carry, but you yourselves do not touch the burdens with one of your fingers.

**47** Woe to you! You build the tombs of the prophets, but it was your ancestors who killed them.

**48** So you are witnesses, and you approve of what your ancestors did, for they killed the prophets, and you build their tombs.[^29]

**49** That is why the Wisdom of God said, 'I will send them prophets and apostles, and some of them they will kill and persecute,'

**50** so that this generation may be charged with the blood of all the prophets that has been shed since the foundation of the world,

**51** from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zechariah, who perished between the altar and the sanctuary.[^30] Yes, I tell you, this generation will be charged with it.

**52** Woe to you experts in the Law! You have taken away the key of knowledge. You yourselves did not go in, and you hindered those who were going in."

**53** When he went out from there,[^31] the scribes and the Pharisees began to oppose him fiercely and to question him closely about many things,

**54** lying in wait to catch him in something he might say.[^32]

[^1]: Many manuscripts read "Our Father in heaven". Compare Matthew 6:9–13.
[^2]: Many manuscripts add "May your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven."
[^3]: Greek *epiousios*, a rare word whose meaning is uncertain. It may mean "for the coming day" (tomorrow's bread, or the bread of the age to come), "needed for existence", or "for today". "Daily" is the traditional rendering.
[^4]: Literally "everyone who owes us". Compare Matthew 6:12, "our debts".
[^5]: Or "temptation". Many manuscripts add "but rescue us from the evil one".
[^6]: Or "persistence". Greek *anaideia*, "lack of shame".
[^7]: Many manuscripts read "if his son asks for bread, will give him a stone? Or if he asks for a fish".
[^8]: Literally "the Father from heaven".
[^9]: A name for the prince of demons. In the Hebrew Bible, Baal-zebub ("lord of flies") was a god of Ekron (2 Kings 1:2). Also in verses 18–19.
[^10]: Literally "house falls on house".
[^11]: Literally "your sons", that is, your followers.
[^12]: Compare Exodus 8:15 (8:19 in most English Bibles), where Pharaoh's magicians say, "This is the finger of God."
[^13]: Or "palace". Greek *aule*, a courtyard or a residence built around one.
[^14]: Some manuscripts read "it finds the house empty, swept, and put in order".
[^15]: The queen of Sheba (1 Kings 10:1–13).
[^16]: Jonah 3:5.
[^17]: Or "in a cellar".
[^18]: Greek *modios*, a container for measuring grain, holding about 8 liters (2 gallons).
[^19]: Literally "single": sound, clear, and also generous.
[^20]: Literally "evil", which can also mean "unhealthy". An "evil eye" was an expression for envy or stinginess.
[^21]: Literally "baptize himself", a ritual washing (compare Mark 7:3–4).
[^22]: Literally "robbery", "plunder".
[^23]: Or "give as gifts to the poor the things you have". The meaning of the Greek is uncertain.
[^24]: Greek *ouai*, a cry of grief and warning, like the prophets' *hoy* ("woe"); also in verses 43–52.
[^25]: The tithe required by the Law (Leviticus 27:30; Deuteronomy 14:22–23). Rue is a bitter herb used in cooking and medicine.
[^26]: Many manuscripts add "scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites!"
[^27]: Contact with a grave made a person ritually unclean (Numbers 19:16).
[^28]: Greek *nomikoi*, experts in the Law of Moses; also in verses 46 and 52.
[^29]: Literally "and you build". Many manuscripts add "their tombs".
[^30]: Literally "the house", the temple building. Abel was murdered by Cain (Genesis 4:8). The Zechariah stoned in the temple court in 2 Chronicles 24:20–22, near the end of the last book of the Hebrew Bible, is the son of Jehoiada.
[^31]: Many manuscripts read "As he was saying these things to them".
[^32]: Many manuscripts add "so that they could accuse him".
