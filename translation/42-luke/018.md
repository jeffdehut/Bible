**1** Then Jesus told them a parable to show that they should always pray and not lose heart.

**2** He said, "In a certain town there was a judge who did not fear God or respect people.

**3** In that town there was a widow who kept coming to him and saying, 'Give me justice against my opponent.'

**4** For a while he refused. But later he said to himself, 'Even though I do not fear God or respect people,

**5** yet because this widow keeps bothering me, I will see that she gets justice, so that she will not wear me out by coming again and again.'"[^1]

**6** And the Lord said, "Listen to what the unjust judge says.

**7** And will not God bring about justice for his elect, who cry out to him day and night? Will he keep putting them off?[^2]

**8** I tell you, he will bring about justice for them quickly. But when the Son of Man comes, will he find faith on the earth?"

**9** He also told this parable to some who trusted in themselves that they were righteous and looked down on everyone else:

**10** "Two men went up to the temple to pray, one a Pharisee and the other a tax collector.

**11** The Pharisee stood and prayed this to himself:[^3] 'God, I thank you that I am not like other people, robbers, evildoers, adulterers, or even like this tax collector.

**12** I fast twice a week, and I give a tenth of everything I get.'

**13** But the tax collector stood at a distance and would not even look up to heaven. He kept beating his chest and saying, 'God, have mercy on me, a sinner!'[^4]

**14** I tell you, this man went down to his home declared righteous[^5] rather than the other. For everyone who exalts themselves will be humbled, and whoever humbles themselves will be exalted."

**15** People were also bringing babies to Jesus for him to touch them. But when the disciples saw it, they rebuked them.

**16** But Jesus called the children to him and said, "Let the little children come to me, and don't stop them, for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these.

**17** Truly I tell you, whoever does not receive the kingdom of God like a little child will never enter it."

**18** A certain ruler asked him, "Good Teacher, what must I do to inherit eternal life?"

**19** Jesus said to him, "Why do you call me good? No one is good except God alone.

**20** You know the commandments: 'Do not commit adultery, do not murder, do not steal, do not give false testimony, honor your father and mother.'"[^6]

**21** He said, "I have kept all these since I was young."

**22** When Jesus heard this, he said to him, "You still lack one thing. Sell everything you have and distribute it to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow me."

**23** But when he heard this, he became very sad, because he was extremely rich.

**24** Jesus looked at him[^7] and said, "How hard it is for those who have wealth to enter the kingdom of God!

**25** For it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich person to enter the kingdom of God."

**26** Those who heard this said, "Then who can be saved?"

**27** He said, "What is impossible with human beings is possible with God."

**28** Peter said, "Look, we have left our homes[^8] and followed you."

**29** Jesus said to them, "Truly I tell you, there is no one who has left house or wife or brothers or parents or children for the sake of the kingdom of God

**30** who will not receive many times as much in this present time, and in the age to come eternal life."

**31** Jesus took the Twelve aside and said to them, "Look, we are going up to Jerusalem, and everything that the prophets wrote about the Son of Man will be fulfilled.

**32** For he will be handed over to the Gentiles. He will be mocked, insulted, and spat on.

**33** They will flog him and kill him, and on the third day he will rise."

**34** But they understood none of these things. This saying was hidden from them, and they did not grasp what he was talking about.

**35** As Jesus was approaching Jericho, a blind man was sitting by the roadside, begging.

**36** When he heard a crowd going by, he asked what was happening.

**37** They told him, "Jesus of Nazareth is passing by."

**38** He shouted, "Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!"

**39** Those who were walking in front rebuked him and told him to be quiet, but he shouted all the more, "Son of David, have mercy on me!"

**40** Jesus stopped and ordered the man to be brought to him. When he came near, Jesus asked him,

**41** "What do you want me to do for you?" He said, "Lord, let me see again."

**42** Jesus said to him, "See again! Your faith has healed you."[^9]

**43** Immediately he regained his sight, and he followed Jesus, glorifying God. And when all the people saw it, they praised God.

[^1]: Or "so that she will not finally come and attack me". The verb means literally "strike under the eye".
[^2]: Or "Is he slow to help them?", or "even though he is patient with them". The meaning of the Greek is uncertain.
[^3]: Or "stood by himself and prayed this".
[^4]: Greek *hilaskomai*, "be merciful", a word from the language of sacrifice and atonement. Literally "me, the sinner".
[^5]: Greek *dikaioo*, traditionally "justified".
[^6]: Exodus 20:12–16; Deuteronomy 5:16–20.
[^7]: Many manuscripts add "seeing that he had become very sad".
[^8]: Literally "our own things". Many manuscripts read "we have left everything".
[^9]: Or "has saved you".
