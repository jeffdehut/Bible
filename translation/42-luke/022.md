**1** The Festival of Unleavened Bread, which is called the Passover, was approaching.

**2** The chief priests and the scribes were looking for a way to do away with Jesus, because they were afraid of the people.

**3** Then Satan entered Judas, called Iscariot, who was one of the Twelve.

**4** He went and discussed with the chief priests and the officers of the temple guard[^1] how he might hand Jesus over to them.

**5** They were delighted and agreed to give him money.

**6** He accepted, and he began looking for an opportunity to hand Jesus over to them when no crowd was present.

**7** Then came the day of Unleavened Bread, on which the Passover lamb had to be sacrificed.

**8** Jesus sent Peter and John, saying, "Go and prepare the Passover for us, so that we may eat it."

**9** They said to him, "Where do you want us to prepare it?"

**10** He said to them, "Look, when you have entered the city, a man carrying a jar of water will meet you. Follow him into the house that he enters,

**11** and say to the owner of the house, 'The Teacher asks you: Where is the guest room,[^2] where I may eat the Passover with my disciples?'

**12** He will show you a large upstairs room, furnished. Make preparations there."

**13** They went and found everything just as he had told them, and they prepared the Passover.

**14** When the hour came, Jesus reclined at table, and the apostles with him.

**15** He said to them, "I have eagerly desired[^3] to eat this Passover with you before I suffer.

**16** For I tell you, I will not eat it[^4] until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God."

**17** Then he took a cup, gave thanks, and said, "Take this and share it among yourselves.

**18** For I tell you, from now on I will not drink of the fruit of the vine until the kingdom of God comes."

**19** Then he took bread, gave thanks, broke it, and gave it to them, saying, "This is my body [given for you. Do this in remembrance of me."[^5]

**20** And in the same way he took the cup after supper,[^6] saying, "This cup is the new covenant in my blood, which is poured out for you."][^7]

**21** "But look, the hand of the one who is betraying me is with me on the table.

**22** For the Son of Man is going as it has been decided, but woe to that man by whom he is betrayed!"

**23** They began to ask one another which of them it could be who was going to do this.

**24** A dispute also arose among them about which of them was to be regarded as the greatest.

**25** He said to them, "The kings of the Gentiles lord it over them, and those who exercise authority over them are called 'Benefactors'.[^8]

**26** But you are not to be like that. Rather, the greatest among you must become like the youngest, and the one who leads like the one who serves.

**27** For who is greater, the one who reclines at table or the one who serves? Isn't it the one who reclines at table? But I am among you as the one who serves.

**28** "You are the ones who have stood by me in my trials.[^9]

**29** And I confer on you, just as my Father has conferred on me, a kingdom,[^10]

**30** so that you may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and you will sit on thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel.

**31** "Simon, Simon,[^11] look, Satan has demanded to have you all,[^12] to sift you like wheat.

**32** But I have prayed for you,[^13] that your faith may not fail. And when you have turned back, strengthen your brothers."

**33** Simon said to him, "Lord, I am ready to go with you both to prison and to death."

**34** Jesus said, "I tell you, Peter, the rooster will not crow today until you have denied three times that you know me."

**35** Then he said to them, "When I sent you out without purse, bag, or sandals, did you lack anything?"[^14] They said, "Nothing."

**36** He said to them, "But now, whoever has a purse must take it, and likewise a bag. And whoever does not have a sword must sell his cloak and buy one.[^15]

**37** For I tell you, this scripture must be fulfilled in me: 'And he was counted among the lawless.'[^16] Indeed, what is written about me is reaching its fulfillment."[^17]

**38** They said, "Lord, look, here are two swords." He said to them, "That is enough."[^18]

**39** Jesus went out and made his way, as was his custom, to the Mount of Olives, and the disciples followed him.

**40** When he reached the place, he said to them, "Pray that you will not come into testing."[^19]

**41** He withdrew from them about a stone's throw, knelt down, and prayed,

**42** "Father, if you are willing, take this cup away from me. Yet not my will, but yours be done."

**43** Then an angel from heaven appeared to him and strengthened him.

**44** In his anguish he prayed more earnestly, and his sweat became like drops of blood falling to the ground.[^20]

**45** When he got up from prayer and came to the disciples, he found them sleeping, worn out by grief.

**46** He said to them, "Why are you sleeping? Get up and pray, so that you will not come into testing."

**47** While he was still speaking, a crowd appeared, and the man called Judas, one of the Twelve, was leading them. He came up to Jesus to kiss him.

**48** But Jesus said to him, "Judas, are you betraying the Son of Man with a kiss?"

**49** When those around him saw what was going to happen, they said, "Lord, should we strike with the sword?"

**50** And one of them struck the high priest's servant and cut off his right ear.

**51** But Jesus answered, "No more of this!"[^21] And he touched the man's ear and healed him.

**52** Then Jesus said to the chief priests, the officers of the temple guard, and the elders who had come out against him, "Have you come out with swords and clubs as though I were a rebel?[^22]

**53** Day after day I was with you in the temple, and you did not lay a hand on me. But this is your hour, and the power of darkness."

**54** Then they seized him, led him away, and brought him into the high priest's house. Peter followed at a distance.

**55** When they had kindled a fire in the middle of the courtyard and sat down together, Peter sat among them.

**56** A servant girl saw him sitting in the firelight. She looked closely at him and said, "This man was with him too."

**57** But he denied it, saying, "Woman, I don't know him."

**58** A little later someone else saw him and said, "You also are one of them." But Peter said, "Man, I am not!"

**59** About an hour later another man insisted, "Certainly this man was with him, for he too is a Galilean."

**60** But Peter said, "Man, I don't know what you are talking about." Immediately, while he was still speaking, a rooster crowed.

**61** The Lord turned and looked at Peter. And Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how he had said to him, "Before the rooster crows today, you will deny me three times."

**62** And he went outside and wept bitterly.

**63** The men who were holding Jesus began to mock him and beat him.

**64** They blindfolded him[^23] and kept asking him, "Prophesy! Who is it that struck you?"

**65** And they said many other insulting things[^24] against him.

**66** When day came, the assembly of the elders of the people, both chief priests and scribes, gathered, and they led him away to their council.[^25]

**67** They said, "If you are the Messiah, tell us." He said to them, "If I tell you, you will not believe,

**68** and if I ask you, you will not answer.[^26]

**69** But from now on the Son of Man will be sitting at the right hand of the power of God."[^27]

**70** They all said, "Are you, then, the Son of God?" He said to them, "You say that I am."[^28]

**71** Then they said, "Why do we need any more testimony? We have heard it ourselves from his own mouth."

[^1]: Greek *strategoi*, the officers in charge of the temple police; also in verse 52.
[^2]: Greek *katalyma*, the word used in 2:7.
[^3]: Literally "With desire I have desired", a Hebrew way of expressing intensity.
[^4]: Many manuscripts read "I will not eat it again".
[^5]: The SBLGNT prints the words from "given for you" to the end of verse 20 in brackets [ ], marking their place in the text as uncertain; this translation does the same. Some manuscripts leave them out. Compare 1 Corinthians 11:24–25.
[^6]: Literally "And the cup likewise after they had eaten".
[^7]: Compare Jeremiah 31:31; Exodus 24:8.
[^8]: Greek *euergetai*, a title of honor given to rulers and patrons.
[^9]: Or "testings". Greek *peirasmoi*.
[^10]: Or "And just as my Father has given me a kingdom, I give it to you". The Greek verb *diatithemai* is related to *diatheke*, "covenant".
[^11]: Many manuscripts add "And the Lord said".
[^12]: "You" is plural in Greek.
[^13]: "You" is singular in Greek here and in the rest of the verse.
[^14]: See 10:4.
[^15]: Or "And whoever does not have a purse must sell his cloak and buy a sword."
[^16]: Isaiah 53:12, following the Greek translation; the Hebrew has "rebels".
[^17]: Or "what concerns me is coming to its end".
[^18]: Or "Enough of this!"
[^19]: Or "temptation"; also in verse 46.
[^20]: Some manuscripts leave out verses 43–44. The SBLGNT prints them as part of the text, without brackets.
[^21]: Or "Let them go this far!" Literally "Allow until this".
[^22]: Or "a robber", "a bandit". Greek *lestes*.
[^23]: Many manuscripts add "struck him on the face".
[^24]: Greek *blasphemountes*, "blaspheming".
[^25]: Greek *synedrion*, the Sanhedrin, the supreme Jewish council.
[^26]: Many manuscripts add "or let me go".
[^27]: See Psalm 110:1. "The power of God" is the phrase that Mark 14:62 gives as "the Power".
[^28]: Or "You are right in saying that I am." The reply can be read as guarded or as affirming.
