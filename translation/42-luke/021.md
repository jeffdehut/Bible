**1** Jesus looked up and saw rich people putting their gifts into the treasury.

**2** He also saw a poor widow putting in two small copper coins.[^1]

**3** He said, "I tell you truly, this poor widow has put in more than all of them.

**4** For all these people put in their gifts[^2] out of their abundance, but she, out of her poverty, put in all she had to live on."

**5** Some were talking about the temple, how it was adorned with beautiful stones and with gifts dedicated to God. Jesus said,

**6** "As for these things you are looking at, the days will come when not one stone will be left on another; every one will be thrown down."

**7** They asked him, "Teacher, when will these things happen? And what will be the sign that they are about to take place?"

**8** He said, "Be careful that you are not deceived. For many will come in my name, saying, 'I am he,'[^3] and, 'The time has come near.' Do not follow them.

**9** When you hear of wars and uprisings, do not be terrified. For these things must happen first, but the end will not come at once."

**10** Then he said to them, "Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom.

**11** There will be great earthquakes, and famines and plagues in various places. There will be terrifying sights and great signs from heaven.

**12** "But before all this, they will arrest you[^4] and persecute you. They will hand you over to synagogues and prisons, and you will be brought before kings and governors because of my name.

**13** This will give you an opportunity to bear witness.[^5]

**14** So make up your minds[^6] not to prepare your defense beforehand,

**15** for I will give you words[^7] and wisdom that none of your opponents will be able to resist or contradict.

**16** You will be handed over even by parents, brothers, relatives, and friends, and they will put some of you to death.

**17** Everyone will hate you because of my name.

**18** But not a hair of your head will perish.

**19** By your endurance gain your lives.[^8]

**20** "When you see Jerusalem surrounded by armies, then know that its desolation has come near.

**21** Then those in Judea must flee to the mountains, those inside the city must leave it, and those out in the country must not go into it.

**22** For these are days of punishment,[^9] when all that is written will be fulfilled.

**23** Woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing in those days! For there will be great distress on the land[^10] and wrath against this people.

**24** They will fall by the edge of the sword and be taken away as captives into all the nations, and Jerusalem will be trampled by the Gentiles until the times of the Gentiles are fulfilled.[^11]

**25** "There will be signs in the sun, the moon, and the stars, and on the earth the nations will be in anguish, bewildered by the roaring of the sea and the waves.

**26** People will faint from fear and from dread of what is coming on the world, for the powers in the heavens will be shaken.[^12]

**27** Then they will see 'the Son of Man coming in a cloud'[^13] with power and great glory.

**28** When these things begin to happen, stand up straight and raise your heads, because your redemption is drawing near."

**29** Then he told them a parable: "Look at the fig tree, and all the trees.

**30** As soon as they put out leaves, you see for yourselves and know that summer is already near.

**31** In the same way, when you see these things happening, you know that the kingdom of God is near.

**32** Truly I tell you, this generation will certainly not pass away until everything has happened.

**33** Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will never pass away.

**34** "Be on your guard, so that your hearts are not weighed down with carousing, drunkenness, and the worries of daily life, and that day does not come upon you suddenly

**35** like a trap. For it will come upon all who live on the face of the whole earth.

**36** Stay alert at all times, praying that you may have the strength[^14] to escape all these things that are going to happen, and to stand before the Son of Man."

**37** Every day Jesus was teaching in the temple, and every night he went out and spent the night on the hill called the Mount of Olives.

**38** And all the people would come to him early in the morning in the temple to listen to him.

[^1]: Greek *lepta*, the smallest coins, each worth about 1/128 of a denarius.
[^2]: Many manuscripts read "their gifts for God".
[^3]: Literally "I am".
[^4]: Literally "lay their hands on you".
[^5]: Literally "It will turn out for you as a testimony."
[^6]: Literally "set it in your hearts".
[^7]: Literally "a mouth".
[^8]: Greek *psychas*, lives, souls, selves. Some manuscripts read "By your endurance you will gain your lives."
[^9]: Or "vengeance". Greek *ekdikesis*, related to the word for "justice" in 18:3–8. Compare Hosea 9:7.
[^10]: Or "on the earth".
[^11]: The Greek word *ethne* means both "nations" and "Gentiles".
[^12]: Compare Isaiah 13:10; 34:4.
[^13]: Daniel 7:13.
[^14]: Many manuscripts read "that you may be counted worthy".
