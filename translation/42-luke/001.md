**1** Since many have undertaken to compile an account of the events that have been fulfilled[^1] among us,

**2** just as they were handed down to us by those who were eyewitnesses from the beginning and became servants of the word,[^2]

**3** it seemed good to me as well, having carefully investigated everything from the start,[^3] to write an orderly account for you, most excellent Theophilus,[^4]

**4** so that you may know the certainty of the things you have been taught.[^5]

**5** In the days of Herod king of Judea,[^6] there was a priest named Zechariah, who belonged to the priestly division of Abijah.[^7] His wife was a descendant of Aaron,[^8] and her name was Elizabeth.

**6** Both of them were righteous in the sight of God, living blamelessly by all the commandments and decrees of the Lord.[^9]

**7** But they had no child, because Elizabeth was unable to have children, and both of them were well along in years.[^10]

**8** Once, when Zechariah was serving as priest before God while his division was on duty,

**9** he was chosen by lot, according to the custom of the priesthood, to go into the sanctuary[^11] of the Lord and burn incense.

**10** At the hour of the incense offering, the whole crowd of people was praying outside.

**11** Then an angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing at the right side of the altar of incense.

**12** When Zechariah saw him, he was shaken, and fear came over him.

**13** But the angel said to him, "Don't be afraid, Zechariah, for your prayer has been heard. Your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you are to name him John.

**14** You will have joy and gladness, and many will rejoice at his birth,

**15** for he will be great in the sight of the Lord. He must never drink wine or strong drink,[^12] and he will be filled with the Holy Spirit even before he is born.[^13]

**16** He will turn many of the people of Israel back to the Lord their God.

**17** And he will go ahead of him[^14] in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of fathers to their children[^15] and the disobedient to the wisdom of the righteous, to make ready for the Lord a people prepared."

**18** Zechariah said to the angel, "How can I be sure of this?[^16] I am an old man, and my wife is well along in years."

**19** The angel answered him, "I am Gabriel.[^17] I stand in the presence of God, and I have been sent to speak to you and to bring you this good news.

**20** Look, you will be silent and unable to speak until the day these things happen, because you did not believe my words, which will be fulfilled at their proper time."

**21** Meanwhile the people were waiting for Zechariah, and they wondered why he was staying so long in the sanctuary.

**22** When he came out, he could not speak to them, and they realized that he had seen a vision in the sanctuary. He kept making signs to them, and he remained mute.[^18]

**23** When his days of service were completed, he went back to his home.

**24** After those days his wife Elizabeth became pregnant, and for five months she kept herself in seclusion. She said,

**25** "This is what the Lord has done for me in these days, when he looked on me to take away my disgrace among people."

**26** In the sixth month[^19] the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a town in Galilee called Nazareth,

**27** to a virgin engaged[^20] to a man named Joseph, of the house of David. The virgin's name was Mary.

**28** The angel came to her and said, "Greetings, you who are highly favored![^21] The Lord is with you."[^22]

**29** She was greatly troubled by his words[^23] and wondered what kind of greeting this might be.

**30** The angel said to her, "Don't be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God.

**31** Look, you will become pregnant and give birth to a son, and you are to name him Jesus.[^24]

**32** He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give him the throne of his ancestor David,[^25]

**33** and he will reign over the house of Jacob forever. His kingdom will never end."

**34** Mary said to the angel, "How will this be, since I have not been with a man?"[^26]

**35** The angel answered her, "The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. So the child to be born will be called holy, the Son of God.[^27]

**36** And look, your relative Elizabeth has also conceived a son in her old age. She who was said to be unable to have children is now in her sixth month.

**37** For no word from God will be without power."[^28]

**38** Mary said, "Here I am, the Lord's servant.[^29] Let it happen to me as you have said." Then the angel left her.

**39** In those days Mary got up and went with haste into the hill country, to a town of Judah.

**40** She went into Zechariah's house and greeted Elizabeth.

**41** When Elizabeth heard Mary's greeting, the baby leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.

**42** She cried out with a loud shout, "Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb!

**43** Why has this happened to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?

**44** For as soon as the sound of your greeting reached my ears, the baby in my womb leaped for joy.

**45** Blessed[^30] is she who believed that what the Lord has said to her will be fulfilled."[^31]

**46** And Mary said: "My soul proclaims the greatness of the Lord,[^32]

**47** and my spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior,

**48** for he has looked with favor on the lowliness[^33] of his servant. Yes, from now on all generations will call me blessed,

**49** for the Mighty One has done great things for me, and holy is his name.

**50** His mercy is from generation to generation for those who fear him.

**51** He has done mighty deeds with his arm; he has scattered those who are proud in the thoughts of their hearts.

**52** He has brought down rulers from their thrones and lifted up the lowly.

**53** He has filled the hungry with good things and sent the rich away empty.

**54** He has come to the help of his servant Israel, remembering his mercy,

**55** as he promised our ancestors, to Abraham and his descendants forever."[^34]

**56** Mary stayed with Elizabeth about three months and then returned to her home.

**57** When the time came for Elizabeth to give birth, she had a son.

**58** Her neighbors and relatives heard that the Lord had shown her his great mercy,[^35] and they rejoiced with her.

**59** On the eighth day they came to circumcise the child, and they were going to name him Zechariah, after his father.

**60** But his mother answered, "No! He is to be called John."

**61** They said to her, "None of your relatives has that name."

**62** Then they made signs to his father to find out what he wanted him to be called.

**63** He asked for a writing tablet and wrote, "His name is John." And they were all amazed.

**64** Immediately his mouth was opened and his tongue set free,[^36] and he began to speak, praising God.

**65** Fear came over all their neighbors, and throughout the hill country of Judea people talked about all these things.

**66** All who heard of them kept them in mind,[^37] asking, "What then will this child become?" For the hand of the Lord was with him.

**67** His father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied:

**68** "Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel,[^38] because he has come to help[^39] his people and has brought them redemption.

**69** He has raised up a horn of salvation[^40] for us in the house of his servant David,

**70** as he spoke through the mouth of his holy prophets long ago:

**71** salvation from our enemies and from the hand of all who hate us,

**72** to show mercy to our ancestors and to remember his holy covenant,

**73** the oath he swore to our father Abraham, to grant us

**74** that, rescued from the hand of enemies, we might serve him without fear

**75** in holiness and righteousness before him all our days.

**76** And you, child, will be called a prophet of the Most High, for you will go before the Lord to prepare his ways,[^41]

**77** to give his people the knowledge of salvation through the forgiveness of their sins,

**78** because of the tender mercy[^42] of our God, by which the dawn[^43] from on high will come to us,[^44]

**79** to shine on those who sit in darkness and in the shadow of death,[^45] to guide our feet into the way of peace."

**80** The child grew and became strong in spirit.[^46] He lived in the wilderness until the day he appeared publicly to Israel.

[^1]: Or "fully established". Verses 1–4 are a single sentence in Greek, written in the formal style that Greek historians used to open their works.
[^2]: That is, the message. Greek *logos*, "word, message".
[^3]: Or "having followed everything closely for a long time". Greek *anothen* can mean "from the beginning" or "for a long time".
[^4]: *Most excellent* is a respectful title for people of high rank, used of Roman governors in Acts 23:26; 24:3; 26:25. *Theophilus* means "friend of God" or "loved by God".
[^5]: Or "informed about".
[^6]: Herod the Great, who ruled Judea as king under Rome from 37 to 4 BC.
[^7]: The priests were divided into twenty-four divisions, which took turns serving in the temple. Abijah was the eighth (1 Chronicles 24:10).
[^8]: Literally "from the daughters of Aaron", that is, from a priestly family.
[^9]: Greek *kyrios*. In Luke 1–2, as in the Greek Old Testament, "the Lord" usually means God, standing for God's personal name, YHWH ("the LORD").
[^10]: Literally "advanced in their days"; also in verse 18.
[^11]: Greek *naos*, the sanctuary building itself, as distinct from the temple courts where the people stood (verse 10); also in verses 21–22.
[^12]: Compare Numbers 6:3; Judges 13:4.
[^13]: Literally "even from his mother's womb".
[^14]: That is, ahead of the Lord their God (verse 16).
[^15]: Malachi 3:23–24 (4:5–6 in most English Bibles).
[^16]: Literally "By what will I know this?"
[^17]: The angel named in Daniel 8:16; 9:21.
[^18]: Greek *kophos*, which can mean unable to speak, unable to hear, or both (compare verse 62).
[^19]: That is, of Elizabeth's pregnancy (verse 36).
[^20]: Or "betrothed". A Jewish engagement was a binding agreement that could be ended only by divorce, but the couple did not yet live together.
[^21]: *Greetings*: Greek *chaire*, the usual greeting, literally "Rejoice!" *Highly favored*: or "graced", from *charis*, "grace, favor".
[^22]: Many manuscripts add "Blessed are you among women."
[^23]: Many manuscripts read "When she saw him, she was greatly troubled at his words".
[^24]: Greek *Iesous*, the Greek form of the Hebrew name Joshua, which means "The LORD saves".
[^25]: Literally "his father David".
[^26]: Literally "since I do not know a man", a Hebrew way of speaking of sexual relations.
[^27]: Or "So the holy child to be born will be called the Son of God."
[^28]: Or "For nothing will be impossible with God." Greek *rhema* means "word" or "thing". Many manuscripts read "with God" for "from God", giving "For with God nothing will be impossible." Compare Genesis 18:14.
[^29]: Or "slave". Greek *doule*, a female servant or slave; also in verse 48.
[^30]: Greek *makaria*, "happy, fortunate", a different word from "blessed" in verse 42.
[^31]: Or "Blessed is she who believed, for what the Lord has said to her will be fulfilled."
[^32]: Traditionally "magnifies the Lord". The song (verses 46–55) is known as the Magnificat, from its first word in Latin. It has many echoes of Hannah's song in 1 Samuel 2:1–10.
[^33]: Or "humiliation". Compare 1 Samuel 1:11.
[^34]: Literally "as he spoke to our fathers, to Abraham and to his seed forever". The words "to Abraham" may also go with "remembering his mercy" in verse 54: "remembering his mercy to Abraham and his descendants forever, as he promised our ancestors."
[^35]: Literally "had magnified his mercy with her".
[^36]: The Greek has no verb for the tongue: literally "his mouth was opened, and his tongue".
[^37]: Literally "laid them up in their heart".
[^38]: Compare Psalm 41:14 (41:13 in most English Bibles); 72:18; 106:48. In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, YHWH ("the LORD"). Zechariah's song (verses 68–79) is known as the Benedictus, from its first word in Latin.
[^39]: Literally "visited", in the sense of coming to look after (Genesis 50:24; Exodus 4:31); also in verse 78.
[^40]: The horn is a picture of strength: a mighty savior. Compare Psalm 18:3 (18:2 in most English Bibles); 132:17.
[^41]: Compare Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3.
[^42]: Literally "the inward parts of mercy", the inner organs being thought of as the seat of feelings.
[^43]: Greek *anatole*, "rising", used of the sunrise and of a rising star. In the Greek Old Testament it also translates "Branch", a title for the coming king (Jeremiah 23:5; Zechariah 3:8; 6:12).
[^44]: Many manuscripts read "has come to us".
[^45]: Compare Isaiah 9:1 (9:2 in most English Bibles); Psalm 107:10. "Shadow of death" is the Greek Old Testament's rendering of the Hebrew word translated "deep darkness" in those verses.
[^46]: Or "in the Spirit".
