**1** Then Job answered:

**2** "I have heard many things like these; you are all miserable comforters.

**3** Is there no end to windy words? What drives you to keep answering?

**4** I too could talk as you do, if you were in my place.[^1] I could string together words against you and shake my head at you.

**5** I could strengthen you with my mouth, and the comfort of my lips would bring you relief.

**6** If I speak, my pain is not relieved; and if I hold back, how much of it leaves me?

**7** But now he has worn me out. You have devastated my whole household.[^2]

**8** You have shriveled me up,[^3] and it has become a witness; my gauntness rises up against me and testifies to my face.

**9** His anger has torn me, and he hates me; he gnashes his teeth at me. My enemy sharpens his eyes against me.

**10** They open their mouths wide against me; they strike my cheeks in contempt; they mass together against me.

**11** God hands me over to the unjust[^4] and throws me into the hands of the wicked.

**12** I was at ease, but he shattered me; he seized me by the neck and dashed me to pieces. He set me up as his target;

**13** his archers[^5] surround me. He pierces my kidneys and does not spare; he pours out my gall on the ground.

**14** He breaks through me, breach after breach; he charges at me like a warrior.

**15** I have sewn sackcloth over my skin and buried my horn[^6] in the dust.

**16** My face is red with weeping, and deep darkness[^7] is on my eyelids,

**17** although there is no violence in my hands, and my prayer is pure.

**18** O earth, do not cover my blood; let my cry find no resting place.

**19** Even now, look, my witness is in heaven, and the one who testifies for me[^8] is on high.

**20** My friends scorn me;[^9] my eye pours out tears to God.[^10]

**21** Let him argue a man's case with God, as a person does for his friend.[^11]

**22** For only a few years will pass, and I will go the way of no return.

[^1]: Literally "if your soul were in place of my soul".
[^2]: Literally "my whole company". In verses 7–14 Job speaks of God as "he" and to him as "you", moving between the two.
[^3]: Hebrew *qamat*, a rare verb: perhaps "shrivel" or "seize".
[^4]: Hebrew *awil*, probably "the unjust". In 19:18 and 21:11 the same word means "young child".
[^5]: Or "his many", "his troops".
[^6]: A symbol of strength and dignity.
[^7]: Hebrew *tsalmavet*, "deep darkness", traditionally read as "the shadow of death".
[^8]: Hebrew *sahadi*, an Aramaic word for "my witness".
[^9]: The Hebrew of this line is uncertain. Literally "my scorners, my friends". Hebrew *melitsai* can also mean "my spokesmen" or "my intercessors": "My intercessor is my friend". Who the witness of verse 19 is, and who would argue for Job in verse 21, is not said.
[^10]: Hebrew *Eloah*, a poetic form of *Elohim*, "God", used often in Job. Also in verse 21.
[^11]: Literally "and a son of man for his friend".
