**1** Then Job answered:

**2** "Even today my complaint is defiant;[^1] the hand on me is heavy in spite of my groaning.[^2]

**3** If only I knew where to find him, so that I could come to his dwelling place![^3]

**4** I would set out my case before him and fill my mouth with arguments.

**5** I would learn the words he would answer me, and understand what he would say to me.

**6** Would he contend with me with his great power? No, he would surely pay attention to me.[^4]

**7** There an upright person could reason with him, and I would escape from my judge forever.[^5]

**8** Look, I go forward, and he is not there; backward, and I do not perceive him.[^6]

**9** When he is at work on the left, I cannot catch hold of him;[^7] he turns aside to the right,[^8] and I do not see him.

**10** But he knows the way I take.[^9] When he has tested me, I will come out like gold.

**11** My foot has held fast to his steps; I have kept to his way and have not turned aside.

**12** I have not departed from the command of his lips; I have treasured the words of his mouth more than my daily food.[^10]

**13** But he is one,[^11] and who can turn him back? What he desires, he does.[^12]

**14** For he will carry out what he has decreed for me,[^13] and he has many such things in mind.[^14]

**15** That is why I am terrified in his presence; when I think about it, I am in dread of him.

**16** God has made my heart faint; the Almighty[^15] has terrified me.

**17** Yet I have not been silenced by the darkness, by the thick darkness that covers my face.[^16]

[^1]: Hebrew *meri*, "rebellion, defiance". With a slight change to the Hebrew, "bitter".
[^2]: Literally "my hand is heavy on my groaning". *My hand* may mean the blow that has fallen on me (compare 19:21). Or "I hold down my groaning with a heavy hand". The Greek Septuagint reads "his hand".
[^3]: Hebrew *tekhunah*, "his fixed place": his seat or throne.
[^4]: Literally "No, only he would set (his mind) on me".
[^5]: That is, from his charges against me. Or "I would be acquitted by my judge forever".
[^6]: Or "east … west". The Hebrew words for "forward" and "backward" also mean "east" and "west", and "left" and "right" in verse 9 also mean "north" and "south": Job searches in every direction.
[^7]: Literally "and I do not grasp". Some read "and I do not behold him", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^8]: Or "he hides himself on the right".
[^9]: Literally "the way with me".
[^10]: Literally "more than my portion". Hebrew *chuqqi*, "what is allotted to me", probably food (compare Proverbs 30:8). The Greek Septuagint reads "in my bosom".
[^11]: Literally "he is in one": he is unique, or unchanging, or of one mind. Some read "he has chosen", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^12]: Literally "His soul desires, and he does it".
[^13]: Literally "he completes my decree". Hebrew *chuqqi*, the word in verse 12.
[^14]: Literally "and like these, many are with him".
[^15]: Hebrew *Shaddai*, traditionally "the Almighty"; the meaning is uncertain.
[^16]: The Hebrew of this verse is uncertain. Literally "For I have not been silenced (or destroyed) before darkness, and from my face he has covered thick darkness". Or "For I was not cut off before the darkness came, and he did not hide the thick darkness from me".
