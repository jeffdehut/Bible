**1** Job took up his discourse again:[^1]

**2** "If only I were as in the months gone by, as in the days when God[^2] watched over me,

**3** when his lamp shone above my head, and by his light I walked through darkness;

**4** as I was in the days of my prime,[^3] when the friendship[^4] of God was over my tent;

**5** when the Almighty[^5] was still with me, and my children[^6] were all around me;

**6** when my steps were bathed in cream,[^7] and the rock poured out streams of oil for me!

**7** When I went out to the gate of the city[^8] and set up my seat in the square,

**8** the young men saw me and drew back,[^9] and the old men rose and stood;

**9** officials stopped talking[^10] and put their hands over their mouths;

**10** the voices of the nobles were hushed, and their tongues stuck to the roof of their mouths.

**11** For any ear that heard me called me blessed, and any eye that saw me spoke well of me,[^11]

**12** because I rescued the poor who cried for help, and the orphan who had no one to help him.

**13** The blessing of the one who was perishing came upon me, and I made the widow's heart sing for joy.

**14** I put on righteousness, and it clothed me;[^12] my justice was like a robe and a turban.

**15** I was eyes to the blind and feet to the lame.

**16** I was a father to the needy, and I looked into the case of the stranger.[^13]

**17** I broke the jaws of the unjust and made them drop their prey from their teeth.

**18** Then I thought, 'I will die in my nest,[^14] and I will multiply my days like the sand.[^15]

**19** My roots will be open to the water, and the dew will lie all night on my branches.

**20** My glory will stay fresh with me,[^16] and my bow will be renewed in my hand.'

**21** People listened to me and waited; they kept silent for my counsel.

**22** After I had spoken, they did not speak again; my words fell on them drop by drop.[^17]

**23** They waited for me as for the rain; they opened their mouths as for the spring rain.[^18]

**24** When I smiled at them, they could hardly believe it;[^19] they did not let the light of my face fall.[^20]

**25** I chose the way for them and sat as their chief; I lived like a king among his troops, like one who comforts mourners.

[^1]: Literally "And Job again lifted up his *mashal* and said" (see 27:1).
[^2]: Hebrew *Eloah*, a poetic form of *Elohim*, "God", used often in Job. Also in verse 4.
[^3]: Literally "the days of my autumn", the season of harvest and fullness.
[^4]: Hebrew *sod*, "intimate counsel, close friendship".
[^5]: Hebrew *Shaddai*, traditionally "the Almighty"; the meaning is uncertain.
[^6]: Or "my young men", "my servants". Hebrew *ne'arim*, as in 1:19.
[^7]: Hebrew *chemah*, "curds, butter": a picture of great abundance.
[^8]: Literally "to the gate, up to the city". Elders sat at the city gate to hear cases and give judgment.
[^9]: Literally "hid themselves".
[^10]: Literally "held back words".
[^11]: Literally "testified for me".
[^12]: Literally "and it clothed itself with me".
[^13]: Literally "and the case I did not know I searched out": either the case of someone I did not know, or a case I did not yet understand.
[^14]: That is, at home among my family.
[^15]: Or "like the phoenix". Hebrew *chol* usually means "sand", but an ancient Jewish tradition takes it here as the name of a long-lived bird, the phoenix.
[^16]: Literally "My glory is new with me". Hebrew *kavod*: honor, dignity, vigor.
[^17]: Literally "and my word dripped on them", like rain.
[^18]: The late rain of spring, which ripened the crops before harvest.
[^19]: Literally "I laughed toward them; they did not believe". Or "I smiled at them when they had lost confidence".
[^20]: The meaning of this line is uncertain. Perhaps "they did not cast down the light of my face", that is, they did nothing to lose my favor.
