**1** Elihu continued:[^1]

**2** "Bear with me a little longer, and I will show you, for there is still more to say on God's behalf.[^2]

**3** I will bring my knowledge from far away, and I will ascribe righteousness to my Maker.

**4** For truly my words are not false; one who is perfect in knowledge[^3] is with you.

**5** Look, God is mighty, and he does not despise anyone; he is mighty in strength of understanding.[^4]

**6** He does not keep the wicked alive, but he gives justice to the afflicted.

**7** He does not take his eyes off the righteous; he seats them with kings on the throne forever, and they are exalted.

**8** But if they are bound in chains, caught in the cords of affliction,

**9** then he tells them what they have done, and their transgressions, that they have behaved arrogantly.

**10** He opens their ears to instruction[^5] and tells them to turn back from wickedness.

**11** If they listen and serve him, they will end their days in prosperity, and their years in pleasantness.

**12** But if they do not listen, they will cross the channel of death[^6] and die without knowledge.

**13** The godless in heart harbor anger;[^7] they do not cry for help when he binds them.

**14** They die in their youth, and their life ends among the shrine prostitutes.[^8]

**15** He rescues the afflicted through their affliction and opens their ears through their distress.

**16** He also lured you[^9] out of the jaws of distress, into a broad place where there was no constraint,[^10] and your table was set with rich food.[^11]

**17** But you are full of the judgment due to the wicked; judgment and justice take hold of you.

**18** Beware that wrath[^12] does not lure you into scoffing,[^13] and do not let a large ransom[^14] turn you aside.

**19** Will your cry for help keep you out of distress, or all the efforts of your strength?[^15]

**20** Do not long for the night, when peoples are taken away from their place.[^16]

**21** Be careful not to turn to wickedness, for you have chosen this rather than affliction.[^17]

**22** Look, God is exalted in his power. Who is a teacher like him?

**23** Who has prescribed his way for him? Who can say, 'You have done wrong'?

**24** Remember to extol his work, which people have praised in song.

**25** All humankind has looked on it; mortals see it from afar.

**26** Look, God is great, beyond our knowledge; the number of his years is past searching out.

**27** For he draws up the drops of water; they distill rain from his mist,[^18]

**28** which the skies pour down and shower on humankind in abundance.

**29** Can anyone understand the spreading of the clouds, the thundering from his pavilion?[^19]

**30** Look, he spreads his light around him, and he covers the roots of the sea.[^20]

**31** For by these he judges[^21] the peoples; he gives food in abundance.

**32** He covers his hands with lightning[^22] and commands it to strike the mark.

**33** His thunder announces him;[^23] even the cattle tell of the storm's approach.[^24]

[^1]: Literally "And Elihu added and said".
[^2]: Literally "for there are still words for God". Hebrew *Eloah*, a poetic form of *Elohim*, "God", used often in Job.
[^3]: Elihu speaks of himself. The same phrase is used of God in 37:16.
[^4]: Literally "mighty in strength of heart". The Hebrew of this verse is uncertain.
[^5]: Or "discipline". Hebrew *musar*.
[^6]: Hebrew *shelach*, as in 33:18. Or "they will perish by the sword".
[^7]: Literally "set anger".
[^8]: Hebrew *qedeshim*, men attached to shrines, probably as cult prostitutes (Deuteronomy 23:18 (23:17 in most English Bibles)). Their lives end early and in shame.
[^9]: Or "He is luring you". The Hebrew of verses 16–20 is very difficult, and the translation is uncertain in places.
[^10]: Literally "breadth, not constraint beneath it".
[^11]: Literally "and the setting of your table was full of fatness".
[^12]: Or "heat", "anger". Literally "For wrath, lest it lure you".
[^13]: Hebrew *safeq*, "clapping", a gesture of scorn (34:37), or "abundance".
[^14]: Or "a large bribe". Hebrew *kofer*, as in 33:24.
[^15]: The Hebrew of this verse is uncertain. Or "Will your wealth be valued, not in gold, nor all the resources of your strength?"
[^16]: Literally "to go up, peoples, beneath them".
[^17]: Some scholars read *bochanta*, "you have been tested", for *bacharta*, "you have chosen", with a small change to the Hebrew: "for because of this you have been tested by affliction".
[^18]: Hebrew *ed*, a rare word also found in Genesis 2:6.
[^19]: Hebrew *sukkah*, "shelter, booth": the clouds around God (Psalm 18:12 (18:11 in most English Bibles)).
[^20]: That is, the depths of the sea.
[^21]: Or "he governs". Hebrew *yadin*.
[^22]: Literally "with light". Also in 37:3, 11 and 15.
[^23]: The Hebrew of this verse is very uncertain. Literally "His noise tells about him".
[^24]: Literally "the cattle, even concerning what goes up". Some scholars read "he is jealous with anger against wrongdoing", with a small change to the Hebrew.
