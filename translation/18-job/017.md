**1** "My spirit is broken; my days are snuffed out; the grave awaits me.[^1]

**2** Surely mockers are all around me, and my eye must dwell on their provocation.

**3** Give a pledge for me, please, with yourself.[^2] Who else will shake hands to guarantee me?[^3]

**4** For you have closed their minds to understanding;[^4] so you will not let them triumph.

**5** One who denounces his friends for gain,[^5] the eyes of his children will fail.

**6** He has made me a byword among the peoples; I have become one in whose face people spit.[^6]

**7** My eye has grown dim with grief, and all my limbs are like a shadow.

**8** The upright are appalled at this, and the innocent are stirred up against the godless.

**9** Yet the righteous hold to their way, and those with clean hands grow stronger and stronger.

**10** But as for all of you, come back again, please! I will not find a wise man among you.

**11** My days are past; my plans are broken off, the desires of my heart.[^7]

**12** They turn night into day: 'Light is near,' they say, in the face of darkness.[^8]

**13** If I look to Sheol[^9] as my home, if I spread out my bed in darkness,

**14** if I say to the pit,[^10] 'You are my father,' and to the worm, 'My mother,' or 'My sister,'

**15** where then is my hope? Who can see any hope for me?

**16** Will it go down to the bars of Sheol?[^11] Shall we rest together in the dust?"

[^1]: Literally "graves are for me".
[^2]: Job speaks to God. Literally "Set, please, pledge me with yourself".
[^3]: Literally "Who is he who will strike my hand?", a gesture of guaranteeing someone's debt (Proverbs 6:1; 17:18).
[^4]: Literally "hidden their heart from insight".
[^5]: The Hebrew of this line is uncertain. Literally "For a portion he informs on friends". Or "He invites friends to share his portion, while…".
[^6]: Hebrew *tofet*, "spitting". Literally "I will be a spitting in the face". The Hebrew of this phrase is uncertain.
[^7]: Literally "the possessions of my heart".
[^8]: The Hebrew of this line is uncertain. Literally "light is near from the face of darkness". Or "light is nearer than darkness".
[^9]: Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead. Also in verse 16.
[^10]: Or "to corruption". Hebrew *shachat*.
[^11]: Literally "They will go down to the bars of Sheol", that is, his hopes. The Hebrew of this verse is uncertain. *Bars* may instead mean "limbs" or "parts".
