**1** Then Job answered:

**2** "Listen carefully to my words, and let this be the comfort you give me.

**3** Bear with me while I speak, and after I have spoken, mock on.[^1]

**4** As for me, is my complaint against a human being? Why should I not be impatient?[^2]

**5** Look at me and be appalled; put your hand over your mouth.

**6** When I think about it I am terrified, and trembling seizes my body.

**7** Why do the wicked live on, grow old, and even grow mighty in power?

**8** Their offspring are established in their presence, and their descendants before their eyes.

**9** Their houses are safe from fear, and the rod of God[^3] is not upon them.

**10** Their bull breeds without fail; their cow gives birth and does not miscarry.

**11** They send out their little ones like a flock, and their children dance.

**12** They sing to the tambourine and the lyre, and rejoice to the sound of the pipe.

**13** They spend their days in prosperity,[^4] and go down to Sheol[^5] in an instant.[^6]

**14** Yet they say to God, 'Leave us alone! We do not want to know your ways.

**15** Who is the Almighty,[^7] that we should serve him? What would we gain if we prayed to him?'

**16** Look, their prosperity is not in their own hands.[^8] The counsel of the wicked is far from me.

**17** How often is the lamp of the wicked put out? How often does disaster come upon them? How often does he deal out pains[^9] in his anger?

**18** How often are they like straw before the wind, like chaff that the storm carries away?

**19** God stores up his punishment for his children?[^10] Let him repay the man himself, so that he knows it!

**20** Let his own eyes[^11] see his ruin, and let him drink of the wrath of the Almighty.

**21** For what does he care about his household after him, when the number of his months has been cut off?

**22** Can anyone teach God knowledge, since he judges even those on high?

**23** One person dies in full vigor, completely at ease and secure.

**24** His pails are full of milk,[^12] and the marrow of his bones is moist.

**25** Another dies with a bitter soul, never having tasted anything good.

**26** They lie down alike in the dust, and worms cover them both.

**27** Look, I know your thoughts, the schemes by which you would wrong me.

**28** For you say, 'Where is the house of the noble? Where is the tent in which the wicked lived?'

**29** Have you not asked those who travel the roads? Do you not accept their accounts,[^13]

**30** that the wicked are spared on the day of disaster, and are rescued on the day of wrath?[^14]

**31** Who denounces his way to his face? Who repays him for what he has done?

**32** He is carried to the grave, and watch is kept over his tomb.

**33** The clods of the valley are sweet to him. All humankind follows after him, and those who went before him are without number.

**34** How then can you comfort me with empty words?[^15] Nothing is left of your answers but falsehood."

[^1]: The Hebrew "you" is singular here, though plural in verse 2.
[^2]: Literally "Why should my spirit not be short?"
[^3]: Hebrew *Eloah*, a poetic form of *Elohim*, "God", used often in Job. Also in verse 19.
[^4]: The Hebrew text is written "they wear out their days" but read "they complete their days".
[^5]: Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead.
[^6]: That is, quickly, without a long illness. Or "in peace".
[^7]: Hebrew *Shaddai*, traditionally "the Almighty"; the meaning is uncertain. Also in verse 20.
[^8]: Or "Is not their prosperity in their own hands?" The Hebrew of this verse is difficult.
[^9]: Hebrew *chavalim*, "pains", especially of labor. Or "portions", measured out by a cord.
[^10]: Job seems to quote the friends' view (compare 5:4; 20:10). Many translations add "You say". Literally "God stores up his iniquity for his children".
[^11]: The Hebrew text is written "his eye" but read "his eyes".
[^12]: Hebrew *atinav*, a word found only here. Perhaps "his pails" or "his loins"; with different vowels, "full of fat" (*chelev*) instead of "full of milk" (*chalav*).
[^13]: Literally "their signs".
[^14]: Or "that the wicked are kept for the day of disaster, and brought to the day of wrath".
[^15]: Literally "with breath". Hebrew *hevel*.
