**1** Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered:

**2** "If someone ventures a word to you, will you lose patience? But who can hold back from speaking?

**3** Look, you have instructed many, and you have strengthened weak hands.

**4** Your words have kept the stumbling on their feet, and you have made firm the knees that gave way.

**5** But now it has come to you, and you lose patience; it touches you, and you are dismayed.

**6** Is not your fear of God[^1] your confidence, and the integrity of your ways your hope?

**7** Think now: who that was innocent ever perished? Where were the upright ever wiped out?

**8** As I have seen, those who plow wickedness and sow trouble reap it.

**9** At the breath of God[^2] they perish; at the blast of his anger they come to an end.

**10** The lion may roar, the fierce lion may growl, but the teeth of the young lions are broken.[^3]

**11** The lion perishes for lack of prey, and the cubs of the lioness are scattered.

**12** Now a word came to me in secret;[^4] my ear caught a whisper of it,

**13** amid troubled thoughts from visions of the night, when deep sleep falls on people.

**14** Dread came upon me, and trembling, and it made all my bones shake.

**15** A spirit[^5] passed before my face; the hair of my body stood on end.

**16** It stood still, but I could not make out its appearance. A form was before my eyes; there was silence, and I heard a voice:[^6]

**17** 'Can a mortal be righteous before God? Can a man be pure before his Maker?[^7]

**18** If he puts no trust in his servants, and charges his angels with error,[^8]

**19** how much more those who live in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, who are crushed as easily as a moth![^9]

**20** Between morning and evening they are shattered; they perish forever, and no one notices.

**21** Is not their tent cord pulled up within them?[^10] They die, and not in wisdom.'

[^1]: Literally "your fear".
[^2]: Hebrew *Eloah*, a poetic form of *Elohim*, "God", used often in Job. Also in verse 17.
[^3]: Literally "The roar of the lion, and the voice of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions are broken". Verses 10–11 use five different Hebrew words for lions.
[^4]: Literally "was stolen to me".
[^5]: Or "a wind", "a breath". Hebrew *ruach*.
[^6]: Or "I heard a silent voice", "a whisper of a voice" (compare 1 Kings 19:12).
[^7]: Or "Can a mortal be more righteous than God? Can a man be purer than his Maker?"
[^8]: Hebrew *toholah*, a word found only here, perhaps "error" or "folly".
[^9]: Literally "before a moth": perhaps "like a moth", or "sooner than a moth".
[^10]: The life of a person is pictured as a tent that collapses when its cord is pulled up. Hebrew *yitram* may instead mean "their excellence": "Is not their excellence taken from them?" The words of the voice may end at verse 17 rather than here.
