**1** "I made a covenant with my eyes; how then could I gaze at a young woman?[^1]

**2** What portion would I have from God[^2] above, what inheritance from the Almighty[^3] on high?

**3** Is there not disaster for the unjust, and calamity for those who do wrong?

**4** Does he not see my ways and count all my steps?

**5** If I have walked with falsehood, or my foot has hurried after deceit,

**6** let him weigh me on honest scales,[^4] and let God know my integrity.

**7** If my steps have turned aside from the way, if my heart has followed my eyes, or if any stain has stuck to my hands,

**8** then let me sow and another eat, and let my crops[^5] be uprooted.

**9** If my heart has been enticed by a woman, or I have lain in wait at my neighbor's door,

**10** then let my wife grind grain for another, and let other men kneel over her.[^6]

**11** For that would be a shameful crime; it would be an offense for the judges to punish.[^7]

**12** For it is a fire that consumes all the way to Abaddon,[^8] and it would uproot all my harvest.

**13** If I have denied justice[^9] to my male or female servant[^10] when they brought a complaint against me,

**14** what then could I do when God rises up? When he calls me to account, what could I answer him?

**15** Did not the one who made me in the womb make them? Did not the same one form us in the womb?

**16** If I have withheld from the poor what they wanted, or let the widow's eyes grow dim with longing,[^11]

**17** or eaten my bit of bread by myself, and the orphan has not eaten any of it,

**18** for from my youth the orphan grew up with me as with a father,[^12] and from my mother's womb I guided the widow,[^13]

**19** if I have seen anyone perishing for lack of clothing, or the needy without covering,

**20** if his body[^14] has not blessed me, as he was warmed by the fleece of my sheep,

**21** if I have raised my hand against the orphan, because I saw I had support[^15] in the gate,

**22** then let my shoulder fall from its socket,[^16] and let my arm be broken off at the elbow.[^17]

**23** For disaster from God was a terror to me, and because of his majesty I could not do such things.[^18]

**24** If I have made gold my confidence, or called fine gold my security,

**25** if I have rejoiced because my wealth was great, or because my hand had gained so much,

**26** if I have looked at the sun[^19] when it shone, or the moon moving in splendor,

**27** and my heart has been secretly enticed, and my hand has thrown them a kiss,[^20]

**28** this too would be an offense for the judges to punish, for I would have denied God above.

**29** If I have rejoiced at the ruin of one who hated me, or gloated when evil found him,

**30** I have not let my mouth[^21] sin by asking for his life with a curse.

**31** If the men of my tent have not said, 'Who is there who has not had his fill of his meat?'[^22]

**32** No stranger had to spend the night in the street; I opened my doors to the traveler.[^23]

**33** If I have covered my transgressions as others do,[^24] hiding my guilt in my bosom,

**34** because I feared the great crowd, and the contempt of the clans terrified me, so that I kept silent and did not go out of my door—

**35** Oh, that I had someone to hear me! Here is my signature;[^25] let the Almighty answer me! Let my opponent write out his charge against me![^26]

**36** Surely I would carry it on my shoulder; I would bind it on like a crown.[^27]

**37** I would give him an account of all my steps; like a prince I would approach him.

**38** If my land has cried out against me, and its furrows have wept together,

**39** if I have eaten its produce[^28] without paying, or made its owners breathe their last,

**40** then let thorns grow instead of wheat, and stinking weeds[^29] instead of barley." The words of Job are ended.[^30]

[^1]: Hebrew *betulah*, "a young woman, a virgin".
[^2]: Hebrew *Eloah*, a poetic form of *Elohim*, "God", used often in Job. Also in verse 6.
[^3]: Hebrew *Shaddai*, traditionally "the Almighty"; the meaning is uncertain. Also in verse 35.
[^4]: Literally "scales of righteousness".
[^5]: Or "my descendants". Hebrew *tse'etsa'im*, "what comes out": produce, or offspring.
[^6]: Grinding grain was the work of a slave woman. *Kneel over her* refers to sexual relations, and the first line may also be meant that way.
[^7]: Literally "an iniquity of judges": a crime that the courts punish. Also in verse 28.
[^8]: Hebrew *Abaddon*, "Destruction", a name for the realm of the dead (see 26:6).
[^9]: Hebrew *mishpat*: justice, right, a legal case.
[^10]: Or "slave".
[^11]: Literally "made the eyes of the widow fail".
[^12]: Literally "he grew me up as a father", a difficult phrase. Perhaps "he (the orphan) grew up with me as with a father", or "he (God) raised me like a father".
[^13]: Literally "I guided her", the widow of verse 16: a way of saying that Job cared for widows all his life.
[^14]: Literally "his loins".
[^15]: Literally "my help": those who would take my side in court at the city gate.
[^16]: Literally "from the shoulder blade".
[^17]: Literally "from the reed", perhaps the upper arm bone.
[^18]: Literally "and from his majesty I was not able".
[^19]: Literally "the light".
[^20]: Literally "and my hand has kissed my mouth", a gesture of worship to the sun and moon.
[^21]: Literally "my palate".
[^22]: Literally "Who will give one who is not satisfied from his flesh?" Job's household could say that everyone had eaten their fill at his table.
[^23]: The Hebrew vowel points read "to the road". The same consonants can be read "to the traveler".
[^24]: Or "as Adam did" (compare Genesis 3:8–10). Hebrew *ke-adam*, "like a human being" or "like Adam".
[^25]: Literally "Here is my *tav*". *Tav* is the last letter of the Hebrew alphabet, written in early Hebrew script as an X: a mark or signature, as at the end of a legal statement.
[^26]: Literally "and the scroll my man of dispute has written".
[^27]: Literally "I would bind it on me as crowns".
[^28]: Literally "its strength".
[^29]: Hebrew *bo'shah*, from a verb meaning "to stink".
[^30]: Job does not speak again until 40:4.
