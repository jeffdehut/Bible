**1** Then Zophar the Naamathite answered:

**2** "Should a flood of words go unanswered? Should a great talker[^1] be proved right?

**3** Should your idle talk silence people? Should you mock, and no one put you to shame?

**4** You say, 'My teaching is pure, and I am clean in your sight.'

**5** But oh, that God[^2] would speak, and open his lips against you,

**6** and tell you the secrets of wisdom! For true wisdom has two sides.[^3] Know then that God is exacting from you less than your guilt deserves.[^4]

**7** Can you find out the deep things of God? Can you reach the limits of the Almighty?[^5]

**8** They are higher than the heavens: what can you do? They are deeper than Sheol:[^6] what can you know?

**9** Their measure is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea.

**10** If he passes by and imprisons someone, and calls an assembly for judgment, who can stop him?

**11** For he knows worthless people; when he sees wickedness, will he not take note of it?[^7]

**12** An empty-headed person will get understanding when a wild donkey's colt is born human.[^8]

**13** If you set your heart right and spread out your hands to him,

**14** if there is wickedness in your hand, put it far away, and do not let injustice live in your tents.

**15** Then you will lift up your face without blemish; you will stand firm[^9] and will not fear.

**16** For you will forget your misery; you will remember it like water that has flowed past.

**17** Your life[^10] will be brighter than noonday; its darkness will be like the morning.[^11]

**18** You will feel secure, because there is hope; you will look around[^12] and lie down in safety.

**19** You will lie down, and no one will make you afraid; many will seek your favor.[^13]

**20** But the eyes of the wicked will fail; every way of escape will be gone from them, and their only hope will be to breathe their last."

[^1]: Literally "a man of lips".
[^2]: Hebrew *Eloah*, a poetic form of *Elohim*, "God", used often in Job. Also in verses 6 and 7.
[^3]: The Hebrew of this line is uncertain. Literally "for double to sound wisdom". Hebrew *tushiyyah*, as in 5:12.
[^4]: Literally "God causes you to forget some of your guilt". Or "God has overlooked some of your guilt".
[^5]: Hebrew *Shaddai*, traditionally "the Almighty"; the meaning is uncertain.
[^6]: Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead.
[^7]: Or "he sees wickedness without needing to look closely". Literally "he sees iniquity and does not consider".
[^8]: Probably a proverb: the first will happen no sooner than the second. Literally "A hollow man will get a heart, and a wild donkey's colt will be born a human".
[^9]: Literally "you will be cast", like metal.
[^10]: Hebrew *cheled*, "lifetime, the span of life".
[^11]: Or "if there is darkness, it will be like the morning". The Hebrew of this line is uncertain.
[^12]: Literally "dig" or "search". Some take the verb as a different *chafar*, "be ashamed": "you were ashamed, but you will lie down in safety".
[^13]: Literally "many will stroke your face".
