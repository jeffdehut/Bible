**1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered:

**2** "How long will you say such things? The words of your mouth are a mighty wind.

**3** Does God pervert justice? Does the Almighty[^1] pervert what is right?

**4** If your children sinned against him, he handed them over to the power of their offense.[^2]

**5** But if you will seek God earnestly and plead with the Almighty,

**6** if you are pure and upright, then surely he will rouse himself for you and restore your rightful home.[^3]

**7** Though your beginning was small, your future will be very great.

**8** Ask the former generation, please, and pay attention to what their ancestors searched out.

**9** For we are only of yesterday and know nothing; our days on earth are a shadow.

**10** Will they not teach you and tell you, and bring out words from their understanding?[^4]

**11** Can papyrus grow tall where there is no marsh? Can reeds flourish without water?

**12** While it is still green and not yet cut, it withers before any other plant.

**13** Such are the paths of all who forget God; the hope of the godless will perish.

**14** His confidence snaps,[^5] and what he trusts in is a spider's web.[^6]

**15** He leans on his house, but it does not stand; he holds on to it, but it does not last.

**16** He is like a well-watered plant in the sun, whose shoots spread out over its garden.[^7]

**17** Its roots wrap around a heap of stones; it looks for a place among the stones.[^8]

**18** But if he is torn from his place, then it will deny him: 'I never saw you.'

**19** See, this is the joy of his way,[^9] and out of the dust others will spring up.

**20** See, God will not reject a blameless person, or take hold of the hand of evildoers.

**21** He will yet fill your mouth with laughter, and your lips with shouts of joy.

**22** Those who hate you will be clothed with shame, and the tent of the wicked will be no more."

[^1]: Hebrew *Shaddai*, traditionally "the Almighty"; the meaning is uncertain. Also in verse 5.
[^2]: Literally "he sent them into the hand of their offense".
[^3]: Literally "the home of your righteousness".
[^4]: Literally "from their heart".
[^5]: Hebrew *yaqot*, a word of uncertain meaning. Perhaps "is cut off", or a noun, "is a gossamer thread".
[^6]: Literally "a spider's house".
[^7]: Verses 16–19 may describe the wicked person (verse 13) as a plant that flourishes for a while, or they may describe a different plant that survives.
[^8]: Literally "it sees a house of stones". The meaning of this line is uncertain.
[^9]: Probably ironic: "So much for the joy of his way".
