**1** "Look, the hope of anyone who tries is false;[^1] is one not thrown down even at the sight of it?

**2** No one is fierce enough to rouse it. Who then can stand before me?[^2]

**3** Who has come to me first, that I should repay him?[^3] Everything under the whole heaven is mine.

**4** Shall I keep silent about it, about its limbs, its mighty strength and the grace of its build?[^4]

**5** Who can strip off its outer garment? Who can come within its double bridle?[^5]

**6** Who can open the doors of its face? All around its teeth is terror.

**7** Its pride is its rows of shields,[^6] shut up closely as with a seal.

**8** One is so close to another that no air[^7] can come between them.

**9** They are joined one to another; they cling together and cannot be separated.

**10** Its sneezes flash out light, and its eyes are like the eyelids of the dawn.[^8]

**11** Out of its mouth go flaming torches; sparks of fire leap out.

**12** Smoke comes out of its nostrils, as from a pot heated and boiling over burning rushes.[^9]

**13** Its breath[^10] sets coals ablaze, and a flame comes out of its mouth.

**14** Strength lodges in its neck, and terror[^11] dances before it.

**15** The folds of its flesh stick together; they are firmly cast on it and cannot be moved.

**16** Its heart is as hard as stone, hard as the lower millstone.

**17** When it rises up, the mighty[^12] are afraid; at its crashing they are beside themselves.[^13]

**18** The sword that reaches it does not prevail, nor does the spear, the dart or the javelin.[^14]

**19** It counts iron as straw, and bronze as rotten wood.

**20** The arrow[^15] does not make it flee; sling stones are turned to chaff for it.

**21** Clubs are counted as chaff; it laughs at the rattle of the javelin.[^16]

**22** Its underside is jagged potsherds;[^17] it spreads itself like a threshing sledge on the mud.

**23** It makes the depths boil like a pot; it makes the sea like a pot of ointment.[^18]

**24** Behind it, it leaves a shining wake; one would think the deep had white hair.

**25** On earth there is nothing like it,[^19] a creature made without fear.

**26** It looks down on everything that is lofty; it is king over all the proud beasts."[^20]

[^1]: Verses 1–26 are 41:9–34 in most English Bibles. Literally "Look, his hope is false": the hope of anyone who tries to capture Leviathan (40:25).
[^2]: The argument moves from the creature to its Maker: if no one dares to face Leviathan, who can stand before God?
[^3]: Literally "Who has come before me, that I should repay?" Romans 11:35 echoes this line.
[^4]: The Hebrew text is written "I will not keep silent about its limbs" but read "Concerning it I will keep silent about its limbs", taken here as a question. *Limbs* (Hebrew *baddav*) may also mean "its boasting" (compare 11:3).
[^5]: Literally "within the doubling of its bridle", perhaps its jaws. Some scholars read *siryono*, "its coat of mail", for *risno*, "its bridle", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^6]: Literally "Pride, channels of shields": its rows of scales. Some scholars read *gevo*, "its back", for *ga'avah*, "pride", with a small change to the Hebrew: "Its back is rows of shields".
[^7]: Or "no breath". Hebrew *ruach*.
[^8]: The first rays of the sun (see 3:9).
[^9]: Literally "like a pot blown upon, and a rush". Hebrew *agmon*, the word for the rope of rushes in 40:26.
[^10]: Hebrew *nephesh*, here "breath" or "throat".
[^11]: Hebrew *de'avah*, "dismay, terror", a word found only here.
[^12]: Or "the gods". Hebrew *elim*.
[^13]: Literally "from crashings they miss (their way)". The meaning is uncertain.
[^14]: Hebrew *massa* and *shiryah*, words of uncertain meaning, perhaps "dart" and "javelin".
[^15]: Literally "the son of the bow".
[^16]: Hebrew *kidon*, as in 39:23.
[^17]: Literally "Beneath it are the sharpest of potsherds".
[^18]: A pot in which perfumed ointment is mixed and heated.
[^19]: Literally "There is not on the dust its like".
[^20]: Literally "the sons of pride", as in 28:8.
