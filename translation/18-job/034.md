**1** Then Elihu answered:[^1]

**2** "Hear my words, you wise men; listen to me, you who have knowledge.

**3** For the ear tests words as the palate tastes food.

**4** Let us choose for ourselves what is right;[^2] let us find out among ourselves what is good.

**5** For Job has said, 'I am in the right, and God has denied me justice.[^3]

**6** In spite of my right, I am counted a liar;[^4] my wound[^5] is incurable, though I am without transgression.'

**7** What man is like Job, who drinks up scorn like water,

**8** who keeps company with evildoers, and walks with wicked men?

**9** For he has said, 'It does a man no good to be on good terms with God.'[^6]

**10** So listen to me, you men of understanding.[^7] Far be it from God to do wickedness, and from the Almighty[^8] to do wrong!

**11** For he repays a person for what he has done, and he brings on each one what his conduct deserves.[^9]

**12** Truly, God will not act wickedly, and the Almighty will not pervert justice.

**13** Who put him in charge of the earth? Who set the whole world in place?

**14** If he were to set his heart on himself,[^10] and gather to himself his spirit and his breath,[^11]

**15** all flesh would perish together, and humankind would return to dust.

**16** If you have understanding, hear this; listen to what I say.[^12]

**17** Can one who hates justice govern? Will you condemn the one who is righteous and mighty?

**18** Is it fitting to say to a king, 'Worthless one,'[^13] or to nobles, 'Wicked'?

**19** He shows no partiality to princes and does not favor the rich over the poor, for they are all the work of his hands.

**20** In a moment they die; at midnight people are shaken and pass away; the mighty are removed without a hand.[^14]

**21** For his eyes are on a person's ways, and he sees all his steps.

**22** There is no darkness, no deep darkness,[^15] where evildoers can hide.

**23** For he does not need to consider a person further[^16] before he comes before God in judgment.

**24** He shatters the mighty without investigation[^17] and sets others in their place.

**25** Because he knows their deeds, he overthrows them in the night, and they are crushed.

**26** He strikes them for their wickedness[^18] where others can see,

**27** because they turned aside from following him and paid no attention to any of his ways,

**28** so that they brought the cry of the poor before him, and he hears the cry of the afflicted.

**29** But if he keeps quiet, who can condemn him? If he hides his face, who can see him? Yet he watches over nation and individual alike,[^19]

**30** so that the godless may not reign, and may not be snares for the people.[^20]

**31** For has anyone said to God, 'I have borne punishment; I will not offend again.[^21]

**32** Teach me what I cannot see; if I have done wrong, I will do it no more'?

**33** Should he repay on your terms, when you have rejected his ways?[^22] For you must choose, not I; so tell us what you know.

**34** Men of understanding will say to me, and any wise man who hears me:

**35** 'Job speaks without knowledge; his words are without insight.'

**36** If only Job were tested to the end[^23] for answering like wicked men!

**37** For he adds rebellion to his sin; he claps his hands among us[^24] and multiplies his words against God."

[^1]: Literally "And Elihu answered and said". No one has spoken since 33:33: the formula marks a new part of Elihu's speech, as in 35:1 and 36:1.
[^2]: Hebrew *mishpat*: justice, what is right.
[^3]: Job's words in 27:2; compare 13:18.
[^4]: Literally "Concerning my right I lie". Or "Should I lie about my right?"
[^5]: Literally "my arrow" (compare 6:4).
[^6]: Literally "when he delights himself with God". Compare 9:22; 21:15.
[^7]: Literally "men of heart". Also in verse 34.
[^8]: Hebrew *Shaddai*, traditionally "the Almighty"; the meaning is uncertain. Also in verse 12.
[^9]: Literally "according to a man's path he makes it find him".
[^10]: Literally "If he sets his heart toward himself", that is, if he thought only of himself. Or "If he set his mind to it".
[^11]: Hebrew *ruach* and *neshamah*, the breath of life that God gives (Genesis 2:7; Psalm 104:29).
[^12]: Here *you* is singular: Elihu speaks to Job.
[^13]: Hebrew *beliyya'al*, "worthless, good-for-nothing".
[^14]: That is, without human help: by God's power.
[^15]: Hebrew *tsalmavet*, "deep darkness", traditionally read as "the shadow of death".
[^16]: Literally "For he does not set upon a man further". Some read *mo'ed*, "an appointed time", for *od*, "further": "he does not set a time for anyone".
[^17]: Literally "without searching".
[^18]: Literally "In place of the wicked he slaps them": either "as wicked people" or "for their wickedness".
[^19]: Literally "both over a nation and over a human being (*adam*) together".
[^20]: The Hebrew of verses 29–33 is very difficult, and the translation is uncertain in places. Literally "from the reigning of a godless man, from snares of a people".
[^21]: Literally "I have borne; I will not act corruptly".
[^22]: Literally "Is it from with you that he will repay it, for you have rejected?"
[^23]: Hebrew *avi*, usually "my father". Here it is perhaps an expression of wish, "If only".
[^24]: A gesture of contempt (compare 27:23).
