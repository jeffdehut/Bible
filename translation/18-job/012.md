**1** Then Job answered:

**2** "No doubt you are the people who matter,[^1] and wisdom will die with you!

**3** But I have understanding as well as you; I am not inferior to you. Who does not know such things as these?

**4** I have become a laughingstock to my friends, I who called on God[^2] and he answered me. A righteous, blameless man is a laughingstock!

**5** Those at ease have contempt for disaster;[^3] it is ready for those whose feet are slipping.

**6** The tents of robbers are at peace, and those who provoke God are secure, those who carry their god in their hand.[^4]

**7** But ask the animals, and they will teach you, the birds of the sky, and they will tell you;

**8** or speak to the earth, and it will teach you, and let the fish of the sea tell you.

**9** Which of all these does not know that the hand of Yahweh[^5] has done this?

**10** In his hand is the life of every living thing and the breath of every human being.

**11** Does not the ear test words as the palate tastes food?

**12** Wisdom is with the aged, and understanding comes with length of days.[^6]

**13** With him are wisdom and strength; counsel and understanding are his.

**14** Look, what he tears down cannot be rebuilt; when he shuts someone in, no one can open.

**15** Look, if he holds back the waters, they dry up; if he sends them out, they overturn the land.

**16** With him are strength and sound wisdom;[^7] the one who is misled and the one who misleads are his.

**17** He leads counselors away stripped[^8] and makes fools of judges.

**18** He loosens the belt of kings and ties a loincloth around their waist.[^9]

**19** He leads priests away stripped and overthrows those long established.

**20** He silences trusted advisers[^10] and takes away the discernment of elders.

**21** He pours contempt on nobles and loosens the belt of the strong.[^11]

**22** He uncovers deep things out of darkness and brings deep darkness[^12] into the light.

**23** He makes nations great, and destroys them; he enlarges nations, and leads them away.

**24** He takes away the understanding of the leaders of the people of the earth and makes them wander in a trackless waste.[^13]

**25** They grope in darkness without light; he makes them stagger like a drunkard.

[^1]: Literally "Truly you are the people".
[^2]: Hebrew *Eloah*, a poetic form of *Elohim*, "God", used often in Job. Also in verse 6.
[^3]: The Hebrew of this verse is uncertain. Hebrew *lappid* usually means "torch"; here it is probably *le-pid*, "for disaster".
[^4]: Or "whom God has brought into his power". The Hebrew of this line is uncertain. The first line has *El*, the second *Eloah*.
[^5]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. This is the only place where the name is used in the speeches of Job and his friends (chapters 3–37). Isaiah 41:20 has the same words.
[^6]: Perhaps a question, or a saying of the friends that Job quotes: "Is wisdom with the aged…?"
[^7]: Hebrew *tushiyyah*, as in 5:12.
[^8]: Hebrew *sholal*, "stripped", perhaps "barefoot" or "plundered", like captives. Also in verse 19.
[^9]: The Hebrew of this verse is uncertain. Perhaps "He undoes the bonds that kings impose, and binds a cloth around their own waist", that is, he makes them captives or slaves.
[^10]: Literally "He removes the lip of the trusted".
[^11]: Hebrew *afiqim*, a word of uncertain meaning here, perhaps "the strong".
[^12]: Hebrew *tsalmavet*, "deep darkness", traditionally read as "the shadow of death".
[^13]: Literally "in a waste (*tohu*) with no road". *Tohu* is the word for the formless earth in Genesis 1:2.
