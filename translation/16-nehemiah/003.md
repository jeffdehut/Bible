**1** Then Eliashib the high priest and his fellow priests set to work and built the Sheep Gate.[^1] They consecrated it and set up its doors. They consecrated it as far as the Tower of the Hundred, as far as the Tower of Hananel.

**2** Next to him[^2] the men of Jericho built, and next to him Zaccur son of Imri built.

**3** The descendants of Hassenaah[^3] built the Fish Gate. They laid its beams and set up its doors, its bolts and its bars.

**4** Next to them Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, made repairs.[^4] Next to them Meshullam son of Berechiah, son of Meshezabel, made repairs. Next to them Zadok son of Baana made repairs.

**5** Next to them the men of Tekoa made repairs, but their nobles would not put their necks to the work of their lords.[^5]

**6** Joiada son of Paseah and Meshullam son of Besodeiah repaired the Old Gate.[^6] They laid its beams and set up its doors, its bolts and its bars.

**7** Next to them Melatiah the Gibeonite and Jadon the Meronothite, the men of Gibeon and Mizpah, made repairs, for the seat of the governor of the province Beyond the River.[^7]

**8** Next to him Uzziel son of Harhaiah, one of the goldsmiths,[^8] made repairs. Next to him Hananiah, one of the perfumers,[^9] made repairs. They restored[^10] Jerusalem as far as the Broad Wall.

**9** Next to them Rephaiah son of Hur, ruler of half the district[^11] of Jerusalem, made repairs.

**10** Next to them Jedaiah son of Harumaph made repairs opposite his house. Next to him Hattush son of Hashabneiah made repairs.

**11** Malchijah son of Harim and Hasshub son of Pahath-moab repaired another section and the Tower of the Ovens.

**12** Next to him Shallum son of Hallohesh, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, made repairs, he and his daughters.

**13** Hanun and the inhabitants of Zanoah repaired the Valley Gate. They rebuilt it and set up its doors, its bolts and its bars, and they repaired a thousand cubits[^12] of the wall, as far as the Dung Gate.

**14** Malchijah son of Rechab, ruler of the district of Beth-haccherem, repaired the Dung Gate. He rebuilt it and set up its doors, its bolts and its bars.

**15** Shallun son of Col-hozeh, ruler of the district of Mizpah, repaired the Fountain Gate. He rebuilt it, roofed it, and set up[^13] its doors, its bolts and its bars. He also repaired the wall of the Pool of Shelah[^14] by the king's garden, as far as the steps that go down from the City of David.

**16** After him Nehemiah son of Azbuk, ruler of half the district of Beth-zur, made repairs as far as a point opposite the tombs of David, as far as the artificial pool[^15] and the House of the Warriors.[^16]

**17** After him the Levites made repairs: Rehum son of Bani. Next to him Hashabiah, ruler of half the district of Keilah, made repairs for his district.

**18** After him their kinsmen made repairs: Bavvai son of Henadad,[^17] ruler of half the district of Keilah.

**19** Next to him Ezer son of Jeshua, ruler of Mizpah, repaired another section, opposite the ascent to the armory at the angle.[^18]

**20** After him Baruch son of Zaccai[^19] eagerly[^20] repaired another section, from the angle to the door of the house of Eliashib the high priest.

**21** After him Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, repaired another section, from the door of Eliashib's house to the end of Eliashib's house.

**22** After him the priests, the men of the surrounding region,[^21] made repairs.

**23** After him Benjamin and Hasshub made repairs opposite their house. After him Azariah son of Maaseiah, son of Ananiah, made repairs beside his house.

**24** After him Binnui son of Henadad repaired another section, from the house of Azariah to the angle and to the corner.

**25** Palal son of Uzai made repairs opposite the angle and the tower that projects from the upper house of the king, at the court of the guard.[^22] After him Pedaiah son of Parosh made repairs.

**26** The temple servants were living on the Ophel,[^23] as far as a point opposite the Water Gate on the east and the projecting tower.[^24]

**27** After him the men of Tekoa repaired another section, from opposite the great projecting tower as far as the wall of the Ophel.

**28** Above the Horse Gate the priests made repairs, each one opposite his own house.

**29** After them Zadok son of Immer made repairs opposite his house. After him Shemaiah son of Shecaniah, the keeper of the East Gate, made repairs.

**30** After him[^25] Hananiah son of Shelemiah and Hanun, the sixth son of Zalaph, repaired another section. After him Meshullam son of Berechiah made repairs opposite his room.

**31** After him Malchijah, one of the goldsmiths, made repairs as far as the house of the temple servants and the merchants, opposite the Muster Gate,[^26] and as far as the upper room at the corner.

**32** And between the upper room at the corner and the Sheep Gate the goldsmiths and the merchants made repairs.

**33** When Sanballat heard that we were building the wall, he was angry and greatly enraged, and he mocked the Jews.[^27]

**34** He said in the presence of his associates[^28] and the army of Samaria, "What are these feeble Jews doing? Will they restore it for themselves?[^29] Will they offer sacrifices? Will they finish it in a day? Will they bring the stones back to life out of the heaps of rubble, burned as they are?"

**35** Tobiah the Ammonite was beside him, and he said, "Even what they are building, if a fox went up on it, it would break down their stone wall!"

**36** Hear us, our God, for we are despised. Turn their insults back on their own heads, and give them up to be plundered in a land where they are captives.

**37** Do not cover their guilt, and do not let their sin be wiped out from before you, for they have provoked you to anger in front of the builders.[^30]

**38** So we built the wall, and the whole wall was joined together up to half its height,[^31] for the people had a heart to work.

[^1]: The account seems to go around the wall counterclockwise, starting from the Sheep Gate north of the temple and returning to it in verse 32.
[^2]: Literally "at his hand"; so also "next to them", "at their hand", throughout the chapter.
[^3]: Compare "the people of Senaah" in Ezra 2:35 and Nehemiah 7:38.
[^4]: Literally "strengthened". This verb is used for the repairs throughout the chapter. Meremoth is named in Ezra 8:33 and again in verse 21.
[^5]: Or "of their Lord". The Hebrew word is plural in form and can refer to God, or to Nehemiah and the other leaders.
[^6]: Or "the Jeshanah Gate". Literally "the gate of the old".
[^7]: The meaning is uncertain. Perhaps "who were under the authority of the governor", or "as far as the residence of the governor".
[^8]: Literally "goldsmiths". Also in verses 31–32.
[^9]: Literally "the son of the perfumers", that is, a member of their guild.
[^10]: The meaning of the Hebrew verb is uncertain. It may mean "restored" or "left (as it was)".
[^11]: Hebrew *pelekh*, a district or administrative region. Also in verses 12 and 14–18.
[^12]: A cubit was about 18 inches (45 cm), so a thousand cubits was about 1,500 feet (450 meters).
[^13]: The Hebrew text is written "they set up" but read "he set up".
[^14]: Or "the Pool of Siloam" (compare Isaiah 8:6; John 9:7).
[^15]: Literally "the pool that was made".
[^16]: Hebrew *beit ha-gibborim*, perhaps barracks for soldiers.
[^17]: Verse 24 has Binnui son of Henadad.
[^18]: Hebrew *miqtsoa*, a corner or angle in the wall. Also in verses 20 and 24–25.
[^19]: The Hebrew text is written "Zabbai" but read "Zaccai".
[^20]: The meaning of Hebrew *hecherah* is uncertain. It may mean "with zeal".
[^21]: Hebrew *ha-kikkar*, "the circle": the region around Jerusalem, or the Jordan valley.
[^22]: The Hebrew of verses 25–26 has no verb for Palal and Pedaiah; "made repairs" is supplied from the context.
[^23]: The hill on the east side of the city between the City of David and the temple.
[^24]: This seems to describe the section that Pedaiah repaired.
[^25]: The Hebrew text is written "After me" but read "After him". Also at the beginning of verse 31.
[^26]: Hebrew *sha'ar ha-mifqad*, perhaps "the Inspection Gate" or "the Gate of the Appointed Place".
[^27]: Verses 33–38 are 4:1–6 in most English Bibles.
[^28]: Literally "his brothers".
[^29]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. Literally "Will they leave to themselves?"
[^30]: Or "for they have insulted the builders to their faces".
[^31]: Literally "up to its half".
