**1** When the wall had been rebuilt and I had set up the doors, the gatekeepers, the singers and the Levites were appointed.

**2** I put my brother Hanani in charge of Jerusalem, along with Hananiah the commander of the fortress, for he was a faithful man and feared God more than most.

**3** I said[^1] to them, "The gates of Jerusalem are not to be opened until the sun is hot. While the gatekeepers are still on duty, let them shut the doors and bar them. And appoint guards from the inhabitants of Jerusalem, each at his post and each opposite his own house."

**4** The city was wide and large, but there were few people in it, and the houses had not been rebuilt.

**5** Then my God put it into my heart to assemble the nobles, the officials and the people, to be registered by their genealogy. I found the book of the genealogy of those who had come up at the first, and I found this written in it:

**6** These are the people of the province[^2] who came up out of the captivity of the exiles, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had taken into exile. They returned to Jerusalem and to Judah, each to his own town.[^3]

**7** They came with Zerubbabel, Jeshua, Nehemiah, Azariah, Raamiah, Nahamani, Mordecai, Bilshan, Mispereth, Bigvai, Nehum and Baanah.[^4] The number of the men of the people of Israel:

**8** the descendants of Parosh, 2,172;

**9** the descendants of Shephatiah, 372;

**10** the descendants of Arah, 652;[^5]

**11** the descendants of Pahath-moab,[^6] through the descendants of Jeshua and Joab, 2,818;[^7]

**12** the descendants of Elam, 1,254;

**13** the descendants of Zattu, 845;[^8]

**14** the descendants of Zaccai, 760;

**15** the descendants of Binnui, 648;[^9]

**16** the descendants of Bebai, 628;[^10]

**17** the descendants of Azgad, 2,322;[^11]

**18** the descendants of Adonikam, 667;[^12]

**19** the descendants of Bigvai, 2,067;[^13]

**20** the descendants of Adin, 655;[^14]

**21** the descendants of Ater, through Hezekiah, 98;

**22** the descendants of Hashum, 328;[^15]

**23** the descendants of Bezai, 324;[^16]

**24** the descendants of Hariph, 112;[^17]

**25** the people of Gibeon,[^18] 95;

**26** the men of Bethlehem and Netophah, 188;[^19]

**27** the men of Anathoth, 128;

**28** the men of Beth-azmaveth, 42;[^20]

**29** the men of Kiriath-jearim, Chephirah and Beeroth, 743;[^21]

**30** the men of Ramah and Geba, 621;[^22]

**31** the men of Michmas, 122;

**32** the men of Bethel and Ai, 123;[^23]

**33** the men of the other Nebo, 52;[^24]

**34** the descendants of the other Elam, 1,254;

**35** the descendants of Harim, 320;

**36** the people of Jericho, 345;

**37** the people of Lod, Hadid and Ono, 721;[^25]

**38** the people of Senaah, 3,930.[^26]

**39** The priests: the descendants of Jedaiah, of the house of Jeshua, 973;

**40** the descendants of Immer, 1,052;

**41** the descendants of Pashhur, 1,247;

**42** the descendants of Harim, 1,017.

**43** The Levites: the descendants of Jeshua, through Kadmiel, through the descendants of Hodevah, 74.[^27]

**44** The singers: the descendants of Asaph, 148.[^28]

**45** The gatekeepers: the descendants of Shallum, the descendants of Ater, the descendants of Talmon, the descendants of Akkub, the descendants of Hatita and the descendants of Shobai, 138.[^29]

**46** The temple servants:[^30] the descendants of Ziha, the descendants of Hasupha, the descendants of Tabbaoth,

**47** the descendants of Keros, the descendants of Sia,[^31] the descendants of Padon,

**48** the descendants of Lebana, the descendants of Hagaba, the descendants of Shalmai,[^32]

**49** the descendants of Hanan, the descendants of Giddel, the descendants of Gahar,

**50** the descendants of Reaiah, the descendants of Rezin, the descendants of Nekoda,

**51** the descendants of Gazzam, the descendants of Uzza, the descendants of Paseah,

**52** the descendants of Besai, the descendants of the Meunim, the descendants of the Nephishesim,[^33]

**53** the descendants of Bakbuk, the descendants of Hakupha, the descendants of Harhur,

**54** the descendants of Bazlith,[^34] the descendants of Mehida, the descendants of Harsha,

**55** the descendants of Barkos, the descendants of Sisera, the descendants of Temah,

**56** the descendants of Neziah and the descendants of Hatipha.

**57** The descendants of Solomon's servants:[^35] the descendants of Sotai, the descendants of Sophereth, the descendants of Perida,[^36]

**58** the descendants of Jaala,[^37] the descendants of Darkon, the descendants of Giddel,

**59** the descendants of Shephatiah, the descendants of Hattil, the descendants of Pochereth-hazzebaim and the descendants of Amon.[^38]

**60** All the temple servants and the descendants of Solomon's servants: 392.

**61** These are the ones who came up from Tel-melah, Tel-harsha, Cherub, Addon[^39] and Immer, but they could not show that their ancestral families and their descent were from Israel:[^40]

**62** the descendants of Delaiah, the descendants of Tobiah and the descendants of Nekoda, 642.[^41]

**63** And of the priests: the descendants of Hobaiah, the descendants of Hakkoz and the descendants of Barzillai, who had married one of the daughters of Barzillai the Gileadite and was called by their name.[^42]

**64** These searched for their entry among those recorded in the genealogies, but it was not found, so they were excluded from the priesthood as unclean.[^43]

**65** The governor[^44] told them not to eat any of the most holy food[^45] until the priest arose who could consult the Urim and Thummim.[^46]

**66** The whole assembly together numbered 42,360,[^47]

**67** besides their male and female servants, who numbered 7,337. They also had 245 male and female singers.[^48]

**68** The camels numbered 435, and the donkeys 6,720.[^49]

**69** Some of the heads of the ancestral families[^50] gave to the work. The governor gave to the treasury 1,000 darics[^51] of gold, 50 basins and 530 priests' garments.

**70** And some of the heads of the ancestral families gave to the treasury for the work 20,000 darics of gold and 2,200 minas[^52] of silver.

**71** What the rest of the people gave was 20,000 darics of gold, 2,000 minas of silver and 67 priests' garments.

**72** So the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, some of the people, the temple servants and all Israel settled in their towns. The seventh month came, and the Israelites were in their towns.[^53]

[^1]: The Hebrew text is written "he said" but read "I said".
[^2]: Literally "the sons of the province": Judah, now a province of the Persian Empire. Verses 6–72 repeat the list of Ezra 2:1–70 and the words of Ezra 3:1, with differences in some names and numbers, which are noted below.
[^3]: Ezra 2:1 reads "taken into exile to Babylon" and "to Jerusalem and Judah".
[^4]: Ezra 2:2 has Seraiah for Azariah, Reelaiah for Raamiah, Mispar for Mispereth and Rehum for Nehum, and does not have Nahamani.
[^5]: Ezra 2:5 has 775.
[^6]: The name means "governor of Moab".
[^7]: Ezra 2:6 has 2,812.
[^8]: Ezra 2:8 has 945.
[^9]: Ezra 2:10 has Bani, 642.
[^10]: Ezra 2:11 has 623.
[^11]: Ezra 2:12 has 1,222.
[^12]: Ezra 2:13 has 666.
[^13]: Ezra 2:14 has 2,056.
[^14]: Ezra 2:15 has 454.
[^15]: Ezra 2:19 has 223, and lists Hashum after Bezai and Jorah.
[^16]: Ezra 2:17 has 323.
[^17]: Ezra 2:18 has Jorah, 112.
[^18]: Ezra 2:20 has "the descendants of Gibbar". From here the list turns mostly to towns, and "the sons of" a town are its people.
[^19]: Ezra 2:21–22 lists them separately: "the people of Bethlehem, 123; the men of Netophah, 56".
[^20]: Ezra 2:24 has "the people of Azmaveth".
[^21]: Ezra 2:25 has "the people of Kiriath-arim".
[^22]: Ezra 2:26 has "the people of Ramah and Geba".
[^23]: Ezra 2:28 has 223.
[^24]: Ezra 2:29 has "the people of Nebo". Ezra 2:30 then lists "the people of Magbish, 156", who are not in this chapter.
[^25]: Ezra 2:33 has 725, and lists Lod, Hadid and Ono before Jericho.
[^26]: Ezra 2:35 has 3,630.
[^27]: Ezra 2:40 has "the descendants of Jeshua and Kadmiel, through the descendants of Hodaviah".
[^28]: Ezra 2:41 has 128.
[^29]: Ezra 2:42 has "The descendants of the gatekeepers" and "139 in all".
[^30]: Hebrew *netinim*, "those given", that is, given to the service of the temple. They were temple workers of lower rank than the Levites (Ezra 8:20). See also 3:26.
[^31]: Ezra 2:44 has Siaha.
[^32]: Ezra 2:45–46 spells Lebana and Hagaba as Lebanah and Hagabah, and adds Akkub and Hagab. Ezra 2:50 also adds Asnah.
[^33]: The Hebrew text is written "Nephushesim" but read "Nephishesim". Ezra 2:50 has Nephusim.
[^34]: Ezra 2:52 has Bazluth.
[^35]: Perhaps descendants of the foreign workers Solomon put to forced labor (1 Kings 9:20–21).
[^36]: Ezra 2:55 has Hassophereth and Peruda.
[^37]: Ezra 2:56 has Jaalah.
[^38]: Ezra 2:57 has Ami.
[^39]: Ezra 2:59 has Addan.
[^40]: Literally "they could not tell their fathers' house and their seed, whether they were from Israel".
[^41]: Ezra 2:60 has 652.
[^42]: That is, he took his father-in-law's name. On Barzillai the Gileadite, see 2 Samuel 17:27; 19:32–40 (English 19:31–39).
[^43]: Literally "they were defiled from the priesthood". Ezra 2:62 has "they were not found".
[^44]: Hebrew *tirshata*, a Persian title of a high official, perhaps meaning "His Excellency". Also in verse 69. It is used of Nehemiah in 8:9 and 10:2 (English 10:1).
[^45]: Literally "the holy of holies": the priests' share of the most holy offerings (Leviticus 2:3; 6:10–11, English 6:17–18).
[^46]: Literally "until the priest stood for Urim and Thummim". These were kept in the high priest's breastpiece and used to learn Yahweh's decision (Exodus 28:30; Numbers 27:21). Ezra 2:63 has "a priest".
[^47]: The numbers given in verses 8–62 add up to 31,089.
[^48]: Ezra 2:65 has 200 singers.
[^49]: Ezra 2:66 adds before this, "Their horses numbered 736, their mules 245". Most English Bibles add this line here as verse 68, so verses 68–72 are 7:69–73 in most English Bibles.
[^50]: Literally "the heads of the fathers", that is, of the fathers' houses: extended families descended from a common ancestor. Verses 69–71 record the gifts differently from Ezra 2:68–69.
[^51]: Hebrew *darkemonim*: probably darics, Persian gold coins weighing about 0.3 ounce (8.4 g) each; or Greek drachmas.
[^52]: A mina was about 1.25 pounds (0.6 kg).
[^53]: Ezra 3:1 has the same words.
