**1** Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arab and the rest of our enemies heard that I had rebuilt the wall and that no gap was left in it, though up to that time I had not yet set up the doors in the gates.

**2** Then Sanballat and Geshem sent me this message: "Come, let us meet together in one of the villages[^1] in the plain of Ono." But they were planning to do me harm.

**3** So I sent messengers to them, saying, "I am doing a great work, and I cannot come down. Why should the work stop while I leave it and come down to you?"

**4** They sent me the same message four times, and I gave them the same answer.

**5** Then Sanballat sent his servant to me with the same message a fifth time, with an open letter in his hand.

**6** In it was written: "It is reported among the nations, and Gashmu[^2] says so too, that you and the Jews are planning to rebel. That is why you are building the wall. And according to these reports, you are about to become their king.

**7** You have even appointed prophets to proclaim about you in Jerusalem, 'There is a king in Judah!' Now this report will reach the king. So come now, and let us take counsel together."

**8** I sent him this reply: "Nothing like what you are saying has happened. You are making it up out of your own head."[^3]

**9** For they were all trying to frighten us, thinking, "Their hands will drop from the work, and it will not be done." But now, strengthen my hands![^4]

**10** I went to the house of Shemaiah son of Delaiah, son of Mehetabel, who was confined at home.[^5] He said, "Let us meet at the house of God, inside the temple,[^6] and let us shut the doors of the temple, for they are coming to kill you. They are coming to kill you tonight."

**11** But I said, "Should a man like me run away? And can a man like me go into the temple and live?[^7] I will not go in."

**12** Then I realized that God had not sent him. He had spoken this prophecy against me because Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him.

**13** He had been hired for this purpose: to frighten me, so that I would do as he said and sin, and they would have a reason to give me a bad name, so that they could taunt me.

**14** Remember, my God, Tobiah and Sanballat for these deeds of theirs, and also Noadiah the prophetess and the rest of the prophets who were trying to frighten me.

**15** So the wall was finished on the twenty-fifth day of Elul,[^8] in fifty-two days.

**16** When all our enemies heard of it, all the nations around us were afraid[^9] and lost their confidence,[^10] for they knew that this work had been done by our God.

**17** In those days too, the nobles of Judah were sending many letters to Tobiah, and Tobiah's letters were coming to them.

**18** For many in Judah were bound to him by oath, because he was the son-in-law of Shecaniah son of Arah, and his son Jehohanan had married the daughter of Meshullam son of Berechiah.

**19** They also kept telling me about his good deeds and reporting my words to him. And Tobiah sent letters to frighten me.

[^1]: Or "at Chephirim". Hebrew *kephirim* may mean "villages" or be the name of a place.
[^2]: Another form of the name Geshem.
[^3]: Literally "from your heart".
[^4]: This is a brief prayer to God, as in verse 14.
[^5]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. Literally "and he was shut in".
[^6]: Hebrew *heikhal*, the main hall of the temple building, which only priests were allowed to enter (Numbers 18:7). Also later in the verse and in verse 11.
[^7]: Or "go into the temple to save his life".
[^8]: The sixth month, around August–September.
[^9]: Or "saw it".
[^10]: Literally "they fell greatly in their own eyes".
