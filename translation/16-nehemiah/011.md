**1** The leaders of the people settled in Jerusalem, and the rest of the people cast lots to bring one out of every ten to live in Jerusalem, the holy city, while the other nine stayed in the towns.[^1]

**2** The people blessed all the men who volunteered to live in Jerusalem.

**3** These are the heads of the province who lived in Jerusalem.[^2] In the towns of Judah, each lived on his own property in their towns: Israelites,[^3] the priests, the Levites, the temple servants[^4] and the descendants of Solomon's servants.

**4** In Jerusalem lived some of the descendants of Judah and some of the descendants of Benjamin. Of the descendants of Judah: Athaiah son of Uzziah, son of Zechariah, son of Amariah, son of Shephatiah, son of Mahalalel, of the descendants of Perez;

**5** and Maaseiah son of Baruch, son of Col-hozeh, son of Hazaiah, son of Adaiah, son of Joiarib, son of Zechariah, son of the Shilonite.[^5]

**6** All the descendants of Perez who lived in Jerusalem were 468 capable men.

**7** These are the descendants of Benjamin: Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Joed, son of Pedaiah, son of Kolaiah, son of Maaseiah, son of Ithiel, son of Jeshaiah;

**8** and after him Gabbai and Sallai,[^6] 928.

**9** Joel son of Zichri was the officer over them, and Judah son of Hassenuah was second in command over the city.[^7]

**10** Of the priests: Jedaiah son of Joiarib, Jachin,[^8]

**11** Seraiah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub, the officer in charge of the house of God,

**12** and their kinsmen who did the work of the house, 822; and Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pelaliah, son of Amzi, son of Zechariah, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah,

**13** and his kinsmen, heads of ancestral families, 242; and Amashsai son of Azarel, son of Ahzai, son of Meshillemoth, son of Immer,

**14** and their kinsmen, capable warriors, 128. The officer over them was Zabdiel son of Haggedolim.[^9]

**15** Of the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, son of Bunni;

**16** and Shabbethai and Jozabad, of the heads of the Levites, who were in charge of the outside work of the house of God;

**17** and Mattaniah son of Mica, son of Zabdi, son of Asaph, the leader who began the thanksgiving at prayer,[^10] and Bakbukiah, the second among his kinsmen; and Abda son of Shammua, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun.

**18** All the Levites in the holy city were 284.

**19** The gatekeepers: Akkub, Talmon and their kinsmen, who kept watch at the gates, 172.

**20** The rest of Israel, the priests and the Levites, were in all the towns of Judah, each on his own inheritance.

**21** The temple servants lived on the Ophel, and Ziha and Gishpa were in charge of the temple servants.

**22** The officer of the Levites in Jerusalem was Uzzi son of Bani, son of Hashabiah, son of Mattaniah, son of Mica, of the descendants of Asaph, the singers, over the work of the house of God.

**23** For there was a command from the king concerning them, and a fixed arrangement for the singers, as each day required.[^11]

**24** Pethahiah son of Meshezabel, of the descendants of Zerah son of Judah, was the king's agent[^12] in every matter concerning the people.

**25** As for the villages with their fields: some of the descendants of Judah lived in Kiriath-arba and its settlements,[^13] in Dibon and its settlements, in Jekabzeel and its villages,

**26** in Jeshua, in Moladah and in Beth-pelet,

**27** in Hazar-shual, in Beersheba and its settlements,

**28** in Ziklag, in Meconah and its settlements,

**29** in En-rimmon, in Zorah and in Jarmuth,

**30** Zanoah, Adullam and their villages, Lachish and its fields, Azekah and its settlements. So they camped from Beersheba to the Valley of Hinnom.

**31** The descendants of Benjamin lived from Geba onward: at Michmash, Aija, Bethel and its settlements,

**32** Anathoth, Nob, Ananiah,

**33** Hazor, Ramah, Gittaim,

**34** Hadid, Zeboim, Neballat,

**35** Lod and Ono, and the Valley of the Craftsmen.[^14]

**36** Some divisions of the Levites of Judah were joined to Benjamin.[^15]

[^1]: Literally "and nine of the hands in the towns".
[^2]: Verses 3–19 have a parallel, with many differences, in 1 Chronicles 9:2–17.
[^3]: Literally "Israel", that is, the people who were not priests or Levites.
[^4]: Hebrew *netinim*: temple workers of lower rank than the Levites; see 7:46. Also in verse 21.
[^5]: Or "the Shelanite", a descendant of Shelah son of Judah (Numbers 26:20).
[^6]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain.
[^7]: Or "was over the Second Quarter of the city" (compare 2 Kings 22:14; Zephaniah 1:10).
[^8]: 1 Chronicles 9:10 has "Jedaiah, Jehoiarib and Jachin".
[^9]: Or "a member of one of the great families". *Haggedolim* means "the great ones".
[^10]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "the head of the beginning, he gave thanks at the prayer".
[^11]: Literally "the matter of a day in its day". Hebrew *amanah*, "firm agreement", is the word used in 10:1 (9:38 in most English Bibles).
[^12]: Literally "at the king's hand".
[^13]: Literally "its daughters": the smaller places that belonged to a town. Also in verses 27–28 and 30–31.
[^14]: Or "Ge-harashim".
[^15]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. Literally "And of the Levites, divisions of Judah to Benjamin".
