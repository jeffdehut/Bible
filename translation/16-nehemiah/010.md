**1** Because of all this, we are making a firm agreement and putting it in writing. On the sealed document are the names of our leaders, our Levites and our priests.[^1]

**2** On the sealed documents were:[^2] Nehemiah the governor,[^3] son of Hacaliah, and Zedekiah;

**3** Seraiah, Azariah, Jeremiah,[^4]

**4** Pashhur, Amariah, Malchijah,

**5** Hattush, Shebaniah, Malluch,

**6** Harim, Meremoth, Obadiah,

**7** Daniel, Ginnethon, Baruch,

**8** Meshullam, Abijah, Mijamin,

**9** Maaziah, Bilgai and Shemaiah. These were the priests.

**10** The Levites: Jeshua son of Azaniah, Binnui of the descendants of Henadad, Kadmiel,[^5]

**11** and their kinsmen: Shebaniah, Hodiah, Kelita, Pelaiah, Hanan,

**12** Mica, Rehob, Hashabiah,

**13** Zaccur, Sherebiah, Shebaniah,

**14** Hodiah, Bani and Beninu.

**15** The leaders of the people: Parosh, Pahath-moab, Elam, Zattu, Bani,[^6]

**16** Bunni, Azgad, Bebai,

**17** Adonijah, Bigvai, Adin,

**18** Ater, Hezekiah, Azzur,

**19** Hodiah, Hashum, Bezai,

**20** Hariph, Anathoth, Nebai,[^7]

**21** Magpiash, Meshullam, Hezir,

**22** Meshezabel, Zadok, Jaddua,

**23** Pelatiah, Hanan, Anaiah,

**24** Hoshea, Hananiah, Hasshub,

**25** Hallohesh, Pilha, Shobek,

**26** Rehum, Hashabnah, Maaseiah,

**27** Ahijah, Hanan, Anan,

**28** Malluch, Harim and Baanah.

**29** The rest of the people, the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, the temple servants[^8] and all who had separated themselves from the peoples of the lands to keep the Law of God, with their wives, their sons and their daughters, all who had knowledge and understanding,

**30** joined with their kinsmen, their nobles, and bound themselves with a curse and an oath[^9] to walk in the Law of God, which was given through Moses the servant of God, and to keep and do all the commands of the LORD[^10] our Lord, and his rulings and his decrees.

**31** We will not give our daughters to the peoples of the land, and we will not take their daughters for our sons.

**32** When the peoples of the land bring goods or any grain to sell on the Sabbath day, we will not buy from them on the Sabbath or on a holy day. And in the seventh year we will leave the land unworked and cancel every debt.[^11]

**33** We also take on ourselves the obligation[^12] to give a third of a shekel[^13] each year for the service of the house of our God:

**34** for the bread of the Presence,[^14] for the regular grain offering and the regular burnt offering, for the Sabbaths, the new moons and the appointed festivals, for the holy offerings, and for the sin offerings[^15] to make atonement for Israel, and for all the work of the house of our God.

**35** We, the priests, the Levites and the people, have cast lots for the wood offering, to bring it to the house of our God by our ancestral families at appointed times year by year, to burn on the altar of the LORD our God, as it is written in the Law.

**36** We will bring the firstfruits of our ground and the firstfruits of all the fruit of every tree year by year to the house of the LORD.

**37** We will also bring to the house of our God, to the priests who minister in the house of our God, the firstborn of our sons and of our livestock, as it is written in the Law, and the firstborn of our herds and of our flocks.

**38** We will bring the first of our dough,[^16] our contributions,[^17] the fruit of every tree, the new wine and the olive oil to the priests, to the storerooms of the house of our God, and the tithe of our ground to the Levites, for it is the Levites who collect the tithes in all the towns where we work.

**39** A priest descended from Aaron is to be with the Levites when the Levites collect the tithes, and the Levites are to bring a tenth of the tithes up to the house of our God, to the storerooms of the treasury.[^18]

**40** For the Israelites and the descendants of Levi are to bring the contribution of grain, new wine and olive oil to the storerooms, where the articles of the sanctuary are kept, and where the priests who minister, the gatekeepers and the singers are. We will not neglect the house of our God.

[^1]: Verse 1 is 9:38 in most English Bibles, and verses 2–40 are 10:1–39.
[^2]: Literally "And on the sealed ones". The names were those who set their seals on the agreement.
[^3]: Hebrew *tirshata*, a Persian title of a high official; see 7:65.
[^4]: Most of the names in verses 3–9 are also names of priestly families in 12:1–7 and 12:12–21.
[^5]: Compare the Levites in 12:8 and 12:24.
[^6]: Many of the names in verses 15–28 are family names from the list of those who returned (7:8–38; Ezra 2:3–35).
[^7]: The Hebrew text is written "Nobai" but read "Nebai".
[^8]: Hebrew *netinim*: temple workers of lower rank than the Levites; see 7:46.
[^9]: That is, an oath that called down a curse on anyone who broke it.
[^10]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^11]: Literally "we will let go the seventh year and the debt of every hand". See Exodus 23:10–11; Deuteronomy 15:1–2.
[^12]: Literally "we have set commands on ourselves".
[^13]: A shekel was about ⅖ ounce (11.5 grams). Compare the half shekel of Exodus 30:11–16.
[^14]: Hebrew *lechem ha-ma'arekhet*, "the bread of the arrangement": the bread set out in rows on the table before the LORD (Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 24:5–9).
[^15]: Or "purification offerings". Hebrew *chatta'ot*: offerings to remove the effects of sin and impurity (Leviticus 4).
[^16]: Or "coarse meal". See Numbers 15:20–21.
[^17]: Hebrew *terumot*: things set apart and given for a sacred purpose. Also "contribution" in verse 40.
[^18]: See Numbers 18:25–32.
