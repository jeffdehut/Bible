**1** The words of Nehemiah son of Hacaliah.[^1] In the month of Kislev,[^2] in the twentieth year,[^3] I was in the citadel of Susa,[^4]

**2** when Hanani, one of my brothers, came with some men from Judah. I asked them about the Jews who had escaped, those who had survived the captivity, and about Jerusalem.

**3** They said to me, "The survivors who are left from the captivity there in the province are in great trouble and disgrace. The wall of Jerusalem is broken down, and its gates have been burned with fire."

**4** When I heard these words, I sat down and wept, and I mourned for days. I kept fasting and praying before the God of heaven.

**5** I said, "Please, LORD,[^5] God of heaven, the great and awesome God, who keeps covenant and faithful love[^6] with those who love him and keep his commands,[^7]

**6** let your ear be attentive and your eyes open, to hear the prayer of your servant that I am praying before you today, day and night, for the Israelites your servants. I confess the sins of the Israelites, which we have committed against you. Both I and my father's house have sinned.

**7** We have acted very corruptly against you, and we have not kept the commands, the decrees and the rulings that you commanded your servant Moses.

**8** Remember the word that you commanded your servant Moses: 'If you are unfaithful, I will scatter you among the peoples.[^8]

**9** But if you return to me and keep my commands and do them, even if your exiles are at the far edge of the heavens, I will gather them from there and bring them to the place that I have chosen to make my name dwell there.'[^9]

**10** They are your servants and your people, whom you redeemed by your great power and your strong hand.

**11** Please, Lord, let your ear be attentive to the prayer of your servant, and to the prayer of your servants who delight to fear your name. Give your servant success today, and grant that he may find compassion before this man." Now I was cupbearer[^10] to the king.

[^1]: In the Hebrew Bible, Ezra and Nehemiah are counted as a single book. Most of Nehemiah is told by Nehemiah himself, in the first person.
[^2]: The ninth month, around November–December.
[^3]: The twentieth year of King Artaxerxes I (2:1), 445 BC.
[^4]: Hebrew *Shushan ha-birah*. Susa, in what is now southwest Iran, was one of the royal cities of the Persian kings.
[^5]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^6]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^7]: Compare Deuteronomy 7:9, 21; Daniel 9:4.
[^8]: Compare Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 4:27; 28:64.
[^9]: Compare Deuteronomy 30:1–5; 12:5, 11.
[^10]: Hebrew *mashqeh*, the official who served the king his wine: a position of great trust and closeness to the king.
