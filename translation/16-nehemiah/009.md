**1** On the twenty-fourth day of this month the Israelites assembled, fasting and wearing sackcloth, with earth on their heads.

**2** Those of Israelite descent[^1] separated themselves from all foreigners, and they stood and confessed their sins and the iniquities of their ancestors.

**3** They stood up in their places and read from the book of the Law of the LORD[^2] their God for a quarter of the day, and for another quarter they confessed and worshiped the LORD their God.

**4** On the platform[^3] of the Levites stood Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani and Chenani, and they cried out with a loud voice to the LORD their God.

**5** Then the Levites, Jeshua, Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabneiah, Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah and Pethahiah, said, "Stand up and bless the LORD your God from everlasting to everlasting! May they bless your glorious name, which is exalted above all blessing and praise.

**6** You alone are the LORD.[^4] You made the heavens, the highest heavens[^5] and all their host, the earth and everything on it, the seas and everything in them. You give life to them all, and the host of heaven worships you.

**7** You are the LORD, the God who chose Abram and brought him out of Ur of the Chaldeans and gave him the name Abraham.

**8** You found his heart faithful before you, and you made the covenant with him to give the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Jebusites and the Girgashites, to give it to his descendants.[^6] And you have kept your promise,[^7] for you are righteous.

**9** You saw the affliction of our ancestors in Egypt and heard their cry at the Red Sea.[^8]

**10** You performed signs and wonders against Pharaoh, against all his servants and against all the people of his land, for you knew how arrogantly they had treated them. You made a name for yourself, as it is today.

**11** You split the sea before them, and they passed through the middle of the sea on dry ground. But you threw their pursuers into the depths, like a stone into mighty waters.[^9]

**12** By a pillar of cloud you led them by day, and by a pillar of fire by night, to light for them the way they were to go.

**13** You came down on Mount Sinai and spoke with them from heaven. You gave them right rulings and true instructions, good decrees and commands.[^10]

**14** You made known to them your holy Sabbath, and through your servant Moses you gave them commands, decrees and a law.

**15** You gave them bread from heaven for their hunger, and you brought water out of the rock for their thirst. You told them to go in and take possession of the land that you had sworn[^11] to give them.

**16** But they, our ancestors,[^12] acted arrogantly. They stiffened their necks and did not listen to your commands.

**17** They refused to listen and did not remember the wonders you had done among them. They stiffened their necks and in their rebellion appointed a leader to go back to their slavery.[^13] But you are a God of forgiveness, gracious and compassionate, slow to anger and abounding in faithful love,[^14] and you did not forsake them.

**18** Even when they made for themselves a calf of cast metal and said, 'This is your God who brought you up out of Egypt,'[^15] and committed great blasphemies,

**19** you in your great compassion did not forsake them in the wilderness. The pillar of cloud did not leave them by day, to lead them on the way, nor the pillar of fire by night, to light for them the way they were to go.

**20** You gave your good spirit[^16] to instruct them. You did not withhold your manna from their mouths, and you gave them water for their thirst.

**21** For forty years you sustained them in the wilderness, and they lacked nothing. Their clothes did not wear out, and their feet did not swell.[^17]

**22** You gave them kingdoms and peoples, and you allotted them to every corner.[^18] They took possession of the land of Sihon, the land of the king of Heshbon, and the land of Og king of Bashan.

**23** You made their children as many as the stars of the sky, and you brought them into the land that you had told their ancestors to enter and possess.

**24** So the children went in and took possession of the land. You subdued before them the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites, and gave them into their hands, with their kings and the peoples of the land, to do with them as they pleased.

**25** They captured fortified cities and fertile land. They took possession of houses full of all good things, cisterns already dug, vineyards, olive groves and fruit trees in abundance. They ate and were filled and grew fat, and they delighted in your great goodness.

**26** But they were disobedient and rebelled against you. They threw your law behind their backs. They killed your prophets, who had warned them in order to turn them back to you, and they committed great blasphemies.

**27** So you gave them into the hand of their enemies, who oppressed them. But in the time of their distress they cried out to you, and you heard from heaven, and in your great compassion you gave them deliverers, who delivered them from the hand of their enemies.

**28** But as soon as they had rest, they again did evil before you. So you abandoned them to the hand of their enemies, who ruled over them. Then they cried out to you again, and you heard from heaven, and in your compassion you rescued them many times.

**29** You warned them, to turn them back to your law, but they acted arrogantly and did not listen to your commands. They sinned against your rulings, which give life to the one who does them.[^19] They turned a stubborn shoulder, stiffened their necks and would not listen.

**30** For many years you were patient with them.[^20] You warned them by your spirit through your prophets, but they would not listen. So you gave them into the hand of the peoples of the lands.

**31** But in your great compassion you did not make an end of them or forsake them, for you are a gracious and compassionate God.

**32** So now, our God, the great, mighty and awesome God, who keeps covenant and faithful love, do not treat as small all the hardship that has come upon us, upon our kings, our leaders, our priests, our prophets, our ancestors and all your people, from the days of the kings of Assyria until this day.

**33** You have been righteous in all that has come upon us, for you have acted faithfully, but we have done wrong.

**34** Our kings, our leaders, our priests and our ancestors did not keep your law. They did not pay attention to your commands and your statutes,[^21] which you solemnly declared to them.

**35** Even in their own kingdom, amid the great goodness that you gave them, and in the broad and fertile land that you set before them, they did not serve you or turn from their evil deeds.

**36** Here we are, slaves today, slaves in the very land that you gave our ancestors to enjoy its fruit and its good things.

**37** Its rich produce goes to the kings whom you have set over us because of our sins. They rule over our bodies and over our livestock as they please, and we are in great distress."[^22]

[^1]: Literally "the seed of Israel".
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Or "stairs".
[^4]: The Septuagint begins this verse "And Ezra said", making the prayer that follows his.
[^5]: Literally "the heavens of the heavens".
[^6]: See Genesis 15:6, 18–21.
[^7]: Literally "your words".
[^8]: Hebrew *Yam Suph*, literally "Sea of Reeds", traditionally the Red Sea.
[^9]: Compare Exodus 15:5, 10.
[^10]: Hebrew *mishpatim* "rulings", *torot* "instructions, laws", *chuqqim* "decrees" and *mitsvot* "commands". Also in verse 14.
[^11]: Literally "lifted up your hand", a gesture of swearing an oath.
[^12]: Literally "they and our fathers".
[^13]: The Septuagint reads "to go back to their slavery in Egypt". Compare Numbers 14:4.
[^14]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Compare Exodus 34:6. The Hebrew text is written "great and faithful love" but read "abounding in faithful love".
[^15]: Exodus 32:4, 8.
[^16]: Hebrew *ruach*. Compare Numbers 11:17, 25. Also in verse 30.
[^17]: Compare Deuteronomy 8:4; 29:4 (29:5 in most English Bibles).
[^18]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. Perhaps "you allotted to them every region", or "you allotted the land to them as a boundary".
[^19]: Literally "which a person shall do and live by them" (Leviticus 18:5).
[^20]: Literally "you drew out over them".
[^21]: Hebrew *edot*, traditionally "testimonies": the solemn terms of the covenant. The verb that follows, "solemnly declared", is from the same root.
[^22]: Most English Bibles number the next verse, 10:1 in the Hebrew, as 9:38.
