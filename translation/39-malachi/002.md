**1** And now, priests, this command is for you.

**2** If you will not listen, and if you will not take it to heart to give honor to my name, says Yahweh of Armies,[^1] I will send the curse on you and curse your blessings.[^2] In fact, I have already cursed them, because you are not taking it to heart.

**3** Look, I am going to rebuke your offspring,[^3] and I will spread dung on your faces, the dung of your festival sacrifices,[^4] and you will be carried off with it.

**4** Then you will know that I have sent you this command, so that my covenant with Levi may continue,[^5] says Yahweh of Armies.

**5** My covenant with him was one of life and peace, and I gave them to him. It called for reverence, and he revered me and stood in awe of my name.

**6** True instruction[^6] was in his mouth, and no wrong was found on his lips. He walked with me in peace and uprightness, and he turned many away from wrongdoing.

**7** For the lips of a priest should guard knowledge, and people should seek instruction from his mouth, because he is the messenger[^7] of Yahweh of Armies.

**8** But you have turned aside from the way. You have made many stumble by your instruction. You have corrupted the covenant of Levi, says Yahweh of Armies.

**9** So I in turn have made you despised and humiliated before all the people, because you have not kept my ways but have shown partiality in your instruction.[^8]

**10** Have we not all one father?[^9] Has not one God created us? Why then are we faithless to one another,[^10] profaning the covenant of our ancestors?

**11** Judah has been faithless, and a detestable thing has been done in Israel and in Jerusalem. For Judah has profaned the holy place of Yahweh, which he loves,[^11] and has married the daughter of a foreign god.[^12]

**12** May Yahweh cut off from the tents of Jacob any man who does this, whoever he may be,[^13] even if he brings an offering to Yahweh of Armies.

**13** And this is another thing you do: you cover the altar of Yahweh with tears, with weeping and groaning, because he no longer looks on the offering or accepts it with pleasure from your hands.

**14** But you say, "Why?" Because Yahweh has been a witness between you and the wife of your youth, to whom you have been faithless, though she is your companion and your wife by covenant.[^14]

**15** Has not the One made them, and are not flesh and spirit his?[^15] And what does the One seek? Godly offspring.[^16] So guard yourselves in your spirit, and let no one be faithless to the wife of his youth.

**16** For the man who hates and divorces,[^17] says Yahweh, the God of Israel, covers his garment with violence,[^18] says Yahweh of Armies. So guard yourselves in your spirit, and do not be faithless.

**17** You have wearied Yahweh with your words. But you say, "How have we wearied him?" By saying, "Everyone who does evil is good in the eyes of Yahweh, and he delights in them," or "Where is the God of justice?"

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. *Yahweh of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^2]: Perhaps the blessings that the priests pronounce on the people (Numbers 6:22–27), or the gifts they receive.
[^3]: Hebrew *zera*, "seed, offspring". The same consonants can be read *zeroa*, "arm" or "shoulder", the priests' portion of the sacrifices (Deuteronomy 18:3), and the Septuagint reads "the shoulder".
[^4]: Hebrew *peresh*, the dung in the entrails of the sacrificed animals, which was carried out of the camp and burned (Exodus 29:14). *You will be carried off with it* is literally "he will carry you to it".
[^5]: Literally "to be my covenant with Levi", the covenant with the priestly tribe (Numbers 25:12–13; Deuteronomy 33:8–11; Jeremiah 33:21).
[^6]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, teaching, law". Also in verses 7–9.
[^7]: Hebrew *mal'akh*, "messenger", the word in Malachi's name (1:1) and in 3:1.
[^8]: Literally "lifting faces in the instruction", favoring some people over others when giving rulings.
[^9]: Probably God (1:6), or perhaps Abraham or Jacob.
[^10]: Literally "each to his brother".
[^11]: Or "has profaned what is holy to Yahweh, whom he loves", that is, the people themselves.
[^12]: A woman who worships another god. Compare Ezra 9–10; Nehemiah 13:23–27.
[^13]: Literally "one who wakes and one who answers", an idiom of uncertain meaning, perhaps "anyone at all".
[^14]: Marriage is called a covenant also in Proverbs 2:17 and Ezekiel 16:8.
[^15]: The Hebrew of this verse is very difficult, and every translation of it is uncertain. Literally "And not one he made, and a remnant of spirit to him". The text is pointed *she'ar*, "remnant"; the same consonants can be read *she'er*, "flesh", as here. Or "Did he not make them one, with flesh and spirit?" (compare Genesis 2:24), or "No one who has a remnant of the spirit would do this".
[^16]: Literally "a seed of God".
[^17]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "For he hates, send away". Traditionally "For I hate divorce", with a small change to the Hebrew. The Septuagint reads "But if you hate her and send her away".
[^18]: Perhaps a picture of marriage, in which a man spread his garment over his wife (Ruth 3:9; Ezekiel 16:8). Here he covers it with violence instead.
