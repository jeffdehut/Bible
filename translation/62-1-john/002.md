**1** My little children,[^1] I am writing these things to you so that you will not sin. But if anyone does sin, we have an Advocate[^2] with the Father, Jesus Christ the Righteous One.[^3]

**2** He himself is the atoning sacrifice[^4] for our sins, and not only for ours but also for the whole world.

**3** This is how we know that we have come to know him: if we keep his commands.

**4** Whoever says, "I have come to know him," but does not keep his commands is a liar, and the truth is not in that person.

**5** But whoever keeps his word, in that person the love of God[^5] has truly been made complete. This is how we know that we are in him:

**6** whoever says that they remain in him ought to walk just as he walked.[^6]

**7** Dear friends,[^7] I am not writing a new command to you, but an old command that you have had from the beginning. The old command is the word that you have heard.[^8]

**8** Yet I am writing a new command to you,[^9] which is true in him and in you, because the darkness is passing away and the true light is already shining.

**9** Whoever says that they are in the light but hates their brother or sister[^10] is still in the darkness.

**10** Whoever loves their brother or sister remains in the light, and there is nothing in them to make them stumble.[^11]

**11** But whoever hates their brother or sister is in the darkness and walks in the darkness, and does not know where they are going, because the darkness has blinded their eyes.

**12** I am writing to you, little children, because your sins have been forgiven for the sake of his name.[^12]

**13** I am writing to you, fathers, because you have come to know the one who is from the beginning. I am writing to you, young men, because you have overcome the evil one.

**14** I have written to you, children,[^13] because you have come to know the Father. I have written to you, fathers, because you have come to know the one who is from the beginning. I have written to you, young men, because you are strong, and the word of God remains in you, and you have overcome the evil one.

**15** Do not love the world or the things in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father[^14] is not in them.

**16** For everything in the world, the desire of the flesh,[^15] the desire of the eyes and pride in one's possessions,[^16] does not come from the Father but from the world.

**17** And the world is passing away, and its desire with it, but whoever does the will of God remains forever.

**18** Children, it is the last hour. Just as you heard that antichrist[^17] is coming, so now many antichrists have come. This is how we know that it is the last hour.

**19** They went out from us, but they did not belong to us. For if they had belonged to us, they would have remained with us. But they went out so that it might be made plain that none of them belong to us.[^18]

**20** But you have an anointing[^19] from the Holy One, and you all know.[^20]

**21** I have not written to you because you do not know the truth, but because you do know it, and because no lie comes from the truth.[^21]

**22** Who is the liar but the one who denies that Jesus is the Messiah?[^22] This is the antichrist: the one who denies the Father and the Son.

**23** No one who denies the Son has the Father. Whoever acknowledges the Son has the Father too.[^23]

**24** As for you, let what you heard from the beginning remain in you. If what you heard from the beginning remains in you, you too will remain in the Son and in the Father.

**25** And this is the promise that he himself made to us: eternal life.

**26** I have written these things to you about those who are trying to lead you astray.

**27** As for you, the anointing that you received from him remains in you, and you have no need for anyone to teach you. But as his anointing[^24] teaches you about everything, and is true and is not a lie, then just as it has taught you, remain in him.[^25]

**28** And now, little children, remain in him, so that when he appears[^26] we may have confidence and not shrink back from him in shame at his coming.[^27]

**29** If you know that he is righteous, you know[^28] that everyone who does what is right has been born of him.

[^1]: Greek *teknia*, an affectionate word for children, as in John 13:33; also in verses 12 and 28.
[^2]: Greek *parakletos*, "one called to someone's side": a helper or advocate, here one who pleads for sinners before the Father. In John 14:16, 26 the word is used of the Holy Spirit.
[^3]: Or "Jesus Christ, who is righteous".
[^4]: Greek *hilasmos*, "atonement, means of atonement": a sacrifice that takes away sin and turns away God's anger; traditionally "propitiation" or "expiation". Also in 4:10. The related word *hilasterion* is the "atonement cover" over the ark (Hebrews 9:5; compare Exodus 25:17).
[^5]: Or "love for God".
[^6]: Literally "just as that one walked". In this letter "that one" (Greek *ekeinos*) refers to Jesus (3:3, 5, 7, 16; 4:17). *Walk*: that is, live.
[^7]: Literally "Beloved". Many manuscripts read "Brothers and sisters".
[^8]: Many manuscripts add "from the beginning".
[^9]: Literally "Again, I am writing a new command to you". *Which is true*: the Greek "which" may refer to the command, or to the fact that it is new.
[^10]: Literally "his brother"; also in verses 10–11. The letter uses "brother" for any fellow believer, man or woman.
[^11]: Greek *skandalon*, something that trips people up. Or "and there is nothing in it to cause stumbling", that is, in the light.
[^12]: Or "I am writing to you, little children, that your sins have been forgiven". The Greek *hoti* can mean "because" or "that"; also in verses 13–14.
[^13]: Greek *paidia*, a different word from "little children" (*teknia*) in verse 12, but with much the same meaning; also in verse 18.
[^14]: Or "love for the Father".
[^15]: *The flesh*: human nature, in its weakness and its pull away from God.
[^16]: Or "pride in one's way of life". Greek *bios*, "life, livelihood, possessions".
[^17]: Greek *antichristos*, "one who is against (or in place of) the Christ". In the Bible the word occurs only in 1 and 2 John (also verse 22; 4:3; 2 John 7).
[^18]: Literally "but so that it might be shown that they all are not from us".
[^19]: Greek *chrisma*, a word related to *christos*, "anointed one", and *antichristos* (verse 18). The letter does not say what the anointing is; it has been understood as the Holy Spirit (compare 3:24; John 14:26) or as the message the readers received (verse 24). Also in verse 27.
[^20]: Many manuscripts read "and you know all things".
[^21]: Or "and that no lie comes from the truth".
[^22]: Literally "who denies, saying that Jesus is not the Christ". *Messiah*: Greek *ho christos*, "the Christ", "the Anointed One".
[^23]: Many manuscripts leave out "Whoever acknowledges the Son has the Father too."
[^24]: Many manuscripts read "the same anointing".
[^25]: Or "you remain in him", a statement rather than a command. Many manuscripts read "you will remain in him". *In him*: or "in it", the anointing.
[^26]: Literally "if he appears". Many manuscripts read "when he appears".
[^27]: Greek *parousia*, "presence, arrival", the word used for Christ's return.
[^28]: Or "know", a command.
