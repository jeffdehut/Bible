**1** Dear friends, do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits to see whether they are from God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world.

**2** This is how you know the Spirit of God:[^1] every spirit that acknowledges that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh[^2] is from God,

**3** and every spirit that does not acknowledge Jesus[^3] is not from God. This is the spirit of the antichrist,[^4] which you have heard is coming, and now it is already in the world.

**4** You are from God, little children, and you have overcome them, because the one who is in you is greater than the one who is in the world.

**5** They are from the world. That is why they speak as the world speaks,[^5] and the world listens to them.

**6** We are from God. Whoever knows God listens to us; whoever is not from God does not listen to us. This is how we know the Spirit of truth and the spirit of error.[^6]

**7** Dear friends, let us love one another, because love comes from God, and everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God.

**8** Whoever does not love has not known God, because God is love.

**9** This is how God's love was revealed among us:[^7] God has sent his only Son[^8] into the world so that we might live through him.

**10** This is love: not that we have loved God, but that he loved us and sent his Son as the atoning sacrifice[^9] for our sins.

**11** Dear friends, if God loved us in this way, we also ought to love one another.

**12** No one has ever seen God. If we love one another, God remains in us, and his love is made complete in us.

**13** This is how we know that we remain in him and he in us: he has given us a share in his Spirit.[^10]

**14** And we have seen and we testify that the Father has sent the Son as the Savior of the world.

**15** Whoever acknowledges that Jesus[^11] is the Son of God, God remains in them, and they in God.

**16** And we have come to know and have believed the love that God has for us.[^12] God is love, and whoever remains in love remains in God, and God remains in them.

**17** This is how love has been made complete among us: we can have confidence on the day of judgment, because as he is, so are we in this world.[^13]

**18** There is no fear in love; perfect love[^14] drives out fear, because fear has to do with punishment. Whoever is afraid has not been made complete in love.

**19** We love[^15] because he first loved us.

**20** If anyone says, "I love God," but hates their brother or sister,[^16] that person is a liar. For whoever does not love their brother or sister, whom they have seen, cannot love God, whom they have not seen.[^17]

**21** And this is the command that we have from him: whoever loves God must also love their brother or sister.

[^1]: Many manuscripts read "This is how the Spirit of God is known".
[^2]: Or "acknowledges Jesus Christ as one who has come in the flesh". *The flesh*: that is, as a human being (compare John 1:14).
[^3]: Many manuscripts read "that does not acknowledge that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh", as in verse 2.
[^4]: Literally "this is the [spirit] of the antichrist". See 2:18.
[^5]: Literally "they speak from the world".
[^6]: Or "the spirit of deception".
[^7]: Or "in our case"; literally "in us".
[^8]: Greek *monogenes*, "only, unique, one of a kind", as in John 1:14; 3:16.
[^9]: Greek *hilasmos*. See 2:2.
[^10]: Literally "he has given us of his Spirit".
[^11]: Some manuscripts read "Jesus Christ".
[^12]: Literally "in us".
[^13]: Or "This is how love has been made complete among us, so that we may have confidence on the day of judgment: as he is, so are we in this world." *He*: literally "that one" (see 2:6).
[^14]: Greek *teleia*, "complete", from the same root as "made complete" in verses 12, 17 and 18.
[^15]: Many manuscripts read "We love him".
[^16]: Literally "his brother"; also later in the verse and in verse 21. The letter uses "brother" for any fellow believer, man or woman.
[^17]: Many manuscripts read "how can they love God, whom they have not seen?"
