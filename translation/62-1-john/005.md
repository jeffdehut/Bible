**1** Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Messiah[^1] has been born of God, and everyone who loves the parent also loves the one born of him.[^2]

**2** This is how we know that we love the children of God: when we love God and carry out his commands.[^3]

**3** For this is the love of God:[^4] that we keep his commands. And his commands are not burdensome,

**4** because everything that has been born of God[^5] overcomes the world. And this is the victory that has overcome the world: our faith.

**5** Who is it that overcomes the world, if not the one who believes that Jesus is the Son of God?

**6** This is the one who came by water and blood, Jesus Christ: not with the water only, but with the water and with the blood.[^6] And the Spirit is the one who testifies, because the Spirit is the truth.

**7** For there are three that testify:[^7]

**8** the Spirit, the water and the blood, and the three agree.[^8]

**9** If we accept human testimony, the testimony of God is greater; for this is the testimony of God: that he has testified about his Son.[^9]

**10** Whoever believes in the Son of God has the testimony in themselves. Whoever does not believe God has made him a liar, because they have not believed the testimony that God has given about his Son.

**11** And this is the testimony: God has given us eternal life, and this life is in his Son.

**12** Whoever has the Son has life; whoever does not have the Son of God does not have life.

**13** I have written these things to you so that you may know that you have eternal life, you who believe in the name of the Son of God.[^10]

**14** And this is the confidence that we have before him: if we ask anything according to his will, he hears us.

**15** And if we know that he hears us in whatever we ask, we know that we have what we have asked of him.

**16** If anyone sees their brother or sister[^11] committing a sin that does not lead to death,[^12] they should ask, and God will give them life:[^13] that is, to those who commit sins that do not lead to death. There is sin that leads to death. I am not saying that they should make a request about that.

**17** All wrongdoing[^14] is sin, but there is sin that does not lead to death.

**18** We know that no one who has been born of God sins,[^15] but the one who was born of God keeps them safe,[^16] and the evil one does not touch them.

**19** We know that we are from God, and that the whole world lies in the power of the evil one.[^17]

**20** And we know that the Son of God has come and has given us understanding, so that we may know the one who is true. And we are in the one who is true, in his Son Jesus Christ. He is the true God and eternal life.[^18]

**21** Little children, keep yourselves from idols.[^19]

[^1]: Greek *ho christos*, "the Christ", "the Anointed One".
[^2]: Literally "everyone who loves the one who fathered loves also the one fathered by him".
[^3]: Many manuscripts read "keep his commands".
[^4]: Or "love for God".
[^5]: The Greek is neuter: "everything", "whatever" has been born of God.
[^6]: The letter does not explain "water and blood". They have often been taken as Jesus' baptism and his death; compare also John 19:34.
[^7]: The Textus Receptus (followed by the King James Version) adds "in heaven: the Father, the Word and the Holy Spirit; and these three are one. And there are three that testify on earth:". These words, known as the Johannine Comma, are not in the SBLGNT or in any of the editions in its apparatus. They are found in only a few late Greek manuscripts, but in many manuscripts of the Latin Vulgate, and entered the printed Greek text in the sixteenth century.
[^8]: Literally "the three are for the one", that is, they agree in one testimony.
[^9]: Many manuscripts read "this is the testimony of God that he has given about his Son".
[^10]: Many manuscripts read "I have written these things to you who believe in the name of the Son of God, so that you may know that you have eternal life, and so that you may believe in the name of the Son of God." Compare John 20:31.
[^11]: Literally "his brother". The letter uses "brother" for any fellow believer, man or woman.
[^12]: Literally "a sin not toward death"; and so later in this verse and in verse 17. The letter does not say what "sin that leads to death" is.
[^13]: Literally "he will ask, and he will give him life". *God* is supplied from the context; the Greek may also mean that the one who prays gives life, through his prayer.
[^14]: Greek *adikia*, "unrighteousness". See 1:9.
[^15]: See the note on 3:6.
[^16]: Many manuscripts read "keeps himself safe", in which case the one born of God is the believer. With the SBLGNT reading, "the one who was born of God" is usually taken to be the Son of God.
[^17]: Literally "lies in the evil one". Or "lies in evil".
[^18]: Greek *houtos*, "this one", may refer to Jesus Christ or to God the Father, "the one who is true".
[^19]: Many manuscripts add "Amen".
