**1** In the twenty-third year of Joash son of Ahaziah king of Judah, Jehoahaz son of Jehu became king over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned seventeen years.

**2** He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh[^1] and followed the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which he had caused Israel to commit. He did not turn away from them.

**3** So Yahweh's anger burned against Israel, and he handed them over to Hazael king of Aram and to Ben-hadad son of Hazael, again and again.[^2]

**4** Then Jehoahaz pleaded with Yahweh,[^3] and Yahweh listened to him, for he saw the oppression of Israel, how the king of Aram oppressed them.

**5** Yahweh gave Israel a deliverer,[^4] and they escaped from the power of Aram. So the Israelites lived in their homes[^5] as they had before.

**6** But they did not turn away from the sins of the house of Jeroboam, which he had caused Israel to commit. They went on in them.[^6] And the Asherah pole[^7] also remained standing in Samaria.

**7** For the king of Aram had left Jehoahaz an army of no more than fifty horsemen, ten chariots and ten thousand foot soldiers, because he had destroyed them and made them like the dust at threshing.

**8** As for the rest of the acts of Jehoahaz, all that he did and his mighty deeds, are they not written in the book of the annals of the kings of Israel?

**9** Jehoahaz slept with his ancestors, and they buried him in Samaria. And his son Joash became king in his place.

**10** In the thirty-seventh year of Joash king of Judah, Jehoash son of Jehoahaz[^8] became king over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned sixteen years.

**11** He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh. He did not turn away from all the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which he had caused Israel to commit, but went on in them.

**12** As for the rest of the acts of Joash, all that he did and his mighty deeds, how he fought against Amaziah king of Judah,[^9] are they not written in the book of the annals of the kings of Israel?

**13** Joash slept with his ancestors, and Jeroboam[^10] sat on his throne. Joash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.

**14** Now Elisha had fallen sick with the illness from which he would die. Joash king of Israel went down to him and wept over him, saying, "My father, my father! The chariots of Israel and its horsemen!"[^11]

**15** Elisha said to him, "Take a bow and arrows." So he took a bow and arrows.

**16** Then he said to the king of Israel, "Put your hand on the bow." So he put his hand on it, and Elisha put his hands on the king's hands.

**17** He said, "Open the window toward the east." So he opened it. Elisha said, "Shoot!" And he shot. Elisha said, "Yahweh's arrow of victory, the arrow of victory over Aram! You will strike down Aram at Aphek until you have made an end of them."

**18** Then he said, "Take the arrows." So he took them. He said to the king of Israel, "Strike the ground."[^12] He struck it three times and stopped.

**19** The man of God was angry with him and said, "You should have struck five or six times. Then you would have struck down Aram until you had made an end of it. But now you will strike down Aram only three times."

**20** Elisha died, and they buried him. Now Moabite raiding bands used to come into the land at the coming of the year.[^13]

**21** Once, as some men were burying a man, they suddenly saw a raiding band, and they threw the man into Elisha's tomb. When the man went down and touched Elisha's bones, he came to life and stood up on his feet.

**22** Hazael king of Aram oppressed Israel all the days of Jehoahaz.

**23** But Yahweh was gracious to them and had compassion on them. He turned toward them because of his covenant with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. He was not willing to destroy them, and he has not cast them out of his presence until now.

**24** Hazael king of Aram died, and his son Ben-hadad became king in his place.

**25** Then Jehoash son of Jehoahaz took back from Ben-hadad son of Hazael the cities that he had taken in war from his father Jehoahaz. Three times Joash defeated him, and he recovered the cities of Israel.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "all the days".
[^3]: Literally "softened the face of Yahweh", that is, sought his favor.
[^4]: The deliverer is not named. Compare verse 25 and 14:25–27.
[^5]: Literally "in their tents".
[^6]: Literally "He walked in it". Also in verse 11.
[^7]: Hebrew *asherah*: a wooden pole or tree that was a symbol of the goddess Asherah. Ahab had set this one up (1 Kings 16:33).
[^8]: The king of Israel, called Joash in verse 9. The Hebrew uses both forms of his name, *Jehoash* and the shorter *Joash*, in verses 9–25. He is not the same as Joash (Jehoash) king of Judah, named earlier in this verse.
[^9]: See 14:8–14. Verses 12–13 are repeated in 14:15–16.
[^10]: Jeroboam II, son of Joash (14:23).
[^11]: Elisha spoke the same words of Elijah (2:12).
[^12]: Or "Shoot at the ground".
[^13]: That is, in spring, the season when kings went to war (2 Samuel 11:1).
