**1** When Yahweh[^1] was about to take Elijah up to heaven in a whirlwind, Elijah and Elisha were on their way from Gilgal.[^2]

**2** Elijah said to Elisha, "Please stay here, for Yahweh has sent me on to Bethel." But Elisha said, "As Yahweh lives, and as you yourself live,[^3] I will not leave you." So they went down to Bethel.

**3** The company of prophets[^4] who were at Bethel came out to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today Yahweh is going to take your master away from you?"[^5] He said, "Yes, I know. Be quiet."

**4** Elijah said to him, "Elisha, please stay here, for Yahweh has sent me on to Jericho." But he said, "As Yahweh lives, and as you yourself live, I will not leave you." So they came to Jericho.

**5** The company of prophets who were at Jericho came up to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today Yahweh is going to take your master away from you?" He said, "Yes, I know. Be quiet."

**6** Then Elijah said to him, "Please stay here, for Yahweh has sent me on to the Jordan." But he said, "As Yahweh lives, and as you yourself live, I will not leave you." So the two of them went on.

**7** Fifty men of the company of prophets went too, and they stood at a distance, facing them, while the two of them stood by the Jordan.

**8** Elijah took his cloak, rolled it up and struck the water. The water divided to one side and the other, and the two of them crossed over on dry ground.

**9** When they had crossed, Elijah said to Elisha, "Tell me what I can do for you before I am taken from you." Elisha said, "Please let me have a double share[^6] of your spirit."

**10** Elijah said, "You have asked for something hard. If you see me as I am taken from you, it will be yours. But if not, it will not."

**11** As they were walking along and talking, suddenly a chariot of fire and horses of fire appeared and separated the two of them, and Elijah went up to heaven in the whirlwind.

**12** Elisha saw it, and he cried out, "My father, my father! The chariots of Israel and its horsemen!"[^7] And he saw him no more. Then he took hold of his own clothes and tore them in two.

**13** He picked up Elijah's cloak, which had fallen from him, and went back and stood on the bank of the Jordan.

**14** He took Elijah's cloak that had fallen from him and struck the water. He said, "Where is Yahweh, the God of Elijah?" When he too had struck the water,[^8] it divided to one side and the other, and Elisha crossed over.

**15** The company of prophets from Jericho, who were facing him, saw him and said, "The spirit of Elijah rests on Elisha." They came to meet him and bowed down to the ground before him.

**16** They said to him, "Look, your servants have fifty strong men with them. Please let them go and search for your master. Perhaps the Spirit of Yahweh[^9] has picked him up and thrown him down on one of the mountains or in one of the valleys." He said, "Do not send them."

**17** But they kept pressing him until he was embarrassed to refuse,[^10] and he said, "Send them." So they sent fifty men, and they searched for three days but did not find him.

**18** They came back to Elisha while he was staying at Jericho, and he said to them, "Didn't I tell you not to go?"

**19** The men of the city said to Elisha, "Look, the city is well situated, as my lord can see, but the water is bad and the land is unfruitful."[^11]

**20** He said, "Bring me a new bowl and put salt in it." So they brought it to him.

**21** He went out to the spring and threw the salt into it, and said, "This is what Yahweh says: I have healed this water. Never again will it cause death or unfruitfulness."[^12]

**22** And the water has stayed healed to this day, just as Elisha had said.

**23** From there Elisha went up to Bethel. As he was going up the road, some young boys[^13] came out of the town and jeered at him. They said to him, "Go on up, baldhead! Go on up, baldhead!"[^14]

**24** He turned around, looked at them and cursed them in the name of Yahweh. Then two female bears came out of the woods and mauled forty-two of the youths.

**25** From there he went on to Mount Carmel, and from there he returned to Samaria.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Probably not the Gilgal near Jericho, since from it they "went down" to Bethel (verse 2). It may be a town in the hills north of Bethel.
[^3]: Literally "and as your soul lives". Hebrew *nephesh*, "life, self". Also in verses 4 and 6.
[^4]: Literally "the sons of the prophets": a community of prophets, perhaps disciples gathered around a leading prophet. Also in verses 5, 7 and 15.
[^5]: Literally "from over your head", that is, from his place as your master. Also in verse 5.
[^6]: Literally "a mouth of two", the share of the firstborn son, who inherited twice as much as each of his brothers (Deuteronomy 21:17).
[^7]: Or "The chariot of Israel and its horsemen!" Elisha speaks of Elijah as Israel's true defense, worth more than its army. The same words are said of Elisha in 13:14.
[^8]: Or "Where is Yahweh, the God of Elijah, he himself?" When he struck the water … The Hebrew words translated "he too" can be read with either sentence.
[^9]: Or "the wind of Yahweh". Hebrew *ruach* means both.
[^10]: Literally "until shame".
[^11]: Hebrew *meshakkelet*, "causing bereavement": causing miscarriage or the loss of young, whether of people, animals or crops. Also in verse 21.
[^12]: Literally "There will no longer be from there death and unfruitfulness."
[^13]: Hebrew *ne'arim*, used of anyone from a small child to a young man. In verse 24 they are called *yeladim*, "boys, youths".
[^14]: "Go up" may mock the report that Elijah had gone up to heaven.
