**1** The wife of one of the company of prophets[^1] cried out to Elisha, "Your servant my husband is dead, and you know that your servant feared the LORD.[^2] But now the creditor has come to take my two children as his slaves."

**2** Elisha said to her, "What shall I do for you? Tell me, what do you have in the house?" She said, "Your servant has nothing at all in the house except a small jar of oil."[^3]

**3** He said, "Go and borrow containers from outside, from all your neighbors, empty containers. Don't get just a few.

**4** Then go in and shut the door behind you and your sons. Pour oil into all these containers, and as each one is filled, set it aside."

**5** So she left him and shut the door behind her and her sons. They kept bringing containers to her, and she kept pouring.

**6** When the containers were full, she said to her son, "Bring me another container." But he said to her, "There are no more containers." Then the oil stopped flowing.

**7** She went and told the man of God, and he said, "Go, sell the oil and pay your debt. You and your sons can live on what is left."

**8** One day Elisha went over to Shunem. A wealthy woman[^4] lived there, and she urged him to stay for a meal. So whenever he passed by, he would stop there for a meal.

**9** She said to her husband, "Look, I am sure that this man who keeps passing our way is a holy man of God.

**10** Let us make a small walled room on the roof and put a bed, a table, a chair and a lamp there for him. Then whenever he comes to us, he can stay there."

**11** One day he came there, and he went into the upper room and lay down there.

**12** He said to Gehazi his servant,[^5] "Call this Shunammite woman." He called her, and she stood before him.

**13** Elisha said to Gehazi, "Tell her, 'Look, you have gone to all this trouble for us.[^6] What can be done for you? Can we speak on your behalf to the king or to the commander of the army?'" She said, "I live among my own people."[^7]

**14** Elisha said, "Then what can be done for her?" Gehazi said, "Well, she has no son, and her husband is old."

**15** Elisha said, "Call her." He called her, and she stood in the doorway.

**16** Elisha said, "At this season next year,[^8] you will be holding a son in your arms." She said, "No, my lord, man of God, do not lie to your servant!"

**17** But the woman became pregnant, and she gave birth to a son at that season the following year, as Elisha had told her.

**18** The child grew. One day he went out to his father, who was with the reapers.

**19** He said to his father, "My head! My head!" His father said to a servant, "Carry him to his mother."

**20** The servant picked him up and brought him to his mother. The boy sat on her lap until noon, and then he died.

**21** She went up and laid him on the bed of the man of God. Then she shut the door on him and went out.

**22** She called to her husband and said, "Please send me one of the servants and one of the donkeys, so that I can hurry to the man of God and come back."

**23** He said, "Why are you going to him today? It is not the New Moon or the Sabbath." She said, "All is well."[^9]

**24** She saddled the donkey and said to her servant, "Drive on fast. Do not slow down for me unless I tell you."

**25** So she set out and came to the man of God at Mount Carmel. When the man of God saw her in the distance, he said to Gehazi his servant, "Look, there is the Shunammite.

**26** Now run to meet her and ask her, 'Is all well with you? Is all well with your husband? Is all well with the child?'" She said, "All is well."

**27** But when she came to the man of God on the mountain, she took hold of his feet. Gehazi came near to push her away, but the man of God said, "Leave her alone, for she is in bitter distress,[^10] and the LORD has hidden it from me and has not told me."

**28** She said, "Did I ask my lord for a son? Didn't I say, 'Do not raise my hopes'?"

**29** Elisha said to Gehazi, "Tuck up your robe,[^11] take my staff in your hand and go. If you meet anyone, do not greet him, and if anyone greets you, do not answer him. Lay my staff on the boy's face."

**30** But the boy's mother said, "As the LORD lives, and as you yourself live,[^12] I will not leave you." So he got up and followed her.

**31** Gehazi went on ahead of them and laid the staff on the boy's face, but there was no sound and no sign of life.[^13] So he went back to meet Elisha and told him, "The boy has not woken up."

**32** When Elisha came into the house, there was the boy, dead, laid on his bed.

**33** He went in and shut the door on the two of them, and he prayed to the LORD.

**34** Then he got up on the bed and lay on the child. He put his mouth on his mouth, his eyes on his eyes and his hands on his hands,[^14] and as he stretched himself out over him,[^15] the child's body grew warm.

**35** Elisha got up again and walked once back and forth in the house. Then he went up and stretched himself out over him again. The boy sneezed seven times and opened his eyes.

**36** Elisha called Gehazi and said, "Call this Shunammite." He called her, and when she came to him, he said, "Pick up your son."

**37** She came in, fell at his feet and bowed down to the ground. Then she picked up her son and went out.

**38** Elisha returned to Gilgal, and there was a famine in the land. While the company of prophets was sitting before him,[^16] he said to his servant, "Put the large pot on the fire and cook stew for the company of prophets."

**39** One of them went out into the field to gather herbs and found a wild vine. From it he gathered wild gourds,[^17] as many as his garment could hold. He came back and cut them up into the pot of stew, though they did not know what they were.

**40** They poured it out for the men to eat. But as they were eating the stew, they cried out, "Man of God, there is death in the pot!" And they could not eat it.

**41** Elisha said, "Bring some flour." He threw it into the pot and said, "Pour it out for the people to eat." And there was nothing harmful in the pot.

**42** A man came from Baal-shalishah, bringing the man of God bread from the first of the harvest: twenty loaves of barley bread, and fresh ears of grain in his sack.[^18] Elisha said, "Give it to the people to eat."

**43** His attendant said, "How can I set this before a hundred men?" He said, "Give it to the people to eat, for this is what the LORD says: They will eat and have some left over."

**44** So he set it before them, and they ate and had some left over, as the LORD had said.

[^1]: Literally "One woman of the wives of the sons of the prophets". On "the company of prophets", see 2:3.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *asukh*, a small jar or flask, perhaps for anointing oil.
[^4]: Literally "a great woman": a woman of standing and wealth.
[^5]: Hebrew *na'ar*, "young man", often used of a personal servant. Also in verses 19, 22, 24, 25 and 38.
[^6]: Literally "you have trembled for us with all this trembling".
[^7]: That is, she is secure among her own family and needs nothing from the king.
[^8]: Literally "At this appointed time, at the time of life". The same phrase is used in Genesis 18:10, 14. Also in verse 17.
[^9]: Hebrew *shalom*, "peace, well-being". Also in verse 26.
[^10]: Literally "for her soul is bitter to her".
[^11]: Literally "Gird up your loins", that is, tuck the long robe into the belt, ready to travel fast.
[^12]: Literally "and as your soul lives".
[^13]: Literally "no voice and no attention".
[^14]: The Hebrew text is written "his hand" but read "his hands".
[^15]: Hebrew *gahar*, a rare verb meaning to bend or crouch over (1 Kings 18:42). Also in verse 35.
[^16]: That is, as his disciples.
[^17]: Probably colocynth, a wild gourd that looks like a small melon and is a strong, dangerous purgative.
[^18]: The meaning of the Hebrew word translated "sack", *tsiqlon*, is uncertain.
