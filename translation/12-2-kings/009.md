**1** Elisha the prophet called one of the company of prophets[^1] and said to him, "Tuck up your robe,[^2] take this flask of oil with you and go to Ramoth-gilead.

**2** When you get there, look for Jehu son of Jehoshaphat son of Nimshi. Go in, get him to leave his companions,[^3] and take him into an inner room.

**3** Then take the flask of oil, pour it on his head and say, 'This is what the LORD[^4] says: I anoint you king over Israel.' Then open the door and run. Do not wait."

**4** So the young man, the young prophet,[^5] went to Ramoth-gilead.

**5** When he arrived, the commanders of the army were sitting together. He said, "I have a message for you, commander." Jehu said, "For which one of us?" He said, "For you, commander."

**6** Jehu got up and went into the house, and the young man poured the oil on his head and said to him, "This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I anoint you king over the people of the LORD, over Israel.

**7** You are to strike down the house of Ahab your master, and I will avenge on Jezebel the blood of my servants the prophets and the blood of all the servants of the LORD.[^6]

**8** The whole house of Ahab will perish. I will cut off from Ahab every male[^7] in Israel, whether slave or free.[^8]

**9** I will make the house of Ahab like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah.[^9]

**10** And the dogs will eat Jezebel on the plot of ground at Jezreel, and no one will bury her."[^10] Then he opened the door and ran.

**11** When Jehu came out to his master's officers, someone said to him, "Is everything all right? Why did this madman come to you?" He said to them, "You know the man and the way he talks."

**12** They said, "That's a lie! Tell us." He said, "This is what he said to me: 'This is what the LORD says: I anoint you king over Israel.'"

**13** At once each of them took his cloak and spread it under him on the bare steps.[^11] They blew the ram's horn[^12] and said, "Jehu is king!"

**14** So Jehu son of Jehoshaphat son of Nimshi conspired against Joram. Now Joram, with all Israel, had been guarding Ramoth-gilead against Hazael king of Aram,

**15** but King Jehoram[^13] had gone back to Jezreel to recover from the wounds the Arameans had given him when he fought against Hazael king of Aram. Jehu said, "If you agree,[^14] let no one slip out of the city to go and tell the news in Jezreel."

**16** Then Jehu got into his chariot and went to Jezreel, because Joram was lying there. And Ahaziah king of Judah had come down to see Joram.

**17** The watchman standing on the tower in Jezreel saw Jehu's troops coming, and he said, "I see a company of troops." Jehoram said, "Take a horseman and send him to meet them, and let him ask, 'Is it peace?'"

**18** So the horseman went to meet him and said, "This is what the king says: Is it peace?" Jehu said, "What do you have to do with peace? Fall in behind me." The watchman reported, "The messenger has reached them, but he is not coming back."

**19** Then the king sent a second horseman. He came to them and said, "This is what the king says: Is it peace?" Jehu said, "What do you have to do with peace? Fall in behind me."

**20** The watchman reported, "He has reached them, but he is not coming back. And the driving is like the driving of Jehu son of Nimshi, for he drives like a madman."

**21** Jehoram said, "Harness the chariot." When his chariot was harnessed, Jehoram king of Israel and Ahaziah king of Judah set out, each in his own chariot. They went out to meet Jehu and found him at the plot of ground belonging to Naboth the Jezreelite.

**22** When Jehoram saw Jehu, he said, "Is it peace, Jehu?" He answered, "How can there be peace, as long as the prostitution[^15] and the many sorceries of your mother Jezebel go on?"

**23** Jehoram turned his chariot around[^16] and fled, calling out to Ahaziah, "Treachery, Ahaziah!"

**24** Then Jehu drew his bow with all his strength[^17] and shot Jehoram between the shoulders. The arrow came out through his heart, and he slumped down in his chariot.

**25** Jehu said to Bidkar his officer,[^18] "Pick him up and throw him onto the plot of ground belonging to Naboth the Jezreelite. Remember how you and I were riding side by side behind Ahab his father, when the LORD pronounced this oracle[^19] against him:

**26** 'As surely as I saw the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons yesterday, declares the LORD, I will repay you on this plot of ground, declares the LORD.'[^20] So now pick him up and throw him onto the plot of ground, as the LORD said."

**27** When Ahaziah king of Judah saw this, he fled by the road to Beth-haggan.[^21] Jehu chased after him and said, "Strike him down too!" And they struck him[^22] in his chariot on the ascent to Gur, which is near Ibleam. He fled to Megiddo and died there.

**28** His servants carried him in a chariot to Jerusalem and buried him in his tomb with his ancestors in the City of David.

**29** Ahaziah had become king over Judah in the eleventh year of Joram son of Ahab.[^23]

**30** Then Jehu came to Jezreel. When Jezebel heard about it, she painted her eyes,[^24] arranged her hair and looked down through the window.

**31** As Jehu came in through the gate, she said, "Is it peace, Zimri, murderer of your master?"[^25]

**32** He looked up at the window and said, "Who is on my side? Who?" Two or three officials[^26] looked down at him.

**33** He said, "Throw her down!"[^27] So they threw her down, and some of her blood spattered on the wall and on the horses, and he trampled her.[^28]

**34** Then he went in and ate and drank. He said, "See to this cursed woman and bury her, for she was a king's daughter."[^29]

**35** But when they went to bury her, they found nothing of her except the skull, the feet and the palms of her hands.

**36** They came back and told him, and he said, "This is the word of the LORD, which he spoke through his servant Elijah the Tishbite: 'On the plot of ground at Jezreel the dogs will eat the flesh of Jezebel.[^30]

**37** Jezebel's corpse will be like dung on the surface of the field on the plot of ground at Jezreel, so that no one will be able to say, "This is Jezebel."'"

[^1]: See 2:3.
[^2]: Literally "Gird up your loins", that is, tuck the long robe into the belt, ready to travel fast.
[^3]: Literally "his brothers".
[^4]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^5]: Literally "the young man, the young man the prophet".
[^6]: Literally "from the hand of Jezebel". On her killing of the LORD's prophets, see 1 Kings 18:4, 13.
[^7]: Literally "everyone who urinates against a wall", a crude expression for a male.
[^8]: The meaning of the Hebrew pair, literally "shut up and left", is uncertain. It probably means "everyone", whether slave or free, or whether under protection or not.
[^9]: Both royal houses were wiped out (1 Kings 15:27–30; 16:8–13).
[^10]: See Elijah's words in 1 Kings 21:23.
[^11]: Literally "on the bone of the steps". The meaning is uncertain: perhaps "on the bare steps" or "at the top of the steps".
[^12]: Hebrew *shofar*, a trumpet made from a ram's horn.
[^13]: Joram (verse 14). *Joram* is the shorter form of the name *Jehoram*. In verses 14–29 the Hebrew uses both forms of this king's name.
[^14]: Literally "If it is your soul", that is, your wish.
[^15]: An image for the worship of other gods.
[^16]: Literally "Jehoram turned his hands", that is, pulled the reins round.
[^17]: Literally "filled his hand with the bow".
[^18]: Hebrew *shalish*: see 7:2.
[^19]: Hebrew *massa*, "burden", often used of a prophet's message of judgment.
[^20]: See 1 Kings 21:19. Naboth's sons are not mentioned there.
[^21]: Or "the garden house".
[^22]: The Hebrew does not have "And they struck him". The words are supplied, as in the Syriac and the Vulgate. Literally "Him too, strike him in the chariot at the ascent of Gur, which is by Ibleam."
[^23]: 8:25 dates Ahaziah's accession to the twelfth year of Joram.
[^24]: Literally "she put her eyes in *pukh*", a dark eye paint (kohl).
[^25]: Zimri killed King Elah and reigned only seven days before he died (1 Kings 16:8–20).
[^26]: Hebrew *sarisim*, court officials. The word can also mean eunuchs.
[^27]: The Hebrew text is written "Throw him down" but read "Throw her down".
[^28]: That is, Jehu trampled her with his horses and chariot.
[^29]: Jezebel was the daughter of Ethbaal king of the Sidonians (1 Kings 16:31).
[^30]: 1 Kings 21:23.
