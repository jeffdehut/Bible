**1** When Athaliah, Ahaziah's mother, saw that her son was dead, she set out to destroy the whole royal family.[^1]

**2** But Jehosheba,[^2] King Joram's daughter and Ahaziah's sister, took Joash[^3] son of Ahaziah and stole him away from among the king's sons who were being put to death. She put him and his nurse in the bedroom.[^4] So they hid him from Athaliah, and he was not put to death.

**3** He remained with her, hidden in the house of the LORD,[^5] for six years, while Athaliah reigned over the land.

**4** In the seventh year Jehoiada sent for the commanders of hundreds of the Carites[^6] and of the guards,[^7] and had them come to him in the house of the LORD. He made a covenant with them and put them under oath in the house of the LORD. Then he showed them the king's son.

**5** He commanded them, "This is what you are to do. A third of you, those who come on duty on the Sabbath, are to keep guard over the king's palace,

**6** a third are to be at the Sur Gate, and a third at the gate behind the guards. So you will keep guard over the palace as a barrier.[^8]

**7** And your two divisions, all those who go off duty on the Sabbath, are to keep guard over the house of the LORD for the king.

**8** Surround the king on every side, each man with his weapons in his hand. Anyone who comes into the ranks is to be put to death. Stay with the king wherever he goes."[^9]

**9** The commanders of hundreds did everything Jehoiada the priest commanded. Each took his men, those coming on duty on the Sabbath together with those going off duty on the Sabbath, and they came to Jehoiada the priest.

**10** The priest gave the commanders of hundreds the spears and the shields[^10] that had belonged to King David, which were in the house of the LORD.

**11** The guards stood, each with his weapons in his hand, from the south side of the house to the north side,[^11] by the altar and the house, all around the king.

**12** Then Jehoiada brought out the king's son and put the crown on him and gave him the statutes.[^12] They made him king and anointed him, and they clapped their hands and said, "Long live the king!"

**13** When Athaliah heard the noise of the guards and the people,[^13] she came to the people at the house of the LORD.

**14** She looked, and there was the king, standing by the pillar, as the custom was. The commanders and the trumpeters were beside the king, and all the people of the land[^14] were rejoicing and blowing trumpets. Athaliah tore her clothes and cried out, "Treason! Treason!"

**15** Jehoiada the priest commanded the commanders of hundreds, who were in charge of the army, "Bring her out between the ranks,[^15] and put to death with the sword anyone who follows her." For the priest had said, "She must not be put to death in the house of the LORD."

**16** So they seized her,[^16] and she went by the way of the horses' entrance to the king's palace, and there she was put to death.

**17** Jehoiada made a covenant between the LORD and the king and the people, that they would be the LORD's people, and also between the king and the people.

**18** Then all the people of the land went to the temple of Baal and tore it down. They smashed its altars and its images to pieces, and they killed Mattan the priest of Baal in front of the altars. And the priest posted guards over the house of the LORD.

**19** He took the commanders of hundreds, the Carites, the guards and all the people of the land, and they brought the king down from the house of the LORD. They went by the way of the Gate of the Guards to the king's palace, and he took his seat on the royal throne.[^17]

**20** All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet, for they had put Athaliah to death with the sword at the king's palace.[^18]

[^1]: Literally "all the offspring of the kingdom".
[^2]: Called Jehoshabeath in 2 Chronicles 22:11.
[^3]: Also called Jehoash (12:1). *Joash* is a shorter form of the same name.
[^4]: Literally "the room of the beds", perhaps a storeroom for bedding.
[^5]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. "The house of the LORD" is the temple.
[^6]: Probably foreign soldiers in the royal guard, perhaps from Caria in southwest Asia Minor. Also in verse 19.
[^7]: Literally "runners", the royal guard. Also in verses 6, 11, 13 and 19.
[^8]: Hebrew *massach*, a word found only here. Its meaning is uncertain.
[^9]: Literally "in his going out and in his coming in".
[^10]: Hebrew *shelatim*, perhaps "shields" or "quivers".
[^11]: Literally "from the right shoulder of the house to the left shoulder of the house". Facing east, the right hand is south.
[^12]: Hebrew *edut*, traditionally "the testimony": the solemn terms of the covenant, perhaps in a written copy.
[^13]: Literally "the noise of the guards, the people".
[^14]: Hebrew *am ha-arets*, the landowning citizens of Judah, who often took part in choosing the king. Also in verses 18–20.
[^15]: Or "out of the temple area".
[^16]: Or "they made way for her". Literally "they put hands to her".
[^17]: Literally "the throne of the kings".
[^18]: In most English Bibles chapter 11 has one more verse, 11:21, which is 12:1 here.
