**1** The sons of Levi: Gershom,[^1] Kohath and Merari.

**2** These are the names of the sons of Gershom: Libni and Shimei.

**3** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.

**4** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These are the clans of the Levites by their ancestral families.[^2]

**5** Of Gershom: Libni his son, Jahath his son, Zimmah his son,

**6** Joah his son, Iddo his son, Zerah his son, Jeatherai his son.

**7** The descendants of Kohath: Amminadab his son,[^3] Korah his son, Assir his son,

**8** Elkanah his son, Ebiasaph his son, Assir his son,

**9** Tahath his son, Uriel his son, Uzziah his son, Shaul his son.

**10** The sons of Elkanah: Amasai and Ahimoth.

**11** As for Elkanah, the sons of Elkanah:[^4] Zophai his son, Nahath his son,

**12** Eliab his son, Jeroham his son, Elkanah his son.

**13** The sons of Samuel: the firstborn, and the second, Abijah.[^5]

**14** The descendants of Merari: Mahli, Libni his son, Shimei his son, Uzzah his son,

**15** Shimea his son, Haggiah his son, Asaiah his son.

**16** These are the men whom David put in charge of the music in the house of the LORD[^6] after the ark came to rest there.

**17** They served with music before the tabernacle, the tent of meeting,[^7] until Solomon built the house of the LORD in Jerusalem. They carried out their service according to the rule laid down for them.

**18** These are the men who served, with their sons. From the Kohathites: Heman the singer, son of Joel, son of Samuel,

**19** son of Elkanah, son of Jeroham, son of Eliel, son of Toah,

**20** son of Zuph,[^8] son of Elkanah, son of Mahath, son of Amasai,

**21** son of Elkanah, son of Joel, son of Azariah, son of Zephaniah,

**22** son of Tahath, son of Assir, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah,

**23** son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi, son of Israel.

**24** His kinsman[^9] Asaph stood at his right hand: Asaph son of Berechiah, son of Shimea,

**25** son of Michael, son of Baaseiah, son of Malchijah,

**26** son of Ethni, son of Zerah, son of Adaiah,

**27** son of Ethan, son of Zimmah, son of Shimei,

**28** son of Jahath, son of Gershom, son of Levi.

**29** On the left were their kinsmen the sons of Merari: Ethan son of Kishi, son of Abdi, son of Malluch,

**30** son of Hashabiah, son of Amaziah, son of Hilkiah,

**31** son of Amzi, son of Bani, son of Shemer,

**32** son of Mahli, son of Mushi, son of Merari, son of Levi.

**33** Their kinsmen the Levites were appointed for all the service of the tabernacle, the house of God.

**34** But Aaron and his sons made offerings[^10] on the altar of burnt offering and on the altar of incense. They did all the work of the most holy place[^11] and made atonement for Israel, in keeping with all that Moses the servant of God had commanded.

**35** These are the descendants of Aaron: Eleazar his son, Phinehas his son, Abishua his son,

**36** Bukki his son, Uzzi his son, Zerahiah his son,

**37** Meraioth his son, Amariah his son, Ahitub his son,

**38** Zadok his son, Ahimaaz his son.

**39** These were their dwelling places,[^12] by their encampments within their territory. To the descendants of Aaron, of the clan of the Kohathites, because the lot fell to them,

**40** they gave Hebron in the land of Judah, with the pasturelands[^13] around it.

**41** But the fields of the town and its villages they had given to Caleb son of Jephunneh.

**42** To the descendants of Aaron they gave the towns of refuge:[^14] Hebron, and Libnah with its pasturelands, Jattir, and Eshtemoa with its pasturelands,

**43** Hilez[^15] with its pasturelands, Debir with its pasturelands,

**44** Ashan with its pasturelands and Beth-shemesh with its pasturelands.[^16]

**45** From the tribe of Benjamin they gave Geba with its pasturelands, Alemeth with its pasturelands and Anathoth with its pasturelands. All their towns, clan by clan, came to thirteen towns.[^17]

**46** The rest of the Kohathites received by lot ten towns from the clan of the tribe, from the half-tribe, the half of Manasseh.[^18]

**47** The Gershonites, clan by clan, received thirteen towns from the tribe of Issachar, the tribe of Asher, the tribe of Naphtali and the tribe of Manasseh in Bashan.

**48** The Merarites, clan by clan, received by lot twelve towns from the tribe of Reuben, the tribe of Gad and the tribe of Zebulun.

**49** So the Israelites gave the Levites the towns with their pasturelands.

**50** By lot they gave these towns, which are mentioned by name, from the tribe of the people of Judah, the tribe of the people of Simeon and the tribe of the people of Benjamin.[^19]

**51** Some of the clans of the Kohathites had towns as their territory from the tribe of Ephraim.

**52** They gave them the towns of refuge:[^20] Shechem with its pasturelands in the hill country of Ephraim, Gezer with its pasturelands,

**53** Jokmeam[^21] with its pasturelands, Beth-horon with its pasturelands,

**54** Aijalon with its pasturelands and Gath-rimmon with its pasturelands.[^22]

**55** From the half-tribe of Manasseh they gave Aner with its pasturelands and Bileam with its pasturelands[^23] to the rest of the clans of the Kohathites.

**56** To the Gershonites they gave from the clan of the half-tribe of Manasseh Golan in Bashan with its pasturelands and Ashtaroth[^24] with its pasturelands;

**57** from the tribe of Issachar, Kedesh[^25] with its pasturelands, Daberath with its pasturelands,

**58** Ramoth with its pasturelands and Anem with its pasturelands;[^26]

**59** from the tribe of Asher, Mashal[^27] with its pasturelands, Abdon with its pasturelands,

**60** Hukok[^28] with its pasturelands and Rehob with its pasturelands;

**61** and from the tribe of Naphtali, Kedesh in Galilee with its pasturelands, Hammon with its pasturelands and Kiriathaim with its pasturelands.[^29]

**62** To the rest of the Merarites they gave from the tribe of Zebulun Rimmono with its pasturelands and Tabor with its pasturelands;[^30]

**63** and beyond the Jordan at Jericho, on the east side of the Jordan, from the tribe of Reuben, Bezer in the wilderness with its pasturelands, Jahzah[^31] with its pasturelands,

**64** Kedemoth with its pasturelands and Mephaath with its pasturelands;

**65** and from the tribe of Gad, Ramoth in Gilead with its pasturelands, Mahanaim with its pasturelands,

**66** Heshbon with its pasturelands and Jazer with its pasturelands.

[^1]: Also spelled Gershon (5:27). Verses 1–66 are 6:16–81 in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "by their fathers".
[^3]: In verse 23 and in Exodus 6:21 Korah is the son of Izhar.
[^4]: The Hebrew text is written "Elkanah his son, Elkanah" but read "Elkanah, the sons of Elkanah".
[^5]: The firstborn's name is missing in the Hebrew. Verse 18 and 1 Samuel 8:2 name him Joel. The Hebrew *va-shni*, "and the second", was once taken as a name, Vashni.
[^6]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^7]: Literally "the tabernacle of the tent of meeting". Hebrew *mishkan*, "dwelling place", and *ohel mo'ed*, the tent where the LORD met with his people (Exodus 27:21).
[^8]: The Hebrew text is written "Ziph" but read "Zuph". Compare 1 Samuel 1:1, where the name is Zuph and Toah (verse 19) is Tohu.
[^9]: Literally "brother", here a fellow Levite; also "kinsmen" in verses 29 and 33.
[^10]: Hebrew *maqtirim*, "burning to smoke", used of both sacrifices and incense.
[^11]: Literally "the holy of holies". Or "the most holy things".
[^12]: Verses 39–66 parallel the list of the Levites' towns in Joshua 21:10–39, in a different order and with some differences in the names.
[^13]: Hebrew *migrash*: the open land around a town, used for grazing. Also throughout the chapter.
[^14]: Hebrew plural. Of the towns listed here, only Hebron was a town of refuge (Joshua 20:7; 21:13).
[^15]: Most English Bibles have "Hilen". Joshua 21:15 has Holon.
[^16]: Joshua 21:16 has Ain instead of Ashan, and also lists Juttah.
[^17]: Only eleven towns are named in verses 42–45. Joshua 21:13–18 also lists Juttah and Gibeon, and has Almon for Alemeth.
[^18]: The Hebrew seems to have lost some words. Joshua 21:5 reads "from the clans of the tribe of Ephraim, the tribe of Dan and the half-tribe of Manasseh".
[^19]: Verses 49–50 correspond to Joshua 21:8–9, which introduce the list of towns given in verses 42–45.
[^20]: Hebrew plural. Of the towns listed here, only Shechem was a town of refuge (Joshua 21:21).
[^21]: Joshua 21:22 has Kibzaim.
[^22]: In Joshua 21:23–24 Aijalon and Gath-rimmon are towns of Dan, listed with Elteke and Gibbethon, which are not named here.
[^23]: Joshua 21:25 has Taanach and Gath-rimmon.
[^24]: Joshua 21:27 has Beeshterah.
[^25]: Joshua 21:28 has Kishion.
[^26]: Joshua 21:29 has Jarmuth and En-gannim.
[^27]: Joshua 21:30 has Mishal.
[^28]: Joshua 21:31 has Helkath.
[^29]: Joshua 21:32 has Hammoth-dor and Kartan.
[^30]: Joshua 21:34–35 has four towns: Jokneam, Kartah, Dimnah and Nahalal.
[^31]: Joshua 21:36 has Jahaz.
