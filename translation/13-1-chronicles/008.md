**1** Benjamin fathered Bela his firstborn,[^1] Ashbel the second, Aharah the third,

**2** Nohah the fourth and Rapha the fifth.

**3** Bela had sons: Addar, Gera, Abihud,

**4** Abishua, Naaman, Ahoah,

**5** Gera, Shephuphan and Huram.

**6** These are the sons of Ehud.[^2] They were heads of ancestral families[^3] of those who lived in Geba, and they were taken into exile to Manahath:

**7** Naaman, Ahijah and Gera. It was Gera who took them into exile,[^4] and he fathered Uzza and Ahihud.

**8** Shaharaim fathered children in the country of Moab after he had sent away his wives Hushim and Baara.[^5]

**9** By his wife Hodesh he fathered Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, Malcam,

**10** Jeuz, Sachia and Mirmah. These were his sons, heads of ancestral families.

**11** By Hushim he had fathered Abitub and Elpaal.

**12** The sons of Elpaal: Eber, Misham and Shemed, who built Ono and Lod with its towns,[^6]

**13** and Beriah and Shema. They were heads of ancestral families of those who lived in Aijalon, and they put the inhabitants of Gath to flight.

**14** Ahio,[^7] Shashak and Jeremoth,

**15** Zebadiah, Arad, Eder,

**16** Michael, Ishpah and Joha were the sons of Beriah.

**17** Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, Heber,

**18** Ishmerai, Izliah and Jobab were the sons of Elpaal.

**19** Jakim, Zichri, Zabdi,

**20** Elienai, Zillethai, Eliel,

**21** Adaiah, Beraiah and Shimrath were the sons of Shimei.[^8]

**22** Ishpan, Eber, Eliel,

**23** Abdon, Zichri, Hanan,

**24** Hananiah, Elam, Anthothijah,

**25** Iphdeiah and Penuel[^9] were the sons of Shashak.

**26** Shamsherai, Shehariah, Athaliah,

**27** Jaareshiah, Elijah and Zichri were the sons of Jeroham.

**28** These were heads of ancestral families, chiefs according to their generations. These lived in Jerusalem.

**29** In Gibeon lived the father of Gibeon,[^10] and his wife's name was Maacah.

**30** His firstborn son was Abdon, then Zur, Kish, Baal, Nadab,[^11]

**31** Gedor, Ahio and Zecher.[^12]

**32** Mikloth fathered Shimeah.[^13] They too lived in Jerusalem near their kinsmen, with their kinsmen.

**33** Ner fathered Kish,[^14] Kish fathered Saul, and Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchi-shua, Abinadab and Eshbaal.[^15]

**34** The son of Jonathan was Merib-baal,[^16] and Merib-baal fathered Micah.

**35** The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech, Tarea and Ahaz.

**36** Ahaz fathered Jehoaddah,[^17] and Jehoaddah fathered Alemeth, Azmaveth and Zimri. Zimri fathered Moza.

**37** Moza fathered Binea. Raphah[^18] was his son, Eleasah his son, Azel his son.

**38** Azel had six sons, and these were their names: Azrikam, Bocheru,[^19] Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah and Hanan. All these were the sons of Azel.

**39** The sons of his brother Eshek: Ulam his firstborn, Jeush the second and Eliphelet the third.

**40** The sons of Ulam were mighty warriors who drew the bow. They had many sons and grandsons, 150 in all. All these were Benjaminites.

[^1]: The sons of Benjamin are listed differently in 7:6, Genesis 46:21 and Numbers 26:38–40.
[^2]: The Hebrew does not say how Ehud was related to the names before. Compare Ehud son of Gera, a Benjaminite (Judges 3:15).
[^3]: Literally "heads of fathers", the leaders of extended families. Also in verses 10, 13 and 28.
[^4]: Or "Gera, that is, Heglam". The Hebrew *hu heglam*, "he took them into exile", can also be read as a name.
[^5]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "after his sending them away; Hushim and Baara his wives". The Hebrew does not say how Shaharaim was related to the names before.
[^6]: Literally "daughters", the smaller settlements around a town.
[^7]: Or "his brother".
[^8]: Perhaps Shema (verse 13).
[^9]: The Hebrew text is written "Peniel" but read "Penuel".
[^10]: Verses 29–38 are repeated, with small differences, in 9:35–44. There the father of Gibeon is named Jeiel.
[^11]: 9:36 has Ner between Baal and Nadab.
[^12]: 9:37 has Zechariah for Zecher, and adds Mikloth.
[^13]: 9:38 has Shimeam.
[^14]: In 1 Samuel 9:1 and 14:51 Kish is the son of Abiel, and Ner is the father of Abner.
[^15]: Called Ish-bosheth in 2 Samuel 2:8–4:12. *Eshbaal* means "man of Baal" or "Baal exists"; on the change of *baal* to *boshet*, "shame", see the note at 2 Samuel 2:8. 1 Samuel 14:49 lists Saul's sons as Jonathan, Ishvi and Malchi-shua.
[^16]: Called Mephibosheth in 2 Samuel 4:4 and 9:6.
[^17]: 9:42 has Jarah.
[^18]: 9:43 has Rephaiah.
[^19]: The Hebrew consonants can also be read "his firstborn", which would leave five names.
