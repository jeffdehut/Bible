**1** The divisions of the gatekeepers. Of the Korahites: Meshelemiah son of Kore, of the sons of Asaph.[^1]

**2** Meshelemiah had sons: Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,

**3** Elam the fifth, Jehohanan the sixth, Eliehoenai the seventh.

**4** Obed-edom had sons: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, Sachar the fourth, Nethanel the fifth,

**5** Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, Peullethai the eighth, for God had blessed him.

**6** Sons were also born to his son Shemaiah, who were leaders in their ancestral family, for they were capable men.

**7** The sons of Shemaiah: Othni, Rephael, Obed and Elzabad. His kinsmen[^2] Elihu and Semachiah were also capable men.

**8** All these were descendants of Obed-edom. They and their sons and kinsmen were capable men with strength for the service: sixty-two of Obed-edom.

**9** Meshelemiah had sons and kinsmen, capable men, eighteen.

**10** Hosah, of the descendants of Merari, had sons: Shimri the chief (although he was not the firstborn, his father made him chief),

**11** Hilkiah the second, Tebaliah the third, Zechariah the fourth. All the sons and kinsmen of Hosah were thirteen.

**12** To these divisions of the gatekeepers, by their chief men, were given duties, just as to their kinsmen, to minister in the house of Yahweh.[^3]

**13** They cast lots for each gate by their ancestral families, small and great alike.

**14** The lot for the east fell to Shelemiah.[^4] They cast lots also for his son Zechariah, a wise counselor, and his lot came out for the north.

**15** Obed-edom's lot was for the south, and his sons' lot was for the storehouse.[^5]

**16** Shuppim[^6] and Hosah's lot was for the west, at the Shallecheth Gate on the road that goes up. Guard was alongside guard.

**17** On the east there were six Levites, on the north four each day, on the south four each day, and at the storehouse two and two.

**18** At the Parbar[^7] on the west there were four at the road and two at the Parbar.

**19** These were the divisions of the gatekeepers among the descendants of Korah and the descendants of Merari.

**20** Of the Levites, Ahijah[^8] was in charge of the treasuries of the house of God and the treasuries of the dedicated gifts.

**21** The sons of Ladan, the descendants of the Gershonites belonging to Ladan, the heads of the ancestral families belonging to Ladan the Gershonite: Jehieli.

**22** The sons of Jehieli, Zetham and his brother Joel,[^9] were in charge of the treasuries of the house of Yahweh.

**23** Of the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites and the Uzzielites:

**24** Shebuel son of Gershom, son of Moses, was the chief officer in charge of the treasuries.

**25** His kinsmen through Eliezer: Rehabiah his son, Jeshaiah his son, Joram his son, Zichri his son and Shelomith[^10] his son.

**26** This Shelomoth[^11] and his kinsmen were in charge of all the treasuries of the dedicated gifts that King David, the heads of the ancestral families, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the commanders of the army had dedicated.

**27** They dedicated some of the plunder from the wars to maintain the house of Yahweh.

**28** And everything that Samuel the seer, Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner and Joab son of Zeruiah had dedicated, all the dedicated gifts, were in the care of Shelomith and his kinsmen.

**29** Of the Izharites, Chenaniah and his sons were assigned to outside duties for Israel, as officials and judges.

**30** Of the Hebronites, Hashabiah and his kinsmen, 1,700 capable men, were in charge of Israel west of the Jordan, for all the work of Yahweh and for the service of the king.

**31** Of the Hebronites, Jerijah[^12] was the chief of the Hebronites by their generations and ancestral families. In the fortieth year of David's reign a search was made, and capable men were found among them at Jazer in Gilead.

**32** His kinsmen, capable men, numbered 2,700, heads of ancestral families. King David put them in charge of the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh, for every matter of God and every matter of the king.

[^1]: Compare 9:19, where Kore is the son of Ebiasaph.
[^2]: Literally "brothers"; also "kinsmen" in verses 8–12, 25–26, 28 and 30–32.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Called Meshelemiah in verses 1–2 and 9.
[^5]: Hebrew *beit ha-asuppim*, "the house of the gatherings", a storehouse. Also in verse 17.
[^6]: Shuppim is not named in the lists of verses 1–11.
[^7]: Hebrew *parbar*, a word of uncertain meaning, perhaps a colonnade or court on the west side of the temple (compare 2 Kings 23:11).
[^8]: The Septuagint reads "their kinsmen" (Hebrew *acheihem*) instead of "Ahijah" (*achiyyah*).
[^9]: In 23:8 Jehiel, Zetham and Joel are brothers, the sons of Ladan.
[^10]: The Hebrew text is written "Shelomoth" but read "Shelomith".
[^11]: Called Shelomith in verses 25 and 28.
[^12]: Called Jeriah in 23:19 and 24:23.
