**1** The sons of Issachar: Tola, Puah,[^1] Jashub[^2] and Shimron, four.

**2** The sons of Tola: Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Ibsam and Shemuel, heads of their ancestral families,[^3] the descendants of Tola. They were mighty warriors in their generations, and in the days of David their number was 22,600.

**3** The son[^4] of Uzzi: Izrahiah. The sons of Izrahiah: Michael, Obadiah, Joel and Isshiah, five,[^5] all of them chiefs.

**4** Along with them, by their generations and their ancestral families, were 36,000 troops of the army ready for war, for they had many wives and sons.

**5** Their kinsmen[^6] of all the clans of Issachar were mighty warriors: 87,000 were listed in the genealogy, all together.

**6** Benjamin:[^7] Bela, Becher and Jediael, three.

**7** The sons of Bela: Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth and Iri, five, heads of ancestral families, mighty warriors. Listed in the genealogy were 22,034.

**8** The sons of Becher: Zemirah, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jeremoth, Abijah, Anathoth and Alemeth. All these were the sons of Becher.

**9** Listed in the genealogy by their generations, as heads of their ancestral families, were 20,200 mighty warriors.

**10** The son of Jediael: Bilhan. The sons of Bilhan: Jeush,[^8] Benjamin, Ehud, Chenaanah, Zethan, Tarshish and Ahishahar.

**11** All these were the sons of Jediael, by the heads of their ancestral families, mighty warriors, 17,200 ready to go out to war in the army.

**12** Shuppim and Huppim were the sons of Ir, and Hushim the sons of Aher.[^9]

**13** The sons of Naphtali: Jahziel, Guni, Jezer and Shallum,[^10] the descendants of Bilhah.

**14** The sons of Manasseh: Asriel, whom his Aramean concubine bore. She bore Machir the father of Gilead.[^11]

**15** Machir took a wife for Huppim and for Shuppim. The name of his sister was Maacah. The name of the second was Zelophehad, and Zelophehad had only daughters.[^12]

**16** Maacah, Machir's wife, bore a son and named him Peresh. His brother's name was Sheresh, and his sons were Ulam and Rakem.

**17** The son of Ulam: Bedan. These were the sons of Gilead son of Machir, son of Manasseh.

**18** His sister Hammolecheth[^13] bore Ishhod, Abiezer and Mahlah.

**19** The sons of Shemida were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi and Aniam.

**20** The descendants of Ephraim: Shuthelah, Bered his son, Tahath his son, Eleadah his son, Tahath his son,

**21** Zabad his son, Shuthelah his son, and Ezer and Elead. The men of Gath, who were born in the land, killed them, because they had gone down to take their livestock.[^14]

**22** Their father Ephraim mourned for many days, and his brothers came to comfort him.

**23** Then he slept with[^15] his wife, and she conceived and bore a son. He named him Beriah,[^16] because disaster had come on his house.

**24** His daughter was Sheerah, who built Lower and Upper Beth-horon, and Uzzen-sheerah.

**25** Rephah was his son, and Resheph, Telah his son, Tahan his son,

**26** Ladan his son, Ammihud his son, Elishama his son,

**27** Nun[^17] his son, Joshua his son.

**28** Their possessions and settlements were Bethel and its towns,[^18] Naaran to the east, Gezer and its towns to the west, and Shechem and its towns, as far as Ayyah and its towns.

**29** Along the borders of the Manassites were Beth-shean and its towns, Taanach and its towns, Megiddo and its towns, and Dor and its towns. In these lived the descendants of Joseph son of Israel.

**30** The sons of Asher:[^19] Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi and Beriah, and their sister Serah.

**31** The sons of Beriah: Heber and Malchiel, who was the father of Birzaith.[^20]

**32** Heber fathered Japhlet, Shomer and Hotham, and their sister Shua.

**33** The sons of Japhlet: Pasach, Bimhal and Ashvath. These were the sons of Japhlet.

**34** The sons of Shemer:[^21] Ahi,[^22] Rohgah, Hubbah[^23] and Aram.

**35** The sons[^24] of his brother Helem: Zophah, Imna, Shelesh and Amal.

**36** The sons of Zophah: Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, Imrah,

**37** Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran and Beera.

**38** The sons of Jether: Jephunneh, Pispa and Ara.

**39** The sons of Ulla: Arah, Hanniel and Rizia.

**40** All these were descendants of Asher, heads of ancestral families, choice men, mighty warriors, chiefs among the leaders. The number listed in the genealogy for service in war was 26,000 men.

[^1]: Genesis 46:13 and Numbers 26:23 have Puvah.
[^2]: The Hebrew text is written "Jashib" but read "Jashub", as in Numbers 26:24. Genesis 46:13 has Iob.
[^3]: Literally "the house of their fathers": an extended family descended from a common ancestor. Also in verses 4, 7, 9, 11 and 40.
[^4]: Literally "sons".
[^5]: Only four sons are named.
[^6]: Literally "brothers".
[^7]: The sons of Benjamin are listed differently in 8:1–2, Genesis 46:21 and Numbers 26:38–40.
[^8]: The Hebrew text is written "Jeish" but read "Jeush".
[^9]: *Aher* means "another". Genesis 46:23 names Hushim as the son of Dan, a tribe that is otherwise missing from these lists.
[^10]: Genesis 46:24 has Jahzeel and Shillem.
[^11]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "Asriel, whom she bore. His Aramean concubine bore Machir". Numbers 26:29–31 lists Asriel as a descendant of Gilead.
[^12]: The Hebrew is difficult and may have lost some words. In verse 16 Maacah is Machir's wife. On Zelophehad's daughters, see Numbers 26:33; 27:1–11.
[^13]: Or "the queen". *Hammolecheth* means "she who reigns".
[^14]: Or "when they came down to take their livestock", that is, the men of Gath.
[^15]: Literally "went in to".
[^16]: *Beriah* sounds like *be-ra'ah*, "in disaster".
[^17]: Hebrew *Non*, another form of Nun (Joshua 1:1).
[^18]: Literally "daughters", the smaller settlements around a town. Also in verse 29.
[^19]: Verses 30–31 match Genesis 46:17, with the addition of Birzaith.
[^20]: The Hebrew text is written "Birzoth" but read "Birzaith".
[^21]: Called Shomer in verse 32.
[^22]: Or "his brother".
[^23]: The Hebrew text is written "Jehubbah" but read "and Hubbah".
[^24]: Literally "son".
