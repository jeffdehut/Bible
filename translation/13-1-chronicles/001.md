**1** Adam, Seth, Enosh,[^1]

**2** Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared,

**3** Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech,

**4** Noah, Shem, Ham and Japheth.[^2]

**5** The sons of Japheth:[^3] Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech and Tiras.

**6** The sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, Diphath[^4] and Togarmah.

**7** The sons of Javan: Elishah, Tarshish, the Kittim and the Rodanim.[^5]

**8** The sons of Ham: Cush, Egypt,[^6] Put and Canaan.

**9** The sons of Cush: Seba, Havilah, Sabta,[^7] Raamah and Sabteca. The sons of Raamah: Sheba and Dedan.

**10** Cush fathered Nimrod. He was the first on earth to become a mighty warrior.

**11** Egypt fathered the Ludim, the Anamim, the Lehabim, the Naphtuhim,

**12** the Pathrusim, the Casluhim (from whom the Philistines came), and the Caphtorim.

**13** Canaan fathered Sidon, his firstborn, and Heth,[^8]

**14** and the Jebusites, the Amorites, the Girgashites,

**15** the Hivites, the Arkites, the Sinites,

**16** the Arvadites, the Zemarites and the Hamathites.

**17** The sons of Shem: Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, Aram, Uz, Hul, Gether and Meshech.[^9]

**18** Arpachshad fathered Shelah, and Shelah fathered Eber.

**19** Two sons were born to Eber. The name of one was Peleg,[^10] for in his days the earth was divided. His brother's name was Joktan.

**20** Joktan fathered Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah,

**21** Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah,

**22** Ebal,[^11] Abimael, Sheba,

**23** Ophir, Havilah and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan.

**24** Shem, Arpachshad, Shelah,[^12]

**25** Eber, Peleg, Reu,

**26** Serug, Nahor, Terah,

**27** Abram, that is, Abraham.[^13]

**28** The sons of Abraham: Isaac and Ishmael.

**29** These are their generations:[^14] Nebaioth, the firstborn of Ishmael, Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,

**30** Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema,

**31** Jetur, Naphish and Kedemah. These were the sons of Ishmael.

**32** The sons of Keturah, Abraham's concubine:[^15] she bore Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak and Shuah. The sons of Jokshan: Sheba and Dedan.

**33** The sons of Midian: Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida and Eldaah. All these were the descendants of Keturah.

**34** Abraham fathered Isaac. The sons of Isaac: Esau and Israel.

**35** The sons of Esau:[^16] Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam and Korah.

**36** The sons of Eliphaz: Teman, Omar, Zephi,[^17] Gatam, Kenaz, Timna and Amalek.[^18]

**37** The sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah and Mizzah.

**38** The sons of Seir: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer and Dishan.

**39** The sons of Lotan: Hori and Homam.[^19] Lotan's sister was Timna.

**40** The sons of Shobal: Alian, Manahath, Ebal, Shephi and Onam.[^20] The sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Anah.

**41** The son[^21] of Anah: Dishon. The sons of Dishon: Hamran, Eshban, Ithran and Cheran.[^22]

**42** The sons of Ezer: Bilhan, Zaavan and Jaakan.[^23] The sons of Dishon: Uz and Aran.[^24]

**43** These are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the Israelites: Bela son of Beor, and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

**44** When Bela died, Jobab son of Zerah from Bozrah became king in his place.

**45** When Jobab died, Husham from the land of the Temanites became king in his place.

**46** When Husham died, Hadad son of Bedad, who defeated Midian in the country of Moab, became king in his place. The name of his city was Avith.

**47** When Hadad died, Samlah from Masrekah became king in his place.

**48** When Samlah died, Shaul from Rehoboth on the River[^25] became king in his place.

**49** When Shaul died, Baal-hanan son of Achbor became king in his place.

**50** When Baal-hanan died, Hadad became king in his place. The name of his city was Pai, and his wife's name was Mehetabel daughter of Matred, daughter of Me-zahab.[^26]

**51** Then Hadad died. The chiefs[^27] of Edom were chief Timna, chief Alvah,[^28] chief Jetheth,

**52** chief Oholibamah, chief Elah, chief Pinon,

**53** chief Kenaz, chief Teman, chief Mibzar,

**54** chief Magdiel and chief Iram. These were the chiefs of Edom.

[^1]: Verses 1–4 list the line from Adam to Noah given in Genesis 5. The Hebrew gives only the names.
[^2]: Shem, Ham and Japheth were Noah's sons (Genesis 5:32; 10:1).
[^3]: Verses 5–23 follow the list of nations in Genesis 10:2–29, without some of its comments.
[^4]: Genesis 10:3 has Riphath. In Hebrew the letters *d* and *r* look very alike.
[^5]: Genesis 10:4 has Dodanim.
[^6]: Hebrew *Mitsrayim*, the usual Hebrew name for Egypt; also in verse 11.
[^7]: Genesis 10:7 has Sabtah.
[^8]: The ancestor of the Hittites. Verses 14–16 name peoples, in the Hebrew as singular names: "the Jebusite, the Amorite …".
[^9]: Genesis 10:23 lists Uz, Hul, Gether and Mash as the sons of Aram. Here the Hebrew has no "The sons of Aram", and has Meshech for Mash.
[^10]: *Peleg* means "division".
[^11]: Genesis 10:28 has Obal.
[^12]: Verses 24–27 list the line from Shem to Abraham given in Genesis 11:10–26.
[^13]: Genesis 17:5.
[^14]: Hebrew *toledot*; see Genesis 2:4. Verses 29–31 follow Genesis 25:13–16.
[^15]: Hebrew *pilegesh*, a wife of lower status than a principal wife. Verses 32–33 follow Genesis 25:1–4, where Keturah is called Abraham's wife.
[^16]: Verses 35–54 follow Genesis 36:4–5 and 10–43.
[^17]: Genesis 36:11 has Zepho.
[^18]: In Genesis 36:12 Timna is the concubine of Eliphaz and the mother of Amalek.
[^19]: Genesis 36:22 has Hemam.
[^20]: Genesis 36:23 has Alvan for Alian and Shepho for Shephi.
[^21]: Literally "sons".
[^22]: Genesis 36:26 has "The sons of Dishan: Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran and Cheran."
[^23]: Genesis 36:27 has Akan.
[^24]: Genesis 36:28 has Dishan.
[^25]: Perhaps the Euphrates, which is often called "the River".
[^26]: Genesis 36:39 has Hadar for Hadad and Pau for Pai.
[^27]: Hebrew *alluf*, the head of a clan. Some take the word to mean "clan" (Hebrew *eleph*). Also in verses 52–54. The list follows Genesis 36:40–43, which introduces it as the chiefs descended from Esau.
[^28]: The Hebrew text is written "Aliah" but read "Alvah", as in Genesis 36:40.
