**1** When David was old and full of days,[^1] he made his son Solomon king over Israel.

**2** He gathered all the leaders of Israel, and the priests and the Levites.

**3** The Levites thirty years old and over were counted, and their number, counted man by man,[^2] was thirty-eight thousand.

**4** Of these, twenty-four thousand were to oversee the work of the house of the LORD,[^3] six thousand were to be officials and judges,

**5** four thousand were to be gatekeepers, and four thousand were to praise the LORD with the instruments I have made for praise.[^4]

**6** David divided them into divisions according to the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath and Merari.

**7** Of the Gershonites: Ladan and Shimei.

**8** The sons of Ladan: Jehiel the chief, Zetham and Joel, three.

**9** The sons of Shimei: Shelomith,[^5] Haziel and Haran, three. These were the heads of the ancestral families of Ladan.

**10** The sons of Shimei:[^6] Jahath, Zina, Jeush and Beriah. These were the sons of Shimei, four.

**11** Jahath was the chief, and Zizah[^7] the second. Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons, so they became one ancestral family with one assignment.

**12** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel, four.

**13** The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. Aaron was set apart to consecrate the most holy things,[^8] he and his sons forever, to burn offerings before the LORD, to minister to him and to pronounce blessings in his name forever.

**14** As for Moses the man of God, his sons were counted as part of the tribe of Levi.

**15** The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.

**16** The sons of Gershom: Shebuel the chief.

**17** The sons of Eliezer: Rehabiah the chief. Eliezer had no other sons, but the sons of Rehabiah were very numerous.

**18** The sons of Izhar: Shelomith the chief.

**19** The sons of Hebron: Jeriah the chief, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third and Jekameam the fourth.

**20** The sons of Uzziel: Micah the chief and Isshiah the second.

**21** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish.

**22** Eleazar died without sons; he had only daughters. Their kinsmen,[^9] the sons of Kish, married them.

**23** The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder and Jeremoth, three.

**24** These were the descendants of Levi by their ancestral families, the heads of the families as they were registered, counted by name, man by man, who did the work for the service of the house of the LORD, from twenty years old and upward.[^10]

**25** For David said, "The LORD, the God of Israel, has given rest to his people, and he dwells in Jerusalem forever.

**26** So the Levites no longer need to carry the tabernacle or any of the articles used in its service."

**27** For by the last words of David the Levites were counted from twenty years old and upward.

**28** Their duty was to assist the descendants of Aaron in the service of the house of the LORD, in charge of the courts and the chambers, the purifying of all the holy things, and the work of the service of the house of God,

**29** and in charge of the bread set out in rows,[^11] the fine flour for the grain offering, the wafers made without yeast, what is baked on the griddle, what is mixed with oil,[^12] and all measures of quantity and size.

**30** They were to stand every morning to thank and praise the LORD, and likewise in the evening,

**31** and whenever burnt offerings were offered to the LORD on Sabbaths, new moons and appointed festivals, in the number required by the rule for them, regularly before the LORD.

**32** So they were to keep charge of[^13] the tent of meeting and of the holy place, and to assist their kinsmen the descendants of Aaron in the service of the house of the LORD.

[^1]: Literally "satisfied with days".
[^2]: Literally "by their skulls, by men", that is, head by head. Also in verse 24.
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: The Hebrew has "I have made", as if David were speaking.
[^5]: The Hebrew text is written "Shelomoth" but read "Shelomith".
[^6]: This Shimei is Ladan's brother (verse 7). The Shimei of verse 9 is counted among the families of Ladan.
[^7]: Called Zina in verse 10.
[^8]: Or "to be consecrated as most holy". Literally "to consecrate him (or it) holy of holies".
[^9]: Literally "brothers". Compare Numbers 36:6–12.
[^10]: Verse 3 counts the Levites from thirty years old. Compare Numbers 4:3; 8:24.
[^11]: See 9:32.
[^12]: Hebrew *murbekhet*, dough soaked or mixed with oil (Leviticus 6:14; 6:21 in most English Bibles).
[^13]: Literally "keep the charge of", that is, guard it and carry out its duties (Numbers 1:53).
