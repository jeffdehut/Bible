**1** King David said to the whole assembly, "My son Solomon, the one whom God has chosen, is young and inexperienced, and the work is great, for this palace[^1] is not for a human being but for Yahweh[^2] God.

**2** With all my resources I have provided for the house of my God the gold for the things of gold, the silver for the things of silver, the bronze for the things of bronze, the iron for the things of iron and the wood for the things of wood, as well as onyx stones[^3] and stones for setting, dark stones and stones of various colors,[^4] all kinds of precious stones, and marble in abundance.

**3** Moreover, in my delight in the house of my God, I have a treasure of my own of gold and silver, and I give it to the house of my God, over and above all that I have provided for the holy house:

**4** three thousand talents of gold, of the gold of Ophir,[^5] and seven thousand talents of refined silver,[^6] for overlaying the walls of the buildings,

**5** gold for the things of gold and silver for the things of silver, and for all the work to be done by craftsmen. Now who will offer willingly, consecrating himself[^7] today to Yahweh?"

**6** Then the heads of the ancestral families, the leaders of the tribes of Israel, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the officials in charge of the king's work offered willingly.

**7** They gave for the service of the house of God five thousand talents and ten thousand darics[^8] of gold, ten thousand talents of silver, eighteen thousand talents of bronze and a hundred thousand talents of iron.

**8** Anyone who had precious stones gave them to the treasury of the house of Yahweh, in the care of Jehiel the Gershonite.

**9** The people rejoiced over their willing offerings, for they had given willingly to Yahweh with a whole heart. King David also rejoiced greatly.

**10** Then David blessed Yahweh in the sight of the whole assembly. David said, "Blessed are you, Yahweh, the God of our father Israel, from everlasting to everlasting.

**11** Yours, Yahweh, are the greatness, the power, the glory, the victory[^9] and the majesty, for everything in heaven and on earth is yours. Yours, Yahweh, is the kingdom, and you are exalted as head over all.

**12** Riches and honor come from you, and you rule over all. In your hand are strength and might, and it is in your hand to make great and to give strength to all.

**13** Now, our God, we give thanks to you and praise your glorious name.

**14** But who am I, and who are my people, that we should be able to offer so willingly? For everything comes from you, and we have given you only what comes from your hand.

**15** For we are foreigners and strangers[^10] before you, as all our ancestors were. Our days on earth are like a shadow, and there is no hope.[^11]

**16** Yahweh our God, all this abundance that we have provided to build you a house for your holy name comes from your hand, and all of it is yours.

**17** I know, my God, that you test the heart and are pleased with uprightness. In the uprightness of my heart I have freely offered all these things, and now I have seen with joy your people who are present here offering freely to you.

**18** Yahweh, the God of Abraham, Isaac and Israel, our ancestors, keep this forever in the inclinations of the thoughts of your people's hearts, and direct their hearts toward you.

**19** Give my son Solomon a whole heart to keep your commands, your statutes[^12] and your decrees, to do everything, and to build the palace for which I have made preparations."

**20** Then David said to the whole assembly, "Now bless Yahweh your God." And the whole assembly blessed Yahweh, the God of their ancestors. They bowed low and prostrated themselves before Yahweh and the king.

**21** The next day they offered sacrifices to Yahweh and burnt offerings to Yahweh: a thousand bulls, a thousand rams and a thousand lambs, with their drink offerings, and sacrifices in abundance for all Israel.

**22** They ate and drank before Yahweh that day with great joy. They made Solomon son of David king a second time,[^13] and they anointed him before Yahweh as ruler,[^14] and Zadok as priest.

**23** Solomon sat on the throne of Yahweh as king in place of his father David. He prospered, and all Israel obeyed him.

**24** All the officials and the mighty warriors, and also all King David's sons, pledged their allegiance[^15] to King Solomon.

**25** Yahweh made Solomon exceedingly great in the sight of all Israel and gave him royal majesty such as no king over Israel had had before him.

**26** So David son of Jesse reigned over all Israel.

**27** He reigned over Israel for forty years. He reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.[^16]

**28** He died at a good old age, full of days, riches and honor, and his son Solomon reigned in his place.

**29** The acts of King David, from first to last, are written in the records of Samuel the seer, the records of Nathan the prophet and the records of Gad the seer,[^17]

**30** together with all his reign and his might, and the events that came upon him, upon Israel and upon all the kingdoms of the lands.[^18]

[^1]: Hebrew *birah*, "citadel, palace", here used of the temple. Also in verse 19.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *shoham*, a precious stone that cannot be identified with certainty.
[^4]: Hebrew *avnei phukh ve-riqmah*. *Phukh* is antimony, a dark mineral used as eye paint; compare Isaiah 54:11. *Riqmah* means "many-colored" or "embroidered work".
[^5]: A land famous for its gold (1 Kings 9:28).
[^6]: A talent was about 75 pounds (34 kg), so three thousand talents was about 110 tons (100 metric tons), and seven thousand talents about 260 tons (240 metric tons).
[^7]: Literally "to fill his hand", an expression used of ordaining priests (Exodus 28:41).
[^8]: Hebrew *adarkonim*, probably darics, Persian gold coins weighing about 1/4 ounce (8.4 grams).
[^9]: Or "the splendor". Hebrew *netsach*, "preeminence, lasting glory, victory".
[^10]: Hebrew *gerim* and *toshavim*, foreigners and temporary residents without the rights of citizens (Leviticus 25:23).
[^11]: Or "nothing that lasts".
[^12]: Hebrew *edot*, traditionally "testimonies": the solemn terms of the covenant.
[^13]: Compare 23:1. 1 Kings 1:32–40 tells how Solomon was first anointed.
[^14]: Hebrew *nagid*, a leader or ruler designated by God.
[^15]: Literally "gave the hand under".
[^16]: Compare 3:4; 2 Samuel 5:4–5; 1 Kings 2:11.
[^17]: Hebrew *divrei*, "words, matters, records", three times. Samuel is called *ro'eh* and Gad *chozeh*; both words mean "seer".
[^18]: Literally "and the times that passed over him and over Israel and over all the kingdoms of the lands".
