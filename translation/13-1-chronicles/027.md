**1** This is the list of the Israelites, the heads of ancestral families, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and their officers, who served the king in every matter concerning the divisions that came and went month by month throughout all the months of the year. Each division numbered twenty-four thousand.

**2** In charge of the first division, for the first month, was Jashobeam[^1] son of Zabdiel, and in his division were twenty-four thousand.

**3** He was a descendant of Perez, and chief of all the commanders of the army for the first month.

**4** In charge of the division of the second month was Dodai the Ahohite, with his division, and Mikloth was the chief officer.[^2] In his division were twenty-four thousand.

**5** The third commander of the army, for the third month, was Benaiah son of Jehoiada the priest, as chief.[^3] In his division were twenty-four thousand.

**6** This is the Benaiah who was a mighty warrior of the Thirty and in command of the Thirty.[^4] His son Ammizabad was over his division.

**7** The fourth, for the fourth month, was Asahel the brother of Joab, and his son Zebadiah after him.[^5] In his division were twenty-four thousand.

**8** The fifth, for the fifth month, was the commander Shamhuth the Izrahite.[^6] In his division were twenty-four thousand.

**9** The sixth, for the sixth month, was Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite. In his division were twenty-four thousand.

**10** The seventh, for the seventh month, was Helez the Pelonite, of the Ephraimites. In his division were twenty-four thousand.

**11** The eighth, for the eighth month, was Sibbecai the Hushathite, of the Zerahites. In his division were twenty-four thousand.

**12** The ninth, for the ninth month, was Abiezer the Anathothite, of the Benjaminites. In his division were twenty-four thousand.

**13** The tenth, for the tenth month, was Maharai the Netophathite, of the Zerahites. In his division were twenty-four thousand.

**14** The eleventh, for the eleventh month, was Benaiah the Pirathonite, of the Ephraimites. In his division were twenty-four thousand.

**15** The twelfth, for the twelfth month, was Heldai[^7] the Netophathite, of Othniel. In his division were twenty-four thousand.

**16** Over the tribes of Israel: the leader of the Reubenites was Eliezer son of Zichri; of the Simeonites, Shephatiah son of Maacah;

**17** of Levi, Hashabiah son of Kemuel; of Aaron, Zadok;

**18** of Judah, Elihu,[^8] one of David's brothers; of Issachar, Omri son of Michael;

**19** of Zebulun, Ishmaiah son of Obadiah; of Naphtali, Jerimoth son of Azriel;

**20** of the Ephraimites, Hoshea son of Azaziah; of the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joel son of Pedaiah;

**21** of the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead, Iddo son of Zechariah; of Benjamin, Jaasiel son of Abner;

**22** of Dan, Azarel son of Jeroham. These were the leaders of the tribes of Israel.[^9]

**23** David did not count those twenty years old and under, for the LORD[^10] had promised to make Israel as numerous as the stars of the sky.

**24** Joab son of Zeruiah began to count them, but he did not finish. Wrath came on Israel because of it,[^11] and the number was not entered in the records of King David.[^12]

**25** Azmaveth son of Adiel was in charge of the king's treasuries. Jonathan son of Uzziah was in charge of the storehouses in the country, in the towns, in the villages and in the towers.

**26** Ezri son of Chelub was in charge of the field workers who tilled the ground.

**27** Shimei the Ramathite was in charge of the vineyards, and Zabdi the Shiphmite was in charge of the produce of the vineyards for the wine cellars.

**28** Baal-hanan the Gederite was in charge of the olive and sycamore-fig trees in the Shephelah,[^13] and Joash was in charge of the stores of oil.

**29** Shirtai[^14] the Sharonite was in charge of the herds that grazed in Sharon, and Shaphat son of Adlai was in charge of the herds in the valleys.

**30** Obil the Ishmaelite was in charge of the camels, and Jehdeiah the Meronothite was in charge of the female donkeys.

**31** Jaziz the Hagrite was in charge of the flocks. All these were the officials in charge of King David's property.

**32** Jonathan, David's uncle,[^15] was a counselor, a man of understanding and a scribe. Jehiel son of Hachmoni was with the king's sons.[^16]

**33** Ahithophel was the king's counselor, and Hushai the Archite was the king's friend.[^17]

**34** After Ahithophel came Jehoiada son of Benaiah, and Abiathar. Joab was the commander of the king's army.

[^1]: Compare 11:11.
[^2]: The Hebrew is difficult: "and his division, and Mikloth the leader". Compare 11:12, Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite.
[^3]: Or "Benaiah son of Jehoiada, the chief priest". Hebrew *ha-kohen rosh*, "the priest, chief".
[^4]: See 11:22–25.
[^5]: Asahel is named first among the Thirty in 11:26. His death is told in 2 Samuel 2:18–23.
[^6]: Perhaps Shammoth the Harorite (11:27).
[^7]: Called Heled in 11:30.
[^8]: Perhaps Eliab, David's eldest brother (2:13).
[^9]: Gad and Asher are not listed.
[^10]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. Compare Genesis 15:5; 22:17.
[^11]: See chapter 21.
[^12]: Literally "in the number of the chronicles of King David". Hebrew *divrei ha-yamim*, "the matters of the days", the phrase that gives the books of Chronicles their Hebrew name.
[^13]: The low hills between the coastal plain and the hill country of Judah.
[^14]: The Hebrew text is written "Shitrai" but read "Shirtai". Most English Bibles have Shitrai.
[^15]: Hebrew *dod*, "uncle", or more generally "relative".
[^16]: Perhaps as their tutor.
[^17]: Perhaps a title: "the King's Friend", a close adviser (2 Samuel 15:37; 16:16).
