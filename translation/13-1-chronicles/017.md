**1** When David was settled in his house,[^1] David said to Nathan the prophet, "Look, I am living in a house of cedar, while the ark of the covenant of Yahweh[^2] is under curtains."[^3]

**2** Nathan said to David, "Do all that is in your heart, for God is with you."

**3** But that same night the word of God came to Nathan:

**4** "Go and tell my servant David, 'This is what Yahweh says: You are not the one who will build me the house to live in.[^4]

**5** For I have not lived in a house from the day I brought Israel up to this day. I have gone from tent to tent and from one tabernacle to another.[^5]

**6** Wherever I have moved about among all Israel, did I ever say a word to any of the judges[^6] of Israel whom I commanded to shepherd my people, "Why have you not built me a house of cedar?"'

**7** Now this is what you are to say to my servant David: 'This is what Yahweh of Armies[^7] says: I took you from the pasture, from following the sheep, to be ruler[^8] over my people Israel.

**8** I have been with you wherever you have gone, and I have cut off all your enemies from before you. I will make a name for you[^9] like the names of the great ones on the earth.

**9** I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and I will plant them so that they may live in their own place and be disturbed no more. Wicked people[^10] will not wear them down any more, as they did at the beginning,

**10** from the days when I appointed judges over my people Israel. I will subdue all your enemies. And I declare to you that Yahweh will build a house for you.[^11]

**11** When your days are complete and you go to be with your ancestors, I will raise up your offspring[^12] after you, one of your own sons, and I will establish his kingdom.

**12** He will build a house for me, and I will establish his throne forever.

**13** I will be his father, and he will be my son.[^13] I will not take my faithful love[^14] away from him, as I took it away from the one who was before you.[^15]

**14** I will establish him in my house and in my kingdom forever, and his throne will be established forever.'"[^16]

**15** Nathan spoke to David in accordance with all these words and all this vision.

**16** Then King David went in and sat before Yahweh and said, "Who am I, Yahweh God, and what is my house, that you have brought me this far?

**17** And yet this was too small a thing in your eyes, God. You have also spoken about your servant's house for a long time to come. You have looked on me as a man of high rank,[^17] Yahweh God.

**18** What more can David say to you about the honor you have given your servant? You know your servant.

**19** Yahweh, for the sake of your servant,[^18] and as your heart desired, you have done all this great thing, to make known all these great things.

**20** Yahweh, there is no one like you, and there is no God besides you, by all that we have heard with our ears.

**21** And who is like your people Israel, the one nation on earth that God went to redeem as a people for himself, to make a name for yourself by great and awesome things, by driving out nations from before your people whom you redeemed from Egypt?

**22** You made your people Israel your own people forever, and you, Yahweh, became their God.

**23** Now, Yahweh, let the word you have spoken concerning your servant and his house be confirmed forever, and do as you have said.

**24** Let it be confirmed, and let your name be great forever, so that people will say, 'Yahweh of Armies, the God of Israel, is God to Israel.' And the house of your servant David will be established before you.

**25** For you, my God, have revealed to your servant[^19] that you will build a house for him. That is why your servant has found the courage[^20] to pray before you.

**26** Now, Yahweh, you are God, and you have promised this good thing to your servant.

**27** Now you have been pleased to bless the house of your servant, so that it may continue forever before you. For you, Yahweh, have blessed it, and it will be blessed forever."

[^1]: Chapter 17 parallels 2 Samuel 7.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *yeri'ot*, the curtains of a tent. 2 Samuel 7:2 has "inside a tent".
[^4]: 2 Samuel 7:5 reads "Are you the one to build me a house to live in?"
[^5]: Literally "and from tabernacle"; the Hebrew seems to be shortened. 2 Samuel 7:6 reads "I have been moving about in a tent and a tabernacle."
[^6]: Hebrew *shophtei*, "judges of". 2 Samuel 7:7 has *shivtei*, "tribes of".
[^7]: Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*, traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verse 24.
[^8]: Hebrew *nagid*, a leader or ruler designated by God.
[^9]: Or "I have made a name for you". The verbs from here to verse 10 can be read as past or future.
[^10]: Literally "sons of injustice".
[^11]: That is, a dynasty, a line of kings from David's descendants. David had wanted to build a "house", a temple, for Yahweh (verse 4).
[^12]: Literally "your seed".
[^13]: Literally "I will be to him a father, and he will be to me a son." 2 Samuel 7:14 adds "When he does wrong, I will discipline him with the rod that men use, with blows inflicted by human beings."
[^14]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^15]: 2 Samuel 7:15 names him: "as I took it away from Saul, whom I removed from before you".
[^16]: 2 Samuel 7:16 reads "Your house and your kingdom will stand firm forever before you. Your throne will be established forever."
[^17]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "and you have seen me like the manner of the man of the going up". The parallel in 2 Samuel 7:19 is also difficult: "And this is instruction for humankind".
[^18]: 2 Samuel 7:21 has "your word".
[^19]: Literally "uncovered the ear of your servant".
[^20]: Literally "has found to pray". 2 Samuel 7:27 has "has found his heart to pray".
