**1** These were the sons of David who were born to him in Hebron.[^1] The firstborn was Amnon, by Ahinoam of Jezreel. The second was Daniel,[^2] by Abigail of Carmel.

**2** The third was Absalom son of Maacah, the daughter of Talmai king of Geshur. The fourth was Adonijah son of Haggith.

**3** The fifth was Shephatiah, by Abital. The sixth was Ithream, by his wife Eglah.

**4** Six were born to him in Hebron. He reigned there for seven years and six months, and in Jerusalem he reigned for thirty-three years.

**5** These were born to him in Jerusalem:[^3] Shimea,[^4] Shobab, Nathan and Solomon, four by Bathshua daughter of Ammiel,[^5]

**6** and Ibhar, Elishama,[^6] Eliphelet,

**7** Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,

**8** Elishama, Eliada and Eliphelet, nine.

**9** All these were David's sons, besides the sons of his concubines.[^7] And Tamar was their sister.

**10** Solomon's son was Rehoboam,[^8] Abijah[^9] his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son,

**11** Joram his son, Ahaziah his son, Joash his son,

**12** Amaziah his son, Azariah[^10] his son, Jotham his son,

**13** Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son,

**14** Amon his son, Josiah his son.

**15** The sons of Josiah: the firstborn Johanan, the second Jehoiakim, the third Zedekiah, the fourth Shallum.[^11]

**16** The descendants of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah[^12] his son, Zedekiah his son.[^13]

**17** The sons of Jeconiah the captive:[^14] Shealtiel his son,

**18** Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama and Nedabiah.

**19** The sons of Pedaiah: Zerubbabel[^15] and Shimei. The sons[^16] of Zerubbabel: Meshullam and Hananiah, and Shelomith was their sister,

**20** and Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah and Jushab-hesed, five.

**21** The sons of Hananiah: Pelatiah and Jeshaiah, the sons of Rephaiah, the sons of Arnan, the sons of Obadiah, the sons of Shecaniah.[^17]

**22** The son[^18] of Shecaniah: Shemaiah. The sons of Shemaiah: Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah and Shaphat, six.[^19]

**23** The sons of Neariah: Elioenai, Hizkiah and Azrikam, three.

**24** The sons of Elioenai: Hodaviah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Delaiah and Anani, seven.

[^1]: Verses 1–4 parallel 2 Samuel 3:2–5 and 5:5.
[^2]: 2 Samuel 3:3 has Chileab.
[^3]: Verses 5–8 parallel 2 Samuel 5:14–16; compare 14:4–7.
[^4]: 2 Samuel 5:14 has Shammua.
[^5]: Called Bathsheba daughter of Eliam in 2 Samuel 11:3.
[^6]: 2 Samuel 5:15 has Elishua. 2 Samuel 5:15–16 does not list the first Eliphelet or Nogah (verse 7); compare 14:5–6.
[^7]: Hebrew *pilagshim*, wives of lower status than principal wives.
[^8]: Verses 10–16 list the kings of Judah, as in 1 and 2 Kings.
[^9]: Called Abijam in 1 Kings 14:31–15:8.
[^10]: Also called Uzziah (2 Kings 15:13; 2 Chronicles 26:1).
[^11]: Shallum is Jehoahaz, who reigned after Josiah (2 Kings 23:30–31; Jeremiah 22:11).
[^12]: Also called Jehoiachin (2 Kings 24:6).
[^13]: In 2 Kings 24:17 the Zedekiah who reigned after Jehoiachin is his uncle, a son of Josiah (verse 15).
[^14]: Or "The sons of Jeconiah: Assir, Shealtiel his son". Hebrew *assir* means "captive".
[^15]: Elsewhere Zerubbabel is called the son of Shealtiel (Ezra 3:2; Haggai 1:1).
[^16]: Literally "son"; also in verses 21 and 23.
[^17]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. The Septuagint reads "Jeshaiah his son, Rephaiah his son, Arnan his son, Obadiah his son, Shecaniah his son".
[^18]: Literally "sons".
[^19]: Only five names are given.
