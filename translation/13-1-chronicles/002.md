**1** These are the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun,

**2** Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad and Asher.

**3** The sons of Judah:[^1] Er, Onan and Shelah. These three were born to him by the daughter of Shua, the Canaanite woman.[^2] Er, Judah's firstborn, was evil in the sight of Yahweh,[^3] so he put him to death.

**4** His daughter-in-law Tamar bore him Perez and Zerah. Judah had five sons in all.

**5** The sons of Perez: Hezron and Hamul.

**6** The sons of Zerah: Zimri,[^4] Ethan, Heman, Calcol and Dara,[^5] five in all.

**7** The son[^6] of Carmi: Achar,[^7] the troubler of Israel, who acted unfaithfully in the matter of what was devoted to destruction.[^8]

**8** The son of Ethan: Azariah.

**9** The sons born to Hezron: Jerahmeel, Ram and Chelubai.[^9]

**10** Ram fathered Amminadab,[^10] and Amminadab fathered Nahshon, the leader of the people of Judah.

**11** Nahshon fathered Salma,[^11] and Salma fathered Boaz.

**12** Boaz fathered Obed, and Obed fathered Jesse.

**13** Jesse fathered Eliab his firstborn, Abinadab the second, Shimea[^12] the third,

**14** Nethanel the fourth, Raddai the fifth,

**15** Ozem the sixth and David the seventh.[^13]

**16** Their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. The sons of Zeruiah: Abishai, Joab and Asahel, three.

**17** Abigail bore Amasa, and the father of Amasa was Jether the Ishmaelite.[^14]

**18** Caleb son of Hezron had children by his wife Azubah and by Jerioth.[^15] These were her sons: Jesher, Shobab and Ardon.

**19** When Azubah died, Caleb married Ephrath, and she bore him Hur.

**20** Hur fathered Uri, and Uri fathered Bezalel.[^16]

**21** Afterward Hezron slept with[^17] the daughter of Machir the father of Gilead.[^18] He married her when he was sixty years old, and she bore him Segub.

**22** Segub fathered Jair, who had twenty-three towns in the land of Gilead.

**23** But Geshur and Aram took Havvoth-jair[^19] from them, with Kenath and its villages,[^20] sixty towns. All these were the descendants of Machir the father of Gilead.

**24** After Hezron died in Caleb-ephrathah,[^21] Abijah, Hezron's wife, bore him Ashhur the father of Tekoa.

**25** The sons of Jerahmeel, Hezron's firstborn: Ram the firstborn, Bunah, Oren, Ozem and Ahijah.[^22]

**26** Jerahmeel had another wife, whose name was Atarah. She was the mother of Onam.

**27** The sons of Ram, Jerahmeel's firstborn: Maaz, Jamin and Eker.

**28** The sons of Onam: Shammai and Jada. The sons of Shammai: Nadab and Abishur.

**29** The name of Abishur's wife was Abihail, and she bore him Ahban and Molid.

**30** The sons of Nadab: Seled and Appaim. Seled died without sons.

**31** The son of Appaim: Ishi. The son of Ishi: Sheshan. The son of Sheshan: Ahlai.[^23]

**32** The sons of Jada, Shammai's brother: Jether and Jonathan. Jether died without sons.

**33** The sons of Jonathan: Peleth and Zaza. These were the descendants of Jerahmeel.

**34** Sheshan had no sons, only daughters. Sheshan had an Egyptian servant whose name was Jarha.

**35** Sheshan gave his daughter to his servant Jarha as his wife, and she bore him Attai.

**36** Attai fathered Nathan, and Nathan fathered Zabad.

**37** Zabad fathered Ephlal, and Ephlal fathered Obed.

**38** Obed fathered Jehu, and Jehu fathered Azariah.

**39** Azariah fathered Helez, and Helez fathered Eleasah.

**40** Eleasah fathered Sismai, and Sismai fathered Shallum.

**41** Shallum fathered Jekamiah, and Jekamiah fathered Elishama.

**42** The sons of Caleb, Jerahmeel's brother: Mesha his firstborn, who was the father of Ziph, and the sons of Mareshah the father of Hebron.[^24]

**43** The sons of Hebron: Korah, Tappuah, Rekem and Shema.

**44** Shema fathered Raham the father of Jorkeam, and Rekem fathered Shammai.

**45** The son of Shammai was Maon, and Maon was the father of Beth-zur.

**46** Ephah, Caleb's concubine,[^25] bore Haran, Moza and Gazez. Haran fathered Gazez.

**47** The sons of Jahdai:[^26] Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah and Shaaph.

**48** Maacah, Caleb's concubine, bore Sheber and Tirhanah.

**49** She also bore Shaaph the father of Madmannah, and Sheva the father of Machbenah and the father of Gibea. Caleb's daughter was Achsah.[^27]

**50** These were the descendants of Caleb. The sons[^28] of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah: Shobal the father of Kiriath-jearim,

**51** Salma the father of Bethlehem, and Hareph the father of Beth-gader.

**52** Shobal the father of Kiriath-jearim had sons: Haroeh, half of the Menuhoth.[^29]

**53** The clans of Kiriath-jearim: the Ithrites, the Puthites, the Shumathites and the Mishraites. From these came the Zorathites and the Eshtaolites.

**54** The descendants of Salma: Bethlehem, the Netophathites, Atroth-beth-joab, half of the Manahathites, the Zorites,

**55** and the clans of the scribes[^30] who lived at Jabez: the Tirathites, the Shimeathites and the Sucathites. These are the Kenites who came from Hammath, the father of the house of Rechab.[^31]

[^1]: Verses 3–4 summarize Genesis 38; compare Genesis 46:12 and Numbers 26:19–21.
[^2]: Or "by Bath-shua the Canaanite woman". Hebrew *bat-shua*, "daughter of Shua" (Genesis 38:2).
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Joshua 7:1 has Zabdi son of Zerah.
[^5]: 1 Kings 5:11 (4:31 in most English Bibles) names Ethan the Ezrahite, Heman, Calcol and Darda as famous wise men.
[^6]: Literally "sons"; also in verses 8 and 31. Carmi was a descendant of Zerah (Joshua 7:1).
[^7]: Called Achan in Joshua 7. *Achar* sounds like the Hebrew *okher*, "troubler", and like Achor, the valley where Achan was put to death (Joshua 7:24–26).
[^8]: Hebrew *cherem*, things set apart for Yahweh, to be destroyed or given wholly to him (Joshua 6:17–19; 7:1).
[^9]: Another form of the name Caleb (verses 18 and 42).
[^10]: Verses 10–12 give the line found in Ruth 4:19–22. Nahshon was the leader of Judah in the wilderness (Numbers 1:7; 2:3).
[^11]: Ruth 4:20–21 has Salmah and Salmon.
[^12]: Called Shammah in 1 Samuel 16:9 and 17:13.
[^13]: 1 Samuel 16:10–11 and 17:12 say that Jesse had eight sons.
[^14]: 2 Samuel 17:25 calls him Ithra the Israelite.
[^15]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "fathered Azubah, a wife, and Jerioth". Jerioth may be a second wife, or another name for Azubah.
[^16]: The craftsman who made the tabernacle (Exodus 31:2–5).
[^17]: Literally "went in to".
[^18]: That is, the ancestor of the Gileadites (Numbers 26:29). In these lists *father* often means the founder or leading man of a clan or town, as in "the father of Tekoa" (verse 24).
[^19]: *Havvoth-jair* means "the tent villages of Jair" (Numbers 32:41; Deuteronomy 3:14).
[^20]: Literally "its daughters", the smaller settlements around a town.
[^21]: The Hebrew is difficult. The Septuagint reads "After Hezron died, Caleb went in to Ephrathah".
[^22]: Or "Ozem, by Ahijah". Verse 26 speaks of Jerahmeel's "other wife".
[^23]: Verse 34 says that Sheshan had no sons, only daughters.
[^24]: The Hebrew seems to be incomplete. Mareshah, Ziph and Hebron are also the names of towns in Judah.
[^25]: Hebrew *pilegesh*, a wife of lower status than a principal wife. Also in verse 48.
[^26]: The Hebrew does not say how Jahdai is related to the others in the list.
[^27]: Joshua 15:16–17 names Achsah as the daughter of Caleb son of Jephunneh.
[^28]: Literally "son". The Hebrew can also be read "These were the descendants of Caleb son of Hur", but in verse 19 Hur is Caleb's son.
[^29]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. *Haroeh* means "the seer"; compare Reaiah son of Shobal in 4:2. The Menuhoth may be the Manahathites of verse 54.
[^30]: Or "the Sopherites", a clan name. Hebrew *sopherim*, "scribes".
[^31]: That is, the ancestor of the Rechabites (Jeremiah 35).
