**1** David and the commanders of the army[^1] also set apart for the service some of the sons of Asaph, Heman and Jeduthun, who prophesied[^2] with lyres, harps and cymbals. This is the list of the men who did the work of their service:

**2** Of the sons of Asaph: Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah and Asarelah, the sons of Asaph, under the direction of Asaph, who prophesied under the direction of the king.

**3** Of Jeduthun, the sons of Jeduthun: Gedaliah, Zeri,[^3] Jeshaiah, Hashabiah and Mattithiah, six,[^4] under the direction of their father Jeduthun, who prophesied with the lyre in giving thanks and praise to Yahweh.[^5]

**4** Of Heman, the sons of Heman: Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir and Mahazioth.[^6]

**5** All these were sons of Heman, the king's seer, according to the promises of God to exalt him.[^7] God gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.

**6** All these were under the direction of their father for the music in the house of Yahweh, with cymbals, harps and lyres, for the service of the house of God. Asaph, Jeduthun and Heman were under the direction of the king.

**7** Their number, with their kinsmen who were trained in singing to Yahweh, all of them skilled, was 288.

**8** They cast lots for their duties, small and great alike, teacher and student alike.

**9** The first lot fell to Asaph, to Joseph;[^8] the second to Gedaliah, him and his kinsmen and his sons, 12;

**10** the third to Zaccur, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**11** the fourth to Izri, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**12** the fifth to Nethaniah, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**13** the sixth to Bukkiah, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**14** the seventh to Jesarelah,[^9] his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**15** the eighth to Jeshaiah, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**16** the ninth to Mattaniah, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**17** the tenth to Shimei, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**18** the eleventh to Azarel,[^10] his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**19** the twelfth to Hashabiah, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**20** the thirteenth to Shubael,[^11] his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**21** the fourteenth to Mattithiah, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**22** the fifteenth to Jeremoth,[^12] his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**23** the sixteenth to Hananiah, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**24** the seventeenth to Joshbekashah, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**25** the eighteenth to Hanani, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**26** the nineteenth to Mallothi, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**27** the twentieth to Eliathah, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**28** the twenty-first to Hothir, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**29** the twenty-second to Giddalti, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**30** the twenty-third to Mahazioth, his sons and his kinsmen, 12;

**31** the twenty-fourth to Romamti-ezer, his sons and his kinsmen, 12.

[^1]: Or "the leaders of the service". Hebrew *tsava*, "army, host", is also used of the service of the Levites (Numbers 4:3).
[^2]: The Hebrew text is written "the prophets" but read "who prophesied".
[^3]: Called Izri in verse 11.
[^4]: Only five are named. Shimei (verse 17) may be the sixth.
[^5]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^6]: Several of these names are also ordinary Hebrew words. Read together, the names from Hananiah to Mahazioth resemble lines of a prayer: "Be gracious to me, Yah, be gracious to me; you are my God; I have magnified and exalted help …".
[^7]: Literally "to lift up the horn", a sign of strength and honor. Or "to sound the horn".
[^8]: The Hebrew does not give a number for Joseph. With 12 for each of the twenty-four lots, the total is the 288 of verse 7.
[^9]: Called Asarelah in verse 2.
[^10]: Called Uzziel in verse 4.
[^11]: Called Shebuel in verse 4.
[^12]: Called Jerimoth in verse 4.
