**1** They brought in the ark of God and set it inside the tent that David had pitched for it.[^1] Then they offered burnt offerings and fellowship offerings[^2] before God.

**2** When David had finished offering the burnt offerings and the fellowship offerings, he blessed the people in the name of Yahweh.[^3]

**3** He distributed to every Israelite, both men and women, to each one a loaf of bread, a date cake[^4] and a raisin cake.

**4** He appointed some of the Levites to minister before the ark of Yahweh, to commemorate,[^5] to thank and to praise Yahweh, the God of Israel.

**5** Asaph was the chief, and second to him Zechariah, then Jeiel,[^6] Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom and Jeiel, with harps and lyres, while Asaph sounded the cymbals.

**6** Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests were to blow the trumpets regularly before the ark of the covenant of God.

**7** That was the day when David first appointed Asaph and his kinsmen to give thanks to Yahweh:[^7]

**8** Give thanks to Yahweh; call on his name.[^8] Make known his deeds among the peoples.

**9** Sing to him, sing praises to him; tell of all his wonders.

**10** Glory in his holy name; let the hearts of those who seek Yahweh rejoice.

**11** Seek Yahweh and his strength; seek his face[^9] always.

**12** Remember the wonders he has done, his miracles and the judgments he has spoken,[^10]

**13** you offspring of Israel his servant,[^11] children of Jacob, his chosen ones.

**14** He is Yahweh our God; his judgments are in all the earth.

**15** Remember his covenant forever,[^12] the word he commanded for a thousand generations,

**16** the covenant he made with Abraham, his oath to Isaac.

**17** He confirmed it to Jacob as a decree, to Israel as an everlasting covenant,

**18** saying, "To you I will give the land of Canaan as the portion of your inheritance."[^13]

**19** When you were few in number,[^14] very few, and foreigners in the land,

**20** they wandered from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another people.

**21** He allowed no one to oppress them; he rebuked kings on their account:

**22** "Do not touch my anointed ones;[^15] do not harm my prophets."

**23** Sing to Yahweh, all the earth;[^16] proclaim his salvation day after day.

**24** Declare his glory among the nations, his wonders among all the peoples.

**25** For Yahweh is great and greatly to be praised; he is to be feared above all gods.

**26** For all the gods of the peoples are idols,[^17] but Yahweh made the heavens.

**27** Splendor and majesty are before him; strength and joy are in his place.[^18]

**28** Ascribe to Yahweh, you families of the peoples, ascribe to Yahweh glory and strength.

**29** Ascribe to Yahweh the glory due his name.[^19] Bring an offering and come before him.[^20] Worship Yahweh in the splendor of holiness.[^21]

**30** Tremble before him, all the earth. The world is firmly established; it cannot be moved.

**31** Let the heavens rejoice and the earth be glad; let them say among the nations, "Yahweh reigns!"[^22]

**32** Let the sea roar, and all that fills it; let the fields exult, and everything in them.

**33** Then the trees of the forest will sing for joy before Yahweh, for he comes to judge the earth.

**34** Give thanks to Yahweh, for he is good; his faithful love[^23] endures forever.

**35** Say, "Save us, God of our salvation![^24] Gather us and rescue us from the nations, so that we may give thanks to your holy name and glory in your praise."

**36** Blessed be Yahweh, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Then all the people said, "Amen!" and praised Yahweh.[^25]

**37** So David left Asaph and his kinsmen there before the ark of the covenant of Yahweh, to minister before the ark regularly, as each day required.

**38** He also left Obed-edom and their kinsmen, sixty-eight of them. Obed-edom son of Jeduthun and Hosah were gatekeepers.

**39** He left Zadok the priest and his kinsmen the priests before the tabernacle of Yahweh at the high place[^26] in Gibeon,

**40** to offer burnt offerings to Yahweh on the altar of burnt offering regularly, morning and evening, according to all that is written in the law of Yahweh, which he commanded Israel.

**41** With them were Heman and Jeduthun and the rest of those chosen and designated by name to give thanks to Yahweh, "for his faithful love endures forever."

**42** Heman and Jeduthun had with them trumpets and cymbals for those who were to play, and instruments for the songs of God.[^27] The sons of Jeduthun were at the gate.

**43** Then all the people went away, each to his own home, and David returned to bless his household.[^28]

[^1]: Verses 1–3 parallel 2 Samuel 6:17–19.
[^2]: Hebrew *shelamim*, traditionally "peace offerings". Most of the meat of these sacrifices was eaten by the worshipers (Leviticus 3; 7:11–21). Also in verse 2.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. 2 Samuel 6:18 has "Yahweh of Armies".
[^4]: Hebrew *eshpar*, a rare word of uncertain meaning: perhaps a cake of dates, or a portion of meat.
[^5]: Or "to invoke". Hebrew *lehazkir*, "to cause to remember".
[^6]: Perhaps the Jaaziel or Aziel of 15:18 and 15:20.
[^7]: Literally "On that day, then David first gave to give thanks to Yahweh by the hand of Asaph and his brothers."
[^8]: Verses 8–22 match Psalm 105:1–15, with small differences.
[^9]: Or "his presence".
[^10]: Literally "the judgments of his mouth".
[^11]: Psalm 105:6 has "Abraham his servant".
[^12]: Psalm 105:8 has "He remembers his covenant forever".
[^13]: Literally "the cord of your inheritance", the measuring line used to mark out a share of land.
[^14]: Psalm 105:12 has "When they were few in number".
[^15]: Hebrew *meshichai*, "my anointed ones", from *mashiach*, the word behind "Messiah". Here it refers to the patriarchs.
[^16]: Verses 23–33 match most of Psalm 96, with some differences in wording and order.
[^17]: Hebrew *elilim*, "worthless things", which sounds like *elohim*, "gods".
[^18]: Psalm 96:6 has "strength and beauty are in his sanctuary".
[^19]: Literally "the glory of his name".
[^20]: Psalm 96:8 has "come into his courts".
[^21]: Or "in holy attire".
[^22]: Or "Yahweh has become king!" In Psalm 96:10 this line comes earlier.
[^23]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Also in verse 41. Verses 34–36 match Psalm 106:1 and 47–48.
[^24]: Psalm 106:47 has "Save us, Yahweh our God".
[^25]: Psalm 106:48 has "Let all the people say, 'Amen!' Praise Yah!" (Hebrew *hallelu-yah*). Here the Hebrew is *vehallel la-YHWH*, "and they praised Yahweh".
[^26]: Hebrew *bamah*, a place of worship, often on a hill.
[^27]: Or "instruments for sacred song".
[^28]: Compare 2 Samuel 6:19–20.
