**1** The sons[^1] of Judah: Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur and Shobal.

**2** Reaiah son of Shobal fathered Jahath, and Jahath fathered Ahumai and Lahad. These were the clans of the Zorathites.

**3** These were the descendants of the father of Etam:[^2] Jezreel, Ishma and Idbash. The name of their sister was Hazzelelponi.

**4** Penuel was the father of Gedor, and Ezer the father of Hushah. These were the sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah, the father of Bethlehem.[^3]

**5** Ashhur the father of Tekoa had two wives, Helah and Naarah.

**6** Naarah bore him Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni and Haahashtari. These were the sons of Naarah.

**7** The sons of Helah: Zereth, Zohar[^4] and Ethnan.

**8** Koz fathered Anub and Zobebah, and the clans of Aharhel son of Harum.

**9** Jabez was more honored than his brothers. His mother had named him Jabez,[^5] saying, "Because I bore him in pain."

**10** Jabez called on the God of Israel, saying, "Oh, that you would bless me[^6] and enlarge my territory! May your hand be with me, and keep me from harm, so that it may not bring me pain."[^7] And God granted what he asked.

**11** Chelub, Shuhah's brother, fathered Mehir, who was the father of Eshton.

**12** Eshton fathered Beth-rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah the father of Ir-nahash. These were the men of Recah.

**13** The sons of Kenaz: Othniel[^8] and Seraiah. The son[^9] of Othniel: Hathath.

**14** Meonothai fathered Ophrah, and Seraiah fathered Joab the father of Ge-harashim,[^10] so called because they were craftsmen.

**15** The sons of Caleb son of Jephunneh: Iru, Elah and Naam. The son of Elah: Kenaz.[^11]

**16** The sons of Jehallelel: Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria and Asarel.

**17** The sons[^12] of Ezrah: Jether, Mered, Epher and Jalon. She conceived[^13] Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah the father of Eshtemoa.

**18** His Judean wife[^14] bore Jered the father of Gedor, Heber the father of Soco, and Jekuthiel the father of Zanoah. These were the sons of Bithiah, Pharaoh's daughter, whom Mered had married.

**19** The sons of the wife of Hodiah, the sister of Naham: the father of Keilah the Garmite, and Eshtemoa the Maacathite.

**20** The sons of Shimon: Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan and Tilon.[^15] The sons of Ishi: Zoheth and Ben-zoheth.[^16]

**21** The sons of Shelah son of Judah: Er the father of Lecah, Laadah the father of Mareshah, and the clans of the house of linen workers[^17] at Beth-ashbea,

**22** and Jokim, the men of Cozeba, and Joash and Saraph, who ruled in Moab, and Jashubi-lehem.[^18] These records are ancient.[^19]

**23** They were the potters and the inhabitants of Netaim and Gederah.[^20] They lived there with the king, in his service.

**24** The sons of Simeon: Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah and Shaul.[^21]

**25** Shallum his son, Mibsam his son, Mishma his son.

**26** The descendants of Mishma: Hammuel his son, Zaccur his son, Shimei his son.

**27** Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters, but his brothers did not have many children, and their whole clan did not become as numerous as the people of Judah.

**28** They lived in Beersheba,[^22] Moladah, Hazar-shual,

**29** Bilhah, Ezem, Tolad,

**30** Bethuel, Hormah, Ziklag,

**31** Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-biri and Shaaraim.[^23] These were their towns until David became king.

**32** Their villages were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen and Ashan, five towns,[^24]

**33** with all their villages around these towns as far as Baal.[^25] These were their settlements, and they kept their own genealogical record.

**34** Meshobab, Jamlech, Joshah son of Amaziah,

**35** Joel, Jehu son of Joshibiah, son of Seraiah, son of Asiel,

**36** Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, Benaiah,

**37** and Ziza son of Shiphi, son of Allon, son of Jedaiah, son of Shimri, son of Shemaiah.

**38** These, listed by name, were leaders in their clans, and their ancestral families[^26] increased greatly.

**39** They went to the entrance of Gedor,[^27] to the east side of the valley, to look for pasture for their flocks.

**40** They found rich, good pasture, and the land was broad,[^28] quiet and peaceful, for those who had lived there before were descended from Ham.

**41** These men whose names are written came in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah. They attacked their tents and the Meunites[^29] who were found there, and they devoted them to destruction,[^30] as it is to this day. Then they settled in their place, because there was pasture there for their flocks.

**42** Some of them, five hundred men of the Simeonites, went to the hill country of Seir, led by Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah and Uzziel, the sons of Ishi.

**43** They struck down the rest of the Amalekites who had escaped, and they have lived there to this day.

[^1]: That is, descendants. Perez was Judah's son and Hezron his grandson (2:4–5); for Hur and Shobal see 2:19 and 2:50.
[^2]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "And these, the father of Etam".
[^3]: In 2:51 Salma, a son of Hur, is called the father of Bethlehem. On *father* in these lists, see 2:21.
[^4]: The Hebrew text is written "Izhar" but read "and Zohar".
[^5]: *Jabez* (Hebrew *Ya'bets*) sounds like *otsev*, "pain", with two letters reversed.
[^6]: Literally "If you will indeed bless me", the beginning of an earnest wish. The sentence has no conclusion.
[^7]: Or "so that I may not suffer pain". Hebrew *otsbi*, "my pain", plays again on the name Jabez.
[^8]: Othniel son of Kenaz was the first of the judges (Judges 3:9–11).
[^9]: Literally "sons".
[^10]: *Ge-harashim* means "Valley of Craftsmen".
[^11]: The Hebrew reads "and Kenaz", as if a name were missing before it.
[^12]: Literally "son".
[^13]: The Hebrew does not say who "she" is. She may be Bithiah, Mered's wife (verse 18).
[^14]: Literally "his wife, the Judahite", perhaps in contrast to Mered's Egyptian wife Bithiah.
[^15]: The Hebrew text is written "Tulon" but read "Tilon".
[^16]: Or "and the son of Zoheth".
[^17]: Hebrew *butz*, fine linen.
[^18]: The Hebrew is difficult. *Ba'alu le-Moav* can mean "ruled in Moab" or "married into Moab". *Jashubi-lehem* may be a name, or mean "and returned to Lehem", perhaps Bethlehem.
[^19]: Or "These things are ancient".
[^20]: Or "and those who lived among plantations and walled enclosures". *Neta'im* means "plantings" and *gederah* "walled enclosure".
[^21]: Genesis 46:10 and Exodus 6:15 list Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar and Shaul; Numbers 26:12–13 has Nemuel, Jamin, Jachin, Zerah and Shaul.
[^22]: Verses 28–33 parallel the list of Simeon's towns in Joshua 19:2–8.
[^23]: Joshua 19:3–6 has Balah for Bilhah, Eltolad for Tolad, Bethul for Bethuel, Hazar-susah for Hazar-susim, Beth-lebaoth for Beth-biri and Sharuhen for Shaaraim.
[^24]: Joshua 19:7 has four towns: Ain, Rimmon, Ether and Ashan.
[^25]: Joshua 19:8 has Baalath-beer.
[^26]: Literally "the house of their fathers": an extended family descended from a common ancestor.
[^27]: The Septuagint reads "Gerar".
[^28]: Literally "wide of hands".
[^29]: The Hebrew text is written "the Meinim" but read "the Meunites".
[^30]: Hebrew *charam*: to give over completely to God, which in war meant destroying it. See 2:7.
