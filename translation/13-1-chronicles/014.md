**1** Huram[^1] king of Tyre sent messengers to David, with cedar logs, stonemasons and carpenters,[^2] to build a house for him.

**2** Then David knew that the LORD[^3] had established him as king over Israel, and that his kingdom was highly exalted for the sake of his people Israel.

**3** David took more wives in Jerusalem,[^4] and David fathered more sons and daughters.

**4** These are the names of the children born to him in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon,

**5** Ibhar, Elishua, Elpelet,[^5]

**6** Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,

**7** Elishama, Beeliada[^6] and Eliphelet.

**8** When the Philistines heard that David had been anointed king over all Israel, all the Philistines went up to search for David. David heard about it and went out to meet them.[^7]

**9** The Philistines had come and made a raid in the Valley of Rephaim.

**10** David asked God, "Shall I go up against the Philistines? Will you hand them over to me?" The LORD said to him, "Go up, and I will hand them over to you."

**11** So they went up to Baal-perazim, and David defeated them there. David said, "God has broken through my enemies by my hand, as water breaks through." That is why that place was called Baal-perazim.[^8]

**12** The Philistines abandoned their gods there, and at David's command they were burned in the fire.[^9]

**13** Once again the Philistines made a raid in the valley.

**14** Again David asked God, and God said to him, "Do not go up after them. Turn away from them and come at them opposite the balsam trees.[^10]

**15** When you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the balsam trees, then go out to battle, for God will have gone out ahead of you to strike down the army of the Philistines."

**16** David did as God had commanded him, and they struck down the army of the Philistines from Gibeon[^11] all the way to Gezer.

**17** David's fame spread through all the lands, and the LORD made all the nations fear him.[^12]

[^1]: The Hebrew text is written "Hiram" but read "Huram". Verses 1–16 parallel 2 Samuel 5:11–25, which has Hiram.
[^2]: Literally "craftsmen of walls and craftsmen of wood".
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: 2 Samuel 5:13 reads "David took more concubines and wives from Jerusalem".
[^5]: Elpelet and Nogah (verse 6) are not in 2 Samuel 5:15; compare 3:6–7, where Elpelet is called Eliphelet.
[^6]: 3:8 and 2 Samuel 5:16 have Eliada.
[^7]: 2 Samuel 5:17 reads "and went down to the stronghold".
[^8]: *Baal-perazim* means "lord of breakings through". Hebrew *perets* is "breaking through".
[^9]: Literally "and David said, and they were burned in the fire". 2 Samuel 5:21 says that David and his men carried them off.
[^10]: Hebrew *bekha'im*, a kind of tree or shrub that cannot be identified with certainty. Also in verse 15.
[^11]: 2 Samuel 5:25 has Geba.
[^12]: Literally "put the fear of him on all the nations".
