**1** David assembled at Jerusalem all the officials of Israel: the officials of the tribes, the commanders of the divisions that served the king, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the officials in charge of all the property and livestock of the king and his sons, together with the court officials,[^1] the mighty warriors and all the valiant men.

**2** Then King David rose to his feet and said, "Listen to me, my brothers and my people. I had it in my heart to build a house of rest for the ark of the covenant of Yahweh,[^2] for the footstool of our God,[^3] and I made preparations to build it.

**3** But God said to me, 'You are not to build a house for my name, because you are a man of war and have shed blood.'

**4** Yet Yahweh, the God of Israel, chose me from all my father's house to be king over Israel forever. For he chose Judah as leader,[^4] and in the house of Judah my father's house, and among my father's sons he was pleased to make me king over all Israel.

**5** And from all my sons, for Yahweh has given me many sons, he has chosen my son Solomon to sit on the throne of the kingdom of Yahweh over Israel.

**6** He said to me, 'Your son Solomon is the one who will build my house and my courts, for I have chosen him to be my son, and I will be his father.

**7** I will establish his kingdom forever if he is resolute in keeping my commands and my rulings, as he is today.'

**8** So now, in the sight of all Israel, the assembly of Yahweh, and in the hearing of our God, keep and seek all the commands of Yahweh your God, so that you may possess this good land and leave it as an inheritance to your children after you forever.

**9** And you, my son Solomon, know the God of your father, and serve him with a whole heart and a willing spirit,[^5] for Yahweh searches every heart and understands every plan and thought. If you seek him, he will let you find him, but if you forsake him, he will reject you forever.

**10** See now, Yahweh has chosen you to build a house for the sanctuary. Be strong and do it."

**11** Then David gave his son Solomon the plan[^6] of the porch of the temple, its buildings, its treasuries, its upper rooms, its inner rooms, and the room for the atonement cover,[^7]

**12** and the plan of all that he had in mind[^8] for the courts of the house of Yahweh, for all the surrounding chambers, for the treasuries of the house of God and for the treasuries of the dedicated gifts,

**13** for the divisions of the priests and the Levites, for all the work of the service of the house of Yahweh, and for all the articles for the service of the house of Yahweh;

**14** the weight of gold for all the gold articles for each kind of service, and the weight of silver for all the silver articles for each kind of service;

**15** the weight of the gold lampstands and their gold lamps, with the weight of each lampstand and its lamps; and the weight of silver for the silver lampstands, for each lampstand and its lamps, according to the use of each lampstand;

**16** the weight of gold for the tables for the bread set out in rows,[^9] for each table, and silver for the silver tables;

**17** pure gold for the forks, the sprinkling bowls[^10] and the jars; the weight of gold for each of the gold bowls, and the weight of silver for each of the silver bowls;

**18** and refined gold by weight for the altar of incense; and gold for the plan of the chariot,[^11] the cherubim that spread their wings and covered the ark of the covenant of Yahweh.

**19** All this was in writing from the hand of Yahweh on me; he made me understand all the work of the plan.[^12]

**20** Then David said to his son Solomon, "Be strong and courageous, and do it. Do not be afraid or dismayed, for Yahweh God, my God, is with you. He will not fail you or abandon you until all the work for the service of the house of Yahweh is finished.

**21** Here are the divisions of the priests and the Levites for all the service of the house of God. With you in all the work will be every willing person who is skilled in any kind of service, and the officials and all the people will be at your command."

[^1]: Hebrew *sarisim*, court officials. The word can also mean "eunuchs".
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Compare Psalm 99:5; 132:7.
[^4]: Hebrew *nagid*, "leader, ruler".
[^5]: Hebrew *nephesh*.
[^6]: Hebrew *tavnit*, the word used for the pattern of the tabernacle (Exodus 25:9, 40). Also in verses 12, 18 and 19.
[^7]: Hebrew *beit ha-kapporet*, "the house of the atonement cover": the most holy place, where the ark with its cover stood (Exodus 25:17–22).
[^8]: Literally "all that was in the spirit with him". Or "all that he had by the Spirit".
[^9]: See 9:32.
[^10]: Hebrew *mizraqot*, bowls used for splashing the blood of sacrifices against the altar (Exodus 27:3).
[^11]: The cherubim above the ark are pictured as God's chariot; compare Psalm 18:11 (18:10 in most English Bibles) and Ezekiel 1 and 10.
[^12]: The Hebrew changes to the first person, as if David were speaking. Literally "All in writing from the hand of Yahweh on me he gave understanding, all the works of the plan."
