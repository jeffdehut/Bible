**1** So all Israel was listed in the genealogies, and they are written in the Book of the Kings of Israel. Judah was taken into exile to Babylon because of their unfaithfulness.[^1]

**2** The first inhabitants who lived in their possessions in their towns[^2] were Israelites, priests, Levites and temple servants.[^3]

**3** Some of the people of Judah, of Benjamin, and of Ephraim and Manasseh lived in Jerusalem:

**4** Uthai son of Ammihud, son of Omri, son of Imri, son of Bani,[^4] from the descendants of Perez son of Judah.

**5** From the Shilonites:[^5] Asaiah the firstborn and his sons.

**6** From the descendants of Zerah: Jeuel and their kinsmen,[^6] 690.

**7** From the Benjaminites: Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Hodaviah, son of Hassenuah,

**8** Ibneiah son of Jeroham, Elah son of Uzzi, son of Michri, and Meshullam son of Shephatiah, son of Reuel, son of Ibnijah,

**9** and their kinsmen according to their generations, 956. All these men were heads of ancestral families[^7] by their families.

**10** From the priests: Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, Jachin,

**11** Azariah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub, the chief officer[^8] of the house of God,

**12** Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah, and Maasai son of Adiel, son of Jahzerah, son of Meshullam, son of Meshillemith, son of Immer,

**13** and their kinsmen, heads of their ancestral families, 1,760, capable men for the work of the service of the house of God.

**14** From the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, from the descendants of Merari;

**15** Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah son of Mica, son of Zichri, son of Asaph;

**16** Obadiah son of Shemaiah, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun; and Berechiah son of Asa, son of Elkanah, who lived in the villages of the Netophathites.

**17** The gatekeepers: Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman and their kinsmen. Shallum was the chief,

**18** and until now they have been stationed at the King's Gate on the east. These were the gatekeepers of the camps of the Levites.

**19** Shallum son of Kore, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah, and his kinsmen of his ancestral family, the Korahites, were in charge of the work of the service, guarding the thresholds of the tent,[^9] as their ancestors had been in charge of the camp of the LORD,[^10] guarding the entrance.

**20** Phinehas son of Eleazar was the officer over them in earlier times. The LORD was with him.[^11]

**21** Zechariah son of Meshelemiah was the gatekeeper at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**22** All those chosen as gatekeepers at the thresholds numbered 212. They were listed in the genealogies in their villages. David and Samuel the seer had appointed them to their position of trust.

**23** They and their sons were in charge of the gates of the house of the LORD, the house of the tent, by watches.

**24** The gatekeepers were on the four sides: east, west, north and south.

**25** Their kinsmen in their villages had to come from time to time to be with them for seven days at a time.

**26** For the four chief gatekeepers, who were Levites, were in a position of trust, and they were in charge of the chambers and the treasuries of the house of God.

**27** They spent the night around the house of God, because they had the duty of guarding it, and they were in charge of the key for opening it every morning.

**28** Some of them were in charge of the utensils used in the service. They counted them when they brought them in and counted them when they took them out.

**29** Others were appointed over the furnishings and all the holy utensils, and over the fine flour, the wine, the oil, the frankincense and the spices.

**30** Some of the priests prepared the mixture of spices.

**31** Mattithiah, one of the Levites, the firstborn of Shallum the Korahite, was entrusted with making the griddle cakes.[^12]

**32** Some of their kinsmen from the Kohathites were in charge of the bread set out in rows,[^13] to prepare it every Sabbath.

**33** These are the singers, heads of Levite families, who stayed in the chambers and were free from other duties, because they were on duty day and night.

**34** These were heads of Levite families, chiefs according to their generations. These lived in Jerusalem.

**35** In Gibeon lived the father of Gibeon, Jeiel,[^14] and his wife's name was Maacah.

**36** His firstborn son was Abdon, then Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, Nadab,

**37** Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah and Mikloth.

**38** Mikloth fathered Shimeam. They too lived in Jerusalem near their kinsmen, with their kinsmen.

**39** Ner fathered Kish, Kish fathered Saul, and Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchi-shua, Abinadab and Eshbaal.

**40** The son of Jonathan was Merib-baal, and Merib-baal[^15] fathered Micah.

**41** The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech and Tahrea.[^16]

**42** Ahaz fathered Jarah, and Jarah fathered Alemeth, Azmaveth and Zimri. Zimri fathered Moza.

**43** Moza fathered Binea. Rephaiah was his son, Eleasah his son, Azel his son.

**44** Azel had six sons, and these were their names: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah and Hanan. These were the sons of Azel.

[^1]: Or "in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah. They were taken into exile to Babylon because of their unfaithfulness."
[^2]: Verses 2–17 have a parallel, with many differences, in Nehemiah 11:3–19, the list of those who lived in Jerusalem after the return from exile.
[^3]: Hebrew *netinim*, "those given", that is, given to the service of the temple. They were temple workers of lower rank than the Levites (Ezra 8:20).
[^4]: The Hebrew text is written as one word, which looks like "Benjamin", but read "Bani, from".
[^5]: Or "the Shelanites", the descendants of Judah's son Shelah (Numbers 26:20).
[^6]: Literally "brothers"; also in verses 9, 13, 17, 19, 25 and 32, and "kinsmen" in verse 38.
[^7]: Literally "heads of fathers by the house of their fathers": leaders of extended families. Also in verses 13 and 33–34.
[^8]: Hebrew *nagid*, "leader, ruler".
[^9]: That is, the sanctuary. The temple is called "the tent" by the name of the tabernacle that came before it; also in verse 23.
[^10]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^11]: Or "May the LORD be with him."
[^12]: Hebrew *chavittim*, cakes made on a griddle (Leviticus 6:14; 6:21 in most English Bibles).
[^13]: Hebrew *lechem ha-ma'arekhet*, "the bread of the arrangement": the bread of the Presence, set out in rows on the table before the LORD (Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 24:5–9).
[^14]: The Hebrew text is written "Jeuel" but read "Jeiel". Verses 35–44 repeat, with small differences, 8:29–38.
[^15]: Hebrew *Meri-baal*, another spelling of Merib-baal. Called Mephibosheth in 2 Samuel 4:4 and 9:6.
[^16]: 8:35 adds "and Ahaz".
