**1** Then all the Israelites came out, from Dan to Beersheba and from the land of Gilead, and the community assembled as one man before the LORD[^1] at Mizpah.

**2** The leaders[^2] of all the people, of all the tribes of Israel, took their places in the assembly of the people of God: four hundred thousand foot soldiers armed with swords.

**3** The Benjaminites heard that the Israelites had gone up to Mizpah. The Israelites said, "Tell us how this evil thing happened."

**4** The Levite, the husband of the murdered woman, answered, "I came with my concubine to Gibeah in Benjamin to spend the night.

**5** The men of Gibeah[^3] rose against me and surrounded the house at night because of me. They meant to kill me, and they violated my concubine, and she died.

**6** So I took hold of my concubine, cut her in pieces, and sent her throughout the whole territory of Israel's inheritance, because they have committed a lewd and outrageous act[^4] in Israel.

**7** Now, all of you Israelites, give your word and your advice here."

**8** All the people rose as one man and said, "None of us will go home to his tent. None of us will return to his house.

**9** Now this is what we will do to Gibeah: we will go up against it by lot.[^5]

**10** We will take ten men out of every hundred from all the tribes of Israel, a hundred out of every thousand, and a thousand out of every ten thousand, to get provisions for the troops. Then, when they come to Geba[^6] in Benjamin, they can pay it back for all the outrage it has committed in Israel."

**11** So all the men of Israel gathered against the city, united as one man.

**12** The tribes of Israel sent men throughout the tribe of Benjamin,[^7] saying, "What is this evil thing that has happened among you?

**13** Now hand over the men, those worthless men[^8] in Gibeah, so that we may put them to death and purge the evil from Israel." But the Benjaminites[^9] would not listen to their brothers, the Israelites.

**14** Instead the Benjaminites came together from their towns to Gibeah, to go out to battle against the Israelites.

**15** On that day the Benjaminites mustered from their towns twenty-six thousand men armed with swords, besides the people of Gibeah, who mustered seven hundred chosen men.

**16** Among all these troops there were seven hundred chosen men who were left-handed.[^10] Every one of them could sling a stone at a hair and not miss.

**17** The men of Israel, not counting Benjamin, mustered four hundred thousand men armed with swords, all of them warriors.

**18** The Israelites set out and went up to Bethel[^11] and asked God, "Which of us is to go up first to battle against the Benjaminites?" The LORD said, "Judah first."

**19** The Israelites got up in the morning and camped against Gibeah.

**20** The men of Israel went out to battle against Benjamin, and the men of Israel drew up their battle lines against them at Gibeah.

**21** The Benjaminites came out of Gibeah and cut down twenty-two thousand Israelites on the battlefield that day.[^12]

**22** But the troops, the men of Israel, took courage, and they again drew up their battle lines in the same place where they had drawn them up on the first day.

**23** The Israelites went up and wept before the LORD until evening. They asked the LORD, "Shall we go out again to battle against our brothers the Benjaminites?"[^13] The LORD said, "Go up against them."

**24** So the Israelites advanced against the Benjaminites on the second day.

**25** On the second day Benjamin came out of Gibeah to meet them, and they cut down eighteen thousand more Israelites on the battlefield, all of them armed with swords.

**26** Then all the Israelites, the whole army, went up to Bethel. They wept and sat there before the LORD, and they fasted that day until evening. They offered burnt offerings and fellowship offerings[^14] before the LORD.

**27** The Israelites asked the LORD (for the ark of the covenant of God was there in those days,

**28** and Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron, served before it[^15] in those days), "Shall we go out yet again to battle against our brothers the Benjaminites, or shall we stop?" The LORD said, "Go up, for tomorrow I will hand them over to you."

**29** So Israel set men in ambush all around Gibeah.

**30** On the third day the Israelites went up against the Benjaminites and drew up their battle lines against Gibeah as before.

**31** The Benjaminites came out to meet the troops and were drawn away from the city. As before, they began to strike down and kill some of the troops on the roads, one of which goes up to Bethel and the other to Gibeah,[^16] in the open country. About thirty men of Israel were killed.

**32** The Benjaminites said, "They are being defeated before us, as before." But the Israelites said, "Let us retreat and draw them away from the city to the roads."

**33** All the men of Israel rose from their places and drew up their battle lines at Baal-tamar, while the Israelites in ambush burst out of their position at Maareh-geba.[^17]

**34** Ten thousand chosen men from all Israel came against Gibeah, and the fighting was heavy. But the Benjaminites did not know that disaster was close upon them.

**35** The LORD defeated Benjamin before Israel, and that day the Israelites cut down 25,100 men of Benjamin, all of them armed with swords.

**36** Then the Benjaminites saw that they were defeated.[^18] The men of Israel had given ground to Benjamin, because they were relying on the ambush they had set against Gibeah.

**37** The men in ambush moved quickly and rushed against Gibeah. They spread out and put the whole city to the sword.[^19]

**38** The men of Israel had agreed on a signal with the men in ambush: they were to send up a great cloud[^20] of smoke from the city.

**39** Then the men of Israel turned around in the battle. Benjamin had begun to strike down and kill about thirty men of Israel, for they said, "They are surely defeated before us, as in the first battle."

**40** But the signal began to rise from the city, a column of smoke, and when the Benjaminites looked behind them, the whole city was going up in smoke to the sky.[^21]

**41** Then the men of Israel turned around, and the men of Benjamin were terrified, for they saw that disaster had struck them.

**42** So they turned their backs before the men of Israel and fled toward the wilderness, but the battle caught up with them, and those who came out of the towns cut them down among them.[^22]

**43** They surrounded the Benjaminites and chased them. They trampled them down with ease[^23] as far as a point opposite Gibeah on the east.

**44** Of Benjamin, 18,000 men fell, all of them valiant warriors.

**45** They turned and fled toward the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon. The Israelites picked off[^24] 5,000 men on the roads, and they pursued them closely as far as Gidom and struck down 2,000 more of them.

**46** So all the Benjaminites who fell that day were 25,000 men armed with swords, all of them valiant warriors.

**47** But 600 men turned and fled toward the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon, and they stayed at the rock of Rimmon for four months.

**48** The men of Israel turned back against the Benjaminites and put them to the sword, the people of the towns and the animals, everything they found. They also set on fire all the towns they came to.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "the corners", that is, the chief men.
[^3]: Literally "the lords of Gibeah", its leading citizens (compare 9:2).
[^4]: Hebrew *zimmah u-nevalah*: shameful wickedness that violates what is sacred or right (compare 19:23). Also in verse 10.
[^5]: Literally "against it by lot". The Hebrew sentence is shortened.
[^6]: Probably another form of the name Gibeah.
[^7]: Literally "all the tribes of Benjamin", that is, its clans.
[^8]: Literally "sons of *beliyya'al*", "sons of worthlessness": scoundrels.
[^9]: The Hebrew text is written "Benjamin", but the reading tradition adds "the sons of", that is, "the Benjaminites".
[^10]: Literally "restricted in his right hand". See 3:15.
[^11]: Or "to the house of God". Also in verse 26.
[^12]: Literally "destroyed to the ground in Israel on that day twenty-two thousand men"; similar wording in verse 25.
[^13]: Literally "my brother, the Benjaminites"; also in verse 28.
[^14]: Hebrew *shelamim*, traditionally "peace offerings", sacrifices shared in a meal by the worshipers.
[^15]: Literally "stood before it".
[^16]: Perhaps "Geba". Compare verse 10.
[^17]: The meaning of *Maareh-geba* is uncertain; perhaps "the open ground near Geba".
[^18]: Verses 36–46 tell the battle again in more detail.
[^19]: Literally "struck the whole city by the mouth of the sword"; similar wording in verse 48.
[^20]: The Hebrew word translated "great" is uncertain.
[^21]: Literally "the whole of the city went up toward heaven". *Kalil*, "whole", is also a word for a whole burnt offering.
[^22]: Literally "and those who were from the towns were destroying him in his midst".
[^23]: Hebrew *menuchah*, "resting place" or "ease". It may be a place name: "from Menuhah".
[^24]: Literally "gleaned".
