**1** Once again the Israelites did what was evil in the sight of Yahweh,[^1] and Yahweh handed them over to the Philistines for forty years.

**2** There was a man from Zorah, of the clan of the Danites, whose name was Manoah. His wife was unable to have children,[^2] and she had borne none.

**3** The angel of Yahweh[^3] appeared to the woman and said to her, "You are unable to have children and have borne none, but you will become pregnant and give birth to a son.

**4** So now be careful not to drink wine or beer,[^4] and do not eat anything unclean.

**5** For you will become pregnant and give birth to a son. No razor is to touch his head,[^5] for the boy will be a Nazirite[^6] dedicated to God from the womb. He will begin to save Israel from the power of the Philistines."

**6** The woman went and told her husband, "A man of God came to me. He looked like the angel of God, very awe-inspiring. I did not ask him where he came from, and he did not tell me his name.

**7** But he said to me, 'You will become pregnant and give birth to a son. So now do not drink wine or beer, and do not eat anything unclean, for the boy will be a Nazirite dedicated to God from the womb until the day he dies.'"

**8** Then Manoah prayed to Yahweh and said, "Please, Lord, let the man of God whom you sent come to us again and teach us what we are to do for the boy who is to be born."

**9** God listened to Manoah,[^7] and the angel of God came again to the woman while she was sitting in the field. Her husband Manoah was not with her.

**10** The woman ran quickly and told her husband. She said to him, "The man who came to me the other day[^8] has appeared to me!"

**11** Manoah got up and followed his wife. He came to the man and said to him, "Are you the man who spoke to the woman?" He said, "I am."

**12** Manoah said, "Now when your words come true, what is to be the boy's way of life, and what is he to do?"[^9]

**13** The angel of Yahweh said to Manoah, "The woman must take care to do everything I told her.

**14** She must not eat anything that comes from the grapevine. She must not drink wine or beer, or eat anything unclean. She must observe everything I commanded her."

**15** Manoah said to the angel of Yahweh, "Please stay with us, and let us prepare a young goat for you."

**16** The angel of Yahweh said to Manoah, "Even if you keep me here, I will not eat your food. But if you prepare a burnt offering, offer it to Yahweh." (For Manoah did not know that he was the angel of Yahweh.)

**17** Then Manoah said to the angel of Yahweh, "What is your name, so that when your word comes true[^10] we may honor you?"

**18** The angel of Yahweh said to him, "Why do you ask my name? It is wonderful."[^11]

**19** So Manoah took the young goat and the grain offering and offered them on the rock to Yahweh. Then he did a wonderful thing,[^12] while Manoah and his wife watched.

**20** As the flame went up from the altar toward heaven, the angel of Yahweh went up in the flame of the altar. Manoah and his wife watched, and they fell face down to the ground.

**21** The angel of Yahweh did not appear again to Manoah and his wife. Then Manoah knew that he was the angel of Yahweh.

**22** Manoah said to his wife, "We will surely die, for we have seen God!"[^13]

**23** But his wife said to him, "If Yahweh had wanted to kill us, he would not have accepted a burnt offering and a grain offering from our hands. He would not have shown us all these things, or told us anything like this now."

**24** The woman gave birth to a son and named him Samson.[^14] The boy grew, and Yahweh blessed him.

**25** The Spirit of Yahweh[^15] began to stir him in Mahaneh-dan,[^16] between Zorah and Eshtaol.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "was barren"; also in verse 3.
[^3]: Or "the messenger of Yahweh". Hebrew *mal'akh* means messenger. The same is true of "the angel of God" in verses 6 and 9.
[^4]: Hebrew *shekhar*, an alcoholic drink, probably beer made from grain. Also in verses 7 and 14.
[^5]: Literally "No razor is to go up on his head."
[^6]: Hebrew *nazir*, "one set apart, dedicated". The Nazirite's vow is described in Numbers 6:1–21. Also in verse 7.
[^7]: Literally "listened to the voice of Manoah".
[^8]: Literally "on the day".
[^9]: Literally "what will be the boy's rule and his work?"
[^10]: The Hebrew text is written "your words come true" but read "your word comes true".
[^11]: Hebrew *peli*, "wonderful", that is, beyond human understanding.
[^12]: Literally "and doing wonderfully". The one who acts is not named: Yahweh, or the angel. Some take the words with what comes before: "to Yahweh, who does wonders".
[^13]: Or "a divine being". Hebrew *Elohim*.
[^14]: Hebrew *Shimshon*, probably from *shemesh*, "sun".
[^15]: Or "the spirit of Yahweh". Hebrew *ruach*, "spirit, wind, breath".
[^16]: "The camp of Dan". Compare 18:12.
