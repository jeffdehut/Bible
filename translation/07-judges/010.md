**1** After Abimelech, Tola son of Puah, son of Dodo,[^1] a man of Issachar, rose to save Israel. He lived in Shamir in the hill country of Ephraim.

**2** He judged Israel for twenty-three years. Then he died and was buried in Shamir.

**3** After him rose Jair the Gileadite, and he judged Israel for twenty-two years.

**4** He had thirty sons who rode on thirty donkeys, and they had thirty towns.[^2] To this day they are called Havvoth-jair,[^3] and they are in the land of Gilead.

**5** Jair died and was buried in Kamon.

**6** Once again the Israelites did what was evil in the sight of the LORD.[^4] They served the Baals and the Ashtoreths,[^5] the gods of Aram, the gods of Sidon, the gods of Moab, the gods of the Ammonites and the gods of the Philistines. They abandoned the LORD and did not serve him.

**7** So the LORD's anger burned against Israel, and he sold them into the power of the Philistines and of the Ammonites.

**8** That year they shattered and crushed the Israelites. For eighteen years they oppressed all the Israelites who were on the other side of the Jordan, in the land of the Amorites, in Gilead.[^6]

**9** The Ammonites also crossed the Jordan to fight against Judah, Benjamin and the house of Ephraim, and Israel was in great distress.

**10** Then the Israelites cried out to the LORD, "We have sinned against you, for we have abandoned our God and served the Baals."

**11** The LORD said to the Israelites, "Didn't I save you from the Egyptians, the Amorites, the Ammonites and the Philistines?[^7]

**12** When the Sidonians, Amalek and Maon[^8] oppressed you, and you cried out to me, I saved you from their power.

**13** But you have abandoned me and served other gods. So I will not save you again.

**14** Go and cry out to the gods you have chosen. Let them save you in your time of trouble."

**15** The Israelites said to the LORD, "We have sinned. Do to us whatever you think best. Only please rescue us today."

**16** So they got rid of the foreign gods among them and served the LORD, and he could no longer bear Israel's misery.[^9]

**17** The Ammonites were called to arms and camped in Gilead, and the Israelites gathered and camped at Mizpah.

**18** The people, the leaders of Gilead, said to one another, "Whoever will begin the fight against the Ammonites will be head over all the people living in Gilead."

[^1]: *Dodo* can also be read "his uncle".
[^2]: In Hebrew the words for "donkeys" and "towns" sound alike here.
[^3]: "The tent villages of Jair". Compare Numbers 32:41; Deuteronomy 3:14.
[^4]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^5]: *Baal* was the Canaanite storm god, and *Ashtoreth* (Astarte) a Canaanite goddess. The plurals refer to their local forms, or to gods and goddesses in general. See 2:11, 13.
[^6]: Literally "They shattered and crushed the Israelites in that year, eighteen years, all the Israelites who were beyond the Jordan …".
[^7]: Literally "Was it not from Egypt and from the Amorites and from the Ammonites and from the Philistines?" The sentence is unfinished in Hebrew.
[^8]: The Septuagint reads "Midian".
[^9]: Literally "his soul became short with the misery of Israel".
