**1** Some time later, at the time of the wheat harvest, Samson went to visit his wife with a young goat. He said, "I am going in to my wife in her room."[^1] But her father would not let him go in.

**2** Her father said, "I was sure that you hated her, so I gave her to your companion. Isn't her younger sister more beautiful than she is? Please take her instead."

**3** Samson said to them, "This time I will be in the right with the Philistines when I do them harm."[^2]

**4** So Samson went and caught three hundred foxes.[^3] He took torches, turned the foxes tail to tail, and put a torch between each pair of tails.

**5** He set the torches on fire and let the foxes loose in the Philistines' standing grain. He burned up the stacked grain and the standing grain, and the vineyards and the olive groves too.[^4]

**6** The Philistines asked, "Who did this?" They were told, "Samson, the son-in-law of the Timnite, because he took Samson's wife and gave her to his companion." So the Philistines went up and burned her and her father to death.

**7** Samson said to them, "Since you act like this, I will take revenge on you, and only then will I stop."

**8** He struck them down without mercy,[^5] a great slaughter. Then he went down and stayed in the cleft of the rock of Etam.

**9** The Philistines went up and camped in Judah, and they spread out at Lehi.[^6]

**10** The men of Judah said, "Why have you come up against us?" They said, "We have come up to tie up Samson, to do to him as he did to us."

**11** Then three thousand men of Judah went down to the cleft of the rock of Etam and said to Samson, "Don't you know that the Philistines rule over us? What is this you have done to us?" He said to them, "As they did to me, so I have done to them."

**12** They said to him, "We have come down to tie you up and hand you over to the Philistines." Samson said to them, "Swear to me that you will not attack me yourselves."

**13** They said to him, "No, we will only tie you up and hand you over to them. We certainly won't kill you." So they tied him up with two new ropes and brought him up from the rock.

**14** When he came to Lehi, the Philistines came shouting to meet him. Then the Spirit of the LORD[^7] rushed on him, and the ropes on his arms became like flax that has caught fire, and the bonds melted off his hands.

**15** He found a fresh jawbone of a donkey. He reached out his hand, took it, and struck down a thousand men with it.

**16** Then Samson said, "With the jawbone of a donkey, heaps upon heaps![^8] With the jawbone of a donkey I have struck down a thousand men."

**17** When he had finished speaking, he threw the jawbone from his hand, and that place was called Ramath-lehi.[^9]

**18** He was very thirsty, and he called out to the LORD, "You have given this great victory by the hand of your servant. Must I now die of thirst and fall into the hands of the uncircumcised?"

**19** So God split open the hollow[^10] at Lehi, and water came out of it. When he drank, his strength returned,[^11] and he revived. That is why it was named En-hakkore.[^12] It is in Lehi to this day.

**20** Samson judged Israel for twenty years in the days of the Philistines.

[^1]: "Go in to" is a Hebrew way of speaking of sexual relations; also later in the verse.
[^2]: Or "This time I am innocent toward the Philistines, when I do them harm."
[^3]: Or "jackals".
[^4]: Literally "from the stacked grain to the standing grain, and to the vineyard, the olive".
[^5]: Literally "leg on thigh", an expression for a complete defeat.
[^6]: *Lehi* means "jawbone". Also in verses 14 and 19.
[^7]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. Or "the spirit of the LORD". Hebrew *ruach*, "spirit, wind, breath".
[^8]: Hebrew *chamor chamoratayim*, "a heap, two heaps". The word for "heap" sounds the same as *chamor*, "donkey".
[^9]: "Jawbone Hill".
[^10]: Hebrew *makhtesh*, literally "mortar", a hollow in the ground.
[^11]: Literally "his spirit returned".
[^12]: "The spring of the one who called". *Qore* can also mean "partridge".
