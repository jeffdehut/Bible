**1** When the seventh month came,[^1] and the Israelites were in their towns, the people gathered as one man in Jerusalem.

**2** Then Jeshua son of Jozadak and his fellow priests,[^2] and Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and his kinsmen, set to work and built the altar of the God of Israel, to offer burnt offerings[^3] on it, as it is written in the Law of Moses, the man of God.

**3** They set up the altar on its foundations, because they were afraid of the peoples of the lands,[^4] and they offered burnt offerings on it to the LORD,[^5] burnt offerings morning and evening.[^6]

**4** They kept the Festival of Shelters,[^7] as it is written, and offered the daily burnt offerings by number, as prescribed for each day.[^8]

**5** After that they offered the regular burnt offering,[^9] the offerings for the new moons and for all the sacred appointed festivals of the LORD, and the offerings of everyone who brought a freewill offering to the LORD.

**6** From the first day of the seventh month they began to offer burnt offerings to the LORD, but the foundation of the temple of the LORD had not yet been laid.

**7** So they gave money to the stonecutters and the craftsmen, and food, drink and oil to the people of Sidon and Tyre, to bring cedar wood from Lebanon by sea to Joppa,[^10] as Cyrus king of Persia had authorized them.

**8** In the second month of the second year after they came to the house of God in Jerusalem, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Jeshua son of Jozadak and the rest of their kinsmen, the priests and the Levites, and all who had come to Jerusalem from the captivity, made a start. They appointed the Levites who were twenty years old or more to supervise the work on the house of the LORD.

**9** Jeshua,[^11] with his sons and his kinsmen, and Kadmiel and his sons, the descendants of Judah,[^12] together took charge of supervising those doing the work on the house of God, along with the descendants of Henadad, their sons and their kinsmen, the Levites.

**10** When the builders laid the foundation of the temple of the LORD, the priests in their vestments[^13] were stationed with trumpets, and the Levites, the descendants of Asaph, with cymbals, to praise the LORD as David king of Israel had directed.[^14]

**11** They sang responsively,[^15] praising and giving thanks to the LORD: "For he is good; his faithful love[^16] toward Israel lasts forever." And all the people shouted with a great shout as they praised the LORD, because the foundation of the house of the LORD had been laid.

**12** But many of the priests and the Levites and the heads of the ancestral families, the old men who had seen the first house, wept aloud when the foundation of this house was laid before their eyes,[^17] while many others shouted aloud for joy.

**13** The people could not tell the sound of the joyful shouting from the sound of the people's weeping, because the people were shouting with a great shout, and the sound was heard far away.

[^1]: The seventh month, Tishri, around September–October, the month of the Day of Atonement and the Festival of Shelters (Leviticus 23:23–43).
[^2]: Literally "his brothers"; also "his kinsmen" later in the verse and in verses 8–9.
[^3]: Hebrew *olot*, from the verb "go up": offerings wholly burned on the altar (Leviticus 1).
[^4]: Or "even though they were afraid of the peoples of the lands". The Hebrew is difficult. *The peoples of the lands* are the other peoples living in and around Judah.
[^5]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^6]: The Hebrew text is written "he offered" but read "they offered".
[^7]: Hebrew *sukkot*, "booths, shelters". Traditionally "Tabernacles". See Leviticus 23:33–43; Numbers 29:12–38.
[^8]: Literally "according to the ruling, the matter of a day in its day".
[^9]: Hebrew *olat tamid*: the burnt offering made every morning and evening (Numbers 28:3–8).
[^10]: Literally "to the sea of Joppa".
[^11]: This Jeshua is probably the Levite of 2:40, not the priest of verse 8.
[^12]: Kadmiel is a Levite in 2:40, where he is listed with "the descendants of Hodaviah".
[^13]: Literally "clothed".
[^14]: Literally "according to the hands of David king of Israel". See 1 Chronicles 16:4–6; 25:1.
[^15]: Literally "They answered", perhaps one group answering another. The words that follow are a refrain found in Psalms 106:1; 107:1; 118:1; 136:1.
[^16]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^17]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "who had seen the first house in its founding, this house in their eyes".
