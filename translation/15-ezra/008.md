**1** These are the heads of their ancestral families, and the genealogical record of those who went up with me from Babylon in the reign of King Artaxerxes:[^1]

**2** From the descendants of Phinehas: Gershom. From the descendants of Ithamar: Daniel. From the descendants of David: Hattush,

**3** of the descendants of Shecaniah.[^2] From the descendants of Parosh: Zechariah, and with him 150 males were registered.

**4** From the descendants of Pahath-moab: Eliehoenai son of Zerahiah, and with him 200 males.

**5** From the descendants of Shecaniah: the son of Jahaziel,[^3] and with him 300 males.

**6** From the descendants of Adin: Ebed son of Jonathan, and with him 50 males.

**7** From the descendants of Elam: Jeshaiah son of Athaliah, and with him 70 males.

**8** From the descendants of Shephatiah: Zebadiah son of Michael, and with him 80 males.

**9** From the descendants of Joab: Obadiah son of Jehiel, and with him 218 males.

**10** From the descendants of Shelomith: the son of Josiphiah,[^4] and with him 160 males.

**11** From the descendants of Bebai: Zechariah son of Bebai, and with him 28 males.

**12** From the descendants of Azgad: Johanan son of Hakkatan, and with him 110 males.

**13** From the descendants of Adonikam, the last ones:[^5] their names were Eliphelet, Jeiel and Shemaiah, and with them 60 males.

**14** From the descendants of Bigvai: Uthai and Zaccur,[^6] and with him 70 males.

**15** I gathered them at the river that runs to Ahava, and we camped there for three days. When I looked over the people and the priests, I found none of the descendants of Levi there.

**16** So I sent for Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah and Meshullam, who were leaders, and for Joiarib and Elnathan, who were teachers.[^7]

**17** I gave them orders for Iddo,[^8] the leader at the place called Casiphia, and I told them what to say to Iddo and his kinsmen,[^9] the temple servants at the place called Casiphia: to bring us ministers for the house of our God.

**18** Because the good hand of our God was on us, they brought us a man of insight[^10] from the descendants of Mahli son of Levi, son of Israel, and Sherebiah with his sons and his kinsmen, 18;

**19** and Hashabiah, and with him Jeshaiah from the descendants of Merari, with his kinsmen and their sons, 20;

**20** and from the temple servants, whom David and the officials had given for the service of the Levites, 220 temple servants. All of them were designated by name.

**21** Then I proclaimed a fast there by the river Ahava, so that we might humble ourselves before our God and ask him for a safe journey[^11] for us, our little ones and all our possessions.

**22** For I was ashamed to ask the king for soldiers and horsemen to protect us from enemies on the way, because we had told the king, "The hand of our God is on all who seek him, for their good, but his power and his anger are against all who forsake him."

**23** So we fasted and asked our God about this, and he answered our prayer.[^12]

**24** Then I set apart twelve of the leading priests, together with Sherebiah, Hashabiah and ten of their kinsmen with them.[^13]

**25** I weighed out to them the silver, the gold and the articles, the contribution[^14] for the house of our God that the king, his advisers, his officials and all Israel who were present had given.

**26** I weighed out into their hands 650 talents[^15] of silver, silver articles weighing 100 talents,[^16] 100 talents of gold,

**27** 20 gold bowls worth 1,000 darics,[^17] and 2 articles of fine polished bronze,[^18] as precious as gold.

**28** I said to them, "You are holy to Yahweh,[^19] and the articles are holy. The silver and the gold are a freewill offering to Yahweh, the God of your ancestors.

**29** Guard them carefully until you weigh them out in Jerusalem, in the chambers of the house of Yahweh, before the leading priests and Levites and the leaders of the ancestral families of Israel."

**30** So the priests and the Levites took charge of the silver, the gold and the articles by weight, to bring them to Jerusalem, to the house of our God.

**31** We set out from the river Ahava on the twelfth day of the first month to go to Jerusalem. The hand of our God was on us, and he rescued us from enemies and from those who lay in ambush along the way.[^20]

**32** We came to Jerusalem and stayed there three days.

**33** On the fourth day the silver, the gold and the articles were weighed out in the house of our God into the hands of Meremoth son of Uriah the priest. With him was Eleazar son of Phinehas, and with them were the Levites Jozabad son of Jeshua and Noadiah son of Binnui.

**34** Everything was counted and weighed, and the whole weight was recorded at that time.

**35** The exiles who had come back from captivity[^21] offered burnt offerings to the God of Israel: twelve bulls for all Israel, ninety-six rams, seventy-seven lambs, and twelve male goats as a sin offering.[^22] All this was a burnt offering to Yahweh.

**36** They delivered the king's orders[^23] to the king's satraps[^24] and the governors of the province Beyond the River,[^25] and they gave their support to the people and to the house of God.

[^1]: Most of the families in verses 2–14 are also among those who returned earlier (2:3–15).
[^2]: Hattush is listed among the descendants of Shecaniah in 1 Chronicles 3:22.
[^3]: The Septuagint reads "From the descendants of Zattu: Shecaniah son of Jahaziel". Zattu is a family in 2:8.
[^4]: The Septuagint reads "From the descendants of Bani: Shelomith son of Josiphiah". Bani is a family in 2:10.
[^5]: Or "who came later".
[^6]: The Hebrew text is written "Zabbud" but read "Zaccur".
[^7]: Hebrew *mevinim*, "men of understanding": those who taught or explained the Law (compare Nehemiah 8:7–9).
[^8]: The Hebrew text is written "I sent them out to Iddo" but read "I gave them orders for Iddo".
[^9]: Literally "Iddo his brother, the temple servants in Casiphia the place". Casiphia is not otherwise known.
[^10]: Or "a capable man". Perhaps this is Sherebiah himself: "namely Sherebiah".
[^11]: Literally "a straight way".
[^12]: Literally "he let himself be entreated by us".
[^13]: Or "namely Sherebiah, Hashabiah". Sherebiah and Hashabiah are Levites in verses 18–19, and verse 30 speaks of both priests and Levites.
[^14]: Hebrew *terumah*: something set apart and given for a sacred purpose.
[^15]: A talent was about 75 pounds (34 kg), so 650 talents was about 24 tons (22 metric tons).
[^16]: Literally "silver articles, 100 by talents". The Hebrew may mean 100 silver articles whose weight in talents is not given.
[^17]: Hebrew *adarkonim*: probably darics, Persian gold coins; see 2:69.
[^18]: The meaning of Hebrew *mutshav tovah* is uncertain: perhaps "fine shining bronze".
[^19]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^20]: Literally "from the hand of the enemy and the one lying in ambush on the way".
[^21]: Literally "Those who came from the captivity, the sons of the exile".
[^22]: Or "purification offering". Hebrew *chatta't*: an offering to remove the effects of sin and impurity (Leviticus 4).
[^23]: Hebrew *datei*, plural of *dat*, "law, order", a word borrowed from Persian (compare 7:12).
[^24]: Hebrew *achashdarpenim*, from a Persian title: the satraps, who governed the large regions of the Persian Empire, above the governors of its provinces.
[^25]: The Persian province west of the Euphrates. Here the Hebrew has *ever ha-nahar*, as in Nehemiah.
