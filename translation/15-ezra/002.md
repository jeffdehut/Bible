**1** These are the people of the province[^1] who came up out of the captivity of the exiles, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had taken into exile to Babylon. They returned to Jerusalem and Judah, each to his own town.[^2]

**2** They came with Zerubbabel, Jeshua, Nehemiah, Seraiah, Reelaiah, Mordecai, Bilshan, Mispar, Bigvai, Rehum and Baanah.[^3] The number of the men of the people of Israel:

**3** the descendants of Parosh, 2,172;

**4** the descendants of Shephatiah, 372;

**5** the descendants of Arah, 775;[^4]

**6** the descendants of Pahath-moab,[^5] through the descendants of Jeshua and Joab,[^6] 2,812;[^7]

**7** the descendants of Elam, 1,254;

**8** the descendants of Zattu, 945;[^8]

**9** the descendants of Zaccai, 760;

**10** the descendants of Bani, 642;[^9]

**11** the descendants of Bebai, 623;[^10]

**12** the descendants of Azgad, 1,222;[^11]

**13** the descendants of Adonikam, 666;[^12]

**14** the descendants of Bigvai, 2,056;[^13]

**15** the descendants of Adin, 454;[^14]

**16** the descendants of Ater, through Hezekiah, 98;

**17** the descendants of Bezai, 323;[^15]

**18** the descendants of Jorah, 112;[^16]

**19** the descendants of Hashum, 223;[^17]

**20** the descendants of Gibbar, 95;[^18]

**21** the people of Bethlehem,[^19] 123;

**22** the men of Netophah, 56;[^20]

**23** the men of Anathoth, 128;

**24** the people of Azmaveth, 42;[^21]

**25** the people of Kiriath-arim,[^22] Chephirah and Beeroth, 743;

**26** the people of Ramah and Geba, 621;

**27** the men of Michmas, 122;

**28** the men of Bethel and Ai, 223;[^23]

**29** the people of Nebo, 52;[^24]

**30** the people of Magbish, 156;[^25]

**31** the descendants of the other Elam, 1,254;

**32** the descendants of Harim, 320;

**33** the people of Lod, Hadid and Ono, 725;[^26]

**34** the people of Jericho, 345;

**35** the people of Senaah, 3,630.[^27]

**36** The priests: the descendants of Jedaiah, of the house of Jeshua, 973;

**37** the descendants of Immer, 1,052;

**38** the descendants of Pashhur, 1,247;

**39** the descendants of Harim, 1,017.

**40** The Levites: the descendants of Jeshua and Kadmiel, through the descendants of Hodaviah, 74.[^28]

**41** The singers: the descendants of Asaph, 128.[^29]

**42** The descendants of the gatekeepers: the descendants of Shallum, the descendants of Ater, the descendants of Talmon, the descendants of Akkub, the descendants of Hatita and the descendants of Shobai, 139 in all.[^30]

**43** The temple servants:[^31] the descendants of Ziha, the descendants of Hasupha, the descendants of Tabbaoth,

**44** the descendants of Keros, the descendants of Siaha,[^32] the descendants of Padon,

**45** the descendants of Lebanah, the descendants of Hagabah, the descendants of Akkub,[^33]

**46** the descendants of Hagab, the descendants of Shalmai,[^34] the descendants of Hanan,

**47** the descendants of Giddel, the descendants of Gahar, the descendants of Reaiah,

**48** the descendants of Rezin, the descendants of Nekoda, the descendants of Gazzam,

**49** the descendants of Uzza, the descendants of Paseah, the descendants of Besai,

**50** the descendants of Asnah, the descendants of the Meunim,[^35] the descendants of the Nephusim,[^36]

**51** the descendants of Bakbuk, the descendants of Hakupha, the descendants of Harhur,

**52** the descendants of Bazluth,[^37] the descendants of Mehida, the descendants of Harsha,

**53** the descendants of Barkos, the descendants of Sisera, the descendants of Temah,

**54** the descendants of Neziah and the descendants of Hatipha.

**55** The descendants of Solomon's servants:[^38] the descendants of Sotai, the descendants of Hassophereth, the descendants of Peruda,[^39]

**56** the descendants of Jaalah, the descendants of Darkon, the descendants of Giddel,

**57** the descendants of Shephatiah, the descendants of Hattil, the descendants of Pochereth-hazzebaim and the descendants of Ami.[^40]

**58** All the temple servants and the descendants of Solomon's servants: 392.

**59** These are the ones who came up from Tel-melah, Tel-harsha, Cherub, Addan[^41] and Immer, but they could not show that their ancestral families and their descent were from Israel:[^42]

**60** the descendants of Delaiah, the descendants of Tobiah and the descendants of Nekoda, 652.[^43]

**61** And of the priests: the descendants of Hobaiah, the descendants of Hakkoz and the descendants of Barzillai, who had married one of the daughters of Barzillai the Gileadite and was called by their name.[^44]

**62** These searched for their entry among those recorded in the genealogies, but they were not found, so they were excluded from the priesthood as unclean.[^45]

**63** The governor[^46] told them not to eat any of the most holy food[^47] until a priest arose who could consult the Urim and Thummim.[^48]

**64** The whole assembly together numbered 42,360,[^49]

**65** besides their male and female servants, who numbered 7,337. They also had 200 male and female singers.[^50]

**66** Their horses numbered 736, their mules 245,[^51]

**67** their camels 435 and their donkeys 6,720.

**68** When they came to the house of Yahweh[^52] in Jerusalem, some of the heads of the ancestral families gave freewill offerings for the house of God, to set it up on its site.

**69** As they were able, they gave to the treasury for the work 61,000 darics[^53] of gold, 5,000 minas[^54] of silver and 100 priests' garments.[^55]

**70** So the priests, the Levites and some of the people, with the singers, the gatekeepers and the temple servants, settled in their towns, and all Israel settled in their towns.

[^1]: Literally "the sons of the province": Judah, now a province of the Persian Empire. This chapter is repeated in Nehemiah 7:6–72, with differences in some names and numbers, which are noted below.
[^2]: Nehemiah 7:6 reads "to Jerusalem and to Judah".
[^3]: Nehemiah 7:7 has Azariah for Seraiah, Raamiah for Reelaiah, Mispereth for Mispar and Nehum for Rehum, and adds Nahamani after Raamiah.
[^4]: Nehemiah 7:10 has 652.
[^5]: The name means "governor of Moab".
[^6]: Literally "of the sons of Jeshua, Joab". Nehemiah 7:11 has "Jeshua and Joab".
[^7]: Nehemiah 7:11 has 2,818.
[^8]: Nehemiah 7:13 has 845.
[^9]: Nehemiah 7:15 has Binnui, 648.
[^10]: Nehemiah 7:16 has 628.
[^11]: Nehemiah 7:17 has 2,322.
[^12]: Nehemiah 7:18 has 667.
[^13]: Nehemiah 7:19 has 2,067.
[^14]: Nehemiah 7:20 has 655.
[^15]: Nehemiah 7:23 has 324, and lists Bezai after Hashum.
[^16]: Nehemiah 7:24 has Hariph, 112.
[^17]: Nehemiah 7:22 has 328.
[^18]: Nehemiah 7:25 has Gibeon, a town.
[^19]: From here the list turns mostly to towns, and "the sons of" a town are its people.
[^20]: Nehemiah 7:26 has "the men of Bethlehem and Netophah, 188".
[^21]: Nehemiah 7:28 has "the men of Beth-azmaveth".
[^22]: Kiriath-jearim, as Nehemiah 7:29 has it.
[^23]: Nehemiah 7:32 has 123.
[^24]: Nehemiah 7:33 has "the men of the other Nebo".
[^25]: Magbish is not in Nehemiah 7.
[^26]: Nehemiah 7:37 has 721.
[^27]: Nehemiah 7:38 has 3,930.
[^28]: Nehemiah 7:43 has "the descendants of Jeshua, of Kadmiel, of the descendants of Hodevah".
[^29]: Nehemiah 7:44 has 148.
[^30]: Nehemiah 7:45 has 138.
[^31]: Hebrew *netinim*, "those given", that is, given to the service of the temple. They were temple workers of lower rank than the Levites (8:20).
[^32]: Nehemiah 7:47 has Sia.
[^33]: Akkub, Hagab and Asnah (verse 50) are not in Nehemiah 7, which spells Lebanah and Hagabah as Lebana and Hagaba.
[^34]: The Hebrew text is written "Shamlai" but read "Shalmai", as in Nehemiah 7:48.
[^35]: The Hebrew text is written "Meinim" but read "Meunim".
[^36]: The Hebrew text is written "Nephisim" but read "Nephusim". Nehemiah 7:52 has Nephishesim.
[^37]: Nehemiah 7:54 has Bazlith.
[^38]: Perhaps descendants of the foreign workers Solomon put to forced labor (1 Kings 9:20–21).
[^39]: Nehemiah 7:57 has Sophereth and Perida.
[^40]: Nehemiah 7:58–59 has Jaala and Amon.
[^41]: Nehemiah 7:61 has Addon.
[^42]: Literally "they could not tell their fathers' house and their seed, whether they were from Israel".
[^43]: Nehemiah 7:62 has 642.
[^44]: That is, he took his father-in-law's name. On Barzillai the Gileadite, see 2 Samuel 17:27; 19:32–40 (English 19:31–39).
[^45]: Literally "they were defiled from the priesthood".
[^46]: Hebrew *tirshata*, a Persian title of a high official, perhaps meaning "His Excellency". It is used of Nehemiah in Nehemiah 8:9 and 10:2 (English 10:1).
[^47]: Literally "the holy of holies": the priests' share of the most holy offerings (Leviticus 2:3; 6:10–11, English 6:17–18).
[^48]: Literally "until a priest stood for Urim and for Thummim". These were kept in the high priest's breastpiece and used to learn Yahweh's decision (Exodus 28:30; Numbers 27:21).
[^49]: The numbers given in verses 3–60 add up to 29,818.
[^50]: Nehemiah 7:67 has 245 singers.
[^51]: The Hebrew text of Nehemiah 7 does not have this line on the horses and mules.
[^52]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^53]: Hebrew *darkemonim*: probably darics, Persian gold coins weighing about 0.3 ounce (8.4 g) each; or Greek drachmas.
[^54]: A mina was about 1.25 pounds (0.6 kg).
[^55]: Nehemiah 7:69–71 (English 7:70–72) records the gifts differently.
