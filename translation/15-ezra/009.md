**1** When these things had been done, the leaders came to me and said, "The people of Israel, the priests and the Levites have not separated themselves from the peoples of the lands and their detestable practices,[^1] those of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Jebusites, the Ammonites, the Moabites, the Egyptians and the Amorites.[^2]

**2** For they have taken some of their daughters as wives for themselves and for their sons, and the holy seed[^3] has mixed itself with the peoples of the lands. And the hand of the leaders and officials[^4] has been foremost in this unfaithfulness."[^5]

**3** When I heard this, I tore my garment and my robe, pulled out some of the hair of my head and my beard, and sat down appalled.

**4** Then everyone who trembled at the words of the God of Israel gathered around me because of the unfaithfulness of the exiles, while I sat appalled until the evening offering.[^6]

**5** At the evening offering I got up from my self-humbling,[^7] with my garment and my robe torn, and I fell on my knees and spread out my hands to the LORD[^8] my God.

**6** I said, "My God, I am ashamed and humiliated to lift up my face to you, my God, for our iniquities have risen higher than our heads, and our guilt has grown as high as the heavens.

**7** From the days of our ancestors until this day we have been deep in guilt. Because of our iniquities, we, our kings and our priests have been handed over to the kings of the lands, to the sword, to captivity, to plunder and to open shame,[^9] as it is today.

**8** But now, for a brief moment, the LORD our God has shown us grace by leaving us survivors and giving us a firm place[^10] in his holy place, so that our God may brighten our eyes and give us a little new life in our slavery.

**9** For we are slaves. Yet our God has not forsaken us in our slavery. He has shown us faithful love[^11] before the kings of Persia, giving us new life to set up the house of our God and repair its ruins, and giving us a wall[^12] in Judah and Jerusalem.

**10** And now, our God, what can we say after this? For we have forsaken your commands,

**11** which you gave through your servants the prophets, saying, 'The land that you are entering to possess is a land made impure[^13] by the impurity of the peoples of the lands, by their detestable practices, with which they have filled it from end to end[^14] with their uncleanness.

**12** So now, do not give your daughters to their sons, and do not take their daughters for your sons. Never seek their peace or their prosperity, so that you may be strong and eat the good things of the land, and leave it as an inheritance to your children forever.'[^15]

**13** After all that has come upon us because of our evil deeds and our great guilt, since you, our God, have punished us less than our iniquities deserved[^16] and have given us survivors such as these,

**14** shall we again break your commands and intermarry with the peoples who practice these detestable things? Would you not be angry with us until you had destroyed us, leaving no remnant or survivors?

**15** LORD, God of Israel, you are righteous, for we are left as survivors, as it is today. Here we are before you in our guilt, though because of this no one can stand before you."

[^1]: Hebrew *to'evot*, "abominations, detestable things". Also in verses 11 and 14.
[^2]: Compare Deuteronomy 7:1–4 and 23:4–9 (23:3–8 in most English Bibles).
[^3]: Or "the holy offspring". Compare Isaiah 6:13.
[^4]: Hebrew *seganim*, a title borrowed from Akkadian: local officials or magistrates.
[^5]: Hebrew *ma'al*: a breach of faith, betraying a trust, especially toward God. Also in verse 4.
[^6]: Hebrew *minchah*, "offering", here the offering made at the end of the afternoon. Also in verse 5.
[^7]: Or "my fasting".
[^8]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^9]: Literally "shame of face".
[^10]: Literally "a peg", like a tent peg driven firmly into the ground.
[^11]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Literally "he has extended faithful love to us".
[^12]: Or "a protective wall". Hebrew *gader*, a wall or fence, such as that around a vineyard.
[^13]: Hebrew *niddah*, the word for the impurity of menstruation. Also later in the verse.
[^14]: Literally "from mouth to mouth".
[^15]: Verses 11–12 are not a quotation of a single passage, but they draw on Leviticus 18:24–30, Deuteronomy 7:1–3 and 23:7 (23:6 in most English Bibles).
[^16]: Literally "you have held back below our iniquities".
