**1** Now the prophets, Haggai the prophet and Zechariah son of Iddo,[^1] prophesied to the Jews who were in Judah and Jerusalem, in the name of the God of Israel, who was over them.[^2]

**2** Then Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak set to work and began to build the house of God in Jerusalem, and with them were the prophets of God, helping them.

**3** At that time Tattenai, the governor[^3] of the province Beyond the River,[^4] Shethar-bozenai and their associates came to them and said this to them: "Who issued you a decree[^5] to build this house and to finish this structure?"[^6]

**4** Then we said to them this:[^7] "What are the names of the men who are constructing this building?"

**5** But the eye of their God was on the elders of the Jews, and they did not make them stop until a report[^8] could go to Darius and a letter be sent back in reply.[^9]

**6** This is a copy of the letter that Tattenai, the governor of the province Beyond the River, Shethar-bozenai and his associates, the officials[^10] in the province Beyond the River, sent to King Darius.

**7** They sent him a report, and this is what was written in it: "To King Darius: All peace!

**8** Let it be known to the king that we went to the province of Judah, to the house of the great God. It is being built with huge stones,[^11] and timber is being laid in the walls. This work is being done with diligence and is succeeding in their hands.

**9** Then we asked those elders, and we said this to them: 'Who issued you a decree to build this house and to finish this structure?'

**10** We also asked them their names, to inform you, so that we could write down the names of the men who are their leaders.[^12]

**11** This is the reply they gave us: 'We are the servants of the God of heaven and earth, and we are rebuilding the house that was built many years ago, which a great king of Israel built and finished.

**12** But because our fathers angered the God of heaven, he gave them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, the Chaldean, who destroyed this house and deported the people to Babylon.

**13** However, in the first year of Cyrus king of Babylon, King Cyrus issued a decree to rebuild this house of God.

**14** King Cyrus also took out of the temple of Babylon the articles of gold and silver from the house of God, which Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the temple in Jerusalem and brought to the temple of Babylon. They were given to a man named Sheshbazzar, whom he had appointed governor.

**15** He said to him, "Take these articles. Go and deposit them in the temple in Jerusalem, and let the house of God be rebuilt on its site."

**16** Then this Sheshbazzar came and laid the foundations of the house of God in Jerusalem. From then until now it has been under construction, and it is not yet finished.'

**17** Now, if it pleases the king, let a search be made in the royal treasury[^13] there in Babylon, to see whether a decree was in fact issued by King Cyrus to rebuild this house of God in Jerusalem. And let the king send us his decision on this matter."

[^1]: See Haggai 1:1 and Zechariah 1:1, where Zechariah is "son of Berechiah, son of Iddo". The text of 4:8–6:18 is in Aramaic.
[^2]: Or "in the name of the God of Israel, which was upon them".
[^3]: Aramaic *pechah*, "governor". Also in verses 6 and 14.
[^4]: The Persian province west of the Euphrates; see 4:10.
[^5]: Aramaic *te'em*, "order, decree". Also in verses 9, 13 and 17.
[^6]: The meaning of Aramaic *ushsharna* is uncertain: perhaps "structure", "woodwork" or "sanctuary". Also in verse 9.
[^7]: The Septuagint reads "Then they said to them", making this a second question asked by Tattenai and his associates.
[^8]: Aramaic *te'em*, here "report".
[^9]: Literally "and then they would return a letter about this".
[^10]: Aramaic *apharsekhaye*, a Persian word of uncertain meaning, perhaps "officials" or "inspectors".
[^11]: Literally "stones of rolling", stones so large they had to be rolled into place.
[^12]: Literally "who are at their head".
[^13]: Literally "the house of the treasures of the king".
