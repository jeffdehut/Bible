**1** At the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim[^1] son of Josiah king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from the LORD:[^2]

**2** This is what the LORD said to me: "Make yourself straps and yoke bars, and put them on your neck.

**3** Then send them to the king of Edom, the king of Moab, the king of the Ammonites, the king of Tyre and the king of Sidon, by the envoys who have come to Jerusalem to Zedekiah king of Judah.

**4** Give them a message for their masters: 'This is what the LORD of Armies,[^3] the God of Israel, says: This is what you are to say to your masters:

**5** By my great power and my outstretched arm I made the earth, and the human beings and the animals on the face of the earth, and I give it to whomever I see fit.

**6** Now I have handed all these lands over to my servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.[^4] I have given him even the wild animals to serve him.

**7** All the nations will serve him and his son and his grandson, until the time comes for his own land too. Then many nations and great kings will make him their slave.[^5]

**8** But if any nation or kingdom will not serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon or put its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon, I will punish that nation with the sword, famine and plague, declares the LORD, until I have destroyed it by his hand.

**9** So do not listen to your prophets, your diviners, your dreamers,[^6] your soothsayers or your sorcerers, who keep telling you, "You will not serve the king of Babylon."

**10** For they are prophesying lies to you, so that you will be removed far from your land; I will drive you out, and you will perish.

**11** But the nation that puts its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon and serves him, I will leave on its own land, declares the LORD, to farm it and live there.'"

**12** I spoke the same message to Zedekiah king of Judah: "Put your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon; serve him and his people, and you will live.

**13** Why should you and your people die by the sword, famine and plague, as the LORD has spoken against any nation that will not serve the king of Babylon?

**14** Do not listen to the words of the prophets who say to you, 'You will not serve the king of Babylon,' for they are prophesying lies to you.

**15** I have not sent them, declares the LORD. They are prophesying falsely in my name, so that I will drive you out and you will perish, you and the prophets who prophesy to you."

**16** Then I spoke to the priests and to all this people: "This is what the LORD says: Do not listen to the words of your prophets who prophesy to you, 'Look, the vessels of the LORD's house will very soon be brought back from Babylon,'[^7] for they are prophesying lies to you.

**17** Do not listen to them. Serve the king of Babylon and live. Why should this city become a ruin?

**18** If they are prophets and if the word of the LORD is with them, let them plead with the LORD of Armies that the vessels still left in the house of the LORD, in the palace of the king of Judah and in Jerusalem may not go to Babylon.

**19** For this is what the LORD of Armies says about the pillars, the Sea,[^8] the stands and the rest of the vessels still left in this city,

**20** which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon did not take when he carried Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon, along with all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem:

**21** this is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says about the vessels left in the house of the LORD, in the palace of the king of Judah and in Jerusalem:

**22** They will be taken to Babylon, and there they will stay until the day I attend to them, declares the LORD. Then I will bring them up and restore them to this place."[^9]

[^1]: The chapter concerns the reign of Zedekiah (verses 3 and 12; 28:1), about 594 BC. Some Hebrew manuscripts and the Syriac read "Zedekiah" here. The Septuagint does not have this verse.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. "The LORD's house" and "the house of the LORD" are the temple.
[^3]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verses 18, 19 and 21.
[^4]: In this chapter and the next the Hebrew spells the name *Nevukhadne'tsar*, Nebuchadnezzar, not *Nevukhadre'tsar* as in most of Jeremiah (21:2).
[^5]: The Septuagint does not have verse 7.
[^6]: Literally "your dreams".
[^7]: The temple vessels taken to Babylon with Jehoiachin in 597 BC (2 Kings 24:13).
[^8]: The great bronze basin in the temple court. The pillars, the Sea and the stands were bronze works made for Solomon's temple (1 Kings 7:15–39). Their fate is told in 2 Kings 25:13–17 and Jeremiah 52:17–23.
[^9]: The Septuagint text of verses 19–22 is much shorter, and it does not have the promise that the vessels will be brought back.
