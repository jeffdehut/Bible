**1** At that time, declares Yahweh,[^1] I will be the God of all the clans of Israel, and they will be my people.

**2** This is what Yahweh says: The people who survived the sword found favor in the wilderness, when Israel went to find its rest.[^2]

**3** Yahweh appeared to me[^3] from far away: I have loved you with an everlasting love; therefore I have drawn you with faithful love.[^4]

**4** Again I will build you, and you will be built, virgin Israel. Again you will take up your tambourines and go out in the dances of those who celebrate.

**5** Again you will plant vineyards on the hills of Samaria; the planters will plant and enjoy the fruit.[^5]

**6** For there will be a day when watchmen will call out on the hills of Ephraim, "Come, let us go up to Zion, to Yahweh our God!"[^6]

**7** For this is what Yahweh says: Sing with joy for Jacob; shout for the chief of the nations. Proclaim it, give praise and say, "Save your people, Yahweh, the remnant of Israel!"[^7]

**8** Look, I am going to bring them from the land of the north and gather them from the farthest parts of the earth. Among them will be the blind and the lame, the pregnant woman and the woman in labor together. A great company will return here.

**9** They will come with weeping, and with pleas for mercy I will lead them. I will lead them beside streams of water, on a level path where they will not stumble. For I am a father to Israel, and Ephraim is my firstborn.

**10** Hear the word of Yahweh, you nations, and declare it in the coastlands far away. Say, "He who scattered Israel will gather him and watch over him as a shepherd watches over his flock."

**11** For Yahweh has ransomed Jacob and redeemed him[^8] from the hand of one stronger than he.

**12** They will come and sing for joy on the heights of Zion, and they will be radiant[^9] over the goodness of Yahweh: over the grain, the new wine and the olive oil, and over the young of the flock and the herd. Their life will be like a well-watered garden, and they will never languish again.

**13** Then young women will rejoice in the dance, and young men and old men together. I will turn their mourning into gladness; I will comfort them and give them joy in place of their sorrow.

**14** I will satisfy the priests with abundance,[^10] and my people will be filled with my goodness, declares Yahweh.

**15** This is what Yahweh says: A voice is heard in Ramah,[^11] lamentation and bitter weeping. Rachel is weeping for her children; she refuses to be comforted for her children, because they are no more.[^12]

**16** This is what Yahweh says: Keep your voice from weeping and your eyes from tears, for your work will be rewarded, declares Yahweh. They will return from the land of the enemy.

**17** There is hope for your future, declares Yahweh. Your children will return to their own territory.

**18** I have surely heard Ephraim rocking in grief: "You disciplined me, and I was disciplined, like a calf not trained to the yoke. Bring me back, so that I may come back, for you are Yahweh my God.

**19** For after I turned away, I repented;[^13] after I was made to understand, I struck my thigh.[^14] I was ashamed and humiliated, because I bore the disgrace of my youth."

**20** Is Ephraim my dear son, my delightful child? For whenever I speak against him, I still remember him. Therefore my heart yearns for him;[^15] I will surely have compassion on him, declares Yahweh.

**21** Set up road markers for yourself; put up signposts.[^16] Set your mind on the highway, the road you traveled.[^17] Return, virgin Israel; return to these cities of yours.

**22** How long will you waver, faithless daughter? For Yahweh has created something new on the earth: a woman will surround a man.[^18]

**23** This is what Yahweh of Armies,[^19] the God of Israel, says: Once more they will use these words in the land of Judah and in its cities, when I restore their fortunes:[^20] "May Yahweh bless you, home of righteousness, holy mountain!"

**24** Judah and all its cities will live there together, the farmers and those who move about with the flocks.

**25** For I will refresh the weary and satisfy all who languish.[^21]

**26** At this I woke up and looked, and my sleep had been sweet to me.[^22]

**27** Look, the days are coming, declares Yahweh, when I will sow the house of Israel and the house of Judah with the seed of human beings and the seed of animals.

**28** Just as I watched over them to uproot and to tear down, to overthrow and to destroy and to bring disaster, so I will watch over them to build and to plant, declares Yahweh.[^23]

**29** In those days they will no longer say, "The fathers have eaten sour grapes, and the children's teeth are set on edge."[^24]

**30** Instead, each will die for his own guilt. Everyone who eats the sour grapes, his own teeth will be set on edge.

**31** Look, the days are coming, declares Yahweh, when I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah.[^25]

**32** It will not be like the covenant I made with their ancestors on the day I took them by the hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt, my covenant that they broke, though I was their husband,[^26] declares Yahweh.

**33** For this is the covenant I will make with the house of Israel after those days, declares Yahweh: I will put my law[^27] within them, and I will write it on their hearts. I will be their God, and they will be my people.

**34** No longer will each one teach his neighbor and each his brother, saying, "Know Yahweh," for they will all know me, from the least of them to the greatest, declares Yahweh. For I will forgive their wrongdoing, and I will remember their sin no more.

**35** This is what Yahweh says, who gives the sun for light by day, and decrees the moon and the stars[^28] for light by night, who stirs up the sea so that its waves roar; Yahweh of Armies is his name:[^29]

**36** If these decrees ever depart from before me, declares Yahweh, then the descendants of Israel will also cease to be a nation before me for all time.

**37** This is what Yahweh says: If the heavens above can be measured and the foundations of the earth below can be searched out, then I will also reject all the descendants of Israel for all that they have done, declares Yahweh.

**38** Look, the days are coming,[^30] declares Yahweh, when the city will be rebuilt for Yahweh from the Tower of Hananel to the Corner Gate.[^31]

**39** The measuring line will go out again straight ahead to the hill of Gareb, and then turn to Goah.[^32]

**40** The whole valley of the corpses and the ashes,[^33] and all the terraces as far as the Wadi Kidron, to the corner of the Horse Gate toward the east, will be holy to Yahweh. It will never again be uprooted or torn down.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "going to give him rest, Israel". Or "when I went to give Israel rest". The wilderness journey after the exodus is the model for the return from exile.
[^3]: The speaker is Israel. Or "From long ago Yahweh appeared to me".
[^4]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Or "therefore I have prolonged my faithful love to you".
[^5]: Literally "and treat it as common", that is, eat its fruit as ordinary food. The fruit of a new vineyard was not eaten for its first three years, and the fourth year's fruit was holy (Leviticus 19:23–25).
[^6]: Ephraim was the leading tribe of the northern kingdom, whose people had not worshiped at Jerusalem since the kingdom divided (1 Kings 12:26–33).
[^7]: The Septuagint reads "Yahweh has saved his people".
[^8]: Hebrew *ga'al*, to act as a kinsman who buys back a relative or his property.
[^9]: Or "they will stream".
[^10]: Literally "I will drench the soul of the priests with fat", the rich portions of the sacrifices that went to the priests.
[^11]: A town about 5 miles (8 km) north of Jerusalem. Rachel, the mother of Joseph and Benjamin and so the ancestor of Ephraim, was buried in that region (1 Samuel 10:2). The exiles were gathered at Ramah before they were taken to Babylon (40:1).
[^12]: Literally "because he is not". Matthew 2:18 quotes this verse.
[^13]: Or "relented". Hebrew *shuvi*, "my turning", which can mean turning away or turning back (see 3:12).
[^14]: A gesture of grief or remorse (Ezekiel 21:17, 21:12 in most English Bibles).
[^15]: Literally "my insides groan for him".
[^16]: Hebrew *tamrurim*, the same word as "bitter" in verse 15, here probably "guideposts".
[^17]: The Hebrew text is written "I traveled" but read "you traveled". The written form may be an old spelling of "you traveled" (compare 2:20, 33).
[^18]: The meaning of this line is uncertain. The words for *woman* (*neqevah*) and *man* (*gever*) stress female and male. It may mean that the woman will protect the man, or that she will seek him out, reversing the usual order.
[^19]: *Yahweh of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verse 35.
[^20]: Literally "turn their turning", an idiom for restoring what was lost. Or "bring back their captives".
[^21]: Literally "For I will drench the weary soul, and every languishing soul I will fill".
[^22]: The speaker is probably the prophet, waking from a dream or vision in which he received the words of verses 2–25. The meaning is uncertain.
[^23]: See 1:10–12.
[^24]: A proverb also quoted in Ezekiel 18:2. The children suffer for what the fathers did.
[^25]: Verses 31–34 are quoted in Hebrews 8:8–12, following the Septuagint, and verses 33–34 again in Hebrews 10:16–17. At the Last Supper Jesus speaks of the new covenant in his blood (Luke 22:20; 1 Corinthians 11:25).
[^26]: Or "their master". Hebrew *ba'alti*: see 3:14. The Septuagint, quoted in Hebrews 8:9, reads "and I did not care for them".
[^27]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law".
[^28]: Literally "the decrees of the moon and the stars".
[^29]: The second half of this verse is the same as Isaiah 51:15. The Septuagint has verse 37 before verses 35–36.
[^30]: The word "coming" is read but not written in the Hebrew text.
[^31]: The Tower of Hananel was at the northeast corner of Jerusalem (Nehemiah 3:1; 12:39), and the Corner Gate at the northwest (2 Kings 14:13).
[^32]: Gareb and Goah are otherwise unknown. They probably lay to the west and south of the city.
[^33]: Probably the Valley of Ben-hinnom, south of Jerusalem (7:31–32). The *ashes* are the fat-soaked ashes of sacrifices.
