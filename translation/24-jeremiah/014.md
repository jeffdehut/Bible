**1** The word of the LORD[^1] that came to Jeremiah about the drought:

**2** Judah mourns, and her gates languish; her people sit in black on the ground,[^2] and the cry of Jerusalem goes up.

**3** Their nobles send their servants for water. They come to the cisterns but find no water; they return with their jars empty. They are ashamed and dismayed, and they cover their heads.

**4** Because the ground is cracked, for there has been no rain in the land, the farmers are ashamed; they cover their heads.

**5** Even the doe in the field gives birth and abandons her fawn, because there is no grass.

**6** Wild donkeys stand on the bare heights and pant for air like jackals; their eyes fail because there is no vegetation.

**7** Though our wrongdoings testify against us, LORD, act for the sake of your name.[^3] For our acts of faithlessness are many; we have sinned against you.

**8** Hope of Israel, its Savior in time of trouble, why are you like a stranger in the land, like a traveler who stops only for a night?

**9** Why are you like a man taken by surprise, like a warrior who cannot save? Yet you are among us, LORD, and we bear your name.[^4] Do not abandon us!

**10** This is what the LORD says about this people: How they love to wander! They do not restrain their feet. So the LORD does not accept them; now he will remember their wrongdoing and punish their sins.[^5]

**11** The LORD said to me: Do not pray for the good of this people.

**12** Though they fast, I will not listen to their cry; though they offer burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them. Instead I will make an end of them by sword, famine and plague.

**13** Then I said, "Ah, Lord GOD![^6] Look, the prophets keep telling them, 'You will not see the sword, and you will not have famine. I will give you lasting peace[^7] in this place.'"

**14** The LORD said to me: The prophets are prophesying lies in my name. I did not send them or command them or speak to them. They are prophesying to you false visions, divination, worthless things and the delusions of their own minds.[^8]

**15** Therefore this is what the LORD says about the prophets who prophesy in my name, though I did not send them, and who say, "Sword and famine will not come on this land": By sword and famine those prophets will meet their end.

**16** And the people they are prophesying to will be thrown out into the streets of Jerusalem because of famine and sword, with no one to bury them, them, their wives, their sons and their daughters. I will pour out on them their own wickedness.

**17** Say this word to them: Let my eyes run with tears night and day, and let them not stop, for the virgin daughter of my people[^9] has been struck a crushing blow, a very grievous wound.

**18** If I go out into the field, look, those killed by the sword! If I enter the city, look, those sick with famine! For both prophet and priest wander through a land they do not know.[^10]

**19** Have you utterly rejected Judah? Do you loathe Zion?[^11] Why have you struck us so that there is no healing for us? We hoped for peace, but no good has come; for a time of healing, but there is only terror.

**20** We acknowledge our wickedness, LORD, the wrongdoing of our ancestors, for we have sinned against you.

**21** For the sake of your name, do not spurn us; do not dishonor your glorious throne.[^12] Remember your covenant with us, and do not break it.

**22** Can any of the worthless idols[^13] of the nations bring rain? Can the heavens give showers by themselves? Is it not you, LORD our God? So we hope in you, for you are the one who does all these things.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "they are dark to the ground", that is, dressed in mourning and sitting on the ground.
[^3]: Verses 7–9 are a prayer of lament, in the voice of the people or of the prophet praying for them. Another follows in verses 19–22.
[^4]: Literally "your name is called over us", that is, we belong to you.
[^5]: The second half of this verse is nearly the same as Hosea 8:13.
[^6]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^7]: Literally "peace of truth".
[^8]: Literally "the deceit of their heart".
[^9]: A tender, poetic name for the nation, pictured as a young woman.
[^10]: Or "go about their business in the land, and have no knowledge". The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain.
[^11]: Literally "Does your soul loathe Zion?"
[^12]: That is, Jerusalem and its temple, where the LORD was enthroned (3:17).
[^13]: Literally "the vapors": see 2:5.
