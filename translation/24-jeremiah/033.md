**1** The word of the LORD[^1] came to Jeremiah a second time, while he was still confined in the courtyard of the guard:

**2** This is what the LORD says, who made it, the LORD who formed it to establish it, the LORD is his name:[^2]

**3** Call to me, and I will answer you and tell you great and hidden things[^3] that you have not known.

**4** For this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says about the houses of this city and the houses of the kings of Judah that have been torn down to make defenses against the siege ramps and against the sword:[^4]

**5** They are coming to fight the Babylonians,[^5] only to fill these houses with the corpses of the people I have struck down in my anger and my wrath, for I have hidden my face from this city because of all their evil.

**6** Look, I am going to bring it new flesh and healing. I will heal them and reveal to them an abundance[^6] of peace and faithfulness.

**7** I will restore the fortunes[^7] of Judah and the fortunes of Israel, and I will rebuild them as they were at first.

**8** I will cleanse them from all the guilt of their sin against me, and I will forgive all the guilt of their sin and their rebellion against me.

**9** This city will be to me a name of joy, a praise and a glory before all the nations of the earth, who will hear of all the good I do for them. They will fear and tremble because of all the good and all the peace that I provide for it.

**10** This is what the LORD says: In this place, of which you say, "It is a ruin, without human beings or animals," in the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem that are desolate, without human beings, without inhabitants and without animals, there will be heard again

**11** the sound of joy and the sound of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride,[^8] and the voices of those who say, "Give thanks to the LORD of Armies,[^9] for the LORD is good; his faithful love[^10] endures forever!" as they bring thank offerings to the house of the LORD. For I will restore the fortunes of the land as they were at first, says the LORD.

**12** This is what the LORD of Armies says: In this place that is a ruin, without human beings or animals, and in all its cities, there will again be pasture for shepherds resting their flocks.

**13** In the cities of the hill country, in the cities of the Shephelah and in the cities of the Negev,[^11] in the land of Benjamin, in the places around Jerusalem and in the cities of Judah, flocks will again pass under the hands of the one who counts them, says the LORD.

**14** Look, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will fulfill the good promise I made to the house of Israel and the house of Judah.[^12]

**15** In those days and at that time I will cause a righteous Branch[^13] to sprout for David, and he will do justice and righteousness in the land.

**16** In those days Judah will be saved, and Jerusalem will live in safety. This is the name by which she will be called: The LORD Is Our Righteousness.[^14]

**17** For this is what the LORD says: David will never lack a man to sit on the throne of the house of Israel,[^15]

**18** and the Levitical priests will never lack a man before me to offer burnt offerings, to burn grain offerings and to make sacrifices for all time.

**19** The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:

**20** This is what the LORD says: If you can break my covenant with the day and my covenant with the night, so that day and night no longer come at their appointed time,

**21** then my covenant with David my servant can also be broken, so that he will have no son to reign on his throne, and my covenant with the Levitical priests, my ministers.

**22** As the host of heaven cannot be counted and the sand of the sea cannot be measured, so I will multiply the descendants of David my servant and the Levites who minister to me.

**23** The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:

**24** Have you not seen what this people have been saying: "The two clans that the LORD chose, he has rejected"?[^16] So they despise my people, and no longer regard them as a nation.

**25** This is what the LORD says: If I have not established my covenant with day and night, and the decrees of heaven and earth,

**26** then I will also reject the descendants of Jacob and of David my servant, and will not choose rulers from his descendants over the descendants of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. For I will restore their fortunes and have compassion on them.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: *It* is probably the earth. The Septuagint reads "who made the earth".
[^3]: Literally "fortified things", that is, things out of reach.
[^4]: The Hebrew of verses 4–5 is difficult. Houses were pulled down to strengthen the city walls during the siege (compare Isaiah 22:10).
[^5]: Hebrew *Kasdim*, traditionally "Chaldeans": the people of southern Mesopotamia whose kings then ruled Babylon.
[^6]: Hebrew *ateret*, a rare word, perhaps "abundance" or "riches".
[^7]: Literally "turn the turning", an idiom for restoring what was lost. Or "bring back the captives". Also in verses 11 and 26.
[^8]: The sounds that the LORD had said he would silence (7:34; 16:9; 25:10).
[^9]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verse 12.
[^10]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. The words of thanksgiving are a refrain of the Psalms (Psalm 106:1; 136:1).
[^11]: The Shephelah is the low hills between the hill country of Judah and the coastal plain, and the Negev the dry region in the south of Judah.
[^12]: Verses 14–26 are not in the Septuagint. Verses 14–16 repeat, with some differences, 23:5–6.
[^13]: Hebrew *tsemach*, "sprout, branch", a title of a coming king of David's line (23:5; Zechariah 3:8; 6:12).
[^14]: Hebrew *YHWH tsidqenu*. In 23:6 this is the name of the king; here it is the name of Jerusalem. It may play on the name of King Zedekiah, Hebrew *Tsidqiyyahu*, "The LORD is my righteousness".
[^15]: Compare 2 Samuel 7:16 and 1 Kings 2:4.
[^16]: The two clans are probably Israel and Judah, or perhaps the families of David and of Levi (verses 17–22).
