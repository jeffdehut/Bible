**1** This is what the LORD[^1] says: I am going to stir up a destroying wind[^2] against Babylon and against the people who live in Leb-kamai.[^3]

**2** I will send foreigners to Babylon, and they will winnow her[^4] and empty her land, for they will come against her from every side on the day of disaster.

**3** Against the archer let the archer bend his bow, and against the one who stands up in his armor.[^5] Do not spare her young men; devote her whole army to destruction.[^6]

**4** They will fall slain in the land of the Babylonians,[^7] pierced through in her streets.

**5** For Israel and Judah have not been widowed by their God, by the LORD of Armies,[^8] though their land is full of guilt against the Holy One of Israel.[^9]

**6** Flee from the midst of Babylon! Save your lives, each of you! Do not be destroyed[^10] because of her guilt. For this is the time of the LORD's vengeance; he is repaying her what she deserves.

**7** Babylon was a golden cup in the LORD's hand, making the whole earth drunk. The nations drank her wine; therefore the nations have gone mad.[^11]

**8** Suddenly Babylon has fallen and is shattered.[^12] Wail for her! Get balm for her pain; perhaps she can be healed.

**9** "We tried to heal Babylon, but she could not be healed. Leave her, and let us each go to our own land, for her judgment reaches to the heavens and rises as high as the clouds.[^13]

**10** The LORD has brought our vindication to light.[^14] Come, let us tell in Zion the work of the LORD our God."

**11** Sharpen the arrows! Fill the quivers![^15] The LORD has stirred up the spirit of the kings of the Medes,[^16] because his purpose is to destroy Babylon. For this is the vengeance of the LORD, vengeance for his temple.

**12** Raise a signal flag against the walls of Babylon! Strengthen the watch; post the watchmen; prepare the ambushes! For the LORD has both planned and done what he spoke concerning the inhabitants of Babylon.

**13** You who live by many waters, rich in treasures, your end has come, the measure of your life is cut off.[^17]

**14** The LORD of Armies has sworn by himself: Surely I will fill you with people like a swarm of locusts, and they will raise the shout of victory[^18] over you.

**15** He made the earth by his power; he established the world by his wisdom, and by his understanding he stretched out the heavens.[^19]

**16** When he thunders,[^20] there is a roar of waters in the heavens; he makes the clouds rise from the ends of the earth. He makes lightning for the rain, and brings out the wind from his storehouses.

**17** Every human being is stupid and without knowledge; every goldsmith is put to shame by his idol. For his cast images are a lie; there is no breath in them.

**18** They are worthless, a work of mockery. At the time of their punishment they will perish.

**19** The Portion of Jacob is not like these, for he is the maker of all things, and of the tribe that is his inheritance.[^21] The LORD of Armies is his name.

**20** You are my war club, my weapon for battle:[^22] with you I shatter nations; with you I destroy kingdoms;

**21** with you I shatter the horse and its rider; with you I shatter the chariot and its driver;

**22** with you I shatter man and woman; with you I shatter the old and the young; with you I shatter the young man and the young woman;

**23** with you I shatter the shepherd and his flock; with you I shatter the farmer and his team; with you I shatter governors and deputies.[^23]

**24** I will repay Babylon and all the inhabitants of Babylonia[^24] for all the evil they have done in Zion before your eyes, declares the LORD.

**25** Look, I am against you, destroying mountain, declares the LORD, you who destroy the whole earth. I will stretch out my hand against you, roll you down from the cliffs and make you a burned-out mountain.

**26** No stone will be taken from you for a cornerstone, nor any stone for a foundation, for you will be desolate forever, declares the LORD.

**27** Raise a signal flag in the land! Blow the ram's horn among the nations! Prepare the nations for war against her;[^25] summon against her the kingdoms of Ararat, Minni and Ashkenaz.[^26] Appoint a commander[^27] against her; bring up horses like bristling locusts.[^28]

**28** Prepare the nations for war against her, the kings of the Medes, their governors and all their deputies, and every land under their rule.

**29** The earth quakes and writhes, for the LORD's purposes against Babylon stand: to make the land of Babylon a desolation, with no one living in it.

**30** Babylon's warriors have stopped fighting; they stay in their strongholds. Their strength has failed; they have become like women. Her dwellings are set on fire; the bars of her gates are broken.

**31** Runner runs to meet runner, and messenger to meet messenger, to tell the king of Babylon that his city has been captured from end to end,

**32** the fords have been seized, the marshes[^29] set on fire, and the soldiers are terrified.

**33** For this is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: Daughter Babylon is like a threshing floor at the time it is trodden; in a little while the time of her harvest will come.

**34** "Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has devoured me;[^30] he has crushed me; he has set me down like an empty jar. He has swallowed me like a sea monster;[^31] he has filled his belly with my delicacies and rinsed me out."[^32]

**35** "May the violence done to me and to my flesh[^33] be on Babylon," the people of Zion will say. "May my blood be on the inhabitants of Babylonia," Jerusalem will say.

**36** Therefore this is what the LORD says: I am going to plead your cause and take vengeance for you. I will dry up her sea[^34] and make her spring run dry.

**37** Babylon will become a heap of ruins, a haunt of jackals, a horror and a thing to hiss at, with no one living there.

**38** They roar together like young lions; they growl like lion cubs.

**39** While they are heated, I will set out a feast for them and make them drunk, so that they celebrate, then sleep a perpetual sleep and never wake, declares the LORD.

**40** I will bring them down like lambs to the slaughter, like rams with male goats.

**41** How Sheshach[^35] is captured, the praise of the whole earth seized! How Babylon has become a horror among the nations!

**42** The sea has risen over Babylon; she is covered with its roaring waves.

**43** Her cities have become a desolation, a dry land and a desert, a land where no one lives, through which no human being passes.

**44** I will punish Bel[^36] in Babylon and make him spit out what he has swallowed.[^37] The nations will no longer stream to him. Even the wall of Babylon has fallen.

**45** Go out from her midst, my people! Save your lives, each of you, from the fierce anger of the LORD![^38]

**46** Do not lose heart or be afraid at the rumors heard in the land, when one rumor comes this year and another the next, with violence in the land and ruler against ruler.

**47** Therefore, look, the days are coming when I will punish the idols of Babylon. Her whole land will be put to shame, and all her slain will fall in her midst.

**48** Then heaven and earth and all that is in them will shout for joy over Babylon, for the destroyers will come against her from the north, declares the LORD.

**49** Babylon must fall because of the slain of Israel, as the slain of all the earth have fallen because of Babylon.

**50** You who have escaped the sword, go! Do not stand still! Remember the LORD from far away, and let Jerusalem come to your mind.

**51** "We are ashamed, for we have heard insults; disgrace has covered our faces, for foreigners have entered the holy places of the LORD's house."[^39]

**52** Therefore, look, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will punish her idols, and throughout her land the wounded will groan.

**53** Though Babylon should climb to the heavens, and though she should fortify her lofty stronghold, destroyers will come against her from me, declares the LORD.

**54** Listen! A cry from Babylon, the sound of great destruction from the land of the Babylonians!

**55** For the LORD is destroying Babylon and silencing her great noise. Their waves roar like many waters; the sound of their voice resounds.

**56** For a destroyer has come against her, against Babylon. Her warriors are captured; their bows are shattered. For the LORD is a God of retribution; he will surely repay.

**57** I will make her officials and her wise men drunk, her governors, her deputies and her warriors; they will sleep a perpetual sleep and never wake, declares the King, whose name is the LORD of Armies.

**58** This is what the LORD of Armies says: The thick wall of Babylon[^40] will be leveled to the ground, and her high gates set on fire. The peoples exhaust themselves for nothing; the nations wear themselves out only for the fire.[^41]

**59** This is the command that Jeremiah the prophet gave to Seraiah son of Neriah son of Mahseiah, when he went with Zedekiah king of Judah to Babylon in the fourth year of his reign.[^42] Seraiah was the quartermaster.[^43]

**60** Jeremiah had written on one scroll all the disaster that would come on Babylon, all these words that are written concerning Babylon.

**61** Jeremiah said to Seraiah, "When you come to Babylon, see that you read all these words aloud.

**62** Then say, 'LORD, you have said concerning this place that you will cut it off, so that no one will live in it, neither human beings nor animals, for it will be desolate forever.'

**63** When you finish reading this scroll, tie a stone to it and throw it into the middle of the Euphrates.

**64** Then say, 'So will Babylon sink and never rise again, because of the disaster I am going to bring on her. And they will grow weary.'"[^44] Thus far the words of Jeremiah.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Or "the spirit of a destroyer". Hebrew *ruach*, "wind, spirit".
[^3]: *Leb-kamai*, "the heart of those who rise against me", is a code name for *Kasdim*, Chaldea, made by the cipher *atbash* (see 25:26), in which each letter is replaced by the letter in the same place counting from the end of the alphabet. So *k-s-d-y* becomes *l-b-q-m-y*. The Septuagint reads "the Chaldeans".
[^4]: A wordplay: *zarim*, "foreigners", and *zeruha*, "they will winnow her". The same consonants can be read *zorim*, "winnowers".
[^5]: The Hebrew of these lines is difficult. Some scholars read *al*, "Let not the archer bend his bow, and let him not stand up in his armor", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^6]: Hebrew *charam*: see 25:9.
[^7]: Hebrew *Kasdim*, traditionally "Chaldeans": the people of southern Mesopotamia whose kings then ruled Babylon. Also in verses 24, 35 and 54.
[^8]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verses 14, 19, 33, 57 and 58.
[^9]: *Their land* may be the land of Israel and Judah, or the land of the Babylonians.
[^10]: Or "Do not be silenced".
[^11]: Compare 25:15–29. Revelation 17:2–4 and 18:3 take up this picture.
[^12]: Compare Isaiah 21:9 and Revelation 14:8; 18:2.
[^13]: The speakers in verses 9–10 are probably the foreigners living in Babylon, and then the exiles of Judah.
[^14]: Literally "has brought out our righteousnesses".
[^15]: Hebrew *shelatim*, perhaps "quivers" or "shields".
[^16]: The Medes, from what is now northwestern Iran, joined the Persians under Cyrus, who took Babylon in 539 BC. Also in verse 28.
[^17]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "the cubit of your cutting off", perhaps the length of your life, like cloth cut from the loom (Isaiah 38:12).
[^18]: Hebrew *hedad*, the shout of grape treaders (25:30; 48:33).
[^19]: Verses 15–19 repeat 10:12–16.
[^20]: Literally "At the sound of his giving".
[^21]: Literally "and the tribe of his inheritance". 10:16 reads "and Israel is the tribe of his inheritance".
[^22]: *You* may be Babylon, the LORD's instrument until now, or the conqueror who is coming against her. The verb *naphats*, "shatter", is repeated nine times in verses 20–23.
[^23]: Hebrew *pachot* and *seganim*, titles of officials in the Babylonian and Persian empires. Also in verses 28 and 57.
[^24]: Hebrew *Kasdim*, here meaning the land. Also in verse 35.
[^25]: Literally "Consecrate nations against her", from the religious rites that opened a campaign (6:4). Also in verse 28.
[^26]: Ararat (Urartu), Minni (the Mannaeans) and Ashkenaz (probably the Scythians) were peoples of the mountains north of Mesopotamia.
[^27]: Hebrew *tifsar*, a word borrowed from the Babylonian language, "official".
[^28]: Hebrew *yeleq samar*, perhaps locusts at a stage when their wings are still sheathed.
[^29]: Hebrew *agammim*, "reed marshes", perhaps the reed thickets or defenses along the river.
[^30]: The Hebrew text is written "us" throughout this verse but read "me". Jerusalem speaks in verses 34–35.
[^31]: Hebrew *tannin*, a sea monster or dragon.
[^32]: Or "spewed me out".
[^33]: Literally "my violence and my flesh".
[^34]: Her *sea* may be the Euphrates with its canals.
[^35]: *Sheshach* is a code name for Babylon made by the cipher *atbash*: see 25:26 and verse 1.
[^36]: A title of Marduk, the chief god of Babylon (50:2).
[^37]: The Septuagint does not have the rest of verse 44, or verses 45–48 and the first half of verse 49.
[^38]: Compare Revelation 18:4.
[^39]: The exiles answer in this verse.
[^40]: Literally "the walls of Babylon, the broad".
[^41]: Habakkuk 2:13 has nearly the same words.
[^42]: In 594–593 BC. Seraiah was the brother of Baruch son of Neriah (32:12).
[^43]: Hebrew *sar menuchah*, "officer of the resting place", perhaps the officer in charge of the stopping places on the journey.
[^44]: These words repeat the end of verse 58. They seem to have been copied here to mark where the words of Jeremiah end.
