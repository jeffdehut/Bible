**1** When Jeremiah had finished speaking to all the people all the words of the LORD[^1] their God, all these words that the LORD their God had sent him to tell them,

**2** Azariah son of Hoshaiah, Johanan son of Kareah and all the arrogant men said to Jeremiah, "You are telling a lie! The LORD our God has not sent you to say, 'Do not go to Egypt to live there.'

**3** Baruch son of Neriah is inciting you against us, to hand us over to the Babylonians,[^2] so that they will kill us or take us into exile in Babylon."

**4** So Johanan son of Kareah, all the commanders of the armed forces and all the people did not obey the voice of the LORD to stay in the land of Judah.

**5** Instead, Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the armed forces took the whole remnant of Judah, who had come back to live in the land of Judah from all the nations where they had been scattered:

**6** the men, the women, the children, the king's daughters and everyone whom Nebuzaradan the commander of the guard[^3] had left with Gedaliah son of Ahikam son of Shaphan, and also Jeremiah the prophet and Baruch son of Neriah.

**7** They came into the land of Egypt, for they did not obey the voice of the LORD, and they went as far as Tahpanhes.[^4]

**8** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah in Tahpanhes:

**9** "Take some large stones in your hands and bury them in the mortar of the brick pavement[^5] at the entrance of Pharaoh's palace[^6] in Tahpanhes, while the men of Judah watch.

**10** Then say to them: 'This is what the LORD of Armies,[^7] the God of Israel, says: I am going to send for my servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and I will set his throne over these stones I have buried, and he will spread his canopy[^8] over them.

**11** He will come and strike the land of Egypt, giving over to death those destined for death, to captivity those destined for captivity, and to the sword those destined for the sword.[^9]

**12** I will set fire to the temples of the gods of Egypt, and he will burn them and carry their gods away captive. He will wrap himself in the land of Egypt as a shepherd wraps himself in his cloak,[^10] and he will go away from there in peace.

**13** He will smash the sacred pillars of Beth-shemesh[^11] in the land of Egypt, and he will burn the temples of the gods of Egypt with fire.'"

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *Kasdim*, traditionally "Chaldeans": the people of southern Mesopotamia whose kings then ruled Babylon.
[^3]: Hebrew *rav-tabbachim*, literally "chief of the butchers" or "chief of the cooks", a title of the commander of the royal bodyguard.
[^4]: A fortress city in the eastern Nile delta (2:16).
[^5]: The meaning of the Hebrew words *melet*, perhaps "mortar" or "clay", and *malben*, perhaps "brickwork" or "brick terrace", is uncertain.
[^6]: Literally "Pharaoh's house", perhaps a royal residence or government building.
[^7]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^8]: Hebrew *shafrir*, a rare word, perhaps a royal canopy or carpet.
[^9]: Compare 15:2. Nebuchadnezzar campaigned against Egypt in 568–567 BC.
[^10]: Or "He will pick the land of Egypt clean as a shepherd picks lice from his cloak". The meaning of the verb *atah* is uncertain here.
[^11]: "House of the Sun", that is, Heliopolis (Egyptian *On*), the city of the sun god, near modern Cairo. Its *pillars* were probably obelisks.
