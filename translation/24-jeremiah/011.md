**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh:[^1]

**2** Hear the words of this covenant, and speak them to the people of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.[^2]

**3** Say to them: This is what Yahweh, the God of Israel, says: Cursed is anyone who does not listen to the words of this covenant,

**4** which I commanded your ancestors when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the iron-smelting furnace: "Listen to my voice and do them, everything I command you. Then you will be my people, and I will be your God,"

**5** so that I may keep the oath I swore to your ancestors, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as it is today. I answered, "Amen,[^3] Yahweh."

**6** Yahweh said to me: Proclaim all these words in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem: Hear the words of this covenant and do them.

**7** For I solemnly warned your ancestors when I brought them up from the land of Egypt, and I have warned them again and again[^4] until this day, saying, "Listen to my voice."

**8** But they did not listen or pay attention; each of them followed the stubbornness of his evil heart. So I brought on them all the words of this covenant, which I had commanded them to do, but they did not do them.[^5]

**9** Yahweh said to me: A conspiracy has been found among the people of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

**10** They have gone back to the wrongdoings of their ancestors of old, who refused to listen to my words. They too have followed other gods to serve them. The house of Israel and the house of Judah have broken my covenant that I made with their ancestors.

**11** Therefore this is what Yahweh says: I am going to bring on them a disaster they cannot escape. They will cry out to me, but I will not listen to them.

**12** Then the cities of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem will go and cry out to the gods they burn incense to,[^6] but these will not save them at all in the time of their trouble.

**13** For you have as many gods as you have cities, Judah, and as many altars as Jerusalem has streets you have set up to the Shameful Thing,[^7] altars to burn incense to Baal.

**14** As for you, do not pray for this people, or raise a cry or a prayer for them, for I will not listen when they call to me because of their trouble.

**15** What right does my beloved have in my house, when she has carried out so many evil schemes? Can sacred meat take away your disaster from you? Then you could rejoice.[^8]

**16** Yahweh once named you "A leafy olive tree, beautiful with well-formed fruit." But with the roar of a great storm he will set fire to it, and its branches will be broken.[^9]

**17** Yahweh of Armies,[^10] who planted you, has pronounced disaster against you, because of the evil that the house of Israel and the house of Judah have done, provoking me to anger by burning incense to Baal.

**18** Yahweh made it known to me, and I knew it.[^11] Then you showed me what they were doing.

**19** But I was like a trusting lamb led to the slaughter. I did not know it was against me they had made their plans: "Let us destroy the tree with its fruit;[^12] let us cut him off from the land of the living, so that his name will be remembered no more."

**20** But Yahweh of Armies, who judges righteously, who tests the heart and mind,[^13] let me see your vengeance on them, for to you I have committed my cause.

**21** Therefore this is what Yahweh says about the people of Anathoth, who seek your life and say, "Do not prophesy in the name of Yahweh, or you will die by our hand":

**22** therefore this is what Yahweh of Armies says: I am going to punish them. The young men will die by the sword; their sons and their daughters will die by famine.

**23** Not even a remnant will be left to them, for I will bring disaster on the people of Anathoth, the year of their punishment.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: The commands in this verse are plural.
[^3]: Hebrew *amen*, "So be it", the people's answer to the curses of the covenant in Deuteronomy 27:15–26.
[^4]: Literally "rising early and warning".
[^5]: The Septuagint does not have verse 7 and most of verse 8. It reads only "but they did not do them".
[^6]: Or "burn offerings to". The Hebrew verb *qatar* means to make something go up in smoke. Also in verses 13 and 17.
[^7]: Hebrew *ha-boshet*, "the shame", a scornful name for Baal.
[^8]: The Hebrew of this verse is very difficult, and the Septuagint differs from it. The translation is uncertain.
[^9]: Or "will be ruined".
[^10]: *Yahweh of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verses 20 and 22.
[^11]: Here begins the first of Jeremiah's personal laments, often called his "confessions" (11:18–12:6; 15:10–21; 17:14–18; 18:18–23; 20:7–18). Jeremiah speaks to Yahweh about the plot against him by the people of his own town, Anathoth (verse 21).
[^12]: Literally "with its bread".
[^13]: Literally "the kidneys and the heart". In Hebrew thought the kidneys were the seat of the emotions and the heart of thought and will.
