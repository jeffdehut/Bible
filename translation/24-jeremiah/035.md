**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh[^1] in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah:[^2]

**2** "Go to the house of the Rechabites[^3] and speak with them. Bring them to the house of Yahweh, into one of the rooms, and give them wine to drink."

**3** So I took Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah son of Habazziniah, his brothers and all his sons, the whole house of the Rechabites.

**4** I brought them into the house of Yahweh, into the room of the sons of Hanan son of Igdaliah, the man of God.[^4] It was next to the room of the officials, above the room of Maaseiah son of Shallum, the keeper of the threshold.[^5]

**5** Then I set before the men of the house of the Rechabites bowls full of wine, and cups, and I said to them, "Drink wine."

**6** But they said, "We will not drink wine, for Jonadab son of Rechab, our ancestor,[^6] commanded us, 'You must never drink wine, neither you nor your children.

**7** You must not build houses, sow seed or plant vineyards, or own any of these. Instead you must live in tents all your days, so that you may live a long time in the land where you are staying as foreigners.'

**8** We have obeyed the voice of Jonadab son of Rechab, our ancestor, in all that he commanded us. We have not drunk wine all our days, we, our wives, our sons or our daughters,

**9** and we have not built houses to live in. We have no vineyard or field or seed.

**10** We have lived in tents, and we have obeyed and done everything that Jonadab our ancestor commanded us.

**11** But when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up against the land, we said, 'Come, let us go into Jerusalem to escape the army of the Babylonians[^7] and the army of the Arameans.' So we are living in Jerusalem."

**12** Then the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah:

**13** "This is what Yahweh of Armies,[^8] the God of Israel, says: Go and say to the people of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem: 'Will you not accept discipline and listen to my words? declares Yahweh.

**14** The words of Jonadab son of Rechab, who commanded his descendants not to drink wine, have been kept. They have not drunk wine to this day, because they have obeyed their ancestor's command. But I have spoken to you again and again,[^9] and you have not listened to me.

**15** I have sent you all my servants the prophets, sending them again and again, to say: Turn now, each of you, from his evil way, and make your deeds good. Do not follow other gods to serve them. Then you will live in the land I gave to you and your ancestors. But you did not pay attention or listen to me.[^10]

**16** The descendants of Jonadab son of Rechab have kept the command their ancestor gave them, but this people has not listened to me.

**17** Therefore this is what Yahweh, the God of Armies, the God of Israel, says:[^11] I am going to bring on Judah and on all the inhabitants of Jerusalem all the disaster I have pronounced against them, because I spoke to them, but they did not listen, and I called to them, but they did not answer.'"

**18** To the house of the Rechabites Jeremiah said, "This is what Yahweh of Armies, the God of Israel, says: Because you have obeyed the command of Jonadab your ancestor, and kept all his commands, and done everything he commanded you,

**19** therefore this is what Yahweh of Armies, the God of Israel, says: Jonadab son of Rechab will never lack a man to stand before me for all time."[^12]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. "The house of Yahweh" is the temple.
[^2]: Jehoiakim reigned from 609 to 598 BC. The events of this chapter probably took place near the end of his reign (verse 11; 2 Kings 24:1–2).
[^3]: A clan descended from Rechab, related to the Kenites (1 Chronicles 2:55). Their ancestor Jonadab had supported Jehu in wiping out the worship of Baal (2 Kings 10:15–27). The *rooms* were chambers in the buildings around the temple courts.
[^4]: That is, a prophet. *The sons of Hanan* may be his disciples.
[^5]: One of three senior officials of the temple who guarded its entrances (52:24; 2 Kings 25:18).
[^6]: Literally "our father". The Hebrew spells his name both *Yonadav* (Jonadab) and *Yehonadav* (Jehonadab, as in 2 Kings 10:15). Also in verses 8, 10, 14, 16, 18 and 19.
[^7]: Hebrew *Kasdim*, traditionally "Chaldeans": the people of southern Mesopotamia whose kings then ruled Babylon.
[^8]: *Yahweh of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verses 18–19.
[^9]: Literally "rising early and speaking". Also "rising early and sending" in verse 15.
[^10]: Literally "you did not incline your ear".
[^11]: Hebrew *YHWH Elohei tseva'ot*, traditionally "the LORD God of hosts".
[^12]: To *stand before* Yahweh is to serve him, as priests and prophets did (15:19; 1 Kings 17:1).
