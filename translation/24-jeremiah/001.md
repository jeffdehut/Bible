**1** The words of Jeremiah son of Hilkiah, one of the priests in Anathoth[^1] in the land of Benjamin.

**2** The word of Yahweh[^2] came to him in the days of Josiah son of Amon king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.[^3]

**3** It came also in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah, until the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah king of Judah, until Jerusalem went into exile in the fifth month.[^4]

**4** The word of Yahweh came to me:

**5** "Before I formed you in the womb I knew you, and before you came out of the womb I set you apart.[^5] I appointed you a prophet to the nations."

**6** I said, "Ah, Lord Yahweh![^6] I don't know how to speak, for I am only a boy."[^7]

**7** But Yahweh said to me, "Do not say, 'I am only a boy.' You will go to everyone I send you to, and you will say whatever I command you.

**8** Do not be afraid of them, for I am with you to rescue you, declares Yahweh."[^8]

**9** Then Yahweh stretched out his hand and touched my mouth, and Yahweh said to me, "Look, I have put my words in your mouth.

**10** See, today I appoint you over nations and over kingdoms, to uproot and to tear down, to destroy and to overthrow, to build and to plant."[^9]

**11** The word of Yahweh came to me: "What do you see, Jeremiah?" I said, "I see a branch of an almond tree."[^10]

**12** Yahweh said to me, "You have seen well, for I am watching over my word to carry it out."

**13** The word of Yahweh came to me a second time: "What do you see?" I said, "I see a boiling pot, and it is tipped away from the north."[^11]

**14** Yahweh said to me, "From the north disaster will be let loose on all who live in the land.

**15** For I am about to summon all the clans of the kingdoms of the north, declares Yahweh. They will come, and each of them will set up his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem, against all its walls around it, and against all the cities of Judah.

**16** I will pronounce my judgments on my people[^12] for all their evil, because they have abandoned me. They have burned incense[^13] to other gods and bowed down to the works of their own hands.

**17** But you, get ready for action.[^14] Stand up and tell them everything I command you. Do not be shattered with fear before them, or I will shatter you before them.[^15]

**18** And I, look, today I have made you a fortified city, an iron pillar and bronze walls against the whole land: against the kings of Judah, its officials, its priests and the people of the land.[^16]

**19** They will fight against you, but they will not overcome you, for I am with you, declares Yahweh, to rescue you."

[^1]: A town of priests about 3 miles (5 km) northeast of Jerusalem (Joshua 21:18; 1 Kings 2:26).
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: In 627 BC.
[^4]: In the summer of 587 or 586 BC (2 Kings 25:8–11; Jeremiah 52:12–15).
[^5]: Or "consecrated you". Literally "made you holy".
[^6]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^7]: Hebrew *na'ar*, a word used of a child, a youth or a young man.
[^8]: Hebrew *ne'um YHWH*, "the declaration of Yahweh", a formula that marks a prophet's words as Yahweh's own solemn statement. It is frequent in Jeremiah.
[^9]: These verbs recur through the book as a summary of Jeremiah's message (18:7–9; 24:6; 31:28; 42:10; 45:4).
[^10]: A wordplay. Hebrew *shaqed*, "almond tree", sounds like *shoqed*, "watching", in verse 12. The almond is the first tree to blossom in late winter, as though it were awake while the others sleep.
[^11]: Literally "and its face is from the face of the north". The pot's mouth faces south, so its contents will pour out from the north over Judah (verse 14).
[^12]: Literally "on them".
[^13]: Or "burned offerings". The Hebrew verb *qatar* means to make something go up in smoke.
[^14]: Literally "gird up your loins", that is, tuck the long robe into the belt, ready for action.
[^15]: The Hebrew uses the same verb, *chatat*, "to be shattered, dismayed", in both lines.
[^16]: Hebrew *am ha-arets*, the landowning citizens of Judah.
