**1** You are always in the right, LORD,[^1] when I bring a case against you.[^2] Yet I would speak with you about justice: Why does the way of the wicked prosper? Why do all the treacherous live at ease?

**2** You planted them, and they have taken root; they grow and bear fruit. You are near in their mouths but far from their hearts.[^3]

**3** But you, LORD, know me; you see me and test my heart toward you. Drag them off like sheep to be slaughtered; set them apart for the day of killing.

**4** How long will the land mourn and the grass of every field wither? Because of the wickedness of those who live in it, the animals and the birds are swept away. For they have said, "He will not see what becomes of us."

**5** If you have raced with men on foot and they have worn you out, how will you compete with horses?[^4] If you feel secure only in a peaceful land, how will you manage in the thickets of the Jordan?[^5]

**6** For even your brothers and your own family,[^6] even they have betrayed you; even they are shouting loudly after you. Do not trust them, even when they speak kindly to you.

**7** I have abandoned my house; I have deserted my inheritance.[^7] I have given the one I dearly love[^8] into the hands of her enemies.

**8** My inheritance has become to me like a lion in the forest. She has roared at me; therefore I hate her.

**9** Has my inheritance become to me like a speckled bird of prey, with birds of prey all around her? Go, gather all the wild animals; bring them to devour.

**10** Many shepherds have ruined my vineyard; they have trampled down my portion. They have turned my pleasant portion into a desolate wilderness.

**11** They have made it a desolation; desolate, it mourns before me. The whole land has been made desolate, but no one takes it to heart.

**12** Over all the bare heights in the wilderness destroyers have come, for the sword of the LORD devours from one end of the land to the other. No living thing[^9] has peace.

**13** They have sown wheat but reaped thorns; they have worn themselves out but gained nothing. Be ashamed of your harvests,[^10] because of the fierce anger of the LORD.

**14** This is what the LORD says about all my evil neighbors who strike at the inheritance I gave my people Israel: I am going to uproot them from their land, and I will uproot the house of Judah from among them.

**15** But after I have uprooted them, I will again have compassion on them, and I will bring them back, each one to his inheritance and each one to his land.

**16** And if they diligently learn the ways of my people, to swear by my name, "As surely as the LORD lives," just as they taught my people to swear by Baal, then they will be built up among my people.

**17** But if any nation will not listen, I will completely uproot that nation and destroy it, declares the LORD.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *riv*, to bring a case against someone, as in a court of law. Jeremiah's lament, which began in 11:18, continues to verse 4.
[^3]: Literally "far from their kidneys", thought of as the seat of the emotions.
[^4]: The LORD answers Jeremiah in verses 5–6: worse is still to come.
[^5]: Literally "in the pride of the Jordan", the dense thickets along the river, where lions lived (49:19).
[^6]: Literally "your father's house".
[^7]: The LORD speaks in verses 7–13. His *inheritance* is his land and his people.
[^8]: Literally "the beloved of my soul".
[^9]: Literally "No flesh".
[^10]: Or "They will be ashamed of their harvests". The Hebrew moves from "they" to "your".
