**1** Then Yahweh[^1] said to me: Even if Moses and Samuel stood before me,[^2] my heart would not turn toward this people.[^3] Send them away from my presence, and let them go!

**2** And if they ask you, "Where shall we go?" tell them: This is what Yahweh says: Those destined for death, to death; those destined for the sword, to the sword; those destined for famine, to famine; and those destined for captivity, to captivity.

**3** I will appoint over them four kinds of destroyers,[^4] declares Yahweh: the sword to kill, the dogs to drag away, and the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth to devour and destroy.

**4** I will make them a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth, because of what Manasseh son of Hezekiah king of Judah did in Jerusalem.[^5]

**5** Who will have pity on you, Jerusalem? Who will grieve for you? Who will turn aside to ask how you are?

**6** You have rejected me, declares Yahweh; you keep going backward. So I have stretched out my hand against you and destroyed you; I am tired of relenting.

**7** I have winnowed them with a winnowing fork in the gates of the land. I have bereaved them, I have destroyed my people, for they did not turn back from their ways.

**8** Their widows have become more numerous before me than the sand of the seas. At noon I have brought a destroyer against the mothers of young men;[^6] I have suddenly brought down on them anguish and terror.

**9** The mother of seven grows faint; she breathes out her life. Her sun has set while it is still day; she is shamed and humiliated. And the rest of them I will give to the sword before their enemies, declares Yahweh.

**10** Woe is me, my mother, that you gave birth to me, a man of strife and contention to the whole land![^7] I have not lent, and no one has lent to me, yet all of them curse me.

**11** Yahweh said: Surely I will set you free for good.[^8] Surely I will make the enemy plead with you in the time of disaster and the time of distress.

**12** Can anyone break iron, iron from the north, and bronze?

**13** Your wealth and your treasures I will give away as plunder, without price, because of all your sins throughout all your territory.[^9]

**14** I will make you go with your enemies[^10] into a land you do not know, for a fire has been kindled by my anger; it will burn against you.

**15** You know, Yahweh. Remember me and take care of me, and take vengeance for me on my persecutors. Do not let your patience with them sweep me away.[^11] Know that for your sake I bear insult.

**16** When your words were found, I ate them, and your word[^12] became a joy to me and the delight of my heart, for I bear your name,[^13] Yahweh, God of Armies.[^14]

**17** I did not sit in the company of those who make merry, or celebrate with them. Because your hand was on me I sat alone, for you had filled me with indignation.

**18** Why is my pain unending, and my wound incurable, refusing to be healed? Will you truly be to me like a deceptive brook, like waters that cannot be relied on?[^15]

**19** Therefore this is what Yahweh says: If you turn back, I will bring you back,[^16] and you will stand before me. If you bring out what is precious and not what is worthless, you will be my mouth. Let them turn to you, but you must not turn to them.

**20** I will make you to this people a fortified wall of bronze. They will fight against you, but they will not overcome you, for I am with you to save you and rescue you, declares Yahweh.

**21** I will rescue you from the hand of the wicked and redeem you from the grasp of the ruthless.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Moses and Samuel were remembered for praying for the people (Exodus 32:11–14; Numbers 14:13–20; 1 Samuel 7:5–9; 12:19–23; Psalm 99:6).
[^3]: Literally "my soul would not be toward this people".
[^4]: Literally "four families".
[^5]: See 2 Kings 21:1–16; 23:26; 24:3–4.
[^6]: Literally "against the mother, the young man". The meaning is uncertain.
[^7]: Here begins another of Jeremiah's personal laments (15:10–21; see 11:18).
[^8]: The Hebrew of this verse is difficult, and the written and read forms of the verb differ slightly. It may mean "I will strengthen you for good".
[^9]: Verses 13–14 are repeated, with small differences, in 17:3–4.
[^10]: Literally "I will make pass your enemies". Some Hebrew manuscripts read "I will make you serve your enemies", as in 17:4.
[^11]: Literally "Do not, in the length of your anger, take me away", that is, do not wait so long to act against them that I am lost.
[^12]: The Hebrew text is written "your words" but read "your word".
[^13]: Literally "your name is called over me", that is, I belong to you.
[^14]: Hebrew *YHWH Elohei tseva'ot*, traditionally "the LORD God of hosts". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^15]: Hebrew *akhzav*, a stream that runs dry in the summer, failing the traveler who counts on it. Compare 2:13.
[^16]: A wordplay on *shuv*, "turn, return" (see 3:12): Jeremiah too must turn back to Yahweh.
