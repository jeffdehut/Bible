**1** Roam through the streets of Jerusalem; look around and take note. Search her squares to see if you can find one person,[^1] anyone who does justice and seeks to be faithful, so that I may forgive her.

**2** Though they say, "As surely as Yahweh[^2] lives," they are still swearing falsely.

**3** Yahweh, do your eyes not look for faithfulness? You struck them, but they felt no pain; you crushed them, but they refused to accept discipline. They made their faces harder than rock; they refused to turn back.

**4** I thought, "These are only the poor. They are foolish, for they do not know the way of Yahweh, what their God requires.[^3]

**5** I will go to the great men and speak to them, for surely they know the way of Yahweh, what their God requires." But they too, all of them, had broken the yoke and torn off the bonds.

**6** So a lion from the forest will strike them down, a wolf from the desert[^4] will destroy them, and a leopard is watching[^5] their cities. Everyone who goes out of them will be torn to pieces, for their rebellions are many, their acts of faithlessness[^6] are numerous.

**7** How can I forgive you for this? Your children have abandoned me and sworn by gods that are not gods. I fed them until they were full, and they committed adultery and thronged[^7] to the house of a prostitute.

**8** They are well-fed, lusty stallions;[^8] each one neighs for his neighbor's wife.

**9** Should I not punish them for these things? declares Yahweh. Should I not avenge myself[^9] on a nation like this?

**10** Go up through her vineyard rows and destroy, but do not make a full end. Strip away her branches, for they do not belong to Yahweh.

**11** For the house of Israel and the house of Judah have been utterly unfaithful to me, declares Yahweh.

**12** They have denied Yahweh and said, "He will do nothing![^10] No disaster will come on us; we will not see sword or famine.

**13** The prophets are only wind;[^11] the word is not in them. So may it be done to them!"[^12]

**14** Therefore this is what Yahweh, the God of Armies,[^13] says: Because you people have spoken this word, I am going to make my words in your mouth[^14] a fire, and this people wood, and it will consume them.

**15** I am going to bring against you a nation from far away, house of Israel, declares Yahweh. It is an enduring nation, an ancient nation, a nation whose language you do not know, and you will not understand what they say.[^15]

**16** Their quiver is like an open grave; all of them are warriors.

**17** They will devour your harvest and your food; they will devour your sons and your daughters. They will devour your flocks and your herds; they will devour your vines and your fig trees. With the sword they will shatter the fortified cities you trust in.

**18** Yet even in those days, declares Yahweh, I will not make a full end of you.

**19** And when you ask, "Why has Yahweh our God done all these things to us?" you[^16] are to say to them, "As you have abandoned me and served foreign gods in your land, so you will serve strangers in a land that is not yours."

**20** Declare this in the house of Jacob, and proclaim it in Judah:

**21** Hear this, you foolish and senseless people,[^17] who have eyes but do not see, who have ears but do not hear.

**22** Do you not fear me? declares Yahweh. Do you not tremble before me? I placed the sand as a boundary for the sea, a permanent limit that it cannot cross. Its waves toss, but they cannot prevail; they roar, but they cannot cross it.

**23** But this people has a stubborn and rebellious heart; they have turned aside and gone away.

**24** They do not say in their hearts, "Let us fear Yahweh our God, who gives the rain, the autumn rain and the spring rain,[^18] in its season, who keeps for us the weeks appointed for the harvest."

**25** Your wrongdoings have turned these away; your sins have kept good things from you.

**26** For among my people are found wicked men. They watch like fowlers lying in wait;[^19] they set a trap; they catch human beings.

**27** Like a cage full of birds, their houses are full of deceit. That is how they have become great and rich.

**28** They have grown fat and sleek. They have even gone beyond all bounds in evil.[^20] They do not plead the cause, the cause of the fatherless, yet they prosper; they do not defend the rights of the needy.

**29** Should I not punish them for these things? declares Yahweh. Should I not avenge myself on a nation like this?

**30** An appalling and horrible thing has happened in the land:

**31** the prophets prophesy falsely, and the priests rule as they direct,[^21] and my people love it so. But what will you do when the end comes?

[^1]: Literally "a man".
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *mishpat*, "justice, ruling". Literally "the justice of their God". Also in verse 5.
[^4]: Or "a wolf of the evening".
[^5]: Hebrew *shoqed*, the word used in 1:12.
[^6]: Hebrew *meshuvot*, "turnings away": see 2:19.
[^7]: Or "gashed themselves", as in rites of mourning or worship (16:6; 1 Kings 18:28).
[^8]: The meaning of the Hebrew words is uncertain.
[^9]: Literally "Should my soul not avenge itself". Also in verse 29.
[^10]: Literally "Not he!", that is, he does not count, or he will not act.
[^11]: Hebrew *ruach*, "wind, spirit". The prophets claim to speak by the spirit, but they are only wind.
[^12]: The people's words may end before this sentence, which would then be Yahweh's answer: "So it will be done to them."
[^13]: Hebrew *YHWH Elohei tseva'ot*, traditionally "the LORD God of hosts". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^14]: *You people* is plural, the people of Judah; *your mouth* is singular, the prophet's.
[^15]: Compare Deuteronomy 28:49.
[^16]: Singular, the prophet. The first "you" in the verse is plural, the people.
[^17]: Literally "without heart". In Hebrew the heart is the seat of thought and understanding.
[^18]: Hebrew *yoreh* and *malqosh*, the rains of autumn (October–November), which soften the ground for sowing, and of spring (March–April), which ripen the crops.
[^19]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "He watches like the crouching of fowlers".
[^20]: Literally "they have passed over the matters of evil". Or "they have overlooked evil deeds".
[^21]: Literally "rule on their hands". Or "rule by their own authority".
