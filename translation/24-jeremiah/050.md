**1** The word that the LORD[^1] spoke concerning Babylon, concerning the land of the Babylonians,[^2] through Jeremiah the prophet:

**2** Declare it among the nations and proclaim it; raise a signal flag and proclaim it; hide nothing. Say: Babylon is captured; Bel is put to shame; Marduk is shattered.[^3] Her images are put to shame; her idols[^4] are shattered.

**3** For a nation from the north has come up against her; it will make her land a desolation, and no one will live in it. Both human beings and animals have fled; they are gone.

**4** In those days and at that time, declares the LORD, the people of Israel will come, they and the people of Judah together. They will come weeping as they go, and they will seek the LORD their God.

**5** They will ask the way to Zion, with their faces turned toward it: "Come, let us join ourselves to the LORD[^5] in an everlasting covenant that will never be forgotten."

**6** My people have been lost sheep; their shepherds have led them astray, making them roam on the mountains.[^6] They have wandered from mountain to hill; they have forgotten their resting place.

**7** All who found them have devoured them, and their foes have said, "We are not guilty, because they sinned against the LORD, their true pasture,[^7] the LORD, the hope of their ancestors."

**8** Flee from the midst of Babylon; go out[^8] from the land of the Babylonians, and be like male goats that go before the flock.

**9** For look, I am stirring up and bringing against Babylon an assembly of great nations from the land of the north. They will draw up their lines against her; from there she will be captured. Their arrows are like those of a skilled warrior who does not return empty-handed.

**10** Babylonia[^9] will become plunder; all who plunder her will be satisfied, declares the LORD.

**11** Though you rejoice, though you exult, you who plunder my inheritance, though you frisk about like a heifer threshing grain[^10] and neigh like stallions,

**12** your mother will be greatly ashamed; she who bore you will be disgraced. Look, she will be the least of the nations, a wilderness, a dry land and a desert.

**13** Because of the wrath of the LORD she will not be inhabited, but will be completely desolate. Everyone who passes by Babylon will be appalled and hiss at all her wounds.

**14** Draw up your lines around Babylon, all you who bend the bow! Shoot at her; spare no arrows, for she has sinned against the LORD.

**15** Raise the battle cry against her on every side! She has surrendered;[^11] her towers[^12] have fallen; her walls are torn down. For this is the vengeance of the LORD. Take vengeance on her; do to her as she has done.

**16** Cut off from Babylon the sower and the one who wields the sickle at harvest time. Because of the sword of the oppressor,[^13] they will each turn back to their own people; they will each flee to their own land.

**17** Israel is a scattered sheep, chased away by lions. First the king of Assyria devoured him, and now at the last Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has gnawed his bones.[^14]

**18** Therefore this is what the LORD of Armies,[^15] the God of Israel, says: I am going to punish the king of Babylon and his land, as I punished the king of Assyria.

**19** I will bring Israel back to his pasture, and he will graze on Carmel and in Bashan, and his appetite will be satisfied on the hills of Ephraim and in Gilead.

**20** In those days and at that time, declares the LORD, search will be made for Israel's guilt, but there will be none, and for Judah's sins, but none will be found, for I will forgive those I leave as a remnant.

**21** Go up against the land of Merathaim, against it, and against the inhabitants of Pekod.[^16] Put them to the sword and devote them to destruction,[^17] declares the LORD; do all that I have commanded you.

**22** The noise of battle is in the land, and great destruction!

**23** How the hammer of the whole earth is cut down and broken! How Babylon has become a horror among the nations!

**24** I set a trap for you, Babylon, and you were caught before you knew it. You were found and seized, because you challenged the LORD.

**25** The LORD has opened his armory and brought out the weapons of his wrath, for the Lord GOD of Armies[^18] has work to do in the land of the Babylonians.

**26** Come against her from every side;[^19] open her granaries; pile her up like heaps of grain and devote her to destruction. Let her have no remnant left.

**27** Put all her young bulls to the sword;[^20] let them go down to the slaughter. Woe to them, for their day has come, the time of their punishment.

**28** Listen! Fugitives and refugees from the land of Babylon are declaring in Zion the vengeance of the LORD our God, vengeance for his temple.

**29** Summon archers[^21] against Babylon, all who bend the bow. Camp all around her; let none of hers escape.[^22] Repay her for her deeds; do to her just as she has done. For she has defied the LORD, the Holy One of Israel.

**30** Therefore her young men will fall in her squares, and all her soldiers will be silenced on that day, declares the LORD.[^23]

**31** Look, I am against you, Arrogant One,[^24] declares the Lord GOD of Armies, for your day has come, the time when I will punish you.

**32** The Arrogant One will stumble and fall, with no one to raise him up. I will set fire to his cities, and it will consume everything around him.

**33** This is what the LORD of Armies says: The people of Israel are oppressed, and the people of Judah with them. All who took them captive hold them fast; they refuse to let them go.

**34** But their Redeemer[^25] is strong; the LORD of Armies is his name. He will surely plead their cause, so that he may give rest to the earth, but unrest to the inhabitants of Babylon.[^26]

**35** A sword against the Babylonians, declares the LORD, and against the inhabitants of Babylon, against her officials and against her wise men!

**36** A sword against her false prophets,[^27] and they will become fools! A sword against her warriors, and they will be shattered!

**37** A sword against his horses and his chariots, and against all the foreign troops[^28] in her midst, and they will become like women! A sword against her treasures, and they will be plundered!

**38** A drought[^29] on her waters, and they will dry up! For it is a land of idols, and they go mad over their terrifying images.

**39** Therefore desert creatures will live there with hyenas,[^30] and ostriches will live in her. She will never again be inhabited or lived in from generation to generation.

**40** As when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighbors, declares the LORD, no one will live there; no human being will stay in it.[^31]

**41** Look, a people is coming from the north; a great nation and many kings are being stirred up from the farthest parts of the earth.[^32]

**42** They grasp bow and javelin; they are cruel and show no mercy. Their sound is like the roaring sea; they ride on horses, drawn up like warriors for battle against you, daughter Babylon.

**43** The king of Babylon has heard the report of them, and his hands hang limp. Anguish has gripped him, pain like that of a woman in labor.

**44** Look, like a lion coming up from the thickets of the Jordan[^33] to a pasture that is always green, I will suddenly chase them from it, and I will appoint over it whoever is chosen. For who is like me? Who can summon me? What shepherd can stand before me?

**45** Therefore hear the plan that the LORD has made against Babylon, and the purposes he has formed against the land of the Babylonians: Surely the little ones of the flock will be dragged away; surely the pasture will be made desolate because of them.

**46** At the sound of Babylon's capture the earth quakes, and a cry is heard among the nations.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. Chapters 50–51 are the last and longest of the oracles against the nations.
[^2]: Hebrew *Kasdim*, traditionally "Chaldeans": the people of southern Mesopotamia whose kings then ruled Babylon. Also in verses 8, 10, 25, 35 and 45.
[^3]: Hebrew *Merodakh*. Marduk was the chief god of Babylon, and *Bel*, "lord", was his title.
[^4]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a scornful word for idols, perhaps "dung pellets".
[^5]: Literally "Come, and they will join themselves to the LORD".
[^6]: The Hebrew text is written "faithless mountains" but read "they made them roam on the mountains".
[^7]: Literally "the pasture of righteousness" (31:23).
[^8]: The Hebrew text is written "they will go out" but read "go out".
[^9]: Hebrew *Kasdim*, here meaning the land.
[^10]: The same consonants can be read "like a heifer in the grass", as the Septuagint reads.
[^11]: Literally "She has given her hand".
[^12]: Hebrew *ashyot*, a rare word, perhaps "towers" or "foundations".
[^13]: Or "the cruel sword". Hebrew *ha-yonah*, perhaps "the oppressor" (46:16).
[^14]: Literally "has broken his bones". Assyria destroyed the northern kingdom of Israel in 722 BC (2 Kings 17:6), and Babylon took Judah into exile.
[^15]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verses 25, 31, 33 and 34.
[^16]: Two wordplays. *Merathaim*, "double rebellion", sounds like *mat marrati*, a Babylonian name for the region at the head of the Persian Gulf. *Pekod*, "punishment", was an Aramean people in eastern Babylonia (Ezekiel 23:23).
[^17]: Literally "Put to the sword and devote to destruction after them". Hebrew *charam*: see 25:9. Also in verse 26.
[^18]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH tseva'ot*, "my Lord YHWH of Armies". Also in verse 31.
[^19]: Literally "from the end".
[^20]: Her *young bulls* are her warriors (compare 46:21).
[^21]: Hebrew *rabbim*, "many", here probably "archers" (compare Job 16:13).
[^22]: The words "of hers" are read but not written in the Hebrew text.
[^23]: Verse 30 repeats 49:26, there said of Damascus.
[^24]: Hebrew *zadon*, "arrogance", used as a name for Babylon. Also in verse 32.
[^25]: Hebrew *go'el*, a kinsman who buys back a relative or his property (31:11).
[^26]: A wordplay: *hirgia*, "give rest", and *hirgiz*, "make tremble".
[^27]: Hebrew *baddim*, "empty talkers", probably her diviners (compare Isaiah 44:25).
[^28]: Hebrew *ha-erev*, "the mixture" (25:20), here probably foreign soldiers.
[^29]: Hebrew *chorev*, "drought", a play on *cherev*, "sword", in verses 35–37.
[^30]: The meaning of the Hebrew words *tsiyyim* and *iyyim* is uncertain. Compare Isaiah 13:21–22; 34:14.
[^31]: Verse 40 repeats 49:18, there said of Edom.
[^32]: Verses 41–43 repeat 6:22–24, there said of Jerusalem.
[^33]: Literally "the pride of the Jordan". Verses 44–46 repeat 49:19–21, there said of Edom.
