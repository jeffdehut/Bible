**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh[^1] in the tenth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, which was the eighteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar.[^2]

**2** At that time the army of the king of Babylon was besieging Jerusalem, and Jeremiah the prophet was confined in the courtyard of the guard,[^3] which was in the palace of the king of Judah.

**3** For Zedekiah king of Judah had confined him, saying, "Why do you prophesy, saying, 'This is what Yahweh says: I am going to give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will capture it.

**4** Zedekiah king of Judah will not escape from the hand of the Babylonians,[^4] but he will certainly be given into the hand of the king of Babylon. He will speak with him face to face and see him eye to eye.[^5]

**5** He will take Zedekiah to Babylon, and he will remain there until I attend to him,[^6] declares Yahweh. Though you fight against the Babylonians, you will not succeed'?"

**6** Jeremiah said: The word of Yahweh came to me:

**7** Hanamel son of Shallum your uncle is going to come to you and say, "Buy my field in Anathoth, for the right of redemption is yours to buy it."[^7]

**8** Then Hanamel my cousin[^8] came to me in the courtyard of the guard, just as Yahweh had said, and he said to me, "Please buy my field in Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin, for the right of possession and of redemption is yours. Buy it for yourself." Then I knew that this was the word of Yahweh.

**9** So I bought the field in Anathoth from Hanamel my cousin, and I weighed out the money for him, seventeen shekels[^9] of silver.

**10** I signed the deed[^10] and sealed it, had witnesses attest it, and weighed the money on scales.

**11** Then I took the deed of purchase, the sealed copy containing the terms and conditions,[^11] and the open copy,

**12** and I gave the deed of purchase to Baruch son of Neriah son of Mahseiah in the presence of Hanamel my cousin,[^12] and of the witnesses who had signed the deed of purchase, and of all the Judeans[^13] who were sitting in the courtyard of the guard.

**13** In their presence I charged Baruch:

**14** This is what Yahweh of Armies,[^14] the God of Israel, says: Take these documents, this deed of purchase, both the sealed copy and this open copy, and put them in a clay jar, so that they will last for a long time.

**15** For this is what Yahweh of Armies, the God of Israel, says: Houses and fields and vineyards will again be bought in this land.

**16** After I had given the deed of purchase to Baruch son of Neriah, I prayed to Yahweh:

**17** "Ah, Lord Yahweh![^15] You made the heavens and the earth by your great power and your outstretched arm. Nothing is too hard for you.

**18** You show faithful love[^16] to thousands, but you repay the guilt of the fathers into the laps of their children after them.[^17] Great and mighty God, whose name is Yahweh of Armies,

**19** great in counsel and mighty in deed, your eyes are open to all the ways of human beings, to give each one according to his ways and according to the fruit of his deeds.

**20** You performed signs and wonders in the land of Egypt, and to this day you have done so in Israel and among all humankind, and you have made a name for yourself, as it is today.

**21** You brought your people Israel out of the land of Egypt with signs and wonders, with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, and with great terror.

**22** You gave them this land, which you swore to their ancestors to give them, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**23** They came in and took possession of it, but they did not listen to your voice or walk in your law.[^18] They did nothing of all you commanded them to do, so you have brought all this disaster on them.

**24** Look, the siege ramps have reached the city to capture it. Because of the sword, famine and plague, the city has been given into the hand of the Babylonians who are fighting against it. What you said has happened, and now you see it.

**25** Yet you, Lord Yahweh, have said to me, 'Buy the field with money and call witnesses,' though the city has been given into the hand of the Babylonians."

**26** Then the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah:

**27** Look, I am Yahweh, the God of all humankind.[^19] Is anything too hard for me?

**28** Therefore this is what Yahweh says: I am going to give this city into the hand of the Babylonians and into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will capture it.

**29** The Babylonians who are fighting against this city will come and set this city on fire and burn it, with the houses on whose roofs they have burned incense[^20] to Baal and poured out drink offerings to other gods to provoke me to anger.

**30** For the people of Israel and the people of Judah have done nothing but evil in my eyes from their youth. The people of Israel have done nothing but provoke me to anger by the work of their hands, declares Yahweh.

**31** This city has roused my anger and my wrath from the day they built it until this day, so that I must remove it from my sight,

**32** because of all the evil that the people of Israel and the people of Judah have done to provoke me to anger, they, their kings, their officials, their priests and their prophets, the people of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

**33** They have turned their backs to me and not their faces. Though I taught them again and again,[^21] they would not listen and accept discipline.

**34** They set up their detestable idols in the house that bears my name,[^22] to defile it.

**35** They built the high places of Baal in the Valley of Ben-hinnom, to hand over their sons and their daughters to Molech,[^23] something I did not command them, nor did it enter my mind, that they should do this detestable thing and cause Judah to sin.

**36** Now therefore this is what Yahweh, the God of Israel, says concerning this city, of which you say, "It has been given into the hand of the king of Babylon by sword, famine and plague":

**37** I am going to gather them from all the lands where I have driven them in my anger, my wrath and great fury. I will bring them back to this place and let them live in safety.

**38** They will be my people, and I will be their God.

**39** I will give them one heart and one way, so that they will fear me always, for their own good and the good of their children after them.

**40** I will make with them an everlasting covenant, that I will not turn away from them,[^24] but will do them good. I will put the fear of me in their hearts, so that they will not turn away from me.

**41** I will rejoice over them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land in faithfulness, with all my heart and with all my soul.

**42** For this is what Yahweh says: Just as I have brought all this great disaster on this people, so I am going to bring on them all the good that I promise them.

**43** Fields will again be bought in this land of which you say, "It is a desolation, without human beings or animals; it has been given into the hand of the Babylonians."

**44** People will buy fields for money, sign deeds, seal them and call witnesses, in the land of Benjamin, in the places around Jerusalem, in the cities of Judah, in the cities of the hill country, in the cities of the Shephelah[^25] and in the cities of the Negev,[^26] for I will restore their fortunes,[^27] declares Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: In 588 or 587 BC, during the Babylonian siege of Jerusalem, about a year before the city fell (39:1–2; 52:4–6).
[^3]: Hebrew *chatsar ha-mattarah*, a court of the palace where prisoners were kept under guard (37:21; 38:13, 28).
[^4]: Hebrew *Kasdim*, traditionally "Chaldeans": the people of southern Mesopotamia whose kings then ruled Babylon. Also in verses 5, 24–25, 28–29 and 43.
[^5]: Literally "His mouth will speak with his mouth, and his eyes will see his eyes". The Hebrew text is written "his eye" but read "his eyes" the second time.
[^6]: Or "until I punish him". Hebrew *paqad*, "visit, attend to" (29:10).
[^7]: When a member of a family had to sell land, his nearest relative had the right and the duty to buy it, so that it stayed in the family (Leviticus 25:25; Ruth 4:1–6).
[^8]: Literally "my uncle's son". Also in verse 9.
[^9]: A shekel is about 2/5 ounce (11.5 grams), so seventeen shekels is about 7 ounces (200 grams). Literally "seven shekels and ten of silver".
[^10]: Literally "I wrote in the document".
[^11]: Literally "the command and the decrees". One copy of the deed was rolled up and sealed, to keep it safe from tampering; the other was left open, to be read.
[^12]: The Hebrew has "my uncle", probably short for "my uncle's son" (verses 8–9).
[^13]: Hebrew *ha-yehudim*, the people of Judah.
[^14]: *Yahweh of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verses 15 and 18.
[^15]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh". Also in verse 25.
[^16]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^17]: Literally "into the bosom of their sons after them". Compare Exodus 20:5–6; 34:6–7.
[^18]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law". The Hebrew text is written "your laws" but read "your law".
[^19]: Literally "all flesh".
[^20]: Or "burned offerings". The Hebrew verb *qatar* means to make something go up in smoke.
[^21]: Literally "teaching them, rising early and teaching".
[^22]: Literally "over which my name is called", that is, which belongs to me (7:10).
[^23]: Literally "to make pass over to Molech", that is, to sacrifice them by burning (7:31; 2 Kings 23:10). Molech was a god to whom children were offered (Leviticus 18:21).
[^24]: Literally "that I will not turn back from after them".
[^25]: The low hills between the hill country of Judah and the coastal plain.
[^26]: The dry region in the south of Judah.
[^27]: Literally "turn their turning", an idiom for restoring what was lost. Or "bring back their captives".
