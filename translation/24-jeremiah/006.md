**1** Run for safety, people of Benjamin, out of the midst of Jerusalem! Blow the ram's horn in Tekoa,[^1] and raise a signal fire over Beth-hakkerem![^2] For disaster looms from the north, and great destruction.

**2** Daughter Zion,[^3] so lovely and delicate, I will destroy.[^4]

**3** Shepherds with their flocks will come to her. They will pitch their tents against her all around; each will graze his own part.

**4** "Prepare for war against her![^5] Up, let us attack at noon! Woe to us, for the day is fading; the evening shadows are lengthening.

**5** Up, let us attack by night and destroy her fortresses!"

**6** For this is what the LORD of Armies[^6] says: Cut down her trees and pile up a siege ramp against Jerusalem. This is the city that must be punished;[^7] there is nothing but oppression within her.

**7** As a well keeps its water fresh, so she keeps her wickedness fresh. Violence and destruction are heard in her; sickness and wounds are always before me.

**8** Take warning, Jerusalem, or I will turn from you in disgust[^8] and make you a desolation, a land where no one lives.

**9** This is what the LORD of Armies says: They will glean the remnant of Israel as thoroughly as a vine. Pass your hand over the branches again, like a grape picker.

**10** To whom can I speak and give warning, so that they will listen? Look, their ears are uncircumcised,[^9] and they cannot pay attention. Look, the word of the LORD has become an object of scorn to them; they take no pleasure in it.

**11** But I am full of the wrath of the LORD; I am weary of holding it in. Pour it out on the children in the street, and on the young men gathered together. For husband and wife alike will be caught, the old and those full of years.

**12** Their houses will be turned over to others, their fields and their wives together, for I will stretch out my hand against those who live in the land, declares the LORD.

**13** For from the least of them to the greatest, every one is greedy for unjust gain; from prophet to priest, every one deals falsely.

**14** They have treated the wound[^10] of my people as though it were nothing, saying, "Peace, peace,"[^11] when there is no peace.

**15** They acted shamefully, for they did detestable things,[^12] yet they were not at all ashamed; they did not even know how to blush. So they will fall among those who fall; when I punish them, they will stumble, says the LORD.

**16** This is what the LORD says: Stand by the roads and look, and ask for the ancient paths, where the good way is, and walk in it, and you will find rest for yourselves.[^13] But they said, "We will not walk in it."

**17** I appointed watchmen over you: "Listen to the sound of the ram's horn!" But they said, "We will not listen."

**18** Therefore hear, you nations, and know, you assembly,[^14] what will happen to them.

**19** Hear, you earth: I am going to bring disaster on this people, the fruit of their own schemes, because they have not paid attention to my words, and they have rejected my instruction.[^15]

**20** What use to me is frankincense that comes from Sheba, or sweet cane[^16] from a distant land? Your burnt offerings are not acceptable, and your sacrifices do not please me.

**21** Therefore this is what the LORD says: I am going to put obstacles before this people. Fathers and sons together will stumble over them; neighbor and friend will perish.

**22** This is what the LORD says: Look, a people is coming from the land of the north; a great nation is being stirred up from the farthest parts of the earth.

**23** They grasp bow and javelin; they are cruel and show no mercy. Their sound is like the roaring sea; they ride on horses, drawn up like warriors for battle against you, daughter Zion.

**24** We have heard the report of them; our hands hang limp. Anguish has gripped us, pain like that of a woman in labor.

**25** Do not go out into the field or walk on the road, for the enemy has a sword. Terror is on every side.[^17]

**26** My dear people,[^18] put on sackcloth and roll in ashes. Mourn as for an only son, with bitter wailing, for suddenly the destroyer will come upon us.

**27** I have made you a tester of metals among my people, a fortress,[^19] so that you may know and test their ways.

**28** They are all the most stubborn of rebels, going about with slander. They are bronze and iron; all of them act corruptly.

**29** The bellows blow fiercely; the lead is consumed by the fire.[^20] The refining goes on in vain; the wicked are not drawn off.

**30** They are called rejected silver, for the LORD has rejected them.

[^1]: A wordplay: *bi-teqoa tiq'u*, "in Tekoa blow". Tekoa was a town about 10 miles (16 km) south of Jerusalem, the home of Amos (Amos 1:1).
[^2]: A town near Jerusalem, probably to the south. The name means "house of the vineyard".
[^3]: A poetic name for Jerusalem and its people, pictured as a young woman. Also in verse 23.
[^4]: Or "I will silence". The same consonants can be read "I have compared Daughter Zion to a lovely pasture", which would lead into the picture of shepherds in verse 3.
[^5]: Literally "Consecrate war against her", from the religious rites that opened a campaign. The enemy is speaking in verses 4–5.
[^6]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verse 9.
[^7]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "She is the city; it has been punished".
[^8]: Literally "or my soul will be wrenched from you".
[^9]: That is, closed, unable to hear.
[^10]: Hebrew *shever*, "breaking, fracture".
[^11]: Hebrew *shalom*, "peace, well-being": "All is well".
[^12]: Or "Were they ashamed when they did detestable things?"
[^13]: Hebrew *nephesh*, "soul, self". Literally "for your souls".
[^14]: Hebrew *edah*, a gathered community. The meaning of the line is uncertain.
[^15]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law".
[^16]: An aromatic reed used in incense and anointing oil (Exodus 30:23).
[^17]: Hebrew *magor missaviv*, a phrase Jeremiah uses often (20:3, 10; 46:5; 49:29).
[^18]: Literally "Daughter of my people", a tender, poetic name for the nation.
[^19]: Hebrew *mivtsar* usually means "fortress" (compare 1:18). Some scholars connect it here with *betser*, "ore". Jeremiah is to test the people as metal is tested.
[^20]: The Hebrew text is written as one word, perhaps "from their fire", but read as two, "by the fire is consumed". In refining silver, lead was added to carry away the impurities. Here the lead burns away, but the impurities remain.
