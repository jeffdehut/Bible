**1** At that time, declares Yahweh,[^1] the bones of the kings of Judah, the bones of its officials, the bones of the priests, the bones of the prophets and the bones of the inhabitants of Jerusalem will be brought out of their graves.

**2** They will be spread out before the sun and the moon and all the host of heaven,[^2] which they have loved and served, which they have followed, consulted and worshiped. They will not be gathered up or buried; they will be like dung on the surface of the ground.

**3** Death will be chosen rather than life by all the remnant that is left of this evil family, in all the places where I have driven them,[^3] declares Yahweh of Armies.[^4]

**4** Say to them: This is what Yahweh says: When people fall, do they not get up? When someone turns away, does he not turn back?[^5]

**5** Why then has this people, Jerusalem, turned away in endless faithlessness? They hold fast to deceit; they refuse to turn back.

**6** I have listened carefully, but they do not speak what is right. No one repents of his wickedness, saying, "What have I done?" Each of them turns to his own course, like a horse charging into battle.

**7** Even the stork in the sky knows her seasons, and the dove, the swift[^6] and the thrush keep the time of their coming. But my people do not know what Yahweh requires.[^7]

**8** How can you say, "We are wise, and the law of Yahweh is with us"? But look, the lying pen of the scribes has turned it into a lie.[^8]

**9** The wise will be put to shame; they will be dismayed and caught. Look, they have rejected the word of Yahweh, so what wisdom do they have?

**10** Therefore I will give their wives to others and their fields to new owners.[^9] For from the least to the greatest, every one is greedy for unjust gain; from prophet to priest, every one deals falsely.

**11** They have treated the wound of my dear people[^10] as though it were nothing, saying, "Peace, peace," when there is no peace.

**12** They acted shamefully, for they did detestable things, yet they were not at all ashamed; they did not know how to blush. So they will fall among those who fall; at the time of their punishment they will stumble, says Yahweh.

**13** I will gather them and make an end of them,[^11] declares Yahweh. There will be no grapes on the vine, no figs on the fig tree, and even the leaves will wither. What I gave them will pass away from them.[^12]

**14** "Why are we sitting here?[^13] Gather together! Let us go into the fortified cities and perish there. For Yahweh our God has doomed us to perish and given us poisoned water to drink, because we have sinned against Yahweh.

**15** We hoped for peace, but no good has come; for a time of healing, but there is only terror."

**16** From Dan the snorting of his horses is heard; at the sound of the neighing of his stallions the whole land quakes. They come and devour the land and everything in it, the city and those who live in it.

**17** For I am sending snakes among you, vipers that no charm can stop, and they will bite you, declares Yahweh.

**18** My joy is gone; grief has come upon me;[^14] my heart is sick within me.

**19** Listen! The cry of my dear people from a land far and wide:[^15] "Is Yahweh not in Zion? Is her King not in her?" Why have they provoked me to anger with their carved images, with their worthless foreign idols?[^16]

**20** "The harvest is past, the summer has ended, and we are not saved."

**21** Because my dear people are broken, I am broken. I go about in mourning; horror has gripped me.

**22** Is there no balm in Gilead?[^17] Is there no physician there? Why then has no new skin grown over the wound of my dear people?[^18]

**23** Oh, that my head were water and my eyes a fountain of tears, so that I could weep day and night for the slain of my dear people![^19]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: The sun, moon and stars, worshiped as gods (2 Kings 21:3–5; 23:4–5).
[^3]: Literally "in all the remaining places where I have driven them".
[^4]: *Yahweh of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^5]: Verses 4–5 play on the verb *shuv*, "turn, return", which can mean both turning away from Yahweh and turning back to him (see 2:19; 3:12).
[^6]: The Hebrew text is written *sus*, "horse", but read *sis*, "swift". The names of some of these birds are uncertain.
[^7]: Hebrew *mishpat*, "justice, ruling". Literally "the justice of Yahweh".
[^8]: Or "has worked for a lie". The scribes copied and taught the law, and Jeremiah says they have falsified it.
[^9]: The Septuagint has only the first part of this verse. Verses 10b–12, which repeat 6:13–15 with small differences, are not in it.
[^10]: Literally "the daughter of my people", a tender, poetic name for the nation. Also in verses 19, 21, 22 and 23.
[^11]: The Hebrew is a wordplay. Literally "Gathering, I will make an end of them", combining *asaf*, "gather, harvest", and *suf*, "come to an end". The picture is a harvest that finds nothing.
[^12]: The Hebrew of this line is obscure. Literally "and I gave to them, they pass over them".
[^13]: The people speak in verses 14–15.
[^14]: The Hebrew of this line is obscure. Literally "My brightening up, on me is grief". The prophet speaks in verses 18–23, quoting the people's cry and Yahweh's reply (verse 19).
[^15]: Or "from a distant land", as if from exile.
[^16]: Hebrew *hevel*, "vapor, breath": see 2:5. Literally "with the vapors of a foreign land".
[^17]: Hebrew *tsori*, a healing resin for which Gilead, east of the Jordan, was famous (Genesis 37:25).
[^18]: Literally "Why has the new flesh of the daughter of my people not come up?"
[^19]: In most English Bibles this verse is 9:1, and the verses of chapter 9 are numbered one higher than in the Hebrew.
