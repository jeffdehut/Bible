**1** This is what the LORD[^1] says: Go and buy a potter's clay jar.[^2] Take with you[^3] some of the elders of the people and some of the senior priests,

**2** and go out to the Valley of Ben-hinnom,[^4] at the entrance of the Potsherd Gate.[^5] There proclaim the words I tell you.

**3** Say: Hear the word of the LORD, you kings of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem. This is what the LORD of Armies,[^6] the God of Israel, says: I am going to bring such a disaster on this place that the ears of everyone who hears of it will tingle.

**4** For they have abandoned me and made this place a foreign place, burning incense[^7] in it to other gods that neither they nor their ancestors nor the kings of Judah ever knew, and they have filled this place with the blood of the innocent.

**5** They have built the high places[^8] of Baal to burn their sons in the fire as burnt offerings to Baal, something I did not command or speak of, nor did it ever enter my mind.

**6** Therefore, look, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when this place will no longer be called Topheth[^9] or the Valley of Ben-hinnom, but the Valley of Slaughter.

**7** In this place I will empty out[^10] the plans of Judah and Jerusalem. I will make them fall by the sword before their enemies, by the hand of those who seek their lives, and I will give their corpses as food to the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth.

**8** I will make this city a horror and a thing to hiss at; everyone who passes by it will be appalled and hiss at all its wounds.

**9** I will make them eat the flesh of their sons and the flesh of their daughters, and they will eat one another's flesh, in the siege and the distress with which their enemies and those who seek their lives will press them hard.[^11]

**10** Then break the jar in the sight of the men who go with you,

**11** and say to them: This is what the LORD of Armies says: So I will break this people and this city, as one breaks a potter's vessel so that it can never be mended. They will bury the dead in Topheth until there is no room left to bury.

**12** This is what I will do to this place and to its inhabitants, declares the LORD: I will make this city like Topheth.

**13** The houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah will be defiled like the place of Topheth, all the houses on whose roofs they burned incense to all the host of heaven[^12] and poured out drink offerings to other gods.

**14** Then Jeremiah came from Topheth, where the LORD had sent him to prophesy, and he stood in the court of the LORD's house and said to all the people,

**15** "This is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: I am going to bring on this city and on all its towns all the disaster I have pronounced against it, because they have stiffened their necks so as not to listen to my words."

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. "The LORD's house" in verse 14 is the temple.
[^2]: Hebrew *baqbuq*, a narrow-necked jar, a name that imitates the gurgling sound of liquid poured from it.
[^3]: The Hebrew has no verb here; "take with you" is supplied.
[^4]: Ben-hinnom, "the Son of Hinnom", was a valley south and west of Jerusalem (7:31).
[^5]: The Hebrew text is written "Harsuth" but read "Harsith". The name probably means "Potsherd Gate", from *cheres*, a piece of broken pottery.
[^6]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verses 11 and 15.
[^7]: Or "burning offerings". The Hebrew verb *qatar* means to make something go up in smoke. Also in verse 13.
[^8]: Hebrew *bamot*: places of worship, often on hilltops.
[^9]: Hebrew *tofet*, perhaps "fireplace, hearth", a place where children were burned as sacrifices (7:31–32; 2 Kings 23:10).
[^10]: A wordplay. Hebrew *u-vaqqoti*, "I will empty out", sounds like *baqbuq*, "jar" (verse 1).
[^11]: Compare Deuteronomy 28:53–57.
[^12]: The sun, moon and stars, worshiped as gods (8:2).
