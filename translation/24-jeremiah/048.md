**1** Concerning Moab. This is what the LORD of Armies,[^1] the God of Israel, says: Woe to Nebo,[^2] for it is devastated! Kiriathaim is put to shame and captured; the stronghold[^3] is put to shame and shattered.

**2** Moab's renown is no more. In Heshbon they have planned disaster against her:[^4] "Come, let us cut her off from being a nation." You too, Madmen, will be silenced; the sword will pursue you.

**3** Listen! A cry from Horonaim: "Devastation and great destruction!"

**4** Moab is shattered; her little ones raise a cry.[^5]

**5** For on the ascent of Luhith they go up weeping bitterly;[^6] for on the descent to Horonaim they have heard the anguished cry of destruction.

**6** Flee! Save your lives! Be like a juniper[^7] in the wilderness.

**7** Because you trusted in your works and your treasures, you too will be captured. Chemosh[^8] will go into exile, together with his priests and his officials.

**8** The destroyer will come against every city, and no city will escape. The valley will perish, and the plateau[^9] will be destroyed, as the LORD has said.

**9** Give wings to Moab,[^10] for she will surely fly away. Her cities will become a desolation, with no one living in them.

**10** Cursed is anyone who does the work of the LORD carelessly; cursed is anyone who keeps his sword from bloodshed!

**11** Moab has been at ease from his youth, settled like wine on its dregs. He has not been poured from jar to jar; he has not gone into exile. So his flavor has stayed in him, and his aroma has not changed.[^11]

**12** Therefore, look, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will send him pourers,[^12] and they will pour him out. They will empty his jars and smash his jugs.

**13** Then Moab will be ashamed of Chemosh, as the house of Israel was ashamed of Bethel, in which they trusted.[^13]

**14** How can you say, "We are warriors, mighty men for battle"?

**15** Moab is devastated, and his cities have been invaded;[^14] the best of his young men have gone down to the slaughter, declares the King, whose name is the LORD of Armies.

**16** Moab's calamity is near at hand; his disaster is coming quickly.

**17** Mourn for him, all you who are around him, all who know his name. Say, "How the mighty scepter is broken, the glorious staff!"

**18** Come down from your glory and sit on the parched ground, you who live there, daughter Dibon,[^15] for the destroyer of Moab has come up against you; he has ruined your strongholds.

**19** Stand by the road and watch, you who live in Aroer. Ask the man who flees and the woman who escapes; say, "What has happened?"

**20** Moab is put to shame, for it is shattered. Wail and cry out! Proclaim by the Arnon[^16] that Moab is devastated.

**21** Judgment has come to the land of the plateau: to Holon, to Jahzah and to Mephaath,[^17]

**22** to Dibon, to Nebo and to Beth-diblathaim,

**23** to Kiriathaim, to Beth-gamul and to Beth-meon,

**24** to Kerioth and to Bozrah,[^18] and to all the cities of the land of Moab, far and near.

**25** The horn of Moab is cut off, and his arm is broken,[^19] declares the LORD.

**26** Make him drunk, for he has made himself great against the LORD. Let Moab wallow in his vomit, and let him also become a laughingstock.

**27** Was not Israel a laughingstock to you? Was he caught among thieves, that whenever you spoke of him you shook your head?

**28** Leave the cities and live among the rocks, you who live in Moab! Be like a dove that nests on the sides of the mouth of a gorge.

**29** We have heard of the pride of Moab, how very proud he is: his loftiness, his pride, his arrogance and his haughty heart.[^20]

**30** I know his insolence, declares the LORD, but it is empty; his boasts accomplish nothing.[^21]

**31** Therefore I wail for Moab; for all Moab I cry out; for the men of Kir-heres[^22] one moans.

**32** More than the weeping for Jazer I weep for you, vine of Sibmah! Your tendrils crossed over the sea; they reached as far as the sea of Jazer.[^23] On your summer fruit and on your grape harvest the destroyer has fallen.

**33** Joy and gladness have been taken away from the orchard[^24] and from the land of Moab. I have stopped the wine from the winepresses; no one treads them with shouts of joy. The shouting is no shout of joy.[^25]

**34** From the outcry at Heshbon as far as Elealeh, as far as Jahaz they raise their voice, from Zoar to Horonaim and Eglath-shelishiyah.[^26] For even the waters of Nimrim have become desolate.

**35** I will put an end in Moab, declares the LORD, to those who offer sacrifices on the high place and burn incense[^27] to their gods.

**36** Therefore my heart moans for Moab like a flute; my heart moans like a flute for the men of Kir-heres. Therefore the wealth they gained has perished.[^28]

**37** For every head is shaved bald and every beard cut off; on every hand are gashes, and around the waist is sackcloth.

**38** On all the roofs of Moab and in its squares there is nothing but mourning, for I have broken Moab like a jar that no one wants, declares the LORD.

**39** How it is shattered! How they wail! How Moab has turned his back in shame! Moab has become a laughingstock and an object of terror to all those around him.

**40** For this is what the LORD says: Look, one swoops down like an eagle and spreads his wings over Moab.[^29]

**41** Kerioth is captured,[^30] and the strongholds are seized. On that day the hearts of Moab's warriors will be like the heart of a woman in labor.

**42** Moab will be destroyed and be a people no more, because he made himself great against the LORD.

**43** Terror, pit and trap are before you, you who live in Moab, declares the LORD.[^31]

**44** Whoever flees from the terror will fall into the pit, and whoever climbs out of the pit will be caught in the trap. For I will bring upon her, upon Moab, the year of their punishment, declares the LORD.

**45** In the shadow of Heshbon the fugitives stand without strength. For fire has gone out from Heshbon, and a flame from the midst of Sihon.[^32] It has devoured the forehead of Moab and the skulls of the boasters.[^33]

**46** Woe to you, Moab! The people of Chemosh have perished, for your sons have been taken into captivity, and your daughters into exile.

**47** Yet I will restore the fortunes[^34] of Moab in the days to come,[^35] declares the LORD. Thus far the judgment on Moab.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verse 15. Much of this chapter has lines in common with Isaiah 15–16.
[^2]: The places named in this chapter are towns of Moab, east of the Dead Sea. Nebo is the town, not the mountain (Numbers 32:38).
[^3]: Hebrew *ha-misgav*, "the high stronghold", perhaps a place name, Misgab.
[^4]: Two wordplays: *Cheshbon*, "Heshbon", and *chashvu*, "they have planned"; *Madmen* and *tiddommi*, "you will be silenced".
[^5]: The Septuagint reads "they raise a cry to Zoar" (compare Isaiah 15:5).
[^6]: Literally "with weeping goes up weeping". Compare Isaiah 15:5.
[^7]: Hebrew *aro'er*, perhaps a desert shrub (compare 17:6), or the town of Aroer (verse 19).
[^8]: The god of Moab (Numbers 21:29; 1 Kings 11:7). The Hebrew text is written "Kemish" but read "Chemosh".
[^9]: Hebrew *ha-mishor*, the high tableland of Moab. Also in verse 21.
[^10]: The meaning of the Hebrew *tsits* is uncertain here. It may mean "wings", "a flower" or "salt".
[^11]: Wine was left to settle on its sediment and then poured off into other jars to clear it.
[^12]: Hebrew *tso'im*, "those who tip", men who tilt jars to pour them out.
[^13]: Bethel was where Jeroboam set up a golden calf for Israel to worship (1 Kings 12:28–29). Or the god Bethel, who was worshiped in the region.
[^14]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "and her cities he has gone up".
[^15]: Dibon was a chief city of Moab, north of the Arnon.
[^16]: The deep gorge of the Arnon cuts through Moab to the Dead Sea.
[^17]: The Hebrew text is written "Mophaath" but read "Mephaath".
[^18]: A town in Moab, not the Bozrah of Edom (49:13).
[^19]: Pictures of strength.
[^20]: Verses 29–38 have many lines in common with Isaiah 16:6–11 and 15:2–7.
[^21]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "and not right; his empty talk, not right they have done".
[^22]: Called Kir-hareseth in Isaiah 16:7, a chief fortress of Moab. Also in verse 36.
[^23]: Isaiah 16:8 reads "they reached as far as Jazer". The *sea* is probably the Dead Sea.
[^24]: Hebrew *karmel*, "orchard, garden land" (2:7).
[^25]: Literally "the shout is not a shout". The cry of the grape treaders has become the battle cry of the enemy (25:30; 51:14).
[^26]: Or "the third Eglath". Compare Isaiah 15:4–6.
[^27]: Or "burn offerings". The Hebrew verb *qatar* means to make something go up in smoke.
[^28]: Literally "the abundance he made has perished" (Isaiah 15:7). Flutes were played at funerals.
[^29]: Verses 40–41 are nearly the same as 49:22, which is said of Edom.
[^30]: Or "The towns are captured". Hebrew *qeriyyot*, "towns", is also the name of Kerioth (verse 24).
[^31]: A wordplay: *pachad*, "terror", *pachat*, "pit", and *pach*, "trap". Verses 43–44 are nearly the same as Isaiah 24:17–18.
[^32]: Verses 45–46 draw on the ancient song in Numbers 21:28–29 and on Numbers 24:17. Sihon was the Amorite king whose capital was Heshbon (Numbers 21:26).
[^33]: Literally "the sons of tumult". Numbers 24:17 has "the sons of Sheth".
[^34]: Literally "turn the turning", an idiom for restoring what was lost. Or "bring back the captives".
[^35]: Literally "in the latter end of the days".
