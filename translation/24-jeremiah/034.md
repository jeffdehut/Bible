**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD,[^1] when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and all his army, all the kingdoms of the earth under his rule and all the peoples were fighting against Jerusalem and all its cities:[^2]

**2** This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: Go and speak to Zedekiah king of Judah and say to him: This is what the LORD says: I am going to give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire.

**3** You will not escape from his hand, but you will certainly be captured and given into his hand. You will see the king of Babylon eye to eye, and he will speak with you face to face.[^3] And you will go to Babylon.

**4** Yet hear the word of the LORD, Zedekiah king of Judah. This is what the LORD says about you: You will not die by the sword.

**5** You will die in peace. As they burned spices for your ancestors, the former kings who were before you, so they will burn spices for you[^4] and mourn for you, "Alas, master!"[^5] For I myself have spoken the word, declares the LORD.

**6** Jeremiah the prophet spoke all these words to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem,

**7** while the army of the king of Babylon was fighting against Jerusalem and against all the cities of Judah that were left, against Lachish and against Azekah.[^6] For these were the only fortified cities left of the cities of Judah.

**8** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD after King Zedekiah had made a covenant with all the people in Jerusalem to proclaim liberty[^7] to them,

**9** that each should set free his Hebrew slaves, male and female, so that no one would keep a fellow Judean[^8] as a slave.

**10** All the officials and all the people who had entered into the covenant agreed that each would set free his male and female slaves and not keep them as slaves any longer. They obeyed and set them free.

**11** But afterward they turned around and took back the male and female slaves they had set free, and forced them to be slaves again.

**12** Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah from the LORD:

**13** This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I made a covenant with your ancestors when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery. I said,

**14** "At the end of seven years each of you must set free his fellow Hebrew who has sold himself to you. He is to serve you six years, and then you must set him free from your service."[^9] But your ancestors did not listen to me or pay attention.[^10]

**15** Recently[^11] you yourselves turned back and did what is right in my eyes, each of you proclaiming liberty to his neighbor. You made a covenant before me in the house that bears my name.[^12]

**16** But then you turned back and profaned my name. Each of you took back his male and female slaves, whom you had set free to go where they wished,[^13] and you forced them to be your slaves again.

**17** Therefore this is what the LORD says: You have not listened to me and proclaimed liberty, each to his brother and each to his neighbor. So I am going to proclaim liberty for you, declares the LORD, liberty to the sword, to plague and to famine. I will make you an object of terror to all the kingdoms of the earth.

**18** As for the men who transgressed my covenant, who did not keep the terms of the covenant they made before me, I will make them like the calf they cut in two and passed between its pieces,[^14]

**19** the officials of Judah and the officials of Jerusalem, the court officials,[^15] the priests and all the people of the land[^16] who passed between the pieces of the calf.

**20** I will give them into the hand of their enemies and into the hand of those who seek their lives. Their corpses will be food for the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth.

**21** Zedekiah king of Judah and his officials I will give into the hand of their enemies and into the hand of those who seek their lives, into the hand of the army of the king of Babylon, which has withdrawn from you.[^17]

**22** I am going to give the command, declares the LORD, and I will bring them back to this city. They will fight against it, capture it and burn it with fire. I will make the cities of Judah a desolation, with no one living in them.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: In 588 BC, after the siege of Jerusalem had begun (39:1; 52:4).
[^3]: Literally "Your eyes will see the eyes of the king of Babylon, and his mouth will speak with your mouth".
[^4]: Literally "As the burnings for your fathers ... so they will burn for you". A fire of spices was burned in honor of a king at his burial (2 Chronicles 16:14; 21:19).
[^5]: Hebrew *hoy adon*, a cry of mourning for the dead (22:18).
[^6]: Fortified towns in the Shephelah, southwest of Jerusalem.
[^7]: Hebrew *deror*, "release, liberty", the word used for the release proclaimed in the year of Jubilee (Leviticus 25:10). Also in verses 15 and 17.
[^8]: Hebrew *yehudi*, "a Judean". Literally "a Judean, his brother".
[^9]: Deuteronomy 15:12; compare Exodus 21:2. "At the end of seven years" is the wording of Deuteronomy 15:1.
[^10]: Literally "did not incline their ear".
[^11]: Literally "Today".
[^12]: Literally "over which my name is called", that is, which belongs to me (7:10).
[^13]: Literally "free, to their soul".
[^14]: The Hebrew is abrupt. Literally "I will give the men who transgressed my covenant ... the calf that they cut in two". A covenant could be made by walking between the halves of a slaughtered animal, calling on oneself the same fate if one broke it (compare Genesis 15:9–17). The Hebrew for "make a covenant" is literally "cut a covenant".
[^15]: Hebrew *sarisim*, court officials. The word can also mean eunuchs.
[^16]: Hebrew *am ha-arets*, the landowning citizens of Judah.
[^17]: The Babylonians had lifted the siege for a time when an Egyptian army approached (37:5–11).
