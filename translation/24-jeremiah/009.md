**1** Oh, that I had in the wilderness a travelers' shelter,[^1] so that I could leave my people and go away from them! For they are all adulterers, a crowd of traitors.

**2** They bend their tongues like bows to shoot lies.[^2] It is not by faithfulness that they have grown strong in the land, for they go from evil to evil, and they do not know me, declares the LORD.[^3]

**3** Let everyone be on guard against his neighbor, and do not trust any brother. For every brother is a cheat like Jacob,[^4] and every neighbor goes about slandering.

**4** Each one deceives his neighbor, and no one speaks the truth. They have taught their tongues to speak lies; they wear themselves out doing wrong.

**5** You live in the midst of deceit; in their deceit they refuse to know me, declares the LORD.

**6** Therefore this is what the LORD of Armies[^5] says: I am going to refine them and test them, for what else can I do because of my dear people?[^6]

**7** Their tongue is a sharpened arrow;[^7] it speaks deceit. With his mouth each one speaks peace to his neighbor, but in his heart he sets an ambush for him.

**8** Should I not punish them for these things? declares the LORD. Should I not avenge myself[^8] on a nation like this?

**9** For the mountains I will take up weeping and wailing, and a lament for the pastures of the wilderness, because they are laid waste, with no one passing through, and the lowing of cattle is not heard. Both the birds of the sky and the animals have fled; they are gone.

**10** I will make Jerusalem a heap of ruins, a haunt of jackals. I will make the cities of Judah a desolation, with no one living there.

**11** Who is wise enough to understand this? To whom has the mouth of the LORD spoken, so that he can explain it? Why has the land perished and been laid waste like a wilderness, with no one passing through?

**12** The LORD said: Because they have abandoned my instruction,[^9] which I set before them, and have not listened to my voice or walked by it,

**13** but have followed the stubbornness of their hearts and gone after the Baals, as their ancestors taught them,

**14** therefore this is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: I am going to feed this people with wormwood[^10] and give them poisoned water to drink.

**15** I will scatter them among nations that neither they nor their ancestors have known, and I will send the sword after them until I have made an end of them.

**16** This is what the LORD of Armies says: Consider, and call for the mourning women to come; send for the most skilled of them[^11] to come.

**17** Let them hurry and raise a lament over us, so that our eyes may run with tears and our eyelids flow with water.

**18** For a sound of wailing is heard from Zion: "How we are ruined! We are utterly put to shame, for we have left the land, because they have torn down our homes."[^12]

**19** Now hear the word of the LORD, you women; let your ears receive the word of his mouth. Teach your daughters a lament, and each woman her neighbor a funeral song.

**20** For death has climbed in through our windows; it has entered our fortresses, to cut off the children from the streets and the young men from the squares.

**21** Speak:[^13] This is what the LORD declares: Human corpses will fall like dung on the open field, like sheaves behind the reaper, with no one to gather them.

**22** This is what the LORD says: Let not the wise boast of their wisdom, or the strong boast of their strength, or the rich boast of their riches.

**23** But let the one who boasts boast of this: that he understands and knows me, that I am the LORD, who acts with faithful love,[^14] justice and righteousness on the earth, for in these I delight, declares the LORD.[^15]

**24** Look, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will punish all who are circumcised only in the flesh:[^16]

**25** Egypt, Judah, Edom, the Ammonites, Moab, and all who clip the hair on their temples[^17] and live in the desert. For all the nations are uncircumcised, and the whole house of Israel is uncircumcised in heart.[^18]

[^1]: Verses 1–25 are 9:2–26 in most English Bibles (see 8:23). Literally "a lodging place of travelers".
[^2]: Literally "They bend their tongue, their bow, falsehood".
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: A wordplay. Hebrew *aqov ya'qov*, "cheats as a cheat", plays on the name Jacob, *Ya'aqov* (Genesis 27:36).
[^5]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verses 14 and 16.
[^6]: Literally "because of the daughter of my people", a tender, poetic name for the nation.
[^7]: The Hebrew text is written "a slaughtering arrow" but read "a sharpened arrow".
[^8]: Literally "Should my soul not avenge itself".
[^9]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law".
[^10]: Hebrew *la'anah*, a plant with a very bitter taste.
[^11]: Literally "the wise women", the women skilled in singing laments for the dead.
[^12]: Or "because our homes have cast us out".
[^13]: The Hebrew word *dabber*, "Speak", is unexpected here.
[^14]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^15]: Paul draws on this verse in 1 Corinthians 1:31 and 2 Corinthians 10:17.
[^16]: Literally "all circumcised in foreskin".
[^17]: Literally "all who are cut at the corners", probably desert tribes who cut their hair short at the sides (25:23; 49:32; compare Leviticus 19:27).
[^18]: Several of the peoples named practiced circumcision. The point seems to be that the outward rite counts for nothing without a changed heart (4:4).
