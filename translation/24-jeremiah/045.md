**1** The word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch son of Neriah, when he wrote these words in a scroll at Jeremiah's dictation,[^1] in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah:[^2]

**2** This is what Yahweh,[^3] the God of Israel, says to you, Baruch:

**3** You said, "Woe is me! For Yahweh has added sorrow to my pain. I am worn out with my groaning, and I find no rest."

**4** This is what you are to say to him:[^4] This is what Yahweh says: What I have built I am tearing down, and what I have planted I am uprooting, the whole land.[^5]

**5** And you, are you seeking great things for yourself? Do not seek them. For I am going to bring disaster on all humankind,[^6] declares Yahweh, but I will let you escape with your life[^7] wherever you go.

[^1]: Literally "from the mouth of Jeremiah".
[^2]: In 605 BC, the year Baruch wrote the scroll described in 36:1–8.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Yahweh now speaks to Jeremiah, telling him what to say to Baruch.
[^5]: Literally "and the whole land, it". See 1:10.
[^6]: Literally "all flesh".
[^7]: Literally "I will give you your life as plunder" (21:9).
