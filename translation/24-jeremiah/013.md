**1** This is what Yahweh[^1] said to me: "Go and buy yourself a linen loincloth[^2] and put it around your waist, but do not put it in water."

**2** So I bought a loincloth, as Yahweh had told me, and put it around my waist.

**3** The word of Yahweh came to me a second time:

**4** "Take the loincloth you bought, the one around your waist, and set out for Perath.[^3] Hide it there in a crevice in the rock."

**5** So I went and hid it at Perath, as Yahweh had commanded me.

**6** After many days Yahweh said to me, "Set out for Perath, and take from there the loincloth I commanded you to hide there."

**7** So I went to Perath and dug, and I took the loincloth from the place where I had hidden it. And look, the loincloth was ruined; it was good for nothing.

**8** Then the word of Yahweh came to me:

**9** "This is what Yahweh says: In the same way I will ruin the pride of Judah and the great pride of Jerusalem.

**10** This evil people, who refuse to listen to my words, who follow the stubbornness of their hearts and have gone after other gods to serve them and worship them, will be like this loincloth, which is good for nothing.

**11** For as a loincloth clings to a man's waist, so I made the whole house of Israel and the whole house of Judah cling to me, declares Yahweh, to be my people, for my renown, my praise and my glory. But they did not listen."

**12** Say this word to them: This is what Yahweh, the God of Israel, says: Every jar should be filled with wine.[^4] And when they say to you, "Don't we know very well that every jar should be filled with wine?"

**13** then say to them: This is what Yahweh says: I am going to fill all the inhabitants of this land with drunkenness: the kings who sit on David's throne, the priests, the prophets and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

**14** I will smash them one against another, fathers and sons together, declares Yahweh. I will not show pity or spare them or have compassion; nothing will keep me from destroying them.

**15** Hear and pay attention; do not be proud, for Yahweh has spoken.

**16** Give glory to Yahweh your God before he brings darkness, before your feet stumble on the mountains at dusk. You will look for light, but he will turn it into deep darkness[^5] and make it thick gloom.

**17** But if you will not listen, I will weep in secret[^6] because of your pride. My eyes will weep bitterly and run with tears, because the flock of Yahweh has been taken captive.

**18** Say to the king and to the queen mother,[^7] "Take a lowly seat, for your glorious crowns have come down from your heads."

**19** The cities of the Negev[^8] are shut up, and there is no one to open them. All Judah has been carried into exile, carried into exile completely.

**20** Lift up your eyes and see those coming from the north. Where is the flock that was given to you, your beautiful flock?

**21** What will you say when he sets over you as rulers those you yourself trained to be your friends?[^9] Will not pains seize you like a woman in labor?

**22** And if you say in your heart, "Why have these things happened to me?" it is because of your many sins that your skirts have been stripped off and your body violated.[^10]

**23** Can a Cushite[^11] change his skin, or a leopard its spots? Then you also can do good, you who are trained to do evil.

**24** I will scatter them like chaff blown away by the desert wind.

**25** This is your lot, the portion I have measured out to you, declares Yahweh, because you have forgotten me and trusted in the Lie.[^12]

**26** I myself will pull your skirts up over your face, and your shame will be seen.

**27** Your adulteries and your lustful neighing, your shameless prostitution on the hills in the countryside: I have seen your detestable acts. Woe to you, Jerusalem! You will not be made clean. How long will it still be?[^13]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ezor*, a cloth wrapped around the waist and hips, worn next to the skin.
[^3]: Hebrew *Perat*, the usual name of the Euphrates River, about 350 miles (560 km) away. Some think instead of Parah, a village near Anathoth (Joshua 18:23), whose name is similar. Also in verses 5–7.
[^4]: Apparently a common saying. Hebrew *nevel*, "jar", is a large clay jar for storing wine.
[^5]: Hebrew *tsalmavet*, "deep darkness", traditionally read as "the shadow of death".
[^6]: Literally "my soul will weep in secret places".
[^7]: Hebrew *gevirah*, "great lady", the king's mother, who held a position of honor at court. These are probably Jehoiachin and his mother Nehushta, taken into exile in 597 BC (2 Kings 24:8–15; Jeremiah 22:26).
[^8]: The dry region in the south of Judah.
[^9]: Hebrew *allufim*, "close friends" or "chiefs". The meaning of the verse is uncertain.
[^10]: Literally "your heels have suffered violence", a polite way of speaking of sexual violence.
[^11]: A person from Cush, the land on the upper Nile, south of Egypt.
[^12]: Hebrew *sheqer*, "falsehood", here probably the false gods.
[^13]: Literally "after when yet?"
