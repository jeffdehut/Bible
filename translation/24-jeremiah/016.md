**1** The word of the LORD[^1] came to me:

**2** You must not take a wife, or have sons or daughters in this place.

**3** For this is what the LORD says about the sons and daughters born in this place, and about the mothers who bear them and the fathers who father them in this land:

**4** They will die of deadly diseases. They will not be mourned or buried; they will be like dung on the surface of the ground. They will perish by sword and famine, and their corpses will be food for the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth.

**5** For this is what the LORD says: Do not enter a house where there is a funeral feast,[^2] and do not go to mourn or to grieve for them, for I have withdrawn my peace from this people, declares the LORD, my faithful love[^3] and my compassion.

**6** Both great and small will die in this land. They will not be buried, and no one will mourn for them; no one will gash himself or shave his head for them.[^4]

**7** No one will break bread for the mourner, to comfort him for the dead, and no one will give them a cup of consolation to drink for a father or a mother.

**8** Do not enter a house where there is feasting, to sit with them and eat and drink.

**9** For this is what the LORD of Armies,[^5] the God of Israel, says: I am going to put an end in this place, before your eyes and in your days, to the sound of joy and the sound of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride.

**10** When you tell this people all these things, and they ask you, "Why has the LORD pronounced all this great disaster against us? What is our wrongdoing? What is the sin we have committed against the LORD our God?"

**11** then say to them: It is because your ancestors abandoned me, declares the LORD, and followed other gods and served them and worshiped them. They abandoned me and did not keep my law.

**12** And you have done worse than your ancestors. Look, each of you is following the stubbornness of his evil heart instead of listening to me.

**13** So I will hurl you out of this land into a land that neither you nor your ancestors have known, and there you will serve other gods day and night, for I will show you no favor.

**14** Therefore, look, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when people will no longer say, "As surely as the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites up out of the land of Egypt,"

**15** but, "As surely as the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites up out of the land of the north and out of all the lands where he had driven them." For I will bring them back to their land, which I gave to their ancestors.[^6]

**16** I am going to send for many fishermen, declares the LORD, and they will catch them. After that I will send for many hunters, and they will hunt them down on every mountain and every hill, and in the crevices of the rocks.

**17** For my eyes are on all their ways. They are not hidden from me, and their wrongdoing is not concealed from my eyes.

**18** First I will repay them double for their wrongdoing and their sin, because they have defiled my land with the corpses of their detestable idols,[^7] and filled my inheritance with their detestable things.

**19** LORD, my strength and my stronghold, my refuge in the day of trouble, to you the nations will come from the ends of the earth and say, "Our ancestors inherited nothing but lies, worthless things[^8] that did them no good.

**20** Can human beings make gods for themselves? Such things are not gods!"

**21** Therefore, look, I will teach them. This time I will make them know my power and my might, and they will know that my name is the LORD.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *beit marzeach*, a house where a ritual feast was held, here a feast for mourners (compare Amos 6:7).
[^3]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^4]: Mourning rites forbidden in the law (Leviticus 19:28; Deuteronomy 14:1), but evidently still practiced (41:5; 47:5).
[^5]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^6]: Verses 14–15 are repeated, with small differences, in 23:7–8.
[^7]: Lifeless idols are scornfully called corpses (compare Leviticus 26:30).
[^8]: Hebrew *hevel*, "breath, vapor": see 2:5.
