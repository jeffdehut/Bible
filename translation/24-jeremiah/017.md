**1** The sin of Judah is written with an iron pen; with a diamond point[^1] it is engraved on the tablet of their hearts and on the horns of their altars.[^2]

**2** Their children remember their altars and their Asherah poles[^3] beside the leafy trees, on the high hills,

**3** my mountain in the open country.[^4] Your wealth and all your treasures I will give away as plunder, along with your high places,[^5] because of sin throughout all your territory.

**4** Through your own fault you will lose your hold on the inheritance I gave you.[^6] I will make you serve your enemies in a land you do not know, for you have kindled a fire in my anger that will burn forever.

**5** This is what Yahweh[^7] says: Cursed is the one who trusts in human beings, who makes mere flesh his strength,[^8] and whose heart turns away from Yahweh.

**6** He will be like a shrub in the desert;[^9] he will not see when good comes. He will live in the parched places of the wilderness, in a salt land where no one lives.

**7** Blessed is the one who trusts in Yahweh, whose trust is Yahweh.

**8** He will be like a tree planted by water, that sends out its roots by a stream. It does not see when heat comes;[^10] its leaves stay green. In a year of drought it is not anxious, and it does not stop bearing fruit.[^11]

**9** The heart is more deceitful than anything,[^12] and desperately sick. Who can understand it?

**10** I, Yahweh, search the heart and test the mind,[^13] to give each one according to his ways,[^14] according to the fruit of his deeds.

**11** Like a partridge that hatches eggs it did not lay[^15] is the one who gains riches unjustly. In the middle of his life his riches will leave him, and at his end he will be a fool.

**12** A glorious throne, exalted from the beginning, is the place of our sanctuary.

**13** Yahweh, the hope of Israel, all who abandon you will be put to shame. Those who turn away from me[^16] will be written in the dust, for they have abandoned Yahweh, the spring of living water.

**14** Heal me, Yahweh, and I will be healed; save me, and I will be saved, for you are my praise.[^17]

**15** Look, they keep saying to me, "Where is the word of Yahweh? Let it come now!"

**16** But I have not run away from being a shepherd who follows you,[^18] nor have I longed for the day of disaster. You know what came from my lips; it was before you.

**17** Do not become a terror to me; you are my refuge in the day of disaster.

**18** Let my persecutors be put to shame, but do not let me be put to shame. Let them be terrified, but do not let me be terrified. Bring on them the day of disaster; shatter them with double destruction.

**19** This is what Yahweh said to me: Go and stand in the People's Gate,[^19] through which the kings of Judah come in and go out, and in all the gates of Jerusalem.

**20** Say to them: Hear the word of Yahweh, you kings of Judah, all Judah and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem who come in through these gates.

**21** This is what Yahweh says: For your lives' sake, be careful not to carry a load on the Sabbath day or bring it in through the gates of Jerusalem.

**22** Do not carry a load out of your houses on the Sabbath day or do any work, but keep the Sabbath day holy, as I commanded your ancestors.[^20]

**23** Yet they did not listen or pay attention. They stiffened their necks so as not to listen or accept discipline.

**24** But if you listen carefully to me, declares Yahweh, and bring no load in through the gates of this city on the Sabbath day, but keep the Sabbath day holy and do no work on it,

**25** then kings and officials who sit on David's throne will come in through the gates of this city, riding in chariots and on horses, they and their officials, the people of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem. And this city will be inhabited forever.

**26** People will come from the cities of Judah and the area around Jerusalem, from the land of Benjamin, from the western foothills, from the hill country and from the Negev, bringing burnt offerings and sacrifices, grain offerings and frankincense, and bringing thanksgiving sacrifices[^21] to the house of Yahweh.

**27** But if you do not listen to me, to keep the Sabbath day holy and not carry a load as you come in through the gates of Jerusalem on the Sabbath day, then I will kindle a fire in its gates that will consume the fortresses of Jerusalem, and it will not be put out.

[^1]: Hebrew *shamir*, a very hard stone, perhaps emery or diamond, used for engraving.
[^2]: The Septuagint does not have verses 1–4.
[^3]: Hebrew *asherim*: wooden poles or trees that were symbols of the goddess Asherah.
[^4]: Literally "my mountain in the field", perhaps Jerusalem, or one of the hills of verse 2. The meaning is uncertain. Verses 3–4 repeat 15:13–14 with small differences.
[^5]: Hebrew *bamot*: places of worship, often on hilltops.
[^6]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "And you will let drop, and in you, from your inheritance that I gave you". The verb *shamat* is used of the release of debts and of land left unsown in the seventh year (Exodus 23:11; Deuteronomy 15:1–3).
[^7]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^8]: Literally "who makes flesh his arm".
[^9]: Hebrew *ar'ar*, perhaps a juniper, in the Arabah, the dry rift valley of the Jordan and the Dead Sea.
[^10]: The Hebrew text is written "It does not fear when heat comes" but read "It does not see when heat comes".
[^11]: Compare Psalm 1:3.
[^12]: Hebrew *aqov*, "crooked, deceitful", the word that plays on the name Jacob in 9:3 (9:4 in most English Bibles).
[^13]: Literally "the kidneys", thought of as the seat of the emotions.
[^14]: The Hebrew text is written "his way" but read "his ways".
[^15]: The partridge was thought to sit on eggs it had not laid, and to lose the young when they hatched.
[^16]: The Hebrew changes from "you" to "me", as if Yahweh were speaking.
[^17]: Here begins another of Jeremiah's personal laments (17:14–18; see 11:18).
[^18]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain.
[^19]: Literally "the gate of the sons of the people", a gate of Jerusalem not mentioned elsewhere.
[^20]: Exodus 20:8–10; Deuteronomy 5:12–14.
[^21]: Hebrew *todah*, "thanksgiving, praise" (Leviticus 7:12).
