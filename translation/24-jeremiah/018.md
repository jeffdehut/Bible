**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh:[^1]

**2** "Get up and go down to the potter's house, and there I will let you hear my words."

**3** So I went down to the potter's house, and there he was, working at the wheel.[^2]

**4** Whenever the vessel he was making from the clay was spoiled in the potter's hand, he would make it again into another vessel, as seemed right to the potter.

**5** Then the word of Yahweh came to me:

**6** "House of Israel, can I not do with you as this potter does? declares Yahweh. Look, like the clay in the potter's hand, so are you in my hand, house of Israel.

**7** At one moment I may speak about a nation or a kingdom, to uproot it, tear it down and destroy it.

**8** But if that nation I have spoken against turns from its evil, I will relent of the disaster I intended to bring on it.

**9** At another moment I may speak about a nation or a kingdom, to build it and to plant it.

**10** But if it does evil in my eyes and does not listen to my voice, I will relent of the good I had said I would do for it.

**11** So now, say to the people of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem: 'This is what Yahweh says: Look, I am shaping[^3] disaster against you and devising a plan against you. Turn back, each of you, from his evil way, and make your ways and your deeds good.'

**12** But they will say, 'It's hopeless! We will follow our own plans; each of us will act according to the stubbornness of his evil heart.'"

**13** Therefore this is what Yahweh says: Ask among the nations: Who has ever heard anything like this? The virgin Israel has done a most horrible thing.

**14** Does the snow of Lebanon ever vanish from the rocks of the field? Do the cold waters flowing from afar ever run dry?[^4]

**15** Yet my people have forgotten me; they burn incense[^5] to what is worthless. They have stumbled in their ways, the ancient paths, to walk in bypaths, on a road not built up.

**16** Their land will become a horror, a thing to hiss at[^6] forever. All who pass by it will be appalled and shake their heads.

**17** Like the east wind I will scatter them before the enemy. I will show them my back and not my face in the day of their disaster.

**18** Then they said, "Come, let us make plans against Jeremiah, for instruction[^7] will not be lost from the priest, nor counsel from the wise, nor the word from the prophet. Come, let us strike at him with our tongues,[^8] and pay no attention to anything he says."

**19** Pay attention to me, Yahweh,[^9] and hear what my accusers are saying.

**20** Should good be repaid with evil? Yet they have dug a pit for me. Remember how I stood before you to speak good on their behalf, to turn your wrath away from them.

**21** So hand their children over to famine, and give them over to the power of the sword.[^10] Let their wives be made childless and widowed; let their men be killed by plague,[^11] their young men struck down by the sword in battle.

**22** Let a cry be heard from their houses when you suddenly bring raiders against them, for they have dug a pit to capture me and hidden snares for my feet.

**23** But you, Yahweh, know all their plotting to kill me. Do not forgive their wrongdoing, or blot out their sin from your sight. Let them be overthrown before you; deal with them in the time of your anger.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "on the two stones", the potter's wheel, with an upper disc on which the clay was shaped and a lower one turned by the feet.
[^3]: Hebrew *yotser*, the word for "potter" in verses 2–6.
[^4]: The Hebrew of this verse is difficult. Literally "Does it leave the rock of the field, the snow of Lebanon? Are they plucked up, the strange, cold, flowing waters?" Nature keeps its course, but Israel has abandoned Yahweh.
[^5]: Or "burn offerings". The Hebrew verb *qatar* means to make something go up in smoke.
[^6]: A gesture of horror and scorn.
[^7]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law", which the priests taught (Deuteronomy 33:10; Malachi 2:7).
[^8]: Literally "strike him with the tongue", that is, bring charges against him.
[^9]: Here begins another of Jeremiah's personal laments (18:19–23; see 11:18).
[^10]: Literally "pour them out on the hands of the sword".
[^11]: Literally "slain of death". Hebrew *mavet*, "death", can mean a deadly disease (15:2).
