**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from Yahweh[^1] when King Zedekiah sent to him Pashhur son of Malchiah[^2] and the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah, saying,

**2** "Please inquire of Yahweh for us, because Nebuchadnezzar[^3] king of Babylon is making war against us. Perhaps Yahweh will act for us in keeping with all his wonderful deeds, and make him withdraw from us."

**3** Jeremiah said to them, "Say this to Zedekiah:

**4** 'This is what Yahweh, the God of Israel, says: I am going to turn back the weapons of war in your hands, with which you are fighting the king of Babylon and the Babylonians[^4] who are besieging you outside the wall, and I will gather them into the middle of this city.

**5** I myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and a strong arm, in anger, in fury and in great wrath.

**6** I will strike down the inhabitants of this city, both human beings and animals; they will die of a great plague.

**7** After that, declares Yahweh, I will hand over Zedekiah king of Judah, his servants and the people in this city who survive the plague, the sword and the famine, to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, to their enemies and to those who seek their lives. He will strike them down with the edge of the sword; he will not spare them, or have pity or compassion.'"

**8** And to this people you are to say: This is what Yahweh says: I am setting before you the way of life and the way of death.[^5]

**9** Whoever stays in this city will die by the sword, famine or plague. But whoever goes out and surrenders to the Babylonians who are besieging you will live; he will escape with his life.[^6]

**10** For I have set my face against this city for harm and not for good, declares Yahweh. It will be handed over to the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire.

**11** And to the royal house of Judah: Hear the word of Yahweh,

**12** house of David. This is what Yahweh says: Do justice every morning, and rescue the one who has been robbed from the hand of the oppressor, or my wrath will break out like fire and burn with no one to put it out, because of the evil of your deeds.[^7]

**13** Look, I am against you, you who are enthroned above the valley,[^8] rock of the plain, declares Yahweh, you who say, "Who can come down against us? Who can enter our dwellings?"

**14** I will punish you according to the fruit of your deeds, declares Yahweh. I will kindle a fire in her forest,[^9] and it will consume everything around her.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. The events of this chapter took place during the Babylonian siege of Jerusalem, 588–587 BC.
[^2]: Not the Pashhur of 20:1–6. He appears again in 38:1. Zephaniah appears in 29:25–29; 37:3; 52:24.
[^3]: Hebrew *Nevukhadre'tsar*, a spelling closer to the Babylonian name *Nabu-kudurri-usur*, used often in Jeremiah and Ezekiel. Elsewhere the name is usually spelled *Nevukhadnetsar*, Nebuchadnezzar. He was king of Babylon from 605 to 562 BC.
[^4]: Hebrew *Kasdim*, traditionally "Chaldeans": the people of southern Mesopotamia whose kings then ruled Babylon. Also in verse 9.
[^5]: Compare Deuteronomy 30:15, 19.
[^6]: Literally "his life will be to him as plunder", that is, the only thing he takes away will be his life (38:2; 39:18; 45:5).
[^7]: The Hebrew text is written "their deeds" but read "your deeds".
[^8]: Literally "you who dwell in the valley", Jerusalem, set on its hill above the valleys (compare 2 Samuel 5:6). *You* is feminine, addressed to the city.
[^9]: Perhaps the royal palace, built with so much cedar that part of it was called the House of the Forest of Lebanon (1 Kings 7:2; Jeremiah 22:6–7, 23).
