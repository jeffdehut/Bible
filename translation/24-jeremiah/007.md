**1** The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD:[^1]

**2** Stand in the gate of the LORD's house and proclaim this word there. Say: Hear the word of the LORD, all you people of Judah who come in through these gates to worship the LORD.[^2]

**3** This is what the LORD of Armies,[^3] the God of Israel, says: Make your ways and your deeds good, and I will let you live in this place.[^4]

**4** Do not trust in deceptive words: "This is the temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD!"[^5]

**5** For if you truly make your ways and your deeds good, if you truly do justice between one person and another,

**6** if you do not oppress the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow, and do not shed innocent blood in this place, and do not follow other gods to your own harm,

**7** then I will let you live in this place, in the land I gave your ancestors, for all time.[^6]

**8** But look, you are trusting in deceptive words that are worthless.

**9** Will you steal, murder, commit adultery, swear falsely, burn incense[^7] to Baal and follow other gods you have not known,

**10** and then come and stand before me in this house, which bears my name,[^8] and say, "We are safe!", only to go on doing all these detestable things?

**11** Has this house, which bears my name, become a den of robbers in your eyes?[^9] But I, look, I have seen it, declares the LORD.

**12** Go now to my place that was in Shiloh, where I first made my name dwell, and see what I did to it because of the wickedness of my people Israel.[^10]

**13** And now, because you have done all these things, declares the LORD, and though I spoke to you again and again,[^11] you did not listen, and when I called you, you did not answer,

**14** I will do to the house that bears my name, the house you trust in, the place I gave to you and your ancestors, just what I did to Shiloh.

**15** I will throw you out of my sight, just as I threw out all your relatives, all the descendants of Ephraim.[^12]

**16** As for you, do not pray for this people. Do not raise a cry or a prayer for them, and do not plead with me, for I will not listen to you.

**17** Do you not see what they are doing in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?

**18** The children gather wood, the fathers light the fire, and the women knead dough to make cakes for the Queen of Heaven.[^13] They pour out drink offerings to other gods to provoke me to anger.

**19** But is it I they are provoking? declares the LORD. Is it not themselves, to their own shame?[^14]

**20** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD[^15] says: My anger and my wrath will be poured out on this place, on human beings and animals, on the trees of the field and the produce of the ground. It will burn and not be put out.

**21** This is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: Add your burnt offerings to your sacrifices, and eat the meat yourselves![^16]

**22** For when I brought your ancestors out of the land of Egypt, I did not speak to them or command them about burnt offerings and sacrifices.

**23** But this is what I commanded them: "Listen to my voice, and I will be your God, and you will be my people. Walk in all the way that I command you, so that it may go well with you."

**24** But they did not listen or pay attention.[^17] They followed their own plans, in the stubbornness of their evil hearts. They went backward and not forward.

**25** From the day your ancestors came out of the land of Egypt until this day, I have sent you all my servants the prophets, sending them again and again, day after day.

**26** But they did not listen to me or pay attention. They stiffened their necks;[^18] they did more evil than their ancestors.

**27** When you tell them all these things, they will not listen to you. When you call to them, they will not answer you.

**28** So say to them: This is the nation that has not listened to the voice of the LORD its God and has not accepted discipline. Truth[^19] has perished; it has been cut off from their mouths.

**29** Cut off your hair[^20] and throw it away; raise a lament on the bare heights, for the LORD has rejected and abandoned the generation that has roused his fury.

**30** For the people of Judah have done what is evil in my eyes, declares the LORD. They have set up their detestable idols in the house that bears my name, and defiled it.

**31** They have built the high places[^21] of Topheth in the Valley of Ben-hinnom,[^22] to burn their sons and their daughters in the fire, something I did not command, nor did it ever enter my mind.

**32** Therefore, look, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when it will no longer be called Topheth or the Valley of Ben-hinnom, but the Valley of Slaughter. They will bury the dead in Topheth until there is no room left.[^23]

**33** The corpses of this people will be food for the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth, with no one to frighten them away.

**34** I will put an end to the sound of joy and the sound of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, in the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem, for the land will become a ruin.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. "The LORD's house" and "this house" in this chapter are the temple. Chapter 26 tells what happened when Jeremiah gave this message.
[^2]: The message Jeremiah is to proclaim runs to verse 15, and verses 16–34 continue the LORD's words to him.
[^3]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verse 21.
[^4]: The same consonants can be read "and I will live with you in this place". Also in verse 7.
[^5]: Literally "the temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD, the temple of the LORD are these". The words are a slogan: The LORD would never let his own temple fall.
[^6]: Literally "from long ago and to forever".
[^7]: Or "burn offerings". The Hebrew verb *qatar* means to make something go up in smoke.
[^8]: Literally "over which my name is called", that is, which belongs to me. Also in verses 11, 14 and 30.
[^9]: Jesus quotes this line in Mark 11:17.
[^10]: The tabernacle stood at Shiloh in the time of Eli (1 Samuel 1–4). The town seems to have been destroyed after the Philistines captured the ark (Psalm 78:60).
[^11]: Literally "rising early and speaking", a phrase Jeremiah uses often for the LORD's persistent warnings. Also in verse 25.
[^12]: That is, the people of the northern kingdom, taken into exile by Assyria (2 Kings 17:6, 18–23).
[^13]: Probably the Mesopotamian goddess Ishtar, or the Canaanite Astarte, worshiped as a goddess of love and of the sky (44:17–19, 25).
[^14]: Literally "to the shame of their faces".
[^15]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^16]: A burnt offering was burned whole on the altar, while the meat of most other sacrifices was eaten by the worshipers. The LORD scornfully tells them to eat it all.
[^17]: Literally "did not incline their ear". Also in verse 26.
[^18]: That is, they were stubborn, like an ox that will not bend its neck to the yoke.
[^19]: Or "Faithfulness". Hebrew *emunah*.
[^20]: Literally "Shear your crown". The word *nezer* is used of the uncut hair of a Nazirite, consecrated to God (Numbers 6:5–7). *Your* is feminine: Jerusalem is addressed.
[^21]: Hebrew *bamot*: places of worship, often on hilltops.
[^22]: Hebrew *tofet*, perhaps "fireplace, hearth", a place where children were burned as sacrifices (2 Kings 23:10). Ben-hinnom, "the Son of Hinnom", was a valley south and west of Jerusalem.
[^23]: Or "because there will be no other place".
