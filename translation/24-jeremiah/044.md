**1** The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the Judeans[^1] living in the land of Egypt, those living at Migdol, at Tahpanhes, at Memphis and in the land of Pathros:[^2]

**2** This is what the LORD of Armies,[^3] the God of Israel, says: You have seen all the disaster I brought on Jerusalem and on all the cities of Judah. Look, today they are a ruin, with no one living in them,

**3** because of the evil they did to provoke me to anger, by going to burn incense[^4] and to serve other gods that they had not known, neither they, nor you, nor your ancestors.

**4** I sent you all my servants the prophets again and again,[^5] saying, "Do not do this detestable thing that I hate!"

**5** But they did not listen or pay attention;[^6] they did not turn from their evil and stop burning incense to other gods.

**6** So my wrath and my anger were poured out and burned in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, and they became a ruin and a desolation, as they are today.

**7** And now this is what the LORD, the God of Armies,[^7] the God of Israel, says: Why are you doing such great harm to yourselves, cutting off from Judah man and woman, child and infant, so that you leave yourselves no remnant?

**8** Why do you provoke me to anger with the works of your hands, burning incense to other gods in the land of Egypt where you have come to live, so that you will be cut off and become a curse and a disgrace among all the nations of the earth?

**9** Have you forgotten the evil deeds of your ancestors, the evil deeds of the kings of Judah and the evil deeds of their wives,[^8] your own evil deeds and the evil deeds of your wives, which they committed in the land of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?

**10** To this day they have not humbled themselves,[^9] nor have they shown fear or walked in my law[^10] and my decrees that I set before you and your ancestors.

**11** Therefore this is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: I am going to set my face against you for harm, to cut off all Judah.

**12** I will take the remnant of Judah who set their faces to go to the land of Egypt to live there, and they will all come to an end. In the land of Egypt they will fall; by the sword and by famine they will come to an end. From the least to the greatest they will die by the sword and by famine. They will become a curse, a horror, a curse and a disgrace.[^11]

**13** I will punish those who live in the land of Egypt as I punished Jerusalem, with the sword, famine and plague.

**14** None of the remnant of Judah who have come to live in the land of Egypt will escape or survive to return to the land of Judah, where they long[^12] to return to live. For none will return except a few fugitives.

**15** Then all the men who knew that their wives were burning incense to other gods, and all the women who stood by, a great assembly, and all the people who lived in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, answered Jeremiah:

**16** "As for the word you have spoken to us in the name of the LORD, we will not listen to you.

**17** Instead we will certainly do everything we have vowed:[^13] we will burn incense to the Queen of Heaven[^14] and pour out drink offerings to her, as we did, we and our ancestors, our kings and our officials, in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. For then we had plenty of food, and we were well off and saw no disaster.

**18** But since we stopped burning incense to the Queen of Heaven and pouring out drink offerings to her, we have lacked everything, and we have been consumed by the sword and by famine.

**19** And when we burned incense to the Queen of Heaven and poured out drink offerings to her, was it without our husbands' consent[^15] that we made cakes for her bearing her image,[^16] and poured out drink offerings to her?"

**20** Then Jeremiah said to all the people, to the men and the women and all the people who had given him this answer,

**21** "As for the incense you burned in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem, you and your ancestors, your kings, your officials and the people of the land,[^17] did not the LORD remember it? Did it not come into his mind?

**22** The LORD could no longer bear your evil deeds and the detestable things you did. So your land became a ruin, a horror and a curse, without inhabitant, as it is today.

**23** It is because you burned incense and sinned against the LORD, and did not obey the voice of the LORD or walk in his law, his decrees and his statutes,[^18] that this disaster has come upon you, as it is today."

**24** Then Jeremiah said to all the people and to all the women, "Hear the word of the LORD, all you people of Judah who are in the land of Egypt.

**25** This is what the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: You and your wives[^19] have spoken with your mouths and fulfilled it with your hands, saying, 'We will certainly carry out the vows we have made, to burn incense to the Queen of Heaven and pour out drink offerings to her.' Go ahead then, keep your vows! Carry out your vows!

**26** Therefore hear the word of the LORD, all you people of Judah who live in the land of Egypt: I have sworn by my great name, says the LORD, that my name will never again be invoked by any man of Judah in all the land of Egypt, saying, 'As surely as the Lord GOD[^20] lives.'

**27** I am watching over them[^21] for harm and not for good. All the people of Judah who are in the land of Egypt will come to an end by the sword and by famine until they are all gone.

**28** Those who escape the sword will return from the land of Egypt to the land of Judah, few in number. Then the whole remnant of Judah who came to the land of Egypt to live there will know whose word will stand, mine or theirs.

**29** This will be the sign to you, declares the LORD, that I will punish you in this place, so that you will know that my words against you will surely stand for harm:

**30** This is what the LORD says: I am going to give Pharaoh Hophra king of Egypt into the hand of his enemies, into the hand of those who seek his life, just as I gave Zedekiah king of Judah into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, his enemy who sought his life."[^22]

[^1]: Hebrew *ha-yehudim*, the people of Judah.
[^2]: Migdol and Tahpanhes were in the eastern Nile delta; Memphis (Hebrew *Noph*) was the old capital of Lower Egypt (2:16). Pathros is Upper Egypt, the Nile valley to the south.
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verses 11 and 25.
[^4]: Or "burn offerings". The Hebrew verb *qatar* means to make something go up in smoke. Also in verses 5, 8, 15, 17–19, 21, 23 and 25.
[^5]: Literally "rising early and sending".
[^6]: Literally "did not incline their ear".
[^7]: Hebrew *YHWH Elohei tseva'ot*, traditionally "the LORD God of hosts".
[^8]: Literally "his wives".
[^9]: Literally "they have not been crushed".
[^10]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law". Also in verse 23.
[^11]: Two different Hebrew words are translated "curse" in this verse, *alah* and *qelalah* (42:18).
[^12]: Literally "lift up their soul".
[^13]: Literally "every word that has gone out of our mouth".
[^14]: See 7:18.
[^15]: Literally "without our men". A vow made by a wife stood only if her husband did not overrule it (Numbers 30:7–16, which is 30:6–15 in most English Bibles). The women speak in this verse.
[^16]: Hebrew *kavvanim*, a word used only here and in 7:18, a kind of cake. The meaning of the words "bearing her image" is uncertain.
[^17]: Hebrew *am ha-arets*, the landowning citizens of Judah.
[^18]: Hebrew *edot*, traditionally "testimonies": the solemn terms of the covenant.
[^19]: In the Hebrew the verbs that follow are feminine: the women are addressed.
[^20]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles.
[^21]: Hebrew *shoqed*, the word used in 1:12.
[^22]: Hophra (Egyptian Wahibre, Greek Apries) ruled Egypt from 589 to 570 BC. He was overthrown by his general Amasis, and later killed.
