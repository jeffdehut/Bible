**1** At the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah,[^1] this word came from the LORD:[^2]

**2** "This is what the LORD says: Stand in the court of the LORD's house and speak to all the people of the towns of Judah who come to worship in the LORD's house. Tell them all the words I command you to speak to them; do not leave out a word.

**3** Perhaps they will listen, and each of them will turn from his evil way. Then I will relent of the disaster I am planning to bring on them because of their evil deeds.

**4** Say to them: 'This is what the LORD says: If you do not listen to me and walk in my instruction,[^3] which I have set before you,

**5** and listen to the words of my servants the prophets, whom I send to you again and again,[^4] though you have not listened,

**6** then I will make this house like Shiloh,[^5] and I will make this city a curse for all the nations of the earth.'"

**7** The priests, the prophets and all the people heard Jeremiah speaking these words in the house of the LORD.

**8** When Jeremiah had finished speaking everything that the LORD had commanded him to speak to all the people, the priests, the prophets and all the people seized him and said, "You must die!

**9** Why have you prophesied in the name of the LORD, 'This house will be like Shiloh, and this city will be laid waste, with no one living in it'?" And all the people crowded around Jeremiah in the house of the LORD.

**10** When the officials of Judah heard these things, they came up from the king's palace to the house of the LORD and took their seats at the entrance of the New Gate of the LORD's house.

**11** Then the priests and the prophets said to the officials and to all the people, "This man deserves the death sentence, because he has prophesied against this city, as you have heard with your own ears."

**12** Then Jeremiah said to all the officials and to all the people, "The LORD sent me to prophesy against this house and this city all the words you have heard.

**13** So now make your ways and your deeds good, and listen to the voice of the LORD your God. Then the LORD will relent of the disaster he has pronounced against you.

**14** As for me, I am in your hands. Do with me whatever seems good and right to you.

**15** But know for certain that if you put me to death, you will bring innocent blood on yourselves, on this city and on its inhabitants, for in truth the LORD has sent me to you to speak all these words in your hearing."

**16** Then the officials and all the people said to the priests and the prophets, "This man does not deserve the death sentence, for he has spoken to us in the name of the LORD our God."

**17** Some of the elders of the land stood up and said to the whole assembly of the people,

**18** "Micah of Moresheth[^6] prophesied in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah. He said to all the people of Judah, 'This is what the LORD of Armies[^7] says: Zion will be plowed like a field, Jerusalem will become a heap of ruins, and the temple mount a wooded height.'[^8]

**19** Did Hezekiah king of Judah and all Judah put him to death? Did he not fear the LORD and seek the LORD's favor?[^9] And the LORD relented of the disaster he had pronounced against them. But we are about to bring a great disaster on ourselves!"

**20** There was also a man who prophesied in the name of the LORD, Uriah son of Shemaiah from Kiriath-jearim. He prophesied against this city and this land just as Jeremiah did.

**21** When King Jehoiakim and all his warriors and all the officials heard his words, the king sought to put him to death. But Uriah heard of it and was afraid, and he fled to Egypt.

**22** Then King Jehoiakim sent men to Egypt: Elnathan son of Achbor[^10] and some men with him.

**23** They brought Uriah out of Egypt and took him to King Jehoiakim, who struck him down with the sword and threw his body into the burial ground of the common people.

**24** But Ahikam son of Shaphan[^11] protected Jeremiah,[^12] so that he was not handed over to the people to be put to death.

[^1]: About 609–608 BC. This chapter tells what happened when Jeremiah gave the message of 7:1–15.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. "The LORD's house" and "this house" are the temple.
[^3]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law".
[^4]: Literally "rising early and sending".
[^5]: See 7:12–14.
[^6]: The Hebrew text is written "Micaiah" but read "Micah". Moresheth was a town in the Shephelah of Judah (Micah 1:1, 14).
[^7]: *The LORD of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^8]: Micah 3:12. *The temple mount* is literally "the mountain of the house".
[^9]: Literally "soften the face of the LORD".
[^10]: Probably the official of 36:12, 25, and perhaps the father of Nehushta, Jehoiachin's mother (2 Kings 24:8). Achbor was one of the men Josiah sent to Huldah (2 Kings 22:12–14).
[^11]: Ahikam was one of Josiah's officials (2 Kings 22:12). His son Gedaliah was later made governor of Judah (Jeremiah 39:14; 40:5).
[^12]: Literally "the hand of Ahikam son of Shaphan was with Jeremiah".
