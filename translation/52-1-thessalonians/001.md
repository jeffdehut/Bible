**1** Paul, Silvanus and Timothy, to the church of the Thessalonians in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ:[^1] Grace to you and peace.[^2]

**2** We always give thanks to God for all of you, mentioning you in our prayers, constantly

**3** remembering before our God and Father your work that comes from faith, your labor prompted by love, and your endurance that comes from hope in our Lord Jesus Christ.[^3]

**4** For we know, brothers and sisters[^4] loved by God, that he has chosen you,[^5]

**5** because our good news came to you not in word only, but also in power and in the Holy Spirit and with full conviction. You know what kind of people we proved to be among you for your sake.

**6** And you became imitators of us and of the Lord, for you received the word in much affliction with the joy of the Holy Spirit,

**7** so that you became an example to all the believers in Macedonia and in Achaia.

**8** For from you the word of the Lord has sounded forth not only in Macedonia and Achaia, but your faith in God has gone out everywhere, so that we have no need to say anything.

**9** For they themselves report about us what kind of welcome we had among you,[^6] and how you turned to God from idols to serve the living and true God,

**10** and to wait for his Son from heaven, whom he raised from the dead, Jesus, who rescues us from the coming wrath.

[^1]: *Christ*: Greek *Christos*, "anointed one", the Greek equivalent of "Messiah". In Paul's letters it usually works as a name, so this translation writes "Christ". Paul's order, "Christ Jesus" or "Jesus Christ", is kept as the Greek has it.
[^2]: Many manuscripts add "from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ".
[^3]: Literally "your work of faith and labor of love and endurance of hope of our Lord Jesus Christ".
[^4]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike.
[^5]: Literally "knowing your election".
[^6]: Literally "what kind of entrance we had to you".
