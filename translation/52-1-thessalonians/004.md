**1** Finally, then, brothers and sisters,[^1] we ask you and urge you in the Lord Jesus that, as you received from us how you ought to live[^2] and to please God, as in fact you are doing,[^3] you do so more and more.

**2** For you know what instructions we gave you through the Lord Jesus.

**3** For this is the will of God, your holiness:[^4] that you abstain from sexual immorality,

**4** that each of you know how to gain control over his own body[^5] in holiness and honor,

**5** not in lustful passion like the Gentiles who do not know God,

**6** and that no one wrong his brother or take advantage of him in this matter,[^6] because the Lord is an avenger in all these things, as we told you before and solemnly warned you.

**7** For God has not called us to impurity, but in holiness.

**8** So whoever rejects this rejects not a human being but God, who also gives his Holy Spirit to you.[^7]

**9** Now concerning love for one another as brothers and sisters,[^8] you have no need for anyone to write to you, for you yourselves are taught by God[^9] to love one another.

**10** And indeed you are doing it toward all the brothers and sisters throughout Macedonia. But we urge you, brothers and sisters, to do so more and more,

**11** and to make it your ambition to live quietly, to mind your own affairs, and to work with your hands, as we instructed you,

**12** so that you may live properly in the eyes of outsiders and be dependent on no one.[^10]

**13** We do not want you to be uninformed, brothers and sisters, about those who are asleep,[^11] so that you may not grieve as others do who have no hope.

**14** For if we believe that Jesus died and rose again, even so God will bring with him those who have fallen asleep through Jesus.[^12]

**15** For this we tell you by the word of the Lord: we who are alive, who are left until the coming[^13] of the Lord, will certainly not go ahead of those who have fallen asleep.

**16** For the Lord himself will come down from heaven with a cry of command, with the voice of an archangel and with the trumpet of God, and the dead in Christ will rise first.

**17** Then we who are alive, who are left, will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet[^14] the Lord in the air. And so we will always be with the Lord.

**18** So encourage one another with these words.

[^1]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike; also in verses 10 and 13.
[^2]: Literally "walk", a common biblical picture for a way of life; also in verse 12.
[^3]: Many manuscripts leave out "as in fact you are doing".
[^4]: Or "your sanctification"; also in verses 4 and 7. Greek *hagiasmos*, "being made holy".
[^5]: Literally "to acquire his own vessel". "Vessel" may mean a man's own body, or a wife (compare 1 Peter 3:7), so this may also mean "to take a wife for himself".
[^6]: Or "in business".
[^7]: Compare Ezekiel 36:27; 37:14.
[^8]: Greek *philadelphia*, "love of brothers".
[^9]: Greek *theodidaktoi*, "God-taught". Compare Isaiah 54:13.
[^10]: Or "lack nothing".
[^11]: That is, those who have died; also "fallen asleep" in verses 14 and 15.
[^12]: Or "God will bring with him, through Jesus, those who have fallen asleep".
[^13]: Greek *parousia*, "presence, arrival", used for the visit of a ruler; here the coming of the Lord (see 2:19).
[^14]: Greek *apantesis*, "meeting", a word used for going out to meet an arriving dignitary and escort him in.
