**1** Now concerning the times and the seasons, brothers and sisters,[^1] you have no need for anything to be written to you.

**2** For you yourselves know very well that the day of the Lord[^2] will come like a thief in the night.

**3** While people are saying, "Peace and security," sudden destruction will come on them, like labor pains on a pregnant woman, and they will not escape.

**4** But you, brothers and sisters, are not in darkness, so that the day would overtake you like a thief.[^3]

**5** For you are all children of light and children of the day.[^4] We do not belong to the night or to darkness.

**6** So then let us not sleep, as others do, but let us stay awake and be sober.

**7** For those who sleep, sleep at night, and those who get drunk are drunk at night.

**8** But since we belong to the day, let us be sober, putting on the breastplate of faith and love, and for a helmet the hope of salvation.[^5]

**9** For God did not appoint us for wrath, but to obtain salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,

**10** who died for us so that, whether we are awake or asleep,[^6] we may live together with him.

**11** So encourage one another and build one another up, just as you are doing.

**12** We ask you, brothers and sisters, to acknowledge[^7] those who labor among you and lead you[^8] in the Lord and admonish you,

**13** and to esteem them very highly in love because of their work. Be at peace among yourselves.[^9]

**14** And we urge you, brothers and sisters: warn the idle,[^10] encourage the fainthearted, help the weak, be patient with everyone.

**15** See that no one repays anyone evil for evil, but always pursue what is good for one another and for everyone.

**16** Always rejoice,

**17** pray constantly,

**18** give thanks in all circumstances; for this is God's will for you in Christ Jesus.

**19** Do not quench the Spirit.

**20** Do not despise prophecies,

**21** but test everything; hold fast to what is good.

**22** Keep away from every form of evil.[^11]

**23** May the God of peace himself make you completely holy, and may your whole spirit and soul and body be kept blameless at the coming[^12] of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**24** The one who calls you is faithful, and he will do it.

**25** Brothers and sisters, pray for us.

**26** Greet all the brothers and sisters with a holy kiss.

**27** I make you swear by the Lord that this letter be read to all the brothers and sisters.[^13]

**28** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.[^14]

[^1]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike; also in verses 4, 12, 14, 25, 26 and 27.
[^2]: In the Old Testament, "the day of the LORD" is the day of his coming in judgment (Amos 5:18–20; Joel 2:1–2).
[^3]: Some manuscripts read "would overtake you like thieves".
[^4]: Literally "sons of light and sons of day".
[^5]: Compare Isaiah 59:17.
[^6]: That is, alive or dead (compare 4:13–15); or, keeping the image of verses 6–7, watchful or not.
[^7]: Literally "to know". Or "to respect".
[^8]: Or "care for you". Greek *proistemi*, "stand before, lead, care for".
[^9]: Some manuscripts read "Be at peace with them".
[^10]: Or "the disorderly". Greek *ataktoi*, "out of order", used of soldiers out of line and of people who neglect their work (compare 4:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:6–11).
[^11]: Or "every kind of evil".
[^12]: Greek *parousia*, "presence, arrival", used for the visit of a ruler; here the coming of the Lord (see 2:19).
[^13]: Many manuscripts read "to all the holy brothers and sisters".
[^14]: Many manuscripts add "Amen".
