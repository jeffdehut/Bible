**1** The famine was still severe in the land.

**2** When they had eaten up the grain they had brought from Egypt, their father said to them, "Go back and buy us a little food."

**3** But Judah said to him, "The man solemnly warned us, 'You will not see my face unless your brother is with you.'

**4** If you will send our brother with us, we will go down and buy food for you.

**5** But if you will not send him, we will not go down, because the man said to us, 'You will not see my face unless your brother is with you.'"

**6** Israel said, "Why did you bring this trouble on me by telling the man you had another brother?"

**7** They said, "The man asked us all about ourselves and our family: 'Is your father still alive? Do you have another brother?' We just answered his questions.[^1] How could we know that he would say, 'Bring your brother down'?"

**8** Then Judah said to his father Israel, "Send the boy with me, and we will get up and go, so that we may live and not die, we and you and our little children.

**9** I myself will guarantee his safety; you can hold me responsible for him. If I do not bring him back to you and set him before you, I will bear the blame before you all my life.[^2]

**10** If we had not delayed, we could have gone there and back twice by now."

**11** Then their father Israel said to them, "If it must be so, then do this: take some of the best products of the land in your bags and take them down to the man as a gift: a little balm and a little honey,[^3] spices and myrrh, pistachio nuts and almonds.

**12** Take double the money with you, and take back with you the money that was returned in the mouths of your sacks. Perhaps it was a mistake.

**13** Take your brother too, and get up and go back to the man.

**14** May God Almighty[^4] make the man show you mercy, so that he will let your other brother and Benjamin come back with you. As for me, if I am to lose my children, I lose them."[^5]

**15** So the men took the gift, and they took double the money with them, and Benjamin. They got up and went down to Egypt, and they stood before Joseph.

**16** When Joseph saw Benjamin with them, he said to the man in charge of his house, "Take these men into the house. Slaughter an animal and prepare a meal, for the men will eat with me at noon."

**17** The man did as Joseph said, and he took the men into Joseph's house.

**18** The men were afraid because they were taken to Joseph's house. They thought, "We have been brought here because of the money that was returned in our sacks the first time. He wants to attack us and overpower us,[^6] and take us as slaves, along with our donkeys."

**19** So they went up to the man in charge of Joseph's house and spoke to him at the entrance of the house.

**20** They said, "Please, my lord, we came down the first time to buy food.

**21** But when we came to the place where we stopped for the night and opened our sacks, there was each man's money in the mouth of his sack, our money in its full weight. So we have brought it back with us.

**22** We have also brought down other money with us to buy food. We don't know who put our money in our sacks."

**23** He said, "Peace to you![^7] Don't be afraid. Your God, the God of your father, has put treasure in your sacks for you. I received your money." Then he brought Simeon out to them.

**24** The man took the men into Joseph's house and gave them water, and they washed their feet. He also gave feed to their donkeys.

**25** They got the gift ready for Joseph's arrival at noon, for they had heard that they were to eat a meal there.

**26** When Joseph came home, they brought the gift they had with them into the house to him, and they bowed down to the ground before him.

**27** He asked how they were, and said, "How is your elderly father you told me about? Is he still alive?"

**28** They said, "Your servant our father is well; he is still alive." And they bowed low and paid him honor.[^8]

**29** When he looked up and saw his brother Benjamin, his own mother's son, he said, "Is this your youngest brother, whom you told me about?" And he said, "May God be gracious to you, my son."

**30** Joseph hurried out, because he was deeply moved at the sight of his brother,[^9] and he was about to weep. He went into a private room and wept there.

**31** Then he washed his face and came out. Controlling himself, he said, "Serve the meal."

**32** They served him by himself, the brothers by themselves, and the Egyptians who ate with him by themselves, because the Egyptians could not eat a meal with the Hebrews, for that is detestable[^10] to the Egyptians.

**33** The brothers were seated before him from the firstborn, as his birthright gave him, to the youngest, as his youth gave him, and the men looked at one another in amazement.

**34** Portions were brought to them from Joseph's table,[^11] but Benjamin's portion was five times as much as any of theirs. So they drank with him and drank freely.[^12]

[^1]: Literally "We told him according to these words".
[^2]: Literally "I will have sinned against you all the days".
[^3]: Hebrew *devash*, which can mean honey or a thick syrup made from dates or grapes. On balm, spices and myrrh, see 37:25.
[^4]: Hebrew *El Shaddai*. The meaning of *Shaddai* is uncertain.
[^5]: Literally "and I, as I am bereaved, I am bereaved".
[^6]: Literally "to roll himself upon us and fall upon us".
[^7]: Hebrew *shalom lakhem*, an ordinary greeting that also reassures: "All is well with you."
[^8]: Literally "they knelt down and bowed down".
[^9]: Literally "because his compassion grew warm for his brother".
[^10]: Hebrew *to'evah*, something that is offensive or forbidden.
[^11]: Literally "from before his face".
[^12]: Or "became drunk".
