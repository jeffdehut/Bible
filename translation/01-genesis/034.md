**1** Now Dinah, the daughter Leah had borne to Jacob, went out to visit the women[^1] of the land.

**2** Shechem son of Hamor the Hivite, the ruler of the region, saw her. He took her, lay with her, and violated her.[^2]

**3** But he became deeply attached[^3] to Dinah, Jacob's daughter. He loved the young woman and spoke tenderly to her.[^4]

**4** Shechem said to his father Hamor, "Get me this girl as my wife."

**5** Jacob heard that Shechem had defiled his daughter Dinah. But his sons were out in the field with his livestock, so Jacob kept quiet until they came home.

**6** Then Shechem's father Hamor went out to Jacob to speak with him.

**7** Jacob's sons came in from the field when they heard. The men were deeply hurt and very angry, because Shechem had done a disgraceful thing in Israel[^5] by lying with Jacob's daughter. Such a thing should not be done.

**8** But Hamor spoke with them and said, "My son Shechem has set his heart on your daughter. Please give her to him as his wife.

**9** Intermarry with us. Give us your daughters, and take our daughters for yourselves.

**10** You can live with us, and the land will be open to you. Live in it, trade[^6] in it, and acquire property in it."

**11** Then Shechem said to Dinah's father and brothers, "Let me find favor with you, and I will give you whatever you ask of me.

**12** Ask me for as great a bride-price and gift as you like, and I will give whatever you tell me. Only give me the young woman as my wife."

**13** Jacob's sons answered Shechem and his father Hamor with deceit when they spoke, because he had defiled their sister Dinah.

**14** They said to them, "We cannot do this. We cannot give our sister to a man who is uncircumcised, for that would be a disgrace to us.

**15** We will agree to your request on only one condition: that you become like us by having every male among you circumcised.

**16** Then we will give our daughters to you, and we will take your daughters for ourselves. We will live with you and become one people.

**17** But if you will not listen to us and be circumcised, we will take our daughter[^7] and go."

**18** Their proposal pleased Hamor and Hamor's son Shechem.

**19** The young man lost no time in doing it, because he was delighted with Jacob's daughter. He was the most honored member of his father's whole household.

**20** So Hamor and his son Shechem went to the gate of their town and spoke to the men of their town.

**21** "These men are friendly toward us," they said. "Let them live in the land and trade in it. Look, the land has plenty of room for them. We can take their daughters as our wives, and we can give them our daughters.

**22** But the men will agree to live with us and become one people on only one condition: that every male among us be circumcised, as they are circumcised.

**23** Won't their livestock, their property and all their animals become ours? Only let us agree to their terms, and they will live with us."

**24** All the men who went out of the gate of his town[^8] listened to Hamor and his son Shechem, and every male was circumcised, all who went out of the gate of his town.

**25** On the third day, while they were still in pain, two of Jacob's sons, Simeon and Levi, Dinah's brothers, each took his sword. They came against the town, which felt secure,[^9] and killed every male.

**26** They killed Hamor and his son Shechem with the sword, took Dinah from Shechem's house, and left.

**27** Jacob's sons came upon the dead and plundered the town, because the people had defiled their sister.

**28** They took their flocks, their cattle and their donkeys, and whatever was in the town and in the fields.

**29** They carried off all their wealth, and they took captive all their little children and their wives. They plundered everything in the houses.

**30** Then Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, "You have brought trouble on me by making me hateful[^10] to the people who live in the land, the Canaanites and the Perizzites. I have only a few men.[^11] If they gather together against me and attack me, I and my household will be destroyed."

**31** But they said, "Should he treat our sister like a prostitute?"

[^1]: Literally "daughters".
[^2]: Hebrew *innah*, "humiliate, violate". Used of sexual relations, it means that he forced her.
[^3]: Literally "his *nephesh* (soul, self) clung".
[^4]: Literally "spoke to the heart of the young woman".
[^5]: Or "against Israel", that is, against Jacob's family. "A disgraceful thing in Israel" became a fixed expression for a serious sexual crime (Deuteronomy 22:21; 2 Samuel 13:12).
[^6]: Or "move about freely". Also in verse 21.
[^7]: That is, Dinah, their sister.
[^8]: That is, all the men of fighting age, or all the citizens who took part in the town's affairs.
[^9]: Hebrew *betach*, "securely". It may mean that the town was off guard, or that the brothers came without meeting any resistance.
[^10]: Literally "making me stink".
[^11]: Literally "I am men of number", that is, few enough to be counted.
