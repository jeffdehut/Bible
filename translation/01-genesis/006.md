**1** When human beings began to multiply on the face of the ground, and daughters were born to them,

**2** the sons of God[^1] saw that the daughters of humankind were beautiful,[^2] and they took as wives any of them they chose.

**3** Then Yahweh[^3] said, "My spirit will not remain[^4] in human beings forever, for they are flesh.[^5] Their days will be 120 years."

**4** The Nephilim[^6] were on the earth in those days, and also afterward, when the sons of God slept with[^7] the daughters of humankind and they bore children to them. These were the mighty men of old, the famous men.[^8]

**5** Yahweh saw that the wickedness of human beings on the earth was great, and that every inclination of the thoughts of their hearts was only evil all the time.

**6** Yahweh regretted that he had made human beings on the earth, and he was grieved to his heart.

**7** So Yahweh said, "I will wipe out from the face of the ground the human beings I have created: people together with livestock, creatures that move along the ground, and birds of the sky. For I regret that I made them."

**8** But Noah found favor in the eyes of Yahweh.

**9** These are the generations[^9] of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, blameless[^10] among the people of his time.[^11] Noah walked with God.

**10** Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham and Japheth.

**11** Now the earth was corrupt[^12] in God's sight, and the earth was filled with violence.

**12** God looked at the earth, and it was corrupt, for all living creatures[^13] had corrupted their ways on the earth.

**13** So God said to Noah, "I have decided to put an end to all living creatures,[^14] for the earth is filled with violence because of them. I am about to destroy them along with the earth.[^15]

**14** Make yourself an ark[^16] of gopher wood.[^17] Make rooms[^18] in the ark, and cover it inside and out with pitch.

**15** This is how you are to make it: the length of the ark is to be three hundred cubits,[^19] its width fifty cubits, and its height thirty cubits.

**16** Make a roof[^20] for the ark, and finish it to a cubit from the top.[^21] Put the door of the ark in its side. Make it with lower, second and third decks.

**17** As for me, I am about to bring the flood, the waters,[^22] on the earth, to destroy from under the sky all living creatures that have the breath of life[^23] in them. Everything on the earth will perish.

**18** But I will establish my covenant with you. You will go into the ark, you and your sons, your wife and your sons' wives with you.

**19** From every living thing, from all living creatures, you are to bring two of each kind into the ark, to keep them alive with you. They are to be male and female.

**20** Of the birds according to their kinds, of the livestock according to their kinds, and of every creature that moves along the ground according to its kind, two of each kind will come to you, to be kept alive.

**21** And take with you every kind of food that is eaten, and store it up. It will be food for you and for them."

**22** Noah did this. He did everything just as God commanded him.

[^1]: Hebrew *bene ha-elohim*; also in verse 4. Elsewhere the phrase refers to heavenly beings (Job 1:6; 2:1). It has also been understood as meaning rulers, or the descendants of Seth.
[^2]: Literally "good".
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: The meaning of the Hebrew verb is uncertain. Or "will not contend with", "will not judge". The Septuagint reads "will not remain".
[^5]: That is, mortal. Literally "in that he also is flesh".
[^6]: A Hebrew word of uncertain meaning, perhaps "fallen ones". Traditionally "giants" (compare Numbers 13:33).
[^7]: Literally "went in to".
[^8]: Literally "the men of the name".
[^9]: Hebrew *toledot*; see 2:4.
[^10]: Hebrew *tamim*, "whole, complete": a man of integrity.
[^11]: Literally "in his generations".
[^12]: The same Hebrew verb, *shachat*, means "corrupt" (verses 11–12) and "destroy" (verses 13 and 17).
[^13]: Literally "all flesh"; also in verses 13, 17 and 19.
[^14]: Literally "The end of all flesh has come before me".
[^15]: Or "destroy them from the earth".
[^16]: Hebrew *tevah*, a box or chest, the word used for the basket in which the baby Moses was placed (Exodus 2:3).
[^17]: The kind of wood is unknown; perhaps cypress.
[^18]: Literally "nests".
[^19]: A cubit is about 18 inches (45 cm). The ark was about 450 feet (135 m) long, 75 feet (23 m) wide and 45 feet (14 m) high.
[^20]: Or "an opening for light". The meaning of Hebrew *tsohar* is uncertain.
[^21]: The meaning of this phrase is uncertain. Literally "and to a cubit you shall finish it from above".
[^22]: Hebrew *mabbul*, a word used almost only of this flood.
[^23]: Literally "the spirit of life". Hebrew *ruach* means spirit, wind or breath.
