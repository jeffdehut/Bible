**1** Sarah lived 127 years; these were the years of Sarah's life.

**2** Sarah died at Kiriath-arba (that is, Hebron) in the land of Canaan, and Abraham went in to mourn for Sarah and to weep for her.

**3** Then Abraham got up from beside his dead and spoke to the Hittites:[^1]

**4** "I am a foreigner and a resident alien[^2] among you. Give me property among you for a burial place, so that I can bury my dead out of my sight."

**5** The Hittites answered Abraham,

**6** "Listen to us, my lord. You are a mighty prince[^3] among us. Bury your dead in the choicest of our tombs. None of us will refuse you his tomb for burying your dead."

**7** Abraham rose and bowed down to the people of the land, the Hittites.

**8** He said to them, "If you are willing[^4] for me to bury my dead out of my sight, listen to me, and ask Ephron son of Zohar on my behalf

**9** to give me the cave of Machpelah, which belongs to him and is at the end of his field. Let him give it to me among you for the full price, as property for a burial place."

**10** Ephron was sitting among the Hittites, and Ephron the Hittite answered Abraham in the hearing of the Hittites, of all who went in at the gate of his city:

**11** "No, my lord, listen to me. I give[^5] you the field, and I give you the cave that is in it. In the presence of my people I give it to you. Bury your dead."

**12** Abraham bowed down before the people of the land,

**13** and he said to Ephron in the hearing of the people of the land, "If only you would listen to me! I will pay the price of the field. Accept it from me, so that I can bury my dead there."

**14** Ephron answered Abraham,

**15** "My lord, listen to me. A piece of land worth four hundred shekels[^6] of silver, what is that between you and me? Bury your dead."

**16** Abraham agreed with Ephron, and Abraham weighed out for Ephron the silver he had named in the hearing of the Hittites, four hundred shekels of silver, by the merchants' standard.[^7]

**17** So Ephron's field in Machpelah, east of[^8] Mamre, the field and the cave in it and all the trees in the field, throughout its whole boundary, passed

**18** to Abraham as his property in the presence of the Hittites, of all who went in at the gate of his city.

**19** After this, Abraham buried his wife Sarah in the cave of the field of Machpelah east of Mamre (that is, Hebron) in the land of Canaan.

**20** So the field and the cave in it passed from the Hittites to Abraham as property for a burial place.

[^1]: Literally "the sons of Heth"; also in verses 5, 7, 10, 16, 18 and 20. See 10:15.
[^2]: Hebrew *ger ve-toshav*: someone living in a land not his own, without the rights of a citizen.
[^3]: Literally "a prince of God". The phrase can mean "a mighty prince".
[^4]: Literally "If it is with your *nephesh*".
[^5]: Literally "I have given"; also twice more in this verse.
[^6]: A shekel is about 2/5 ounce (11.5 grams), so four hundred shekels is about 10 pounds (4.6 kg).
[^7]: Literally "passing to the merchant", that is, silver of the weight accepted in trade.
[^8]: Literally "facing"; also in verse 19.
