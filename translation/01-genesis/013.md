**1** So Abram went up from Egypt to the Negev, he and his wife and everything he had, and Lot with him.

**2** Abram was very rich[^1] in livestock, in silver and in gold.

**3** He went on by stages from the Negev as far as Bethel, to the place between Bethel and Ai where his tent had been at first,

**4** the place where he had made the altar before. There Abram called on the name of the LORD.[^2]

**5** Lot, who was traveling with Abram, also had flocks and herds and tents.

**6** The land could not support them both living together, for their possessions were so great that they could not live together.

**7** And there was quarreling between the herders of Abram's livestock and the herders of Lot's livestock. At that time the Canaanites and the Perizzites were living in the land.

**8** So Abram said to Lot, "Please, let there be no quarrel between you and me, or between my herders and your herders, for we are relatives.[^3]

**9** Isn't the whole land before you? Please separate from me. If you go to the left, I will go to the right; if you go to the right, I will go to the left."

**10** Lot looked up[^4] and saw that the whole plain[^5] of the Jordan was well watered everywhere, like the garden of the LORD, like the land of Egypt, as far as Zoar. This was before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah.

**11** So Lot chose for himself the whole plain of the Jordan, and Lot traveled east. So they separated from each other.

**12** Abram settled in the land of Canaan, while Lot settled among the cities of the plain and moved his tent as far as Sodom.

**13** Now the men of Sodom were wicked, great sinners against the LORD.

**14** After Lot had separated from him, the LORD said to Abram, "Look up from the place where you are, and look north and south, east and west.

**15** All the land that you see I will give to you and to your offspring[^6] forever.

**16** I will make your offspring like the dust of the earth, so that if anyone could count the dust of the earth, your offspring could be counted too.

**17** Get up, walk through the land, its length and its breadth, for I will give it to you."

**18** So Abram moved his tent and came and settled by the oaks[^7] of Mamre, which are at Hebron, and there he built an altar to the LORD.

[^1]: Literally "heavy".
[^2]: See 4:26. *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "for we are men, brothers". Lot was Abram's nephew (11:27; 12:5).
[^4]: Literally "lifted up his eyes"; also in verse 14.
[^5]: Hebrew *kikkar*, "circle": the round valley of the Jordan; also in verses 11–12.
[^6]: Literally "seed"; also in verse 16.
[^7]: Or "terebinths".
