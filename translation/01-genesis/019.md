**1** The two angels[^1] came to Sodom in the evening, while Lot was sitting in the gateway of Sodom. When Lot saw them, he got up to meet them and bowed down with his face to the ground.

**2** He said, "Please, my lords, turn aside to your servant's house. Spend the night and wash your feet; then you can get up early and go on your way." They said, "No, we will spend the night in the square."

**3** But he urged them strongly, so they turned aside to him and entered his house. He made a meal for them and baked bread without yeast, and they ate.

**4** Before they lay down to sleep, the men of the city, the men of Sodom, surrounded the house, young and old, all the people from every quarter.

**5** They called to Lot and said to him, "Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us so that we can have sex with them."[^2]

**6** Lot went out to them at the entrance and shut the door behind him.

**7** He said, "Please, my brothers, do not do this wicked thing.

**8** Look, I have two daughters who have never slept with a man.[^3] Let me bring them out to you, and you can do to them whatever you like. Only don't do anything to these men, for they have come under the protection of my roof."[^4]

**9** But they said, "Stand back!" And they said, "This one came here as a foreigner, and now he wants to play the judge! Now we will treat you worse than them." They pressed hard against the man, against Lot, and moved forward to break down the door.

**10** But the men inside reached out their hands, brought Lot into the house with them, and shut the door.

**11** And they struck the men who were at the entrance of the house, small and great alike, with blindness,[^5] so that they wore themselves out trying to find the entrance.

**12** The men said to Lot, "Who else do you have here? A son-in-law, your sons, your daughters, anyone you have in the city, bring them out of this place.

**13** For we are about to destroy this place, because the outcry against its people has become great before Yahweh,[^6] and Yahweh has sent us to destroy it."

**14** So Lot went out and spoke to his sons-in-law, who were to marry his daughters.[^7] He said, "Get up, get out of this place, for Yahweh is about to destroy the city." But his sons-in-law thought he was joking.

**15** As dawn was breaking, the angels urged Lot, "Get up! Take your wife and your two daughters who are here, or you will be swept away in the punishment[^8] of the city."

**16** But he hesitated. So the men seized his hand and the hands of his wife and his two daughters, because Yahweh had compassion on him, and they brought him out and left him outside the city.

**17** As soon as they had brought them out, he said,[^9] "Run for your life! Don't look back, and don't stop anywhere in the plain. Flee to the hills, or you will be swept away."

**18** But Lot said to them, "Oh no, my lord![^10]

**19** Look, your servant has found favor in your eyes, and you have shown me great faithful love[^11] by saving my life. But I cannot flee to the hills, or disaster will overtake me and I will die.

**20** Look, this town is near enough to flee to, and it is small. Please let me flee there. It is only small, isn't it? Then my life will be saved."

**21** He said to him, "Very well, I grant you this request too.[^12] I will not overthrow the town you have spoken of.

**22** Hurry, flee there, for I cannot do anything until you arrive there." That is why the town was called Zoar.[^13]

**23** The sun had risen over the land when Lot reached Zoar.

**24** Then Yahweh rained down sulfur[^14] and fire on Sodom and Gomorrah, from Yahweh out of the heavens.

**25** He overthrew those cities and the whole plain, all the inhabitants of the cities, and whatever grew from the ground.

**26** But Lot's wife, behind him, looked back, and she became a pillar of salt.

**27** Abraham got up early in the morning and went to the place where he had stood before Yahweh.

**28** He looked down toward Sodom and Gomorrah and toward all the land of the plain, and he saw smoke rising from the land like the smoke of a furnace.

**29** So when God destroyed the cities of the plain, God remembered Abraham, and he sent Lot out of the midst of the overthrow when he overthrew the cities where Lot had lived.

**30** Lot went up from Zoar and settled in the hills with his two daughters, because he was afraid to stay in Zoar. He and his two daughters lived in a cave.

**31** The older daughter said to the younger, "Our father is old, and there is no man in the land to sleep with us,[^15] as is the custom all over the earth.

**32** Come, let us get our father to drink wine, and we will lie with him, so that we may keep our family line alive[^16] through our father."

**33** So that night they got their father to drink wine, and the older one went in and lay with her father. He did not know when she lay down or when she got up.

**34** The next day the older said to the younger, "Look, last night I lay with my father. Let us get him to drink wine tonight as well, and you go in and lie with him, so that we may keep our family line alive through our father."

**35** So that night as well they got their father to drink wine, and the younger one got up and lay with him. He did not know when she lay down or when she got up.

**36** So both of Lot's daughters became pregnant by their father.

**37** The older bore a son and named him Moab.[^17] He is the ancestor of the Moabites to this day.

**38** The younger also bore a son and named him Ben-ammi.[^18] He is the ancestor of the Ammonites[^19] to this day.

[^1]: Or "messengers"; also in verse 15. In verses 5–16 they are called "the men".
[^2]: Literally "so that we may know them".
[^3]: Literally "who have not known a man".
[^4]: Literally "under the shadow of my roof".
[^5]: Hebrew *sanverim*, a rare word, perhaps a dazzling, blinding light.
[^6]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^7]: Or "who had married his daughters". Literally "who were taking his daughters".
[^8]: Or "guilt". Hebrew *avon*; see 4:13.
[^9]: Literally "he said"; one of the two speaks.
[^10]: The Hebrew vowel points read *Adonai*, "Lord", the form used of God (compare 18:3). The same consonants can be read "my lords".
[^11]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^12]: Literally "I have lifted up your face in this matter too".
[^13]: *Zoar* sounds like *mits'ar*, "small" (verse 20).
[^14]: Traditionally "brimstone".
[^15]: Literally "to come in to us".
[^16]: Literally "keep seed alive"; also in verse 34.
[^17]: *Moab* sounds like *me-av*, "from father".
[^18]: *Ben-ammi* means "son of my kin".
[^19]: Literally "the sons of Ammon".
