**1** Abraham took another wife, whose name was Keturah.

**2** She bore him Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak and Shuah.

**3** Jokshan fathered Sheba and Dedan. The descendants of Dedan were the Asshurim, the Letushim and the Leummim.

**4** The sons of Midian were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida and Eldaah. All these were descendants of Keturah.

**5** Abraham gave everything he had to Isaac.

**6** But to the sons of his concubines[^1] Abraham gave gifts, and while he was still living he sent them away from his son Isaac, eastward to the land of the east.

**7** These are the days of the years of Abraham's life: he lived 175 years.

**8** Abraham breathed his last and died at a good old age, old and full of years,[^2] and he was gathered to his people.[^3]

**9** His sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him in the cave of Machpelah, in the field of Ephron son of Zohar the Hittite, east of Mamre,

**10** the field that Abraham had bought from the Hittites.[^4] There Abraham was buried with his wife Sarah.

**11** After Abraham's death, God blessed his son Isaac. And Isaac lived near Beer-lahai-roi.

**12** These are the generations[^5] of Ishmael, Abraham's son, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah's servant, bore to Abraham.

**13** These are the names of the sons of Ishmael, by their names, according to their generations: Nebaioth, the firstborn of Ishmael, Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,

**14** Mishma, Dumah, Massa,

**15** Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish and Kedemah.

**16** These are the sons of Ishmael, and these are their names, by their villages and their encampments: twelve chieftains according to their tribes.

**17** These are the years of Ishmael's life: 137 years. He breathed his last and died, and he was gathered to his people.

**18** His descendants settled from Havilah to Shur, which is east of Egypt in the direction of Assyria. He settled in defiance of all his brothers.[^6]

**19** These are the generations of Isaac, Abraham's son. Abraham fathered Isaac.

**20** Isaac was 40 years old when he married Rebekah, daughter of Bethuel the Aramean of Paddan-aram[^7] and sister of Laban the Aramean.

**21** Isaac prayed to Yahweh[^8] for his wife, because she was barren. Yahweh answered his prayer, and his wife Rebekah became pregnant.

**22** The children struggled together within her, and she said, "If this is how it is, why am I like this?"[^9] So she went to inquire of Yahweh.

**23** Yahweh said to her, "Two nations are in your womb, and two peoples from within you will be divided. One people will be stronger than the other, and the older will serve the younger."

**24** When her time to give birth was complete, there were twins in her womb.

**25** The first came out red,[^10] his whole body like a hairy[^11] cloak, so they named him Esau.

**26** After this his brother came out, with his hand grasping Esau's heel, so he was named Jacob.[^12] Isaac was 60 years old when she bore them.

**27** When the boys grew up, Esau became a skillful hunter, a man of the open country, while Jacob was a quiet[^13] man, living in tents.

**28** Isaac loved Esau, because he had a taste for wild game,[^14] but Rebekah loved Jacob.

**29** Once when Jacob was cooking stew, Esau came in from the open country, exhausted.

**30** Esau said to Jacob, "Let me gulp down some of that red stuff, that red stuff, for I am exhausted." That is why he was called Edom.[^15]

**31** Jacob said, "First sell me your birthright."[^16]

**32** Esau said, "Look, I am about to die. What good is a birthright to me?"

**33** Jacob said, "Swear to me first." So he swore to him and sold his birthright to Jacob.

**34** Then Jacob gave Esau bread and lentil stew. He ate and drank, then got up and left. So Esau despised his birthright.

[^1]: Hebrew *pilegesh*, a wife of lower status than a principal wife; see 22:24.
[^2]: Literally "old and satisfied". The Samaritan Pentateuch and the Septuagint read "satisfied with days".
[^3]: An expression for death, joining one's ancestors; also in verse 17.
[^4]: Literally "the sons of Heth"; see 23:3.
[^5]: Hebrew *toledot*; see 2:4. Also in verses 13 and 19.
[^6]: Or "He settled to the east of all his brothers", "alongside all his brothers". Literally "he fell over against the face of all his brothers"; compare 16:12.
[^7]: A region of Aram in northern Mesopotamia; compare 24:10.
[^8]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^9]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. Literally "If so, why this I?"
[^10]: Hebrew *admoni*, which sounds like *Edom* (verse 30).
[^11]: Hebrew *se'ar*, which sounds like *Seir*, the hill country where Esau's descendants lived (36:8).
[^12]: *Jacob* sounds like the Hebrew *aqev*, "heel". The related verb means "grasp by the heel", "deceive" (27:36).
[^13]: Hebrew *tam*, "complete, whole", the root of the word translated "blameless" in 6:9 and 17:1.
[^14]: Literally "because game was in his mouth".
[^15]: *Edom* means "red".
[^16]: The rights of the firstborn son, including a double share of the inheritance (Deuteronomy 21:17).
