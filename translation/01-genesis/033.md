**1** Jacob looked up and saw Esau coming, and four hundred men with him. So he divided the children among Leah, Rachel and the two servants.

**2** He put the servants and their children in front, Leah and her children behind them, and Rachel and Joseph at the back.

**3** He himself went on ahead of them, and he bowed down to the ground seven times until he came near his brother.

**4** But Esau ran to meet him and hugged him. He threw his arms around his neck[^1] and kissed him,[^2] and they wept.

**5** When Esau looked up and saw the women and the children, he said, "Who are these with you?" Jacob said, "The children God has graciously given your servant."

**6** Then the servants came near with their children, and they bowed down.

**7** Leah also came near with her children, and they bowed down. Last of all Joseph and Rachel came near, and they bowed down.

**8** Esau said, "What do you mean by all this company[^3] that I met?" Jacob said, "To find favor with my lord."

**9** But Esau said, "I have plenty, my brother. Keep what you have for yourself."

**10** Jacob said, "No, please! If I have found favor with you, accept my gift from my hand. For seeing your face is like seeing the face of God, now that you have received me kindly.

**11** Please accept my blessing[^4] that has been brought to you, because God has been gracious to me, and I have everything I need." And he kept urging him, and Esau accepted it.

**12** Then Esau said, "Let us set out and be on our way. I will go alongside you."

**13** But Jacob said to him, "My lord knows that the children are frail, and that the flocks and cattle that are nursing their young are in my care. If they are driven hard for even one day, all the flocks will die.

**14** Please let my lord go on ahead of his servant. I will move along slowly, at the pace of the livestock in front of me and at the pace of the children, until I come to my lord in Seir."

**15** Esau said, "Then let me leave with you some of the people who are with me." But Jacob said, "Why do that? Just let me find favor with my lord."

**16** So that day Esau went back on his way to Seir.

**17** But Jacob traveled on to Succoth. He built a house for himself there and made shelters for his livestock. That is why the place is called Succoth.[^5]

**18** After Jacob came from Paddan-aram, he arrived safely[^6] at the town of Shechem in the land of Canaan, and he camped in front of the town.

**19** He bought the piece of land where he had pitched his tent from the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father, for a hundred *qesitah*.[^7]

**20** There he set up an altar and called it El-Elohe-Israel.[^8]

[^1]: Literally "fell on his neck".
[^2]: In the Hebrew text this word has dots written above its letters, one of a few places where the scribes marked a word in this way. The reason is not known; later rabbis took it to question whether Esau's kiss was sincere.
[^3]: Hebrew *machaneh*, "camp", the word used in 32:8–11 and 32:22.
[^4]: Hebrew *berakhah*, "blessing", here meaning a gift given with good wishes.
[^5]: *Succoth* means "shelters".
[^6]: Hebrew *shalem*, "safe, whole". Or "Jacob came to Shalem, a town of Shechem".
[^7]: A *qesitah* was a unit of money; its value is not known.
[^8]: *El-Elohe-Israel* means "God, the God of Israel", or "God is the God of Israel".
