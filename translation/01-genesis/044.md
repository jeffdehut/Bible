**1** Then Joseph instructed the man in charge of his house, "Fill the men's sacks with as much food as they can carry, and put each man's money in the mouth of his sack.

**2** Put my cup, the silver cup, in the mouth of the youngest one's sack, along with his money for the grain." And he did as Joseph told him.

**3** At first light the men were sent off with their donkeys.

**4** They had gone only a short distance from the city when Joseph said to the man in charge of his house, "Get up, go after the men. When you catch up with them, say to them, 'Why have you repaid good with evil?[^1]

**5** Isn't this the cup my master drinks from? He uses it to read omens.[^2] What you have done is wicked.'"

**6** When he caught up with them, he said these words to them.

**7** They said to him, "Why does my lord say such things? Your servants would never do such a thing!

**8** Look, we brought back to you from the land of Canaan the money we found in the mouths of our sacks. So why would we steal silver or gold from your master's house?

**9** If any of your servants is found to have it, he will die, and the rest of us will become my lord's slaves."[^3]

**10** He said, "Very well, let it be as you say. The one who is found to have it will become my slave, and the rest of you will go free."[^4]

**11** Each of them quickly lowered his sack to the ground and opened it.

**12** He searched, beginning with the oldest and ending with the youngest, and the cup was found in Benjamin's sack.

**13** Then they tore their clothes. Each of them loaded his donkey, and they went back to the city.

**14** When Judah and his brothers came into Joseph's house, he was still there, and they threw themselves on the ground before him.

**15** Joseph said to them, "What is this you have done? Don't you know that a man like me can read omens?"

**16** Judah said, "What can we say to my lord? What can we tell you? How can we prove our innocence? God has uncovered your servants' guilt. We are now my lord's slaves, both we and the one who was found with the cup."

**17** But he said, "I would never do that! The man who was found with the cup will become my slave. The rest of you, go back in peace to your father."

**18** Then Judah went up to him and said, "Please, my lord, let your servant say a word in my lord's ears, and do not be angry with your servant, for you are like Pharaoh himself.

**19** My lord asked his servants, 'Do you have a father or a brother?'

**20** And we said to my lord, 'We have an elderly father, and there is a young son born to him in his old age. His brother is dead, so he is the only one of his mother's sons left, and his father loves him.'

**21** Then you said to your servants, 'Bring him down to me, so that I can see him for myself.'[^5]

**22** We said to my lord, 'The boy cannot leave his father. If he leaves his father, his father will die.'

**23** But you said to your servants, 'Unless your youngest brother comes down with you, you will never see my face again.'

**24** When we went back up to your servant my father, we told him what my lord had said.

**25** Then our father said, 'Go back and buy us a little food.'

**26** But we said, 'We cannot go down. If our youngest brother is with us, we will go down. For we cannot see the man's face unless our youngest brother is with us.'

**27** Your servant my father said to us, 'You know that my wife bore me two sons.

**28** One of them went away from me, and I said, "He has surely been torn to pieces." And I have not seen him since.

**29** If you take this one from me too, and anything terrible happens to him, you will bring my gray head down to Sheol[^6] in misery.'

**30** So now, if I go back to your servant my father and the boy is not with us, since his life is bound up with the boy's life,[^7]

**31** when he sees that the boy is not there, he will die. Your servants will bring the gray head of your servant our father down to Sheol in sorrow.

**32** For your servant guaranteed the boy's safety to my father. I said, 'If I do not bring him back to you, I will bear the blame before my father all my life.'

**33** So now, please let your servant stay here as my lord's slave in place of the boy, and let the boy go back up with his brothers.

**34** For how can I go back up to my father if the boy is not with me? I could not bear to see the misery that would come upon my father."

[^1]: The Septuagint adds "Why have you stolen my silver cup?"
[^2]: Hebrew *nachesh*, to look for signs or omens, perhaps here by watching liquid in a cup. Also in verse 15.
[^3]: Hebrew *avadim*, "slaves" or "servants". Also in verses 10, 16, 17 and 33.
[^4]: Literally "you will be innocent".
[^5]: Literally "so that I may set my eye on him".
[^6]: Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead.
[^7]: Literally "his *nephesh* (soul, life) is bound up with his *nephesh*".
