**1** After these things the word of Yahweh[^1] came to Abram in a vision: "Do not be afraid, Abram. I am your shield. Your reward will be very great."[^2]

**2** But Abram said, "Lord Yahweh,[^3] what will you give me, since I go on childless, and the heir of my household[^4] is Eliezer of Damascus?"

**3** Abram said, "Look, you have given me no offspring,[^5] so a member of my household will be my heir."

**4** Then the word of Yahweh came to him: "This man will not be your heir. Your heir will be one who comes from your own body."[^6]

**5** He brought him outside and said, "Look up at the sky and count the stars, if you are able to count them." Then he said to him, "So will your offspring be."

**6** Abram believed[^7] Yahweh, and Yahweh credited it to him as righteousness.[^8]

**7** Then he said to him, "I am Yahweh, who brought you out of Ur of the Chaldeans to give you this land to possess."

**8** But he said, "Lord Yahweh, how can I know that I will possess it?"

**9** He said to him, "Bring me a three-year-old heifer, a three-year-old female goat, a three-year-old ram, a turtledove and a young pigeon."

**10** He brought him all these, cut them in two, and laid each half opposite the other; but he did not cut the birds in two.

**11** Birds of prey came down on the carcasses, but Abram drove them away.

**12** As the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell on Abram, and a terror, a great darkness, came over him.

**13** Then he said to Abram, "Know for certain that your offspring will be foreigners in a land that is not theirs. They will be slaves there, and they will be oppressed for 400 years.

**14** But I will judge the nation they serve, and afterward they will come out with great possessions.

**15** As for you, you will go to your ancestors in peace; you will be buried at a good old age.

**16** In the fourth generation[^9] they will come back here, for the guilt of the Amorites is not yet complete."

**17** When the sun had gone down and it was dark, a smoking firepot and a flaming torch appeared and passed between these pieces.

**18** On that day Yahweh made a covenant[^10] with Abram, saying, "To your offspring I give[^11] this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the river Euphrates:

**19** the Kenites, the Kenizzites, the Kadmonites,

**20** the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Rephaim,

**21** the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Girgashites and the Jebusites."

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Or "I am your shield, your very great reward".
[^3]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*; also in verse 8.
[^4]: The meaning of the Hebrew phrase, *ben-mesheq beti*, is uncertain. *Mesheq* sounds like *Dammeseq*, "Damascus".
[^5]: Literally "seed"; also in verses 5, 13 and 18.
[^6]: Literally "who comes out of your inner parts".
[^7]: Or "trusted".
[^8]: Literally "and he credited it to him as righteousness". Some understand Abram as the subject: "and he counted it to Yahweh as righteousness".
[^9]: Hebrew *dor*, which can also mean a lifetime.
[^10]: Literally "cut a covenant", referring to the ceremony of cutting the animals in verses 9–17.
[^11]: Literally "I have given".
