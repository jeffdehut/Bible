**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Abram, "Go from your land, your relatives[^2] and your father's household to the land that I will show you.

**2** I will make you into a great nation. I will bless you and make your name great, and you will be a blessing.[^3]

**3** I will bless those who bless you, and whoever treats you with contempt I will curse.[^4] In you all the families of the earth will be blessed."[^5]

**4** So Abram went, as Yahweh had told him, and Lot went with him. Abram was 75 years old when he left Haran.

**5** Abram took his wife Sarai, his nephew Lot, all the possessions they had gathered, and the people they had acquired[^6] in Haran, and they set out for the land of Canaan. They came to the land of Canaan,

**6** and Abram passed through the land as far as the place[^7] at Shechem, to the oak[^8] of Moreh. At that time the Canaanites were in the land.

**7** Then Yahweh appeared to Abram and said, "To your offspring[^9] I will give this land." So he built an altar there to Yahweh, who had appeared to him.

**8** From there he moved on to the hill country east of Bethel and pitched his tent, with Bethel to the west[^10] and Ai to the east. There he built an altar to Yahweh and called on the name of Yahweh.[^11]

**9** Then Abram journeyed on by stages toward the Negev.[^12]

**10** Now there was a famine in the land, and Abram went down to Egypt to stay there as a foreigner,[^13] because the famine was severe in the land.

**11** As he was about to enter Egypt, he said to his wife Sarai, "Look, I know that you are a beautiful woman.

**12** When the Egyptians see you, they will say, 'This is his wife.' Then they will kill me, but they will let you live.

**13** Please say you are my sister, so that it will go well with me because of you, and my life will be spared on your account."

**14** When Abram came to Egypt, the Egyptians saw that the woman was very beautiful.

**15** Pharaoh's officials saw her and praised her to Pharaoh,[^14] and the woman was taken into Pharaoh's household.

**16** He treated Abram well because of her, and Abram acquired flocks and herds, male donkeys, male and female servants,[^15] female donkeys and camels.

**17** But Yahweh struck Pharaoh and his household with great plagues because of Sarai, Abram's wife.

**18** So Pharaoh summoned Abram and said, "What is this you have done to me? Why didn't you tell me she was your wife?

**19** Why did you say, 'She is my sister,' so that I took her as my wife? Now then, here is your wife. Take her and go!"

**20** Pharaoh gave his men orders about him, and they sent him away with his wife and everything he had.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Or "your birthplace".
[^3]: Or "Be a blessing!"
[^4]: Two different Hebrew verbs: *qalal*, "treat lightly, speak of with contempt", and *arar*, "curse".
[^5]: Or "will bless themselves by you", that is, will use your name when they ask for a blessing. Also "earth" here is Hebrew *adamah*, "ground", as in 2:5–7.
[^6]: Literally "the *nephesh* they had made", that is, the servants and others who had joined their household.
[^7]: Hebrew *maqom*, "place", often meaning a holy site.
[^8]: Or "the terebinth". *Moreh* means "teacher".
[^9]: Literally "seed".
[^10]: Literally "from the sea", the Mediterranean.
[^11]: See 4:26.
[^12]: The dry region in the south of Canaan.
[^13]: Hebrew *gur*, to live somewhere as a resident foreigner, without the rights of a citizen.
[^14]: *Pharaoh*: the title of the king of Egypt.
[^15]: Or "slaves".
