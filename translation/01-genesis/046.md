**1** So Israel set out with everything he had, and he came to Beersheba. There he offered sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac.

**2** God spoke to Israel in visions of the night and said, "Jacob, Jacob!" He said, "Here I am."

**3** He said, "I am God,[^1] the God of your father. Don't be afraid to go down to Egypt, for I will make you into a great nation there.

**4** I myself will go down with you to Egypt, and I myself will surely bring you back up again. And Joseph will close your eyes."[^2]

**5** Then Jacob set out from Beersheba. Israel's sons carried their father Jacob, their little children and their wives in the carts Pharaoh had sent to carry him.

**6** They also took their livestock and the property they had acquired in the land of Canaan, and they came to Egypt, Jacob and all his descendants with him.

**7** He brought with him to Egypt his sons and his grandsons, his daughters and his granddaughters: all his descendants.

**8** These are the names of the Israelites, Jacob and his sons, who came to Egypt: Reuben, Jacob's firstborn.

**9** The sons of Reuben: Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron and Carmi.

**10** The sons of Simeon: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul the son of a Canaanite woman.

**11** The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath and Merari.

**12** The sons of Judah: Er, Onan, Shelah, Perez and Zerah; but Er and Onan had died in the land of Canaan. The sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul.

**13** The sons of Issachar: Tola, Puvah, Iob[^3] and Shimron.

**14** The sons of Zebulun: Sered, Elon and Jahleel.

**15** These were the sons of Leah, whom she bore to Jacob in Paddan-aram, together with his daughter Dinah. All the persons,[^4] his sons and his daughters, numbered thirty-three.

**16** The sons of Gad: Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi and Areli.

**17** The sons of Asher: Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi and Beriah, and their sister Serah. The sons of Beriah: Heber and Malchiel.

**18** These were the sons of Zilpah, whom Laban gave to his daughter Leah. She bore these to Jacob: sixteen persons.

**19** The sons of Jacob's wife Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin.

**20** In the land of Egypt, Manasseh and Ephraim were born to Joseph. Asenath daughter of Potiphera, priest of On, bore them to him.[^5]

**21** The sons of Benjamin: Bela, Becher, Ashbel, Gera, Naaman, Ehi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim and Ard.

**22** These were the sons of Rachel who were born to Jacob: fourteen persons in all.

**23** The son of Dan:[^6] Hushim.

**24** The sons of Naphtali: Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer and Shillem.

**25** These were the sons of Bilhah, whom Laban gave to his daughter Rachel. She bore these to Jacob: seven persons in all.

**26** All the persons who came with Jacob to Egypt, his own descendants,[^7] not counting the wives of Jacob's sons, numbered sixty-six in all.

**27** With the two sons who were born to Joseph in Egypt, all the persons of Jacob's household who came to Egypt numbered seventy.[^8]

**28** Jacob sent Judah ahead of him to Joseph, to get directions to Goshen.[^9] When they arrived in the land of Goshen,

**29** Joseph got his chariot ready and went up to Goshen to meet his father Israel. As soon as Joseph appeared before him, he threw his arms around his neck and wept on his neck for a long time.

**30** Israel said to Joseph, "Now I can die, since I have seen your face and know that you are still alive."

**31** Then Joseph said to his brothers and to his father's household, "I will go up and tell Pharaoh. I will say to him, 'My brothers and my father's household, who were in the land of Canaan, have come to me.

**32** The men are shepherds; they have always kept livestock.[^10] They have brought their flocks and their cattle and everything they have.'

**33** When Pharaoh calls you in and asks, 'What is your occupation?'

**34** you are to say, 'Your servants have kept livestock from our youth until now, both we and our ancestors.' Then you will be allowed to settle in the land of Goshen, for all shepherds are detestable[^11] to the Egyptians."

[^1]: Hebrew *ha-El*, "the God".
[^2]: Literally "will put his hand on your eyes", that is, he will be with you when you die.
[^3]: The Samaritan Pentateuch and the Septuagint read "Jashub", as in Numbers 26:24 and 1 Chronicles 7:1.
[^4]: Hebrew *nephesh*, here "person", as in the counts in verses 18, 22 and 25–27.
[^5]: The Septuagint adds the names of sons and grandsons of Manasseh and Ephraim.
[^6]: Literally "the sons of Dan".
[^7]: Literally "those who came out of his thigh".
[^8]: The Septuagint reads "nine persons" for Joseph's sons in Egypt and "seventy-five" for the total, the number given in Acts 7:14.
[^9]: Literally "to point out before him to Goshen". The Hebrew may mean that Judah was to show the way, or that Joseph was to give directions.
[^10]: Literally "they have been men of livestock". Also in verse 34.
[^11]: Hebrew *to'evah*, something that is offensive or forbidden.
