**1** After these things God tested Abraham. He said to him, "Abraham!" And he said, "Here I am."

**2** He said, "Take your son, your only son,[^1] whom you love, Isaac, and go to the land of Moriah. Offer him there as a burnt offering[^2] on one of the mountains that I will tell you about."

**3** Abraham got up early in the morning and saddled his donkey. He took two of his young men with him, and his son Isaac. He split the wood for the burnt offering, and set out for the place God had told him about.

**4** On the third day Abraham looked up and saw the place in the distance.

**5** Abraham said to his young men, "Stay here with the donkey. The boy and I will go over there. We will worship and then come back to you."

**6** Abraham took the wood for the burnt offering and laid it on his son Isaac, and he took the fire and the knife in his hand. And the two of them walked on together.

**7** Isaac said to his father Abraham, "My father!" He said, "Here I am, my son." He said, "Here are the fire and the wood, but where is the lamb for a burnt offering?"

**8** Abraham said, "God will provide[^3] the lamb for a burnt offering himself, my son." And the two of them walked on together.

**9** When they came to the place God had told him about, Abraham built the altar there and arranged the wood. He bound his son Isaac and laid him on the altar, on top of the wood.

**10** Then Abraham reached out his hand and took the knife to slaughter his son.

**11** But the angel of Yahweh[^4] called to him from heaven, "Abraham! Abraham!" He said, "Here I am."

**12** He said, "Do not lay your hand on the boy or do anything to him. For now I know that you fear God, since you have not withheld your son, your only son, from me."

**13** Abraham looked up and saw a ram behind him,[^5] caught in a thicket by its horns. Abraham went and took the ram and offered it as a burnt offering in place of his son.

**14** Abraham named that place "Yahweh will provide."[^6] As it is said to this day, "On the mountain of Yahweh it will be provided."[^7]

**15** The angel of Yahweh called to Abraham a second time from heaven

**16** and said, "I swear by myself, declares Yahweh, that because you have done this and have not withheld your son, your only son,

**17** I will richly bless you, and I will greatly multiply your offspring,[^8] like the stars of the sky and like the sand on the seashore. Your offspring will take possession of the gates of their enemies.[^9]

**18** And in your offspring all the nations of the earth will be blessed,[^10] because you have obeyed my voice."

**19** So Abraham returned to his young men, and they got up and went together to Beersheba. And Abraham lived in Beersheba.

**20** After these things Abraham was told, "Milcah too has borne children to your brother Nahor:

**21** Uz his firstborn, Buz his brother, Kemuel the father of Aram,

**22** Chesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph and Bethuel."

**23** Bethuel fathered Rebekah. These eight Milcah bore to Nahor, Abraham's brother.

**24** His concubine,[^11] whose name was Reumah, also bore children: Tebah, Gaham, Tahash and Maacah.

[^1]: Hebrew *yachid*, "only, one and only"; also in verses 12 and 16.
[^2]: An offering burned whole on the altar.
[^3]: Literally "will see"; see verse 14.
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. On "the angel of Yahweh", see 16:7.
[^5]: The Samaritan Pentateuch and the Septuagint read "a ram", "one ram". In Hebrew, "behind" (*achar*) and "one" (*echad*) differ only by letters that look alike.
[^6]: Hebrew *YHWH yir'eh*, "Yahweh will see", that is, will see to it.
[^7]: Or "he will be seen", "he will appear".
[^8]: Literally "seed"; also later in this verse and in verse 18.
[^9]: Literally "his enemies". The gate stands for the city.
[^10]: Or "will bless themselves by your offspring". The verb form differs from the one in 12:3 and 18:18.
[^11]: Hebrew *pilegesh*, a wife of lower status than a principal wife.
