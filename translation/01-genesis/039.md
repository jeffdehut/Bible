**1** Now Joseph had been taken down to Egypt. Potiphar, an Egyptian, one of Pharaoh's officials and the captain of the guard, bought him from the Ishmaelites who had taken him down there.

**2** Yahweh[^1] was with Joseph, and he became a successful man. He lived in the house of his Egyptian master.

**3** His master saw that Yahweh was with him and that Yahweh made everything he did succeed.[^2]

**4** Joseph found favor with him and became his personal attendant. Potiphar put him in charge of his household and entrusted him with everything he owned.

**5** From the time he put him in charge of his household and of everything he owned, Yahweh blessed the Egyptian's household because of Joseph. Yahweh's blessing was on everything he owned, in the house and in the fields.

**6** So he left everything he owned in Joseph's care, and with Joseph there he did not concern himself with anything except the food he ate.[^3] Now Joseph was well built and handsome.

**7** Some time later his master's wife cast her eyes on Joseph and said, "Sleep with me."

**8** But he refused. He said to his master's wife, "Look, with me here, my master does not concern himself with anything in the house, and he has entrusted me with everything he owns.

**9** No one in this house is greater than I am. He has kept nothing back from me except you, because you are his wife. How then could I do this great evil and sin against God?"

**10** Though she spoke to Joseph day after day, he would not listen to her and lie beside her or be with her.

**11** One day he went into the house to do his work, and none of the men of the household were there in the house.

**12** She grabbed him by his garment and said, "Sleep with me!" But he left his garment in her hand and ran out of the house.

**13** When she saw that he had left his garment in her hand and run outside,

**14** she called the men of her household and said to them, "Look, he[^4] has brought us a Hebrew man to make fools of us.[^5] He came to me to sleep with me, and I screamed.

**15** When he heard me raise my voice and scream, he left his garment beside me and ran outside."

**16** She kept his garment beside her until his master came home.

**17** Then she told him the same story: "The Hebrew slave[^6] you brought us came to me to make a fool of me.

**18** But when I raised my voice and screamed, he left his garment beside me and ran outside."

**19** When his master heard what his wife told him, "This is what your slave did to me," he was furious.

**20** Joseph's master took him and put him in the prison,[^7] the place where the king's prisoners were held. And he remained there in the prison.

**21** But Yahweh was with Joseph and showed him faithful love.[^8] He gave him favor in the eyes of the prison warden.

**22** The warden put Joseph in charge of all the prisoners in the prison, and he was responsible for everything that was done there.

**23** The warden paid no attention to anything in Joseph's care, because Yahweh was with him, and Yahweh made whatever he did succeed.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "made everything he did succeed in his hand".
[^3]: Literally "he did not know anything with him except the bread he ate". This may mean that Potiphar paid attention only to his meals, or that food was the one thing Joseph did not handle, perhaps because of Egyptian rules about eating (compare 43:32).
[^4]: That is, her husband. She does not name him.
[^5]: Hebrew *letsachek*, "to mock, to play with", here with a sexual meaning. Also in verse 17.
[^6]: Hebrew *eved*, "slave" or "servant". Joseph had been bought (verse 1). Also in verse 19.
[^7]: Hebrew *bet ha-sohar*, a term found only in the Joseph story. It may mean "round house" or "fortress".
[^8]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
