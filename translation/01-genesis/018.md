**1** Yahweh[^1] appeared to Abraham[^2] by the oaks[^3] of Mamre as he was sitting at the entrance of his tent in the heat of the day.

**2** He looked up and saw three men standing near him. When he saw them, he ran from the entrance of the tent to meet them and bowed down to the ground.

**3** He said, "My lord,[^4] if I have found favor in your eyes, please do not pass your servant by.

**4** Let a little water be brought, and wash your feet, and rest under the tree.

**5** Let me bring a bit of bread, so that you can refresh yourselves.[^5] After that you can go on, since you have come to your servant." They said, "Do as you have said."

**6** So Abraham hurried into the tent to Sarah and said, "Quick! Three seahs[^6] of fine flour! Knead it and make bread cakes."

**7** Then Abraham ran to the herd, took a tender, choice calf, and gave it to the servant,[^7] who hurried to prepare it.

**8** He took curds and milk and the calf that he had prepared, and set them before them. He stood near them under the tree while they ate.

**9** They said to him, "Where is your wife Sarah?" He said, "There, in the tent."

**10** Then he said, "I will surely come back to you at this time next year,[^8] and your wife Sarah will have a son." Sarah was listening at the entrance of the tent, which was behind him.

**11** Now Abraham and Sarah were old, advanced in years, and Sarah was past the age of childbearing.[^9]

**12** So Sarah laughed to herself, saying, "After I am worn out, and my lord is old, shall I have pleasure?"

**13** Yahweh said to Abraham, "Why did Sarah laugh and say, 'Will I really have a child, now that I am old?'

**14** Is anything too hard[^10] for Yahweh? At the appointed time I will come back to you, at this time next year, and Sarah will have a son."

**15** But Sarah denied it, saying, "I didn't laugh," because she was afraid. He said, "No, you did laugh."

**16** Then the men got up from there and looked down toward Sodom, and Abraham walked with them to see them on their way.

**17** Yahweh said, "Shall I hide from Abraham what I am about to do,

**18** since Abraham will surely become a great and powerful nation, and all the nations of the earth will be blessed in him?[^11]

**19** For I have chosen[^12] him, so that he will command his children and his household after him to keep the way of Yahweh by doing what is right and just,[^13] so that Yahweh may bring about for Abraham what he has promised him."

**20** Then Yahweh said, "The outcry against Sodom and Gomorrah is great, and their sin is very grave.

**21** I will go down and see whether they have done altogether as the outcry that has come to me says.[^14] And if not, I will know."

**22** The men turned from there and went toward Sodom, but Abraham remained standing before Yahweh.[^15]

**23** Abraham came near and said, "Will you really sweep away the righteous with the wicked?

**24** Suppose there are fifty righteous people in the city. Will you really sweep it away, and not spare the place for the sake of the fifty righteous people in it?

**25** Far be it from you to do such a thing, to put the righteous to death with the wicked, so that the righteous and the wicked are treated alike! Far be it from you! Shall not the Judge of all the earth do what is just?"[^16]

**26** Yahweh said, "If I find fifty righteous people in the city of Sodom, I will spare the whole place for their sake."

**27** Abraham answered, "Look, I have dared to speak to the Lord,[^17] though I am only dust and ashes.

**28** Suppose the fifty righteous lack five. Will you destroy the whole city because of five?" He said, "I will not destroy it if I find forty-five there."

**29** Once again he spoke to him and said, "Suppose forty are found there." He said, "For the sake of the forty I will not do it."

**30** Then he said, "Please do not let the Lord be angry, and I will speak. Suppose thirty are found there." He said, "I will not do it if I find thirty there."

**31** He said, "Look, I have dared to speak to the Lord. Suppose twenty are found there." He said, "For the sake of the twenty I will not destroy it."

**32** He said, "Please do not let the Lord be angry, and I will speak just once more. Suppose ten are found there." He said, "For the sake of the ten I will not destroy it."

**33** When Yahweh had finished speaking to Abraham, he went his way, and Abraham returned to his place.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "to him".
[^3]: Or "terebinths".
[^4]: The Hebrew vowel points read *Adonai*, "Lord", the form used of God. The same consonants can be read *adoni*, "my lord", a polite way of addressing a man.
[^5]: Literally "sustain your hearts".
[^6]: A seah is about 7 quarts (7 liters), so three seahs is about 20 quarts (22 liters).
[^7]: Literally "the young man".
[^8]: Literally "at the time of life", that is, when the season comes round again; also in verse 14.
[^9]: Literally "the way of women had ceased to be for Sarah".
[^10]: Or "too wonderful".
[^11]: Or "will bless themselves by him"; compare 12:3.
[^12]: Literally "known".
[^13]: Hebrew *tsedaqah u-mishpat*, "righteousness and justice".
[^14]: Literally "whether according to its outcry that has come to me they have done completely".
[^15]: Jewish tradition lists this verse among the "corrections of the scribes" (*tiqqune sopherim*), saying the text once read "Yahweh remained standing before Abraham".
[^16]: Literally "do justice". Hebrew *mishpat*.
[^17]: Hebrew *Adonai*; also in verses 30–32.
