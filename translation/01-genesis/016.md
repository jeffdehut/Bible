**1** Now Sarai, Abram's wife, had borne him no children. She had an Egyptian servant[^1] whose name was Hagar.

**2** So Sarai said to Abram, "Look, Yahweh[^2] has kept me from having children. Please sleep with[^3] my servant; perhaps I can build a family through her."[^4] And Abram listened to Sarai.

**3** So after Abram had lived ten years in the land of Canaan, Sarai, Abram's wife, took her servant Hagar the Egyptian and gave her to her husband Abram as his wife.

**4** He slept with Hagar, and she became pregnant. When she saw that she was pregnant, her mistress became of little worth in her eyes.

**5** Then Sarai said to Abram, "This wrong done to me is your fault![^5] I put my servant in your arms, and when she saw that she was pregnant, I became of little worth in her eyes. May Yahweh judge between me and you!"

**6** Abram said to Sarai, "Look, your servant is in your hands. Do to her whatever you think best." Then Sarai mistreated her, and she ran away from her.

**7** The angel of Yahweh[^6] found her by a spring of water in the wilderness, the spring on the road to Shur.

**8** He said, "Hagar, servant of Sarai, where have you come from, and where are you going?" She said, "I am running away from my mistress Sarai."

**9** The angel of Yahweh said to her, "Go back to your mistress and submit to her."[^7]

**10** The angel of Yahweh also said to her, "I will greatly multiply your offspring,[^8] so that they will be too many to count."

**11** And the angel of Yahweh said to her, "Look, you are pregnant and will give birth to a son. You are to name him Ishmael,[^9] for Yahweh has heard of your misery.

**12** He will be a wild donkey of a man. His hand will be against everyone, and everyone's hand against him. He will live in defiance of all his brothers."[^10]

**13** She called Yahweh, who spoke to her, by this name: "You are El-roi,"[^11] for she said, "Have I really seen here the one who sees me?"[^12]

**14** That is why the well was called Beer-lahai-roi.[^13] It is between Kadesh and Bered.

**15** Hagar bore Abram a son, and Abram named his son, whom Hagar bore, Ishmael.

**16** Abram was 86 years old when Hagar bore Ishmael to Abram.

[^1]: Or "slave woman". Hebrew *shiphchah*, a female servant who belonged to her mistress.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "go in to"; also in verse 4.
[^4]: Literally "perhaps I will be built from her". The verb "build", *banah*, sounds like *ben*, "son".
[^5]: Literally "My wrong is on you". Hebrew *chamas*, "violence, injustice".
[^6]: Or "the messenger of Yahweh". Hebrew *malakh* means messenger. Here the angel speaks in Yahweh's name (verse 10), and Hagar addresses Yahweh as the one who spoke to her (verse 13).
[^7]: Literally "humble yourself under her hands".
[^8]: Literally "seed".
[^9]: *Ishmael* means "God hears".
[^10]: Or "He will live to the east of all his brothers", "He will live alongside all his brothers". Literally "over against the face of all his brothers".
[^11]: *El-roi* means "God who sees me" or "God of seeing".
[^12]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. Literally "Have I also here seen after the one who sees me?"
[^13]: *Beer-lahai-roi* means "the well of the Living One who sees me".
