**1** When Isaac was old and his eyes were too weak to see, he called his older son Esau and said to him, "My son." He said to him, "Here I am."

**2** He said, "Look, I am old, and I do not know the day of my death.

**3** Now take your weapons, your quiver and your bow, go out to the open country and hunt game for me.

**4** Then prepare me the tasty food that I love, and bring it to me to eat, so that I may bless you[^1] before I die."

**5** Now Rebekah was listening when Isaac spoke to his son Esau. When Esau went out to the open country to hunt game and bring it back,

**6** Rebekah said to her son Jacob, "Look, I heard your father say to your brother Esau,

**7** 'Bring me game and prepare me tasty food to eat, so that I may bless you before the LORD[^2] before I die.'

**8** Now, my son, listen to me and do what I tell you.

**9** Go to the flock and bring me two good young goats from there, so that I can prepare them as tasty food for your father, the way he loves it.

**10** Then take it to your father to eat, so that he may bless you before he dies."

**11** But Jacob said to his mother Rebekah, "Look, my brother Esau is a hairy man, and I am a smooth man.

**12** Perhaps my father will touch me, and then I will seem to him to be mocking him, and I will bring a curse on myself and not a blessing."

**13** His mother said to him, "Let your curse be on me, my son. Just obey me, and go get them for me."

**14** So he went and got them and brought them to his mother, and his mother prepared tasty food, the way his father loved it.

**15** Then Rebekah took the best clothes of her older son Esau, which she had with her in the house, and put them on her younger son Jacob.

**16** She put the skins of the young goats on his hands and on the smooth part of his neck.

**17** Then she put the tasty food and the bread she had made into the hand of her son Jacob.

**18** He went in to his father and said, "My father." He said, "Here I am. Who are you, my son?"

**19** Jacob said to his father, "I am Esau, your firstborn. I have done as you told me. Please sit up and eat some of my game, so that you may bless me."

**20** Isaac said to his son, "How did you find it so quickly, my son?" He said, "Because the LORD your God made it happen for me."

**21** Then Isaac said to Jacob, "Please come near, so that I can touch you, my son, to know whether you are really my son Esau or not."

**22** So Jacob came near to his father Isaac, who touched him and said, "The voice is the voice of Jacob, but the hands are the hands of Esau."

**23** He did not recognize him, because his hands were hairy like his brother Esau's hands. So he blessed him.

**24** He said, "Are you really my son Esau?" He said, "I am."

**25** Then he said, "Bring it near to me, so that I may eat some of my son's game and bless you." So he brought it to him, and he ate; and he brought him wine, and he drank.

**26** Then his father Isaac said to him, "Please come near and kiss me, my son."

**27** So he came near and kissed him. When Isaac smelled the smell of his clothes, he blessed him and said, "See, the smell of my son is like the smell of a field that the LORD has blessed.

**28** May God give you of the dew of heaven and of the richness[^3] of the earth, and plenty of grain and new wine.

**29** May peoples serve you and nations bow down to you. Be lord over your brothers, and may your mother's sons bow down to you. Cursed be those who curse you, and blessed be those who bless you."

**30** As soon as Isaac had finished blessing Jacob, and Jacob had scarcely gone out from the presence of his father Isaac, his brother Esau came in from his hunting.

**31** He too prepared tasty food and brought it to his father. He said to his father, "Let my father sit up and eat some of his son's game, so that you may bless me."

**32** His father Isaac said to him, "Who are you?" He said, "I am your son, your firstborn, Esau."

**33** Isaac trembled violently and said, "Who was it, then, who hunted game and brought it to me? I ate it all before you came, and I blessed him. Yes, and he will be blessed!"

**34** When Esau heard his father's words, he cried out with a loud and very bitter cry and said to his father, "Bless me, me too, my father!"

**35** But he said, "Your brother came deceitfully and took your blessing."

**36** Esau said, "Isn't he rightly named Jacob? He has cheated me[^4] these two times. He took my birthright,[^5] and look, now he has taken my blessing!" Then he said, "Haven't you kept back a blessing for me?"

**37** Isaac answered Esau, "Look, I have made him lord over you, and I have given him all his brothers as servants, and I have sustained him with grain and new wine. What then can I do for you, my son?"

**38** Esau said to his father, "Do you have only one blessing, my father? Bless me, me too, my father!" And Esau wept aloud.

**39** Then his father Isaac answered him, "Look, away from the richness of the earth will your dwelling be, and away from the dew of heaven above.[^6]

**40** By your sword you will live, and you will serve your brother. But when you grow restless,[^7] you will break his yoke from your neck."

**41** Esau held a grudge against Jacob because of the blessing his father had given him. Esau said to himself, "The days of mourning for my father are near; then I will kill my brother Jacob."

**42** When Rebekah was told the words of her older son Esau, she sent for her younger son Jacob and said to him, "Look, your brother Esau is consoling himself with the thought of killing you.

**43** Now, my son, listen to me. Get up and flee to my brother Laban in Haran.

**44** Stay with him a while, until your brother's fury subsides,

**45** until your brother's anger turns away from you and he forgets what you have done to him. Then I will send for you and bring you back from there. Why should I lose you both in one day?"

**46** Then Rebekah said to Isaac, "I am sick of my life because of the Hittite women.[^8] If Jacob marries one of the Hittite women like these, one of the women of the land, what good will my life be to me?"

[^1]: Literally "so that my *nephesh* may bless you"; similarly in verses 19, 25 and 31.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "fatness"; also in verse 39.
[^4]: Hebrew *aqav*, "grasp by the heel, deceive", a play on the name Jacob (25:26).
[^5]: Hebrew *bekhorah*, "birthright", sounds like *berakhah*, "blessing".
[^6]: Or "Look, your dwelling will be of the richness of the earth, and of the dew of heaven above". The Hebrew preposition can mean "away from" or "of" (as in verse 28).
[^7]: The meaning of the Hebrew verb is uncertain.
[^8]: Literally "the daughters of Heth"; also later in this verse. See 10:15.
