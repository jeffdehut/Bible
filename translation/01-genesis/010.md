**1** These are the generations[^1] of the sons of Noah, Shem, Ham and Japheth. Sons were born to them after the flood.

**2** The sons of Japheth: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech and Tiras.

**3** The sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, Riphath and Togarmah.

**4** The sons of Javan: Elishah, Tarshish, the Kittim and the Dodanim.[^2]

**5** From these the peoples of the coastlands[^3] spread out into their lands, each with its own language, by their clans, in their nations.

**6** The sons of Ham: Cush, Egypt,[^4] Put and Canaan.

**7** The sons of Cush: Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah and Sabteca. The sons of Raamah: Sheba and Dedan.

**8** Cush fathered Nimrod. He was the first on earth to become a mighty warrior.

**9** He was a mighty hunter before Yahweh.[^5] That is why it is said, "Like Nimrod, a mighty hunter before Yahweh."

**10** The beginning of his kingdom was Babel,[^6] Erech, Accad and Calneh, in the land of Shinar.[^7]

**11** From that land he went out to Assyria[^8] and built Nineveh, Rehoboth-ir, Calah,

**12** and Resen, between Nineveh and Calah; that is the great city.

**13** Egypt fathered the Ludim, the Anamim, the Lehabim, the Naphtuhim,

**14** the Pathrusim, the Casluhim (from whom the Philistines came), and the Caphtorim.[^9]

**15** Canaan fathered Sidon, his firstborn, and Heth,[^10]

**16** and the Jebusites, the Amorites, the Girgashites,

**17** the Hivites, the Arkites, the Sinites,

**18** the Arvadites, the Zemarites and the Hamathites. Afterward the clans of the Canaanites spread out.

**19** The territory of the Canaanites extended from Sidon toward Gerar as far as Gaza, and toward Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, as far as Lasha.

**20** These are the sons of Ham, by their clans and their languages, in their lands and their nations.

**21** Sons were also born to Shem, the ancestor of all the sons of Eber, and the older brother of Japheth.[^11]

**22** The sons of Shem: Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud and Aram.

**23** The sons of Aram: Uz, Hul, Gether and Mash.

**24** Arpachshad fathered Shelah,[^12] and Shelah fathered Eber.

**25** Two sons were born to Eber. The name of one was Peleg,[^13] for in his days the earth was divided. His brother's name was Joktan.

**26** Joktan fathered Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah,

**27** Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah,

**28** Obal, Abimael, Sheba,

**29** Ophir, Havilah and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan.

**30** The region where they lived extended from Mesha toward Sephar, the hill country of the east.

**31** These are the sons of Shem, by their clans and their languages, in their lands, by their nations.

**32** These are the clans of the sons of Noah, according to their generations, in their nations. From these the nations spread out over the earth after the flood.

[^1]: Hebrew *toledot*; see 2:4.
[^2]: The Samaritan Pentateuch and the Septuagint read "Rodanim" (the people of Rhodes), as 1 Chronicles 1:7 does in the Hebrew. In Hebrew the letters *d* and *r* look very alike.
[^3]: Literally "the coastlands of the nations".
[^4]: Hebrew *Mitsrayim*, the usual Hebrew name for Egypt; also in verse 13.
[^5]: Or "in Yahweh's sight". *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^6]: That is, Babylon.
[^7]: That is, Babylonia, in southern Mesopotamia.
[^8]: Or "From that land Asshur went out".
[^9]: Amos 9:7 and Jeremiah 47:4 connect the Philistines with Caphtor.
[^10]: The ancestor of the Hittites. Verses 16–18 name peoples, in the Hebrew as singular names: "the Jebusite, the Amorite …".
[^11]: Or "the brother of Japheth the elder".
[^12]: The Septuagint reads "Arpachshad fathered Cainan, and Cainan fathered Shelah" (compare Luke 3:36).
[^13]: *Peleg* means "division".
