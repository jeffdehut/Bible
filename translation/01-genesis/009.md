**1** God blessed Noah and his sons and said to them, "Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth.

**2** The fear and dread of you will fall on every wild animal of the earth and every bird of the sky, on everything that moves along the ground, and on all the fish of the sea. They are given into your hands.

**3** Every moving thing that lives will be food for you. Just as I gave you the green plants, I now give you everything.

**4** But you must not eat meat with its life, that is, its blood, still in it.[^1]

**5** And for your lifeblood I will surely demand an accounting. I will demand it from every animal. And from human beings, from each one for the life of another,[^2] I will demand an accounting for human life.

**6** Whoever sheds the blood of a human being, by a human being[^3] his blood will be shed, for in the image of God he made humankind.

**7** As for you, be fruitful and multiply. Swarm over the earth and multiply on it."

**8** Then God said to Noah and to his sons with him,

**9** "As for me, I am establishing my covenant with you and with your offspring[^4] after you,

**10** and with every living creature that is with you: the birds, the livestock and every wild animal of the earth with you, all that came out of the ark, every animal of the earth.

**11** I establish my covenant with you: never again will all living creatures[^5] be cut off by the waters of a flood, and never again will there be a flood to destroy the earth."

**12** God said, "This is the sign of the covenant that I am making between me and you and every living creature that is with you, for all generations to come:

**13** I have set my bow[^6] in the clouds, and it will be the sign of a covenant between me and the earth.

**14** Whenever I bring clouds over the earth and the bow appears in the clouds,

**15** I will remember my covenant that is between me and you and every living creature of every kind.[^7] The waters will never again become a flood to destroy all living creatures.

**16** When the bow is in the clouds, I will see it and remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living creature of every kind on the earth."

**17** God said to Noah, "This is the sign of the covenant that I have established between me and all living creatures on the earth."

**18** The sons of Noah who came out of the ark were Shem, Ham and Japheth. Ham was the father of Canaan.

**19** These three were the sons of Noah, and from them people spread out over the whole earth.[^8]

**20** Noah, a man of the soil, was the first to plant a vineyard.[^9]

**21** He drank some of the wine and became drunk, and he lay uncovered inside his tent.

**22** Ham, the father of Canaan, saw his father's nakedness and told his two brothers outside.

**23** But Shem and Japheth took the cloak, laid it across both their shoulders, walked in backward and covered their father's nakedness. Their faces were turned away, and they did not see their father's nakedness.

**24** When Noah woke up from his wine and learned what his youngest son had done to him,

**25** he said, "Cursed be Canaan! He will be the lowest of slaves[^10] to his brothers."

**26** He also said, "Blessed be Yahweh,[^11] the God of Shem! May Canaan be his[^12] slave.

**27** May God make room for Japheth,[^13] and may he live in the tents of Shem. May Canaan be his slave."

**28** After the flood Noah lived 350 years.

**29** Noah lived 950 years in all, and then he died.

[^1]: Literally "flesh with its *nephesh*, its blood". Hebrew *nephesh* here means life; the same word is used in verse 5.
[^2]: Literally "from the hand of each man, his brother".
[^3]: Or "in exchange for that human being".
[^4]: Literally "seed".
[^5]: Literally "all flesh"; also in verses 15–17.
[^6]: Hebrew *qeshet*, the ordinary word for a bow used in war or hunting; here, the rainbow.
[^7]: Literally "every living creature among all flesh"; also in verse 16.
[^8]: Literally "from these the whole earth was scattered".
[^9]: Or "Noah began to be a farmer, and he planted a vineyard".
[^10]: Literally "a slave of slaves".
[^11]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^12]: Or "their"; also in verse 27.
[^13]: Hebrew *yapht*, "make room, enlarge", sounds like the name Japheth.
