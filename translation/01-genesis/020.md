**1** Abraham journeyed from there to the region of the Negev and settled between Kadesh and Shur. While he was staying as a foreigner in Gerar,

**2** Abraham said of his wife Sarah, "She is my sister." So Abimelech king of Gerar sent and took Sarah.

**3** But God came to Abimelech in a dream by night and said to him, "You are as good as dead because of the woman you have taken, for she is a married woman."

**4** Now Abimelech had not gone near her. He said, "Lord,[^1] will you kill a nation even though it is innocent?[^2]

**5** Didn't he himself say to me, 'She is my sister'? And she herself said, 'He is my brother.' I did this with a clear conscience and clean hands."[^3]

**6** Then God said to him in the dream, "Yes, I know that you did this with a clear conscience, and I also kept you from sinning against me. That is why I did not let you touch her.

**7** Now give the man's wife back, for he is a prophet, and he will pray for you and you will live. But if you do not give her back, know that you will certainly die, you and all who are yours."

**8** Abimelech got up early in the morning and called all his servants and told them all these things, and the men were very afraid.

**9** Then Abimelech called Abraham and said to him, "What have you done to us? How have I sinned against you, that you have brought such great guilt on me and my kingdom? You have done things to me that should not be done."

**10** Abimelech also said to Abraham, "What were you thinking of, that you did this?"[^4]

**11** Abraham said, "I thought, 'Surely there is no fear of God in this place, and they will kill me because of my wife.'

**12** Besides, she really is my sister, the daughter of my father but not the daughter of my mother, and she became my wife.

**13** When God had me wander[^5] from my father's house, I said to her, 'This is how you can show me your faithful love:[^6] wherever we go, say of me, "He is my brother."'"

**14** Then Abimelech took flocks and herds, and male and female servants, and gave them to Abraham, and he gave his wife Sarah back to him.

**15** Abimelech said, "Look, my land is before you. Live wherever you like."

**16** To Sarah he said, "Look, I have given your brother a thousand pieces of silver.[^7] It is to clear you in the eyes of all who are with you, and before everyone you are vindicated."[^8]

**17** Then Abraham prayed to God, and God healed Abimelech, his wife and his slave women, so that they bore children again.

**18** For the LORD[^9] had closed every womb in Abimelech's household because of Sarah, Abraham's wife.

[^1]: Hebrew *Adonai*.
[^2]: Literally "righteous".
[^3]: Literally "in the integrity of my heart and the innocence of my hands"; also in verse 6.
[^4]: Literally "What did you see, that you did this thing?"
[^5]: The Hebrew verb is plural, which is unusual when *elohim* means God. Or "When the gods had me wander".
[^6]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^7]: Literally "a thousand of silver", probably shekels: about 25 pounds (11.5 kg) of silver.
[^8]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. Literally "it is for you a covering of the eyes for all who are with you, and with all, and you are vindicated".
[^9]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
