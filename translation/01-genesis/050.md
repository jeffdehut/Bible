**1** Joseph threw himself on his father's face. He wept over him and kissed him.

**2** Then Joseph ordered his servants, the physicians, to embalm his father. So the physicians embalmed Israel.

**3** It took forty days, for that is the full time needed for embalming. And the Egyptians wept for him seventy days.

**4** When the days of weeping for him were over, Joseph spoke to Pharaoh's household. "If I have found favor with you," he said, "please speak to Pharaoh for me[^1] and tell him:

**5** 'My father made me swear an oath. He said, "I am about to die. Bury me in the tomb I dug[^2] for myself in the land of Canaan." So now please let me go up and bury my father, and then I will come back.'"

**6** Pharaoh said, "Go up and bury your father, as he made you swear."

**7** So Joseph went up to bury his father. All Pharaoh's officials went up with him, the elders of his household and all the elders of the land of Egypt,

**8** together with all Joseph's household, his brothers and his father's household. Only their little children, their flocks and their cattle were left in the land of Goshen.

**9** Chariots and horsemen also went up with him. It was a very large company.

**10** When they came to the threshing floor of Atad, which is beyond the Jordan, they lamented there with a very great and bitter lamentation. And Joseph observed seven days of mourning for his father.

**11** When the Canaanites who lived in the land saw the mourning at the threshing floor of Atad, they said, "This is a solemn mourning for the Egyptians." That is why the place beyond the Jordan was named Abel-mizraim.[^3]

**12** So Jacob's sons did for him as he had commanded them.

**13** His sons carried him to the land of Canaan and buried him in the cave in the field of Machpelah, near Mamre, the field that Abraham had bought from Ephron the Hittite as a burial site of his own.

**14** After he had buried his father, Joseph returned to Egypt, he and his brothers and all who had gone up with him to bury his father.

**15** When Joseph's brothers saw that their father was dead, they said, "What if Joseph bears a grudge against us and pays us back in full for all the wrong we did to him?"

**16** So they sent word to Joseph: "Before your father died, he gave these instructions:

**17** 'This is what you are to say to Joseph: Please forgive the crime of your brothers and their sin, for they did you wrong.' So now please forgive the crime of the servants of the God of your father." Joseph wept when they spoke to him.

**18** Then his brothers also came and threw themselves down before him. They said, "We are your slaves."

**19** But Joseph said to them, "Don't be afraid. Am I in the place of God?

**20** You planned evil against me, but God planned it for good, to bring about what is happening today: to keep many people alive.

**21** So now, don't be afraid. I will provide for you and your little children." And he comforted them and spoke kindly to them.[^4]

**22** Joseph stayed in Egypt, he and his father's household. Joseph lived a hundred and ten years.

**23** Joseph saw Ephraim's children to the third generation. The children of Machir son of Manasseh were also born on Joseph's knees.[^5]

**24** Then Joseph said to his brothers, "I am about to die. But God will surely come to your aid[^6] and bring you up out of this land to the land he promised on oath to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob."

**25** Joseph made Israel's sons[^7] swear an oath. He said, "God will surely come to your aid, and then you must carry my bones up from here."

**26** So Joseph died at the age of a hundred and ten. They embalmed him, and he was placed in a coffin in Egypt.

[^1]: Literally "in the ears of Pharaoh".
[^2]: Hebrew *kariti*, "I dug", or possibly "I bought".
[^3]: *Abel-mizraim* sounds like *evel mitsrayim*, "the mourning of Egypt". *Abel* can also mean "meadow".
[^4]: Literally "spoke to their heart".
[^5]: That is, Joseph received them as members of his own family (compare 30:3; 48:12).
[^6]: Hebrew *paqad*, "visit, take note of, attend to". Also in verse 25.
[^7]: Literally "the sons of Israel": Joseph's brothers, and through them the people of Israel (compare Exodus 13:19).
