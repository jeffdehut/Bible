**1** About that time Judah left his brothers and went down to stay[^1] with a man of Adullam named Hirah.

**2** There Judah saw the daughter of a Canaanite man named Shua. He married her and slept with her.[^2]

**3** She became pregnant and gave birth to a son, and he named him Er.

**4** She became pregnant again and gave birth to a son, and she named him Onan.

**5** She gave birth to yet another son and named him Shelah. He was at Chezib when she gave birth to him.[^3]

**6** Judah took a wife for Er, his firstborn, and her name was Tamar.

**7** But Er, Judah's firstborn, was evil in the sight of the LORD,[^4] so the LORD put him to death.

**8** Then Judah said to Onan, "Sleep with your brother's wife and do your duty to her as her husband's brother.[^5] Raise up children[^6] for your brother."

**9** But Onan knew that the children would not be his. So whenever he slept with his brother's wife, he spilled his semen on the ground,[^7] so that he would not give children to his brother.

**10** What he did was evil in the sight of the LORD, so he put him to death too.

**11** Then Judah said to his daughter-in-law Tamar, "Live as a widow in your father's house until my son Shelah grows up." For he thought, "He too may die like his brothers." So Tamar went and lived in her father's house.

**12** A long time passed, and Shua's daughter, Judah's wife, died. When Judah had been comforted, he went up to Timnah to the men who were shearing his sheep, he and his friend Hirah the Adullamite.

**13** Tamar was told, "Your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his sheep."

**14** So she took off her widow's clothes, covered herself with a veil, and wrapped herself up. Then she sat down at the entrance to Enaim,[^8] which is on the road to Timnah. For she saw that Shelah had grown up, and she had not been given to him as his wife.

**15** When Judah saw her, he thought she was a prostitute, because she had covered her face.

**16** He turned aside to her by the road and said, "Come, let me sleep with you," for he did not know that she was his daughter-in-law. She said, "What will you give me for sleeping with me?"

**17** He said, "I will send you a young goat from the flock." She said, "Only if you give me a pledge until you send it."

**18** He said, "What pledge shall I give you?" She said, "Your seal[^9] and its cord, and the staff in your hand." So he gave them to her and slept with her, and she became pregnant by him.

**19** Then she got up and left. She took off her veil and put on her widow's clothes again.

**20** Judah sent the young goat by his friend the Adullamite to get the pledge back from the woman, but he did not find her.

**21** He asked the men of that place, "Where is the shrine prostitute[^10] who was by the road at Enaim?" They said, "There has been no shrine prostitute here."

**22** So he went back to Judah and said, "I did not find her. Also, the men of the place said, 'There has been no shrine prostitute here.'"

**23** Judah said, "Let her keep the things, or we will become a laughingstock. After all, I did send this young goat, but you could not find her."

**24** About three months later Judah was told, "Your daughter-in-law Tamar has acted as a prostitute, and now she is pregnant as a result of her prostitution." Judah said, "Bring her out, and let her be burned!"

**25** As she was being brought out, she sent word to her father-in-law: "I am pregnant by the man who owns these things." And she said, "Please look closely. Whose are these, the seal, the cord and the staff?"

**26** Judah recognized them and said, "She is more in the right than I am, because I did not give her to my son Shelah." And he did not sleep with her[^11] again.

**27** When the time came for her to give birth, there were twins in her womb.

**28** While she was giving birth, one of them put out a hand. The midwife took a scarlet thread and tied it on his hand, saying, "This one came out first."

**29** But as he drew back his hand, his brother came out. She said, "What a breach you have made for yourself!" So he was named Perez.[^12]

**30** Afterward his brother came out, with the scarlet thread on his hand, and he was named Zerah.[^13]

[^1]: Literally "turned aside".
[^2]: Literally "took her and went in to her". "Went in to" is a Hebrew way of speaking of sexual relations; also in verses 8, 9, 16 and 18.
[^3]: Or "It was at Chezib that she gave birth to him." The Septuagint reads "She was at Chezib".
[^4]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^5]: Hebrew *yabbem*: to marry the widow of a brother who had died without a son, so that a son born to her would carry on the dead brother's name (Deuteronomy 25:5–10).
[^6]: Literally "seed", meaning offspring. Also in verse 9.
[^7]: Literally "he spoiled it on the ground".
[^8]: Hebrew *petach Enayim*, "the entrance of Enaim". *Enaim* means "two springs" or "two eyes", so the phrase may also mean "in an open place". Also in verse 21.
[^9]: A small engraved stone or cylinder, worn on a cord, used to sign documents by pressing it into clay. Together with the staff it identified its owner.
[^10]: Hebrew *qedeshah*, literally "holy woman", a woman connected with a shrine. Verses 15 and 24 use the ordinary words for a prostitute (*zonah*) and prostitution. Whether a *qedeshah* was a prostitute who served at a shrine is debated. Also in verse 22.
[^11]: Literally "know her", a Hebrew way of speaking of sexual relations.
[^12]: *Perez* means "breach" or "bursting out".
[^13]: *Zerah* means "dawning" or "shining", perhaps recalling the bright scarlet thread.
