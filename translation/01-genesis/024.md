**1** Abraham was now old, advanced in years, and the LORD[^1] had blessed Abraham in every way.

**2** Abraham said to his servant, the senior servant of his household,[^2] who was in charge of all he had, "Put your hand under my thigh,[^3]

**3** and I will make you swear by the LORD, the God of heaven and the God of the earth, that you will not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, among whom I am living,

**4** but will go to my country and to my relatives[^4] and take a wife for my son Isaac."

**5** The servant said to him, "Perhaps the woman will not be willing to follow me to this land. Must I then take your son back to the land you came from?"

**6** Abraham said to him, "Make sure that you do not take my son back there.

**7** The LORD, the God of heaven, who took me from my father's household and from the land of my birth, and who spoke to me and swore to me, 'To your offspring[^5] I will give this land,' he will send his angel ahead of you, and you will take a wife for my son from there.

**8** If the woman is not willing to follow you, you will be released from this oath of mine. Only do not take my son back there."

**9** So the servant put his hand under the thigh of his master Abraham and swore to him about this matter.

**10** Then the servant took ten of his master's camels and set out, with all kinds of good things from his master in his hand. He set out and went to Aram-naharaim,[^6] to the city of Nahor.

**11** He made the camels kneel down outside the city by the well of water at evening, the time when the women go out to draw water.

**12** He said, "LORD, God of my master Abraham, please give me success today, and show faithful love[^7] to my master Abraham.

**13** Look, I am standing by the spring of water, and the daughters of the men of the city are coming out to draw water.

**14** Let the young woman to whom I say, 'Please lower your jar so that I may drink,' and who says, 'Drink, and I will water your camels too,' be the one you have chosen for your servant Isaac. By this I will know that you have shown faithful love to my master."

**15** Before he had finished speaking, there was Rebekah coming out with her jar on her shoulder. She was the daughter of Bethuel son of Milcah, the wife of Abraham's brother Nahor.

**16** The young woman was very beautiful, a virgin; no man had slept with her.[^8] She went down to the spring, filled her jar and came up.

**17** The servant ran to meet her and said, "Please give me a little water from your jar to drink."

**18** She said, "Drink, my lord," and she quickly lowered her jar onto her hand and gave him a drink.

**19** When she had finished giving him a drink, she said, "I will draw water for your camels too, until they have finished drinking."

**20** So she quickly emptied her jar into the trough and ran back to the well to draw water, and she drew water for all his camels.

**21** The man was gazing at her in silence, waiting to learn whether the LORD had made his journey successful or not.

**22** When the camels had finished drinking, the man took a gold nose ring weighing a beka,[^9] and two bracelets for her wrists weighing ten shekels[^10] of gold.

**23** He said, "Whose daughter are you? Please tell me. Is there room in your father's house for us to spend the night?"

**24** She said to him, "I am the daughter of Bethuel, the son whom Milcah bore to Nahor."

**25** She also said to him, "We have plenty of straw and fodder, and room to spend the night."

**26** Then the man bowed down and worshiped the LORD.

**27** He said, "Blessed be the LORD, the God of my master Abraham, who has not withdrawn his faithful love and his faithfulness[^11] from my master. As for me, the LORD has led me on the way to the house of my master's relatives."[^12]

**28** The young woman ran and told her mother's household about these things.

**29** Rebekah had a brother named Laban. Laban ran out to the man at the spring.

**30** As soon as he saw the nose ring, and the bracelets on his sister's wrists, and heard his sister Rebekah say, "This is what the man said to me," he went to the man, and there he was, standing by the camels at the spring.

**31** He said, "Come in, you who are blessed by the LORD. Why are you standing outside? I have prepared the house and a place for the camels."

**32** So the man came into the house. The camels were unloaded[^13] and given straw and fodder, and water was brought to wash his feet and the feet of the men who were with him.

**33** Then food was set before him to eat,[^14] but he said, "I will not eat until I have told what I have to say." He said, "Speak."

**34** So he said, "I am Abraham's servant.

**35** The LORD has greatly blessed my master, and he has become rich. He has given him flocks and herds, silver and gold, male and female servants,[^15] camels and donkeys.

**36** Sarah, my master's wife, bore my master a son when she was old, and he has given him everything he has.

**37** My master made me swear, saying, 'You must not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, in whose land I am living,

**38** but you must go to my father's household and to my clan, and take a wife for my son.'

**39** I said to my master, 'Perhaps the woman will not follow me.'

**40** He said to me, 'The LORD, before whom I have walked, will send his angel with you and make your journey successful, and you will take a wife for my son from my clan and from my father's household.

**41** Then you will be free from my oath,[^16] when you come to my clan. Even if they will not give her to you, you will be free from my oath.'

**42** Today I came to the spring, and I said, 'LORD, God of my master Abraham, if only you would make the journey on which I am going successful!

**43** Look, I am standing by the spring of water. Let the young woman[^17] who comes out to draw water, to whom I say, "Please give me a little water from your jar to drink,"

**44** and who says to me, "Drink, and I will draw water for your camels too," let her be the woman the LORD has chosen for my master's son.'

**45** Before I had finished speaking in my heart, there was Rebekah coming out with her jar on her shoulder. She went down to the spring and drew water, and I said to her, 'Please give me a drink.'

**46** She quickly lowered her jar from her shoulder and said, 'Drink, and I will water your camels too.' So I drank, and she watered the camels too.

**47** I asked her, 'Whose daughter are you?' She said, 'The daughter of Bethuel son of Nahor, whom Milcah bore to him.' So I put the ring in her nose and the bracelets on her wrists.

**48** Then I bowed down and worshiped the LORD, and I blessed the LORD, the God of my master Abraham, who had led me by the right way[^18] to take the daughter of my master's brother[^19] for his son.

**49** Now, if you will show faithful love and faithfulness to my master, tell me. And if not, tell me, so that I may turn to the right or to the left."

**50** Laban and Bethuel answered, "This has come from the LORD. We cannot say anything to you, bad or good.

**51** Here is Rebekah before you. Take her and go, and let her be the wife of your master's son, as the LORD has said."

**52** When Abraham's servant heard their words, he bowed down to the ground before the LORD.

**53** Then the servant brought out articles of silver and gold, and clothing, and gave them to Rebekah. He also gave costly gifts to her brother and to her mother.

**54** Then he and the men who were with him ate and drank and spent the night. When they got up in the morning, he said, "Send me back to my master."

**55** Her brother and her mother said, "Let the young woman stay with us a few days, ten or so.[^20] After that she may go."

**56** But he said to them, "Do not delay me, since the LORD has made my journey successful. Send me away so that I may go to my master."

**57** They said, "Let us call the young woman and ask her."[^21]

**58** So they called Rebekah and said to her, "Will you go with this man?" She said, "I will go."

**59** So they sent away their sister Rebekah and her nurse, with Abraham's servant and his men.

**60** They blessed Rebekah and said to her, "Our sister, may you become thousands of ten thousands, and may your offspring take possession of the gate of those who hate them."[^22]

**61** Then Rebekah and her young women got up, mounted the camels and followed the man. So the servant took Rebekah and went.

**62** Now Isaac had come back from Beer-lahai-roi,[^23] for he was living in the Negev.

**63** Isaac went out toward evening to walk[^24] in the field. He looked up and saw camels coming.

**64** Rebekah looked up and saw Isaac, and she quickly got down[^25] from the camel.

**65** She said to the servant, "Who is that man walking in the field to meet us?" The servant said, "It is my master." So she took her veil and covered herself.

**66** The servant told Isaac everything he had done.

**67** Then Isaac brought her into the tent of his mother Sarah.[^26] He took Rebekah, and she became his wife, and he loved her. So Isaac was comforted after his mother's death.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "the elder of his household".
[^3]: A solemn gesture made when swearing an oath (compare 47:29).
[^4]: Or "my birthplace".
[^5]: Literally "seed"; also in verse 60.
[^6]: "Aram of the two rivers", in northern Mesopotamia.
[^7]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love; also in verses 14, 27 and 49.
[^8]: Literally "no man had known her".
[^9]: Half a shekel: about ⅕ ounce (5.7 grams).
[^10]: A shekel is about ⅖ ounce (11.5 grams), so ten shekels is about 4 ounces (115 grams).
[^11]: Hebrew *emet*, "truth, reliability"; also in verse 49.
[^12]: Literally "brothers".
[^13]: Literally "He unloaded the camels and gave straw and fodder to the camels, and water to wash his feet …". The Hebrew does not say who did this.
[^14]: The Hebrew text is written "he set food before him" but read "food was set before him".
[^15]: Or "slaves".
[^16]: Hebrew *alah*, an oath sworn under a curse; twice in this verse.
[^17]: Hebrew *almah*, a young woman of age to be married. In verses 14, 16, 28, 55 and 57 the word is *na'arah*, "girl, young woman".
[^18]: Literally "by the way of truth".
[^19]: That is, of his relative. Rebekah was the granddaughter of Abraham's brother Nahor.
[^20]: Literally "days, or ten".
[^21]: Literally "ask her mouth".
[^22]: Literally "him".
[^23]: Literally "had come from coming to Beer-lahai-roi". The Hebrew is unusual. See 16:14.
[^24]: The meaning of the Hebrew verb is uncertain; perhaps "to meditate".
[^25]: Literally "she fell".
[^26]: Literally "into the tent, Sarah his mother".
