**1** So Isaac called Jacob, blessed him and commanded him, "You must not marry one of the Canaanite women.[^1]

**2** Get up, go to Paddan-aram, to the house of Bethuel, your mother's father, and take a wife from there, from among the daughters of Laban, your mother's brother.

**3** May God Almighty[^2] bless you and make you fruitful and multiply you, so that you become an assembly of peoples.

**4** May he give the blessing of Abraham to you and to your offspring[^3] with you, so that you may take possession of the land where you are now living as a foreigner,[^4] which God gave to Abraham."

**5** So Isaac sent Jacob away, and he went to Paddan-aram, to Laban son of Bethuel the Aramean, the brother of Rebekah, the mother of Jacob and Esau.

**6** Esau saw that Isaac had blessed Jacob and sent him away to Paddan-aram to take a wife from there, and that as he blessed him he commanded him, "You must not marry one of the Canaanite women,"

**7** and that Jacob had obeyed his father and his mother and gone to Paddan-aram.

**8** Esau saw that the Canaanite women displeased his father Isaac.

**9** So Esau went to Ishmael and married Mahalath, the daughter of Abraham's son Ishmael and the sister of Nebaioth, in addition to the wives he had.

**10** Jacob left Beersheba and set out for Haran.

**11** He came to a certain place[^5] and spent the night there, because the sun had set. He took one of the stones of the place, put it under his head,[^6] and lay down to sleep in that place.

**12** He dreamed, and there was a stairway[^7] set up on the earth, with its top reaching to heaven, and there were angels of God going up and down on it.

**13** And there was Yahweh,[^8] standing above it.[^9] He said, "I am Yahweh, the God of your father Abraham and the God of Isaac. The land on which you are lying I will give to you and to your offspring.

**14** Your offspring will be like the dust of the earth, and you will spread out to the west and to the east, to the north and to the south. All the families of the earth will be blessed[^10] in you and in your offspring.

**15** Look, I am with you and will watch over you wherever you go, and I will bring you back to this land. For I will not leave you until I have done what I have promised you."

**16** Then Jacob woke from his sleep and said, "Surely Yahweh is in this place, and I did not know it."

**17** He was afraid and said, "How awesome this place is! This is none other than the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven."

**18** Jacob got up early in the morning, took the stone that he had put under his head, set it up as a pillar[^11] and poured oil on top of it.

**19** He named that place Bethel,[^12] though the name of the city was originally Luz.

**20** Then Jacob made a vow, saying, "If God will be with me and watch over me on this journey that I am taking, and give me bread to eat and clothes to wear,

**21** and I return safely[^13] to my father's house, then Yahweh will be my God,[^14]

**22** and this stone that I have set up as a pillar will be God's house, and of everything you give me I will surely give you a tenth."

[^1]: Literally "the daughters of Canaan"; also in verses 6 and 8.
[^2]: Hebrew *El Shaddai*. The meaning of *Shaddai* is uncertain.
[^3]: Literally "seed"; also in verses 13 and 14.
[^4]: Literally "the land of your sojournings"; see 17:8.
[^5]: Literally "the place". Hebrew *maqom* often means a holy site (12:6).
[^6]: Literally "at his head"; also in verse 18.
[^7]: Hebrew *sullam*, a word found only here: perhaps a ramp or stairway. Traditionally "ladder".
[^8]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^9]: Or "beside him".
[^10]: Or "will bless themselves by you"; see 12:3. "Earth" here is Hebrew *adamah*, "ground".
[^11]: Hebrew *matsevah*, a standing stone set up as a memorial or for worship; also in verse 22.
[^12]: *Bethel* means "house of God".
[^13]: Literally "in peace". Hebrew *shalom*.
[^14]: Or "and if Yahweh will be my God, then this stone …".
