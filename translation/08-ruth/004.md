**1** Meanwhile Boaz went up to the town gate[^1] and sat down there. Just then the guardian-redeemer[^2] Boaz had spoken about came by. Boaz said, "Come over here, my friend,[^3] and sit down." So he came over and sat down.

**2** Boaz took ten men from the elders of the town and said, "Sit down here." And they sat down.

**3** Then he said to the guardian-redeemer, "Naomi, who has come back from the country of Moab, is selling[^4] the piece of land that belonged to our relative[^5] Elimelech.

**4** I thought I should let you know[^6] and say, 'Buy it in the presence of those sitting here[^7] and in the presence of the elders of my people.' If you will redeem it, redeem it. But if you will not redeem it,[^8] tell me, so that I may know. For there is no one but you to redeem it, and I come after you." He said, "I will redeem it."

**5** Then Boaz said, "On the day you buy the field from Naomi, you are also acquiring Ruth the Moabite, the dead man's widow,[^9] in order to keep the dead man's name alive on his inheritance."[^10]

**6** The guardian-redeemer said, "Then I cannot redeem it for myself, or I would ruin my own inheritance. Take over my right of redemption yourself, for I cannot redeem it."

**7** In earlier times in Israel, this was the custom for redeeming and exchanging property, to make any transaction final: one man took off his sandal and gave it to the other. This was the way of confirming a transaction in Israel.[^11]

**8** So the guardian-redeemer said to Boaz, "Buy it yourself," and he took off his sandal.

**9** Then Boaz said to the elders and all the people, "You are witnesses today that I have bought from Naomi everything that belonged to Elimelech and everything that belonged to Chilion and Mahlon.

**10** I have also acquired Ruth the Moabite, Mahlon's widow, to be my wife, in order to keep the dead man's name alive on his inheritance, so that the dead man's name will not be cut off from among his relatives or from the gate of his town.[^12] You are witnesses today."

**11** All the people at the gate and the elders said, "We are witnesses. May Yahweh[^13] make the woman who is coming into your house like Rachel and Leah, the two who built up the house of Israel. May you prosper[^14] in Ephrathah and be famous in Bethlehem.

**12** And through the children[^15] that Yahweh will give you by this young woman, may your family be like the family of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah."[^16]

**13** So Boaz took Ruth, and she became his wife. He slept with her,[^17] and Yahweh enabled her to conceive, and she gave birth to a son.

**14** Then the women said to Naomi, "Blessed be Yahweh, who has not left you without a guardian-redeemer today! May his name be famous in Israel!

**15** He will restore your life and take care of you in your old age. For your daughter-in-law, who loves you and is better to you than seven sons, has given birth to him."

**16** Then Naomi took the child, held him in her lap,[^18] and cared for him.

**17** The neighbor women gave him a name, saying, "A son has been born to Naomi!" They named him Obed.[^19] He was the father of Jesse, the father of David.

**18** These are the generations[^20] of Perez: Perez fathered Hezron,

**19** Hezron fathered Ram, Ram fathered Amminadab,

**20** Amminadab fathered Nahshon, Nahshon fathered Salmon,[^21]

**21** Salmon fathered Boaz, Boaz fathered Obed,

**22** Obed fathered Jesse, and Jesse fathered David.

[^1]: The gate of a town was where its elders met and where legal business was done.
[^2]: Hebrew *go'el*. See 2:20.
[^3]: Hebrew *peloni almoni*, "so-and-so", used when the narrator does not give a name.
[^4]: Or "has sold".
[^5]: Literally "our brother".
[^6]: Literally "uncover your ear".
[^7]: Or "in the presence of the people who live here".
[^8]: Literally "if he will not redeem it". The Septuagint reads "if you will not redeem it".
[^9]: Literally "and from Ruth the Moabite, the wife of the dead man, you are acquiring". The Hebrew text is written "I am acquiring" but read "you are acquiring". With the written form, Boaz says: "On the day you buy the field from Naomi, I am acquiring Ruth the Moabite …"
[^10]: Literally "to raise up the name of the dead man on his inheritance", that is, to provide him with an heir who would inherit his land. Also in verse 10.
[^11]: Compare Deuteronomy 25:9–10.
[^12]: Literally "from the gate of his place".
[^13]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^14]: Or "show your worth". Hebrew *chayil*, "strength, worth, wealth", as in 2:1 and 3:11.
[^15]: Literally "seed".
[^16]: Genesis 38:27–30. Perez was an ancestor of Boaz (verses 18–21).
[^17]: Literally "went in to her", a Hebrew way of speaking of sexual relations.
[^18]: Literally "laid him in her bosom".
[^19]: *Obed* means "one who serves".
[^20]: Hebrew *toledot*: the descendants of someone and their story. Compare Genesis 2:4.
[^21]: Hebrew *Salmah* here, but *Salmon* in verse 21: two forms of the same name.
