**1** "At that time the kingdom of heaven will be like ten young women[^1] who took their lamps[^2] and went out to meet the bridegroom.

**2** Five of them were foolish, and five were wise.

**3** The foolish ones took their lamps but did not take any oil with them.

**4** But the wise ones took oil in jars along with their lamps.

**5** The bridegroom was a long time coming, and they all became drowsy and fell asleep.

**6** At midnight there was a shout: 'Here is the bridegroom! Come out to meet him!'

**7** Then all those young women got up and trimmed their lamps.

**8** The foolish ones said to the wise, 'Give us some of your oil, because our lamps are going out.'

**9** But the wise ones answered, 'No, there may not be enough for both us and you. Go instead to those who sell oil and buy some for yourselves.'

**10** While they were on their way to buy it, the bridegroom arrived. Those who were ready went in with him to the wedding banquet, and the door was shut.

**11** Later the other young women came too. 'Lord, Lord,'[^3] they said, 'open the door for us!'

**12** But he answered, 'Truly I tell you, I do not know you.'

**13** So keep watch, because you do not know the day or the hour.[^4]

**14** "For it is like a man going on a journey, who called his servants[^5] and entrusted his possessions to them.

**15** To one he gave five talents,[^6] to another two, and to another one, each according to his ability. Then he went away on his journey. At once[^7]

**16** the one who had received the five talents went and put them to work and gained five more.

**17** In the same way, the one with the two gained two more.

**18** But the one who had received the one went off, dug a hole in the ground, and hid his master's money.

**19** After a long time the master of those servants came back and settled accounts with them.

**20** The one who had received the five talents came forward and brought five more talents. 'Master,' he said, 'you entrusted me with five talents. See, I have gained five more talents.'

**21** His master said to him, 'Well done, good and faithful servant! You have been faithful over a few things; I will put you in charge of many things. Come and share your master's joy.'[^8]

**22** The one who had received the two talents also came forward. 'Master,' he said, 'you entrusted me with two talents. See, I have gained two more talents.'

**23** His master said to him, 'Well done, good and faithful servant! You have been faithful over a few things; I will put you in charge of many things. Come and share your master's joy.'

**24** Then the one who had received the one talent came forward. 'Master,' he said, 'I knew that you are a harsh man. You harvest where you have not sown and gather where you have not scattered seed.

**25** So I was afraid, and I went and hid your talent in the ground. Here, you have what is yours.'

**26** His master answered him, 'You wicked and lazy servant! You knew that I harvest where I have not sown and gather where I have not scattered seed?

**27** Then you should have put my money with the bankers, and when I came back I would have received what is mine with interest.

**28** So take the talent from him and give it to the one who has the ten talents.

**29** For to everyone who has, more will be given, and they will have more than enough. But from the one who does not have, even what they have will be taken away.

**30** And throw that worthless servant into the outer darkness, where there will be weeping and grinding of teeth.'

**31** "When the Son of Man comes in his glory, and all the angels[^9] with him, he will sit on his glorious throne.[^10]

**32** All the nations will be gathered before him, and he will separate them from one another, as a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats.

**33** He will put the sheep at his right and the goats at his left.

**34** Then the king will say to those at his right, 'Come, you who are blessed by my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you since the creation of the world.

**35** For I was hungry and you gave me something to eat. I was thirsty and you gave me something to drink. I was a stranger and you welcomed me.

**36** I was naked and you clothed me. I was sick and you looked after me. I was in prison and you came to me.'

**37** Then the righteous will answer him, 'Lord, when did we see you hungry and feed you, or thirsty and give you something to drink?

**38** When did we see you a stranger and welcome you, or naked and clothe you?

**39** When did we see you sick or in prison and come to you?'

**40** The king will answer them, 'Truly I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine,[^11] you did for me.'

**41** Then he will say to those at his left, 'Go away from me, you who are cursed, into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels.

**42** For I was hungry and you gave me nothing to eat. I was thirsty and you gave me nothing to drink.

**43** I was a stranger and you did not welcome me, naked and you did not clothe me, sick and in prison and you did not look after me.'

**44** Then they too will answer, 'Lord, when did we see you hungry or thirsty or a stranger or naked or sick or in prison, and did not take care of you?'

**45** He will answer them, 'Truly I tell you, whatever you did not do for one of the least of these, you did not do for me.'

**46** And these will go away to eternal punishment, but the righteous to eternal life."

[^1]: Or "virgins". Greek *parthenoi*, unmarried young women; also in verses 7 and 11.
[^2]: Or "torches"; also in verses 3–8.
[^3]: Or "Sir, sir".
[^4]: Many manuscripts add "in which the Son of Man is coming".
[^5]: Or "slaves"; also in verses 19–30. Greek *doulos*.
[^6]: A talent was worth about 6,000 denarii, some twenty years' wages for a laborer.
[^7]: Or "Then he went away on his journey at once." The Greek word for "at once" can belong to either sentence.
[^8]: Literally "Enter into the joy of your master"; also in verse 23.
[^9]: Many manuscripts read "all the holy angels".
[^10]: Literally "the throne of his glory".
[^11]: Literally "brothers".
