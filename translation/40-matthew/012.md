**1** At that time Jesus went through the grainfields on the Sabbath. His disciples were hungry, and they began to pick heads of grain and eat them.

**2** When the Pharisees saw this, they said to him, "Look, your disciples are doing what is not allowed on the Sabbath."

**3** He said to them, "Haven't you read what David did when he and his companions were hungry?

**4** He went into the house of God, and they ate[^1] the sacred bread,[^2] which was not lawful for him or his companions to eat, but only for the priests.

**5** Or haven't you read in the Law that on the Sabbath the priests in the temple break the Sabbath and yet are innocent?[^3]

**6** I tell you, something greater than the temple is here.

**7** If you had known what this means, 'I desire mercy, not sacrifice,'[^4] you would not have condemned the innocent.

**8** For the Son of Man is lord of the Sabbath."

**9** He went on from there and entered their synagogue.

**10** A man was there with a withered hand. And they asked Jesus, "Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?" so that they could accuse him.

**11** He said to them, "Which of you, if you have one sheep and it falls into a pit on the Sabbath, will not take hold of it and lift it out?

**12** How much more valuable is a person than a sheep! So it is lawful to do good on the Sabbath."

**13** Then he said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." He stretched it out, and it was restored, as healthy as the other.

**14** But the Pharisees went out and plotted against him, how they might destroy him.

**15** Aware of this, Jesus withdrew from there. Many[^5] followed him, and he healed them all,

**16** and he ordered them not to make him known.

**17** This was so that what had been spoken through the prophet Isaiah would be fulfilled:

**18** "Here is my servant,[^6] whom I have chosen, my beloved, in whom my soul delights. I will put my Spirit on him, and he will proclaim justice to the nations.

**19** He will not quarrel or cry out, and no one will hear his voice in the streets.

**20** A bruised reed he will not break, and a smoldering wick he will not snuff out, until he leads justice to victory.

**21** And in his name the nations will hope."[^7]

**22** Then a man possessed by a demon, who was blind and could not speak, was brought to him. Jesus healed him, so that the man could speak and see.

**23** All the crowds were astonished and said, "Could this be the Son of David?"

**24** But when the Pharisees heard this, they said, "This man drives out demons only by Beelzebul,[^8] the ruler of the demons."

**25** Knowing their thoughts, Jesus said to them, "Every kingdom divided against itself is laid waste, and no town or household divided against itself will stand.

**26** And if Satan drives out Satan, he is divided against himself. How then will his kingdom stand?

**27** And if I drive out demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your own people[^9] drive them out? So they will be your judges.

**28** But if it is by the Spirit of God that I drive out demons, then the kingdom of God[^10] has come upon you.

**29** Or how can anyone go into a strong man's house and carry off his possessions without first tying up the strong man? Then he can plunder his house.

**30** Whoever is not with me is against me, and whoever does not gather with me scatters.

**31** "That is why I tell you, people will be forgiven every sin and blasphemy, but blasphemy against the Spirit will not be forgiven.

**32** Whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man will be forgiven, but whoever speaks against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven, either in this age or in the age to come.

**33** "Either make the tree good and its fruit good, or make the tree rotten and its fruit rotten, for a tree is known by its fruit.

**34** You brood of vipers! How can you say good things when you are evil? For the mouth speaks from what fills the heart.

**35** A good person brings good things out of a good treasure, and an evil person brings evil things out of an evil treasure.

**36** I tell you, on the day of judgment people will give an account for every careless word they speak.

**37** For by your words you will be declared righteous,[^11] and by your words you will be condemned."

**38** Then some of the scribes and Pharisees said to him, "Teacher, we want to see a sign from you."

**39** He answered them, "An evil and unfaithful[^12] generation asks for a sign, but no sign will be given to it except the sign of the prophet Jonah.

**40** For just as Jonah was in the belly of the sea creature for three days and three nights, so the Son of Man will be in the heart of the earth for three days and three nights.[^13]

**41** The people of Nineveh will rise up at the judgment with this generation and condemn it, because they repented at Jonah's preaching.[^14] And look, something greater than Jonah is here.

**42** The queen of the South will rise up at the judgment with this generation and condemn it, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear Solomon's wisdom.[^15] And look, something greater than Solomon is here.

**43** "When an unclean spirit goes out of a person, it goes through waterless places looking for rest, but does not find any.

**44** Then it says, 'I will go back to my house that I came from.' When it arrives, it finds the house empty, swept, and put in order.

**45** Then it goes and brings with it seven other spirits more evil than itself, and they go in and live there. And the final condition of that person is worse than the first. That is how it will be with this evil generation also."

**46** While he was still speaking to the crowds, his mother and brothers were standing outside, wanting to speak to him.

**47** Someone told him, "Look, your mother and your brothers are standing outside, wanting to speak to you."[^16]

**48** He answered the man who told him, "Who is my mother, and who are my brothers?"

**49** Pointing to his disciples, he said, "Here are my mother and my brothers!

**50** For whoever does the will of my Father in heaven is my brother and sister and mother."

[^1]: Some manuscripts read "he ate".
[^2]: Literally "the bread of the presentation", the twelve loaves set out before God in the sanctuary (Leviticus 24:5–9). See 1 Samuel 21:2–7 (21:1–6 in most English Bibles).
[^3]: The priests offered sacrifices on the Sabbath (Numbers 28:9–10).
[^4]: Hosea 6:6; see 9:13.
[^5]: Some manuscripts read "Large crowds".
[^6]: Greek *pais*, which can mean "servant" or "child" (as in 8:6).
[^7]: Isaiah 42:1–4, in a form that differs in places from both the Hebrew and the Septuagint. Verse 21 follows the Septuagint; the Hebrew reads "and the coastlands wait for his instruction".
[^8]: See 10:25; also in verse 27.
[^9]: Literally "your sons", that is, your followers.
[^10]: Matthew usually says "the kingdom of heaven" (3:2); here he has "the kingdom of God", as also in 19:24; 21:31, 43.
[^11]: Greek *dikaioo*, traditionally "justified": here the opposite of "condemned".
[^12]: Literally "adulterous", unfaithful to God.
[^13]: Jonah 2:1 (1:17 in most English Bibles). Greek *ketos*, a large sea creature, the Septuagint's word for Jonah's "great fish".
[^14]: Jonah 3:5.
[^15]: The queen of Sheba (1 Kings 10:1–13).
[^16]: Some manuscripts leave out verse 47.
