**1** In those days John the Baptist appeared in the wilderness of Judea, proclaiming

**2** and saying, "Repent,[^1] for the kingdom of heaven[^2] has come near."

**3** This is the one the prophet Isaiah spoke about when he said: "A voice is shouting in the wilderness: 'Prepare the way for the Lord; make his paths straight.'"[^3]

**4** John wore clothing made of camel's hair, with a leather belt around his waist,[^4] and his food was locusts and wild honey.

**5** Then the people of Jerusalem, all Judea, and the whole region around the Jordan went out to him.

**6** They confessed their sins and were baptized by him in the Jordan River.

**7** But when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees[^5] coming to his baptism, he said to them, "You brood of vipers! Who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?

**8** Produce fruit, then, that fits your repentance.

**9** And don't think you can say to yourselves, 'We have Abraham as our father.' For I tell you, God can raise up children for Abraham from these stones.

**10** The ax is already lying at the root of the trees. So every tree that does not produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.

**11** I baptize you with water for repentance, but the one who comes after me is more powerful than I am. I am not worthy to carry his sandals. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire.[^6]

**12** His winnowing fork[^7] is in his hand. He will clear his threshing floor and gather his wheat into the barn, but he will burn up the chaff with fire that never goes out."

**13** Then Jesus came from Galilee to John at the Jordan to be baptized by him.

**14** But John tried to stop him. "I need to be baptized by you," he said, "and do you come to me?"

**15** Jesus answered him, "Let it be so for now. It is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness[^8] in this way." Then John allowed him.

**16** When Jesus had been baptized, he came up from the water at once. Just then the heavens were opened,[^9] and he saw the Spirit of God coming down like a dove and coming on him.

**17** And a voice from the heavens said, "This is my Son, whom I love. I am delighted with him."[^10]

[^1]: Greek *metanoeo*, to change one's mind and way of life, to turn around. Also in verses 8 and 11 (*metanoia*, "repentance").
[^2]: Literally "the kingdom of the heavens", Matthew's usual phrase. Many Jews of the time spoke of "heaven" instead of naming God. Mark and Luke have "the kingdom of God".
[^3]: Isaiah 40:3. Matthew follows the Septuagint, which joins "in the wilderness" to the voice; the Hebrew reads "A voice is shouting: 'In the wilderness prepare the way for the LORD.'" In Isaiah's Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, YHWH ("the LORD").
[^4]: Elijah was dressed in the same way (2 Kings 1:8).
[^5]: The Pharisees were a movement of teachers and laypeople devoted to keeping the Law of Moses and the traditions that explained it. The Sadducees were a party of priests and leading families, who accepted only the written Law and did not believe in the resurrection (22:23).
[^6]: Many manuscripts leave out "and fire".
[^7]: A wooden fork for tossing threshed grain into the air, so that the wind would blow the chaff away.
[^8]: Or "to do everything that is right".
[^9]: Many manuscripts read "opened to him".
[^10]: Or "This is my beloved Son; I take great pleasure in him." Compare Psalm 2:7; Isaiah 42:1.
