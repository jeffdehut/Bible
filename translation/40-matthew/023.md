**1** Then Jesus spoke to the crowds and to his disciples:

**2** "The scribes and the Pharisees sit on Moses' seat.[^1]

**3** So do whatever they tell you and keep it, but don't do what they do, for what they say, they do not do.

**4** They tie up heavy loads[^2] and put them on people's shoulders, but they themselves are not willing to lift a finger to move them.

**5** Everything they do is done to be seen by people. They make their phylacteries[^3] wide and their tassels[^4] long.

**6** They love the places of honor at banquets and the best seats in the synagogues,

**7** and to be greeted in the marketplaces and to be called 'Rabbi'[^5] by people.

**8** But you are not to be called 'Rabbi', for you have one teacher,[^6] and you are all brothers.[^7]

**9** And do not call anyone on earth your father, for you have one Father, the one in heaven.

**10** Nor are you to be called instructors,[^8] for you have one instructor, the Messiah.

**11** The greatest among you will be your servant.

**12** Whoever exalts themselves will be humbled, and whoever humbles themselves will be exalted.

**13** "Woe to you,[^9] scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You shut the kingdom of heaven in people's faces. You yourselves do not go in, and you do not let those who are trying to go in enter.[^10]

**15** "Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You travel over sea and land to make one convert,[^11] and when he becomes one, you make him twice as much a child of hell[^12] as you are.

**16** "Woe to you, blind guides! You say, 'If anyone swears by the temple,[^13] it means nothing; but if anyone swears by the gold of the temple, they are bound by their oath.'

**17** You blind fools! Which is greater: the gold, or the temple that makes the gold holy?

**18** You also say, 'If anyone swears by the altar, it means nothing; but if anyone swears by the gift on it, they are bound by their oath.'

**19** You blind people![^14] Which is greater: the gift, or the altar that makes the gift holy?

**20** So whoever swears by the altar swears by it and by everything on it.

**21** And whoever swears by the temple swears by it and by the one who dwells in it.

**22** And whoever swears by heaven swears by the throne of God and by the one who sits on it.

**23** "Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You give a tenth[^15] of your mint, dill, and cumin, but you have neglected the more important matters of the Law: justice, mercy, and faithfulness.[^16] These you should have done, without neglecting the others.

**24** You blind guides! You strain out a gnat but swallow a camel.

**25** "Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You clean the outside of the cup and the dish, but inside they are full of greed[^17] and self-indulgence.[^18]

**26** You blind Pharisee! First clean the inside of the cup and the dish,[^19] so that the outside of it may become clean too.

**27** "Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You are like whitewashed tombs,[^20] which look beautiful on the outside but inside are full of dead people's bones and every kind of impurity.

**28** In the same way, on the outside you appear righteous to people, but inside you are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness.

**29** "Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You build the tombs of the prophets and decorate the monuments of the righteous,

**30** and you say, 'If we had lived in the days of our ancestors, we would not have taken part with them in shedding the blood of the prophets.'

**31** So you testify against yourselves that you are the descendants[^21] of those who murdered the prophets.

**32** Go on, then, and finish what your ancestors started![^22]

**33** You snakes, you brood of vipers! How will you escape being sentenced to hell?

**34** Therefore I am sending you prophets, wise men, and scribes. Some of them you will kill and crucify, and some you will flog in your synagogues and pursue from town to town,

**35** so that all the righteous blood shed on earth will come upon you, from the blood of righteous Abel to the blood of Zechariah son of Barachiah, whom you murdered between the temple and the altar.[^23]

**36** Truly I tell you, all this will come upon this generation.

**37** "Jerusalem, Jerusalem, you who kill the prophets and stone those sent to you! How often I have wanted to gather your children together, as a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, and you were not willing!

**38** Look, your house is left to you desolate.[^24]

**39** For I tell you, you will not see me again until you say, 'Blessed is the one who comes in the name of the Lord.'"[^25]

[^1]: That is, they hold the authority to teach the Law of Moses.
[^2]: Many manuscripts add "and hard to carry".
[^3]: Small leather boxes containing verses of scripture, worn on the forehead and arm at prayer (compare Deuteronomy 6:8).
[^4]: Worn at the corners of a garment as a reminder of God's commands (Numbers 15:38–39). Many manuscripts add "of their garments".
[^5]: A Hebrew title, "my teacher", "my master".
[^6]: Many manuscripts read "for one is your leader, the Messiah".
[^7]: Or "brothers and sisters".
[^8]: Or "leaders". Greek *kathegetes*, "guide, teacher"; also later in the verse.
[^9]: Greek *ouai*, a cry of grief and warning, like the prophets' *hoy* ("woe"); also in verses 15–29.
[^10]: Many manuscripts add before verse 13: "Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You devour widows' houses and for show make long prayers. Therefore you will receive the greater condemnation" (compare Mark 12:40). The Textus Receptus (followed by the King James Version) has these words after verse 13, as verse 14. The SBLGNT and other critical editions leave them out, so this translation has no verse 14.
[^11]: Greek *proselytos*, a Gentile who has converted to Judaism.
[^12]: Literally "a son of Gehenna", one who belongs there. Greek *Gehenna*, from the Valley of Hinnom outside Jerusalem, used as a name for the place of final judgment; also in verse 33.
[^13]: Greek *naos*, the sanctuary building itself; also in verses 17, 21 and 35.
[^14]: Many manuscripts read "You blind fools!"
[^15]: The tithe required by the Law (Leviticus 27:30; Deuteronomy 14:22–23).
[^16]: Or "faith".
[^17]: Literally "robbery", "plunder".
[^18]: Many manuscripts read "wrongdoing".
[^19]: Some manuscripts leave out "and the dish".
[^20]: Tombs were whitewashed so that people would see them and avoid touching them, since contact with a grave made a person ritually unclean (Numbers 19:16).
[^21]: Literally "sons".
[^22]: Literally "Fill up the measure of your fathers."
[^23]: Abel was murdered by Cain (Genesis 4:8). The prophet Zechariah is called "son of Berechiah" in Zechariah 1:1. The Zechariah stoned in the temple court in 2 Chronicles 24:20–22, near the end of the last book of the Hebrew Bible, is the son of Jehoiada.
[^24]: Some manuscripts leave out "desolate".
[^25]: Psalm 118:26. In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, YHWH ("the LORD").
