**1** After the Sabbath,[^1] as the first day of the week was dawning, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to look at the tomb.

**2** Suddenly there was a great earthquake, for an angel of the Lord came down from heaven, went to the tomb, rolled back the stone,[^2] and sat on it.

**3** His appearance was like lightning, and his clothing was as white as snow.

**4** The guards were so afraid of him that they shook and became like dead men.

**5** The angel said to the women, "As for you, don't be afraid. I know that you are looking for Jesus, who was crucified.

**6** He is not here; he has been raised, just as he said. Come, see the place where he lay.[^3]

**7** Then go quickly and tell his disciples, 'He has been raised from the dead, and he is going ahead of you into Galilee. There you will see him.' Now I have told you."

**8** So they left the tomb quickly, afraid yet filled with great joy, and ran to tell his disciples.

**9** Suddenly[^4] Jesus met them and said, "Greetings!"[^5] They came to him, took hold of his feet, and worshiped him.

**10** Then Jesus said to them, "Don't be afraid. Go and tell my brothers to go to Galilee; there they will see me."

**11** While they were on their way, some of the guards went into the city and reported to the chief priests everything that had happened.

**12** After meeting with the elders and agreeing on a plan, the chief priests gave the soldiers a large sum of money,

**13** telling them, "You are to say, 'His disciples came during the night and stole him while we were asleep.'

**14** If this comes to the governor's ears, we will persuade him and keep you out of trouble."

**15** So they took the money and did as they were instructed. And this story has been spread among Jews to this day.

**16** The eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus had told them to go.

**17** When they saw him, they worshiped him, but some doubted.[^6]

**18** Jesus came to them and said, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me.[^7]

**19** So go and make disciples of all nations,[^8] baptizing them in the name[^9] of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,

**20** teaching them to keep everything I have commanded you. And look, I am with you always,[^10] to the end of the age."[^11]

[^1]: Or "Late on the Sabbath".
[^2]: Many manuscripts add "from the entrance".
[^3]: Many manuscripts read "where the Lord lay".
[^4]: Many manuscripts begin the verse "As they were going to tell his disciples".
[^5]: Greek *chairete*, the ordinary greeting, literally "Rejoice!"
[^6]: Or "but they hesitated". Greek *distazo*, "doubt, hesitate". The Greek can mean that some of the Eleven doubted, or that they all did.
[^7]: Compare Daniel 7:14, "He was given authority".
[^8]: Or "all peoples". Greek *panta ta ethne*, the phrase used in 24:9, 14 and 25:32.
[^9]: Literally "into the name".
[^10]: Literally "all the days".
[^11]: Many manuscripts add "Amen".
