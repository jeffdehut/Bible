**1** The Pharisees and Sadducees came, and to test him they asked him to show them a sign from heaven.

**2** He answered them, "When evening comes, you say, 'It will be fair weather, for the sky is red.'

**3** And in the morning you say, 'Today there will be a storm, for the sky is red and threatening.'[^1] You know how to interpret the appearance of the sky, but you cannot interpret the signs of the times.[^2]

**4** An evil and unfaithful[^3] generation asks for a sign, but no sign will be given to it except the sign of Jonah."[^4] Then he left them and went away.

**5** When the disciples reached the other side, they had forgotten to bring bread.

**6** Jesus said to them, "Watch out! Beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees."

**7** They discussed this among themselves and said, "It is because we didn't bring any bread."

**8** Aware of this, Jesus said, "You of little faith, why are you discussing among yourselves that you didn't bring any bread?[^5]

**9** Don't you understand yet? Don't you remember the five loaves for the five thousand, and how many baskets you picked up?

**10** Or the seven loaves for the four thousand, and how many large baskets you picked up?

**11** How is it that you don't understand that I was not talking to you about bread? But beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees."

**12** Then they understood that he had not told them to beware of the yeast used in bread, but of the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees.

**13** When Jesus came to the region of Caesarea Philippi, he asked his disciples, "Who do people say the Son of Man is?"[^6]

**14** They said, "Some say John the Baptist, others Elijah, and still others Jeremiah or one of the prophets."

**15** He said to them, "But who do you say I am?"

**16** Simon Peter answered, "You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God."

**17** Jesus answered him, "Blessed are you, Simon son of Jonah,[^7] because flesh and blood[^8] did not reveal this to you, but my Father in heaven.

**18** And I tell you, you are Peter,[^9] and on this rock I will build my church,[^10] and the gates of Hades[^11] will not overpower it.

**19** I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven.[^12] Whatever you bind on earth will have been bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on earth will have been loosed in heaven."[^13]

**20** Then he ordered the disciples not to tell anyone that he was the Messiah.[^14]

**21** From that time Jesus[^15] began to show his disciples that he must go to Jerusalem and suffer many things from the elders, the chief priests, and the scribes, and be killed, and on the third day be raised.

**22** Peter took him aside and began to rebuke him. "God forbid it, Lord!"[^16] he said. "This will never happen to you!"

**23** But he turned and said to Peter, "Get behind me, Satan! You are a stumbling block[^17] to me, because you are not thinking about the things of God, but about human things."

**24** Then Jesus said to his disciples, "If anyone wants to follow after me, they must deny themselves, take up their cross, and follow me.

**25** For whoever wants to save their life[^18] will lose it, but whoever loses their life for my sake will find it.

**26** For what good will it be for someone to gain the whole world, yet forfeit their life? Or what can anyone give in exchange for their life?

**27** For the Son of Man is going to come in the glory of his Father with his angels, and then he will repay each person according to what they have done.[^19]

**28** Truly I tell you, some of those standing here will not taste death before they see the Son of Man coming in his kingdom."

[^1]: Many manuscripts add "You hypocrites!"
[^2]: Some manuscripts leave out the words from "When evening comes" in verse 2 to the end of verse 3. Westcott and Hort print them in double brackets.
[^3]: Literally "adulterous"; see 12:39.
[^4]: Many manuscripts read "the sign of the prophet Jonah".
[^5]: Some manuscripts read "that you have no bread".
[^6]: Many manuscripts read "Who do people say that I, the Son of Man, am?"
[^7]: Aramaic *bar-yona*, "son of Jonah". John 1:42 calls Peter "Simon son of John".
[^8]: That is, a human being.
[^9]: Greek *Petros*, a man's name formed from *petra*, "rock". Compare John 1:42.
[^10]: Greek *ekklesia*, "assembly", the community of Jesus' followers.
[^11]: Greek *Hades*, the realm of the dead, like the Hebrew Sheol.
[^12]: Compare Isaiah 22:22.
[^13]: Or "will be bound … will be loosed". *Bind* and *loose* were terms used by Jewish teachers for forbidding and permitting.
[^14]: Many manuscripts read "that he was Jesus the Messiah".
[^15]: Some manuscripts read "Jesus Christ".
[^16]: Literally "Mercy to you", meaning "May God spare you this".
[^17]: Greek *skandalon*, a trap or an obstacle that makes someone stumble.
[^18]: Greek *psyche*, life, soul, self; also in verse 26.
[^19]: Compare Psalm 62:13 (62:12 in most English Bibles); Proverbs 24:12.
