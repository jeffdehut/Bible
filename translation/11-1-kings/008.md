**1** Then Solomon assembled the elders of Israel, all the heads of the tribes, the leaders of the ancestral families[^1] of the Israelites, before King Solomon in Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the covenant of Yahweh[^2] from the City of David, which is Zion.

**2** All the men of Israel assembled before King Solomon at the festival in the month of Ethanim,[^3] which is the seventh month.

**3** When all the elders of Israel had come, the priests took up the ark.

**4** They brought up the ark of Yahweh, the tent of meeting[^4] and all the holy utensils that were in the tent. The priests and the Levites brought them up.

**5** King Solomon and the whole community of Israel who had gathered to him were with him before the ark, sacrificing so many sheep and cattle that they could not be counted or numbered.

**6** Then the priests brought the ark of the covenant of Yahweh to its place, in the inner sanctuary of the house, in the most holy place,[^5] beneath the wings of the cherubim.

**7** For the cherubim spread out their wings over the place of the ark, and the cherubim formed a covering above the ark and its poles.

**8** The poles were so long that their ends could be seen from the holy place[^6] in front of the inner sanctuary, but they could not be seen from outside. They are there to this day.

**9** There was nothing in the ark except the two stone tablets that Moses had put there at Horeb,[^7] where Yahweh made a covenant[^8] with the Israelites when they came out of the land of Egypt.

**10** When the priests came out of the holy place, the cloud filled the house of Yahweh,

**11** and the priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud, for the glory of Yahweh filled the house of Yahweh.

**12** Then Solomon said, "Yahweh has said that he would live in thick darkness.[^9]

**13** I have indeed built you a lofty house, a place for you to live in forever."

**14** Then the king turned around and blessed the whole assembly of Israel, while the whole assembly of Israel stood.

**15** He said, "Blessed be Yahweh, the God of Israel, who spoke with his own mouth to my father David, and with his own hand has fulfilled it. He said,

**16** 'Since the day I brought my people Israel out of Egypt, I have not chosen a city from any of the tribes of Israel in which to build a house, so that my name would be there. But I chose David to be over my people Israel.'[^10]

**17** "My father David had it in his heart to build a house for the name of Yahweh, the God of Israel.

**18** But Yahweh said to my father David, 'You had it in your heart to build a house for my name. You did well to have this in your heart.

**19** But you are not the one who will build the house. Your son, who will come from your own body,[^11] is the one who will build the house for my name.'

**20** Now Yahweh has kept the promise he made. I have succeeded my father David and sit on the throne of Israel, as Yahweh promised, and I have built the house for the name of Yahweh, the God of Israel.

**21** There I have provided a place for the ark, in which is the covenant[^12] of Yahweh that he made with our ancestors when he brought them out of the land of Egypt."

**22** Then Solomon stood before the altar of Yahweh in front of the whole assembly of Israel, and he spread out his hands toward heaven

**23** and said, "Yahweh, God of Israel, there is no God like you in heaven above or on earth below. You keep your covenant and show faithful love[^13] to your servants who walk before you with all their heart.

**24** You have kept what you promised to your servant, my father David. With your mouth you promised it, and with your hand you have fulfilled it, as it is today.

**25** So now, Yahweh, God of Israel, keep for your servant, my father David, what you promised him when you said, 'You will never lack a man before me to sit on the throne of Israel,[^14] if only your sons take care how they live,[^15] walking before me as you have walked before me.'

**26** So now, God of Israel, please let your word[^16] that you spoke to your servant, my father David, be confirmed.

**27** "But will God really live on the earth? Look, the heavens, even the highest heavens,[^17] cannot contain you. How much less this house that I have built!

**28** Yet turn to your servant's prayer and his plea, Yahweh my God. Listen to the cry and the prayer that your servant is praying before you today,

**29** that your eyes may be open toward this house night and day, toward the place of which you said, 'My name will be there,' so that you will listen to the prayer that your servant prays toward this place.

**30** Listen to the plea of your servant and of your people Israel when they pray toward this place. Listen in heaven, the place where you live. Listen and forgive.

**31** "When someone sins against his neighbor and is made to take an oath,[^18] and he comes and swears the oath before your altar in this house,

**32** then listen in heaven and act. Judge your servants: condemn the guilty, bringing what they have done on their own heads, and vindicate the innocent,[^19] rewarding them as their righteousness deserves.

**33** "When your people Israel are defeated by an enemy because they have sinned against you, and they turn back to you and praise your name,[^20] and pray and plead with you in this house,

**34** then listen in heaven and forgive the sin of your people Israel, and bring them back to the land you gave to their ancestors.

**35** "When the heavens are shut up and there is no rain because they have sinned against you, and they pray toward this place and praise your name, and turn from their sin because you afflict them,[^21]

**36** then listen in heaven and forgive the sin of your servants, your people Israel. For you will teach them the good way in which they should walk. And send rain on your land, which you have given to your people as their inheritance.

**37** "When there is famine in the land, or plague, or blight or mildew, or locusts or grasshoppers,[^22] or when their enemy besieges them in the land of their cities,[^23] whatever disaster, whatever sickness there is,

**38** whatever prayer, whatever plea is made by anyone among all your people Israel, each knowing the affliction of his own heart and spreading out his hands toward this house,

**39** then listen in heaven, the place where you live, and forgive. Act, and give to each person according to all his ways, since you know his heart, for you alone know the hearts of all human beings,

**40** so that they may fear you all the days they live in the land you gave to our ancestors.

**41** "Likewise the foreigner, who is not of your people Israel but comes from a distant land because of your name,

**42** for they will hear of your great name and your strong hand and your outstretched arm, when he comes and prays toward this house,

**43** then listen in heaven, the place where you live, and do all that the foreigner asks of you, so that all the peoples of the earth may know your name and fear you, as your people Israel do, and may know that this house I have built bears your name.[^24]

**44** "When your people go out to battle against their enemy, wherever you send them, and they pray to Yahweh toward the city you have chosen and the house I have built for your name,

**45** then listen in heaven to their prayer and their plea, and uphold their cause.[^25]

**46** "When they sin against you, for there is no one who does not sin, and you are angry with them and hand them over to an enemy, and their captors carry them off to the enemy's land, far away or near,

**47** and they come to their senses[^26] in the land where they have been taken captive, and turn and plead with you in the land of their captors, saying, 'We have sinned, we have done wrong, we have acted wickedly,'

**48** and they turn back to you with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their enemies who took them captive, and pray to you toward their land, which you gave to their ancestors, the city you have chosen and the house I have built[^27] for your name,

**49** then listen in heaven, the place where you live, to their prayer and their plea, and uphold their cause.

**50** Forgive your people who have sinned against you, and all the offenses they have committed against you. Let them find compassion with their captors, so that they will have compassion on them.

**51** For they are your people and your inheritance, whom you brought out of Egypt, out of the iron furnace.[^28]

**52** "May your eyes be open to your servant's plea and to the plea of your people Israel, to listen to them whenever they call to you.

**53** For you set them apart from all the peoples of the earth to be your inheritance, as you declared through your servant Moses when you brought our ancestors out of Egypt, Lord Yahweh."

**54** When Solomon had finished praying all this prayer and plea to Yahweh, he rose from before the altar of Yahweh, where he had been kneeling with his hands spread out toward heaven.

**55** He stood and blessed the whole assembly of Israel in a loud voice, saying,

**56** "Blessed be Yahweh, who has given rest to his people Israel, just as he promised. Not one word has failed of all the good promises he made through his servant Moses.

**57** May Yahweh our God be with us as he was with our ancestors. May he never leave us or abandon us.

**58** May he turn our hearts to himself, to walk in all his ways and to keep his commands, his decrees and his rulings, which he commanded our ancestors.

**59** And may these words of mine, with which I have pleaded before Yahweh, be near to Yahweh our God day and night, so that he will uphold the cause of his servant and the cause of his people Israel, as each day requires,

**60** so that all the peoples of the earth may know that Yahweh is God; there is no other.

**61** So let your heart be fully devoted to[^29] Yahweh our God, to walk in his decrees and keep his commands, as you do today."

**62** Then the king and all Israel with him offered sacrifices before Yahweh.

**63** Solomon offered as fellowship offerings[^30] to Yahweh 22,000 cattle and 120,000 sheep and goats. So the king and all the Israelites dedicated the house of Yahweh.

**64** On that same day the king consecrated the middle of the courtyard in front of the house of Yahweh. There he offered the burnt offerings, the grain offerings and the fat of the fellowship offerings, because the bronze altar that was before Yahweh was too small to hold the burnt offerings, the grain offerings and the fat of the fellowship offerings.

**65** So at that time Solomon held the festival, and all Israel with him, a great assembly from Lebo-hamath[^31] to the Wadi of Egypt,[^32] before Yahweh our God, for seven days and seven more days, fourteen days.[^33]

**66** On the eighth day he sent the people away. They blessed the king and went to their homes,[^34] joyful and glad of heart for all the good that Yahweh had done for his servant David and for his people Israel.

[^1]: Literally "the chiefs of the fathers".
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: An old name for the seventh month, around September–October. The festival is the Festival of Shelters, held in that month (Leviticus 23:34).
[^4]: Hebrew *ohel mo'ed*: the tent where Yahweh met with Moses and the people (Exodus 27:21), here probably the tabernacle kept at Gibeon (3:4; 2 Chronicles 1:3).
[^5]: Literally "the holy of holies".
[^6]: The main hall in front of the inner sanctuary (6:3, 17). Also in verse 10.
[^7]: Another name for Mount Sinai.
[^8]: Literally "where Yahweh cut", the usual idiom for making a covenant.
[^9]: Hebrew *arafel*, the thick dark cloud in which God appeared at Sinai (Exodus 20:21). The Septuagint gives a longer form of verses 12–13 after verse 53 and says the words are written in "the Book of the Song".
[^10]: 2 Chronicles 6:5–6 adds: "nor did I choose anyone to be ruler over my people Israel. But I have chosen Jerusalem for my name to be there, and".
[^11]: Literally "who will come out from your loins".
[^12]: That is, the stone tablets of the covenant (verse 9).
[^13]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^14]: Literally "A man will not be cut off for you from before me, sitting on the throne of Israel." See 2:4.
[^15]: Literally "keep their way".
[^16]: The Hebrew text is written "your words" but read "your word".
[^17]: Literally "the heavens and the heaven of heavens".
[^18]: Literally "and he lifts against him a curse to curse him": the accused is made to swear an oath that calls down a curse on himself if he is guilty (Exodus 22:9–10, English 22:10–11; Numbers 5:19–22).
[^19]: Literally "declaring the wicked guilty … and declaring the righteous righteous".
[^20]: Or "acknowledge your name". Also in verse 35.
[^21]: Or "when you answer them". The Hebrew *ta'anem* can come from either verb.
[^22]: Hebrew *arbeh* and *chasil*, two kinds of locusts, or locusts at two stages of growth.
[^23]: Literally "in the land of his gates".
[^24]: Literally "that your name is called over this house", a phrase that marks ownership.
[^25]: Literally "do their justice". Also in verses 49 and 59.
[^26]: Literally "bring back to their heart".
[^27]: The Hebrew text is written "you have built" but read "I have built".
[^28]: A furnace for smelting iron, an image of harsh suffering (Deuteronomy 4:20).
[^29]: Literally "be whole with".
[^30]: Hebrew *shelamim*, traditionally "peace offerings": sacrifices shared in a meal by the worshipers (3:15). Also in verse 64.
[^31]: Or "the entrance to Hamath", at the northern limit of the land (Numbers 34:8).
[^32]: Hebrew *nachal Mitsrayim*, a seasonal stream on the border of Egypt, probably the Wadi el-Arish in northern Sinai.
[^33]: The dedication of the altar lasted seven days, and the Festival of Shelters seven more (2 Chronicles 7:8–10). The Septuagint reads only "for seven days".
[^34]: Literally "to their tents".
