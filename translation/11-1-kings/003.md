**1** Solomon made a marriage alliance with Pharaoh king of Egypt. He married Pharaoh's daughter and brought her to the City of David, until he had finished building his own house, the house of Yahweh,[^1] and the wall around Jerusalem.

**2** The people, however, were still sacrificing at the high places,[^2] because no house had yet been built for the name of Yahweh in those days.

**3** Solomon loved Yahweh and walked in the decrees of his father David, except that he offered sacrifices and burned incense[^3] at the high places.

**4** The king went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, because that was the great high place. Solomon offered a thousand burnt offerings on that altar.

**5** At Gibeon Yahweh appeared to Solomon in a dream at night, and God said, "Ask for whatever you want me to give you."

**6** Solomon said, "You have shown great faithful love[^4] to your servant, my father David, because he walked before you in faithfulness, in righteousness and with an upright heart toward you. And you have kept this great faithful love for him and have given him a son to sit on his throne, as it is today.

**7** Now, Yahweh my God, you have made your servant king in place of my father David. But I am only a young boy. I do not know how to go out or come in.[^5]

**8** And your servant is here among your people whom you have chosen, a great people, too many to be numbered or counted.

**9** So give your servant a listening heart[^6] to govern[^7] your people and to discern between good and evil. For who is able to govern this great people of yours?"[^8]

**10** It was good in the eyes of the Lord[^9] that Solomon had asked for this.

**11** So God said to him, "Because you have asked for this, and have not asked for a long life for yourself, or asked for riches for yourself, or asked for the lives of your enemies, but have asked for understanding to discern what is just,[^10]

**12** I am now doing as you asked. I am giving you a wise and discerning heart, so that there has been no one like you before you, and no one like you will arise after you.

**13** I am also giving you what you have not asked for, both riches and honor, so that no one among the kings will be like you as long as you live.[^11]

**14** And if you walk in my ways, keeping my decrees and my commands, as your father David walked, I will give you a long life."[^12]

**15** Then Solomon woke up, and he realized it had been a dream. He went to Jerusalem and stood before the ark of the covenant of the Lord. He offered burnt offerings and fellowship offerings,[^13] and he held a feast for all his servants.

**16** Then two women who were prostitutes came to the king and stood before him.

**17** One of the women said, "Please, my lord,[^14] this woman and I live in the same house, and I gave birth to a child while she was in the house.

**18** On the third day after I gave birth, this woman also gave birth. We were alone. There was no one else[^15] with us in the house, only the two of us in the house.

**19** During the night this woman's son died, because she lay on him.

**20** She got up in the middle of the night and took my son from beside me while your servant was asleep. She laid him in her arms,[^16] and she laid her dead son in my arms.

**21** When I got up in the morning to nurse my son, there he was, dead. But when I looked at him closely in the morning, I saw that he was not the son I had borne."

**22** The other woman said, "No! The living one is my son, and the dead one is your son." But the first woman said, "No! The dead one is your son, and the living one is my son." So they argued before the king.

**23** Then the king said, "This one says, 'This is my son, the living one, and your son is the dead one.' And that one says, 'No! Your son is the dead one, and my son is the living one.'"

**24** So the king said, "Bring me a sword." And they brought a sword before the king.

**25** Then the king said, "Cut the living child in two, and give half to one and half to the other."

**26** But the woman whose son was the living one spoke to the king, because her heart went out to her son.[^17] She said, "Please, my lord, give her the living baby! Do not kill him!" But the other said, "He will be neither mine nor yours. Cut him in two!"

**27** Then the king answered, "Give the living baby to the first woman,[^18] and do not kill him. She is his mother."

**28** When all Israel heard the judgment the king had given, they stood in awe of the king,[^19] for they saw that the wisdom of God was in him to do justice.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. "The house of Yahweh" is the temple.
[^2]: Hebrew *bamot*: places of worship, often on hilltops. Also in verses 3 and 4.
[^3]: Or "burned offerings". The Hebrew verb *qatar* means to make something go up in smoke.
[^4]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^5]: That is, how to carry out the duties of a leader.
[^6]: Or "an understanding mind". The heart was thought of as the seat of understanding.
[^7]: Literally "to judge". Also later in the verse.
[^8]: Literally "this heavy people of yours", that is, so many.
[^9]: Hebrew *Adonai*, "the Lord", here instead of God's personal name. Also in verse 15.
[^10]: Literally "to understand, to hear justice".
[^11]: Literally "so that there has not been a man like you among the kings all your days".
[^12]: Literally "I will lengthen your days."
[^13]: Hebrew *shelamim*, traditionally "peace offerings": sacrifices shared in a meal by the worshipers.
[^14]: Literally "On me, my lord", a polite way of beginning a request to a superior. Also in verse 26.
[^15]: Literally "no stranger".
[^16]: Literally "in her bosom". Also later in the verse.
[^17]: Literally "her compassion grew warm for her son".
[^18]: Literally "to her".
[^19]: Literally "they feared before the king".
