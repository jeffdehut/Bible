**1** After many days, in the third year, the word of the LORD[^1] came to Elijah: "Go, show yourself to Ahab, and I will send rain on the face of the earth."

**2** So Elijah went to show himself to Ahab. The famine was severe in Samaria.

**3** Ahab called Obadiah, who was in charge of the palace.[^2] Obadiah feared the LORD greatly.

**4** When Jezebel was killing off the prophets of the LORD, Obadiah had taken a hundred prophets and hidden them, fifty to a cave,[^3] and he had supplied them with bread and water.

**5** Ahab said to Obadiah, "Go through the land to all the springs of water and to all the valleys.[^4] Perhaps we can find grass to keep the horses and mules alive, so that we will not have to kill any of the animals."

**6** So they divided the land between them to pass through it. Ahab went one way by himself, and Obadiah went another way by himself.

**7** As Obadiah was on his way, Elijah met him. Obadiah recognized him, fell on his face and said, "Is it you, my lord Elijah?"

**8** He said to him, "It is. Go and tell your lord, 'Elijah is here.'"

**9** But he said, "What sin have I committed, that you are handing your servant over to Ahab to kill me?

**10** As the LORD your God lives, there is no nation or kingdom where my lord has not sent to look for you. When they said, 'He is not here,' he made the kingdom or nation swear that they had not found you.

**11** And now you say, 'Go and tell your lord, "Elijah is here."'

**12** As soon as I leave you, the Spirit of the LORD[^5] will carry you off I do not know where. Then when I go to tell Ahab and he does not find you, he will kill me. Yet I, your servant, have feared the LORD from my youth.

**13** Has my lord not been told what I did when Jezebel killed the prophets of the LORD, how I hid a hundred of the LORD's prophets, fifty to a cave, and supplied them with bread and water?

**14** And now you say, 'Go and tell your lord, "Elijah is here."' He will kill me!"

**15** Elijah said, "As the LORD of Armies[^6] lives, whom I serve,[^7] I will show myself to him today."

**16** So Obadiah went to meet Ahab and told him, and Ahab went to meet Elijah.

**17** When Ahab saw Elijah, Ahab said to him, "Is it you, you troubler of Israel?"[^8]

**18** He said, "I have not troubled Israel. You have, you and your father's house, by abandoning the commands of the LORD and following the Baals.[^9]

**19** Now send and gather all Israel to me at Mount Carmel, with the 450 prophets of Baal and the 400 prophets of Asherah,[^10] who eat at Jezebel's table."

**20** So Ahab sent word among all the Israelites and gathered the prophets at Mount Carmel.

**21** Elijah came near to all the people and said, "How long will you go limping between two opinions?[^11] If the LORD is God, follow him. But if Baal is, follow him." But the people did not answer him a word.

**22** Then Elijah said to the people, "I am the only one left as a prophet of the LORD, but Baal's prophets are 450 men.

**23** Let two bulls be given to us. Let them choose one bull for themselves, cut it in pieces and lay it on the wood, but put no fire to it. I will prepare the other bull and lay it on the wood, and I will put no fire to it.

**24** Then you call on the name of your god, and I will call on the name of the LORD. The God who answers by fire, he is God." All the people answered, "That is good."[^12]

**25** Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, "Choose one bull for yourselves and prepare it first, for you are many. Call on the name of your god, but put no fire to it."

**26** So they took the bull that was given them[^13] and prepared it. Then they called on the name of Baal from morning until noon, saying, "Baal, answer us!" But there was no voice, and no one answered. They limped around[^14] the altar they had made.[^15]

**27** At noon Elijah mocked them. He said, "Shout louder! For he is a god. Perhaps he is deep in thought, or he has stepped aside,[^16] or he is on a journey. Perhaps he is asleep and needs to wake up."

**28** So they shouted louder, and they cut themselves with swords and spears, as was their custom, until blood poured out on them.

**29** When midday had passed, they raved on[^17] until the time of the evening offering.[^18] But there was no voice, no one answered, and no one paid attention.

**30** Then Elijah said to all the people, "Come near to me." All the people came near to him, and he repaired the altar of the LORD that had been torn down.

**31** Elijah took twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Jacob, to whom the word of the LORD had come, saying, "Israel will be your name."[^19]

**32** With the stones he built an altar in the name of the LORD, and he made a trench around the altar large enough to hold two seahs[^20] of seed.

**33** He arranged the wood, cut the bull in pieces and laid it on the wood.

**34** Then he said, "Fill four jars with water and pour it on the burnt offering and on the wood." Then he said, "Do it a second time," and they did it a second time. He said, "Do it a third time," and they did it a third time.[^21]

**35** The water ran around the altar, and he also filled the trench with water.

**36** At the time of the evening offering, Elijah the prophet came near and said, "LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac and Israel, let it be known today that you are God in Israel, and that I am your servant and have done all these things at your word.[^22]

**37** Answer me, LORD, answer me, so that this people may know that you, LORD, are God, and that you have turned their hearts back again."

**38** Then the fire of the LORD fell and consumed the burnt offering, the wood, the stones and the dust, and it licked up the water that was in the trench.

**39** When all the people saw it, they fell on their faces and said, "The LORD, he is God! The LORD, he is God!"

**40** Elijah said to them, "Seize the prophets of Baal! Do not let one of them escape." They seized them, and Elijah brought them down to the Kishon Valley[^23] and slaughtered them there.

**41** Elijah said to Ahab, "Go up, eat and drink, for there is the sound of roaring rain."

**42** So Ahab went up to eat and drink. But Elijah went up to the top of Carmel, bowed down to the ground and put his face between his knees.[^24]

**43** He said to his servant, "Go up and look toward the sea." He went up and looked and said, "There is nothing." Seven times Elijah said, "Go back."

**44** The seventh time the servant said, "Look, a cloud as small as a man's hand is rising from the sea." Elijah said, "Go up and say to Ahab, 'Harness your chariot[^25] and go down, so that the rain does not stop you.'"

**45** In a little while[^26] the sky grew black with clouds and wind, and there was a heavy rain. Ahab rode off and went to Jezreel.

**46** The hand of the LORD was on Elijah. He tucked his robe into his belt[^27] and ran ahead of Ahab all the way to Jezreel.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "over the house" (4:6).
[^3]: Literally "fifty men in the cave". Verse 13 has "fifty, fifty".
[^4]: Hebrew *nechalim*: valleys with streams that flow in the rainy season.
[^5]: Or "the wind of the LORD". Hebrew *ruach* means both.
[^6]: Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*, traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^7]: Literally "before whom I stand".
[^8]: Hebrew *okher*, one who brings disaster on a community (Joshua 7:25). Also in verse 18.
[^9]: The plural refers to Baal as worshiped in many local forms.
[^10]: The Canaanite goddess Asherah (15:13).
[^11]: Literally "limping on the two branches". The meaning of *se'ippim* is uncertain: perhaps "opinions", or "crutches", or "forks of the road".
[^12]: Literally "The word is good."
[^13]: Literally "that he gave them".
[^14]: The same verb as "limping" in verse 21, here perhaps a ritual dance.
[^15]: Literally "that he had made".
[^16]: Hebrew *sig*, perhaps a polite way of saying he has gone to relieve himself.
[^17]: Hebrew *yitnabbe'u*, "acted as prophets": here a frenzied ecstasy.
[^18]: Literally "until the offering up of the grain offering", the offering made in the late afternoon. Also in verse 36.
[^19]: See Genesis 32:29 (32:28 in most English Bibles); 35:10.
[^20]: A seah was about 7 quarts (7 liters). The phrase may also mean that the trench enclosed the area two seahs of seed would sow.
[^21]: In most English Bibles the first sentence of this verse ends verse 33, and verse 34 begins "Do it a second time".
[^22]: The Hebrew text is written "at your words" but read "at your word".
[^23]: Hebrew *nachal Qishon*, the valley of the Kishon stream, below Mount Carmel.
[^24]: The Hebrew text is written "his knee" but read "his knees".
[^25]: Literally "Tie up", that is, hitch the horses to the chariot.
[^26]: Literally "until thus and until thus".
[^27]: Literally "girded up his loins".
