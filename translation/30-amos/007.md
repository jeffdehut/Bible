**1** This is what the Lord Yahweh[^1] showed me: he was forming a swarm of locusts when the late crop[^2] was beginning to come up. It was the late crop after the king's mowings.[^3]

**2** When they had finished eating the plants of the land, I said, "Lord Yahweh, forgive, please! How can Jacob survive?[^4] He is so small."

**3** Yahweh relented[^5] concerning this. "It will not happen," said Yahweh.

**4** This is what the Lord Yahweh showed me: the Lord Yahweh was calling for judgment by fire.[^6] It devoured the great deep[^7] and was devouring the land.[^8]

**5** Then I said, "Lord Yahweh, stop, please! How can Jacob survive? He is so small."

**6** Yahweh relented concerning this. "This too will not happen," said the Lord Yahweh.

**7** This is what he showed me: the Lord was standing beside a wall built with a plumb line, with a plumb line in his hand.[^9]

**8** Yahweh said to me, "What do you see, Amos?" I said, "A plumb line." Then the Lord said, "Look, I am setting a plumb line among my people Israel. I will not pass them by again.[^10]

**9** The high places[^11] of Isaac[^12] will be made desolate, and the sanctuaries of Israel will be laid waste. I will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword."

**10** Then Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent a message to Jeroboam king of Israel: "Amos has conspired against you in the very heart of the house of Israel. The land cannot bear all his words.

**11** For this is what Amos has said: 'Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel will surely go into exile away from its land.'"

**12** Amaziah said to Amos, "Seer,[^13] go! Run away to the land of Judah. Earn your living[^14] there, and prophesy there.

**13** But never prophesy at Bethel again, for it is the king's sanctuary and a royal temple."[^15]

**14** Amos answered Amaziah, "I am not a prophet, and I am not a prophet's son.[^16] I am a herdsman and a tender of sycamore figs.[^17]

**15** But Yahweh took me from following the flock, and Yahweh said to me, 'Go, prophesy to my people Israel.'

**16** So now hear the word of Yahweh. You say, 'Do not prophesy against Israel, and do not preach[^18] against the house of Isaac.'

**17** Therefore this is what Yahweh says: Your wife will become a prostitute in the city, and your sons and your daughters will fall by the sword. Your land will be divided up with a measuring line, and you yourself will die in an unclean land.[^19] And Israel will surely go into exile away from its land."

[^1]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh", traditionally "the Lord GOD". *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. Also in verses 2, 4, 5 and 6.
[^2]: Hebrew *leqesh*, the later growth of grass and grain after the spring rains.
[^3]: Perhaps the first cutting of the grass, taken as a tax for the king's animals (1 Kings 18:5). After it, the late crop was all the people had.
[^4]: Literally "Who is Jacob, that he could stand?" Also in verse 5.
[^5]: Or "changed his mind". Also in verse 6.
[^6]: Literally "calling to contend by fire", as in a lawsuit. Some scholars read "calling for a shower of fire", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^7]: Hebrew *tehom rabbah*, the waters under the earth that fed the springs and rivers (Genesis 7:11).
[^8]: Literally "the portion", perhaps the land allotted to Israel.
[^9]: Hebrew *anakh*, found only in verses 7–8. The traditional meaning is "plumb line", a cord with a weight of lead, used to test whether a wall stands straight. The word may instead mean "tin", a metal used in making weapons: "standing on a wall of tin, with tin in his hand".
[^10]: That is, I will no longer pass over their sins and spare them. Also in 8:2.
[^11]: Hill shrines where sacrifices were offered.
[^12]: A poetic name for Israel, found only in Amos (also verse 16). Isaac was the father of Jacob.
[^13]: Hebrew *chozeh*, "one who sees visions", from the verb in 1:1.
[^14]: Literally "eat bread".
[^15]: Literally "a house of the kingdom".
[^16]: The Hebrew has no verb. Or "I was not a prophet …; I was a herdsman". A *prophet's son* is a member of a group of prophets (2 Kings 2:3, 5).
[^17]: The fruit of the sycamore-fig tree had to be slit or pinched to make it ripen. The tree does not grow at the height of Tekoa (1:1), so Amos may have worked in the lowlands.
[^18]: Literally "drip", as words flow from the mouth (Micah 2:6, 11).
[^19]: A foreign land, unclean because other gods are worshiped there.
