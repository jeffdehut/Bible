**1** Hear this word that the LORD[^1] has spoken against you, people of Israel, against the whole clan that I brought up from the land of Egypt:

**2** You alone have I known[^2] of all the clans of the earth. Therefore I will punish you for all your wrongdoing.

**3** Do two walk together unless they have agreed to meet?

**4** Does a lion roar in the forest when it has no prey? Does a young lion growl from its den unless it has caught something?

**5** Does a bird fall into a trap on the ground when no snare[^3] is set for it? Does a trap spring up from the ground when it has caught nothing?

**6** If a ram's horn[^4] is blown in a city, are the people not afraid? If disaster comes to a city, has the LORD not done it?

**7** Surely the Lord GOD[^5] does nothing without revealing his plan[^6] to his servants the prophets.

**8** The lion has roared; who will not be afraid? The Lord GOD has spoken; who can do anything but prophesy?

**9** Proclaim it to the fortresses in Ashdod[^7] and to the fortresses in the land of Egypt, and say: "Gather on the mountains of Samaria, and see the great turmoil within her and the oppression in her midst."

**10** They do not know how to do what is right, declares the LORD,[^8] those who store up violence and destruction in their fortresses.

**11** Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: An enemy will surround the land.[^9] He will strip you of your strength,[^10] and your fortresses will be plundered.

**12** This is what the LORD says: As a shepherd rescues from the lion's mouth two legs or a piece of an ear,[^11] so the people of Israel who live in Samaria will be rescued, with the corner of a couch and a piece of a bed.[^12]

**13** Hear, and testify against the house of Jacob, declares the Lord GOD, the God of Armies.[^13]

**14** On the day I punish Israel for its crimes,[^14] I will punish the altars of Bethel.[^15] The horns of the altar will be cut off and fall to the ground.

**15** I will strike the winter house together with the summer house. The houses of ivory[^16] will perish, and the great houses[^17] will come to an end, declares the LORD.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: That is, chosen and cared for as my own (Genesis 18:19; Hosea 13:5).
[^3]: Hebrew *moqesh*, perhaps the bait or the trigger of the trap.
[^4]: Hebrew *shofar*, a trumpet made from a ram's horn, sounded as an alarm in war.
[^5]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord YHWH". After "Lord", God's personal name is written GOD in capitals, as in most English Bibles. Also in verses 8, 11 and 13.
[^6]: Hebrew *sod*, the private counsel of a ruler and his advisers (Jeremiah 23:18, 22).
[^7]: The Septuagint reads "Assyria".
[^8]: Hebrew *ne'um YHWH*, "the declaration of the LORD", a formula that marks a prophet's words as the LORD's own solemn statement. Also in verses 13 and 15.
[^9]: Literally "An enemy, and around the land!" The Hebrew is abrupt.
[^10]: Literally "He will bring down your strength from you". The Hebrew addresses Samaria as "you" (feminine).
[^11]: A shepherd brought back such remains as proof that a wild animal had killed the sheep, so that he would not have to repay it (Exodus 22:12; 22:13 in most English Bibles; Genesis 31:39).
[^12]: The meaning is uncertain. Literally "with the corner of a bed and with Damascus of a couch". *Damascus* may be a word for fine fabric, or a word for part of a couch. Or "so the people of Israel will be rescued, those who sit in Samaria on the corner of a bed and on the cushions of a couch".
[^13]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH Elohei ha-tseva'ot*, "the Lord GOD, the God of the Armies". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Compare "the LORD of Armies", traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty".
[^14]: Literally "On the day I punish the crimes of Israel on him".
[^15]: The royal shrine of the northern kingdom (7:13; 1 Kings 12:28–33). The horns were the projections at the four corners of an altar. A person seeking refuge took hold of them (1 Kings 1:50).
[^16]: Houses decorated with carved ivory panels, like Ahab's palace in Samaria (1 Kings 22:39).
[^17]: Or "the many houses".
