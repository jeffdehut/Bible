**1** There was a man of the Pharisees named Nicodemus, a leader of the Jews.[^1]

**2** He came to Jesus at night and said to him, "Rabbi, we know that you are a teacher who has come from God. For no one can do these signs that you do unless God is with him."

**3** Jesus answered him, "Truly, truly, I tell you, no one can see the kingdom of God without being born from above."[^2]

**4** Nicodemus said to him, "How can a man be born when he is old? He can't go back into his mother's womb a second time and be born, can he?"

**5** Jesus answered, "Truly, truly, I tell you, no one can enter the kingdom of God without being born of water and the Spirit.[^3]

**6** What is born of the flesh is flesh, and what is born of the Spirit is spirit.

**7** Don't be amazed that I told you, 'You[^4] must be born from above.'

**8** The wind[^5] blows where it wants. You hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it is going. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit."

**9** Nicodemus answered him, "How can these things be?"

**10** Jesus answered him, "You are the teacher of Israel, and you do not understand these things?

**11** Truly, truly, I tell you, we speak about what we know and testify to what we have seen, and yet you[^6] do not accept our testimony.

**12** If I have told you about earthly things and you do not believe, how will you believe if I tell you about heavenly things?

**13** No one has gone up into heaven except the one who came down from heaven, the Son of Man.[^7]

**14** Just as Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness,[^8] so the Son of Man must be lifted up,[^9]

**15** so that everyone who believes may have eternal life in him.[^10]

**16** For God loved the world so much that he gave his only Son,[^11] so that everyone who believes in him will not perish but will have eternal life.[^12]

**17** For God did not send the Son into the world to condemn[^13] the world, but so that the world might be saved through him.

**18** Whoever believes in him is not condemned, but whoever does not believe is condemned already, because they have not believed in the name of the only Son of God.

**19** This is the judgment:[^14] the light has come into the world, and people loved the darkness rather than the light, because their deeds were evil.

**20** Everyone who does evil hates the light and does not come to the light, so that their deeds will not be exposed.

**21** But whoever does what is true[^15] comes to the light, so that it may be seen plainly that their deeds have been done in God."

**22** After this Jesus and his disciples went into the Judean countryside. He spent time with them there and baptized.

**23** John was also baptizing at Aenon near Salim, because there was plenty of water there, and people kept coming and being baptized.

**24** For John had not yet been put in prison.

**25** A dispute arose between John's disciples and a Jew about purification.

**26** They came to John and said to him, "Rabbi, the man who was with you on the other side of the Jordan, the one you testified about: look, he is baptizing, and everyone is going to him."

**27** John answered, "No one can receive anything unless it has been given to them from heaven.

**28** You yourselves can testify that I said, 'I am not the Messiah, but I have been sent ahead of him.'

**29** The bride belongs to the bridegroom.[^16] The bridegroom's friend, who stands by and listens for him, is overjoyed when he hears the bridegroom's voice. So this joy of mine is now complete.

**30** He must become greater; I must become less.

**31** The one who comes from above[^17] is above all. The one who is from the earth belongs to the earth and speaks as one from the earth.[^18] The one who comes from heaven is above all.

**32** He testifies to what he has seen and heard, yet no one accepts his testimony.

**33** Whoever has accepted his testimony has confirmed[^19] that God is truthful.

**34** For the one whom God has sent speaks the words of God, for he gives the Spirit without measure.[^20]

**35** The Father loves the Son and has put everything into his hands.

**36** Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life. Whoever rejects[^21] the Son will not see life; instead, God's anger remains on him."

[^1]: That is, a member of the Jewish ruling council in Jerusalem. Literally "a ruler of the Jews".
[^2]: Or "born again", also in verse 7. Greek *anothen* means both "from above" and "again"; Nicodemus takes it in the second sense (verse 4).
[^3]: Or "of water and spirit". Greek *pneuma* means "spirit", "wind" or "breath"; see verse 8.
[^4]: *You* is plural in Greek.
[^5]: Greek *pneuma*, the same word as "Spirit" in this verse. *Sound*: Greek *phone*, also "voice".
[^6]: *You* is plural in Greek here and in verse 12.
[^7]: Many manuscripts add "who is in heaven".
[^8]: Numbers 21:8–9.
[^9]: John uses this verb, "lift up", both of Jesus being raised on the cross and of his exaltation (8:28; 12:32–34).
[^10]: Or "so that everyone who believes in him may have eternal life". Many manuscripts read "so that everyone who believes in him may not perish but may have eternal life", as in verse 16. *Eternal life*: Greek *zoe aionios*, literally "the life of the age", that is, of the age to come. In John, those who believe have it already (verse 36; 5:24).
[^11]: Or "For this is how God loved the world: he gave his only Son". *Only*: Greek *monogenes*, "only, unique, one of a kind" (see 1:14); also in verse 18.
[^12]: The Greek has no quotation marks, and it is unclear where Jesus' words end. They may end with verse 15, so that verses 16–21 are the Gospel writer's own reflection. This translation keeps verses 16–21 as part of what Jesus says.
[^13]: Or "to judge". Greek *krino* means "judge", and often "condemn"; also in verse 18.
[^14]: Or "the verdict", "the reason for condemnation".
[^15]: Literally "does the truth".
[^16]: Literally "The one who has the bride is the bridegroom."
[^17]: Greek *anothen*, as in verses 3 and 7. As at verse 16, it is unclear whether John's words end with verse 30 and verses 31–36 are the Gospel writer's reflection; this translation keeps them as part of what John says.
[^18]: Literally "is from the earth and speaks from the earth".
[^19]: Literally "has set his seal", as on a document, to certify it.
[^20]: Or "for God gives the Spirit without measure". The Greek does not say who gives; many manuscripts add "God".
[^21]: Or "disobeys". Greek *apeitheo*, "refuse to believe, refuse to obey".
