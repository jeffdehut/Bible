**1** ⟦[^1]But Jesus went to the Mount of Olives.

**2** Early in the morning he came again to the temple, and all the people came to him. He sat down and began to teach them.

**3** The scribes and the Pharisees brought a woman who had been caught in adultery. They made her stand in the middle

**4** and said to him, to test him, "Teacher, this woman was caught in the very act of committing adultery.

**5** In the law Moses commanded us to stone such women.[^2] So what do you say?"

**6** They were saying this to test him, so that they would have grounds to accuse him. But Jesus bent down and began writing on the ground with his finger, as though he did not notice them.[^3]

**7** When they kept on questioning him, he straightened up and said to them, "Let the one among you who is without sin be the first to throw a stone at her."

**8** And again he bent down and wrote on the ground.

**9** When they heard this, convicted by their conscience, they went away one by one, beginning with the elders. Jesus was left alone, with the woman still standing there in the middle.

**10** Jesus straightened up and, seeing no one but the woman, said to her, "Where are those accusers of yours? Has no one condemned you?"

**11** She said, "No one, sir."[^4] Jesus said, "Neither do I condemn you.[^5] Go, and from now on sin no more."⟧

**12** Jesus spoke to them again: "I am the light of the world.[^6] Whoever follows me will never walk in the darkness, but will have the light of life."

**13** So the Pharisees said to him, "You are testifying about yourself. Your testimony is not true."[^7]

**14** Jesus answered them, "Even if I testify about myself, my testimony is true, because I know where I came from and where I am going. But you do not know where I come from or where I am going.

**15** You judge by human standards.[^8] I judge no one.

**16** Yet even if I do judge, my judgment is true, because I am not alone. It is I and the Father who sent me.

**17** In your own law it is written that the testimony of two people is true.[^9]

**18** I am one who testifies about myself, and the Father who sent me testifies about me."

**19** Then they said to him, "Where is your Father?" Jesus answered, "You know neither me nor my Father. If you knew me, you would know my Father too."

**20** He spoke these words in the treasury,[^10] while he was teaching in the temple. Yet no one seized him, because his hour had not yet come.

**21** Then he said to them again, "I am going away, and you will look for me, and you will die in your sin. Where I am going, you cannot come."

**22** So the Jews[^11] said, "He isn't going to kill himself, is he? Is that why he says, 'Where I am going, you cannot come'?"

**23** He said to them, "You are from below; I am from above. You are of this world; I am not of this world.

**24** That is why I told you that you will die in your sins. For unless you believe that I am he,[^12] you will die in your sins."

**25** So they said to him, "Who are you?" Jesus said to them, "Why do I speak to you at all?[^13]

**26** I have much to say about you and much to judge. But the one who sent me is true, and what I have heard from him, that is what I say to the world."

**27** They did not understand that he was speaking to them about the Father.

**28** So Jesus said, "When you have lifted up[^14] the Son of Man, then you will know that I am he,[^15] and that I do nothing on my own, but I speak just as the Father has taught me.

**29** The one who sent me is with me. He has not left me alone, because I always do what pleases him."

**30** As he was saying these things, many believed in him.

**31** Then Jesus said to the Jews who had believed him, "If you remain in my word, you are truly my disciples,

**32** and you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free."

**33** They answered him, "We are descendants[^16] of Abraham, and we have never been slaves to anyone. How can you say, 'You will become free'?"

**34** Jesus answered them, "Truly, truly, I tell you, everyone who commits sin is a slave of sin.

**35** The slave does not remain in the household forever; the son remains forever.

**36** So if the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed.

**37** I know that you are descendants of Abraham. Yet you are trying to kill me, because my word finds no place in you.

**38** I speak of what I have seen with the Father, and you, then, do what you have heard from the Father."[^17]

**39** They answered him, "Our father is Abraham." Jesus said to them, "If you are Abraham's children, you would be doing the works of Abraham.[^18]

**40** But now you are trying to kill me, a man who has told you the truth that I heard from God. Abraham did not do this.

**41** You are doing the works of your father." They said to him, "We were not born of sexual immorality.[^19] We have one Father: God."

**42** Jesus said to them, "If God were your Father, you would love me, for I came from God and now I am here. I have not come on my own; he sent me.

**43** Why do you not understand what I am saying? It is because you cannot bear to hear my word.

**44** You are from your father the devil, and you want to carry out your father's desires. He was a murderer from the beginning, and he does not stand in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he lies, he speaks from his own nature, because he is a liar and the father of lies.[^20]

**45** But because I tell the truth, you do not believe me.

**46** Which of you can prove me guilty of sin? If I tell the truth, why do you not believe me?

**47** Whoever is from God hears the words of God. The reason you do not hear them is that you are not from God."

**48** The Jews answered him, "Aren't we right in saying that you are a Samaritan and have a demon?"

**49** Jesus answered, "I do not have a demon. I honor my Father, and you dishonor me.

**50** I do not seek my own glory. There is one who seeks it, and he is the judge.

**51** Truly, truly, I tell you, if anyone keeps my word, he will never see death."

**52** The Jews said to him, "Now we know that you have a demon! Abraham died, and so did the prophets, yet you say, 'If anyone keeps my word, he will never taste death.'

**53** Are you greater than our father Abraham, who died? The prophets died too. Who do you make yourself out to be?"

**54** Jesus answered, "If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing. It is my Father who glorifies me, the one of whom you say, 'He is our God.'

**55** Yet you have not known him, but I know him. If I said that I do not know him, I would be a liar like you. But I do know him, and I keep his word.

**56** Your father Abraham rejoiced that he would see my day. He saw it and was glad."

**57** So the Jews said to him, "You are not yet fifty years old, and you have seen Abraham?"

**58** Jesus said to them, "Truly, truly, I tell you, before Abraham was, I am."[^21]

**59** So they picked up stones to throw at him, but Jesus hid himself[^22] and went out of the temple.[^23]

[^1]: The double brackets continue from 7:53: verses 1–11 are the end of the passage that the earliest manuscripts do not have (see the note on 7:53). The SBLGNT prints it in the wording of the Byzantine text (RP), as given in its apparatus.
[^2]: See Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:22–24.
[^3]: Literally "not paying attention".
[^4]: Or "Lord". Greek *kyrie*.
[^5]: Greek *krino*, "judge", which here means "condemn"; in verse 10 the verb is *katakrino*, "condemn".
[^6]: See the note on 6:35.
[^7]: That is, it is not valid as evidence; see 5:31.
[^8]: Literally "according to the flesh".
[^9]: See Deuteronomy 17:6; 19:15.
[^10]: Probably the area in the temple courts where the offering boxes stood (compare Mark 12:41).
[^11]: Greek *hoi Ioudaioi*. In this chapter the word refers to Jesus' hearers in the temple who dispute with him, among them the Pharisees (verse 13). In verse 31 it is some of them who had believed him.
[^12]: Literally "that I am". The Greek has no word completing "I am" (Greek *ego eimi*); also in verse 28, and compare verse 58. It can mean "I am he", the one I say I am. But it is also how God speaks of himself in the Greek Old Testament: "so that you may know and believe and understand that I am" (Isaiah 43:10; compare Deuteronomy 32:39 and Exodus 3:14).
[^13]: Or "What I have been telling you from the beginning." The Greek of this reply is very difficult, and it is uncertain what it means.
[^14]: See the note on 3:14.
[^15]: Literally "that I am". See the note on verse 24.
[^16]: Literally "the seed"; also in verse 37.
[^17]: Or "and you, then, should do what you have heard from the Father." Many manuscripts read "I speak of what I have seen with my Father, and you do what you have seen with your father."
[^18]: Many manuscripts read "If you were Abraham's children, you would do the works of Abraham." Some read "If you are Abraham's children, do the works of Abraham."
[^19]: Greek *porneia*. That is, "We are not illegitimate children."
[^20]: Literally "and the father of it", that is, of the lie.
[^21]: Literally "before Abraham came to be, I am". Jesus sets Abraham's coming into being (Greek *genesthai*) against his own "I am" (*ego eimi*), in the present tense. The words recall God's own "I am" (see the note on verse 24). In verse 59 the hearers try to stone him; compare 10:31–33.
[^22]: Or "was hidden".
[^23]: Many manuscripts add "passing through the middle of them, and so he went on his way."
