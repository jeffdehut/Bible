**1** In the plains of Moab by the Jordan, across from Jericho, the LORD[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Command the Israelites to give the Levites towns to live in from the inheritance they will possess. You are also to give the Levites pasturelands[^2] around the towns.

**3** The towns will be theirs to live in, and their pasturelands will be for their cattle, their goods and all their animals.

**4** The pasturelands of the towns that you give to the Levites are to extend a thousand cubits[^3] from the town wall outward, all around.

**5** Measure outside the town two thousand cubits on the east side, two thousand cubits on the south side, two thousand cubits on the west side and two thousand cubits on the north side, with the town in the middle. This will be the pastureland of their towns.[^4]

**6** "Among the towns you give to the Levites will be the six cities of refuge, which you are to provide so that anyone who has killed someone[^5] may flee there. In addition to them you are to give forty-two towns.

**7** All the towns you give to the Levites will be forty-eight towns, together with their pasturelands.

**8** The towns you give from the property of the Israelites are to be in proportion: take more from a tribe that has more, and fewer from a tribe that has fewer. Each is to give some of its towns to the Levites in proportion to the inheritance it receives."

**9** The LORD said to Moses,

**10** "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: 'When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan,

**11** select towns to be your cities of refuge, so that anyone who kills a person by mistake[^6] may flee there.

**12** The towns will be a refuge for you from the avenger,[^7] so that the killer will not die until he has stood trial before the community.

**13** The towns you give will be your six cities of refuge.

**14** Give three towns across the Jordan and three towns in the land of Canaan. They will be cities of refuge.

**15** These six towns will be a refuge for the Israelites, and for the foreigner and the temporary resident among them, so that anyone who kills a person by mistake may flee there.

**16** "'But if someone strikes a person with an iron object and he dies, he is a murderer. The murderer must be put to death.

**17** If someone strikes a person with a stone in his hand that could cause death, and he dies, he is a murderer. The murderer must be put to death.

**18** Or if someone strikes a person with a wooden object in his hand that could cause death, and he dies, he is a murderer. The murderer must be put to death.

**19** The avenger of blood himself is to put the murderer to death. When he meets him, he is to put him to death.

**20** If someone pushes another out of hatred, or throws something at him with intent,[^8] and he dies,

**21** or strikes him with his hand out of hostility, and he dies, then the one who struck him must be put to death. He is a murderer. The avenger of blood is to put the murderer to death when he meets him.

**22** "'But if someone pushes another suddenly, without hostility, or throws any object at him without intent,

**23** or, without seeing him, drops on him a stone that could cause death, and he dies, though he was not his enemy and did not seek to harm him,

**24** then the community must judge between the one who struck him and the avenger of blood according to these rules.

**25** The community must rescue the killer from the hand of the avenger of blood and return him to the city of refuge to which he fled. He must live there until the death of the high priest, who was anointed with the holy oil.

**26** But if the killer ever goes outside the boundary of the city of refuge to which he fled,

**27** and the avenger of blood finds him outside the boundary of his city of refuge, and the avenger of blood kills the killer, he is not guilty of bloodshed.[^9]

**28** For the killer must live in his city of refuge until the death of the high priest. After the death of the high priest the killer may return to the land he owns.

**29** "'These are to be legal rules[^10] for you for all your generations, wherever you live.

**30** "'If anyone kills a person, the murderer is to be put to death on the evidence of witnesses. But one witness alone may not testify against a person to have him put to death.

**31** Do not accept a ransom[^11] for the life of a murderer who is guilty and deserves to die.[^12] He must be put to death.

**32** And do not accept a ransom for anyone who has fled to his city of refuge, so that he may return and live in the land before the death of the priest.

**33** Do not defile[^13] the land where you are, for blood defiles the land, and no atonement can be made for the land for the blood that has been shed on it, except by the blood of the one who shed it.

**34** Do not make the land where you live unclean, the land where I live, for I, the LORD, live among the Israelites.'"

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *migrash*: open land around a town, used for grazing. Also in verses 3–7.
[^3]: A cubit was about 18 inches (45 cm), so a thousand cubits was about 1,500 feet (450 meters), and two thousand cubits (verse 5) about 3,000 feet (900 meters).
[^4]: How the measurements of verses 4 and 5 fit together is not explained.
[^5]: Hebrew *rotseach*, a word used both of someone who kills by accident, as here, and of a murderer (verses 16–21 and 30–31).
[^6]: Hebrew *bishgagah*, "by mistake, inadvertently": without intending to. Also in verse 15.
[^7]: Hebrew *go'el*, the nearest relative (see 5:8). Here he is the one who avenges a death; in verses 19–27, "the avenger of blood".
[^8]: Literally "with lying in wait": deliberately.
[^9]: Literally "there is no blood for him".
[^10]: Literally "a decree of judgment".
[^11]: Hebrew *kofer*: a payment in place of a life.
[^12]: Literally "who is guilty to die".
[^13]: Hebrew *chanaf*, "pollute, profane". Also later in the verse.
