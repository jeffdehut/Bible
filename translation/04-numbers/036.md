**1** The heads of the ancestral families of the clan of the descendants of Gilead son of Machir, son of Manasseh, from the clans of the descendants of Joseph, came forward. They spoke before Moses and before the leaders, the heads of the ancestral families of the Israelites.

**2** They said, "The LORD[^1] commanded my lord to give the land to the Israelites as an inheritance by lot, and my lord was commanded by the LORD to give the inheritance of our brother Zelophehad to his daughters.

**3** But if they marry men from any of the other tribes of the Israelites, their inheritance will be taken away from the inheritance of our ancestors and added to the inheritance of the tribe they marry into. So it will be taken away from the inheritance allotted to us.

**4** And when the Jubilee[^2] comes for the Israelites, their inheritance will be added to the inheritance of the tribe they marry into, and their inheritance will be taken away from the inheritance of our ancestral tribe."

**5** Then at the LORD's command Moses commanded the Israelites: "What the tribe of the descendants of Joseph says is right.

**6** This is what the LORD commands concerning the daughters of Zelophehad: They may marry anyone they please,[^3] but they must marry within a clan of their father's tribe.

**7** No inheritance of the Israelites may pass from tribe to tribe, for each of the Israelites is to keep hold of the inheritance of his ancestral tribe.

**8** Every daughter who inherits property in any of the tribes of the Israelites must marry someone from a clan of her father's tribe, so that each of the Israelites may possess the inheritance of his ancestors.

**9** No inheritance may pass from one tribe to another, for each of the tribes of the Israelites is to keep hold of its own inheritance."

**10** The daughters of Zelophehad did just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**11** Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah and Noah, the daughters of Zelophehad, married the sons of their uncles.[^4]

**12** They married into the clans of the descendants of Manasseh son of Joseph, and their inheritance remained with the tribe of their father's clan.

**13** These are the commands and the rules that the LORD gave to the Israelites through Moses[^5] in the plains of Moab by the Jordan, across from Jericho.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *yovel*: every fiftieth year, when land that had been sold returned to its original owners (Leviticus 25:8–17).
[^3]: Literally "to whoever is good in their eyes".
[^4]: Probably their father's brothers. The order of the daughters' names differs from 26:33 and 27:1.
[^5]: Literally "by the hand of Moses".
