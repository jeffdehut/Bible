**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Aaron, "You and your sons and your father's family with you will bear the guilt for offenses against the sanctuary,[^2] and you and your sons with you will bear the guilt for offenses against your priesthood.

**2** Bring with you also your relatives, the tribe of Levi, the tribe of your father, so that they may join you[^3] and serve you while you and your sons with you are in front of the tent of the testimony.[^4]

**3** They are to carry out their duties to you and their duties for the whole tent. But they must not come near the holy furnishings or the altar, or both they and you will die.

**4** They are to join you and carry out the duties of the tent of meeting, all the work of the tent. No outsider[^5] may come near you.

**5** You are to carry out the duties of the sanctuary and the duties of the altar, so that wrath will not come on the Israelites again.

**6** I myself have taken your relatives the Levites from among the Israelites. They are a gift to you, given to Yahweh, to do the work of the tent of meeting.

**7** But you and your sons with you are to carry out your priesthood in everything that concerns the altar and what is inside the veil.[^6] You are to do this service. I am giving you your priesthood as a gift.[^7] Any outsider who comes near is to be put to death."

**8** Then Yahweh spoke to Aaron: "I myself have put you in charge of the contributions[^8] made to me. All the holy gifts of the Israelites I have given to you and to your sons as your portion,[^9] as your permanent share.[^10]

**9** This will be yours from the most holy offerings, kept back from the fire:[^11] every offering of theirs, every grain offering, every sin offering[^12] and every guilt offering that they give back to me.[^13] It is most holy, for you and for your sons.

**10** You are to eat it as a most holy thing.[^14] Every male may eat it. It is to be holy to you.

**11** This also is yours: the contribution from their gifts, all the wave offerings[^15] of the Israelites. I have given them to you, and to your sons and your daughters with you, as your permanent share. Everyone in your household who is clean may eat it.

**12** All the best of the fresh olive oil and all the best of the new wine and the grain,[^16] the first portion of them that they give to Yahweh, I have given to you.

**13** The first ripe fruits of everything in their land, which they bring to Yahweh, will be yours. Everyone in your household who is clean may eat them.

**14** Everything in Israel that is devoted[^17] will be yours.

**15** Every firstborn[^18] of every living creature that is offered to Yahweh, whether human or animal, will be yours. But you must redeem[^19] the firstborn of human beings, and you must redeem the firstborn of unclean animals.

**16** As for their redemption price, you are to redeem them from the age of one month, at the fixed value:[^20] five shekels[^21] of silver, by the sanctuary shekel,[^22] which is twenty gerahs.[^23]

**17** But you must not redeem the firstborn of a cow, or the firstborn of a sheep, or the firstborn of a goat. They are holy. Splash their blood against the altar, and burn their fat as an offering by fire,[^24] a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.

**18** Their meat will be yours, just as the breast of the wave offering and the right thigh are yours.

**19** All the holy contributions that the Israelites present to Yahweh I have given to you, and to your sons and your daughters with you, as your permanent share. It is a covenant of salt[^25] forever before Yahweh, for you and for your descendants with you."

**20** Yahweh said to Aaron, "You will have no inheritance in their land, and you will have no share among them. I am your share and your inheritance among the Israelites.

**21** "To the Levites I have given every tithe[^26] in Israel as their inheritance, in return for the work they do, the work of the tent of meeting.

**22** The Israelites must never again come near the tent of meeting, or they will incur sin and die.[^27]

**23** The Levites are the ones who are to do the work of the tent of meeting, and they will bear their guilt.[^28] This is a permanent rule for all your generations. They will have no inheritance among the Israelites.

**24** For I have given to the Levites as their inheritance the tithe of the Israelites, which they present to Yahweh as a contribution. That is why I have said of them, 'They will have no inheritance among the Israelites.'"

**25** Yahweh said to Moses,

**26** "Speak to the Levites and tell them: 'When you receive from the Israelites the tithe that I have given you from them as your inheritance, you are to present a contribution from it to Yahweh, a tenth of the tithe.

**27** Your contribution will be counted for you as if it were grain from the threshing floor or the full yield of the winepress.[^29]

**28** In the same way you too are to present a contribution to Yahweh from all the tithes that you receive from the Israelites. From them you are to give Yahweh's contribution to Aaron the priest.

**29** From all the gifts you receive you are to present every contribution due to Yahweh, from all the best of it, the part of it that is holy.'[^30]

**30** "Say to them, 'When you present the best of it, it will be counted for the Levites as the produce of the threshing floor and the produce of the winepress.

**31** You may eat it anywhere, you and your households, because it is your wages in return for your work at the tent of meeting.

**32** You will not incur sin because of it once you have presented the best of it. But you must not profane the holy gifts of the Israelites, so that you will not die.'"

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "will bear the iniquity of the sanctuary": that is, be held responsible for any offense against its holiness. The same expression is used of the priesthood in the second half of the verse.
[^3]: Hebrew *yillavu*, "they will join", a play on the name *Levi* (compare Genesis 29:34). Also in verse 4.
[^4]: The tent containing the ark of the testimony, that is, the tabernacle (Exodus 25:16, 22).
[^5]: Hebrew *zar*, "stranger": here anyone who is not a Levite. In verse 7, anyone who is not a priest.
[^6]: The veil in front of the most holy place (see 4:5).
[^7]: Literally "I give your priesthood as a service of gift."
[^8]: Hebrew *terumot*, things set apart for a sacred purpose (see 5:9). Also in verses 11, 19 and 24–29.
[^9]: Hebrew *moshchah*, usually "anointing". Here it probably means "portion" (from a different root); or "because of your anointing".
[^10]: Hebrew *choq olam*, "a permanent due", the same phrase translated "a permanent rule" elsewhere. Also in verses 11 and 19.
[^11]: Literally "from the fire": the parts of the offerings that were not burned on the altar.
[^12]: Or "purification offering". See 6:11.
[^13]: Literally "that they return to me". This may refer especially to the guilt offering, which involved making restitution.
[^14]: Or "You are to eat it in the most holy place". Literally "In the holy of holies you shall eat it."
[^15]: Hebrew *tenufot*, offerings presented to Yahweh by lifting or moving them (see 6:20).
[^16]: Literally "all the fat of the fresh oil and all the fat of the new wine and grain".
[^17]: Hebrew *cherem*: something given over completely to Yahweh, which could not be bought back.
[^18]: Literally "Every opening of the womb".
[^19]: That is, buy back by paying a price. The Hebrew repeats the verb for emphasis.
[^20]: Literally "by your valuation", the fixed price for such cases (Leviticus 27:2–7).
[^21]: A shekel was about 2/5 ounce (11.5 grams), so five shekels were about 2 ounces (57 grams).
[^22]: Literally "the shekel of the holy place": the standard weight used at the sanctuary.
[^23]: A gerah was the smallest weight, a twentieth of a shekel.
[^24]: Hebrew *ishsheh*. See 15:3.
[^25]: A covenant that cannot be broken. Salt preserves, and it was used in sealing agreements (compare Leviticus 2:13; 2 Chronicles 13:5).
[^26]: A tithe is a tenth, here a tenth of the produce of the land and the herds.
[^27]: Literally "to bear sin, to die".
[^28]: Or "their iniquity": either the Levites' own guilt or the guilt of the Israelites for any offense against the tent.
[^29]: Literally "as the fullness from the wine vat".
[^30]: Literally "from all its fat, its holy part from it".
