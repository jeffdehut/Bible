**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses,[^2]

**2** "Tell Eleazar son of Aaron the priest to take the censers out of the blaze, and to scatter the coals[^3] some distance away, for the censers are holy.

**3** Take the censers of these men who sinned at the cost of their own lives,[^4] and have them hammered into thin sheets as plating for the altar. They were presented before Yahweh, and so they have become holy. Let them be a sign to the Israelites."

**4** So Eleazar the priest took the bronze censers that had been presented by those who were burned, and they were hammered out as plating for the altar,

**5** as a reminder to the Israelites that no outsider,[^5] no one who is not a descendant of Aaron, should come near to burn incense before Yahweh, so that he would not become like Korah and his followers. This was as Yahweh had told him through Moses.

**6** The next day the whole community of the Israelites grumbled against Moses and Aaron. "You have killed Yahweh's people," they said.

**7** But when the community had assembled against Moses and Aaron, they turned toward the tent of meeting, and there was the cloud covering it, and the glory of Yahweh appeared.

**8** Moses and Aaron went to the front of the tent of meeting,

**9** and Yahweh said to Moses,

**10** "Get away from this community, so that I may destroy them in an instant." And they fell face down.

**11** Moses said to Aaron, "Take the censer, put fire from the altar in it, and put incense on it. Then go quickly to the community and make atonement for them, for wrath has gone out from Yahweh. The plague has begun."

**12** Aaron took it, as Moses had said, and ran into the middle of the assembly. The plague had already begun among the people. He put on the incense and made atonement for the people.

**13** He stood between the dead and the living, and the plague was stopped.

**14** Those who died in the plague numbered 14,700, besides those who had died because of Korah.

**15** Then Aaron returned to Moses at the entrance of the tent of meeting, for the plague had been stopped.

**16** Yahweh said to Moses,[^6]

**17** "Speak to the Israelites and get a staff[^7] from them for each ancestral family, one from each of their leaders by their ancestral families, twelve staffs. Write each man's name on his staff.

**18** Write Aaron's name on the staff of Levi, for there is to be one staff for the head of each ancestral family.

**19** Place them in the tent of meeting in front of the testimony,[^8] where I meet with you.

**20** The staff of the man I choose will sprout. In this way I will rid myself of the grumbling of the Israelites, which they keep making against you."

**21** So Moses spoke to the Israelites, and all their leaders gave him a staff, one for each leader, by their ancestral families, twelve staffs. Aaron's staff was among their staffs.

**22** Moses placed the staffs before Yahweh in the tent of the testimony.

**23** The next day Moses went into the tent of the testimony, and there was Aaron's staff, the staff for the house of Levi. It had sprouted. It had put out buds, produced blossoms and borne ripe almonds.[^9]

**24** Then Moses brought out all the staffs from Yahweh's presence to all the Israelites. They looked, and each man took his own staff.

**25** Yahweh said to Moses, "Put Aaron's staff back in front of the testimony, to be kept as a sign for the rebels,[^10] so that you may put an end to their grumbling against me, and they will not die."

**26** Moses did so. He did just as Yahweh had commanded him.

**27** The Israelites said to Moses, "We are going to die! We are lost, all of us lost!

**28** Everyone who comes near, anyone who comes near to Yahweh's tabernacle, will die. Are we all going to perish?"[^11]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Verses 1–15 are 16:36–50 in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "the fire".
[^4]: Literally "these sinners against their own lives" (Hebrew *nephesh*).
[^5]: Hebrew *zar*, "stranger": here anyone who is not a priest.
[^6]: Verses 16–28 are 17:1–13 in most English Bibles.
[^7]: Hebrew *matteh*, which means both "staff" and "tribe". Also in verses 18, 20, 21 and 23–25.
[^8]: Hebrew *edut*, "testimony": the stone tablets of the covenant kept in the ark (Exodus 25:16, 22). Also in verses 22, 23 and 25.
[^9]: Hebrew *sheqedim*, "almonds", sounds like *shaqad*, "watch, be awake" (compare Jeremiah 1:11–12).
[^10]: Literally "the sons of rebellion".
[^11]: Or "Will there be no end to our dying?"
