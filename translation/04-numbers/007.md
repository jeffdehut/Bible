**1** On the day Moses finished setting up the tabernacle, he anointed it and consecrated it and all its furnishings, and the altar and all its utensils. When he had anointed them and consecrated them,

**2** the leaders of Israel, the heads of their ancestral families, made an offering. They were the leaders of the tribes, the men who had supervised the registration.[^1]

**3** They brought their offering before the LORD:[^2] six covered carts[^3] and twelve oxen, a cart for every two leaders and an ox for each one. They presented them in front of the tabernacle.

**4** The LORD said to Moses,

**5** "Accept these from them, so that they may be used for the work of the tent of meeting. Give them to the Levites, to each group as its work requires."

**6** So Moses took the carts and the oxen and gave them to the Levites.

**7** He gave two carts and four oxen to the Gershonites, as their work required,

**8** and he gave four carts and eight oxen to the Merarites, as their work required, under the supervision of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.

**9** But he gave none to the Kohathites, because they were responsible for the holy things, which they carried on their shoulders.

**10** The leaders brought offerings for the dedication[^4] of the altar on the day it was anointed. The leaders presented their offering in front of the altar.

**11** The LORD said to Moses, "Each day one leader, one leader each day, is to present his offering for the dedication of the altar."

**12** The one who presented his offering on the first day was Nahshon son of Amminadab, of the tribe of Judah.

**13** His offering was one silver plate weighing 130 shekels[^5] and one silver basin weighing 70 shekels, by the sanctuary shekel, both of them full of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering;

**14** one gold dish weighing 10 shekels,[^6] full of incense;

**15** one young bull,[^7] one ram and one year-old male lamb as a burnt offering;

**16** one male goat as a sin offering;

**17** and as the fellowship offering,[^8] two head of cattle, five rams, five male goats and five year-old male lambs. This was the offering of Nahshon son of Amminadab.

**85** Each silver plate weighed 130 shekels and each basin 70. All the silver of the vessels came to 2,400 shekels,[^9] by the sanctuary shekel.

**86** The twelve gold dishes full of incense weighed 10 shekels each, by the sanctuary shekel. All the gold of the dishes came to 120 shekels.[^10]

**87** All the cattle for the burnt offering came to twelve bulls, together with twelve rams and twelve year-old male lambs, with their grain offerings, and twelve male goats as a sin offering.

**88** All the cattle for the fellowship offering came to twenty-four bulls, together with sixty rams, sixty male goats and sixty year-old male lambs. This was the dedication offering for the altar after it was anointed.

**89** When Moses went into the tent of meeting to speak with him,[^11] he heard the voice speaking to him from above the atonement cover[^12] that was on the ark of the testimony, from between the two cherubim. And he spoke to him.

[^1]: Literally "who stood over those registered" (chapter 1).
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *tsav*, a word of uncertain meaning, perhaps "covered" or "for carrying loads".
[^4]: Hebrew *chanukkah*, "dedication"; also in verses 11, 84 and 88.
[^5]: The Hebrew gives only the number here: literally "130 its weight". A shekel was about ⅖ ounce (11.5 grams), so the plate weighed about 3¼ pounds (1.5 kilograms) and the basin about 1¾ pounds (0.8 kilogram).
[^6]: Literally "one dish of ten, gold". The dish weighed about 4 ounces (115 grams).
[^7]: Literally "a bull, a son of the herd".
[^8]: Hebrew *shelamim*, traditionally "peace offering". In these sacrifices most of the meat was eaten by the worshipers.
[^9]: About 60 pounds (28 kilograms).
[^10]: About 3 pounds (1.4 kilograms).
[^11]: That is, with the LORD.
[^12]: Hebrew *kapporet*, traditionally "mercy seat": the gold lid of the ark (Exodus 25:17–22).
