**1** The LORD[^1] spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tent of meeting,[^2] on the first day of the second month, in the second year after the Israelites came out of the land of Egypt. He said,

**2** "Take a census[^3] of the whole community of the Israelites by their clans and their ancestral families,[^4] counting the names of every male, one by one.[^5]

**3** You and Aaron are to register[^6] them by their divisions,[^7] all those in Israel who are twenty years old or more and able to serve in the army.

**4** A man from each tribe is to be with you, each of them the head of his ancestral family.

**5** These are the names of the men who are to stand with you: from Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur;

**6** from Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai;

**7** from Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab;

**8** from Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar;

**9** from Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon;

**10** from the sons of Joseph: from Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud; from Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur;

**11** from Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni;

**12** from Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai;

**13** from Asher, Pagiel son of Ochran;

**14** from Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel;

**15** from Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan."

**16** These were the men chosen from the community, the leaders of their ancestral tribes. They were the heads of the clans[^8] of Israel.

**17** Moses and Aaron took these men who had been named,

**18** and on the first day of the second month they assembled the whole community. The people recorded their descent[^9] by their clans and their ancestral families, and those twenty years old or more were counted by name, one by one,

**19** as the LORD had commanded Moses. So he registered them in the wilderness of Sinai.

**20** From the descendants of Reuben, Israel's firstborn, according to their records of descent[^10] by their clans and their ancestral families, every male twenty years old or more who was able to serve in the army was counted by name, one by one.

**21** Those registered from the tribe of Reuben numbered 46,500.

**22** From the descendants of Simeon, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, those registered were counted by name, one by one: every male twenty years old or more who was able to serve in the army.

**23** Those registered from the tribe of Simeon numbered 59,300.

**24** From the descendants of Gad, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**25** Those registered from the tribe of Gad numbered 45,650.

**26** From the descendants of Judah, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**27** Those registered from the tribe of Judah numbered 74,600.

**28** From the descendants of Issachar, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**29** Those registered from the tribe of Issachar numbered 54,400.

**30** From the descendants of Zebulun, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**31** Those registered from the tribe of Zebulun numbered 57,400.

**32** From the sons of Joseph: from the descendants of Ephraim, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**33** Those registered from the tribe of Ephraim numbered 40,500.

**34** From the descendants of Manasseh, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**35** Those registered from the tribe of Manasseh numbered 32,200.

**36** From the descendants of Benjamin, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**37** Those registered from the tribe of Benjamin numbered 35,400.

**38** From the descendants of Dan, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**39** Those registered from the tribe of Dan numbered 62,700.

**40** From the descendants of Asher, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**41** Those registered from the tribe of Asher numbered 41,500.

**42** The descendants of Naphtali: according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**43** Those registered from the tribe of Naphtali numbered 53,400.

**44** These were the men registered by Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel, twelve men, each one representing his ancestral family.

**45** All the Israelites registered by their ancestral families, those twenty years old or more, everyone in Israel who was able to serve in the army,

**46** all those registered numbered 603,550.

**47** But the Levites were not registered among them by their ancestral tribe.

**48** The LORD said to Moses,

**49** "Only the tribe of Levi you are not to register. Do not include them in the census[^11] among the Israelites.

**50** Instead, put the Levites in charge of the tabernacle of the testimony,[^12] all its furnishings and everything that belongs to it. They are to carry the tabernacle and all its furnishings. They are to serve[^13] in it, and they are to camp around the tabernacle.

**51** Whenever the tabernacle sets out, the Levites are to take it down. Whenever the tabernacle is to be pitched,[^14] the Levites are to set it up. Any outsider[^15] who comes near it is to be put to death.

**52** The Israelites are to camp by their divisions, each man in his own camp and each under his own banner.

**53** But the Levites are to camp around the tabernacle of the testimony, so that wrath will not fall on the community of the Israelites. The Levites are to take charge of[^16] the tabernacle of the testimony."

**54** The Israelites did this. They did everything just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ohel mo'ed*: the tent where the LORD met with Moses, here the tabernacle (Exodus 27:21; 40:34–35).
[^3]: Literally "Lift up the head"; also in verse 49.
[^4]: Literally "by the house of their fathers": an extended family descended from a common ancestor, a smaller unit within the clan.
[^5]: Literally "by their skulls", that is, head by head.
[^6]: Hebrew *paqad*: count, muster, enroll for service. The same verb, and the noun *pequdim*, "those registered", run through the chapter.
[^7]: Literally "by their armies". Also in verse 52.
[^8]: Literally "the thousands of Israel". Hebrew *eleph* means "thousand", but it is also used of a clan or of a military unit.
[^9]: Hebrew *yityaldu*, "they had themselves recorded by birth", that is, by their descent.
[^10]: Hebrew *toledot*, "generations": the record of a person's descendants (Genesis 2:4). Also in verses 22–42.
[^11]: Literally "do not lift up their head"; compare verse 2.
[^12]: Hebrew *edut*, "testimony": the stone tablets of the covenant kept in the ark (Exodus 25:16). The tabernacle is named after them.
[^13]: Hebrew *sharat*, "minister, attend to", used of service at the sanctuary.
[^14]: Literally "is to camp".
[^15]: Hebrew *zar*, "stranger": here anyone who is not a Levite. Also in 3:10 and 3:38.
[^16]: Literally "keep the charge of", that is, guard it and carry out its duties. Hebrew *mishmeret* recurs often in chapters 3–4.
