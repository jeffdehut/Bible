**1** Then the daughters of Zelophehad came forward. Zelophehad was the son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, of the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph. These were the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah.

**2** They stood before Moses, Eleazar the priest, the leaders and the whole community at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and said,

**3** "Our father died in the wilderness. He was not among the group that gathered together against Yahweh[^1] in Korah's company. He died because of his own sin,[^2] and he had no sons.

**4** Why should our father's name disappear from his clan because he had no son? Give us property among our father's brothers."

**5** So Moses brought their case before Yahweh.

**6** Yahweh said to Moses,

**7** "The daughters of Zelophehad are right in what they say. You must give them property as an inheritance among their father's brothers, and pass on their father's inheritance to them.

**8** And tell the Israelites: 'If a man dies and has no son, you are to pass on his inheritance to his daughter.

**9** If he has no daughter, you are to give his inheritance to his brothers.

**10** If he has no brothers, you are to give his inheritance to his father's brothers.

**11** If his father has no brothers, you are to give his inheritance to the nearest relative in his clan, and he is to take possession of it.' This is to be a rule of law[^3] for the Israelites, as Yahweh commanded Moses."

**12** Yahweh said to Moses, "Go up this mountain in the Abarim range[^4] and look at the land that I have given to the Israelites.

**13** When you have seen it, you too will be gathered to your people,[^5] as Aaron your brother was gathered.

**14** For in the wilderness of Zin, when the community quarreled, you both rebelled against my command[^6] to show my holiness at the waters before their eyes." These were the waters of Meribah at Kadesh, in the wilderness of Zin.

**15** Moses spoke to Yahweh, saying,

**16** "May Yahweh, the God of the spirits of all living beings,[^7] appoint a man over the community

**17** who will go out before them and come in before them, who will lead them out and bring them in,[^8] so that Yahweh's community will not be like sheep that have no shepherd."

**18** Yahweh said to Moses, "Take Joshua son of Nun, a man in whom is the Spirit,[^9] and lay your hand on him.

**19** Have him stand before Eleazar the priest and before the whole community, and commission him in their sight.

**20** Give him some of your authority,[^10] so that the whole community of the Israelites will obey him.

**21** He is to stand before Eleazar the priest, who will ask for him the decision of the Urim[^11] before Yahweh. At his word[^12] they will go out, and at his word they will come in, he and all the Israelites with him, the whole community."

**22** Moses did as Yahweh commanded him. He took Joshua and had him stand before Eleazar the priest and before the whole community.

**23** He laid his hands on him and commissioned him, as Yahweh had said through Moses.[^13]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "in his own sin he died": he died for his own sin, as one of the generation that died in the wilderness (14:29–35), and not for joining Korah's rebellion (chapter 16).
[^3]: Literally "a statute of judgment".
[^4]: Literally "this mountain of the Abarim". *Abarim* means "regions beyond" or "crossings": the mountains east of the Jordan. According to Deuteronomy 32:49 the mountain is Mount Nebo.
[^5]: That is, die, joining your ancestors who had died before you.
[^6]: Literally "my mouth".
[^7]: Literally "of all flesh".
[^8]: An expression for leading the people in all their affairs, especially in war.
[^9]: Or "a man who has the spirit". The Hebrew does not say whether this is God's Spirit or Joshua's own spirit.
[^10]: Hebrew *hod*, "splendor, majesty".
[^11]: Hebrew *urim*, objects kept in the high priest's breastpiece and used to learn Yahweh's decision (Exodus 28:30; 1 Samuel 28:6). Literally "the judgment of the Urim".
[^12]: Literally "at his mouth": the word given through Eleazar, or Eleazar's own command. Also later in the verse.
[^13]: Literally "by the hand of Moses".
