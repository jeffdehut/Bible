**1** Korah son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi, took action,[^1] together with Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, and On son of Peleth, who were descendants of Reuben.

**2** They rose up against Moses, along with 250 Israelite men, leaders of the community, chosen from the assembly,[^2] men of renown.

**3** They assembled against Moses and Aaron and said to them, "You have gone too far![^3] The whole community is holy, every one of them, and the LORD[^4] is among them. So why do you set yourselves above the assembly of the LORD?"

**4** When Moses heard this, he fell face down.

**5** Then he said to Korah and all his followers,[^5] "In the morning the LORD will make known who belongs to him and who is holy, and he will let that one come near to him. The one he chooses he will let come near to him.

**6** Do this, Korah and all your followers: take censers.[^6]

**7** Tomorrow put fire in them and put incense on them before the LORD. The man the LORD chooses will be the one who is holy. You have gone too far, you Levites!"

**8** Moses said to Korah, "Listen, you Levites!

**9** Is it not enough for you that the God of Israel has set you apart from the community of Israel to bring you near to himself, to do the work of the LORD's tabernacle, and to stand before the community and serve them?

**10** He has brought you near, and all your fellow Levites with you, and now you are seeking the priesthood as well?

**11** So it is against the LORD that you and all your followers have banded together. As for Aaron, who is he, that you grumble against him?"

**12** Then Moses sent for Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, but they said, "We will not come up!

**13** Is it not enough that you have brought us up out of a land flowing with milk and honey to kill us in the wilderness? Must you also make yourself a prince over us?

**14** What is more, you have not brought us into a land flowing with milk and honey, or given us an inheritance of fields and vineyards. Will you gouge out the eyes of these men?[^7] We will not come up!"

**15** Moses became very angry and said to the LORD, "Do not accept their offering.[^8] I have not taken a single donkey from them, and I have not harmed any one of them."

**16** Moses said to Korah, "You and all your followers are to appear before the LORD tomorrow, you and they and Aaron.

**17** Each of you is to take his censer and put incense on it, and each of you is to present his censer before the LORD, 250 censers. You and Aaron are also to bring your censers."

**18** So each man took his censer, put fire in it and placed incense on it, and they stood at the entrance of the tent of meeting with Moses and Aaron.

**19** When Korah had gathered the whole community against them at the entrance of the tent of meeting, the glory of the LORD appeared to the whole community.

**20** The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,

**21** "Separate yourselves from this community, so that I may destroy them in an instant."

**22** But they fell face down and said, "O God, the God of the spirits of all flesh,[^9] when one man sins, will you be angry with the whole community?"

**23** The LORD said to Moses,

**24** "Tell the community: 'Move away from around the dwelling[^10] of Korah, Dathan and Abiram.'"

**25** Then Moses got up and went to Dathan and Abiram, and the elders of Israel followed him.

**26** He said to the community, "Move away from the tents of these wicked men, and do not touch anything that belongs to them, or you will be swept away because of all their sins."

**27** So they moved away from around the dwelling of Korah, Dathan and Abiram. Dathan and Abiram had come out and were standing at the entrance of their tents with their wives, their children and their little ones.

**28** Moses said, "This is how you will know that the LORD has sent me to do all these things, and that it was not my own idea.[^11]

**29** If these men die a natural death, as all people do, and if they suffer the fate of all people,[^12] then the LORD has not sent me.

**30** But if the LORD brings about something new,[^13] and the ground opens its mouth and swallows them and everything that belongs to them, and they go down alive into Sheol,[^14] then you will know that these men have treated the LORD with contempt."

**31** As soon as he had finished saying all these words, the ground under them split open.

**32** The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them and their households,[^15] and all the people who belonged to Korah, and all their possessions.

**33** They and everything that belonged to them went down alive into Sheol. The earth closed over them, and they vanished from among the assembly.

**34** All the Israelites who were around them fled at their cry, for they said, "The earth may swallow us too!"

**35** And fire came out from the LORD and consumed the 250 men who were offering the incense.[^16]

[^1]: Literally "took", with no object. It may mean "took men", or "took it upon himself".
[^2]: Literally "called ones of the meeting".
[^3]: Literally "Enough for you!"; also in verse 7.
[^4]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^5]: Literally "his community" (Hebrew *edah*); also in verses 6, 11 and 16.
[^6]: Hebrew *machtot*: pans for carrying burning coals, the same word translated "firepans" in 4:14.
[^7]: Probably meaning "Do you think you can blind these men to what you are doing?" Literally "Will you bore out the eyes of those men?"
[^8]: Literally "Do not turn to their offering". Hebrew *minchah*, here an offering of any kind.
[^9]: That is, the God who gives life and breath to every living creature. Hebrew *ruchot*, plural of *ruach*, "spirit, breath".
[^10]: Hebrew *mishkan*, the word used elsewhere for the tabernacle. Also in verse 27.
[^11]: Literally "not from my heart".
[^12]: Literally "if the visitation of all humankind is visited on them".
[^13]: Literally "creates a creation". Hebrew *bara*, "create", is the verb used in Genesis 1:1.
[^14]: Hebrew *Sheol*: the realm of the dead. Also in verse 33.
[^15]: Literally "their houses".
[^16]: In most English Bibles chapter 16 continues with verses 36–50, which are 17:1–15 in the Hebrew text.
