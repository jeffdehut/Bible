**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Command the Israelites and say to them: 'When you enter the land of Canaan, this is the land that will fall to you as an inheritance: the land of Canaan with its borders.

**3** Your southern side will extend from the wilderness of Zin along the side of Edom. Your southern border will begin in the east at the end of the Salt Sea.[^2]

**4** Your border will turn south of the Ascent of Akrabbim[^3] and pass on to Zin, and its far end will be south of Kadesh-barnea. Then it will go out to Hazar-addar and pass on to Azmon.

**5** From Azmon the border will turn to the Wadi of Egypt,[^4] and its far end will be at the sea.

**6** "'As for the western border, the Great Sea[^5] and its coast will be your border. This will be your western border.

**7** "'This will be your northern border: from the Great Sea mark out your line[^6] to Mount Hor.[^7]

**8** From Mount Hor mark it out to Lebo-hamath,[^8] and the far end of the border will be at Zedad.

**9** Then the border will go out to Ziphron, and its far end will be at Hazar-enan. This will be your northern border.

**10** "'For your eastern border, mark out your line from Hazar-enan to Shepham.

**11** The border will go down from Shepham to Riblah, on the east side of Ain.[^9] Then the border will go down and reach the slope[^10] east of the Sea of Kinnereth.[^11]

**12** Then the border will go down along the Jordan, and its far end will be at the Salt Sea. This will be your land, with its borders all around.'"

**13** Moses commanded the Israelites: "This is the land that you are to receive as your inheritance by lot, which the LORD has commanded to be given to the nine and a half tribes.

**14** For the tribe of the Reubenites by their ancestral families and the tribe of the Gadites by their ancestral families have received their inheritance, and the half-tribe of Manasseh has received its inheritance.

**15** The two and a half tribes have received their inheritance across the Jordan from Jericho, to the east, toward the sunrise."

**16** The LORD said to Moses,

**17** "These are the names of the men who are to divide the land among you as an inheritance: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun.

**18** Also take one leader from each tribe to divide the land as an inheritance.

**19** These are the names of the men: from the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;

**20** from the tribe of the Simeonites, Shemuel son of Ammihud;

**21** from the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon;

**22** from the tribe of the Danites, a leader, Bukki son of Jogli;

**23** from the sons of Joseph: from the tribe of the Manassites, a leader, Hanniel son of Ephod;

**24** and from the tribe of the Ephraimites, a leader, Kemuel son of Shiphtan;

**25** from the tribe of the Zebulunites, a leader, Elizaphan son of Parnach;

**26** from the tribe of the Issacharites, a leader, Paltiel son of Azzan;

**27** from the tribe of the Asherites, a leader, Ahihud son of Shelomi;

**28** from the tribe of the Naphtalites, a leader, Pedahel son of Ammihud."

**29** These are the men whom the LORD commanded to divide the inheritance among the Israelites in the land of Canaan.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: The Dead Sea. Also in verse 12.
[^3]: The name means "ascent of the scorpions": a pass southwest of the Dead Sea.
[^4]: Hebrew *nachal mitsrayim*, a seasonal stream on the border of Egypt, probably the Wadi el-Arish in northern Sinai.
[^5]: The Mediterranean Sea. Also in verse 7.
[^6]: The meaning of the Hebrew verb *ta'u* is uncertain; perhaps "draw a line". Also in verses 8 and 10.
[^7]: Not the Mount Hor where Aaron died (20:22–29), but a mountain in the far north, probably in the Lebanon range.
[^8]: Or "to the entrance of Hamath".
[^9]: Or "the spring".
[^10]: Literally "the shoulder".
[^11]: The Sea of Galilee.
