**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Command the Israelites to send out of the camp everyone with a skin disease,[^2] everyone who has a discharge, and everyone who is unclean through contact with a dead body.[^3]

**3** Send out both male and female. Send them outside the camp, so that they do not make their camp unclean, where I live among them."

**4** The Israelites did so and sent them outside the camp. The Israelites did just as Yahweh had told Moses.

**5** Yahweh said to Moses,

**6** "Tell the Israelites: When a man or a woman commits any of the sins that people commit,[^4] and so is unfaithful to Yahweh, that person is guilty.

**7** They are to confess the sin they have committed. The guilty person is to repay in full what is owed for the wrong,[^5] add a fifth to it, and give it to the one who was wronged.

**8** But if that person has no close relative[^6] to whom the repayment can be made, the repayment for the wrong belongs to Yahweh, for the priest. This is in addition to the ram of atonement with which atonement is made for the guilty person.

**9** Every contribution[^7] among all the holy gifts that the Israelites bring to the priest belongs to him.

**10** The holy gifts of each person are his own, but what anyone gives to the priest belongs to the priest."[^8]

**11** Yahweh said to Moses,

**12** "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: Suppose a man's wife goes astray and is unfaithful to him,

**13** and another man has sexual relations with her, but it is hidden from her husband and she remains undetected, though she has made herself unclean. There is no witness against her, and she was not caught in the act.

**14** And suppose a spirit of jealousy[^9] comes over him and he is jealous of his wife, and she has made herself unclean; or a spirit of jealousy comes over him and he is jealous of his wife, though she has not made herself unclean.

**15** Then the man is to bring his wife to the priest. He is to bring the offering required for her: a tenth of an ephah[^10] of barley flour. He must not pour oil on it or put frankincense on it, because it is a grain offering for jealousy, a grain offering of remembrance, which brings wrongdoing to remembrance.

**16** "The priest is to bring her near and have her stand before Yahweh.

**17** The priest is to take holy water in a clay jar, and take some of the dust from the floor of the tabernacle and put it in the water.

**18** When the priest has had the woman stand before Yahweh, he is to loosen the hair of her head[^11] and put in her hands the grain offering of remembrance, which is the grain offering for jealousy. The priest himself is to hold the bitter water that brings the curse.[^12]

**19** Then the priest is to put her under oath and say to the woman, 'If no man has had sexual relations with you, and if you have not gone astray into uncleanness while under your husband's authority,[^13] may you be free[^14] from this bitter water that brings the curse.

**20** But if you have gone astray while under your husband's authority, and if you have made yourself unclean, and a man other than your husband has had sexual relations with you,'

**21** then the priest is to put the woman under the oath of the curse, and the priest is to say to the woman, 'May Yahweh make you a curse and an oath[^15] among your people, by making your thigh fall away[^16] and your belly swell.

**22** May this water that brings the curse enter your body[^17] and make your belly swell and your thigh fall away.' And the woman is to say, 'Amen, amen.'[^18]

**23** "The priest is to write these curses on a scroll and wash them off into the bitter water.

**24** He is to make the woman drink the bitter water that brings the curse, and the water that brings the curse will enter her and cause bitter pain.[^19]

**25** The priest is to take the grain offering for jealousy from the woman's hand, wave the grain offering before Yahweh,[^20] and bring it to the altar.

**26** The priest is to take a handful of the grain offering as its memorial portion[^21] and burn it on the altar. After that he is to make the woman drink the water.

**27** When he has made her drink the water, then if she has made herself unclean and has been unfaithful to her husband, the water that brings the curse will enter her and cause bitter pain. Her belly will swell and her thigh will fall away, and the woman will become a curse among her people.

**28** But if the woman has not made herself unclean, and she is clean, she will be free and will be able to conceive children.[^22]

**29** "This is the law[^23] for cases of jealousy, when a wife goes astray while under her husband's authority and makes herself unclean,

**30** or when a spirit of jealousy comes over a man and he is jealous of his wife. He is to have the woman stand before Yahweh, and the priest is to carry out for her this whole law.

**31** The man will be free from guilt, but that woman will bear the consequences of her guilt."[^24]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *tsarua*, traditionally "leper": someone with one of the skin diseases described in Leviticus 13.
[^3]: Literally "unclean for a *nephesh*". Here *nephesh*, "person", means a dead person (Leviticus 21:1; Numbers 6:6).
[^4]: Or "any sin against another person". Literally "any of the sins of humankind".
[^5]: Literally "return his guilt in its head". Hebrew *asham*, "guilt", here means the compensation owed for the wrong; also in verse 8.
[^6]: Hebrew *go'el*: the nearest relative, who had the right and duty to act on behalf of a family member.
[^7]: Hebrew *terumah*: something set apart and given for a sacred purpose (Exodus 25:2).
[^8]: Literally "A man, his holy things will be his. A man who gives to the priest, it will be his." The first "his" may also refer to the priest.
[^9]: Hebrew *ruach*, "spirit, wind": here a mood or feeling of jealousy that takes hold of him. Also in verse 30.
[^10]: About 2 quarts (2.2 liters).
[^11]: Or "uncover her head".
[^12]: Or "the water of bitterness that brings the curse". The exact sense of *marim*, "bitterness", here is debated. Also in verses 19, 23, 24 and 27.
[^13]: Literally "under your husband"; also in verses 20 and 29.
[^14]: Hebrew *hinnaqi*, "be clean, be innocent": be free from its effects. Also in verse 28.
[^15]: That is, may your name be used by others in curses and oaths.
[^16]: "Thigh" may be a polite way of referring to the reproductive organs. The exact meaning of "your thigh fall away" (perhaps miscarriage or the loss of the ability to bear children; compare verse 28) is uncertain. Also in verses 22 and 27.
[^17]: Literally "your inner parts".
[^18]: *Amen* means "So be it", "It is true".
[^19]: Literally "for bitterness"; also in verse 27.
[^20]: Hebrew *henif*, traditionally "wave": to lift or move an offering before Yahweh as a sign that it is presented to him.
[^21]: Hebrew *azkarah*, "reminder": the token portion of a grain offering that was burned on the altar (Leviticus 2:2).
[^22]: Literally "she will be sown with seed".
[^23]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law"; also in verse 30.
[^24]: Literally "will bear her iniquity".
