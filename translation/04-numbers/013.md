**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Send men to explore the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the Israelites. Send one man from each ancestral tribe, each of them a leader among them."

**3** So at the LORD's command[^2] Moses sent them from the wilderness of Paran. All of them were men who were heads of the Israelites.

**4** These were their names: from the tribe of Reuben, Shammua son of Zaccur;

**5** from the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat son of Hori;

**6** from the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;

**7** from the tribe of Issachar, Igal son of Joseph;

**8** from the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Nun;

**9** from the tribe of Benjamin, Palti son of Raphu;

**10** from the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel son of Sodi;

**11** from the tribe of Joseph, that is, the tribe of Manasseh, Gaddi son of Susi;

**12** from the tribe of Dan, Ammiel son of Gemalli;

**13** from the tribe of Asher, Sethur son of Michael;

**14** from the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi son of Vophsi;

**15** from the tribe of Gad, Geuel son of Machi.

**16** These were the names of the men Moses sent to explore the land. Moses gave Hoshea son of Nun the name Joshua.[^3]

**17** When Moses sent them to explore the land of Canaan, he said to them, "Go up through the Negev,[^4] and go up into the hill country.

**18** See what the land is like, and whether the people who live in it are strong or weak, few or many;

**19** whether the land they live in is good or bad; and whether the towns they live in are open camps or fortified.

**20** See whether the land is rich or poor,[^5] and whether there are trees in it or not. Be brave, and bring back some of the fruit of the land." It was the season of the first ripe grapes.

**21** So they went up and explored the land from the wilderness of Zin as far as Rehob, toward Lebo-hamath.[^6]

**22** They went up through the Negev and came[^7] to Hebron, where Ahiman, Sheshai and Talmai, the descendants of Anak,[^8] lived. Hebron had been built seven years before Zoan in Egypt.

**23** They came to the Valley of Eshcol,[^9] and there they cut off a branch with a single cluster of grapes. Two of them carried it on a pole, along with some pomegranates and figs.

**24** That place was called the Valley of Eshcol because of the cluster that the Israelites cut off there.

**25** At the end of forty days they returned from exploring the land.

**26** They came back to Moses and Aaron and the whole community of the Israelites in the wilderness of Paran, at Kadesh. They brought back a report to them and to the whole community, and showed them the fruit of the land.

**27** They told Moses,[^10] "We went into the land where you sent us. It does indeed flow with milk and honey,[^11] and this is its fruit.

**28** But the people who live in the land are strong, and the towns are fortified and very large. We even saw the descendants of Anak there.

**29** The Amalekites live in the land of the Negev. The Hittites, the Jebusites and the Amorites live in the hill country, and the Canaanites live by the sea and along the Jordan."

**30** Then Caleb quieted the people before Moses and said, "Let us go up at once and take possession of it, for we can certainly overcome it."

**31** But the men who had gone up with him said, "We cannot go up against those people, for they are stronger than we are."

**32** So they spread among the Israelites a bad report about the land they had explored. They said, "The land we passed through to explore is a land that devours those who live in it. All the people we saw in it are men of great size.

**33** We saw the Nephilim[^12] there, the descendants of Anak, who come from the Nephilim. In our own eyes we seemed like grasshoppers, and that is how we seemed to them."

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "at the mouth of the LORD".
[^3]: *Hoshea* means "salvation". *Joshua* (Hebrew *Yehoshua*) means "The LORD saves".
[^4]: The dry region in the south of Canaan; also in verses 22 and 29.
[^5]: Literally "fat or lean".
[^6]: Or "toward the entrance of Hamath".
[^7]: Literally "he came".
[^8]: Hebrew *ha-anaq*. The Anakim were remembered as a people of great height (Deuteronomy 2:10–11). Also in verses 28 and 33.
[^9]: *Eshcol* means "cluster". Hebrew *nachal*, "valley", is a stream bed that is dry for much of the year.
[^10]: Literally "him".
[^11]: A picture of a rich land, good for herds and crops. *Honey* may include a syrup made from dates.
[^12]: Hebrew *nephilim*, perhaps "fallen ones": the giants of ancient times (Genesis 6:4).
