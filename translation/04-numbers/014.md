**1** Then the whole community raised a loud cry,[^1] and the people wept that night.

**2** All the Israelites grumbled against Moses and Aaron, and the whole community said to them, "If only we had died in the land of Egypt! Or if only we had died in this wilderness!

**3** Why is the LORD[^2] bringing us into this land, only for us to fall by the sword? Our wives and our little ones will be taken as plunder. Wouldn't it be better for us to go back to Egypt?"

**4** And they said to one another, "Let us choose a leader[^3] and go back to Egypt."

**5** Then Moses and Aaron fell face down in front of the whole assembly of the community of the Israelites.

**6** Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh, who were among those who had explored the land, tore their clothes

**7** and said to the whole community of the Israelites, "The land we passed through to explore is an exceedingly good land.

**8** If the LORD is pleased with us, he will bring us into this land and give it to us, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**9** Only do not rebel against the LORD, and do not be afraid of the people of the land, for we will devour them.[^4] Their protection[^5] has left them, and the LORD is with us. Do not be afraid of them."

**10** But the whole community talked of stoning them. Then the glory of the LORD appeared at the tent of meeting to all the Israelites.

**11** The LORD said to Moses, "How long will this people treat me with contempt? How long will they refuse to trust me,[^6] in spite of all the signs I have done among them?

**12** I will strike them with a plague and disinherit them, and I will make you into a nation greater and mightier than they are."

**13** But Moses said to the LORD, "Then the Egyptians will hear of it, for by your power you brought this people up from among them.[^7]

**14** They will tell the people who live in this land. They have heard that you, LORD, are among this people, that you, LORD, are seen face to face,[^8] that your cloud stands over them, and that you go before them in a pillar of cloud by day and in a pillar of fire by night.

**15** If you put this people to death all at once,[^9] the nations who have heard reports about you will say,

**16** 'The LORD was not able to bring this people into the land he swore to give them, so he slaughtered them in the wilderness.'

**17** "So now, please let the power of the Lord be great, just as you have said:

**18** 'The LORD is slow to anger and abounding in faithful love,[^10] forgiving wrongdoing and rebellion. Yet he will by no means leave the guilty unpunished.[^11] He punishes the children for the wrongdoing of their fathers, to the third and the fourth generation.'[^12]

**19** Please forgive the wrongdoing of this people, in keeping with the greatness of your faithful love, just as you have forgiven[^13] this people from Egypt until now."

**20** The LORD said, "I have forgiven them, as you asked.

**21** But as surely as I live, and as surely as the whole earth will be filled with the glory of the LORD,

**22** not one of the men who have seen my glory and the signs I did in Egypt and in the wilderness, and yet have tested me these ten times and have not listened to my voice,

**23** will see the land I swore to give their ancestors.[^14] None of those who treated me with contempt will see it.

**24** But because my servant Caleb has a different spirit and has followed me wholeheartedly,[^15] I will bring him into the land he went to, and his descendants will possess it.

**25** Now the Amalekites and the Canaanites live in the valley. Tomorrow turn back and set out for the wilderness by the road to the Red Sea."[^16]

**26** The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,

**27** "How long must I put up with this wicked community that keeps grumbling against me? I have heard the grumbling of the Israelites, which they keep making against me.

**28** Say to them: 'As surely as I live, declares the LORD, I will do to you exactly what I heard you say.[^17]

**29** Your dead bodies will fall in this wilderness, all of you who were registered, by your full number, those twenty years old or more who have grumbled against me.

**30** Not one of you will enter the land where I swore[^18] to settle you, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.

**31** But your little ones, who you said would be taken as plunder, I will bring in, and they will come to know the land you have rejected.

**32** But as for you, your dead bodies will fall in this wilderness.

**33** Your children will be shepherds in the wilderness for forty years. They will suffer for your unfaithfulness[^19] until the last of your dead bodies lies in the wilderness.

**34** You explored the land for forty days. For forty years, a year for each day, you will bear the consequences of your wrongdoing, and you will know what it is to have me against you.[^20]

**35** I, the LORD, have spoken. I will certainly do this to this whole wicked community that has banded together against me. In this wilderness they will come to an end, and there they will die.'"

**36** The men Moses had sent to explore the land, who returned and made the whole community grumble[^21] against him by spreading a bad report about the land,

**37** those men who spread the bad report about the land died of a plague before the LORD.

**38** Of the men who went to explore the land, only Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh remained alive.

**39** When Moses told these words to all the Israelites, the people mourned bitterly.

**40** Early the next morning they set out to go up to the heights of the hill country.[^22] They said, "Here we are. We will go up to the place the LORD spoke of, for we have sinned."

**41** But Moses said, "Why are you disobeying the LORD's command?[^23] It will not succeed.

**42** Do not go up, for the LORD is not with you.[^24] Do not let yourselves be struck down by your enemies.

**43** For the Amalekites and the Canaanites are there ahead of you, and you will fall by the sword. Because you have turned back from following the LORD, the LORD will not be with you."

**44** But they defiantly[^25] went up to the heights of the hill country, though neither the ark of the covenant of the LORD nor Moses left the camp.

**45** Then the Amalekites and the Canaanites who lived in that hill country came down and struck them and beat them down as far as Hormah.[^26]

[^1]: Literally "lifted up and gave their voice".
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "a head".
[^4]: Literally "they are our bread".
[^5]: Literally "their shade".
[^6]: Or "believe in me".
[^7]: The Hebrew of verses 13–14 is difficult, and the connection between the clauses is uncertain.
[^8]: Literally "eye to eye".
[^9]: Literally "like one man".
[^10]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Also in verse 19.
[^11]: Literally "clearing he will not clear".
[^12]: Exodus 34:6–7.
[^13]: Literally "carried", "borne with".
[^14]: Literally "If they see the land …", a form of oath meaning "they will certainly not see".
[^15]: Literally "has filled after me".
[^16]: Hebrew *Yam Suf*, literally "Sea of Reeds", traditionally the Red Sea. Here it probably means the Gulf of Aqaba.
[^17]: Literally "just as you have spoken in my ears".
[^18]: Literally "lifted up my hand", a gesture made when swearing an oath.
[^19]: Literally "bear your prostitution", an image often used in the Bible for unfaithfulness to the LORD.
[^20]: Hebrew *tenu'ati*, a rare word: "my opposition", "my displeasure".
[^21]: The Hebrew text is written "who returned and grumbled against him, all the community" but read "who returned and made the whole community grumble against him".
[^22]: Or "the top of the mountain"; also in verse 44.
[^23]: Literally "the mouth of the LORD".
[^24]: Literally "is not among you".
[^25]: Hebrew *ya'pilu*, a rare verb: "they acted presumptuously", "they dared".
[^26]: *Hormah* sounds like Hebrew *cherem*, "destruction" (21:3).
