**1** The Israelites, the whole community, came into the wilderness of Zin in the first month,[^1] and the people stayed at Kadesh. Miriam died there and was buried there.

**2** There was no water for the community, and they gathered together against Moses and Aaron.

**3** The people quarreled with Moses. They said, "If only we had died when our brothers died before Yahweh![^2]

**4** Why have you brought Yahweh's assembly into this wilderness, for us and our livestock to die here?

**5** Why did you bring us up out of Egypt, to bring us to this terrible place? It is no place for grain,[^3] or figs, or vines, or pomegranates, and there is no water to drink!"

**6** Moses and Aaron went away from the assembly to the entrance of the tent of meeting and fell face down. Then the glory of Yahweh appeared to them.

**7** Yahweh said to Moses,

**8** "Take the staff, and gather the community together, you and Aaron your brother. Speak to the rock while they watch, and it will give out its water. So you will bring water out of the rock for them, and give the community and their livestock water to drink."

**9** So Moses took the staff from before Yahweh, as he had commanded him.

**10** Moses and Aaron gathered the assembly together in front of the rock, and Moses said to them, "Listen, you rebels! Are we to bring water out of this rock for you?"[^4]

**11** Then Moses raised his hand and struck the rock twice with his staff. Water came out in abundance, and the community and their livestock drank.

**12** But Yahweh said to Moses and Aaron, "Because you did not trust me enough to show my holiness[^5] in the sight of the Israelites, you will not bring this assembly into the land that I have given them."

**13** These were the waters of Meribah,[^6] where the Israelites quarreled with Yahweh, and he showed his holiness among them.[^7]

**14** Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom: "This is what your brother Israel says: You know all the hardship that has come upon us.

**15** Our ancestors went down to Egypt, and we lived in Egypt for a long time. The Egyptians treated us and our ancestors badly.

**16** We cried out to Yahweh, and he heard our voice and sent an angel[^8] and brought us out of Egypt. Now here we are at Kadesh, a town on the edge of your territory.

**17** Please let us pass through your land. We will not go through any field or vineyard, and we will not drink water from any well. We will keep to the King's Highway.[^9] We will not turn aside to the right or to the left until we have passed through your territory."

**18** But Edom answered him, "You will not pass through my land,[^10] or I will come out against you with the sword."

**19** The Israelites said to him, "We will go up by the main road. If we drink any of your water, I and my livestock, I will pay for it. Let me only pass through on foot. That is all."[^11]

**20** But Edom said, "You will not pass through." And Edom came out against them with a large army and a strong force.[^12]

**21** So Edom refused to let Israel pass through its territory, and Israel turned away from it.

**22** They set out from Kadesh, and the Israelites, the whole community, came to Mount Hor.

**23** At Mount Hor, on the border of the land of Edom, Yahweh said to Moses and Aaron,

**24** "Aaron is to be gathered to his people.[^13] He will not enter the land that I have given to the Israelites, because you both rebelled against my command[^14] at the waters of Meribah.

**25** Take Aaron and Eleazar his son, and bring them up Mount Hor.

**26** Take Aaron's garments off him and put them on Eleazar his son. Aaron will be gathered to his people and will die there."

**27** Moses did as Yahweh had commanded. They went up Mount Hor in the sight of the whole community.

**28** Moses took Aaron's garments off him and put them on Eleazar his son, and Aaron died there on the top of the mountain. Then Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain.

**29** When the whole community saw that Aaron had died, all the house of Israel mourned for Aaron thirty days.[^15]

[^1]: The year is not stated. Aaron's death later in this chapter is dated to the fortieth year after the exodus (33:38).
[^2]: See 16:31–35 and 17:14 (16:49 in most English Bibles). *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "seed".
[^4]: Or "Can we bring water out of this rock for you?"
[^5]: Literally "to sanctify me", that is, to treat me as holy.
[^6]: *Meribah* means "quarreling". Compare Exodus 17:7.
[^7]: Hebrew *vayyiqqadesh*, "he showed himself holy", which sounds like the name *Kadesh*.
[^8]: Or "a messenger". Hebrew *mal'akh* means both.
[^9]: Literally "the way of the king": the main road running north and south through the highlands east of the Jordan and the Dead Sea.
[^10]: Literally "You will not pass through me".
[^11]: Literally "Only there is no matter. On my feet let me pass through."
[^12]: Literally "with a heavy people and with a strong hand".
[^13]: That is, die, joining his ancestors who had died before him. Also in verse 26.
[^14]: Literally "my mouth".
[^15]: Literally "wept for Aaron thirty days".
