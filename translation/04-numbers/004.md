**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses and Aaron,

**2** "Take a census of the Kohathites from among the Levites, by their clans and their ancestral families,

**3** all those from thirty to fifty years old who enter the service[^2] to do work at the tent of meeting.

**4** This is the work of the Kohathites at the tent of meeting: the most holy things.[^3]

**5** When the camp is to set out, Aaron and his sons are to go in and take down the screening veil[^4] and cover the ark of the testimony with it.

**6** Then they are to put a covering of fine leather[^5] over it, spread a cloth made entirely of blue[^6] on top, and put its poles in place.[^7]

**7** Over the table of the Presence[^8] they are to spread a blue cloth and put on it the plates, the dishes, the bowls and the jars for drink offerings. The regular bread[^9] is to remain on it.

**8** They are to spread a scarlet cloth over them, cover it with a covering of fine leather, and put its poles in place.

**9** They are to take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand for the light, with its lamps, its tongs, its trays, and all the jars for its oil that are used in tending it.

**10** They are to put it and all its utensils in a covering of fine leather and put it on a carrying frame.[^10]

**11** Over the gold altar[^11] they are to spread a blue cloth, cover it with a covering of fine leather, and put its poles in place.

**12** They are to take all the utensils used for serving in the sanctuary, put them in a blue cloth, cover them with a covering of fine leather, and put them on a carrying frame.

**13** They are to clear the ashes from the altar[^12] and spread a purple cloth over it.

**14** They are to put on it all the utensils used in serving at it: the firepans, the forks, the shovels and the basins, all the utensils of the altar. They are to spread a covering of fine leather over it and put its poles in place.[^13]

**15** "When Aaron and his sons have finished covering the holy things and all the holy utensils, as the camp is about to set out, the Kohathites are to come and carry them. But they must not touch the holy things, or they will die. These are the things in the tent of meeting that the Kohathites are to carry.

**16** "Eleazar son of Aaron the priest is to have charge of the oil for the light, the fragrant incense, the regular grain offering[^14] and the anointing oil. He is to have charge of the whole tabernacle and everything in it, the sanctuary and its furnishings."

**17** The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,

**18** "Do not let the tribe of the clans of the Kohathites be cut off from among the Levites.

**19** Do this for them, so that they will live and not die when they come near the most holy things: Aaron and his sons are to go in and assign each of them to his work and to what he is to carry.

**20** But the Kohathites must not go in to look at the holy things even for a moment,[^15] or they will die."

**21** The LORD said to Moses,

**22** "Take a census of the Gershonites as well, by their ancestral families and their clans.

**23** Register those from thirty to fifty years old, all who enter to serve in the work force[^16] and do the work at the tent of meeting.

**24** This is the work of the clans of the Gershonites, in serving and in carrying:

**25** they are to carry the curtains of the tabernacle and the tent of meeting, its covering and the covering of fine leather on top of it, the screen for the entrance of the tent of meeting,

**26** the hangings of the courtyard, the screen for the entrance of the gate of the courtyard that surrounds the tabernacle and the altar, their ropes and all the equipment for their service. They are to do all the work that needs to be done with these.

**27** All the service of the Gershonites, all that they carry and all their work, is to be under the direction[^17] of Aaron and his sons. You are to assign to them as their duty all that they are to carry.

**28** This is the work of the clans of the Gershonites at the tent of meeting. Their duties are to be under the supervision[^18] of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.

**29** "As for the Merarites, register them by their clans and their ancestral families.

**30** Register those from thirty to fifty years old, all who enter the service to do the work of the tent of meeting.

**31** This is what they are responsible for carrying, as all their work at the tent of meeting: the frames of the tabernacle, its crossbars, its posts and its bases,

**32** and the posts of the courtyard all around with their bases, their tent pegs and their ropes, with all their equipment and all the work connected with them. Assign to each man by name the items he is responsible for carrying.

**33** This is the work of the clans of the Merarites, all their work at the tent of meeting, under the supervision of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest."

**34** So Moses and Aaron and the leaders of the community registered the Kohathites by their clans and their ancestral families,

**35** those from thirty to fifty years old, all who entered the service for work at the tent of meeting.

**36** Those registered by their clans numbered 2,750.

**37** These were those registered from the clans of the Kohathites, all who served at the tent of meeting, whom Moses and Aaron registered at the command of the LORD through Moses.

**38** Those registered from the Gershonites by their clans and their ancestral families,

**39** those from thirty to fifty years old, all who entered the service for work at the tent of meeting,

**40** those registered by their clans and their ancestral families, numbered 2,630.

**41** These were those registered from the clans of the Gershonites, all who served at the tent of meeting, whom Moses and Aaron registered at the command of the LORD.

**42** Those registered from the clans of the Merarites by their clans and their ancestral families,

**43** those from thirty to fifty years old, all who entered the service for work at the tent of meeting,

**44** those registered by their clans, numbered 3,200.

**45** These were those registered from the clans of the Merarites, whom Moses and Aaron registered at the command of the LORD through Moses.

**46** All those registered from the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel registered by their clans and their ancestral families,

**47** those from thirty to fifty years old, all who entered to do the work of serving and the work of carrying at the tent of meeting,

**48** numbered 8,580.

**49** At the command of the LORD through Moses they were registered, each man according to his work and what he was to carry. So they were registered[^19] as the LORD had commanded Moses.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "who come into the army". Hebrew *tsava*, the word used for military service in chapter 1, here means the work force of the sanctuary. Also in verses 23, 30, 35, 39 and 43.
[^3]: Literally "the holy of holies". Or "This is the work of the Kohathites at the tent of meeting concerning the most holy things."
[^4]: Hebrew *parokhet*: the veil, the hanging curtain that separated the most holy place from the rest of the tabernacle (Exodus 26:31–33).
[^5]: Hebrew *tachash*, of uncertain meaning. It may be the hide of a sea animal (such as the dugong), or leather of a special kind or color. Traditionally "badgers' skins" or "goatskins". Also in verses 8–14 and 25.
[^6]: Hebrew *tekhelet*, a costly violet-blue dye; also in verses 7, 9, 11 and 12.
[^7]: Literally "put its poles". According to Exodus 25:15 the poles of the ark were never to be removed, so here they are probably adjusted for carrying.
[^8]: Literally "the table of the face"; compare "the bread of the Presence" (Exodus 25:30).
[^9]: Literally "the bread of continuity", the bread that was always on the table.
[^10]: Hebrew *mot*, a pole or bar; here probably a frame for carrying. Also in verse 12.
[^11]: The altar of incense (Exodus 30:1–5).
[^12]: Literally "remove the fat from the altar", that is, the ashes soaked with fat. This is the bronze altar for burnt offerings.
[^13]: The Septuagint adds a sentence here about covering the basin and its stand with a purple cloth.
[^14]: Hebrew *minchah*, "gift, offering": an offering of grain or flour (Leviticus 2), traditionally "meal offering".
[^15]: Literally "as a swallowing", for as long as it takes to swallow. Or "while the holy things are being covered up".
[^16]: Literally "all who come to serve in the army"; see the note on verse 3.
[^17]: Literally "at the mouth".
[^18]: Literally "in the hand"; also in verse 33.
[^19]: Literally "and his registered ones". The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain.
