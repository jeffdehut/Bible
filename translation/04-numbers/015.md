**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: When you enter the land where you are to live, which I am giving you,

**3** and you present to the LORD an offering by fire,[^2] whether a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to fulfill a special vow or as a freewill offering or at your appointed festivals, to make a pleasing aroma[^3] for the LORD from the herd or from the flock,

**4** the one who brings his offering is to present to the LORD a grain offering of a tenth of an ephah[^4] of fine flour mixed with a quarter of a hin[^5] of oil.

**5** With each lamb for the burnt offering or the sacrifice, you are also to prepare a quarter of a hin of wine as a drink offering.

**6** For a ram, prepare a grain offering of two tenths of an ephah[^6] of fine flour mixed with a third of a hin[^7] of oil,

**7** and present a third of a hin of wine as a drink offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**8** When you prepare a young bull as a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to fulfill a special vow or as a fellowship offering[^8] to the LORD,

**9** you are to present with the young bull a grain offering of three tenths of an ephah[^9] of fine flour mixed with half a hin[^10] of oil,

**10** and you are to present half a hin of wine as a drink offering, an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**11** This is how it is to be done for each bull or each ram, or for each lamb or young goat.

**12** However many you prepare, do this for each one, according to their number.

**13** Everyone who is native-born is to do these things in this way when he presents an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**14** If a foreigner[^11] is living with you, or anyone else who is among you throughout your generations, and he presents an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD, he is to do just as you do.

**15** For the assembly there is to be one decree for you and for the foreigner who lives among you. It is a permanent decree throughout your generations: you and the foreigner are to be alike before the LORD.

**16** There is to be one law and one ruling for you and for the foreigner who lives among you."

**17** The LORD said to Moses,

**18** "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: When you come into the land where I am bringing you

**19** and you eat the food of the land, you are to set aside a contribution[^12] for the LORD.

**20** From the first of your dough[^13] you are to set aside a loaf as a contribution. Set it aside just as you do the contribution from the threshing floor.

**21** Throughout your generations you are to give the LORD a contribution from the first of your dough.

**22** "But suppose you unintentionally fail to keep[^14] all these commands that the LORD gave Moses,

**23** everything that the LORD has commanded you through Moses, from the day the LORD gave his commands and on throughout your generations.

**24** If it was done unintentionally, without the community being aware of it,[^15] the whole community is to offer one young bull as a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma to the LORD, with its grain offering and its drink offering as prescribed, and one male goat as a sin offering.[^16]

**25** The priest is to make atonement for the whole community of the Israelites, and they will be forgiven, because it was unintentional, and they have brought their offering, an offering by fire to the LORD, and their sin offering before the LORD for their unintentional wrong.

**26** The whole community of the Israelites will be forgiven, and the foreigner who lives among them, because all the people were involved in the unintentional wrong.

**27** "If one person sins unintentionally, he is to present a year-old female goat as a sin offering.

**28** The priest is to make atonement before the LORD for the person who has done wrong by sinning unintentionally, to make atonement for him, and he will be forgiven.

**29** You are to have one law for anyone who acts unintentionally, whether he is a native-born Israelite or a foreigner who lives among them.

**30** But anyone who acts defiantly,[^17] whether native-born or foreigner, is insulting the LORD. That person is to be cut off from among his people.[^18]

**31** Because he has despised the word of the LORD and broken his command, that person must certainly be cut off. His guilt remains on him."

**32** While the Israelites were in the wilderness, they found a man gathering wood on the Sabbath day.

**33** Those who found him gathering wood brought him to Moses and Aaron and to the whole community.

**34** They put him in custody, because it had not been made clear what should be done to him.

**35** Then the LORD said to Moses, "The man must be put to death. The whole community is to stone him outside the camp."

**36** So the whole community took him outside the camp and stoned him to death, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**37** The LORD said to Moses,

**38** "Speak to the Israelites and tell them to make tassels[^19] on the corners of their garments throughout their generations, and to put a blue[^20] cord on the tassel at each corner.

**39** You will have the tassel so that when you see it you will remember all the LORD's commands and do them, and not go about following[^21] your own heart and your own eyes, which lead you to be unfaithful.[^22]

**40** So you will remember and do all my commands, and you will be holy to your God.

**41** I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God. I am the LORD your God."

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ishsheh*, traditionally "an offering made by fire", as if from *esh*, "fire". Some think it means "gift" or "food offering". Also in verses 10, 13, 14 and 25.
[^3]: Literally "a soothing smell", an expression for an offering that the LORD accepts with pleasure. Also in verses 7, 10, 13, 14 and 24.
[^4]: Literally "a tenth", that is, a tenth of an ephah: about 2 quarts (2.2 liters).
[^5]: About 1 quart (1 liter).
[^6]: About 4 1/2 quarts (4.4 liters).
[^7]: About 1 1/4 quarts (1.2 liters).
[^8]: Hebrew *shelamim*, traditionally "peace offering". In these sacrifices most of the meat was eaten by the worshipers.
[^9]: About 7 quarts (6.6 liters).
[^10]: About 2 quarts (1.9 liters).
[^11]: Hebrew *ger*: someone from elsewhere who lives among the Israelites, a resident foreigner. Also in verses 15–16, 26 and 29–30.
[^12]: Hebrew *terumah*: something set apart and given for a sacred purpose (Exodus 25:2). Also in verses 20 and 21.
[^13]: Hebrew *arisah*, a rare word: perhaps "dough" or "coarse meal". Also in verse 21.
[^14]: Literally "go astray and do not do".
[^15]: Literally "away from the eyes of the community".
[^16]: Hebrew *chatta't*, traditionally "sin offering": an offering that removed the uncleanness caused by sin or impurity. Some translate it "purification offering". Also in verses 25 and 27.
[^17]: Literally "with a high hand", that is, openly and deliberately.
[^18]: See the note on 9:13. Also in verse 31.
[^19]: Hebrew *tsitsit*: tassels or fringes. Also in verse 39.
[^20]: Hebrew *tekhelet*, a costly violet-blue dye.
[^21]: Hebrew *tur*, the verb used for exploring the land in chapters 13–14.
[^22]: Literally "after which you prostitute yourselves", an image often used for unfaithfulness to the LORD.
