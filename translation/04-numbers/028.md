**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Command the Israelites and say to them: 'Be careful to present to me at its appointed time my offering, my food for my offerings by fire,[^2] a pleasing aroma to me.'

**3** Say to them: 'This is the offering by fire that you are to present to the LORD: two male lambs a year old without defect, every day, as a regular[^3] burnt offering.

**4** Offer one lamb in the morning, and offer the other lamb at twilight,[^4]

**5** with a tenth of an ephah[^5] of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with a quarter of a hin[^6] of oil from pressed olives.

**6** It is the regular burnt offering that was instituted at Mount Sinai, as a pleasing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD.

**7** Its drink offering is to be a quarter of a hin for each lamb. Pour out the drink offering of beer[^7] to the LORD in the holy place.

**8** Offer the other lamb at twilight. Offer it with the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning, as an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.

**9** "'On the Sabbath day: two male lambs a year old without defect, and two tenths[^8] of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with oil, with its drink offering.

**10** This is the burnt offering for every Sabbath, in addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering.

**11** "'At the beginning of each month[^9] you are to present a burnt offering to the LORD: two young bulls,[^10] one ram and seven male lambs a year old, without defect,

**12** with three tenths of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering for each bull, two tenths of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering for the one ram,

**13** and a tenth of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering for each lamb. It is a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD.

**14** Their drink offerings are to be half a hin of wine for each bull, a third of a hin for the ram, and a quarter of a hin for each lamb. This is the burnt offering for each new month, for all the months of the year.

**15** One male goat[^11] is to be offered to the LORD as a sin offering,[^12] in addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering.

**16** "'On the fourteenth day of the first month is the LORD's Passover.

**17** On the fifteenth day of this month there is to be a festival.[^13] For seven days bread made without yeast is to be eaten.

**18** On the first day you are to hold a holy assembly. Do no regular work.[^14]

**19** Present to the LORD an offering by fire, a burnt offering: two young bulls, one ram and seven male lambs a year old. They must be without defect.

**20** With them offer their grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for each bull and two tenths for the ram.

**21** Offer a tenth for each lamb, for each of the seven lambs.

**22** Offer one male goat as a sin offering, to make atonement for you.

**23** Offer these in addition to the morning burnt offering, which belongs to the regular burnt offering.

**24** In this way you are to offer every day for seven days the food of the offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD. It is to be offered in addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering.

**25** On the seventh day you are to hold a holy assembly. Do no regular work.

**26** "'On the day of the firstfruits, when you present to the LORD an offering of new grain at your Festival of Weeks,[^15] you are to hold a holy assembly. Do no regular work.

**27** Present a burnt offering as a pleasing aroma to the LORD: two young bulls, one ram and seven male lambs a year old,

**28** with their grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for each bull, two tenths for the one ram,

**29** and a tenth for each lamb, for each of the seven lambs;

**30** and one male goat to make atonement for you.

**31** Offer them, with their drink offerings, in addition to the regular burnt offering and its grain offering. They must be without defect.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "my offering, my bread, for my offerings by fire". On *ishsheh*, "offering by fire", see 15:3.
[^3]: Hebrew *tamid*, "continual": offered every day without fail. Also in verses 6, 10, 15, 23, 24 and 31.
[^4]: Literally "between the two evenings", probably the time between sunset and dark. Also in verse 8.
[^5]: Literally "a tenth of the ephah". A tenth of an ephah was about 2 quarts (2 liters).
[^6]: A hin was about a gallon (4 liters), so a quarter of a hin was about a quart (1 liter).
[^7]: Hebrew *shekhar*, usually beer, a fermented drink. Here it probably means wine, as in verse 14 and Exodus 29:40.
[^8]: Hebrew *esronim*, "tenths", that is, tenths of an ephah. Also in verses 12–13, 20–21 and 28–29.
[^9]: Literally "at the heads of your months", the day of the new moon.
[^10]: Literally "bulls, sons of the herd". Also in verses 19 and 27.
[^11]: Literally "one he-goat of the goats". Also in verses 22 and 30.
[^12]: Or "purification offering" (see 6:11). Also in verse 22.
[^13]: Hebrew *chag*: one of the three pilgrimage festivals (Exodus 23:14–17). This is the Festival of Bread Made without Yeast.
[^14]: Literally "any work of labor": ordinary daily work. Also in verses 25 and 26.
[^15]: Literally "in your weeks": the festival held seven weeks after the barley harvest began (Leviticus 23:15–16; Deuteronomy 16:9–10). It is also called the Festival of Harvest (Exodus 23:16).
