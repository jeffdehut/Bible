**1** "'On the first day of the seventh month you are to hold a sacred assembly. Do no regular work.[^1] It is to be a day of trumpet blasts[^2] for you.

**2** Offer a burnt offering as a pleasing aroma to Yahweh:[^3] one young bull,[^4] one ram and seven male lambs a year old, without defect,

**3** with their grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths[^5] for the bull, two tenths for the ram,

**4** and one tenth for each lamb, for each of the seven lambs;

**5** and one male goat[^6] as a sin offering,[^7] to make atonement for you.

**6** These are in addition to the burnt offering of the new month and its grain offering, and the regular burnt offering and its grain offering, and their drink offerings, as prescribed for them, as a pleasing aroma, an offering by fire[^8] to Yahweh.

**7** "'On the tenth day of this seventh month you are to hold a sacred assembly. You are to deny yourselves,[^9] and do no work at all.

**8** Present to Yahweh a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma: one young bull, one ram and seven male lambs a year old. They must be without defect.

**9** With them offer their grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for the bull, two tenths for the one ram,

**10** and a tenth for each lamb, for each of the seven lambs;

**11** and one male goat as a sin offering. These are in addition to the sin offering for atonement,[^10] and the regular burnt offering and its grain offering, and their drink offerings.

**12** "'On the fifteenth day of the seventh month you are to hold a sacred assembly. Do no regular work. Celebrate a festival to Yahweh for seven days.

**13** Present a burnt offering, an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh: thirteen young bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old. They must be without defect.

**14** With them offer their grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for each of the thirteen bulls, two tenths for each of the two rams,

**15** and a tenth for each of the fourteen lambs;

**16** and one male goat as a sin offering. These are in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

**17** "'On the second day: twelve young bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old, without defect,

**18** with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed;

**19** and one male goat as a sin offering. These are in addition to the regular burnt offering and its grain offering, and their drink offerings.

**20** "'On the third day: eleven bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old, without defect,

**21** with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed;

**22** and one male goat as a sin offering. These are in addition to the regular burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offering.

**23** "'On the fourth day: ten bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old, without defect,

**24** with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed;

**25** and one male goat as a sin offering. These are in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

**26** "'On the fifth day: nine bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old, without defect,

**27** with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed;

**28** and one male goat as a sin offering. These are in addition to the regular burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offering.

**29** "'On the sixth day: eight bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old, without defect,

**30** with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed;

**31** and one male goat as a sin offering. These are in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offerings.

**32** "'On the seventh day: seven bulls, two rams and fourteen male lambs a year old, without defect,

**33** with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed for them;

**34** and one male goat as a sin offering. These are in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering and its drink offering.

**35** "'On the eighth day you are to hold a closing assembly.[^11] Do no regular work.

**36** Present a burnt offering, an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh: one bull, one ram and seven male lambs a year old, without defect,

**37** with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bull, the ram and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed;

**38** and one male goat as a sin offering. These are in addition to the regular burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offering.

**39** "'These are what you are to offer to Yahweh at your appointed festivals, in addition to your vow offerings and your freewill offerings, whether burnt offerings, grain offerings, drink offerings or fellowship offerings.'"[^12]

[^1]: Literally "any work of labor": ordinary daily work. Also in verses 12 and 35.
[^2]: Hebrew *teru'ah*, a loud blast or shout, here probably of the ram's horn (Leviticus 23:24). This day later became the Jewish New Year (*Rosh Hashanah*).
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Literally "a bull, a son of the herd". Also in verses 8, 13 and 17.
[^5]: That is, tenths of an ephah. A tenth of an ephah was about 2 quarts (2 liters).
[^6]: Literally "one he-goat of the goats".
[^7]: Or "purification offering". Hebrew *chatta't*. Also in verses 11–38.
[^8]: Hebrew *ishsheh*, traditionally "an offering made by fire", as if from *esh*, "fire". Some think it means "gift" or "food offering". Also in verses 13 and 36.
[^9]: Literally "afflict your souls", usually understood as fasting and going without other comforts. This is the Day of Atonement (Leviticus 16:29–31; 23:27–32).
[^10]: Hebrew *chatta't ha-kippurim*: the sin offering of the Day of Atonement (Leviticus 16:5–19).
[^11]: Hebrew *atseret*, "assembly", perhaps "closing assembly", since it ends the festival season.
[^12]: Hebrew *shelamim*, traditionally "peace offerings": sacrifices in which most of the meat was eaten by the worshipers.
