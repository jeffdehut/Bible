**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses and to Eleazar son of Aaron the priest,[^2]

**2** "Take a census[^3] of the whole community of the Israelites, those twenty years old or more, by their ancestral families: everyone in Israel who is able to serve in the army."

**3** So Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke with them in the plains of Moab by the Jordan, across from Jericho. They said,

**4** "Take a census of those twenty years old or more, as Yahweh commanded Moses."[^4] The Israelites who came out of the land of Egypt were these:

**5** Reuben was Israel's firstborn. The descendants of Reuben: of Hanoch, the clan of the Hanochites; of Pallu, the clan of the Palluites;

**6** of Hezron, the clan of the Hezronites; of Carmi, the clan of the Carmites.

**7** These were the clans of the Reubenites. Those registered numbered 43,730.

**8** The son of Pallu was Eliab.

**9** The sons of Eliab were Nemuel, Dathan and Abiram. These were the same Dathan and Abiram, chosen men of the community, who rebelled against Moses and Aaron with Korah's followers,[^5] when they rebelled against Yahweh.

**10** The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them up together with Korah, when his followers died, when the fire consumed 250 men. They became a warning.[^6]

**11** But the sons of Korah did not die.

**12** The descendants of Simeon by their clans: of Nemuel, the clan of the Nemuelites; of Jamin, the clan of the Jaminites; of Jachin, the clan of the Jachinites;

**13** of Zerah, the clan of the Zerahites; of Shaul, the clan of the Shaulites.

**14** These were the clans of the Simeonites: 22,200.

**15** The descendants of Gad by their clans: of Zephon, the clan of the Zephonites; of Haggi, the clan of the Haggites; of Shuni, the clan of the Shunites;

**16** of Ozni, the clan of the Oznites; of Eri, the clan of the Erites;

**17** of Arod, the clan of the Arodites; of Areli, the clan of the Arelites.

**18** These were the clans of the descendants of Gad. Those registered numbered 40,500.

**19** The sons of Judah were Er and Onan, but Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan.

**20** The descendants of Judah by their clans were: of Shelah, the clan of the Shelanites; of Perez, the clan of the Perezites; of Zerah, the clan of the Zerahites.

**21** The descendants of Perez were: of Hezron, the clan of the Hezronites; of Hamul, the clan of the Hamulites.

**22** These were the clans of Judah. Those registered numbered 76,500.

**23** The descendants of Issachar by their clans: of Tola, the clan of the Tolaites; of Puvah, the clan of the Punites;

**24** of Jashub, the clan of the Jashubites; of Shimron, the clan of the Shimronites.

**25** These were the clans of Issachar. Those registered numbered 64,300.

**26** The descendants of Zebulun by their clans: of Sered, the clan of the Seredites; of Elon, the clan of the Elonites; of Jahleel, the clan of the Jahleelites.

**27** These were the clans of the Zebulunites. Those registered numbered 60,500.

**28** The descendants of Joseph by their clans were Manasseh and Ephraim.

**29** The descendants of Manasseh: of Machir, the clan of the Machirites. Machir was the father of Gilead. Of Gilead, the clan of the Gileadites.

**30** These were the descendants of Gilead: of Iezer,[^7] the clan of the Iezerites; of Helek, the clan of the Helekites;

**31** of Asriel, the clan of the Asrielites; of Shechem, the clan of the Shechemites;

**32** of Shemida, the clan of the Shemidaites; of Hepher, the clan of the Hepherites.

**33** Zelophehad son of Hepher had no sons, only daughters. The names of Zelophehad's daughters were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah.

**34** These were the clans of Manasseh. Those registered numbered 52,700.

**35** These were the descendants of Ephraim by their clans: of Shuthelah, the clan of the Shuthelahites; of Becher, the clan of the Becherites; of Tahan, the clan of the Tahanites.

**36** These were the descendants of Shuthelah: of Eran, the clan of the Eranites.

**37** These were the clans of the descendants of Ephraim. Those registered numbered 32,500. These were the descendants of Joseph by their clans.

**38** The descendants of Benjamin by their clans: of Bela, the clan of the Belaites; of Ashbel, the clan of the Ashbelites; of Ahiram, the clan of the Ahiramites;

**39** of Shephupham, the clan of the Shuphamites; of Hupham, the clan of the Huphamites.

**40** The sons of Bela were Ard and Naaman: the clan of the Ardites; of Naaman, the clan of the Naamites.

**41** These were the descendants of Benjamin by their clans. Those registered numbered 45,600.

**42** These were the descendants of Dan by their clans: of Shuham, the clan of the Shuhamites. These were the clans of Dan, by their clans.

**43** All the clans of the Shuhamites, those registered, numbered 64,400.

**44** The descendants of Asher by their clans: of Imnah, the clan of the Imnites; of Ishvi, the clan of the Ishvites; of Beriah, the clan of the Beriites.

**45** Of the descendants of Beriah: of Heber, the clan of the Heberites; of Malchiel, the clan of the Malchielites.

**46** The name of Asher's daughter was Serah.

**47** These were the clans of the descendants of Asher. Those registered numbered 53,400.

**48** The descendants of Naphtali by their clans: of Jahzeel, the clan of the Jahzeelites; of Guni, the clan of the Gunites;

**49** of Jezer, the clan of the Jezerites; of Shillem, the clan of the Shillemites.

**50** These were the clans of Naphtali by their clans. Those registered numbered 45,400.

**51** These were the registered Israelites: 601,730.[^8]

**52** Yahweh said to Moses,

**53** "The land is to be divided among these as their inheritance, according to the number of names.

**54** To a large group you are to give a larger inheritance, and to a small group a smaller inheritance. Each is to be given its inheritance according to the number of those registered in it.

**55** But the land is to be divided by lot. They are to receive their inheritance according to the names of their ancestral tribes.

**56** Each inheritance is to be divided by lot, between the larger and the smaller."

**57** These were the Levites registered by their clans: of Gershon, the clan of the Gershonites; of Kohath, the clan of the Kohathites; of Merari, the clan of the Merarites.

**58** These were the clans of Levi: the clan of the Libnites, the clan of the Hebronites, the clan of the Mahlites, the clan of the Mushites, the clan of the Korahites. Kohath was the father of Amram.

**59** The name of Amram's wife was Jochebed daughter of Levi, who was born to Levi in Egypt.[^9] She bore to Amram Aaron and Moses and their sister Miriam.

**60** To Aaron were born Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**61** But Nadab and Abihu died when they offered unauthorized fire[^10] before Yahweh.

**62** Those registered numbered 23,000, every male a month old or more. They were not registered among the Israelites, because no inheritance was given to them among the Israelites.

**63** These were the people registered by Moses and Eleazar the priest, who registered the Israelites in the plains of Moab by the Jordan, across from Jericho.

**64** Among them there was not one of those registered by Moses and Aaron the priest when they registered the Israelites in the wilderness of Sinai.

**65** For Yahweh had said of them, "They will certainly die in the wilderness."[^11] Not one of them was left except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: In most English Bibles this verse begins with the words of 25:19, "After the plague".
[^3]: Literally "Lift up the head", as in 1:2.
[^4]: The Hebrew is very brief. Literally "From twenty years old and upward, as Yahweh commanded Moses and the sons of Israel who came out of the land of Egypt." The words "Take a census" are supplied from verse 2.
[^5]: Literally "in the community of Korah". See chapter 16.
[^6]: Literally "a sign".
[^7]: Called Abiezer in Joshua 17:2.
[^8]: The total at the first census was 603,550 (1:46).
[^9]: Literally "whom she bore to Levi in Egypt". The mother is not named.
[^10]: Hebrew *esh zarah*, "strange fire": fire that Yahweh had not commanded (Leviticus 10:1–2).
[^11]: See 14:28–35.
