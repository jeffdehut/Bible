**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Take vengeance for the Israelites on the Midianites. After that you will be gathered to your people."[^2]

**3** So Moses said to the people, "Arm some of your men for war, so that they may go against Midian to carry out Yahweh's vengeance on Midian.

**4** Send a thousand men from each tribe, from all the tribes of Israel, to the war."

**5** So from the clans[^3] of Israel a thousand were provided from each tribe, 12,000 men armed for war.

**6** Moses sent them to the war, a thousand from each tribe, together with Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest, who took with him the holy objects[^4] and the trumpets for sounding the alarm.

**7** They made war on Midian, as Yahweh had commanded Moses, and killed every male.

**8** Along with the others they killed,[^5] they killed the kings of Midian: Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur and Reba, the five kings of Midian. They also killed Balaam son of Beor with the sword.

**9** The Israelites took the women of Midian and their little ones captive, and they carried off as plunder all their animals, all their herds and flocks, and all their goods.

**10** They burned with fire all the towns where they had settled and all their encampments.[^6]

**11** They took all the spoil and all the plunder, both people and animals.

**12** They brought the captives, the plunder and the spoil to Moses, to Eleazar the priest and to the community of the Israelites, at the camp on the plains of Moab by the Jordan, across from Jericho.

**13** Moses, Eleazar the priest and all the leaders of the community went out to meet them outside the camp.

**14** Moses was angry with the officers of the army, the commanders of thousands and the commanders of hundreds, who had come back from the campaign.

**15** Moses said to them, "Have you let all the women live?

**16** These were the very ones who, on Balaam's advice,[^7] caused the Israelites to be unfaithful to Yahweh in the matter of Peor, so that the plague came on Yahweh's community.

**17** Now kill every male among the little ones, and kill every woman who has slept with a man.[^8]

**18** But keep alive for yourselves all the girls[^9] who have not slept with a man.

**19** As for you, stay outside the camp for seven days. All of you who have killed anyone[^10] or touched anyone who was killed must purify yourselves on the third day and on the seventh day, you and your captives.

**20** Purify every garment, every article of leather, everything made of goats' hair, and every article of wood."

**21** Then Eleazar the priest said to the soldiers who had gone into battle, "This is the rule of the law[^11] that Yahweh commanded Moses:

**22** Only the gold, the silver, the bronze, the iron, the tin and the lead,

**23** everything that can stand fire, you must pass through the fire, and it will be clean. But it must also be purified with the water for impurity.[^12] And everything that cannot stand fire you must pass through the water.

**24** On the seventh day wash your clothes, and you will be clean. After that you may come into the camp."

**25** Yahweh said to Moses,

**26** "You and Eleazar the priest and the heads of the ancestral families of the community are to count[^13] the plunder that was captured, both people and animals.

**27** Divide the plunder into two halves, between the warriors who went out to battle and the whole community.

**28** From the soldiers who went out to battle, set aside a tribute[^14] for Yahweh: one life out of every five hundred, whether of people, cattle, donkeys, or sheep and goats.[^15]

**29** Take it from their half and give it to Eleazar the priest as Yahweh's contribution.[^16]

**30** From the Israelites' half, take one out of every fifty, whether of people, cattle, donkeys, or sheep and goats, of all the animals, and give them to the Levites, who carry out the duties of Yahweh's tabernacle."

**31** So Moses and Eleazar the priest did as Yahweh commanded Moses.

**32** The plunder, what remained of the spoil that the soldiers had taken, was 675,000 sheep and goats,

**33** 72,000 cattle,

**34** 61,000 donkeys,

**35** and 32,000 people in all, women who had not slept with a man.

**36** The half that was the share of those who had gone out to battle was 337,500 sheep and goats,

**37** and the tribute for Yahweh from the sheep and goats was 675;

**38** 36,000 cattle, and the tribute from them for Yahweh was seventy-two;

**39** 30,500 donkeys, and the tribute from them for Yahweh was sixty-one;

**40** and 16,000 people, and the tribute from them for Yahweh was thirty-two people.

**41** Moses gave the tribute, Yahweh's contribution, to Eleazar the priest, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**42** From the Israelites' half, which Moses had separated from that of the men who had fought,

**43** the community's half was 337,500 sheep and goats,

**44** 36,000 cattle,

**45** 30,500 donkeys,

**46** and 16,000 people.

**47** From the Israelites' half Moses took one out of every fifty, of people and of animals, and gave them to the Levites, who carry out the duties of Yahweh's tabernacle, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**48** Then the officers who were over the units[^17] of the army, the commanders of thousands and the commanders of hundreds, came to Moses

**49** and said to him, "Your servants have counted the soldiers under our command, and not one of us is missing.

**50** So we have brought as an offering to Yahweh what each man found, articles of gold: armlets, bracelets, signet rings, earrings and pendants,[^18] to make atonement for ourselves before Yahweh."

**51** Moses and Eleazar the priest received the gold from them, all of it crafted articles.

**52** All the gold of the contribution that they presented to Yahweh, from the commanders of thousands and from the commanders of hundreds, came to 16,750 shekels.[^19]

**53** The soldiers had each taken plunder for themselves.

**54** Moses and Eleazar the priest received the gold from the commanders of thousands and of hundreds and brought it into the tent of meeting as a reminder[^20] for the Israelites before Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: That is, you will die. See 20:24.
[^3]: Literally "the thousands" (see 1:16).
[^4]: Literally "the vessels of the holiness". The text does not say what they were.
[^5]: Literally "upon their slain".
[^6]: Hebrew *tirot*, perhaps walled camps or circles of tents.
[^7]: Literally "by the word of Balaam". The earlier chapters do not record this advice.
[^8]: Literally "every woman who has known a man by lying with a male". Also in verses 18 and 35.
[^9]: Literally "all the little ones among the women".
[^10]: Hebrew *nephesh*, "a person".
[^11]: Literally "the statute of the instruction (*torah*)", as in 19:2.
[^12]: See 19:9.
[^13]: Literally "lift up the head of".
[^14]: Hebrew *mekhes*, a tax or levy. Also in verses 37–41.
[^15]: Hebrew *tson*, the flock: sheep and goats together. Also in verses 30–43.
[^16]: Hebrew *terumah* (see 5:9). Also in verses 41 and 52.
[^17]: Literally "the thousands".
[^18]: Hebrew *kumaz*, of uncertain meaning: perhaps a pendant or necklace.
[^19]: A shekel was about 2/5 ounce (11.5 grams), so this was about 420 pounds (190 kilograms).
[^20]: Or "a memorial".
