**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Speak to Aaron and tell him: When you set up[^2] the lamps, the seven lamps are to give light in front of the lampstand."

**3** Aaron did so. He set up its lamps to give light in front of the lampstand, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**4** This is how the lampstand was made. It was hammered work[^3] of gold, from its base[^4] to its blossoms. It was hammered work. The lampstand was made according to the pattern that the LORD had shown Moses.

**5** The LORD said to Moses,

**6** "Take the Levites from among the Israelites and make them clean.

**7** This is what you are to do to them to make them clean: sprinkle the water of purification[^5] on them, and have them shave their whole bodies[^6] and wash their clothes, and so make themselves clean.

**8** Then they are to take a young bull with its grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil, and you are to take a second young bull as a sin offering.[^7]

**9** Bring the Levites to the front of the tent of meeting, and assemble the whole community of the Israelites.

**10** When you bring the Levites before the LORD, the Israelites are to lay their hands on the Levites.

**11** Aaron is to present the Levites before the LORD as a wave offering[^8] from the Israelites, so that they may do the work of the LORD.

**12** "Then the Levites are to lay their hands on the heads of the bulls, and you are to offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering to the LORD, to make atonement for the Levites.

**13** Have the Levites stand before Aaron and his sons, and present them as a wave offering to the LORD.

**14** In this way you are to set the Levites apart from among the Israelites, and the Levites will be mine.

**15** After that the Levites may go in to serve at the tent of meeting. You are to make them clean and present them as a wave offering,

**16** for they are wholly given[^9] to me from among the Israelites. I have taken them for myself in place of all who first open the womb, the firstborn of all the Israelites.

**17** For every firstborn among the Israelites is mine, both human and animal. On the day I struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I set them apart for myself as holy.

**18** And I have taken the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the Israelites.

**19** From among the Israelites I have given the Levites as a gift[^10] to Aaron and his sons, to do the work for the Israelites at the tent of meeting and to make atonement for the Israelites, so that no plague will strike the Israelites when the Israelites come near the sanctuary."

**20** Moses and Aaron and the whole community of the Israelites did this to the Levites. The Israelites did to them everything that the LORD had commanded Moses concerning the Levites.

**21** The Levites purified themselves from sin[^11] and washed their clothes. Aaron presented them as a wave offering before the LORD, and Aaron made atonement for them to make them clean.

**22** After that the Levites went in to do their work at the tent of meeting under Aaron and his sons.[^12] They did to the Levites just as the LORD had commanded Moses concerning them.

**23** The LORD said to Moses,

**24** "This is what applies to the Levites. From the age of twenty-five[^13] a man is to enter the service[^14] and do the work of the tent of meeting.

**25** From the age of fifty he is to retire from the service of the work and work no longer.

**26** He may assist his brothers at the tent of meeting in carrying out their duties,[^15] but he must not do the work himself. This is how you are to arrange the duties of the Levites."

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "make the lamps go up": either setting them in place on the lampstand or lighting them (compare Exodus 25:37). Also in verse 3.
[^3]: Hebrew *miqshah*: hammered out of a single piece of metal (Exodus 25:31).
[^4]: Literally "its thigh".
[^5]: Literally "water of sin": water that removes the uncleanness of sin (compare 19:9).
[^6]: Literally "make a razor pass over all their flesh".
[^7]: Hebrew *chatta't*, traditionally "sin offering": an offering that removed the uncleanness caused by sin or impurity. Some translate it "purification offering". Also in verse 12.
[^8]: Literally "wave the Levites as a wave offering": present them to the LORD with the gesture of lifting or moving used for offerings (Hebrew *tenufah*). Also in verses 13, 15 and 21.
[^9]: Literally "given, given", an emphatic form (compare 3:9).
[^10]: Hebrew *netunim*, "given ones", the same word as in verse 16.
[^11]: Hebrew *yitchatte'u*, from the same root as *chatta't*, "sin offering".
[^12]: Literally "before Aaron and before his sons".
[^13]: In 4:3 the age for entering the service is thirty.
[^14]: Literally "come to serve in the army"; see the note on 4:3. Also in verse 25.
[^15]: Literally "to keep the charge".
