**1** These are the generations[^1] of Aaron and Moses at the time when Yahweh[^2] spoke with Moses on Mount Sinai.

**2** These are the names of the sons of Aaron: Nadab the firstborn, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**3** These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests, whom he ordained[^3] to serve as priests.

**4** But Nadab and Abihu died in Yahweh's presence when they offered unauthorized fire[^4] before Yahweh in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no sons. So Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests during the lifetime of their father Aaron.[^5]

**5** Yahweh said to Moses,

**6** "Bring the tribe of Levi near and present them to Aaron the priest, to assist him.

**7** They are to carry out duties[^6] for him and for the whole community in front of the tent of meeting, doing the work of the tabernacle.

**8** They are to take care of all the furnishings of the tent of meeting and carry out the duties of the Israelites, doing the work of the tabernacle.

**9** Give the Levites to Aaron and his sons. They are given wholly[^7] to him from among the Israelites.

**10** Appoint Aaron and his sons to carry out their priesthood. Any outsider who comes near is to be put to death."

**11** Yahweh said to Moses,

**12** "Look, I have taken the Levites from among the Israelites in place of every firstborn, the first to open the womb among the Israelites. The Levites are mine,

**13** for every firstborn is mine. On the day I struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I set apart for myself every firstborn in Israel, both human and animal, as holy. They are to be mine. I am Yahweh."

**14** Yahweh said to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai,

**15** "Register the Levites by their ancestral families and their clans. Register every male a month old or more."

**16** So Moses registered them at the command[^8] of Yahweh, as he had been commanded.

**17** These were the sons of Levi by name: Gershon, Kohath and Merari.

**18** These are the names of the sons of Gershon by their clans: Libni and Shimei.

**19** The sons of Kohath by their clans: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.

**20** The sons of Merari by their clans: Mahli and Mushi. These were the clans of the Levites by their ancestral families.

**21** To Gershon belonged the clan of the Libnites and the clan of the Shimeites. These were the clans of the Gershonites.

**22** Those registered, counting every male a month old or more, numbered 7,500.

**23** The clans of the Gershonites were to camp behind the tabernacle, on the west.

**24** The leader of the ancestral family of the Gershonites was Eliasaph son of Lael.

**25** At the tent of meeting the Gershonites were responsible for the tabernacle and the tent,[^9] its covering, the screen for the entrance of the tent of meeting,

**26** the hangings of the courtyard, the screen for the entrance of the courtyard that surrounds the tabernacle and the altar, and its ropes: all the work connected with these.

**27** To Kohath belonged the clan of the Amramites, the clan of the Izharites, the clan of the Hebronites and the clan of the Uzzielites. These were the clans of the Kohathites.

**28** Counting every male a month old or more, there were 8,600.[^10] They were responsible for the care of the sanctuary.[^11]

**29** The clans of the Kohathites were to camp on the south side of the tabernacle.

**30** The leader of the ancestral family of the clans of the Kohathites was Elizaphan son of Uzziel.

**31** They were responsible for the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, the holy utensils used in the service, and the screen:[^12] all the work connected with these.

**32** The chief leader of the Levites was Eleazar son of Aaron the priest. He had oversight of those responsible for the care of the sanctuary.

**33** To Merari belonged the clan of the Mahlites and the clan of the Mushites. These were the clans of Merari.

**34** Those registered from them, counting every male a month old or more, numbered 6,200.

**35** The leader of the ancestral family of the clans of Merari was Zuriel son of Abihail. They were to camp on the north side of the tabernacle.

**36** The Merarites were appointed to take charge of the frames of the tabernacle, its crossbars, its posts, its bases and all its equipment, all the work connected with these,

**37** and the posts of the courtyard all around, with their bases, their tent pegs and their ropes.

**38** Those who were to camp in front of the tabernacle on the east, in front of the tent of meeting toward the sunrise, were Moses and Aaron and his sons. They were responsible for the care of the sanctuary, carrying out the duty owed by the Israelites. Any outsider who came near was to be put to death.

**39** All the Levites registered, whom Moses and Aaron[^13] registered at the command of Yahweh by their clans, every male a month old or more, numbered twenty-two thousand.

**40** Yahweh said to Moses, "Register every firstborn male among the Israelites a month old or more, and count the number of their names.

**41** Take the Levites for me in place of all the firstborn among the Israelites, and the livestock of the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the livestock of the Israelites. I am Yahweh."

**42** So Moses registered all the firstborn among the Israelites, as Yahweh had commanded him.

**43** All the firstborn males a month old or more, counted by name as they were registered, numbered 22,273.

**44** Yahweh said to Moses,

**45** "Take the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the Israelites, and the livestock of the Levites in place of their livestock. The Levites are to be mine. I am Yahweh.

**46** As the redemption price for the 273 firstborn of the Israelites who outnumber the Levites,

**47** collect five shekels[^14] for each one.[^15] Collect it by the sanctuary shekel, twenty gerahs[^16] to the shekel.

**48** Give the money to Aaron and his sons as the redemption price for those of them who are in excess."

**49** So Moses collected the redemption money from those who were in excess of those redeemed by the Levites.

**50** From the firstborn of the Israelites he collected the money: 1,365 shekels,[^17] by the sanctuary shekel.

**51** Moses gave the redemption money to Aaron and his sons at the command of Yahweh, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

[^1]: Hebrew *toledot*: the record of a person's descendants (Genesis 2:4). Only Aaron's sons are then listed.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "whose hand he filled", an expression for installing a priest in office. "He" is Moses (Exodus 28:41).
[^4]: Hebrew *esh zarah*, "strange fire": fire that Yahweh had not commanded (Leviticus 10:1–2).
[^5]: Literally "on the face of Aaron their father": in his presence, under his supervision.
[^6]: Literally "keep his charge and the charge of the whole community". Hebrew *mishmeret*, "charge, duty, guard", runs through this chapter.
[^7]: Literally "given, given", an emphatic form. Compare 8:16.
[^8]: Literally "at the mouth"; also in verses 39 and 51.
[^9]: *The tabernacle* here is probably the inner layer of linen curtains, and *the tent* the layer of goats' hair curtains over it (Exodus 26:1–14).
[^10]: The figures in verses 22, 28 and 34 add up to 22,300, but verse 39 gives a total of twenty-two thousand. Some manuscripts of the Septuagint read 8,300 here, which would match that total.
[^11]: Literally "keepers of the charge of the holy place"; also in verse 32.
[^12]: Probably the curtain that hung in front of the ark (4:5).
[^13]: In the Hebrew text the word "and Aaron" has dots written above each of its letters, one of the fifteen places in the Hebrew Bible marked in this way. Traditionally the dots mark a word as doubtful. Aaron is not named in the commands of verses 15 and 40.
[^14]: A shekel was about 0.4 ounce (11.5 grams) of silver.
[^15]: Literally "five, five shekels per skull".
[^16]: A gerah was about 1/50 ounce (0.6 gram).
[^17]: About 35 pounds (16 kilograms).
