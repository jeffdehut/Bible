**1** While Israel was staying at Shittim,[^1] the people began to commit sexual immorality[^2] with the women of Moab.

**2** The women invited the people to the sacrifices to their gods, and the people ate and bowed down to their gods.

**3** So Israel joined in the worship of the Baal of Peor,[^3] and the LORD's[^4] anger burned against Israel.

**4** The LORD said to Moses, "Take all the leaders of the people and execute them[^5] before the LORD in broad daylight,[^6] so that the LORD's fierce anger may turn away from Israel."

**5** So Moses said to the judges of Israel, "Each of you must kill those of his men who have joined in the worship of the Baal of Peor."

**6** Just then one of the Israelites came and brought a Midianite woman to his family,[^7] in the sight of Moses and in the sight of the whole community of the Israelites, while they were weeping at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**7** When Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, saw it, he got up from among the community and took a spear in his hand.

**8** He went after the Israelite man into the inner tent[^8] and drove the spear through both of them, the Israelite man and the woman, through her belly.[^9] Then the plague on the Israelites was stopped.

**9** Those who died in the plague numbered twenty-four thousand.

**10** The LORD said to Moses,

**11** "Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, has turned my wrath away from the Israelites, because he was zealous with my zeal[^10] among them, so that I did not wipe out the Israelites in my zeal.

**12** Therefore say: 'I am giving him my covenant of peace.

**13** It will be for him and for his descendants after him a covenant of a permanent priesthood, because he was zealous for his God and made atonement for the Israelites.'"

**14** The name of the Israelite man who was killed, who was killed with the Midianite woman, was Zimri son of Salu, the leader of an ancestral family of the Simeonites.

**15** The name of the Midianite woman who was killed was Cozbi daughter of Zur. He was the tribal head of an ancestral family in Midian.[^11]

**16** The LORD said to Moses,

**17** "Treat the Midianites as enemies and strike them down,

**18** because they treated you as enemies by the tricks they used to deceive you in the matter of Peor, and in the matter of Cozbi, the daughter of a leader of Midian, their sister, who was killed on the day of the plague that came because of Peor."

[^1]: The name means "acacia trees".
[^2]: Hebrew *liznot*, "to prostitute themselves". The word is also used of unfaithfulness to the LORD by worshiping other gods, which follows in verse 2.
[^3]: Literally "Israel attached itself to the Baal of Peor": the god worshiped at Peor (23:28). *Baal* means "lord, master, owner". Also in verse 5.
[^4]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^5]: The meaning of the Hebrew verb is uncertain: perhaps "impale", "hang" or "expose" their bodies. *Them* may be the leaders themselves or those who were guilty.
[^6]: Literally "in front of the sun".
[^7]: Literally "to his brothers": his relatives, or his fellow Israelites.
[^8]: Hebrew *qubbah*, a word found only here: perhaps an inner room of a tent, a vaulted tent, or a shrine.
[^9]: Hebrew *qevatah*, "her belly" or "her stomach", which sounds like *qubbah* earlier in the verse.
[^10]: Or "with my jealousy". The Hebrew word *qin'ah* means both zeal and jealousy. Also later in the verse and in verse 13.
[^11]: Literally "He was head of the peoples of a father's house in Midian."
[^12]: The sentence continues in 26:1. Verse 19 is the beginning of 26:1 in most English Bibles.
