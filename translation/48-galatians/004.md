**1** What I mean is this: as long as the heir is a child, he is no different from a slave, though he is the owner of everything.

**2** He is under guardians and managers until the time set by his father.

**3** So also we, when we were children, were enslaved under the elemental forces[^1] of the world.

**4** But when the fullness of time had come, God sent his Son, born of a woman, born under the Law,

**5** to redeem those who were under the Law, so that we might receive adoption as sons.[^2]

**6** And because you are sons, God has sent the Spirit of his Son into our hearts,[^3] crying, "Abba,[^4] Father!"

**7** So you are no longer a slave, but a son; and if a son, then also an heir through God.[^5]

**8** Formerly, when you did not know God, you were slaves to beings that by nature are not gods.

**9** But now that you have come to know God, or rather to be known by God, how can you turn back again to the weak and worthless elemental forces? Do you want to be enslaved to them all over again?

**10** You observe days and months and seasons and years!

**11** I am afraid for you, that perhaps I have labored over you in vain.

**12** I beg you, brothers and sisters,[^6] become as I am, for I also have become as you are. You did me no wrong.

**13** You know that it was because of a bodily illness[^7] that I proclaimed the good news to you the first time.

**14** And though my condition was a trial to you, you did not despise or reject me,[^8] but you welcomed me as an angel of God,[^9] as Christ Jesus.

**15** Where then is your blessedness?[^10] For I testify that, if it had been possible, you would have torn out your eyes and given them to me.

**16** Have I then become your enemy by telling you the truth?

**17** They court you eagerly, but not for good. They want to shut you out, so that you will court them.

**18** It is good to be courted eagerly for a good purpose at all times, and not only when I am present with you,

**19** my children,[^11] for whom I am again in the pains of childbirth until Christ is formed in you.

**20** I wish I could be present with you now and change my tone, for I am perplexed about you.

**21** Tell me, you who want to be under the Law, do you not listen to the Law?

**22** For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one by the slave woman and one by the free woman.[^12]

**23** But the son of the slave woman was born according to the flesh,[^13] and the son of the free woman through a promise.

**24** These things are spoken as an allegory. These women are two covenants. One is from Mount Sinai, bearing children for slavery; she is Hagar.

**25** Now Hagar[^14] is Mount Sinai in Arabia; she corresponds to the present Jerusalem, for she is in slavery with her children.

**26** But the Jerusalem above is free, and she is our mother.[^15]

**27** For it is written: "Rejoice, barren woman, you who have not given birth! Break into song and shout, you who have not been in labor! For the children of the desolate woman are more than the children of the married woman."[^16]

**28** Now you,[^17] brothers and sisters, like Isaac, are children of promise.

**29** But just as at that time the one born according to the flesh persecuted the one born according to the Spirit, so it is now also.[^18]

**30** But what does the scripture say? "Drive out the slave woman and her son, for the son of the slave woman will never share the inheritance with the son of the free woman."[^19]

**31** Therefore, brothers and sisters, we are not children of the slave woman but of the free woman.

[^1]: Greek *stoicheia*, "elements": the basic principles of the world, or the elemental spirits thought to rule it; also in verse 9.
[^2]: Greek *huiothesia*, "adoption as sons", the legal act that made someone a son and heir (see Romans 8:15). *Sons* in verses 6–7 includes women and men (3:26–28).
[^3]: Many manuscripts read "your hearts".
[^4]: Aramaic for "father". Compare Mark 14:36; Romans 8:15.
[^5]: Many manuscripts read "an heir of God through Christ".
[^6]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike; also in verses 28 and 31.
[^7]: Literally "weakness of the flesh".
[^8]: Literally "your trial in my flesh you did not despise or spit out". Many manuscripts read "my trial in my flesh".
[^9]: Or "a messenger of God". Greek *angelos* means both.
[^10]: Or "your blessing of me". Greek *makarismos*, as in Romans 4:6, 9. Many manuscripts read "What then was your blessedness?"
[^11]: Many manuscripts read "my little children".
[^12]: Genesis 16:15; 21:2. *Slave woman*: Greek *paidiske*, a female slave; also in verses 23 and 30–31.
[^13]: That is, in the ordinary human way; also in verse 29.
[^14]: Literally "the Hagar", perhaps meaning the name Hagar. Many manuscripts read "For Hagar".
[^15]: Many manuscripts read "the mother of us all".
[^16]: Isaiah 54:1, as in the Septuagint, which has "Rejoice" where the Hebrew has "Sing". The Hebrew adds "says Yahweh".
[^17]: Many manuscripts read "Now we … are".
[^18]: Compare Genesis 21:9.
[^19]: Genesis 21:10, where Sarah says "with my son, with Isaac".
