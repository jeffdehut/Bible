**1** Brothers and sisters,[^1] if anyone is caught in some wrongdoing, you who are spiritual should restore such a person in a spirit of gentleness. Watch yourself, so that you are not tempted too.

**2** Carry one another's burdens, and in this way you will fulfill[^2] the law of Christ.

**3** For if anyone thinks he is something when he is nothing, he deceives himself.

**4** But let each one test his own work, and then he will have reason to boast in himself alone, and not in comparison with someone else.

**5** For each one will carry his own load.

**6** Let the one who is taught the word share all good things with the one who teaches.

**7** Do not be deceived: God is not mocked. For whatever a person sows, that he will also reap.

**8** For the one who sows to his own flesh[^3] will reap destruction from the flesh, but the one who sows to the Spirit will reap eternal life from the Spirit.

**9** And let us not grow weary of doing good, for at the proper time we will reap, if we do not give up.

**10** So then, as we have opportunity, let us do good to everyone, and especially to those who belong to the household of faith.

**11** See with what large letters I am writing to you with my own hand.

**12** Those who want to make a good showing in the flesh[^4] are the ones who are trying to compel you to be circumcised, only so that they may not be persecuted for the cross of Christ.[^5]

**13** For even those who are circumcised[^6] do not themselves keep the Law, but they want you to be circumcised so that they may boast in your flesh.

**14** But may I never boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which[^7] the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.

**15** For neither circumcision nor uncircumcision is anything, but a new creation.[^8]

**16** And as for all who follow this rule, peace be on them, and mercy, and on the Israel of God.[^9]

**17** From now on let no one cause me trouble, for I bear on my body the marks[^10] of Jesus.[^11]

**18** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brothers and sisters. Amen.

[^1]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike; also in verse 18.
[^2]: Many manuscripts read "and in this way fulfill".
[^3]: Greek *sarx*, "flesh". In Paul's moral sense, human nature in its weakness and opposition to God.
[^4]: That is, in outward things, here circumcision; also "your flesh" in verse 13.
[^5]: Some manuscripts read "Christ Jesus".
[^6]: Many manuscripts read "those who have been circumcised".
[^7]: Or "through whom".
[^8]: Many manuscripts read "For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision has any value, but a new creation" (compare 5:6).
[^9]: Or "peace and mercy be on them, that is, on the Israel of God". Greek *kai* can mean "and" or "even". Compare Psalms 125:5; 128:6.
[^10]: Greek *stigmata*, marks branded or cut on the body, as on slaves; here probably the scars of his sufferings.
[^11]: Many manuscripts read "the Lord Jesus".
