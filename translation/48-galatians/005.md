**1** For freedom Christ has set us free. So stand firm, and do not submit again to a yoke of slavery.[^1]

**2** Listen! I, Paul, tell you that if you have yourselves circumcised, Christ will be of no benefit to you.

**3** And I testify again to every man who has himself circumcised that he is obligated to keep the whole Law.

**4** You who are seeking to be declared righteous[^2] by the Law have been cut off from Christ; you have fallen away from grace.

**5** For through the Spirit, by faith, we eagerly wait for the hope of righteousness.[^3]

**6** For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision has any value, but faith working through love.

**7** You were running well. Who hindered you from obeying the truth?

**8** This persuasion does not come from him who calls you.

**9** A little yeast leavens the whole batch of dough.

**10** I am confident in the Lord about you, that you will take no other view. But the one who is troubling you will bear his judgment, whoever he may be.

**11** But as for me, brothers and sisters,[^4] if I am still preaching circumcision, why am I still being persecuted? In that case the offense[^5] of the cross has been done away with.

**12** I wish those who are unsettling you would even castrate themselves![^6]

**13** For you were called to freedom, brothers and sisters. Only do not use your freedom as an opportunity for the flesh,[^7] but through love serve[^8] one another.

**14** For the whole Law is fulfilled in one word: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself."[^9]

**15** But if you bite and devour one another, watch out that you are not consumed by one another.

**16** But I say, live[^10] by the Spirit, and you will not carry out the desire of the flesh.

**17** For the flesh desires what is against the Spirit, and the Spirit what is against the flesh; for these are opposed to each other, so that you do not do the things you want.

**18** But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the Law.

**19** Now the works of the flesh are evident:[^11] sexual immorality, impurity, debauchery,

**20** idolatry, sorcery, hostility, strife, jealousy, fits of rage, selfish ambitions, dissensions, factions,

**21** envy,[^12] drunkenness, carousing, and things like these. I warn you, as I warned you before, that those who practice such things will not inherit the kingdom of God.

**22** But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness,[^13]

**23** gentleness, self-control. Against such things there is no law.

**24** And those who belong to Christ[^14] have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires.

**25** If we live by the Spirit, let us also keep in step with the Spirit.

**26** Let us not become conceited, provoking one another, envying one another.

[^1]: Many manuscripts read "So stand firm in the freedom with which Christ has set us free, and do not submit again to a yoke of slavery."
[^2]: Greek *dikaioo*, traditionally "justify".
[^3]: That is, the righteousness for which we hope. Or "the hope that righteousness brings".
[^4]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike; also in verse 13.
[^5]: Greek *skandalon*, "stumbling block, cause of offense".
[^6]: Literally "would even cut themselves off". Paul's sarcasm turns on circumcision.
[^7]: Greek *sarx*, "flesh". In Paul's moral sense, human nature in its weakness and opposition to God; also in verses 16–17, 19 and 24.
[^8]: Greek *douleuo*, "serve as a slave".
[^9]: Leviticus 19:18.
[^10]: Literally "walk".
[^11]: Many manuscripts add "adultery".
[^12]: Many manuscripts add "murders".
[^13]: Or "faith". Greek *pistis*.
[^14]: Many modern editions read "Christ Jesus". The SBLGNT here follows the Byzantine text.
