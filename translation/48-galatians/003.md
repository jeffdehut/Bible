**1** You foolish Galatians! Who has bewitched you? Before your eyes Jesus Christ was publicly portrayed as crucified.[^1]

**2** I want to learn only this from you: did you receive the Spirit by works of the Law, or by hearing with faith?[^2]

**3** Are you so foolish? Having begun by the Spirit, are you now being made complete by the flesh?[^3]

**4** Did you suffer[^4] so many things for nothing, if indeed it was for nothing?

**5** So does he who supplies the Spirit to you and works miracles among you do it by works of the Law, or by hearing with faith?

**6** It is just as with Abraham: he believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness.[^5]

**7** Know then that it is those who are of faith who are the sons[^6] of Abraham.

**8** And the scripture, foreseeing that God would declare the Gentiles righteous[^7] by faith, proclaimed the good news beforehand to Abraham, saying, "In you all the nations will be blessed."[^8]

**9** So those who are of faith are blessed along with Abraham, the man of faith.[^9]

**10** For all who rely on works of the Law are under a curse. For it is written, "Cursed is everyone who does not continue in all the things written in the book of the Law, to do them."[^10]

**11** Now it is clear that no one is declared righteous before God by the Law, because "the righteous will live by faith."[^11]

**12** But the Law does not rest on faith; on the contrary, "The person who does them will live by them."[^12]

**13** Christ redeemed us from the curse of the Law by becoming a curse for us, for it is written, "Cursed is everyone who is hung on a tree,"[^13]

**14** so that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles, so that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.

**15** Brothers and sisters,[^14] I am speaking in human terms: even with a human covenant,[^15] once it has been ratified, no one sets it aside or adds to it.

**16** Now the promises were spoken to Abraham and to his seed.[^16] It does not say, "And to seeds," as referring to many, but as referring to one, "And to your seed,"[^17] who is Christ.

**17** This is what I mean: the Law, which came 430 years later,[^18] does not cancel a covenant previously ratified by God,[^19] so as to nullify the promise.

**18** For if the inheritance comes from the Law, it no longer comes from the promise; but God gave it freely to Abraham through a promise.

**19** Why then the Law? It was added because of transgressions,[^20] until the seed would come to whom the promise had been made, and it was put in place through angels by the hand of a mediator.[^21]

**20** Now a mediator is not of one party; but God is one.[^22]

**21** Is the Law then against the promises of God? By no means! For if a law had been given that could give life, then righteousness would indeed come from the Law.

**22** But the scripture has shut up all things under sin, so that the promise might be given, by faith in Jesus Christ,[^23] to those who believe.

**23** Before faith came, we were held in custody under the Law, shut up until the faith that was to come would be revealed.

**24** So the Law has been our guardian[^24] until Christ came,[^25] so that we might be declared righteous by faith.

**25** But now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian.

**26** For you are all sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus.[^26]

**27** For all of you who were baptized into Christ have put on Christ.

**28** There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is no male and female,[^27] for you are all one in Christ Jesus.

**29** And if you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham's seed, heirs according to the promise.

[^1]: Many manuscripts read "Who has bewitched you, so that you do not obey the truth? Before your eyes Jesus Christ was publicly portrayed among you as crucified."
[^2]: Or "by believing what you heard", or "by the message that brings faith". Literally "from hearing of faith"; also in verse 5.
[^3]: Greek *sarx*, "flesh". In Paul's moral sense, human nature in its weakness and opposition to God; here perhaps also with a glance at circumcision, a rite performed on the flesh.
[^4]: Or "experience".
[^5]: Genesis 15:6, as in the Septuagint (see Romans 4:3). The Hebrew has "Yahweh", God's personal name, where Paul has "God".
[^6]: Greek *huioi*, "sons". Paul includes women and men (verses 26–28).
[^7]: Greek *dikaioo*, traditionally "justify"; also in verses 11 and 24. *Gentiles* and *nations* later in the verse translate the same Greek word, *ethne*.
[^8]: Genesis 12:3, with "all the nations" from 18:18 (compare 22:18).
[^9]: Or "faithful Abraham". Greek *pistos*, "believing, faithful".
[^10]: Deuteronomy 27:26, in a form close to the Septuagint. The Hebrew reads "Cursed is anyone who does not uphold the words of this law by doing them".
[^11]: Habakkuk 2:4 (see Romans 1:17). Or "the one who is righteous by faith will live". The Hebrew has "by his faithfulness", and the Septuagint "by my faithfulness", that is, God's.
[^12]: Leviticus 18:5 (compare Romans 10:5).
[^13]: Deuteronomy 21:23, which has "cursed by God".
[^14]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike.
[^15]: Greek *diatheke*, "covenant", also used of a will; also in verse 17.
[^16]: Greek *sperma*, "seed", that is, offspring; also in verses 19 and 29. Like Hebrew *zera*, which this translation renders "offspring" in Genesis, it is a singular word that can mean one descendant or many. Paul's argument turns on the singular; in verse 29 he applies the word to all who belong to Christ.
[^17]: Genesis 12:7; 13:15; 17:8; 24:7.
[^18]: Compare Exodus 12:40.
[^19]: Many manuscripts add "in Christ" or "for Christ".
[^20]: Or "for the sake of transgressions", perhaps meaning to show sin up as transgression.
[^21]: Compare Deuteronomy 33:2 in the Septuagint; Acts 7:53.
[^22]: The meaning is uncertain. Perhaps "a mediator stands between two parties, but God acts alone". Compare Deuteronomy 6:4.
[^23]: Or "by the faithfulness of Jesus Christ". Literally "from faith of Jesus Christ".
[^24]: Greek *paidagogos*, a slave who supervised a boy's conduct and took him to school; also in verse 25.
[^25]: Or "to lead us to Christ". Literally "toward Christ".
[^26]: Or "through faith, in Christ Jesus". *Sons*: see verse 7.
[^27]: Literally "there is not male and female", echoing Genesis 1:27.
