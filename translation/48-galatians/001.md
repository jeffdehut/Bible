**1** Paul, an apostle, not from human beings nor through a human being, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised him from the dead,

**2** and all the brothers[^1] who are with me, to the churches of Galatia:

**3** Grace to you and peace from God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ,[^2]

**4** who gave himself for our sins to rescue us from the present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father,

**5** to whom be the glory forever and ever. Amen.

**6** I am astonished that you are so quickly deserting him who called you by the grace of Christ, and are turning to a different good news[^3] —

**7** not that there is another one; but there are some who are troubling you and want to distort the good news of Christ.

**8** But even if we, or an angel from heaven, should proclaim good news to you contrary to what we proclaimed to you, let him be accursed.[^4]

**9** As we have said before, so now I say again: if anyone is proclaiming good news to you contrary to what you received, let him be accursed.

**10** For am I now trying to win the approval of people, or of God? Or am I seeking to please people? If I were still pleasing people, I would not be a servant[^5] of Christ.

**11** For[^6] I want you to know, brothers and sisters,[^7] that the good news that was proclaimed by me is not of human origin.[^8]

**12** For I did not receive it from any human being, nor was I taught it, but I received it through a revelation of Jesus Christ.[^9]

**13** For you have heard of my former way of life in Judaism, how I persecuted the church of God beyond measure and tried to destroy it.

**14** I was advancing in Judaism beyond many of my own age among my people, so extremely zealous was I for the traditions of my ancestors.

**15** But when he who had set me apart from my mother's womb,[^10] and called me through his grace, was pleased

**16** to reveal his Son in me,[^11] so that I might proclaim him as good news among the Gentiles,[^12] I did not immediately consult with any human being,[^13]

**17** nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me. Instead I went away into Arabia,[^14] and returned again to Damascus.

**18** Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to get to know Cephas,[^15] and I stayed with him fifteen days.

**19** But I saw none of the other apostles except James, the Lord's brother.[^16]

**20** In what I am writing to you, I assure you before God that I am not lying.[^17]

**21** Then I went into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.

**22** I was still unknown by sight to the churches of Judea that are in Christ.

**23** They only kept hearing, "The one who once persecuted us is now proclaiming the good news of the faith he once tried to destroy."

**24** And they glorified God because of me.

[^1]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", here Paul's companions; the word can include women.
[^2]: Some manuscripts read "from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ".
[^3]: Greek *euangelion*, "good news", traditionally "gospel"; also in verses 7 and 11. *Proclaim good news* in verses 8–9, 11, 16 and 23 is the related verb, *euangelizo*.
[^4]: Greek *anathema*, something devoted to God for destruction, and so under a curse; also in verse 9.
[^5]: Or "slave". Greek *doulos*.
[^6]: Many manuscripts read "Now".
[^7]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike.
[^8]: Literally "is not according to a human being".
[^9]: That is, a revelation from Jesus Christ, or a revelation of who Jesus Christ is.
[^10]: Compare Isaiah 49:1; Jeremiah 1:5. Many manuscripts read "when God, who had set me apart". The SBLGNT leaves out "God", following a marginal reading of Westcott and Hort.
[^11]: Or "to me".
[^12]: Or "nations". Greek *ethne* means both.
[^13]: Literally "flesh and blood".
[^14]: Probably the kingdom of the Nabateans, to the south and east of Damascus.
[^15]: *Cephas* is the Aramaic for "rock", the name translated in Greek as Peter (John 1:42). Many manuscripts read "Peter".
[^16]: Or "I saw no other apostle; I saw only James, the Lord's brother".
[^17]: Literally "What I am writing to you, look, before God, I am not lying".
