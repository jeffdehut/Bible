**1** Finally,[^1] my brothers and sisters,[^2] rejoice in the Lord. To write the same things to you is no trouble for me, and it is a safeguard for you.

**2** Watch out for the dogs, watch out for the evil workers, watch out for the mutilation.[^3]

**3** For we are the circumcision, who worship by the Spirit of God and boast in Christ Jesus and put no confidence in the flesh,[^4]

**4** though I myself have reason for confidence in the flesh too. If anyone else thinks they have reason for confidence in the flesh, I have more:

**5** circumcised on the eighth day, of the people of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew born of Hebrews;[^5] as to the Law, a Pharisee;

**6** as to zeal, a persecutor of the church; as to the righteousness that is in the Law, blameless.

**7** But whatever gains I had, these I have come to regard as loss because of Christ.

**8** More than that, I regard everything as loss because of the surpassing worth of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord. For his sake I have suffered the loss of all things, and I regard them as rubbish,[^6] so that I may gain Christ

**9** and be found in him, not having a righteousness of my own that comes from the Law, but the righteousness that comes through faith in Christ,[^7] the righteousness from God that depends on faith,

**10** that I may know him and the power of his resurrection and the sharing of his sufferings, being conformed to his death,

**11** if somehow I may attain to the resurrection from the dead.

**12** Not that I have already obtained it or have already been made perfect,[^8] but I press on to take hold of it, because Christ[^9] has taken hold of me.[^10]

**13** Brothers and sisters, I do not[^11] consider that I have taken hold of it myself. But one thing I do: forgetting what lies behind and straining forward to what lies ahead,

**14** I press on toward the goal for the prize of the upward call[^12] of God in Christ Jesus.

**15** So let those of us who are mature[^13] think this way. And if you think differently about anything, God will reveal that to you too.

**16** Only let us keep in step with what we have already reached.[^14]

**17** Brothers and sisters, join in imitating me, and watch those who live[^15] as we do, since you have us as an example.

**18** For many live, as I have often told you and now tell you even with tears, as enemies of the cross of Christ.

**19** Their end is destruction, their god is their belly,[^16] and their glory is in their shame, with their minds set on earthly things.

**20** For our citizenship[^17] is in heaven, and from there we eagerly await a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ,

**21** who will transform our lowly body[^18] to be like his glorious body, by the working of the power that enables him even to subject all things to himself.

[^1]: Or "Furthermore". Greek *to loipon*, "as for the rest".
[^2]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike; also in verses 13 and 17.
[^3]: Greek *katatome*, "cutting up, mutilation", a play on *peritome*, "circumcision", in verse 3.
[^4]: Greek *sarx*, "flesh": here what is merely human and outward, such as the physical rite and the descent listed in verses 5–6; also in verse 4. Compare "the flesh" in Paul's moral sense, human nature in its weakness and opposition to God.
[^5]: Literally "a Hebrew from Hebrews".
[^6]: Greek *skybala*, "refuse, dung".
[^7]: Or "through the faithfulness of Christ". Literally "through faith of Christ".
[^8]: Or "have already reached the goal". Greek *teleioo*, "complete, make perfect".
[^9]: The SBLGNT here follows Tregelles; other editions read "Christ Jesus".
[^10]: Or "to take hold of that for which Christ took hold of me".
[^11]: Some manuscripts read "not yet".
[^12]: Or "heavenly call". Literally "the call above".
[^13]: Greek *teleioi*, "mature, complete", from the same root as "made perfect" in verse 12.
[^14]: Literally "Only, to what we have attained, keep in line by the same." Many manuscripts add "rule; let us think the same thing".
[^15]: Literally "walk", a common biblical picture for a way of life; also in verse 18.
[^16]: Or "stomach", that is, their own appetites.
[^17]: Greek *politeuma*, "commonwealth, state, citizenship", related to the verb in 1:27.
[^18]: Literally "the body of our humiliation". *His glorious body*: literally "the body of his glory".
