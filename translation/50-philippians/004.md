**1** Therefore, my brothers and sisters,[^1] whom I love and long for, my joy and crown, stand firm in the Lord in this way, my beloved.

**2** I urge Euodia and I urge Syntyche to agree with each other[^2] in the Lord.

**3** Yes, and I ask you too, true companion,[^3] help these women, who have struggled together with me in the good news, along with Clement and the rest of my fellow workers, whose names are in the book of life.

**4** Rejoice in the Lord always. Again I will say, rejoice.

**5** Let your gentleness[^4] be known to everyone. The Lord is near.[^5]

**6** Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything, by prayer and petition with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God.

**7** And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.

**8** Finally, brothers and sisters, whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is just, whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, whatever is commendable, if there is any excellence and if there is anything worthy of praise, think about these things.

**9** What you have learned and received and heard and seen in me, practice these things. And the God of peace will be with you.

**10** I rejoiced greatly in the Lord that now at last you have revived your concern for me.[^6] You were indeed concerned, but you had no opportunity.

**11** Not that I am speaking out of need, for I have learned to be content[^7] in whatever circumstances I am.

**12** I know how to be brought low, and I know how to have plenty. In any and every circumstance I have learned the secret[^8] of being well fed and of going hungry, of having plenty and of being in need.

**13** I can do all things through him who strengthens me.[^9]

**14** Yet you did well to share with me in my trouble.

**15** And you Philippians know yourselves that in the beginning of the good news, when I left Macedonia, no church shared with me in the matter of giving and receiving[^10] except you only,

**16** for even in Thessalonica you sent help for my need more than once.[^11]

**17** Not that I seek the gift, but I seek the fruit that increases to your account.

**18** I have received full payment, and more. I am well supplied, now that I have received from Epaphroditus what you sent, a pleasing aroma,[^12] an acceptable sacrifice, pleasing to God.

**19** And my God will supply every need of yours according to his riches in glory in Christ Jesus.

**20** To our God and Father be the glory forever and ever.[^13] Amen.

**21** Greet every one of God's holy people[^14] in Christ Jesus. The brothers and sisters who are with me greet you.

**22** All God's holy people greet you, especially those of Caesar's household.[^15]

**23** The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.[^16]

[^1]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike; also in verses 8 and 21.
[^2]: Literally "to think the same thing".
[^3]: Greek *syzygos*, "yokefellow, partner". It may also be a personal name: "true Syzygus".
[^4]: Or "forbearance", "reasonableness".
[^5]: Or "The Lord is close at hand".
[^6]: Literally "you have blossomed again in thinking of me".
[^7]: Greek *autarkes*, "self-sufficient", a word the Stoic philosophers used for a person not ruled by circumstances.
[^8]: Greek *myeo*, "initiate", a word used of initiation into the mystery religions.
[^9]: Many manuscripts read "through Christ who strengthens me".
[^10]: Literally "in an account of giving and receiving", the language of business; also "account" in verse 17 and "received full payment" in verse 18.
[^11]: Literally "both once and twice".
[^12]: Literally "a smell of fragrance", the language of the sacrifices in the Old Testament (Exodus 29:18; Leviticus 1:9).
[^13]: Literally "to the ages of the ages".
[^14]: Literally "every holy one", traditionally "every saint"; also "God's holy people" in verse 22.
[^15]: That is, people in the emperor's service, whether slaves or freed slaves.
[^16]: Many manuscripts read "be with you all. Amen."
