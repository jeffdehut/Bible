**1** David spoke the words of this song to the LORD[^1] on the day when the LORD rescued him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul.[^2]

**2** He said: "The LORD is my rock, my fortress and my deliverer.[^3]

**3** My God, my rock,[^4] in whom I take refuge; my shield and the horn[^5] of my salvation, my stronghold and my refuge; my savior, you save me from violence.[^6]

**4** I call on the LORD, who is worthy of praise, and I am saved from my enemies.

**5** For the breakers of death[^7] surrounded me; the torrents of destruction[^8] terrified me.

**6** The cords of Sheol[^9] coiled around me; the snares of death confronted me.

**7** In my distress I called on the LORD; to my God I called. From his temple he heard my voice, and my cry for help reached his ears.

**8** Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations of the heavens[^10] quaked; they shook because he was angry.

**9** Smoke went up from his nostrils, and devouring fire from his mouth; coals blazed out from him.

**10** He bent the heavens and came down, with thick darkness under his feet.

**11** He rode on a cherub[^11] and flew; he was seen[^12] on the wings of the wind.

**12** He made darkness a shelter around him, a mass of waters,[^13] thick clouds of the skies.

**13** Out of the brightness before him coals of fire blazed.

**14** The LORD thundered from heaven; the Most High[^14] uttered his voice.

**15** He sent out arrows and scattered them, lightning, and threw them into confusion.

**16** The channels of the sea were seen; the foundations of the world were laid bare at the rebuke of the LORD, at the blast of the breath of his nostrils.

**17** He reached down from on high and took me; he drew me out of many waters.

**18** He rescued me from my strong enemy, from those who hated me, for they were too strong for me.

**19** They confronted me on the day of my disaster, but the LORD was my support.

**20** He brought me out into a wide place; he rescued me because he delighted in me.

**21** The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands he repaid me.

**22** For I have kept the ways of the LORD, and I have not done evil by turning from my God.

**23** For all his rulings[^15] are before me, and from his decrees I have not turned aside.[^16]

**24** I was blameless before him, and I kept myself from my guilt.[^17]

**25** So the LORD has repaid me according to my righteousness, according to my cleanness in his sight.

**26** With the faithful you show yourself faithful;[^18] with the blameless warrior you show yourself blameless.

**27** With the pure you show yourself pure, but with the crooked you show yourself shrewd.

**28** You save a humble people, but your eyes are on the haughty, to bring them low.

**29** For you are my lamp, LORD; the LORD lights up my darkness.

**30** For with you I can charge against a troop;[^19] with my God I can leap over a wall.

**31** This God, his way is perfect; the word of the LORD is refined.[^20] He is a shield to all who take refuge in him.

**32** For who is God besides the LORD? And who is a rock besides our God?

**33** This God is my strong refuge,[^21] and he sets my way free and blameless.[^22]

**34** He makes my feet[^23] like the feet of a deer, and sets me on my heights.

**35** He trains my hands for war, so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze.

**36** You have given me the shield of your salvation; your help[^24] has made me great.

**37** You make a wide place for my steps beneath me, and my ankles have not slipped.

**38** I pursued my enemies and destroyed them; I did not turn back until they were finished off.

**39** I finished them off and crushed them, so that they could not rise; they fell beneath my feet.

**40** You armed me with strength for battle; you made those who rose against me bow down beneath me.

**41** You made my enemies turn their backs to me;[^25] those who hated me, I destroyed them.

**42** They looked for help,[^26] but there was no one to save them; they looked to the LORD, but he did not answer them.

**43** I beat them as fine as the dust of the earth; I crushed them and trampled them like the mud of the streets.

**44** You rescued me from the strife of my people; you kept me as the head of nations. A people I did not know now serve me.

**45** Foreigners come cringing to me;[^27] as soon as they hear of me, they obey me.

**46** Foreigners lose heart, and they come limping[^28] out of their strongholds.

**47** The LORD lives! Blessed be my rock! Exalted be God, the rock of my salvation,

**48** the God who grants me vengeance and brings peoples down under me,

**49** who brings me out from my enemies. You lift me high above those who rise against me; you rescue me from violent men.

**50** So I will praise you, LORD, among the nations, and sing praises to your name.[^29]

**51** He is a tower of salvation[^30] for his king; he shows faithful love[^31] to his anointed,[^32] to David and his offspring forever."

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: This song appears again, with small differences, as Psalm 18. From verse 4 on, its verse numbers match those of Psalm 18 in the Hebrew; in English Bibles the psalm's verses are numbered one lower. The notes below give some of the differences.
[^3]: Psalm 18 begins "I love you, LORD, my strength" (18:2) before these words.
[^4]: Literally "the God of my rock". Psalm 18:3 has "my God, my rock".
[^5]: A symbol of strength, like the horns of a wild ox.
[^6]: Psalm 18:3 does not have "and my refuge; my savior, you save me from violence".
[^7]: Psalm 18:5 has "the cords of death".
[^8]: Hebrew *beliyya'al*, "worthlessness", here perhaps destruction or the realm of death.
[^9]: Hebrew *Sheol*: the realm of the dead.
[^10]: Psalm 18:8 has "the foundations of the mountains".
[^11]: Hebrew *keruv*, one of the winged heavenly beings (see 6:2).
[^12]: Hebrew *vayyera*. Psalm 18:11 and many Hebrew manuscripts of Samuel read *vayyede*, "he swooped", which differs by one similar letter.
[^13]: Hebrew *chashrat mayim*, a phrase found only here, perhaps "a gathering of waters". Psalm 18:12 has "dark waters".
[^14]: Hebrew *Elyon*, a title of God.
[^15]: The Hebrew text is written "his ruling" but read "his rulings".
[^16]: Literally "and his decrees, I do not turn aside from it".
[^17]: That is, from doing wrong.
[^18]: Hebrew *chasid*, "faithful, loyal", and the verb from the same root, related to *chesed*, "faithful love".
[^19]: Or "I can run through a barricade".
[^20]: That is, tested and pure, like metal refined in a furnace.
[^21]: Literally "God is my refuge of strength". Psalm 18:33 has "the God who arms me with strength".
[^22]: Literally "and he sets free blameless my way". The meaning of the verb is uncertain. The Hebrew text is written "his way" but read "my way". Psalm 18:33 has "and makes my way blameless".
[^23]: The Hebrew text is written "his feet" but read "my feet".
[^24]: Hebrew *anotkha*, perhaps "your answering" or "your help". Psalm 18:36 has "your gentleness" and adds "your right hand supports me".
[^25]: Literally "My enemies, you gave me their neck."
[^26]: Hebrew *yish'u*, "they looked". Psalm 18:42 has "they cried for help".
[^27]: Or "pretend to obey me".
[^28]: Hebrew *yachgeru*, perhaps "limp", or "gird themselves". Psalm 18:46 has "come trembling".
[^29]: Psalm 18:50 (18:49 in most English Bibles) is quoted in Romans 15:9.
[^30]: The Hebrew text is written "He gives great victories", as in Psalm 18:51, but read "He is a tower of salvation".
[^31]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^32]: Hebrew *meshicho*, "his anointed one", the king anointed at the LORD's command.
