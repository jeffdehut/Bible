**1** My son, if you accept my words and store up my commands within you,

**2** turning your ear to wisdom and inclining your heart to insight,[^1]

**3** if you call out for understanding[^2] and raise your voice for insight,

**4** if you look for it as for silver and search for it as for hidden treasure,

**5** then you will understand the fear of the LORD[^3] and find the knowledge[^4] of God.

**6** For the LORD gives wisdom; from his mouth come knowledge and insight.

**7** He stores up[^5] sound wisdom[^6] for the upright; he is a shield to those who walk in integrity,

**8** guarding the paths of justice and watching over the way of his faithful ones.[^7]

**9** Then you will understand righteousness and justice and fairness, every good path.

**10** For wisdom will come into your heart, and knowledge will be pleasant to your soul.

**11** Discretion[^8] will watch over you; insight will guard you,

**12** to rescue you from the way of evil, from men who speak perversely,

**13** who abandon the paths of uprightness to walk in the ways of darkness,

**14** who delight in doing evil and rejoice in the perversity of evil,

**15** whose paths are crooked and whose ways are devious;

**16** to rescue you from the strange woman,[^9] from the foreign woman[^10] with her smooth words,

**17** who abandons the companion of her youth[^11] and forgets the covenant of her God.[^12]

**18** For her house sinks down to death,[^13] and her paths lead to the dead.[^14]

**19** None who go to her come back, and they do not reach the paths of life.

**20** So you will walk in the way of good people and keep to the paths of the righteous.[^15]

**21** For the upright will live in the land,[^16] and the blameless will remain in it,

**22** but the wicked[^17] will be cut off from the land, and the treacherous will be torn out of it.

[^1]: Hebrew *tevunah*, from the same root as *binah*, "understanding" (verse 3). The two words are often paired; this translation uses "insight" for *tevunah* and "understanding" for *binah*. Also in verses 3, 6 and 11.
[^2]: Hebrew *binah*: the ability to discern, to see what matters and tell things apart.
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles. On *the fear of the LORD*, see 1:7.
[^4]: Hebrew *da'at*: knowledge gained by learning and experience. *The knowledge of God* means knowing him personally, not only knowing about him.
[^5]: The Hebrew text is written "And he has stored up" but read "He stores up".
[^6]: Hebrew *tushiyyah*: sound wisdom, and the success it brings.
[^7]: The Hebrew text is written "his faithful one" but read "his faithful ones". Hebrew *chasidim*, those who are loyal to him, a word related to *chesed*, "faithful love".
[^8]: Hebrew *mezimmah*: the ability to think things through and plan.
[^9]: Hebrew *ishah zarah*. *Zar* means "strange, outside, not one's own": someone outside one's family or circle. In chapters 2–7 the *strange woman* is a woman outside a man's marriage, usually another man's wife (verse 17; 6:26; 7:19). Other renderings are "the forbidden woman" and "the adulteress".
[^10]: Hebrew *nokhriyyah*, "foreign woman". The word can mean a woman from another people, or simply one who is a stranger to a man's household. It stands in parallel with *ishah zarah* in 5:20, 6:24 and 7:5.
[^11]: That is, her husband.
[^12]: Probably her marriage covenant, made before God (compare Malachi 2:14).
[^13]: Literally "For she sinks down to death, her house", or "For her house sinks down to death". The grammar of the Hebrew is unusual.
[^14]: Hebrew *rephaim*, the spirits of the dead (also 9:18; 21:16).
[^15]: Hebrew *tsaddiqim*, plural of *tsaddiq*: a righteous person, one who does what is right toward God and other people. The opposite is *rasha*, "wicked" (verse 22).
[^16]: Or "the earth". Also in verse 22.
[^17]: Hebrew *resha'im*, plural of *rasha*: a wicked person, one who is guilty of wrong.
