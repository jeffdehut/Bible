**1** Listen, sons, to a father's discipline,[^1] and pay attention so that you may know understanding.[^2]

**2** For I am giving you good teaching; do not abandon my instruction.

**3** When I was a son to my father, tender, the only child in my mother's eyes,

**4** he taught me and said to me, "Let your heart hold on to my words; keep my commands, and live.[^3]

**5** Get wisdom, get understanding; do not forget the words of my mouth, and do not turn away from them.

**6** Do not abandon her, and she will guard you; love her, and she will watch over you.

**7** The beginning of wisdom is this: get wisdom. Whatever else you get, get understanding.[^4]

**8** Prize her, and she will lift you up; she will bring you honor if you embrace her.

**9** She will place a graceful garland on your head; she will present you with a beautiful crown."

**10** Listen, my son, and accept my words, and the years of your life will be many.

**11** I have taught you in the way of wisdom; I have led you along straight paths.

**12** When you walk, your steps will not be hampered, and if you run, you will not stumble.

**13** Hold on to discipline; do not let it go. Guard her,[^5] for she is your life.

**14** Do not set foot on the path of the wicked,[^6] and do not walk in the way of evildoers.

**15** Avoid it; do not travel on it. Turn away from it and pass on.

**16** For they cannot sleep unless they have done wrong; they are robbed of sleep unless they have made someone stumble.[^7]

**17** For they eat the bread of wickedness and drink the wine of violence.

**18** But the path of the righteous[^8] is like the light of dawn, shining brighter and brighter until full day.[^9]

**19** The way of the wicked is like deep darkness; they do not know what makes them stumble.

**20** My son, pay attention to my words; turn your ear to my sayings.

**21** Do not let them slip from your sight; keep them deep within your heart.

**22** For they are life to those who find them, and healing for their whole body.

**23** Above all else that you guard, guard your heart,[^10] for from it flow the springs of life.

**24** Put away from you a crooked mouth, and keep devious lips far from you.

**25** Let your eyes look straight ahead, and your gaze be fixed straight in front of you.

**26** Make level the path for your feet,[^11] and all your ways will be secure.

**27** Do not turn to the right or to the left; keep your foot away from evil.[^12]

[^1]: Hebrew *musar*: discipline, training and correction (see 1:2). Also in verse 13.
[^2]: Hebrew *binah*, as in 1:2. Also in verses 5 and 7.
[^3]: The father's quotation of his own father may end here, or at the end of verse 5 or of verse 9.
[^4]: Or "Wisdom is the first thing: get wisdom, and with all you have, get understanding". Literally "and with all your getting". The Septuagint does not have this verse.
[^5]: The pronoun is feminine, while the Hebrew word for *discipline* is masculine. *Her* is probably wisdom, as in verses 6–9.
[^6]: Hebrew *resha'im*, plural of *rasha*: a wicked person, one who is guilty of wrong (see 2:22). Also in verse 19.
[^7]: The Hebrew text is written "unless they stumble" but read "unless they make someone stumble".
[^8]: Hebrew *tsaddiqim*, plural of *tsaddiq*: one who does what is right (see 2:20).
[^9]: Literally "until the day is established", perhaps noon.
[^10]: Or "With all watchfulness guard your heart". In Hebrew thought the heart is the center of thought and will as well as feeling.
[^11]: Or "Watch the path of your feet". Hebrews 12:13 echoes this line in the wording of the Septuagint: "make straight paths for your feet".
[^12]: The Septuagint adds: "For God knows the ways on the right, but those on the left are crooked. He will make your paths straight and lead your journeys in peace."
