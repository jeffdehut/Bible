**1** My son, do not forget my instruction, and let your heart keep my commands,

**2** for they will add to your life many days and years, and peace.[^1]

**3** Do not let faithful love and truth[^2] leave you. Tie them around your neck; write them on the tablet of your heart.

**4** Then you will find favor and a good name[^3] in the sight of God and of people.

**5** Trust in Yahweh[^4] with all your heart, and do not rely on your own understanding.[^5]

**6** In all your ways acknowledge him,[^6] and he will make your paths straight.

**7** Do not be wise in your own eyes; fear Yahweh and turn away from evil.

**8** It will be healing for your body[^7] and refreshment for your bones.

**9** Honor Yahweh with your wealth and with the firstfruits of all your harvest.

**10** Then your barns will be filled with plenty, and your vats will overflow with new wine.

**11** My son, do not reject the discipline[^8] of Yahweh, and do not resent his correction,

**12** for Yahweh corrects the one he loves, as a father corrects the son he delights in.[^9]

**13** Happy[^10] is the one who finds wisdom, the one who gains insight.[^11]

**14** For she is more profitable than silver, and her yield is better than fine gold.

**15** She is more precious than coral;[^12] nothing you desire can compare with her.

**16** Long life is in her right hand; in her left hand are riches and honor.

**17** Her ways are pleasant ways, and all her paths are peace.

**18** She is a tree of life[^13] to those who take hold of her; those who hold on to her are happy.

**19** By wisdom Yahweh founded the earth; by insight he established the heavens.

**20** By his knowledge the deep waters burst open, and the clouds drop down dew.

**21** My son, do not let them out of your sight:[^14] keep sound wisdom[^15] and discretion,[^16]

**22** and they will be life for your soul and a lovely ornament for your neck.[^17]

**23** Then you will walk on your way in safety, and your foot will not stumble.

**24** When you lie down, you will not be afraid; when you lie down, your sleep will be sweet.

**25** Do not be afraid of sudden terror, or of the ruin of the wicked when it comes.

**26** For Yahweh will be your confidence[^18] and will keep your foot from being caught.

**27** Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due, when it is in your power to do it.

**28** Do not say to your neighbor,[^19] "Go away and come back; I will give it to you tomorrow," when you have it with you.

**29** Do not plot harm against your neighbor while he lives trustingly beside you.

**30** Do not quarrel with anyone for no reason, when he has done you no harm.

**31** Do not envy a violent man, and do not choose any of his ways.

**32** For the devious are detestable[^20] to Yahweh, but he takes the upright into his confidence.

**33** Yahweh's curse is on the house of the wicked, but he blesses the home of the righteous.

**34** He mocks the mockers,[^21] but he gives favor to the humble.[^22]

**35** The wise will inherit honor, but fools[^23] carry off disgrace.[^24]

[^1]: Literally "length of days and years of life and peace (*shalom*) they will add to you".
[^2]: Hebrew *chesed ve-emet*. *Chesed* is loyal, faithful love; *emet* means both "truth" and "faithfulness, reliability".
[^3]: Hebrew *sekhel tov*, literally "good sense", here perhaps the good name that good sense earns.
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^5]: Hebrew *binah*, as in 1:2.
[^6]: Literally "know him".
[^7]: Literally "your navel". The Septuagint reads "your body".
[^8]: Hebrew *musar*: discipline, training and correction (see 1:2). Verses 11–12 are quoted in Hebrews 12:5–6, following the Septuagint, which reads the last line of verse 12 as "and he punishes every son he accepts".
[^9]: Or "even as a father the son he delights in".
[^10]: Hebrew *ashrei*: happy, fortunate; traditionally "blessed". *Happy* in verse 18 translates a verb from the same root.
[^11]: Hebrew *tevunah*, from the same root as *binah*, "understanding" (see 2:2). Also in verse 19.
[^12]: Hebrew *peninim*, probably red coral or pearls; traditionally "rubies".
[^13]: Compare Genesis 2:9; 3:22. Also in 11:30; 13:12; 15:4.
[^14]: Literally "do not let them slip from your eyes". *Them* are the things named in the next line.
[^15]: Hebrew *tushiyyah*, as in 2:7.
[^16]: Hebrew *mezimmah*, as in 1:4.
[^17]: Literally "and grace for your neck", as in 1:9.
[^18]: Or "at your side". Hebrew *kesel* can mean "confidence" or "side, loins".
[^19]: The Hebrew text is written "to your neighbors" but read "to your neighbor".
[^20]: Hebrew *to'evah*, something loathsome, traditionally "an abomination". The phrase "detestable to Yahweh" is frequent in Proverbs.
[^21]: Hebrew *letsim*, as in 1:22: those who scorn wisdom and laugh at correction. The verb translated *mocks* is from the same root.
[^22]: The Hebrew text is written "to the afflicted" but read "to the humble". James 4:6 and 1 Peter 5:5 quote this verse from the Septuagint: "God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble."
[^23]: Hebrew *kesilim*, plural of *kesil* (see 1:22).
[^24]: Literally "but fools lift up disgrace". The sense may be that disgrace is the only honor fools receive.
