**1** The plans of the heart belong to people, but the answer of the tongue comes from Yahweh.[^1]

**2** All a person's ways seem pure in his own eyes, but Yahweh weighs the spirits.[^2]

**3** Commit your works to Yahweh,[^3] and your plans will be established.

**4** Yahweh has made everything for its purpose,[^4] even the wicked[^5] for the day of disaster.

**5** Everyone who is proud in heart is detestable to Yahweh; you can be sure[^6] he will not go unpunished.

**6** By faithful love and truth[^7] iniquity is atoned for,[^8] and by the fear of Yahweh one turns away from evil.

**7** When a person's ways please Yahweh, he makes even his enemies be at peace with him.

**8** Better a little with righteousness than great income with injustice.

**9** A person's heart plans his way, but Yahweh directs his steps.

**10** Inspired decisions[^9] are on the lips of a king; his mouth does not betray justice.

**11** Honest balances and scales belong to Yahweh; all the weights in the bag are his work.

**12** Doing wickedness is detestable to kings,[^10] for a throne is established by righteousness.

**13** Righteous lips are the delight of kings, and they love one who speaks what is right.

**14** A king's wrath is a messenger of death, but a wise person will appease it.

**15** In the light of a king's face is life, and his favor is like a cloud that brings the spring rain.[^11]

**16** How much better to get wisdom than gold! To get understanding[^12] is better than silver.

**17** The highway of the upright turns away from evil; whoever watches his way preserves his life.

**18** Pride comes before destruction, and a haughty spirit before a fall.

**19** Better to be lowly in spirit with the humble[^13] than to divide plunder with the proud.

**20** Whoever pays attention to the word[^14] will find good, and happy[^15] is the one who trusts in Yahweh.

**21** The wise in heart are called discerning, and sweet speech increases learning.[^16]

**22** Good sense[^17] is a fountain of life to those who have it, but folly is the discipline of fools.[^18]

**23** The heart of the wise makes his mouth wise and adds learning to his lips.

**24** Pleasant words are a honeycomb, sweet to the soul and healing to the bones.

**25** There is a way that seems right to a person, but in the end it leads to death.[^19]

**26** A worker's appetite works for him, for his hunger[^20] drives him on.

**27** A worthless man[^21] digs up evil, and on his lips it is like a scorching fire.

**28** A perverse person spreads strife, and a gossip[^22] separates close friends.

**29** A violent man entices his neighbor and leads him in a way that is not good.

**30** Whoever winks his eyes is plotting perversity; whoever purses his lips has brought evil about.

**31** Gray hair is a glorious crown; it is found in the way of righteousness.

**32** Better to be slow to anger than a mighty warrior, and to rule one's spirit than to capture a city.

**33** The lot is cast into the lap, but its every decision is from Yahweh.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Or "the motives". Hebrew *ruchot*, plural of *ruach*.
[^3]: Literally "Roll your works onto Yahweh".
[^4]: Or "for himself".
[^5]: Hebrew *rasha*: one who is guilty of wrong (see 2:22).
[^6]: Literally "hand to hand", as in 11:21.
[^7]: Hebrew *chesed ve-emet*: faithful love and reliability (see 3:3).
[^8]: Literally "is covered". Hebrew *kipper*, the verb used for making atonement in the sacrifices (Leviticus 16:30).
[^9]: Hebrew *qesem*, "divination", elsewhere forbidden (Deuteronomy 18:10); here a decision given as if with divine authority.
[^10]: Or "It is detestable for kings to do wickedness".
[^11]: Hebrew *malqosh*, the late rain of spring that ripened the crops.
[^12]: Hebrew *binah*, as in 1:2.
[^13]: The Hebrew text is written "with the poor" but read "with the humble".
[^14]: Or "Whoever handles a matter wisely". Hebrew *maskil*, as in 10:5.
[^15]: Hebrew *ashrei*: happy, fortunate; traditionally "blessed".
[^16]: Hebrew *leqach*, "learning", as in 1:5, or "persuasiveness" (7:21). Also in verse 23. Literally "sweetness of lips".
[^17]: Hebrew *sekhel*, "good sense, insight".
[^18]: Or "but fools are disciplined by their own folly". Literally "and the discipline (*musar*) of fools (*ewilim*) is folly (*iwwelet*)". On *ewil*, see 1:7.
[^19]: The verse repeats 14:12.
[^20]: Literally "his mouth".
[^21]: Hebrew *ish beliyya'al*, "a man of worthlessness" (see 6:12).
[^22]: Hebrew *nirgan*, "whisperer". In the Hebrew text one letter of this word is written small, a scribal mark.
