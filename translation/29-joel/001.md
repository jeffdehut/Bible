**1** The word of Yahweh[^1] that came to Joel[^2] son of Pethuel.

**2** Hear this, you elders! Listen, all who live in the land! Has anything like this happened in your days, or in the days of your ancestors?

**3** Tell your children about it, and let your children tell their children, and their children the next generation.

**4** What the cutting locust left, the swarming locust has eaten; what the swarming locust left, the hopping locust has eaten; and what the hopping locust left, the destroying locust has eaten.[^3]

**5** Wake up, you drunkards, and weep! Wail, all you wine drinkers, because of the sweet wine, for it has been cut off from your mouth.

**6** For a nation has come up against my land, mighty and without number. Its teeth are a lion's teeth, and it has the fangs of a lioness.

**7** It has laid waste my vine and splintered my fig tree. It has stripped off their bark and thrown it away; their branches are left white.

**8** Mourn like a young woman[^4] dressed in sackcloth for the husband of her youth.[^5]

**9** Grain offering and drink offering are cut off from the house of Yahweh. The priests mourn, those who serve Yahweh.

**10** The fields are ruined; the ground mourns,[^6] for the grain is ruined, the new wine dries up, and the olive oil fails.

**11** Be dismayed, you farmers;[^7] wail, you vinedressers, over the wheat and the barley, because the harvest of the field is lost.

**12** The vine has dried up, and the fig tree withers. Pomegranate, palm and apple tree, all the trees of the field have dried up. Surely joy has dried up and left the people.

**13** Put on sackcloth and lament, you priests! Wail, you who serve at the altar! Come, spend the night in sackcloth, you who serve my God! For grain offering and drink offering are withheld from the house of your God.

**14** Set apart a fast;[^8] call a solemn assembly. Gather the elders and all who live in the land to the house of Yahweh your God, and cry out to Yahweh.

**15** Alas for the day! For the day of Yahweh is near; it comes like destruction from the Almighty.[^9]

**16** Has not food been cut off before our eyes, joy and gladness from the house of our God?

**17** The seeds shrivel under their clods.[^10] The storehouses are desolate; the granaries are torn down, for the grain has dried up.

**18** How the animals groan! The herds of cattle wander in confusion, because there is no pasture for them. Even the flocks of sheep suffer punishment.[^11]

**19** To you, Yahweh, I call, for fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness, and flame has burned up all the trees of the field.

**20** Even the wild animals long for you,[^12] because the streams of water have dried up, and fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Joel means "Yahweh is God".
[^3]: Hebrew *gazam*, *arbeh*, *yeleq* and *chasil*, four words for locusts. They may be different kinds of locust or stages in the insect's growth; the exact meanings are uncertain. *Arbeh* is the usual word for locust. Also in 2:25.
[^4]: Hebrew *betulah*, a young woman of marriageable age.
[^5]: Literally "for the *ba'al* of her youth", probably the man she was to marry.
[^6]: Or "dries up". The Hebrew verb can have either meaning.
[^7]: Or "Be ashamed". Hebrew *hovishu* sounds like *hovish*, "dries up", in verses 10 and 12.
[^8]: Literally "Sanctify a fast". Also in 2:15.
[^9]: A play on words: *shod*, "destruction", and *Shaddai*, "the Almighty", a title of God whose meaning is uncertain. The same line is found in Isaiah 13:6.
[^10]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. Three of its words are found only here.
[^11]: Literally "are held guilty".
[^12]: Literally "pant for you".
