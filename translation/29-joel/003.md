**1** After this I will pour out my Spirit[^1] on all people.[^2] Your sons and your daughters will prophesy; your old men will dream dreams, and your young men will see visions.

**2** Even on the male and female servants[^3] in those days I will pour out my Spirit.

**3** I will show wonders in the heavens and on the earth: blood and fire and columns of smoke.

**4** The sun will be turned to darkness and the moon to blood before the great and terrible day of the LORD[^4] comes.

**5** And everyone who calls on the name of the LORD will be saved,[^5] for on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be those who escape, as the LORD has said, and among the survivors will be those whom the LORD calls.

[^1]: Verses 1–5 are 2:28–32 in most English Bibles. Peter quotes them at Pentecost in Acts 2:17–21, following the Septuagint, with "in the last days" for "after this" and some other changes. *My Spirit*: or "my spirit", Hebrew *ruach*.
[^2]: Literally "all flesh".
[^3]: Or "slaves". Acts 2:18 has "my servants".
[^4]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^5]: Or "will escape". The first half of the verse is quoted in Acts 2:21 and Romans 10:13.
