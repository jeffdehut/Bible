**1** If you see a fellow Israelite's[^1] ox or sheep wandering off, do not ignore it.[^2] You must take it back to him.

**2** If he does not live near you, or you do not know who he is, bring the animal home and keep it with you until he comes looking for it. Then give it back to him.

**3** Do the same with his donkey, and with his cloak, and with anything else he has lost that you find. You must not ignore it.

**4** If you see a fellow Israelite's donkey or ox fallen on the road, do not ignore it. You must help him get it back on its feet.

**5** A woman must not wear a man's things,[^3] and a man must not put on a woman's clothing, for anyone who does these things is detestable to Yahweh[^4] your God.

**6** If you come across a bird's nest beside the road, in any tree or on the ground, with young birds or eggs, and the mother sitting on the young or on the eggs, do not take the mother along with the young.

**7** You must let the mother go, but you may take the young for yourself, so that it may go well with you and you may live long.

**8** When you build a new house, make a railing around your roof,[^5] so that you will not bring the guilt of bloodshed on your house if someone falls from it.

**9** Do not sow your vineyard with a second kind of seed, or the whole crop, both the seed you have sown and the fruit of the vineyard, will be forfeited.[^6]

**10** Do not plow with an ox and a donkey together.

**11** Do not wear clothing of mixed material,[^7] wool and linen together.

**12** Make tassels[^8] on the four corners of the cloak you wrap yourself in.

**13** Suppose a man marries a woman and sleeps with her,[^9] and then turns against her.[^10]

**14** He makes up charges against her[^11] and gives her a bad name, saying, "I married this woman, but when I came near her I did not find the evidence of her virginity."[^12]

**15** Then the young woman's[^13] father and mother are to take the evidence of her virginity and bring it out to the elders of the town at the gate.

**16** The young woman's father is to say to the elders, "I gave my daughter to this man as his wife, but he has turned against her.

**17** Now he has made up charges against her, saying, 'I did not find the evidence of your daughter's virginity.' But here is the evidence of my daughter's virginity." Then they are to spread out the cloth before the elders of the town.

**18** The elders of that town are to take the man and punish him.[^14]

**19** They are to fine him a hundred shekels of silver[^15] and give it to the young woman's father, because the man has given an Israelite virgin a bad name. She will remain his wife, and he may not divorce her[^16] as long as he lives.

**20** But if the charge is true, and no evidence of the young woman's virginity is found,

**21** they are to bring the young woman out to the door of her father's house, and the men of her town are to stone her to death. She has done a disgraceful thing in Israel by being promiscuous[^17] while in her father's house. So you must purge[^18] the evil from among you.

**22** If a man is found sleeping with another man's wife,[^19] both of them must die, the man who slept with the woman and the woman. So you must purge the evil from Israel.

**23** If a young woman who is a virgin is engaged to a man, and another man meets her in the town and sleeps with her,

**24** you are to take both of them out to the gate of that town and stone them to death: the young woman because she did not cry for help in the town, and the man because he violated[^20] his neighbor's wife. So you must purge the evil from among you.

**25** But if the man meets the engaged young woman in the open country, and seizes her and sleeps with her, only the man who slept with her must die.

**26** Do nothing to the young woman. She has committed no sin that deserves death. This case is like that of a man who attacks his neighbor and murders him.[^21]

**27** For he met her in the open country, and though the engaged young woman cried out, there was no one to rescue her.

**28** If a man meets a young woman who is a virgin and is not engaged, and seizes her and sleeps with her, and they are found out,

**29** the man who slept with her must give the young woman's father fifty shekels of silver,[^22] and she must become his wife. Because he has violated her, he may not divorce her as long as he lives.

[^1]: Literally "your brother's"; also in verses 2, 3 and 4.
[^2]: Literally "do not hide yourself from them"; also in verses 3 and 4. Compare Exodus 23:4–5.
[^3]: Hebrew *keli gever*, "a man's article": his clothing, equipment or weapons.
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^5]: Roofs were flat and were used as living space.
[^6]: Literally "become holy", that is, forfeited to the sanctuary.
[^7]: Hebrew *sha'atnez*, a word of uncertain origin.
[^8]: Hebrew *gedilim*, "twisted threads". Compare Numbers 15:37–41.
[^9]: Literally "goes in to her".
[^10]: Literally "hates her"; also in verse 16.
[^11]: Hebrew *alilot devarim*, a phrase of uncertain meaning, perhaps "accusations of misconduct". Also in verse 17.
[^12]: Hebrew *betulim*, "signs of virginity", probably the bloodstained cloth from the wedding night (verse 17). Also in verses 15, 17 and 20.
[^13]: Here and in verses 16–29 the Hebrew text is written *na'ar*, the form for "young man", but read *na'arah*, "young woman". The Pentateuch often writes the word this way.
[^14]: Or "discipline him", perhaps by flogging.
[^15]: Literally "a hundred of silver", probably shekels: about 2½ pounds (1.15 kg) of silver.
[^16]: Literally "send her away"; also in verse 29.
[^17]: Hebrew *zanah*, "act as a prostitute", used of sexual relations outside marriage.
[^18]: Literally "burn out"; also in verses 22 and 24.
[^19]: Literally "with a woman married to a husband".
[^20]: Hebrew *innah*, "humble, force, violate"; also in verse 29.
[^21]: Literally "and murders him, a life".
[^22]: Literally "fifty of silver", probably shekels: about 1¼ pounds (575 grams) of silver.
