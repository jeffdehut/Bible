**1** Give ear, you heavens, and I will speak; let the earth hear the words of my mouth.[^1]

**2** Let my teaching fall like rain, my words come down like dew, like gentle rain on new grass, like showers on green plants.

**3** For I will proclaim the name of Yahweh.[^2] Ascribe greatness to our God!

**4** The Rock:[^3] his work is perfect, for all his ways are just. A faithful God, without wrong, righteous and upright is he.

**5** They have acted corruptly toward him; they are not his children, because of their flaw.[^4] They are a crooked and twisted generation.

**6** Is this how you repay Yahweh, you foolish and unwise people? Is he not your father, who created you,[^5] who made you and established you?

**7** Remember the days of old; consider the years of generation after generation. Ask your father, and he will tell you, your elders, and they will explain to you.

**8** When the Most High[^6] gave the nations their inheritance, when he divided humankind,[^7] he set the boundaries of the peoples according to the number of the sons of Israel.[^8]

**9** For Yahweh's portion is his people, Jacob his allotted inheritance.[^9]

**10** He found him in a desert land, in an empty, howling wasteland. He surrounded him and cared for him; he guarded him like the pupil of his eye.[^10]

**11** Like an eagle[^11] that stirs up its nest and hovers over its young, he spread out his wings and took him up; he carried him on his feathers.

**12** Yahweh alone guided him; no foreign god was with him.

**13** He made him ride on the heights of the land, and he ate the produce of the field. He nursed him with honey from the rock, and oil from the flint rock,

**14** with curds from the herd and milk from the flock, with the fat of lambs, with rams of Bashan's breed and goats, with the finest of the wheat.[^12] And you drank the blood of the grape, foaming wine.[^13]

**15** But Jeshurun[^14] grew fat and kicked. You grew fat, you grew thick, you were gorged! He abandoned the God[^15] who made him, and scorned the Rock of his salvation.

**16** They made him jealous with strange gods; with detestable things they provoked him to anger.

**17** They sacrificed to demons[^16] that were not God,[^17] to gods they had not known, new gods that had come lately, gods your ancestors had not feared.[^18]

**18** You neglected the Rock who fathered you; you forgot the God who gave you birth.[^19]

**19** Yahweh saw it and spurned them, because his sons and his daughters provoked him.

**20** He said:[^20] I will hide my face from them; I will see what their end will be. For they are a perverse generation, children who cannot be trusted.

**21** They made me jealous with what is no god; they provoked me with their worthless idols.[^21] So I will make them jealous with what is no people; I will provoke them with a foolish nation.[^22]

**22** For a fire has been kindled by my anger, and it burns to the depths of Sheol.[^23] It devours the earth and its produce, and sets fire to the foundations of the mountains.

**23** I will heap disasters on them; I will spend my arrows on them.

**24** They will be wasted by hunger, consumed by plague[^24] and bitter destruction. I will send the teeth of wild beasts against them, with the venom of creatures that crawl in the dust.

**25** Outside, the sword will bereave them, and in the inner rooms, terror, for young man and young woman alike, for the nursing child and the gray-haired man.

**26** I said I would cut them to pieces[^25] and wipe out their memory from humankind,

**27** had I not feared the enemy's taunts, that their adversaries might misunderstand, that they might say, "Our hand has triumphed;[^26] it was not Yahweh who did all this."

**28** For they are a nation without sense,[^27] and there is no understanding in them.

**29** If they were wise, they would understand this; they would see what their end will be.

**30** How could one chase a thousand, and two put ten thousand to flight, unless their Rock had sold them, unless Yahweh had handed them over?

**31** For their rock is not like our Rock, as even our enemies must judge.[^28]

**32** For their vine comes from the vine of Sodom, and from the fields of Gomorrah. Their grapes are grapes of poison; their clusters are bitter.

**33** Their wine is the venom of serpents, the deadly poison of cobras.

**34** Is this not stored up with me, sealed up in my storehouses?

**35** Vengeance is mine, and repayment,[^29] for the time when their foot slips. For the day of their disaster is near, and their doom comes swiftly.

**36** For Yahweh will vindicate[^30] his people and have compassion on his servants, when he sees that their strength is gone, and no one is left, slave or free.[^31]

**37** Then he will say: Where are their gods, the rock they took refuge in,

**38** who ate the fat of their sacrifices and drank the wine of their drink offerings? Let them rise up and help you! Let them be your shelter!

**39** See now that I, I am he, and there is no god beside me. I put to death and I bring to life; I have wounded and I will heal, and no one can rescue from my hand.

**40** For I lift up my hand to heaven and declare: As surely as I live forever,

**41** when I sharpen my flashing sword[^32] and my hand takes hold of judgment, I will take vengeance on my adversaries and repay those who hate me.

**42** I will make my arrows drunk with blood, and my sword will devour flesh, with the blood of the slain and the captives, from the long-haired heads of the enemy.[^33]

**43** Praise his people, you nations,[^34] for he will avenge the blood of his servants. He will take vengeance on his adversaries and make atonement for his land, his people.[^35]

**44** Moses came and recited all the words of this song in the hearing of the people, he and Hoshea[^36] son of Nun.

**45** When Moses had finished speaking all these words to all Israel,

**46** he said to them, "Take to heart[^37] all the words I am solemnly declaring to you today, so that you may command your children to be careful to follow all the words of this law.

**47** For this is no empty word for you; it is your life. By this word you will live long in the land you are crossing the Jordan to possess."

**48** That same day Yahweh said to Moses,

**49** "Go up into these mountains of the Abarim,[^38] to Mount Nebo in the land of Moab, opposite Jericho, and look at the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the Israelites as their possession.

**50** You will die on the mountain you go up, and be gathered to your people,[^39] just as your brother Aaron died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his people.

**51** For both of you broke faith with me among the Israelites at the waters of Meribath-kadesh[^40] in the wilderness of Zin, because you did not treat me as holy among the Israelites.

**52** You will see the land from a distance, but you will not enter it, the land that I am giving to the Israelites."

[^1]: Verses 1–43 are a poem, the Song of Moses (31:19–22, 30).
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *tsur*, "rock": a title for God as a place of safety and strength. It is used often in this song (verses 15, 18, 30, 31 and 37).
[^4]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "He corrupted to him, not his sons, their blemish". Or "His children have acted corruptly toward him; the flaw is theirs". The last line is echoed in Philippians 2:15.
[^5]: Or "who acquired you". Hebrew *qanah*.
[^6]: Hebrew *Elyon*, a title of God.
[^7]: Literally "the sons of *adam* (humankind)".
[^8]: A Dead Sea Scroll from Qumran (4QDeut-j) reads "the sons of God", and the Greek Septuagint reads "the angels of God" or "the sons of God". On that reading, the nations were divided according to the number of the heavenly beings around God (compare Job 1:6), while Israel was Yahweh's own portion (verse 9). The Masoretic Text, followed here, reads "the sons of Israel".
[^9]: Literally "the measuring line of his inheritance".
[^10]: Literally "the little man of his eye", the pupil, which is guarded with great care.
[^11]: Or "a vulture". Hebrew *nesher*. *Hovers* is the verb used of the Spirit of God in Genesis 1:2.
[^12]: Literally "with the fat of the kidneys of wheat".
[^13]: Hebrew *chemer*, perhaps "foaming" or "fermented".
[^14]: A poetic name for Israel, probably from *yashar*, "upright". Also in 33:5 and 33:26.
[^15]: Hebrew *Eloah*, a poetic form of *Elohim*, "God". Also in verse 17.
[^16]: Hebrew *shedim*, a word found elsewhere only in Psalm 106:37: spirits or minor gods.
[^17]: Or "to demons, to a no-god".
[^18]: Hebrew *se'arum*, a rare verb. It may mean "shuddered at", "feared", or "known".
[^19]: Literally "who writhed in labor with you". The first line uses a rare verb, *teshi*, probably "neglect, forget".
[^20]: Yahweh speaks from here, perhaps to verse 35, and again in verses 37–42. The poem does not mark where his words begin and end, so they are not put in quotation marks.
[^21]: Literally "their breaths", a scornful word for idols.
[^22]: Paul quotes the second half of this verse in Romans 10:19.
[^23]: Hebrew *Sheol*, the realm of the dead. Literally "to the lowest Sheol".
[^24]: Hebrew *resheph*, "flame, plague", also the name of a Canaanite god of plague.
[^25]: Hebrew *af'ehem*, a word found only here, perhaps "I will scatter them" or "I will make an end of them".
[^26]: Literally "Our hand is high".
[^27]: Literally "a nation lost of counsels". It is not clear whether "they" are Israel or Israel's enemies.
[^28]: Hebrew *pelilim*, a word of uncertain meaning, perhaps "judges": "and our enemies are judges".
[^29]: The Septuagint reads "On the day of vengeance I will repay". Romans 12:19 and Hebrews 10:30 quote this line as "Vengeance is mine, I will repay".
[^30]: Or "judge". Hebrews 10:30 quotes this line, "The Lord will judge his people". In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, Yahweh.
[^31]: Literally "and there is none, shut up or left at large", a phrase of uncertain meaning, probably "no one at all".
[^32]: Literally "the lightning of my sword".
[^33]: Hebrew *par'ot*, perhaps the long hair of warriors, or "leaders". Literally "from the head of the long hair of the enemy".
[^34]: Or "Make his people rejoice, you nations". A Dead Sea Scroll (4QDeut-q) and the Septuagint have a longer form of this verse. The scroll begins "Rejoice, you heavens, with him; bow down to him, all you gods". The Septuagint reads "Rejoice, you nations, with his people", the line quoted in Romans 15:10, and Hebrews 1:6 quotes another line of its longer form.
[^35]: Literally "his land, his people". The Dead Sea Scroll and the Septuagint read "the land of his people".
[^36]: Joshua's earlier name (Numbers 13:16).
[^37]: Literally "Set your hearts on".
[^38]: *Abarim* means "regions beyond": the mountains east of the Dead Sea.
[^39]: A way of speaking of death, as joining one's ancestors.
[^40]: *Meribah* means "quarreling" (Numbers 20:1–13; 27:14).
