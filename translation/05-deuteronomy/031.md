**1** Then Moses went and spoke these words to all Israel.[^1]

**2** He said to them, "I am 120 years old today. I am no longer able to lead you,[^2] and Yahweh[^3] has said to me, 'You will not cross this Jordan.'

**3** Yahweh your God himself will cross over ahead of you. He will destroy these nations before you, and you will dispossess them. Joshua will cross over ahead of you, as Yahweh has said.

**4** Yahweh will do to them as he did to Sihon and Og, the kings of the Amorites, and to their land, when he destroyed them.

**5** Yahweh will hand them over to you, and you must do to them everything I have commanded you.[^4]

**6** Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid or terrified of them, for Yahweh your God is the one who goes with you. He will not fail you or abandon you."

**7** Then Moses summoned Joshua and said to him in the sight of all Israel, "Be strong and courageous, for you will go with this people into the land that Yahweh swore to their ancestors[^5] to give them, and you will divide it among them as their inheritance.

**8** Yahweh himself goes ahead of you. He will be with you. He will not fail you or abandon you. Do not be afraid or discouraged."

**9** So Moses wrote down this law and gave it to the priests, the sons of Levi, who carried the ark of Yahweh's covenant, and to all the elders of Israel.

**10** Moses commanded them, "At the end of every seven years, at the set time in the year of release,[^6] at the Festival of Shelters,[^7]

**11** when all Israel comes to appear before Yahweh your God[^8] at the place he will choose, you are to read this law aloud before all Israel, in their hearing.

**12** Assemble the people, the men, the women and the children, and the foreigners[^9] who live in your towns,[^10] so that they may listen and learn to fear Yahweh your God, and be careful to follow all the words of this law.

**13** Their children, who do not know it, must also listen and learn to fear Yahweh your God, as long as you live in the land you are crossing the Jordan to possess."

**14** Then Yahweh said to Moses, "The day of your death is near.[^11] Call Joshua, and present yourselves at the tent of meeting,[^12] so that I may give him his orders." So Moses and Joshua went and presented themselves at the tent of meeting.

**15** Yahweh appeared at the tent in a pillar of cloud, and the pillar of cloud stood over the entrance of the tent.

**16** Yahweh said to Moses, "You are about to rest with your ancestors.[^13] Then this people will rise up and prostitute themselves to the foreign gods of the land they are entering.[^14] They will abandon me and break my covenant that I made with them.

**17** On that day my anger will burn against them. I will abandon them and hide my face from them, and they will be devoured.[^15] Many disasters and troubles will come on them, and on that day they will say, 'Haven't these disasters come on us because our God is not among us?'

**18** And I will surely hide my face on that day, because of all the evil they have done by turning to other gods.

**19** So now write down this song for yourselves[^16] and teach it to the Israelites. Have them sing it,[^17] so that this song may be my witness against the Israelites.

**20** When I have brought them into the land flowing with milk and honey that I swore to give their ancestors, and they eat their fill and grow fat, they will turn to other gods and serve them. They will despise me and break my covenant.

**21** And when many disasters and troubles have come on them, this song will testify against them as a witness, for it will not be forgotten from the mouths of their descendants. For I know what they are inclined to do[^18] even now, before I have brought them into the land that I swore to give them."

**22** So Moses wrote down this song that day and taught it to the Israelites.

**23** Then he[^19] gave Joshua son of Nun his orders: "Be strong and courageous, for you will bring the Israelites into the land that I swore to give them, and I will be with you."

**24** When Moses had finished writing the words of this law in a book, to the very end,

**25** Moses gave this command to the Levites who carried the ark of Yahweh's covenant:

**26** "Take this book of the law and put it beside the ark of the covenant of Yahweh your God. Let it remain there as a witness against you.

**27** For I know how rebellious and stubborn you are.[^20] Even now, while I am still alive with you, you have been rebels against Yahweh. How much more after my death!

**28** Assemble before me all the elders of your tribes and your officials, so that I may speak these words in their hearing and call heaven and earth as witnesses against them.

**29** For I know that after my death you will act very corruptly and turn aside from the way I have commanded you. In days to come,[^21] disaster will come on you, because you will do what is evil in the eyes of Yahweh, provoking him to anger by what your hands have made."

**30** Then Moses recited the words of this song to the very end, in the hearing of the whole assembly of Israel:

[^1]: A Dead Sea Scroll (1QDeut-b) and the Septuagint read "When Moses had finished speaking all these words to all Israel".
[^2]: Literally "to go out and to come in", that is, to lead you in war and in daily life.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Literally "according to all the commandment that I have commanded you".
[^5]: Literally "fathers"; also in verses 16 and 20.
[^6]: Hebrew *shemittah*, "letting drop": the cancellation of debts every seventh year (15:1–11).
[^7]: Hebrew *sukkot*, "booths, shelters". Traditionally "Tabernacles". See 16:13.
[^8]: Literally "to be seen at the face of Yahweh your God". The same consonants could be read "to see the face of Yahweh your God".
[^9]: Hebrew *ger*: someone from elsewhere who lives among the Israelites, a resident foreigner.
[^10]: Literally "gates".
[^11]: Literally "Look, your days have come near to die".
[^12]: Hebrew *ohel mo'ed*: the tent where Yahweh met with Moses and the people (Exodus 33:7–11). Also later in the verse.
[^13]: Literally "You are about to lie down with your fathers", a way of speaking of death.
[^14]: Literally "and prostitute themselves after the gods of the foreigners of the land into whose midst they are going". Unfaithfulness to Yahweh is often pictured as sexual unfaithfulness.
[^15]: Literally "and he will be for devouring".
[^16]: The command is plural, to Moses and Joshua.
[^17]: Literally "put it in their mouths".
[^18]: Hebrew *yetser*, "inclination, intention": what they are forming in their minds.
[^19]: Yahweh is the speaker, as in verse 14.
[^20]: Literally "your rebellion and your hard neck".
[^21]: Literally "in the end of the days".
