**1** "This is the commandment, the statutes and the rulings, that Yahweh[^1] your God commanded me to teach you, for you to follow in the land you are crossing over into to take possession of,

**2** so that you may fear Yahweh your God all the days of your life, you and your children and your grandchildren, by keeping all his statutes and his commandments that I am giving you, and so that you may live long.

**3** Listen, Israel, and be careful to follow them, so that it may go well with you and you may increase greatly in number, as Yahweh, the God of your ancestors,[^2] has promised you, in a land flowing with milk and honey.[^3]

**4** Hear, Israel: Yahweh our God, Yahweh is one.[^4][^5]

**5** You shall love Yahweh your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your strength.[^6]

**6** These words that I am commanding you today are to be on your heart.

**7** Impress them on your children.[^7] Talk about them when you sit in your house and when you walk along the road, when you lie down and when you get up.

**8** Tie them as a sign on your hand, and let them be a band on your forehead.[^8]

**9** Write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates.[^9]

**10** Yahweh your God will bring you into the land he swore to your ancestors, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, to give you: a land with large and fine cities that you did not build,

**11** houses full of all kinds of good things that you did not fill, cisterns cut in the rock that you did not dig, and vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant. When you eat and are satisfied,

**12** be careful not to forget Yahweh, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.[^10]

**13** Fear Yahweh your God. Serve him, and swear by his name.[^11]

**14** Do not follow other gods, the gods of the peoples around you,

**15** for Yahweh your God, who is among you, is a jealous God.[^12] Otherwise the anger of Yahweh your God will burn against you, and he will wipe you off the face of the earth.

**16** Do not put Yahweh your God to the test as you tested him at Massah.[^13]

**17** Be sure to keep the commandments of Yahweh your God, and the decrees[^14] and statutes he has commanded you.

**18** Do what is right and good in the eyes of Yahweh, so that it may go well with you, and so that you may go in and take possession of the good land that Yahweh swore to give your ancestors,

**19** by driving out all your enemies before you, as Yahweh has promised.

**20** In the future,[^15] when your son asks you, 'What is the meaning of the decrees, the statutes and the rulings that Yahweh our God has commanded you?'

**21** then tell your son, 'We were Pharaoh's slaves in Egypt, but Yahweh brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand.

**22** Before our eyes Yahweh did great and terrible signs and wonders against Egypt, against Pharaoh and against all his household.

**23** But he brought us out from there, to bring us in and give us the land he swore to give our ancestors.

**24** Yahweh commanded us to follow all these statutes and to fear Yahweh our God, for our own good always, so that he might keep us alive, as we are today.

**25** And it will be righteousness for us[^16] if we are careful to follow all this commandment before Yahweh our God, as he has commanded us.'

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "fathers"; also in verses 10, 18 and 23.
[^3]: Literally "as Yahweh, the God of your fathers, has spoken to you: a land flowing with milk and honey". A land rich in pasture for flocks and herds, and in honey, either from bees or a syrup made from dates.
[^4]: Or "Yahweh is our God, Yahweh alone", or "Yahweh our God is one Yahweh". The Hebrew has four words, "Yahweh our-God Yahweh one". Verses 4–9 are the opening of the *Shema* (Hebrew "Hear"), and Jesus quotes verses 4–5 in Mark 12:29–30.
[^5]: In the Hebrew text, the last letter of the first word (*shema*, "hear") and of the last word (*echad*, "one") are written large, a scribal mark. Together the two letters spell *ed*, "witness".
[^6]: Hebrew *levav*, "heart", the center of thought and will as well as feeling; *nephesh*, "life, self, soul"; and *me'od*, literally "muchness", all your might and all you have. Mark 12:30 adds "with all your mind".
[^7]: Hebrew *shinnen*, "sharpen": to teach by repeating.
[^8]: Literally "and they shall be *totaphot* between your eyes". *Totaphot* is a word of uncertain meaning, probably a band or emblem worn on the forehead. Later Jewish practice understood this verse and 11:18 literally, in the wearing of *tefillin* (phylacteries).
[^9]: Later Jewish practice fixes a small scroll with these words, called a *mezuzah* ("doorpost"), to the doorposts of homes.
[^10]: Literally "house of slaves".
[^11]: Jesus quotes this verse, in a form close to the Greek Septuagint, in Matthew 4:10 and Luke 4:8.
[^12]: Hebrew *el qanna*: a God who will not share his people's devotion with any other.
[^13]: *Massah* means "testing" (Exodus 17:1–7). Jesus quotes the first part of this verse in Matthew 4:7 and Luke 4:12.
[^14]: Hebrew *edot*, "testimonies, witnesses": the solemn terms of the covenant. Also in verse 20.
[^15]: Literally "Tomorrow".
[^16]: Or "This will be counted to us as righteousness".
