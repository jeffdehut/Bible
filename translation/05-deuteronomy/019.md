**1** When Yahweh[^1] your God has cut off the nations whose land Yahweh your God is giving you, and you have dispossessed them and settled in their towns and their houses,

**2** set apart for yourself three cities in the land that Yahweh your God is giving you to possess.

**3** Prepare the roads,[^2] and divide into three parts the territory of the land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance, so that anyone who has killed someone[^3] can flee there.

**4** This is the rule for someone who kills another person and flees there to save his life: he must have killed his neighbor unintentionally, without having been his enemy before.[^4]

**5** For example, a man goes into the forest with his neighbor to cut wood. As he swings the axe to cut down a tree, the head flies off the handle and hits his neighbor, and he dies.[^5] That man may flee to one of these cities and save his life.

**6** Otherwise the avenger of blood,[^6] in hot anger,[^7] might pursue the killer and overtake him because the distance is great, and kill him,[^8] though he did not deserve to die,[^9] since he had not been his enemy before.

**7** That is why I command you to set apart for yourself three cities.

**8** Yahweh your God may enlarge your territory, as he swore to your ancestors,[^10] and give you all the land he promised to give your ancestors,

**9** if you are careful to keep all this command that I am giving you today, to love Yahweh your God and to walk in his ways always. Then you are to add three more cities to these three.

**10** This is so that innocent blood will not be shed in the land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance, and you will not be guilty of bloodshed.[^11]

**11** But suppose someone hates his neighbor, lies in wait for him, attacks him and kills him, and then flees to one of these cities.

**12** The elders of his town are to send for him and bring him back from there, and hand him over to the avenger of blood to be put to death.

**13** Show him no pity.[^12] You must purge[^13] the guilt of innocent blood from Israel, so that it may go well with you.

**14** Do not move your neighbor's boundary marker, set up by those who came before you,[^14] in the inheritance you will receive in the land that Yahweh your God is giving you to possess.

**15** A single witness is not enough to convict anyone of any wrongdoing or any sin, whatever sin he may have committed. A matter must be established on the testimony[^15] of two witnesses or three witnesses.

**16** If a malicious witness[^16] comes forward against someone to accuse him of wrongdoing,[^17]

**17** the two people involved in the dispute must stand before Yahweh, before the priests and the judges who are in office at that time.

**18** The judges must investigate thoroughly. If the witness turns out to be a false witness, who has given false testimony against his brother,

**19** then do to him what he intended to do to his brother. So you must purge the evil from among you.

**20** The rest of the people will hear and be afraid, and such an evil thing will never again be done among you.

**21** Show no pity: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.[^18]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "Prepare the way for yourself". Or "Measure the distances".
[^3]: Hebrew *rotseach*, "killer", from the verb used in "You must not murder" (5:17). Here it is someone who has killed without meaning to. Also in verses 4 and 6.
[^4]: Literally "and he was not hating him yesterday and the day before"; also in verse 6.
[^5]: Literally "and his hand is driven with the axe to cut the tree, and the iron slips off the wood and finds his neighbor, and he dies".
[^6]: Hebrew *go'el ha-dam*: the relative responsible for avenging the death of a family member. Also in verse 12.
[^7]: Literally "because his heart is hot".
[^8]: Literally "strike him, life", that is, fatally. Also in verse 11.
[^9]: Literally "and there is no judgment of death for him".
[^10]: Literally "fathers"; also later in the verse.
[^11]: Literally "and there will be blood on you".
[^12]: Literally "Your eye shall not pity him"; also in verse 21.
[^13]: Literally "burn out"; also in verse 19.
[^14]: Literally "which the first ones set as a border".
[^15]: Literally "at the mouth".
[^16]: Literally "a witness of violence" (compare Exodus 23:1).
[^17]: Hebrew *sarah*, "turning aside": a crime, or perhaps turning away from Yahweh (compare 13:6).
[^18]: Compare Exodus 21:23–25 and Leviticus 24:19–20. Jesus quotes "eye for eye and tooth for tooth" in Matthew 5:38.
