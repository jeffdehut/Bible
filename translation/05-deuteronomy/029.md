**1** Moses summoned all Israel and said to them,[^1] "You have seen all that Yahweh[^2] did before your eyes in the land of Egypt, to Pharaoh and to all his servants and to all his land:

**2** the great trials that your eyes saw, those great signs and wonders.

**3** But to this day Yahweh has not given you a mind to understand, or eyes to see, or ears to hear.[^3]

**4** I led you forty years in the wilderness. Your clothes did not wear out on you, and your sandals did not wear out on your feet.[^4]

**5** You did not eat bread, and you did not drink wine or beer, so that you might know that I am Yahweh your God.

**6** When you came to this place, Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out against us to fight, but we defeated them.

**7** We took their land and gave it as an inheritance to the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

**8** So be careful to follow the words of this covenant, so that you may succeed[^5] in everything you do.

**9** All of you are standing today before Yahweh your God: your leaders and your tribes,[^6] your elders and your officials, all the men of Israel,

**10** your little ones, your wives, and the foreigners[^7] who live in your camp, from those who cut your wood to those who draw your water,

**11** so that you may enter into the covenant of Yahweh your God, and into the oath[^8] that Yahweh your God is making with you today,

**12** in order to establish you today as his people, and so that he may be your God, as he promised you and as he swore to your ancestors,[^9] to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob.

**13** It is not with you alone that I am making this covenant and this oath,

**14** but with those who are standing here with us today before Yahweh our God, and also with those who are not here with us today.

**15** For you know how we lived in the land of Egypt, and how we passed through the middle of the nations you passed through.

**16** You saw their detestable things and their idols,[^10] of wood and stone, of silver and gold, which they had among them.

**17** Make sure that there is no man or woman, no clan or tribe, among you whose heart turns away today from Yahweh our God, to go and serve the gods of those nations. Make sure that there is no root among you that bears poisonous and bitter fruit.[^11]

**18** When such a person hears the words of this oath, he may bless himself in his heart and think, 'I will be safe,[^12] though I follow my own stubborn heart.' That would sweep away the watered land and the dry alike.[^13]

**19** Yahweh will never be willing to forgive him. Instead, the anger and jealousy of Yahweh will smoke against that man. Every curse written in this book will settle on him, and Yahweh will wipe out his name from under heaven.

**20** Yahweh will single him out from all the tribes of Israel for disaster, according to all the curses of the covenant written in this book of the law.

**21** The generation to come, your children who rise up after you, and the foreigner[^14] who comes from a distant land, will speak about it, when they see the plagues of that land and the diseases that Yahweh has brought on it:

**22** the whole land burned out with sulfur and salt, nothing sown, nothing growing, no plant coming up on it, like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, which Yahweh overthrew in his anger and his fury.

**23** All the nations will say, 'Why has Yahweh done this to this land? Why this great, burning anger?'

**24** And people will say, 'It is because they abandoned the covenant of Yahweh, the God of their ancestors, which he made with them when he brought them out of the land of Egypt.

**25** They went and served other gods and bowed down to them, gods they had not known, and that he had not given to them.[^15]

**26** So the anger of Yahweh burned against that land, and he brought on it every curse written in this book.

**27** Yahweh uprooted them from their land in anger, in fury and in great rage, and threw them into another land, as it is today.'

**28** The secret things belong to Yahweh our God, but the things that are revealed belong to us and to our children[^16] forever, so that we may follow all the words of this law.

[^1]: Verses 1–28 are 29:2–29 in most English Bibles. Moses' third speech begins here and runs to 30:20.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "a heart to know". Paul quotes this verse, with Isaiah 29:10, in Romans 11:8.
[^4]: In verses 4–5 Moses speaks in Yahweh's own words.
[^5]: Or "act wisely".
[^6]: Literally "your heads, your tribes". The Septuagint and the Syriac read "the heads of your tribes".
[^7]: Hebrew *ger*: someone from elsewhere who lives among the Israelites, a resident foreigner.
[^8]: Hebrew *alah*, a solemn oath with a curse attached to it. Also in verses 13, 18, 19 and 20, where it is translated "oath" or "curse".
[^9]: Literally "fathers"; also in verse 24.
[^10]: Hebrew *gillulim*, a scornful word for idols, perhaps meaning "lumps of dung".
[^11]: Literally "a root bearing poison and wormwood". Compare Hebrews 12:15.
[^12]: Literally "I will have peace".
[^13]: Literally "in order to sweep away the watered with the thirsty". The meaning is uncertain; it may be a saying meaning "everything".
[^14]: Hebrew *nokhri*: a foreigner from another land who has not settled among the Israelites.
[^15]: Literally "and he had not allotted to them" (compare 4:19).
[^16]: In the Hebrew text, dots are written over the words "to us and to our children" and over the first letter of the next word, a scribal mark of uncertain meaning.
