**1** Hear, Israel. Today you are about to cross the Jordan, to go in and dispossess nations greater and stronger than you, with large cities that have walls up to the sky,[^1]

**2** a people great and tall, the Anakim.[^2] You know about them, and you have heard it said, "Who can stand up to the Anakim?"[^3]

**3** Know then today that Yahweh[^4] your God is the one who is crossing over ahead of you, a consuming fire. He will destroy them, and he will bring them down before you. You will drive them out and destroy them quickly, as Yahweh has promised you.

**4** When Yahweh your God has driven them out before you, do not say to yourself,[^5] "It is because of my righteousness that Yahweh has brought me in to take possession of this land." It is because of the wickedness of these nations that Yahweh is driving them out before you.

**5** It is not because of your righteousness or the uprightness of your heart that you are going in to take possession of their land. It is because of the wickedness of these nations that Yahweh your God is driving them out before you, and in order to fulfill the promise[^6] that Yahweh swore to your ancestors,[^7] to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob.

**6** So know that it is not because of your righteousness that Yahweh your God is giving you this good land to possess, for you are a stubborn people.[^8]

**7** Remember, and do not forget, how you provoked Yahweh your God to anger in the wilderness. From the day you left the land of Egypt until you came to this place, you have been rebels against Yahweh.

**8** At Horeb you provoked Yahweh to anger, and Yahweh was angry enough with you to destroy you.

**9** When I went up the mountain to receive the stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant that Yahweh made with you, I stayed on the mountain forty days and forty nights. I ate no bread and drank no water.

**10** Yahweh gave me the two stone tablets, written with the finger of God. On them were all the words that Yahweh had spoken to you at the mountain, out of the fire, on the day of the assembly.

**11** At the end of the forty days and forty nights, Yahweh gave me the two stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant.

**12** Then Yahweh said to me, "Get up, go down from here at once, for your people, whom you brought out of Egypt, have acted corruptly. They have quickly turned aside from the way I commanded them. They have made themselves a cast idol."[^9]

**13** Yahweh also said to me, "I have seen this people, and they are indeed a stubborn people.

**14** Leave me alone, so that I may destroy them and wipe out their name from under heaven. Then I will make you into a nation stronger and more numerous than they are."

**15** So I turned and went down from the mountain, while the mountain was blazing with fire. The two tablets of the covenant were in my two hands.

**16** I looked, and there you were: you had sinned against Yahweh your God. You had made yourselves a calf, a cast idol. You had quickly turned aside from the way that Yahweh had commanded you.

**17** So I took hold of the two tablets and threw them out of my two hands, and I broke them before your eyes.

**18** Then I lay face down before Yahweh, as I had the first time, for forty days and forty nights. I ate no bread and drank no water, because of all the sin you had committed, doing what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh and provoking him to anger.

**19** For I was afraid of the anger and the fury that Yahweh felt against you, enough to destroy you. But Yahweh listened to me that time too.

**20** Yahweh was very angry with Aaron, enough to destroy him, and at that time I prayed for Aaron too.

**21** Then I took the sinful thing you had made, the calf, and burned it in the fire. I crushed it, grinding it thoroughly until it was as fine as dust, and I threw its dust into the stream that flowed down from the mountain.

**22** At Taberah, at Massah and at Kibroth-hattaavah[^10] you also provoked Yahweh to anger.

**23** And when Yahweh sent you from Kadesh-barnea and said, "Go up and take possession of the land I have given you," you rebelled against the command[^11] of Yahweh your God. You did not trust him or listen to his voice.

**24** You have been rebels against Yahweh from the day I first knew you.

**25** So I lay face down before Yahweh for those forty days and forty nights, because Yahweh had said he would destroy you.

**26** I prayed to Yahweh and said, "Lord Yahweh,[^12] do not destroy your people, your inheritance, whom you redeemed by your greatness and brought out of Egypt with a mighty hand.

**27** Remember your servants, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. Do not look at the stubbornness of this people, or at their wickedness, or at their sin.

**28** Otherwise the land you brought us out of[^13] will say, 'It was because Yahweh was not able to bring them into the land he had promised them, and because he hated them, that he brought them out to kill them in the wilderness.'

**29** But they are your people, your inheritance, whom you brought out by your great power and your outstretched arm."

[^1]: Literally "fortified up to the heavens".
[^2]: Literally "the sons of the Anakim", a people known for their great height (1:28).
[^3]: Literally "the sons of Anak".
[^4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^5]: Literally "do not say in your heart".
[^6]: Literally "the word".
[^7]: Literally "fathers".
[^8]: Literally "a people hard of neck", like an ox that will not bend its neck to the yoke. Also in verse 13.
[^9]: Hebrew *massekhah*, an image made of molten metal. Also in verse 16.
[^10]: *Taberah* means "burning" (Numbers 11:1–3), *Massah* "testing" (Exodus 17:7), and *Kibroth-hattaavah* "graves of craving" (Numbers 11:34).
[^11]: Literally "mouth".
[^12]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^13]: That is, the people of that land.
