**1** This is the blessing with which Moses, the man of God, blessed the Israelites before his death.

**2** He said:[^1] Yahweh[^2] came from Sinai and dawned on them from Seir; he shone out from Mount Paran. He came from among ten thousands of holy ones,[^3] with a fiery law for them at his right hand.[^4]

**3** Truly he loves the peoples;[^5] all his holy ones are in your hand. They bow down at your feet; each receives your words.[^6]

**4** Moses gave us a law,[^7] a possession for the assembly of Jacob.

**5** He[^8] became king in Jeshurun,[^9] when the leaders of the people gathered, the tribes of Israel together.

**6** Let Reuben live and not die, though his men be few.[^10]

**7** And this he said about Judah: Hear, Yahweh, the voice of Judah, and bring him to his people. With his own hands he fights for himself;[^11] be a help to him against his adversaries.

**8** About Levi he said:[^12] Your Thummim and your Urim[^13] belong to your faithful one,[^14] whom you tested at Massah, with whom you quarreled at the waters of Meribah.

**9** He said of his father and his mother, "I do not regard them."[^15] He did not acknowledge his brothers, or recognize his own children.[^16] For they kept your word and guarded your covenant.

**10** They teach your rulings to Jacob and your law to Israel. They put incense before you[^17] and whole burnt offerings on your altar.

**11** Bless his strength,[^18] Yahweh, and accept the work of his hands. Crush the loins of those who rise up against him, of those who hate him, so that they rise no more.

**12** About Benjamin he said: The one Yahweh loves will live securely beside him.[^19] He shields him all day long, and he dwells between his shoulders.[^20]

**13** About Joseph he said: May his land be blessed by Yahweh with the choicest gifts[^21] of heaven, with the dew, and with the deep waters that lie below,

**14** with the choicest of what the sun brings forth, and the choicest of what the months bring out,[^22]

**15** with the best of the ancient mountains,[^23] and the choicest of the everlasting hills,

**16** with the choicest gifts of the earth and all that fills it, and with the favor of him who dwelt in the bush.[^24] Let these rest on the head of Joseph, on the brow of the one set apart among his brothers.[^25]

**17** His firstborn bull has majesty,[^26] and his horns are the horns of a wild ox.[^27] With them he gores the peoples, all of them together, to the ends of the earth. Such are the ten thousands of Ephraim, and such are the thousands of Manasseh.

**18** About Zebulun he said: Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out, and Issachar, in your tents.

**19** They will call peoples to the mountain; there they will offer right sacrifices.[^28] For they will draw from the abundance of the seas, and from the treasures hidden in the sand.

**20** About Gad he said: Blessed is he who enlarges Gad! Gad lies down like a lion, and tears at arm and head.[^29]

**21** He chose the best land for himself, for there a commander's portion was kept for him.[^30] He came with the leaders of the people; he carried out the justice of Yahweh, and his rulings for Israel.[^31]

**22** About Dan he said: Dan is a lion's cub, leaping out from Bashan.

**23** About Naphtali he said: Naphtali, satisfied with favor and full of the blessing of Yahweh, take possession of the sea and the south.[^32]

**24** About Asher he said: Most blessed of sons is Asher.[^33] May he be the favorite of his brothers, and may he dip his foot in oil.

**25** Your bolts will be iron and bronze, and your strength will last as long as your days.[^34]

**26** There is none like God, Jeshurun,[^35] who rides through the heavens to help you, through the skies in his majesty.

**27** The eternal God is your refuge,[^36] and underneath are the everlasting arms. He drove out the enemy before you, and said, "Destroy!"

**28** So Israel lives in safety, the spring of Jacob alone,[^37] in a land of grain and new wine, where the heavens drip with dew.

**29** How happy you are, Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by Yahweh? He is the shield that helps you, the sword of your triumph.[^38] Your enemies will cringe before you, and you will tread on their backs.[^39]

[^1]: Verses 2–29 are a poem, the Blessing of Moses. Compare Jacob's blessing of his sons in Genesis 49.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Or "He came with ten thousands of holy ones". Literally "he came from ten thousands of holiness".
[^4]: The Hebrew text is written as one word, *eshdat*, of uncertain meaning, but read as two words, *esh dat*, "a fire of law". The meaning of the line is uncertain.
[^5]: Or "the tribes". The Septuagint reads "his people".
[^6]: The meaning of this verse is uncertain. The verb translated "bow down" occurs only here.
[^7]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law".
[^8]: Probably Yahweh, but possibly Moses.
[^9]: A poetic name for Israel, probably from *yashar*, "upright" (32:15). Also in verse 26.
[^10]: Or "and let not his men be few". Literally "and let his men be a number".
[^11]: Literally "his hands contend for him".
[^12]: A Dead Sea Scroll (4QDeut-h) and the Septuagint read "Give to Levi your Thummim, and your Urim to your faithful one".
[^13]: Hebrew *tummim* and *urim*: objects the priest used to learn God's will (Exodus 28:30).
[^14]: Hebrew *chasid*, "loyal one", a word related to *chesed*, "faithful love".
[^15]: Literally "I have not seen him". Compare Exodus 32:26–29.
[^16]: The Hebrew text is written "his son" but read "his sons".
[^17]: Literally "in your nostril".
[^18]: Or "his wealth". Hebrew *chayil*.
[^19]: Literally "on him".
[^20]: The meaning is uncertain. Yahweh may dwell among Benjamin's hills (his "shoulders"), or Benjamin may rest on Yahweh's shoulders, like a child carried by a parent.
[^21]: Hebrew *meged*, "choice gift", repeated in verses 13–16. Compare Genesis 49:25–26.
[^22]: Literally "the yield of the moons".
[^23]: Literally "the head of the mountains of old".
[^24]: Exodus 3:2–4.
[^25]: Hebrew *nezir*, "one consecrated, a prince" (Genesis 49:26).
[^26]: Literally "The firstborn of his bull, majesty is his".
[^27]: Hebrew *re'em*, traditionally "unicorn": the wild ox, now extinct.
[^28]: Or "sacrifices of righteousness". Hebrew *zivche tsedeq*.
[^29]: Literally "the arm, and also the crown of the head".
[^30]: The meaning is uncertain. Literally "He saw the first part for himself, for there the portion of a commander was stored away".
[^31]: Or "the righteousness of Yahweh". Hebrew *tsidqat YHWH*.
[^32]: Or "the west and the south". Hebrew *yam*, "sea", here perhaps the Sea of Galilee.
[^33]: Or "Blessed above other sons is Asher". *Asher* sounds like the Hebrew word for "happy, blessed".
[^34]: Hebrew *dove*, a word found only here, perhaps "strength" or "rest".
[^35]: The Hebrew vowel points read "like God, Jeshurun". The same consonants can be read "like the God of Jeshurun".
[^36]: Or "dwelling place". Hebrew *me'onah*.
[^37]: The meaning is uncertain. Hebrew *ein* can mean "spring" or "eye"; the line may mean that Jacob's descendants live apart, on their own.
[^38]: Literally "the sword of your majesty".
[^39]: Or "on their high places". Hebrew *bamot*.
