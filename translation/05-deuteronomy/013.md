**1** Be careful to do everything I command you. Do not add to it or take anything away from it.[^1]

**2** If a prophet or someone who has dreams[^2] appears among you and gives you a sign or a wonder,

**3** and the sign or wonder he told you about comes true, and he says, "Let us follow other gods," gods you have not known, "and let us serve them,"

**4** do not listen to the words of that prophet or that dreamer. For the LORD[^3] your God is testing you, to find out whether you love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul.[^4]

**5** You must follow the LORD your God and fear him. Keep his commands and listen to his voice. Serve him and hold fast to him.

**6** That prophet or that dreamer must be put to death, because he has preached rebellion against the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and redeemed you from the place of slavery.[^5] He has tried to lead you away from the way that the LORD your God commanded you to walk in. So you must purge[^6] the evil from among you.

**7** Your brother, your mother's son,[^7] or your son or your daughter, or the wife you embrace,[^8] or your friend who is as dear to you as your own life,[^9] may secretly entice you, saying, "Let us go and serve other gods," gods that neither you nor your ancestors[^10] have known,

**8** some of the gods of the peoples around you, whether near you or far from you, from one end of the earth to the other.

**9** Do not give in to him or listen to him. Show him no pity. Do not spare him or shield him.

**10** You must kill him.[^11] Your hand must be the first against him to put him to death, and then the hands of all the people.

**11** Stone him to death,[^12] because he tried to lead you away from the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the place of slavery.

**12** Then all Israel will hear and be afraid, and no one among you will ever again do such an evil thing.

**13** You may hear it said about one of the towns that the LORD your God is giving you to live in

**14** that worthless men[^13] have gone out from among you and have led the people of their town astray, saying, "Let us go and serve other gods," gods you have not known.

**15** Then you must inquire, investigate and ask thoroughly. If it is true and certain that this detestable thing has been done among you,

**16** you must put the inhabitants of that town to the sword.[^14] Devote it to destruction,[^15] with everything in it, and put its livestock to the sword.

**17** Gather all its plunder into the middle of its public square, and burn the town and all its plunder in the fire as a whole offering[^16] to the LORD your God. It must remain a ruin mound[^17] forever. It must never be rebuilt.

**18** Nothing that is set apart for destruction must stick to your hand, so that the LORD may turn from his burning anger, and show you mercy and have compassion on you, and multiply you, as he swore to your ancestors,

**19** because you listen to the voice of the LORD your God, keeping all his commands that I am giving you today, and doing what is right in the eyes of the LORD your God.

[^1]: Verse 1 is 12:32 in most English Bibles, and verses 2–19 are 13:1–18.
[^2]: Literally "a dreamer of a dream"; also in verses 4 and 6.
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Hebrew *nephesh*: life, self, soul.
[^5]: Literally "house of slaves"; also in verse 11.
[^6]: Literally "burn out".
[^7]: The Samaritan Pentateuch and the Septuagint read "your brother, your father's son or your mother's son".
[^8]: Literally "the wife of your bosom".
[^9]: Literally "who is like your own *nephesh* (self)".
[^10]: Literally "fathers"; also in verse 18.
[^11]: The Septuagint reads "You must report him".
[^12]: Literally "Stone him with stones, and he shall die".
[^13]: Literally "sons of *beliyya'al*", "sons of worthlessness": scoundrels.
[^14]: Literally "strike … by the mouth of the sword"; also later in the verse.
[^15]: Hebrew *charam*: to give something over completely to God, which in war meant destroying it and keeping nothing for oneself. In verse 18, "set apart for destruction" is the related noun *cherem*.
[^16]: Hebrew *kalil*, "whole": an offering burned up completely.
[^17]: Hebrew *tel*: a mound formed by the ruins of a town.
