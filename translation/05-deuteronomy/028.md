**1** "If you listen carefully to the voice of Yahweh[^1] your God[^2] and are careful to follow all his commands that I am giving you today, Yahweh your God will set you high above all the nations of the earth.

**2** All these blessings will come on you and overtake you, if you listen to the voice of Yahweh your God:

**3** You will be blessed in the city, and blessed in the country.[^3]

**4** The fruit of your womb will be blessed, and the fruit of your ground and the fruit of your livestock, the calves of your herds and the lambs of your flocks.

**5** Your basket and your kneading bowl will be blessed.

**6** You will be blessed when you come in, and blessed when you go out.

**7** Yahweh will make your enemies who rise up against you be defeated before you. They will come out against you by one road, and flee from you by seven roads.

**8** Yahweh will command the blessing to be with you in your barns and in everything you set your hand to. He will bless you in the land that Yahweh your God is giving you.

**9** Yahweh will establish you as a people holy to himself, as he swore to you, if you keep the commands of Yahweh your God and walk in his ways.

**10** Then all the peoples of the earth will see that you are called by the name of Yahweh,[^4] and they will be afraid of you.

**11** Yahweh will make you abound in good things:[^5] in the fruit of your womb, the fruit of your livestock and the fruit of your ground, in the land that Yahweh swore to your ancestors[^6] to give you.

**12** Yahweh will open for you his rich storehouse, the heavens, to give rain to your land in its season and to bless all the work of your hands. You will lend to many nations, but you will not borrow.

**13** Yahweh will make you the head and not the tail. You will always be at the top and never at the bottom, if you listen to the commands of Yahweh your God that I am giving you today, and are careful to follow them,

**14** and if you do not turn aside from any of the words that I am commanding you today, to the right or to the left, to follow other gods and serve them.

**15** But if you do not listen to the voice of Yahweh your God, and are not careful to follow all his commands and his decrees that I am giving you today, then all these curses will come on you and overtake you:

**16** You will be cursed in the city, and cursed in the country.

**17** Your basket and your kneading bowl will be cursed.

**18** The fruit of your womb will be cursed, and the fruit of your ground, the calves of your herds and the lambs of your flocks.

**19** You will be cursed when you come in, and cursed when you go out.

**20** Yahweh will send on you curse, confusion and rebuke in everything you set your hand to, until you are destroyed and quickly perish because of the evil of your deeds, because you have abandoned me.[^7]

**21** Yahweh will make disease cling to you until he has wiped you out from the land you are entering to take possession of.

**22** Yahweh will strike you with wasting disease, fever and inflammation, with scorching heat and drought,[^8] and with blight and mildew. They will pursue you until you perish.

**23** The sky over your head will be bronze, and the earth under you iron.

**24** Yahweh will turn the rain of your land into powder and dust. It will come down on you from the sky until you are destroyed.

**25** Yahweh will make you be defeated before your enemies. You will go out against them by one road, and flee from them by seven roads. You will become a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth.

**26** Your dead bodies will be food for all the birds of the sky and the wild animals,[^9] and there will be no one to frighten them away.

**27** Yahweh will strike you with the boils of Egypt, with tumors,[^10] with festering sores and with the itch, and you will not be able to be healed.

**28** Yahweh will strike you with madness, blindness and confusion of mind.[^11]

**29** At noon you will grope about, as a blind person gropes in the darkness. You will not succeed in anything you do.[^12] You will be nothing but oppressed and robbed all the time, and there will be no one to rescue you.

**30** You will become engaged to a woman, but another man will sleep with her.[^13] You will build a house, but you will not live in it. You will plant a vineyard, but you will not enjoy its fruit.[^14]

**31** Your ox will be slaughtered before your eyes, but you will not eat any of it. Your donkey will be taken from you by force[^15] and will not be given back. Your sheep will be given to your enemies, and no one will rescue you.

**32** Your sons and your daughters will be given to another people, while your eyes look on and grow weary with longing for them all day long, and you will be powerless.[^16]

**33** A people you have not known will eat the fruit of your ground and everything you have worked for. You will be nothing but oppressed and crushed all the time.

**34** You will be driven mad by the sights your eyes will see.

**35** Yahweh will strike you on the knees and on the legs with painful boils that cannot be healed, from the sole of your foot to the top of your head.

**36** Yahweh will bring you, and the king you set over you, to a nation that neither you nor your ancestors have known. There you will serve other gods, gods of wood and stone.

**37** You will become a horror, a proverb and a byword[^17] among all the peoples where Yahweh will drive you.

**38** You will take much seed out to the field but gather little in, for the locusts will devour it.

**39** You will plant vineyards and work them, but you will not drink the wine or gather the grapes, for the worms will eat them.

**40** You will have olive trees throughout your territory, but you will not anoint yourself with the oil, for your olives will drop off.

**41** You will have sons and daughters, but they will not be yours, for they will go into captivity.

**42** Swarming locusts[^18] will take over all your trees and the fruit of your ground.

**43** The foreigner[^19] who lives among you will rise higher and higher above you, while you sink lower and lower.

**44** He will lend to you, but you will not lend to him. He will be the head, and you will be the tail.

**45** All these curses will come on you. They will pursue you and overtake you until you are destroyed, because you did not listen to the voice of Yahweh your God, to keep his commands and his decrees that he commanded you.

**46** They will be a sign and a wonder against you and your descendants forever.

**47** Because you did not serve Yahweh your God with joy and gladness of heart when you had plenty of everything,[^20]

**48** you will serve the enemies whom Yahweh will send against you, in hunger and thirst, in nakedness and in lack of everything. He will put an iron yoke on your neck until he has destroyed you.

**49** Yahweh will bring against you a nation from far away, from the end of the earth, swooping down like an eagle,[^21] a nation whose language you do not understand,

**50** a fierce-looking nation,[^22] which shows no respect for the old and no mercy to the young.

**51** It will eat the young of your livestock and the produce of your ground until you are destroyed. It will leave you no grain, new wine or olive oil, no calves of your herds or lambs of your flocks, until it has ruined you.

**52** It will besiege you in all your towns,[^23] until the high, fortified walls that you trust in come down throughout your land. It will besiege you in all your towns throughout the land that Yahweh your God has given you.

**53** In the siege and the distress that your enemy will bring on you, you will eat the fruit of your womb, the flesh of your sons and your daughters whom Yahweh your God has given you.

**54** Even the most gentle and refined man among you will look grudgingly[^24] at his brother, at the wife he embraces,[^25] and at the last of his children who are left,

**55** refusing to give any of them the flesh of his children that he is eating, because he has nothing else left in the siege and the distress that your enemy will bring on you in all your towns.

**56** The most gentle and refined woman among you, so gentle and refined that she would not think of setting the sole of her foot on the ground, will look grudgingly at the husband she embraces, at her son and at her daughter,

**57** and at the afterbirth that comes out from between her legs and at the children she bears. For she will eat them in secret, because she lacks everything, in the siege and the distress that your enemy will bring on you in your towns.

**58** If you are not careful to follow all the words of this law that are written in this book, and to fear this glorious and awesome name, Yahweh your God,

**59** Yahweh will strike you and your descendants with extraordinary plagues,[^26] great and lasting plagues, and severe and lasting illnesses.

**60** He will bring back on you all the diseases of Egypt that you dreaded, and they will cling to you.

**61** Yahweh will also bring on you every sickness and every plague that is not written in the book of this law, until you are destroyed.

**62** You who were as numerous as the stars of the sky will be left few in number,[^27] because you did not listen to the voice of Yahweh your God.

**63** Just as Yahweh delighted in doing you good and multiplying you, so Yahweh will delight in ruining you and destroying you. You will be torn from the land you are entering to take possession of.

**64** Yahweh will scatter you among all the peoples, from one end of the earth to the other. There you will serve other gods, gods of wood and stone, which neither you nor your ancestors have known.

**65** Among those nations you will find no rest, no resting place for the sole of your foot. There Yahweh will give you an anxious heart, eyes that fail with longing, and a despairing spirit.[^28]

**66** Your life will hang in doubt before you.[^29] You will be afraid night and day, and you will never be sure of your life.

**67** In the morning you will say, 'If only it were evening!' and in the evening you will say, 'If only it were morning!' because of the dread that fills your heart and the sights your eyes will see.

**68** Yahweh will bring you back to Egypt in ships, by the way that I told you you would never see again. There you will offer yourselves for sale to your enemies as male and female slaves, but no one will buy you."

**69** These are the words of the covenant that Yahweh commanded Moses to make with the Israelites in the land of Moab, in addition to the covenant he had made with them at Horeb.[^30]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Moses is still speaking, continuing the instructions he began in 27:11. His speech ends at verse 68.
[^3]: Literally "field"; also in verse 16.
[^4]: Literally "that the name of Yahweh is called over you", a sign that Israel belongs to him.
[^5]: Literally "make you have more than enough, for good".
[^6]: Literally "fathers"; also in verses 36 and 64.
[^7]: Here Moses speaks in Yahweh's own words.
[^8]: The Hebrew vowel points read "sword". The same consonants can be read "drought", which fits the list of crop diseases that follows.
[^9]: Literally "the beasts of the earth".
[^10]: The Hebrew text is written *ophalim*, "swellings", but read *techorim*, "tumors". Both words probably mean tumors or hemorrhoids; the read form was felt to be less coarse.
[^11]: Literally "of heart".
[^12]: Literally "You will not make your ways prosper".
[^13]: The Hebrew text is written with a coarser verb, "ravish her", but read "sleep with her".
[^14]: Hebrew *chillel*, "make common": begin to use the fruit for ordinary purposes (20:6).
[^15]: Literally "robbed from before your face".
[^16]: Literally "and there will be no power in your hand".
[^17]: Hebrew *shammah*, "something appalling", *mashal*, "proverb", and *sheninah*, "a sharp taunt".
[^18]: Hebrew *tselatsal*, a rare word, probably an insect named for its whirring sound.
[^19]: Hebrew *ger*: someone from elsewhere who lives among the Israelites, a resident foreigner.
[^20]: Literally "because of the abundance of everything".
[^21]: Or "vulture". Hebrew *nesher*.
[^22]: Literally "a nation strong of face".
[^23]: Literally "gates"; also later in the verse and in verses 55 and 57.
[^24]: Literally "his eye will be evil toward"; also in verse 56.
[^25]: Literally "the wife of his bosom". In verse 56, "the husband she embraces" is literally "the husband of her bosom".
[^26]: Literally "Yahweh will make your plagues wonderful".
[^27]: Literally "men of few".
[^28]: Literally "a trembling heart, a failing of eyes and a pining of *nephesh* (self)".
[^29]: Literally "Your life will be hung up for you in front of you".
[^30]: Verse 69 is 29:1 in most English Bibles.
