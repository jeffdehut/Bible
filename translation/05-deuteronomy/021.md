**1** If someone is found killed, lying in the open country in the land that Yahweh[^1] your God is giving you to possess, and it is not known who killed him,

**2** your elders and your judges are to go out and measure the distance to the towns around the body.

**3** The elders of the town nearest to the body are to take a heifer that has never been worked and has never pulled in a yoke.

**4** The elders of that town are to bring the heifer down to a valley with a stream that never runs dry,[^2] which has not been plowed or sown, and there in the valley they are to break the heifer's neck.

**5** Then the priests, the sons of Levi, are to come forward, for Yahweh your God has chosen them to serve him and to bless in the name of Yahweh, and by their word every dispute and every case of assault[^3] is to be settled.

**6** All the elders of the town nearest to the body are to wash their hands over the heifer whose neck was broken in the valley,

**7** and they are to declare,[^4] "Our hands did not shed this blood, and our eyes did not see it.

**8** Yahweh, forgive[^5] your people Israel, whom you have redeemed, and do not hold the guilt of innocent blood against your people Israel."[^6] Then the bloodshed will be forgiven them.

**9** So you will purge[^7] the guilt of innocent blood from among you, for you will be doing what is right in the eyes of Yahweh.

**10** When you go out to war against your enemies, and Yahweh your God hands them over to you and you take some of them captive,

**11** you may see among the captives a beautiful woman, and desire her, and want to take her as your wife.

**12** Bring her into your house. She is to shave her head and trim her nails,

**13** and take off the clothes she was wearing when she was captured. She is to live in your house and mourn her father and her mother for a full month.[^8] After that you may go to her and be her husband, and she will be your wife.

**14** But if you are no longer pleased with her, let her go wherever she wishes.[^9] You must not sell her for money or treat her as a slave,[^10] because you have dishonored her.[^11]

**15** If a man has two wives, one loved and the other unloved,[^12] and both the loved wife and the unloved wife bear him sons, and the firstborn son is the son of the unloved wife,

**16** then on the day he divides his property among his sons as their inheritance, he may not treat the son of the loved wife as his firstborn in place of the son of the unloved wife, who is the firstborn.

**17** He must acknowledge the son of the unloved wife as the firstborn by giving him a double share[^13] of everything he has, for he is the first sign of his father's strength.[^14] The right of the firstborn belongs to him.

**18** If someone has a stubborn and rebellious son who will not obey his father or his mother, and will not listen to them even when they discipline him,

**19** his father and his mother are to take hold of him and bring him out to the elders of his town, at the gate of the place where he lives.

**20** They are to say to the elders of his town, "This son of ours is stubborn and rebellious. He will not obey us. He is a glutton and a drunkard."

**21** Then all the men of his town are to stone him to death. So you must purge the evil from among you, and all Israel will hear and be afraid.

**22** If someone has committed a sin that deserves the death penalty and has been put to death, and you hang his body on a tree,[^15]

**23** his body must not remain on the tree overnight. You must bury him that same day, for anyone who is hung on a tree is cursed by God.[^16] You must not defile the land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *nachal eitan*: a valley with a stream that flows all year, or perhaps a valley with rough, rocky ground.
[^3]: Hebrew *nega*, "stroke": an injury, or perhaps a skin disease, as in 24:8. Compare 17:8.
[^4]: Literally "they shall answer and say".
[^5]: Hebrew *kapper*, "make atonement for, cover"; also later in the verse.
[^6]: Literally "and do not put innocent blood in the midst of your people Israel".
[^7]: Literally "burn out"; also in verse 21.
[^8]: Literally "a month of days".
[^9]: Literally "send her away to her *nephesh* (self)".
[^10]: Hebrew *hit'ammer*, a rare verb, perhaps "treat as merchandise" or "deal harshly with".
[^11]: Hebrew *innah*, "humble, force, violate".
[^12]: Literally "hated"; also in verses 16 and 17.
[^13]: Literally "a mouth of two", that is, twice as much as each of the other sons.
[^14]: Literally "the beginning of his strength".
[^15]: Or "on a pole": the body of the executed person was displayed.
[^16]: Literally "for a hanged one is a curse of God". Or "is an insult to God". Paul quotes this verse in Galatians 3:13.
