**1** "Love Yahweh[^1] your God, and always keep his charge,[^2] his statutes, his rulings and his commandments.

**2** Know today that it is not your children I am speaking to,[^3] who have not known or seen the discipline of Yahweh your God: his greatness, his mighty hand and his outstretched arm,

**3** his signs and the deeds he did in Egypt, to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to all his land,

**4** what he did to the army of Egypt, to its horses and its chariots, when he made the waters of the Red Sea[^4] flow over them as they pursued you, and Yahweh destroyed them, as can be seen to this day,[^5]

**5** what he did for you in the wilderness until you came to this place,

**6** and what he did to Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, son of Reuben, when the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them, with their households, their tents and every living thing that belonged to them,[^6] in the middle of all Israel.

**7** For it is your own eyes that have seen all the great work that Yahweh has done.

**8** So keep the whole commandment that I am giving you today, so that you may be strong, and go in and take possession of the land you are crossing over into to possess,

**9** and so that you may live long in the land that Yahweh swore to your ancestors[^7] to give to them and to their descendants, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**10** For the land you are entering to take possession of is not like the land of Egypt, from which you have come, where you sowed your seed and watered it with your foot,[^8] like a vegetable garden.

**11** The land you are crossing over into to possess is a land of hills and valleys, which drinks water from the rain of the sky,

**12** a land that Yahweh your God cares for. The eyes of Yahweh your God are always on it, from the beginning of the year to the end of the year.

**13** If you truly listen to my commandments[^9] that I am giving you today, to love Yahweh your God and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul,[^10]

**14** then I will give the rain for your land in its season, the autumn rain and the spring rain,[^11] and you will gather in your grain, your new wine and your olive oil.

**15** I will give grass in your fields for your livestock, and you will eat and be satisfied.

**16** Be careful, or your hearts will be led astray, and you will turn aside and serve other gods and bow down to them.

**17** Then the anger of Yahweh will burn against you. He will shut up the sky so that there is no rain, and the ground will not yield its produce, and you will quickly perish from the good land that Yahweh is giving you.

**18** Fix these words of mine in your hearts and in your souls. Tie them as a sign on your hands, and let them be a band on your foreheads.[^12]

**19** Teach them to your children, talking about them when you sit in your house and when you walk along the road, when you lie down and when you get up.

**20** Write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates,

**21** so that you and your children may live long in the land that Yahweh swore to your ancestors to give them, as long as the heavens are above the earth.[^13]

**22** For if you are careful to keep all this commandment that I am giving you to follow, to love Yahweh your God, to walk in all his ways and to hold fast to him,

**23** then Yahweh will drive out all these nations before you, and you will dispossess nations greater and stronger than you.

**24** Every place where the sole of your foot treads will be yours. Your territory will reach from the wilderness and Lebanon, and from the river, the river Euphrates, as far as the western sea.[^14]

**25** No one will be able to stand against you. Yahweh your God will put the dread and the fear of you on all the land you tread on, as he has promised you.

**26** See, I am setting before you today a blessing and a curse:

**27** the blessing, if you listen to the commandments of Yahweh your God that I am giving you today,

**28** and the curse, if you do not listen to the commandments of Yahweh your God, but turn aside from the way that I am commanding you today, to follow other gods that you have not known.

**29** When Yahweh your God brings you into the land you are entering to take possession of, you are to proclaim[^15] the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal.

**30** Are they not on the other side of the Jordan,[^16] beyond the road to the west, in the land of the Canaanites who live in the Arabah, opposite Gilgal, beside the oaks[^17] of Moreh?

**31** For you are about to cross the Jordan to go in and take possession of the land that Yahweh your God is giving you. When you have taken possession of it and are living in it,

**32** be careful to follow all the statutes and the rulings that I am setting before you today.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *mishmeret*, "what is to be kept": his requirements.
[^3]: Literally "Know today that not your children, who have not known and who have not seen …". The sentence is not finished; it is taken up again in verse 7: "for it is your own eyes that have seen …".
[^4]: Hebrew *Yam Suph*, literally "Sea of Reeds", traditionally the Red Sea.
[^5]: Literally "and Yahweh destroyed them until this day".
[^6]: Literally "all the living things that were at their feet" (Numbers 16:25–33).
[^7]: Literally "fathers"; also in verse 21.
[^8]: Perhaps by working a water wheel with the feet, or by opening and closing small irrigation channels with the foot.
[^9]: Here Moses speaks in Yahweh's own words, as also in verses 14–15.
[^10]: Hebrew *nephesh*: life, self, soul. Also in verse 18.
[^11]: The early rains of October and November and the late rains of March and April.
[^12]: Literally "and they shall be *totaphot* between your eyes"; see 6:8.
[^13]: Literally "so that your days and the days of your children may be many on the land … like the days of the heavens over the earth".
[^14]: Literally "the sea behind", the Mediterranean.
[^15]: Literally "put". See 27:11–13.
[^16]: Literally "beyond the Jordan". Here it means west of the river, seen from where Moses stands.
[^17]: Hebrew *elonim*, large trees, oaks or terebinths (Genesis 12:6).
