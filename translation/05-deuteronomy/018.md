**1** The Levitical priests,[^1] the whole tribe of Levi, are to have no share or inheritance with Israel. They are to eat the food offerings for Yahweh,[^2] which are his inheritance.[^3]

**2** They will have no inheritance among their brothers.[^4] Yahweh is their inheritance, as he promised them.

**3** This is what is due to the priests from the people, from those who offer a sacrifice, whether an ox or a sheep: they are to give the priest the shoulder, the two cheeks and the stomach.

**4** You are to give him the first of your grain, your new wine and your olive oil, and the first wool from the shearing of your sheep.

**5** For Yahweh your God has chosen him and his sons out of all your tribes to stand and serve in the name of Yahweh for all time.

**6** If a Levite comes from one of your towns anywhere in Israel where he is living,[^5] he may come whenever he wishes[^6] to the place that Yahweh will choose,

**7** and serve in the name of Yahweh his God like all his fellow Levites who stand there before Yahweh.

**8** They are to have equal portions to eat, besides what he receives from the sale of his family property.[^7]

**9** When you come into the land that Yahweh your God is giving you, do not learn to imitate the detestable practices[^8] of those nations.

**10** There must not be found among you anyone who makes his son or his daughter pass through the fire,[^9] or who practices divination, or tells fortunes, or interprets omens, or practices sorcery,[^10]

**11** or casts spells, or consults ghosts or spirits,[^11] or inquires of the dead.

**12** For anyone who does these things is detestable to Yahweh, and it is because of these detestable practices that Yahweh your God is driving those nations out before you.

**13** You must be blameless[^12] before Yahweh your God.

**14** These nations you are about to dispossess listen to fortune-tellers and diviners. But as for you, Yahweh your God has not allowed you to do so.

**15** Yahweh your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among you, from your own people.[^13] You must listen to him.

**16** This is just what you asked of Yahweh your God at Horeb on the day of the assembly, when you said, "Let me not hear the voice of Yahweh my God again, or see this great fire any more, or I will die."

**17** And Yahweh said to me, "What they have said is good.

**18** I will raise up for them a prophet like you from among their own people. I will put my words in his mouth, and he will tell them everything I command him.

**19** If anyone does not listen to my words that he speaks in my name, I myself will hold him accountable.[^14]

**20** But a prophet who presumes to speak in my name a word I have not commanded him to speak, or who speaks in the name of other gods, that prophet must die."

**21** You may say to yourself,[^15] "How can we know which word Yahweh has not spoken?"

**22** If a prophet speaks in the name of Yahweh and the thing does not happen or come true, that is a word Yahweh has not spoken. The prophet has spoken it presumptuously. Do not be afraid of him.

[^1]: Literally "the priests, the Levites".
[^2]: Hebrew *ishshe YHWH*, gifts given to Yahweh and usually burned on the altar, traditionally "offerings made by fire". *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "and his inheritance": the portions that belong to Yahweh, or the portion that is Levi's.
[^4]: Literally "He will have no inheritance among his brothers. Yahweh is his inheritance, as he promised him." The singular refers to the tribe of Levi.
[^5]: Hebrew *gar*, "lives as a newcomer", the verb related to *ger*, "resident foreigner".
[^6]: Literally "with all the desire of his *nephesh* (self)".
[^7]: Literally "apart from his sales by the fathers". The meaning is uncertain.
[^8]: Hebrew *to'evot*, things that are abhorrent, especially in worship. Also in verse 12.
[^9]: That is, sacrifices them by burning (12:31).
[^10]: The exact meaning of several of the terms in verses 10–11 is uncertain.
[^11]: Hebrew *ov* and *yidde'oni*: the spirits of the dead, or the people who claim to call them up.
[^12]: Hebrew *tamim*, "whole, complete": wholehearted and without fault.
[^13]: Literally "from your brothers"; also in verse 18. Acts 3:22–23 and 7:37 apply verses 15–19 to Jesus.
[^14]: Literally "I will require it from him".
[^15]: Literally "You may say in your heart".
