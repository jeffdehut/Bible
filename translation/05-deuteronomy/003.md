**1** Then we turned and went up the road to Bashan, and Og king of Bashan came out against us, he and all his people, to fight at Edrei.

**2** Yahweh[^1] said to me, "Do not be afraid of him, for I have handed him over to you, with all his people and his land. Do to him what you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon."

**3** So Yahweh our God also handed over to us Og king of Bashan and all his people. We struck him down until no survivor was left to him.

**4** At that time we captured all his towns. There was not a town that we did not take from them: sixty towns, the whole region[^2] of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.

**5** All these were towns fortified with high walls, gates and bars, not counting a great many unwalled villages.[^3]

**6** We devoted them to destruction,[^4] as we had done to Sihon king of Heshbon, devoting every town to destruction, men, women and children.

**7** But all the livestock and the spoil of the towns we took as plunder for ourselves.

**8** So at that time we took the land from the two kings of the Amorites east of the Jordan,[^5] from the Arnon Valley to Mount Hermon.

**9** (The Sidonians call Hermon Sirion, and the Amorites call it Senir.)

**10** We took all the towns of the plateau,[^6] all Gilead and all Bashan as far as Salecah and Edrei, towns of Og's kingdom in Bashan.

**11** (Only Og king of Bashan was left of the remnant of the Rephaim. His bed[^7] was made of iron. Isn't it still in Rabbah of the Ammonites? It is nine cubits long and four cubits wide, by the standard cubit.[^8])

**12** So we took possession of this land at that time. I gave the Reubenites and the Gadites the land from Aroer, by the Arnon Valley, and half the hill country of Gilead with its towns.

**13** The rest of Gilead, and all Bashan, the kingdom of Og, I gave to the half-tribe of Manasseh. (The whole region of Argob, all that part of Bashan, is called the land of the Rephaim.

**14** Jair, a descendant of Manasseh,[^9] took the whole region of Argob as far as the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites. He named Bashan after himself, Havvoth-jair,[^10] as it is called to this day.)

**15** To Machir I gave Gilead.

**16** To the Reubenites and the Gadites I gave the territory from Gilead as far as the Arnon Valley, with the middle of the valley as the border, and as far as the Jabbok River,[^11] the border of the Ammonites.

**17** Their territory also included the Arabah, with the Jordan as its border, from Kinnereth[^12] to the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea,[^13] below the slopes of Pisgah on the east.

**18** At that time I commanded you, "Yahweh your God has given you this land to possess. All your fighting men[^14] are to cross over armed ahead of your fellow Israelites.[^15]

**19** Only your wives, your little ones and your livestock (I know you have a great deal of livestock) may stay in the towns I have given you,

**20** until Yahweh gives your relatives rest as he has given you, and they too take possession of the land that Yahweh your God is giving them on the other side of the Jordan.[^16] Then each of you may return to the possession I have given you."

**21** At that time I commanded Joshua, "Your own eyes have seen everything Yahweh your God has done to these two kings. Yahweh will do the same to all the kingdoms you are crossing over into.

**22** Do not be afraid of them, for Yahweh your God is the one who fights for you."

**23** At that time I pleaded with Yahweh:

**24** "Lord Yahweh,[^17] you have begun to show your servant your greatness and your mighty hand. What god is there in heaven or on earth who can do deeds and mighty acts like yours?

**25** Please let me cross over and see the good land on the other side of the Jordan, that good hill country, and Lebanon."

**26** But Yahweh was angry with me because of you, and he would not listen to me. Yahweh said to me, "That is enough![^18] Do not speak to me about this matter again.

**27** Go up to the top of Pisgah and look west, north, south and east.[^19] See it with your own eyes, for you will not cross this Jordan.

**28** Give Joshua his orders. Encourage him and strengthen him, for he will cross over at the head of this people, and he will lead them to take possession of the land that you will see."

**29** So we stayed in the valley opposite Beth-peor.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *chevel*, literally "measuring line", here a measured-out region. Also in verses 13 and 14.
[^3]: Literally "towns of the open country".
[^4]: Hebrew *charam*: to give something over completely to God, which in war meant destroying it and keeping nothing for oneself.
[^5]: Literally "beyond the Jordan", seen from the land west of the river.
[^6]: Hebrew *mishor*, "level land": the high tableland north of the Arnon.
[^7]: Or "his coffin". Hebrew *eres*, "bed, couch". Some take it to mean a coffin made of a dark, hard stone like iron.
[^8]: A cubit was about 18 inches (45 cm), so the bed was about 13 feet long and 6 feet wide (4 by 1.8 meters). Literally "by the cubit of a man", that is, the ordinary cubit.
[^9]: Literally "son of Manasseh". In Hebrew "son" can mean any descendant.
[^10]: *Havvoth-jair* means "the tent villages of Jair".
[^11]: Hebrew *nachal*: a valley with a stream that flows in the rainy season. Also earlier in the verse.
[^12]: The Sea of Galilee, which the Hebrew elsewhere calls the Sea of Kinnereth.
[^13]: The Dead Sea.
[^14]: Literally "all the sons of strength".
[^15]: Literally "your brothers, the sons of Israel". In verse 20 "your relatives" is literally "your brothers".
[^16]: Literally "beyond the Jordan". Here it means west of the river, seen from where Moses stands. Also in verse 25.
[^17]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^18]: Literally "Much for you", the words translated "long enough" in 1:6 and 2:3.
[^19]: Literally "lift up your eyes toward the sea and north and south and east". West is "toward the sea", the Mediterranean.
