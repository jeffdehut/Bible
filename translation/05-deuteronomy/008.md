**1** Be careful to follow every commandment[^1] that I am giving you today, so that you may live and multiply, and go in and take possession of the land that Yahweh[^2] swore to give your ancestors.[^3]

**2** Remember the whole way that Yahweh your God has led you these forty years in the wilderness, to humble you and to test you, to know what was in your heart, whether you would keep his commandments[^4] or not.

**3** He humbled you and let you go hungry, and then he fed you with manna,[^5] which neither you nor your ancestors had known, to teach you that people[^6] do not live on bread alone, but on everything that comes from the mouth of Yahweh.[^7]

**4** Your clothes did not wear out on you, and your feet did not swell, these forty years.

**5** Know then in your heart that Yahweh your God disciplines you as a man disciplines his son.

**6** Keep the commandments of Yahweh your God by walking in his ways and fearing him.

**7** For Yahweh your God is bringing you into a good land, a land of streams of water, of springs and deep waters[^8] that flow out in the valleys and in the hills,

**8** a land of wheat and barley, of vines, fig trees and pomegranates, a land of olive oil[^9] and honey,

**9** a land where you will eat food[^10] without scarcity, where you will lack nothing, a land whose stones are iron and from whose hills you can dig copper.

**10** When you have eaten and are satisfied, you will bless Yahweh your God for the good land he has given you.

**11** Be careful not to forget Yahweh your God by failing to keep his commandments, his rulings and his statutes that I am giving you today.

**12** Otherwise, when you have eaten and are satisfied, when you have built fine houses and live in them,

**13** and when your herds and your flocks have grown large, and your silver and gold have increased, and everything you have has increased,

**14** your heart will become proud,[^11] and you will forget Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.[^12]

**15** He led you through the great and terrible wilderness, with its poisonous snakes[^13] and scorpions, a thirsty land where there was no water. He brought water out for you from the flint rock.

**16** He fed you in the wilderness with manna, which your ancestors had not known, to humble you and to test you, so that in the end he might do you good.

**17** You might say to yourself,[^14] "My power and the strength of my hand have gained me this wealth."

**18** But remember Yahweh your God, for it is he who gives you the power to gain wealth, so that he may confirm his covenant that he swore to your ancestors, as he is doing today.

**19** If you ever forget Yahweh your God and follow other gods, and serve them and bow down to them, I warn you today[^15] that you will surely perish.

**20** Like the nations that Yahweh is destroying before you, so you will perish, because you would not listen to the voice of Yahweh your God.

[^1]: Literally "all the commandment", the law as a whole.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "fathers"; also in verses 3, 16 and 18.
[^4]: The Hebrew text is written "his commandment" but read "his commandments".
[^5]: Hebrew *man*, the food God gave in the wilderness (Exodus 16:14–31).
[^6]: Hebrew *adam*, "a human being, humankind".
[^7]: Literally "but on all that goes out of the mouth of Yahweh". Jesus quotes this verse in Matthew 4:4 and Luke 4:4, where the Greek has "every word".
[^8]: Hebrew *tehomot*, "deeps": water from under the ground.
[^9]: Literally "olive trees of oil".
[^10]: Literally "bread".
[^11]: Literally "and your heart will be lifted up".
[^12]: Literally "house of slaves".
[^13]: Literally "fiery serpent", perhaps from the burning of their bite (Numbers 21:6).
[^14]: Literally "You might say in your heart".
[^15]: Literally "I testify against you today".
