**1** When you have come into the land that Yahweh[^1] your God is giving you as an inheritance, and have taken possession of it and settled in it,

**2** take some of the first of all the produce of the soil that you bring in from the land that Yahweh your God is giving you, and put it in a basket. Then go to the place that Yahweh your God will choose as a dwelling for his name.[^2]

**3** Go to the priest who is in office at that time and say to him, "I declare today to Yahweh your God that I have come into the land that Yahweh swore to our ancestors[^3] to give us."

**4** The priest is to take the basket from your hand and set it down in front of the altar of Yahweh your God.

**5** Then you are to declare[^4] before Yahweh your God, "My father was a wandering Aramean.[^5] He went down to Egypt with only a few people and lived there as a foreigner,[^6] and there he became a great nation, mighty and numerous.

**6** But the Egyptians treated us harshly and oppressed us, and they imposed hard labor on us.

**7** Then we cried out to Yahweh, the God of our ancestors, and Yahweh heard our voice and saw our misery, our toil and our oppression.

**8** Yahweh brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm, with great terror,[^7] and with signs and wonders.

**9** He brought us to this place and gave us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey.

**10** And now, here I have brought the first of the produce of the soil that you, Yahweh, have given me." Then set it down before Yahweh your God, and bow down before Yahweh your God.

**11** Then you and the Levite and the foreigner who lives among you are to rejoice in all the good things that Yahweh your God has given to you and your household.

**12** When you have finished setting aside the whole tithe of your produce in the third year, the year of the tithe, and have given it to the Levite, the foreigner, the fatherless[^8] and the widow, so that they may eat in your towns[^9] and be satisfied,

**13** then say before Yahweh your God, "I have removed the holy portion from my house, and I have given it to the Levite, the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow, just as you commanded me. I have not turned aside from any of your commandments, and I have not forgotten them.

**14** I have not eaten any of it while I was in mourning, or removed any of it while I was unclean, or given any of it for the dead.[^10] I have listened to the voice of Yahweh my God. I have done everything you commanded me.

**15** Look down from your holy dwelling, from heaven, and bless your people Israel and the land you have given us, as you swore to our ancestors, a land flowing with milk and honey."

**16** This day Yahweh your God commands you to follow these statutes and rulings. Be careful to follow them with all your heart and with all your soul.[^11]

**17** Today you have declared[^12] that Yahweh is your God, and that you will walk in his ways, and keep his statutes, his commandments and his rulings, and listen to his voice.

**18** And today Yahweh has declared that you are his people, his treasured possession,[^13] as he promised you, and that you are to keep all his commandments,

**19** and that he will set you high above all the nations he has made, for praise, fame and honor, and that you will be a people holy to Yahweh your God, as he promised.[^14]

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "to make his name dwell there".
[^3]: Literally "fathers"; also in verses 7 and 15.
[^4]: Literally "answer and say".
[^5]: Or "a perishing Aramean", "an Aramean about to perish". Hebrew *arammi oved avi*. The father is probably Jacob, who lived for many years in Aram (Genesis 29–31).
[^6]: Hebrew *gur*, "live as a newcomer", the verb related to *ger*, "resident foreigner".
[^7]: Or "with great and awesome deeds". Hebrew *mora*.
[^8]: Hebrew *yatom*, a child who has lost his father, traditionally "orphan". Also in verse 13.
[^9]: Literally "gates".
[^10]: Perhaps food offered to the dead, or food given for a funeral meal.
[^11]: Hebrew *nephesh*: life, self, soul.
[^12]: Hebrew *he'emarta*, a form of the verb "say" found only here and in verse 18. It may mean "you have declared" or "you have made Yahweh declare".
[^13]: Hebrew *segullah*, a king's or an owner's personal treasure.
[^14]: Moses' speech that began at 5:1 ends here.
