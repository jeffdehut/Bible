**1** If there is a dispute between people, and they go to court and the judges decide between them, they are to acquit the innocent and condemn the guilty.[^1]

**2** If the guilty one deserves to be beaten,[^2] the judge is to make him lie down and have him beaten in his presence with the number of lashes his crime deserves.

**3** He may be given forty lashes, but no more. If he is beaten with many more lashes than these, your fellow Israelite[^3] will be degraded before your eyes.

**4** Do not muzzle an ox while it is treading out the grain.[^4]

**5** If brothers live together and one of them dies without a son, the dead man's widow must not marry outside the family, to a stranger. Her husband's brother is to go to her and take her as his wife, and do the duty of a husband's brother[^5] to her.

**6** The first son she bears is to carry on the name of the dead brother, so that his name will not be wiped out from Israel.

**7** But if the man does not want to marry his brother's widow, she is to go up to the elders at the gate and say, "My husband's brother refuses to keep his brother's name alive in Israel. He is not willing to do the duty of a husband's brother to me."

**8** Then the elders of his town are to summon him and speak to him. If he stands firm and says, "I do not want to marry her,"

**9** his brother's widow is to go up to him in the presence of the elders, pull his sandal off his foot, spit in his face, and declare,[^6] "This is what is done to the man who will not build up his brother's family."[^7]

**10** And his family will be known in Israel as "The house of the man whose sandal was pulled off."[^8]

**11** If two men are fighting each other, and the wife of one of them comes near to rescue her husband from the man who is hitting him, and she reaches out and seizes him by his private parts,

**12** you are to cut off her hand. Show her no pity.[^9]

**13** Do not have two different weights in your bag, one heavy and one light.[^10]

**14** Do not have two different measures[^11] in your house, one large and one small.

**15** You must have a full and honest weight and a full and honest measure, so that you may live long in the land that Yahweh[^12] your God is giving you.

**16** For anyone who does such things, anyone who acts dishonestly, is detestable to Yahweh your God.

**17** Remember what Amalek did to you on the way when you came out of Egypt,[^13]

**18** how he met you on the way when you were faint and weary, and cut off all the stragglers at your rear. He did not fear God.

**19** So when Yahweh your God has given you rest from all your enemies around you in the land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance to possess, you must wipe out the memory of Amalek from under heaven. Do not forget!

[^1]: Literally "declare righteous the righteous one and declare wicked the wicked one".
[^2]: Literally "if the wicked one is a son of beating".
[^3]: Literally "your brother". Compare 2 Corinthians 11:24.
[^4]: Paul quotes this verse in 1 Corinthians 9:9 and 1 Timothy 5:18.
[^5]: Hebrew *yabbem*: the custom traditionally called levirate marriage, from Latin *levir*, "husband's brother". Also in verse 7. Mark 12:19 refers to this law.
[^6]: Literally "answer and say".
[^7]: Literally "house".
[^8]: Literally "And his name shall be called in Israel 'House of the one whose sandal was pulled off.'"
[^9]: Literally "Your eye shall not pity".
[^10]: Literally "a stone and a stone, a large and a small". Weights were made of stone.
[^11]: Hebrew *ephah*, a measure for grain, used here for any measure of volume. Also in verse 15.
[^12]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^13]: Exodus 17:8–16.
