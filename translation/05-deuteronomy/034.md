**1** Then Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, opposite Jericho. And the LORD[^1] showed him the whole land: Gilead as far as Dan,

**2** all Naphtali, the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, all the land of Judah as far as the western sea,[^2]

**3** the Negev, and the plain of the valley of Jericho,[^3] the city of palm trees, as far as Zoar.

**4** Then the LORD said to him, "This is the land I swore to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, when I said, 'I will give it to your descendants.' I have let you see it with your own eyes, but you will not cross over into it."

**5** So Moses the servant of the LORD died there in the land of Moab, as the LORD had said.[^4]

**6** He[^5] buried him in the valley in the land of Moab, opposite Beth-peor, and to this day no one knows where his grave is.

**7** Moses was 120 years old when he died. His eyesight was not dim, and his vigor had not left him.

**8** The Israelites wept for Moses in the plains of Moab for thirty days. Then the days of weeping and mourning for Moses came to an end.

**9** Joshua son of Nun was filled with the spirit of wisdom,[^6] because Moses had laid his hands on him. So the Israelites listened to him and did as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**10** Never since has a prophet arisen in Israel like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face,

**11** in all the signs and wonders that the LORD sent him to do in the land of Egypt, to Pharaoh and to all his servants and to all his land,

**12** and in all the mighty power[^7] and all the great and terrifying deeds that Moses performed in the sight of all Israel.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "the sea behind", the Mediterranean.
[^3]: Hebrew *kikkar*, "circle": the round plain of the Jordan near Jericho.
[^4]: Literally "at the mouth of the LORD".
[^5]: The Hebrew does not name the subject. It is probably the LORD; or "he was buried". The Septuagint reads "they buried him".
[^6]: Hebrew *ruach*: spirit, breath.
[^7]: Literally "all the strong hand".
