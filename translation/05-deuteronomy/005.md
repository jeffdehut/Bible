**1** Moses summoned all Israel and said to them,[^1] "Hear, Israel, the decrees and the rulings[^2] that I am speaking in your hearing today. Learn them and be careful to follow them.

**2** Yahweh[^3] our God made a covenant with us at Horeb.

**3** It was not with our ancestors[^4] that Yahweh made this covenant, but with us, all of us who are alive here today.

**4** Yahweh spoke with you face to face at the mountain, out of the fire.

**5** At that time I was standing between Yahweh and you to tell you the word of Yahweh, because you were afraid of the fire and did not go up the mountain. He said:

**6** 'I am Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the place of slavery.[^5]

**7** You must have no other gods besides me.[^6]

**8** You must not make for yourself an idol,[^7] an image of anything in the heavens above, or on the earth below, or in the waters under the earth.

**9** You must not bow down to them or serve them, for I, Yahweh your God, am a jealous God.[^8] I punish children for the wrongdoing of their parents, to the third and the fourth generation of those who hate me,[^9]

**10** but I show faithful love[^10] to thousands[^11] of those who love me and keep my commands.[^12]

**11** You must not misuse the name of Yahweh your God,[^13] for Yahweh will not leave unpunished anyone who misuses his name.

**12** Observe the Sabbath day, to keep it holy, as Yahweh your God has commanded you.[^14]

**13** For six days you are to work and do all your work,

**14** but the seventh day is a Sabbath[^15] to Yahweh your God. On it you must not do any work, neither you, nor your son or your daughter, nor your male or female slave,[^16] nor your ox or your donkey or any of your livestock, nor the foreigner who lives in your towns,[^17] so that your male and female slaves may rest as you do.

**15** Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and Yahweh your God brought you out from there with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm. That is why Yahweh your God has commanded you to keep the Sabbath day.

**16** Honor your father and your mother, as Yahweh your God has commanded you, so that you may live long, and so that it may go well with you in the land that Yahweh your God is giving you.[^18]

**17** You must not murder.[^19]

**18** You must not commit adultery.[^20]

**19** You must not steal.

**20** You must not give false testimony against your neighbor.[^21]

**21** You must not covet your neighbor's wife. You must not desire your neighbor's house, his field, his male or female slave, his ox or his donkey, or anything that belongs to your neighbor.'[^22]

**22** These are the words that Yahweh spoke with a loud voice to your whole assembly at the mountain, out of the fire, the cloud and the thick darkness, and he added nothing more.[^23] He wrote them on two stone tablets and gave them to me.

**23** When you heard the voice out of the darkness, while the mountain was blazing with fire, all the heads of your tribes and your elders came to me

**24** and said, 'See, Yahweh our God has shown us his glory and his greatness, and we have heard his voice out of the fire. Today we have seen that God can speak with a human being and that person still live.

**25** But now, why should we die? This great fire will consume us. If we hear the voice of Yahweh our God any longer, we will die.

**26** For who among all humankind[^24] has heard the voice of the living God speaking out of fire, as we have, and lived?

**27** You go near and listen to everything that Yahweh our God says. Then tell us everything that Yahweh our God tells you, and we will listen and do it.'

**28** Yahweh heard what you said[^25] when you spoke to me, and Yahweh said to me, 'I have heard what this people said to you. Everything they have said is good.

**29** If only they would always have a heart like this, to fear me and keep all my commands, so that it might go well with them and with their children forever!

**30** Go and tell them, "Return to your tents."

**31** But you, stand here with me, and I will tell you the whole command,[^26] the decrees and the rulings that you are to teach them, so that they may follow them in the land that I am giving them to possess.'

**32** So be careful to do as Yahweh your God has commanded you. Do not turn aside to the right or to the left.

**33** Walk in all the way that Yahweh your God has commanded you, so that you may live and prosper, and live long in the land you will possess.

[^1]: Moses' second speech begins here. It continues through the laws of chapters 12–26, which are set without quotation marks, and ends at 26:19.
[^2]: Hebrew *chuqqim*, "decrees", and *mishpatim*, "judgments, rulings".
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Literally "fathers".
[^5]: Literally "house of slaves". Verses 6–21 are the Ten Commandments, also given in Exodus 20:2–17.
[^6]: Or "before me", "in addition to me". Literally "against my face".
[^7]: Hebrew *pesel*, a carved or cast image. Exodus 20:4 reads "an idol, or an image of anything".
[^8]: Hebrew *el qanna*: a God who will not share his people's devotion with any other.
[^9]: Literally "visiting the iniquity of fathers on sons, and on the third and on the fourth of those who hate me".
[^10]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^11]: Or "to a thousand generations".
[^12]: The Hebrew text is written "his command" but read "my commands".
[^13]: Literally "Do not lift up the name of Yahweh your God for emptiness". Traditionally "take the name in vain". It may refer to false oaths or to any empty or wrongful use of God's name.
[^14]: Exodus 20:8 reads "Remember the Sabbath day". Here the verb is *shamor*, "keep, observe".
[^15]: Hebrew *shabbat*, from the verb *shavat*, "stop, rest".
[^16]: Or "servant". Hebrew *eved*, male slave or servant, and *amah*, female slave or servant. Also in verse 21.
[^17]: Literally "your foreigner who is within your gates". Hebrew *ger*: someone from elsewhere who lives among the Israelites, a resident foreigner.
[^18]: Exodus 20:12 does not have "as Yahweh your God has commanded you" or "so that it may go well with you".
[^19]: Hebrew *ratsach*: to kill a person unlawfully.
[^20]: In the Hebrew, this commandment and those in verses 19–21 each begin with "and", joining them in a series. In Exodus 20:14–17 they stand alone.
[^21]: Literally "Do not answer against your neighbor as a worthless witness". Exodus 20:16 has "a false witness".
[^22]: Two different verbs are used: *chamad*, "covet", and *hit'avvah*, "desire, crave". Exodus 20:17 has "covet" both times, puts "house" before "wife", and does not have "his field".
[^23]: Or "and it did not stop". Literally "and he did not add".
[^24]: Literally "all flesh".
[^25]: Literally "the sound of your words"; also later in the verse.
[^26]: The singular means the law as a whole.
