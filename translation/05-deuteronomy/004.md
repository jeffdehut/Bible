**1** Now, Israel, listen to the statutes and the rulings[^1] that I am teaching you to follow, so that you may live, and go in and take possession of the land that Yahweh,[^2] the God of your ancestors,[^3] is giving you.

**2** Do not add anything to what I command you, and do not take anything away from it. Keep the commandments of Yahweh your God that I am giving you.

**3** You saw with your own eyes what Yahweh did at Baal-peor.[^4] Yahweh your God destroyed from among you everyone who followed the Baal of Peor.

**4** But all of you who held fast to Yahweh your God are alive today.

**5** See, I have taught you statutes and rulings, as Yahweh my God commanded me, so that you may follow them in the land you are entering to take possession of.

**6** Keep them and follow them, for this will show your wisdom and understanding to the peoples.[^5] When they hear about all these statutes, they will say, "Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people."

**7** For what great nation has a god[^6] so near to it as Yahweh our God is to us whenever we call on him?

**8** And what great nation has statutes and rulings as righteous as this whole law that I am setting before you today?

**9** Only be careful, and watch yourselves closely,[^7] so that you do not forget the things your eyes have seen, and so that they do not slip from your heart[^8] as long as you live. Make them known to your children and your grandchildren:

**10** the day you stood before Yahweh your God at Horeb, when Yahweh said to me, "Assemble the people for me, and I will let them hear my words, so that they may learn to fear me as long as they live on the earth, and may teach their children."

**11** You came near and stood at the foot of the mountain, while the mountain blazed with fire up to the heart of the sky,[^9] with darkness, cloud and thick gloom.

**12** Then Yahweh spoke to you out of the fire. You heard the sound of words, but you saw no form. There was only a voice.

**13** He declared to you his covenant, which he commanded you to keep:[^10] the Ten Commandments.[^11] And he wrote them on two stone tablets.

**14** At that time Yahweh commanded me to teach you statutes and rulings, for you to follow in the land you are crossing over into to take possession of.

**15** So watch yourselves very carefully.[^12] Since you saw no form of any kind on the day Yahweh spoke to you at Horeb out of the fire,

**16** do not act corruptly by making for yourselves an idol[^13] in the form of any figure: the likeness of a male or a female,

**17** the likeness of any animal on the earth, the likeness of any winged bird that flies in the sky,

**18** the likeness of anything that creeps on the ground, or the likeness of any fish in the waters below the earth.

**19** And when you look up to the sky and see the sun, the moon and the stars, the whole array of heaven,[^14] do not be drawn away to bow down to them and serve them. Yahweh your God has allotted them to all the peoples everywhere under heaven.

**20** But you are the ones Yahweh took and brought out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be the people of his inheritance,[^15] as you are today.

**21** Yahweh was angry with me because of you,[^16] and he swore that I would not cross the Jordan or enter the good land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance.

**22** For I am going to die in this land. I will not cross the Jordan. But you are going to cross over and take possession of this good land.

**23** Be careful not to forget the covenant that Yahweh your God made with you. Do not make for yourselves an idol in the form of anything that Yahweh your God has forbidden you.[^17]

**24** For Yahweh your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God.[^18]

**25** When you have had children and grandchildren and have been in the land a long time, if you act corruptly by making an idol in the form of anything, and do what is evil in the eyes of Yahweh your God, provoking him to anger,

**26** I call heaven and earth as witnesses against you today that you will soon perish completely from the land you are crossing the Jordan to take possession of. You will not live long on it, but will be utterly destroyed.

**27** Yahweh will scatter you among the peoples, and only a few of you[^19] will be left among the nations where Yahweh will drive you.

**28** There you will serve gods made by human hands, of wood and stone, which cannot see or hear or eat or smell.

**29** But from there you will seek Yahweh your God, and you will find him, if you search for him with all your heart and with all your soul.[^20]

**30** When you are in distress and all these things have happened to you,[^21] in days to come,[^22] you will return to Yahweh your God and listen to his voice.

**31** For Yahweh your God is a compassionate God. He will not abandon you or destroy you, and he will not forget the covenant with your ancestors that he swore to them.

**32** Ask now about the former days, the days before you, from the day God created human beings[^23] on the earth. Ask from one end of heaven to the other: has anything as great as this ever happened, or has anything like it ever been heard of?

**33** Has any people ever heard the voice of God[^24] speaking out of fire, as you have heard it, and lived?

**34** Or has any god ever tried to go and take for himself one nation from within another nation, by trials,[^25] by signs and wonders, by war, by a mighty hand and an outstretched arm, and by great and terrifying deeds, as Yahweh your God did for you in Egypt before your eyes?

**35** You were shown these things so that you would know that Yahweh is God. There is no other besides him.

**36** From heaven he let you hear his voice to discipline you, and on earth he let you see his great fire, and you heard his words out of the fire.

**37** Because he loved your ancestors, he chose their descendants after them,[^26] and he himself[^27] brought you out of Egypt by his great power,

**38** to drive out before you nations greater and stronger than you, to bring you in and give you their land as an inheritance, as it is today.

**39** So know today, and take it to heart, that Yahweh is God in heaven above and on the earth below. There is no other.

**40** Keep his statutes and his commandments, which I am giving you today, so that it may go well with you and with your children after you, and so that you may live long in the land that Yahweh your God is giving you for all time.

**41** Then Moses set apart three cities east of the Jordan,[^28] toward the sunrise,

**42** for anyone who had killed someone[^29] to flee to: anyone who had killed his neighbor unintentionally, without having been his enemy before.[^30] He could flee to one of these cities and save his life.

**43** These were Bezer in the wilderness, on the plateau,[^31] for the Reubenites; Ramoth in Gilead for the Gadites; and Golan in Bashan for the Manassites.

**44** This is the law that Moses set before the Israelites.

**45** These are the decrees,[^32] the statutes and the rulings that Moses spoke to the Israelites after they came out of Egypt,

**46** east of the Jordan, in the valley opposite Beth-peor, in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon. Moses and the Israelites defeated him after they came out of Egypt,

**47** and they took possession of his land and the land of Og king of Bashan, the two kings of the Amorites who were east of the Jordan, toward the sunrise.

**48** Their land stretched from Aroer, on the edge of the Arnon Valley, as far as Mount Sion,[^33] that is, Hermon,

**49** together with all the Arabah east of the Jordan, as far as the Sea of the Arabah,[^34] below the slopes of Pisgah.

[^1]: Hebrew *chuqqim*, "statutes, decrees", and *mishpatim*, "judgments, rulings". Deuteronomy often pairs the two words for the laws as a whole.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "fathers"; also in verses 31 and 37.
[^4]: Or "in the matter of Baal-peor", the worship of the Baal of Peor (Numbers 25:1–9).
[^5]: Literally "for it is your wisdom and your understanding in the eyes of the peoples".
[^6]: Or "gods". Hebrew *elohim*.
[^7]: Literally "guard yourself, and guard your *nephesh* (life, self) very much".
[^8]: Hebrew *levav*, "heart", the seat of thought and will as well as feeling.
[^9]: Literally "the heart of the heavens".
[^10]: Literally "to do".
[^11]: Literally "the ten words".
[^12]: Literally "guard yourselves very much, for your lives".
[^13]: Hebrew *pesel*, a carved or cast image. Also in verses 23 and 25.
[^14]: Literally "all the host of the heavens", the heavenly bodies pictured as an army.
[^15]: Or "to be a people that is his own possession".
[^16]: Literally "because of your words".
[^17]: Literally "that Yahweh your God has commanded you".
[^18]: Hebrew *el qanna*: a God who will not share his people's devotion with any other.
[^19]: Literally "men of number", that is, few enough to count.
[^20]: Hebrew *nephesh*: life, self, soul.
[^21]: Literally "have found you".
[^22]: Literally "in the end of the days". Or "in the last days".
[^23]: Hebrew *adam*, "humankind".
[^24]: Or "the voice of a god". Hebrew *elohim*. Also in verse 34.
[^25]: Or "tests". Hebrew *massot*.
[^26]: Literally "his seed after him". The Samaritan Pentateuch and the Septuagint read "their seed after them".
[^27]: Literally "with his face", that is, by his own presence.
[^28]: Literally "beyond the Jordan", seen from the land west of the river. Also in verses 46, 47 and 49.
[^29]: Hebrew *rotseach*, "killer", from the verb used in "You shall not murder" (5:17). Here it is someone who has killed without meaning to.
[^30]: Literally "and he was not hating him yesterday and the day before".
[^31]: Hebrew *mishor*, "level land": the high tableland north of the Arnon.
[^32]: Hebrew *edot*, "testimonies, witnesses": the solemn terms of the covenant.
[^33]: Hebrew *Si'on*, not the same name as Zion in Jerusalem. The Syriac reads "Sirion", as in 3:9.
[^34]: The Dead Sea.
