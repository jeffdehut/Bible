**1** Do not sacrifice to Yahweh[^1] your God an ox or a sheep that has a defect or anything wrong with it, for that is detestable[^2] to Yahweh your God.

**2** Suppose a man or a woman is found among you, in one of the towns[^3] that Yahweh your God is giving you, who does what is evil in the eyes of Yahweh your God by breaking his covenant.

**3** That person has gone and served other gods and bowed down to them, or to the sun or the moon or any of the host of heaven,[^4] which I have forbidden.[^5]

**4** If you are told about it and hear of it, you must investigate thoroughly. If it is true and certain that this detestable thing has been done in Israel,

**5** you must bring out to your town gates the man or the woman who has done this evil thing, the man or the woman, and stone that person to death.[^6]

**6** The one who is to die must be put to death on the testimony[^7] of two witnesses or three witnesses. No one is to be put to death on the testimony of only one witness.

**7** The hands of the witnesses must be the first against him to put him to death, and then the hands of all the people. So you must purge[^8] the evil from among you.

**8** If a case comes before you that is too hard for you to judge,[^9] whether it concerns bloodshed, a legal claim or an assault,[^10] or any dispute in your towns, then get up and go to the place that Yahweh your God will choose.

**9** Go to the Levitical priests[^11] and to the judge who is in office at that time. Inquire of them, and they will declare the verdict to you.

**10** You must act according to the verdict they declare to you at the place that Yahweh will choose. Be careful to do everything they instruct you.

**11** Act according to the instruction[^12] they give you and the ruling they tell you. Do not turn aside from the verdict they declare to you, either to the right or to the left.

**12** Anyone who acts presumptuously and will not listen to the priest who stands there to serve Yahweh your God, or to the judge, must die. So you must purge the evil from Israel.

**13** Then all the people will hear and be afraid, and they will not act presumptuously again.

**14** When you come into the land that Yahweh your God is giving you, and have taken possession of it and settled in it, you may say, "Let me set a king over me, like all the nations around me."

**15** Then be sure to set over you a king whom Yahweh your God will choose. You are to set over you a king from among your own people.[^13] You may not put a foreigner over you, one who is not your brother.

**16** But he must not acquire many horses for himself, or make the people go back to Egypt to acquire many horses, for Yahweh has said to you, "You must never go back that way again."

**17** He must not take many wives for himself, or his heart will turn away. And he must not acquire great amounts of silver and gold for himself.

**18** When he sits on the throne of his kingdom, he is to write for himself on a scroll a copy of this law,[^14] taken from the one kept by the Levitical priests.[^15]

**19** It is to be with him, and he is to read from it all the days of his life, so that he may learn to fear Yahweh his God by keeping all the words of this law and these decrees and doing them,

**20** so that his heart will not be lifted up above his brothers, and so that he will not turn aside from the command, to the right or to the left. Then he and his sons will reign long over his kingdom in Israel.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *to'evah*: something abhorrent, especially in worship. Also in verse 4.
[^3]: Literally "gates"; also in verses 5 and 8.
[^4]: Literally "all the host of the heavens", the heavenly bodies pictured as an army.
[^5]: Literally "which I have not commanded". Moses speaks in Yahweh's own words.
[^6]: Literally "and stone them with stones, and they shall die".
[^7]: Literally "at the mouth"; also later in the verse.
[^8]: Literally "burn out"; also in verse 12.
[^9]: Literally "If a matter is too wonderful for you for judgment".
[^10]: Literally "between blood and blood, between claim and claim, and between stroke and stroke". *Stroke* (Hebrew *nega*) can also mean a skin disease, as in 24:8.
[^11]: Literally "the priests, the Levites"; also in verse 18.
[^12]: Hebrew *torah*.
[^13]: Literally "from among your brothers".
[^14]: Hebrew *mishneh ha-torah ha-zot*, "a copy (or repetition) of this law". The Greek Septuagint translated it *to deuteronomion touto*, "this second law", which gave the book its English name.
[^15]: Literally "from before the priests, the Levites".
