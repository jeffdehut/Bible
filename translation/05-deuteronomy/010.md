**1** "At that time the LORD[^1] said to me, 'Cut two stone tablets like the first ones, and come up to me on the mountain. Also make yourself an ark[^2] of wood.

**2** I will write on the tablets the words that were on the first tablets, which you broke, and you are to put them in the ark.'

**3** So I made an ark of acacia wood and cut two stone tablets like the first ones, and I went up the mountain with the two tablets in my hand.

**4** He wrote on the tablets the same words as before,[^3] the Ten Commandments[^4] that the LORD had spoken to you at the mountain, out of the fire, on the day of the assembly. And the LORD gave them to me.

**5** Then I turned and went down from the mountain, and I put the tablets in the ark that I had made. And they are there, as the LORD commanded me.

**6** (The Israelites set out from Beeroth Bene-jaakan[^5] to Moserah. There Aaron died, and he was buried there, and his son Eleazar became priest in his place.[^6]

**7** From there they set out to Gudgodah, and from Gudgodah to Jotbathah, a land of streams of water.

**8** At that time the LORD set apart the tribe of Levi to carry the ark of the LORD's covenant, to stand before the LORD to serve him, and to bless in his name, as they still do today.

**9** That is why Levi has no share or inheritance among his brothers. The LORD is his inheritance, as the LORD your God told him.)

**10** I stayed on the mountain forty days and forty nights, as I had the first time, and the LORD listened to me that time too. The LORD was not willing to destroy you.

**11** Then the LORD said to me, 'Get up, set out at the head of the people, so that they may go in and take possession of the land that I swore to their ancestors[^7] to give them.'

**12** And now, Israel, what does the LORD your God ask of you? Only to fear the LORD your God, to walk in all his ways, to love him, and to serve the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul,[^8]

**13** and to keep the commands and decrees of the LORD that I am giving you today, for your own good.

**14** See, the heavens, even the highest heavens,[^9] belong to the LORD your God, and the earth and everything in it.

**15** Yet the LORD set his heart on your ancestors and loved them, and he chose their descendants after them, you, out of all the peoples, as you are today.

**16** So circumcise your hearts,[^10] and do not be stubborn[^11] any longer.

**17** For the LORD your God is God of gods and Lord of lords, the great God, mighty and awesome, who shows no partiality[^12] and takes no bribe.

**18** He secures justice for the fatherless[^13] and the widow, and he loves the foreigner,[^14] giving him food and clothing.

**19** So you too must love the foreigner, for you were foreigners in the land of Egypt.

**20** Fear the LORD your God. Serve him, hold fast to him, and swear by his name.

**21** He is your praise,[^15] and he is your God, who has done for you these great and awesome things that your own eyes have seen.

**22** Your ancestors went down to Egypt seventy persons[^16] in all, and now the LORD your God has made you as numerous as the stars of the sky.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *aron*, "chest, box": the ark of the covenant (verse 8).
[^3]: Literally "according to the first writing".
[^4]: Literally "the ten words".
[^5]: Or "from the wells of the Bene-jaakan".
[^6]: Compare Numbers 33:30–38, where the stages of the journey come in a different order and Aaron dies on Mount Hor.
[^7]: Literally "fathers"; also in verses 15 and 22.
[^8]: Hebrew *nephesh*: life, self, soul.
[^9]: Literally "the heavens and the heavens of the heavens".
[^10]: Literally "circumcise the foreskin of your heart", that is, remove whatever closes your heart to God.
[^11]: Literally "do not harden your neck".
[^12]: Literally "who does not lift up faces".
[^13]: Hebrew *yatom*, a child who has lost his father, traditionally "orphan".
[^14]: Hebrew *ger*: someone from elsewhere who lives among the Israelites, a resident foreigner. Also in verse 19.
[^15]: Or "He is the one you praise".
[^16]: Hebrew *nephesh*, "person" (Genesis 46:27; Exodus 1:5).
