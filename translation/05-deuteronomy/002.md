**1** "Then we turned and set out for the wilderness by the road to the Red Sea,[^1] as the LORD[^2] had told me. For a long time we went around the hill country of Seir.

**2** Then the LORD said to me,

**3** 'You have been going around this hill country long enough. Turn north.

**4** Give the people this command: "You are about to pass through the territory of your relatives,[^3] the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir. They will be afraid of you, but be very careful.

**5** Do not start a fight with them, for I will not give you any of their land, not even enough to put your foot on.[^4] I have given the hill country of Seir to Esau as his possession.

**6** You are to buy food from them with money, so that you may eat, and you are also to buy water from them with money, so that you may drink.

**7** For the LORD your God has blessed you in all the work of your hands. He has watched over your journey[^5] through this great wilderness. For these forty years the LORD your God has been with you, and you have not lacked anything."'

**8** So we went on past our relatives, the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir, away from the Arabah road, away from Elath and Ezion-geber. Then we turned and went along the road to the wilderness of Moab.

**9** The LORD said to me, 'Do not harass Moab or start a war with them, for I will not give you any of their land as a possession. I have given Ar[^6] to the descendants of Lot[^7] as their possession.'

**10** (The Emim used to live there, a people great and numerous, and as tall as the Anakim.

**11** Like the Anakim, they too were counted as Rephaim,[^8] but the Moabites call them Emim.

**12** The Horites also used to live in Seir, but the descendants of Esau drove them out. They destroyed them and settled in their place, just as Israel did in the land that the LORD gave them as their possession.)

**13** 'Now get up and cross the Zered Valley.'[^9] So we crossed the Zered Valley.

**14** From the time we left Kadesh-barnea until we crossed the Zered Valley was thirty-eight years, until the whole generation of fighting men had died out from the camp, as the LORD had sworn to them.

**15** Indeed, the hand of the LORD was against them, to wipe them out from the camp until they were all gone.

**16** When the last of the fighting men among the people had died,

**17** the LORD said to me,

**18** 'Today you are to cross the border of Moab at Ar.

**19** When you come near the Ammonites,[^10] do not harass them or start a fight with them, for I will not give you any of the land of the Ammonites as a possession. I have given it to the descendants of Lot as their possession.'

**20** (That too was counted as a land of the Rephaim. Rephaim used to live there, but the Ammonites call them Zamzummim,

**21** a people great and numerous, and as tall as the Anakim. But the LORD destroyed them before the Ammonites,[^11] who drove them out and settled in their place.

**22** He had done the same for the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir, when he destroyed the Horites before them. They drove them out and have lived in their place to this day.

**23** And as for the Avvim, who lived in villages as far as Gaza, the Caphtorim, who came from Caphtor,[^12] destroyed them and settled in their place.)

**24** 'Get up, set out and cross the Arnon Valley. See, I have handed over to you Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land. Begin to take possession of it. Go to war against him.

**25** This very day I will begin to put the dread and fear of you on the peoples everywhere under heaven. When they hear the report of you, they will tremble and be in anguish because of you.'

**26** So I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon with an offer of peace.[^13] I said,

**27** 'Let me pass through your land. I will keep strictly to the road.[^14] I will not turn aside to the right or to the left.

**28** Sell me food for money, so that I may eat, and give me water for money, so that I may drink. Only let me pass through on foot,

**29** as the descendants of Esau who live in Seir and the Moabites who live in Ar did for me, until I cross the Jordan into the land that the LORD our God is giving us.'

**30** But Sihon king of Heshbon was not willing to let us pass through his land,[^15] for the LORD your God had made his spirit stubborn and his heart hard, to hand him over to you, as he has now done.[^16]

**31** The LORD said to me, 'See, I have begun to hand Sihon and his land over to you. Begin to take possession, so that you may possess his land.'

**32** Then Sihon came out against us, he and all his people, to fight at Jahaz.

**33** The LORD our God handed him over to us,[^17] and we struck him down, together with his sons[^18] and all his people.

**34** At that time we captured all his towns and devoted every town to destruction,[^19] men, women and children. We left no survivors.

**35** We kept only the livestock as plunder for ourselves, along with the spoil of the towns we had captured.

**36** From Aroer, on the edge of the Arnon Valley, and from the town in the valley, as far as Gilead, there was not one town too strong for us.[^20] The LORD our God handed them all over to us.

**37** But you did not go near the land of the Ammonites: the whole bank of the Jabbok River, the towns of the hill country, or any place that the LORD our God had forbidden.[^21]

[^1]: Hebrew *Yam Suph*, literally "Sea of Reeds", traditionally the Red Sea. Here it is probably the Gulf of Aqaba.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "your brothers"; also in verse 8. Esau, the ancestor of the Edomites, was Jacob's brother (Genesis 36:1, 8).
[^4]: Literally "not even as much as the treading of the sole of a foot".
[^5]: Literally "He has known your walking".
[^6]: A city or district of Moab, near the Arnon (Numbers 21:15, 28).
[^7]: Lot, Abraham's nephew, was the ancestor of Moab and Ammon (Genesis 19:36–38).
[^8]: An ancient people remembered for their great height (3:11).
[^9]: Hebrew *nachal*: a valley with a stream that flows in the rainy season. Also in verses 14, 24, 36 and 37.
[^10]: Literally "the sons of Ammon"; also in verses 20, 21 and 37.
[^11]: Literally "before them".
[^12]: Caphtor is probably Crete. The Philistines came from there (Amos 9:7).
[^13]: Literally "words of peace".
[^14]: Literally "In the road, in the road I will go".
[^15]: Literally "to let us pass through him".
[^16]: Literally "as this day".
[^17]: Literally "gave him before us".
[^18]: The Hebrew text is written "his son" but read "his sons".
[^19]: Hebrew *charam*: to give something over completely to God, which in war meant destroying it and keeping nothing for oneself.
[^20]: Literally "too high for us".
[^21]: Literally "and all that the LORD our God had commanded".
