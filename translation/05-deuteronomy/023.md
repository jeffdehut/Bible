**1** A man must not marry his father's wife. He must not uncover his father's garment.[^1]

**2** No one whose testicles are crushed or whose penis is cut off[^2] may enter the assembly of Yahweh.[^3]

**3** No one born of a forbidden union[^4] may enter the assembly of Yahweh. Even to the tenth generation, none of his descendants may enter the assembly of Yahweh.

**4** No Ammonite or Moabite may enter the assembly of Yahweh. Even to the tenth generation, none of their descendants may ever enter the assembly of Yahweh,

**5** because they did not come to meet you with food[^5] and water on the way when you came out of Egypt, and because Moab[^6] hired Balaam son of Beor, from Pethor in Aram-naharaim,[^7] to curse you.

**6** But Yahweh your God would not listen to Balaam. Yahweh your God turned the curse into a blessing for you, because Yahweh your God loves you.

**7** Never seek their peace or their prosperity, as long as you live.[^8]

**8** Do not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother. Do not abhor an Egyptian, because you were a foreigner[^9] in his land.

**9** The children born to them in the third generation may enter the assembly of Yahweh.

**10** When you go out as an army against your enemies, keep away from everything improper.[^10]

**11** If one of your men is unclean because of an emission during the night,[^11] he must go outside the camp. He must not come inside the camp.

**12** As evening approaches he is to wash himself with water, and at sunset he may come back into the camp.

**13** You are to have a place[^12] outside the camp, and you are to go out there to relieve yourself.

**14** Among your equipment you are to have a digging tool.[^13] When you squat outside,[^14] dig a hole with it, and then turn and cover your excrement.

**15** For Yahweh your God walks about in the middle of your camp to rescue you and to hand over your enemies to you. So your camp must be holy, so that he does not see anything indecent[^15] among you and turn away from you.

**16** If a slave has escaped from his master and taken refuge with you, do not hand him over to his master.

**17** Let him live among you, in whatever place he chooses in one of your towns,[^16] wherever he likes. Do not oppress him.

**18** No daughter of Israel is to be a shrine prostitute, and no son of Israel is to be a shrine prostitute.[^17]

**19** Do not bring the earnings of a prostitute or the wages of a dog[^18] into the house of Yahweh your God to pay any vow, for both of them are detestable to Yahweh your God.

**20** Do not charge interest to your fellow Israelite,[^19] whether on money or on food or on anything else that can earn interest.

**21** You may charge interest to a foreigner,[^20] but not to your fellow Israelite, so that Yahweh your God may bless you in everything you set your hand to in the land you are entering to take possession of.

**22** If you make a vow to Yahweh your God, do not be slow to fulfill it, for Yahweh your God will certainly require it of you, and you will be guilty of sin.

**23** But if you refrain from making a vow, you will not be guilty of sin.

**24** Be careful to do what has passed your lips, just as you freely vowed to Yahweh your God with your own mouth.[^21]

**25** When you go into your neighbor's vineyard, you may eat as many grapes as you want, until you are satisfied,[^22] but you must not put any in your basket.

**26** When you go into your neighbor's standing grain, you may pick the ears with your hand, but you must not put a sickle to your neighbor's standing grain.

[^1]: Verse 1 is 22:30 in most English Bibles, and verses 2–26 are 23:1–25. *He must not uncover his father's garment*: literally "uncover the corner of his father's garment", that is, take what belongs to his father (compare Ruth 3:9).
[^2]: Literally "one wounded by crushing, or cut off in the member".
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: Hebrew *mamzer*, a word of uncertain meaning, traditionally "bastard": probably the child of an incestuous or otherwise forbidden union.
[^5]: Literally "bread".
[^6]: Literally "he". See Numbers 22–24.
[^7]: "Aram of the two rivers": northern Mesopotamia.
[^8]: Literally "all your days, forever".
[^9]: Hebrew *ger*: someone from elsewhere who lives in a land not his own, a resident foreigner.
[^10]: Literally "guard yourself from every evil thing".
[^11]: Literally "because of what happens at night", a discharge of semen (Leviticus 15:16).
[^12]: Literally "a hand".
[^13]: Hebrew *yated*, "peg, stake".
[^14]: Literally "sit outside".
[^15]: Literally "nakedness of a thing", the phrase used in 24:1.
[^16]: Literally "gates".
[^17]: Hebrew *qedeshah* (feminine) and *qadesh* (masculine), "consecrated one": probably a person who served at a shrine and took part in sexual acts as part of its worship. The exact practice is uncertain.
[^18]: Probably a male prostitute.
[^19]: Literally "your brother"; also in verse 21.
[^20]: Hebrew *nokhri*: a foreigner from another land who has not settled among the Israelites.
[^21]: Literally "What goes out of your lips you shall keep and do, as you vowed to Yahweh your God a freewill offering that you spoke with your mouth."
[^22]: Literally "as your *nephesh* (appetite) wishes, your fill".
