**1** "When all these things have come on you, the blessing and the curse that I have set before you, and you take them to heart among all the nations where Yahweh[^1] your God has driven you,

**2** and you return to Yahweh your God and listen to his voice, you and your children, with all your heart and with all your soul,[^2] just as I am commanding you today,

**3** then Yahweh your God will restore your fortunes[^3] and have compassion on you. He will gather you again from all the peoples where Yahweh your God has scattered you.

**4** Even if you have been driven away to the farthest edge of the heavens,[^4] from there Yahweh your God will gather you, and from there he will bring you back.

**5** Yahweh your God will bring you into the land that your ancestors[^5] possessed, and you will possess it. He will make you more prosperous and more numerous than your ancestors.

**6** Yahweh your God will circumcise your heart and the hearts of your descendants, so that you will love Yahweh your God with all your heart and with all your soul, and live.[^6]

**7** Yahweh your God will put all these curses on your enemies and on those who hate you and persecuted you.

**8** And you will again[^7] listen to the voice of Yahweh and follow all his commandments that I am giving you today.

**9** Yahweh your God will make you prosper abundantly in all the work of your hands, in the fruit of your womb, the fruit of your livestock and the fruit of your ground. For Yahweh will again delight in you and make you prosper, as he delighted in your ancestors,

**10** when you listen to the voice of Yahweh your God by keeping his commandments and his statutes that are written in this book of the law, when you turn back to Yahweh your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

**11** For this commandment that I am giving you today is not too hard for you,[^8] and it is not far away.

**12** It is not in heaven, so that you should say, 'Who will go up to heaven for us and bring it to us, and tell it to us so that we may do it?'[^9]

**13** And it is not beyond the sea, so that you should say, 'Who will cross the sea for us and bring it to us, and tell it to us so that we may do it?'

**14** No, the word is very near you. It is in your mouth and in your heart, so that you can do it.

**15** See, today I have set before you life and good, death and evil.[^10]

**16** For I am commanding you today to love Yahweh your God, to walk in his ways, and to keep his commandments, his statutes and his rulings.[^11] Then you will live and multiply, and Yahweh your God will bless you in the land you are entering to take possession of.

**17** But if your heart turns away and you do not listen, and you are drawn away to bow down to other gods and serve them,

**18** I declare to you today that you will surely perish. You will not live long in the land you are crossing the Jordan to enter and possess.

**19** I call heaven and earth as witnesses against you today that I have set before you life and death, blessing and curse. So choose life, so that you and your descendants may live,

**20** by loving Yahweh your God, listening to his voice and holding fast to him. For he is your life and the length of your days,[^12] so that you may live in the land that Yahweh swore to give to your ancestors, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob."

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *nephesh*: life, self, soul. Also in verses 6 and 10.
[^3]: Hebrew *shav et shevutekha*, literally "turn your turning". Or "bring back your captives".
[^4]: Literally "to the end of the heavens".
[^5]: Literally "fathers"; also in verses 9 and 20.
[^6]: Literally "for the sake of your life". Compare 10:16.
[^7]: Literally "you will return and".
[^8]: Literally "too wonderful for you".
[^9]: Paul uses verses 12–14 in Romans 10:6–8.
[^10]: Or "prosperity and disaster". Hebrew *tov* and *ra*.
[^11]: The Septuagint begins the verse "If you listen to the commandments of the Lord your God that I am commanding you today, to love …".
[^12]: Or "For that means life for you and length of days".
