**1** These are the statutes and the rulings that you must be careful to follow in the land that Yahweh,[^1] the God of your ancestors,[^2] has given you to possess, all the days you live on the earth.

**2** You must completely destroy all the places where the nations you are dispossessing served their gods, on the high mountains, on the hills and under every leafy tree.

**3** Tear down their altars, smash their standing stones,[^3] burn their Asherah poles[^4] in the fire, and cut down the idols of their gods. Wipe out their names from that place.

**4** You must not worship Yahweh your God in their way.[^5]

**5** Instead, you must seek the place that Yahweh your God will choose out of all your tribes to put his name there, for his dwelling,[^6] and you must go there.

**6** There you are to bring your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and your contributions,[^7] your vow offerings and your freewill offerings, and the firstborn of your herds and your flocks.

**7** There you and your households are to eat before Yahweh your God, and rejoice in everything you have set your hand to,[^8] because Yahweh your God has blessed you.

**8** You must not do as we are doing here today, each of us doing whatever seems right in his own eyes,

**9** for you have not yet come to the place of rest and the inheritance that Yahweh your God is giving you.

**10** But you will cross the Jordan and live in the land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance. He will give you rest from all your enemies around you, and you will live in safety.

**11** Then you are to bring everything I command you to the place that Yahweh your God will choose as a dwelling for his name:[^9] your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and your contributions, and all the choice offerings you vow to Yahweh.

**12** And you are to rejoice before Yahweh your God, you and your sons and your daughters, your male and female slaves, and the Levite who lives in your towns,[^10] since he has no share or inheritance among you.

**13** Be careful not to offer your burnt offerings in any place you happen to see.

**14** Only in the place that Yahweh will choose in one of your tribes, there you are to offer your burnt offerings, and there you are to do everything I command you.

**15** However, you may slaughter and eat meat in any of your towns, as much as you want,[^11] according to the blessing that Yahweh your God has given you. Both the unclean and the clean[^12] may eat it, as they would a gazelle or a deer.

**16** But you must not eat the blood. Pour it out on the ground like water.

**17** In your towns you may not eat the tithe of your grain, your new wine or your olive oil, or the firstborn of your herds or your flocks, or any of the offerings you vow, or your freewill offerings, or your contributions.

**18** Instead, you are to eat them before Yahweh your God in the place that Yahweh your God will choose, you and your son and your daughter, your male and female slaves, and the Levite who lives in your towns. And you are to rejoice before Yahweh your God in everything you set your hand to.

**19** Be careful not to neglect the Levite as long as you live in your land.

**20** When Yahweh your God enlarges your territory, as he has promised you, and you say, "I want to eat meat," because you long to eat meat, you may eat as much meat as you want.

**21** If the place that Yahweh your God will choose to put his name is too far from you, you may slaughter animals from the herds and flocks that Yahweh has given you, as I have commanded you, and you may eat as much as you want in your towns.

**22** Eat it just as a gazelle or a deer is eaten. Both the unclean and the clean may eat it.

**23** But be sure not to eat the blood, for the blood is the life,[^13] and you must not eat the life with the meat.

**24** Do not eat it. Pour it out on the ground like water.

**25** Do not eat it, so that it may go well with you and with your children after you, because you will be doing what is right in the eyes of Yahweh.

**26** But take the holy things that are yours to give,[^14] and your vow offerings, and go to the place that Yahweh will choose.

**27** Offer your burnt offerings, both the meat and the blood, on the altar of Yahweh your God. The blood of your sacrifices is to be poured out on the altar of Yahweh your God, but you may eat the meat.

**28** Be careful to obey all these words that I am commanding you, so that it may go well with you and with your children after you forever, because you will be doing what is good and right in the eyes of Yahweh your God.

**29** When Yahweh your God cuts off before you the nations you are going in to dispossess, and you dispossess them and live in their land,

**30** be careful not to be trapped into following their ways after they have been destroyed before you. Do not inquire about their gods, saying, "How did these nations serve their gods? I will do the same."

**31** You must not worship Yahweh your God in their way, for they have done for their gods every detestable thing that Yahweh hates. They even burn their sons and their daughters in the fire for their gods.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "fathers".
[^3]: Hebrew *matsevot*: standing stones set up as objects of worship.
[^4]: Hebrew *asherim*: wooden poles or trees that were symbols of the goddess Asherah.
[^5]: Literally "You shall not do so to Yahweh your God". Also in verse 31.
[^6]: Or "to put his name there, to make it dwell there". The place is not named in Deuteronomy.
[^7]: Literally "the contribution of your hand"; also in verses 11 and 17. Hebrew *terumah*, something set apart and given for a sacred purpose.
[^8]: Literally "in all the sending out of your hand"; also in verse 18.
[^9]: Literally "to make his name dwell there".
[^10]: Literally "within your gates"; also in verses 15, 17, 18 and 21.
[^11]: Literally "in all the desire of your *nephesh* (self)"; also in verses 20 and 21.
[^12]: That is, people who are ritually unclean as well as people who are clean. Also in verse 22.
[^13]: Hebrew *nephesh*, "life"; also later in the verse.
[^14]: Literally "your holy things that will be for you".
