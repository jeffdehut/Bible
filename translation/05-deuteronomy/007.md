**1** "When Yahweh[^1] your God brings you into the land you are entering to take possession of, he will drive out many nations before you: the Hittites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites and the Jebusites, seven nations larger and stronger than you.

**2** When Yahweh your God hands them over to you and you defeat them, you must devote them completely to destruction.[^2] Do not make a covenant with them, and show them no mercy.

**3** Do not intermarry with them. Do not give your daughters to their sons, or take their daughters for your sons,

**4** for they would turn your children away from following me,[^3] to serve other gods. Then the anger of Yahweh would burn against you, and he would quickly destroy you.

**5** Instead, this is what you are to do to them: tear down their altars, smash their standing stones,[^4] cut down their Asherah poles[^5] and burn their idols in the fire.

**6** For you are a people holy to Yahweh your God. Yahweh your God has chosen you out of all the peoples on the face of the earth to be his people, his treasured possession.[^6]

**7** It was not because you were more numerous than all the other peoples that Yahweh set his heart on you and chose you, for you were the fewest of all peoples.

**8** It was because Yahweh loved you, and because he kept the oath that he swore to your ancestors,[^7] that Yahweh brought you out with a mighty hand and redeemed you from the house of slavery,[^8] from the power[^9] of Pharaoh king of Egypt.

**9** So know that Yahweh your God is God. He is the faithful God, who keeps his covenant and his faithful love[^10] for a thousand generations with those who love him and keep his commandments,[^11]

**10** but who repays those who hate him to their face by destroying them. He will not be slow to repay anyone who hates him; he will repay him to his face.

**11** So keep the commandment,[^12] the statutes and the rulings that I am giving you today, and follow them.

**12** If you listen to these rulings, and keep them and follow them, then Yahweh your God will keep with you the covenant and the faithful love that he swore to your ancestors.

**13** He will love you, bless you and multiply you. He will bless the fruit of your womb[^13] and the fruit of your ground, your grain, your new wine and your olive oil,[^14] the calves of your herds and the lambs of your flocks,[^15] in the land that he swore to your ancestors to give you.

**14** You will be blessed more than all the peoples. There will be no infertile male or female among you or among your livestock.

**15** Yahweh will take away from you every sickness. He will not put on you any of the dreadful diseases of Egypt that you know, but he will lay them on all who hate you.

**16** You must destroy[^16] all the peoples that Yahweh your God is handing over to you. Do not look on them with pity,[^17] and do not serve their gods, for that would be a trap for you.

**17** You may say to yourself,[^18] 'These nations are more numerous than I am. How can I drive them out?'

**18** But do not be afraid of them. Remember well what Yahweh your God did to Pharaoh and to all Egypt:

**19** the great trials that your eyes saw, the signs and the wonders, and the mighty hand and the outstretched arm by which Yahweh your God brought you out. Yahweh your God will do the same to all the peoples you are afraid of.

**20** Yahweh your God will also send the hornet[^19] among them, until those who are left and those who hide from you are destroyed.

**21** Do not be terrified of them, for Yahweh your God is among you, a great and awesome God.

**22** Yahweh your God will drive out those nations before you little by little. You will not be able to make an end of them quickly, or the wild animals would become too many for you.

**23** But Yahweh your God will hand them over to you, and throw them into great confusion until they are destroyed.

**24** He will hand their kings over to you, and you will wipe out their names from under heaven. No one will be able to stand against you until you have destroyed them.

**25** Burn the idols of their gods in the fire. Do not covet the silver or the gold on them, and do not take it for yourself, or you will be trapped by it, for it is detestable[^20] to Yahweh your God.

**26** Do not bring anything detestable into your house, or you will be set apart for destruction[^21] like it. You must utterly abhor it and detest it, for it is set apart for destruction.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *charam*: to give something over completely to God, which in war meant destroying it and keeping nothing for oneself.
[^3]: Literally "for he will turn your son away from after me". Here Moses speaks in Yahweh's own words.
[^4]: Hebrew *matsevot*: standing stones set up as objects of worship.
[^5]: Hebrew *asherim*: wooden poles or trees that were symbols of the goddess Asherah.
[^6]: Hebrew *segullah*, a king's or an owner's personal treasure (Exodus 19:5).
[^7]: Literally "fathers"; also in verses 12 and 13.
[^8]: Literally "house of slaves".
[^9]: Literally "hand".
[^10]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Also in verse 12.
[^11]: The Hebrew text is written "his commandment" but read "his commandments".
[^12]: The singular means the law as a whole.
[^13]: That is, your children.
[^14]: Hebrew *dagan*, *tirosh* and *yitshar*: grain, new wine and fresh olive oil, the three main crops of the land.
[^15]: Hebrew *shegar* and *ashterot*, rare words for the young of cattle and of sheep. They were also the names of a Canaanite god and goddess, Sheger and Astarte.
[^16]: Literally "eat, devour".
[^17]: Literally "Your eye shall not pity them".
[^18]: Literally "If you say in your heart".
[^19]: Hebrew *tsir'ah*, of uncertain meaning. It is usually taken as "hornet" or "hornets", but some understand it as "panic" or "plague" (Exodus 23:28).
[^20]: Hebrew *to'evah*: something abhorrent, especially in worship. Also in verse 26.
[^21]: Hebrew *cherem*: something devoted to God, which must be destroyed. Also at the end of the verse.
