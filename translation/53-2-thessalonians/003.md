**1** Finally, brothers and sisters,[^1] pray for us, that the word of the Lord may spread rapidly[^2] and be honored, just as it was with you,

**2** and that we may be rescued from perverse and evil people; for not everyone has faith.

**3** But the Lord is faithful; he will strengthen you and guard you from the evil one.[^3]

**4** And we have confidence in the Lord about you, that you are doing and will do the things we command.

**5** May the Lord direct your hearts into the love of God[^4] and into the endurance of Christ.[^5]

**6** Now we command you, brothers and sisters, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to keep away from any brother or sister[^6] who is living in idleness[^7] and not according to the tradition they received[^8] from us.

**7** For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us, because we were not idle when we were with you,

**8** nor did we eat anyone's bread without paying for it, but with labor and toil we worked night and day so as not to be a burden to any of you.

**9** It was not that we do not have the right, but to give you ourselves as an example to imitate.

**10** For even when we were with you, we gave you this command: if anyone is not willing to work, they should not eat.

**11** For we hear that some among you are living in idleness, not busy at work but busybodies.[^9]

**12** Now such people we command and urge in the Lord Jesus Christ to work quietly and earn their own living.[^10]

**13** As for you, brothers and sisters, do not grow weary in doing good.

**14** If anyone does not obey what we say in this letter,[^11] take note of that person and do not associate with them, so that they may be ashamed.

**15** Yet do not regard them as an enemy, but warn them as a brother or sister.

**16** Now may the Lord of peace himself give you peace at all times and in every way. The Lord be with all of you.

**17** I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand.[^12] This is the mark in every letter; this is how I write.

**18** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you.[^13]

[^1]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike; also in verses 6 and 13.
[^2]: Literally "may run".
[^3]: Or "from evil".
[^4]: Or "into love for God".
[^5]: Or "the steadfastness that Christ gives", or "patient waiting for Christ".
[^6]: Greek *adelphos*, "brother", here any fellow believer; also in verse 15.
[^7]: Greek *ataktos*, "out of order, disorderly", used of soldiers out of line and of people who neglect their work; also in verses 7 and 11.
[^8]: Some manuscripts read "you received".
[^9]: There is a play on words: *ergazomai*, "work", and *periergazomai*, "be a busybody", literally "work around".
[^10]: Literally "eat their own bread".
[^11]: Or "If anyone does not obey what we say, take note of that person by letter".
[^12]: Literally "The greeting with my own hand, of Paul". Paul usually dictated his letters to a scribe and wrote the last words himself.
[^13]: Many manuscripts add "Amen".
