**1** The word of Yahweh of Armies[^1] came:

**2** This is what Yahweh of Armies says: I am very jealous for Zion; I am jealous for her with great fury.[^2]

**3** This is what Yahweh says: I have returned to Zion, and I will live in the middle of Jerusalem. Jerusalem will be called the City of Faithfulness,[^3] and the mountain of Yahweh of Armies the Holy Mountain.

**4** This is what Yahweh of Armies says: Old men and old women will again sit in the squares of Jerusalem, each with a staff in hand because of great age.

**5** And the squares of the city will be full of boys and girls playing there.

**6** This is what Yahweh of Armies says: If it seems too wonderful[^4] in the eyes of the remnant of this people in those days, must it also seem too wonderful in my eyes? declares Yahweh of Armies.[^5]

**7** This is what Yahweh of Armies says: I am going to save my people from the land of the east and from the land of the setting sun.

**8** I will bring them to live in the middle of Jerusalem. They will be my people, and I will be their God,[^6] in faithfulness and righteousness.

**9** This is what Yahweh of Armies says: Let your hands be strong, you who in these days are hearing these words from the mouth of the prophets[^7] who spoke on the day the foundation was laid for the house of Yahweh of Armies, so that the temple might be built.

**10** For before those days there were no wages for people or for animals, and no one who went out or came in was safe from the enemy,[^8] for I set everyone against his neighbor.

**11** But now I will not treat the remnant of this people as in the former days, declares Yahweh of Armies.

**12** For there will be a sowing in peace:[^9] the vine will give its fruit, the earth will give its produce, and the heavens will give their dew. I will give the remnant of this people all these things to possess.

**13** As you have been a curse among the nations, house of Judah and house of Israel, so I will save you, and you will be a blessing.[^10] Do not be afraid; let your hands be strong.

**14** For this is what Yahweh of Armies says: Just as I planned to bring disaster on you when your ancestors made me angry, says Yahweh of Armies, and I did not relent,

**15** so now in these days I have again planned to do good to Jerusalem and to the house of Judah. Do not be afraid.

**16** These are the things you are to do: Speak the truth to one another;[^11] in your gates give judgments that are true and make for peace.

**17** Do not plot evil against one another in your hearts, and do not love a false oath, for all these are things that I hate, declares Yahweh.

**18** The word of Yahweh of Armies came to me:

**19** This is what Yahweh of Armies says: The fasts of the fourth month, the fifth, the seventh and the tenth[^12] will become times of joy and gladness and cheerful festivals for the house of Judah. So love truth and peace.

**20** This is what Yahweh of Armies says: Peoples will yet come, the inhabitants of many cities.

**21** The inhabitants of one city will go to another and say, "Let us go at once to seek the favor of Yahweh[^13] and to seek Yahweh of Armies. I am going too."

**22** Many peoples and strong nations will come to seek Yahweh of Armies in Jerusalem and to seek the favor of Yahweh.

**23** This is what Yahweh of Armies says: In those days ten men from nations of every language will take hold, they will take hold of the hem[^14] of a Jew and say, "Let us go with you, for we have heard that God is with you."

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. *Yahweh of Armies* (Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*) is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. The chapter is made up of ten sayings, each beginning "This is what Yahweh (of Armies) says".
[^2]: Or "with great passion". Compare 1:14.
[^3]: Or "the City of Truth". Hebrew *emet*, "faithfulness, truth". Compare Isaiah 1:26.
[^4]: Or "too hard". The same Hebrew verb is used in Genesis 18:14: "Is anything too hard for Yahweh?"
[^5]: Hebrew *ne'um YHWH tseva'ot*, "the declaration of Yahweh of Armies", a formula that marks a prophet's words as Yahweh's own solemn statement. Also in verses 11 and 17.
[^6]: The words of the covenant (Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 36:28).
[^7]: Haggai and Zechariah (Ezra 5:1–2; Haggai 2:18).
[^8]: Or "from distress". Compare Haggai 1:6.
[^9]: Literally "the seed of peace". The Hebrew is difficult.
[^10]: Compare Genesis 12:2–3, and the curse of Jeremiah 24:9.
[^11]: Literally "each with his neighbor". Ephesians 4:25 quotes these words.
[^12]: These fasts mourned the fall of Jerusalem: the breach in its wall in the fourth month (2 Kings 25:3–4; Jeremiah 39:2), the burning of the temple in the fifth (7:3), the murder of Gedaliah in the seventh (7:5), and the start of the siege in the tenth (2 Kings 25:1).
[^13]: Literally "to soften the face of Yahweh", as in 7:2. Also in verse 22.
[^14]: Hebrew *kanaf*, the corner of the robe, where the tassels that reminded Israelites of Yahweh's commands were worn (Numbers 15:38–39).
