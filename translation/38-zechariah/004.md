**1** Then the angel who was speaking with me came back and woke me, as someone is woken from sleep.

**2** He asked me, "What do you see?" I said,[^1] "I see a lampstand all of gold, with a bowl on top of it. It has seven lamps on it, with seven spouts for each of the lamps on top of it.[^2]

**3** There are two olive trees beside it, one on the right of the bowl and the other on its left."

**4** I asked the angel who was speaking with me, "What are these, my lord?"

**5** The angel who was speaking with me answered, "Do you not know what these are?" I said, "No, my lord."

**6** Then he said to me, "This is the word of the LORD[^3] to Zerubbabel: Not by might and not by power, but by my Spirit,[^4] says the LORD of Armies.[^5]

**7** What are you, great mountain? Before Zerubbabel you will become a plain. He will bring out the top stone[^6] amid shouts of 'Grace, grace to it!'"[^7]

**8** Then the word of the LORD came to me:

**9** The hands of Zerubbabel have laid the foundation of this house, and his hands will complete it. Then you will know that the LORD of Armies has sent me to you.

**10** For who has despised the day of small things? They will rejoice when they see the plumb line[^8] in the hand of Zerubbabel. These seven are the eyes of the LORD, which range over the whole earth.[^9]

**11** Then I asked him, "What are these two olive trees on the right and on the left of the lampstand?"

**12** And a second time I asked him, "What are these two branches[^10] of the olive trees, beside the two gold pipes, which pour out the golden oil[^11] from themselves?"

**13** He said to me, "Do you not know what these are?" I said, "No, my lord."

**14** Then he said, "These are the two anointed ones[^12] who stand by the Lord of the whole earth."

[^1]: The Hebrew text is written "he said" but read "I said".
[^2]: Or "and seven spouts for the seven lamps". Literally "seven and seven spouts for the lamps". The lampstand recalls the seven-branched lampstand of the tabernacle (Exodus 25:31–40), but its form is different and uncertain.
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Hebrew *ruach*, "spirit, wind, breath". *Might* is *chayil*, which can also mean "army".
[^5]: Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*, traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verse 9.
[^6]: Hebrew *ha-even ha-roshah*, "the head stone", perhaps the last stone set at the top of the building, or a stone from the first temple laid at the start of the work.
[^7]: Hebrew *chen chen lah*. Or "Beautiful, beautiful!"
[^8]: Literally "the stone of tin", probably the weight of a plumb line, used to see that a wall is straight. Or "the chosen stone".
[^9]: The seven eyes are the seven lamps of verse 2, and this answers Zechariah's question in verse 4. Verses 6–10 may join an oracle about Zerubbabel to the explanation of the vision. Compare 3:9; 2 Chronicles 16:9; Revelation 5:6.
[^10]: Literally "ears of grain", perhaps clusters of olives or branches heavy with fruit.
[^11]: Literally "the gold".
[^12]: Literally "the two sons of the fresh oil". The word is not *mashiach*, "anointed", but the picture is of anointing with oil. They are probably Zerubbabel, of the royal line, and Joshua the high priest. Revelation 11:4 speaks of two witnesses as "the two olive trees and the two lampstands that stand before the Lord of the earth".
