**1** Again I looked up, and I saw four chariots coming out from between two mountains, and the mountains were mountains of bronze.[^1]

**2** The first chariot had red horses, the second chariot black horses,

**3** the third chariot white horses, and the fourth chariot dappled horses, all of them strong.[^2]

**4** I asked the angel who was speaking with me, "What are these, my lord?"

**5** The angel answered me, "These are the four winds[^3] of heaven, going out after presenting themselves before the Lord of the whole earth.

**6** The chariot with the black horses is going out toward the land of the north, the white ones have gone out after them,[^4] and the dappled ones have gone out toward the land of the south."

**7** When the strong ones[^5] went out, they were eager to go and patrol the earth. He said, "Go, patrol the earth!" So they patrolled the earth.

**8** Then he called to me and said to me, "Look, those going out toward the land of the north have given my Spirit rest[^6] in the land of the north."

**9** The word of Yahweh[^7] came to me:

**10** Take an offering from the exiles,[^8] from Heldai, Tobijah and Jedaiah, who have come from Babylon. Go that same day to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah.

**11** Take silver and gold and make crowns,[^9] and set the crown on the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.

**12** Say to him: This is what Yahweh of Armies[^10] says: Here is a man whose name is the Branch.[^11] He will branch out from his place, and he will build the temple of Yahweh.

**13** It is he who will build the temple of Yahweh. He will be clothed with majesty, and he will sit and rule on his throne. There will be a priest on his throne,[^12] and there will be a counsel of peace between the two of them.

**14** The crown will be a memorial in the temple of Yahweh for Helem, Tobijah, Jedaiah and Hen son of Zephaniah.[^13]

**15** Those who are far away will come and help to build the temple of Yahweh. Then you will know that Yahweh of Armies has sent me to you. This will happen if you carefully obey the voice of Yahweh your God.[^14]

[^1]: Perhaps the gateway of heaven, from which the chariots go out. In the ancient Near East the sun was pictured as rising between two mountains.
[^2]: Hebrew *amutsim*, "strong", perhaps a color: "piebald" or "gray". Compare the horses of 1:8.
[^3]: Or "spirits". Hebrew *ruchot* can mean either.
[^4]: Or "toward the west", with a small change to the Hebrew. The red horses are not mentioned.
[^5]: Probably the dappled horses, or all four teams. Some scholars read "the red ones", with a small change to the Hebrew.
[^6]: That is, they have calmed my anger, or they have set my Spirit on the land of the north (compare Ezekiel 5:13). Hebrew *ruach* can mean spirit, wind or breath.
[^7]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^8]: Literally "Take from the exile". These men had brought gifts from the Jews in Babylon.
[^9]: Hebrew *atarot*, plural, perhaps one crown made of several circlets. Also in verse 14, where the verb is singular. Many scholars think the crown was meant for Zerubbabel, as the title "the Branch" suggests (3:8; Haggai 2:23), but no manuscript reads his name here.
[^10]: Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*, traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. Also in verse 15.
[^11]: Hebrew *tsemach*, "sprout, growth", a title of a coming king of David's line (3:8). *He will branch out* is *yitsmach*, from the same word. The Aramaic Targum reads "the anointed one (Messiah)".
[^12]: Or "He will be a priest on his throne". The Septuagint reads "and the priest will be at his right hand".
[^13]: *Helem* is probably Heldai (verse 10), and *Hen*, "favor", perhaps another name for Josiah (verse 10), or "for the kindness of the son of Zephaniah".
[^14]: The sentence is left unfinished in the Hebrew, as if to say, "This will happen, if …" Compare Deuteronomy 28:1.
