**1** Ask the LORD[^1] for rain in the season of the spring rain.[^2] It is the LORD who makes the storm clouds, and he will give them showers of rain, and plants in the field for everyone.

**2** For the household gods[^3] speak nonsense, and the diviners see lies. They tell false dreams and give empty comfort. So the people wander like sheep; they suffer for lack of a shepherd.[^4]

**3** My anger burns against the shepherds, and I will punish the leaders.[^5] For the LORD of Armies[^6] cares for his flock,[^7] the house of Judah, and he will make them like his majestic horse in battle.

**4** From Judah[^8] will come the cornerstone, from Judah the tent peg, from Judah the battle bow, from Judah every ruler,[^9] all together.

**5** They will be like warriors trampling the enemy into the mud of the streets in battle. They will fight, because the LORD is with them, and they will put the horsemen to shame.

**6** I will strengthen the house of Judah and save the house of Joseph. I will bring them back[^10] because I have compassion on them, and they will be as though I had never rejected them. For I am the LORD their God, and I will answer them.

**7** Ephraim will be like a warrior, and their hearts will be glad as with wine. Their children will see it and be glad; their hearts will rejoice in the LORD.

**8** I will whistle for them[^11] and gather them in, for I have redeemed them, and they will be as many as they were before.

**9** Though I scattered them like seed[^12] among the peoples, in far countries they will remember me. They and their children will live, and they will return.

**10** I will bring them back from the land of Egypt and gather them from Assyria. I will bring them to the land of Gilead and to Lebanon,[^13] until there is no room left for them.

**11** He will pass through the sea of distress[^14] and strike the waves of the sea, and all the depths of the Nile will dry up. The pride of Assyria will be brought down, and the scepter of Egypt will depart.[^15]

**12** I will strengthen them in the LORD, and they will walk in his name, declares the LORD.[^16]

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *malqosh*, the spring rain of March and April, which ripens the crops before the harvest.
[^3]: Hebrew *teraphim*, images kept in the home and consulted for guidance (Genesis 31:19; Ezekiel 21:26; 21:21 in most English Bibles).
[^4]: Compare Numbers 27:17; Mark 6:34.
[^5]: Literally "the male goats", the leaders of the flock (Isaiah 14:9; Jeremiah 50:8). The verb *paqad* means "punish" here and "care for" in the next line.
[^6]: Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*, traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^7]: The same Hebrew verb, *paqad*, as *punish* in the first line.
[^8]: Literally "from him", that is, from Judah. The cornerstone and the tent peg are pictures of leaders on whom the people rely (Isaiah 22:23; Psalm 118:22).
[^9]: Hebrew *noges*, a word usually used of an oppressor or taskmaster (9:8).
[^10]: The Hebrew word, *ve-hoshevotim*, seems to combine "I will bring them back" and "I will settle them".
[^11]: Like a shepherd calling his flock (compare Isaiah 5:26; 7:18).
[^12]: Literally "I will sow them". Compare Hosea 2:25 (2:23 in most English Bibles).
[^13]: Gilead, east of the Jordan, and Lebanon, in the north, had been part of the land of the northern tribes.
[^14]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "He will pass through the sea, distress". The words recall the crossing of the Red Sea at the exodus.
[^15]: Compare Genesis 49:10.
[^16]: Hebrew *ne'um YHWH*, "the declaration of the LORD", a formula that marks a prophet's words as the LORD's own solemn statement.
