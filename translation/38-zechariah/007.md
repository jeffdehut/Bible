**1** In the fourth year of King Darius, the word of Yahweh[^1] came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month, the month of Kislev.[^2]

**2** Now the people of Bethel had sent Sharezer and Regem-melech and their men[^3] to seek the favor of Yahweh,[^4]

**3** and to ask the priests of the house of Yahweh of Armies[^5] and the prophets, "Should I weep in the fifth month and abstain,[^6] as I have done for so many years?"

**4** Then the word of Yahweh of Armies came to me:

**5** Say to all the people of the land and to the priests: When you fasted and mourned in the fifth month and in the seventh month[^7] for these seventy years, was it really for me that you fasted?

**6** And when you eat and when you drink, are you not eating and drinking for yourselves?

**7** Are not these the words that Yahweh proclaimed through the earlier prophets,[^8] when Jerusalem was inhabited and at peace, with its towns around it, and when the Negev and the Shephelah[^9] were inhabited?

**8** The word of Yahweh came to Zechariah:

**9** This is what Yahweh of Armies says: Judge with true justice; show faithful love[^10] and compassion to one another.[^11]

**10** Do not oppress the widow or the fatherless, the foreigner or the poor, and do not plot evil against one another in your hearts.

**11** But they refused to pay attention. They turned a stubborn shoulder[^12] and stopped their ears so as not to hear.

**12** They made their hearts as hard as flint,[^13] so as not to hear the law[^14] and the words that Yahweh of Armies had sent by his Spirit through the earlier prophets. So great anger came from Yahweh of Armies.

**13** Just as I called[^15] and they would not listen, so they called and I would not listen, says Yahweh of Armies.

**14** I scattered them with a storm among all the nations they had not known, and the land was left desolate behind them, with no one coming or going. So they made the pleasant land a desolation.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: 7 December 518 BC. *Kislev* is the Babylonian name of the month.
[^3]: Literally "Bethel sent Sharezer and Regem-melech and his men". Or "Bethel-sharezer sent Regem-melech and his men". Bethel, about 11 miles (18 km) north of Jerusalem, was resettled after the exile (Ezra 2:28).
[^4]: Literally "to soften the face of Yahweh", that is, to pray for his favor.
[^5]: Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*, traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^6]: Literally "separate myself", that is, by fasting. The fast of the fifth month mourned the burning of the temple and of Jerusalem by the Babylonians (2 Kings 25:8–9; Jeremiah 52:12–13). Now that the temple was being rebuilt, the people asked whether to keep it.
[^7]: The fast of the seventh month probably mourned the murder of Gedaliah, the governor appointed by Babylon (2 Kings 25:25; Jeremiah 41:1–3). Compare 8:19.
[^8]: The prophets before the exile (1:4).
[^9]: The dry south of Judah and the low hills to the west.
[^10]: Hebrew *chesed*, loyal, covenant love.
[^11]: Literally "each to his brother". Also in verse 10.
[^12]: Like an ox that will not take the yoke (Nehemiah 9:29; Hosea 4:16).
[^13]: Hebrew *shamir*, a very hard stone, perhaps emery or diamond (Jeremiah 17:1; Ezekiel 3:9).
[^14]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, teaching, law".
[^15]: Literally "Just as he called". The Hebrew moves from Yahweh speaking of himself as "he" to "I".
