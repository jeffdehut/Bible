**1** In the eighth month, in the second year of Darius,[^1] the word of Yahweh[^2] came to Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, the prophet:[^3]

**2** Yahweh was very angry with your ancestors.[^4]

**3** So say to them: This is what Yahweh of Armies[^5] says: Return to me, declares Yahweh of Armies,[^6] and I will return to you, says Yahweh of Armies.[^7]

**4** Do not be like your ancestors, to whom the earlier prophets[^8] proclaimed, "This is what Yahweh of Armies says: Turn back from your evil ways and your evil deeds." But they did not listen or pay attention to me, declares Yahweh.

**5** Your ancestors, where are they now? And the prophets, do they live forever?

**6** But my words and my decrees, which I commanded my servants the prophets, did they not overtake your ancestors? Then they repented and said, "Yahweh of Armies has dealt with us as he planned to do, according to our ways and our deeds."

**7** On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, the month of Shebat, in the second year of Darius,[^9] the word of Yahweh came to Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, the prophet.[^10]

**8** I saw in the night, and there was a man riding on a red horse! He was standing among the myrtle trees in the ravine,[^11] and behind him were red, sorrel[^12] and white horses.

**9** I asked, "What are these, my lord?"[^13] The angel who was speaking with me[^14] said to me, "I will show you what they are."

**10** Then the man who was standing among the myrtle trees answered, "These are the ones Yahweh has sent to patrol the earth."[^15]

**11** They answered the angel of Yahweh who was standing among the myrtle trees,[^16] "We have patrolled the earth, and the whole earth is still and at rest."[^17]

**12** Then the angel of Yahweh said, "Yahweh of Armies, how long will you withhold your compassion from Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, with which you have been angry these seventy years?"[^18]

**13** Yahweh answered the angel who was speaking with me with kind and comforting words.

**14** Then the angel who was speaking with me said to me, "Proclaim: 'This is what Yahweh of Armies says: I am very jealous[^19] for Jerusalem and for Zion,

**15** and I am very angry with the nations that are at ease. For I was only a little angry, but they made the disaster worse.[^20]

**16** Therefore this is what Yahweh says: I have returned to Jerusalem with compassion. My house will be rebuilt in it, declares Yahweh of Armies, and a measuring line will be stretched out over Jerusalem.'[^21]

**17** Proclaim again: 'This is what Yahweh of Armies says: My cities will again overflow with prosperity, and Yahweh will again comfort Zion and again choose Jerusalem.'"[^22]

[^1]: October or November 520 BC, two months after Haggai's first message (Haggai 1:1). Darius I was king of Persia.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: *Zechariah* means "Yahweh remembers". He prophesied together with Haggai (Ezra 5:1; 6:14), and was probably of the priestly family of Iddo, which returned from exile with Zerubbabel (Nehemiah 12:4, 16). Ezra calls him "son of Iddo", perhaps meaning "grandson".
[^4]: Literally "Yahweh was angry with your ancestors with anger".
[^5]: Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*, traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. The title is used more than fifty times in Zechariah.
[^6]: Hebrew *ne'um YHWH tseva'ot*, "the declaration of Yahweh of Armies", a formula that marks a prophet's words as Yahweh's own solemn statement.
[^7]: Malachi 3:7 repeats the words "Return to me, and I will return to you".
[^8]: The prophets before the exile, such as Jeremiah (Jeremiah 25:4–5; 35:15).
[^9]: 15 February 519 BC. *Shebat* is the Babylonian name of the month, used after the exile.
[^10]: From here to 6:15 Zechariah tells of eight visions he saw in one night, each explained to him by an angel. Many of their details are uncertain.
[^11]: Hebrew *metsulah*, "deep place", perhaps a valley near Jerusalem, or the deep waters at the edge of the world.
[^12]: Hebrew *seruqqim*, a color of uncertain meaning, perhaps reddish brown or speckled.
[^13]: Hebrew *adoni*, a polite form of address, not the title of God.
[^14]: Hebrew *mal'akh*, "messenger, angel". This angel explains the visions throughout chapters 1–6.
[^15]: Literally "to walk about in the earth", like the patrols and messengers of the Persian king. Compare Job 1:7.
[^16]: The angel of Yahweh is apparently the man of verses 8 and 10.
[^17]: The Persian empire was at peace after Darius had put down the revolts at the start of his reign, but Jerusalem still lay in ruins.
[^18]: Jeremiah had foretold seventy years of exile (Jeremiah 25:11–12; 29:10). The temple had been destroyed in 587 or 586 BC, nearly seventy years before.
[^19]: Literally "I am jealous with great jealousy", the passion of Yahweh for his people. Compare 8:2.
[^20]: Literally "they helped for evil".
[^21]: A line for measuring the ground before building (Jeremiah 31:38–39).
[^22]: In most English Bibles chapter 1 has verses 18–21, which are 2:1–4 in the Hebrew.
