**1** Where do the wars and fights among you come from? Do they not come from this, from your desires for pleasure that are at war within you?[^1]

**2** You desire and do not have. You murder and covet, and you cannot obtain. You fight and wage war.[^2] You do not have because you do not ask.

**3** You ask and do not receive, because you ask wrongly, to spend what you get on your pleasures.

**4** You adulterous people![^3] Do you not know that friendship with the world is hostility toward God? So whoever chooses to be a friend of the world becomes an enemy of God.

**5** Or do you think the scripture means nothing when it says, "He yearns jealously for the spirit he made to live in us"?[^4]

**6** But he gives greater grace. That is why it says, "God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble."[^5]

**7** So submit yourselves to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.

**8** Come near to God, and he will come near to you. Wash your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you double-minded.[^6]

**9** Grieve, mourn and weep. Let your laughter be turned to mourning and your joy to gloom.

**10** Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will lift you up.

**11** Do not speak against one another, brothers and sisters.[^7] Whoever speaks against a brother or sister or judges them speaks against the law and judges the law. But if you judge the law, you are not a doer of the law but a judge of it.

**12** There is only one lawgiver and judge,[^8] the one who is able to save and to destroy. But who are you to judge your neighbor?

**13** Come now, you who say, "Today or tomorrow we will go to this or that city, spend a year there, do business and make a profit."

**14** You do not know what your life will be like tomorrow.[^9] You are a mist[^10] that appears for a little while and then vanishes.

**15** Instead, you ought to say, "If the Lord wills, we will live and do this or that."

**16** But as it is, you boast in your arrogance. All such boasting is evil.

**17** So whoever knows the right thing to do and does not do it, for them it is sin.

[^1]: Literally "in your members", the parts of your body (compare 3:6).
[^2]: Or, punctuated differently, "You desire and do not have, so you murder. You covet and cannot obtain, so you fight and wage war."
[^3]: Literally "Adulteresses!", the prophets' picture of God's people as an unfaithful wife (Hosea 3:1; Jeremiah 3:20). Many manuscripts read "Adulterers and adulteresses!"
[^4]: No passage of the Old Testament has these words. The Greek can be understood in several ways: "He (God) yearns jealously for the spirit he made to live in us" (compare Exodus 20:5; 34:14); "The spirit he made to live in us longs enviously", of the craving of the human spirit; or "The Spirit he made to live in us yearns jealously". Some punctuate it as a second question. Many manuscripts read "the spirit that lives in us".
[^5]: Proverbs 3:34, as in the Septuagint; also quoted in 1 Peter 5:5. The Hebrew reads "He mocks the mockers, but he gives favor to the humble."
[^6]: Literally "two-souled", as in 1:8.
[^7]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike.
[^8]: Many manuscripts leave out "and judge".
[^9]: Or "You do not know what tomorrow will bring. What is your life?"
[^10]: Or "a vapor", "a puff of smoke". Many manuscripts read "It is a mist", that is, your life.
