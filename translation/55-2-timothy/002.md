**1** You then, my child, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus,

**2** and what you have heard from me in the presence of many witnesses,[^1] entrust to faithful people who will be able to teach others also.

**3** Share in suffering as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.

**4** No one serving as a soldier gets entangled in the affairs of everyday life, since he wants to please the one who enlisted him.

**5** And anyone who competes as an athlete is not crowned unless he competes according to the rules.

**6** The hardworking farmer ought to be the first to receive a share of the crops.

**7** Think about what I am saying, for the Lord will give you[^2] understanding in everything.

**8** Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, a descendant of David,[^3] as proclaimed in my good news,

**9** for which I am suffering, even to the point of being chained like a criminal. But the word of God is not chained.

**10** Therefore I endure everything for the sake of the chosen, so that they also may obtain the salvation that is in Christ Jesus, with eternal glory.

**11** The saying is trustworthy:[^4] If we have died with him, we will also live with him;

**12** if we endure, we will also reign with him; if we deny him, he also will deny us;[^5]

**13** if we are faithless, he remains faithful; he cannot deny himself.[^6]

**14** Remind them of these things, and solemnly charge them before the Lord[^7] not to quarrel about words, which does no good but only ruins those who listen.

**15** Do your best to present yourself to God as one approved, a worker who has no need to be ashamed, rightly handling[^8] the word of truth.

**16** But avoid irreverent, empty chatter, for those who indulge in it will advance further and further into ungodliness,

**17** and their talk will spread like gangrene. Among them are Hymenaeus and Philetus,

**18** who have swerved from the truth, saying that the resurrection has already happened, and they are overturning the faith of some.

**19** Nevertheless, God's firm foundation stands, bearing this seal: "The Lord knows those who are his,"[^9] and, "Let everyone who names the name of the Lord turn away from wickedness."[^10]

**20** In a large house there are not only vessels of gold and silver but also vessels of wood and clay, some for honorable use, some for dishonorable.

**21** So if anyone cleanses himself from these things, he will be a vessel for honorable use, set apart as holy, useful to the master of the house, ready for every good work.

**22** Flee youthful passions, and pursue righteousness, faith, love and peace, along with those who call on the Lord from a pure heart.

**23** Have nothing to do with foolish, ignorant controversies; you know that they breed quarrels.

**24** And the Lord's servant[^11] must not be quarrelsome but kind to everyone, able to teach, patient when wronged,

**25** correcting his opponents with gentleness. Perhaps God will grant them repentance leading to a knowledge of the truth,

**26** and they will come to their senses and escape from the snare of the devil, after being captured by him to do his will.[^12]

[^1]: Or "through many witnesses".
[^2]: Many manuscripts read "may the Lord give you".
[^3]: Literally "from the seed of David".
[^4]: See 1 Timothy 1:15. Verses 11–13 appear to quote an early Christian hymn or saying.
[^5]: Compare Matthew 10:33.
[^6]: The SBLGNT here follows the Byzantine text; other editions read "for he cannot deny himself".
[^7]: The SBLGNT here follows the Byzantine text; other editions read "before God".
[^8]: Literally "cutting straight", as a road is laid out straight or a furrow cut straight.
[^9]: Numbers 16:5, in a form close to the Septuagint, which reads "God knew those who are his". In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, Yahweh, and the verse reads "Yahweh will make known who belongs to him".
[^10]: Compare Numbers 16:26; Isaiah 26:13; 52:11.
[^11]: Or "slave". Greek *doulos*.
[^12]: Or "captured by him to do God's will". The Greek uses two different pronouns, "him" and "that one", which may refer to the same person, the devil, or to different persons (the devil and God, or the Lord's servant and God).
