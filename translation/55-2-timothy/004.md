**1** I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus, who is to judge the living and the dead, and by his appearing[^1] and his kingdom:[^2]

**2** proclaim the word; be ready in season and out of season;[^3] reprove, rebuke and encourage, with complete patience and teaching.

**3** For the time will come when people will not put up with sound teaching, but, wanting to have their ears tickled, they will gather around themselves teachers to suit their own desires,

**4** and they will turn their ears away from the truth and turn aside to myths.

**5** But you, be sober-minded in all things, endure suffering, do the work of an evangelist,[^4] fulfill your ministry.[^5]

**6** For I am already being poured out like a drink offering,[^6] and the time of my departure has come.

**7** I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.

**8** From now on there is reserved for me the crown of righteousness,[^7] which the Lord, the righteous judge, will award to me on that day, and not only to me but also to all who have loved his appearing.

**9** Do your best to come to me soon,

**10** for Demas, in love with this present age, has deserted me and gone to Thessalonica. Crescens has gone to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia.

**11** Only Luke is with me. Get Mark and bring him with you, for he is useful to me for service.

**12** I have sent Tychicus to Ephesus.

**13** When you come, bring the cloak that I left with Carpus at Troas, and the books, especially the parchments.[^8]

**14** Alexander the coppersmith did me much harm; the Lord will repay him according to his deeds.[^9]

**15** You too must beware of him, for he strongly opposed our message.

**16** At my first defense no one came to my support, but all deserted me. May it not be counted against them!

**17** But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me, so that through me the proclamation might be fully made and all the Gentiles[^10] might hear it. And I was rescued from the lion's mouth.[^11]

**18** The Lord will rescue me from every evil deed and bring me safely into his heavenly kingdom. To him be the glory forever and ever.[^12] Amen.

**19** Greet Prisca and Aquila and the household of Onesiphorus.

**20** Erastus remained at Corinth, and I left Trophimus, who was ill, at Miletus.

**21** Do your best to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, as do Pudens, Linus, Claudia and all the brothers and sisters.[^13]

**22** The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you all.[^14]

[^1]: Greek *epiphaneia*, "appearance, manifestation"; also in verse 8.
[^2]: Many manuscripts read "who is to judge the living and the dead at his appearing and his kingdom".
[^3]: That is, whether the time seems right or not.
[^4]: Greek *euangelistes*, "one who proclaims the good news".
[^5]: Greek *diakonia*, "service, ministry"; the same word as "service" in verse 11.
[^6]: Compare Philippians 2:17.
[^7]: Or "the crown that is righteousness", or "the crown given for righteousness".
[^8]: *Books*: Greek *biblia*, "scrolls" or "books". *Parchments*: Greek *membranai*, writing materials made from animal skin.
[^9]: Compare Psalm 62:13 (62:12 in most English Bibles); Proverbs 24:12. Many manuscripts read "may the Lord repay him".
[^10]: Or "all the nations".
[^11]: Compare Psalm 22:22 (22:21 in most English Bibles).
[^12]: Literally "to the ages of the ages".
[^13]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike.
[^14]: "Your" in the first sentence is singular; "you" in the second is plural. Many manuscripts read "The Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit" and add "Amen".
