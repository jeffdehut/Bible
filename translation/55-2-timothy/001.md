**1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus[^1] by the will of God, according to the promise of the life that is in Christ Jesus,

**2** to Timothy, my beloved child: Grace, mercy and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

**3** I thank God, whom I serve with a clear conscience as my ancestors did,[^2] as I remember you constantly in my prayers night and day.

**4** As I remember your tears, I long to see you, so that I may be filled with joy.

**5** I am reminded of your sincere faith, which lived first in your grandmother Lois and in your mother Eunice, and I am convinced that it lives in you too.

**6** For this reason I remind you to fan into flame the gift of God that is in you through the laying on of my hands.

**7** For God did not give us a spirit[^3] of cowardice, but of power and love and self-control.[^4]

**8** So do not be ashamed of the testimony about our Lord, nor of me his prisoner, but share in suffering for the good news by the power of God,

**9** who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not because of our works but because of his own purpose and grace, which was given to us in Christ Jesus before the ages began,[^5]

**10** and which has now been revealed through the appearing[^6] of our Savior Jesus Christ, who abolished death and brought life and immortality to light through the good news,

**11** for which I was appointed a herald and an apostle and a teacher.[^7]

**12** That is why I suffer as I do. But I am not ashamed, for I know whom I have believed, and I am convinced that he is able to guard what I have entrusted to him[^8] until that day.

**13** Hold to the pattern of sound words that you heard from me, in the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.

**14** Guard the good deposit entrusted to you, through the Holy Spirit who dwells in us.

**15** You know that all who are in Asia[^9] have turned away from me, among them Phygelus and Hermogenes.

**16** May the Lord grant mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, for he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain.

**17** On the contrary, when he arrived in Rome, he searched for me eagerly and found me

**18** (may the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord[^10] on that day!), and you know very well how much he served in Ephesus.

[^1]: *Christ*: Greek *Christos*, "anointed one", the Greek equivalent of "Messiah". In Paul's letters it usually works as a name, so this translation writes "Christ". Paul's order, "Christ Jesus" or "Jesus Christ", is kept as the Greek has it.
[^2]: Literally "from my forebears".
[^3]: Or "the Spirit, not of cowardice but". Greek *pneuma*, "spirit, Spirit".
[^4]: Greek *sophronismos*, "self-discipline, sound judgment".
[^5]: Literally "before eternal times".
[^6]: Greek *epiphaneia*, "appearance, manifestation".
[^7]: Many manuscripts read "a teacher of the Gentiles".
[^8]: Literally "my deposit" (Greek *paratheke*, as in verse 14). Or "what he has entrusted to me".
[^9]: The Roman province of Asia, in the west of what is now Turkey, with Ephesus as its chief city.
[^10]: The Greek has "the Lord" twice in this prayer.
