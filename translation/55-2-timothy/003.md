**1** But understand this: in the last days difficult times will come.

**2** For people will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, abusive, disobedient to their parents, ungrateful, unholy,

**3** without natural affection, unwilling to be reconciled, slanderers, without self-control, brutal, not loving what is good,

**4** treacherous, reckless, conceited, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,

**5** having an outward form of godliness but denying its power. Avoid such people.

**6** For among them are those who make their way into households and capture weak women,[^1] who are weighed down with sins and led by all kinds of desires,

**7** always learning and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth.

**8** Just as Jannes and Jambres[^2] opposed Moses, so these people also oppose the truth, people corrupted in mind and disqualified in regard to the faith.

**9** But they will not get very far, for their folly will be plain to everyone, as the folly of those two was.

**10** You, however, have closely followed my teaching, my conduct, my purpose, my faith, my patience, my love, my endurance,

**11** my persecutions and sufferings, the things that happened to me at Antioch, at Iconium and at Lystra,[^3] the persecutions I endured. Yet from them all the Lord rescued me.[^4]

**12** Indeed, all who want to live a godly life in Christ Jesus will be persecuted,

**13** while evil people and impostors[^5] will go from bad to worse, deceiving and being deceived.

**14** But you, continue in what you have learned and have firmly believed, knowing from whom you learned it,

**15** and that from childhood you have known the sacred writings, which are able to make you wise for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus.

**16** All scripture is God-breathed[^6] and useful for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness,

**17** so that the man of God[^7] may be complete, fully equipped for every good work.

[^1]: Greek *gynaikaria*, "little women", a diminutive of *gyne*, "woman", used with contempt.
[^2]: Jewish tradition gave these names to the magicians of Egypt who opposed Moses before Pharaoh (Exodus 7:11, 22). The names are not found in the Old Testament.
[^3]: Compare Acts 13:14–14:20.
[^4]: Compare Psalm 34:20 (34:19 in most English Bibles).
[^5]: Greek *goetes*, "sorcerers", also "swindlers, impostors".
[^6]: Greek *theopneustos*, "God-breathed", from *theos*, "God", and *pneo*, "breathe"; traditionally "inspired by God". The word is not found in Greek writings before this verse. The sentence has no verb, and it can be read in two ways: as here, saying that all scripture is God-breathed and useful; or "Every God-breathed scripture is also useful", with "God-breathed" describing scripture rather than saying something about it. *All scripture*: or "every scripture". In context the scriptures meant are those Timothy has known from childhood (verse 15).
[^7]: Greek *anthropos*, "human being". Compare 1 Timothy 6:11.
