**1** In the third month after the Israelites had come out of the land of Egypt, on that very day,[^1] they came to the wilderness of Sinai.

**2** They had set out from Rephidim, and they came to the wilderness of Sinai and camped in the wilderness. Israel camped there in front of the mountain.

**3** Then Moses went up to God, and Yahweh[^2] called to him from the mountain: "This is what you are to say to the house of Jacob and tell the Israelites:

**4** 'You have seen for yourselves what I did to the Egyptians, and how I carried you on eagles'[^3] wings and brought you to myself.

**5** Now if you truly obey me[^4] and keep my covenant, you will be my own treasured possession[^5] out of all the peoples, for the whole earth is mine.

**6** You will be for me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation.' These are the words you are to speak to the Israelites."

**7** So Moses came and summoned the elders of the people and set before them all these words that Yahweh had commanded him.

**8** All the people answered together, "We will do everything Yahweh has said." And Moses took the people's answer back to Yahweh.

**9** Yahweh said to Moses, "I am going to come to you in a thick cloud, so that the people will hear when I speak with you, and will always believe you." Then Moses told Yahweh what the people had said.

**10** Yahweh said to Moses, "Go to the people and consecrate them[^6] today and tomorrow. Have them wash their clothes

**11** and be ready by the third day, for on the third day Yahweh will come down on Mount Sinai in the sight of all the people.

**12** Set limits for the people all around, and say, 'Be careful not to go up the mountain or touch its edge. Whoever touches the mountain must be put to death.

**13** No hand is to touch him.[^7] He must be stoned or shot with arrows. Whether animal or person, he must not live.' Only when the ram's horn sounds a long blast may they go up the mountain."

**14** So Moses went down from the mountain to the people. He consecrated the people, and they washed their clothes.

**15** He said to the people, "Be ready by the third day. Do not go near a woman."

**16** On the morning of the third day there was thunder[^8] and lightning, with a thick cloud[^9] on the mountain and a very loud blast of the ram's horn,[^10] and all the people in the camp trembled.

**17** Then Moses brought the people out of the camp to meet God, and they stood at the foot of the mountain.

**18** Mount Sinai was all in smoke, because Yahweh had come down on it in fire. Its smoke went up like the smoke of a kiln, and the whole mountain shook violently.[^11]

**19** As the sound of the ram's horn grew louder and louder, Moses spoke, and God answered him in thunder.[^12]

**20** Yahweh came down on Mount Sinai, on the top of the mountain. Yahweh called Moses to the top of the mountain, and Moses went up.

**21** Yahweh said to Moses, "Go down and warn the people not to force their way through to Yahweh to look, or many of them will die.[^13]

**22** Even the priests who come near Yahweh must consecrate themselves, or Yahweh will break out against them."

**23** Moses said to Yahweh, "The people cannot come up Mount Sinai, for you yourself warned us, 'Set limits around the mountain and make it holy.'"

**24** Yahweh said to him, "Go down, and then come up, you and Aaron with you. But the priests and the people must not force their way through to come up to Yahweh, or he will break out against them."

**25** So Moses went down to the people and spoke to them.[^14]

[^1]: Literally "on this day", probably the first day of the month.
[^2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *nesher*, an eagle or a griffon vulture.
[^4]: Literally "if you truly listen to my voice".
[^5]: Hebrew *segullah*, a personal treasure, a king's own property.
[^6]: Literally "make them holy": prepare them by washing and keeping themselves ritually pure.
[^7]: Or "it", the mountain.
[^8]: Literally "voices".
[^9]: Literally "a heavy cloud". Hebrew *kaved*.
[^10]: Hebrew *shofar*, a trumpet made from a ram's horn; also in verse 19. In verse 13 the word is *yovel*, "ram's horn".
[^11]: The Greek Septuagint reads "all the people were greatly amazed".
[^12]: Or "with a voice".
[^13]: Literally "many of them will fall".
[^14]: Literally "and said to them". What he said is not given.
