**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "See, I have chosen by name[^2] Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah.

**3** I have filled him with the Spirit of God,[^3] with skill,[^4] understanding and knowledge, and with ability in every kind of craft,

**4** to make artistic designs, to work in gold, silver and bronze,

**5** to cut stones for setting, to carve wood, and to work in every kind of craft.

**6** And I myself have appointed Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, to work with him. I have given skill to all who are skilled,[^5] so that they can make everything I have commanded you:

**7** the tent of meeting, the ark of the testimony and the atonement cover on it, and all the furnishings of the tent;

**8** the table and its utensils, the pure gold lampstand[^6] and all its utensils, the altar of incense,

**9** the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils, the basin and its stand;

**10** the woven garments,[^7] that is, the sacred garments for Aaron the priest and the garments of his sons for serving as priests;

**11** and the anointing oil and the fragrant incense for the holy place. They are to make them just as I have commanded you."

**12** The LORD said to Moses,

**13** "Tell the Israelites, 'Above all, you must keep my Sabbaths.[^8] For this is a sign between me and you for all your generations, so that you may know that I am the LORD, who makes you holy.

**14** Keep the Sabbath, because it is holy for you. Anyone who profanes it must be put to death. Whoever does any work on it, that person[^9] must be cut off from among his people.

**15** For six days work may be done, but the seventh day is a Sabbath of complete rest,[^10] holy to the LORD. Anyone who does any work on the Sabbath day must be put to death.

**16** The Israelites must keep the Sabbath, observing the Sabbath for all their generations as a permanent covenant.

**17** It is a sign between me and the Israelites forever. For in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, and on the seventh day he stopped working and was refreshed.'"[^11]

**18** When he had finished speaking with Moses on Mount Sinai, he gave him the two tablets of the testimony, tablets of stone written with the finger of God.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "I have called by name".
[^3]: Or "a divine spirit". Hebrew *ruach elohim*.
[^4]: Hebrew *chokhmah*, "wisdom", here practical skill.
[^5]: Literally "in the heart of everyone wise of heart I have put wisdom".
[^6]: Literally "the pure lampstand".
[^7]: The meaning of the Hebrew word *serad* is uncertain. It may refer to finely woven or plaited cloth, or to garments for service.
[^8]: Or "But you must keep my Sabbaths." Hebrew *akh*, "surely, only, but". It may set the Sabbath against the work on the tabernacle just described: even that work must stop on the Sabbath.
[^9]: Hebrew *nephesh*, "living being, person".
[^10]: Hebrew *shabbat shabbaton*, an emphatic phrase: "a Sabbath of Sabbath rest".
[^11]: Literally "took breath", from the same root as *nephesh*.
