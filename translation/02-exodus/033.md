**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses, "Go, leave this place, you and the people you brought up out of the land of Egypt. Go up to the land I promised on oath to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, when I said, 'I will give it to your descendants.'

**2** I will send an angel[^2] ahead of you, and I will drive out the Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites and Jebusites.

**3** Go up to a land flowing with milk and honey. But I will not go up among you, because you are a stiff-necked people,[^3] and I might destroy you on the way."

**4** When the people heard this bad news, they mourned, and no one put on his ornaments.

**5** The LORD said to Moses, "Say to the Israelites, 'You are a stiff-necked people. If I went up among you for a single moment, I would destroy you. So now take off your ornaments, and I will decide what to do with you.'"[^4]

**6** So the Israelites stripped off their ornaments from Mount Horeb onward.[^5]

**7** Now Moses used to take the tent[^6] and pitch it outside the camp, some distance from the camp. He called it the tent of meeting. Anyone who wanted to seek the LORD would go out to the tent of meeting outside the camp.

**8** Whenever Moses went out to the tent, all the people would get up and stand at the entrances of their tents, watching Moses until he went into the tent.

**9** When Moses went into the tent, the pillar of cloud would come down and stand at the entrance of the tent, and he would speak with Moses.[^7]

**10** When all the people saw the pillar of cloud standing at the entrance of the tent, they would all get up and bow down, each at the entrance of his tent.

**11** The LORD would speak to Moses face to face, as someone speaks to a friend. Then Moses would return to the camp, but his assistant Joshua son of Nun, a young man, would not leave the tent.

**12** Moses said to the LORD, "Look, you keep telling me, 'Lead these people up,' but you have not let me know whom you will send with me. Yet you have said, 'I know you by name, and you have found favor in my sight.'

**13** Now, if I have found favor in your sight, please let me know your ways, so that I may know you and keep on finding favor in your sight. And remember that this nation is your people."

**14** He said, "My presence[^8] will go with you, and I will give you rest."

**15** Moses said to him, "If your presence does not go with us, do not lead us up from here.

**16** How else will it be known that I have found favor in your sight, I and your people? Is it not by your going with us, so that we are set apart, I and your people, from all the peoples on the face of the earth?"

**17** The LORD said to Moses, "I will do this very thing you have asked, because you have found favor in my sight, and I know you by name."

**18** Moses said, "Please show me your glory."

**19** He said, "I will make all my goodness pass in front of you, and I will proclaim my name, the LORD,[^9] before you. I will be gracious to whom I will be gracious, and I will show compassion to whom I will show compassion."

**20** But he said, "You cannot see my face, for no human being can see me and live."

**21** The LORD said, "Look, there is a place beside me. Stand on the rock,

**22** and when my glory passes by, I will put you in a crevice in the rock and cover you with my hand until I have passed by.

**23** Then I will take away my hand, and you will see my back. But my face must not be seen."

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Or "a messenger". Hebrew *mal'akh*.
[^3]: Literally "hard of neck", that is, stubborn. Also in verse 5.
[^4]: Literally "that I may know what I will do to you".
[^5]: Literally "from Mount Horeb". It may also mean "at Mount Horeb". Horeb is another name for Sinai.
[^6]: The Hebrew verbs in verses 7–11 describe repeated action. The text does not say which tent this was; the tabernacle of chapters 25–31 had not yet been made.
[^7]: The speaker is the LORD (verse 11).
[^8]: Literally "My face". Also in verse 15.
[^9]: Literally "I will call in the name of the LORD".
