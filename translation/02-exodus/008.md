**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses,[^2] "Tell Aaron, 'Stretch out your hand with your staff over the rivers, the canals and the ponds, and make frogs come up on the land of Egypt.'"

**2** So Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt, and the frogs came up and covered the land of Egypt.

**3** But the magicians did the same thing by their secret arts, and they too made frogs come up on the land of Egypt.

**4** Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said, "Pray to Yahweh to take the frogs away from me and my people, and I will let the people go to sacrifice to Yahweh."

**5** Moses said to Pharaoh, "I leave it to you[^3] to tell me when I should pray for you, for your officials[^4] and for your people, that the frogs may be removed from you and your houses and remain only in the Nile."

**6** "Tomorrow," he said. Moses said, "It will be as you say, so that you may know that there is no one like Yahweh our God.

**7** The frogs will leave you and your houses, your officials and your people. They will remain only in the Nile."

**8** Then Moses and Aaron left Pharaoh, and Moses cried out to Yahweh about the frogs he had brought on Pharaoh.[^5]

**9** Yahweh did what Moses asked. The frogs died in the houses, in the courtyards and in the fields.

**10** They piled them up in heaps, and the land stank.

**11** But when Pharaoh saw that there was relief, he made his heart stubborn,[^6] and he did not listen to them, just as Yahweh had said.

**12** Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Tell Aaron, 'Stretch out your staff and strike the dust of the ground, and it will become gnats[^7] throughout the land of Egypt.'"

**13** They did so. Aaron stretched out his hand with his staff and struck the dust of the ground, and gnats came on people and animals. All the dust of the ground became gnats throughout the land of Egypt.

**14** The magicians tried to bring out gnats by their secret arts, but they could not. The gnats were on people and animals.

**15** The magicians said to Pharaoh, "This is the finger of God." But Pharaoh's heart grew hard,[^8] and he did not listen to them, just as Yahweh had said.

**16** Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Get up early in the morning and stand in front of Pharaoh as he goes out to the water. Say to him, 'This is what Yahweh says: Let my people go, so that they may serve[^9] me.

**17** If you do not let my people go, I will send swarms of flies[^10] on you, your officials, your people and your houses. The houses of the Egyptians will be full of the swarms, and so will the ground they stand on.

**18** But on that day I will set apart the land of Goshen, where my people live, so that there will be no swarms there. Then you will know that I, Yahweh, am here in this land.

**19** I will make a distinction[^11] between my people and your people. This sign will happen tomorrow.'"

**20** And Yahweh did so. Dense swarms of flies came into Pharaoh's house and into the houses of his officials. Throughout the land of Egypt the land was ruined by the swarms.

**21** Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said, "Go, sacrifice to your God here in the land."

**22** But Moses said, "It would not be right to do that, because what we sacrifice to Yahweh our God is detestable to the Egyptians.[^12] If we sacrifice what is detestable to the Egyptians right in front of them, won't they stone us?

**23** We must go on a three-day journey into the wilderness and sacrifice to Yahweh our God, as he tells us."

**24** Pharaoh said, "I will let you go to sacrifice to Yahweh your God in the wilderness. Only, you must not go very far away. Pray for me."

**25** Moses said, "As soon as I leave you, I will pray to Yahweh, and tomorrow the swarms of flies will leave Pharaoh, his officials and his people. Only, Pharaoh must not deceive us again by refusing to let the people go to sacrifice to Yahweh."

**26** So Moses left Pharaoh and prayed to Yahweh.

**27** Yahweh did what Moses asked. He removed the swarms of flies from Pharaoh, from his officials and from his people. Not one was left.

**28** But this time too Pharaoh made his heart stubborn, and he did not let the people go.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Verses 1–28 are 8:5–32 in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "Have the glory over me", a courteous way of letting Pharaoh choose the time.
[^4]: Literally "servants"; also in verses 7, 17, 20, 25 and 27.
[^5]: Or "about the frogs, as he had agreed with Pharaoh".
[^6]: Literally "made his heart heavy". Hebrew *kaved*; also in verse 28. On the verbs used for Pharaoh's heart, see the note on 4:21.
[^7]: Hebrew *kinnim*. The insect is uncertain: gnats, lice or mosquitoes.
[^8]: Hebrew *chazaq*, "be strong, firm".
[^9]: Or "worship".
[^10]: Hebrew *arov*, "swarm" or "mixture". The text does not say what kind of insect.
[^11]: Hebrew *pedut*, which elsewhere means "redemption". The Greek Septuagint reads "distinction", as if from a similar Hebrew word, *pelut*, which matches "set apart" (*palah*) in verse 18.
[^12]: Literally "the abomination of Egypt we sacrifice to Yahweh our God"; likewise later in the verse.
