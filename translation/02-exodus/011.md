**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses, "I will bring one more plague on Pharaoh and on Egypt. After that he will let you go from here. When he lets you go, he will drive you out of here completely.[^2]

**2** Tell the people[^3] that every man is to ask his neighbor, and every woman her neighbor, for objects of silver and gold."

**3** The LORD made the Egyptians look with favor on the people.[^4] And the man Moses was highly respected in the land of Egypt, by Pharaoh's officials[^5] and by the people.

**4** So Moses said, "This is what the LORD says: About midnight I will go out through the middle of Egypt.

**5** Every firstborn in the land of Egypt will die, from the firstborn of Pharaoh who sits on his throne to the firstborn of the slave woman who grinds grain at the hand mill,[^6] and every firstborn of the livestock.

**6** There will be a great cry throughout the land of Egypt, such as there has never been and never will be again.

**7** But not even a dog will growl[^7] at any of the Israelites, whether person or animal. Then you will know that the LORD makes a distinction between Egypt and Israel.

**8** All these officials of yours will come down to me and bow down to me, saying, 'Go, you and all the people who follow you.'[^8] After that I will go." And he left Pharaoh in hot anger.

**9** The LORD said to Moses, "Pharaoh will not listen to you, so that my wonders may be multiplied in the land of Egypt."

**10** Moses and Aaron performed all these wonders in front of Pharaoh, but the LORD hardened[^9] Pharaoh's heart, and he did not let the Israelites go out of his land.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Or "When he lets you go, it will be for good; he will drive you out of here."
[^3]: Literally "Speak in the ears of the people".
[^4]: Literally "The LORD gave the people favor in the eyes of Egypt".
[^5]: Literally "servants"; also in verse 8.
[^6]: Literally "who is behind the millstones".
[^7]: Literally "sharpen its tongue".
[^8]: Literally "who are at your feet".
[^9]: Hebrew *chazaq*, "make strong, firm". On the verbs used for Pharaoh's heart, see the note on 4:21.
