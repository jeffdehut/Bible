**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses, "See, I have made you like God to Pharaoh,[^2] and your brother Aaron will be your prophet.

**2** You are to say everything I command you, and your brother Aaron is to tell Pharaoh to let the Israelites go out of his land.

**3** But I will make Pharaoh's heart unyielding,[^3] and I will multiply my signs and my wonders in the land of Egypt.

**4** Pharaoh will not listen to you. Then I will lay my hand on Egypt, and with great acts of judgment I will bring my divisions,[^4] my people the Israelites, out of the land of Egypt.

**5** The Egyptians will know that I am the LORD when I stretch out my hand against Egypt and bring the Israelites out from among them."

**6** Moses and Aaron did so. They did just as the LORD commanded them.

**7** Moses was eighty years old and Aaron eighty-three when they spoke to Pharaoh.

**8** The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,

**9** "When Pharaoh says to you, 'Perform a wonder,' then say to Aaron, 'Take your staff and throw it down in front of Pharaoh.' It will become a serpent."[^5]

**10** So Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh and did just as the LORD had commanded. Aaron threw down his staff in front of Pharaoh and his officials,[^6] and it became a serpent.

**11** Then Pharaoh also summoned the wise men and the sorcerers, and the magicians[^7] of Egypt did the same thing by their secret arts.[^8]

**12** Each of them threw down his staff, and they became serpents. But Aaron's staff swallowed up their staffs.

**13** Yet Pharaoh's heart grew hard,[^9] and he did not listen to them, just as the LORD had said.

**14** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Pharaoh's heart is stubborn.[^10] He refuses to let the people go.

**15** Go to Pharaoh in the morning, when he goes out to the water. Stand on the bank of the Nile to meet him, and take in your hand the staff that was turned into a snake.

**16** Say to him, 'The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has sent me to you to say: Let my people go, so that they may serve[^11] me in the wilderness. But until now you have not listened.

**17** This is what the LORD says: By this you will know that I am the LORD. Look, with the staff that is in my hand I will strike the water in the Nile, and it will be turned into blood.

**18** The fish in the Nile will die, and the Nile will stink, and the Egyptians will be unable to drink water from the Nile.'"

**19** The LORD said to Moses, "Tell Aaron, 'Take your staff and stretch out your hand over the waters of Egypt, over their rivers, their canals,[^12] their ponds and all their pools of water, so that they become blood.' There will be blood throughout the land of Egypt, even in containers of wood and of stone."[^13]

**20** Moses and Aaron did just as the LORD had commanded. He raised the staff and struck the water in the Nile in the sight of Pharaoh and his officials, and all the water in the Nile was turned into blood.

**21** The fish in the Nile died, and the Nile stank, so that the Egyptians could not drink water from the Nile. There was blood throughout the land of Egypt.

**22** But the magicians of Egypt did the same thing by their secret arts. So Pharaoh's heart grew hard, and he did not listen to them, just as the LORD had said.

**23** Pharaoh turned and went into his palace,[^14] and he did not take even this to heart.

**24** All the Egyptians dug around the Nile for water to drink, because they could not drink the water of the Nile.

**25** Seven days passed after the LORD struck the Nile.

**26** Then the LORD said to Moses,[^15] "Go to Pharaoh and say to him, 'This is what the LORD says: Let my people go, so that they may serve me.

**27** If you refuse to let them go, I will strike your whole territory with frogs.

**28** The Nile will swarm with frogs. They will come up and go into your palace, into your bedroom and onto your bed, into the houses of your officials and among your people, into your ovens and into your kneading bowls.

**29** The frogs will come up on you, on your people and on all your officials.'"

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "I have made you God to Pharaoh". Compare 4:16.
[^3]: Hebrew *qashah*, "make hard, harsh". On the three verbs used for Pharaoh's heart, see the note on 4:21.
[^4]: Literally "my armies". Hebrew *tsava'ot*; see 6:26.
[^5]: Hebrew *tannin*, a large reptile, a serpent, perhaps a crocodile; also in verses 10 and 12. In 4:3 and in verse 15 the word is *nachash*, "snake".
[^6]: Literally "servants"; also in verses 20, 28 and 29.
[^7]: Hebrew *chartummim*, a word borrowed from Egyptian for priests skilled in ritual and sacred writings.
[^8]: Hebrew *lahatim*; in verse 22, the related word *latim*. Both suggest something secret or hidden.
[^9]: Hebrew *chazaq*, "be strong, firm"; also in verse 22.
[^10]: Literally "heavy". Hebrew *kaved*.
[^11]: Or "worship"; also in verse 26.
[^12]: Hebrew *ye'orim*, the channels of the Nile.
[^13]: Literally "in wood and in stone".
[^14]: Literally "house"; also in verse 28.
[^15]: Verses 26–29 are 8:1–4 in most English Bibles.
