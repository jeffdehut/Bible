**1** Then he said to Moses,[^1] "Come up to the LORD,[^2] you and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel. Bow down in worship at a distance.

**2** Moses alone is to come near to the LORD. The others must not come near, and the people must not come up with him."

**3** Moses came and told the people all the LORD's words and all the rulings.[^3] All the people answered with one voice, "We will do everything the LORD has said."

**4** Then Moses wrote down all the LORD's words. He got up early in the morning and built an altar at the foot of the mountain, and set up twelve standing stones[^4] for the twelve tribes of Israel.

**5** He sent some young men of the Israelites, and they offered burnt offerings and sacrificed bulls to the LORD as fellowship offerings.[^5]

**6** Moses took half of the blood and put it in bowls, and the other half of the blood he splashed against the altar.

**7** Then he took the Book of the Covenant[^6] and read it aloud to the people.[^7] They said, "We will do everything the LORD has said, and we will obey."[^8]

**8** Moses took the blood and splashed it on the people. He said, "This is the blood of the covenant that the LORD has made with you on the basis of all these words."

**9** Then Moses and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel went up.

**10** They saw the God of Israel. Under his feet was something like a pavement made of lapis lazuli,[^9] as clear as the sky itself.[^10]

**11** But God did not lay a hand on these leaders of the Israelites.[^11] They saw God,[^12] and they ate and drank.

**12** The LORD said to Moses, "Come up to me on the mountain and stay there. I will give you the stone tablets with the instruction and the commandment that I have written to teach them."[^13]

**13** So Moses set out with Joshua his assistant, and Moses went up the mountain of God.

**14** He said to the elders, "Wait here for us until we come back to you. Aaron and Hur are with you. Anyone who has a dispute can go to them."

**15** When Moses went up the mountain, the cloud covered the mountain.

**16** The glory of the LORD settled[^14] on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it for six days. On the seventh day he called to Moses from inside the cloud.

**17** To the Israelites, the glory of the LORD looked like a consuming fire on the top of the mountain.[^15]

**18** Moses went into the cloud and went up the mountain. He was on the mountain for forty days and forty nights.

[^1]: The speaker is the LORD, though he speaks of himself in the third person in this verse.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Hebrew *mishpatim*, "judgments, rulings": the laws of 21:1–23:19.
[^4]: Hebrew *matsevot*, as in 23:24, but here set up for the worship of the LORD.
[^5]: Hebrew *zevachim shelamim*, traditionally "peace offerings". In these sacrifices most of the meat was eaten by the worshipers.
[^6]: Or "the scroll of the covenant".
[^7]: Literally "read it in the ears of the people".
[^8]: Literally "we will hear".
[^9]: Hebrew *sappir*, traditionally "sapphire". It was probably lapis lazuli, a deep blue stone.
[^10]: Literally "like the very substance of the heavens for purity".
[^11]: Literally "against the nobles of the sons of Israel he did not send out his hand".
[^12]: The verb here (*chazah*) differs from the one in verse 10. It is often used of seeing in a vision.
[^13]: Or "the stone tablets, and the instruction and the commandment, which I have written to teach them." Hebrew *torah* here is "instruction" or "law".
[^14]: Hebrew *shakhan*, "dwell, settle", the verb from which *mishkan*, "tabernacle", comes.
[^15]: Literally "The appearance of the glory of the LORD was like a consuming fire on the top of the mountain in the eyes of the sons of Israel."
