**1** Bezalel made the ark of acacia wood, 2½ cubits[^1] long, 1½ cubits wide and 1½ cubits high.

**2** He overlaid it with pure gold, inside and out, and made a gold molding all around it.

**3** He cast four gold rings for it, for its four feet,[^2] with two rings on one side and two rings on the other side.

**4** He made poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold.

**5** He put the poles through the rings on the sides of the ark, for carrying the ark.

**6** He made an atonement cover[^3] of pure gold, 2½ cubits long and 1½ cubits wide.

**7** He made two cherubim of gold. He made them of hammered work at the two ends of the atonement cover,

**8** one cherub at one end and one cherub at the other end. He made the cherubim of one piece with the atonement cover, at its two ends.

**9** The cherubim had their wings spread out upward, sheltering the atonement cover with their wings. They faced each other, with their faces turned toward the atonement cover.

**10** He made the table of acacia wood, 2 cubits long, 1 cubit wide and 1½ cubits high.

**11** He overlaid it with pure gold and made a gold molding all around it.

**12** He made a rim 1 handbreadth[^4] wide all around it, and made a gold molding all around its rim.

**13** He cast four gold rings for it and fastened the rings to the four corners, where its four legs were.

**14** The rings were close to the rim, to hold the poles for carrying the table.

**15** He made the poles for carrying the table of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold.

**16** He made the utensils that were to be on the table of pure gold: its plates and its dishes, its bowls and its jars for pouring out drink offerings.

**17** He made the lampstand of pure gold. He made the lampstand of hammered work. Its base and its shaft, its cups, its buds and its blossoms were of one piece with it.

**18** Six branches came out from its sides: three branches of the lampstand from one side and three branches of the lampstand from the other side.

**19** On one branch there were three cups shaped like almond blossoms, each with a bud and a blossom, and on the next branch three cups shaped like almond blossoms, each with a bud and a blossom. It was the same for the six branches that came out from the lampstand.

**20** On the lampstand itself there were four cups shaped like almond blossoms, with its buds and its blossoms.

**21** There was a bud under the first pair of branches that came out from it, a bud under the second pair of branches, and a bud under the third pair of branches:[^5] so for the six branches that came out from it.

**22** Their buds and their branches were of one piece with it. The whole of it was a single piece of hammered work of pure gold.

**23** He made its seven lamps, and its tongs and its trays, of pure gold.

**24** He made the lampstand and all its utensils from 1 talent[^6] of pure gold.

**25** He made the altar of incense of acacia wood. It was square, 1 cubit long and 1 cubit wide, and it was 2 cubits high. Its horns were of one piece with it.

**26** He overlaid it with pure gold, its top, its sides all around and its horns, and he made a gold molding all around it.

**27** He made two gold rings for it below its molding, on its two sides, on opposite sides,[^7] to hold the poles for carrying it.

**28** He made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold.

**29** He also made the sacred anointing oil and the pure fragrant incense, the work of a perfumer.

[^1]: A cubit was about 18 inches (45 cm), so the ark was about 45 inches long and 27 inches wide and high (113 by 68 by 68 cm).
[^2]: Or "corners".
[^3]: Hebrew *kapporet*, traditionally "mercy seat". See 25:17.
[^4]: About 3 inches (7.5 cm).
[^5]: Literally "a bud under two of the branches from it", three times.
[^6]: About 75 pounds (34 kg).
[^7]: Literally "on its two side walls, on its two sides".
