**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses, "Cut two stone tablets like the first ones, and I will write on the tablets the words that were on the first tablets, which you broke.

**2** Be ready in the morning. In the morning come up Mount Sinai and present yourself to me there on the top of the mountain.

**3** No one is to come up with you, and no one is to be seen anywhere on the mountain. Not even the flocks or the herds may graze in front of that mountain."

**4** So Moses cut two stone tablets like the first ones. He got up early in the morning and went up Mount Sinai, as Yahweh had commanded him, and he took the two stone tablets in his hand.

**5** Yahweh came down in the cloud and stood there with him, and he proclaimed the name Yahweh.[^2]

**6** Yahweh passed by in front of him and proclaimed, "Yahweh, Yahweh,[^3] a God compassionate[^4] and gracious, slow to anger,[^5] and abounding in faithful love and faithfulness,[^6]

**7** keeping faithful love for thousands,[^7] forgiving wrongdoing, rebellion and sin.[^8] Yet he does not let the guilty go unpunished.[^9] He punishes the children and the grandchildren for the wrongdoing of their fathers, to the third and the fourth generation."[^10]

**8** At once Moses bowed down to the ground and worshiped.

**9** He said, "If I have found favor in your sight, Lord, please let the Lord go among us. This is a stiff-necked people,[^11] but forgive our wrongdoing and our sin, and take us as your own possession."

**10** He said, "Look, I am making a covenant. In front of all your people I will do wonders such as have never been done[^12] in all the earth or in any nation. All the people among whom you live will see the work of Yahweh, for what I am going to do with you is awesome.

**11** Be careful to do what I command you today. Look, I am going to drive out before you the Amorites, Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites and Jebusites.

**12** Be careful not to make a covenant with the people who live in the land you are going into, or it will become a trap among you.

**13** Instead, tear down their altars, smash their standing stones and cut down their Asherah poles.[^13]

**14** You must not bow down to any other god, for Yahweh, whose name is Jealous,[^14] is a jealous God.

**15** Do not make a covenant with the people who live in the land. Otherwise, when they prostitute themselves to their gods[^15] and sacrifice to their gods, someone will invite you, and you will eat from his sacrifice.

**16** And you will take some of their daughters as wives for your sons,[^16] and their daughters will prostitute themselves to their gods and make your sons prostitute themselves to their gods too.

**17** Do not make yourself any gods of cast metal.

**18** "Keep the Festival of Unleavened Bread. For seven days you are to eat bread made without yeast, as I commanded you, at the appointed time in the month of Abib,[^17] for in the month of Abib you came out of Egypt.

**19** Every firstborn[^18] belongs to me, including every male among your livestock,[^19] the firstborn of cattle and of sheep.

**20** Redeem the firstborn of a donkey with a lamb.[^20] If you do not redeem it, break its neck. Redeem every firstborn of your sons. No one is to appear before me empty-handed.[^21]

**21** "For six days you are to work, but on the seventh day you must rest.[^22] Even at plowing time and harvest you must rest.

**22** Celebrate the Festival of Weeks with the firstfruits of the wheat harvest, and the Festival of Ingathering at the turn of the year.[^23]

**23** Three times a year all your males are to appear before the Lord Yahweh, the God of Israel.

**24** For I will drive out nations before you and enlarge your territory. No one will covet your land when you go up three times a year to appear before Yahweh your God.

**25** "Do not offer the blood of my sacrifice with anything that contains yeast,[^24] and the sacrifice of the Passover festival must not be left until morning.

**26** Bring the best of the firstfruits of your soil to the house of Yahweh your God. Do not boil a young goat in its mother's milk."

**27** Yahweh said to Moses, "Write down these words, for on the basis of these words I have made a covenant with you and with Israel."

**28** Moses was there with Yahweh for forty days and forty nights. He did not eat bread or drink water. And he wrote on the tablets the words of the covenant, the Ten Commandments.[^25]

**29** When Moses came down from Mount Sinai with the two tablets of the testimony in his hand, as he came down from the mountain, Moses did not know that the skin of his face was shining[^26] because he had been speaking with him.[^27]

**30** When Aaron and all the Israelites saw Moses, the skin of his face was shining, and they were afraid to come near him.

**31** But Moses called to them, and Aaron and all the leaders of the community came back to him, and Moses spoke to them.

**32** After that all the Israelites came near, and he gave them all the commands that Yahweh had spoken to him on Mount Sinai.

**33** When Moses had finished speaking with them, he put a covering[^28] over his face.

**34** But whenever Moses went in before Yahweh to speak with him, he would take the covering off until he came out. When he came out, he would tell the Israelites what he had been commanded.

**35** The Israelites would see Moses' face, that the skin of Moses' face was shining. Then Moses would put the covering back over his face until he went in to speak with him.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "he called in the name of Yahweh". The subject is probably Yahweh, as promised in 33:19, though it could be Moses.
[^3]: Or "Yahweh, Yahweh is a God compassionate and gracious…". The Hebrew can also be read "Yahweh passed in front of him, and Yahweh proclaimed, 'Yahweh, a God compassionate…'"
[^4]: Hebrew *rachum*, a word related to *rechem*, "womb": tender, motherly compassion.
[^5]: Literally "long of nostrils", that is, slow to become angry.
[^6]: Hebrew *rav chesed ve-emet*. *Chesed* is loyal, faithful love, the love that keeps a covenant; *emet* is firmness, reliability, truth. Traditionally "abundant in goodness and truth" or "steadfast love and faithfulness". These words in verses 6–7 are echoed often in the Bible, for example Numbers 14:18; Psalm 86:15; 103:8; 145:8; Joel 2:13; Jonah 4:2.
[^7]: Or "to the thousandth generation". Compare 20:6.
[^8]: Literally "lifting iniquity and rebellion and sin". The three Hebrew words, *avon*, *pesha* and *chatta'ah*, together cover every kind of wrongdoing.
[^9]: Literally "and clearing, he will not clear". The words "the guilty" are not in the Hebrew but are implied.
[^10]: Literally "visiting the iniquity of fathers on sons and on sons of sons, on the third and on the fourth". The verb *paqad* means "attend to, call to account". Compare 20:5.
[^11]: Or "Because this is a stiff-necked people". Literally "hard of neck", that is, stubborn.
[^12]: Literally "have never been created". The verb is *bara*, used of God's creating in Genesis 1:1.
[^13]: Hebrew *asherim*: wooden poles or trees set up as symbols of the goddess Asherah.
[^14]: Or "Zealous". Hebrew *qanna*: one who will not share the loyalty that is owed to him.
[^15]: Literally "when they go whoring after their gods", a common image for unfaithfulness to Yahweh. Also in verse 16.
[^16]: Literally "you will take from his daughters for your sons".
[^17]: *Abib* means "new ears of grain": the first month of the year, around March–April.
[^18]: Literally "everything that first opens the womb".
[^19]: The Hebrew word translated "male" (*tizzakhar*) is unusual, and the clause is difficult.
[^20]: Hebrew *seh*, a lamb or a young goat.
[^21]: Literally "My face is not to be seen empty-handed."
[^22]: Hebrew *tishbot*, "cease, rest", the verb related to *Shabbat*, "Sabbath". Also later in the verse.
[^23]: In the autumn. Compare 23:16.
[^24]: Literally "Do not slaughter the blood of my sacrifice with yeast."
[^25]: Literally "the ten words". The Hebrew does not say who wrote. In verse 1 Yahweh says that he will write on the tablets.
[^26]: Hebrew *qaran*, "shine, send out rays", a verb related to *qeren*, "horn". The Latin Vulgate translated it "horned", which is why Moses is often shown with horns in medieval art. Also in verses 30 and 35.
[^27]: That is, with Yahweh. Also in verse 35.
[^28]: Hebrew *masveh*, a covering for the face, traditionally "veil". Also in verses 34 and 35.
