**1** Moses assembled the whole community of the Israelites and said to them, "These are the things the LORD[^1] has commanded you to do.

**2** For six days work may be done, but the seventh day is to be holy for you, a Sabbath of complete rest[^2] to the LORD. Anyone who does any work on it must be put to death.

**3** Do not light a fire in any of your homes on the Sabbath day."

**4** Moses said to the whole community of the Israelites, "This is what the LORD has commanded:[^3]

**5** Take from among you a contribution[^4] for the LORD. Let everyone whose heart is willing bring it as the LORD's contribution: gold, silver and bronze;

**6** blue, purple and scarlet yarn;[^5] fine linen; goats' hair;

**7** rams' skins dyed red; fine leather;[^6] acacia wood;

**8** oil for the light; spices for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense;

**9** onyx stones and other stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece.

**10** "Let all who are skilled[^7] among you come and make everything the LORD has commanded:

**11** the tabernacle, with its tent and its covering, its clasps and its frames, its crossbars, its posts and its bases;

**12** the ark with its poles, the atonement cover, and the veil that screens it off;[^8]

**13** the table with its poles and all its utensils, and the bread of the Presence;

**14** the lampstand for the light with its utensils and its lamps, and the oil for the light;

**15** the altar of incense with its poles, the anointing oil and the fragrant incense, and the screen for the entrance, at the entrance of the tabernacle;

**16** the altar of burnt offering with its bronze grating, its poles and all its utensils; the basin with its stand;

**17** the hangings of the courtyard, its posts and its bases, and the screen for the gate of the courtyard;

**18** the tent pegs of the tabernacle and the tent pegs of the courtyard, and their ropes;

**19** the woven garments[^9] for serving in the holy place, the sacred garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons for serving as priests."

**20** Then the whole community of the Israelites left Moses' presence.

**21** Everyone whose heart stirred him and everyone whose spirit moved him came and brought the LORD's contribution for the work on the tent of meeting, for all its service, and for the sacred garments.

**22** They came, men and women alike, all who were willing. They brought brooches, earrings, rings and necklaces,[^10] all kinds of gold jewelry. Everyone presented gold as a wave offering[^11] to the LORD.

**23** Everyone who had blue, purple or scarlet yarn, fine linen, goats' hair, rams' skins dyed red or fine leather brought them.

**24** Everyone who could make a contribution of silver or bronze brought it as the LORD's contribution, and everyone who had acacia wood for any part of the work brought it.

**25** All the skilled women spun with their own hands and brought what they had spun: blue, purple and scarlet yarn and fine linen.

**26** All the women whose hearts stirred them and who had the skill spun the goats' hair.

**27** The leaders brought the onyx stones and the other stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece,

**28** and the spices and the oil for the light, for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense.

**29** All the men and women whose hearts moved them to bring something for all the work that the LORD had commanded through Moses to be done brought it. The Israelites brought it as a freewill offering to the LORD.

**30** Then Moses said to the Israelites, "See, the LORD has chosen by name[^12] Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah.

**31** He has filled him with the Spirit of God,[^13] with skill, understanding and knowledge, and with ability in every kind of craft,

**32** to make artistic designs, to work in gold, silver and bronze,

**33** to cut stones for setting, to carve wood, and to work in every kind of artistic craft.

**34** And he has given him the ability to teach others, both him and Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan.[^14]

**35** He has filled them with skill[^15] to do every kind of work as craftsmen, as designers, as embroiderers in blue, purple and scarlet yarn and fine linen, and as weavers. They can do every kind of work and make artistic designs.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *shabbat shabbaton*, an emphatic phrase: "a Sabbath of Sabbath rest".
[^3]: Verses 5–19 repeat the instructions of 25:2–7 and the list of what was to be made in chapters 25–31.
[^4]: Hebrew *terumah*: something set apart and given for a sacred purpose. See 25:2.
[^5]: Literally "blue and purple and crimson worm": wools dyed violet-blue, red-purple and crimson. See 25:4.
[^6]: Hebrew *tachash*, of uncertain meaning. See 25:5.
[^7]: Literally "all the wise of heart". Also in verse 25.
[^8]: Literally "the veil of the screen".
[^9]: The meaning of the Hebrew word *serad* is uncertain. See 31:10.
[^10]: The identification of these ornaments is uncertain. The last, *kumaz*, may be a pendant or a bead.
[^11]: Hebrew *tenufah*. See 29:24. Literally "every man who waved a wave offering of gold to the LORD".
[^12]: Literally "The LORD has called by name".
[^13]: Or "a divine spirit". Hebrew *ruach elohim*.
[^14]: Literally "And to teach he put in his heart, he and Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan."
[^15]: Literally "wisdom of heart".
