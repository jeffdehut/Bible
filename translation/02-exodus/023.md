**1** Do not spread a false report. Do not join hands with a wicked person by being a malicious witness.[^1]

**2** Do not follow the crowd in doing wrong. When you give evidence in a lawsuit, do not side with the majority to twist justice.[^2]

**3** And do not show favor to a poor person in his lawsuit.

**4** If you come across your enemy's ox or donkey wandering off, you must take it back to him.

**5** If you see the donkey of someone who hates you lying down under its load, do not walk away and leave him with it. You must help him with it.[^3]

**6** Do not twist justice for the poor among you in their lawsuits.[^4]

**7** Keep far away from a false charge. Do not put an innocent or righteous person to death, for I will not acquit the guilty.

**8** Do not accept a bribe, for a bribe blinds those who see clearly and twists the words of those who are in the right.[^5]

**9** Do not oppress a foreigner.[^6] You yourselves know how a foreigner feels,[^7] because you were foreigners in the land of Egypt.

**10** For six years you are to sow your land and gather in its produce,

**11** but in the seventh year you must let it rest and leave it alone. Then the poor among your people may eat from it, and the wild animals may eat what they leave. Do the same with your vineyard and your olive grove.

**12** For six days you are to do your work, but on the seventh day you must stop,[^8] so that your ox and your donkey may rest, and the son of your slave woman and the foreigner may be refreshed.

**13** Pay attention to everything I have said to you. Do not call on the names of other gods. Do not even let them be heard on your lips.[^9]

**14** Three times[^10] a year you are to hold a festival for me.

**15** Keep the Festival of Bread Made without Yeast. For seven days you are to eat bread made without yeast, as I commanded you, at the appointed time in the month of Abib,[^11] for in that month you came out of Egypt. No one is to appear before me empty-handed.[^12]

**16** Keep the Festival of Harvest, with the first produce of your work, of what you sow in the field. And keep the Festival of Ingathering at the end of the year,[^13] when you gather in the produce of your work from the field.[^14]

**17** Three times a year all your males are to appear before the Lord GOD.[^15]

**18** Do not offer the blood of my sacrifice with anything that contains yeast. And the fat of my festival offering must not be left overnight until morning.

**19** Bring the best of the firstfruits of your soil to the house of the LORD your God. Do not boil a young goat in its mother's milk.

**20** Look, I am sending an angel[^16] ahead of you to guard you on the way and to bring you to the place I have prepared.

**21** Pay attention to him and listen to his voice. Do not rebel against him,[^17] for he will not pardon your wrongdoing, because my name is in him.

**22** But if you listen carefully to his voice and do everything I say, I will be an enemy to your enemies and a foe to your foes.

**23** For my angel will go ahead of you and bring you to the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Canaanites, the Hivites and the Jebusites, and I will wipe them out.

**24** Do not bow down to their gods or serve them, and do not follow their practices.[^18] You must tear them down completely and smash their standing stones to pieces.[^19]

**25** Serve the LORD your God, and he will bless your bread and your water.[^20] I will take sickness away from among you.

**26** No woman in your land will miscarry or be unable to have children. I will give you a full life span.[^21]

**27** I will send my terror ahead of you. I will throw into confusion every people you come against, and I will make all your enemies turn their backs and run from you.[^22]

**28** I will send the hornet[^23] ahead of you, and it will drive the Hivites, the Canaanites and the Hittites out of your way.

**29** I will not drive them out from before you in a single year, or the land would become a wasteland and the wild animals would become too many for you.

**30** Little by little I will drive them out from before you, until you have grown in number and can take possession of the land.

**31** I will set your borders from the Red Sea[^24] to the Sea of the Philistines,[^25] and from the wilderness to the River.[^26] For I will hand over to you the people who live in the land, and you will drive them out from before you.

**32** Do not make a covenant with them or with their gods.

**33** They must not live in your land, or they will make you sin against me. For if you serve their gods, it will be a trap for you.

[^1]: Literally "Do not put your hand with the wicked to be a witness of violence".
[^2]: Literally "Do not be after many for evil things, and do not answer in a dispute to turn aside after many, to turn aside."
[^3]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "and you will refrain from leaving (it) to him; you must surely leave (it) with him." The verb *azav* usually means "leave". Here it is often taken as a similar word meaning "help" or "set free", so: "you must help him free it."
[^4]: Literally "Do not turn aside the justice of your poor in his lawsuit."
[^5]: Or "subverts the cause of the innocent".
[^6]: Hebrew *ger*: someone from elsewhere who lives among the Israelites, a resident foreigner. Also in verse 12.
[^7]: Literally "you know the *nephesh* (life, soul) of the foreigner".
[^8]: Hebrew *tishbot*, "cease, rest", the verb related to *Shabbat*, "Sabbath".
[^9]: Literally "Do not cause the name of other gods to be remembered; let it not be heard on your mouth."
[^10]: Hebrew *regalim*, literally "feet", here "times". The same word came to mean the pilgrimage festivals.
[^11]: *Abib* means "new ears of grain": the first month of the year, around March–April.
[^12]: Literally "My face is not to be seen empty-handed."
[^13]: Literally "at the going out of the year".
[^14]: The Festival of Harvest is called the Festival of Weeks in 34:22, and the Festival of Ingathering is later called the Festival of Shelters (Leviticus 23:34).
[^15]: Hebrew *ha-adon YHWH*. *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^16]: Or "a messenger". Hebrew *mal'akh* means both. Also in verse 23.
[^17]: Or "Do not provoke him".
[^18]: Literally "do not do according to their works".
[^19]: Hebrew *matsevot*: standing stones set up as objects of worship.
[^20]: The Hebrew moves from "he" to "I" within the verse.
[^21]: Literally "I will fill the number of your days."
[^22]: Literally "I will give all your enemies to you, a back of the neck."
[^23]: Hebrew *tsir'ah*, of uncertain meaning. It is usually taken as "hornet" or "hornets", but some understand it as "panic" or "plague".
[^24]: Hebrew *Yam Suph*, literally "Sea of Reeds", traditionally the Red Sea. Here it probably means the Gulf of Aqaba.
[^25]: The Mediterranean Sea.
[^26]: The Euphrates.
