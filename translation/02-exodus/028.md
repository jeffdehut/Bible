**1** "Bring your brother Aaron near to you, together with his sons, from among the Israelites, to serve me as priests: Aaron, and Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron's sons.

**2** Make sacred garments for your brother Aaron, to give him dignity and splendor.[^1]

**3** Speak to all who are skilled,[^2] whom I have filled with a spirit of wisdom, and have them make Aaron's garments, to consecrate him to serve me as priest.

**4** These are the garments they are to make: a breastpiece,[^3] an ephod,[^4] a robe, a tunic of checkered weave,[^5] a turban and a sash. They are to make sacred garments for your brother Aaron and his sons, so that he may serve me as priest.

**5** They are to use gold, and blue, purple and scarlet yarn,[^6] and fine linen.

**6** "They are to make the ephod of gold, of blue, purple and scarlet yarn, and of fine twisted linen, the work of a skilled designer.

**7** It is to have two shoulder pieces attached to its two edges, so that it can be joined together.

**8** The woven waistband[^7] on it is to be made in the same way and of one piece with it: of gold, of blue, purple and scarlet yarn, and of fine twisted linen.

**9** "Take two onyx stones[^8] and engrave on them the names of the sons of Israel,

**10** six of their names on one stone and the names of the remaining six on the other stone, in the order of their birth.

**11** Engrave the names of the sons of Israel on the two stones as a gem cutter engraves a seal.[^9] Mount them in settings of gold.

**12** Put the two stones on the shoulder pieces of the ephod, as stones of remembrance for the Israelites. Aaron is to carry their names before the LORD[^10] on his two shoulders as a reminder.

**13** "Make settings of gold,

**14** and two chains of pure gold, twisted like cords, and fasten the corded chains to the settings.

**15** "Make a breastpiece of judgment,[^11] the work of a skilled designer. Make it in the same way as the ephod: make it of gold, of blue, purple and scarlet yarn, and of fine twisted linen.

**16** It is to be square and folded double, a span[^12] long and a span wide.

**17** Mount on it a setting of stones, four rows of stones. The first row is to be a carnelian, a chrysolite and an emerald;[^13]

**18** the second row a turquoise, a lapis lazuli and a moonstone;

**19** the third row a jacinth, an agate and an amethyst;

**20** and the fourth row a beryl, an onyx and a jasper. They are to be mounted in gold settings in their places.

**21** There are to be twelve stones, one for each of the names of the sons of Israel. Each is to be engraved like a seal with its name, for the twelve tribes.

**22** "For the breastpiece make chains of pure gold, twisted like cords.

**23** Make two gold rings for the breastpiece, and fasten the two rings to the two corners of the breastpiece.

**24** Fasten the two gold cords to the two rings at the corners of the breastpiece,

**25** and fasten the other two ends of the two cords to the two settings. In this way attach them to the shoulder pieces of the ephod, at the front.

**26** Make two gold rings and put them on the other two corners of the breastpiece, on its inside edge, next to the ephod.

**27** Make two more gold rings and fasten them to the bottom of the two shoulder pieces on the front of the ephod, close to its seam, just above the woven waistband of the ephod.

**28** The breastpiece is to be tied by its rings[^14] to the rings of the ephod with a cord of blue, so that it lies on the woven waistband of the ephod. Then the breastpiece will not come loose from the ephod.

**29** "So Aaron will carry the names of the sons of Israel on the breastpiece of judgment over his heart whenever he goes into the holy place, as a reminder before the LORD at all times.

**30** Put the Urim and the Thummim[^15] into the breastpiece of judgment, so that they will be over Aaron's heart when he goes in before the LORD. So Aaron will always carry the means of judgment for the Israelites[^16] over his heart before the LORD.

**31** "Make the robe of the ephod entirely of blue.

**32** It is to have an opening for the head in the middle of it. Around the opening there is to be a woven binding, like the opening of a coat of armor,[^17] so that it will not tear.

**33** On its hem make pomegranates of blue, purple and scarlet yarn, all around its hem, with gold bells between them all around:

**34** a gold bell and a pomegranate, a gold bell and a pomegranate, all around the hem of the robe.

**35** Aaron is to wear it when he serves. Its sound will be heard when he goes into the holy place before the LORD and when he comes out, so that he will not die.

**36** "Make a plate[^18] of pure gold, and engrave on it, as on a seal, 'Holy to the LORD.'

**37** Fasten it to a cord of blue, so that it is on the turban. It is to be on the front of the turban.

**38** It will be on Aaron's forehead, and Aaron will bear the guilt connected with the holy things[^19] that the Israelites consecrate, all their holy gifts. It is to be on his forehead always, so that they may be accepted before the LORD.

**39** "Weave the tunic of fine linen in a checkered pattern. Make a turban of fine linen, and make a sash, the work of an embroiderer.

**40** For Aaron's sons make tunics, and make sashes for them, and make caps[^20] for them, to give them dignity and splendor.

**41** Dress your brother Aaron and his sons with him in these garments. Anoint them, ordain them[^21] and consecrate them, so that they may serve me as priests.

**42** Make linen undergarments for them to cover their naked bodies.[^22] They are to reach from the waist to the thighs.

**43** Aaron and his sons are to wear them when they go into the tent of meeting or when they come near the altar to serve in the holy place, so that they do not incur guilt and die.[^23] This is to be a permanent decree for Aaron and for his descendants after him.

[^1]: Literally "for glory and for beauty". Also in verse 40.
[^2]: Literally "all the wise of heart". The heart was thought of as the seat of understanding.
[^3]: Hebrew *choshen*, a square of woven cloth worn on the chest, described in verses 15–30.
[^4]: Hebrew *ephod*, a priestly garment worn over the robe, described in verses 6–14.
[^5]: The meaning of the Hebrew word *tashbets* is uncertain: perhaps woven in a checkered pattern (as in verse 39), or embroidered.
[^6]: Literally "the blue and the purple and the crimson worm": wools dyed violet-blue, red-purple and crimson. See 25:4.
[^7]: Hebrew *chesheb*: a decorated band or belt that tied the ephod around the body.
[^8]: Hebrew *shoham*. The identification of this stone is uncertain.
[^9]: Literally "the work of a craftsman in stone, the engravings of a seal".
[^10]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^11]: Or "breastpiece of decision". It held the Urim and Thummim, through which God's decisions were sought (verse 30).
[^12]: About 9 inches (23 cm).
[^13]: The identification of many of the stones in verses 17–20 is uncertain. *Lapis lazuli* is Hebrew *sappir*, traditionally "sapphire" (see 24:10); *moonstone* is *yahalom*, traditionally "diamond"; *beryl* is *tarshish*.
[^14]: The Hebrew text is written "its ring" but read "its rings".
[^15]: Hebrew *urim* and *tummim*, perhaps "lights" and "perfections". They were objects used to learn God's will (Numbers 27:21; 1 Samuel 14:41), but how they worked is not known.
[^16]: Literally "carry the judgment of the sons of Israel".
[^17]: The meaning of the Hebrew word *tachra* is uncertain.
[^18]: Hebrew *tsits*, literally "flower" or "shining thing".
[^19]: Literally "bear the iniquity of the holy things": that is, take responsibility for any fault in the people's offerings.
[^20]: Or "headbands".
[^21]: Literally "fill their hand", an idiom for installing someone as a priest.
[^22]: Literally "the flesh of nakedness".
[^23]: Literally "so that they do not bear iniquity and die".
