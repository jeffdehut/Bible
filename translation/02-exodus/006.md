**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses, "Now you will see what I will do to Pharaoh. Forced by a mighty hand,[^2] he will let them go; forced by a mighty hand, he will drive them out of his land."

**2** God spoke to Moses and said to him, "I am Yahweh.

**3** I appeared to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob as God Almighty,[^3] but by my name Yahweh I did not make myself known to them.[^4]

**4** I also established my covenant with them, to give them the land of Canaan, the land where they lived as foreigners.

**5** And I have heard the groaning of the Israelites, whom the Egyptians are holding in slavery, and I have remembered my covenant.

**6** So say to the Israelites: 'I am Yahweh. I will bring you out from under the forced labor of the Egyptians. I will rescue you from slavery to them, and I will redeem[^5] you with an outstretched arm and with great acts of judgment.

**7** I will take you as my own people, and I will be your God. Then you will know that I am Yahweh your God, who brought you out from under the forced labor of the Egyptians.

**8** I will bring you to the land that I swore[^6] to give to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, and I will give it to you as your possession. I am Yahweh.'"

**9** Moses told this to the Israelites, but they did not listen to Moses, because they were so discouraged[^7] and their labor was so hard.

**10** Then Yahweh said to Moses,

**11** "Go and tell Pharaoh king of Egypt to let the Israelites go out of his land."

**12** But Moses said to Yahweh, "If the Israelites have not listened to me, how will Pharaoh listen to me, when I speak so poorly?"[^8]

**13** Then Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron and gave them orders concerning the Israelites and Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring the Israelites out of the land of Egypt.

**14** These are the heads of their ancestral families.[^9] The sons of Reuben, Israel's firstborn: Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron and Carmi. These were the clans of Reuben.

**15** The sons of Simeon: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jakin, Zohar, and Shaul the son of a Canaanite woman. These were the clans of Simeon.

**16** These are the names of the sons of Levi according to their family records: Gershon, Kohath and Merari. Levi lived a hundred and thirty-seven years.

**17** The sons of Gershon: Libni and Shimei, by their clans.

**18** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel. Kohath lived a hundred and thirty-three years.

**19** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These were the clans of the Levites according to their family records.

**20** Amram married his father's sister[^10] Jochebed, and she bore him Aaron and Moses.[^11] Amram lived a hundred and thirty-seven years.

**21** The sons of Izhar: Korah, Nepheg and Zichri.

**22** The sons of Uzziel: Mishael, Elzaphan and Sithri.

**23** Aaron married Elisheba, the daughter of Amminadab and sister of Nahshon, and she bore him Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**24** The sons of Korah: Assir, Elkanah and Abiasaph. These were the clans of the Korahites.

**25** Aaron's son Eleazar married one of the daughters of Putiel, and she bore him Phinehas. These were the heads of the Levite families, by their clans.

**26** It was this Aaron and Moses to whom Yahweh said, "Bring the Israelites out of the land of Egypt, division by division."[^12]

**27** They were the ones who spoke to Pharaoh king of Egypt about bringing the Israelites out of Egypt. It was this Moses and Aaron.

**28** On the day when Yahweh spoke to Moses in the land of Egypt,

**29** Yahweh said to Moses, "I am Yahweh. Tell Pharaoh king of Egypt everything that I tell you."

**30** But Moses said to Yahweh, "I speak so poorly.[^13] How will Pharaoh listen to me?"

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "with a mighty hand"; also in the next clause. It may refer to God's hand or to the force that will compel Pharaoh.
[^3]: Hebrew *El Shaddai*. The meaning of *Shaddai* is uncertain.
[^4]: Or, read as a question, "did I not make myself known to them by my name Yahweh?" In Genesis, the name Yahweh is used in the stories of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob (for example Genesis 15:7; 28:13). The verse has been understood in different ways: that they did not know the full meaning of the name, or that the stories come from different traditions.
[^5]: Hebrew *ga'al*, to act as a family's redeemer: a relative who buys back a family member from slavery or debt.
[^6]: Literally "raised my hand", a gesture of taking an oath.
[^7]: Literally "because of shortness of spirit". Hebrew *ruach*, "spirit, breath". Or "because they were out of breath".
[^8]: Literally "I am uncircumcised of lips"; also in verse 30.
[^9]: Literally "houses of their fathers"; also in verse 25.
[^10]: Hebrew *dodah*, "aunt". The Greek Septuagint reads "the daughter of his father's brother", that is, his cousin.
[^11]: The Samaritan Pentateuch and the Septuagint add "and Miriam their sister".
[^12]: Literally "by their armies". Hebrew *tsava'ot*, organized companies like the divisions of an army.
[^13]: See the note on verse 12.
