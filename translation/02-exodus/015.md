**1** Then Moses and the Israelites sang this song to Yahweh.[^1] They said: "I will sing to Yahweh, for he has risen up in triumph.[^2] Horse and rider[^3] he has hurled into the sea.

**2** Yah[^4] is my strength and my song;[^5] he has become my salvation. This is my God, and I will praise him;[^6] the God of my father, and I will exalt him.

**3** Yahweh is a warrior;[^7] Yahweh is his name.

**4** Pharaoh's chariots and his army he has thrown into the sea. His best officers[^8] are drowned in the Red Sea.[^9]

**5** The deep waters have covered them; they sank to the depths like a stone.

**6** Your right hand, Yahweh, is majestic in power. Your right hand, Yahweh, shatters the enemy.

**7** In the greatness of your majesty you overthrow those who rise against you. You send out your burning anger; it consumes them like stubble.

**8** At the blast of your nostrils[^10] the waters piled up. The flowing waters stood up like a wall;[^11] the deep waters turned solid in the heart of the sea.

**9** The enemy said, 'I will pursue, I will overtake, I will divide the plunder. I will have my fill of them.[^12] I will draw my sword; my hand will destroy them.'

**10** You blew with your breath,[^13] and the sea covered them. They sank like lead in the mighty waters.

**11** Who is like you among the gods,[^14] Yahweh? Who is like you, majestic in holiness, awesome in glorious deeds, doing wonders?

**12** You stretched out your right hand, and the earth swallowed them.

**13** In your faithful love[^15] you led the people you redeemed. In your strength you guided them to your holy dwelling.

**14** The peoples heard; they tremble. Anguish has seized the people of Philistia.

**15** Then the chiefs of Edom were terrified; trembling seized the leaders[^16] of Moab; all the people of Canaan melted away.

**16** Terror and dread fall on them. Because of the greatness of your arm they are as still as a stone, until your people pass by, Yahweh, until the people you have acquired[^17] pass by.

**17** You will bring them in and plant them on the mountain of your inheritance, the place, Yahweh, that you made for your dwelling, the sanctuary, Lord, that your hands established.

**18** Yahweh will reign forever and ever."

**19** When Pharaoh's horses, with his chariots and his horsemen, went into the sea, Yahweh brought the waters of the sea back over them, but the Israelites walked on dry ground through the middle of the sea.

**20** Then Miriam the prophet,[^18] Aaron's sister, took a tambourine in her hand, and all the women went out after her with tambourines and dancing.

**21** Miriam sang to them:[^19] "Sing to Yahweh, for he has risen up in triumph. Horse and rider he has hurled into the sea."

**22** Then Moses led Israel on from the Red Sea, and they went out into the wilderness of Shur. They traveled for three days in the wilderness and found no water.

**23** When they came to Marah, they could not drink the water of Marah, because it was bitter. That is why it was named Marah.[^20]

**24** The people grumbled against Moses and said, "What are we to drink?"

**25** He cried out to Yahweh, and Yahweh showed him a piece of wood.[^21] He threw it into the water, and the water became sweet. There Yahweh gave them a decree and a ruling,[^22] and there he tested them.

**26** He said, "If you listen carefully to the voice of Yahweh your God and do what is right in his eyes, if you pay attention to his commands and keep all his decrees, I will not bring on you any of the diseases I brought on the Egyptians, for I am Yahweh who heals you."

**27** Then they came to Elim, where there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees, and they camped there by the water.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *ga'oh ga'ah*, "he has risen up high", "he has triumphed gloriously"; also in verse 21.
[^3]: Or "horse and its chariot driver"; also in verse 21.
[^4]: Hebrew *Yah*, a short form of the name Yahweh, as in *hallelu-yah*, "praise Yah".
[^5]: Or "my might", "my protection". Hebrew *zimrah*.
[^6]: The meaning of the Hebrew verb is uncertain. Traditionally "I will glorify him"; perhaps "I will make him a home".
[^7]: Literally "a man of war".
[^8]: Hebrew *shalishim*; see 14:7.
[^9]: Hebrew *Yam Suph*, literally "Sea of Reeds". The Greek Septuagint calls it "the Red Sea"; also in verse 22.
[^10]: Hebrew *ruach*, "wind, breath". The east wind of 14:21 is pictured as God's breath.
[^11]: Literally "a heap".
[^12]: Literally "My appetite will be filled with them". Hebrew *nephesh*, "appetite, desire, self".
[^13]: Or "wind". Hebrew *ruach*.
[^14]: Hebrew *elim*, "gods" or "mighty ones".
[^15]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^16]: Literally "rams".
[^17]: Or "created". Hebrew *qanah*.
[^18]: Or "prophetess".
[^19]: Literally "answered them". She may have led the women in singing back and forth.
[^20]: *Marah* means "bitter".
[^21]: Or "a tree".
[^22]: Hebrew *choq u-mishpat*. *Mishpat* is "ruling", "law" or "justice", by context.
