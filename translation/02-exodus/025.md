**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Tell the Israelites to bring me a contribution.[^2] You are to receive my contribution from everyone whose heart moves them to give.

**3** This is the contribution you are to receive from them: gold, silver and bronze;[^3]

**4** blue, purple and scarlet yarn;[^4] fine linen;[^5] goats' hair;

**5** rams' skins dyed red; fine leather;[^6] acacia wood;

**6** oil for the light; spices for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense;

**7** onyx stones[^7] and other stones for setting, for the ephod[^8] and for the breastpiece.[^9]

**8** Have them make a sanctuary for me, so that I may live[^10] among them.

**9** Make it exactly like the pattern I am showing you, the pattern of the tabernacle[^11] and the pattern of all its furnishings.

**10** "They are to make an ark[^12] of acacia wood, 2½ cubits[^13] long, 1½ cubits wide and 1½ cubits high.

**11** Overlay it with pure gold. Overlay it inside and out, and make a gold molding[^14] all around it.

**12** Cast four gold rings for it and fasten them to its four feet,[^15] with two rings on one side and two rings on the other side.

**13** Make poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold.

**14** Put the poles through the rings on the sides of the ark, so that the ark can be carried with them.

**15** The poles are to stay in the rings of the ark. They must not be removed from it.

**16** Put into the ark the testimony[^16] that I will give you.

**17** "Make an atonement cover[^17] of pure gold, 2½ cubits long and 1½ cubits wide.

**18** Make two cherubim[^18] of gold. Make them of hammered work[^19] at the two ends of the atonement cover.

**19** Make one cherub at one end and one cherub at the other end. Make the cherubim of one piece with the atonement cover, at its two ends.

**20** The cherubim are to have their wings spread out upward, sheltering the atonement cover with their wings. They are to face each other, with their faces turned toward the atonement cover.

**21** Put the atonement cover on top of the ark, and put into the ark the testimony that I will give you.

**22** There I will meet with you. From above the atonement cover, from between the two cherubim that are on the ark of the testimony, I will speak with you about everything I command you for the Israelites.

**23** "Make a table of acacia wood, 2 cubits long, 1 cubit wide and 1½ cubits high.

**24** Overlay it with pure gold, and make a gold molding all around it.

**25** Make a rim 1 handbreadth[^20] wide all around it, and make a gold molding all around its rim.

**26** Make four gold rings for it, and fasten the rings to the four corners, where its four legs are.

**27** The rings are to be close to the rim, to hold the poles for carrying the table.

**28** Make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold. The table is to be carried with them.

**29** Make its plates and its dishes, and its jars and its bowls for pouring out drink offerings.[^21] Make them of pure gold.

**30** Put the bread of the Presence[^22] on the table, to be in front of me always.

**31** "Make a lampstand of pure gold. The lampstand is to be made of hammered work. Its base and its shaft,[^23] its cups, its buds[^24] and its blossoms are to be of one piece with it.

**32** Six branches are to come out from its sides: three branches of the lampstand from one side and three branches of the lampstand from the other side.

**33** On one branch there are to be three cups shaped like almond blossoms, each with a bud and a blossom, and on the next branch three cups shaped like almond blossoms, each with a bud and a blossom. It is to be the same for the six branches that come out from the lampstand.

**34** On the lampstand itself there are to be four cups shaped like almond blossoms, with its buds and its blossoms.

**35** There is to be a bud under the first pair of branches that come out from it, a bud under the second pair of branches, and a bud under the third pair of branches:[^25] so for the six branches that come out from the lampstand.

**36** Their buds and their branches are to be of one piece with it. The whole of it is to be a single piece of hammered work of pure gold.

**37** Make its seven lamps. Its lamps are to be set up[^26] so that they give light to the space in front of it.

**38** Its tongs and its trays[^27] are to be of pure gold.

**39** It is to be made from 1 talent[^28] of pure gold, together with all these utensils.

**40** See that you make them according to the pattern you are being shown on the mountain.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *terumah*: something set apart and given for a sacred purpose, traditionally "heave offering". Literally "Speak to the sons of Israel, and let them take for me a contribution."
[^3]: Or "copper". Hebrew *nechoshet* means both.
[^4]: Literally "blue and purple and crimson worm". These were wools dyed with costly dyes: *tekhelet*, a violet-blue, and *argaman*, a red-purple, both made from sea snails, and *tola'at shani*, a crimson made from an insect.
[^5]: Hebrew *shesh*, a fine linen, probably from Egypt.
[^6]: Hebrew *tachash*, of uncertain meaning. It may be the hide of a sea animal (such as the dugong), or leather of a special kind or color. Traditionally "badgers' skins" or "goatskins".
[^7]: Hebrew *shoham*. The identification of this stone is uncertain.
[^8]: Hebrew *ephod*, a priestly garment worn over the robe, described in 28:6–14.
[^9]: Hebrew *choshen*, a square of woven cloth worn on the high priest's chest, described in 28:15–30.
[^10]: Hebrew *shakhan*, "dwell", the verb from which *mishkan*, "tabernacle", comes.
[^11]: Hebrew *mishkan*, "dwelling place": the portable sanctuary described in chapters 25–31 and 35–40.
[^12]: Hebrew *aron*, "chest, box".
[^13]: A cubit was about 18 inches (45 cm), so the ark was about 45 inches long and 27 inches wide and high (113 by 68 by 68 cm).
[^14]: Or "rim", "border".
[^15]: Or "corners".
[^16]: Hebrew *edut*, "testimony, witness": the stone tablets bearing the terms of the covenant (31:18; 32:15).
[^17]: Hebrew *kapporet*, traditionally "mercy seat". The word comes from the same root as *kipper*, "make atonement" (perhaps also "cover"). It was the lid of the ark, where blood was sprinkled on the Day of Atonement (Leviticus 16:14–15).
[^18]: Hebrew *keruvim* (singular *keruv*, "cherub"): winged heavenly beings who guard holy places (Genesis 3:24).
[^19]: Hebrew *miqshah*: hammered out of a single piece of metal.
[^20]: About 3 inches (7.5 cm).
[^21]: Literally "with which it is poured out". The exact form and use of these vessels are uncertain.
[^22]: Literally "bread of the face": bread set out in the LORD's presence (Leviticus 24:5–9).
[^23]: Literally "its thigh and its reed". *Shaft* (Hebrew *qaneh*) is the same word as "branch" in verse 32.
[^24]: Hebrew *kaftor*: probably a rounded knob, like the base of a flower.
[^25]: Literally "a bud under two of the branches from it", three times.
[^26]: Literally "he shall make its lamps go up": either setting them in place on the lampstand or lighting them.
[^27]: Or "firepans": here probably small trays for the burned-out wicks.
[^28]: About 75 pounds (34 kg).
