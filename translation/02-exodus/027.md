**1** "Make the altar[^1] of acacia wood, five cubits[^2] long and five cubits wide. The altar is to be square, and it is to be three cubits high.

**2** Make horns for it on its four corners. Its horns are to be of one piece with it. Then overlay it with bronze.

**3** Make its pots for removing its ashes,[^3] and its shovels, its bowls, its forks and its firepans. Make all its utensils of bronze.

**4** Make a grating for it, a network of bronze, and on the network make four bronze rings at its four corners.

**5** Put the grating under the ledge of the altar, below it, so that the network reaches halfway up the altar.

**6** Make poles for the altar, poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with bronze.

**7** Its poles are to be put through the rings, so that the poles are on the two sides of the altar when it is carried.

**8** Make it hollow, out of boards. They are to make it just as you were shown on the mountain.

**9** "Make the courtyard of the tabernacle. On the south side the courtyard is to have hangings[^4] of fine twisted linen, a hundred cubits long for that side,

**10** with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases. The hooks of the posts and their bands[^5] are to be of silver.

**11** In the same way, on the north side there are to be hangings a hundred cubits long, with twenty posts[^6] and twenty bronze bases. The hooks of the posts and their bands are to be of silver.

**12** Across the width of the courtyard on the west side[^7] there are to be hangings fifty cubits long, with ten posts and ten bases.

**13** The width of the courtyard on the east side, toward the sunrise, is to be fifty cubits.

**14** On one side of the entrance[^8] there are to be hangings fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases,

**15** and on the other side hangings fifteen cubits long, with three posts and three bases.

**16** For the gate of the courtyard there is to be a screen twenty cubits long, of blue, purple and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer, with four posts and four bases.

**17** All the posts around the courtyard are to have silver bands and silver hooks, and bronze bases.

**18** The courtyard is to be a hundred cubits long and fifty cubits wide,[^9] with hangings of fine twisted linen five cubits high, and with bronze bases.

**19** All the utensils of the tabernacle, for all its service, and all its tent pegs and all the tent pegs of the courtyard are to be of bronze.

**20** "Command the Israelites to bring you pure oil of pressed olives[^10] for the light, so that a lamp can be kept burning always.

**21** In the tent of meeting,[^11] outside the veil that is in front of the testimony,[^12] Aaron and his sons are to keep it burning before Yahweh[^13] from evening until morning. This is a permanent decree for the Israelites, for all their generations.

[^1]: The altar of burnt offering, which stood in the courtyard (30:28; 40:6).
[^2]: A cubit was about 18 inches (45 cm), so the altar was about 7½ feet square and 4½ feet high (2.3 by 2.3 by 1.4 m).
[^3]: Literally "its pots to remove its fat ashes". The ashes were soaked with the fat of the sacrifices.
[^4]: Hebrew *qela'im*: long curtains forming the fence of the courtyard.
[^5]: Hebrew *chashuqim*: probably bands or rods joining the posts.
[^6]: The Hebrew text is written "its post" but read "its posts".
[^7]: Literally "the side of the sea", that is, toward the Mediterranean.
[^8]: Literally "for the shoulder", the side wing of the entrance. Also in verse 15.
[^9]: Literally "fifty by fifty".
[^10]: Literally "pure olive oil, beaten": oil from olives crushed by hand, the finest grade.
[^11]: Hebrew *ohel mo'ed*: the tent where Yahweh met with Moses and the people (25:22; 29:42–43). It is another name for the tabernacle.
[^12]: Literally "outside the veil that is over the testimony". The testimony was in the ark (25:16).
[^13]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
