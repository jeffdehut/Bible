**1** The LORD[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "Set apart to me as holy every firstborn, the first to open the womb among the Israelites, both people and animals. It belongs to me."

**3** Moses said to the people, "Remember this day, the day you came out of Egypt, out of the place of slavery,[^2] for the LORD brought you out of here by the strength of his hand. Nothing leavened is to be eaten.

**4** Today you are going out, in the month of Abib.[^3]

**5** When the LORD brings you into the land of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Hivites and Jebusites, which he swore to your ancestors[^4] to give you, a land flowing with milk and honey, you are to observe this ceremony[^5] in this month.

**6** For seven days you are to eat bread made without yeast, and on the seventh day there is to be a festival to the LORD.

**7** Bread made without yeast is to be eaten throughout the seven days. Nothing leavened is to be seen among you, and no yeast is to be seen anywhere in your territory.

**8** On that day you are to tell your son, 'This is because of what the LORD did for me when I came out of Egypt.'

**9** It will be a sign for you on your hand and a reminder on your forehead,[^6] so that the LORD's instruction[^7] may be on your lips. For with a mighty hand the LORD brought you out of Egypt.

**10** You are to keep this decree at its appointed time, year after year.

**11** When the LORD brings you into the land of the Canaanites, as he swore to you and to your ancestors, and gives it to you,

**12** you are to hand over[^8] to the LORD every first to open the womb. Every firstborn that your animals bear belongs to the LORD, if it is a male.

**13** Every firstborn donkey you must redeem with a lamb,[^9] and if you do not redeem it, you must break its neck. Every firstborn among your sons you must redeem.

**14** In the future, when your son asks you, 'What does this mean?' you are to say to him, 'By the strength of his hand the LORD brought us out of Egypt, out of the place of slavery.

**15** When Pharaoh was unyielding[^10] and would not let us go, the LORD killed every firstborn in the land of Egypt, both the firstborn of people and the firstborn of animals. That is why I sacrifice to the LORD every male that first opens the womb, but every firstborn of my sons I redeem.'

**16** It will be a sign on your hand and a band on your forehead,[^11] for by the strength of his hand the LORD brought us out of Egypt."

**17** When Pharaoh let the people go, God did not lead them by the road through the land of the Philistines, although that was the shorter way.[^12] For God said, "If they face war, the people may change their minds and go back to Egypt."

**18** So God led the people around by the wilderness road toward the Red Sea.[^13] The Israelites went up out of the land of Egypt ready for battle.[^14]

**19** Moses took the bones of Joseph with him, because Joseph had made the Israelites swear an oath. He had said, "God will surely come to your aid,[^15] and then you must carry my bones up from here with you."

**20** They set out from Succoth and camped at Etham, on the edge of the wilderness.

**21** The LORD went ahead of them, by day in a pillar of cloud to guide them on their way, and by night in a pillar of fire to give them light, so that they could travel by day and by night.

**22** The pillar of cloud by day and the pillar of fire by night never left its place in front of the people.

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "out of the house of slaves"; also in verse 14.
[^3]: *Abib* means "ear of grain". It falls in March–April. See 12:2.
[^4]: Literally "fathers"; also in verse 11.
[^5]: Literally "serve this service". Hebrew *avodah*; see 12:25.
[^6]: Literally "between your eyes"; also in verse 16. In later Jewish practice these words were taken literally in the wearing of tefillin (phylacteries); compare Deuteronomy 6:8.
[^7]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law".
[^8]: Literally "cause to pass over".
[^9]: Hebrew *seh*, a young sheep or goat.
[^10]: Hebrew *qashah*, "be hard, harsh", one of the verbs used for Pharaoh's heart (see 7:3 and the note on 4:21).
[^11]: Hebrew *totafot*, a band or emblem worn on the forehead.
[^12]: Or "because it was near".
[^13]: Hebrew *Yam Suph*, literally "Sea of Reeds". The Greek Septuagint calls it "the Red Sea".
[^14]: Hebrew *chamushim*, a rare word. It may mean "armed", "in battle formation" or "organized in fifties".
[^15]: Hebrew *paqod yifqod*; see 3:16. Joseph's words are in Genesis 50:25.
