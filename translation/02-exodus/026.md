**1** "Make the tabernacle[^1] with ten curtains of fine twisted linen[^2] and blue, purple and scarlet yarn,[^3] with cherubim worked into them by a skilled designer.[^4]

**2** Each curtain is to be 28 cubits[^5] long and 4 cubits wide. All the curtains are to be the same size.

**3** Five of the curtains are to be joined to one another, and the other five curtains are to be joined to one another.

**4** Make loops of blue yarn on the edge of the end curtain in the first set, and do the same on the edge of the end curtain in the second set.

**5** Make fifty loops on the one curtain, and make fifty loops on the edge of the curtain at the end of the second set, with the loops opposite one another.

**6** Make fifty gold clasps, and join the curtains to one another with the clasps, so that the tabernacle becomes a single whole.

**7** "Make curtains of goats' hair for a tent over the tabernacle. Make eleven of these curtains.

**8** Each curtain is to be 30 cubits long and 4 cubits wide. All eleven curtains are to be the same size.

**9** Join five of the curtains together by themselves, and six of the curtains by themselves. Fold the sixth curtain double at the front of the tent.

**10** Make fifty loops on the edge of the end curtain in the first set, and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain in the second set.

**11** Make fifty bronze clasps. Put the clasps into the loops and join the tent together, so that it becomes a single whole.

**12** As for the extra length that is left over in the curtains of the tent, the half curtain that is left over is to hang down over the back of the tabernacle.

**13** The cubit on one side and the cubit on the other side that is left over in the length of the curtains of the tent is to hang down over the sides of the tabernacle, on both sides, to cover it.

**14** Make a covering for the tent of rams' skins dyed red, and over that a covering of fine leather.[^6]

**15** "Make upright frames[^7] of acacia wood for the tabernacle.

**16** Each frame is to be 10 cubits long and 1½ cubits wide.

**17** Each frame is to have two tenons,[^8] joined to each other.[^9] Do this for all the frames of the tabernacle.

**18** Make the frames for the tabernacle: twenty frames for the south side,

**19** and make forty silver bases[^10] to go under the twenty frames, two bases under one frame for its two tenons, and two bases under the next frame for its two tenons.

**20** For the second side of the tabernacle, the north side, make twenty frames,

**21** with their forty silver bases, two bases under one frame and two bases under the next frame.

**22** For the back of the tabernacle, toward the west,[^11] make six frames.

**23** Make two frames for the corners of the tabernacle at the back.

**24** They are to be double at the bottom, and together they are to be complete up to the top, to the first ring.[^12] Both of them are to be made this way. They will form the two corners.

**25** So there will be eight frames, with their silver bases, sixteen bases: two bases under one frame and two bases under the next frame.

**26** "Make crossbars of acacia wood: five for the frames of one side of the tabernacle,

**27** five crossbars for the frames of the second side of the tabernacle, and five crossbars for the frames of the side of the tabernacle at the back, toward the west.

**28** The middle crossbar is to run halfway up the frames,[^13] from one end to the other.

**29** Overlay the frames with gold, and make their rings of gold to hold the crossbars. Overlay the crossbars with gold as well.

**30** Set up the tabernacle according to the plan[^14] you were shown on the mountain.

**31** "Make a veil[^15] of blue, purple and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, with cherubim worked into it by a skilled designer.

**32** Hang it on four posts of acacia wood overlaid with gold, with gold hooks, standing on four silver bases.

**33** Hang the veil under the clasps, and bring the ark of the testimony in there, behind the veil. The veil will separate the holy place from the most holy place[^16] for you.

**34** Put the atonement cover on the ark of the testimony in the most holy place.

**35** Place the table outside the veil, and the lampstand opposite the table on the south side of the tabernacle. Put the table on the north side.

**36** "For the entrance of the tent, make a screen[^17] of blue, purple and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer.[^18]

**37** Make five posts of acacia wood for the screen and overlay them with gold, with gold hooks. And cast five bronze bases for them.

[^1]: Hebrew *mishkan*, "dwelling place". Here it means the inner layer of curtains; the layer of goats' hair over it is called the "tent" (verse 7).
[^2]: Hebrew *shesh moshzar*, fine linen with its thread twisted from several strands.
[^3]: Literally "blue and purple and crimson worm": wools dyed violet-blue, red-purple and crimson. See 25:4.
[^4]: Literally "cherubim, the work of a designer". Also in verse 31.
[^5]: A cubit was about 18 inches (45 cm), so each curtain was about 42 feet long and 6 feet wide (12.6 by 1.8 m).
[^6]: Hebrew *tachash*, of uncertain meaning. See 25:5.
[^7]: Hebrew *qerashim*, traditionally "boards". They may have been open frames rather than solid planks.
[^8]: Literally "two hands": projecting pegs at the bottom of each frame, which fitted into the bases.
[^9]: The meaning of the Hebrew word translated "joined" is uncertain.
[^10]: Hebrew *adanim*: bases with sockets for the tenons.
[^11]: Literally "toward the sea", that is, the Mediterranean.
[^12]: The meaning of this verse is uncertain. Literally "They shall be twins from below, and together they shall be complete at its head, to the one ring."
[^13]: Literally "in the middle of the frames".
[^14]: Hebrew *mishpat*, here "proper arrangement".
[^15]: Hebrew *paroket*: the curtain that closed off the most holy place.
[^16]: Literally "the holy of holies".
[^17]: Hebrew *masakh*, a curtain that screened an entrance.
[^18]: Or "a weaver in colors".
