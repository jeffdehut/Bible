**1** From the blue, purple and scarlet yarn[^1] they made woven garments[^2] for serving in the holy place. They also made the sacred garments for Aaron, as Yahweh[^3] had commanded Moses.

**2** He made the ephod of gold, of blue, purple and scarlet yarn, and of fine twisted linen.

**3** They hammered out thin sheets of gold and cut them into threads, to work them into the blue, purple and scarlet yarn and the fine linen, the work of a skilled designer.

**4** They made shoulder pieces for the ephod, attached to its two edges, so that it was joined together.

**5** The woven waistband on it was of one piece with it and made in the same way: of gold, of blue, purple and scarlet yarn, and of fine twisted linen, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**6** They prepared the onyx stones, mounted in settings of gold and engraved like a seal with the names of the sons of Israel.

**7** He put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod as stones of remembrance for the Israelites, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**8** He made the breastpiece, the work of a skilled designer, in the same way as the ephod: of gold, of blue, purple and scarlet yarn, and of fine twisted linen.

**9** It was square. They made the breastpiece folded double, a span[^4] long and a span wide, folded double.

**10** They mounted on it four rows of stones. The first row was a carnelian, a chrysolite and an emerald;[^5]

**11** the second row a turquoise, a lapis lazuli and a moonstone;

**12** the third row a jacinth, an agate and an amethyst;

**13** and the fourth row a beryl, an onyx and a jasper. They were mounted in settings of gold in their places.

**14** There were twelve stones, one for each of the names of the sons of Israel. Each was engraved like a seal with its name, for the twelve tribes.

**15** For the breastpiece they made chains of pure gold, twisted like cords.

**16** They made two gold settings and two gold rings, and fastened the two rings to the two corners of the breastpiece.

**17** They fastened the two gold cords to the two rings at the corners of the breastpiece,

**18** and they fastened the other two ends of the two cords to the two settings. In this way they attached them to the shoulder pieces of the ephod, at the front.

**19** They made two gold rings and put them on the other two corners of the breastpiece, on its inside edge, next to the ephod.

**20** They made two more gold rings and fastened them to the bottom of the two shoulder pieces on the front of the ephod, close to its seam, just above the woven waistband of the ephod.

**21** They tied the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a cord of blue, so that it lay on the woven waistband of the ephod and the breastpiece would not come loose from the ephod, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**22** He made the robe of the ephod, the work of a weaver, entirely of blue.

**23** The opening of the robe was in the middle of it, like the opening of a coat of armor,[^6] with a binding around the opening, so that it would not tear.

**24** On the hem of the robe they made pomegranates of blue, purple and scarlet yarn, twisted.[^7]

**25** They made bells of pure gold and put the bells between the pomegranates, all around the hem of the robe, between the pomegranates:

**26** a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, all around the hem of the robe, to be worn for serving, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**27** They made the tunics of fine linen, the work of a weaver, for Aaron and his sons,

**28** and the turban of fine linen, the decorated caps[^8] of fine linen, and the linen undergarments of fine twisted linen,

**29** and the sash of fine twisted linen and blue, purple and scarlet yarn, the work of an embroiderer, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**30** They made the plate, the holy crown,[^9] of pure gold, and wrote on it an inscription, engraved like a seal: "Holy to Yahweh."

**31** They fastened a cord of blue to it, to attach it to the turban above, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**32** So all the work on the tabernacle, the tent of meeting, was finished. The Israelites did everything just as Yahweh had commanded Moses. That is what they did.

**33** Then they brought the tabernacle to Moses: the tent and all its furnishings, its clasps, its frames, its crossbars,[^10] its posts and its bases;

**34** the covering of rams' skins dyed red, the covering of fine leather,[^11] and the veil that screens off;[^12]

**35** the ark of the testimony with its poles, and the atonement cover;

**36** the table, all its utensils, and the bread of the Presence;

**37** the pure gold lampstand[^13] with its lamps, the lamps set in their places, and all its utensils, and the oil for the light;

**38** the gold altar, the anointing oil, the fragrant incense, and the screen for the entrance of the tent;

**39** the bronze altar with its bronze grating, its poles and all its utensils; the basin with its stand;

**40** the hangings of the courtyard, its posts and its bases, and the screen for the gate of the courtyard, its ropes and its tent pegs, and all the utensils for the service of the tabernacle, for the tent of meeting;

**41** the woven garments for serving in the holy place, the sacred garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons for serving as priests.

**42** The Israelites had done all the work just as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**43** Moses saw all the work, and they had done it just as Yahweh had commanded. That is what they had done. And Moses blessed them.

[^1]: Literally "the blue and the purple and the crimson worm": wools dyed violet-blue, red-purple and crimson. See 25:4.
[^2]: The meaning of the Hebrew word *serad* is uncertain. See 31:10. Also in verse 41.
[^3]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4]: About 9 inches (23 cm).
[^5]: The identification of many of the stones in verses 10–13 is uncertain. See 28:17.
[^6]: The meaning of the Hebrew word *tachra* is uncertain.
[^7]: Hebrew *moshzar*, "twisted", the word used for the "fine twisted linen". It probably means the yarn was twisted.
[^8]: Literally "the ornaments of the caps".
[^9]: Hebrew *tsits nezer ha-qodesh*, "the plate (or flower) of the holy diadem". See 28:36 and 29:6.
[^10]: The Hebrew text is written "its crossbar" but read "its crossbars".
[^11]: Hebrew *tachash*, of uncertain meaning. See 25:5.
[^12]: Literally "the veil of the screen".
[^13]: Literally "the pure lampstand".
