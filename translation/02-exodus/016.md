**1** They set out from Elim, and the whole community of the Israelites came to the wilderness of Sin, which is between Elim and Sinai, on the fifteenth day of the second month after they had come out of the land of Egypt.

**2** In the wilderness the whole community of the Israelites grumbled against Moses and Aaron.

**3** The Israelites said to them, "If only we had died by the hand of the LORD[^1] in the land of Egypt, when we sat by the pots of meat and ate all the bread we wanted! But you have brought us out into this wilderness to kill this whole assembly with hunger."

**4** Then the LORD said to Moses, "I am going to rain down bread from heaven for you. The people are to go out each day and gather enough for that day. In this way I will test them, to see whether they will follow my instructions[^2] or not.

**5** On the sixth day, when they prepare what they bring in, it will be twice as much as they gather on other days."

**6** So Moses and Aaron said to all the Israelites, "This evening you will know that it was the LORD who brought you out of the land of Egypt,

**7** and in the morning you will see the glory[^3] of the LORD, because he has heard your grumbling against the LORD. Who are we, that you grumble against us?"

**8** Moses also said, "You will know it when the LORD gives you meat to eat in the evening and all the bread you want in the morning, because the LORD has heard the grumbling you are directing against him. Who are we? Your grumbling is not against us, but against the LORD."

**9** Then Moses said to Aaron, "Say to the whole community of the Israelites, 'Come near before the LORD, for he has heard your grumbling.'"

**10** While Aaron was speaking to the whole community of the Israelites, they turned toward the wilderness, and there was the glory of the LORD appearing in the cloud.

**11** The LORD said to Moses,

**12** "I have heard the grumbling of the Israelites. Tell them, 'At twilight[^4] you will eat meat, and in the morning you will have your fill of bread. Then you will know that I am the LORD your God.'"

**13** That evening quail came up and covered the camp, and in the morning there was a layer of dew around the camp.

**14** When the layer of dew had gone, there on the surface of the wilderness was something fine and flaky,[^5] as fine as frost on the ground.

**15** When the Israelites saw it, they said to one another, "What is it?"[^6] For they did not know what it was. Moses said to them, "It is the bread that the LORD has given you to eat.

**16** This is what the LORD has commanded: 'Each of you is to gather as much of it as he needs to eat, an omer[^7] for each person.[^8] Each of you is to take it for the number of people in his tent.'"

**17** The Israelites did so. Some gathered more and some less.

**18** When they measured it by the omer, those who had gathered much had nothing left over, and those who had gathered little did not have too little. Each had gathered as much as he needed to eat.

**19** Moses said to them, "No one is to keep any of it until morning."

**20** But they did not listen to Moses. Some of them kept part of it until morning, and it became full of maggots and began to stink. And Moses was angry with them.

**21** Every morning each of them gathered as much as he needed to eat, and when the sun grew hot, it melted.

**22** On the sixth day they gathered twice as much bread, two omers for each person. All the leaders of the community came and told Moses.

**23** He said to them, "This is what the LORD said: 'Tomorrow is a day of complete rest, a holy Sabbath[^9] to the LORD. Bake what you want to bake and boil what you want to boil, and set aside whatever is left over to be kept until morning.'"

**24** So they set it aside until morning, as Moses had commanded, and it did not stink, and there were no maggots in it.

**25** Moses said, "Eat it today, because today is a Sabbath to the LORD. Today you will not find any in the field.

**26** For six days you are to gather it, but on the seventh day, the Sabbath, there will be none."

**27** Yet on the seventh day some of the people went out to gather it, but they found none.

**28** Then the LORD said to Moses, "How long will you all refuse to keep my commands and my instructions?

**29** See, the LORD has given you the Sabbath. That is why on the sixth day he gives you bread for two days. Each of you is to stay where he is. No one is to leave his place on the seventh day."

**30** So the people rested on the seventh day.

**31** The house of Israel called it manna.[^10] It was like coriander seed, white, and it tasted like wafers made with honey.

**32** Moses said, "This is what the LORD has commanded: 'Keep a full omer of it throughout your generations, so that they can see the bread I fed you in the wilderness when I brought you out of the land of Egypt.'"

**33** So Moses said to Aaron, "Take a jar and put a full omer of manna in it. Then place it before the LORD, to be kept throughout your generations."

**34** As the LORD had commanded Moses, Aaron placed it in front of the testimony,[^11] to be kept.

**35** The Israelites ate manna for forty years, until they came to a land where people lived. They ate manna until they came to the border of the land of Canaan.

**36** An omer is a tenth of an ephah.[^12]

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Hebrew *torah*, "instruction, law"; also in verse 28.
[^3]: Hebrew *kavod*, "weight, honor, glory", from the same root as *kaved*, "be heavy"; also in verse 10.
[^4]: Literally "between the two evenings"; see 12:6.
[^5]: The meaning of the Hebrew word translated "flaky" is uncertain.
[^6]: Hebrew *man hu*. The question sounds like the name *man*, "manna" (verse 31).
[^7]: An omer is about 2 quarts (2 liters).
[^8]: Literally "for each skull".
[^9]: Hebrew *shabbat*, from the verb *shavat*, "stop, rest".
[^10]: Hebrew *man*; see the note on verse 15.
[^11]: Hebrew *edut*, "testimony": the tablets of the covenant, later kept in the ark (25:16).
[^12]: An ephah is a dry measure of about 20 quarts (22 liters).
