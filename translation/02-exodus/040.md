**1** Yahweh[^1] said to Moses,

**2** "On the first day of the first month, set up the tabernacle, the tent of meeting.[^2]

**3** Put the ark of the testimony in it, and screen off the ark with the veil.

**4** Bring in the table and set out the things that belong on it.[^3] Bring in the lampstand and set up its lamps.[^4]

**5** Place the gold altar for incense in front of the ark of the testimony, and put up the screen at the entrance of the tabernacle.

**6** Place the altar of burnt offering in front of the entrance of the tabernacle, the tent of meeting.

**7** Place the basin between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water in it.

**8** Set up the courtyard all around, and put up the screen at the gate of the courtyard.

**9** "Take the anointing oil and anoint the tabernacle and everything in it. Consecrate it and all its furnishings, and it will be holy.

**10** Anoint the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils. Consecrate the altar, and the altar will be most holy.

**11** Anoint the basin and its stand, and consecrate it.

**12** "Bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the tent of meeting and wash them with water.

**13** Dress Aaron in the sacred garments, anoint him and consecrate him, so that he may serve me as priest.

**14** Bring his sons and dress them in tunics.

**15** Anoint them as you anointed their father, so that they may serve me as priests. Their anointing will make them a priesthood that lasts forever, for all their generations."

**16** Moses did everything just as Yahweh had commanded him. That is what he did.

**17** So on the first day of the first month in the second year,[^5] the tabernacle was set up.

**18** Moses set up the tabernacle. He put its bases in place, set up its frames, put in its crossbars and set up its posts.

**19** He spread the tent over the tabernacle and put the covering of the tent on top of it, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**20** He took the testimony[^6] and put it into the ark. He put the poles on the ark, and he put the atonement cover on top of the ark.

**21** He brought the ark into the tabernacle and hung the veil that screens it off,[^7] and so he screened off the ark of the testimony, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**22** He put the table in the tent of meeting, on the north side of the tabernacle, outside the veil.

**23** He set out the bread on it in order before Yahweh, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**24** He put the lampstand in the tent of meeting, opposite the table, on the south side of the tabernacle.

**25** He set up[^8] the lamps before Yahweh, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**26** He put the gold altar in the tent of meeting, in front of the veil,

**27** and burned fragrant incense on it, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**28** He put up the screen at the entrance of the tabernacle.

**29** He placed the altar of burnt offering at the entrance of the tabernacle, the tent of meeting, and offered on it the burnt offering and the grain offering, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**30** He placed the basin between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water in it for washing.

**31** Moses and Aaron and his sons would wash their hands and their feet with water from it.

**32** Whenever they went into the tent of meeting, and whenever they came near the altar, they would wash, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.

**33** He set up the courtyard around the tabernacle and the altar, and put up the screen at the gate of the courtyard. So Moses finished the work.

**34** Then the cloud covered the tent of meeting, and the glory of Yahweh filled the tabernacle.

**35** Moses could not go into the tent of meeting, because the cloud had settled[^9] on it, and the glory of Yahweh filled the tabernacle.

**36** Whenever the cloud lifted from above the tabernacle, the Israelites would set out. So it was in all their journeys.

**37** But if the cloud did not lift, they did not set out until the day it lifted.

**38** For the cloud of Yahweh was over the tabernacle by day, and there was fire in it[^10] by night, in the sight of the whole house of Israel, in all their journeys.

[^1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "the tabernacle of the tent of meeting". Also in verses 6 and 29.
[^3]: Literally "arrange its arrangement": the bread of the Presence (verse 23; 25:30).
[^4]: Literally "make its lamps go up": either setting them in place on the lampstand or lighting them (see 25:37).
[^5]: The second year after the Israelites came out of Egypt.
[^6]: The stone tablets (25:16; 31:18).
[^7]: Literally "the veil of the screen".
[^8]: Or "lit". Literally "made the lamps go up", as in verse 4.
[^9]: Hebrew *shakhan*, "dwell, settle", the verb from which *mishkan*, "tabernacle", comes.
[^10]: That is, in the cloud.
