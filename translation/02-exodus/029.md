**1** "This is what you are to do to consecrate them to serve me as priests. Take one young bull and two rams without defect,

**2** and bread made without yeast, cakes made without yeast mixed with oil, and wafers made without yeast spread with oil. Make them of fine wheat flour.

**3** Put them in one basket and present them in the basket, along with the bull and the two rams.

**4** Bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the tent of meeting and wash them with water.

**5** Take the garments and dress Aaron in the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod and the breastpiece. Fasten the ephod on him with its woven waistband.

**6** Put the turban on his head and put the holy crown[^1] on the turban.

**7** Take the anointing oil, pour it on his head and anoint him.

**8** Bring his sons and dress them in tunics.

**9** Wrap sashes around them, Aaron and his sons, and tie caps on them. The priesthood will be theirs by a permanent decree. In this way you are to ordain Aaron and his sons.[^2]

**10** "Bring the bull to the front of the tent of meeting, and Aaron and his sons are to lay their hands on the bull's head.

**11** Slaughter the bull before the LORD[^3] at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**12** Take some of the bull's blood and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger, and pour out all the blood at the base of the altar.

**13** Take all the fat that covers the internal organs, the long lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys with the fat on them, and burn them on the altar.[^4]

**14** But burn the bull's flesh, its skin and its dung with fire outside the camp. It is a sin offering.[^5]

**15** "Take one of the rams, and Aaron and his sons are to lay their hands on the ram's head.

**16** Slaughter the ram, take its blood and splash it against the altar on all sides.

**17** Cut the ram into its pieces. Wash its internal organs and its legs, and put them with its pieces and its head.

**18** Then burn the whole ram on the altar. It is a burnt offering to the LORD, a pleasing aroma,[^6] an offering by fire[^7] to the LORD.

**19** "Take the other ram, and Aaron and his sons are to lay their hands on the ram's head.

**20** Slaughter the ram and take some of its blood. Put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear and on the lobes of his sons' right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands and on the big toes of their right feet. Then splash the blood against the altar on all sides.

**21** Take some of the blood that is on the altar and some of the anointing oil, and sprinkle it on Aaron and his garments, and on his sons and his sons' garments. Then he and his garments will be holy, and his sons and his sons' garments with him.

**22** "Take the fat from the ram: the fat tail, the fat that covers the internal organs, the long lobe of the liver, the two kidneys with the fat on them, and the right thigh. For it is a ram of ordination.[^8]

**23** Also take one loaf of bread, one cake of bread made with oil, and one wafer from the basket of bread made without yeast that is before the LORD.

**24** Put all these in the hands of Aaron and his sons, and wave them as a wave offering[^9] before the LORD.

**25** Then take them from their hands and burn them on the altar on top of the burnt offering, as a pleasing aroma before the LORD. It is an offering by fire to the LORD.

**26** Take the breast of Aaron's ram of ordination and wave it as a wave offering before the LORD. It will be your share.

**27** "Consecrate the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution,[^10] the parts that were waved and lifted up from the ram of ordination, from what belongs to Aaron and to his sons.

**28** This is to be the permanent share of Aaron and his sons from the Israelites, for it is a contribution. It will be a contribution from the Israelites from their fellowship offerings,[^11] their contribution to the LORD.

**29** "Aaron's sacred garments are to belong to his sons after him, so that they can be anointed and ordained in them.[^12]

**30** The son who becomes priest in his place and comes into the tent of meeting to serve in the holy place is to wear them for seven days.

**31** "Take the ram of ordination and boil its meat in a holy place.

**32** Aaron and his sons are to eat the ram's meat and the bread that is in the basket, at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

**33** They are to eat the things by which atonement was made for them when they were ordained and consecrated. But no one else may eat them,[^13] because they are holy.

**34** If any of the meat of ordination or any of the bread is left over until morning, burn what is left over. It must not be eaten, because it is holy.

**35** "Do this for Aaron and his sons, just as I have commanded you. Spend seven days ordaining them.

**36** Each day offer a bull as a sin offering to make atonement. Purify the altar[^14] by making atonement for it, and anoint it to consecrate it.

**37** For seven days make atonement for the altar and consecrate it. Then the altar will be most holy, and whatever touches the altar will become holy.[^15]

**38** "This is what you are to offer on the altar: two lambs a year old, every day, regularly.

**39** Offer one lamb in the morning and the other lamb at twilight.[^16]

**40** With the first lamb offer a tenth of an ephah[^17] of fine flour mixed with a quarter of a hin[^18] of oil from pressed olives, and a quarter of a hin of wine as a drink offering.

**41** Offer the other lamb at twilight. Offer with it the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning, as a pleasing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD.

**42** This is to be a regular burnt offering for all your generations, at the entrance of the tent of meeting before the LORD, where I will meet with you, to speak to you there.

**43** There I will meet with the Israelites, and it will be made holy[^19] by my glory.

**44** I will consecrate the tent of meeting and the altar, and I will consecrate Aaron and his sons to serve me as priests.

**45** I will live among the Israelites and be their God.

**46** They will know that I am the LORD their God, who brought them out of the land of Egypt so that I might live among them. I am the LORD their God.

[^1]: Hebrew *nezer ha-qodesh*, "the holy diadem", the sign of Aaron's consecration: here the gold plate of 28:36–37.
[^2]: Literally "you shall fill the hand of Aaron and the hand of his sons", an idiom for installing someone as a priest. Also in verses 29, 33 and 35.
[^3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4]: Hebrew *hiqtir*, "turn into smoke". Also in verses 18 and 25.
[^5]: Hebrew *chatta't*, traditionally "sin offering". Its main purpose was to purify, so it is also called the "purification offering".
[^6]: Literally "a soothing smell", a phrase that pictures God accepting the offering with pleasure.
[^7]: Hebrew *ishsheh*, traditionally "an offering made by fire". Some take it to mean "a food gift".
[^8]: Hebrew *millu'im*, literally "filling", from the idiom "fill the hand" for installing a priest (see verse 9).
[^9]: Hebrew *tenufah*: an offering lifted or moved toward the altar and back, to present it to the LORD.
[^10]: Hebrew *terumah*, "something lifted up, set apart", as in 25:2. Traditionally "heave offering".
[^11]: Hebrew *zivchei shalmeihem*, traditionally "their peace offerings". See 24:5.
[^12]: Literally "for anointing in them and for filling their hand in them".
[^13]: Literally "a stranger shall not eat them": here anyone who is not a priest.
[^14]: Hebrew *chitte*, literally "un-sin", the verb related to *chatta't*, "sin offering".
[^15]: Or "whoever touches the altar must be holy".
[^16]: Literally "between the two evenings". Also in verse 41.
[^17]: Literally "a tenth". A tenth of an ephah was about 2 quarts (2 liters).
[^18]: A hin was about a gallon (4 liters), so a quarter of a hin was about a quart (1 liter).
[^19]: Literally "it will be made holy": the tent, or the place where God meets them.
