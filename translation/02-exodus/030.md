**1** "Make an altar for burning incense.[^1] Make it of acacia wood.

**2** It is to be square, 1 cubit[^2] long and 1 cubit wide, and it is to be 2 cubits high. Its horns are to be of one piece with it.

**3** Overlay it with pure gold, its top, its sides all around and its horns, and make a gold molding all around it.

**4** Make two gold rings for it below its molding. Make them on its two sides, on opposite sides,[^3] to hold the poles for carrying it.

**5** Make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold.

**6** Put it in front of the veil that is in front of the ark of the testimony, in front of the atonement cover that is over the testimony, where I will meet with you.

**7** Aaron is to burn fragrant incense on it. Every morning, when he tends the lamps, he is to burn it.

**8** And when Aaron lights the lamps[^4] at twilight,[^5] he is to burn it. This is to be a regular offering of incense before the LORD[^6] for all your generations.

**9** Do not offer on it any unauthorized incense,[^7] or a burnt offering or a grain offering, and do not pour a drink offering on it.

**10** Once a year Aaron is to make atonement on its horns. Once a year he is to make atonement for it with the blood of the sin offering of atonement,[^8] for all your generations. It is most holy to the LORD."

**11** The LORD said to Moses,

**12** "When you take a census of the Israelites[^9] to count them, each man must pay the LORD a ransom for his life when they are counted. Then no plague will come on them when they are counted.

**13** Everyone who crosses over to those already counted[^10] is to give this: ½ shekel,[^11] by the sanctuary shekel[^12] (a shekel is 20 gerahs[^13]). This ½ shekel is a contribution to the LORD.

**14** Everyone who crosses over to those counted, twenty years old and over, is to give the LORD's contribution.

**15** When you give the LORD's contribution to make atonement for your lives, the rich must not give more and the poor must not give less than ½ shekel.

**16** Take the atonement money from the Israelites and use it for the service of the tent of meeting. It will be a reminder of the Israelites before the LORD, to make atonement for your lives."

**17** The LORD said to Moses,

**18** "Make a bronze basin[^14] with a bronze stand, for washing. Put it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water in it.

**19** Aaron and his sons are to wash their hands and their feet with water from it.

**20** Whenever they go into the tent of meeting, they are to wash with water, so that they will not die. And when they come near the altar to serve, to burn an offering by fire[^15] to the LORD,

**21** they are to wash their hands and their feet, so that they will not die. This is to be a permanent decree for them, for Aaron and for his descendants, for all their generations."

**22** The LORD said to Moses,

**23** "Take the finest spices: 500 shekels[^16] of liquid myrrh, half as much fragrant cinnamon, 250 shekels, 250 shekels of fragrant cane,

**24** and 500 shekels of cassia, by the sanctuary shekel, and 1 hin[^17] of olive oil.

**25** Make these into a sacred anointing oil, a fragrant blend, the work of a perfumer. It will be a sacred anointing oil.

**26** Use it to anoint the tent of meeting and the ark of the testimony,

**27** the table and all its utensils, the lampstand and its utensils, the altar of incense,

**28** the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils, and the basin with its stand.

**29** Consecrate them, so that they will be most holy. Whatever touches them will become holy.[^18]

**30** Anoint Aaron and his sons, and consecrate them to serve me as priests.

**31** Say to the Israelites, 'This is to be my sacred anointing oil for all your generations.

**32** It must not be poured on anyone's body,[^19] and you must not make anything like it with the same formula. It is holy, and it is to be holy to you.

**33** Anyone who mixes perfume like it, or puts any of it on someone who is not a priest,[^20] is to be cut off from his people.'"[^21]

**34** The LORD said to Moses, "Take fragrant spices: gum resin,[^22] onycha and galbanum, fragrant spices together with pure frankincense, all in equal amounts.

**35** Make them into incense, a fragrant blend, the work of a perfumer, salted,[^23] pure and holy.

**36** Grind some of it into fine powder and put some of it in front of the testimony in the tent of meeting, where I will meet with you. It is to be most holy to you.

**37** As for the incense you are to make, do not make any for yourselves with the same formula. You are to treat it as holy to the LORD.

**38** Anyone who makes anything like it, to enjoy its fragrance, is to be cut off from his people."

[^1]: Literally "an altar, a place for burning incense". It is the "gold altar" of 39:38 and 40:5, which stood inside the tent.
[^2]: A cubit was about 18 inches (45 cm).
[^3]: Literally "on its two side walls, on its two sides".
[^4]: Literally "makes the lamps go up", as in 25:37.
[^5]: Literally "between the two evenings".
[^6]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^7]: Literally "strange incense": incense not made by the formula of verses 34–38.
[^8]: The offering made on the Day of Atonement (Leviticus 16:18–19; 23:27).
[^9]: Literally "When you lift up the head of the sons of Israel".
[^10]: Literally "Everyone who passes over to the counted ones". Also in verse 14.
[^11]: A shekel was about ⅖ ounce (11.5 grams), here of silver.
[^12]: Literally "the shekel of the holy place", the standard weight used at the sanctuary.
[^13]: A gerah was about 1/50 ounce (0.6 gram).
[^14]: Hebrew *kiyyor*, traditionally "laver".
[^15]: Hebrew *ishsheh*. See 29:18.
[^16]: Literally "five hundred". The weights are in shekels (verse 24); 500 shekels was about 12½ pounds (5.7 kg).
[^17]: About a gallon (4 liters).
[^18]: Or "Whoever touches them must be holy."
[^19]: Literally "on the flesh of a human being".
[^20]: Literally "on a stranger", here anyone who is not a priest.
[^21]: The exact meaning of "cut off" is debated: removed from the community, or put to death, perhaps by God himself. Also in verse 38.
[^22]: Hebrew *nataf*, "drops", traditionally "stacte", a resin. The identification of *nataf* and *shechelet* ("onycha") is uncertain.
[^23]: Or "mixed". Hebrew *memullach*.
