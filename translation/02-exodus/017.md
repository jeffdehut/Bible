**1** The whole community of the Israelites set out from the wilderness of Sin, moving from place to place as the LORD[^1] commanded.[^2] They camped at Rephidim, but there was no water for the people to drink.

**2** So the people quarreled with Moses and said, "Give us water to drink." Moses said to them, "Why are you quarreling with me? Why are you testing the LORD?"

**3** But the people were thirsty for water there, and the people grumbled against Moses. They said, "Why did you bring us up out of Egypt, to kill us and our children and our livestock with thirst?"[^3]

**4** Then Moses cried out to the LORD, "What am I to do with this people? A little more and they will stone me!"

**5** The LORD said to Moses, "Go on ahead of the people, and take some of the elders of Israel with you. Take in your hand the staff with which you struck the Nile, and go.

**6** I will stand there in front of you on the rock at Horeb. Strike the rock, and water will come out of it, and the people will drink." Moses did so in the sight of the elders of Israel.

**7** He named the place Massah and Meribah,[^4] because the Israelites quarreled and because they tested the LORD, saying, "Is the LORD among us or not?"

**8** Then Amalek[^5] came and fought against Israel at Rephidim.

**9** Moses said to Joshua, "Choose men for us, and go out and fight against Amalek. Tomorrow I will stand on the top of the hill with the staff of God in my hand."

**10** Joshua did as Moses had told him and fought against Amalek, while Moses, Aaron and Hur went up to the top of the hill.

**11** Whenever Moses held up his hand, Israel was winning, and whenever he lowered his hand, Amalek was winning.

**12** When Moses' hands grew tired,[^6] they took a stone and put it under him, and he sat on it. Aaron and Hur held up his hands, one on one side and one on the other, so that his hands stayed steady until the sun went down.

**13** So Joshua defeated Amalek and his army[^7] with the sword.

**14** Then the LORD said to Moses, "Write this in a book to be remembered, and make sure that Joshua hears it:[^8] I will completely wipe out the memory of Amalek from under heaven."

**15** Moses built an altar and named it The LORD Is My Banner.[^9]

**16** He said, "A hand was raised against the throne of the LORD![^10] The LORD will be at war with Amalek from generation to generation."

[^1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2]: Literally "by their stages, at the mouth of the LORD".
[^3]: Literally "to kill me and my children and my livestock". The people speak as one person.
[^4]: *Massah* means "testing" and *Meribah* "quarreling".
[^5]: The Amalekites, a desert people descended from Esau's grandson Amalek (Genesis 36:12).
[^6]: Literally "heavy". Hebrew *kaved*.
[^7]: Literally "his people".
[^8]: Literally "put it in the ears of Joshua".
[^9]: Hebrew *YHWH nissi*.
[^10]: The Hebrew is uncertain. Literally "for a hand on (or against) the throne of Yah". It may be an oath sworn with the hand raised to God's throne. Many scholars read *nes*, "banner", for the unusual word *kes*, "throne", which gives "A hand on the banner of Yah!" and matches the name in verse 15. *Yah* is a short form of the name YHWH.
