**1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus[^1] by the will of God, and Timothy our brother,

**2** to the holy and faithful brothers and sisters[^2] in Christ at Colossae: Grace to you and peace from God our Father.[^3]

**3** We always thank God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, when we pray for you,

**4** since we heard of your faith in Christ Jesus and of the love you have for all God's holy people,[^4]

**5** because of the hope laid up for you in heaven. You heard of this hope before in the word of the truth, the good news

**6** that has come to you. Just as in the whole world it is bearing fruit and growing, so it has been among you also, from the day you heard it and came to know the grace of God in truth,

**7** just as you learned it from Epaphras, our beloved fellow servant,[^5] who is a faithful servant[^6] of Christ on our behalf,[^7]

**8** and who has told us of your love in the Spirit.

**9** For this reason, from the day we heard of it, we too have not stopped praying for you and asking that you may be filled with the knowledge of his will in all spiritual wisdom and understanding,

**10** so that you may live[^8] in a way worthy of the Lord, pleasing him in every way, bearing fruit in every good work and growing in the knowledge of God,

**11** being strengthened with all power according to his glorious might,[^9] for all endurance and patience with joy,[^10]

**12** giving thanks to the Father, who has qualified you[^11] to share in the inheritance[^12] of God's holy people in the light.

**13** He has rescued us from the authority of darkness and transferred us into the kingdom of the Son he loves,[^13]

**14** in whom we have redemption,[^14] the forgiveness of sins.

**15** He is the image of the invisible God,[^15] the firstborn of all creation,[^16]

**16** for in him[^17] all things were created, in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones or dominions or rulers or authorities. All things have been created through him and for him.

**17** He is before all things, and in him all things hold together.

**18** He is the head of the body, the church. He is the beginning,[^18] the firstborn from the dead, so that in everything he himself might be first.

**19** For in him all the fullness was pleased to dwell,[^19]

**20** and through him to reconcile all things to himself,[^20] making peace through the blood of his cross, [through him,][^21] whether the things on earth or the things in heaven.

**21** And you, who once were alienated and hostile in mind, doing evil deeds[^22]—

**22** but now you have been reconciled[^23] in his body of flesh[^24] through his death—so as to present you holy and blameless and above reproach before him,

**23** if indeed you continue in the faith, established and firm, not shifting from the hope of the good news that you heard, which has been proclaimed in all creation[^25] under heaven, and of which I, Paul, became a servant.

**24** Now I rejoice in my sufferings for your sake, and in my flesh I am filling up what is lacking in Christ's afflictions[^26] for the sake of his body, which is the church.

**25** I became its servant according to the stewardship[^27] from God that was given to me for you, to make the word of God fully known,[^28]

**26** the mystery[^29] that has been hidden for ages and generations, but now has been revealed to his holy people.

**27** To them God chose to make known how great among the Gentiles are the riches of the glory of this mystery, which is Christ in you,[^30] the hope of glory.

**28** Him we proclaim, warning everyone and teaching everyone with all wisdom, so that we may present everyone mature[^31] in Christ.

**29** For this I toil, struggling with all his energy, which he powerfully works within me.[^32]

[^1]: *Christ*: Greek *Christos*, "anointed one", the Greek equivalent of "Messiah". In Paul's letters it usually works as a name, so this translation writes "Christ". Paul's order, "Christ Jesus" or "Jesus Christ", is kept as the Greek has it.
[^2]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike. *Holy*: or "to God's holy people and faithful brothers and sisters". Greek *hagioi*, "holy ones", traditionally "saints".
[^3]: Many manuscripts add "and the Lord Jesus Christ".
[^4]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "saints"; also in verses 12 and 26.
[^5]: Greek *syndoulos*, "fellow slave".
[^6]: Greek *diakonos*, "servant", traditionally "minister"; also in verses 23 and 25.
[^7]: Many manuscripts read "on your behalf".
[^8]: Literally "walk", a common biblical picture for a way of life.
[^9]: Literally "according to the might of his glory".
[^10]: Or "… patience. With joy give thanks …", taking "with joy" with verse 12.
[^11]: Many manuscripts read "us".
[^12]: Literally "for the share of the lot", the language of the dividing of the land among the tribes of Israel (Joshua 14–19).
[^13]: Literally "the Son of his love".
[^14]: Greek *apolytrosis*, release, as of a slave or captive set free by a payment. The Textus Receptus (followed by the King James Version) adds "through his blood"; compare Ephesians 1:7.
[^15]: Verses 15–20 have the form of a hymn or poem in two parts: Christ and creation (verses 15–17) and Christ and the new creation (verses 18–20). Each part opens with "He is" and speaks of him as "firstborn".
[^16]: Greek *prototokos*, "firstborn". The word can mean first in time or first in rank, the one who holds a firstborn son's rights (compare Psalm 89:28, 89:27 in most English Bibles). *Of all creation*: or "over all creation".
[^17]: Or "by him".
[^18]: Greek *arche*, "beginning, origin, first place"; the same word is translated "rulers" in verse 16.
[^19]: Or "For God was pleased to have all the fullness dwell in him". The subject of "was pleased" is not stated, and may be "all the fullness" or God.
[^20]: Or "to him".
[^21]: The SBLGNT prints these words in brackets [ ], marking their place in the text as uncertain; this translation does the same. Some manuscripts leave them out.
[^22]: Literally "in evil works". The sentence breaks off here and starts again in verse 22.
[^23]: The SBLGNT here reads "you have been reconciled", following Holmes; other editions and many manuscripts read "he has now reconciled you".
[^24]: That is, his physical body. Greek *sarx*, "flesh", in its physical sense; also in verse 24.
[^25]: Or "to every creature".
[^26]: Literally "I fill up in turn the things lacking of the afflictions of the Christ in my flesh". The meaning is debated.
[^27]: Greek *oikonomia*, the task of a steward who manages what belongs to another.
[^28]: Literally "to fulfill the word of God".
[^29]: Greek *mysterion*, a secret plan that was hidden until God revealed it; also in verse 27.
[^30]: Or "among you".
[^31]: Greek *teleios*, "mature, complete".
[^32]: Literally "according to his working, which works in me in power".
