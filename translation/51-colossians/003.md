**1** So if you have been raised with Christ, seek the things above, where Christ is, seated at the right hand of God.[^1]

**2** Set your minds on the things above, not on the things on earth,

**3** for you have died, and your life is hidden with Christ in God.

**4** When Christ, who is your life,[^2] is revealed, then you also will be revealed with him in glory.

**5** So put to death what is earthly in you:[^3] sexual immorality, impurity, passion, evil desire, and greed, which is idolatry.

**6** Because of these things the wrath of God is coming on those who disobey.[^4]

**7** In these things[^5] you too once walked,[^6] when you were living in them.

**8** But now you too must put them all away: anger, rage, malice, slander, and obscene talk from your mouth.

**9** Do not lie to one another, since you have taken off the old self[^7] with its practices

**10** and have put on the new self, which is being renewed in knowledge according to the image of its Creator.[^8]

**11** Here there is no Greek and Jew, circumcised and uncircumcised, barbarian, Scythian,[^9] slave,[^10] free; but Christ is all and in all.

**12** So, as God's chosen ones, holy and beloved, put on a heart of compassion,[^11] kindness, humility, gentleness and patience,

**13** bearing with one another and forgiving each other, if anyone has a complaint against another. Just as the Lord[^12] has forgiven you, so you also must forgive.

**14** And over all these put on love, which is the bond of perfection.[^13]

**15** And let the peace of Christ[^14] rule[^15] in your hearts, to which indeed you were called in one body. And be thankful.

**16** Let the word of Christ dwell in you[^16] richly, in all wisdom. Teach and admonish one another with psalms, hymns and spiritual songs, singing with gratitude[^17] in your hearts to God.[^18]

**17** And whatever you do, in word or in deed, do everything in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God the Father through him.

**18** Wives,[^19] submit[^20] to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord.

**19** Husbands, love your wives, and do not be harsh with them.

**20** Children, obey your parents in everything, for this pleases the Lord.

**21** Fathers,[^21] do not provoke your children, so that they do not lose heart.

**22** Slaves,[^22] obey your earthly masters[^23] in everything, not only when they are watching, to please people,[^24] but with sincerity of heart, fearing the Lord.[^25]

**23** Whatever you do, work at it wholeheartedly,[^26] as for the Lord and not for people,

**24** knowing that from the Lord you will receive the inheritance as your reward. Serve the Lord Christ.[^27]

**25** For the wrongdoer will be paid back for the wrong they have done, and there is no favoritism.

[^1]: Compare Psalm 110:1.
[^2]: Many manuscripts read "our life".
[^3]: Literally "the members that are on the earth".
[^4]: Literally "on the sons of disobedience". Some manuscripts leave out these words.
[^5]: Or "Among them".
[^6]: Literally "walked", a common biblical picture for a way of life.
[^7]: Literally "the old man"; also "the new" in verse 10.
[^8]: Literally "according to the image of the one who created him". Compare Genesis 1:26–27.
[^9]: *Barbarian*: Greek *barbaros*, someone who did not speak Greek. The Scythians, a people from north of the Black Sea, were thought of by Greeks as the least civilized of all.
[^10]: Greek *doulos*, "slave".
[^11]: Literally "the inward parts of compassion", the seat of the deepest feelings.
[^12]: Many manuscripts read "Christ".
[^13]: Or "the bond that makes perfect", "the bond of completeness".
[^14]: Many manuscripts read "the peace of God".
[^15]: Greek *brabeuo*, "act as umpire, decide".
[^16]: Or "among you".
[^17]: Or "with grace". Greek *charis*.
[^18]: Many manuscripts read "to the Lord".
[^19]: Verses 3:18–4:1 give instructions to the members of a household: wives and husbands, children and fathers, slaves and masters. Instructions of this kind are also found in Greek and Jewish writers of the time. Compare Ephesians 5:21–6:9.
[^20]: Greek *hypotasso*, "to place oneself under, to be subject to". Compare Ephesians 5:21–24.
[^21]: Greek *pateres*, "fathers", a word that can sometimes mean "parents".
[^22]: Greek *douloi*, "slaves". Slavery was part of the social order of the Roman world, and many members of the early churches were slaves or owned slaves. This passage addresses both (3:22–4:1).
[^23]: Literally "masters according to the flesh". Greek *kyrioi*, "lords, masters, owners", the same word as "Lord" later in this verse and in verses 23–24.
[^24]: Literally "not in eye-services, as people-pleasers".
[^25]: Many manuscripts read "fearing God".
[^26]: Literally "from the soul".
[^27]: Or "You are serving the Lord Christ". Many manuscripts read "for you serve the Lord Christ". *Serve*: Greek *douleuo*, "serve as a slave".
