**1** How[^1] lonely sits the city that was full of people! She has become like a widow, she who was great among the nations. The princess among the provinces has been put to forced labor.

**2** She weeps bitterly in the night, with tears on her cheeks. Among all her lovers she has no one to comfort her. All her friends have betrayed her; they have become her enemies.

**3** Judah has gone into exile after affliction and harsh servitude.[^2] She lives among the nations, but finds no resting place. All her pursuers have overtaken her in the narrow places.[^3]

**4** The roads to Zion mourn, for no one comes to the festivals. All her gates are desolate; her priests groan; her young women grieve, and she herself is in bitterness.

**5** Her foes have become her masters;[^4] her enemies are at ease. For the LORD[^5] has made her suffer for her many rebellions. Her little children have gone into captivity before the foe.

**6** All her splendor has departed from Daughter Zion.[^6] Her princes have become like deer that find no pasture; they have gone on without strength before the pursuer.

**7** In the days of her affliction and her homelessness, Jerusalem remembers all the precious things that were hers in days of old. When her people fell into the hand of the foe, and there was no one to help her, the foes looked at her; they laughed at her ruin.[^7]

**8** Jerusalem has sinned greatly; therefore she has become a thing to scorn.[^8] All who honored her despise her, for they have seen her nakedness. She herself groans and turns away.

**9** Her uncleanness is in her skirts; she did not consider her end. Her fall was astonishing; there is no one to comfort her. Look, LORD, at my affliction, for the enemy has triumphed![^9]

**10** The foe has stretched out his hand over all her precious things; for she has seen the nations enter her sanctuary, those you commanded not to enter your assembly.[^10]

**11** All her people groan as they search for bread; they give their treasures for food to keep themselves alive.[^11] Look, LORD, and see, for I am despised.

**12** Is it nothing to you,[^12] all you who pass by on the road? Look and see if there is any pain like my pain, which was dealt out to me, which the LORD inflicted on the day of his burning anger.

**13** From on high he sent fire into my bones, and it overcame them.[^13] He spread a net for my feet; he turned me back. He has made me desolate, sick all day long.

**14** The yoke of my rebellions has been bound on;[^14] by his hand they were woven together. They have come up on my neck; he has made my strength fail. The Lord has given me into the hands of those I cannot withstand.

**15** The Lord has rejected[^15] all my mighty men in my midst; he has called an assembly[^16] against me to crush my young men. The Lord has trodden Virgin Daughter Judah as in a winepress.[^17]

**16** For these things I weep; my eye, my eye runs down with water, for a comforter is far from me, one to revive my soul. My children are desolate, for the enemy has prevailed.

**17** Zion stretches out her hands, but there is no one to comfort her.[^18] The LORD has commanded against Jacob that his neighbors should be his foes. Jerusalem has become an unclean thing[^19] among them.

**18** The LORD is in the right, for I rebelled against his word.[^20] Hear now, all peoples, and see my pain! My young women and my young men have gone into captivity.

**19** I called to my lovers, but they deceived me. My priests and my elders perished in the city as they searched for food to keep themselves alive.[^21]

**20** Look, LORD, for I am in distress! My insides churn; my heart is overturned within me, for I have been very rebellious. Outside, the sword bereaves; inside, there is death.[^22]

**21** They have heard that I groan; there is no one to comfort me. All my enemies have heard of my trouble; they rejoice that you have done it. Bring on the day you have announced,[^23] and let them become like me.

**22** Let all their evil come before you, and deal with them as you have dealt with me because of all my rebellions. For my groans are many, and my heart is faint.

[^1]: Hebrew *eikhah*, "How!", a cry of lament (compare 2 Samuel 1:19), which gives the book its Hebrew name. Chapters 1–4 are acrostic poems. In this chapter the twenty-two verses begin with the twenty-two letters of the Hebrew alphabet in order, from *alef* to *tav*. Each verse has three lines, except verse 7, which has four.
[^2]: Or "because of affliction and harsh servitude".
[^3]: Or "in the midst of her distress".
[^4]: Literally "have become the head". Compare Deuteronomy 28:44.
[^5]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^6]: Hebrew *bat-tsiyyon*, a poetic name for Jerusalem and its people, pictured as a young woman.
[^7]: Hebrew *mishbatteha*, a word found only here, perhaps "her ruin" or "her ceasing".
[^8]: Hebrew *nidah*, a word found only here. It may mean "an object of scorn" (from *nud*, "to shake the head"), or it may be another form of *niddah*, "unclean thing" (verse 17).
[^9]: Jerusalem herself speaks in this line, in the last line of verse 11, and in verses 12–16 and 18–22. The poem does not mark where her words begin and end, so they are not put in quotation marks.
[^10]: Compare Deuteronomy 23:4 (23:3 in most English Bibles).
[^11]: Literally "to bring back life (*nephesh*)". Also in verse 19.
[^12]: Literally "Not to you". The meaning is uncertain: perhaps "May it not happen to you".
[^13]: Literally "and it ruled over it".
[^14]: Hebrew *nisqad*, a word found only here, of uncertain meaning.
[^15]: Or "trampled".
[^16]: Hebrew *mo'ed*, an appointed time or festival, as in verse 4.
[^17]: Literally "A winepress the Lord has trodden for the virgin, Daughter Judah".
[^18]: The poet speaks in this verse.
[^19]: Hebrew *niddah*, the uncleanness of a woman during her monthly period (Leviticus 15:19–24).
[^20]: Literally "his mouth".
[^21]: The Septuagint adds "but they found none".
[^22]: Literally "in the house, it is like death".
[^23]: Literally "You have brought the day you announced".
