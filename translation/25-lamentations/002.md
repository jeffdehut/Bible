**1** How[^1] the Lord in his anger has covered Daughter Zion[^2] with a cloud![^3] He has thrown down from heaven to earth the splendor of Israel; he has not remembered his footstool[^4] in the day of his anger.

**2** The Lord has swallowed up without pity all the dwellings of Jacob. In his fury he has torn down the strongholds of Daughter Judah; he has brought them down to the ground. He has profaned the kingdom and its princes.

**3** In his burning anger he has cut off every horn[^5] of Israel. He has drawn back his right hand in the face of the enemy. He has burned against Jacob like a flaming fire that consumes all around.

**4** He has bent his bow like an enemy; his right hand is set like a foe's. He has killed all who were pleasing to the eye. On the tent of Daughter Zion he has poured out his wrath like fire.

**5** The Lord has become like an enemy; he has swallowed up Israel. He has swallowed up all her palaces; he has destroyed his strongholds.[^6] He has multiplied mourning and moaning[^7] in Daughter Judah.

**6** He has wrecked his booth[^8] like a garden; he has destroyed his place of meeting.[^9] The LORD[^10] has made Zion forget festival and Sabbath, and in his fierce anger he has spurned king and priest.

**7** The Lord has rejected his altar and disowned his sanctuary. He has handed over to the enemy the walls of her palaces. They raised a shout in the house of the LORD as on a festival day.

**8** The LORD determined to destroy the wall of Daughter Zion. He stretched out the measuring line; he did not hold back his hand from destroying.[^11] He made rampart and wall lament; together they languish.

**9** Her gates have sunk into the ground; he has ruined and broken her bars. Her king and her princes are among the nations; there is no instruction,[^12] and her prophets find no vision from the LORD.

**10** The elders of Daughter Zion sit on the ground in silence. They have thrown dust on their heads and put on sackcloth. The young women of Jerusalem have bowed their heads to the ground.

**11** My eyes are worn out with tears; my insides churn. My heart is poured out on the ground[^13] because of the destruction of my dear people,[^14] because children and infants faint in the streets of the city.

**12** They say to their mothers, "Where is the grain and the wine?" as they faint like the wounded in the streets of the city, as their life is poured out on their mothers' laps.

**13** What can I say for you?[^15] To what can I compare you, Daughter Jerusalem? What can I liken to you, to comfort you, Virgin Daughter Zion? For your ruin is as vast as the sea; who can heal you?

**14** Your prophets have seen for you visions that were empty and false;[^16] they did not expose your guilt to restore your fortunes. They have seen for you oracles that were empty and misleading.

**15** All who pass along the road clap their hands at you. They hiss and shake their heads at Daughter Jerusalem: "Is this the city that was called the perfection of beauty, the joy of all the earth?"[^17]

**16** All your enemies open their mouths against you; they hiss and grind their teeth. They say, "We have swallowed her up! Ah, this is the day we hoped for; we have found it, we have seen it!"

**17** The LORD has done what he planned; he has carried out his word, which he commanded in days of old. He has torn down without pity; he has let the enemy rejoice over you; he has raised up the horn of your foes.

**18** Their heart cried out to the Lord. O wall of Daughter Zion,[^18] let your tears run down like a torrent day and night! Give yourself no relief; do not let your eyes rest.[^19]

**19** Arise, cry out in the night, at the beginning of the night watches! Pour out your heart like water before the face of the Lord. Lift up your hands to him for the lives of your children, who faint with hunger at the head of every street.

**20** Look, LORD, and consider: to whom have you done this?[^20] Should women eat their offspring, the children they have cared for?[^21] Should priest and prophet be killed in the sanctuary of the Lord?

**21** Young and old lie on the ground in the streets; my young women and my young men have fallen by the sword. You have killed them on the day of your anger; you have slaughtered without pity.

**22** You summoned, as for a festival day, my terrors from all around.[^22] On the day of the LORD's anger, no one escaped or survived. Those I cared for and raised, my enemy has destroyed.

[^1]: Hebrew *eikhah*, "How!"; see 1:1. Like chapter 1, this chapter is an acrostic poem: its twenty-two verses begin with the letters of the Hebrew alphabet in order. Here the letter *pe* (verse 16) comes before *ayin* (verse 17), as in chapters 3 and 4. Each verse has three lines, except verse 19, which has four.
[^2]: Hebrew *bat-tsiyyon*; see 1:6.
[^3]: Hebrew *ya'iv*, a word found only here, probably "has covered with a cloud". Or "has treated with contempt".
[^4]: That is, the temple, or the ark of the covenant in it (Psalm 99:5; 132:7; 1 Chronicles 28:2).
[^5]: A picture of strength. Also in verse 17.
[^6]: The Hebrew changes from "her" to "his", both referring to Israel.
[^7]: A play on sounds: *ta'aniyyah va-aniyyah*.
[^8]: That is, the temple. Hebrew *sukko*, "his booth" or "his shelter".
[^9]: Or "his appointed festival". Hebrew *mo'ed*, the word translated "festival" later in the verse.
[^10]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^11]: Literally "from swallowing".
[^12]: Or "the law is no more". Hebrew *torah*, the teaching given by the priests.
[^13]: Literally "My liver". The poet speaks in verses 11–19.
[^14]: Literally "the daughter of my people", a tender way of speaking of the people as a young woman. Also in 3:48; 4:3, 6, 10.
[^15]: Or "What witness can I bring for you?" The meaning of the verb is uncertain.
[^16]: Literally "emptiness and whitewash".
[^17]: Compare Psalm 48:3 (48:2 in most English Bibles) and 50:2.
[^18]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "Their heart cried out to the Lord, wall of Daughter Zion".
[^19]: Literally "do not let the daughter of your eye be still". The "daughter of the eye" is the pupil.
[^20]: Zion speaks in verses 20–22.
[^21]: Hebrew *tippuchim*, a word found only here, "tender care". Compare "cared for" in verse 22.
[^22]: Compare "terror on every side" in Jeremiah 6:25; 20:10.
