**1** In the second year of King Darius, on the first day of the sixth month,[^1] the word of the LORD[^2] came through[^3] Haggai the prophet[^4] to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest:[^5]

**2** This is what the LORD of Armies[^6] says: These people say, "The time has not come, the time for the house of the LORD to be rebuilt."

**3** Then the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet:

**4** Is it a time for you yourselves to live in your paneled houses,[^7] while this house lies in ruins?

**5** Now this is what the LORD of Armies says: Consider your ways.[^8]

**6** You have sown much, but harvested little. You eat, but never have enough; you drink, but never have your fill; you put on clothes, but no one is warm. And whoever earns wages earns them to put into a bag with holes.

**7** This is what the LORD of Armies says: Consider your ways.

**8** Go up into the hills, bring wood and build the house, so that I may take pleasure in it and be honored,[^9] says the LORD.

**9** You looked for much, but see, it came to little. When you brought it home, I blew it away. Why? declares the LORD of Armies.[^10] Because of my house, which lies in ruins, while each of you runs off to his own house.

**10** Therefore, because of you, the heavens have held back the dew, and the earth has held back its produce.

**11** I have called for a drought[^11] on the land and on the hills, on the grain, the new wine and the olive oil, on what the ground produces, on human beings and animals, and on all the work of your hands.

**12** Then Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, and Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and all the rest of the people[^12] obeyed the voice of the LORD their God and the words of Haggai the prophet, because the LORD their God had sent him. And the people feared the LORD.

**13** Then Haggai, the LORD's messenger,[^13] gave the LORD's message to the people: "I am with you, declares the LORD."

**14** So the LORD stirred up the spirit of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and the spirit of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of all the rest of the people. They came and began work on the house of the LORD of Armies, their God,

**15** on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month, in the second year of King Darius.[^14]

[^1]: Darius I, king of Persia (522–486 BC). The date is 29 August 520 BC. The months are counted from the spring, so the sixth month fell in August and September.
[^2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3]: Literally "by the hand of". Also in verses 3 and 13 and in 2:1.
[^4]: Haggai and Zechariah urged the people of Judah to rebuild the temple (Ezra 5:1–2; 6:14). Exiles had returned from Babylon after 538 BC and laid the foundation of the temple, but the work had stopped (Ezra 3:8–13; 4:24). The name *Haggai* probably means "born on a festival".
[^5]: Zerubbabel was a grandson of King Jehoiachin (1 Chronicles 3:17–19). He was governor of the Persian province of Judah. Joshua, called Jeshua in Ezra 3:2, was the grandson of the last high priest before the exile (1 Chronicles 5:40–41, 6:14–15 in most English Bibles).
[^6]: Hebrew *YHWH tseva'ot*, traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel. The title is used often in Haggai, Zechariah and Malachi.
[^7]: Hebrew *sefunim*, "covered", that is, roofed or paneled with wood, as were the royal buildings (1 Kings 7:3, 7).
[^8]: Literally "Set your heart on your ways". Also in verse 7.
[^9]: Or "glorified".
[^10]: Hebrew *ne'um YHWH tseva'ot*, "the declaration of the LORD of Armies", a formula that marks a prophet's words as the LORD's own solemn statement. *Declares the LORD* is also in verse 13.
[^11]: Hebrew *chorev*, "drought", which sounds like *charev*, "in ruins" (verses 4 and 9).
[^12]: Literally "all the remnant of the people", those who had come back from the exile. Also in verse 14.
[^13]: Hebrew *mal'akh*, "messenger", the word also used for an angel. Compare Malachi 3:1.
[^14]: 21 September 520 BC.
