**1** Since Christ suffered in the flesh,[^1] arm yourselves also with the same way of thinking, because whoever has suffered in the flesh has finished with sin,[^2]

**2** so as to live the rest of your time in the flesh no longer for human desires but for the will of God.

**3** For you have spent enough time in the past doing what the Gentiles like to do, living in debauchery, lust, drunkenness, carousing, drinking parties and lawless idolatry.

**4** They are surprised that you no longer join them in the same flood of wild living, and they slander you.[^3]

**5** But they will give an account to the one who is ready to judge the living and the dead.

**6** This is why the good news was proclaimed even to the dead,[^4] so that, though they were judged in the flesh as human beings are, they might live in the spirit as God does.[^5]

**7** The end of all things is near. So be self-controlled and sober-minded for the sake of your prayers.

**8** Above all, keep your love for one another constant, because love covers a multitude of sins.[^6]

**9** Be hospitable to one another without complaining.

**10** As each of you has received a gift,[^7] use it to serve one another, as good stewards of the grace of God in its many forms.

**11** Whoever speaks should do so as one speaking the very words of God.[^8] Whoever serves should do so with the strength God supplies, so that in all things God may be glorified through Jesus Christ. To him belong glory and power forever and ever.[^9] Amen.

**12** Dear friends,[^10] do not be surprised at the fiery ordeal that has come upon you to test you, as though something strange were happening to you.

**13** But rejoice insofar as you share in the sufferings of Christ, so that you may also rejoice and be glad when his glory is revealed.

**14** If you are insulted for the name of Christ, you are blessed, because the Spirit of glory, the Spirit of God, rests on you.[^11]

**15** None of you should suffer as a murderer, a thief, a criminal or a meddler.[^12]

**16** But if anyone suffers as a Christian,[^13] they should not be ashamed but should glorify God in this name.[^14]

**17** For it is time for judgment to begin with the household of God.[^15] And if it begins with us, what will be the end of those who do not obey the good news of God?

**18** And if the righteous is scarcely saved, what will become of the ungodly and the sinner?[^16]

**19** So then, those who suffer according to God's will should entrust their souls to a faithful Creator while continuing to do good.

[^1]: Many manuscripts add "for us".
[^2]: Or "has ceased from sin". The meaning is debated: whoever suffers in this way has broken with sin; or Christ, having suffered in the flesh, is done with sin.
[^3]: Or "and they blaspheme".
[^4]: *The dead* may be those who heard the good news while they were alive and have since died, or the dead in the realm of the dead (compare 3:19).
[^5]: Literally "according to human beings … according to God". *In the spirit*: or "by the Spirit" (compare 3:18).
[^6]: Compare Proverbs 10:12, "love covers all offenses", and James 5:20.
[^7]: Greek *charisma*, a gift of God's grace (*charis*).
[^8]: Literally "as oracles of God".
[^9]: *To him* may refer to God or to Jesus Christ.
[^10]: Literally "Beloved".
[^11]: Literally "the (Spirit) of glory and the Spirit of God". Compare Isaiah 11:2, "The Spirit of Yahweh will rest on him". Many manuscripts add "On their part he is blasphemed, but on your part he is glorified."
[^12]: Greek *allotriepiskopos*, a rare word, perhaps "one who meddles in other people's affairs", or "one who misuses what belongs to others".
[^13]: Greek *Christianos*, "a follower of Christ", a name first given to the disciples by others (Acts 11:26; 26:28).
[^14]: Many manuscripts read "in this matter".
[^15]: Compare Ezekiel 9:6, "Begin at my sanctuary".
[^16]: Proverbs 11:31, as in the Septuagint. The Hebrew reads "If the righteous are repaid on earth, how much more the wicked and the sinner!"
