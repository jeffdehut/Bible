**1** Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to God's chosen people who live as foreigners[^1] in the Dispersion,[^2] in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia and Bithynia,[^3]

**2** chosen according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, through the Spirit's work of making you holy, so that you may obey and be sprinkled with the blood of Jesus Christ:[^4] May grace and peace be yours in abundance.[^5]

**3** Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! In his great mercy he has given us new birth into a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,

**4** and into an inheritance that can never perish, spoil or fade. It is kept in heaven for you,

**5** who through faith are guarded by God's power for a salvation that is ready to be revealed in the last time.

**6** In this you rejoice,[^6] though now for a little while, if it must be, you have been grieved by trials of many kinds,

**7** so that the genuineness of your faith, more precious than gold, which perishes even though it is tested by fire, may be found to result in praise and glory and honor when Jesus Christ is revealed.

**8** Though you have not seen him,[^7] you love him. Though you do not see him now, you believe in him and rejoice with a joy that is beyond words and full of glory,

**9** for you are receiving the goal of your faith, the salvation of your souls.

**10** Concerning this salvation, the prophets who prophesied about the grace that was to be yours searched and inquired carefully.

**11** They tried to find out what person or what time[^8] the Spirit of Christ[^9] within them was pointing to when it testified in advance to the sufferings destined for Christ and the glories that would follow.

**12** It was revealed to them that they were serving not themselves but you in the things that have now been announced to you by those who brought you the good news through the Holy Spirit sent from heaven. These are things that angels long to look into.

**13** So prepare your minds for action,[^10] keep sober, and set your hope completely[^11] on the grace that will be brought to you when Jesus Christ is revealed.

**14** As obedient children,[^12] do not be shaped by the desires you had in the past, in your ignorance.

**15** But just as the one who called you is holy, you too must be holy in all your conduct,

**16** for it is written, "You shall be holy, because I am holy."[^13]

**17** If you call on as Father the one who judges each person's work impartially, live in reverent fear during the time of your stay as foreigners.[^14]

**18** For you know that it was not with perishable things like silver or gold that you were ransomed from the empty way of life handed down from your ancestors,

**19** but with the precious blood of Christ, like that of a lamb without defect or blemish.[^15]

**20** He was foreknown before the foundation of the world, but has been revealed at the end of the times for your sake.

**21** Through him you believe in God, who raised him from the dead and gave him glory, so that your faith and hope are in God.

**22** Now that you have purified your souls by obeying the truth,[^16] so as to have sincere love for one another as brothers and sisters,[^17] love one another earnestly from the heart.[^18]

**23** For you have been born anew, not from perishable seed but from imperishable, through the living and enduring word of God.[^19]

**24** For "All people are like grass,[^20] and all their glory[^21] like the flower of grass. The grass withers, and the flower falls,

**25** but the word of the Lord endures forever."[^22] And this is the word that was proclaimed to you as good news.

[^1]: Greek *parepidemoi*, people staying for a time in a land not their own (also 2:11; compare Genesis 23:4).
[^2]: Greek *diaspora*, "scattering", the usual term for the Jews living outside the land of Israel, here used of the believers (compare James 1:1).
[^3]: Roman provinces in Asia Minor, in what is now Turkey.
[^4]: Literally "for obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ". Compare Exodus 24:7–8, where the people promise to obey and are sprinkled with the blood of the covenant.
[^5]: Literally "Grace to you and peace be multiplied".
[^6]: Or "Rejoice in this", a command. *In this*: or "In him", God (verse 3) or Christ.
[^7]: Many manuscripts read "Though you have not known him".
[^8]: Or "what time or what kind of time".
[^9]: Greek *Christos*, "the Anointed One", "the Messiah"; also later in the verse.
[^10]: Literally "girding up the loins of your mind", tucking up the long robe into the belt so as to be ready for work or travel (Exodus 12:11).
[^11]: Or "keep fully sober, and set your hope".
[^12]: Literally "children of obedience".
[^13]: Leviticus 11:44–45; 19:2; 20:7.
[^14]: Greek *paroikia*, the time of living as resident foreigners (compare 2:11).
[^15]: The Passover lamb had to be "without any defect" (Exodus 12:5), as did animals offered in sacrifice (Leviticus 22:18–20). *Without defect*: Greek *amomos* (compare Hebrews 9:14).
[^16]: Many manuscripts add "through the Spirit".
[^17]: Greek *philadelphia*, "love for brothers (and sisters)", the believers, men and women alike.
[^18]: Many manuscripts read "from a pure heart".
[^19]: Or "through the word of the living and enduring God". Many manuscripts add "forever".
[^20]: Literally "all flesh". Isaiah 40:6–8, quoted in verses 24–25 from the Septuagint. The Hebrew reads "All people are grass, and all their faithfulness is like the flower of the field", and repeats "The grass withers, the flower fades".
[^21]: Many manuscripts read "all human glory".
[^22]: Isaiah reads "the word of our God will stand forever". In verse 25 Peter applies "the word" to the good news.
