    The Web of Scripture

    Every chapter of the Bible, every person and place it names, and the events of its history, drawn as one web.

    • Threads between chapters are cross-references from OpenBible.info: for each chapter, the ten other chapters it shares the most-voted references with.
    • People and places are linked to each chapter that names them, to each other when two are named together in at least two verses, and to parents and children. Names and family links are from STEPBible's TIPNR.
    • Events, with their dates, are from Theographic, after Ussher's chronology as refined by Floyd Nolen Jones.

    In Web, linked things are pulled together and everything else pushed apart, so the books that quote each other gather, and people cluster round the chapters that tell their stories. In Wheel, the chapters stand in order round a circle from Genesis, at the top, to Revelation. The arrangement is worked out when the site is built (scripts/graph.py).

    Chapters are colored by section, people are pale points, places are rings and events are diamonds. Sources: OpenBible.info cross-references (CC BY), STEPBible TIPNR (CC BY 4.0), Theographic (CC BY-SA 4.0).
