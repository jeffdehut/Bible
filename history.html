Streams of Time

History from the death and resurrection of Jesus, about AD 30, to today: the empires, churches, scriptures and thinkers of every continent, side by side. After Sebastian Adams’ Synchronological Chart of 1871, with modern dates throughout.

Drag the chart or the window in the strip below to move through time. Pinch, or hold Ctrl and scroll, to zoom.

Sources and method. The rows are curated in this project’s data/ tables (periods, rulers, events and lives), using the conventional dates of standard references, with Wikidata identifiers wherever an item’s English label matched exactly. Ancient and early medieval dates are often approximate and are marked so.

The Bible lane shows events the Bible narrates that outside evidence dates: modern dates, with Ussher’s date in each item’s details. The chart follows the plan of Sebastian C. Adams’ Synchronological Chart, or Map of History (1871). Part of the Plain English Bible, built from the project’s data by scripts/connections.py.
