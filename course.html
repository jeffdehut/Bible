The Complete Bible Story

The Bible tells one complete story.

The major stories and themes of the whole Bible, from Genesis to Revelation, in three years: twelve quarters of thirteen weeks, each quarter one part of the story. Each week is one lesson; the thirteenth week of each quarter is kept for review, catching up, or a missed Sunday.
