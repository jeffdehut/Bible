# Bible maps: sources for bibledata.org (research notes, 2026-10-30)

Scope: (A) ready-made map images we can include as-is, (B) open data to draw new sheets ourselves,
(C) a prioritised list of new map subjects. Licences were checked against the source pages, the
Internet Archive metadata API, or the Commons file wikitext (`action=raw`). Links marked "200" were
fetched and resolved during this research. Where I could not verify something it says so.

Verdict legend: **OK** = fine for a free public website with attribution. **SA** = OK but
share-alike (derivatives must carry the same licence). **NC** = NonCommercial: avoid or ask.
**?** = unclear or unverified.

---

## A. Ready-made map images

### A1. George Adam Smith & J. G. Bartholomew, *Atlas of the Historical Geography of the Holy Land* (London: Hodder & Stoughton, 1915). **OK (public domain)**

The best single source. It has 57 colour maps on 60 leaves, with hypsometric tints and Bartholomew engraving.
- **Copyright:** published 1915, so it is PD in the US. Smith died 1942 and Bartholomew 1920, so it is also PD in life+70 countries.
  - Columbia scan, IA metadata: "Digital version available with no restrictions. Unrestricted online access".
  - Commons tags: `{{PD-US-expired}}` (IA-derived files) and the CC Public Domain Mark (NLI-derived files).
- **High-res files:**
  - Columbia, 600 ppi, 116 images, 253 MB PDF and 240 MB JP2 zip: https://archive.org/details/ldpd_7032971_000 (200). Best master.
  - Boston Public Library, 350 ppi, 182 images: https://archive.org/details/atlasofhistorica00smit (200).
  - Commons category with 66 files: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Atlas_of_the_Historical_Geography_of_the_Holy_Land
    - "(Smith, 1915)" files come from IA.
    - "John Bartholomew & Co., … (FL…)" files come from the National Library of Israel (NLI), Eran Laor collection, roughly 2,400×3,900 px.
- **Plates, from the OCR'd list of plates:**
  - Part I, Bible lands:
    - 1 The Semitic World
    - 2 Western Asia before 1400 BC
    - 3–4 Empires of the Ancient World
    - 5 Western Asia 4th–2nd c. BC
    - 6 The World and its Races according to the OT (Table of Nations)
    - 7–8 Egypt and Sinai Peninsula
  - Part II, general maps of Palestine:
    - 9 Ancient trade routes to Palestine
    - 10 Economic map
    - 11–12 Orographical map with roads
    - 13 Geology
    - 14 Vegetation
    - 15–30 Palestine at ¼ in to the mile in 8 sections (Phoenicia & Lebanon; Anti-Lebanon & Damascus; Galilee; Gilead & Hauran; Samaria & Judaea; South Gilead & Ammon; Beersheba & Negeb; Moab & Dead Sea)
  - Part III, Palestine at particular periods:
    - 31 Before the coming of Israel (1500–1250 BC)
    - 32 Israel's settlement and the Judges
    - 33 Time of Saul
    - 34 David and Solomon
    - 35 Times of Elijah and Elisha
    - 36 From 720 BC to the exile of Judah

- It also has about 12 pages of "Notes to the Maps" with sources. These are useful as captions, and are also PD.

### A2. Jesse Lyman Hurlbut, *Bible Atlas: A Manual of Biblical Geography and History* (Rand McNally, 1884, rev. 1899 and 1910). **OK (public domain)**

- **Copyright:** the latest edition's copyright is 1910, so it is PD in the US. Hurlbut died 1930, so it is also PD under life+70.
  - IA records `bibleatlasmanual00hurl` as `possible-copyright-status: NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT`.
  - Commons NLI copies are tagged `{{PD-old-assumed}}`.
- **Files:**
  - 1899 edition, BYU, 350 ppi: https://archive.org/details/bibleatlasmanual1899hurl
  - 1910 edition, UC, 300 ppi: https://archive.org/details/bibleatlasmanual00hurl
  - Project Gutenberg #41140, 1910 text with clickable larger map images: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/41140. The text and images are PD. Only the "Project Gutenberg" trademark terms apply if we redistribute PG's files under its name, so it is simpler to take the images from IA.
  - Some plates are scanned at NLI and on Commons, e.g. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jesse_Lyman_Hurlbut,_Journeys_of_the_Patriarchs_(FL36567326_3908839).jpg (200).
- **About 65 maps.** They are small, two-colour, textbook style, and mostly half-page, so the resolution is modest. Subjects in the 1910 list:
  - Ancient world and descendants of Noah; OT world; physical Palestine; journeys of the patriarchs; empire of Chedorlaomer; Palestine before the conquest
  - Lands of the sojourn and wandering; Egypt under Rameses II; Sinai; Kadesh-barnea; Beth-horon
  - Conquest of Canaan; Palestine as promised and possessed; Palestine among the tribes; Palestine under the Judges; plain of Esdraelon
  - Kingdom of Saul; Saul's last battle; empire of David and Solomon; Oriental world in David's time; division of Solomon's empire
  - Syrian period 884–840 BC; restoration of Israel 800 BC
  - Assyrian, Babylonian and Persian empires; Nineveh; Palestine under the Maccabees; division of Alexander's empire
  - Roman Empire in NT times; kingdom of Herod; Palestine in Jesus' ministry; the two provinces AD 44–70
  - Seven "periods of the life of Christ" maps: preparation, inauguration, early Galilean ministry, later Galilean ministry, retirement, Judaean ministry, Peraean ministry, and passion
  - Acts and Paul: early apostolic history; Philip's journey; Peter's journey; Saul and Barnabas; Damascus; Paul's 1st, 2nd and 3rd journeys; Cyprus; Corinth; the voyage to Rome; St Paul's Bay, Malta; Paul's last journeys
  - Isles of Greece and the Seven Churches; Patmos; ancient and modern Jerusalem; environs of Jerusalem

- **Classical plates useful for context:**

  - 10–11 Successors of Alexander
  - 12–13 Roman Empire
  - 45–49 Asia Minor
  - 54 Aegyptus

### A4. Townsend MacCoun, *The Holy Land in Geography and in History* (1899), and *Bible Atlas (non-sectarian)* (1912). **OK (public domain)**

- **1899 edition on Commons:** British Library scans via Flickr, 148 files, tagged `{{PD-old-70-1923}}`. Category: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:The_Holy_Land_in_Geography_and_in_History_(1899)_by_MACCOUN
- **1912 Bible Atlas:** BPL Leventhal, 500 ppi: https://archive.org/details/bibleatlasnonsec00macc
- MacCoun died 1932, so it is PD under life+70 too.
- **Subjects:** a long run of dated period maps, including:
  - Garden of Eden; dispersion of the race; Mesopotamia and Chaldea c.2300 BC; Canaan in the time of the patriarchs; Abram in Canaan; Abraham and Isaac; Joseph in Egypt
  - Lower Egypt; Elim to Rephidim; Rephidim to Sinai; Desert of the Wanderings; Mt Hor to the Arnon; Canaan at the death of Moses; crossing the Jordan; Gilgal to Beth-horon; Joshua's campaigns
  - Israel under the Judges; kingdom of David and Solomon; division of the kingdom (930 BC); Syrian conquest (830 BC); Assyrian conquest of Syria; fall of Israel (722 BC); Sennacherib invades Judah (701 BC)
  - Persian period; Alexander; Pompey; kingdom of Herod; the tetrarchy; Palestine in the time of Christ; Paul's 1st and 2nd tours
  - Many Jerusalem plans: Aelia Capitolina, Byzantine, Muslim, Crusader, and the Haram
- These are good "single-episode" maps.

    - 09 Assyrian Empire c.700 BC
    - 10 Babylonian Empire c.600 BC
    - 11 Persian Empire c.450 BC
    - 13 Ministry of Jesus
    - 14 Jesus' final week in Jerusalem
    - 15 Early Spread of Christianity
    - 16–17 Paul's missionary journeys

### A7. National Library of Israel, Eran Laor Cartographic Collection (via Commons). **OK (PD Mark), per item**

- The NLI released hundreds of hi-res scans to Commons ("FL…" filenames), including much of Smith 1915 and some Hurlbut and Rand McNally plates. Commons tags them with the CC Public Domain Mark / `{{PD-old-assumed}}`.
- NLI item pages carry their own "Terms of use" field. Search snippets show "You may copy and use the item for any purpose", but some items differ, so check each one. nli.org.il returns 403 to automated fetches, so I could not quote one directly.

### A8. Library of Congress (incl. World Digital Library). **OK**

- Example: https://www.loc.gov/item/2021668400/ (Ortelius, *Journey and Life of the Patriarch Abraham*, 1590).
  - JSON rights field: "The Library of Congress is unaware of any copyright or other restrictions in the World Digital Library Collection. Absent any such restrictions, these materials are free to use and reuse."

- LoC holds mostly early-modern decorative Bible maps, such as the twelve tribes (1719), the apostles' travels (1642) and Jerusalem (1756). They are lovely as illustrations but less useful as study maps.

### A10. Sources to AVOID (licence problems)

- **David Rumsey Map Collection: NC.**
  - davidrumsey.com says: "Images from this web site and database may be reproduced or transmitted, but not for commercial use." The linked licence is CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.
  - The `dr_…` items it mirrors on IA say: "Images may be downloaded and used following Creative Commons CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 license… Please contact the David Rumsey Map Collection for commercial use."
  - The underlying maps are PD, so get the same atlas from another scan (as for Smith & Grove above).
- **AWMC wall maps: NC, and the sources conflict.**
  - https://awmc.unc.edu/wall-maps/ says: "Rights have reverted to the Center, and we are pleased to make them available under the CC-BY-NC 4.0 License". The seven maps include *Egypt and the Near East 3000–1200 BCE*, *1200–500 BCE*, and *The World of the New Testament and the Journeys of Paul*.
  - The Commons upload (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_World_of_the_New_Testament_and_the_Journeys_of_Paul.pdf) is tagged CC BY-SA 4.0 by "Awmcstaff".
  - Treat it as NC unless AWMC confirms the Commons licence in writing.
  - AWMC's *GIS data* is a separate matter (ODbL, see B).
- **Bible Mapper (biblemapper.com) published maps: NC.** freely-given.org quotes "copyright © by David P. Barrett … welcome to use these maps for any non-commercial purposes", and FreeBibleImages lists them as CC BY-NC 4.0. Maps you *make yourself* with the Bible Mapper program are said to be unrestricted. I could not verify the current biblemapper.com wording.
- **FreeBibleImages.org:** adds a content restriction on top of the CC terms. Get Biblica maps from Commons instead.
- **Rand McNally / Kraeling *Bible Atlas* (1956, 1962) and Rand McNally *Historical Atlas of the Holy Land* (1938/1959):** still in copyright. These are borrow-only on IA.
- **Eilers Sunday-school wall maps (A. H. Eilers & Co., St Louis, c.1890–1930):**
  - I found no public scan. Only dealer listings turned up, e.g. curtiswrightmaps.com.
  - Pre-1929 sheets would be PD in the US, but there are no high-res files to use, so carry on drawing in their style.
- **biblicalstudies.org.uk:** returns 403 to automated fetches, so I could not verify it. Its PDFs carry the notice "Unless it is in the public domain, it may not be sold for profit or hosted on a webserver without the permission of the copyright holder." It is not a reliable source of map images. Go to the original PD atlases above instead.

**What is still missing from open data:** tribal allotment boundaries, the borders of Israel and Judah
and their neighbours by period, and Saul/David/Solomon extents. No open dataset holds these as polygons. Options:
1. Trace them from the PD Smith 1915 plates 32–36. The facts and boundaries in a PD map are free to copy, which gives us a defensible, citable source ("after G. A. Smith, 1915, Map 32").
2. Keep the existing algorithmic `territories()` approach in `scripts/maps.py`, seeded with the cities of Joshua 13–19 from OpenBible/TIPNR.

Avoid tracing the CC BY-SA Commons SVGs unless we accept share-alike.
