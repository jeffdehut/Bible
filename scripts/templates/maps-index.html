The Bible Maps

Eight sheets after the Eilers Sunday School wall maps (St. Louis, about 1890–1930), redrawn from open data. Each zooms on a phone, prints whole on a single page, and follows its journeys stop by stop, every stop named in the verse given. They are style studies and drafts: the borders are approximate and wait for review.

The numbers are those of the Eilers series where a sheet of the same subject is known: No. 1 Palestine, No. 2 Old Testament history, No. 3 the Roman Empire, No. 4 Western Asia, No. 5 the Exodus and No. 7 the kingdoms, with the plan of Ancient Jerusalem. No Eilers No. 6 is known, so that number goes to the empires of the exile.

Places from the OpenBible.info Bible Geocoding Data (CC BY 4.0); elevation from the AWS Terrain Tiles; rivers and lakes from Natural Earth. Names are spelled as in the Open Plain English Translation. Maps CC BY 4.0.
