The Generations

Every family tie the Bible records, from Adam to the apostles. See the whole family of Adam at once, follow the line from Adam to Jesus, or search for anyone to see their parents, children and descendants.

The family of Adam

Everyone descended from Adam whom the Bible names, with the families who married into them. Time runs from left to right, a generation to a column, and the line from Adam to Jesus runs straight through in gold. Pinch or drag to move around, and tap anyone to see their family.

    A chart of the whole family of Adam. With the chart focused, the arrow keys move to a parent, a child or the next person in the same generation.
