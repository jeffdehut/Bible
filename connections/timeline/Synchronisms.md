# Synchronisms

Adams' *Synchronological Chart* (1871) set biblical history side by side with the nations around Israel, dated by Archbishop Ussher's chronology. The events below are those the Bible narrates and outside evidence (Assyrian annals, Babylonian chronicles, inscriptions, Roman historians) also dates. Comparing the two shows where Ussher's chronology, and so Adams' chart, departs from modern dating.

*Negative differences mean Ussher dates the event earlier.* Ussher's dates are the marginal dates of the *Treasury of Scripture Knowledge* as recorded by Theographic. In the divided monarchy they run 30–45 years earlier than modern dates, which count overlapping reigns (co-regencies) from the Assyrian eponym lists. From Josiah on, the two agree within a few years.
