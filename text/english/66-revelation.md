## Chapter 1

*Status: draft*

**1** The revelation[^1-1] of Jesus Christ, which God gave him to show his servants[^1-2] what must soon take place.[^1-3] He made it known[^1-4] by sending his angel to his servant John,

**2** who testified to the word of God and to the testimony of Jesus Christ,[^1-5] everything that he saw.

**3** Blessed are the one who reads aloud[^1-6] the words of this prophecy and those who hear them and keep what is written in it, for the time is near.[^1-7]

**4** John, to the seven churches in Asia:[^1-8] Grace to you and peace from him who is and who was and who is to come,[^1-9] and from the seven spirits[^1-10] who are before his throne,

**5** and from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead and the ruler of the kings of the earth.[^1-11] To him who loves us and has freed us from our sins by his blood,[^1-12]

**6** and has made us a kingdom, priests[^1-13] to his God and Father, to him be the glory and the power forever and ever. Amen.

**7** Look, he is coming with the clouds, and every eye will see him, even those who pierced him, and all the tribes of the earth will mourn over him.[^1-14] Yes, amen.

**8** "I am the Alpha and the Omega,"[^1-15] says the Lord God, "who is and who was and who is to come, the Almighty."[^1-16]

**9** I, John, your brother, who share with you in the suffering[^1-17] and the kingdom and the endurance that are in Jesus,[^1-18] was on the island called Patmos[^1-19] because of the word of God and the testimony of Jesus.

**10** I was in the Spirit[^1-20] on the Lord's day,[^1-21] and I heard behind me a loud voice like a trumpet,

**11** saying, "Write what you see in a scroll,[^1-22] and send it to the seven churches: to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia and Laodicea."

**12** I turned to see whose voice was speaking to me,[^1-23] and when I turned I saw seven golden lampstands,

**13** and in the middle of the lampstands one like a son of man,[^1-24] dressed in a robe that reached to his feet, with a golden sash around his chest.[^1-25]

**14** His head and his hair were white like white wool, like snow, and his eyes were like a flame of fire.[^1-26]

**15** His feet were like fine bronze[^1-27] glowing in a furnace, and his voice was like the sound of many waters.[^1-28]

**16** In his right hand he held seven stars, and out of his mouth came a sharp two-edged sword,[^1-29] and his face was like the sun shining in its full strength.

**17** When I saw him, I fell at his feet as though dead. He placed his right hand on me and said, "Do not be afraid. I am the first and the last,[^1-30]

**18** and the living one. I was dead, and look, I am alive forever and ever,[^1-31] and I hold the keys of death and of Hades.[^1-32]

**19** So write what you have seen, what is, and what is going to take place after this.[^1-33]

**20** As for the mystery of the seven stars that you saw in my right hand, and of the seven golden lampstands: the seven stars are the angels[^1-34] of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are the seven churches.

[^1-1]: Greek *apokalypsis*, "unveiling, revelation", from which comes the book's other name, "the Apocalypse".
[^1-2]: Or "slaves". Greek *douloi*; also *doulos*, "servant", later in the verse.
[^1-3]: Compare Daniel 2:28–29, 45, "what will happen".
[^1-4]: Or "He made it known by signs". Greek *semaino*, "signify, indicate".
[^1-5]: Or "the testimony about Jesus Christ"; also in verse 9.
[^1-6]: Literally "the one who reads", that is, aloud to the gathered church.
[^1-7]: This is the first of seven sayings in Revelation that begin "Blessed" (14:13; 16:15; 19:9; 20:6; 22:7, 14).
[^1-8]: The Roman province of Asia, in the west of what is now Turkey.
[^1-9]: Greek *apo ho on*, "from he who is". After *apo*, "from", Greek grammar requires another form of *ho on* (the genitive), but John keeps the title unchanged, as though it were a name that cannot be altered. "Who was" (*ho en*) is also irregular: the verb "be" has no past participle in Greek, so John uses the past tense with the article. The title recalls Exodus 3:14, where the Greek Old Testament has "I am the one who is" (*ho on*). Many manuscripts read "from God, who is …".
[^1-10]: Or "the sevenfold Spirit". The seven spirits appear again in 3:1; 4:5; 5:6, where they are seven torches burning before the throne and the seven eyes of the Lamb (compare Zechariah 4:2, 10). They have been understood as the Holy Spirit in his fullness, seven being a number of completeness (compare Isaiah 11:2), or as seven angels who stand before God (compare 8:2).
[^1-11]: Psalm 89:28, 38 (89:27, 37 in most English Bibles).
[^1-12]: Or "at the cost of his blood"; literally "in his blood". Many manuscripts read "washed us from our sins in his blood".
[^1-13]: Compare Exodus 19:6, "a kingdom of priests"; also Revelation 5:10.
[^1-14]: Daniel 7:13; Zechariah 12:10–14. *Mourn*: literally "beat their breasts".
[^1-15]: The first and last letters of the Greek alphabet. Compare 21:6; 22:13.
[^1-16]: Greek *pantokrator*, "ruler of all". The Greek Old Testament uses it to translate "of Armies" in the title "Yahweh of Armies", and *Shaddai*, "the Almighty" (as in Job 5:17). "The Lord God, the Almighty" is its rendering of "Yahweh, the God of Armies" in Amos 4:13, where in the Hebrew "the Lord" is God's personal name, Yahweh.
[^1-17]: Or "the tribulation". Greek *thlipsis*, "pressure, affliction, suffering".
[^1-18]: Many manuscripts read "in Christ Jesus" here, and "the testimony of Jesus Christ" later in the verse.
[^1-19]: A small island in the Aegean Sea, off the west coast of what is now Turkey.
[^1-20]: Or "in the spirit", that is, in a state of vision; also in 4:2; 17:3; 21:10.
[^1-21]: Greek *kyriake hemera*, "the day belonging to the Lord", usually understood as Sunday, the first day of the week.
[^1-22]: Greek *biblion*, a book, here in the form of a scroll.
[^1-23]: Literally "to see the voice that was speaking with me".
[^1-24]: That is, one in human form. See Daniel 7:13, and compare Daniel 10:16. The phrase has no article, as in Daniel; contrast "the Son of Man" in the Gospels.
[^1-25]: Compare Daniel 10:5, "a man dressed in linen, with a belt of fine gold … around his waist".
[^1-26]: Daniel 7:9, "the hair of his head like pure wool"; 10:6, "his eyes like flaming torches".
[^1-27]: Greek *chalkolibanon*, a word found only here and in 2:18, probably a fine alloy of copper or bronze. Compare Daniel 10:6, "his arms and his legs like the gleam of polished bronze", and Ezekiel 1:7.
[^1-28]: Ezekiel 43:2, of the glory of God, "Its sound was like the sound of many waters"; compare Ezekiel 1:24.
[^1-29]: Compare Isaiah 49:2, "He made my mouth like a sharp sword", and 11:4.
[^1-30]: Isaiah 44:6; 48:12, where God says, "I am the first and I am the last." Compare also Daniel 10:9–12.
[^1-31]: Many manuscripts add "Amen".
[^1-32]: Greek *Hades*, the realm of the dead, like the Hebrew Sheol.
[^1-33]: Or "So write what you have seen: both what is and what is going to take place after this."
[^1-34]: Or "messengers". Greek *angeloi*, used throughout Revelation for angels; also in chapters 2 and 3.

## Chapter 2

*Status: draft*

**1** "To the angel[^2-1] of the church in Ephesus write: This is what the one who holds the seven stars in his right hand says, the one who walks among the seven golden lampstands:[^2-2]

**2** I know your works, your labor and your endurance, and that you cannot tolerate evil people. You have tested those who call themselves apostles but are not, and you have found them to be false.

**3** You have endurance, and you have borne up for the sake of my name, and you have not grown weary.

**4** But I have this against you: you have abandoned the love you had at first.

**5** So remember how far you have fallen. Repent, and do the works you did at first. If not, I will come to you[^2-3] and remove your lampstand from its place, unless you repent.

**6** But you have this in your favor: you hate the works of the Nicolaitans,[^2-4] which I also hate.

**7** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who conquers[^2-5] I will give the right to eat[^2-6] from the tree of life, which is in the paradise of God.[^2-7]

**8** "To the angel of the church in Smyrna write: This is what the first and the last says, who died and came to life:

**9** I know your suffering[^2-8] and your poverty, yet you are rich. And I know the slander of those who say that they are Jews and are not, but are a synagogue of Satan.[^2-9]

**10** Do not fear what you are about to suffer. Look, the devil is about to throw some of you into prison so that you may be tested, and for ten days you will have suffering. Be faithful until death, and I will give you the crown of life.[^2-10]

**11** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches. The one who conquers will not be harmed at all by the second death.[^2-11]

**12** "To the angel of the church in Pergamum write: This is what the one who has the sharp two-edged sword says:

**13** I know where you live:[^2-12] where Satan's throne is.[^2-13] Yet you hold fast to my name, and you did not deny your faith in me[^2-14] even in the days of Antipas, my witness, my faithful one, who was put to death among you, where Satan lives.

**14** But I have a few things against you: you have some there who hold to the teaching of Balaam, who taught Balak to put a stumbling block before the Israelites, so that they would eat food sacrificed to idols and commit sexual immorality.[^2-15]

**15** In the same way you also have some who hold to the teaching of the Nicolaitans.

**16** So repent. If not, I will come to you soon and make war against them with the sword of my mouth.

**17** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who conquers I will give some of the hidden manna,[^2-16] and I will give them a white stone, with a new name written on the stone that no one knows except the one who receives it.[^2-17]

**18** "To the angel of the church in Thyatira write: This is what the Son of God says, whose eyes are like a flame of fire and whose feet are like fine bronze:[^2-18]

**19** I know your works: your love and faith and service and endurance, and that your last works are greater than the first.

**20** But I have this against you: you tolerate the woman Jezebel,[^2-19] who calls herself a prophet, and who teaches and misleads my servants to commit sexual immorality and eat food sacrificed to idols.

**21** I have given her time to repent, but she refuses to repent of her sexual immorality.

**22** Look, I am throwing her onto a bed,[^2-20] and those who commit adultery with her into great suffering, unless they repent of her works.

**23** And I will strike her children dead.[^2-21] Then all the churches will know that I am the one who searches minds and hearts,[^2-22] and I will give to each of you according to your works.

**24** But to the rest of you in Thyatira, who do not hold this teaching and have not learned what they call 'the deep things of Satan,' I say this: I am not putting any other burden on you.

**25** Only hold fast to what you have until I come.

**26** To the one who conquers and keeps my works until the end, I will give authority over the nations,

**27** and he will rule them with an iron rod, as clay pots are shattered,[^2-23]

**28** just as I have received authority from my Father.[^2-24] And I will give him the morning star.[^2-25]

**29** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

[^2-1]: Or "messenger"; also in verses 8, 12 and 18. See 1:20.
[^2-2]: *This is what … says*: Greek *tade legei*, the Greek Old Testament's rendering of the prophets' "This is what Yahweh says"; also in verses 8, 12 and 18. *Walks among the seven golden lampstands*: see 1:13, 16, 20.
[^2-3]: Many manuscripts add "soon".
[^2-4]: A group known only from this chapter (also verse 15). Later Christian writers connected them with Nicolaus of Antioch (Acts 6:5), but what they taught is not known.
[^2-5]: Greek *nikao*, "conquer, be victorious", the verb translated "overcome" in John 16:33 and 1 John 5:4–5; also in verses 11, 17 and 26.
[^2-6]: Literally "I will give him to eat".
[^2-7]: Genesis 2:9; 3:22–24. *Paradise*: Greek *paradeisos*, "garden, park", the word used in the Greek Old Testament for the garden of Eden. Many manuscripts read "the paradise of my God".
[^2-8]: Many manuscripts read "I know your works, and your suffering". *Suffering*: or "tribulation", as in 1:9; also in verses 10 and 22.
[^2-9]: *Satan*: from the Hebrew *satan*, "adversary, accuser" (compare "the Accuser" in Zechariah 3:1–2); also in verses 13 and 24.
[^2-10]: Greek *stephanos*, a wreath given to a victor, rather than a king's crown (*diadema*, 12:3; 19:12).
[^2-11]: See 20:6, 14; 21:8.
[^2-12]: Many manuscripts read "I know your works and where you live".
[^2-13]: Pergamum was a center of the worship of the Roman emperor and of other gods. What is meant by "Satan's throne" is not certain.
[^2-14]: Literally "my faith".
[^2-15]: Numbers 25:1–3; 31:16. The prophets also use "sexual immorality" (Greek *porneia*) for unfaithfulness to God (Hosea 1–3; Ezekiel 16); also in verses 20–21.
[^2-16]: The food God gave Israel in the wilderness (Exodus 16:31–35). A jar of manna was kept before God in the ark (Exodus 16:33–34; Hebrews 9:4).
[^2-17]: Compare Isaiah 62:2; 65:15. The meaning of the white stone is not certain; such stones were used as tokens of admission and as votes to acquit.
[^2-18]: See 1:14–15.
[^2-19]: The name of the queen of Israel who promoted the worship of Baal (1 Kings 16:31; 18:19; 2 Kings 9:22). Many manuscripts read "your wife Jezebel".
[^2-20]: That is, probably a sickbed.
[^2-21]: Literally "kill with death"; perhaps "kill with plague" (compare 6:8).
[^2-22]: Literally "kidneys and hearts", the seat of the emotions and of thought. Compare Jeremiah 17:10, "I, Yahweh, search the heart and test the mind, to give each one according to his ways"; Psalm 7:10 (7:9 in most English Bibles).
[^2-23]: Psalm 2:8–9, following the Septuagint, "You will shepherd them with a rod of iron". The Hebrew reads "You will break them with a rod of iron; you will smash them like a potter's jar." *Rule*: Greek *poimaino*, "shepherd", and so "rule". Also in 12:5; 19:15.
[^2-24]: Literally "as I also have received from my Father".
[^2-25]: Compare 22:16, where Jesus is "the bright morning star", and Numbers 24:17, "A star will march out of Jacob".

## Chapter 3

*Status: draft*

**1** "To the angel[^3-1] of the church in Sardis write: This is what the one who has the seven spirits of God[^3-2] and the seven stars says: I know your works. You have a reputation for being alive, but you are dead.[^3-3]

**2** Wake up,[^3-4] and strengthen what remains, which was about to die,[^3-5] for I have not found your works complete in the sight of my God.

**3** So remember what you have received and heard. Keep it, and repent. If you do not wake up, I will come like a thief, and you will not know at what hour I will come upon you.

**4** Yet you have a few people[^3-6] in Sardis who have not soiled their clothes, and they will walk with me dressed in white, because they are worthy.

**5** The one who conquers will be dressed like them in white clothes, and I will never erase their name from the book of life.[^3-7] I will acknowledge their name before my Father and before his angels.

**6** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

**7** "To the angel of the church in Philadelphia write: This is what the Holy One, the True One, says, who has the key of David, who opens and no one will shut, and shuts and no one opens:[^3-8]

**8** I know your works. Look, I have set before you an open door, which no one is able to shut. I know that you have little strength, and yet you have kept my word and have not denied my name.

**9** Look, I will give you some of the synagogue of Satan,[^3-9] who say that they are Jews and are not, but are lying: look, I will make them come and bow down[^3-10] at your feet, and they will know that I have loved you.[^3-11]

**10** Because you have kept my word about endurance,[^3-12] I will also keep you from the hour of testing that is about to come on the whole world, to test those who live on the earth.

**11** I am coming soon. Hold fast to what you have, so that no one takes your crown.

**12** The one who conquers I will make a pillar in the temple of my God, and they will never go out of it again. I will write on them the name of my God, and the name of the city of my God, the new Jerusalem, which comes down out of heaven from my God, and my own new name.

**13** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

**14** "To the angel of the church in Laodicea write: This is what the Amen says,[^3-13] the faithful and true witness, the beginning of God's creation:[^3-14]

**15** I know your works: you are neither cold nor hot. I wish you were either cold or hot.

**16** So, because you are lukewarm, and neither hot nor cold, I am about to spit you out of my mouth.

**17** For you say, 'I am rich; I have become wealthy and need nothing.' And you do not realize that you are wretched, pitiful, poor, blind and naked.

**18** I advise you to buy from me gold refined by fire, so that you may be rich; and white clothes to wear, so that the shame of your nakedness may not be seen; and salve to put on your eyes, so that you may see.

**19** Those whom I love, I correct and discipline.[^3-15] So be earnest, and repent.

**20** Look, I am standing at the door and knocking. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in to them and eat with them, and they with me.

**21** To the one who conquers I will give the right to sit with me on my throne,[^3-16] just as I also conquered and sat down with my Father on his throne.

**22** Whoever has ears, let them hear what the Spirit says to the churches."

[^3-1]: Or "messenger"; also in verses 7 and 14. See 1:20.
[^3-2]: See the note on 1:4.
[^3-3]: Literally "You have a name that you are alive, and you are dead."
[^3-4]: Literally "Become watchful"; also in verse 3.
[^3-5]: Many manuscripts read "which you were about to throw away".
[^3-6]: Literally "a few names".
[^3-7]: Compare Exodus 32:32–33; Psalm 69:29 (69:28 in most English Bibles); Daniel 12:1.
[^3-8]: Isaiah 22:22, "What he opens, no one will shut, and what he shuts, no one will open." Many manuscripts read "who opens and no one will shut it except the one who opens, and no one will open".
[^3-9]: Literally "I give from the synagogue of Satan". The sentence breaks off and begins again with "look, I will make them come".
[^3-10]: Greek *proskyneo*, the verb translated "worship" elsewhere in Revelation. Compare Isaiah 60:14, "all who despised you will bow down at the soles of your feet"; also 45:14; 49:23.
[^3-11]: Compare Isaiah 43:4, "and I love you".
[^3-12]: Literally "the word of my endurance". Or "my command to endure".
[^3-13]: Hebrew *amen*, "firm, sure, true". Compare Isaiah 65:16, "the God of truth" (literally "the God of Amen").
[^3-14]: Or "the origin of God's creation", or "the ruler of God's creation". Greek *arche*, "beginning, origin, first place".
[^3-15]: Compare Proverbs 3:12, "for Yahweh corrects the one he loves". *Love*: Greek *phileo*.
[^3-16]: Literally "I will give him to sit with me on my throne".

## Chapter 4

*Status: draft*

**1** After this I looked, and there was a door standing open in heaven. And the first voice, which I had heard speaking with me like a trumpet, said, "Come up here, and I will show you what must take place after this."[^4-1]

**2** At once I was in the Spirit,[^4-2] and there was a throne standing in heaven, with someone seated on the throne.

**3** The One seated there looked like jasper and carnelian,[^4-3] and around the throne was a rainbow that looked like an emerald.[^4-4]

**4** Around the throne were twenty-four thrones, and on the thrones sat twenty-four elders, dressed in white clothes, with golden crowns on their heads.

**5** From the throne came flashes of lightning and rumblings and peals of thunder.[^4-5] Before the throne seven torches of fire were burning, which are the seven spirits of God.[^4-6]

**6** Before the throne there was something like a sea of glass, like crystal.[^4-7] In the middle of the throne and around the throne[^4-8] were four living creatures, full of eyes in front and behind.[^4-9]

**7** The first living creature was like a lion, the second living creature was like an ox,[^4-10] the third living creature had a face like a human face, and the fourth living creature was like an eagle in flight.[^4-11]

**8** The four living creatures each had six wings,[^4-12] and they were full of eyes all around and within. Day and night they never stopped saying,[^4-13] "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty,[^4-14] who was and who is and who is to come."

**9** Whenever the living creatures gave glory and honor and thanks to the One seated on the throne, who lives forever and ever,

**10** the twenty-four elders would fall down before the One seated on the throne and worship him who lives forever and ever, and they would lay their crowns before the throne, saying,[^4-15]

**11** "You are worthy, our Lord and God,[^4-16] to receive the glory and the honor and the power, for you created all things, and by your will they existed and were created."[^4-17]

[^4-1]: The last words, "after this", may instead begin verse 2: "After this, at once I was in the Spirit."
[^4-2]: Or "in the spirit". See 1:10.
[^4-3]: Greek *sardion*, a red stone.
[^4-4]: Compare Ezekiel 1:26–28, where above the dome over the living creatures is "the likeness of a throne", with brightness all around it "like the appearance of the rainbow in the clouds". *Rainbow*: or "halo".
[^4-5]: Literally "lightnings and voices and thunders". Greek *phonai*, "voices, sounds". Compare Exodus 19:16; Ezekiel 1:13.
[^4-6]: See the note on 1:4. Compare Zechariah 4:2, the lampstand with seven lamps.
[^4-7]: Compare Ezekiel 1:22, "a dome, gleaming like awesome crystal".
[^4-8]: Or "In the middle, around the throne", perhaps meaning one on each side of it.
[^4-9]: The four living creatures are drawn from Ezekiel's vision (Ezekiel 1:5–21; 10:12–14), where the rims of the wheels are "full of eyes all around" (1:18), and from the seraphim of Isaiah 6:2–3.
[^4-10]: Or "a calf", "a young bull". Greek *moschos*.
[^4-11]: Compare Ezekiel 1:10.
[^4-12]: As do the seraphim in Isaiah 6:2.
[^4-13]: Literally "They have no rest day and night, saying".
[^4-14]: Isaiah 6:3, "Holy, holy, holy is Yahweh of Armies!" In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, Yahweh. *Almighty*: see 1:8.
[^4-15]: The Greek has future tenses in verses 9–10 ("Whenever the living creatures will give …, the twenty-four elders will fall down …"), describing what happens again and again.
[^4-16]: Many manuscripts read "our Lord and God, the Holy One". The Roman emperor Domitian (AD 81–96) is reported to have had himself called "our lord and god".
[^4-17]: *They existed*: literally "they were".

## Chapter 5

*Status: draft*

**1** Then I saw in the right hand of the One seated on the throne a scroll with writing on the inside and on the back, sealed with seven seals.[^5-1]

**2** And I saw a mighty angel proclaiming in a loud voice, "Who is worthy to open the scroll and break its seals?"

**3** But no one in heaven or on earth or under the earth was able to open the scroll or to look into it.

**4** I wept and wept, because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or to look into it.

**5** Then one of the elders said to me, "Do not weep. Look, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David,[^5-2] has conquered, so that he can open the scroll and its seven seals."

**6** Then I saw, between the throne with the four living creatures and the elders,[^5-3] a Lamb[^5-4] standing, looking as though he had been slaughtered.[^5-5] He had seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven spirits of God sent out into all the earth.[^5-6]

**7** He went and took it[^5-7] from the right hand of the One seated on the throne.

**8** When he had taken the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb. Each of them held a harp and golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of God's holy people.[^5-8]

**9** And they sang a new song:[^5-9] "You are worthy to take the scroll and to open its seals, for you were slaughtered, and by your blood you bought for God people from every tribe and language and people and nation.[^5-10]

**10** You have made them a kingdom and priests to our God, and they reign on the earth."[^5-11]

**11** Then I looked, and I heard the voice of many angels around the throne and the living creatures and the elders. Their number was ten thousand times ten thousand, and thousands upon thousands,[^5-12]

**12** saying in a loud voice, "Worthy is the Lamb who was slaughtered to receive the power and wealth and wisdom and might and honor and glory and blessing!"

**13** And I heard every creature in heaven and on earth and under the earth and on the sea, and everything in them, saying, "To the One seated on the throne and to the Lamb be the blessing and the honor and the glory and the power forever and ever!"[^5-13]

**14** The four living creatures said, "Amen!" And the elders fell down and worshiped.

[^5-1]: Compare Ezekiel 2:9–10, a scroll with "writing on the front and on the back"; and Isaiah 29:11, "the words of a sealed scroll". *Seals*: a document was sealed with wax or clay, impressed with its owner's mark, so that it could not be opened without breaking them.
[^5-2]: Genesis 49:9–10, "Judah is a lion's cub"; Isaiah 11:1, 10, "the root of Jesse", David's father.
[^5-3]: Literally "in the middle of the throne and of the four living creatures, and in the middle of the elders".
[^5-4]: Greek *arnion*, "lamb", the word used for Christ throughout Revelation. John 1:29, 36 has a different word, *amnos*.
[^5-5]: Greek *sphazo*, "slaughter", the word used for killing a sacrificial animal; also in verses 9 and 12. Compare Isaiah 53:7, "like a lamb that is led to the slaughter".
[^5-6]: Zechariah 4:10, "These seven are the eyes of Yahweh, which range over the whole earth". See the note on 1:4.
[^5-7]: The Greek has no object; the scroll is meant.
[^5-8]: Literally "of the holy ones", traditionally "of the saints". Compare Psalm 141:2, "May my prayer be set before you like incense".
