## Chapter 1

*Status: draft*

**1** Adam, Seth, Enosh,[^1-1]

**2** Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared,

**3** Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech,

**4** Noah, Shem, Ham and Japheth.[^1-2]

**5** The sons of Japheth:[^1-3] Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech and Tiras.

**6** The sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, Diphath[^1-4] and Togarmah.

**7** The sons of Javan: Elishah, Tarshish, the Kittim and the Rodanim.[^1-5]

**8** The sons of Ham: Cush, Egypt,[^1-6] Put and Canaan.

**9** The sons of Cush: Seba, Havilah, Sabta,[^1-7] Raamah and Sabteca. The sons of Raamah: Sheba and Dedan.

**10** Cush fathered Nimrod. He was the first on earth to become a mighty warrior.

**11** Egypt fathered the Ludim, the Anamim, the Lehabim, the Naphtuhim,

**12** the Pathrusim, the Casluhim (from whom the Philistines came), and the Caphtorim.

**13** Canaan fathered Sidon, his firstborn, and Heth,[^1-8]

**14** and the Jebusites, the Amorites, the Girgashites,

**15** the Hivites, the Arkites, the Sinites,

**16** the Arvadites, the Zemarites and the Hamathites.

**17** The sons of Shem: Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, Aram, Uz, Hul, Gether and Meshech.[^1-9]

**18** Arpachshad fathered Shelah, and Shelah fathered Eber.

**19** Two sons were born to Eber. The name of one was Peleg,[^1-10] for in his days the earth was divided. His brother's name was Joktan.

**20** Joktan fathered Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah,

**21** Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah,

**22** Ebal,[^1-11] Abimael, Sheba,

**23** Ophir, Havilah and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan.

**24** Shem, Arpachshad, Shelah,[^1-12]

**25** Eber, Peleg, Reu,

**26** Serug, Nahor, Terah,

**27** Abram, that is, Abraham.[^1-13]

**28** The sons of Abraham: Isaac and Ishmael.

**29** These are their generations:[^1-14] Nebaioth, the firstborn of Ishmael, Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,

**30** Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema,

**31** Jetur, Naphish and Kedemah. These were the sons of Ishmael.

**32** The sons of Keturah, Abraham's concubine:[^1-15] she bore Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak and Shuah. The sons of Jokshan: Sheba and Dedan.

**33** The sons of Midian: Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida and Eldaah. All these were the descendants of Keturah.

**34** Abraham fathered Isaac. The sons of Isaac: Esau and Israel.

**35** The sons of Esau:[^1-16] Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam and Korah.

**36** The sons of Eliphaz: Teman, Omar, Zephi,[^1-17] Gatam, Kenaz, Timna and Amalek.[^1-18]

**37** The sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah and Mizzah.

**38** The sons of Seir: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer and Dishan.

**39** The sons of Lotan: Hori and Homam.[^1-19] Lotan's sister was Timna.

**40** The sons of Shobal: Alian, Manahath, Ebal, Shephi and Onam.[^1-20] The sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Anah.

**41** The son[^1-21] of Anah: Dishon. The sons of Dishon: Hamran, Eshban, Ithran and Cheran.[^1-22]

**42** The sons of Ezer: Bilhan, Zaavan and Jaakan.[^1-23] The sons of Dishon: Uz and Aran.[^1-24]

**43** These are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the Israelites: Bela son of Beor, and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

**44** When Bela died, Jobab son of Zerah from Bozrah became king in his place.

**45** When Jobab died, Husham from the land of the Temanites became king in his place.

**46** When Husham died, Hadad son of Bedad, who defeated Midian in the country of Moab, became king in his place. The name of his city was Avith.

**47** When Hadad died, Samlah from Masrekah became king in his place.

**48** When Samlah died, Shaul from Rehoboth on the River[^1-25] became king in his place.

**49** When Shaul died, Baal-hanan son of Achbor became king in his place.

**50** When Baal-hanan died, Hadad became king in his place. The name of his city was Pai, and his wife's name was Mehetabel daughter of Matred, daughter of Me-zahab.[^1-26]

**51** Then Hadad died. The chiefs[^1-27] of Edom were chief Timna, chief Alvah,[^1-28] chief Jetheth,

**52** chief Oholibamah, chief Elah, chief Pinon,

**53** chief Kenaz, chief Teman, chief Mibzar,

**54** chief Magdiel and chief Iram. These were the chiefs of Edom.

[^1-1]: Verses 1–4 list the line from Adam to Noah given in Genesis 5. The Hebrew gives only the names.
[^1-2]: Shem, Ham and Japheth were Noah's sons (Genesis 5:32; 10:1).
[^1-3]: Verses 5–23 follow the list of nations in Genesis 10:2–29, without some of its comments.
[^1-4]: Genesis 10:3 has Riphath. In Hebrew the letters *d* and *r* look very alike.
[^1-5]: Genesis 10:4 has Dodanim.
[^1-6]: Hebrew *Mitsrayim*, the usual Hebrew name for Egypt; also in verse 11.
[^1-7]: Genesis 10:7 has Sabtah.
[^1-8]: The ancestor of the Hittites. Verses 14–16 name peoples, in the Hebrew as singular names: "the Jebusite, the Amorite …".
[^1-9]: Genesis 10:23 lists Uz, Hul, Gether and Mash as the sons of Aram. Here the Hebrew has no "The sons of Aram", and has Meshech for Mash.
[^1-10]: *Peleg* means "division".
[^1-11]: Genesis 10:28 has Obal.
[^1-12]: Verses 24–27 list the line from Shem to Abraham given in Genesis 11:10–26.
[^1-13]: Genesis 17:5.
[^1-14]: Hebrew *toledot*; see Genesis 2:4. Verses 29–31 follow Genesis 25:13–16.
[^1-15]: Hebrew *pilegesh*, a wife of lower status than a principal wife. Verses 32–33 follow Genesis 25:1–4, where Keturah is called Abraham's wife.
[^1-16]: Verses 35–54 follow Genesis 36:4–5 and 10–43.
[^1-17]: Genesis 36:11 has Zepho.
[^1-18]: In Genesis 36:12 Timna is the concubine of Eliphaz and the mother of Amalek.
[^1-19]: Genesis 36:22 has Hemam.
[^1-20]: Genesis 36:23 has Alvan for Alian and Shepho for Shephi.
[^1-21]: Literally "sons".
[^1-22]: Genesis 36:26 has "The sons of Dishan: Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran and Cheran."
[^1-23]: Genesis 36:27 has Akan.
[^1-24]: Genesis 36:28 has Dishan.
[^1-25]: Perhaps the Euphrates, which is often called "the River".
[^1-26]: Genesis 36:39 has Hadar for Hadad and Pau for Pai.
[^1-27]: Hebrew *alluf*, the head of a clan. Some take the word to mean "clan" (Hebrew *eleph*). Also in verses 52–54. The list follows Genesis 36:40–43, which introduces it as the chiefs descended from Esau.
[^1-28]: The Hebrew text is written "Aliah" but read "Alvah", as in Genesis 36:40.

## Chapter 2

*Status: draft*

**1** These are the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun,

**2** Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad and Asher.

**3** The sons of Judah:[^2-1] Er, Onan and Shelah. These three were born to him by the daughter of Shua, the Canaanite woman.[^2-2] Er, Judah's firstborn, was evil in the sight of the LORD,[^2-3] so he put him to death.

**4** His daughter-in-law Tamar bore him Perez and Zerah. Judah had five sons in all.

**5** The sons of Perez: Hezron and Hamul.

**6** The sons of Zerah: Zimri,[^2-4] Ethan, Heman, Calcol and Dara,[^2-5] five in all.

**7** The son[^2-6] of Carmi: Achar,[^2-7] the troubler of Israel, who acted unfaithfully in the matter of what was devoted to destruction.[^2-8]

**8** The son of Ethan: Azariah.

**9** The sons born to Hezron: Jerahmeel, Ram and Chelubai.[^2-9]

**10** Ram fathered Amminadab,[^2-10] and Amminadab fathered Nahshon, the leader of the people of Judah.

**11** Nahshon fathered Salma,[^2-11] and Salma fathered Boaz.

**12** Boaz fathered Obed, and Obed fathered Jesse.

**13** Jesse fathered Eliab his firstborn, Abinadab the second, Shimea[^2-12] the third,

**14** Nethanel the fourth, Raddai the fifth,

**15** Ozem the sixth and David the seventh.[^2-13]

**16** Their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. The sons of Zeruiah: Abishai, Joab and Asahel, three.

**17** Abigail bore Amasa, and the father of Amasa was Jether the Ishmaelite.[^2-14]

**18** Caleb son of Hezron had children by his wife Azubah and by Jerioth.[^2-15] These were her sons: Jesher, Shobab and Ardon.

**19** When Azubah died, Caleb married Ephrath, and she bore him Hur.

**20** Hur fathered Uri, and Uri fathered Bezalel.[^2-16]

**21** Afterward Hezron slept with[^2-17] the daughter of Machir the father of Gilead.[^2-18] He married her when he was sixty years old, and she bore him Segub.

**22** Segub fathered Jair, who had twenty-three towns in the land of Gilead.

**23** But Geshur and Aram took Havvoth-jair[^2-19] from them, with Kenath and its villages,[^2-20] sixty towns. All these were the descendants of Machir the father of Gilead.

**24** After Hezron died in Caleb-ephrathah,[^2-21] Abijah, Hezron's wife, bore him Ashhur the father of Tekoa.

**25** The sons of Jerahmeel, Hezron's firstborn: Ram the firstborn, Bunah, Oren, Ozem and Ahijah.[^2-22]

**26** Jerahmeel had another wife, whose name was Atarah. She was the mother of Onam.

**27** The sons of Ram, Jerahmeel's firstborn: Maaz, Jamin and Eker.

**28** The sons of Onam: Shammai and Jada. The sons of Shammai: Nadab and Abishur.

**29** The name of Abishur's wife was Abihail, and she bore him Ahban and Molid.

**30** The sons of Nadab: Seled and Appaim. Seled died without sons.

**31** The son of Appaim: Ishi. The son of Ishi: Sheshan. The son of Sheshan: Ahlai.[^2-23]

**32** The sons of Jada, Shammai's brother: Jether and Jonathan. Jether died without sons.

**33** The sons of Jonathan: Peleth and Zaza. These were the descendants of Jerahmeel.

**34** Sheshan had no sons, only daughters. Sheshan had an Egyptian servant whose name was Jarha.

**35** Sheshan gave his daughter to his servant Jarha as his wife, and she bore him Attai.

**36** Attai fathered Nathan, and Nathan fathered Zabad.

**37** Zabad fathered Ephlal, and Ephlal fathered Obed.

**38** Obed fathered Jehu, and Jehu fathered Azariah.

**39** Azariah fathered Helez, and Helez fathered Eleasah.

**40** Eleasah fathered Sismai, and Sismai fathered Shallum.

**41** Shallum fathered Jekamiah, and Jekamiah fathered Elishama.

**42** The sons of Caleb, Jerahmeel's brother: Mesha his firstborn, who was the father of Ziph, and the sons of Mareshah the father of Hebron.[^2-24]

**43** The sons of Hebron: Korah, Tappuah, Rekem and Shema.

**44** Shema fathered Raham the father of Jorkeam, and Rekem fathered Shammai.

**45** The son of Shammai was Maon, and Maon was the father of Beth-zur.

**46** Ephah, Caleb's concubine,[^2-25] bore Haran, Moza and Gazez. Haran fathered Gazez.

**47** The sons of Jahdai:[^2-26] Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah and Shaaph.

**48** Maacah, Caleb's concubine, bore Sheber and Tirhanah.

**49** She also bore Shaaph the father of Madmannah, and Sheva the father of Machbenah and the father of Gibea. Caleb's daughter was Achsah.[^2-27]

**50** These were the descendants of Caleb. The sons[^2-28] of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah: Shobal the father of Kiriath-jearim,

**51** Salma the father of Bethlehem, and Hareph the father of Beth-gader.

**52** Shobal the father of Kiriath-jearim had sons: Haroeh, half of the Menuhoth.[^2-29]

**53** The clans of Kiriath-jearim: the Ithrites, the Puthites, the Shumathites and the Mishraites. From these came the Zorathites and the Eshtaolites.

**54** The descendants of Salma: Bethlehem, the Netophathites, Atroth-beth-joab, half of the Manahathites, the Zorites,

**55** and the clans of the scribes[^2-30] who lived at Jabez: the Tirathites, the Shimeathites and the Sucathites. These are the Kenites who came from Hammath, the father of the house of Rechab.[^2-31]

[^2-1]: Verses 3–4 summarize Genesis 38; compare Genesis 46:12 and Numbers 26:19–21.
[^2-2]: Or "by Bath-shua the Canaanite woman". Hebrew *bat-shua*, "daughter of Shua" (Genesis 38:2).
[^2-3]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2-4]: Joshua 7:1 has Zabdi son of Zerah.
[^2-5]: 1 Kings 5:11 (4:31 in most English Bibles) names Ethan the Ezrahite, Heman, Calcol and Darda as famous wise men.
[^2-6]: Literally "sons"; also in verses 8 and 31. Carmi was a descendant of Zerah (Joshua 7:1).
[^2-7]: Called Achan in Joshua 7. *Achar* sounds like the Hebrew *okher*, "troubler", and like Achor, the valley where Achan was put to death (Joshua 7:24–26).
[^2-8]: Hebrew *cherem*, things set apart for the LORD, to be destroyed or given wholly to him (Joshua 6:17–19; 7:1).
[^2-9]: Another form of the name Caleb (verses 18 and 42).
[^2-10]: Verses 10–12 give the line found in Ruth 4:19–22. Nahshon was the leader of Judah in the wilderness (Numbers 1:7; 2:3).
[^2-11]: Ruth 4:20–21 has Salmah and Salmon.
[^2-12]: Called Shammah in 1 Samuel 16:9 and 17:13.
[^2-13]: 1 Samuel 16:10–11 and 17:12 say that Jesse had eight sons.
[^2-14]: 2 Samuel 17:25 calls him Ithra the Israelite.
[^2-15]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "fathered Azubah, a wife, and Jerioth". Jerioth may be a second wife, or another name for Azubah.
[^2-16]: The craftsman who made the tabernacle (Exodus 31:2–5).
[^2-17]: Literally "went in to".
[^2-18]: That is, the ancestor of the Gileadites (Numbers 26:29). In these lists *father* often means the founder or leading man of a clan or town, as in "the father of Tekoa" (verse 24).
[^2-19]: *Havvoth-jair* means "the tent villages of Jair" (Numbers 32:41; Deuteronomy 3:14).
[^2-20]: Literally "its daughters", the smaller settlements around a town.
[^2-21]: The Hebrew is difficult. The Septuagint reads "After Hezron died, Caleb went in to Ephrathah".
[^2-22]: Or "Ozem, by Ahijah". Verse 26 speaks of Jerahmeel's "other wife".
[^2-23]: Verse 34 says that Sheshan had no sons, only daughters.
[^2-24]: The Hebrew seems to be incomplete. Mareshah, Ziph and Hebron are also the names of towns in Judah.
[^2-25]: Hebrew *pilegesh*, a wife of lower status than a principal wife. Also in verse 48.
[^2-26]: The Hebrew does not say how Jahdai is related to the others in the list.
[^2-27]: Joshua 15:16–17 names Achsah as the daughter of Caleb son of Jephunneh.
[^2-28]: Literally "son". The Hebrew can also be read "These were the descendants of Caleb son of Hur", but in verse 19 Hur is Caleb's son.
[^2-29]: The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain. *Haroeh* means "the seer"; compare Reaiah son of Shobal in 4:2. The Menuhoth may be the Manahathites of verse 54.
[^2-30]: Or "the Sopherites", a clan name. Hebrew *sopherim*, "scribes".
[^2-31]: That is, the ancestor of the Rechabites (Jeremiah 35).

## Chapter 3

*Status: draft*

**1** These were the sons of David who were born to him in Hebron.[^3-1] The firstborn was Amnon, by Ahinoam of Jezreel. The second was Daniel,[^3-2] by Abigail of Carmel.

**2** The third was Absalom son of Maacah, the daughter of Talmai king of Geshur. The fourth was Adonijah son of Haggith.

**3** The fifth was Shephatiah, by Abital. The sixth was Ithream, by his wife Eglah.

**4** Six were born to him in Hebron. He reigned there for seven years and six months, and in Jerusalem he reigned for thirty-three years.

**5** These were born to him in Jerusalem:[^3-3] Shimea,[^3-4] Shobab, Nathan and Solomon, four by Bathshua daughter of Ammiel,[^3-5]

**6** and Ibhar, Elishama,[^3-6] Eliphelet,

**7** Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,

**8** Elishama, Eliada and Eliphelet, nine.

**9** All these were David's sons, besides the sons of his concubines.[^3-7] And Tamar was their sister.

**10** Solomon's son was Rehoboam,[^3-8] Abijah[^3-9] his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son,

**11** Joram his son, Ahaziah his son, Joash his son,

**12** Amaziah his son, Azariah[^3-10] his son, Jotham his son,

**13** Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son,

**14** Amon his son, Josiah his son.

**15** The sons of Josiah: the firstborn Johanan, the second Jehoiakim, the third Zedekiah, the fourth Shallum.[^3-11]

**16** The descendants of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah[^3-12] his son, Zedekiah his son.[^3-13]

**17** The sons of Jeconiah the captive:[^3-14] Shealtiel his son,

**18** Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama and Nedabiah.

**19** The sons of Pedaiah: Zerubbabel[^3-15] and Shimei. The sons[^3-16] of Zerubbabel: Meshullam and Hananiah, and Shelomith was their sister,

**20** and Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah and Jushab-hesed, five.

**21** The sons of Hananiah: Pelatiah and Jeshaiah, the sons of Rephaiah, the sons of Arnan, the sons of Obadiah, the sons of Shecaniah.[^3-17]

**22** The son[^3-18] of Shecaniah: Shemaiah. The sons of Shemaiah: Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah and Shaphat, six.[^3-19]

**23** The sons of Neariah: Elioenai, Hizkiah and Azrikam, three.

**24** The sons of Elioenai: Hodaviah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Delaiah and Anani, seven.

## Chapter 4

*Status: draft*

**1** The sons[^4-1] of Judah: Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur and Shobal.

**2** Reaiah son of Shobal fathered Jahath, and Jahath fathered Ahumai and Lahad. These were the clans of the Zorathites.

**3** These were the descendants of the father of Etam:[^4-2] Jezreel, Ishma and Idbash. The name of their sister was Hazzelelponi.

**4** Penuel was the father of Gedor, and Ezer the father of Hushah. These were the sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah, the father of Bethlehem.[^4-3]

**5** Ashhur the father of Tekoa had two wives, Helah and Naarah.

**6** Naarah bore him Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni and Haahashtari. These were the sons of Naarah.

**7** The sons of Helah: Zereth, Zohar[^4-4] and Ethnan.

**8** Koz fathered Anub and Zobebah, and the clans of Aharhel son of Harum.

**9** Jabez was more honored than his brothers. His mother had named him Jabez,[^4-5] saying, "Because I bore him in pain."

**10** Jabez called on the God of Israel, saying, "Oh, that you would bless me[^4-6] and enlarge my territory! May your hand be with me, and keep me from harm, so that it may not bring me pain."[^4-7] And God granted what he asked.

**11** Chelub, Shuhah's brother, fathered Mehir, who was the father of Eshton.

**12** Eshton fathered Beth-rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah the father of Ir-nahash. These were the men of Recah.

**13** The sons of Kenaz: Othniel[^4-8] and Seraiah. The son[^4-9] of Othniel: Hathath.

**14** Meonothai fathered Ophrah, and Seraiah fathered Joab the father of Ge-harashim,[^4-10] so called because they were craftsmen.

**15** The sons of Caleb son of Jephunneh: Iru, Elah and Naam. The son of Elah: Kenaz.[^4-11]

**16** The sons of Jehallelel: Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria and Asarel.

**17** The sons[^4-12] of Ezrah: Jether, Mered, Epher and Jalon. She conceived[^4-13] Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah the father of Eshtemoa.

**18** His Judean wife[^4-14] bore Jered the father of Gedor, Heber the father of Soco, and Jekuthiel the father of Zanoah. These were the sons of Bithiah, Pharaoh's daughter, whom Mered had married.

**19** The sons of the wife of Hodiah, the sister of Naham: the father of Keilah the Garmite, and Eshtemoa the Maacathite.

**20** The sons of Shimon: Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan and Tilon.[^4-15] The sons of Ishi: Zoheth and Ben-zoheth.[^4-16]

**21** The sons of Shelah son of Judah: Er the father of Lecah, Laadah the father of Mareshah, and the clans of the house of linen workers[^4-17] at Beth-ashbea,

**22** and Jokim, the men of Cozeba, and Joash and Saraph, who ruled in Moab, and Jashubi-lehem.[^4-18] These records are ancient.[^4-19]

**23** They were the potters and the inhabitants of Netaim and Gederah.[^4-20] They lived there with the king, in his service.

**24** The sons of Simeon: Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah and Shaul.[^4-21]

**25** Shallum his son, Mibsam his son, Mishma his son.

**26** The descendants of Mishma: Hammuel his son, Zaccur his son, Shimei his son.

**27** Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters, but his brothers did not have many children, and their whole clan did not become as numerous as the people of Judah.

**28** They lived in Beersheba,[^4-22] Moladah, Hazar-shual,

**29** Bilhah, Ezem, Tolad,

**30** Bethuel, Hormah, Ziklag,

**31** Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-biri and Shaaraim.[^4-23] These were their towns until David became king.

**32** Their villages were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen and Ashan, five towns,[^4-24]

**33** with all their villages around these towns as far as Baal.[^4-25] These were their settlements, and they kept their own genealogical record.

**34** Meshobab, Jamlech, Joshah son of Amaziah,

**35** Joel, Jehu son of Joshibiah, son of Seraiah, son of Asiel,

**36** Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, Benaiah,

**37** and Ziza son of Shiphi, son of Allon, son of Jedaiah, son of Shimri, son of Shemaiah.

**38** These, listed by name, were leaders in their clans, and their ancestral families[^4-26] increased greatly.

**39** They went to the entrance of Gedor,[^4-27] to the east side of the valley, to look for pasture for their flocks.

**40** They found rich, good pasture, and the land was broad,[^4-28] quiet and peaceful, for those who had lived there before were descended from Ham.

**21** Here are the divisions of the priests and the Levites for all the service of the house of God. With you in all the work will be every willing person who is skilled in any kind of service, and the officials and all the people will be at your command."

[^28-1]: Hebrew *sarisim*, court officials. The word can also mean "eunuchs".
[^28-2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^28-3]: Compare Psalm 99:5; 132:7.
[^28-4]: Hebrew *nagid*, "leader, ruler".
[^28-5]: Hebrew *nephesh*.
[^28-6]: Hebrew *tavnit*, the word used for the pattern of the tabernacle (Exodus 25:9, 40). Also in verses 12, 18 and 19.
[^28-7]: Hebrew *beit ha-kapporet*, "the house of the atonement cover": the most holy place, where the ark with its cover stood (Exodus 25:17–22).
[^28-8]: Literally "all that was in the spirit with him". Or "all that he had by the Spirit".
[^28-9]: See 9:32.
[^28-10]: Hebrew *mizraqot*, bowls used for splashing the blood of sacrifices against the altar (Exodus 27:3).

## Chapter 29

*Status: draft*

**1** King David said to the whole assembly, "My son Solomon, the one whom God has chosen, is young and inexperienced, and the work is great, for this palace[^29-1] is not for a human being but for the LORD[^29-2] God.

**2** With all my resources I have provided for the house of my God the gold for the things of gold, the silver for the things of silver, the bronze for the things of bronze, the iron for the things of iron and the wood for the things of wood, as well as onyx stones[^29-3] and stones for setting, dark stones and stones of various colors,[^29-4] all kinds of precious stones, and marble in abundance.

**3** Moreover, in my delight in the house of my God, I have a treasure of my own of gold and silver, and I give it to the house of my God, over and above all that I have provided for the holy house:

**4** three thousand talents of gold, of the gold of Ophir,[^29-5] and seven thousand talents of refined silver,[^29-6] for overlaying the walls of the buildings,

**5** gold for the things of gold and silver for the things of silver, and for all the work to be done by craftsmen. Now who will offer willingly, consecrating himself[^29-7] today to the LORD?"

**6** Then the heads of the ancestral families, the leaders of the tribes of Israel, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the officials in charge of the king's work offered willingly.

**7** They gave for the service of the house of God five thousand talents and ten thousand darics[^29-8] of gold, ten thousand talents of silver, eighteen thousand talents of bronze and a hundred thousand talents of iron.

**8** Anyone who had precious stones gave them to the treasury of the house of the LORD, in the care of Jehiel the Gershonite.

**9** The people rejoiced over their willing offerings, for they had given willingly to the LORD with a whole heart. King David also rejoiced greatly.

**10** Then David blessed the LORD in the sight of the whole assembly. David said, "Blessed are you, LORD, the God of our father Israel, from everlasting to everlasting.

**11** Yours, LORD, are the greatness, the power, the glory, the victory[^29-9] and the majesty, for everything in heaven and on earth is yours. Yours, LORD, is the kingdom, and you are exalted as head over all.

**12** Riches and honor come from you, and you rule over all. In your hand are strength and might, and it is in your hand to make great and to give strength to all.

**13** Now, our God, we give thanks to you and praise your glorious name.

**14** But who am I, and who are my people, that we should be able to offer so willingly? For everything comes from you, and we have given you only what comes from your hand.

**15** For we are foreigners and strangers[^29-10] before you, as all our ancestors were. Our days on earth are like a shadow, and there is no hope.[^29-11]

**16** LORD our God, all this abundance that we have provided to build you a house for your holy name comes from your hand, and all of it is yours.

**17** I know, my God, that you test the heart and are pleased with uprightness. In the uprightness of my heart I have freely offered all these things, and now I have seen with joy your people who are present here offering freely to you.

**18** LORD, the God of Abraham, Isaac and Israel, our ancestors, keep this forever in the inclinations of the thoughts of your people's hearts, and direct their hearts toward you.

**19** Give my son Solomon a whole heart to keep your commands, your statutes[^29-12] and your decrees, to do everything, and to build the palace for which I have made preparations."

**20** Then David said to the whole assembly, "Now bless the LORD your God." And the whole assembly blessed the LORD, the God of their ancestors. They bowed low and prostrated themselves before the LORD and the king.

**21** The next day they offered sacrifices to the LORD and burnt offerings to the LORD: a thousand bulls, a thousand rams and a thousand lambs, with their drink offerings, and sacrifices in abundance for all Israel.

**22** They ate and drank before the LORD that day with great joy. They made Solomon son of David king a second time,[^29-13] and they anointed him before the LORD as ruler,[^29-14] and Zadok as priest.

**23** Solomon sat on the throne of the LORD as king in place of his father David. He prospered, and all Israel obeyed him.

**24** All the officials and the mighty warriors, and also all King David's sons, pledged their allegiance[^29-15] to King Solomon.

**25** The LORD made Solomon exceedingly great in the sight of all Israel and gave him royal majesty such as no king over Israel had had before him.

**26** So David son of Jesse reigned over all Israel.

**27** He reigned over Israel for forty years. He reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.[^29-16]

**28** He died at a good old age, full of days, riches and honor, and his son Solomon reigned in his place.

**29** The acts of King David, from first to last, are written in the records of Samuel the seer, the records of Nathan the prophet and the records of Gad the seer,[^29-17]

**30** together with all his reign and his might, and the events that came upon him, upon Israel and upon all the kingdoms of the lands.[^29-18]

[^29-1]: Hebrew *birah*, "citadel, palace", here used of the temple. Also in verse 19.
[^29-2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^29-3]: Hebrew *shoham*, a precious stone that cannot be identified with certainty.
[^29-4]: Hebrew *avnei phukh ve-riqmah*. *Phukh* is antimony, a dark mineral used as eye paint; compare Isaiah 54:11. *Riqmah* means "many-colored" or "embroidered work".
[^29-5]: A land famous for its gold (1 Kings 9:28).
[^29-6]: A talent was about 75 pounds (34 kg), so three thousand talents was about 110 tons (100 metric tons), and seven thousand talents about 260 tons (240 metric tons).
[^29-7]: Literally "to fill his hand", an expression used of ordaining priests (Exodus 28:41).
[^29-8]: Hebrew *adarkonim*, probably darics, Persian gold coins weighing about 1/4 ounce (8.4 grams).
[^29-9]: Or "the splendor". Hebrew *netsach*, "preeminence, lasting glory, victory".
[^29-10]: Hebrew *gerim* and *toshavim*, foreigners and temporary residents without the rights of citizens (Leviticus 25:23).
[^29-11]: Or "nothing that lasts".
[^29-12]: Hebrew *edot*, traditionally "testimonies": the solemn terms of the covenant.
[^29-13]: Compare 23:1. 1 Kings 1:32–40 tells how Solomon was first anointed.
[^29-14]: Hebrew *nagid*, a leader or ruler designated by God.
[^29-15]: Literally "gave the hand under".
[^29-16]: Compare 3:4; 2 Samuel 5:4–5; 1 Kings 2:11.
[^29-17]: Hebrew *divrei*, "words, matters, records", three times. Samuel is called *ro'eh* and Gad *chozeh*; both words mean "seer".
[^29-18]: Literally "and the times that passed over him and over Israel and over all the kingdoms of the lands".
