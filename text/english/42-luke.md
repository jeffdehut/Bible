## Chapter 1

*Status: draft*

**1** Since many have undertaken to compile an account of the events that have been fulfilled[^1-1] among us,

**2** just as they were handed down to us by those who were eyewitnesses from the beginning and became servants of the word,[^1-2]

**3** it seemed good to me as well, having carefully investigated everything from the start,[^1-3] to write an orderly account for you, most excellent Theophilus,[^1-4]

**4** so that you may know the certainty of the things you have been taught.[^1-5]

**5** In the days of Herod king of Judea,[^1-6] there was a priest named Zechariah, who belonged to the priestly division of Abijah.[^1-7] His wife was a descendant of Aaron,[^1-8] and her name was Elizabeth.

**6** Both of them were righteous in the sight of God, living blamelessly by all the commandments and decrees of the Lord.[^1-9]

**7** But they had no child, because Elizabeth was unable to have children, and both of them were well along in years.[^1-10]

**8** Once, when Zechariah was serving as priest before God while his division was on duty,

**9** he was chosen by lot, according to the custom of the priesthood, to go into the sanctuary[^1-11] of the Lord and burn incense.

**10** At the hour of the incense offering, the whole crowd of people was praying outside.

**11** Then an angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing at the right side of the altar of incense.

**12** When Zechariah saw him, he was shaken, and fear came over him.

**13** But the angel said to him, "Don't be afraid, Zechariah, for your prayer has been heard. Your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you are to name him John.

**14** You will have joy and gladness, and many will rejoice at his birth,

**15** for he will be great in the sight of the Lord. He must never drink wine or strong drink,[^1-12] and he will be filled with the Holy Spirit even before he is born.[^1-13]

**16** He will turn many of the people of Israel back to the Lord their God.

**17** And he will go ahead of him[^1-14] in the spirit and power of Elijah, to turn the hearts of fathers to their children[^1-15] and the disobedient to the wisdom of the righteous, to make ready for the Lord a people prepared."

**18** Zechariah said to the angel, "How can I be sure of this?[^1-16] I am an old man, and my wife is well along in years."

**19** The angel answered him, "I am Gabriel.[^1-17] I stand in the presence of God, and I have been sent to speak to you and to bring you this good news.

**20** Look, you will be silent and unable to speak until the day these things happen, because you did not believe my words, which will be fulfilled at their proper time."

**21** Meanwhile the people were waiting for Zechariah, and they wondered why he was staying so long in the sanctuary.

**22** When he came out, he could not speak to them, and they realized that he had seen a vision in the sanctuary. He kept making signs to them, and he remained mute.[^1-18]

**23** When his days of service were completed, he went back to his home.

**24** After those days his wife Elizabeth became pregnant, and for five months she kept herself in seclusion. She said,

**25** "This is what the Lord has done for me in these days, when he looked on me to take away my disgrace among people."

**26** In the sixth month[^1-19] the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a town in Galilee called Nazareth,

**27** to a virgin engaged[^1-20] to a man named Joseph, of the house of David. The virgin's name was Mary.

**28** The angel came to her and said, "Greetings, you who are highly favored![^1-21] The Lord is with you."[^1-22]

**29** She was greatly troubled by his words[^1-23] and wondered what kind of greeting this might be.

**30** The angel said to her, "Don't be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God.

**31** Look, you will become pregnant and give birth to a son, and you are to name him Jesus.[^1-24]

**32** He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give him the throne of his ancestor David,[^1-25]

**33** and he will reign over the house of Jacob forever. His kingdom will never end."

**34** Mary said to the angel, "How will this be, since I have not been with a man?"[^1-26]

**35** The angel answered her, "The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. So the child to be born will be called holy, the Son of God.[^1-27]

**36** And look, your relative Elizabeth has also conceived a son in her old age. She who was said to be unable to have children is now in her sixth month.

**37** For no word from God will be without power."[^1-28]

**38** Mary said, "Here I am, the Lord's servant.[^1-29] Let it happen to me as you have said." Then the angel left her.

**39** In those days Mary got up and went with haste into the hill country, to a town of Judah.

**40** She went into Zechariah's house and greeted Elizabeth.

**41** When Elizabeth heard Mary's greeting, the baby leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.

**42** She cried out with a loud shout, "Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb!

**43** Why has this happened to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?

**44** For as soon as the sound of your greeting reached my ears, the baby in my womb leaped for joy.

**45** Blessed[^1-30] is she who believed that what the Lord has said to her will be fulfilled."[^1-31]

**46** And Mary said: "My soul proclaims the greatness of the Lord,[^1-32]

**47** and my spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior,

**48** for he has looked with favor on the lowliness[^1-33] of his servant. Yes, from now on all generations will call me blessed,

**49** for the Mighty One has done great things for me, and holy is his name.

**50** His mercy is from generation to generation for those who fear him.

**51** He has done mighty deeds with his arm; he has scattered those who are proud in the thoughts of their hearts.

**52** He has brought down rulers from their thrones and lifted up the lowly.

**53** He has filled the hungry with good things and sent the rich away empty.

**54** He has come to the help of his servant Israel, remembering his mercy,

**55** as he promised our ancestors, to Abraham and his descendants forever."[^1-34]

**56** Mary stayed with Elizabeth about three months and then returned to her home.

**57** When the time came for Elizabeth to give birth, she had a son.

**58** Her neighbors and relatives heard that the Lord had shown her his great mercy,[^1-35] and they rejoiced with her.

**59** On the eighth day they came to circumcise the child, and they were going to name him Zechariah, after his father.

**60** But his mother answered, "No! He is to be called John."

**61** They said to her, "None of your relatives has that name."

**62** Then they made signs to his father to find out what he wanted him to be called.

**63** He asked for a writing tablet and wrote, "His name is John." And they were all amazed.

**64** Immediately his mouth was opened and his tongue set free,[^1-36] and he began to speak, praising God.

**65** Fear came over all their neighbors, and throughout the hill country of Judea people talked about all these things.

**66** All who heard of them kept them in mind,[^1-37] asking, "What then will this child become?" For the hand of the Lord was with him.

**67** His father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied:

**68** "Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel,[^1-38] because he has come to help[^1-39] his people and has brought them redemption.

**69** He has raised up a horn of salvation[^1-40] for us in the house of his servant David,

**70** as he spoke through the mouth of his holy prophets long ago:

**71** salvation from our enemies and from the hand of all who hate us,

**72** to show mercy to our ancestors and to remember his holy covenant,

**73** the oath he swore to our father Abraham, to grant us

**74** that, rescued from the hand of enemies, we might serve him without fear

**75** in holiness and righteousness before him all our days.

**76** And you, child, will be called a prophet of the Most High, for you will go before the Lord to prepare his ways,[^1-41]

**77** to give his people the knowledge of salvation through the forgiveness of their sins,

**78** because of the tender mercy[^1-42] of our God, by which the dawn[^1-43] from on high will come to us,[^1-44]

**79** to shine on those who sit in darkness and in the shadow of death,[^1-45] to guide our feet into the way of peace."

**80** The child grew and became strong in spirit.[^1-46] He lived in the wilderness until the day he appeared publicly to Israel.

[^1-3]: Or "having followed everything closely for a long time". Greek *anothen* can mean "from the beginning" or "for a long time".
[^1-4]: *Most excellent* is a respectful title for people of high rank, used of Roman governors in Acts 23:26; 24:3; 26:25. *Theophilus* means "friend of God" or "loved by God".
[^1-5]: Or "informed about".
[^1-6]: Herod the Great, who ruled Judea as king under Rome from 37 to 4 BC.
[^1-7]: The priests were divided into twenty-four divisions, which took turns serving in the temple. Abijah was the eighth (1 Chronicles 24:10).
[^1-8]: Literally "from the daughters of Aaron", that is, from a priestly family.
[^1-9]: Greek *kyrios*. In Luke 1–2, as in the Greek Old Testament, "the Lord" usually means God, standing for God's personal name, YHWH ("the LORD").
[^1-10]: Literally "advanced in their days"; also in verse 18.
[^1-11]: Greek *naos*, the sanctuary building itself, as distinct from the temple courts where the people stood (verse 10); also in verses 21–22.
[^1-12]: Compare Numbers 6:3; Judges 13:4.
[^1-13]: Literally "even from his mother's womb".
[^1-14]: That is, ahead of the Lord their God (verse 16).
[^1-15]: Malachi 3:23–24 (4:5–6 in most English Bibles).
[^1-16]: Literally "By what will I know this?"
[^1-17]: The angel named in Daniel 8:16; 9:21.
[^1-18]: Greek *kophos*, which can mean unable to speak, unable to hear, or both (compare verse 62).
[^1-19]: That is, of Elizabeth's pregnancy (verse 36).
[^1-20]: Or "betrothed". A Jewish engagement was a binding agreement that could be ended only by divorce, but the couple did not yet live together.
[^1-21]: *Greetings*: Greek *chaire*, the usual greeting, literally "Rejoice!" *Highly favored*: or "graced", from *charis*, "grace, favor".
[^1-22]: Many manuscripts add "Blessed are you among women."
[^1-23]: Many manuscripts read "When she saw him, she was greatly troubled at his words".
[^1-24]: Greek *Iesous*, the Greek form of the Hebrew name Joshua, which means "The LORD saves".
[^1-25]: Literally "his father David".
[^1-26]: Literally "since I do not know a man", a Hebrew way of speaking of sexual relations.
[^1-27]: Or "So the holy child to be born will be called the Son of God."
[^1-28]: Or "For nothing will be impossible with God." Greek *rhema* means "word" or "thing". Many manuscripts read "with God" for "from God", giving "For with God nothing will be impossible." Compare Genesis 18:14.
[^1-29]: Or "slave". Greek *doule*, a female servant or slave; also in verse 48.
[^1-30]: Greek *makaria*, "happy, fortunate", a different word from "blessed" in verse 42.
[^1-31]: Or "Blessed is she who believed, for what the Lord has said to her will be fulfilled."
[^1-32]: Traditionally "magnifies the Lord". The song (verses 46–55) is known as the Magnificat, from its first word in Latin. It has many echoes of Hannah's song in 1 Samuel 2:1–10.
[^1-33]: Or "humiliation". Compare 1 Samuel 1:11.
[^1-34]: Literally "as he spoke to our fathers, to Abraham and to his seed forever". The words "to Abraham" may also go with "remembering his mercy" in verse 54: "remembering his mercy to Abraham and his descendants forever, as he promised our ancestors."
[^1-35]: Literally "had magnified his mercy with her".
[^1-36]: The Greek has no verb for the tongue: literally "his mouth was opened, and his tongue".
[^1-37]: Literally "laid them up in their heart".
[^1-38]: Compare Psalm 41:14 (41:13 in most English Bibles); 72:18; 106:48. In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, YHWH ("the LORD"). Zechariah's song (verses 68–79) is known as the Benedictus, from its first word in Latin.
[^1-39]: Literally "visited", in the sense of coming to look after (Genesis 50:24; Exodus 4:31); also in verse 78.
[^1-40]: The horn is a picture of strength: a mighty savior. Compare Psalm 18:3 (18:2 in most English Bibles); 132:17.
[^1-41]: Compare Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3.
[^1-42]: Literally "the inward parts of mercy", the inner organs being thought of as the seat of feelings.
[^1-43]: Greek *anatole*, "rising", used of the sunrise and of a rising star. In the Greek Old Testament it also translates "Branch", a title for the coming king (Jeremiah 23:5; Zechariah 3:8; 6:12).
[^1-44]: Many manuscripts read "has come to us".
[^1-45]: Compare Isaiah 9:1 (9:2 in most English Bibles); Psalm 107:10. "Shadow of death" is the Greek Old Testament's rendering of the Hebrew word translated "deep darkness" in those verses.
[^1-46]: Or "in the Spirit".

## Chapter 2

*Status: draft*

**1** In those days a decree went out from Caesar Augustus[^2-1] that the whole world[^2-2] should be registered.

**2** (This was the first registration, made while Quirinius was governor of Syria.)[^2-3]

**3** Everyone went to be registered, each to his own town.

**4** Joseph also went up from the town of Nazareth in Galilee to Judea, to the town of David called Bethlehem,[^2-4] because he was of the house and family line of David.

**5** He went to be registered with Mary, who was engaged to him[^2-5] and was expecting a child.

**6** While they were there, the time came for her to give birth,

**7** and she gave birth to her firstborn son. She wrapped him in strips of cloth[^2-6] and laid him in a manger,[^2-7] because there was no room for them in the guest room.[^2-8]

**8** In the same region there were shepherds living out in the fields, keeping watch over their flock at night.

**9** An angel of the Lord stood before them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them, and they were terrified.[^2-9]

**10** But the angel said to them, "Don't be afraid. Look, I am bringing you good news of great joy, which will be for all the people.

**11** Today in the town of David a Savior has been born for you. He is the Messiah, the Lord.[^2-10]

**12** This will be the sign for you: you will find a baby wrapped in strips of cloth and lying in a manger."

**13** Suddenly a great company of the heavenly army[^2-11] appeared with the angel, praising God and saying,

**14** "Glory to God in the highest heaven, and on earth peace among people with whom he is pleased!"[^2-12]

**15** When the angels had left them and gone back into heaven, the shepherds said to one another, "Let us go over to Bethlehem and see this thing that has happened, which the Lord has made known to us."

**16** So they hurried off and found Mary and Joseph, and the baby lying in the manger.

**17** When they had seen him, they made known what they had been told about this child.

**18** All who heard it were amazed at what the shepherds told them.

**19** But Mary treasured up all these things, pondering them in her heart.

**20** The shepherds went back, glorifying and praising God for all they had heard and seen, just as they had been told.

**21** When eight days had passed and it was time to circumcise him, he was named Jesus, the name the angel had given him before he was conceived in the womb.

**22** When the days of their purification[^2-13] according to the Law of Moses were completed, they brought him up to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord

**23** (as it is written in the Law of the Lord, "Every male who first opens the womb will be called holy to the Lord"),[^2-14]

**24** and to offer a sacrifice according to what is said in the Law of the Lord: "a pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons."[^2-15]

**25** Now there was a man in Jerusalem named Simeon. This man was righteous and devout, and he was waiting for the comfort of Israel.[^2-16] The Holy Spirit was on him,

**26** and it had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit that he would not see death before he had seen the Lord's Messiah.[^2-17]

**27** Led by the Spirit,[^2-18] he came into the temple. When the parents brought in the child Jesus to do for him what the Law required,

**28** Simeon took him in his arms, praised God, and said:

**29** "Now, Master, you are letting your servant go[^2-19] in peace, as you promised,[^2-20]

**30** for my eyes have seen your salvation,

**31** which you have prepared in the presence of all peoples:

**32** a light for revelation to the Gentiles,[^2-21] and glory for your people Israel."

**33** The child's father and mother were amazed at what was being said about him.[^2-22]

**34** Then Simeon blessed them and said to his mother Mary, "Look, this child is destined to cause the falling and rising of many in Israel, and to be a sign that will be opposed,[^2-23]

**35** and a sword will pierce your own soul too, so that the thoughts of many hearts may be revealed."

**36** There was also a prophet, Anna, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher. She was very old. After her marriage[^2-24] she had lived with her husband seven years,

**37** and then she had been a widow until she was eighty-four.[^2-25] She never left the temple, but worshiped night and day with fasting and prayer.

**38** At that very hour she came up and began to give thanks to God and to speak about the child to all who were waiting for the redemption of Jerusalem.[^2-26]

**39** When they had done everything the Law of the Lord required, they returned to Galilee, to their own town of Nazareth.

**40** The child grew and became strong,[^2-27] filled with wisdom, and the favor[^2-28] of God was on him.

**41** Every year his parents went to Jerusalem for the festival of the Passover.

**42** When he was twelve years old, they went up as usual for the festival.[^2-29]

**43** When the days of the festival were over, as they were returning, the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem, but his parents did not know it.[^2-30]

**44** Thinking he was in their group of travelers, they went a day's journey. Then they began looking for him among their relatives and friends.

**45** When they did not find him, they went back to Jerusalem to search for him.

**46** After three days they found him in the temple, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking them questions.

**47** Everyone who heard him was astonished at his understanding and his answers.

**48** When his parents saw him, they were overwhelmed, and his mother said to him, "Son,[^2-31] why have you treated us like this? Look, your father and I have been searching for you in great distress."

**49** He said to them, "Why were you searching for me? Didn't you know that I had to be in my Father's house?"[^2-32]

**50** But they did not understand what he was saying to them.

**51** Then he went down with them and came to Nazareth, and he was obedient to them. His mother treasured all these things in her heart.

**52** And Jesus grew in wisdom and stature,[^2-33] and in favor with God and people.

[^2-33]: Or "in years". Greek *helikia* means both age and height.

## Chapter 3

*Status: draft*

**1** In the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar,[^3-1] when Pontius Pilate was governor of Judea,[^3-2] Herod was tetrarch[^3-3] of Galilee, his brother Philip tetrarch of the region of Ituraea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias tetrarch of Abilene,

**2** during the high priesthood of Annas and Caiaphas,[^3-4] the word of God came to John son of Zechariah in the wilderness.

**3** He went into all the region around the Jordan, proclaiming a baptism of repentance[^3-5] for the forgiveness of sins,

**4** as it is written in the book of the words of the prophet Isaiah: "A voice is shouting in the wilderness: 'Prepare the way for the Lord; make his paths straight.

**5** Every valley will be filled, and every mountain and hill made low. The crooked ways will become straight, and the rough places smooth roads,

**6** and all people[^3-6] will see the salvation of God.'"[^3-7]

**7** So John said to the crowds who came out to be baptized by him, "You brood of vipers![^3-8] Who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?

**8** Produce fruit, then, that fits your repentance,[^3-9] and don't begin to say to yourselves, 'We have Abraham as our father.' For I tell you, God can raise up children for Abraham from these stones.

**9** Even now the ax is lying at the root of the trees. So every tree that does not produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire."

**10** The crowds asked him, "What then should we do?"

**11** He answered them, "Whoever has two tunics should share with the one who has none, and whoever has food should do the same."

**12** Tax collectors also came to be baptized, and they said to him, "Teacher, what should we do?"

**13** He told them, "Collect no more than you have been ordered to."

**14** Soldiers also asked him, "And what should we do?" He told them, "Don't take money from anyone by force or by false accusation, and be content with your wages."

**15** The people were waiting expectantly, and all of them were wondering in their hearts about John, whether he might be the Messiah.

**16** John answered them all, "I baptize you with water, but someone more powerful than I am is coming. I am not worthy to untie the strap of his sandals. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire.

**17** His winnowing fork[^3-10] is in his hand, to clear his threshing floor and to gather the wheat into his barn, but he will burn up the chaff with fire that never goes out."

**18** So with many other appeals he proclaimed the good news to the people.

**19** But Herod the tetrarch had been rebuked by John because of Herodias, his brother's wife, and because of all the evil things Herod had done.

**20** He added this to them all: he locked John up in prison.

**21** When all the people were being baptized, Jesus also was baptized. And while he was praying, heaven was opened,

**22** and the Holy Spirit came down on him in bodily form like a dove. And a voice came from heaven: "You are my Son, whom I love. I am delighted with you."[^3-11]

**23** Jesus was about thirty years old when he began his work.[^3-12] He was the son, as people supposed,[^3-13] of Joseph, the son of Heli,[^3-14]

**24** the son of Matthat, the son of Levi, the son of Melchi, the son of Jannai, the son of Joseph,

**25** the son of Mattathias, the son of Amos, the son of Nahum, the son of Esli, the son of Naggai,

**26** the son of Maath, the son of Mattathias, the son of Semein, the son of Josech,[^3-15] the son of Joda,

**27** the son of Joanan, the son of Rhesa, the son of Zerubbabel, the son of Shealtiel,[^3-16] the son of Neri,

**28** the son of Melchi, the son of Addi, the son of Cosam, the son of Elmadam, the son of Er,

**29** the son of Joshua,[^3-17] the son of Eliezer, the son of Jorim, the son of Matthat, the son of Levi,

**30** the son of Simeon, the son of Judah, the son of Joseph, the son of Jonam, the son of Eliakim,

**31** the son of Melea, the son of Menna, the son of Mattatha, the son of Nathan,[^3-18] the son of David,

**32** the son of Jesse, the son of Obed,[^3-19] the son of Boaz, the son of Sala,[^3-20] the son of Nahshon,

**33** the son of Amminadab, the son of Admin, the son of Arni,[^3-21] the son of Hezron, the son of Perez, the son of Judah,

**34** the son of Jacob, the son of Isaac, the son of Abraham, the son of Terah, the son of Nahor,

**35** the son of Serug, the son of Reu, the son of Peleg, the son of Eber, the son of Shelah,

**36** the son of Cainan,[^3-22] the son of Arpachshad, the son of Shem, the son of Noah, the son of Lamech,

**37** the son of Methuselah, the son of Enoch, the son of Jared, the son of Mahalalel, the son of Kenan,[^3-23]

**38** the son of Enosh, the son of Seth, the son of Adam, the son of God.

[^3-1]: Tiberius became emperor in AD 14, so his fifteenth year was about AD 28.

**50** Now there was a man named Joseph, a member of the council,[^23-27] a good and righteous man—

**51** he had not agreed with their plan and their action—who came from Arimathea, a town of the Jews, and was waiting for the kingdom of God.

**52** This man went to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus.

**53** Then he took it down, wrapped it in a linen cloth, and laid him in a tomb cut out of the rock, where no one had yet been laid.

**54** It was the day of Preparation, and the Sabbath was about to begin.[^23-28]

**55** The women who had come with Jesus from Galilee followed, and they saw the tomb and how his body was laid.

**56** Then they went back and prepared spices and perfumes. On the Sabbath they rested according to the commandment.[^23-29]

## Chapter 24

**1** But on the first day of the week, very early in the morning, the women came to the tomb, bringing the spices they had prepared.[^24-1]

**2** They found the stone rolled away from the tomb,

**3** but when they went in, they did not find the body.[^24-2]

**4** While they were puzzled about this, suddenly two men in dazzling clothes stood beside them.

**5** The women were terrified and bowed their faces to the ground, but the men said to them, "Why are you looking for the living among the dead?

**6** He is not here, but he has been raised.[^24-3] Remember how he told you, while he was still in Galilee,

**7** that the Son of Man must be handed over into the hands of sinful people, be crucified, and on the third day rise."

**8** Then they remembered his words.

**9** When they came back from the tomb, they told all these things to the Eleven and to all the others.

**10** It was Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and the other women with them who told these things to the apostles.

**11** But these words seemed to them like nonsense, and they did not believe the women.

**12** But Peter got up and ran to the tomb. Stooping down to look in, he saw only the strips of linen,[^24-4] and he went home,[^24-5] amazed at what had happened.[^24-6]

**13** That same day two of them were going to a village called Emmaus, sixty stadia[^24-7] from Jerusalem,

**14** and they were talking with each other about all these things that had happened.

**15** While they were talking and discussing, Jesus himself came near and began to walk with them,

**16** but their eyes were kept from recognizing him.

**17** He said to them, "What are you discussing with each other as you walk along?" They stood still, their faces downcast.[^24-8]

**18** One of them, named Cleopas, answered him, "Are you the only visitor in Jerusalem who does not know the things that have happened there in these days?"

**19** He said to them, "What things?" They said to him, "The things about Jesus of Nazareth, who was a prophet, powerful in deed and word before God and all the people,

**20** and how our chief priests and leaders handed him over to be condemned to death, and crucified him.

**21** But we had hoped that he was the one who was going to redeem Israel. And besides all this, it is now the third day since these things happened.

**22** Moreover, some women of our group astounded us. They went to the tomb early this morning

**23** and did not find his body. They came back and said that they had even seen a vision of angels, who said that he is alive.

**24** Some of those who were with us went to the tomb and found it just as the women had said, but they did not see him."

**25** Then he said to them, "How foolish you are, and how slow of heart to believe all that the prophets have spoken!

**26** Wasn't it necessary for the Messiah to suffer these things and to enter into his glory?"

**27** Then, beginning with Moses and all the Prophets, he explained to them what was said about himself in all the scriptures.

**28** As they came near the village where they were going, he acted as if he were going farther.

**29** But they urged him strongly, saying, "Stay with us, because it is almost evening, and the day is already nearly over." So he went in to stay with them.

**30** While he was reclining at table with them, he took the bread, blessed it, broke it, and gave it to them.

**31** Then their eyes were opened, and they recognized him, and he vanished from their sight.

**32** They said to each other, "Weren't our hearts burning within us while he was talking with us on the road, while he was opening the scriptures to us?"

**33** They got up at that very hour and returned to Jerusalem. There they found the Eleven and those with them gathered together,

**34** who were saying, "The Lord has really been raised, and he has appeared to Simon!"

**35** Then the two told what had happened on the road, and how he had been made known to them in the breaking of the bread.

**36** While they were saying these things, Jesus himself stood among them.[^24-9]

**37** But they were startled and terrified, and they thought they were seeing a ghost.[^24-10]

**38** He said to them, "Why are you troubled, and why do doubts arise in your hearts?

**39** Look at my hands and my feet; it is I myself. Touch me and see, for a ghost does not have flesh and bones, as you see that I have."

**40** [When he had said this, he showed them his hands and his feet.][^24-11]

**41** While they still could not believe it for joy, and were amazed, he said to them, "Do you have anything here to eat?"

**42** They gave him a piece of broiled fish,[^24-12]

**43** and he took it and ate it in front of them.

**44** Then he said to them, "These are my words that I spoke to you while I was still with you: everything written about me in the Law of Moses, the Prophets, and the Psalms must be fulfilled."[^24-13]

**45** Then he opened their minds to understand the scriptures.

**46** He said to them, "This is what is written:[^24-14] the Messiah would suffer and rise from the dead on the third day,

**47** and repentance and forgiveness of sins[^24-15] would be proclaimed in his name to all nations, beginning from Jerusalem.[^24-16]

**48** You are witnesses of these things.

**49** And look, I am sending upon you what my Father promised. But stay in the city until you are clothed with power from on high."

**50** Then he led them out as far as Bethany, and he lifted up his hands and blessed them.

**51** While he was blessing them, he parted from them [and was carried up into heaven].[^24-17]

**52** And they [worshiped him and][^24-18] returned to Jerusalem with great joy,

**53** and they were continually in the temple, blessing God.[^24-19]
