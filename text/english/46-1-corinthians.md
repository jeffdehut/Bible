## Chapter 1

*Status: draft*

**1** Paul, called to be an apostle of Christ Jesus[^1-1] by the will of God, and our brother Sosthenes,[^1-2]

**2** to the church of God in Corinth, to those made holy[^1-3] in Christ Jesus, called to be God's holy people,[^1-4] together with all those in every place who call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ,[^1-5] their Lord and ours:[^1-6]

**3** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**4** I always thank my God for you, because of the grace of God that was given to you in Christ Jesus.

**5** For in him you have been made rich in every way, in all speech[^1-7] and all knowledge,

**6** just as the testimony about Christ[^1-8] was firmly established among you.

**7** So you do not lack any gift[^1-9] as you eagerly wait for the revealing[^1-10] of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**8** He[^1-11] will also keep you firmly established to the end, blameless on the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.

**9** God is faithful, and by him you were called into fellowship[^1-12] with his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

**10** I appeal to you, brothers and sisters,[^1-13] by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that all of you agree with one another,[^1-14] and that there be no divisions among you, but that you be united in the same mind and the same purpose.

**11** For it has been made clear to me about you, my brothers and sisters, by Chloe's people,[^1-15] that there are quarrels among you.

**12** What I mean is this: each of you says, "I belong to Paul,"[^1-16] or "I belong to Apollos," or "I belong to Cephas,"[^1-17] or "I belong to Christ."

**13** Has Christ been divided?[^1-18] Was Paul crucified for you? Or were you baptized in the name[^1-19] of Paul?

**14** I am thankful[^1-20] that I baptized none of you except Crispus and Gaius,[^1-21]

**15** so that no one can say that you were baptized in my name.[^1-22]

**16** I also baptized the household of Stephanas. Beyond that, I do not know whether I baptized anyone else.

**17** For Christ did not send me to baptize but to proclaim the good news, and not with wise and clever speech,[^1-23] so that the cross of Christ would not be emptied of its power.[^1-24]

**18** For the message[^1-25] of the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing, but to us who are being saved it is the power of God.

**19** For it is written: "I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and the understanding of the discerning I will set aside."[^1-26]

**20** Where is the wise person? Where is the scribe?[^1-27] Where is the debater of this age? Hasn't God made the wisdom of the world foolish?

**21** For since, in the wisdom of God, the world did not know God through its wisdom, God was pleased, through the foolishness of what we proclaim,[^1-28] to save those who believe.

**22** For Jews ask for signs and Greeks look for wisdom,

**23** but we proclaim Christ crucified,[^1-29] a stumbling block[^1-30] to Jews and foolishness to Gentiles,[^1-31]

**24** but to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the power of God and the wisdom of God.

**25** For the foolishness of God is wiser than humans are, and the weakness of God is stronger than humans are.

**26** Consider your own calling, brothers and sisters: not many of you were wise by human standards,[^1-32] not many were powerful, not many were of noble birth.

**27** But God chose what is foolish in the world to shame the wise, and God chose what is weak in the world to shame the strong.

**28** God chose what is lowborn[^1-33] in the world and what is despised, things that are not, to bring to nothing[^1-34] things that are,

**29** so that no human being[^1-35] may boast before God.

**30** It is because of him that you are in Christ Jesus, who became for us wisdom from God, and righteousness and holiness and redemption,[^1-36]

**31** so that, as it is written, "Let the one who boasts boast in the Lord."[^1-37]

[^1-1]: *Christ*: Greek *Christos*, "anointed one", the Greek equivalent of "Messiah". In Paul's letters it usually works as a name, so this translation writes "Christ". Paul's order, "Christ Jesus" or "Jesus Christ", is kept as the Greek has it.
[^1-2]: Literally "Sosthenes the brother". A Sosthenes was leader of the synagogue in Corinth (Acts 18:17); whether he is the same man is not known.
[^1-3]: Or "sanctified", "set apart as holy".
[^1-4]: Literally "called holy ones", traditionally "called to be saints".
[^1-5]: To "call on the name" of the Lord is the Old Testament's expression for praying to and worshiping Yahweh (Genesis 12:8; Joel 3:5, 2:32 in most English Bibles).
[^1-6]: Or "in every place, theirs and ours".
[^1-7]: Greek *logos*, "word, speech".
[^1-8]: Literally "the testimony of Christ". *Among you*: or "in you".
[^1-9]: Greek *charisma*, "gift of grace", from *charis*, "grace".
[^1-10]: Greek *apokalypsis*, "revelation, unveiling".
[^1-11]: Greek "who", referring either to God (verse 4) or to Jesus Christ (verse 7).
[^1-12]: Greek *koinonia*, "fellowship, partnership, sharing".
[^1-13]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike; also in verses 11 and 26.
[^1-14]: Literally "that you all say the same thing".
[^1-15]: Literally "by those of Chloe", probably members of her household or people who worked for her.
[^1-16]: Literally "I am of Paul"; and so for each of the names.
[^1-17]: The Aramaic name of the apostle Peter (John 1:42).
[^1-18]: Or "Christ has been divided!"
[^1-19]: Literally "into the name"; also in verse 15.
[^1-20]: Many manuscripts read "I thank God".
[^1-21]: Crispus had been the leader of the synagogue in Corinth (Acts 18:8). A Gaius is Paul's host in Romans 16:23.
[^1-22]: Many manuscripts read "that I baptized in my own name".
[^1-23]: Literally "not in wisdom of word".
[^1-24]: Literally "would not be emptied".
[^1-25]: Greek *logos*, the word translated "speech" in verse 17.
[^1-26]: Isaiah 29:14, following the Septuagint, in which God says "I will destroy". The Hebrew reads "The wisdom of their wise will perish, and the understanding of their discerning will vanish." The Septuagint ends "I will hide"; Paul has "I will set aside".
[^1-27]: Or "the expert in the law". Compare Isaiah 19:12 and 33:18.
[^1-28]: Greek *kerygma*, "proclamation, preaching". Literally "the foolishness of the proclamation".
[^1-29]: Or "a crucified Messiah".
[^1-30]: Greek *skandalon*, something that trips people up, a cause of offense.
[^1-31]: Many manuscripts read "Greeks".
[^1-32]: Literally "according to the flesh".
[^1-33]: Literally "low-born", the opposite of "of noble birth" in verse 26.
[^1-34]: Greek *katargeo*, "make ineffective, abolish, bring to an end".
[^1-35]: Literally "no flesh".
[^1-36]: Or "who became for us wisdom from God, that is, righteousness, holiness and redemption". *Holiness*: or "sanctification". *Redemption*: release, as of a slave or captive, by payment of a price.
[^1-37]: A summary of Jeremiah 9:22–23 (9:23–24 in most English Bibles), where the one who boasts is to boast that he knows God, "that I am Yahweh". In the Hebrew, "the Lord" here is God's personal name, Yahweh. Also in 2 Corinthians 10:17.

## Chapter 2

*Status: draft*

**1** When I came to you, brothers and sisters,[^2-1] I did not come with superior eloquence or wisdom[^2-2] as I proclaimed to you the testimony of God.[^2-3]

**2** For I decided to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ, and him crucified.

**3** And I was with you in weakness, in fear and in much trembling.

**4** My speech and my proclamation[^2-4] were not with the persuasiveness of wisdom,[^2-5] but with a demonstration of the Spirit and of power,[^2-6]

**5** so that your faith would not rest on human wisdom but on the power of God.

**6** Yet we do speak wisdom among the mature,[^2-7] but not the wisdom of this age, or of the rulers of this age,[^2-8] who are being brought to nothing.[^2-9]

**7** We speak God's wisdom in a mystery,[^2-10] the wisdom that has been hidden, which God destined before the ages for our glory.

**8** None of the rulers of this age understood it, for if they had understood it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.

**9** But as it is written, "What no eye has seen, and no ear has heard, and has not entered the human heart,[^2-11] all that God has prepared for those who love him."[^2-12]

**10** For God has revealed it to us through the Spirit.[^2-13] For the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God.

**11** For who among human beings knows the thoughts of a person[^2-14] except the person's own spirit within him? In the same way, no one knows the thoughts of God except the Spirit of God.

**12** We have not received the spirit of the world, but the Spirit that is from God, so that we may know the things God has freely given us.

**13** And we speak of these things, not in words taught by human wisdom, but in words taught by the Spirit,[^2-15] explaining spiritual things in spiritual words.[^2-16]

**14** The natural person[^2-17] does not accept the things of the Spirit of God, for they are foolishness to him, and he cannot understand them, because they are discerned spiritually.[^2-18]

**15** The spiritual person judges all things, but he himself is judged by no one.

**16** For "who has known the mind of the Lord, so as to instruct him?"[^2-19] But we have the mind of Christ.

[^2-1]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike (see 1:10).
[^2-2]: Literally "not according to excellence of word or of wisdom".
[^2-3]: Some manuscripts read "the mystery of God".
[^2-4]: Greek *kerygma*, "proclamation, preaching" (see 1:21).
[^2-5]: The SBLGNT reads "the persuasiveness of wisdom". Most editions read "persuasive words of wisdom", and many manuscripts "persuasive words of human wisdom".
[^2-6]: Or "a demonstration of the Spirit's power".
[^2-7]: Greek *teleioi*, "mature, complete, perfect".
[^2-8]: Either the human authorities, such as those who crucified Jesus (verse 8), or spiritual powers thought to rule the present age; also in verse 8.
[^2-9]: Greek *katargeo* (see 1:28).
[^2-10]: Greek *mysterion*, a secret purpose of God, now made known. Or "We speak God's wisdom, a mystery that has been hidden".
[^2-11]: Literally "has not come up on the heart of a human being".
[^2-12]: The words do not match any one Old Testament passage. Compare Isaiah 64:3 (64:4 in most English Bibles) and 65:17, where "come to mind" is literally "come up on the heart".
[^2-13]: The SBLGNT here follows Westcott and Hort; other editions read "But".
[^2-14]: Literally "the things of the person"; and "the things of God" later in the verse.
[^2-15]: Many manuscripts read "the Holy Spirit".
[^2-16]: Or "interpreting spiritual things to spiritual people", or "comparing spiritual things with spiritual things".
[^2-17]: Greek *psychikos*, from *psyche*, "life, soul": a person with natural life only, without the Spirit of God. Or "the unspiritual person".
[^2-18]: Greek *anakrino*, "examine, judge, discern", the verb translated "judges" and "judged" in verse 15.
[^2-19]: Isaiah 40:13, following the Septuagint. The Hebrew reads "Who has measured the Spirit of Yahweh, or instructed him as his counselor?" In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, Yahweh.

## Chapter 3

*Status: draft*

**1** Brothers and sisters,[^3-1] I could not speak to you as spiritual people, but as people of the flesh,[^3-2] as infants in Christ.

**2** I gave you milk to drink, not solid food, for you were not yet able to take it. Indeed, even now you are not able,

**3** for you are still people of the flesh. For where there is jealousy and strife among you,[^3-3] are you not people of the flesh, and living in a merely human way?[^3-4]

**4** For when one says, "I belong to Paul," and another, "I belong to Apollos," are you not merely human?[^3-5]

**5** What then is Apollos? And what is Paul? Servants through whom you came to believe, as the Lord gave to each.[^3-6]

**6** I planted, Apollos watered, but God made it grow.

**7** So neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but only God, who makes it grow.

**8** The one who plants and the one who waters are one,[^3-7] and each will receive his own wages according to his own labor.

**9** For we are God's fellow workers;[^3-8] you are God's field, God's building.

**10** According to the grace of God given to me, I laid a foundation like a skilled master builder,[^3-9] and someone else is building on it. But let each one take care how he builds on it.

**11** For no one can lay any foundation other than the one already laid, which is Jesus Christ.

**12** If anyone builds on the foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay or straw,

**13** each one's work will become evident, for the Day[^3-10] will make it clear, because it will be revealed with fire,[^3-11] and the fire will test what sort of work each one has done.

**14** If the work that anyone has built on the foundation lasts, he will receive a reward.[^3-12]

**15** If anyone's work is burned up, he will suffer loss; he himself will be saved, but only as through fire.

**16** Don't you know that you are God's temple and that the Spirit of God lives in you?[^3-13]

**17** If anyone destroys[^3-14] God's temple, God will destroy that person. For God's temple is holy, and you are that temple.

**18** Let no one deceive himself. If anyone among you thinks he is wise in this age, let him become foolish so that he may become wise.

**19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness in God's sight. For it is written, "He catches the wise in their craftiness,"[^3-15]

**20** and again, "The Lord knows the thoughts of the wise, that they are futile."[^3-16]

**21** So let no one boast about human beings. For all things are yours,

**22** whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas or the world or life or death or the present or the future: all are yours,

**23** and you are Christ's, and Christ is God's.

[^3-1]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike (see 1:10).
[^3-2]: Greek *sarkinoi*, "made of flesh", and in verse 3 *sarkikoi*, "fleshly". *The flesh* in Paul's moral sense is human nature in its weakness and its opposition to God, contrasted here with the Spirit. Many manuscripts have *sarkikoi* in this verse too.
[^3-3]: Many manuscripts add "and divisions".
[^3-4]: Literally "walking according to a human being".
[^3-5]: Literally "are you not human beings?" Many manuscripts read "are you not people of the flesh?"
[^3-6]: Literally "and to each as the Lord gave". This may mean that the Lord gave each servant his task, or that he gave faith to each believer.
[^3-7]: Or "are equal", "have one purpose".
[^3-8]: Or "fellow workers with God". The Greek can mean working together with God or working together in God's service.
[^3-9]: Literally "a wise master builder". Greek *architekton*.
[^3-10]: The day of the Lord (1:8), the day of judgment.
[^3-11]: The subject may be the Day or each one's work.
[^3-12]: Or "wages", the word used in verse 8.
[^3-13]: "You" is plural in Greek, here and in verse 17: the believers together are God's temple. *In you*: or "among you".
[^3-14]: Or "ruins", "corrupts". The same Greek verb, *phtheiro*, is used twice in the sentence.
[^3-15]: Job 5:13. Paul's Greek is closer to the Hebrew than to the Septuagint.
[^3-16]: Psalm 94:11, with "the wise" in place of "humankind". The Hebrew says that their thoughts are "only a breath". In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, Yahweh.

## Chapter 4

*Status: draft*

**1** This is how people should regard us: as servants[^4-1] of Christ and stewards[^4-2] of the mysteries of God.

**2** In this case, what is required of stewards is that each one be found faithful.

**3** But to me it is a very small thing that I should be examined[^4-3] by you or by any human court.[^4-4] I do not even examine myself.

**4** For I am not aware of anything against myself, but I am not declared righteous[^4-5] by that. It is the Lord who examines me.

**5** So do not judge anything before the time, until the Lord comes. He will bring to light what is hidden in darkness and will reveal the purposes of the heart.[^4-6] Then each one will receive his praise from God.

**6** Brothers and sisters,[^4-7] I have applied all this to myself and Apollos[^4-8] for your sake, so that you may learn from us what "Not beyond what is written"[^4-9] means, so that none of you will be puffed up[^4-10] in favor of one person against another.

**7** For who makes you different from anyone else?[^4-11] What do you have that you did not receive? And if you did receive it, why do you boast as if you had not received it?

**8** Already you have all you want! Already you have become rich! Without us you have become kings! And I wish you had become kings, so that we too might reign with you.

**9** For I think God has put us apostles on display at the very end, like people condemned to death,[^4-12] because we have become a spectacle[^4-13] to the world, both to angels and to human beings.

**10** We are fools for Christ's sake, but you are wise in Christ. We are weak, but you are strong. You are honored, but we are dishonored.

**11** To this very hour we go hungry and thirsty, we are poorly clothed,[^4-14] we are beaten, and we have no home.

**12** We work hard, laboring with our own hands. When we are insulted, we bless; when we are persecuted, we endure it;

**13** when we are slandered, we answer kindly.[^4-15] We have become like the scum of the world, the filth of everyone,[^4-16] to this day.

**14** I am not writing this to shame you, but to warn you as my dear children.

**15** For even if you have ten thousand guardians[^4-17] in Christ, you do not have many fathers. For in Christ Jesus I became your father[^4-18] through the good news.

**16** So I urge you, be imitators of me.

**17** That is why I have sent[^4-19] Timothy to you. He is my dear and faithful child in the Lord, and he will remind you of my ways in Christ Jesus, as I teach them everywhere in every church.

**18** Some of you have become puffed up, as though I were not coming to you.

**19** But I will come to you soon, if the Lord wills, and I will find out not the talk of these puffed-up people but their power.

**20** For the kingdom of God is not a matter of talk but of power.

**21** What do you want? Shall I come to you with a rod,[^4-20] or with love and a spirit of gentleness?

[^4-1]: Greek *hyperetai*, "assistants, attendants", not the word for "servants" in 3:5.
[^4-2]: Greek *oikonomoi*, household managers, often slaves, entrusted with their master's affairs; also in verse 2.
[^4-3]: Greek *anakrino*, "examine, judge" (see 2:14); also later in this verse and in verse 4.
[^4-4]: Literally "by a human day", in contrast with the Day of the Lord (3:13).
[^4-5]: Greek *dikaioo*, traditionally "justify". Here it may mean "acquitted".
[^4-6]: Literally "of the hearts".
[^4-7]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike (see 1:10).
[^4-8]: Or "I have applied these things in a figure to myself and Apollos". The Greek verb means to change the form of something.
[^4-9]: The meaning of this saying is uncertain. It may refer to the scriptures, perhaps those quoted in 1:19, 1:31 and 3:19–20, or it may be a slogan or proverb. Many manuscripts read "not to think beyond what is written".
[^4-10]: Greek *physioo*, "inflate, puff up": to be arrogant; also in verses 18 and 19.
[^4-11]: Or "Who regards you as superior?" "You" is singular in Greek in this verse.
[^4-12]: The picture is of condemned prisoners brought out at the end of a show in the arena.
[^4-13]: Greek *theatron*, "theater, spectacle".
[^4-14]: Literally "we are naked".
[^4-15]: Or "we appeal to them", "we speak gently". Greek *parakaleo*.
[^4-16]: Both Greek words mean what is scraped or swept off as rubbish. They were also used of people expelled or put to death to cleanse a community.
[^4-17]: Greek *paidagogoi*, slaves who looked after children and took them to school. They were not teachers.
[^4-18]: Literally "I fathered you".
[^4-19]: Or "I am sending".
[^4-20]: Or "a stick", for punishment.

## Chapter 5

*Status: draft*

**1** It is actually reported that there is sexual immorality[^5-1] among you, and of a kind that is not found even among the Gentiles:[^5-2] a man has his father's wife.[^5-3]

**2** And you are puffed up![^5-4] Shouldn't you rather have mourned, so that the man who did this would be removed from among you?

**3** For I, though absent in body, am present in spirit, and as if I were present I have already passed judgment on the one who has done such a thing,

**4** in the name of our Lord Jesus.[^5-5] When you are assembled, and my spirit is with you, with the power of our Lord Jesus,

**5** you are to hand this man over to Satan for the destruction of the flesh,[^5-6] so that the spirit[^5-7] may be saved on the day of the Lord.

**6** Your boasting is not good. Don't you know that a little yeast leavens the whole batch of dough?

**7** Clean out the old yeast so that you may be a new batch, as you really are unleavened. For Christ, our Passover lamb,[^5-8] has been sacrificed.[^5-9]

**8** So let us celebrate the festival, not with the old yeast, the yeast of malice and evil, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.

**9** I wrote to you in my letter[^5-10] not to associate with sexually immoral people.

**10** I did not mean the sexually immoral people of this world at all, or the greedy and swindlers, or idolaters, since then you would have to leave the world.

**11** But now I am writing to you[^5-11] not to associate with anyone who bears the name of brother or sister[^5-12] if he is sexually immoral or greedy, or an idolater, a slanderer, a drunkard or a swindler. Do not even eat with such a person.

**12** For what business is it of mine to judge those outside? Is it not those inside whom you are to judge?

**13** God judges[^5-13] those outside. "Purge the evil person from among you."[^5-14]

[^5-1]: Greek *porneia*, sexual relations outside marriage, including prostitution and forbidden unions; also later in this verse. In verses 9–11, "sexually immoral people" is *pornoi*.
[^5-2]: Many manuscripts read "of a kind that is not even named among the Gentiles".
[^5-3]: That is, he is living with her as his wife. "His father's wife" probably means his stepmother. Compare Leviticus 18:8.
[^5-4]: Greek *physioo* (see 4:6). The sentence can also be read as a question: "And are you puffed up?"
[^5-5]: The phrases in verses 3–5 can be connected in several ways. "In the name of our Lord Jesus" may go with "passed judgment", with "assembled", or with "hand this man over" in verse 5.
[^5-6]: Greek *sarx*. This may mean the man's body, through suffering or death, or "the flesh" in Paul's moral sense, human nature in its opposition to God (see 3:1).
[^5-7]: Probably the man's spirit. Some take it as the spirit of the community.
[^5-8]: Greek *pascha*, "Passover", here the lamb sacrificed at Passover (Exodus 12:21). Yeast had to be removed from every house during the festival that followed (Exodus 12:15, 19).
[^5-9]: Many manuscripts add "for us".
[^5-10]: Literally "in the letter", probably an earlier letter that has not survived.
[^5-11]: Or "But in fact, I wrote to you".
[^5-12]: Greek *adelphos*, "brother", meaning any fellow believer.
[^5-13]: Other editions accent the verb as a future: "God will judge".
[^5-14]: Deuteronomy 17:7, and several times in Deuteronomy (for example 13:6, 13:5 in most English Bibles), where this translation has "purge the evil from among you". The Greek, like the Hebrew, can mean "the evil" or "the evil person".

## Chapter 6

*Status: draft*

**1** When any of you has a dispute with another, does he dare to go to court before the unrighteous,[^6-1] and not before God's holy people?[^6-2]

**2** Or don't you know that God's holy people will judge the world? And if the world is to be judged by you, are you not competent to try the smallest cases?

**3** Don't you know that we will judge angels? How much more, then, the matters of this life!

**4** So if you have cases about the matters of this life, do you appoint as judges those who have no standing in the church?[^6-3]

**5** I say this to your shame. Is there really no one among you wise enough to decide between one brother or sister and another?[^6-4]

**6** Instead, brother or sister goes to court against brother or sister, and that before unbelievers!

**7** In fact, to have lawsuits with one another at all is already a defeat for you. Why not rather be wronged? Why not rather be cheated?

**8** Instead, you yourselves do wrong and cheat, and you do it to your brothers and sisters.[^6-5]

**9** Or don't you know that the unrighteous will not inherit the kingdom of God? Do not be deceived: neither the sexually immoral,[^6-6] nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor soft men,[^6-7] nor men who lie with males,[^6-8]

**10** nor thieves, nor the greedy, nor drunkards, nor slanderers, nor swindlers will inherit the kingdom of God.

**11** And that is what some of you were. But you were washed, you were made holy,[^6-9] you were declared righteous[^6-10] in the name of the Lord Jesus[^6-11] and by the Spirit of our God.

**12** "I have the right to do anything,"[^6-12] but not everything is beneficial. "I have the right to do anything," but I will not be brought under the power of anything.[^6-13]

**13** "Food is for the stomach and the stomach for food,"[^6-14] and God will bring both the one and the other to nothing.[^6-15] But the body is not for sexual immorality; it is for the Lord, and the Lord is for the body.

**14** And God raised the Lord, and he will also raise us by his power.

**15** Don't you know that your bodies are members[^6-16] of Christ? Shall I then take the members of Christ and make them members of a prostitute? By no means![^6-17]

**16** Or don't you know that whoever joins himself to a prostitute is one body with her? For it says,[^6-18] "The two will become one flesh."[^6-19]

**17** But whoever joins himself to the Lord is one spirit with him.

**18** Flee from sexual immorality. Every sin a person commits is outside the body,[^6-20] but the one who commits sexual immorality sins against his own body.

**19** Or don't you know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you,[^6-21] whom you have from God? You are not your own,

**20** for you were bought with a price. So glorify God in your body.[^6-22]

[^6-1]: That is, before judges who are not believers.
[^6-2]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "saints"; also in verse 2.
[^6-3]: Or, as a command, "appoint as judges even those who have the lowest standing in the church!"
[^6-4]: Literally "between his brother", a shortened expression. The SBLGNT's editor notes a possible conjectural change, adding "and his brother". Greek *adelphos*, "brother", means any fellow believer; so also in verse 6.
[^6-5]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike (see 1:10).

**40** There are also heavenly bodies and earthly bodies, but the glory[^15-29] of the heavenly is one kind and the glory of the earthly is another.

**41** There is one glory of the sun, another glory of the moon, and another glory of the stars; for star differs from star in glory.

**42** So it is with the resurrection of the dead. It is sown in decay; it is raised imperishable.

**43** It is sown in dishonor; it is raised in glory. It is sown in weakness; it is raised in power.

**44** It is sown a natural body;[^15-30] it is raised a spiritual body. If there is a natural body, there is also a spiritual body.[^15-31]

**45** So also it is written, "The first man, Adam, became a living being";[^15-32] the last Adam became a life-giving spirit.

**46** But it is not the spiritual that comes first, but the natural, and then the spiritual.

**47** The first man was from the earth, made of dust;[^15-33] the second man is from heaven.[^15-34]

**48** As was the man of dust, so are those who are of the dust; and as is the man of heaven, so are those who are of heaven.

**49** And just as we have borne the image of the man of dust, we will also bear[^15-35] the image of the man of heaven.

**50** What I mean, brothers and sisters, is this: flesh and blood[^15-36] cannot inherit the kingdom of God, nor does decay inherit what is imperishable.

**51** Listen, I tell you a mystery: we will not all fall asleep, but we will all be changed,

**52** in a moment, in the blink of an eye, at the last trumpet. For the trumpet will sound, and the dead will be raised imperishable, and we will be changed.

**53** For this perishable body must put on the imperishable, and this mortal body must put on immortality.[^15-37]

**54** When this perishable body puts on the imperishable, and this mortal body puts on immortality,[^15-38] then the saying that is written will come true: "Death has been swallowed up in victory."[^15-39]

**55** "Where, O death, is your victory? Where, O death, is your sting?"[^15-40]

**56** The sting of death is sin, and the power of sin is the Law.

**57** But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ!

**58** So, my dear brothers and sisters, be steadfast, immovable, always abounding in the work of the Lord, knowing that your labor is not in vain in the Lord.

## Chapter 16

*Status: draft*

**1** Now concerning the collection for God's holy people:[^16-1] you should do what I directed the churches of Galatia to do.

**2** On the first day of every week,[^16-2] each of you is to put something aside and save it up, as he may prosper, so that collections will not have to be made when I come.

**3** When I arrive, I will send those whom you approve with letters[^16-3] to take your gift[^16-4] to Jerusalem.

**4** If it is fitting for me to go too, they will go with me.

**5** I will come to you after I pass through Macedonia, for I am passing through Macedonia.

**6** Perhaps I will stay with you, or even spend the winter, so that you may send me on my way, wherever I go.

**7** For I do not want to see you now just in passing; I hope to spend some time with you, if the Lord permits.

**8** But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost,[^16-5]

**9** for a wide and effective door has opened to me, and there are many who oppose me.

**10** If Timothy comes, see that he has nothing to fear among you, for he is doing the work of the Lord, as I am.

**11** So let no one despise him. Send him on his way in peace, so that he may come to me, for I am expecting him with the brothers.[^16-6]

**12** Now concerning our brother Apollos: I strongly urged him to come to you with the brothers, but it was not at all his will[^16-7] to come now. He will come when he has the opportunity.

**13** Be watchful, stand firm in the faith, be courageous,[^16-8] be strong.

**14** Let all that you do be done in love.

**15** Now I urge you, brothers and sisters[^16-9] (you know that the household of Stephanas were the first converts in Achaia,[^16-10] and that they have devoted themselves to serving God's holy people),

**16** to submit to such people, and to everyone who works with them and labors.

**17** I am glad that Stephanas, Fortunatus and Achaicus have come, because they have made up for your absence,[^16-11]

**18** for they refreshed my spirit and yours. So give recognition to such people.

**19** The churches of Asia[^16-12] send you greetings. Aquila and Prisca,[^16-13] together with the church in their house, send you warm greetings in the Lord.

**20** All the brothers and sisters send you greetings. Greet one another with a holy kiss.

**21** I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand.[^16-14]

**22** If anyone does not love the Lord, let him be cursed![^16-15] Marana tha![^16-16]

**23** The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you.

**24** My love be with all of you in Christ Jesus.[^16-17]
