## Chapter 1

*Status: draft*

**1** What was from the beginning,[^1-1] what we have heard, what we have seen with our eyes, what we have looked at and our hands have touched, concerning the word of life[^1-2]

**2** (and the life was revealed, and we have seen it, and we testify to it and announce to you the eternal life[^1-3] that was with the Father and was revealed to us),

**3** what we have seen and heard we announce to you too, so that you also may have fellowship[^1-4] with us. And our fellowship is with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ.

**4** We are writing these things[^1-5] so that our joy may be complete.

**5** This is the message that we have heard from him and announce to you: God is light, and in him there is no darkness at all.

**6** If we say that we have fellowship with him and yet walk in the darkness, we are lying and are not doing what is true.[^1-6]

**7** But if we walk in the light, as he himself is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus his Son[^1-7] cleanses us from all sin.

**8** If we say that we have no sin, we are deceiving ourselves, and the truth is not in us.

**9** If we confess our sins, he is faithful and righteous, and will forgive us our sins and cleanse us from all wrongdoing.[^1-8]

**10** If we say that we have not sinned, we make him a liar, and his word is not in us.

[^1-1]: Compare John 1:1–2. The letter's opening sentence (verses 1–3) is interrupted by verse 2 and resumed in verse 3.
[^1-2]: Or "the Word of life". Greek *logos*, "word, message", the title given to Jesus in John 1:1, 14.
[^1-3]: Greek *zoe aionios*, literally "the life of the age", that is, of the age to come (see John 3:15).
[^1-4]: Greek *koinonia*, "fellowship, partnership, sharing"; also in verses 6–7.
[^1-5]: Many manuscripts read "We are writing these things to you".
[^1-6]: Literally "are not doing the truth".
[^1-7]: Many manuscripts read "Jesus Christ his Son".
[^1-8]: Greek *adikia*, "unrighteousness", the opposite of *dikaios*, "righteous", earlier in the verse.

## Chapter 2

*Status: draft*

**1** My little children,[^2-1] I am writing these things to you so that you will not sin. But if anyone does sin, we have an Advocate[^2-2] with the Father, Jesus Christ the Righteous One.[^2-3]

**2** He himself is the atoning sacrifice[^2-4] for our sins, and not only for ours but also for the whole world.

**3** This is how we know that we have come to know him: if we keep his commands.

**4** Whoever says, "I have come to know him," but does not keep his commands is a liar, and the truth is not in that person.

**5** But whoever keeps his word, in that person the love of God[^2-5] has truly been made complete. This is how we know that we are in him:

**6** whoever says that they remain in him ought to walk just as he walked.[^2-6]

**7** Dear friends,[^2-7] I am not writing a new command to you, but an old command that you have had from the beginning. The old command is the word that you have heard.[^2-8]

**8** Yet I am writing a new command to you,[^2-9] which is true in him and in you, because the darkness is passing away and the true light is already shining.

**9** Whoever says that they are in the light but hates their brother or sister[^2-10] is still in the darkness.

**10** Whoever loves their brother or sister remains in the light, and there is nothing in them to make them stumble.[^2-11]

**11** But whoever hates their brother or sister is in the darkness and walks in the darkness, and does not know where they are going, because the darkness has blinded their eyes.

**12** I am writing to you, little children, because your sins have been forgiven for the sake of his name.[^2-12]

**13** I am writing to you, fathers, because you have come to know the one who is from the beginning. I am writing to you, young men, because you have overcome the evil one.

**14** I have written to you, children,[^2-13] because you have come to know the Father. I have written to you, fathers, because you have come to know the one who is from the beginning. I have written to you, young men, because you are strong, and the word of God remains in you, and you have overcome the evil one.

**15** Do not love the world or the things in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father[^2-14] is not in them.

**16** For everything in the world, the desire of the flesh,[^2-15] the desire of the eyes and pride in one's possessions,[^2-16] does not come from the Father but from the world.

**17** And the world is passing away, and its desire with it, but whoever does the will of God remains forever.

**18** Children, it is the last hour. Just as you heard that antichrist[^2-17] is coming, so now many antichrists have come. This is how we know that it is the last hour.

**19** They went out from us, but they did not belong to us. For if they had belonged to us, they would have remained with us. But they went out so that it might be made plain that none of them belong to us.[^2-18]

**20** But you have an anointing[^2-19] from the Holy One, and you all know.[^2-20]

**21** I have not written to you because you do not know the truth, but because you do know it, and because no lie comes from the truth.[^2-21]

**22** Who is the liar but the one who denies that Jesus is the Messiah?[^2-22] This is the antichrist: the one who denies the Father and the Son.

**23** No one who denies the Son has the Father. Whoever acknowledges the Son has the Father too.[^2-23]

**24** As for you, let what you heard from the beginning remain in you. If what you heard from the beginning remains in you, you too will remain in the Son and in the Father.

**25** And this is the promise that he himself made to us: eternal life.

**26** I have written these things to you about those who are trying to lead you astray.

**27** As for you, the anointing that you received from him remains in you, and you have no need for anyone to teach you. But as his anointing[^2-24] teaches you about everything, and is true and is not a lie, then just as it has taught you, remain in him.[^2-25]

**28** And now, little children, remain in him, so that when he appears[^2-26] we may have confidence and not shrink back from him in shame at his coming.[^2-27]

**29** If you know that he is righteous, you know[^2-28] that everyone who does what is right has been born of him.

[^2-1]: Greek *teknia*, an affectionate word for children, as in John 13:33; also in verses 12 and 28.
[^2-2]: Greek *parakletos*, "one called to someone's side": a helper or advocate, here one who pleads for sinners before the Father. In John 14:16, 26 the word is used of the Holy Spirit.
[^2-3]: Or "Jesus Christ, who is righteous".
[^2-4]: Greek *hilasmos*, "atonement, means of atonement": a sacrifice that takes away sin and turns away God's anger; traditionally "propitiation" or "expiation". Also in 4:10. The related word *hilasterion* is the "atonement cover" over the ark (Hebrews 9:5; compare Exodus 25:17).
[^2-5]: Or "love for God".
[^2-6]: Literally "just as that one walked". In this letter "that one" (Greek *ekeinos*) refers to Jesus (3:3, 5, 7, 16; 4:17). *Walk*: that is, live.
[^2-7]: Literally "Beloved". Many manuscripts read "Brothers and sisters".
[^2-8]: Many manuscripts add "from the beginning".
[^2-9]: Literally "Again, I am writing a new command to you". *Which is true*: the Greek "which" may refer to the command, or to the fact that it is new.
[^2-10]: Literally "his brother"; also in verses 10–11. The letter uses "brother" for any fellow believer, man or woman.
[^2-11]: Greek *skandalon*, something that trips people up. Or "and there is nothing in it to cause stumbling", that is, in the light.
[^2-12]: Or "I am writing to you, little children, that your sins have been forgiven". The Greek *hoti* can mean "because" or "that"; also in verses 13–14.
[^2-13]: Greek *paidia*, a different word from "little children" (*teknia*) in verse 12, but with much the same meaning; also in verse 18.
[^2-14]: Or "love for the Father".
[^2-15]: *The flesh*: human nature, in its weakness and its pull away from God.
[^2-16]: Or "pride in one's way of life". Greek *bios*, "life, livelihood, possessions".
[^2-17]: Greek *antichristos*, "one who is against (or in place of) the Christ". In the Bible the word occurs only in 1 and 2 John (also verse 22; 4:3; 2 John 7).
[^2-18]: Literally "but so that it might be shown that they all are not from us".
[^2-19]: Greek *chrisma*, a word related to *christos*, "anointed one", and *antichristos* (verse 18). The letter does not say what the anointing is; it has been understood as the Holy Spirit (compare 3:24; John 14:26) or as the message the readers received (verse 24). Also in verse 27.
[^2-20]: Many manuscripts read "and you know all things".
[^2-21]: Or "and that no lie comes from the truth".
[^2-22]: Literally "who denies, saying that Jesus is not the Christ". *Messiah*: Greek *ho christos*, "the Christ", "the Anointed One".
[^2-23]: Many manuscripts leave out "Whoever acknowledges the Son has the Father too."
[^2-24]: Many manuscripts read "the same anointing".
[^2-25]: Or "you remain in him", a statement rather than a command. Many manuscripts read "you will remain in him". *In him*: or "in it", the anointing.
[^2-26]: Literally "if he appears". Many manuscripts read "when he appears".
[^2-27]: Greek *parousia*, "presence, arrival", the word used for Christ's return.
[^2-28]: Or "know", a command.

## Chapter 3

*Status: draft*

**1** See what great love the Father has given us, that we should be called children of God; and so we are![^3-1] The reason the world does not know us[^3-2] is that it did not know him.

**2** Dear friends, we are God's children now, and what we will be has not yet been revealed. We know that when he appears,[^3-3] we will be like him, because we will see him as he is.

**3** And everyone who has this hope set on him purifies themselves, just as he is pure.[^3-4]

**4** Everyone who commits sin also commits lawlessness; sin is lawlessness.

**5** You know that he appeared in order to take away sins,[^3-5] and in him there is no sin.

**6** No one who remains in him sins.[^3-6] No one who sins has seen him or known him.

**7** Little children, let no one lead you astray. Whoever does what is right is righteous, just as he is righteous.

**8** Whoever commits sin is from the devil,[^3-7] because the devil has been sinning from the beginning. The Son of God appeared for this purpose: to destroy the works of the devil.

**9** No one who has been born of God commits sin, because God's seed[^3-8] remains in them; and they cannot sin, because they have been born of God.

**10** This is how the children of God and the children of the devil are made plain: anyone who does not do what is right is not from God, and neither is anyone who does not love their brother or sister.[^3-9]

**11** For this is the message that you heard from the beginning: that we should love one another.

**12** We must not be like Cain,[^3-10] who was from the evil one and murdered[^3-11] his brother. And why did he murder him? Because his own works were evil, and his brother's were righteous.

**13** Do not be surprised, brothers and sisters,[^3-12] if the world hates you.

**14** We know that we have passed out of death into life, because we love our brothers and sisters. Whoever does not love[^3-13] remains in death.

**15** Everyone who hates their brother or sister is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life remaining in them.

**16** This is how we have come to know love: he laid down his life for us. And we ought to lay down our lives for our brothers and sisters.

**17** But if anyone has the world's goods[^3-14] and sees their brother or sister in need, yet closes their heart against them,[^3-15] how does the love of God remain in them?

**18** Little children, let us not love with words or with the tongue, but in action and in truth.

**19** This is how we will know that we are from the truth, and how we will reassure our hearts[^3-16] before him

**20** whenever our hearts condemn us; for God is greater than our hearts, and he knows everything.[^3-17]

**21** Dear friends, if our hearts do not condemn us, we have confidence before God,

**22** and we receive from him whatever we ask, because we keep his commands and do what pleases him.[^3-18]

**23** And this is his command: that we believe in the name of his Son Jesus Christ and love one another, just as he commanded us.

**24** Whoever keeps his commands remains in him, and he in them. And this is how we know that he remains in us: by the Spirit whom he has given us.

[^3-1]: Many manuscripts leave out "and so we are".
[^3-2]: Many manuscripts read "you".
[^3-3]: Or "when it is revealed", that is, what we will be.
[^3-4]: Literally "just as that one is pure", that is, Jesus (see 2:6); also in verses 5, 7 and 16.
[^3-5]: Many manuscripts read "our sins".
[^3-6]: The Greek present tense here and in verses 8–9 can mean "keeps on sinning", "goes on sinning". Compare 1:8–10.
[^3-7]: Or "belongs to the devil". Literally "is of the devil"; and so "from God" (verses 9–10) and "from the evil one" (verse 12).
[^3-8]: Literally "his seed".
[^3-9]: Literally "his brother"; also in verses 12, 15 and 17. The letter uses "brother" for any fellow believer, man or woman.
[^3-10]: Literally "Not as Cain". See Genesis 4:1–16.
[^3-11]: Greek *sphazo*, "slaughter", the word used for killing a sacrificial animal; also later in the verse.
[^3-12]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike; also in verses 14 and 16.
[^3-13]: Many manuscripts read "Whoever does not love their brother or sister".
[^3-14]: Literally "the life of the world". Greek *bios*, "life, livelihood, possessions", as in 2:16.
[^3-15]: Literally "shuts his inner parts from him", the seat of the feelings, especially of compassion.
[^3-16]: Literally "persuade our heart". The Greek has the singular "heart" here and in verses 20–21.
[^3-17]: The Greek of verses 19–20 is difficult. Verse 20 may also be read "that if our hearts condemn us, God is greater than our hearts …", or "in whatever our hearts condemn us, for God is greater …".
[^3-18]: Literally "do what is pleasing in his sight".

## Chapter 4

*Status: draft*

**1** Dear friends, do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits to see whether they are from God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world.

**2** This is how you know the Spirit of God:[^4-1] every spirit that acknowledges that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh[^4-2] is from God,

**3** and every spirit that does not acknowledge Jesus[^4-3] is not from God. This is the spirit of the antichrist,[^4-4] which you have heard is coming, and now it is already in the world.

**4** You are from God, little children, and you have overcome them, because the one who is in you is greater than the one who is in the world.

**5** They are from the world. That is why they speak as the world speaks,[^4-5] and the world listens to them.

**6** We are from God. Whoever knows God listens to us; whoever is not from God does not listen to us. This is how we know the Spirit of truth and the spirit of error.[^4-6]

**7** Dear friends, let us love one another, because love comes from God, and everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God.

**8** Whoever does not love has not known God, because God is love.

**9** This is how God's love was revealed among us:[^4-7] God has sent his only Son[^4-8] into the world so that we might live through him.

**10** This is love: not that we have loved God, but that he loved us and sent his Son as the atoning sacrifice[^4-9] for our sins.

**11** Dear friends, if God loved us in this way, we also ought to love one another.

**12** No one has ever seen God. If we love one another, God remains in us, and his love is made complete in us.

**13** This is how we know that we remain in him and he in us: he has given us a share in his Spirit.[^4-10]

**14** And we have seen and we testify that the Father has sent the Son as the Savior of the world.

**15** Whoever acknowledges that Jesus[^4-11] is the Son of God, God remains in them, and they in God.

**16** And we have come to know and have believed the love that God has for us.[^4-12] God is love, and whoever remains in love remains in God, and God remains in them.

**17** This is how love has been made complete among us: we can have confidence on the day of judgment, because as he is, so are we in this world.[^4-13]

**18** There is no fear in love; perfect love[^4-14] drives out fear, because fear has to do with punishment. Whoever is afraid has not been made complete in love.

**19** We love[^4-15] because he first loved us.

**20** If anyone says, "I love God," but hates their brother or sister,[^4-16] that person is a liar. For whoever does not love their brother or sister, whom they have seen, cannot love God, whom they have not seen.[^4-17]

**21** And this is the command that we have from him: whoever loves God must also love their brother or sister.

[^4-1]: Many manuscripts read "This is how the Spirit of God is known".
[^4-2]: Or "acknowledges Jesus Christ as one who has come in the flesh". *The flesh*: that is, as a human being (compare John 1:14).
[^4-3]: Many manuscripts read "that does not acknowledge that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh", as in verse 2.
[^4-4]: Literally "this is the [spirit] of the antichrist". See 2:18.
[^4-5]: Literally "they speak from the world".
[^4-6]: Or "the spirit of deception".
[^4-7]: Or "in our case"; literally "in us".
[^4-8]: Greek *monogenes*, "only, unique, one of a kind", as in John 1:14; 3:16.
[^4-9]: Greek *hilasmos*. See 2:2.
[^4-10]: Literally "he has given us of his Spirit".
[^4-11]: Some manuscripts read "Jesus Christ".
[^4-12]: Literally "in us".
[^4-13]: Or "This is how love has been made complete among us, so that we may have confidence on the day of judgment: as he is, so are we in this world." *He*: literally "that one" (see 2:6).
[^4-14]: Greek *teleia*, "complete", from the same root as "made complete" in verses 12, 17 and 18.
[^4-15]: Many manuscripts read "We love him".
[^4-16]: Literally "his brother"; also later in the verse and in verse 21. The letter uses "brother" for any fellow believer, man or woman.
[^4-17]: Many manuscripts read "how can they love God, whom they have not seen?"

## Chapter 5

*Status: draft*

**1** Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Messiah[^5-1] has been born of God, and everyone who loves the parent also loves the one born of him.[^5-2]

**2** This is how we know that we love the children of God: when we love God and carry out his commands.[^5-3]

**3** For this is the love of God:[^5-4] that we keep his commands. And his commands are not burdensome,

**4** because everything that has been born of God[^5-5] overcomes the world. And this is the victory that has overcome the world: our faith.

**5** Who is it that overcomes the world, if not the one who believes that Jesus is the Son of God?

**6** This is the one who came by water and blood, Jesus Christ: not with the water only, but with the water and with the blood.[^5-6] And the Spirit is the one who testifies, because the Spirit is the truth.

**7** For there are three that testify:[^5-7]

**8** the Spirit, the water and the blood, and the three agree.[^5-8]

**9** If we accept human testimony, the testimony of God is greater; for this is the testimony of God: that he has testified about his Son.[^5-9]

**10** Whoever believes in the Son of God has the testimony in themselves. Whoever does not believe God has made him a liar, because they have not believed the testimony that God has given about his Son.

**11** And this is the testimony: God has given us eternal life, and this life is in his Son.

**12** Whoever has the Son has life; whoever does not have the Son of God does not have life.

**13** I have written these things to you so that you may know that you have eternal life, you who believe in the name of the Son of God.[^5-10]

**14** And this is the confidence that we have before him: if we ask anything according to his will, he hears us.

**15** And if we know that he hears us in whatever we ask, we know that we have what we have asked of him.

**16** If anyone sees their brother or sister[^5-11] committing a sin that does not lead to death,[^5-12] they should ask, and God will give them life:[^5-13] that is, to those who commit sins that do not lead to death. There is sin that leads to death. I am not saying that they should make a request about that.

**17** All wrongdoing[^5-14] is sin, but there is sin that does not lead to death.

**18** We know that no one who has been born of God sins,[^5-15] but the one who was born of God keeps them safe,[^5-16] and the evil one does not touch them.

**19** We know that we are from God, and that the whole world lies in the power of the evil one.[^5-17]

**20** And we know that the Son of God has come and has given us understanding, so that we may know the one who is true. And we are in the one who is true, in his Son Jesus Christ. He is the true God and eternal life.[^5-18]

**21** Little children, keep yourselves from idols.[^5-19]

[^5-1]: Greek *ho christos*, "the Christ", "the Anointed One".
[^5-2]: Literally "everyone who loves the one who fathered loves also the one fathered by him".
[^5-3]: Many manuscripts read "keep his commands".
[^5-4]: Or "love for God".
[^5-5]: The Greek is neuter: "everything", "whatever" has been born of God.
[^5-6]: The letter does not explain "water and blood". They have often been taken as Jesus' baptism and his death; compare also John 19:34.
[^5-7]: The Textus Receptus (followed by the King James Version) adds "in heaven: the Father, the Word and the Holy Spirit; and these three are one. And there are three that testify on earth:". These words, known as the Johannine Comma, are not in the SBLGNT or in any of the editions in its apparatus. They are found in only a few late Greek manuscripts, but in many manuscripts of the Latin Vulgate, and entered the printed Greek text in the sixteenth century.
[^5-8]: Literally "the three are for the one", that is, they agree in one testimony.
[^5-9]: Many manuscripts read "this is the testimony of God that he has given about his Son".
[^5-10]: Many manuscripts read "I have written these things to you who believe in the name of the Son of God, so that you may know that you have eternal life, and so that you may believe in the name of the Son of God." Compare John 20:31.
[^5-11]: Literally "his brother". The letter uses "brother" for any fellow believer, man or woman.
[^5-12]: Literally "a sin not toward death"; and so later in this verse and in verse 17. The letter does not say what "sin that leads to death" is.
[^5-13]: Literally "he will ask, and he will give him life". *God* is supplied from the context; the Greek may also mean that the one who prays gives life, through his prayer.
[^5-14]: Greek *adikia*, "unrighteousness". See 1:9.
[^5-15]: See the note on 3:6.
[^5-16]: Many manuscripts read "keeps himself safe", in which case the one born of God is the believer. With the SBLGNT reading, "the one who was born of God" is usually taken to be the Son of God.
[^5-17]: Literally "lies in the evil one". Or "lies in evil".
[^5-18]: Greek *houtos*, "this one", may refer to Jesus Christ or to God the Father, "the one who is true".
[^5-19]: Many manuscripts add "Amen".
