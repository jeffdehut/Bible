## Chapter 1

*Status: draft*

**1** The words of Jeremiah son of Hilkiah, one of the priests in Anathoth[^1-1] in the land of Benjamin.

**2** The word of Yahweh[^1-2] came to him in the days of Josiah son of Amon king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.[^1-3]

**3** It came also in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah, until the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah king of Judah, until Jerusalem went into exile in the fifth month.[^1-4]

**4** The word of Yahweh came to me:

**5** "Before I formed you in the womb I knew you, and before you came out of the womb I set you apart.[^1-5] I appointed you a prophet to the nations."

**6** I said, "Ah, Lord Yahweh![^1-6] I don't know how to speak, for I am only a boy."[^1-7]

**7** But Yahweh said to me, "Do not say, 'I am only a boy.' You will go to everyone I send you to, and you will say whatever I command you.

**8** Do not be afraid of them, for I am with you to rescue you, declares Yahweh."[^1-8]

**9** Then Yahweh stretched out his hand and touched my mouth, and Yahweh said to me, "Look, I have put my words in your mouth.

**10** See, today I appoint you over nations and over kingdoms, to uproot and to tear down, to destroy and to overthrow, to build and to plant."[^1-9]

**11** The word of Yahweh came to me: "What do you see, Jeremiah?" I said, "I see a branch of an almond tree."[^1-10]

**12** Yahweh said to me, "You have seen well, for I am watching over my word to carry it out."

**13** The word of Yahweh came to me a second time: "What do you see?" I said, "I see a boiling pot, and it is tipped away from the north."[^1-11]

**14** Yahweh said to me, "From the north disaster will be let loose on all who live in the land.

**15** For I am about to summon all the clans of the kingdoms of the north, declares Yahweh. They will come, and each of them will set up his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem, against all its walls around it, and against all the cities of Judah.

**16** I will pronounce my judgments on my people[^1-12] for all their evil, because they have abandoned me. They have burned incense[^1-13] to other gods and bowed down to the works of their own hands.

**17** But you, get ready for action.[^1-14] Stand up and tell them everything I command you. Do not be shattered with fear before them, or I will shatter you before them.[^1-15]

**18** And I, look, today I have made you a fortified city, an iron pillar and bronze walls against the whole land: against the kings of Judah, its officials, its priests and the people of the land.[^1-16]

**19** They will fight against you, but they will not overcome you, for I am with you, declares Yahweh, to rescue you."

[^1-1]: A town of priests about 3 miles (5 km) northeast of Jerusalem (Joshua 21:18; 1 Kings 2:26).
[^1-2]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^1-3]: In 627 BC.
[^1-4]: In the summer of 587 or 586 BC (2 Kings 25:8–11; Jeremiah 52:12–15).
[^1-5]: Or "consecrated you". Literally "made you holy".
[^1-6]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^1-7]: Hebrew *na'ar*, a word used of a child, a youth or a young man.
[^1-8]: Hebrew *ne'um YHWH*, "the declaration of Yahweh", a formula that marks a prophet's words as Yahweh's own solemn statement. It is frequent in Jeremiah.
[^1-9]: These verbs recur through the book as a summary of Jeremiah's message (18:7–9; 24:6; 31:28; 42:10; 45:4).
[^1-10]: A wordplay. Hebrew *shaqed*, "almond tree", sounds like *shoqed*, "watching", in verse 12. The almond is the first tree to blossom in late winter, as though it were awake while the others sleep.
[^1-11]: Literally "and its face is from the face of the north". The pot's mouth faces south, so its contents will pour out from the north over Judah (verse 14).
[^1-12]: Literally "on them".
[^1-13]: Or "burned offerings". The Hebrew verb *qatar* means to make something go up in smoke.
[^1-14]: Literally "gird up your loins", that is, tuck the long robe into the belt, ready for action.
[^1-15]: The Hebrew uses the same verb, *chatat*, "to be shattered, dismayed", in both lines.
[^1-16]: Hebrew *am ha-arets*, the landowning citizens of Judah.

## Chapter 2

*Status: draft*

**1** The word of Yahweh[^2-1] came to me:

**2** Go and proclaim in the hearing of Jerusalem:[^2-2] This is what Yahweh says: I remember the faithful love[^2-3] of your youth, your love as a bride, how you followed me in the wilderness, in a land not sown.

**3** Israel was holy to Yahweh, the firstfruits of his harvest.[^2-4] All who ate of it were held guilty; disaster came on them, declares Yahweh.

**4** Hear the word of Yahweh, house of Jacob, and all the clans of the house of Israel.

**5** This is what Yahweh says: What wrong did your ancestors find in me, that they went far from me? They followed what is worthless and became worthless themselves.[^2-5]

**6** They did not say, "Where is Yahweh, who brought us up from the land of Egypt, who led us through the wilderness, through a land of deserts and pits, through a land of drought and deep darkness,[^2-6] a land that no one travels through, where no human being lives?"

**7** I brought you into a fruitful land,[^2-7] to eat its fruit and its good things. But you came in and defiled my land, and made my inheritance detestable.

**8** The priests did not say, "Where is Yahweh?" Those who handle the law did not know me. The shepherds[^2-8] rebelled against me. The prophets prophesied by Baal and followed things that do not profit.[^2-9]

**9** Therefore I will still bring charges against you,[^2-10] declares Yahweh, and against your children's children I will bring charges.

**10** Cross over to the coasts of Kittim and look; send to Kedar and consider carefully;[^2-11] see if there has ever been anything like this.

**11** Has a nation ever exchanged its gods, even though they are not gods? But my people have exchanged their Glory[^2-12] for what does not profit.

**12** Be appalled at this, you heavens; shudder and be utterly desolate, declares Yahweh.

**13** For my people have done two evils: they have abandoned me, the spring of living water,[^2-13] to dig out cisterns for themselves, cracked cisterns that cannot hold water.

**14** Is Israel a slave? Was he born a slave in the household?[^2-14] Then why has he become plunder?

**15** Young lions have roared over him; they have raised their voices. They have made his land a desolation; his cities are burned,[^2-15] with no one living in them.

**16** The people of Memphis and Tahpanhes[^2-16] have also shaved your head.[^2-17]

**17** Have you not brought this on yourself by abandoning Yahweh your God, when he was leading you on the way?

**18** So now, what good is it for you to take the road to Egypt, to drink the waters of the Shihor?[^2-18] What good is it for you to take the road to Assyria, to drink the waters of the River?

**19** Your own evil will discipline you, and your faithlessness[^2-19] will rebuke you. Know and see how evil and bitter it is for you to abandon Yahweh your God, and to have no fear of me,[^2-20] declares the Lord Yahweh of Armies.[^2-21]

**20** For long ago I broke your yoke[^2-22] and tore off your bonds, and you said, "I will not transgress."[^2-23] Yet on every high hill and under every leafy tree you lay down as a prostitute.

**21** I had planted you as a choice vine, wholly of true seed. How then have you turned against me into the wild shoots of a foreign vine?

**22** Even if you wash yourself with lye and use much soap,[^2-24] the stain of your guilt is still before me, declares the Lord Yahweh.[^2-25]

**23** How can you say, "I am not defiled; I have not gone after the Baals"?[^2-26] Look at your way in the valley;[^2-27] know what you have done, you swift young camel running here and there,[^2-28]

**24** a wild donkey used to the wilderness, sniffing the wind in her desire![^2-29] In her season of heat, who can turn her back? None who look for her need to tire themselves; in her month they will find her.

**25** Keep your feet from going bare and your throat from thirst![^2-30] But you said, "It's hopeless! No! For I love strangers,[^2-31] and I will go after them."

**26** As a thief is put to shame when he is caught, so the house of Israel is put to shame: they, their kings, their officials, their priests and their prophets.

**27** They say to a tree, "You are my father," and to a stone, "You gave birth to us."[^2-32] For they have turned their backs to me, not their faces. Yet in the time of their trouble they say, "Get up and save us!"

**28** But where are your gods that you made for yourself? Let them get up, if they can save you in the time of your trouble! For you have as many gods as you have cities, Judah.

**29** Why do you bring charges against me? All of you have rebelled against me, declares Yahweh.

**30** In vain I have struck your children; they accepted no discipline. Your own sword has devoured your prophets like a ravaging lion.

**31** You people of this generation, consider the word of Yahweh![^2-33] Have I been a wilderness to Israel, or a land of thick darkness? Why do my people say, "We roam free;[^2-34] we will not come to you again"?

**32** Does a young woman forget her jewelry, or a bride her sash? Yet my people have forgotten me for days without number.

**33** How well you set your course to seek love! So you have even taught your ways to wicked women.[^2-35]

**34** Even on your skirts[^2-36] is found the lifeblood of the innocent poor. You did not catch them breaking in.[^2-37] Yet in spite of all this[^2-38]

**35** you say, "I am innocent; surely his anger has turned away from me." Look, I will bring you to judgment because you say, "I have not sinned."

**36** How lightly you go about changing your way! You will be put to shame by Egypt, as you were put to shame by Assyria.

**37** From there too you will go out with your hands on your head,[^2-39] for Yahweh has rejected those you trust, and you will not prosper with them.

[^2-1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^2-2]: Jerusalem, and in much of this chapter Israel, is addressed as a woman. In the oracles of this book, the words of Yahweh and of the prophet are not put in quotation marks, because the speaker often changes without being marked. Quotation marks are kept for conversations, and for words quoted within an oracle.
[^2-3]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love. Here it is the bride's devotion.
[^2-4]: The firstfruits of a harvest belonged to Yahweh, and anyone else who ate them was guilty (Leviticus 22:10–16).
[^2-5]: Hebrew *hevel*, "breath, vapor", a word for what is empty and worthless, often used of idols. "Became worthless" is the verb from the same word: literally "they went after vapor and became vapor".
[^2-6]: Hebrew *tsalmavet*, "deep darkness", traditionally read as "the shadow of death".
[^2-7]: Hebrew *erets ha-karmel*, "the land of the orchard, garden land".
[^2-8]: That is, the rulers.
[^2-9]: Hebrew *lo yo'ilu*, "what does not profit", perhaps a play on the sound of *Baal*. Also in verse 11.
[^2-10]: Hebrew *riv*, to bring a case against someone, as in a court of law. Also in verse 29.
[^2-11]: Kittim (Cyprus) lay to the west, and Kedar, the tribes of the Arabian desert, to the east: search the world from one side to the other.
[^2-12]: Literally "his glory", meaning the glorious God who was theirs (compare Psalm 106:20). Jewish scribal tradition counts this among the places where the scribes changed the wording out of reverence, and says that it once read "my glory".
[^2-13]: Fresh, flowing water from a spring, as opposed to water stored in a cistern.
[^2-14]: A slave born in the master's house belonged to him for life.
[^2-15]: Or "laid in ruins".
[^2-16]: Hebrew *Noph*: Memphis, the old capital of Lower Egypt. Tahpanhes was a fortress city in the eastern Nile delta (43:7–9). The Hebrew text is written "Tahpanes" but read "Tahpanhes".
[^2-17]: Literally "will graze the crown of your head". Or "have cracked your skull".
[^2-18]: A branch of the Nile in the eastern delta; here, the Nile. "The River" is the Euphrates.
[^2-19]: Hebrew *meshuvah*, "turning away", from *shuv*, "turn, return". Jeremiah plays on this word often, especially in chapter 3.
[^2-20]: Literally "and my dread is not on you".
[^2-21]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH tseva'ot*, "my Lord Yahweh of Armies". *Yahweh of Armies* is traditionally "the LORD of hosts" or "the LORD Almighty". *Tseva'ot* means armies: the heavenly hosts and the armies of Israel.
[^2-22]: Or "you broke your yoke". The Hebrew is pointed "I broke", referring to Yahweh's freeing Israel from Egypt, but the ending can be an old form of "you" (compare verse 33), and the Septuagint reads "you broke".
[^2-23]: The Hebrew text is written "I will not serve" but read "I will not transgress".
[^2-24]: Hebrew *neter* (natron, a mineral used for washing) and *borit* (a soap made from plant ash).
[^2-25]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^2-26]: Local forms of the Canaanite god Baal.
[^2-27]: Probably the Valley of Ben-hinnom, outside Jerusalem, where children were sacrificed (7:31).
[^2-28]: Literally "crisscrossing her ways".
[^2-29]: The Hebrew text is written "his desire" but read "her desire".
[^2-30]: That is, don't wear out your sandals and parch your throat running after your lovers.
[^2-31]: That is, foreign gods.
[^2-32]: The tree and the stone are probably the wooden pole of Asherah and the standing stone of Canaanite worship. The Hebrew text is written "You gave birth to me" but read "You gave birth to us".
[^2-33]: The Hebrew is unusual. Literally "The generation, you, see the word of Yahweh".
[^2-34]: Hebrew *radnu*, a rare verb, perhaps "we roam freely" or "we are free".
[^2-35]: Or "you have trained your ways in wickedness". The Hebrew text is written "I have taught" but read "you have taught". The written form may be an old spelling of "you have taught" (compare verse 20).
[^2-36]: Literally "on your wings", the edges of a robe.
[^2-37]: A householder who killed a burglar caught breaking in was not guilty of murder (Exodus 22:1). These victims had done nothing of the kind.
[^2-38]: The Hebrew of this line is obscure. Literally "for on all these".
[^2-39]: A gesture of grief and shame (2 Samuel 13:19).

## Chapter 3

*Status: draft*

**1** If a man sends his wife away,[^3-1] and she leaves him and becomes another man's wife, will he go back to her again? Would that land not be utterly defiled?[^3-2] But you have prostituted yourself with many lovers, and would you return to me?[^3-3] declares Yahweh.[^3-4]

**2** Lift up your eyes to the bare heights and look: where have you not been lain with?[^3-5] You sat by the roads waiting for them, like a nomad[^3-6] in the desert. You have defiled the land with your prostitution and your wickedness.

**3** So the showers have been held back, and the spring rain[^3-7] has not come. Yet you have the brazen look of a prostitute;[^3-8] you refuse to be ashamed.

**4** Have you not just now called to me,[^3-9] "My father, you are the companion of my youth!

**5** Will he stay angry forever? Will he keep his anger to the end?" Look, that is how you have spoken, yet you have done all the evil you could.

**6** Yahweh said to me in the days of King Josiah: Have you seen what Faithless Israel[^3-10] has done? She went up on every high mountain and under every leafy tree, and prostituted herself there.

**7** I thought, "After she has done all this, she will return to me." But she did not return, and her treacherous sister Judah saw it.

**8** I saw that, though I had sent Faithless Israel away and given her a certificate of divorce for all her adulteries, her treacherous sister Judah had no fear. She too went and prostituted herself.

**9** Because her prostitution meant so little to her,[^3-11] she defiled the land, committing adultery with stone and tree.[^3-12]

**10** Yet for all this her treacherous sister Judah did not return to me with all her heart, but only in pretense, declares Yahweh.

**11** Yahweh said to me: Faithless Israel has shown herself more righteous than treacherous Judah.

**12** Go and proclaim these words toward the north, and say: Return, Faithless Israel,[^3-13] declares Yahweh. I will not look on you in anger,[^3-14] for I am faithful,[^3-15] declares Yahweh; I will not stay angry forever.

**13** Only acknowledge your guilt, that you have rebelled against Yahweh your God. You have spread your favors[^3-16] to strangers under every leafy tree, and you have not listened to my voice, declares Yahweh.

**14** Return, faithless children,[^3-17] declares Yahweh, for I am your master.[^3-18] I will take you, one from a city and two from a clan, and bring you to Zion.

**15** I will give you shepherds after my own heart, and they will feed you with knowledge and understanding.

**16** When you have multiplied and become fruitful in the land in those days, declares Yahweh, people will no longer say, "The ark of the covenant of Yahweh." It will not come to mind. They will not remember it or miss it, and another will not be made.

**17** At that time they will call Jerusalem "The Throne of Yahweh," and all the nations will gather to it, to the name of Yahweh, to Jerusalem. They will no longer follow the stubbornness of their evil hearts.

**18** In those days the house of Judah will join the house of Israel, and together they will come from the land of the north to the land I gave your ancestors as an inheritance.

**19** I myself said: How I would set you among my sons,[^3-19] and give you a pleasant land, the most beautiful inheritance among the nations![^3-20] I thought you would call me "My father," and would not turn back from following me.

**20** But as a woman is unfaithful to her lover, so you have been unfaithful to me, house of Israel, declares Yahweh.

**21** A voice is heard on the bare heights, the weeping and pleading of the Israelites, because they have twisted their way; they have forgotten Yahweh their God.

**22** Return, faithless children; I will heal your faithlessness. "Here we are;[^3-21] we come to you, for you are Yahweh our God.

**23** Surely the hills are a delusion, the uproar on the mountains.[^3-22] Surely in Yahweh our God is the salvation of Israel.

**24** But the Shameful Thing[^3-23] has devoured what our ancestors worked for since our youth: their flocks and their herds, their sons and their daughters.

**25** Let us lie down in our shame, and let our disgrace cover us. For we have sinned against Yahweh our God, we and our ancestors, from our youth until this day, and we have not listened to the voice of Yahweh our God."

[^3-1]: The Hebrew begins with *lemor*, "saying", perhaps left from an introduction like the one in 2:1. The law in view is Deuteronomy 24:1–4, which forbids a man to take back a wife he has divorced after she has married another man.
[^3-2]: Literally "Would that land not be greatly polluted?"
[^3-3]: Or "yet return to me!"
[^3-4]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^3-5]: The Hebrew text is written with a coarser verb, *shagal*, "been ravished", but read "been lain with".
[^3-6]: Hebrew *aravi*, "an Arab", a desert nomad, who waits by the road for travelers.
[^3-7]: Hebrew *malqosh*, the "latter rain" of March and April, which the crops need before harvest.
[^3-8]: Literally "the forehead of a prostitute".
[^3-9]: The Hebrew text is written "I have called" but read "you have called", and in verse 5 is written "I have spoken" but read "you have spoken". The written forms are probably an old spelling of "you" (compare 2:20, 33).
[^3-10]: Hebrew *meshuvah yisra'el*, "Turning-away Israel", as though it were her name. The northern kingdom of Israel had been destroyed by Assyria about a century before (2 Kings 17).
[^3-11]: Literally "from the lightness of her prostitution".
[^3-12]: That is, with idols of stone and wood (2:27).
[^3-13]: A wordplay on *shuv*, "turn, return": *shuvah meshuvah yisra'el*, "Turn back, Turning-away Israel". Verses 14 and 22 play on the same root.
[^3-14]: Literally "I will not make my face fall on you".
[^3-15]: Hebrew *chasid*, related to *chesed*, "faithful love".
[^3-16]: Literally "you have scattered your ways".
[^3-17]: Literally "Return, turning-away sons" (Hebrew *shuvu banim shovavim*). Also in verse 22.
[^3-18]: Or "your husband". Hebrew *ba'alti*, from *ba'al*, "lord, owner, husband", the same word as the name of the god Baal.
[^3-19]: Israel, pictured as a daughter, is given a son's share of the inheritance.
[^3-20]: Hebrew *nachalat tsevi tsiv'ot goyim*, literally "an inheritance of beauty, of the beauties of the nations".
[^3-21]: The people's answer, to the end of verse 25.
[^3-22]: The Hebrew is difficult. Literally "Surely for a lie from the hills, the tumult of the mountains". The hills and mountains were places of worship of other gods (verse 6).
[^3-23]: Hebrew *ha-boshet*, "the shame", a scornful name for Baal (11:13).

## Chapter 4

*Status: draft*

**1** If you return, Israel, declares Yahweh,[^4-1] return to me. If you put your detestable idols[^4-2] out of my sight and do not stray,

**2** and if you swear, "As surely as Yahweh lives," in truth, in justice and in righteousness, then the nations will bless themselves by him, and in him they will glory.[^4-3]

**3** For this is what Yahweh says to the people of Judah and to Jerusalem: Break up your unplowed ground,[^4-4] and do not sow among thorns.

**4** Circumcise yourselves to Yahweh; remove the foreskins of your hearts,[^4-5] people of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem, or my wrath will break out like fire and burn with no one to put it out, because of the evil of your deeds.

**5** Declare it in Judah, proclaim it in Jerusalem, and say: Blow the ram's horn[^4-6] throughout the land! Shout aloud[^4-7] and say, "Gather together, and let us go into the fortified cities!"

**6** Raise a signal flag toward Zion! Run for safety; do not stand still! For I am bringing disaster from the north, and great destruction.[^4-8]

**7** A lion has come up from its thicket; a destroyer of nations has set out. He has left his place to make your land a desolation. Your cities will be ruined, with no one living in them.

**8** So put on sackcloth, mourn and wail, for the fierce anger of Yahweh has not turned away from us.

**9** On that day, declares Yahweh, the king and the officials will lose heart.[^4-9] The priests will be appalled, and the prophets will be astounded.

**10** Then I said, "Ah, Lord Yahweh![^4-10] Surely you have utterly deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying, 'You will have peace,' when the sword is at our throat!"[^4-11]

**11** At that time it will be said to this people and to Jerusalem: A scorching wind from the bare heights in the wilderness is coming toward my dear people,[^4-12] not to winnow and not to cleanse,

**12** a wind too strong for that,[^4-13] coming at my command. Now I myself will pronounce judgments against them.

**13** Look, he comes up like clouds, his chariots like the whirlwind; his horses are swifter than eagles. Woe to us, for we are ruined!

**14** Wash the evil from your heart, Jerusalem, so that you may be saved. How long will your wicked thoughts lodge within you?

**15** For a voice is declaring from Dan, and proclaiming disaster from the hill country of Ephraim.[^4-14]

**16** Tell the nations, "Look!"[^4-15] Proclaim against Jerusalem: Besiegers[^4-16] are coming from a distant land; they raise their voices against the cities of Judah.

**17** Like men guarding a field, they surround her on every side, because she has rebelled against me, declares Yahweh.

**18** Your own way and your deeds have brought this on you. This is your evil. How bitter it is! How it reaches your heart!

**19** My insides,[^4-17] my insides! I writhe in pain! The walls of my heart! My heart pounds within me; I cannot keep silent. For you have heard, my soul,[^4-18] the sound of the ram's horn, the battle cry.

**20** Disaster follows disaster;[^4-19] the whole land is devastated. Suddenly my tents are devastated, my tent curtains in a moment.

**21** How long must I see the signal flag and hear the sound of the ram's horn?

**22** For my people are foolish; they do not know me. They are senseless children; they have no understanding. They are skilled at doing evil, but they do not know how to do good.

**23** I looked at the earth, and it was formless and empty;[^4-20] at the heavens, and their light was gone.

**24** I looked at the mountains, and they were quaking; all the hills were swaying.

**25** I looked, and there were no human beings, and all the birds of the sky had fled.

**26** I looked, and the fruitful land[^4-21] was a wilderness, and all its cities were torn down before Yahweh, before his fierce anger.

**27** For this is what Yahweh says: The whole land will be a desolation, yet I will not make a full end of it.

**28** Because of this the earth will mourn, and the heavens above will grow dark, because I have spoken, I have purposed, and I have not relented, nor will I turn back from it.

**29** At the noise of horsemen and archers the whole city flees. They go into the thickets and climb up among the rocks. Every city is abandoned; no one lives in them.

**30** And you, devastated one, what are you doing? Though you dress in scarlet, though you put on gold jewelry, though you enlarge your eyes with paint,[^4-22] you make yourself beautiful in vain. Your lovers despise you; they seek your life.

**31** For I hear a cry like that of a woman in labor, the anguish of one bearing her first child, the cry of daughter Zion[^4-23] gasping for breath, stretching out her hands: "Woe is me! My life is fainting before the killers!"

[^4-1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
[^4-2]: Hebrew *shiqqutsim*, "detestable things", a contemptuous word for idols.
[^4-3]: Or "will be blessed by him". Compare Genesis 12:3; 22:18.
[^4-4]: Hebrew *nir*, ground left unplowed, which has to be broken up before it can be sown.
[^4-5]: Compare Deuteronomy 10:16; 30:6.
[^4-6]: Hebrew *shofar*, a trumpet made from a ram's horn, sounded as an alarm in war. The Hebrew text is written "and blow" but read "Blow".
[^4-7]: Literally "Call out, fill", that is, cry out with a full voice.
[^4-8]: Hebrew *shever*, "breaking". Also in verse 20.
[^4-9]: Literally "the heart of the king and the heart of the officials will perish".
[^4-10]: Hebrew *Adonai YHWH*, "my Lord Yahweh".
[^4-11]: Literally "and the sword has reached to the life".
[^4-12]: Literally "the daughter of my people", a tender, poetic name for the nation.
[^4-13]: Literally "a wind fuller than these". Winnowing needed a gentle breeze to carry off the chaff; this wind will blow everything away.
[^4-14]: Dan was in the far north of the land, and the hill country of Ephraim just north of Judah: the news comes nearer and nearer to Jerusalem.
[^4-15]: Or "Remind the nations: Look!" The Hebrew is difficult.
[^4-16]: Literally "watchers" or "guards", here those who keep watch around a besieged city.

**4** Yet hear the word of Yahweh, Zedekiah king of Judah. This is what Yahweh says about you: You will not die by the sword.

**5** You will die in peace. As they burned spices for your ancestors, the former kings who were before you, so they will burn spices for you[^34-4] and mourn for you, "Alas, lord!"[^34-5] For I myself have spoken the word, declares Yahweh.

**6** Jeremiah the prophet spoke all these words to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem,

So I am going to proclaim liberty for you, declares Yahweh, liberty to the sword, to plague and to famine. I will make you an object of terror to all the kingdoms of the earth.
