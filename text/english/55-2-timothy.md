## Chapter 1

*Status: draft*

**1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus[^1-1] by the will of God, according to the promise of the life that is in Christ Jesus,

**2** to Timothy, my beloved child: Grace, mercy and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

**3** I thank God, whom I serve with a clear conscience as my ancestors did,[^1-2] as I remember you constantly in my prayers night and day.

**4** As I remember your tears, I long to see you, so that I may be filled with joy.

**5** I am reminded of your sincere faith, which lived first in your grandmother Lois and in your mother Eunice, and I am convinced that it lives in you too.

**6** For this reason I remind you to fan into flame the gift of God that is in you through the laying on of my hands.

**7** For God did not give us a spirit[^1-3] of cowardice, but of power and love and self-control.[^1-4]

**8** So do not be ashamed of the testimony about our Lord, nor of me his prisoner, but share in suffering for the good news by the power of God,

**9** who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not because of our works but because of his own purpose and grace, which was given to us in Christ Jesus before the ages began,[^1-5]

**10** and which has now been revealed through the appearing[^1-6] of our Savior Jesus Christ, who abolished death and brought life and immortality to light through the good news,

**11** for which I was appointed a herald and an apostle and a teacher.[^1-7]

**12** That is why I suffer as I do. But I am not ashamed, for I know whom I have believed, and I am convinced that he is able to guard what I have entrusted to him[^1-8] until that day.

**13** Hold to the pattern of sound words that you heard from me, in the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.

**14** Guard the good deposit entrusted to you, through the Holy Spirit who dwells in us.

**15** You know that all who are in Asia[^1-9] have turned away from me, among them Phygelus and Hermogenes.

**16** May the Lord grant mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, for he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain.

**17** On the contrary, when he arrived in Rome, he searched for me eagerly and found me

**18** (may the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord[^1-10] on that day!), and you know very well how much he served in Ephesus.

[^1-1]: *Christ*: Greek *Christos*, "anointed one", the Greek equivalent of "Messiah". In Paul's letters it usually works as a name, so this translation writes "Christ". Paul's order, "Christ Jesus" or "Jesus Christ", is kept as the Greek has it.
[^1-2]: Literally "from my forebears".
[^1-3]: Or "the Spirit, not of cowardice but". Greek *pneuma*, "spirit, Spirit".
[^1-4]: Greek *sophronismos*, "self-discipline, sound judgment".
[^1-5]: Literally "before eternal times".
[^1-6]: Greek *epiphaneia*, "appearance, manifestation".
[^1-7]: Many manuscripts read "a teacher of the Gentiles".
[^1-8]: Literally "my deposit" (Greek *paratheke*, as in verse 14). Or "what he has entrusted to me".
[^1-9]: The Roman province of Asia, in the west of what is now Turkey, with Ephesus as its chief city.
[^1-10]: The Greek has "the Lord" twice in this prayer.

## Chapter 2

*Status: draft*

**1** You then, my child, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus,

**2** and what you have heard from me in the presence of many witnesses,[^2-1] entrust to faithful people who will be able to teach others also.

**3** Share in suffering as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.

**4** No one serving as a soldier gets entangled in the affairs of everyday life, since he wants to please the one who enlisted him.

**5** And anyone who competes as an athlete is not crowned unless he competes according to the rules.

**6** The hardworking farmer ought to be the first to receive a share of the crops.

**7** Think about what I am saying, for the Lord will give you[^2-2] understanding in everything.

**8** Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, a descendant of David,[^2-3] as proclaimed in my good news,

**9** for which I am suffering, even to the point of being chained like a criminal. But the word of God is not chained.

**10** Therefore I endure everything for the sake of the chosen, so that they also may obtain the salvation that is in Christ Jesus, with eternal glory.

**11** The saying is trustworthy:[^2-4] If we have died with him, we will also live with him;

**12** if we endure, we will also reign with him; if we deny him, he also will deny us;[^2-5]

**13** if we are faithless, he remains faithful; he cannot deny himself.[^2-6]

**14** Remind them of these things, and solemnly charge them before the Lord[^2-7] not to quarrel about words, which does no good but only ruins those who listen.

**15** Do your best to present yourself to God as one approved, a worker who has no need to be ashamed, rightly handling[^2-8] the word of truth.

**16** But avoid irreverent, empty chatter, for those who indulge in it will advance further and further into ungodliness,

**17** and their talk will spread like gangrene. Among them are Hymenaeus and Philetus,

**18** who have swerved from the truth, saying that the resurrection has already happened, and they are overturning the faith of some.

**19** Nevertheless, God's firm foundation stands, bearing this seal: "The Lord knows those who are his,"[^2-9] and, "Let everyone who names the name of the Lord turn away from wickedness."[^2-10]

**20** In a large house there are not only vessels of gold and silver but also vessels of wood and clay, some for honorable use, some for dishonorable.

**21** So if anyone cleanses himself from these things, he will be a vessel for honorable use, set apart as holy, useful to the master of the house, ready for every good work.

**22** Flee youthful passions, and pursue righteousness, faith, love and peace, along with those who call on the Lord from a pure heart.

**23** Have nothing to do with foolish, ignorant controversies; you know that they breed quarrels.

**24** And the Lord's servant[^2-11] must not be quarrelsome but kind to everyone, able to teach, patient when wronged,

**25** correcting his opponents with gentleness. Perhaps God will grant them repentance leading to a knowledge of the truth,

**26** and they will come to their senses and escape from the snare of the devil, after being captured by him to do his will.[^2-12]

[^2-1]: Or "through many witnesses".
[^2-2]: Many manuscripts read "may the Lord give you".
[^2-3]: Literally "from the seed of David".
[^2-4]: See 1 Timothy 1:15. Verses 11–13 appear to quote an early Christian hymn or saying.
[^2-5]: Compare Matthew 10:33.
[^2-6]: The SBLGNT here follows the Byzantine text; other editions read "for he cannot deny himself".
[^2-7]: The SBLGNT here follows the Byzantine text; other editions read "before God".
[^2-8]: Literally "cutting straight", as a road is laid out straight or a furrow cut straight.
[^2-9]: Numbers 16:5, in a form close to the Septuagint, which reads "God knew those who are his". In the Hebrew, "the Lord" is God's personal name, Yahweh, and the verse reads "Yahweh will make known who belongs to him".
[^2-10]: Compare Numbers 16:26; Isaiah 26:13; 52:11.
[^2-11]: Or "slave". Greek *doulos*.
[^2-12]: Or "captured by him to do God's will". The Greek uses two different pronouns, "him" and "that one", which may refer to the same person, the devil, or to different persons (the devil and God, or the Lord's servant and God).

## Chapter 3

*Status: draft*

**1** But understand this: in the last days difficult times will come.

**2** For people will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, abusive, disobedient to their parents, ungrateful, unholy,

**3** without natural affection, unwilling to be reconciled, slanderers, without self-control, brutal, not loving what is good,

**4** treacherous, reckless, conceited, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,

**5** having an outward form of godliness but denying its power. Avoid such people.

**6** For among them are those who make their way into households and capture weak women,[^3-1] who are weighed down with sins and led by all kinds of desires,

**7** always learning and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth.

**8** Just as Jannes and Jambres[^3-2] opposed Moses, so these people also oppose the truth, people corrupted in mind and disqualified in regard to the faith.

**9** But they will not get very far, for their folly will be plain to everyone, as the folly of those two was.

**10** You, however, have closely followed my teaching, my conduct, my purpose, my faith, my patience, my love, my endurance,

**11** my persecutions and sufferings, the things that happened to me at Antioch, at Iconium and at Lystra,[^3-3] the persecutions I endured. Yet from them all the Lord rescued me.[^3-4]

**12** Indeed, all who want to live a godly life in Christ Jesus will be persecuted,

**13** while evil people and impostors[^3-5] will go from bad to worse, deceiving and being deceived.

**14** But you, continue in what you have learned and have firmly believed, knowing from whom you learned it,

**15** and that from childhood you have known the sacred writings, which are able to make you wise for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus.

**16** All scripture is God-breathed[^3-6] and useful for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness,

**17** so that the man of God[^3-7] may be complete, fully equipped for every good work.

[^3-1]: Greek *gynaikaria*, "little women", a diminutive of *gyne*, "woman", used with contempt.
[^3-2]: Jewish tradition gave these names to the magicians of Egypt who opposed Moses before Pharaoh (Exodus 7:11, 22). The names are not found in the Old Testament.
[^3-3]: Compare Acts 13:14–14:20.
[^3-4]: Compare Psalm 34:20 (34:19 in most English Bibles).
[^3-5]: Greek *goetes*, "sorcerers", also "swindlers, impostors".
[^3-6]: Greek *theopneustos*, "God-breathed", from *theos*, "God", and *pneo*, "breathe"; traditionally "inspired by God". The word is not found in Greek writings before this verse. The sentence has no verb, and it can be read in two ways: as here, saying that all scripture is God-breathed and useful; or "Every God-breathed scripture is also useful", with "God-breathed" describing scripture rather than saying something about it. *All scripture*: or "every scripture". In context the scriptures meant are those Timothy has known from childhood (verse 15).
[^3-7]: Greek *anthropos*, "human being". Compare 1 Timothy 6:11.

## Chapter 4

*Status: draft*

**1** I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus, who is to judge the living and the dead, and by his appearing[^4-1] and his kingdom:[^4-2]

**2** proclaim the word; be ready in season and out of season;[^4-3] reprove, rebuke and encourage, with complete patience and teaching.

**3** For the time will come when people will not put up with sound teaching, but, wanting to have their ears tickled, they will gather around themselves teachers to suit their own desires,

**4** and they will turn their ears away from the truth and turn aside to myths.

**5** But you, be sober-minded in all things, endure suffering, do the work of an evangelist,[^4-4] fulfill your ministry.[^4-5]

**6** For I am already being poured out like a drink offering,[^4-6] and the time of my departure has come.

**7** I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.

**8** From now on there is reserved for me the crown of righteousness,[^4-7] which the Lord, the righteous judge, will award to me on that day, and not only to me but also to all who have loved his appearing.

**9** Do your best to come to me soon,

**10** for Demas, in love with this present age, has deserted me and gone to Thessalonica. Crescens has gone to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia.

**11** Only Luke is with me. Get Mark and bring him with you, for he is useful to me for service.

**12** I have sent Tychicus to Ephesus.

**13** When you come, bring the cloak that I left with Carpus at Troas, and the books, especially the parchments.[^4-8]

**14** Alexander the coppersmith did me much harm; the Lord will repay him according to his deeds.[^4-9]

**15** You too must beware of him, for he strongly opposed our message.

**16** At my first defense no one came to my support, but all deserted me. May it not be counted against them!

**17** But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me, so that through me the proclamation might be fully made and all the Gentiles[^4-10] might hear it. And I was rescued from the lion's mouth.[^4-11]

**18** The Lord will rescue me from every evil deed and bring me safely into his heavenly kingdom. To him be the glory forever and ever.[^4-12] Amen.

**19** Greet Prisca and Aquila and the household of Onesiphorus.

**20** Erastus remained at Corinth, and I left Trophimus, who was ill, at Miletus.

**21** Do your best to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, as do Pudens, Linus, Claudia and all the brothers and sisters.[^4-13]

**22** The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you all.[^4-14]

[^4-1]: Greek *epiphaneia*, "appearance, manifestation"; also in verse 8.
[^4-2]: Many manuscripts read "who is to judge the living and the dead at his appearing and his kingdom".
[^4-3]: That is, whether the time seems right or not.
[^4-4]: Greek *euangelistes*, "one who proclaims the good news".
[^4-5]: Greek *diakonia*, "service, ministry"; the same word as "service" in verse 11.
[^4-6]: Compare Philippians 2:17.
[^4-7]: Or "the crown that is righteousness", or "the crown given for righteousness".
[^4-8]: *Books*: Greek *biblia*, "scrolls" or "books". *Parchments*: Greek *membranai*, writing materials made from animal skin.
[^4-9]: Compare Psalm 62:13 (62:12 in most English Bibles); Proverbs 24:12. Many manuscripts read "may the Lord repay him".
[^4-10]: Or "all the nations".
[^4-11]: Compare Psalm 22:22 (22:21 in most English Bibles).
[^4-12]: Literally "to the ages of the ages".
[^4-13]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike.
[^4-14]: "Your" in the first sentence is singular; "you" in the second is plural. Many manuscripts read "The Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit" and add "Amen".
