## Chapter 1

*Status: draft*

**1** King David was old, well on in years.[^1-1] They covered him with blankets,[^1-2] but he could not get warm.

**2** So his servants said to him, "Let a young woman, a virgin, be found for my lord the king. Let her wait on the king and take care of him.[^1-3] She can lie in your arms,[^1-4] and then my lord the king will get warm."

**3** So they searched throughout the territory of Israel for a beautiful young woman. They found Abishag the Shunammite[^1-5] and brought her to the king.

**4** The young woman was very beautiful. She took care of the king and served him, but the king did not have sexual relations with her.[^1-6]

**5** Now Adonijah son of Haggith was putting himself forward, saying, "I will be king." He got himself chariots and horsemen,[^1-7] with fifty men to run ahead of him.

**6** His father had never once crossed him[^1-8] by asking, "Why have you done this?" He was also very handsome, and he was born next after Absalom.[^1-9]

**7** He conferred with Joab son of Zeruiah and with Abiathar the priest,[^1-10] and they followed Adonijah and supported him.

**8** But Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei and David's warriors[^1-11] did not side with Adonijah.

**9** Adonijah sacrificed sheep, cattle and fattened calves by the Stone of Zoheleth,[^1-12] which is beside En-rogel.[^1-13] He invited all his brothers, the king's sons, and all the men of Judah who were the king's servants.[^1-14]

**10** But he did not invite Nathan the prophet, Benaiah, the warriors, or his brother Solomon.

**11** Then Nathan said to Bathsheba, Solomon's mother, "Haven't you heard that Adonijah son of Haggith has become king, and our lord David does not know it?

**12** So now come, let me give you some advice, so that you can save your own life and the life of your son Solomon.

**13** Go in to King David and say to him, 'My lord the king, did you not swear to your servant,[^1-15] "Your son Solomon will be king after me, and he will sit on my throne"? Why then has Adonijah become king?'

**14** Then, while you are still there speaking with the king, I will come in after you and confirm what you say."[^1-16]

**15** So Bathsheba went in to the king in his room. The king was very old, and Abishag the Shunammite was serving the king.

**16** Bathsheba bowed low and knelt before the king. The king said, "What do you want?"[^1-17]

**17** She said to him, "My lord, you yourself swore to your servant by Yahweh[^1-18] your God, 'Your son Solomon will be king after me, and he will sit on my throne.'

**18** But now, look, Adonijah has become king, and now, my lord the king, you do not know it.

**19** He has sacrificed oxen, fattened calves and sheep in great numbers, and he has invited all the king's sons, Abiathar the priest and Joab the commander of the army. But he has not invited your servant Solomon.

**20** As for you, my lord the king, the eyes of all Israel are on you, for you to tell them who will sit on the throne of my lord the king after him.

**21** Otherwise, when my lord the king sleeps with his ancestors,[^1-19] my son Solomon and I will be treated as criminals."[^1-20]

**22** While she was still speaking with the king, Nathan the prophet arrived.

**23** The king was told, "Nathan the prophet is here." He came before the king and bowed down to the king with his face to the ground.

**24** Nathan said, "My lord the king, have you said, 'Adonijah will be king after me, and he will sit on my throne'?[^1-21]

**25** For today he has gone down and sacrificed oxen, fattened calves and sheep in great numbers. He has invited all the king's sons, the commanders of the army and Abiathar the priest. They are there now, eating and drinking in his presence, and they are saying, 'Long live King Adonijah!'

**26** But he has not invited me, your servant, or Zadok the priest, or Benaiah son of Jehoiada, or your servant Solomon.

**27** Has my lord the king done this without letting your servant[^1-22] know who will sit on the throne of my lord the king after him?"

**28** Then King David answered, "Call Bathsheba to me." She came into the king's presence and stood before the king.

**29** The king swore an oath and said, "As Yahweh lives, who has rescued my life from every trouble,

**30** just as I swore to you by Yahweh, the God of Israel, 'Your son Solomon will be king after me, and he will sit on my throne in my place,' so I will do this very day."

**31** Bathsheba bowed with her face to the ground and knelt before the king. She said, "May my lord King David live forever!"

**32** King David said, "Call Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet and Benaiah son of Jehoiada to me." They came before the king,

**33** and the king said to them, "Take your lord's servants with you. Have my son Solomon ride on my own mule, and take him down to Gihon.[^1-23]

**34** There Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet are to anoint him king over Israel. Blow the ram's horn[^1-24] and say, 'Long live King Solomon!'

**35** Then come up after him, and he is to come and sit on my throne. He will be king in my place. I have appointed him to be ruler[^1-25] over Israel and over Judah."

**36** Benaiah son of Jehoiada answered the king, "Amen! May Yahweh, the God of my lord the king, say so too.

**37** As Yahweh has been with my lord the king, so may he be with Solomon, and may he make his throne even greater than the throne of my lord King David."

**38** So Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites and the Pelethites[^1-26] went down. They had Solomon ride on King David's mule, and they took him to Gihon.

**39** Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tent[^1-27] and anointed Solomon. They blew the ram's horn, and all the people said, "Long live King Solomon!"

**40** All the people went up after him, playing flutes and rejoicing greatly, so that the earth split with the noise.[^1-28]

**41** Adonijah and all the guests who were with him heard it as they finished eating. When Joab heard the sound of the ram's horn, he said, "Why is the city in such an uproar?"[^1-29]

**42** While he was still speaking, Jonathan son of Abiathar the priest arrived. Adonijah said, "Come in, for you are a worthy man, and you must be bringing good news."

**43** Jonathan answered Adonijah, "Not at all! Our lord King David has made Solomon king.

**44** The king has sent with him Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites and the Pelethites, and they have had him ride on the king's mule.

**45** Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet have anointed him king at Gihon. They have gone up from there rejoicing, and the city is in an uproar. That is the noise you have heard.

**46** Solomon has even taken his seat on the royal throne.

**47** And the king's servants have come to congratulate[^1-30] our lord King David, saying, 'May God[^1-31] make the name of Solomon more famous than your name, and make his throne greater than your throne!' And the king bowed down on his bed.

**48** The king also said this: 'Blessed be Yahweh, the God of Israel, who has today provided someone to sit on my throne,[^1-32] while my own eyes see it.'"

**49** Then all Adonijah's guests trembled. They got up and went their own ways.

**50** Adonijah was afraid of Solomon. He got up, went and took hold of the horns of the altar.[^1-33]

**51** Solomon was told, "Adonijah is afraid of King Solomon. He has taken hold of the horns of the altar and is saying, 'Let King Solomon swear to me today that he will not put his servant to death with the sword.'"

**52** Solomon said, "If he proves to be a worthy man, not one hair of his head will fall to the ground. But if wrongdoing is found in him, he will die."

**53** Then King Solomon sent men, and they brought him down from the altar. He came and bowed down to King Solomon, and Solomon said to him, "Go home."[^1-34]

## Chapter 2

*Status: draft*

**1** When the time drew near for David to die,[^2-1] he gave his son Solomon these instructions:

**2** "I am about to go the way of all the earth. So be strong, and show yourself a man.

**3** Keep the charge of Yahweh[^2-2] your God:[^2-3] walk in his ways, and keep his statutes, his commandments, his rulings and his decrees,[^2-4] as it is written in the Law of Moses, so that you may succeed[^2-5] in everything you do and wherever you turn,

**4** and so that Yahweh may keep the promise he made about me: 'If your sons take care how they live,[^2-6] walking before me faithfully with all their heart and with all their soul, you will never lack a man on the throne of Israel.'[^2-7]

**5** "You also know what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me, what he did to the two commanders of the armies of Israel, Abner son of Ner and Amasa son of Jether. He killed them, shedding the blood of war in peacetime,[^2-8] and he put the blood of war on the belt around his waist and on the sandals on his feet.[^2-9]

**6** Act according to your wisdom, and do not let his gray head go down to Sheol[^2-10] in peace.

**7** But show faithful love[^2-11] to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite, and let them be among those who eat at your table, for they stood by me[^2-12] when I fled from your brother Absalom.

**8** "Look, you have with you Shimei son of Gera, the Benjaminite from Bahurim. He cursed me with a bitter curse on the day I went to Mahanaim. When he came down to meet me at the Jordan, I swore to him by Yahweh, 'I will not put you to death with the sword.'[^2-13]

**9** But now, do not let him go unpunished. You are a wise man, and you will know what to do to him. Bring his gray head down to Sheol in blood."

**10** Then David slept with his ancestors[^2-14] and was buried in the City of David.[^2-15]

**11** David reigned over Israel for forty years.[^2-16] He reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.

**12** So Solomon sat on the throne of his father David, and his kingdom was firmly established.

**13** Then Adonijah son of Haggith came to Bathsheba, Solomon's mother. She said, "Do you come in peace?" He said, "In peace."

**14** Then he said, "I have something to say to you." She said, "Speak."

**15** He said, "You know that the kingship was mine, and all Israel looked to me to be their king.[^2-17] But the kingship has turned around and gone to my brother, for it came to him from Yahweh.

**16** Now I have one request to ask of you. Do not refuse me."[^2-18] She said to him, "Speak."

**17** He said, "Please speak to King Solomon, for he will not refuse you, and ask him to give me Abishag the Shunammite as my wife."

**18** Bathsheba said, "Very well. I will speak to the king for you."

**19** So Bathsheba went to King Solomon to speak to him for Adonijah. The king rose to meet her and bowed down to her. Then he sat down on his throne and had a throne set for the king's mother, and she sat at his right hand.

**20** She said, "I have one small request to ask of you. Do not refuse me." The king said to her, "Ask, my mother, for I will not refuse you."

**21** She said, "Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to your brother Adonijah as his wife."

**22** King Solomon answered his mother, "Why are you asking for Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? Ask for the kingdom for him too, since he is my older brother, for him and for Abiathar the priest and for Joab son of Zeruiah!"[^2-19]

**23** Then King Solomon swore by Yahweh, "May God deal with me, and more besides,[^2-20] if Adonijah has not made this request at the cost of his life!

**24** Now, as Yahweh lives, who has established me and set me on the throne of my father David, and who has made me a house[^2-21] as he promised, Adonijah will be put to death today."

**25** So King Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada,[^2-22] and he struck Adonijah down, and he died.

**26** To Abiathar the priest the king said, "Go to Anathoth, to your fields. You deserve to die,[^2-23] but I will not put you to death today, because you carried the ark[^2-24] of the Lord Yahweh before my father David, and because you shared in all the hardships my father suffered."

**27** So Solomon banished Abiathar from being a priest of Yahweh, fulfilling the word that Yahweh had spoken at Shiloh about the house of Eli.[^2-25]

**28** When the news reached Joab, for Joab had sided with Adonijah, though he had not sided with Absalom, Joab fled to the tent of Yahweh and took hold of the horns of the altar.[^2-26]

**29** King Solomon was told, "Joab has fled to the tent of Yahweh, and he is there beside the altar." So Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada, saying, "Go, strike him down."

**30** Benaiah went to the tent of Yahweh and said to him, "This is what the king says: 'Come out!'" But he said, "No, I will die here." Benaiah brought word back to the king: "This is what Joab said, and this is how he answered me."

**31** The king said to him, "Do as he has said. Strike him down and bury him, and so take away from me and from my father's house the blood that Joab shed without cause.

**32** Yahweh will bring his blood back on his own head,[^2-27] because he struck down two men more righteous and better than he was, and killed them with the sword without my father David knowing it: Abner son of Ner, commander of the army of Israel, and Amasa son of Jether, commander of the army of Judah.

**33** Their blood will come back on the head of Joab and on the head of his descendants forever. But for David and his descendants, his house and his throne, there will be peace from Yahweh forever."

**34** So Benaiah son of Jehoiada went up, struck Joab down and killed him. He was buried at his own home[^2-28] in the wilderness.

**35** The king put Benaiah son of Jehoiada over the army in his place, and the king put Zadok the priest in place of Abiathar.

**36** Then the king sent for Shimei and said to him, "Build yourself a house in Jerusalem and live there. Do not go out from there anywhere at all.[^2-29]

**37** On the day you go out and cross the Kidron Valley,[^2-30] know for certain that you will die. Your blood will be on your own head."

**38** Shimei said to the king, "What you say is good. Your servant will do just as my lord the king has said." So Shimei lived in Jerusalem for a long time.

**39** But at the end of three years, two of Shimei's slaves[^2-31] ran away to Achish son of Maacah, king of Gath. Shimei was told, "Your slaves are in Gath."

**40** So Shimei got up, saddled his donkey and went to Achish at Gath to look for his slaves. Shimei went and brought his slaves back from Gath.

**41** Solomon was told that Shimei had gone from Jerusalem to Gath and had come back.

**42** The king sent for Shimei and said to him, "Did I not make you swear by Yahweh, and warn you, 'On the day you go out and go anywhere at all, know for certain that you will die'? And you said to me, 'What you say is good. I have heard it.'[^2-32]

**43** Why then have you not kept your oath to Yahweh[^2-33] and the command I gave you?"

**44** The king also said to Shimei, "You know in your heart all the wrong you did to my father David.[^2-34] Yahweh will bring your wrongdoing back on your own head.

**45** But King Solomon will be blessed, and the throne of David will be established before Yahweh forever."

**46** Then the king gave orders to Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and he went out and struck Shimei down, and he died. So the kingdom was firmly established in Solomon's hands.

## Chapter 3

*Status: draft*

**1** Solomon made a marriage alliance with Pharaoh king of Egypt. He married Pharaoh's daughter and brought her to the City of David, until he had finished building his own house, the house of Yahweh,[^3-1] and the wall around Jerusalem.

**2** The people, however, were still sacrificing at the high places,[^3-2] because no house had yet been built for the name of Yahweh in those days.

**3** Solomon loved Yahweh and walked in the statutes of his father David, except that he offered sacrifices and burned incense[^3-3] at the high places.

**4** The king went to Gibeon to sacrifice there, because that was the great high place. Solomon offered a thousand burnt offerings on that altar.

**5** At Gibeon Yahweh appeared to Solomon in a dream at night, and God said, "Ask for whatever you want me to give you."

**6** Solomon said, "You have shown great faithful love[^3-4] to your servant, my father David, because he walked before you in faithfulness, in righteousness and with an upright heart toward you. And you have kept this great faithful love for him and have given him a son to sit on his throne, as it is today.

**7** Now, Yahweh my God, you have made your servant king in place of my father David. But I am only a young boy. I do not know how to go out or come in.[^3-5]

**8** And your servant is here among your people whom you have chosen, a great people, too many to be numbered or counted.

**9** So give your servant a listening heart[^3-6] to govern[^3-7] your people and to discern between good and evil. For who is able to govern this great people of yours?"[^3-8]

**10** It was good in the eyes of the Lord[^3-9] that Solomon had asked for this.

**11** So God said to him, "Because you have asked for this, and have not asked for a long life for yourself, or asked for riches for yourself, or asked for the lives of your enemies, but have asked for understanding to discern what is just,[^3-10]

**12** I am now doing as you asked. I am giving you a wise and discerning heart, so that there has been no one like you before you, and no one like you will arise after you.

**13** I am also giving you what you have not asked for, both riches and honor, so that no one among the kings will be like you as long as you live.[^3-11]

**14** And if you walk in my ways, keeping my statutes and my commandments, as your father David walked, I will give you a long life."[^3-12]

**15** Then Solomon woke up, and he realized it had been a dream. He went to Jerusalem and stood before the ark of the covenant of the Lord. He offered burnt offerings and fellowship offerings,[^3-13] and he held a feast for all his servants.

**16** Then two women who were prostitutes came to the king and stood before him.

**17** One of the women said, "Please, my lord,[^3-14] this woman and I live in the same house, and I gave birth to a child while she was in the house.

**18** On the third day after I gave birth, this woman also gave birth. We were alone. There was no one else[^3-15] with us in the house, only the two of us in the house.

**19** During the night this woman's son died, because she lay on him.

**20** She got up in the middle of the night and took my son from beside me while your servant was asleep. She laid him in her arms,[^3-16] and she laid her dead son in my arms.

**21** When I got up in the morning to nurse my son, there he was, dead. But when I looked at him closely in the morning, I saw that he was not the son I had borne."

**22** The other woman said, "No! The living one is my son, and the dead one is your son." But the first woman said, "No! The dead one is your son, and the living one is my son." So they argued before the king.

**23** Then the king said, "This one says, 'This is my son, the living one, and your son is the dead one.' And that one says, 'No! Your son is the dead one, and my son is the living one.'"

**24** So the king said, "Bring me a sword." And they brought a sword before the king.

**25** Then the king said, "Cut the living child in two, and give half to one and half to the other."

**26** But the woman whose son was the living one spoke to the king, because her heart went out to her son.[^3-17] She said, "Please, my lord, give her the living baby! Do not kill him!" But the other said, "He will be neither mine nor yours. Cut him in two!"

**27** Then the king answered, "Give the living baby to the first woman,[^3-18] and do not kill him. She is his mother."

**28** When all Israel heard the judgment the king had given, they stood in awe of the king,[^3-19] for they saw that the wisdom of God was in him to do justice.

[^3-1]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. "The house of Yahweh" is the temple.
[^3-2]: Hebrew *bamot*: places of worship, often on hilltops. Also in verses 3 and 4.
[^3-3]: Or "burned offerings". The Hebrew verb *qatar* means to make something go up in smoke.
[^3-4]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^3-5]: That is, how to carry out the duties of a leader.
[^3-6]: Or "an understanding mind". The heart was thought of as the seat of understanding.
[^3-7]: Literally "to judge". Also later in the verse.
[^3-8]: Literally "this heavy people of yours", that is, so many.
[^3-9]: Hebrew *Adonai*, "the Lord", here instead of God's personal name. Also in verse 15.
[^3-10]: Literally "to understand, to hear justice".
[^3-11]: Literally "so that there has not been a man like you among the kings all your days".
[^3-12]: Literally "I will lengthen your days."
[^3-13]: Hebrew *shelamim*, traditionally "peace offerings": sacrifices shared in a meal by the worshipers.
[^3-14]: Literally "On me, my lord", a polite way of beginning a request to a superior. Also in verse 26.
[^3-15]: Literally "no stranger".
[^3-16]: Literally "in her bosom". Also later in the verse.
[^3-17]: Literally "her compassion grew warm for her son".
[^3-18]: Literally "to her".
[^3-19]: Literally "they feared before the king".

## Chapter 4

*Status: draft*

**1** King Solomon was king over all Israel.

**2** These were his officials:[^4-1] Azariah son of Zadok was the priest.[^4-2]

**3** Elihoreph and Ahijah, the sons of Shisha, were secretaries.[^4-3] Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was the recorder.[^4-4]

**4** Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the army. Zadok and Abiathar were priests.

**5** Azariah son of Nathan was over the governors.[^4-5] Zabud son of Nathan was a priest and the king's friend.[^4-6]

**6** Ahishar was in charge of the palace.[^4-7] Adoniram son of Abda was in charge of the forced labor.[^4-8]

**7** Solomon had twelve governors over all Israel, who provided food for the king and his household. Each one had to provide food for one month in the year.

**8** These are their names: Ben-hur,[^4-9] in the hill country of Ephraim;

**9** Ben-deker, in Makaz, Shaalbim, Beth-shemesh and Elon-beth-hanan;

**10** Ben-hesed, in Arubboth (Socoh and all the land of Hepher were his);

**11** Ben-abinadab, in all Naphath-dor[^4-10] (he had Taphath daughter of Solomon as his wife);

**12** Baana son of Ahilud, in Taanach and Megiddo, and all Beth-shean, which is beside Zarethan below Jezreel, from Beth-shean to Abel-meholah, as far as the other side of Jokmeam;

**13** Ben-geber, in Ramoth-gilead (the villages of Jair son of Manasseh, which are in Gilead, were his,[^4-11] and the region of Argob, which is in Bashan, was his: sixty large cities with walls and bronze bars);[^4-12]

**14** Ahinadab son of Iddo, in Mahanaim;

**15** Ahimaaz, in Naphtali (he too had married Basemath daughter of Solomon);

**16** Baana son of Hushai, in Asher and Bealoth;[^4-13]

**17** Jehoshaphat son of Paruah, in Issachar;

**18** Shimei son of Ela, in Benjamin;

**19** Geber son of Uri, in the land of Gilead, the land of Sihon king of the Amorites and of Og king of Bashan. And there was one governor who was in the land.[^4-14]

**20** The people of Judah and Israel were as many as the sand on the seashore. They ate and drank and were happy.[^4-15]

[^4-1]: Hebrew *sarim*, "princes, officials".
[^4-2]: Or "Azariah son of Zadok the priest". The Hebrew can be read either way.
[^4-3]: Hebrew *soferim*, "scribes": royal secretaries who handled the king's correspondence and records.
[^4-4]: Hebrew *mazkir*, literally "one who brings to remembrance": a high official, perhaps the king's herald or the keeper of the royal records.
[^4-5]: Hebrew *nitsavim*, "those set over": the officials over the districts listed in verses 7–19.
[^4-6]: "The king's friend" was probably an official title, for a trusted personal adviser (2 Samuel 15:37; 16:16).
[^4-7]: Literally "over the house": the official who managed the royal household and estates.
[^4-8]: Hebrew *mas*: work gangs conscripted to labor for the king.
[^4-9]: Several governors are named only by their father: *Ben-hur* means "son of Hur". So also Ben-deker, Ben-hesed, Ben-abinadab and Ben-geber in verses 9–13.
[^4-10]: Or "in all the heights of Dor".
[^4-11]: Hebrew *Havvoth-jair*, "the tent villages of Jair" (Numbers 32:41; Deuteronomy 3:14).
[^4-12]: That is, bars of bronze for securing the city gates.
[^4-13]: Or "in Asher and in Aloth".
[^4-14]: The meaning is uncertain. It may mean that Geber was the only governor over this large region, or that there was one more governor, over the land of Judah.
[^4-15]: In most English Bibles chapter 4 continues with verses 21–34, which are 5:1–14 in the Hebrew.

## Chapter 5

*Status: draft*

**1** Solomon ruled over all the kingdoms from the River[^5-1] to the land of the Philistines,[^5-2] as far as the border of Egypt. They brought tribute and served Solomon all the days of his life.

**2** Solomon's provisions[^5-3] for one day were thirty kors[^5-4] of fine flour and sixty kors of meal,

**3** ten fattened oxen, twenty oxen from the pasture and a hundred sheep and goats, besides deer, gazelles, roebucks and fattened fowl.[^5-5]

**4** For he had dominion over the whole region west of the River,[^5-6] from Tiphsah to Gaza, over all the kings west of the River, and he had peace on every side around him.

**5** Judah and Israel lived in safety, each man under his own vine and under his own fig tree, from Dan to Beersheba, all the days of Solomon.

**6** Solomon had forty thousand stalls of horses for his chariots,[^5-7] and twelve thousand horsemen.[^5-8]

**7** Those governors provided food for King Solomon and for everyone who came to King Solomon's table, each in his month. They let nothing be lacking.

**8** They also brought barley and straw for the horses and the chariot horses[^5-9] to the place where it was needed,[^5-10] each according to his assignment.

**9** God gave Solomon wisdom and very great understanding, and breadth of mind[^5-11] like the sand on the seashore.

**10** Solomon's wisdom was greater than the wisdom of all the people of the east,[^5-12] and greater than all the wisdom of Egypt.

**11** He was wiser than anyone else, wiser than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, Calcol and Darda, the sons of Mahol.[^5-13] His fame spread through all the surrounding nations.

**12** He spoke three thousand proverbs, and his songs numbered 1,005.

**13** He spoke about trees, from the cedar in Lebanon to the hyssop that grows out of the wall. He also spoke about animals, birds, creatures that creep on the ground, and fish.

**14** People came from all nations to hear Solomon's wisdom, sent by all the kings of the earth who had heard of his wisdom.

**15** Hiram king of Tyre sent his servants to Solomon when he heard that he had been anointed king in place of his father, for Hiram had always been a friend of David.[^5-14]

**16** Solomon sent this message to Hiram:

**17** "You know that my father David could not build a house for the name of Yahweh[^5-15] his God because of the wars that surrounded him, until Yahweh put his enemies[^5-16] under the soles of my feet.[^5-17]

**18** But now Yahweh my God has given me rest on every side. There is no adversary[^5-18] and no misfortune.

**19** So I intend to build a house for the name of Yahweh my God, as Yahweh told my father David: 'Your son, whom I will put on your throne in your place, will build the house for my name.'

**20** So now give orders for cedars to be cut for me from Lebanon. My servants will work with your servants, and I will pay you whatever wages you set for your servants. For you know that there is no one among us who knows how to cut timber like the Sidonians."

**21** When Hiram heard Solomon's message, he was very glad and said, "Blessed be Yahweh today, who has given David a wise son over this great people."

**22** Hiram sent word to Solomon: "I have heard the message you sent me. I will do all you want in the matter of cedar timber and cypress timber.[^5-19]

**23** My servants will bring them down from Lebanon to the sea, and I will make them into rafts on the sea to go to the place you tell me. There I will break them up, and you can take them away. And you will do what I want by providing food for my household."

**24** So Hiram[^5-20] gave Solomon all the cedar timber and cypress timber he wanted.

**25** Solomon gave Hiram twenty thousand kors of wheat as food for his household, and twenty kors of pressed olive oil.[^5-21] Solomon gave this to Hiram year after year.

**26** Yahweh gave Solomon wisdom, as he had promised him. There was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and the two of them made a treaty.[^5-22]

**27** King Solomon conscripted forced labor[^5-23] from all Israel, and the forced labor numbered thirty thousand men.

**28** He sent them to Lebanon in shifts of ten thousand a month. They spent a month in Lebanon and two months at home. Adoniram was in charge of the forced labor.

**29** Solomon had seventy thousand men who carried loads and eighty thousand who cut stone in the hills,

**30** besides Solomon's 3,300 chief officers[^5-24] who were in charge of the work and directed the people doing the work.

**31** At the king's command they quarried large stones, costly stones, to lay the foundation of the house with dressed stone.[^5-25]

**32** Solomon's builders and Hiram's builders and the men of Gebal[^5-26] cut them, and they prepared the timber and the stones to build the house.

[^5-1]: The Euphrates. Verses 1–14 are 4:21–34 in most English Bibles, and verses 15–32 are 5:1–18.
[^5-2]: Literally "from the River, the land of the Philistines". 2 Chronicles 9:26 reads "from the River to the land of the Philistines".
[^5-3]: Literally "Solomon's bread".
[^5-4]: A kor was probably about 6 bushels (220 liters). Also in verse 25.
[^5-5]: The identification of some of these animals is uncertain. *Yachmur* (roebuck) may be a fallow deer; *barburim* (fowl) may be geese, cuckoos or guinea fowl.
[^5-6]: Literally "beyond the River", that is, west of the Euphrates. Also later in the verse.
[^5-7]: Or "forty thousand teams of chariot horses". 2 Chronicles 9:25 reads "four thousand stalls".
[^5-8]: Or "horses". Hebrew *parashim*.
[^5-9]: Hebrew *rekhesh*: swift horses, perhaps those used for chariots.
[^5-10]: Literally "to the place where it would be". Or "to the place where he (the king) would be".
[^5-11]: Literally "breadth of heart".
[^5-12]: Literally "the sons of the east": the peoples east of Israel, in Arabia and Mesopotamia.
[^5-13]: Ethan the Ezrahite and Heman are named in the titles of Psalms 88 and 89. *Mahol* may be a name, or the phrase may mean "members of the guild of musicians" (Hebrew *machol*, "dance").
[^5-14]: Literally "a lover of David all the days". The word "love" was used of loyal allies.
[^5-15]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles. "A house for the name of Yahweh" is a temple.
[^5-16]: Literally "them".
[^5-17]: The Hebrew text is written "his feet" but read "my feet".
[^5-18]: Hebrew *satan*, "adversary, opponent".
[^5-19]: Hebrew *berosh*, a tall conifer: perhaps the cypress or the juniper; traditionally "fir". Also in verse 24.
[^5-20]: Hebrew *Hirom*, another spelling of the name. Also in verse 32.
[^5-21]: Literally "beaten oil": oil from olives crushed by hand, the finest grade (Exodus 27:20). 2 Chronicles 2:9 (English 2:10) reads "twenty thousand baths of oil".
[^5-22]: Hebrew *berit*, "covenant".
[^5-23]: Hebrew *mas*: work gangs conscripted to labor for the king (4:6). Also in verse 28.

[^5-25]: Hebrew *gazit*: stone cut and dressed to shape.
[^5-26]: Hebrew *ha-givlim*, the people of Gebal (Byblos), a Phoenician city north of Sidon known for its craftsmen.

## Chapter 6

*Status: draft*

**1** In the four hundred and eightieth year[^6-1] after the Israelites came out of the land of Egypt, in the fourth year of Solomon's reign over Israel, in the month of Ziv,[^6-2] which is the second month, he began to build[^6-3] the house for Yahweh.[^6-4]

**2** The house that King Solomon built for Yahweh was sixty cubits[^6-5] long, twenty wide and thirty cubits high.

**3** The porch[^6-6] in front of the main hall[^6-7] of the house was twenty cubits long, across the width of the house, and it extended ten cubits in front of the house.[^6-8]

**4** He made windows for the house with recessed frames.[^6-9]

**5** Against the wall of the house he built a side structure[^6-10] all around, against the walls of the house all around the main hall and the inner sanctuary,[^6-11] and he made side rooms[^6-12] all around.

**6** The lowest story was five cubits wide, the middle one six cubits wide and the third seven cubits wide. For he made offsets[^6-13] all around the outside of the house, so that the beams would not be set into the walls of the house.

**7** When the house was built, it was built with stone finished at the quarry.[^6-14] No hammer or axe or any iron tool was heard in the house while it was being built.

**8** The entrance to the middle story of side rooms[^6-15] was on the south side of the house.[^6-16] Stairs[^6-17] went up to the middle story, and from the middle story to the third.

**9** So he built the house and finished it. He roofed the house with beams and planks of cedar.[^6-18]

**10** He built the side structure against the whole house, each story five cubits high, and it was joined to the house with cedar beams.

**11** Then the word of Yahweh came to Solomon:

**12** "As for this house you are building, if you walk in my statutes, carry out my rulings and keep all my commandments by walking in them, I will fulfill through you the promise I made to your father David.[^6-19]

**13** I will live[^6-20] among the Israelites, and I will not abandon my people Israel."

**14** So Solomon built the house and finished it.

**15** He lined the walls of the house on the inside with boards of cedar. From the floor of the house up to the ceiling[^6-21] he covered them with wood on the inside, and he covered the floor of the house with boards of cypress.[^6-22]

**16** He built off twenty cubits at the rear of the house with boards of cedar from the floor up to the ceiling.[^6-23] He built this inside as an inner sanctuary, as the most holy place.[^6-24]

**17** The house, that is, the main hall in front of it,[^6-25] was forty cubits long.

**18** The cedar inside the house was carved with gourds and open flowers.[^6-26] All was cedar. No stone was seen.

**19** He prepared the inner sanctuary inside the house, to put the ark of the covenant of Yahweh there.

**20** The inside of the inner sanctuary[^6-27] was twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide and twenty cubits high, and he overlaid it with pure gold.[^6-28] He also overlaid the altar with cedar.[^6-29]

**21** Solomon overlaid the inside of the house with pure gold. He stretched chains of gold across the front of the inner sanctuary, and he overlaid it with gold.

**22** He overlaid the whole house with gold, until the whole house was finished. He also overlaid with gold the whole altar that belonged to the inner sanctuary.[^6-30]

**23** In the inner sanctuary he made two cherubim[^6-31] of olive wood,[^6-32] each ten cubits high.

**24** One wing of the cherub was five cubits long, and the other wing of the cherub was five cubits long: ten cubits from the tip of one wing to the tip of the other.

**25** The second cherub also measured ten cubits. Both cherubim had the same measurements and the same shape.

**26** The height of the one cherub was ten cubits, and so was that of the other cherub.

**27** He put the cherubim in the middle of the inner room of the house.[^6-33] The wings of the cherubim were spread out, so that the wing of one touched the one wall and the wing of the other cherub touched the other wall, while their wings touched each other in the middle of the room, wing to wing.

**28** He overlaid the cherubim with gold.

**29** On all the walls of the house all around he carved engravings of cherubim, palm trees and open flowers, in both the inner and the outer rooms.[^6-34]

**30** He overlaid the floor of the house with gold, in both the inner and the outer rooms.

**31** For the entrance of the inner sanctuary he made doors of olive wood. The lintel and the doorposts formed a fifth.[^6-35]

**32** On the two doors of olive wood he carved cherubim, palm trees and open flowers, and he overlaid them with gold. He spread the gold over the cherubim and the palm trees.[^6-36]

**33** In the same way, for the entrance of the main hall he made doorposts of olive wood, a fourth.[^6-37]

**34** There were two doors of cypress wood. The two leaves of the one door turned on pivots, and the two leaves[^6-38] of the other door turned on pivots.

**35** He carved cherubim, palm trees and open flowers on them, and he overlaid them with gold fitted evenly over the carving.

**36** He built the inner courtyard with three courses of dressed stone[^6-39] and one course of cedar beams.

**37** In the fourth year the foundation of the house of Yahweh was laid, in the month of Ziv.

**38** In the eleventh year, in the month of Bul,[^6-40] which is the eighth month, the house was finished in all its details and according to all its specifications.[^6-41] He spent seven years building it.
