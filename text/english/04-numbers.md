## Chapter 1

*Status: draft*

**1** The LORD[^1-1] spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tent of meeting,[^1-2] on the first day of the second month, in the second year after the Israelites came out of the land of Egypt. He said,

**2** "Take a census[^1-3] of the whole community of the Israelites by their clans and their ancestral families,[^1-4] counting the names of every male, one by one.[^1-5]

**3** You and Aaron are to register[^1-6] them by their divisions,[^1-7] all those in Israel who are twenty years old or more and able to serve in the army.

**4** A man from each tribe is to be with you, each of them the head of his ancestral family.

**5** These are the names of the men who are to stand with you: from Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur;

**6** from Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai;

**7** from Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab;

**8** from Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar;

**9** from Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon;

**10** from the sons of Joseph: from Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud; from Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur;

**11** from Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni;

**12** from Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai;

**13** from Asher, Pagiel son of Ochran;

**14** from Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel;

**15** from Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan."

**16** These were the men chosen from the community, the leaders of their ancestral tribes. They were the heads of the clans[^1-8] of Israel.

**17** Moses and Aaron took these men who had been named,

**18** and on the first day of the second month they assembled the whole community. The people recorded their descent[^1-9] by their clans and their ancestral families, and those twenty years old or more were counted by name, one by one,

**19** as the LORD had commanded Moses. So he registered them in the wilderness of Sinai.

**20** From the descendants of Reuben, Israel's firstborn, according to their records of descent[^1-10] by their clans and their ancestral families, every male twenty years old or more who was able to serve in the army was counted by name, one by one.

**21** Those registered from the tribe of Reuben numbered 46,500.

**22** From the descendants of Simeon, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, those registered were counted by name, one by one: every male twenty years old or more who was able to serve in the army.

**23** Those registered from the tribe of Simeon numbered 59,300.

**24** From the descendants of Gad, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**25** Those registered from the tribe of Gad numbered 45,650.

**26** From the descendants of Judah, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**27** Those registered from the tribe of Judah numbered 74,600.

**28** From the descendants of Issachar, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**29** Those registered from the tribe of Issachar numbered 54,400.

**30** From the descendants of Zebulun, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**31** Those registered from the tribe of Zebulun numbered 57,400.

**32** From the sons of Joseph: from the descendants of Ephraim, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**33** Those registered from the tribe of Ephraim numbered 40,500.

**34** From the descendants of Manasseh, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**35** Those registered from the tribe of Manasseh numbered 32,200.

**36** From the descendants of Benjamin, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**37** Those registered from the tribe of Benjamin numbered 35,400.

**38** From the descendants of Dan, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**39** Those registered from the tribe of Dan numbered 62,700.

**40** From the descendants of Asher, according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**41** Those registered from the tribe of Asher numbered 41,500.

**42** The descendants of Naphtali: according to their records of descent by their clans and their ancestral families, all those twenty years old or more who were able to serve in the army were counted by name.

**43** Those registered from the tribe of Naphtali numbered 53,400.

**44** These were the men registered by Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel, twelve men, each one representing his ancestral family.

**45** All the Israelites registered by their ancestral families, those twenty years old or more, everyone in Israel who was able to serve in the army,

**46** all those registered numbered 603,550.

**47** But the Levites were not registered among them by their ancestral tribe.

**48** The LORD said to Moses,

**49** "Only the tribe of Levi you are not to register. Do not include them in the census[^1-11] among the Israelites.

**50** Instead, put the Levites in charge of the tabernacle of the testimony,[^1-12] all its furnishings and everything that belongs to it. They are to carry the tabernacle and all its furnishings. They are to serve[^1-13] in it, and they are to camp around the tabernacle.

**51** Whenever the tabernacle sets out, the Levites are to take it down. Whenever the tabernacle is to be pitched,[^1-14] the Levites are to set it up. Any outsider[^1-15] who comes near it is to be put to death.

**52** The Israelites are to camp by their divisions, each man in his own camp and each under his own banner.

**53** But the Levites are to camp around the tabernacle of the testimony, so that wrath will not fall on the community of the Israelites. The Levites are to take charge of[^1-16] the tabernacle of the testimony."

**54** The Israelites did this. They did everything just as the LORD had commanded Moses.

[^1-1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^1-2]: Hebrew *ohel mo'ed*: the tent where the LORD met with Moses, here the tabernacle (Exodus 27:21; 40:34–35).
[^1-3]: Literally "Lift up the head"; also in verse 49.
[^1-4]: Literally "by the house of their fathers": an extended family descended from a common ancestor, a smaller unit within the clan.
[^1-5]: Literally "by their skulls", that is, head by head.
[^1-6]: Hebrew *paqad*: count, muster, enroll for service. The same verb, and the noun *pequdim*, "those registered", run through the chapter.
[^1-7]: Literally "by their armies". Also in verse 52.
[^1-8]: Literally "the thousands of Israel". Hebrew *eleph* means "thousand", but it is also used of a clan or of a military unit.
[^1-9]: Hebrew *yityaldu*, "they had themselves recorded by birth", that is, by their descent.
[^1-10]: Hebrew *toledot*, "generations": the record of a person's descendants (Genesis 2:4). Also in verses 22–42.
[^1-11]: Literally "do not lift up their head"; compare verse 2.
[^1-12]: Hebrew *edut*, "testimony": the stone tablets of the covenant kept in the ark (Exodus 25:16). The tabernacle is named after them.
[^1-13]: Hebrew *sharat*, "minister, attend to", used of service at the sanctuary.
[^1-14]: Literally "is to camp".
[^1-15]: Hebrew *zar*, "stranger": here anyone who is not a Levite. Also in 3:10 and 3:38.
[^1-16]: Literally "keep the charge of", that is, guard it and carry out its duties. Hebrew *mishmeret* recurs often in chapters 3–4.

## Chapter 2

*Status: draft*

**1** The LORD[^2-1] said to Moses and Aaron,

**2** "The Israelites are to camp each man under his own banner, with the emblems[^2-2] of their ancestral families. They are to camp around the tent of meeting, facing it at a distance.[^2-3]

**3** On the east side, toward the sunrise,[^2-4] the banner of the camp of Judah is to camp by its divisions. The leader of the people of Judah is Nahshon son of Amminadab.

**4** His division, those registered, numbers 74,600.

**5** The tribe of Issachar is to camp next to him. The leader of the people of Issachar is Nethanel son of Zuar.

**6** His division, those registered, numbers 54,400.

**7** Then the tribe of Zebulun. The leader of the people of Zebulun is Eliab son of Helon.

**8** His division, those registered, numbers 57,400.

**9** All those registered for the camp of Judah, by their divisions, number 186,400. They are to set out first.

**10** On the south side is to be the banner of the camp of Reuben by its divisions. The leader of the people of Reuben is Elizur son of Shedeur.

**11** His division, those registered, numbers 46,500.

**12** The tribe of Simeon is to camp next to him. The leader of the people of Simeon is Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai.

**13** His division, those registered, numbers 59,300.

**14** Then the tribe of Gad. The leader of the people of Gad is Eliasaph son of Reuel.[^2-5]

**15** His division, those registered, numbers 45,650.

**16** All those registered for the camp of Reuben, by their divisions, number 151,450. They are to set out second.

**17** Then the tent of meeting is to set out, with the camp of the Levites in the middle of the camps. They are to set out in the same order as they camp, each man in his place,[^2-6] under their banners.

**18** On the west side[^2-7] is to be the banner of the camp of Ephraim by its divisions. The leader of the people of Ephraim is Elishama son of Ammihud.

**19** His division, those registered, numbers 40,500.

**20** Next to him is the tribe of Manasseh. The leader of the people of Manasseh is Gamaliel son of Pedahzur.

**21** His division, those registered, numbers 32,200.

**22** Then the tribe of Benjamin. The leader of the people of Benjamin is Abidan son of Gideoni.

**23** His division, those registered, numbers 35,400.

**24** All those registered for the camp of Ephraim, by their divisions, number 108,100. They are to set out third.

**25** On the north side is to be the banner of the camp of Dan by its divisions. The leader of the people of Dan is Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.

**26** His division, those registered, numbers 62,700.

**27** The tribe of Asher is to camp next to him. The leader of the people of Asher is Pagiel son of Ochran.

**28** His division, those registered, numbers 41,500.

**29** Then the tribe of Naphtali. The leader of the people of Naphtali is Ahira son of Enan.

**30** His division, those registered, numbers 53,400.

**31** All those registered for the camp of Dan number 157,600. They are to set out last, under their banners."

**32** These were the Israelites registered by their ancestral families. All those registered in the camps, by their divisions, numbered 603,550.

**33** But the Levites were not registered among the Israelites, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

**34** The Israelites did everything just as the LORD had commanded Moses. That is how they camped under their banners, and that is how they set out, each man by his clan, with his ancestral family.

[^2-1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^2-2]: Hebrew *otot*, "signs": probably flags or insignia for each family.
[^2-3]: Or "at a distance all around the tent of meeting".
[^2-4]: The Hebrew has two words for east: *qedmah*, "toward the front", and *mizrachah*, "toward the sunrise".
[^2-5]: In 1:14, 7:42, 7:47 and 10:20 his father's name is Deuel. In Hebrew the letters *d* and *r* look very much alike.
[^2-6]: Literally "each man at his hand", that is, at his position.
[^2-7]: Literally "toward the sea", the Mediterranean, which lay west of Israel.

## Chapter 3

*Status: draft*

**1** These are the generations[^3-1] of Aaron and Moses at the time when the LORD[^3-2] spoke with Moses on Mount Sinai.

**2** These are the names of the sons of Aaron: Nadab the firstborn, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

**3** These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests, whom he ordained[^3-3] to serve as priests.

**4** But Nadab and Abihu died in the LORD's presence when they offered unauthorized fire[^3-4] before the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no sons. So Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests during the lifetime of their father Aaron.[^3-5]

**5** The LORD said to Moses,

**6** "Bring the tribe of Levi near and present them to Aaron the priest, to assist him.

**7** They are to carry out duties[^3-6] for him and for the whole community in front of the tent of meeting, doing the work of the tabernacle.

**8** They are to take care of all the furnishings of the tent of meeting and carry out the duties of the Israelites, doing the work of the tabernacle.

**9** Give the Levites to Aaron and his sons. They are given wholly[^3-7] to him from among the Israelites.

**10** Appoint Aaron and his sons to carry out their priesthood. Any outsider who comes near is to be put to death."

**11** The LORD said to Moses,

**12** "Look, I have taken the Levites from among the Israelites in place of every firstborn, the first to open the womb among the Israelites. The Levites are mine,

**13** for every firstborn is mine. On the day I struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I set apart for myself every firstborn in Israel, both human and animal, as holy. They are to be mine. I am the LORD."

**14** The LORD said to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai,

**15** "Register the Levites by their ancestral families and their clans. Register every male a month old or more."

**16** So Moses registered them at the command[^3-8] of the LORD, as he had been commanded.

**17** These were the sons of Levi by name: Gershon, Kohath and Merari.

**18** These are the names of the sons of Gershon by their clans: Libni and Shimei.

**19** The sons of Kohath by their clans: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.

**20** The sons of Merari by their clans: Mahli and Mushi. These were the clans of the Levites by their ancestral families.

**21** To Gershon belonged the clan of the Libnites and the clan of the Shimeites. These were the clans of the Gershonites.

**22** Those registered, counting every male a month old or more, numbered 7,500.

**23** The clans of the Gershonites were to camp behind the tabernacle, on the west.

**24** The leader of the ancestral family of the Gershonites was Eliasaph son of Lael.

**25** At the tent of meeting the Gershonites were responsible for the tabernacle and the tent,[^3-9] its covering, the screen for the entrance of the tent of meeting,

**26** the hangings of the courtyard, the screen for the entrance of the courtyard that surrounds the tabernacle and the altar, and its ropes: all the work connected with these.

**27** To Kohath belonged the clan of the Amramites, the clan of the Izharites, the clan of the Hebronites and the clan of the Uzzielites. These were the clans of the Kohathites.

**28** Counting every male a month old or more, there were 8,600.[^3-10] They were responsible for the care of the sanctuary.[^3-11]

**29** The clans of the Kohathites were to camp on the south side of the tabernacle.

**30** The leader of the ancestral family of the clans of the Kohathites was Elizaphan son of Uzziel.

**31** They were responsible for the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, the holy utensils used in the service, and the screen:[^3-12] all the work connected with these.

**32** The chief leader of the Levites was Eleazar son of Aaron the priest. He had oversight of those responsible for the care of the sanctuary.

**33** To Merari belonged the clan of the Mahlites and the clan of the Mushites. These were the clans of Merari.

**34** Those registered from them, counting every male a month old or more, numbered 6,200.

**35** The leader of the ancestral family of the clans of Merari was Zuriel son of Abihail. They were to camp on the north side of the tabernacle.

**36** The Merarites were appointed to take charge of the frames of the tabernacle, its crossbars, its posts, its bases and all its equipment, all the work connected with these,

**37** and the posts of the courtyard all around, with their bases, their tent pegs and their ropes.

**38** Those who were to camp in front of the tabernacle on the east, in front of the tent of meeting toward the sunrise, were Moses and Aaron and his sons. They were responsible for the care of the sanctuary, carrying out the duty owed by the Israelites. Any outsider who came near was to be put to death.

**39** All the Levites registered, whom Moses and Aaron[^3-13] registered at the command of the LORD by their clans, every male a month old or more, numbered twenty-two thousand.

**40** The LORD said to Moses, "Register every firstborn male among the Israelites a month old or more, and count the number of their names.

**41** Take the Levites for me in place of all the firstborn among the Israelites, and the livestock of the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the livestock of the Israelites. I am the LORD."

**42** So Moses registered all the firstborn among the Israelites, as the LORD had commanded him.

**43** All the firstborn males a month old or more, counted by name as they were registered, numbered 22,273.

**44** The LORD said to Moses,

**45** "Take the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the Israelites, and the livestock of the Levites in place of their livestock. The Levites are to be mine. I am the LORD.

**46** As the redemption price for the 273 firstborn of the Israelites who outnumber the Levites,

**47** collect five shekels[^3-14] for each one.[^3-15] Collect it by the sanctuary shekel, twenty gerahs[^3-16] to the shekel.

**48** Give the money to Aaron and his sons as the redemption price for those of them who are in excess."

**49** So Moses collected the redemption money from those who were in excess of those redeemed by the Levites.

**50** From the firstborn of the Israelites he collected the money: 1,365 shekels,[^3-17] by the sanctuary shekel.

**51** Moses gave the redemption money to Aaron and his sons at the command of the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

[^3-1]: Hebrew *toledot*: the record of a person's descendants (Genesis 2:4). Only Aaron's sons are then listed.
[^3-2]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^3-3]: Literally "whose hand he filled", an expression for installing a priest in office. "He" is Moses (Exodus 28:41).
[^3-4]: Hebrew *esh zarah*, "strange fire": fire that the LORD had not commanded (Leviticus 10:1–2).
[^3-5]: Literally "on the face of Aaron their father": in his presence, under his supervision.
[^3-6]: Literally "keep his charge and the charge of the whole community". Hebrew *mishmeret*, "charge, duty, guard", runs through this chapter.
[^3-7]: Literally "given, given", an emphatic form. Compare 8:16.
[^3-8]: Literally "at the mouth"; also in verses 39 and 51.
[^3-9]: *The tabernacle* here is probably the inner layer of linen curtains, and *the tent* the layer of goats' hair curtains over it (Exodus 26:1–14).
[^3-10]: The figures in verses 22, 28 and 34 add up to 22,300, but verse 39 gives a total of twenty-two thousand. Some manuscripts of the Septuagint read 8,300 here, which would match that total.
[^3-11]: Literally "keepers of the charge of the holy place"; also in verse 32.
[^3-12]: Probably the veil that hung in front of the ark (4:5).
[^3-13]: In the Hebrew text the word "and Aaron" has dots written above each of its letters, one of the fifteen places in the Hebrew Bible marked in this way. Traditionally the dots mark a word as doubtful. Aaron is not named in the commands of verses 15 and 40.
[^3-14]: A shekel was about 0.4 ounce (11.5 grams) of silver.
[^3-15]: Literally "five, five shekels per skull".
[^3-16]: A gerah was about 1/50 ounce (0.6 gram).
[^3-17]: About 35 pounds (16 kilograms).

## Chapter 4

*Status: draft*

**1** The LORD[^4-1] said to Moses and Aaron,

**2** "Take a census of the Kohathites from among the Levites, by their clans and their ancestral families,

**3** all those from thirty to fifty years old who enter the service[^4-2] to do work at the tent of meeting.

**4** This is the work of the Kohathites at the tent of meeting: the most holy things.[^4-3]

**5** When the camp is to set out, Aaron and his sons are to go in and take down the screening veil[^4-4] and cover the ark of the testimony with it.

**6** Then they are to put a covering of fine leather[^4-5] over it, spread a cloth made entirely of blue[^4-6] on top, and put its poles in place.[^4-7]

**7** Over the table of the Presence[^4-8] they are to spread a blue cloth and put on it the plates, the dishes, the bowls and the jars for drink offerings. The regular bread[^4-9] is to remain on it.

**8** They are to spread a scarlet cloth over them, cover it with a covering of fine leather, and put its poles in place.

**9** They are to take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand for the light, with its lamps, its tongs, its trays, and all the jars for its oil that are used in tending it.

**10** They are to put it and all its utensils in a covering of fine leather and put it on a carrying frame.[^4-10]

**11** Over the gold altar[^4-11] they are to spread a blue cloth, cover it with a covering of fine leather, and put its poles in place.

**12** They are to take all the utensils used for serving in the sanctuary, put them in a blue cloth, cover them with a covering of fine leather, and put them on a carrying frame.

**13** They are to clear the ashes from the altar[^4-12] and spread a purple cloth over it.

**14** They are to put on it all the utensils used in serving at it: the firepans, the forks, the shovels and the basins, all the utensils of the altar. They are to spread a covering of fine leather over it and put its poles in place.[^4-13]

**15** "When Aaron and his sons have finished covering the holy things and all the holy utensils, as the camp is about to set out, the Kohathites are to come and carry them. But they must not touch the holy things, or they will die. These are the things in the tent of meeting that the Kohathites are to carry.

**16** "Eleazar son of Aaron the priest is to have charge of the oil for the light, the fragrant incense, the regular grain offering[^4-14] and the anointing oil. He is to have charge of the whole tabernacle and everything in it, the sanctuary and its furnishings."

**17** The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,

**18** "Do not let the tribe of the clans of the Kohathites be cut off from among the Levites.

**19** Do this for them, so that they will live and not die when they come near the most holy things: Aaron and his sons are to go in and assign each of them to his work and to what he is to carry.

**20** But the Kohathites must not go in to look at the holy things even for a moment,[^4-15] or they will die."

**21** The LORD said to Moses,

**22** "Take a census of the Gershonites as well, by their ancestral families and their clans.

**23** Register those from thirty to fifty years old, all who enter to serve in the work force[^4-16] and do the work at the tent of meeting.

**24** This is the work of the clans of the Gershonites, in serving and in carrying:

**25** they are to carry the curtains of the tabernacle and the tent of meeting, its covering and the covering of fine leather on top of it, the screen for the entrance of the tent of meeting,

**26** the hangings of the courtyard, the screen for the entrance of the gate of the courtyard that surrounds the tabernacle and the altar, their ropes and all the equipment for their service. They are to do all the work that needs to be done with these.

**27** All the service of the Gershonites, all that they carry and all their work, is to be under the direction[^4-17] of Aaron and his sons. You are to assign to them as their duty all that they are to carry.

**28** This is the work of the clans of the Gershonites at the tent of meeting. Their duties are to be under the supervision[^4-18] of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.

**29** "As for the Merarites, register them by their clans and their ancestral families.

**30** Register those from thirty to fifty years old, all who enter the service to do the work of the tent of meeting.

**31** This is what they are responsible for carrying, as all their work at the tent of meeting: the frames of the tabernacle, its crossbars, its posts and its bases,

**32** and the posts of the courtyard all around with their bases, their tent pegs and their ropes, with all their equipment and all the work connected with them. Assign to each man by name the items he is responsible for carrying.

**33** This is the work of the clans of the Merarites, all their work at the tent of meeting, under the supervision of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest."

**34** So Moses and Aaron and the leaders of the community registered the Kohathites by their clans and their ancestral families,

**35** those from thirty to fifty years old, all who entered the service for work at the tent of meeting.

**36** Those registered by their clans numbered 2,750.

**37** These were those registered from the clans of the Kohathites, all who served at the tent of meeting, whom Moses and Aaron registered at the command of the LORD through Moses.

**38** Those registered from the Gershonites by their clans and their ancestral families,

**39** those from thirty to fifty years old, all who entered the service for work at the tent of meeting,

**40** those registered by their clans and their ancestral families, numbered 2,630.

**41** These were those registered from the clans of the Gershonites, all who served at the tent of meeting, whom Moses and Aaron registered at the command of the LORD.

**42** Those registered from the clans of the Merarites by their clans and their ancestral families,

**43** those from thirty to fifty years old, all who entered the service for work at the tent of meeting,

**44** those registered by their clans, numbered 3,200.

**45** These were those registered from the clans of the Merarites, whom Moses and Aaron registered at the command of the LORD through Moses.

**46** All those registered from the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron and the leaders of Israel registered by their clans and their ancestral families,

**47** those from thirty to fifty years old, all who entered to do the work of serving and the work of carrying at the tent of meeting,

**48** numbered 8,580.

**49** At the command of the LORD through Moses they were registered, each man according to his work and what he was to carry. So they were registered[^4-19] as the LORD had commanded Moses.

[^4-1]: *LORD*: God's personal name, Hebrew YHWH, written "the LORD" in capitals as in most English Bibles.
[^4-2]: Literally "who come into the army". Hebrew *tsava*, the word used for military service in chapter 1, here means the work force of the sanctuary. Also in verses 23, 30, 35, 39 and 43.
[^4-3]: Literally "the holy of holies". Or "This is the work of the Kohathites at the tent of meeting concerning the most holy things."
[^4-4]: Hebrew *parokhet*: the veil, the hanging curtain that separated the most holy place from the rest of the tabernacle (Exodus 26:31–33).
[^4-5]: Hebrew *tachash*, of uncertain meaning. It may be the hide of a sea animal (such as the dugong), or leather of a special kind or color. Traditionally "badgers' skins" or "goatskins". Also in verses 8–14 and 25.
[^4-6]: Hebrew *tekhelet*, a costly violet-blue dye; also in verses 7, 9, 11 and 12.
[^4-7]: Literally "put its poles". According to Exodus 25:15 the poles of the ark were never to be removed, so here they are probably adjusted for carrying.
[^4-8]: Literally "the table of the face"; compare "the bread of the Presence" (Exodus 25:30).
[^4-9]: Literally "the bread of continuity", the bread that was always on the table.
[^4-10]: Hebrew *mot*, a pole or bar; here probably a frame for carrying. Also in verse 12.
[^4-11]: The altar of incense (Exodus 30:1–5).
[^4-12]: Literally "remove the fat from the altar", that is, the ashes soaked with fat. This is the bronze altar for burnt offerings.
