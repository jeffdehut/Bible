## Chapter 1

*Status: draft*

**1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus[^1-1] by the will of God, to God's holy people[^1-2] who are [in Ephesus][^1-3] and are faithful[^1-4] in Christ Jesus:

**2** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**3** Blessed be[^1-5] the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual blessing[^1-6] in the heavenly places[^1-7] in Christ,

**4** just as he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, so that we would be holy and blameless before him in love.[^1-8]

**5** He destined us in advance to be adopted as his own children[^1-9] through Jesus Christ, according to the good pleasure of his will,

**6** to the praise of his glorious grace,[^1-10] which he has freely given us in the Beloved.

**7** In him we have redemption[^1-11] through his blood, the forgiveness of our wrongdoings, according to the riches of his grace,

**8** which he lavished on us in all wisdom and understanding,[^1-12]

**9** making known to us the mystery[^1-13] of his will, according to his good pleasure, which he purposed in him,[^1-14]

**10** as a plan for the fullness of the times:[^1-15] to bring all things together under one head[^1-16] in Christ, the things in heaven and the things on earth, in him.

**11** In him we were also made his portion,[^1-17] having been destined in advance according to the purpose of the one who works all things according to the counsel of his will,

**12** so that we, who were the first to hope[^1-18] in Christ, would be for the praise of his glory.

**13** In him you also, when you heard the message of truth, the good news of your salvation, and when you believed in him,[^1-19] were sealed[^1-20] with the promised Holy Spirit.[^1-21]

**14** He is the first installment[^1-22] of our inheritance, until the redemption of God's own possession,[^1-23] to the praise of his glory.

**15** For this reason I too, since I heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all God's holy people,[^1-24]

**16** do not stop giving thanks for you, remembering you in my prayers,

**17** that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the glorious Father,[^1-25] may give you the Spirit[^1-26] of wisdom and revelation as you come to know him,

**18** with the eyes of your heart enlightened, so that you may know what is the hope of his calling,[^1-27] what are the riches of the glory of his inheritance among God's holy people,[^1-28]

**19** and what is the surpassing greatness of his power toward us who believe, according to the working of his mighty strength,[^1-29]

**20** which he put to work in Christ when he raised him from the dead and seated him at his right hand in the heavenly places,

**21** far above every ruler and authority and power and dominion,[^1-30] and above every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the age to come.

**22** And he put everything under his feet,[^1-31] and he gave him as head over all things to the church,

**23** which is his body, the fullness of the one who fills everything in every way.[^1-32]

[^1-1]: *Christ*: Greek *Christos*, "anointed one", the Greek equivalent of "Messiah". In Paul's letters it usually works as a name, so this translation writes "Christ". Paul's order, "Christ Jesus" or "Jesus Christ", is kept as the Greek has it.
[^1-2]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "saints"; also in verses 15 and 18.
[^1-3]: The SBLGNT prints "in Ephesus" in brackets [ ], marking the words as uncertain; this translation does the same. A reading given by Westcott and Hort leaves them out, as do some of the earliest manuscripts (Papyrus 46 and the first hands of Codex Sinaiticus and Codex Vaticanus). Without them the verse reads "to God's holy people who are also faithful in Christ Jesus". This has led some to suggest that the letter was meant for more than one church.
[^1-4]: Or "and are believers". Greek *pistos* means "faithful, trustworthy" or "believing".
[^1-5]: Or "Praise be to". Greek *eulogetos*, from the same root as *blessed* and *blessing* later in the verse.
[^1-6]: Or "every blessing of the Spirit".
[^1-7]: Literally "in the heavenlies"; also in verse 20.
[^1-8]: Or, taking "in love" with verse 5, "… blameless before him. In love he destined us …".
[^1-9]: Greek *huiothesia*, "adoption as sons", the legal standing of an heir. Literally "for adoption as sons through Jesus Christ to him".
[^1-10]: Literally "to the praise of the glory of his grace". *Freely given*: the verb (*charitoo*) is formed from *charis*, "grace": "with which he graced us".
[^1-11]: Greek *apolytrosis*, release, as of a slave or captive set free by a payment; also in verse 14.
[^1-12]: Or "… lavished on us. In all wisdom and understanding he made known …", taking these words with verse 9.
[^1-13]: Greek *mysterion*, a secret plan that was hidden until God revealed it.
[^1-14]: That is, in Christ. Or "which he purposed in himself".
[^1-15]: Literally "for an administration of the fullness of the times". Greek *oikonomia*, the management of a household; here a plan that God carries out when the time is complete.
[^1-16]: Greek *anakephalaioomai*, "to sum up, to gather together", related to *kephale*, "head" (verse 22).
[^1-17]: Or "In him we have also received an inheritance". Greek *eklerothemen*, "we were allotted", which can mean being given a share or being chosen as someone's share.
[^1-18]: Literally "who hoped beforehand". "We" may be Paul and other Jewish believers, who hoped in the Messiah before the Gentiles addressed as "you" in verse 13; or it may mean all believers.
[^1-19]: Or "In him you also, when you heard … ; in him, when you believed, you were sealed". The Greek repeats "in whom".
[^1-20]: A seal marked a thing as belonging to its owner and guaranteed it.
[^1-21]: Literally "with the Holy Spirit of the promise".
[^1-22]: Greek *arrabon*, a deposit or down payment that guarantees the full amount will follow.
[^1-23]: Literally "for the redemption of the possession": either the redemption of God's people as his own possession, or the time when believers take possession of their inheritance.
[^1-24]: Some manuscripts leave out "your love", reading "your faith in the Lord Jesus and toward all God's holy people".
[^1-25]: Literally "the Father of glory".
[^1-26]: Or "a spirit". *As you come to know him*: literally "in knowledge of him".
[^1-27]: That is, the hope to which God has called you.
[^1-28]: Literally "in the holy ones".
[^1-29]: Literally "the working of the might of his strength".
[^1-30]: Greek *arche*, *exousia*, *dynamis*, *kyriotes*. These words were used for human rulers and also for ranks of spiritual powers.
[^1-31]: Psalm 8:7 (8:6 in most English Bibles).
[^1-32]: Literally "who fills all things in all". The verb can also be read as passive: "the fullness of the one who is being filled, all in all".

## Chapter 2

*Status: draft*

**1** And you were dead in your wrongdoings and sins,

**2** in which you once lived,[^2-1] following the ways of this world,[^2-2] following the ruler of the authority of the air, the spirit that is now at work in those who disobey.[^2-3]

**3** Among them we too all once lived in the desires of our flesh,[^2-4] doing what the flesh and the mind wanted, and we were by nature children of wrath,[^2-5] like the rest.

**4** But God, who is rich in mercy, because of the great love with which he loved us,

**5** even when we were dead in our wrongdoings, made us alive together with Christ (by grace you have been saved)

**6** and raised us up with him and seated us with him in the heavenly places[^2-6] in Christ Jesus,

**7** so that in the ages to come he might show the surpassing riches of his grace in his kindness toward us in Christ Jesus.

**8** For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this[^2-7] is not from yourselves; it is the gift of God.

**9** It is not from works, so that no one can boast.

**10** For we are what he has made,[^2-8] created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared in advance so that we would live in them.[^2-9]

**11** So remember that at one time you, the Gentiles in the flesh,[^2-10] called "the uncircumcision" by what is called "the circumcision", which is made in the flesh by human hands,

**12** remember that at that time you were without Christ,[^2-11] shut out from the citizenship of Israel and strangers to the covenants of the promise, with no hope and without God in the world.

**13** But now in Christ Jesus you who once were far away have been brought near by the blood of Christ.

**14** For he himself is our peace. He made the two one and broke down the dividing wall that kept them apart,[^2-12] the hostility, in his flesh,[^2-13]

**15** abolishing the law of commandments expressed in decrees, so that in himself he might create one new humanity[^2-14] out of the two, making peace,

**16** and might reconcile both of them to God in one body through the cross, by which he put the hostility to death.[^2-15]

**17** He came and proclaimed the good news of peace to you who were far away and peace to those who were near.[^2-16]

**18** For through him we both have access to the Father in one Spirit.

**19** So then you are no longer strangers and foreigners,[^2-17] but you are fellow citizens with God's holy people[^2-18] and members of God's household,

**20** built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with Christ Jesus himself as the cornerstone.[^2-19]

**21** In him the whole building,[^2-20] joined together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord.

**22** In him you too are being built together into a dwelling place for God in the Spirit.[^2-21]

[^2-1]: Literally "walked", a common biblical picture for a way of life; also in verse 10.
[^2-2]: Literally "according to the age of this world".
[^2-3]: Literally "in the sons of disobedience".
[^2-4]: Greek *sarx*, "flesh": here human nature in its weakness and opposition to God. In verses 11 and 14 the word has its physical sense.
[^2-5]: That is, people facing God's wrath. *Children of*, like "sons of" in verse 2, is a Hebrew way of saying what belongs to someone or marks them.
[^2-6]: Literally "in the heavenlies".
[^2-7]: "This" is neuter in Greek and may refer to the whole of being saved by grace through faith, or to faith itself.
[^2-8]: Greek *poiema*, "a thing made, a work".
[^2-9]: Literally "walk in them".
[^2-10]: That is, Gentiles by physical birth.
[^2-11]: Or "without the Messiah".
[^2-12]: Literally "the middle wall of the partition".
[^2-13]: Or "broke down the dividing wall that kept them apart. In his flesh he abolished the hostility, the law …", taking "the hostility" with verse 15.
[^2-14]: Literally "one new man".
[^2-15]: Or "having put the hostility to death in himself".
[^2-16]: Compare Isaiah 57:19, "Peace, peace, to the far and to the near", and 52:7.
[^2-17]: Greek *paroikoi*, foreigners who live in a place without the rights of citizens.
[^2-18]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "saints".
[^2-19]: Greek *akrogoniaios*, the chief stone at the corner of a foundation; some take it to mean the top stone of a building. Compare Isaiah 28:16.
[^2-20]: Or "every building".
[^2-21]: Or "by the Spirit".

## Chapter 3

*Status: draft*

**1** For this reason I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus for the sake of you Gentiles—[^3-1]

**2** Surely you have heard[^3-2] of the stewardship[^3-3] of God's grace that was given to me for you.

**3** By revelation the mystery[^3-4] was made known to me,[^3-5] as I have written briefly before.[^3-6]

**4** When you read this, you can understand my insight into the mystery of Christ,

**5** which in other generations was not made known to human beings[^3-7] as it has now been revealed by the Spirit to his holy apostles and prophets:

**6** that the Gentiles are heirs together with them, members together of one body, and sharers together in the promise in Christ Jesus through the good news.

**7** Of this good news I became a servant[^3-8] according to the gift of God's grace, which was given to me by the working of his power.

**8** To me, the very least[^3-9] of all God's holy people,[^3-10] this grace was given: to proclaim to the Gentiles the good news of the unfathomable riches of Christ,

**9** and to bring to light for everyone[^3-11] what is the plan[^3-12] of the mystery that for ages past was hidden in God, who created all things,[^3-13]

**10** so that through the church the wisdom of God in all its variety might now be made known to the rulers and the authorities in the heavenly places,[^3-14]

**11** according to the eternal purpose[^3-15] that he carried out in Christ Jesus our Lord.

**12** In him we have boldness and access with confidence through faith in him.[^3-16]

**13** So I ask you not to lose heart[^3-17] over my sufferings for you, which are your glory.

**14** For this reason I kneel before the Father,[^3-18]

**15** from whom every family[^3-19] in heaven and on earth gets its name,

**16** that, according to the riches of his glory, he may grant you to be strengthened with power through his Spirit in your inner being,[^3-20]

**17** and that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith, in love,[^3-21] so that you, rooted and firmly founded,

**18** may have strength to grasp, with all God's holy people, what is the breadth and length and height and depth,

**19** and to know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge, so that you may be filled to all the fullness of God.[^3-22]

**20** Now to him who is able to do far more than all we ask or imagine, according to the power that is at work in us,

**21** to him be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus[^3-23] for all generations, forever and ever.[^3-24] Amen.

[^3-6]: Probably earlier in this letter (1:9–10; 2:11–22).
[^3-7]: Literally "to the sons of men".
[^3-8]: Greek *diakonos*, "servant", traditionally "minister".
[^3-9]: Literally "the leaster", a comparative formed from a superlative.
[^3-10]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "saints"; also in verse 18.
[^3-11]: Some manuscripts leave out "for everyone", reading "to bring to light what is the plan …".
[^3-12]: Greek *oikonomia*, as in verse 2 and 1:10.
[^3-13]: Many manuscripts add "through Jesus Christ".
[^3-14]: Literally "in the heavenlies".
[^3-15]: Literally "the purpose of the ages".

## Chapter 4

*Status: draft*

**1** So I, the prisoner in the Lord, urge you to live[^4-1] in a way worthy of the calling to which you have been called,

**2** with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love,

**3** making every effort to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.

**4** There is one body and one Spirit, just as you were also called to one hope when you were called;

**5** one Lord, one faith, one baptism;

**6** one God and Father of all, who is over all and through all and in all.[^4-2]

**7** But to each one of us grace was given according to the measure of Christ's gift.

**8** Therefore it says,[^4-3] "When he went up on high, he led captives away;[^4-4] he gave gifts to people."[^4-5]

**9** Now what does "he went up" mean, except that he also went down[^4-6] into the lower parts of the earth?[^4-7]

**10** The one who went down is the same one who also went up far above all the heavens, so that he might fill all things.

**11** And he gave some to be apostles, some prophets, some evangelists,[^4-8] some shepherds[^4-9] and teachers,

**12** to equip God's holy people[^4-10] for the work of service, for building up the body of Christ,[^4-11]

**13** until we all reach the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, mature adulthood,[^4-12] the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ,

**14** so that we may no longer be infants, tossed by the waves and carried around by every wind of teaching, by human trickery,[^4-13] by craftiness in the service of deceitful scheming.

**15** Instead, speaking the truth[^4-14] in love, we are to grow up in every way into him who is the head, Christ.

**16** From him the whole body, joined and held together by every ligament that supplies it,[^4-15] according to the working of each part in its measure, makes the body grow, building itself up in love.

**17** So I say this and insist on it in the Lord: you must no longer live as the Gentiles[^4-16] live, in the futility of their minds.

**18** They are darkened in their understanding and alienated from the life of God, because of the ignorance that is in them, because of the hardness of their hearts.

**19** Having lost all feeling, they have given themselves over to sensuality, to practice every kind of impurity with greed.[^4-17]

**20** But that is not how you learned Christ,

**21** assuming that you have heard him[^4-18] and were taught in him, as the truth is in Jesus:

**22** that you were to put off your old self,[^4-19] which belonged to your former way of life and is being ruined by its deceitful desires,[^4-20]

**23** and to be renewed in the spirit of your minds,

**24** and to put on the new self, created to be like God[^4-21] in the righteousness and holiness that come from the truth.

**25** So, putting away falsehood, speak the truth, each of you, with your neighbor,[^4-22] because we are members of one another.

**26** Be angry, and do not sin.[^4-23] Do not let the sun go down on your anger,

**27** and do not give the devil a foothold.[^4-24]

**28** Whoever steals must not steal any longer. Instead, let them work hard, doing what is good with their own hands, so that they will have something to share with anyone in need.

**29** Let no rotten[^4-25] word come out of your mouths, but only what is good for building up, as the need requires, so that it may give grace to those who hear.

**30** And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God,[^4-26] with whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.

**31** Let all bitterness and rage and anger and shouting and slander be put away from you, along with all malice.

**32** Be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, just as God in Christ forgave you.[^4-27]

## Chapter 5

*Status: draft*

**1** So be imitators of God, as beloved children,

**2** and live[^5-1] in love, just as Christ also loved us and gave himself up for us,[^5-2] an offering and a sacrifice to God, a pleasing aroma.[^5-3]

**3** But sexual immorality and any kind of impurity or greed must not even be mentioned among you, as is fitting for God's holy people,[^5-4]

**4** and there must be no obscenity or foolish talk or crude joking, which are out of place, but rather thanksgiving.

**5** For you can be sure of this: no sexually immoral or impure or greedy person (that is, an idolater) has any inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God.[^5-5]

**6** Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things the wrath of God comes on those who disobey.[^5-6]

**7** So do not be partners with them.

**8** For once you were darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Live as children of light

**9** (for the fruit of the light[^5-7] is found in all goodness and righteousness and truth),

**10** discerning what pleases the Lord.

**11** Take no part in the fruitless works of darkness, but rather expose them.

**12** For it is shameful even to speak of the things they do in secret.

**13** But everything that is exposed by the light becomes visible,

**14** for everything that becomes visible is light.[^5-8] Therefore it says,[^5-9] "Wake up, sleeper, and rise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you."[^5-10]

**15** So watch carefully how you live, not as unwise people but as wise,

**16** making the most of the time,[^5-11] because the days are evil.

**17** For this reason do not be foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord is.

**18** And do not get drunk with wine, which leads to reckless living, but be filled with the Spirit,[^5-12]

**19** speaking to one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making music with your heart to the Lord,

**20** always giving thanks for everything to God the Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ,

**21** submitting[^5-13] to one another in the fear of Christ.[^5-14]

**22** Wives, submit to your own husbands as to the Lord,[^5-15]

**23** because the husband is the head[^5-16] of the wife as Christ also is the head of the church, he himself being the savior of the body.

**24** But as the church submits to Christ, so also wives should submit to their husbands in everything.

**25** Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ also loved the church and gave himself up for her,

**26** to make her holy, cleansing her by the washing of water with the word,[^5-17]

**27** so that he might present the church to himself in splendor, without spot or wrinkle or anything of the kind, but so that she would be holy and blameless.

**28** In the same way, husbands also ought to love their wives as their own bodies. He who loves his wife loves himself.

**29** For no one ever hated his own flesh,[^5-18] but he nourishes it and cares for it, just as Christ[^5-19] does the church,

**30** because we are members of his body.[^5-20]

**31** "For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh."[^5-21]

**32** This mystery[^5-22] is great, but I am speaking about Christ and the church.

**33** However, each one of you also must love his wife as himself, and the wife must respect[^5-23] her husband.

[^5-3]: Literally "for a smell of fragrance", the language of the sacrifices in the Old Testament (Exodus 29:18; Leviticus 1:9).
[^5-4]: Literally "for holy ones", traditionally "saints".

## Chapter 6

*Status: draft*

**1** Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.

**2** "Honor your father and mother" (this is the first commandment with a promise),

**3** "so that it may go well with you and you may live long on the earth."[^6-1]

**4** Fathers,[^6-2] do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord.

**5** Slaves,[^6-3] obey your earthly masters[^6-4] with fear and trembling, in sincerity of heart, as you obey Christ,

**6** not only when they are watching, to please people,[^6-5] but as slaves of Christ, doing the will of God from the heart,[^6-6]

**7** serving with goodwill, as serving the Lord and not people,

**8** knowing that whatever good each one does, this they will receive back from the Lord, whether they are slave or free.

**9** And masters, treat them in the same way, and stop threatening them, knowing that the one who is Master[^6-7] of both them and you is in heaven, and with him there is no favoritism.

**10** Finally,[^6-8] be strong in the Lord and in the strength of his might.

**11** Put on the full armor of God, so that you can stand against the schemes of the devil.

**12** For our struggle[^6-9] is not against flesh and blood,[^6-10] but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the world rulers of this darkness,[^6-11] against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places.[^6-12]

**13** Therefore take up the full armor of God, so that you may be able to resist on the evil day, and having done everything,[^6-13] to stand.

**14** Stand, then, having fastened truth around your waist like a belt and put on the breastplate of righteousness,[^6-14]

**15** and having put on your feet the readiness that comes from the good news of peace.[^6-15]

**16** In all circumstances[^6-16] take up the shield of faith, with which you will be able to put out all the flaming arrows of the evil one.

**17** And take the helmet of salvation[^6-17] and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.

**18** With every kind of prayer and request, pray at all times in the Spirit. To this end stay alert, with all perseverance and with requests for all God's holy people,[^6-18]

**19** and for me, that a message may be given to me when I open my mouth, to make known with boldness the mystery of the good news,

**20** for which I am an ambassador in chains, so that I may speak of it boldly, as I ought to speak.

**21** So that you too may know how things are with me and what I am doing, Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful servant[^6-19] in the Lord, will tell you everything.

**22** I am sending[^6-20] him to you for this very purpose, so that you may know how we are and so that he may encourage your hearts.

**23** Peace to the brothers and sisters,[^6-21] and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**24** Grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ with an undying love.[^6-22]

[^6-1]: Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16, as worded in the Septuagint. *On the earth*: or "in the land". The commandment continues "that Yahweh your God is giving you".
[^6-2]: Greek *pateres*, "fathers", a word that can sometimes mean "parents".
[^6-3]: Greek *douloi*, "slaves"; also in verses 6 and 8. Slavery was part of the social order of the Roman world, and many members of the early churches were slaves or owned slaves. This passage addresses both (verses 5–9).
[^6-4]: Literally "masters according to the flesh". Greek *kyrioi*, "lords, masters, owners", the same word as "Lord" in verses 7–9.
[^6-5]: Literally "not according to eye-service, as people-pleasers".
[^6-6]: Or "doing the will of God, serving from the heart with goodwill", taking "from the heart" with verse 7. Literally "from the soul".
[^6-7]: Or "Lord". Greek *kyrios*, the same word as "masters" earlier in the verse.
[^6-8]: Or "From now on". Many manuscripts read "Finally, my brothers and sisters".
[^6-9]: Literally "wrestling".
[^6-10]: Literally "blood and flesh", that is, human beings.
[^6-11]: Many manuscripts read "of the darkness of this age".
[^6-12]: Literally "in the heavenlies".
[^6-13]: Or "having overcome everything".
[^6-14]: Compare Isaiah 11:5; 59:17.
[^6-15]: Compare Isaiah 52:7.
[^6-16]: Many manuscripts read "Above all".
[^6-17]: Compare Isaiah 59:17.
[^6-18]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "saints".
[^6-19]: Greek *diakonos*, "servant", traditionally "minister".
[^6-20]: Literally "I sent", written from the point of view of the readers, who will receive the letter when Tychicus arrives.
[^6-21]: Greek *adelphoi*, "brothers", used of the believers, men and women alike.
[^6-22]: Literally "in incorruptibility". The phrase may describe their love, or it may go with "grace", meaning "in immortality". Many manuscripts add "Amen".
