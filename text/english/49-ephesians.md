## Chapter 1

*Status: draft*

**1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, to God's holy people[^1-1] who are [in Ephesus][^1-2] and are faithful[^1-3] in Christ Jesus:

**2** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

**3** Blessed be[^1-4] the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual blessing[^1-5] in the heavenly places[^1-6] in Christ,

**4** just as he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, so that we would be holy and blameless before him in love.[^1-7]

**5** He destined us in advance to be adopted as his own children[^1-8] through Jesus Christ, according to the good pleasure of his will,

**6** to the praise of his glorious grace,[^1-9] which he has freely given us in the Beloved.

**7** In him we have redemption[^1-10] through his blood, the forgiveness of our wrongdoings, according to the riches of his grace,

**8** which he lavished on us in all wisdom and understanding,[^1-11]

**9** making known to us the mystery[^1-12] of his will, according to his good pleasure, which he purposed in him,[^1-13]

**10** as a plan for the fullness of the times:[^1-14] to bring all things together under one head[^1-15] in Christ, the things in heaven and the things on earth, in him.

**11** In him we were also made his portion,[^1-16] having been destined in advance according to the purpose of the one who works all things according to the counsel of his will,

**12** so that we, who were the first to hope[^1-17] in Christ, would be for the praise of his glory.

**13** In him you also, when you heard the message of truth, the good news of your salvation, and when you believed in him,[^1-18] were sealed[^1-19] with the promised Holy Spirit.[^1-20]

**14** He is the first installment[^1-21] of our inheritance, until the redemption of God's own possession,[^1-22] to the praise of his glory.

**15** For this reason I too, since I heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all God's holy people,[^1-23]

**16** do not stop giving thanks for you, remembering you in my prayers,

**17** that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the glorious Father,[^1-24] may give you the Spirit[^1-25] of wisdom and revelation as you come to know him,

**18** with the eyes of your heart enlightened, so that you may know what is the hope of his calling,[^1-26] what are the riches of the glory of his inheritance among God's holy people,[^1-27]

**19** and what is the surpassing greatness of his power toward us who believe, according to the working of his mighty strength,[^1-28]

**20** which he put to work in Christ when he raised him from the dead and seated him at his right hand in the heavenly places,

**21** far above every ruler and authority and power and dominion,[^1-29] and above every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the age to come.

**22** And he put everything under his feet,[^1-30] and he gave him as head over all things to the church,

**23** which is his body, the fullness of the one who fills everything in every way.[^1-31]

[^1-1]: Literally "the holy ones", traditionally "saints"; also in verses 15 and 18.
[^1-2]: The SBLGNT prints "in Ephesus" in brackets [ ], marking the words as uncertain; this translation does the same. A reading given by Westcott and Hort leaves them out, as do some of the earliest manuscripts (Papyrus 46 and the first hands of Codex Sinaiticus and Codex Vaticanus). Without them the verse reads "to God's holy people who are also faithful in Christ Jesus". This has led some to suggest that the letter was meant for more than one church.
[^1-3]: Or "and are believers". Greek *pistos* means "faithful, trustworthy" or "believing".
[^1-4]: Or "Praise be to". Greek *eulogetos*, from the same root as *blessed* and *blessing* later in the verse.
[^1-5]: Or "every blessing of the Spirit".
[^1-6]: Literally "in the heavenlies"; also in verse 20.
[^1-7]: Or, taking "in love" with verse 5, "… blameless before him. In love he destined us …".
[^1-8]: Greek *huiothesia*, "adoption as sons", the legal standing of an heir. Literally "for adoption as sons through Jesus Christ to him".
[^1-9]: Literally "to the praise of the glory of his grace". *Freely given*: the verb (*charitoo*) is formed from *charis*, "grace": "with which he graced us".
[^1-10]: Greek *apolytrosis*, release, as of a slave or captive set free by a payment; also in verse 14.
[^1-11]: Or "… lavished on us. In all wisdom and understanding he made known …", taking these words with verse 9.
[^1-12]: Greek *mysterion*, a secret plan that was hidden until God revealed it.
[^1-13]: That is, in Christ. Or "which he purposed in himself".
[^1-14]: Literally "for an administration of the fullness of the times". Greek *oikonomia*, the management of a household; here a plan that God carries out when the time is complete.
[^1-15]: Greek *anakephalaioomai*, "to sum up, to gather together", related to *kephale*, "head" (verse 22).
[^1-16]: Or "In him we have also received an inheritance". Greek *eklerothemen*, "we were allotted", which can mean being given a share or being chosen as someone's share.
[^1-17]: Literally "who hoped beforehand". "We" may be Paul and other Jewish believers, who hoped in the Messiah before the Gentiles addressed as "you" in verse 13; or it may mean all believers.
[^1-18]: Or "In him you also, when you heard … ; in him, when you believed, you were sealed". The Greek repeats "in whom".
[^1-19]: A seal marked a thing as belonging to its owner and guaranteed it.
[^1-20]: Literally "with the Holy Spirit of the promise".
[^1-21]: Greek *arrabon*, a deposit or down payment that guarantees the full amount will follow.
[^1-22]: Literally "for the redemption of the possession": either the redemption of God's people as his own possession, or the time when believers take possession of their inheritance.
[^1-23]: Some manuscripts leave out "your love", reading "your faith in the Lord Jesus and toward all God's holy people".
[^1-24]: Literally "the Father of glory".
[^1-25]: Or "a spirit". *As you come to know him*: literally "in knowledge of him".
[^1-26]: That is, the hope to which God has called you.
[^1-27]: Literally "in the holy ones".
[^1-28]: Literally "the working of the might of his strength".
[^1-29]: Greek *arche*, *exousia*, *dynamis*, *kyriotes*. These words were used for human rulers and also for ranks of spiritual powers.
[^1-30]: Psalm 8:7 (8:6 in most English Bibles).
[^1-31]: Literally "who fills all things in all". The verb can also be read as passive: "the fullness of the one who is being filled, all in all".
