## Chapter 23

*Status: draft*

**1** A psalm of David.[^23-1] Yahweh[^23-11] is my shepherd; I have everything I need.[^23-2]

**2** He lets me lie down in green pastures. He leads me beside quiet waters.[^23-3]

**3** He restores my life.[^23-4] He guides me along the right paths[^23-5] for the sake of his name.

**4** Even when I walk through the darkest valley,[^23-6] I will fear no harm, for you are with me. Your rod and your staff, they comfort me.

**5** You spread a table for me in front of my enemies. You anoint my head with oil; my cup overflows.

**6** Surely goodness and faithful love[^23-7] will follow me[^23-8] all the days of my life, and I will live in the house of Yahweh[^23-9] for all my days.[^23-10]

[^23-1]: Or "for David", "about David". In the Hebrew text the title is part of verse 1. In this psalm it shares the verse with the first line, so the verse numbers match English Bibles.
[^23-2]: Literally "I shall not lack".
[^23-3]: Literally "waters of rest".
[^23-4]: Hebrew *nephesh*: soul, life, self. Or "He refreshes me", "He brings me back".
[^23-5]: Or "paths of righteousness".
[^23-6]: Hebrew *tsalmavet*, "deep darkness", traditionally read as "the shadow of death".
[^23-7]: Hebrew *chesed*: loyal, faithful love.
[^23-8]: Literally "pursue me".
[^23-9]: The Hebrew vowel points read "I will return to the house of Yahweh". The same consonants can be read "I will dwell", as the Greek Septuagint understood them.
[^23-10]: Literally "for length of days", that is, for as long as I live.
[^23-11]: *Yahweh*: God's personal name (Hebrew YHWH), traditionally rendered "the LORD" in English Bibles.
